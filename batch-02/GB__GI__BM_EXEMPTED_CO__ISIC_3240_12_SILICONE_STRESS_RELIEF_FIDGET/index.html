Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Her Majesty's Government of Gibraltar - Income Tax Office (HMGoG ITO), operating under the Income Tax Act as amended; company and overseas-company registration is administered by the Gibraltar Companies Registry within the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC). Official portal: https://www.gibraltartax.gov.gi, with further corporate information available at https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi.

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company is not automatically within the Gibraltar tax net. A Gibraltar TIN is required only where the company establishes a place of business or registrable presence in Gibraltar, or where the company is managed and controlled in Gibraltar. Procedure: (1) file constitutional documents, registered office details, and beneficial ownership information with the Gibraltar Companies Registry; (2) apply to the HMGoG Income Tax Office on the prescribed registration form, providing legal form, directors, shareholders, registered office, and expected Gibraltar-source income; (3) submit a current Bermuda tax residence or exemption certificate and evidence of economic activity wherever applicable; (4) obtain the Gibraltar Income Tax TIN. No VAT ID is issued in Gibraltar because Gibraltar does not operate a VAT or GST system. If the entity makes taxable supplies in the United Kingdom or is treated as a non-established taxable person there, a separate UK VAT registration with HMRC is required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not provide a direct analogue to the U.S. check-the-box election. A foreign company may arrange for central management and control to be exercised outside Gibraltar, thereby remaining non-resident; however, if de facto management and control is in Gibraltar, the Income Tax Office will treat the company as tax resident notwithstanding any contrary foreign classification. Gibraltar's anti-hybrid rules, consistent with OECD BEPS Action 2, prevent deduction or non-inclusion mismatches from creating double non-taxation. A Bermuda Exempted Company's home-status exemption does not create a Gibraltar election to disregard the entity, and if another jurisdiction applies CFC or equivalent rules to the entity, no Gibraltar election can override that treatment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GIP is fully convertible at parity into GBP and, through London correspondent banking, freely into other reserve currencies. The current account is completely open: trade payments, service fees, royalties, and platform payouts require no licence or exchange-control filing. The capital account is equally open: inbound equity subscription funds, shareholder loans, and outbound dividend remittances may flow freely. The only practical hurdles are AML-driven KYC and source-of-funds verification, not statutory convertibility restrictions. To preserve the Bermuda Exempted Company's tax-exempt characteristic, management and control must remain outside Gibraltar; a Gibraltar-resident bank account does not in itself create Gibraltar tax residence, but profit remittances from a Gibraltar bank account are unrestricted and tax-free at source for a non-resident owner unless the company is managed and controlled in Gibraltar.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Gibraltar imposes no export proceeds surrender requirement and no mandatory foreign-exchange conversion ratio for export receipts. A Gibraltar-licensed commercial bank may allow exporters to retain 100% of export receipts indefinitely in USD, EUR, GBP, or GIP without any statutory timeline or surrender obligation. The only compliance duty is to keep underlying trade invoices, bills of lading, and payment orders for AML and tax-record purposes.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar has no independent central bank. The Gibraltar Currency Commission of H.M. Government of Gibraltar issues Gibraltar pound (GIP) banknotes and coinage at a fixed 1:1 parity with sterling. Prudential and AML supervision is performed by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC). Foreign exchange administration is effectively delegated to the market; there are no Gibraltar exchange-control statutes requiring central bank approval. The Bank of England acts as settlement agent for sterling clearing but has no supervisory jurisdiction over Gibraltar deposit-takers.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium as a secondary settlement rail; Low as the principal treasury account. Legal constraints: Offshore digital EMIs licensed in the UK, Lithuania, Latvia, or Malta do not hold any automatic passporting or branch-recognition rights in Gibraltar after Brexit. A non-Gibraltar EMI may not lawfully hold corporate liquidity for a Gibraltar-based treasury operation unless it obtains a Gibraltar e-money licence or a branch authorisation under the Financial Services (Electronic Money) Act. In practice, payment platforms such as PayPal, Wise, or Payoneer may be used for low-value platform payouts, but they are not permissible for warehouse capital injections, LG-backed trade settlements, or FX hedging collateral because they are not credit institutions under the Gibraltar banking framework.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Use only direct SWIFT MT103 from the registered shareholder's personal or corporate bank account into the Gibraltar capital escrow account. The beneficiary legal name must exactly match the Bermuda Exempted Company's registered name, and the remittance information must quote 'Share Capital Contribution – Board Resolution No. [date]'. No exchange-control approval is required. The bank will run OFAC/UN sanctions screening, match the remitter legal name to the registered shareholder register, and may request a certified board resolution, updated capital verification certificate, and certificate of good standing before crediting funds above GBP 50,000. Inbound shareholder loans are accepted under MT103 with a separate intercompany loan agreement and an arm's length interest rate; the wire must state 'Intercompany Loan' in the purpose field. Funds in GIP/GBP clear via London correspondent banks; funds in USD clear through the New York Fedwire system with the final leg to the Gibraltar bank's correspondent.Settlement of import invoices for raw silicone polymer, pigment, and moulded tactile parts should be executed by direct SWIFT MT103 from the operational account when the single invoice value is below GBP 100,000 or a confirmed business relationship exists. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: A mandatory Local File is triggered where annual cross-border transactions with related parties exceed GBP 1 million; a Master File is required where the multinational group reports consolidated revenue exceeding EUR 750 million; regardless of threshold, all controlled transactions must be supported by contemporaneous arm's length pricing evidence filed with or on request by the Commissioner, and Box 13 of the CT-1 constitutes the annual self-certification.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance (Company and Provision of Information) Regulations 2019 requires any Gibraltar tax-resident company performing a 'relevant activity' to demonstrate real presence through local management and control, adequate premises, proportionate local employees, and core income-generating activities performed in Gibraltar; the manufacture of silicone stress-relief fidget toys under ISIC 3240 is not a listed relevant activity, but the company must nonetheless maintain genuine commercial substance to preserve its Gibraltar residence and treaty access; separately, the Bermuda Exempted Company is subject to the Bermuda Economic Substance Act 2018, under which manufacturing is also not a relevant activity, yet the annual filing obligations and the assessment of whether any incidental IP-holding or distribution function creates ESR exposure must be reviewed each year.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Misstatement

Statutory sanction: Civil tax-geared penalty of up to 100% of the underpaid tax plus statutory interest at 8% per annum; criminal prosecution for tax evasion under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010 attracts a fine of up to GBP 100,000 and imprisonment of up to seven years; confiscation orders and asset-freezing orders may be imposed under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly authorized the evasion are jointly and severally liable for the company's tax and penalties; fraudulent trading liability arises under the Gibraltar Companies Act 2014, permitting the court to hold directors personally accountable for the company's debts; travel bans, director disqualification orders, and imprisonment are available for the controlling officers.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Denial or revocation of the Gibraltar certificate of tax residence; reallocation of profit under the UK-Gibraltar DTA tie-breaker to the UK or other jurisdiction with full tax and interest; economic substance penalties in Gibraltar of up to GBP 50,000 per year plus daily default fines; separate Bermuda ESR penalties of up to BMD 250,000 per year for non-compliance with economic substance and filing duties.

Director personal liability: Directors face wrongful trading liability if the company traded without genuine substance and became insolvent; personal liability for restitution of improperly extracted funds; disqualification under the Gibraltar Company Directors Disqualification Act and immigration travel bans where flight risk is established.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Adjustment by the Commissioner reallocating the arm's length profit to Gibraltar; a penalty of up to 100% of the additional tax assessed; statutory interest on the underpayment; disclosure of the adjustment in the public tax assessment register in egregious cases.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed the financial statements owe a fiduciary duty to ensure arm's length pricing; they are personally liable for fraudulent or negligent misstatements made in the CT-1; criminal sanctions apply where the transfer pricing documentation contains false declarations.
Disguised Profit Repatriation and Director Loan Account Abuse

Statutory sanction: Recharacterization of the loans as deemed distributions or employment-related benefits; corporate penalty and interest on the unreported amounts; failure to file distribution returns attracts an additional penalty; a UK CFC charge may arise where the Bermuda parent is managed or controlled from the UK.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally taxed on benefits-in-kind received; they may be ordered by the Gibraltar court to restore misappropriated company funds; abuse of fiduciary duty leads to director disqualification and, where dishonesty is proven, criminal liability for Theft Act offences.
Anti-Money Laundering and Beneficial Ownership Concealment

Statutory sanction: Unlimited corporate fines under the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and AML/CFT Regulations; personal fines of up to GBP 5 million and imprisonment of up to fourteen years for the responsible officers; freezing orders, travel bans, and revocation of the company's registration and banking facilities by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission.

Director personal liability: Directors and the nominated Money Laundering Reporting Officer are individually liable for compliance failures; the court may impose travel bans to prevent departure, disqualification from acting as a director, extradition assistance under UK-Gibraltar arrangements, and confiscation of personal assets derived from the concealed profits.
