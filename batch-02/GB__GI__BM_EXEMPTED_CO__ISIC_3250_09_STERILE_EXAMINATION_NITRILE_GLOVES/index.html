Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax), HM Government of Gibraltar, Ministry of Finance; online filing portal 'GTO e-Services' (www.gto.gov.gi); customs administration via Gibraltar Customs and Borders Agency with declarations processed through the UK Customs Declaration Service (CDS).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Register the Bermuda Exempted Company as an overseas company under the Gibraltar Companies Act 2014 with the Registrar of Companies within one month of establishing a place of business. Step 2: Submit Form A (Company Registration Particulars) to the Gibraltar Tax Office to obtain a Gibraltar Income Tax Reference Number; no minimum capital is required. Step 3: Apply for a UK-format EORI number (prefix GB) through HMRC for all customs transactions in the joint UK-Gibraltar customs territory, unless only the income tax registration is needed. Step 4: Register for the Payment of Tax (PAYE) system and Social Insurance contributions where any Gibraltar payroll arises. Step 5: Notify the Commissioner of any Gibraltar-source income; the Commissioner may open an assessment file even in the absence of incorporation in Gibraltar. Step 6: Maintain the statutory medical device conformity file and appoint a UK/EU responsible person where required for ISIC 3250 sterile nitrile gloves.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No US-style 'check-the-box' election exists. Available elections include: (i) designation of the group parent entity for Gibraltar Country-by-Country reporting; (ii) hybrid mismatch relief adjustments under the Income Tax (International Tax Compliance) (Hybrid Mismatch) Regulations, including the opt-in to OECD-aligned modifications for structured arrangements; (iii) simplified return and record-keeping treatment for small/micro companies; and (iv) election to be treated as non-resident where central management and control is exercised outside Gibraltar and no Gibraltar-source income arises.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST - Gibraltar is excluded from the EU VAT Area; indirect taxation is limited to customs duties, excise duties and stamp duties under the Customs Ordinance (Cap. 121) and Stamp Duties Ordinance.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT registration threshold exists. All importers/exporters must hold a UK-format GB EORI number. For income tax, every company carrying on activity in Gibraltar, regardless of turnover, must notify the Gibraltar Tax Office and file an annual return; no minimum turnover threshold applies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar levies no VAT, so no export VAT zero-rating or refund substantiation mechanism applies; exports remain outside the scope of any consumption tax. For customs purposes, goods exported from Gibraltar attract zero export duty provided an export declaration is lodged and the goods are physically removed from the joint UK-Gibraltar customs territory within statutory time limits. The ISIC 3250 sterile examination nitrile gloves must also carry the required UKCA/CE conformity marking under the Medical Devices Regulations 2002 (SI 2002/618, as amended) for the customs file, and compliant export permits must be retained for the UK-Gibraltar customs framework.

  • Customs Export Declaration (UK CDS submission with Movement Reference Number)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (proof of physical export)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate, SWIFT MT103 or verified settlement evidence (proof of export proceeds)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin (EUR.1/GSP Form A where the destination trade agreement requires it)
  • Medical device conformity file (UKCA/CE certificate and Declaration of Conformity under MDR 2017/745 for sterile examination gloves)

No real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing API or digital stamping mandate operates in Gibraltar. B2B invoicing and record keeping follow the Income Tax Act 2010 (retention for six years). Customs declarations are submitted electronically via the UK CDS with mandatory pre-arrival/pre-departure filing and electronic supporting documentation, aligned with UK ICS2 requirements for goods entering the UK-Gibraltar joint customs territory.

Fraudulent evasion of customs duty or export control misdeclaration is a criminal offence punishable on indictment by up to seven years' imprisonment and/or an unlimited fine, with forfeiture of goods. False or defective export declarations attract civil penalties equal to 100% of the duty avoided plus interest and retrospective clawback of any tax-geared benefit. Under the Income Tax Act 2010, a deliberate inaccuracy in a return or claim carries a penalty of up to 100% of the additional tax, the Commissioner may reopen the preceding six years (20 years in cases of fraud), and anti-facilitation provisions extend civil and criminal liability to officers, advisors and connected shareholders.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current-account and capital-account convertibility with zero exchange controls; the Gibraltar Pound (GIP) is pegged at 1:1 to GBP and circulates interchangeably with GBP as legal tender in Gibraltar; no prior administrative authorization is required for dividend repatriation, capital redemption, intercompany loan servicing, or transfer of liquidation proceeds back to Bermuda; no Gibraltarian withholding tax exists on dividends, interest, royalties, or service fees, so outbound remittances to the Bermuda Exempted Company suffer zero effective leakage; the only practical constraint is the bank's form T1 tax-residency questionnaire, which confirms that the entity does not derive Gibraltar-source trading income, or if it does, that such income is disclosed and taxed at the 12.5% Gibraltar corporate rate; the historical high-velocity nature of PPE trade and sanctioned-jurisdiction screening require accurate ultimate-beneficial-owner declarations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Gibraltar and the UK impose no statutory surrender timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no requirement to deposit export receipts with any central authority; proceeds from the sale of sterile examination gloves may be held indefinitely in USD, EUR, GBP, or GIP as treasury policy dictates; the only obligation is statistical: companies with annual export receipts exceeding GBP 1,000,000 equivalent must submit an annual Balance of Payments return (Bank of England/Gibraltar Statistics category B - export of goods), and failure to file within 30 days of the statutory notice attracts a warning rather than a monetary penalty; there is no restriction on netting invoiced exports against raw-material imports in a single USD clearing stream.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England and HM Treasury (monetary anchor for GBP); Government of Gibraltar Commissioner of Currency (GIP note issuance and 1:1 GBP parity maintenance); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) (prudential supervision of all banks and EMIs licensed in Gibraltar); Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) (consolidated supervisor of the Bermuda Exempted Company parent). There is no separate Gibraltar central bank; monetary policy is UK-determined.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium feasibility with strict legal constraints. Only EMIs licensed by the GFSC under the Financial Services Act 2019 may onboard the Bermuda Exempted Company as a Gibraltar customer; post-Brexit, UK FCA-regulated and EU-licensed EMIs cannot passport into Gibraltar, and most such platforms reject pure offshore structures without a Gibraltar incorporation or physical board presence. GFSC-licensed EMIs offer virtual IBANs in GBP/EUR/USD with interbank-plus-30-to-50-bps FX conversion and account opening within 5-10 business days; however, e-money safeguarding must be held in a segregated client account at an approved Gibraltar or UK bank, eliminating float yield, and USD inbound clearing adds T+1 to T+2 settlement delay versus a direct commercial-bank account. Recommendation: use a GFSC EMI as a secondary collection rail for small EU hospital orders, never as the primary treasury account.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GBP 10,000,000

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations 2020 apply to companies conducting relevant activities including distribution and service centre operations and pure equity holding. Although high-speed dipping manufacturing of nitrile gloves is not itself a classified relevant activity, the Bermuda exempted company must satisfy the Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations 2018 equivalent tests and, to the extent any distribution or head-office activity is performed in Gibraltar, must demonstrate locally performed core income-generating activities, operating expenditure, premises, and full-time resident employees in Gibraltar. Annual economic substance declarations are mandatory; failure to file is penal and may result in information exchange with HMRC under the UK-Gibraltar arrangement and loss of tax-treaty benefits.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Penalty of up to 100% of the tax undercharged plus interest at 8% per annum; on conviction for fraudulent tax evasion, a fine of up to GIP 100,000 or imprisonment for up to five years, or both.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign false returns or cause the company to conceal profits are jointly and severally liable for the penalty; the GITO may obtain travel restriction orders, a director disqualification for up to 15 years, and criminal convictions exposing directors to imprisonment.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Daily penalties of GIP 500 escalating to GIP 2,500 per day for continued economic substance failure; strike-off of the Gibraltar overseas company registration; cross-border information exchange to Bermuda and HMRC; loss of access to treaty and zero-tax treatment.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal fines of up to GIP 10,000 and imprisonment for up to two years for a false economic substance declaration; travel restrictions may be imposed and the entity's bank accounts frozen upon referral to the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit.
AML / Trade-Based Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 (Gibraltar), money laundering carries a maximum penalty of 14 years' imprisonment and an unlimited fine; the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission may also impose civil fines of up to GIP 3 million or 10% of annual turnover.

Director personal liability: Directors involved in trade-based money laundering are subject to confiscation orders for the full value of laundered funds, civil restitution claims by creditors and liquidators, personal travel bans, and imprisonment; enforcement extends to shadow directors and beneficial owners.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Default

Statutory sanction: The GITO may adjust profits to the arm's-length level and impose a penalty of 50% of the additional tax arising, increased to 100% where the default is deliberate; interest at 8% per annum runs from the original filing date.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign accounts incorporating non-arm's-length related-party pricing may be personally assessed for the company's additional tax liability where the company is dissolved or struck off; disqualification proceedings may also be commenced.
Hidden UK Permanent Establishment / CFC Attribution

Statutory sanction: HMRC may assess the UK-attributable profits at the UK corporation tax rate of 25%, impose penalties of up to 100% of the tax under HMRC's failure-to-notify and deliberate-inaccuracy regimes, and charge late-payment interest.

Director personal liability: Directors face joint and several liability for the UK permanent establishment's tax where the entity ceases business without proper notification; prosecution is possible under the Criminal Finances Act 2017 for facilitation of tax evasion, with civil restitution and travel restrictions available to HMRC.
