Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone - treated as a non-resident tax-exempt corporation under Gibraltar income tax legislation, subject to 0% tax on non-Gibraltar income; under Bermuda law, an exempted company receives an undertaking that it shall be exempt from all Bermuda taxes until 31 March 2035.

Available election forms

No check-the-box election or pass-through election is recognized in Gibraltar. Elective statuses include: (i) applying for non-resident company status (Income Tax Act s.25) to restrict tax to Gibraltar-source income; (ii) making a Bermuda annual compliance filing to maintain exempted status; (iii) selecting a 12-month financial period for tax accounting; (iv) opting to claim unilateral foreign tax credits against any residual Gibraltar tax under the Income Tax (Unilateral Relief) Rules. Hybrid mismatch elections under EU/ATAD are not applicable; Gibraltar has not implemented OECD hybrid entity elections.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None - Gibraltar does not impose VAT/GST or general sales tax; indirect taxation is limited to customs import duty, excise duty, and stamp duty.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A - There is no VAT registration threshold because no VAT regime is in force. Any person importing goods is required to be registered with HM Customs of Gibraltar. A Consumable Goods Import Licence may be required for low-value commercial samples; no monetary threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable for Gibraltar VAT because no VAT/GST is charged. If the entity is also registered for UK VAT (e.g., via a UK branch) then the export of designer toys from the UK to non-UK customers may be zero-rated under UK VAT law (section 30, VATA 1994) when the goods are exported by the VAT-registered vendor and evidence is held within 3 months. For Gibraltar-customs purposes, export goods are free of export duty.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration (SAD C88) or Exit Summary Declaration
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill with consignee and destination port
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin (for any preferential origin claim)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / Bank SWIFT Statement evidencing actual receipt in a Gibraltar or Bermuda bank account
  • Certificate of Free Sale (if required by importing jurisdiction)
  • UK C88 Export Accompanying Document (where UK VAT zero-rating is claimed)

Gibraltar has no mandatory real-time e-invoicing/clearance regime (no continuous transaction controls, no digital stamping). Taxpayers must maintain verifiable invoice records for 6 years. For customs, electronic declarations are made through the Gibraltar Customs System (GCS); an Electronic Data Interchange (EDI) and AES portal are used for export clearances. UK VAT zero-rating compliance may require MTD-compatible e-invoicing when selling into the UK.

The Gibraltar Commissioner of Income Tax may conduct post-clearance audits and customs checks for up to six years from the date of export. Where export zero-rating is claimed improperly or substantiating documents are false, the tax/customs authority may issue a duty/tax assessment demanding the unpaid VAT (if applicable in UK context), a maximum penalty of 200% of the tax evaded, criminal fines, and imprisonment for directors under the Customs and Income Tax Acts. If proceeds are not repatriated within 180 days, Gibraltar exchange-control regulations may treat the export as an unremitted sale and impose 10% territorial tax on deemed profits; UK customs may disqualify the trader from future simplified export declarations. A clawback of VAT zero-rating previously granted may apply with interest from the original export date.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar maintains full current and capital account convertibility. There are no administrative exchange controls, no profit remittance licensing requirements, and no restrictions on dividend repatriation for an exempted company. However, all cross-border flows remain subject to applicable AML/CTF and sanctions screening by the ordering, intermediary, and beneficiary banks; certain receivables from sanctioned jurisdictions may be blocked and require enhanced due diligence before release.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion ratio applies in Gibraltar or the United Kingdom. Exporters are free to retain receipts in any convertible currency, but must maintain supporting trade documentation (invoices, customs declarations, proof of shipment) for a minimum of six years for GFSC and HM Revenue and Customs eventual information access purposes.

Central bank supervision: Government of Gibraltar (for Gibraltar pound issuance) and the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) (as integrated banking conduct and prudential regulator); the Bank of England operates as the ultimate monetary backstop for the Gibraltar pound's one-to-one sterling convertibility.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Digital cross-border EMI services (e.g., Revolut Business, Wise Business, Currencycloud) are feasible for day-to-day stream payments, marketplace settlements, and FX conversions, but remain risk-mitigation tools rather than primary treasury anchors. Feasibility rating: High for subsidiary operational liquidity, Low for statutory escrowed capital. Legal constraints: EMIs are not full credit institutions under Gibraltar Banking Act 2019; balances are safeguarded rather than deposit-protected; many EMIs restrict onboarding for tax-exempt offshore vehicles and may require proof of physical trading substance. An exempted company dealing in designer toys should therefore secure at least one GFSC-regulated tier-1 bank account before relying on EMI rails.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File required when controlled cross-border transactions with associated enterprises exceed GIP 250,000 in aggregate per annum; Master File required when the worldwide group's consolidated turnover exceeds EUR 750 million (or GIP equivalent); SMEs (fewer than 250 employees, turnover not exceeding GIP 50 million, balance sheet not exceeding GIP 43 million) are exempt from documentation but must still apply the arm's length principle.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda ESR (2019) applies only to relevant activities. Manufacturing of vinyl art collectibles (ISIC 3240) is not a Bermuda relevant activity; the entity must nonetheless file Form ESR01. If the entity also operates a distribution or service-centre for finished toys, the Distribution and Service Centre relevant activity is triggered and requires demonstrable physical presence, full-time employees and CIGA management in Bermuda, failing which exchange of information notices and BMD 250,000 penalties follow. Gibraltar applies substance-over-form and OECD PE profit attribution: a Bermuda Exempted Company without employees, premises or board activity in Bermuda, managed from Gibraltar, risks Gibraltar tax residency at 12.5% and GAAR counteraction of artificial offshore profit diversion.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion - Gibraltar Corporation Tax

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty up to 100% of the tax understated (up to 200% for deliberate concealment), statutory simple interest at 8%, and criminal liability upon conviction: unlimited fine and imprisonment up to 7 years under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act and Criminal Justice framework.

Director personal liability: Directors and shadow directors who knowingly sign false returns are personally liable to restitution of the tax; Proceeds of Crime Act confiscation orders; travel bans and exit alert restrictions; disqualification for up to 15 years.
