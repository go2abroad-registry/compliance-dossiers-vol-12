Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Her Majesty's Government of Gibraltar - Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax); customs and excise enforcement is administered by the Gibraltar Customs Department (GCommunity electronic gateway). Bermuda counterpart co-competent for the Exempted Company: Registrar of Companies and Department of Corporate Tax, Government of Bermuda.

E-government filing portal

For a Bermuda Exempted Company ('Exempted Co') engaging the Gibraltar territory: (1) obtain the Bermuda Certificate of Incorporation and the certificate of tax protection under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 to evidence full tax-exempt status in the home jurisdiction; (2) apply to the Gibraltar Commissioner of Income Tax for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (GRN) using the company registration details form if the company intends to maintain assets, personnel, contracts, or a branch in Gibraltar; (3) concurrently file an External Company Registration statement with the Gibraltar Companies Registry if a physical place of business is opened; (4) register with the Gibraltar Customs Department for a Trader Identification Number (TIN) and electronic customs system access to clear export consignments of instant rice noodle products; and (5) confirm, through a tax opinion, that no Gibraltar income-tax registration is required if the Exempted Co confines its activities to export sales concluded outside Gibraltar and avoids having central management and control in Gibraltar.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — For a Bermuda Exempted Company ('Exempted Co') engaging the Gibraltar territory: (1) obtain the Bermuda Certificate of Incorporation and the certificate of tax protection under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 to evidence full tax-exempt status in the home jurisdiction; (2) apply to the Gibraltar Commissioner of Income Tax for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (GRN) using the company registration details form if the company intends to maintain assets, personnel, contracts, or a branch in Gibraltar; (3) concurrently file an External Company Registration statement with the Gibraltar Companies Registry if a physical place of business is opened; (4) register with the Gibraltar Customs Department for a Trader Identification Number (TIN) and electronic customs system access to clear export consignments of instant rice noodle products; and (5) confirm, through a tax opinion, that no Gibraltar income-tax registration is required if the Exempted Co confines its activities to export sales concluded outside Gibraltar and avoids having central management and control in Gibraltar.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

The Exempted Co may activate the Bermuda Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection election (guaranteed exemption from future Bermuda taxation until 2035). In Gibraltar, there is no check-the-box election, but an annual election may be made to be assessed on a non-resident basis, restricting chargeability to Gibraltar-source income only; hybrid mismatch rules under the Income Tax Act 2010, as amended, disapply a Gibraltarian deduction where the same economic expense is not included in any taxable income in Bermuda; a large Exempted Co may also be subject to the statutory Gross Income Based Assessment (GIBA), which applies a gross-receipts floor to the otherwise computed profits-based tax charge.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT or GST applies in Gibraltar; the Purchase Tax Act 1972 imposes tax on the importation of listed goods and on certain local supplies. Registration is mandatory for any importer or trader dealing in taxable goods from the first importation; no de minimis turnover threshold applies. Export-only businesses, such as an Exempted Co exporting instant rice noodles, are not required to register for Purchase Tax unless they also make taxable domestic supplies or claim input remission; in all cases a customs Trader Identification Number (TIN) is required for export clearance.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% rate is available under the Gibraltar Purchase Tax system for goods physically exported from Gibraltar to a destination outside Gibraltar, provided that: (i) the Exempted Co holds a binding contract of sale with a non-Gibraltar buyer; (ii) the goods are shipped within three months of the supply date through the Gibraltar customs territory; (iii) transport documentation (bill of lading or airway bill) names the overseas consignee; (iv) sale proceeds are invoiced in a freely convertible currency and remitted to the Exempted Co's bank account within 180 days of export; and (v) no value attributable to branding, packaging, or ancillary services is derived from a supply connected with Gibraltar in respect of which Purchase Tax was chargeable.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration (electronic Single Administrative Document entry filed in GCommunity)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill citing the overseas port of discharge
  • Commercial invoice and detailed packing list showing HS Code 1902.30 treatment for instant rice noodles
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank credit advice evidencing receipt of export proceeds in foreign currency)
  • Certificate of Origin (Bermuda or Gibraltar) and, where applicable, a non-preferential movement certificate

Gibraltar does not operate a real-time national e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, or digital stamping mandate. Customs export clearance must be submitted electronically through the GCommunity Electronic Customs Data System using the Single Administrative Document format, no earlier than 30 days and no later than 24 hours before goods depart Gibraltar by sea or air. Invoices, packing lists, export declarations, and certificates of origin must be retained in a machine-readable format and made available to the Commissioner of Income Tax and the Comptroller of Customs for six years.

The Gibraltar Commissioner of Income Tax and the Gibraltar Customs Department jointly audit zero-rated export claims. Where a claimed export is unsupported by the mandatory documentation bundle, or the foreign currency proceeds are not repatriated within 180 days without prior approval, the zero-rating is reversed and the company is assessed to Purchase Tax at 15% on the invoice value, plus statutory late-payment interest. Fraudulent export practices trigger clawback of all previously refunded tax, monetary penalties of up to 200% of the tax involved, criminal fines and confiscation of the goods, and disqualification of directors or officers of the Exempted Co from serving as officers of any Gibraltarian company for up to 15 years; criminal liability may also arise for knowingly submitting false export documents.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar Pound (GIP) is fully convertible at a statutory 1:1 peg to GBP. Both current-account and capital-account transactions are unrestricted in Gibraltar; no Exchange Control Act applies. Profit remittance (dividends, interest, royalties, branch repatriation) is free of prior approval or withholding, provided CRS/FATCA filings and Gibraltar economic-substance obligations are current. The only practical hurdle is bank-level documentation proving tax residence, economic substance and source of funds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or mandatory conversion of export proceeds under Gibraltar or UK law. Exporters may retain 100% of foreign-currency receipts indefinitely; conversion of USD to GIP/GBP is commercially driven and executed at the pegged rate, not mandated by regulation.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority exercising oversight through HM Treasury); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as prudential banking regulator; Government of Gibraltar Currency Notes Board for Gibraltar Pound issuance

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File/Master File are mandatory where intra-group transactions exceed GBP 1,000,000 in aggregate per accounting period; Country-by-Country Reporting applies to groups with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar ESR targets geographically mobile activities only; the ISIC 1079 instant noodle manufacturing trade is not a relevant activity, but commercial substance (premises, staff, expenditure and records) must still be evidenced for the PE. The Bermuda Exempted Company must nonetheless file the annual Bermuda Economic Substance Declaration and maintain its exempted status fee schedule; manufacturing in Gibraltar does not prejudice the Bermuda exemption.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Tax-geared penalty up to 100% of the additional tax (50% for neglect) plus interest; criminal prosecution for tax fraud with imprisonment of up to 5 years and unlimited fines.

Director personal liability: Directors, effective managers and the Gibraltar tax agent are jointly exposed to civil restitution, personal tax assessments following veil-piercing, asset freezing orders, travel bans and disqualification orders.
Shell Company

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar ESR administrative penalties and enhanced information exchange; Bermuda ESR: penalty of BD$10,000 plus BD$10,000 per additional month of default, with deregistration risk.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors face personal penalties under both regimes, disqualification from directorships, civil liability for corporate taxes where the company is treated as a sham, and travel bans pending enforcement.
Transfer Pricing Misstatement

Statutory sanction: Unilateral reassessment to the arm's-length price; additional tax at 12.5%; late-payment interest; accuracy-related penalty of up to 100% of the resulting tax.

Director personal liability: Liability is assumed where directors sign the tax return knowing the pricing to be false; joint and several liability for the assessment, professional conduct referrals, and criminal sanctions for false declarations.
AML and Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Fines up to GBP 500,000 for AML/BO infringements; personal fines up to GBP 50,000 for officers in default; confiscation and increased tax assessments; deregistration of the branch.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal criminal liability, unlimited monetary penalties, disqualification, travel bans where there is a flight risk, and civil exposure to third-party creditors for trading without proper substance.
