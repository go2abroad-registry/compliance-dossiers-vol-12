Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Commissioner of Income Tax – Income Tax Office, Government of Gibraltar (official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/tax-and-duties); corporate registry: Companies House Gibraltar (https://www.companieshouse.gi).

E-government filing portal

1) File Form OS-1 with Companies House Gibraltar to register the Bermuda Exempted Company as an overseas company, appointing a Gibraltar resident agent and registered office. 2) Apply to the Income Tax Office for a Tax Reference Number (TRN) presenting certified Bermuda incorporation and exempted status certificates, company constitution, and directors' particulars. 3) Obtain a Gibraltar EORI number (GI prefix) from the Customs Department for import/export operations. 4) File annual corporate tax returns (Form C) and make payments on account; maintain books and records per the Companies Act 2014.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope – the Bermuda exempt status does not exempt Gibraltar-source profits; resident status arises from incorporation in Gibraltar or central management and control exercised in Gibraltar, and is subject to 12.5% corporate tax on Gibraltar-source income (foreign-source income taxable only on remittance).

Available election forms

No check-the-box. Elections include: SME 'Qualifying Company' election (10% rate on first £400,000 profits); group loss-relief election (ITA 2010 ss.70–72); Notional Interest Deduction (NID) election on incremental qualifying equity; remittance-basis election for foreign-source profits; and capital allowances election. OECD hybrid mismatch rules are implemented via the Income Tax (Hybrid Mismatches) Rules 2019 – no elective carve-outs for US-style disregarded entities.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar Indirect Tax System: Import Duty and Excise Duty only – no VAT, GST, or sales/use tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT/GST threshold (no VAT in Gibraltar). Customs (EORI) registration and import declarations are mandatory from the first commercial import, without a monetary threshold; excise duty licences apply to alcohol, tobacco and hydrocarbon oil.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

VAT zero-rating is inapplicable because Gibraltar levies no VAT. Export supplies of self-heating meal products from Gibraltar are outside the scope of import duty and are thus taxed at 0%. Claiming duty-free treatment for previously duty-suspended inputs under inward processing relief requires proof of export within the customs-authorised period; no VAT refund mechanism exists.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD / Single Administrative Declaration C88)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical removal from Gibraltar
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank credit advice evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin (non-preferential or applicable UK/EU preferental origin)
  • Inward Processing Relief records for duty-suspended inputs

No statutory e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, or digital stamping regime. Customs SAD declarations are filed electronically via the Gibraltar Customs electronic data interchange; e-invoicing is voluntary (PEPPOL standards optionally used).

As no VAT zero-rating applies, anti-abuse scrutiny focuses on customs duty relief and transfer pricing. Fraudulent export declarations attract duty clawback (100% of evaded duty), civil penalties up to treble the evaded amount (Customs Ordinance s.125), forfeiture of goods and vehicles, and up to 7 years' imprisonment. Inward processing relief is retrospectively clawed back with interest upon failure to evidence re-export within the approved period; HM Commissioner may also apply a 120% gross-up under general penalty provisions.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar maintains full convertibility on both current and capital account. There is no separation between capital and current account for GIP/GBP by statute, and profit repatriation is not subject to prior central bank approval. Practical hurdles are limited to bank-driven Anti-Money Laundering (AML) due diligence, source-of-funds verification, and confirmation that any profit remittance is supported by audited/trial balance evidence and a valid tax residence certificate where applicable.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no mandatory surrender requirement or statutory conversion ratio for export proceeds receivable in GIP/GBP. Export receipts may be retained in a convertible foreign currency. Reporting obligations exist under the Exchange Control (Reporting) Rules for settlements exceeding GIP/GBP 10,000, including the submission of a Foreign Exchange Settlement Statement to the Exchange Control Authority within 30 days of the month-end in which the receipt was cleared.

Central bank supervision: HM Government of Gibraltar – Exchange Control Authority, acting jointly with the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the licensed banking regulator; the Bank of England anchors the Gibraltar pound (GIP) at 1:1 parity with Sterling.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Rated Medium for regulated UK and EU e-money institutions (e.g. Wise Platform, Revolut Business) provided they explicitly accept Gibraltar-registered corporate clients under their internal jurisdiction policies. Rated Low for offshore digital EMIs lacking a GFSC e-money licence, because Gibraltar does not provide passporting for non-EEA EMIs, and an unlicensed EMI cannot access Gibraltar clearing systems. A local GFSC-licensed EMI may be used as a treasury overlay but will ultimately settle through a partner Tier-1 commercial bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity or shareholder-loan capital must arrive via SWIFT MT103 or MT202 from the parent or an institutional lender. The payment should reference purpose code 'G02' (trade settlement) for operational funding and 'L230' (shareholder loan / equity participation) for capital injection. For injections above GIP/GBP 50,000, the Gibraltar receiving bank will require a board resolution, a copy of the share certificate or loan agreement, and a completed Exchange Control Acknowledgement form to be retained on the entity’s corporate banking file.Primary clearing rail is SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfer routed through the correspondent banking network to London CHAPS, with ultimate USD/EUR settlement via clearing intermediaries. For bulk imported exothermic components or export sales above GIP/GBP 250,000, a confirmed commercial letter of credit (MT700) or documentary collection (MT400) is recommended.
