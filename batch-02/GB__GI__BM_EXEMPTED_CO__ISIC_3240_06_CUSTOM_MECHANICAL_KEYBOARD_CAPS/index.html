Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a US-style check-the-box election. Classification follows the statutory residence test (central management and control) and source rules: a BEC managed and controlled in Gibraltar is a Gibraltar-resident company taxable at 12.5% on worldwide income; a BEC with only a Gibraltar permanent establishment is taxable only on Gibraltar-source profits attributable to that PE; a BEC with neither is not Gibraltar-taxable. Available elections and claims: documented governance election to hold board meetings and conclude contracts outside Gibraltar (preserving non-residence); group relief and consortium relief claims for resident groups; unilateral foreign-tax credit relief; and ATAD-aligned hybrid mismatch adjustments (double-deduction denial) where the BEC's exempt status creates mismatch outcomes. The historic exempt-company status (0% tax) was abolished with effect from 1 January 2011 and cannot be elected.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Customs Import Duty, Excise Duty and Purchase Tax regime; Gibraltar levies no VAT, GST, or sales and use tax, and turnover of goods and services within Gibraltar is outside any indirect tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT or GST turnover threshold exists. Customs and EORI registration is mandatory for every importer or exporter without any de minimis turnover; excise and purchase-tax licensing is required only for specified goods (e.g., alcohol, tobacco, fuel, luxury items). Reference currency: GIP pegged 1:1 to GBP.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar has no VAT, so no VAT zero-rating claim or output-VAT refund mechanism exists. The relevant export relief is the absence of Gibraltar duty when goods do not enter Gibraltar: double-shot PBT keyboard keycaps manufactured in Asia and drop-shipped directly to foreign end-customers (including UK and EU buyers) never cross Gibraltar customs territory, generating no Gibraltar import duty, excise, or purchase tax. Where goods are temporarily imported into Gibraltar for onward re-export, duty relief is available under inward-processing or customs-warehousing procedures subject to re-export within the authorised period. Shipments into the UK or EU may attract destination-country import VAT or OSS/IOSS obligations, which are outside Gibraltar's system.

  • Customs Export Declaration, Bill of Lading, Inward Remittance Certificate

No real-time fiscal clearance, prescribed XML/API e-invoicing, or digital stamping regime applies in Gibraltar. Customs declarations must be submitted electronically through licensed customs agents on the Gibraltar electronic customs clearance platform. B2B invoices must be issued and retained under Companies Act record-keeping obligations (6 years) but carry no fiscal QR code, government stamping, or continuous transaction-control requirement.

HM Customs Gibraltar and the Income Tax Office may audit shipping manifests, courier tracking records, customs broker declarations, board minutes, payment and remittance flows, and e-commerce platform reports. Misstatements or abusive reliance on Bermuda exempt status to avoid Gibraltar taxation trigger: forfeiture of goods and customs penalties of up to three times the duty evaded under the Customs Ordinance; criminal prosecution for fraudulent evasion with fines and imprisonment of up to 7 years; retrospective duty and excise clawback for up to 7 years; and re-assessment of the BEC as Gibraltar-resident or as holding a Gibraltar permanent establishment, exposing worldwide or Gibraltar-source profits to 12.5% corporate tax plus interest and penalties of up to 100% of tax, with ATAD, CFC, and hybrid-mismatch adjustments applied where relevant.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar, as a British Overseas Territory, uses the Gibraltar pound (GIP) at par with the pound sterling (GBP); sterling is fully convertible for both current and capital account transactions. There are no general exchange control restrictions on profit remittance, dividend distribution, or capital repatriation for non-resident owned exempted companies. Although a Bermudian exempted company is a foreign-incorporated entity, its Gibraltar banking and treasury flows are treated under the territory's open current and capital account regime. The only practical hurdles are standard anti-money laundering (AML) source-of-funds verification and the need to evidence economic substance or permitted offshore activity where applicable.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory requirement exists in Gibraltar or the United Kingdom to surrender export proceeds or to convert foreign currency receipts into local currency. Under the retained EU Capital Movement Directive framework and current UK/Gibraltar domestic law, export receipts may be held in any freely convertible currency, and there is no mandatory conversion ratio or timeline. For goods or services exported from a non-Gibraltar operating entity, the relevant invoicing and settlement currency is a matter of commercial contract, subject only to financial sanctions screening and UK trade controls.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority) together with His Majesty's Treasury and the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission for oversight of Gibraltar-based payment and banking activity; exchange control is not separately administered by a Gibraltar central bank.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-High for non-bank electronic money institutions (EMIs) and payment institutions. Gibraltar licences EMIs and payment institutions under the Financial Services (Electronic Money) Act 2011 and the Financial Services (Payment Services) Act 2011, as amended. A Bermudian exempted company may open an EMI account remotely, but the EMI itself must maintain a safeguarding account with a regulated credit institution. The key legal constraint is that the EMI cannot offer credit and must not be used as a substitute for a full corporate bank account when large capital injections or trade finance instruments (e.g. letters of credit) are required. For low-risk standard operations using Shopify Payments and Stripe, an EMI gateway is viable, but the final settlement layer must clear through a licensed bank.

0% - Gibraltar does not levy withholding tax on dividends paid to residents or non-residents; no deduction at source is applied to dividends declared by the Gibraltar entity or remitted as branch profits to the Bermuda Exempted Company's head office.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Gibraltar applies OECD-aligned arm's length rules. Contemporaneous Local File documentation is mandatory where controlled transactions exceed GBP 1,000,000 per accounting period; a Master File is required where group turnover exceeds GBP 50,000,000; and a Country-by-Country Report is required where consolidated group revenue exceeds EUR 750,000,000. Benchmarking studies must be refreshed at least every three years.

Economic substance scope: The International Tax Co-operation (Economic Substance) Act 2019 applies. Manufacturing and trading of custom keycaps is a relevant activity; the company must demonstrate in Gibraltar: core income-generating activities (production scheduling, raw-material procurement, moulding quality control, inventory management), a physical manufacturing or warehousing facility, proportionate local employees, and substantive board meetings. An annual Economic Substance notification is mandatory; failure triggers penalties and potential strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion - Deliberate Suppression of E-Commerce Turnover

Statutory sanction: Deliberate and concealed inaccuracy penalty of 150% of the tax lost; default interest at 1.5% per month; criminal prosecution with unlimited fines and imprisonment of up to 7 years under the Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended).

Director personal liability: Civil restitution orders for the full tax lost and penalties; freezing and confiscation of personal assets; travel bans and surrender of passports; personal criminal conviction and director disqualification for up to 15 years.
Shell-Company / Sham Economic Substance Structure

Statutory sanction: Economic Substance Act penalty: initial administrative fine of up to GBP 100,000, plus GBP 10,000 per day for continuing default; strike-off from the Gibraltar Companies Register; revocation of the Gibraltar Residence Certificate and back-assessment at 10% on the full profit base.

Director personal liability: Directors and the local corporate secretary are jointly liable for the ESR fines; courts may order civil restitution of profits routed through the sham arrangement; travel bans are imposed for persistent non-compliance; directors face up to 2 years' imprisonment for false ESR declarations.
AML / Sanctions and Payment-Route Abuse

Statutory sanction: Fines under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and the Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2019 of up to GBP 500,000 per breach, plus confiscation orders; Stripe and Shopify de-risking; withdrawal of the company's access to payment rails and bank accounts.

Director personal liability: Directors who direct or knowingly facilitate layering face civil restitution of all concealed funds, asset-freezing, travel bans, imprisonment of up to 14 years for money laundering, automatic disqualification as directors, and revocation of any AML/MLRO licences.
Bermuda-Gibraltar Residence Mismatch / Managed-and-Control Abuse

Statutory sanction: Retrospective re-determination of tax residence: back-assessment of 10% CT for up to 7 years, 100% carelessness penalty, interest at 1.5% per month, denial of the Patent Box deduction for affected periods, and disregard of the Bermuda tax-exempt classification for Gibraltar purposes.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally assessed for underpaid CT where profits were distributed as shadow-director payments; civil restitution of dividend flows to the Bermuda parent; travel bans pending full settlement of the tax; criminal prosecution for dishonest tax declarations with imprisonment of up to 7 years.
