Stage 02

Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

BVI Registry of Corporate Affairs, Financial Services Commission (FSC), Tortola, BVI — primary incorporation registry; Companies House, UK Registrar of Companies — secondary registration of the overseas company and its UK establishment.

Governing statute

BVI Business Companies Act, 2004 (as amended), sections 3–11 (incorporation), 16 (registered office), 46 and 53 (registered agent), 99 (written resolutions), 107–122 (shares); UK Companies Act 2006, sections 1044–1059 (overseas companies) and the Overseas Companies Regulations 2009 (SI 2009/1801) for UK establishment registration with Companies House.

Formation / constitutional filings

BVI Form 1 (Application for Registration) resulting in the Certificate of Incorporation and Memorandum and Articles of Association; for the UK, Companies House Form OS01 (Application by an overseas company to register particulars) together with Form OS02 (Register of a UK establishment).

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 20 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum authorized or paid-up share capital is imposed by the BVI Business Companies Act, 2004; shares of no par value may be allotted for such consideration as the board determines, and there is no statutory escrow or capital deposit account requirement. The UK overseas-company registration imposes no separate capitalization or escrow condition, but the directors remain subject to the UK Insolvency Act 1986 wrongful-trading rules once the UK establishment commences trading.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification and a fire risk assessment are required under the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 for any occupied premises; workplace compliance with the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 and the Workplace (Health, Safety and Welfare) Regulations 1992; display-screen-equipment (DSE) workstation assessments must be completed for all helpdesk agents; employers' liability insurance (minimum GBP 5 million cover) is mandatory; if a residential address is used as the UK establishment, local-authority permitted-use and business-rates clearance must be obtained before occupation.

Paid-in capital rule: No minimum authorized or paid-up share capital is imposed by the BVI Business Companies Act, 2004; shares of no par value may be allotted for such consideration as the board determines, and there is no statutory escrow or capital deposit account requirement. The UK overseas-company registration imposes no separate capitalization or escrow condition, but the directors remain subject to the UK Insolvency Act 1986 wrongful-trading rules once the UK establishment commences trading.

No industrial licensing board is applicable to ISIC 6201 activities in the UK. General workplace regulation is exercised by the Health and Safety Executive (HSE) and the local authority environmental health department; the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) regulates data-processing aspects of the helpdesk service under the Data Protection Act 2018.

Category: None applicable — the supply of multi-channel helpdesk and conversational bot cloud services is an unregulated business activity under UK law and does not require a sectoral operating or manufacturing permit. The only mandatory formative registrations are the ICO data-protection notification and, when triggered, HMRC VAT/PAYE registrations.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC); online portal: GOV.UK (www.gov.uk) - HMRC 'Government Gateway' and 'Making Tax Digital for VAT' APIs

E-government filing portal

A BVI Business Company is a non-UK incorporated entity and is treated as UK tax resident only if its central management and control is exercised in the UK (CTA 2009 s 14). If not UK resident, no automatic UK corporation tax registration applies; however, if the BVI BC carries on a trade in the UK through a branch or permanent establishment (PE), it must register for Corporation Tax using HMRC Form CT603 (non-resident company registration), obtain a Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR), and file CT600 returns on UK-source trading profits attributable to that PE. For UK VAT, a non-established BVI taxable person supplying digitally delivered helpdesk services to UK non-business consumers must register compulsorily from the first supply (no threshold) via Form VAT1, using the HMRC non-established taxable person registration route or an approved agent; a UK-established branch supplying taxable services must register once taxable turnover exceeds £90,000, or may register voluntarily. Pay-As-You-Earn (PAYE) and National Insurance registration is required only if the BVI BC employs UK staff.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation with Territorial_Scope. The BVI BC is opaque (not pass-through) and, if UK non-resident, is chargeable to UK corporation tax only on profits from a UK PE or other UK-source income (subject to CFC and Diverted Profits Tax rules). The Tax_Exempt_Zone designation applies only within BVI domestic law, where the BVI BC is exempt from BVI income and capital gains tax; the UK does not recognise tax exemption for this offshore entity and will tax any UK PE profits at 19% (small profits rate band) or 25% (main rate) as applicable. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation with Territorial_Scope. The BVI BC is opaque (not pass-through) and, if UK non-resident, is chargeable to UK corporation tax only on profits from a UK PE or other UK-source income (subject to CFC and Diverted Profits Tax rules). The Tax_Exempt_Zone designation applies only within BVI domestic law, where the BVI BC is exempt from BVI income and capital gains tax; the UK does not recognise tax exemption for this offshore entity and will tax any UK PE profits at 19% (small profits rate band) or 25% (main rate) as applicable. Pass_Through_Eligible: No.

Available election forms

The UK has no check-the-box election regime for entity classification. Available statutory elections: (i) corporation tax accounting basis election between UK GAAP and IFRS (CTA 2010 s 407); (ii) VAT cash accounting scheme election; (iii) VAT annual accounting scheme election; (iv) option to tax land and buildings (VATA 1994 Sch 10); (v) loan relationship de-designation elections (CTA 2009 s 411). No UK-BVI double taxation treaty is in force, so no treaty election is available; unilateral foreign tax credit relief and hybrid mismatch rules (TIOPA 2010 Part 6A) operate automatically with no elective opt-out. A BVI BC may elect for the UK's offshore fund or reporting fund treatment for UK investors if it is an alternative investment fund, but this does not alter its corporate tax identity.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

£90,000 annual taxable turnover for UK-established taxable persons (effective 1 April 2024); zero threshold for non-established overseas suppliers of digitally supplied services (including helpdesk SaaS) to UK non-business consumers - compulsory registration from the first supply; B2B customers in the UK receiving imported helpdesk services must account for UK VAT under the reverse charge procedure where the BVI BC does not charge VAT.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from Great Britain or Northern Ireland to a non-UK destination qualify for zero-rating where the supplier retains valid commercial export evidence within 3 months of export and the goods leave the UK within the statutory timeframe (VAT Notice 703). For the helpdesk/SaaS scope, B2B supplies of services to an overseas business customer are 'outside the scope' of UK VAT where the customer belongs outside the UK (place of supply rule), and B2C digital services to consumers whose usual country of residence is outside the UK are similarly outside scope; these are not zero-rated but are not charged UK VAT. B2C digitally supplied helpdesk services to UK consumers are chargeable to UK VAT at 20%. Post-Brexit, no export VAT refund mechanism exists for UK exporters; credit for recoverable UK input VAT is taken through the normal VAT return without a separate refund claim.

  • Customs Export Declaration submitted via the Customs Declaration Service (CDS), including the Movement Reference Number (MRN)
  • Proof of physical export: Bill of Lading, sea waybill, or Air Waybill (AWB) evidencing departure from the UK
  • Commercial invoice and packing list matching the declared export values
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT credit advice evidencing offshore settlement of export proceeds
  • Customer due diligence file confirming the overseas recipient's business status and attested location

No general mandatory e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping regime exists in the UK. However, Making Tax Digital for VAT ('MTD for VAT') requires all VAT-registered businesses to keep digital VAT records and submit all VAT returns to HMRC exclusively through HMRC MTD-compatible software via HMRC MTD API endpoints (JSON payloads authenticated by OAuth 2.0 with Government Gateway credentials). Commercial electronic invoices (PEPPOL, UBL, EDI, or structured PDF) are valid; B2B UK VAT invoices must be issued within 15 days of the time of supply. No pre-clearance authorization is required for individual invoices.

HMRC may retrospectively claw back zero-rated export treatment and associated input VAT claims where the statutory export evidence bundle is not obtained or is inaccurate, raising assessments within 4 years for ordinary inaccuracies and up to 20 years for deliberate non-disclosure or fraud (FA 2008 Sch 36). Penalties: up to 100% of the VAT lost for inaccurate returns (FA 2007 Sch 24); up to 30% of the VAT evasion attributable to the taxpayer (VATA 1994 s 60); default interest at Bank of England base rate plus 2.5%; criminal prosecution for fraudulent export supply chains and intra-community missing-trader fraud carries up to 7 years' imprisonment; dishonest representations and evasion penalties may reach 100% of VAT; artificial offshore routing of exports is scrutinised under the substance-over-form doctrine (Furniss/Fidessa line of authority).

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The UK operates an unrestricted capital and current account convertibility regime under Article VIII of the IMF Articles of Agreement. No exchange-control authorization is required for BVI-parented equity injections, intercompany loans, dividend remittances, or royalty/service payments. UK FCA/Sanctions (OFSI) regulations do not impose residency-based conversion quotas; the only denominators are (i) HMRC CFC and transfer-pricing rules for related-party cross-border flows, and (ii) DBT/OFSI export and sanctions licensing for restricted goods/parties. Profit remittances are freely permitted; any lender credit reference to 'UK exchange control' should refer to the historical Exchange Control Act 1947 which is repealed.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or mandatory conversion ratio applies in the UK. Export/software-as-a-service proceeds in foreign currency may be retained indefinitely in any foreign-currency account; there is no central-bank timeline for repatriation or conversion to GBP. BVI parent companies of a UK service subsidiary must, however, provide HMRC with transaction-level evidence (CA3821/Country-by-Country reporting positions) to demonstrate arm's length pricing and residence for the permanent establishment.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (BoE) — prudential regulator; HM Treasury retains overall macroeconomic and sanctions/export-control policy. The Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) supervises banks' conduct and the Payment Services Regulations 2017 (as amended).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: £200,000,000 (annual turnover) or £2,000,000,000 (gross assets) for mandatory Local File and Master File; medium-sized enterprises (turnover > £50m) must maintain contemporaneous records but not full documentation

Economic substance scope: UK applies the 'Diverted Profits Tax' (25%) and general anti-abuse rules (GAAR) to counter artificial arrangements lacking economic substance. A UK tax-resident BVI company must demonstrate real management, staff, and assets in the UK to avoid recharacterisation.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Penalty up to 200% of tax understated, plus interest; criminal prosecution possible under the Fraud Act 2006

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for penalties if they knowingly participate; travel bans and imprisonment up to 7 years
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Disallowance of tax residence status, retroactive tax assessments, and application of Diverted Profits Tax at 25%

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally assessed for unpaid tax if they knowingly allowed the company to operate without substance
AML / Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Civil penalties up to £5,000 per breach, criminal fines unlimited, and imprisonment up to 2 years

Director personal liability: Directors can be personally fined and disqualified from acting as directors for up to 15 years
Transfer Pricing Misdeclaration

Statutory sanction: Adjustment of profits, penalties up to 100% of additional tax, and interest

Director personal liability: Directors may be liable for penalties if they fail to ensure proper TP documentation is prepared and filed
