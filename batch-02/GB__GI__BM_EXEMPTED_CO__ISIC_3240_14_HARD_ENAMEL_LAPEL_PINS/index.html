Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No US check-the-box regime applies in Gibraltar. In respect of a Bermuda Exempted Company, the entity may elect to be treated as a 'Pure Exempted Company' by obtaining an exemption certificate from Bermuda and, for Gibraltar tax purposes, make a 'Non-Residence Election' (Form ELEC-NR) to remain outside the territorial scope of Gibraltar Income Tax. For hybrid mismatch purposes, an 'Election to Classify the Hybrid Entity for OECD BEPS Action 2 purposes' may be filed with the Income Tax Office to align entity classification with the principal jurisdiction (Bermuda or United Kingdom), but such election is not recognized as a check-the-box and does not override Gibraltar territorial source rules.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GIP 30,000 of annual taxable turnover in Gibraltar (including distance sales of goods delivered to Gibraltar); no threshold applies to importers, who are liable at importation; voluntary registration is available below the threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under the General Sales Tax Act 2015, the supply of goods is zero-rated if the supplier exports the goods outside Gibraltar, or supplies qualifying services to a non-resident business/consumer outside Gibraltar. Eligibility requires: (i) goods are physically removed from Gibraltar within 3 months of supply; (ii) the supplier retains transportation documents (courier tracking, waybill, bill of lading or CMR) evidencing departure; and (iii) the export is to a customer outside Gibraltar. For hard-enamel lapel pins sold through online marketplaces, the supplier may zero-rate if the goods are shipped directly from Gibraltar to an overseas customer and the marketplace does not act as principal. Export refunds can be claimed from the Commissioner of Income Tax in the relevant quarterly GST return where the supplier's input tax exceeds output tax.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Gibraltar Combined Declaration / GD Form or UK/EU SAD where applicable)
  • Bill of Lading or airway bill / courier consignment note evidencing export
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank/PSP FX settlement report showing export proceeds received
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, and (where required) for hard-enamel pins, the CPSC/CE compliance declaration

Gibraltar does not currently mandate continuous transaction control or state-run e-invoicing/clearance. Taxable persons must issue an invoice within 30 days of supply, keep a chronological journal, and report GST by quarterly returns. Online platform records (Etsy/Stripe) must be exportable in XML/CSV but are not transmitted through any Gibraltar fiscal API. If goods are imported/exported through the Gibraltar EU/UK frontiers, a combined customs declaration (SAD/GD) and digital Cargo Summary Declaration are required; no digital fiscal stamp applies to invoices.

The Income Tax Office may retrospectively disallow zero-rating and claw back output GST plus simple interest at 1.5% per month (or part thereof) where the supporting export documentation is not produced within the statutory time frame. Fraudulent export zero-rating or shell-company invoicing schemes attract a penalty equal to 100% of the tax evaded, daily default penalties up to GIP 250, and, upon conviction in the Supreme Court of Gibraltar, imprisonment for up to seven years and adverse public disqualification of local registered officers.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar maintains full current-account and capital-account convertibility. Both residents and non-residents may freely hold GBP, EUR, USD, and other convertible currencies; there are no restrictions on profit remittances, branch repatriations, or dividends to a Bermuda Exempted Company. Profit distribution to a non-resident corporate shareholder is made without Gibraltar withholding tax, provided the company is not carrying on business in Gibraltar and does not have taxable profits attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment. A substance and beneficial-ownership filing is still required under the Gibraltar Beneficial Ownership (Resident Individuals) Regulations or equivalent statutory registers; the absence of exchange-control approval does not remove the reporting requirement.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no export-proceeds surrender rules or mandatory conversion ratios in Gibraltar or in the UK legal framework applicable to Gibraltar. Export receipts for hard-enamel lapel pins from platforms such as Etsy, Stripe, or direct B2B trade may be retained in the invoiced currency indefinitely. A prudent treasury policy should voluntarily maintain a 20–30% GBP operating cushion to satisfy local expenses and corporate governance, but no statutory timeline, percentage, or surrender obligation exists.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) for banking licensing and AML/CFT supervision, with the Bank of England acting as the currency-issuing authority for circulating pound sterling in Gibraltar. There is no separate foreign-exchange-control authority or exchange-control statute in Gibraltar; HM Treasury retains residual jurisdiction over international sanctions and financial crime, not routine FX conversion.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: HIGH for merchant payout settlement, but ONLY as a payment-orchestration rail and not as a substitute for a licensed commercial bank. UK-authorised electronic money institutions such as Wise and Revolut Business may support a Bermuda Exempted Company's onboarding if the company has authentic operating evidence, direct marketplace revenue, and a clean UBO profile. Legal constraints: an offshore digital EMI is generally not authorised to provide commercial letters of credit, trade finance, nostro/vostro aggregation, capital escrow segregation, or treasury hedging. EMIs also may impose transaction velocity caps and reject the entity if the UBO is resident in an embargoed or high-risk jurisdiction. For the enamel-pins e-commerce model, an EMI current account can be used for Etsy/Stripe payouts and supplier payments below material value, whereas the principal liquidity reserve and FX lines must remain with a Gibraltar-licensed bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital from the Bermuda Exempted Company shareholder must be routed through one of the SWIFT GPI corridors: CHIPS for USD payments, TARGET2/EBA for EUR payments, or SWIFT GPI for GBP payments. The payment field 72 (or MT103 narrative) must include purpose code 'CAP/SUB' with a plain-text explanation such as 'Capital Subscription and Contribution to Paid-in Share Capital of GB__GI__BM_EXEMPTED_CO__ISIC_3240_14_HARD_ENAMEL_LAPEL_PINS'. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File applies where the Gibraltar company executes controlled cross-border transactions with associated enterprises and annual turnover exceeds GBP 2,000,000; Master File is required where the consolidated worldwide group turnover equals or exceeds EUR 750,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar ESR applies to any company carrying on a relevant activity. A hard enamel lapel pin trading company using Etsy and Stripe is in the category of distribution and service centre, requireing sufficient physical presence, employees, management, expenditure and records in Gibraltar rather than relying on passive invoicing or mail-box operations.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Returns

Statutory sanction: Criminal liability under the Income Tax Act; fine of up to GBP 50,000 and imprisonment for up to seven years, plus civil tax and interest and a tax-geared penalty of up to 100% of tax evaded.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly participate in the evasion are jointly and severally liable for restitution; civil forfeiture orders, travel bans, and director disqualification orders for up to 15 years may be made.
Shell Company / Dummy Facility

Statutory sanction: Deregistration, strike-off, and fines under the Gibraltar Companies Act; obligation to pay a default penalty of GBP 50 per day while failing to maintain adequate economic substance.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for fraudulent trading and may be ordered by the court to contribute to company assets; travel bans and restrictions on new incorporations apply.
AML / Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Proceeds of Crime Act (Gibraltar) penalties: unlimited fines and imprisonment up to 14 years for laundering proceeds from the sale of counterfeit or unlawful merchandise and false declarations to the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners face confiscation of personal assets, arrest without bail where there is flight risk, and imprisonment; directors' gatekeeper duties attach to every beneficial-owner update under AML legislation.
