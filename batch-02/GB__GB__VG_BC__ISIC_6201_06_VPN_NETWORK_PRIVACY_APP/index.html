Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC); registration portal: HMRC Online Services via GOV.UK Government Gateway (https://www.gov.uk/vat-registration and https://www.gov.uk/topic/business-tax). For the BVI leg, the BVI International Tax Authority (ITA) administers annual Economic Substance filings. Note: no UK-BVI comprehensive double tax treaty is in force (2011 treaty signed but unratified); only a Tax Information Exchange Agreement operates.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Ensure the BVI Business Company is in good standing with the BVI Registry and has filed its Economic Substance return. Step 2: Register with HMRC as a Non-Established Taxable Person (NETP) for UK VAT before the first B2C supply of VPN subscription services to UK consumers; no VAT turnover threshold applies to non-established suppliers of digital services, so first sale triggers registration; file form VAT1 online and be prepared for a potential HMRC VAT security deposit. Step 3: Obtain a GB-prefixed VAT number and enrol in the UK OSS (Non-Union) scheme for quarterly B2C digital-services returns. Step 4: Register for UK Corporation Tax (CT41G) only if a UK permanent establishment exists (UK office, employees, or dependent agent); otherwise no CT UTR required. Step 5: Register for PAYE only if UK payroll is established. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone - the BVI BC is exempt from BVI income/capital gains/corporation tax; for UK purposes it is a non-UK resident company liable to UK Corporation Tax (19%-25%) only on profits attributable to a UK permanent establishment, and liable to UK VAT at 20% from the first B2C supply of VPN digital services to UK consumers; Diverted Profits Tax (25%) applies where structures are designed to avoid a UK taxable presence.

Available election forms

UK law has no US-style check-the-box election. Key mechanisms: (1) s.17 CTA 2009 clearance to confirm that central management and control (and therefore UK tax residence) of the BC is not in the UK; (2) VAT group election under VATA 1994 s.43 where the BC has a UK establishment; (3) UK OSS scheme election for quarterly B2C digital-services VAT returns; (4) no elective relief for hybrid mismatches - Part 6A TIOPA 2010 imposes mandatory counteractions where the BC's tax-exempt status creates deduction/non-inclusion mismatches with UK counterparties; (5) unilateral foreign tax credit relief only, since no UK-BVI treaty relief exists.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

£100,000 annual taxable turnover for UK-established businesses (effective 1 April 2025; previously £90,000). Non-established suppliers of B2C digital services (VPN subscriptions) have NO threshold - registration is mandatory from the first supply. Deregistration threshold: £96,000. UK OSS (Non-Union) scheme available for all UK B2C ESS sales regardless of volume.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: zero-rating under VATA 1994 s.30 and VAT Notice 703 applies where goods are exported to a non-UK destination within 3 months of supply, with export evidence retained. Services: B2B supplies to non-UK businesses are outside the scope of UK VAT (reverse charge in the customer's territory); B2C VPN/digital-service subscriptions are outside the scope of UK VAT where the consumer belongs outside the UK, substantiated by two non-conflicting evidence items per customer (billing address, IP address, SIM country code, bank country, telephone country code, or self-declaration). NETPs may recover UK input VAT via quarterly OSS returns or the non-established taxable person refund procedure (form VAT65A).

  • Customs Export Declaration (C88/XML via the Customs Declaration Service) for physical goods such as VPN hardware
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (or SWIFT MT103 / bank credit advice) evidencing receipt of export sale proceeds

No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or per-invoice API authorisation mandate exists in the UK; HMRC operates post-audit compliance. Making Tax Digital (MTD) for VAT is mandatory for all VAT-registered businesses (extended to all from April 2022), requiring digital records and quarterly VAT returns submitted via MTD-compatible software using HMRC APIs. Invoices may be issued on paper, PDF, or EDI if they contain VATA 1994 Sch. 11 content. A PEPPOL-aligned B2B e-invoicing mandate is under government consultation (announced 2024; prospective legislation post-2025, not yet in force).

HMRC assessment windows under VATA 1994 s.73(6): 4 years for genuine understatements and errors, extended to 20 years for deliberate or fraudulent conduct. Incorrect export zero-rating claims attract Schedule 24 Finance Act 2007 penalties: 30% careless, 70% deliberate, 100% deliberate and concealed, mitigated only by unprompted disclosure. Civil evasion penalty of 100% of tax under s.60 VATA 1994; criminal prosecution for fraudulent evasion under s.72 VATA 1994 carries up to 7 years' imprisonment and unlimited fine on indictment; s.62 adds a £250 fixed penalty plus 100% of tax for regulatory breaches. Interest accrues under Finance Act 2009. Retrospective clawback is enforced via s.73 assessments, protective assessments against connected UK entities, and possible Diverted Profits Tax (25%) or GAAR counteractions where the BVI zero-tax structure is used to avoid UK tax.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The UK operates a fully liberalised and convertible capital and current account system; there are no exchange controls or restrictions on profit remittance, dividend distributions, or liquidation proceeds. Foreign currency accounts are freely held; conversion into GBP or any convertible currency is permitted at market rates. BVI parent profit remittance to BVI is likewise unrestricted and not subject to UK withholding on distributions from non-resident entities.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory requirement under UK law for export receipts to be surrendered to the central bank or converted into GBP. Export proceeds may be retained in foreign currency, offset against operating costs, or repatriated without a conversion ratio. The BVI Business Companies Act is also silent on mandatory surrender; therefore all VPN SaaS subscription receipts are freely disposable.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (Prudential Regulation Authority) and HM Treasury (foreign exchange policy; Exchange Control Act 1947 repealed in 1979).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-High for authorised Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) such as Revolut, Wise, and Currencycloud; however, several EMIs refuse BVI-registered corporates due to tax-haven risk appetite and FATF guidance on beneficial ownership. Legal constraints: UK EMIs operating under the Electronic Money Regulations 2011 must perform enhanced due diligence (EDD) on non-resident corporate customers; VPN/privacy software is categorised as medium-risk for money laundering, requiring additional business substance evidence. Only EMIs with FCA permissions and SWIFT/Bank of England settlement access can facilitate liquid cross-border clearing rails.
