Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar - Income Tax Office (Ministry of Finance), online portal at https://www.tax.gov.gi; HM Customs Gibraltar for import and export duties at https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/customs; corporate registry with Gibraltar Companies House (Registrar of Companies).

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda-incorporated Exempted Company establishing a place of business in Gibraltar must: (1) file with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under the Companies Act 2014 certified copies of its Bermuda Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles of Association, a list of directors, and obtain a Gibraltar external company registration number; (2) apply to the Income Tax Office using Form C1 (company registration) to obtain a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number and employer reference; (3) register with HM Customs Gibraltar as a customs trader to obtain a Customs Declarant/Goods Operator ID for import duty (Purchase Tax) entries; (4) register as an employer for PAYE and Gibraltar Social Security if it hires staff in Gibraltar. No VAT identification number is obtainable because Gibraltar is outside the EU VAT territory and imposes no VAT or GST.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible: False; Entity_Level_Taxation: False for Bermuda tax residence; Territorial_Scope: Non-resident in Gibraltar if central management and control is exercised in Bermuda; Tax_Exempt_Zone: True - the Bermuda Exempted Company holds a Bermuda Minister of Finance Tax Exemption Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act, exempting it from Bermuda income, dividend, interest, capital gains and other taxes until 31 March 2035. For Gibraltar tax purposes, the entity is classified as a non-resident external company when its effective management and board meetings remain in Bermuda, so its foreign-source business profits are not chargeable to Gibraltar Gross Income Based (GIB) tax; no Gibraltar corporation tax arises on the offshore trading income of a Bermuda tax-resident Exempted Company.

Available election forms

(1) Bermuda: election to obtain and maintain the Tax Exemption Certificate and to designate Bermuda-resident status; no check-the-box election exists, but Bermuda exempts the company from local taxes. (2) Gibraltar: a company that becomes Gibraltar tax resident may elect to be taxed under Gross Income Based (GIB) rules on prescribed categories of income rather than on deemed taxable profits; an election may be made for the company to be treated as tax resident in Bermuda through the Bermuda/Gibraltar exchange of information and double taxation relief provisions. (3) Hybrid mismatch rules: Gibraltar implements ATAD-equivalent controlled foreign company and hybrid mismatch legislation; the entity may file elections to apply the matching adjustment rules where an instrument or entity is treated as transparent in one jurisdiction and opaque in another, although a Bermuda Exempted Company is generally not fiscally transparent. (4) Qualifying Company status: an eligible relationship manager approval (Article 8 or Category 2) may be applied for under the Gibraltar Financial Services regime to ring-fence qualifying income, subject to substance requirements and grandfathering.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax in Gibraltar; Gibraltar levies a Purchase Tax as a customs import duty; UK import VAT applies when goods enter the United Kingdom customs territory (including Northern Ireland), and UK VAT on supplies made from a UK establishment would apply if the entity registers for UK VAT.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VAT registration: not applicable (no VAT system in Gibraltar, zero threshold). Purchase Tax: no registration threshold - any commercial importation of plush toys into Gibraltar requires HM Customs trader registration and a customs declaration; Purchase Tax rates range from 0% to 22% with a standard headline rate of 17.5% and a reduced/zero rate for certain goods. UK VAT registration: relevant only if the entity makes taxable supplies from a UK establishment or distance sales into the UK; the mandatory threshold is GBP 90,000 of taxable turnover (April 2024 level) on a rolling 12-month basis, with voluntary registration permitted to reclaim input VAT.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar Purchase Tax zero-rating on export: goods shipped from Gibraltar to a destination outside Gibraltar (United Kingdom, EU, or third countries) are exempt from Gibraltar Purchase Tax provided the goods physically leave Gibraltar within 30 days of purchase and a valid export declaration is retained. For UK VAT purposes, a supplier exporting the custom plush stuffed animals from mainland Great Britain to a non-UK customer, or from Northern Ireland to an EU customer, may zero-rate the supply under VATA 1994 s.30 and the Value Added Tax (Export of Goods) Order 2000, subject to the export evidence requirements in HMRC Notice 703 (physical export within 3 months and commercial evidence retained). A Bermuda/Gibraltar entity not registered for UK VAT will instead suffer UK import VAT at the border (standard 20% rate; no reduced rate applies to plush toys) when the goods are sold into the UK, with Payment Deferral (PVA) available only to UK VAT-registered importers. The UK Global Tariff classifies stuffed toys under HS code 9503 00 with zero customs duty for most origins, but UK import VAT remains chargeable on the customs value plus freight and insurance.

  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill (including house and master bills) evidencing actual shipment from Gibraltar or UK port/airport
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank SWIFT MT103 or treasury remittance confirmation evidencing receipt of export sales proceeds in foreign currency or GBP)

Gibraltar does not operate a real-time fiscal e-invoicing clearance or digital stamping mandate. HM Customs Gibraltar requires electronic customs declarations through the DSL Electronic Customs System, which is interoperable with the UK CDS/CHIEF environment for imports and exports; no continuous transaction control (CTC) e-invoicing is imposed. For UK VAT purposes, electronic invoicing is optional; paper and PDF invoices are equally valid, and UK Peppol B2B e-invoicing is available only by mutual buyer-seller agreement. Post-Brexit, the UK has announced a future e-invoicing consultation framework (2024-2027), but no live CTC mandate applies as of the current date. If the entity sells through a UK online marketplace, the 'online marketplace' joint liability rules for import VAT and TOMS-like arrangements may apply and the marketplace may be deemed the supplier.

Gibraltar: enforcement under the Purchase Tax Ordinance empowers HM Customs to assess unaccounted Purchase Tax (17.5%) plus a penalty not exceeding three times the tax and to confiscate the goods where export evidence is false or absent; fraudulent evasion is a criminal offence with unlimited fines and imprisonment. United Kingdom (VATA 1994): if zero-rating is claimed without sufficient export evidence, HMRC claws back the VAT and issues an assessment for the undeclared output VAT, default interest at Bank of England rate plus 2.5% per annum, and a penalty up to 100% of the VAT lost (reduced for prompted or voluntary disclosure); dishonest evasion of VAT is a criminal offence carrying imprisonment up to seven years and unlimited fines. Anti-avoidance rules permit retroactive recharacterisation of an export as a domestic supply where goods are returned, diverted, or re-imported within 12 months, and the 'principal purpose' test regardless of separate legal steps. UK online marketplace rules may impose joint and several liability on the digital platform for import VAT where the marketplace was aware or should have been aware of the non-compliance.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Absolute convertibility on both current and capital account. Gibraltar imposes no foreign-exchange controls; the GIP is freely convertible at par with GBP and payments clear through the UK banking system (CHAPS and Faster Payments) and SWIFT without regulatory approval. Dividends, intercompany loan repayments, capital repatriation and trade settlements may be executed to or from any jurisdiction without GFSC or Bank of England consent. On the Bermuda side, the Exempted Company may obtain non-resident designation, which automatically exempts it from Bermuda exchange-control restrictions and permits unrestricted share capital, loan capital, and foreign-currency accounts; the only practical frictions are bank-level AML and beneficial-ownership due diligence plus annual economic substance filings.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No rules applicable. Neither Gibraltar nor Bermuda (for a non-resident designated Exempted Company) imposes any mandatory surrender ratio, conversion ratio, or repatriation timeline for export receipts. USD, EUR, GBP and offshore CNH proceeds from the plush-mascot OEM business may be retained indefinitely in the Gibraltar treasury account and freely converted through the London CNH hub. The only ongoing obligations are GFSC AML record retention (five years), the Bermuda economic substance declaration, and proper documentation of the Gibraltar banking and trading nexus to defend any UK/EEA tax-residency challenge.

Central bank supervision: The Bank of England (monetary authority and settlement agent for GBP circulating in Gibraltar); the Government of Gibraltar, as sole note-issuing authority under the Currency Notes Act 1934, issues the Gibraltar pound (GIP) on a strict 1:1 currency-board peg to GBP; the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) licenses and prudentially supervises all deposit-taking institutions in Gibraltar; the Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) regulates the parent entity under Bermuda's Exchange Control Act 1972 and Companies Act 1981.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: HIGH (conditional). UK FCA-regulated electronic-money institutions (Wise Business, Revolut Business, Airwallex, Verto) will onboard a Gibraltar-registered offshore vehicle with a local registered-office nexus and provide SWIFT, local-currency, offshore CNH, and API Platform_Pay payout rails. Legal constraints: post-Brexit EEA passporting into Gibraltar has lapsed, so the EMI must hold an FCA e-money licence or a Gibraltar Financial Services (Electronic Money) Regulations 2020 authorisation to service the account; the EMI's own AML/UBO and source-of-funds diligence mirrors tier-1 bank standards, and anonymous or nominee structures are uniformly rejected. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder capital injections from the Bermuda parent or third-party investors are routed by SWIFT MT103 or ISO 20022 pacs.008 to the Gibraltar Capital Escrow Account. Gibraltar and the UK operate no statutory purpose-code regime (unlike SAFE or FIRMS), but the remittance must carry the SWIFT category-purpose code 'EQUI' for equity subscription or 'DCMP' for intercompany loan inflow, with field 70 quoting the board resolution number and the words 'SHARE CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION' or the loan agreement date. The receiving bank holds funds in escrow until the notarised capital contribution resolution, updated register of members, and BMA non-resident exchange-control letter are delivered; funds are then released to the Operational Treasury Account. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% statutory corporate tax rate in Bermuda as a Bermuda Exempted Company; if the entity is treated as Gibraltar tax-resident through central management and control exercised in Gibraltar, the Gibraltar corporation tax rate of 12.5% applies to chargeable profits arising in Gibraltar.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% statutory dividend withholding tax. Neither Bermuda imposes any withholding tax on dividends paid by an exempted company nor Gibraltar imposes withholding tax on distributions by a company to non-resident shareholders. A Bermuda exempted company may declare dividends of accumulated profits without Gibraltar tax deduction.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within nine months after the end of the accounting period; the self-assessed corporation tax is payable on the same date unless a written deferral arrangement has been approved by the Commissioner of Income Tax.

Deadline: · Frequency:

An exempted company must file the annual return and pay the annual government fee on or before the anniversary of its incorporation, usually administered by the registered agent; no Bermuda income tax return is required because Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File preparation applies when aggregate associated-party transactions in a fiscal year exceed GIP 1,000,000; a Master File is required when the worldwide group's consolidated turnover exceeds GIP 100,000,000 and the Gibraltar entity participates in controlled cross-border transactions. Arm's-length compliance is required regardless of the documentation threshold.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance Act 2020 requires Gibraltar tax-resident companies that perform relevant activities to maintain adequate people, premises, expenditure, and core income-generating activities in Gibraltar. Plush-toy manufacturing is not itself a listed relevant activity, but the distribution and service center category will apply if the Bermuda exempted company is engaged in the distribution of custom plush mascots to European customers or receives commission income for coordinating an OEM supply chain. Under the Bermuda Economic Substance Act 2018, a Bermuda exempted company that carries on a relevant activity in Bermuda must demonstrate economic substance in Bermuda; a manufacturing-and-distribution model operated outside Bermuda is not required to hire local Bermuda substance, but the entity must file an economic substance notification where applicable. The entity should document local board meetings, employment of substantive staff, and performance of strategic functions in the jurisdiction claimed as tax resident.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Concealment of Gibraltar-Source Profit

Statutory sanction: Re-assessment on actual Gibraltar-source profits at 12.5%; late-payment interest; penalty up to 100% of the undercharged tax; criminal conviction for tax evasion attracting a financial fine of up to GIP 500,000 or twice the revenue lost, at the court's discretion, and imprisonment up to 14 years.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced where the director knowingly caused a false return or treated the company as a bare conduit; directors are jointly and severally liable for civil restitution of the tax loss, subject to asset-freezing orders, travel bans, disqualification, and imprisonment for serious fraud.
Shell Company / Abuse of Bermuda Exempted Status

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner may reclassify the entity as Gibraltar tax resident and assess 12.5% on deemed Gibraltar profits; sanctions include penalties of up to 100% of the additional corporation tax, revocation of tax residence declarations, and compulsory strike-off under Gibraltar's Companies Act. In Bermuda, false declarations under the Companies Act 1981 may lead to revocation of exempted status, forfeiture of the annual government fee, and cancellation of the registration.

Director personal liability: Courts will disregard limited liability where the exempted company is an instrument of illegality; directors may be ordered to make personal restitution, may face disqualification from acting as directors, may be banned from leaving Gibraltar or Bermuda pending inquiry, and may be imprisoned where the sham structure is used to evade tax or defraud creditors.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing with Associated Enterprises

Statutory sanction: The tax authority will adjust the taxable profit to the arm's-length result using the approved methodology, assess additional tax at 12.5%, charge statutory interest, and impose a penalty of up to 100% of the additional tax, with the option to publish a negotiated adjustment schedule where disclosure was deliberately defective.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign transfer-pricing declarations they know to be false are personally liable for penalties attributable to their dishonesty; civil recovery may extend to personal bank accounts in Gibraltar and the United Kingdom, and real travel bans may be issued pending the completion of offshore tax investigations.
AML / Beneficial Ownership / Economic Substance Default

Statutory sanction: Civil fines of up to GIP 500,000 for beneficial-ownership non-compliance, daily default fines of GIP 100 for continued failure, suspension of the Bermuda exempted company, and referral to the Financial Intelligence Unit for suspected money-laundering. On criminal conviction, assets derived from concealed trading may be confiscated under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015.

Director personal liability: Directors are exposed to personal money-laundering charges where they participate in concealing the true ownership or revenue flow; the company shield is not recognized for conduct involving fraud or money laundering, so directors face civil restitution, travel bans, disqualification, asset seizure, and imprisonment up to 14 years.
