Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax), HM Government of Gibraltar; official online portal https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities, including a Bermuda Exempted Company, are not required to obtain a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TIN) if they remain non-resident and derive no Gibraltar-source income. If the company carries on a trade in or from Gibraltar, has a permanent establishment, or receives Gibraltar-source income, it must register with the Income Tax Office on Form GITX-1 within 30 days of becoming chargeable, providing the certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, Gibraltar registered office address, and director/principal officer details. Gibraltar does not levy VAT/GST, so no separate VAT registration number is issued.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — Foreign entities, including a Bermuda Exempted Company, are not required to obtain a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TIN) if they remain non-resident and derive no Gibraltar-source income. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election mechanism. A non-resident Bermuda Exempted Company may elect to be assessed on a territorial basis in respect of profits arising from a Gibraltar permanent establishment or Gibraltar-source income. If managed and controlled in Gibraltar, the company may be treated as resident and taxed on worldwide income. The former 'exempt company' statutory election has been closed and is no longer available for newly incorporated or newly electing entities.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar does not levy VAT, GST, or a general sales tax; indirect taxation is limited to customs and excise import duties under the Customs Duties Ordinance.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable – no VAT/GST turnover threshold. A customs registration and relevant consignment declarations are required only for businesses importing or exporting controlled goods; there is no economic-turnover-based indirect tax registration obligation.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable – Gibraltar does not impose VAT/GST and therefore has no zero-rating mechanism. Goods physically exported from Gibraltar are outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect taxation; customs duty relief may apply subject to valid export evidence. Offshore services supplied by a non-resident Bermuda Exempted Company to non-Gibraltar recipients without a Gibraltar permanent establishment are not chargeable to Gibraltar tax if the place of supply is outside Gibraltar.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Electronic eCRS/CDS declaration)
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / Bank Credit Advice

No real-time fiscal clearance, API-based e-invoicing, or digital stamping mandate applies. Commercial invoices may be issued in paper or PDF format. Customs declarations must be lodged electronically through the Gibraltar Customs declarations system (eCRS) or, where cross-border with the UK/EU, through the UK Customs Declaration Service (CDS); no mandatory digital invoice stamping.

There is no VAT/GST zero-rating fraud. Falsifying export documentation, understating customs values, or engaging in artificial arrangements to avoid Gibraltar tax attracts penalties under the Customs Duties Ordinance and Income Tax Act, including fines up to £50,000, seizure of goods, criminal prosecution, and GAAR adjustments of up to 100% of tax avoided plus interest and late payment penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The jurisdiction imposes no capital or exchange controls. Current-account transactions covering trade invoices, royalties, service fees and operating disbursements, as well as capital-account transactions comprising equity injections, intercompany loans, loan repayments and profit distributions, are fully convertible and freely remittable without prior regulatory permit. The Gibraltar pound is pegged at par to the British pound and is freely convertible; USD and Euro accounts are routinely supported by licensed banks. The only substantive friction is mandatory AML/CFT documentary verification; no exchange-control approval or profit-remittance permit is required for a Bermuda Exempted Company operating through Gibraltar.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender obligation, mandatory conversion ratio or repatriation deadline applies to export proceeds held in a Gibraltar-resident account. Exporters may invoice in foreign currency, retain balances in USD, EUR, GBP or GIP, and apply those funds to onward payments once source-of-funds documentation is presented to the clearing bank. This reflects the complete absence of exchange controls in Gibraltar and in the United Kingdom since 1979.

Central bank supervision: HM Government of Gibraltar (Financial Services Commission) together with the Bank of England; Gibraltar does not operate an independent central bank. The Gibraltar pound is a domestic legal-tender issue at parity with sterling, fully backed by Bank of England reserve assets.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-Medium for this operating profile. Licensed e-money institutions may provide faster account opening and multi-currency corridors, but most EMIs are not authorized to accept deposits, issue documentary letters of credit, or provide trade finance products. A company engaged in ISIC 3250 extrusion and potting of hollow-fiber polyethersulfone dialyzers typically requires direct-bank SWIFT MT103 clearing and commercial LC capacity. The EMI route is therefore suitable only as a secondary receivables collection rail, not as the primary liquidity and clearing infrastructure. Additional legal constraints include mandatory safeguarding of client funds under UK/Gibraltar e-money regulations and the absence of full banking licences for trade settlement.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% (Gibraltar flat corporate rate on the trading profits of the Gibraltar permanent establishment of the Bermuda Exempted Company; 0% Bermuda statutory tax on the head-office entity for non-Bermuda-source income)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on dividends distributed by a Gibraltar-resident or Gibraltar PE-attributable company to its Bermudian parent or any foreign shareholder; Bermuda equally imposes no dividend withholding tax on distributions by the Exempted Company.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The corporate income tax return must be filed within twelve months after the end of the chargeable period (accounting period). A group operating through a Gibraltar PE may apply to the Commissioner for an extension to fifteen months. Assessed tax is payable within two months after the date of the assessment, subject to the operation of interim payments on account for larger taxpayers.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Box 1 Company identification: company registration number, tax reference number, registered office in Gibraltar (care of a licensed resident agent), and financial statement period end For the Bermuda Exempted Company enter the Bermuda-incorporated name and the Gibraltar resident agent address; mark the return as filed by a non-resident company under section 9(1) of the Income Tax Act 2010 in respect of its Gibraltar PE only. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: A mandatory transfer pricing local file must be prepared where Gibraltar trading receipts from connected-party transactions exceed GIP 1,000,000 in an accounting period; a master file is required where consolidated group turnover exceeds GIP 10,000,000. Below the threshold, a reduced transfer pricing policy statement is retained at the registered office.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations 2019-2023 apply. For the manufacturing and sales-distribution of dialysis consumables, the distribution-and-service-centre relevant activity is engaged where goods are purchased from the Bermuda parent and on-sold to hospitals and NHS trusts. The entity must maintain: (i) economic presence in Gibraltar comprising owned or leased cleanroom extrusion facilities, qualified operations staff and board meetings at the Gibraltar registered office; (ii) core income-generating activities performed in Gibraltar; (iii) an annual ESR information return filed with the Commissioner within twelve months after the financial period-end; and (iv) contemporaneous CIGA documentation. Failure to satisfy the substance threshold permits recharacterisation of profits to the Bermuda head office and triggers penalty provisions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Misstatement of Income

Statutory sanction: Under sections 123-128 of the Income Tax Act 2010: civil penalty up to 100% of the undeclared tax plus interest at 8% per annum; criminal prosecution for fraudulent evasion carries an unlimited fine on conviction in the Crown Court and imprisonment up to two years; back-duty assessments can extend to ten years where fraud is established.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced under the fraudulent-trading provisions of the Companies Act 2014. Directors and shadow directors of the Bermuda Exempted Company who knowingly orchestrate the omission are jointly and severally liable for the tax shortfall and civil restitution to the GRO, face director disqualification of up to 15 years, travel bans preventing departure from Gibraltar into the UK and EU, and imprisonment.
Shell Company / Failure of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance Regulations: initial penalty of GIP 10,000 and daily default penalties of GIP 1,000 from the date of the Commissioner's notice; for a second consecutive non-compliant year, penalties up to GIP 300,000, a direction to file amended returns, possible strike-off from the Gibraltar Register of Companies and forfeiture of protected banking arrangements.

Director personal liability: Directors knowingly party to an ESR misrepresentation face up to 12 months' imprisonment and fines of up to GIP 50,000 on indictment; they are personally liable for unpaid penalties where the entity is insolvent; the Bermuda parent is simultaneously exposed to the Bermuda Economic Substance Act 2018 penalties and adverse reporting to HMRC under the UK-Gibraltar exchange-of-information arrangement.
