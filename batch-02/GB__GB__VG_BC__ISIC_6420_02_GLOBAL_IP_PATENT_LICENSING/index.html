Competent corporate registry

BVI Business Companies Act, 2004 (as amended), Sections 5, 8, 15 and 21 (incorporation procedure, memorandum and articles, registered office and registered agent, and share capital / no par value shares).

Formation / constitutional filings

Memorandum and Articles of Association (M&A) submitted to the Registry of Corporate Affairs together with the incorporation application and government fee.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 14 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum capital. Shares may have no par value and be issued for such consideration as the Board determines. No escrow account or capital deposit is required; the paid-up amount may be nil or deferred as determined by the subscriber/Board.

Funding & unlock path: No fire service, structural or civil-defense clearance is required for a pure licensing entity. Any physical office in the UK must satisfy the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 (fire risk assessment), the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 (workplace risk assessment), and planning/building regulations approval (structural load) if the property is converted or changed in use.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum capital. Shares may have no par value and be issued for such consideration as the Board determines. No escrow account or capital deposit is required; the paid-up amount may be nil or deferred as determined by the subscriber/Board.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) – GOV.UK Tax Administration (www.gov.uk/hmrc); Corporate tax registration via HMRC Corporation Tax Online; VAT registration via HMRC VAT Registration Service (overseas entity path).

E-government filing portal

For an overseas BVI Business Company, apply for a UK Corporation Tax UTR only when the entity has UK-source taxable profits or a UK permanent establishment; use HMRC's 'Register an overseas company for Corporation Tax' service, providing certificate of incorporation, board resolution, registered agent details, UK place of business address, estimated UK profits and expected filing dates. For UK VAT, complete VAT1A (non-UK established businesses) with business plan, director/beneficial owner IDs, banking evidence and forecast of UK taxable turnover; HMRC will issue a GB VAT registration number (e.g. GB123456789) usually within 30 days of receipt. Where the entity only receives foreign passive royalties, no UK corporate tax registration is triggered, but a non-resident landlord or royalty withholding registration may be required for UK-sourced patent royalties (20% withholding).

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone (inherent BVI zero-tax status; in the UK, such overseas company is not tax resident unless central management and control is in the UK. UK liability is limited to UK-source income, UK real estate, and profits attributable to a UK permanent establishment, subject to double taxation treaty relief). — For an overseas BVI Business Company, apply for a UK Corporation Tax UTR only when the entity has UK-source taxable profits or a UK permanent establishment; use HMRC's 'Register an overseas company for Corporation Tax' service, providing certificate of incorporation, board resolution, registered agent details, UK place of business address, estimated UK profits and expected filing dates. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (inherent BVI zero-tax status; in the UK, such overseas company is not tax resident unless central management and control is in the UK. UK liability is limited to UK-source income, UK real estate, and profits attributable to a UK permanent establishment, subject to double taxation treaty relief).

Available election forms

UK has no check-the-box entity election; companies are categorised by statute. Available mechanisms: (a) Patent Box election (Form via CT600) to apply 10% corporate tax rate to qualifying patent profits; (b) if non-UK resident, election under CTA 2010 / double taxation conventions to treat branch profits as a permanent establishment and claim foreign tax credit relief; (c) mixed-mismatch rules automatically neutralise hybrid deductions without elective boxes; (d) for VAT, an overseas person may elect to register VAT voluntarily, appoint a UK tax representative, or use the VAT OSS non-Union scheme for B2C digital services with an effective June 2021 post-Brexit levy.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GBP 90,000 (taxable turnover over the previous 12 months, from 1 April 2024). For non-UK established businesses, there is no distance-selling threshold for services; VAT registration is required for any UK-taxable supplies from a UK establishment, or for supplies to UK consumers of intellectual property-related services if the UK resident customer is not otherwise taxable.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exported from the UK to a destination outside the UK (or supplied to a foreign seller who removes the goods within 3 months) are zero-rated if official evidence of export is kept (VAT Notice 703). For intellectual property and patent licensing treated as services, the general rule (VAT Notice 741A) siting B2B services at the customer’s location: a royalty or licence fee paid by a non-UK business customer is outside the scope of UK VAT and thus rendered at 0% for accounting purposes without HMRC input tax recovery. To rely on this, the IP holder must obtain and retain: written contract (customer name, IP description, consideration), customer’s business status/VAT registration number, and evidence of payment receipt from a non-UK financial institution. If the IP is supplied to a UK resident business, 20% VAT applies and UK VAT invoice must be issued; if supplied directly to a UK consumer, VAT applies at 20% on the licence fee.

  • For goods segment: Customs Export Declaration (CDS SAD), Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading/Air Waybill, Packing List, and Inward Remittance Certificate (or bank SWIFT evidence) as proof of export and payment; for pure IP licensing there is no physical export and the documentary bundle is substituted by: executed licence agreement, underlying patent/trademark registration certificates, tax residence certificates of licensee, and royalty remittance/SWIFT MT103 records.

No real-time fiscal clearance and no HMRC digital stamp are currently mandated for invoices. Making Tax Digital for VAT (MTD VAT) requires all VAT-registered businesses above the threshold (all since April 2022) to keep digital records and submit returns using functional-compatible software through HMRC’s Application Programming Interface (API). Invoices may be issued in PDF/paper; e-invoice is not compulsory. For imported/exported goods, customs declarations must be electronically filed via the Customs Declaration Service (CDS) utilising the Economic Operator Registration and Identification (EORI) number; no pre-clearance tax stamp is required.

HMRC qualification checks under VAT Notice 703/741A may verify export evidence, payment platforms and beneficial ownership of the IP. Where zero-rating is disallowed, VAT becomes due with interest (currently Bank of England base rate plus 2.5%) and penalties: up to 30% for carelessness/deliberate error, up to 100% for deliberate and concealed error (Finance Act 2009 Schedule 24). The disallowance creates clawback of previously reclaimed input VAT, denial of export relief, and late payment penalties on the VAT liability. Dishonest evasion of VAT is a criminal offence in the UK (VATA 1994 s.72) carrying imprisonment up to 7 years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Currency is fully convertible on both current and capital account. The UK is an IMF Article VIII member and imposes no approval requirement for dividend, royalty, interest, principal, or capital profit repatriation. A BVI Business Company receives patent/trademark sub-licence royalties into a UK sterling account without FX surrender constraints; however, the bank must be satisfied that (i) no OFSI-sanctioned party is connected to the transaction, (ii) the entity does not acquire UK tax residence through treasury management exercised in the UK, and (iii) UK royalty withholding tax (20% patent royalty WHT unless treaty-reduced) is administered by the UK licensee at source rather than by the treasury function. Profits may be remitted to the BVI parent or shareholders without BoE/FCA clearance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory timeline, mandatory conversion ratio, or surrender requirement applies to export receipts, royalty proceeds, or any foreign currency earned by a BVI entity banking in the UK. Balances may be retained indefinitely in GBP, USD, or EUR. Compliance obligations are indirect only: (i) OFSI sanctions screening and asset-freeze duties; (ii) BVI ITA Economic Substance Notification and filing requiring demonstrable BVI core income-generating activity (strategic IP acquisition/exploitation decisions taken in BVI); (iii) CRS/FATCA reporting via BVI ITA to HMRC of year-end account balances; (iv) UK Money Laundering Regulations 2017 transaction-monitoring duties imposed on the UK bank, which may require the BVI entity to provide underlying licence agreements and invoices on demand.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority and RTGS/CHAPS operator); HM Treasury (financial sanctions and counter-terrorism policy owner); Office of Financial Sanctions Implementation (OFSI) under HM Treasury; Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) (banking and e-money conduct regulation); BVI International Tax Authority (ITA) (Economic Substance certification and CRS/FATCA exchange). The UK has no foreign-exchange control statute since the Exchange Control Act 1947 was repealed on 24 October 1979.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

High viability as auxiliary collection/aggregation rails; Low viability as a primary treasury account. FCA-authorised EMIs (Wise plc, Revolut Ltd, Airwallex (UK) Ltd, ClearBank, Currencycloud/Tide) hold Electronic Money Institution licences under EMR 2011 and PSR 2017 and provide GBP/IBAN virtual accounts, SWIFT MT103 send/receive, and mid-market spot conversion with low cost. Hard constraints: (i) EMI balances are e-money safeguarded, not bank deposits, and EMIs cannot offer credit, overdraft, or structured FX derivatives; (ii) high-volume, high-frequency intercompany royalty flows without a sponsoring Tier-1 bank relationship trigger enhanced transaction monitoring and possible account freeze by the EMI's sponsor bank; (iii) ISDA/EMIR hedging capability is absent. Recommended architecture: EMI accounts in the BVI company's name receive sub-licensee micro-royalties below GBP 50,000 daily and sweep automatically to the Tier-1 HSBC/Barclays account at end of day.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injection (equity or shareholder loan) from the BVI owner/parent must arrive via SWIFT MT103 with ISO 20022 purpose code EQUI (equity injection) or INTP (intercompany loan), accompanied by (i) board resolution approving the capital call, (ii) executed share-subscription or intercompany loan agreement, (iii) source-of-wealth breakdown of the ultimate fund provider for amounts above GBP 20,000, and (iv) pre-credit sanctions screening. The credit narrative in Field 70 must quote the BVI company legal name exactly and the underlying licence-agreement or capital-resolution reference. UK banks apply customer diligence at account opening plus per-transaction screening; a one-off capital injection with no prior trading history will trigger Level-2 EDD and a 5-10 business day onboarding hold. Use a direct correspondent corridor (e.g., Bank of New York Mellon, JPM) to avoid intermediary delays; do not route through unregulated money services businesses.Primary rail: SWIFT MT103 via CHAPS-linked correspondent banking for outbound royalties, intercompany service fees, and IP acquisition payments; Field 70 must embed the invoice number, licence-agreement reference, and OECD transfer-pricing file reference to prevent OFSI/AML holds. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — BVI Business Company is exempt from all corporate income tax in BVI (BVI Business Companies Act 2004; no CIT enacted). If the BC becomes UK tax-resident (central management and control in GB) or has a UK permanent establishment: UK corporation tax at 25% (profits above GBP 250,000), 19% (profits up to GBP 50,000), marginal relief between GBP 50,000–250,000 from 1 April 2023.
