E-government filing portal

Step 1 - External company registration: Within one month of establishing a place of business or a registered office in Gibraltar, the Bermuda Exempted Company must file with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies an application under the external-companies provisions of the Gibraltar Companies Act 2014, attaching an apostilled Bermuda Certificate of Incorporation, a Certificate of Good Standing, the Memorandum and Articles of Association, the Gibraltar registered-office address, and the particulars of directors and of the licensed resident agent. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation with Territorial_Scope (Gibraltar). The Bermuda Exempted Company is taxed by Gibraltar as a separate corporate taxpayer at the 12.5% corporation tax rate and is never treated as a pass-through vehicle. If central management and control are exercised in Gibraltar, the company is Gibraltar-resident and taxable on worldwide profits; if not controlled from Gibraltar, it is a non-resident foreign company taxable only on profits arising in Gibraltar through its manufacturing permanent establishment. The Bermuda 'Tax_Exempt_Zone' status conferred by the Bermuda Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act (exemption valid to 2050) is ineffective in Gibraltar and creates a hybrid-mismatch exposure rather than a tax relief.

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no US-style check-the-box election. Available statutory mechanisms comprise: (i) a residence determination, evidenced by formal board resolutions and contemporaneous minutes fixing central management and control in Gibraltar under the Income Tax Act 2010, which aligns the company with Gibraltar's 12.5% rate and the UK-Gibraltar double taxation agreement; (ii) unilateral foreign branch/dividend relief claims in the corporate tax return for qualifying foreign permanent establishments and subsidiaries, reducing double taxation of the Bermuda structure; (iii) group relief loss-surrender elections between 75% Gibraltar-resident group companies; (iv) hybrid-mismatch rules implementing the OECD BEPS Action 2/ATAD framework, which automatically disallow a deduction on payments to the Bermuda exempt-zone head office where no corresponding income inclusion arises, with no elective opt-out; and (v) the mandatory annual economic substance filing under the Income Tax (Substance Requirements) Regulations 2021, which requires manufacturing to be supported by premises, full-time staff, and qualifying expenditure in Gibraltar.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility prevails on both current and capital account in the United Kingdom, Gibraltar, and Bermuda. The GIP is pegged at parity with GBP and freely exchangeable into USD, EUR, and all major trade currencies; no capital-account gatekeeping, no profit-remittance tax, and no dividend-blocking regime applies. The Bermuda Exempted Company is presumed exchange-control non-resident, permitting unrestricted receipt of share capital and unrestricted remittance of dividends; if it transacts as an exchange-control-resident entity, BMA permission for external share issuances and inward transfers is required before banking. Principal convertibility risk is not regulatory but correspondent-bank de-risking, mitigated by maintaining the settlement account at a GFSC-licensed bank with prime UK/US/EU correspondent relationships.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable - the UK, Gibraltar, and Bermuda impose no statutory surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts. Proceeds from ICU ventilator sales and government LC draws may be retained indefinitely in the original invoicing currency (USD, EUR, or GBP) without any conversion obligation. Documentary duties are limited to trade-credit classification: banks will require the export contract, airway bill or bill of lading, and LC documents to tag the credit as legitimate trade proceeds for AML monitoring; no foreign-exchange certificate or central-bank surrender receipt is required.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (sterling monetary anchor for the United Kingdom); H.M. Government of Gibraltar (issuer of the Gibraltar pound at par to GBP under the Currency Notes Act 1934); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) (banking, e-money, and FX licensing supervisor for the Gibraltar treasury); Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) (exchange-control authority over the Bermuda Exempted Company)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-LOW. GFSC-licensed electronic-money institutions (EMIs) can host low-value operational accounts and FX conversion, but none possesses the balance-sheet capacity or correspondent-banking reach to execute Government_LC disbursements or high-value SWIFT MT103 settlements. UK FCA-authorised EMIs (e.g., Wise Platform, Currencycloud or Embedded Finance) are usable for a UK treasury sub-account but, post-Brexit, cannot passport into Gibraltar and would require a GFSC branch licence or a Gibraltar-licensed agent to service the entity locally. Offshore digital EMIs without UK or Gibraltar authorisation are legally non-viable for this treasury: they cannot hold the entity's operational account, cannot clear through CHAPS/Fedwire/SEPA as principals, and would trigger adverse AML classification. Recommended architecture: GFSC-licensed commercial bank as the single settlement node; EMIs permitted only for sub-GBP 10,000 operational payments.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 for interbank legs) from the Bermuda shareholder's prime-bank account to the Gibraltar settlement account; GBP credits clear over CHAPS backed by the GFSC-licensed bank's UK correspondent; USD via Fedwire and CHIPS; EUR via SEPA and TARGET2. The remittance instruction must carry ISO 20022 purpose code INVE (investment) or CAPT (capital contribution) and the narrative 'Subscription of shares - Bermuda Exempted Company - ICU ventilator treasury'. Verification requires a complete capital packet: share-subscription resolution, board minutes, certificate of incumbency, and, where the company is exchange-control resident, the BMA's exchange-control approval for the external share issue. Under EDD, source-of-wealth statements and the shareholder's latest audited accounts must accompany the first transfer; amounts above GBP 250,000 trigger enhanced review and may require a pre-inward funds check five business days before value date.Two described rails govern disbursement. (1) Swift_Wire: MT103 single-credit transfers to component suppliers with dual-approval authority, full OFAC and HMT screening at execution, and automatic reconciliation to the underlying purchase order; GBP via CHAPS, EUR via TARGET2, USD via Fedwire and CHIPS. (2) Government_LC: irrevocable documentary Letters of Credit opened, advised, or confirmed through the GFSC-licensed trade desk; recommended structure is a confirmed irrevocable LC confirmed by a top-50 global confirming bank to eliminate sovereign-payment risk; suppliers may be paid by transferable or back-to-back LC, or by MT103 after document examination and bank acceptance. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-LOW. GFSC-licensed electronic-money institutions (EMIs) can host low-value operational accounts and FX conversion, but none possesses the balance-sheet capacity or correspondent-banking reach to execute Government_LC disbursements or high-value SWIFT MT103 settlements. UK FCA-authorised EMIs (e.g., Wise Platform, Currencycloud or Embedded Finance) are usable for a UK treasury sub-account but, post-Brexit, cannot passport into Gibraltar and would require a GFSC branch licence or a Gibraltar-licensed agent to service the entity locally. Offshore digital EMIs without UK or Gibraltar authorisation are legally non-viable for this treasury: they cannot hold the entity's operational account, cannot clear through CHAPS/Fedwire/SEPA as principals, and would trigger adverse AML classification. Recommended architecture: GFSC-licensed commercial bank as the single settlement node; EMIs permitted only for sub-GBP 10,000 operational payments.
