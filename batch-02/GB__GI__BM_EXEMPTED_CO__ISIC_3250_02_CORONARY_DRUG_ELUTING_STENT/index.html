Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar; portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation. For indirect and customs duties: Gibraltar Customs, HM Government of Gibraltar; portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/customs.

E-government filing portal

1. Register the Bermuda Exempted Company as an overseas company with the Gibraltar Companies Registry under the Companies Act 2014 by filing Form OC/2014, certified copies of the Bermudian certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles, registered office details, and directors/secretary details; obtain a Gibraltar Foreign Company Registration Number. 2. Within 30 days of commencing any trade, business, or economic activity in Gibraltar, submit the Income Tax Registration Form (TIN-1) to the Income Tax Office, declaring the company's Bermuda tax-exempt status and appointing a Gibraltar tax representative. 3. The Income Tax Office will issue a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN) and open a Gibraltar Tax File; a branch/permanent establishment registration number is endorsed where the company has a physical office, warehouse, or permanent sales establishment. 4. For import/export of goods through Gibraltar, the company must also obtain a Gibraltar Customs Economic Operator Registration and Identification (EORI) number, even without VAT/GST liability.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Under Bermuda law, the company holds an Exemption Permit under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, guaranteeing no Bermuda tax until at least 2056. For Gibraltar tax purposes, the company may, where central management and control is outside Gibraltar, obtain a non-resident certificate to restrict tax liability to Gibraltar-source profits only; no check-the-box election exists in Gibraltar. Under the Taxation (International Tax Compliance) (Hybrids) Regulations, eligible hybrid entities may make commensurate-tier or transparent classification elections to prevent tax mismatch outcomes in cross-border transactions.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/sales tax in Gibraltar; limited customs and excise duties apply to specific imported goods (alcohol, tobacco, fuel) under the Customs & Excise Ordinance.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — Gibraltar does not operate a VAT/GST registration threshold; any person importing or exporting controlled goods must be registered with Gibraltar Customs for EORI regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — Gibraltar does not impose VAT/GST, so no export zero-rating or VAT refund regime exists. The export of Class III coronary drug-eluting stents is subject to ordinary customs export procedures; customs duty exemption may apply to goods manufactured or warehoused in Gibraltar and exported outside Gibraltar, provided the exporter holds the relevant medical device export licence and files a complete export SAD.

Not applicable — Gibraltar has no real-time electronic invoicing, digital stamping, or fiscal clearance requirement. Customs SAD filings are made electronically via the Gibraltar Customs Electronic System (GCES); no government clearance stamp is required for ordinary B2B commercial invoices.

Gibraltar Customs may claw back or deny duty relief on goods that do not leave Gibraltar, are returned to Gibraltar, or are diverted for domestic consumption; fraudulent export declarations attract civil penalties up to GBP 50,000 per violation, criminal penalties including imprisonment up to seven years, confiscation of goods/assets, and retrospective assessments extending six years from the export date (twelve years in cases of fraud or culpable negligence).

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is fully convertible at a hard peg of 1:1 with GBP. Exchange controls were abolished in 1979 and never reintroduced; both current-account and capital-account transactions are free of restriction, pre-approval, or local reinvestment requirements. A Bermuda Exempted Company operating in Gibraltar through a branch or permanent establishment may freely remit profits, dividends, interest, royalties, and management fees without any source-country withholding tax. Practical hurdles are limited to tax form-filing (Gibraltar income tax at the standard 12.5% corporate rate, with no capital gains tax or WHT), transfer-pricing documentation for intercompany payments, CRS/TIE reporting for the Gibraltar branch, and sanctions/AML screening of all beneficiaries. There is no licensing regime for ordinary currency conversion and no restrictions on maintaining USD, EUR, or GBP balances domestically or abroad.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Gibraltar imposes no export proceeds surrender rules, no statutory repatriation timelines, and no mandatory conversion ratios. Exporters of finished coronary drug-eluting stents may retain proceeds in any freely convertible currency (USD, EUR, GBP) indefinitely and may hold the receipts in offshore or domestic accounts. The only compliance obligation is statistical (e.g., balance-of-payments reporting via the bank) and AML/CTF monitoring for cash or unusual transactions; no legal obligation exists to convert export receipts into GIP/GBP.

Central bank supervision: Her Majesty's Government of Gibraltar, Gibraltar Finance Centre (issuer of the Gibraltar pound under a 100% Currency Board reserve backing by Bank of England sterling assets); prudential and conduct supervision of banks is exercised by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) under the Financial Services Act 2019 and the Banking Act 2019. The Bank of England is the anchor monetary authority of the pegged reserve currency, while the GFSC holds licensing, AML/CTF, and FATCA/CRS enforcement powers in Gibraltar.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Revolut Fit: High

Medium-Low. Gibraltar-licensed Xapo Bank Ltd (full bank, formerly EMI) and UK FCA-authorised EMIs (Ebury, WorldFirst, Revolut Business) provide rapid remote opening and multi-currency transaction accounts, but they are unsuitable as the primary treasury rail for this High_Risk_Special_License profile. Legal constraints under the Financial Services Act 2019 (Gibraltar) and Payment Services Regulations 2018/2019 prohibit EMIs from taking deposits, holding escrowed third-party funds for more than 90 days, issuing or confirming letters of credit, or extending factoring/forfaiting credit. Because the underlying commercial activity is Class III implantable cardiovascular drug-eluting stent manufacturing (ISIC 3250 / NAICS 339112) with target rails of Letter of Credit and Swift Wire, most automated EMI risk engines will decline onboarding or impose a monthly throughput cap of approximately GBP 50,000 per wire. An EMI rail may be used only for low-value inbound distributor collections and small vendor payments; the mandatory settlement and escrow rails must rest with a GFSC-licensed tier-1 commercial bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

SWIFT MT103 equity injection from the Bermuda shareholder's account to the designated Capital Escrow Account at the tier-1 Gibraltar bank, using field 70 narrative 'CAPITAL CALL - GI BRANCH OF BM EXEMPTED CO' and referencing the board resolution and escrow deed. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% flat Corporation Tax rate under the Income Tax Act 2010, applicable to the Gibraltar-source trading profits attributable to the Gibraltar permanent establishment (PE) of the Bermuda Exempted Company.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Gibraltar does not impose withholding tax on dividends under domestic law; a non-resident beneficial owner of a Gibraltar company or PE is not subject to Gibraltar tax on dividend receipts. Profits repatriated from the Gibraltar PE to the Bermuda Exempted Company via internal fund transfers are likewise free of any branch remittance tax.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: MNE groups with consolidated group revenue of at least EUR 750 million are required to prepare and file a Country-by-Country Report within 12 months of the reporting year-end and to maintain a Master File and Local File. A Local File is also mandatory where controlled transactions with a linked party (including the Bermuda Exempted Company or other group entities) exceed GIP 1,000,000 in aggregate for the accounting period. All controlled transactions, regardless of value, require contemporaneous arm's length records, benchmarking evidence, and written TP policy, with documentation to be retained for six years.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations (Income Tax (Economic Substance) Rules 2019, effective 1 July 2019) apply to Gibraltar-resident companies and to Gibraltar PEs of non-resident companies, including a Bermuda Exempted Company, performing a 'Relevant Activity'. Pure contract manufacturing of coronary drug-eluting stents is not a designated Relevant Activity; however, the PE must file the annual ESR notification form (ESR-01) to confirm out-of-scope or exempt status. If the Gibraltar PE also conducts distribution, financing/leasing, headquarters, or IP-holding functions, it must satisfy the full economic substance test: physical premises in Gibraltar, qualified local employees, local board meetings, adequate expenditure, and core income-generating activities performed in Gibraltar. Failure to meet ESR requirements allows the Commissioner to reclassify the entity's profits, assess deemed Gibraltar-source income, impose penalties, and disregard the separateness of the Bermuda entity.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Tax Avoidance

Statutory sanction: Assessment to tax on the diverted profits plus interest at 12% per annum; tax-geared penalties up to 100% of the understated tax for deliberate concealment; additional daily fines; public naming by the Income Tax Office; criminal fines and confiscation orders under the Income Tax Act 2010 and the Criminal Justice Act 2014.

Director personal liability: Fraudulent tax evasion is a criminal offence punishable by up to seven years' imprisonment and unlimited fines. Courts will pierce the corporate veil where the Bermuda Exempted Company is used as a facade, exposing directors to civil restitution, disqualification orders of up to 15 years, asset-freezing orders, and travel restriction orders preventing departure from Gibraltar.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner may deem the Bermuda Exempted Company to be Gibraltar-tax-resident, assess deemed profits at 12.5%, impose ESR fines of up to GIP 100,000 per year, strike the PE from the Gibraltar register under the Companies Act 2014, and notify the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) for regulatory action.

Director personal liability: Directors of the Exempted Company and of the Gibraltar PE are jointly and severally liable for tax, penalties, and enforcement costs where the corporate form was used to evade tax; directors face personal fines of up to GIP 50,000, disqualification, and criminal prosecution for false ESR declarations.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: Profits are adjusted to the arm's length result with interest at 12% per annum; a disproportionate penalty of 35% of the additional tax attributable to the TP adjustment applies, increasing to 100% in cases of deliberate concealment; failure to file a Country-by-Country Report attracts a fixed penalty of up to GIP 50,000 plus daily penalties.

Director personal liability: Signing directors of the inaccurate GTC-1 return and the TP documentation are personally exposed to penalties; where the TP documentation is fraudulent or knowingly misleading, directors face criminal liability for tax evasion and potential imprisonment of up to seven years, together with disqualification from acting as directors.
AML / Sanctions and High-Risk Payment Rails Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Proceedings under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 (as amended) carry penalties of up to GIP 10,000,000 and imprisonment of up to 14 years for money laundering; GFSC administrative fines for carrying on regulated financial activity without a license; revocation of the statutory Class III MedTech license; inclusion on UK and Gibraltar sanctions and AML watchlists, resulting in asset freezes and loss of correspondent banking.

Director personal liability: Directors are 'relevant persons' under the Gibraltar AML framework; individual exposure includes unlimited fines, confiscation orders, disqualification for up to 15 years, travel bans, and custodial sentences of up to 14 years for money laundering and up to seven years for failure to disclose suspicious activity to the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit.
Persistent Non-Filing and Non-Payment of Corporation Tax

Statutory sanction: Automatic fixed and daily penalties capped at GIP 5,000; interest at 12% per annum; the Commissioner may apply to the Supreme Court for a travel restriction order prohibiting the departure of directors and officers from Gibraltar; final tax demands are enforceable against Gibraltar assets of the PE and the Bermuda parent; persistent default leads to compulsory strike-off and confiscation of assets.

Director personal liability: Directors may be detained pending settlement of the tax debt; personal liability arises where taxes withheld from employees or customers were not remitted, and where directors knowingly permit continued trading while tax debts are outstanding, exposing them to personal assessments, disqualification, and civil recovery proceedings.
