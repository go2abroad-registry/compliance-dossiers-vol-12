Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar Commissioner of Income Tax (Gibraltar Income Tax Office), Government of Gibraltar Ministry of Finance; official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation-and-duties; primary online services via the Gibraltar Tax Portal / GIRO (Gibraltar Income Tax Office online filing system).

E-government filing portal

The Bermuda Exempted Company's home tax status is secured in Bermuda via a Tax Assurance Certificate issued under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966; no Bermuda TIN is issued as Bermuda levies no income tax. For Gibraltar operations: if the company establishes a permanent establishment, derives Gibraltar-source income, or opens a taxable payroll/bank account, it must submit Form G-REG/Income Tax Registration to the Gibraltar Commissioner of Income Tax, providing certified constitutional documents (Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles, Register of Directors), a Gibraltar registered office address, and bank/SWIFT references; the Commissioner issues a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (GTIN) within 10–15 working days. Where operations are limited to warehousing, transit, or online distribution without a Gibraltar branch, the company may remain outside the Gibraltar income tax net but must still obtain a Customs Registration and Economic Operators Registration and Identification (EORI-GI) number with HM Customs Gibraltar for export declarations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — Bermuda Exempted Company is statutorily protected from Bermudian income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, and corporate taxes until 31 December 2035 (extendable by the Minister of Finance); Gibraltar applies a strictly territorial income tax, charging 12.5% corporate tax only on income derived from, or attributable to, Gibraltar activities or permanent establishment; no taxation on foreign-source trading income not remitted to Gibraltar, with the consequence that the combined tax status is an effective tax-exempt zone on the freeze-dried pet food trading margin where the manufacturing/principal trading risk is located outside Gibraltar.

Available election forms

(i) Bermuda Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 — formal election for a written Exemption Certificate guaranteeing zero tax until 2035/extension, renewable by application to the Bermuda Minister of Finance; (ii) under Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010, election to file on a territorial basis with allowable deduction for foreign branch losses and receipt of a 'Qualifying Company' certificate is no longer available for new applicants — instead, standard corporate registration with the tax benefit of no tax on offshore trading income; (iii) US check-the-box election: under US Treasury Reg. § 301.7701-3 the Bermuda vehicle may elect disregarded entity or partnership status, thereby altering US federal tax classification (applicable only if US owners/directors); (iv) BEPS Action 2 hybrid mismatch election — Gibraltar does not statutorily deny hybrid deductions for entities that are tax-exempt in their home jurisdiction, but the UK Finance Act 2016 hybrid mismatch rules may apply to UK-resident investors; written tax advice is required if the structure is mirrored through a UK limited company or UK branch. The entity should also elect to be a 'tax-exempt undertaking' for Bermuda payroll tax and social insurance by filing the annual TB-90 return, maintaining its exempt status.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar does not operate a VAT, GST, or general sales-and-use tax; indirect taxation consists of (a) Customs Duties under the Customs Duties Ordinance (import tariffs on designated goods: alcohol, tobacco, hydrocarbons, and motor vehicles only), (b) General Duties on a narrow basket of non-EU foods, and (c) environmental/health levies. Freeze-dried pet food manufactured in or transiting Gibraltar is outside the scope of Gibraltar import duties and is not subject to VAT; upon import into Great Britain, UK VAT at 20% applies under the UK VAT Act 1994, collectible by the importer of record via postponed VAT accounting.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no Gibraltar VAT/GST exists and therefore no turnover threshold triggers indirect tax registration. For customs purposes, however, any physical import/export of freeze-dried pet raw meat through Gibraltar requires the Bermuda Exempted Company to register with HM Customs Gibraltar as an economic operator (customs registration is a legal formality with no minimum turnover threshold), obtain an EORI-GI number, and submit entry/exit declarations for every consignment regardless of value. Where the same company registers as importer into Great Britain, a UK EORI (GB) number is required and a VAT registration may be triggered where annual UK import turnover exceeds the £90,000 registration threshold (or immediately for import declarations under postponed VAT accounting).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar has no VAT so there is no domestic export zero-rating to claim; exports of freeze-dried pet food from Gibraltar are outside the scope of Gibraltar customs duties and no Gibraltar output tax is charged. To the extent the Bermuda Exempted Company exports through/into the United Kingdom and treats the UK as the place of supply, the relevant zero-rating is the UK VAT Act 1994, s.30(6) zero-rating for goods exported from Great Britain to diplomatic/non-EU destinations or goods sold with export evidence; intra-EU supply (the 'pan-European digital trade gateway' model) may qualify for UK zero rating only where goods physically leave Great Britain to another EU Member State and the supplier holds the buyer's EU VAT number and monthly EC sales list. The entity must hold contemporaneous export evidence (consignment logs, customs declaration receipts, transport documentation), and cannot claim zero-rating where goods are merely placed in an excise/warehouse or remain in UK free circulation without export movement.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration (SAD/C88 electronic form via ICS/GDP system) — full customs export declaration showing HS code 2309.10 (dog cat food retail) or 2309.90 (other animal feed preparations)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export movement from Gibraltar/UK port with issuer stamp and vessel/flight details
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List (meeting UK VAT zero-rating evidence rules where applicable)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103 credit confirmation in the entity's Bermuda corporate bank account (EBANX/EMI platform payouts acceptable as corroborative evidence)
  • Certificate of Origin (Gibraltar Chamber of Commerce or UK Chamber) confirming EU-origin/Gibraltar-origin status for customs preference claims
  • Isiscan UK Health Certificate for Animal Feed (NOI/import notification) and competent authority prior-notification proof for pet food raw meat imports/exports
  • HM Customs Gibraltar EORI-GI and HM Revenue & Customs GB EORI (where importing into Great Britain) registration proof

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility without licensing hurdles applies on both the current and capital accounts of the Gibraltar economy. The Gibraltar pound (GIP) circulates at a fixed 1:1 parity with GBP and is freely convertible; GBP, EUR, and USD may be held and settled through Gibraltar-licensed banks. Profit remittance faces no exchange-control impediment: dividends, loan repayments, and director or management-fee remittances may be made freely, subject only to anti-money-laundering source-of-funds verification, CRS/FATCA reporting, and the entity's own Gibraltar tax-compliance position. For a Bermuda Exempted Company operating with management and control in Gibraltar, the practical remittance hurdle is not FX but the need to evidence commercial substance and to file applicable Gibraltar tax returns (potentially subject to the standard 10% corporate rate on Gibraltar-source profits); no withholding taxes or prior approvals apply.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. No statutory surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio apply to export proceeds in Gibraltar or the United Kingdom. A freeze-dried pet-food exporter may retain export receipts in the invoicing currency (GBP, EUR, or USD), apply them to supplier payments, or convert them through permitted hedging instruments at the company's discretion. Nevertheless, the compliance expectation under the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and GFSC AML/CTF policy is that proceeds are credited to the declared operational account, supported by invoices, customs declarations (UK CDS/CHIEF equivalents for the port of clearance), bills of lading, and a cash-management reconciliation trail retained for at least five years.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority for the pound sterling); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the licensing and prudential supervisor of all banks conducting business in Gibraltar. Gibraltar has no independent central bank and no dedicated foreign-exchange administration; the Exchange Control Act 1947 (UK) and local exchange-control regulations are effectively repealed/inoperative for legitimate current and capital account transactions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium feasibility, restricted to ancillary clearance rails. GFSC-licensed electronic money institutions (EMIs) and UK/FCA-authorised digital EMI platforms (e.g., Wise Business, Revolut Business, Airwallex, Payoneer) are legally usable in Gibraltar for low-value B2B collections, supplier disbursements, and 'Platform_Pay' marketplace flows; however, none may substitute for a full-service GFSC-licensed bank. EMI balances constitute safeguarded e-money rather than deposits, carry no statutory Gibraltar deposit-protection coverage, and the platforms cannot issue documentary letters of credit, provide trade finance, or support physical meat-import settlements. GFSC AML expectations and UK/EU correspondent-bank policy require the primary settlement ledger to sit with a licensed bank; the recommended architecture is therefore one GFSC Tier-1 bank account as the treasury spine with an FCA/GFSC EMI layered as a daily-swept, low-value payment engine.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Routing: parent shareholder instructs its bank to transmit the injection via SWIFT MT103 (or ISO 20022 pacs.008) in GBP, EUR, or USD to the beneficiary 'BM_EXEMPTED_CO' at the GFSC-licensed bank; field 70 must state purpose code 'EQUI' (equity capital) or 'CAPC', and the narrative must reference the board resolution date and the capital-escrow sub-account. Field 72 must repeat the exact account name to prevent name-mismatch rejection. The GFSC bank will place funds under a provisional escrow suspense and will release them to the operational account only after receiving the certified board resolution, updated register of members, source-of-funds documentation, and CRS self-certification; pre-notify the bank 48 hours before any injection exceeding GBP 50,000. Clearing: USD through CHIPS/Fedwire via a US correspondent; GBP through CHAPS or the Gibraltar clearing arrangement; EUR through TARGET2; all transfers must show the unique end-to-end transaction reference enabling the bank to map the wire to the capital-account entries.Rail 1 - SWIFT MT103 recurring supplier payments: purpose code 'TRDP' or field-70 narrative 'Payment for freeze-dried pet raw meat inventory / finished goods'; require two-signatory approval, store the bank's MT199/MT900 confirmations, and reconcile each wire to the corresponding invoice and customs entry. Rail 2 - Commercial letters of credit (ICC UCP 600 / eUCP 2.1) issued by the GFSC bank in favour of raw-meat suppliers for high-value or first-time counterparties: the credit must be collateralised by the purchase order, packing list, veterinary certificate, bill of lading, certificate of origin, and insurance documents; discrepancies must be resolved before the presentation deadline to avoid trade-based money-laundering flags. Rail 3 - Platform_Pay (B2B marketplace and low-value digital receivables): route through the authorised EMI/PSP with transaction caps of GBP 10,000 per settlement and mandatory end-of-day sweeping to the GFSC operational account; each sweep must carry a payment-reference remapping to the underlying sales invoices to maintain full garnishment and audit trail.Vanilla deliverable FX forward contracts (USD/GBP, EUR/GBP, EUR/USD) up to 24 months for hedging documented commercial exposuresFX swaps (near-leg plus far-leg) for temporary conversion of export proceeds or injected capital between GBP, EUR, and USDCross-currency swaps (CCS) for matched funding on plant and equipment finance or long-term shareholder loansNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) restricted to emerging-market currency pairs with no active deliverable market, supported by underlying trade contractsZero-cost FX collars / range forwards on EUR/USD or GBP/USD exposure under a board-approved treasury-hedging policy

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Medium feasibility, restricted to ancillary clearance rails. GFSC-licensed electronic money institutions (EMIs) and UK/FCA-authorised digital EMI platforms (e.g., Wise Business, Revolut Business, Airwallex, Payoneer) are legally usable in Gibraltar for low-value B2B collections, supplier disbursements, and 'Platform_Pay' marketplace flows; however, none may substitute for a full-service GFSC-licensed bank. EMI balances constitute safeguarded e-money rather than deposits, carry no statutory Gibraltar deposit-protection coverage, and the platforms cannot issue documentary letters of credit, provide trade finance, or support physical meat-import settlements. GFSC AML expectations and UK/EU correspondent-bank policy require the primary settlement ledger to sit with a licensed bank; the recommended architecture is therefore one GFSC Tier-1 bank account as the treasury spine with an FCA/GFSC EMI layered as a daily-swept, low-value payment engine.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Transfer pricing documentation is required for every controlled transaction in Gibraltar, but mandatory Local File and Master File maintenance applies where the Gibraltar entity has a group turnover above GIP 10,000,000 (approximately EUR 11,600,000). Country-by-Country Reporting applies to multinational groups with consolidated group revenue of at least EUR 750,000,000 (approximately GBP 670,000,000). Where a Bermuda Exempted Co has no local file, contemporaneous evidence of an arm's-length price supporting margins must still be retained.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to businesses carrying out 'relevant activities', including distribution, service, financing, holding, and headquarters operations. Manufacturing of freeze-dried pet raw meat is a physical production activity; the core income-generating activities (procurement, quality control, production, storage, shipping, and invoicing) must be conducted in Gibraltar, with a minimum number of qualified staff, physical premises, and board meetings held locally. An annual economic substance filing must be delivered within five months of the financial year end; failure to meet the substance test subjects the company to penalties and automatic exchange of information with the United Kingdom and Bermuda.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty equal to 100% of the undercharged tax, plus interest at 8% per annum; criminal conviction carries a fine of up to £25,000 and imprisonment for up to two years. Any directors knowingly concerned are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors who approved the fraudulent return are personally liable for the company's tax arrears and penalties; the Gibraltar Supreme Court may issue a travel ban and sequestration of personal assets, and chronic violations carry disqualification from acting as a director for a period of up to 10 years.
Shell Company / Insufficient Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The Gibraltar Economic Substance Office may levy a penalty of up to £50,000 for the initial filing breach, plus further penalties of up to £10,000 per month for continued non-compliance. The company is also subject to automatic exchange of information with Bermuda and full tax assessment on its worldwide income.

Director personal liability: A director who signs a false economic substance declaration is subject to a personal fine of £25,000 and a Civil Restitution Order; upon criminal conviction, a director may be imprisoned for one year and stripped of any right to manage a company in Gibraltar.
AML / CTF and Fraudulent Cross-Border Payment Flows

Statutory sanction: Under Gibraltar's Proceeds of Crime Act, the criminal sanction for money laundering is imprisonment for up to fourteen years, an unlimited fine, and confiscation of property. Civil liability includes disgorgement of profits and the freezing of bank accounts.

Director personal liability: Directors are treated as responsible persons and may be subjected to personal confiscation orders, travel bans pending trial, and provisional asset seizures. A non-resident director is exposed to an Interpol red notice and exclusion from the Common Travel Area (UK, Gibraltar, Ireland).
Transfer Pricing Mispricing / Shifting of Profits

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar tax authority computes an arm's-length adjustment by upward adjustment of taxable profits; an additional penalty of 35% of the avoided tax is imposed, increasing to 100% for deliberate concealment. Interest runs at 8% per annum from the due date.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly conclude related-party agreements without board approval and knowingly mispresent margins are personally liable for the adjusted tax; they may be required to make restitution to the company for diverted profits and may be disqualified for a period of two years.
