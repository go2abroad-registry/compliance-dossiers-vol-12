Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
Income Tax Office (ITO), HM Government of Gibraltar — e-filing and corporate registration portal: https://taxation.gi (eTax system). Customs and indirect tax administration: Gibraltar Borders and Coastguard Agency (GBCA), operating under the Customs Ordinance (Cap. 58) and Excise Ordinance (Cap. 60). Corporate legal registration: Companies House Gibraltar (Registrar of Companies, Companies Act 2014).

        
E-government filing portal
Step 1 — Registro Mercantil de Gibraltar: the Bermuda Exempted Company must first register as an overseas company under the Companies Act 2014 (Cap. 291) if it establishes a place of business or conducts trade in Gibraltar (filing of constitutional documents, Form 18 particulars, registered office in Gibraltar and local company secretary). Step 2 — Statutory Tax Identification: within 30 days of commencing commercial activity, file Form ITO-501 (Notification of Chargeability) with the Income Tax Office to obtain a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (GIN / TRN). Non-resident companies are required to notify if Gibraltar-source income arises or if management and control is exercised in Gibraltar. Step 3 — Business License: obtain a commercial licence under the Business Licensing Act 2012 (Category: Manufacturing / Wholesale Distribution of Medical Devices) from the Gibraltar Business Licensing Authority — mandatory for the surgical-laparoscopy-tower manufacturing scope. Step 4 — Customs Registration: obtain a GBCA Customs Registration Account (no minimum threshold; applies to all import/export operators) and, if importing duty-exclusive medical components, apply for an Authorised Importer / Bonded Warehouse authorisation. Step 5 — Employer Registration: if personnel are engaged in Gibraltar, register with the ITO for PAYE and with the Department of Social Security for social insurance contributions. Step 6 — Effective management: file Form ITO-502 together with Bermuda Exemption Certificate evidence (Companies Act 1981, Bermuda, s. 48) and DNT — Day-to-Day Management Statement — to establish whether the entity is Gibraltar tax-resident by place of central management and control or alternatively deemed foreign-resident for Gibraltar tax purposes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Tax_Exempt_Zone — the Bermuda Exempted Company benefits from a statutory tax exemption in Bermuda (no income tax, no capital gains tax, no withholding tax until at least 2035 under the Exemption Certificate). Upon activity in Gibraltar, the entity becomes subject to Gibraltar entity-level corporate income tax at the standard rate of 12.5% only to the extent that profits are sourced from or attributable to Gibraltar activities or management; territorial sourcing rules apply. Manufacturing of medical devices in or from Gibraltar triggers Gibraltar corporate income tax on apportioned trading income. The entity is not eligible for pass-through treatment in Gibraltar.

        
Available election forms
(i) Qualifying Company Election (Income Tax Act 2010, Part 2A): eligible companies may elect Qualifying Company status, reducing the Gibraltar corporate tax rate to 2% on qualifying trading income (advanced manufacturing, medical device production, international trade, logistics); conditions include at least 8 full-time employees in Gibraltar, minimum qualifying capital expenditure of GBP 100,000 in the qualifying activity, audited financial statements, and annual substance verification by the ITO. (ii) Hybrid / Mismatch Filing (OECD BEPS Action 2): lodge Form HM-01 declaring the Bermuda parent's tax-exempt status to neutralise any hybrid instrument or dual-deduction mismatch between Gibraltar, Bermuda, and the UK. (iii) Territorial Source Election: file Form ITO-401 to declare the entity's foreign-resident status for tax years in which management and control are exercised outside Gibraltar, preserving the Bermuda tax-exempt profile and limiting Gibraltar exposure to Gibraltar-source transactions. (iv) No US-style 'check-the-box' or charter-based flow-through election exists under Gibraltar law; all companies are taxed at entity level.

      

    

    

      
VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

      

        
Standard VAT / GST rate
No VAT / GST / Sales and Use Tax applies in Gibraltar (Gibraltar remains outside the EU VAT area under the Treaty on the Functioning of the EU Article 355(3) and the UK-Gibraltar Trade Agreement). Indirect taxation is limited to import duties, excise duties on alcohol, tobacco, and fuel, and stamp duties on property transfers. Goods manufactured in or exported from Gibraltar do not attract any national output VAT or transaction tax.

        
Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)
GIP 0 — No de minimis economic turnover threshold. Any natural person or legal entity importing, exporting, manufacturing (ex-factory), storing or distributing commercial goods in Gibraltar — including surgical endoscopes, laparoscopy towers, and ISO 13485 production components under HS heading 9018.90 — must obtain a GBCA Customs Registration Account and file electronic import/export declarations. Voluntary registration is not required because taxation at a zero rate is embedded, not elective.

      

      
      

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
Because Gibraltar imposes no VAT/GST, export sales of ultra-HD 4K minimally invasive rigid endoscope towers are inherently exempt from any Gibraltar output tax — an effective 0% rate exists at statutory nil base. Exports are also free Czechoslovak of Customs/Excise duty where goods leave Gibraltar for a third-country destination (Customs Ordinance Cap. 58, s. 65). To sustain the export treatment, the Bermuda Exempted Company must demonstrate: (i) actual removal of goods from Gibraltar to a foreign destination within 3 months of departure; (ii) the export entry processed and closed on the GBCA ICTMS platform; (iii) direct transportation to the non-Gibraltar customer or bonded onward transit (in conformity with the UK-Gibraltar Transit Convention); and (iv) compliance with destination-market regulatory prerequisites — including UKCA marking (UK and Gibraltar market) or CE marking (EU market), MHRA registration of the medical device, and an ISO 13485:2016 Quality Management System certificate covering the laparoscopy towers. There is no input tax credit mechanism in Gibraltar; consequently, no VAT refund exists and no refund claims are admissible.

Gibraltar does not mandate real-time Government-authorisation of invoices, no API-based fiscal data streaming, and no digital stamping regime for standard B2B invoices. However, inbound and outbound customs clearance is mandatory and electronic-only through the GBCA Single Administrative Document (SAD) e-Platform, with a unique Movement Reference Number (MRN) generated in the Integrated Customs and Tax Management System (ICTMS) — a real-time electronic declaration and risk-assessment clearance environment. Invoices issued by the Bermuda Exempted Company to customers must satisfy the content requirements of the Commercial Data Protection Act 2004 (seller identity, GIN, VAT-exempt statement, description, quantity, price, currency, Incoterms) and must be preserved for at least 6 years. Authorised Economic Operator (AEO) certification is available for the expedited clearance of medical devices.

      
The ITO and GBCA jointly operate export-fraud and transfer-pricing audit programmes. False or overstated export declarations trigger: (a) retrospective clawback of all duties and taxes avoided for up to 6 years (Income Tax Act 2010, s. 316; Customs Ordinance Cap. 58, s. 68); (b) a monetary penalty of three times the amount of duty evaded plus interest at the statutory rate, and additional administrative fines up to GIP 300,000 per false declaration (Customs and Excise (Procedures) Regulations 2011, reg. 14); (c) loss of Qualifying Company status and recapture at the standard 12.5% rate for the 4 preceding years of assessment; and (d) criminal liability — upon conviction, imprisonment of up to 3 years (or 7 years for conspiracy) for fraudulent export-exemption claiming, as well as disgorgement of the full export proceeds via civil recovery proceedings by HM Attorney General. For related-parties export sales to the UK or EU importer, the Commissioner may recharacterise prices under the arm's-length standard (OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines) and impose penalties up to 100% of the under-assessed corporate tax. Voluntary disclosure reductions apply for self-correction before the start of audit (up to 100% penalty abatement under the ITO Disclosure Facility).

Banking & Treasury Architecture

      
Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
GIP is fully convertible to GBP at the fixed statutory peg with no discriminatory exchange-rate arrangements operative against non-resident owned companies. Current-account transactions, repatriation of dividends, capital contributions, loan repayments, and liquidation proceeds are freely transferable without exchange control approval. The Bermuda Exempted Company, being a non-resident entity, may hold and settle in GBP, EUR, USD, and GIP without mandatory conversion. The absence of exchange controls does not eliminate compliance obligations under the UK/Gibraltar sanctions regimes, the Criminal Finances Act 2017, and the GFSC Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism Rulebook.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
None. Gibraltar imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender, no centralised repatriation requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no specific bank-account time limitation for foreign sales receipts. Inward trade proceeds from the sale of surgical laparoscopy towers may remain in the original receipt currency or be converted through a licensed Gibraltar bank at market rates. To satisfy AML/KYC and prove the underlying commercial transaction, the company must retain shipping documents, commercial invoices, customs declarations, and corresponding SWIFT payment messages for at least the statutory record-keeping period.

      

      
Central bank supervision: HM Government of Gibraltar and the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC). Gibraltar has no conventional central bank; the Gibraltar pound (GIP) is issued by the Government of Gibraltar under the Currency Notes Act at a fixed 1:1 parity with the British pound sterling (GBP). Prudential banking supervision, AML/CFT enforcement, and financial-market oversight are exercised by the GFSC, while monetary and reserve conditions track the Bank of England through the currency-board mechanism.

    

    

      
Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

      

        
        

          

            Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails
            Fit: High
          

          
Medium-to-low viability for the Bermuda exempted company. Authorised Gibraltar payment and electronic-money institutions may open virtual IBAN facilities, but most modern EMIs restrict onboarding of offshore entities that lack a clear economic nexus in Gibraltar or are tax-exempt in their home jurisdiction. EEA/UK EMI passporting into Gibraltar is no longer automatic post-Brexit, and licences issued by the GFSC do not automatically grant open-banking or trade-finance LC capabilities. For cross-border clearing, an internationally active EMI with FCA or GFSC authorisation can be used for supplier disbursements and receivable collection, but a tier-1 licensed commercial bank remains mandatory for documentary credits, standby LCs, and substantial wire settlements.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

      

        Inbound capital from the Bermuda shareholder must be transmitted by SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 on cover basis when routed through an intermediate) using the receiving Gibraltar bank's correspondent account. The beneficiary must be named exactly as registered on the certificate of incorporation, with the Gibraltar account presented in IBAN format (GI code) and the remittance narrative specifying ISO purpose code 'EQUI' (equity investment) together with a cross-reference to the board resolution approving the injection. For a capital increase based on cash either in GBP, EUR, or USD, the receiving bank's AML unit should be given the share subscription agreement, directors' signature mandate, latest audited balance sheet, and a Board-approved funding memorandum stating 'Non-resident equity contribution for working capital of the surgical laparoscopy tower manufacturing venture'.Trade disbursements for components, goods, licenses, and supplier invoices must be settled through London/ClearBank or primary correspondent banks using SWIFT MT103 with full reconciliation information: invoice number, shipping reference, ISIC scope code, and ISO purpose code 'INVC' or 'SUPP' for trade payments. Where the seller requires documentary assurance, a commercial letter of credit (LC) should be issued by the Gibraltar account bank under SWIFT MT700, governed by UCP 600, with the advising/confirming bank identified at the beneficiary's location. The LC must specify the Ultra-HD 4K minimally invasive rigid endoscope product description, Incoterms edition, latest shipment date, and required transport documents. For intragroup treasury optimisation, outbound settlement to the Bermuda parent or affiliated manufacturing affiliates can be executed as MT103 open-account payments or periodic netting, provided the underlying transfer-pricing documentation and GFSC/general AML records are complete.GBP/USD deliverable foreign-exchange forwards for 100% of anticipated US-dollar export receivables up to 18 monthsGBP/EUR deliverable foreign-exchange forwards for Euro-denominated component imports and EU distributor revenueUSD/GBP non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) limited to hedging currency exposure of non-GBP receivables in jurisdictions with extended settlement lagsGBP/USD cross-currency swaps for exact matching of long-term capital injection and trade-related debt obligationsFX swaps for temporary liquidity positioning between GIP/GBP and EUR or USD with a maximum tenor of 12 months
      

      
E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Medium-to-low viability for the Bermuda exempted company. Authorised Gibraltar payment and electronic-money institutions may open virtual IBAN facilities, but most modern EMIs restrict onboarding of offshore entities that lack a clear economic nexus in Gibraltar or are tax-exempt in their home jurisdiction. EEA/UK EMI passporting into Gibraltar is no longer automatic post-Brexit, and licences issued by the GFSC do not automatically grant open-banking or trade-finance LC capabilities. For cross-border clearing, an internationally active EMI with FCA or GFSC authorisation can be used for supplier disbursements and receivable collection, but a tier-1 licensed commercial bank remains mandatory for documentary credits, standby LCs, and substantial wire settlements.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

      
Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 aggregate value of controlled transactions requiring contemporaneous documentation; mandatory Local File/Master File applies where the Gibraltar entity is part of a multinational group exceeding the Gibraltar Country-by-Country reporting threshold of EUR 750 million consolidated group turnover.

      
Economic substance scope: Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) 2020 apply to all tax-resident companies undertaking 'relevant activities', expressly including manufacturing, distribution, and headquarter services. The manufacture of ultra-HD 4K minimally invasive rigid endoscopes constitutes a relevant activity; the Gibraltar entity must prove physical presence (employees, premises, equipment), senior management on the ground, proportionate operating expenditure, and core decision-making in Gibraltar. A Bermuda Exempted Company relying solely on board meetings in Bermuda while the CEO and sales function sit in Gibraltar will fall outside Bermuda's substance protection and become a Gibraltar tax-resident, with all profits exposed to 12.5% tax.

      

    

    

      
Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

      
      

        Tax Evasion / Concealed Gibraltar Permanent Establishment
        
Statutory sanction: Assessment on deemed profits, interest from the due date, fixed penalties plus daily surcharges (GIP 100 plus GIP 30/day), and civil fraud penalties up to 100% of the tax evaded; criminal conviction for tax evasion under the Income Tax (Amendment) Act 2011 carries imprisonment up to five years and unlimited fines.

        
Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly caused the suppression of profits can be personally assessed for the unpaid tax as officer-restoration liability; the court may issue travel bans, freezing orders on personal assets, and confiscation orders under the Proceeds of Crime Act; imprisonment up to five years.

      

      
      

        Sham Shell Company / Failure of Economic Substance
        
Statutory sanction: ESR violation: administrative fine of up to GIP 50,000 in the first year and GIP 100,000 per subsequent year, strike-off or compulsory winding-up, and a declaration that the company is a 'shell entity' causing all intra-group payments to be re-characterised as taxable Gibraltar-source profits at 12.5%.

        
Director personal liability: Directors face personal disqualification orders for up to ten years, joint and several liability for tax on re-characterised profits, and, in fraudulent shell schemes, criminal liability for fraudulent trading with attachment of personal assets.

      

      
      

        Transfer Pricing Misstatement / Base Erosion
        
Statutory sanction: Compulsory transfer-pricing adjustment to the arm's-length profit, plus interest; careless inaccuracy penalty of 25% of the resulting tax; deliberate offset penalty of 100%. Failure to maintain the GIP 1,000,000 controlled-transaction documentation triggers an automatic documentation penalty of GIP 10,000 and adverse inference in any transfer-pricing audit.

        
Director personal liability: Company officers may be assessed for the penalty as responsible persons, and the tax authorities can apply to the Supreme Court for a reimbursement order; in flagrant manipulation, a criminal investigation for money laundering may freeze personal and corporate bank accounts.

      

      
      

        Illicit Repatriation and AML Disclosure Failure
        
Statutory sanction: Breach of the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act 2000 and AML Regulations 2017: fine up to GIP 1,000,000 or 5% of the transaction value, plus confiscation of the whole amount laundered; directors and officers involved face prosecution for money laundering with a maximum custodial sentence of fourteen years.

        
Director personal liability: Corporate veil is pierced: the court will issue a civil recovery order against directors for all repatriated sums, travel bans pending extradition, and imprisonment up to fourteen years for predicate money laundering; a parent guarantee from the Bermuda entity will not shield Gibraltar directors.
