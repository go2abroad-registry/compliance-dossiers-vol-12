E-government filing portal
Step 1 – Confirm Bermuda exempted-company status: maintain the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 Tax Exemption Certificate with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies (exempts Bermuda income tax, capital taxes, and withholding taxes until 2035/2050). Step 2 – Register the Bermuda company’s Gibraltar branch/establishment with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under the Companies Act 2014 (overseas-company registration) through the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC); appoint a Gibraltar-resident company secretary/authorised representative and a registered office; no statutory minimum capital. Step 3 – Apply to the Gibraltar Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar Income Tax Reference Number (TIN/GRN) by filing the corporate registration return (directors, shareholders, FPV drone retail business description, registered office) within 30 days of commencing activity in Gibraltar. Step 5 – For sales into the UK: register with HMRC for UK VAT as a non-established taxable person (online Form VAT1 via Government Gateway; mandatory from the first GBP 1 of UK taxable turnover) and obtain a GB EORI number through HMRC for customs declarations. Step 6 – File the annual Gibraltar Economic Substance Return under the Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2020 with the Commissioner of Income Tax, evidencing sufficient substance in Gibraltar proportionate to the drone retail and integration activity.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Entity_Level_Taxation (Gibraltar: the Bermuda Exempted Company, if centrally managed and controlled in Gibraltar, is a Gibraltar tax-resident company taxed at a standard 12.5% corporate income tax on a territorial basis; profits arising in or derived from Gibraltar are chargeable; no pass-through or transparency treatment). Tax_Exempt_Zone (Bermuda: the exempted company holds a statutory guarantee of exemption from Bermuda income tax and all Bermuda capital and dividend taxes until 2035/2050; the Bermuda exemption does not extend to Gibraltar-source profits, UK VAT, or customs obligations).

        
Available election forms
No US-style check-the-box or entity-classification election exists in Gibraltar or Bermuda. Available statutory elections and mechanisms: (1) Gibraltar corporate residence election – a foreign-incorporated company managed and controlled in Gibraltar is treated as Gibraltar tax resident; (2) Notional Interest Deduction (NID) election on qualifying new equity to reduce chargeable profits; (3) election for territorial-remittance treatment where applicable under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010; (4) Advance Ruling application to the Gibraltar Commissioner of Income Tax on permanent establishment, transfer pricing, and indirect-export treatment; (5) Bermuda election for exempted-undertakings status (single irrevocable election per entity); (6) UK hybrid mismatch rules under the Taxation (International and Other Provisions) Act 2010 may restrict deductions where the Bermuda entity is treated as tax-transparent in one jurisdiction and opaque in another – no elective carve-out exists other than OECD-facilitated timing reliefs; anti-hybrid countermeasures apply automatically.

      

    

    

      
VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

      

        
Standard VAT / GST rate
None – Gibraltar has no domestic VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax; indirect taxation in Gibraltar is limited to customs import duties and excise duties on fuels, alcohol, and tobacco. UK VAT (value added tax) at a 20% standard rate applies to imports of goods into the United Kingdom and to UK taxable supplies; UK import VAT is collected at the border through HMRC (Customs Declaration Service) and, for low-value consignments not exceeding GBP 135, at the point of sale under the UK seller/online-marketplace collection rules.

        
Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)
Gibraltar: Not applicable – Gibraltar operates no VAT/GST registration regime, no turnover threshold, and no VAT ID. UK: a business not established in the UK must register for UK VAT from the first GBP 1 of UK taxable turnover (the GBP 90,000 annual threshold applies only to UK-established businesses); distance sales of goods into the UK from Gibraltar are imports, with UK import VAT payable on arrival (or collected at point of sale where consignment value does not exceed GBP 135).

      

      
      

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
Gibraltar: not applicable – with no VAT in Gibraltar, there is no export zero-rating claim or refund mechanism for FPV drone exports from Gibraltar. UK VAT (if the entity registers voluntarily or is required to register): supplies of goods physically exported from the UK to a destination outside the UK are zero-rated at 0%, provided the exporter obtains and retains valid evidence of export within the prescribed time limits. Offshore services (e.g., carbon-fiber milling and brushless-motor integration supplied to non-UK customers) fall outside the scope of UK VAT under place-of-supply rules (B2B: customer country; B2C: supplier country) rather than qualifying for zero-rating. Where drones are physically located in Gibraltar when sold to UK consumers, the sale is not a UK supply for zero-rating purposes; UK import VAT is triggered at import, and any VAT incorrectly reclaimed as input VAT is subject to clawback.

Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended): incorrect returns or wilful tax evasion attract penalties up to 100% of the tax undercharged plus statutory interest (currently 8% per annum); fraudulent evasion is a criminal offence under the Crimes Act 2011 (imprisonment up to 7 years and unlimited fines). Goods that are not exported but diverted into UK or Gibraltar domestic consumption are treated as smuggled: full import VAT and excise duties become immediately due, goods are subject to seizure, and HMRC and HM Customs Gibraltar may impose penalty assessments of up to 100% plus forfeiture penalties.

Stage 04
Banking & Treasury Architecture

      
Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
The Gibraltar pound is freely convertible and pegged at 1:1 to sterling; exchange controls have been abolished in both the United Kingdom and Gibraltar. Current-account and capital-account transactions are fully open, with no prior approval requirement for profit remittances, dividend distributions, capital injections, or trade settlements. Practical hurdles are limited to AML/KYC, CRS/FATCA reporting, and the need to demonstrate economic substance where a bank applies enhanced due diligence to an offshore tax-exempt entity.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
None. Gibraltar and the United Kingdom impose no statutory timeline, no mandatory surrender percentage, and no forced conversion ratio for export receipts. Export proceeds, including FPV drone retail settlement funds from PayPal and Stripe, may be retained 100% in the invoice currency or freely converted at market rates.

      

      
Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority for GBP); HM Government of Gibraltar, Commissioner of Currency (sole issuer of Gibraltar pound banknotes and coin); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (integrated banking and foreign-exchange supervision authority). Gibraltar has no resident central bank.

The remitting bank should use a clear purpose narrative such as 'CAPITAL INJECTION – SHARE SUBSCRIPTION' or 'SHAREHOLDER LOAN – WORKING CAPITAL'. Receiving-bank verification requires a board resolution approving the injection, a signed share subscription agreement or shareholder loan agreement, an updated register of members, SWIFT confirmation, source-of-funds evidence from the remitting bank, and IRS/CRS certification. No Gibraltar exchange-control approval is required, but OFAC and UK/Gibraltar financial-sanctions screening will occur on both legs. Amounts above typical transaction thresholds may attract enhanced large-deposit EDD.Use SWIFT MT103 for direct supplier wires in GBP, USD, or EUR for carbon-fibre milling inputs and brushless motor components; use commercial letters of credit issued by MT700 and confirmed by MT720 for large or documentary shipments; use UK Faster Payments/CHAPS for Gibraltar and UK overheads; use SEPA in EUR for European component suppliers. PayPal and Stripe are collection bridges only and should not be used as vendor payment rails; their settlement funds must be swept daily to the operational bank account. Payment narratives should include 'GOODS INVOICE', 'ADVANCE PAYMENT', or 'LC SETTLEMENT', accompanied by invoice, packing list, airway bill or bill of lading, customs declaration, and Incoterms.Outright GBP/USD and GBP/EUR forward contracts under an ISDA or bank RAF master agreementNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for illiquid or restricted currencies where physical settlement is prohibitedCross-currency swaps for matching long-term USD-denominated supplier liabilities with GBP treasury balancesPlain vanilla purchased FX options to protect against adverse GBP/USD moves without upfront premium exposure if permitted by the counterpartyPhysical currency swaps on a short-tenor basis for working-capital repositioning between USD, EUR, and GBP
      

      
E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Medium, with restrictions. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise Business, Revolut Business, and Payoneer may be used as supplementary collection and low-value disbursement rails, but they are not licensed banks and cannot act as the primary treasury or clearing account for this entity. After Brexit, EEA EMI passporting no longer extends automatically into Gibraltar; any EMI serving the structure must be licensed or appropriately registered in Gibraltar or accepted by the receiving licensed bank. Legal constraints include absence of Gibraltar deposit-protection coverage, prohibition on EMI credit intermediation, stricter onboarding rejections for tax-exempt offshore vehicles, and settlement dormancy risk when retail revenue is high-volume.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

      
Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File preparation where annual cross-border related-party revenue exceeds GBP 1,000,000; mandatory Master File where group consolidated revenue exceeds GBP 50,000,000, covering transactions with Bermuda group suppliers and the tax-exempt parent.

      
Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations 2020 apply to all Gibraltar-resident companies performing relevant activities. The FPV drone e-commerce business maps to 'distribution and service centre' and 'headquarters' categories, requiring physical premises, resident employees and proportionate management oversight. The Bermuda exempted company is simultaneously subject to Bermuda's parallel economic substance regime, and a substance deficiency in either jurisdiction triggers penalties and automatic exchange of information with UK HMRC and OECD partner authorities.

      

    

    

      
Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

      
      

        Tax Evasion – concealment of Gibraltar-source trading profit
        
Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of up to 100% of the understated tax plus statutory interest; on conviction on indictment, an unlimited fine and imprisonment of up to three years under the Gibraltar Criminal Code and Income Tax Act.

        
Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign false returns are jointly and severally liable; the corporate veil is pierced for fraud, enabling the Supreme Court of Gibraltar to freeze personal assets, impose travel bans and subject directors to custodial sentences.

      

      
      

        Shell Company and Economic Substance failure
        
Statutory sanction: Annual penalty of up to EUR 100,000 under the Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations and the parallel Bermuda ESR; automatic exchange of information with UK HMRC and OECD competent authorities; strike-off and dissolution upon persistent non-compliance.

        
Director personal liability: Directors and senior officers may incur personal administrative fines for false substance declarations, disqualification as directors for up to five years, and personal liability for penalties where they consented to or connived in the default.

      

      
      

        AML and Beneficial Ownership breaches
        
Statutory sanction: Unlimited financial penalties imposed by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and AML Regulations 2020; criminal fines and imprisonment of up to eight years for money laundering predicate compliance failures.

        
Director personal liability: GFSC senior-management attestation liability; travel bans, asset-freezing orders and extradition exposure for directors who knowingly process tainted proceeds; disqualification from financial-services roles for up to ten years.

      

      
      

        Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Artificial Profit Shifting
        
Statutory sanction: The Commissioner may disregard non-arm's-length transactions, adjust chargeable profits to the arm's-length result, deny the participation exemption and apply a penalty of up to 100% of the tax deficiency; interest runs from the original due date.

        
Director personal liability: Directors who sign deficient transfer-pricing documentation without reasonable professional care are personally liable for penalty amounts; fraudulent documentation triggers criminal proceedings, travel bans, asset restraint orders and disqualification from managing any Gibraltar company for up to ten years.
