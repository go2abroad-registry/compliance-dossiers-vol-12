Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC), UK Government portal GOV.UK (HMRC Online Services / Government Gateway); customs declarations via the Customs Declaration Service (CDS). Complementary BVI authority for the entity's home status: BVI International Tax Authority (economic substance and CRS filings only).

E-government filing portal

Step 1 - UK exposure test: a BVI Business Company (BC) is non-UK resident and is subject to UK Corporation Tax only if it carries on a trade through a UK permanent establishment (CTA 2009 s.19; CTA 2010 Pt 24). A pure offshore BVI booking/trading model without a UK fixed place, dependent agent, or substantive UK sales activity creates no UK CT registration obligation. Step 2 - Corporation Tax registration: if a UK PE exists, register with HMRC within 3 months of commencing the UK trade by submitting form CT41G (Non-UK) to HMRC Corporation Tax, accompanied by the BVI certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, and UK agent particulars; HMRC issues a 10-digit Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR). Online Companies House-integrated CT registration is for UK-incorporated companies only. Step 3 - VAT registration: as a non-established taxable person (NETP), the BVI BC must register for VAT via form VAT1A on GOV.UK before making its first UK taxable supply because the GBP 90,000 registration threshold applies only to UK-established businesses; HMRC issues a 9-digit GB VAT Registration Number. Step 4 - Customs identifiers: obtain a GB EORI number through HMRC CDS for import/export clearances of polymer granules and appoint a UK customs broker where required. Step 5 - Complementary BVI filings: submit the BVI Economic Substance Notification; if the BC is tax resident in the UK, file the BVI 'tax residence outside BVI' declaration, otherwise meet BVI substance requirements.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope / Tax_Exempt_Zone (opaque non-UK resident company; no pass-through). The BVI BC is chargeable in the UK only to the extent of UK-source trading profits attributable to a UK permanent establishment or UK land income (territorial scope, CTA 2009 s.19). In BVI it holds Tax_Exempt_Zone status with 0% corporate income tax and only the annual government licence fee. UK Corporation Tax rates for the UK PE from 1 April 2023: 19% small profits rate (profits up to GBP 50,000), 25% main rate (profits above GBP 250,000), with marginal relief in between.

Available election forms

The UK has no US-style check-the-box election; entity classification follows the foreign entity's corporate form (CTA 2009 ss.1123-1129), and the BVI BC is recognized as an opaque company. Available elections include: (a) CTA 2010 s.1141 election for a non-UK resident company to treat assets as attributable to a UK branch for chargeable gains purposes; (b) hybrid mismatch elections under TIOPA 2010 Part 6A permitting taxpayers to align treatment of hybrid entities/instruments (e.g., joint elections on payee income inclusion or deduction allocation) to prevent double non-taxation; (c) VAT group registration election (VATA 1994 s.43) to treat qualifying UK entities as a single taxable person; (d) option to tax land (VATA 1994 Sch.10) if a UK property interest is held; (e) election to use Postponed VAT Accounting (PVA) for import VAT on polymer granules entering the UK. Note: a UK permanent establishment arises by fact and cannot be disclaimed by contract.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GBP 90,000 (rolling 12-month taxable turnover for UK-established businesses from 1 April 2024; deregistration threshold GBP 88,000). The threshold does not apply to a non-established taxable person (NETP): a BVI BC making any taxable supply in the UK must register compulsorily before the first supply. Import VAT applies on goods entering the UK from non-UK jurisdictions unless Postponed VAT Accounting (PVA) is elected.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating under VATA 1994 s.30 and Sch.8 Group 6 (exports of goods) / Group 7 (freight transport) applies where the BVI BC supplies bulk polyethylene/polypropylene granules that are physically removed from the UK to a place outside the UK within 3 months of the time of supply, and where the BC holds contemporaneous export evidence. Related input VAT incurred on UK-procured handling, storage, or agent services can be recovered via the BC's VAT return. Offshore B2B services supplied to a non-UK customer are outside the scope of UK VAT under the place-of-supply rules (VATA 1994 s.7A, deemed supply where the customer belongs) rather than zero-rated, with the non-UK customer accounting for reverse-charge VAT locally.

  • Electronic Customs Export Declaration (SAD C88/CDS consignment record with Movement Reference Number)
  • Bill of Lading (or combined transport/CMR document) showing UK port of departure and non-UK final destination for the polymer cargo
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and third-party cargo surveyor/weight certificate for the petrochemical granules
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank SWIFT MT103 credit advice evidencing receipt of export sale proceeds
  • Incoterms sale contract, vessel nomination or container movement reference, and transport insurance evidence

Making Tax Digital (MTD) for VAT is mandatory: all VAT-registered businesses must maintain digital records and file VAT returns via HMRC-recognized API-compatible software (MTD VAT API v1/v2). There is NO real-time continuous transaction control, no government invoice clearance platform, and no digital fiscal stamping in the UK. E-invoicing is permissible on a contractual basis, and PEPPOL is accepted for public procurement invoicing, but it is not a statutory VAT control mechanism.

Export evidence must be retained for 6 years. If HMRC disallows zero-rating due to insufficient export proof, output VAT at 20% is assessed under VATA 1994 s.73 with late-payment interest. Civil penalties under FA 2007 Sch.24: up to 30% for careless inaccuracy, 70% for deliberate, and 100% for deliberate and concealed; late VAT returns attract default surcharges. Fraudulent evasion (VATA 1994 s.72) is a criminal offence punishable by up to 7 years' imprisonment and unlimited fines. HMRC applies MTIC 'knowledge tests' (Mobilx) enabling retrospective clawback where the BVI BC knew or should have known of supply-chain fraud. Assessment window is 4 years, extended to 20 years for deliberate misstatement. Note: the UK abolished exchange controls in 1979 and imposes no statutory 180-day FX repatriation deadline; the 180-day benchmark is adopted as the audit substantiation standard for matching export proceeds to zero-rated removals.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current and capital account convertibility under IMF Article VIII (accepted February 1961). No regulatory approval is required for equity injections, shareholder loans, dividend repatriation, profit remittance, intercompany payments, or liquidation distributions for a BVI Business Company clearing through the UK. GBP is freely deliverable and accounts may be maintained in GBP, USD, EUR, CNY and other major currencies without Bank of England consent. De facto restrictions can only derive from OFSI sanctions screening where polymer feedstock, vessels, or counterparties are connected with Russia, Belarus, Iran, Syria, North Korea, or sanctioned persons in Venezuela; banks will therefore apply enhanced sanctions-listing diligence on every payment narrative, intermediary, and counterparty.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender, mandatory conversion, or repatriation timeline exists for export sales proceeds in the United Kingdom. A BVI Business Company may retain all USD/EUR/CNY receipts offshore or convert freely through its UK clearing bank; no Bank of England Balance of Payments reporting thresholds apply to a non-resident BVI entity whose trading is conducted without a UK permanent establishment. Where the company holds a UK VAT registration for intra-EU movements, HMRC Customs Declaration Service (CDS) filings and, where legally applicable, Intrastat/EC Sales List returns must be maintained to evidence the underlying goods flows.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (central bank and Prudential Regulation Authority); HM Treasury (financial sanctions policy via the Office of Financial Sanctions Implementation - OFSI); Financial Conduct Authority (banking conduct and FX market conduct); HMRC (trade and customs reporting). The UK abolished all exchange controls under the Exchange Control (Repeal) Act 1979.
