Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up share capital. A BVI Business Company may be incorporated with zero issued capital; shares may be issued with no par value. No escrow account, capital deposit, or prior bank account is required before the Certificate of Incorporation is issued.

Funding & unlock path: Not applicable for a non-resident offshore company with no physical premises. If a UK or BVI office is later opened, the company must obtain fire safety certification under the applicable fire and rescue service legislation, complete any required electrical installation inspection, and maintain workplace health and safety clearance for the occupied premises.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up share capital. A BVI Business Company may be incorporated with zero issued capital; shares may be issued with no par value. No escrow account, capital deposit, or prior bank account is required before the Certificate of Incorporation is issued.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC), UK Government department; main portal at www.gov.uk/government/organisations/hm-revenue-customs, with online services accessed via HMRC Online Services / Government Gateway and Making Tax Digital (MTD) API.

E-government filing portal

A BVI Business Company (BVI BC) is not automatically entered into any UK tax register. Step 1: Determine UK tax residence under TCGA 1992/CTA 2010 case-law principles; if central management and control is exercised from the UK, the BVI BC must notify HMRC and register for Corporation Tax within 3 months of starting to trade, obtaining a Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR). Step 2: If the BVI BC is non-UK resident but carries on a trade in the UK through a permanent establishment, has UK-source chargeable profits, or makes taxable supplies in the UK, it must register for the relevant UK taxes: Corporation Tax for PE profits; VAT if it makes taxable supplies (UK-established persons: VAT registration when taxable turnover exceeds £90,000 in a rolling 12-month period; non-established taxable persons: registration required from the first UK taxable supply unless using the Non-Union VAT OSS for B2C digital services, in which case simplified registration applies). Step 3: For customs purposes when exporting goods, the company must obtain a GB EORI number. Step 4: If UK employees are engaged, register for PAYE and National Insurance. Step 5: Appoint a UK tax representative if HMRC directs, and provide security/guarantee where required for non-established VAT registrations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope; a BVI BC is treated under UK law as an opaque incorporated company. If it is not UK tax resident, only UK-source profits and UK permanent establishment income fall within UK corporation tax; if UK central management and control exists, the entity is UK-resident and taxable on worldwide profits at entity level. The entity is not within any UK tax-exempt zone and is not pass-through eligible.

Available election forms

The UK has no check-the-box election and no elective recognition of a BVI BC as a transparent partnership for tax purposes. A BVI BC is generally treated as a company. Hybrid mismatch adjustments apply mandatorily under TIOPA 2010 Part 6A to neutralise mismatch outcomes; no election is available to override those rules. UK VAT groups and options to tax are available only to UK-established participants; a standalone non-resident BVI BC cannot avail of those election mechanisms absent a UK fixed establishment.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

£90,000 (from 1 April 2024) for taxable turnover of UK-established persons; non-established taxable persons have no registration threshold and must register from the first UK taxable supply unless using the Non-Union VAT OSS or equivalent simplified scheme for B2C electronic/digital services.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: zero-rating is available where the BVI BC supplies goods for export from the UK to a destination outside the UK, provided the goods leave the UK in the same state and are exported within the applicable statutory time limit (generally within 3 months of the time of supply), and the exporter holds valid export evidence. Services: most cross-border services are treated as outside the scope of UK VAT where the place of supply is outside the UK (for B2B supplies, the customer's jurisdiction; for B2C supplies, the customer's country of residence). Purely domestic supply of digital fitness-app services to UK consumers remains standard-rated; offshore supply to non-UK consumers with no UK establishment nexus is outside UK VAT and does not require zero-rating relief.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic declaration submitted through the UK Customs Declaration Service CDS)
  • Bill of Lading, or alternative transport evidence such as air waybill or CMR
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or equivalent bank/payment-settlement confirmation evidencing receipt of export consideration

UK law does not mandate real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, or digital stamping. VAT-registered businesses under Making Tax Digital (MTD) must keep digital records and submit VAT returns to HMRC via functional compatible software using HMRC's MTD API. Electronic invoicing is permitted voluntarily, but invoices must satisfy VATA 1994 section 78 and the VAT Regulations. No continuous transaction-control clearance is required.

HMRC may verify export evidence at any time. If zero-rating is claimed without valid documentary proof or the goods did not in fact leave the UK, HMRC will assess the output VAT clawback under VATA 1994 section 73, with statutory interest. Penalties apply under Finance Act 2007 Schedule 24 for careless (up to 30%) or deliberate (up to 100%) inaccuracies, under Finance Act 2008 Schedule 41 for failure to notify/register, and criminal prosecution is possible for fraudulent evasion under VATA 1994 section 72. For deliberate concealment, HMRC can extend assessment periods up to 20 years. The company must retain all export, transport, and consideration evidence for at least 6 years; inconsistent payment repatriation or app-store settlement data may trigger deemed domestic supply and clawback of associated input VAT.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The United Kingdom operates a fully liberalised capital and current account convertibility regime. Exchange controls were dismantled in 1979 and no statutory approvals apply to capital movements or profit remittances. Sterling is freely convertible into USD, EUR, and all other deliverable currencies at market rates. A BVI Business Company may remit dividends, service fees, loan repayments, and liquidation proceeds from a UK bank account without HM Treasury permission, subject only to bank-level source-of-funds verification and CRS/FATCA reporting. The UK-BVI tax information exchange arrangement does not impose remittance restrictions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. The UK imposes no export proceeds surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no statutory repatriation timeline. Digital health app revenue settled by Apple and Google may be retained indefinitely in USD, EUR, or GBP, whether held onshore in the UK or offshore in the BVI. Residual obligations are limited to the Bank of England's statistical surveys on external assets and liabilities, which are institutional data returns, not control measures.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (Prudential Regulation Authority); HM Treasury (Office of Financial Sanctions Implementation) is the sanctioning authority; the UK does not maintain a standalone Foreign Exchange Administration body. The Bank of England collects balance-of-payments statistics and oversees liquidity through the Sterling Monetary Framework.
