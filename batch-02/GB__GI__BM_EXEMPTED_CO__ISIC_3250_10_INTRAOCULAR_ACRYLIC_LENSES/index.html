Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar, Income Tax Office and HM Customs & Excise Department; official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation-and-duty

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company establishing tax/operational substance in Gibraltar must register as an overseas company with the Gibraltar Companies House, then file Form TAX-REG-100 with the Income Tax Office, submitting certified copies of the Bermuda certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles, board resolution, Gibraltar registered office details, and proof of local place of business; the Income Tax Office issues a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN), while HM Customs & Excise issues a General Indirect Tax (GIT) registration number for taxable supplies. Payroll Tax and Business Property Occupation Tax registration are required if staff are engaged or premises occupied.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current-account and capital-account convertibility; Gibraltar maintains no exchange-control legislation, no prior-approval filing for inward or outward remittances, no dividend/interest/royalty repatriation ceilings, and no surrender obligations. GIP circulates only within Gibraltar and is interchangeable at par with GBP notes within the jurisdiction; all GIP-denominated commercial bank accounts are effectively GBP monetary claims, eliminating conversion friction and basis risk for GBP clearing. EUR and USD convertibility operates through ordinary correspondent banking (London, Frankfurt, New York clearers) with same-day or T+1 settlement; no GFSC notification is required for any quantum of cross-border transfer. The residual practical limitation is acute de-risking appetite: Bermuda exempted company shareholders, non-EEA ultimate beneficial owners, and medical-device trade flows are screened rigorously; banks may impose a minimum operational balance of GBP 150,000 to GBP 250,000 and require a local Gibraltar corporate-services provider as the appointed compliance point of contact.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender requirement exists in Gibraltar. Intraocular lens export receivables denominated in USD, EUR, GBP, or third currencies may be retained indefinitely in any licensed Gibraltar bank account without mandated conversion into GIP or GBP, and without a time-limited repatriation window. For financial reporting purposes, IFRS 9 / IAS 21 revaluation is required at each period-end, and the GFSC-licensed auditor will expect a documented FX risk-management policy covering retained USD/EUR balances. Where export proceeds are received via trade finance instruments (documentary collections or letters of credit), the bank requires the full trade-document set (commercial invoice, bill of lading, packing list, certificate of origin, and any EU MDR/UKCA conformity certificate) to be presented within the LC expiry terms, but this is documentary compliance, not an FX surrender rule.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) acting as the integrated prudential, conduct, and AML/CFT supervisor; Government of Gibraltar Currency Notes Act 1934 establishes a currency-board arrangement under which the Gibraltar pound (GIP) is issued at a hard 1:1 peg against the Bank of England pound sterling (GBP); the Bank of England provides no direct lender-of-last-resort standing to Gibraltar-licensed banks, but HM Treasury sanctions and UK financial sanctions regulations apply extraterritorially to Gibraltar under the Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018, as extended by the Gibraltar Sanctions Regulations 2023.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: CONDITIONAL-MEDIUM for payments and FX conversion; LOW for institutional trade finance. Gibraltar-licensed electronic money institutions and UK FCA-authorized EMIs operating under the Gibraltar-UK financial services arrangements can open multi-currency accounts (GBP, EUR, USD) with virtual IBANs, SWIFT, SEPA, and Faster Payments rails within 3–10 business days. Legal constraints: (1) an EMI is not a bank, cannot issue letters of credit, cannot open a formal correspondent banking relationship, and cannot offer overdraft or merchant-cash-advance facilities; (2) many EMI underwriting engines auto-decline Bermuda exempted companies or any GIP-domiciled vehicle with cross-border medical-device distribution unless the applicant first completes a Gibraltar company registration under Part II of the Companies Act 2014 and appoints a GFSC-licensed registered agent; (3) high-risk activity classification (Class III intraocular lens manufacturing) triggers mandatory source-of-wealth evidence for every UBO above 25% and a personal residential address verification via notary or embassy apostille; (4) the EMI must not be used for LC settlement, payroll disbursement to sanctioned regions, or cash-intensive supplier networks in Vietnam/China without fully documented purchase orders and customs declarations. Recommended structure: use an EMI as the disbursement gate for small-value supplier payments and for EUR/USD conversion; carry all receivable collections and trade finance through Santander or GIB.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity or shareholder-loan capital from the Bermuda parent or UBO into the Gibraltar operating account must be executed via SWIFT MT103 or MT202 from a regulated bank. Recommended purpose codes in Field 70: 'EQUI' (capital contribution), 'TRTS' (treasury settlement), or if loan funding, 'LOAN' with an accompanying loan agreement. No Gibraltar exchange-control approval or declaration is required; however, the receiving bank will require: (a) a certified board resolution authorising the injection and account signatories; (b) a capital-allocation letter referencing the ISIC 3250 operating plan and projected monthly burn; (c) the UBO's passport and source-of-funds evidence for any amount above GBP 100,000; (d) a completed GFSC-compliant incoming-funds questionnaire. For EUR inbound from EU corporate treasury, use SEPA Credit Transfer (SCT) with purpose code 'COMC' or 'CAPT'; for USD inbound from a US parent or distributor, route via London CHIPS/EBA correspondent with the Gibraltar bank's SWIFT BIC and include the account IBAN in Field 59; verification includes an incoming-remittance form, a copy of the UBO passport, and a signed indemnity for any payment that references cryptolinked intermediaries.Clearing rails are tiered by value and counterparty risk. (1) Supplier payments for consumable materials under USD 50,000: SWIFT MT103 with payment terms 'CRED' through the bank's online portal, or SEPA/CHAPS for UK/EU vendors; advance payment requires a supplier intercompany or purchase-order reference, with wire approval authority (single/double signature) set at board level. (2) High-value procurement of hydrophobic acrylic foldable lens blanks, lathe tooling, and diamond cutters: irrevocable commercial Letter of Credit issued by the Gibraltar bank under UCP 600 terms, payable at sight in favour of the Asian supplier, with a SWIFT MT700/707 chain; the LC mandates the full document set - commercial invoice, full set clean-on-board bill of lading, packing list, certificate of origin, and third-party quality inspection certificate - and debits the GIP/GBP/USD treasury position only on compliant presentation. (3) Institutional receivables from EU/UK hospital distributors: the buyer's MT103/MT102 is collected through the Gibraltar bank's clearing account; for large tender contracts above EUR 1 million, a receivable-assignment or forfaiting arrangement may be used where the Gibraltar bank purchases the accepted bill of exchange or deferred-payment LC receivable with full recourse to the issuer, subject to a board-approved treasury risk policy. (4) Sweep arrangements: end-of-day JPY and minor-currency balances are netted into GBP or USD through a spot conversion to minimise FX drag, with automated reporting to the GFSC-designated compliance officer.Plain-vanilla spot GBP/USD, GBP/EUR and USD/EUR transactions executed at the interbank rate with same-day or T+2 settlement; no GFSC restriction provided the counterparty is an authorised institution and the transaction is booked against genuine trade exposures.Outright FX forwards (deliverable) of maturity up to 12 months between GBP and USD/EUR, documented under an ISDA 2002 master agreement; eligible for hedge accounting under IFRS 9 where the hedged item is the forecast procurement or receivables cash flow.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) referencing USD/CNY or USD/INR, permitted as bilateral OTC derivatives solely to hedge the FX exposure embedded in Asian supplier contracts; only the GBP/USD settlement leg reflects in the Gibraltar account, and all trades must be reported under UK EMIR to an FCA-authorised TR if the counterparty is a UK or EU institution.Cross-currency swaps (GBP↔USD, GBP↔EUR) of tenors up to 5 years, permitted for refinancing the shareholder loan without currency-basis leakage; collateralisation under a Credit Support Annex with a transferable AIB/BBVA or UK clearing bank is standard.Prohibited instruments and guardrails: leveraged speculative derivatives, binary options, crypto-denominated or crypto-collateralised hedges, and any transaction which breaches the Financial Services Act 2019 dealing-in-investments licence boundary; the treasury policy must contain a board-approved notional exposure cap of 150% of projected quarterly FX inflows and require dual sign-off by the finance director and the GFSC-licensed AMLCO for any hedge exceeding GBP 500,000 equivalent.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 of aggregate controlled cross-border transactions in an accounting period triggers mandatory Local File documentation; a Master File is required when the multinational group's annual consolidated turnover exceeds GIP 50,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar's Economic Substance Act 2020 applies to Gibraltar-resident companies carrying on listed relevant activities, requiring adequate physical presence, locally resident management, and proportionate employees/expenditure. Manufacturing of intraocular lenses is not a listed relevant activity; however, a non-resident Bermuda Exempted Company claiming PE-only status must maintain its central management and control outside Gibraltar and ensure the Gibraltar branch undertakes substantive operational activities attributable to the PE, otherwise the tax-exempt or PE treatment may be disregarded.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Statements

Statutory sanction: On conviction in Gibraltar, imprisonment for up to 7 years and/or a fine up to GIP 100,000; on indictment, imprisonment for up to 14 years and/or an unlimited fine. Civil tax penalties of up to 200% of undercharged tax are also levied.

Director personal liability: Directors, officers, and shadow directors who consent to or connive in the evasion are jointly and severally liable for the evaded tax, may face criminal conviction, disqualification as a director, and the courts may lift the corporate veil to order restitution and impose travel bans.
Shell Company and Sham Substance

Statutory sanction: The GTO may issue a denial of tax residence/profit attribution, impose a fine of up to GIP 50,000, disregard the artificial arrangements, and reallocate profits to the Gibraltar PE. Continued disregard can lead to the striking off of the Gibraltar establishment and revocation of manufacturing permits.

Director personal liability: Directors who certify false substance declarations face individual fines and imprisonment of up to 2 years and may be made personally liable for tax, social security, and penalties when the corporate veil is pierced; travel restrictions apply under AML ancillary rules.
