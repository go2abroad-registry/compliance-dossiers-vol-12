Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registrar of Corporate Affairs, BVI Financial Services Commission, Road Town, Tortola, British Virgin Islands; electronic lodgement is effected exclusively through the VIRRGIN portal by a licensed BVI Registered Agent.

Governing statute

BVI Business Companies Act, 2004 (No. 16 of 2004, as amended) - the exclusive incorporation statute for a BVI Business Company; the UK Companies Act 2006 does not apply to a BVI-registered company (the British Virgin Islands is a British Overseas Territory with its own separate corporate registry). Key provisions: incorporation by registration, registered agent requirement, registered office requirement, memorandum and articles of association, share capital and no-par-value shares, directors and officers, and rectification of the register.

Formation / constitutional filings

Memorandum and Articles of Association (statutory charter instrument filed through the licensed registered agent via the VIRRGIN corporate registry platform); the Certificate of Incorporation is issued by the Registrar upon registration.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 18 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital; a company may be incorporated with one no-par-value ordinary share; authorized capital not exceeding USD 50,000 attracts the standard USD 350 Government incorporation fee; no escrow account, capital deposit, or similar deposit-to-escrow rule applies.

Funding & unlock path: Not applicable to the BVI registered office, which is a service address only. If the company later operates a UK office, the office must satisfy the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 fire-risk assessment, the Workplace (Health, Safety and Welfare) Regulations 1992 work-station and welfare clearances, and standard employers' liability insurance; no structural load inspection is triggered for low-density office occupancy.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital; a company may be incorporated with one no-par-value ordinary share; authorized capital not exceeding USD 50,000 attracts the standard USD 350 Government incorporation fee; no escrow account, capital deposit, or similar deposit-to-escrow rule applies.

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

None - no industrial licensing board is engaged because ISIC 7110 civil-infrastructure CAD/BIM is an office-based, cloud-delivered professional engineering-drafting service; it does not constitute manufacturing, mining, or a facility-based activity under BVI or UK law.

Category: None - no statutory operating or manufacturing permit is required for the BVI Business Company's offshore BIM and structural-drafting services; registration with the BVI International Tax Authority for economic substance notification is the only administrative condition precedent.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC), Government of the United Kingdom; digital portal at https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/hm-revenue-customs.

E-government filing portal

A BVI Business Company without a UK permanent establishment is not automatically registered for UK Corporation Tax. Once a UK permanent establishment is created, the foreign company must register under the Corporation Tax provisions within three months of commencing the UK business using the HMRC 'Register a new company for Corporation Tax' online service and obtain a Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR). For VAT, the BVI BC must register with HMRC if it makes taxable supplies in the UK and taxable turnover exceeds the statutory threshold, or voluntarily, using the VAT Registration Service (Form VAT1 for non-UK established businesses) to obtain a VAT registration number (VRN); a VAT representative may be required for a non-UK established business in prescribed cases. If the BVI BC exports or imports goods, it must also obtain a GB EORI number through HMRC's customs portal.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; as a non-UK resident company the BVI BC is taxed on a territorial basis on UK permanent establishment profits and other UK-source income only. The BVI company's domestic tax-exempt status does not create a UK exemption.

Available election forms

The UK has no general check-the-box election comparable to U.S. rules; classification follows legal form and the foreign law characteristics. No election is available to treat a BVI Business Company as transparent for UK Corporation Tax. Hybrid mismatch elections, including the option to reduce or allocate a deduction under Part 6A TIOPA 2010 and the 'taxed twice' mutual adjustment under CTA 2009, may be made in the corporation tax return where a hybrid financial instrument or hybrid entity arrangement is present.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

£90,000 of taxable turnover in a rolling 12-month period (effective 1 April 2024); a non-UK established business may also have mandatory registration for distance sales or UK acquisitions, and voluntary registration is available where taxable supplies are made.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies when tangible goods are exported from the UK to a destination outside the UK and the supplier holds proof of export; for offshore BIM/CAD and structural drafting services, the supply is zero-rated if made to a business customer established outside the UK under the general B2B place-of-supply rule, provided the supplier retains evidence that the customer belongs in a foreign territory, that the service deliverable is effectively used or enjoyed outside the UK, and that no UK VAT is otherwise due.

  • Customs Export Declaration (C88/SAD) or Customs Declaration Service electronic declaration with Movement Reference Number (MRN) where goods are exported
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export from the UK
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT/MT103 payment confirmation demonstrating settlement from the foreign buyer
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Underlying BIM/CAD engineering services contract or purchase order and evidence of the customer's non-UK business status

HMRC operates Making Tax Digital (MTD) for VAT: VAT-registered businesses must maintain digital records and file returns through MTD-compatible software using HMRC's Application Programming Interface (API) platform. There is no real-time fiscal pre-clearance, mandatory coding, or digital stamping of e-invoices; however, PEPPOL-compliant e-invoicing is accepted for UK public procurement and encouraged in B2G contracts.

HMRC may disapply zero-rating and assess VAT if the required evidence is not held or conditions are not satisfied. Penalties for deliberate or concealed VAT inaccuracies reach up to 100% of the tax at stake; civil fraud penalties may likewise reach 100% of VAT avoided. Criminal prosecution under the Value Added Tax Act 1994 s72 can result in imprisonment for up to seven years and an unlimited fine. Retrospective assessments may be issued for up to four years, or up to 20 years for deliberate conduct involving international tax loss or concealment, together with interest on under-declared VAT. Where an export is found not to have been made, the zero-rated VAT is clawed back and applicable customs penalties may be pursued.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The United Kingdom operates a fully liberalised foreign exchange regime. Pound Sterling is freely convertible on both current and capital account for residents and non-residents, with no exchange control licensing, no surrender obligations, and no restrictions on profit remittance, dividend distribution, or loan repayment. Cross-border payments involving a BVI Business Company and a UK operational account are permitted provided the transaction does not breach UK financial sanctions regulations, proceeds meet AML due diligence standards, and the beneficial owner declaration is current. No matter is treated as capital-account controlled except under emergency reserve powers, which have not been exercised.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no statutory surrender rules or mandatory conversion ratios in the United Kingdom. Export proceeds may be retained in any permitted currency, held offshore, or repatriated as treasury management dictates. No timeline exists for repatriation or conversion; the only practical requirements are bank-specific confirmation of the underlying trade and standard economic crime transaction monitoring. For a BVI entity, no UK tax charge arises merely on export receipts unless the company is UK tax resident or carries on a UK trade through a permanent establishment; accordingly, the offshore cash may remain in a GBP-denominated UK account without forced FX conversion.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (BoE), operating in coordination with His Majesty's Treasury (HMT); HMT retains policy responsibility for exchange control and financial sanctions, while the BoE provides Real-Time Gross Settlement (RTGS) and oversees systemic liquidity through the Sterling Monetary Framework.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium for FCA-regulated UK EMIs (e.g., Wise, Revolut, Currencycloud) and Low for non-EEA or newly regulated digital EMIs. Legal constraints: post-Brexit, EEA-authorised EMI passports no longer extend to the UK; the entity must onboard through a UK FCA-authorised e-money institution. UK EMIs must safeguard client funds in separate accounts and complete full KYC; they may reject BVI structures where ownership layers are opaque or where expected transaction values materially exceed the entity's disclosed engineering outsourcing profile. An EMI is suitable primarily as a receivable collection rail, not as the sole treasury account for capital escrow or large trade settlements.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital must be routed as a SWIFT MT103 wire transfer from the registered BVI shareholder, using field 50K with the making bank, field 59 as the UK receiving account, and payment purpose code SUPPL (capital contribution) or LOAN (shareholder financing). The remittance instruction must quote the unique BVI company identifier and the compliance reference issued by the onboarding bank. Receiving banks require a copy of the shareholder resolution, the equity subscription or loan agreement, and source-of-funds statements before release. Settlement credits through the Bank of England CHAPS/RTGS system are executed in real time during London cut-off hours, with intraday finality for standard business days. UK banks will screen the payment against HM Treasury's Consolidated List and any secondary sanctions exposure; funds are blocked pending completion of sanctions verification.Outbound trade and operational payments may be settled via SWIFT MT103 for single payments, SWIFT MT202 for interbank cover, commercial letters of credit opened by the UK bank in favour of foreign engineering counterparties, and documentary collections where milestone drawings are released against payment. For domestic suppliers, CHAPS payments provide instant final settlement; for cross-border settlements, the bank may convert GBP at spot or on an executed forward using standalone instructions. The BVI company may issue payment orders referencing each project under ISIC 7110/CAD-BIM, with invoice numbers and tax references embedded in the structured remittance field of the MT103. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: £1 million for Local File; £50 million for Master File (based on turnover and related party transactions).

Economic substance scope: UK does not have ESR, but CFC rules and tax residence tests apply; a BVI company managed and controlled in the UK is UK tax resident and must demonstrate substance to avoid CFC attribution.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Penalty up to 200% of tax due; criminal prosecution with unlimited fine and imprisonment up to 7 years.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for penalties if they knowingly participate; travel bans and asset freezes possible.
Shell Company

Statutory sanction: CFC rules may attribute profits to UK shareholders; penalties for failure to file CT600; potential strike-off.

Director personal liability: Directors may be jointly liable for unpaid tax if company is a sham; disqualification as director.
AML Breach

Statutory sanction: Fines up to £500,000 or 5% of turnover; suspension of operations; criminal prosecution.

Director personal liability: Directors can be personally fined and imprisoned up to 2 years for consenting to AML violations.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Adjustment of profits, penalties up to 100% of additional tax, and interest.

Director personal liability: Directors may be liable for penalties if they knowingly approved non-compliant pricing.
