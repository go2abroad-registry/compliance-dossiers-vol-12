Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar; official e-services are accessed through the Government of Gibraltar Taxation Portal, with CRS/FATCA filings submitted through the Gibraltar Reporting System (GRS).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the Bermuda Exempted Company with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies (no minimum capital required; Certificate of Exemption obtained). Step 2: If a place of business is established in Gibraltar, register the foreign company with Gibraltar Companies House under the Companies Act 2014. Step 3: Apply to the Commissioner of Income Tax on the prescribed corporate registration form to obtain the Gibraltar Tax Identification Number, disclosing registered office, directors, beneficiaries, and UBO particulars. Step 4: Register with HM Customs Gibraltar for a customs trader identification code when importing or exporting goods. Step 5: Register under GRS for CRS/FATCA automatic exchange and ensure compliance with the Beneficial Ownership Register filing. Step 6: Assess any UK VAT registration requirement arising from UK-based sales activity or stock-holding.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope : A non-resident Bermuda-incorporated company is taxable in Gibraltar only on profits arising in or derived from Gibraltar; a Bermuda Exempted Company is separately within Tax_Exempt_Zone treatment in Bermuda, where no income or capital gains tax applies and only an annual government fee is payable.

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a check-the-box election. Available mechanisms include: advance tax ruling requests to the Commissioner of Income Tax regarding territorial scope and rate; group relief election for Gibraltar-resident group companies; hybrid mismatch notification and adjustment elections under the Income Tax (Amendment) Act 2019; and transfer pricing contemporaneous documentation elections, including an option for a unilateral or bilateral advance pricing agreement.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Import Duty and Excise Duty. Gibraltar has no VAT, GST, or sales and use tax; under Protocol 3 of the Treaty of Accession, Gibraltar is outside the EU VAT area and does not charge VAT on goods or services supplied in its territory.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable, as no VAT/GST regime exists. Any importer of goods must register with HM Customs Gibraltar for a customs trader identification code with no de minimis turnover threshold; import duty is assessed per the UK-Gibraltar customs arrangement under the relevant tariff classification, with polyhedral tabletop dice typically classified under HS heading 9504.90 and frequently attracting a zero or low positive duty rate depending on origin and applicable trade preference.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in Gibraltar because the jurisdiction levies no VAT or sales tax; therefore no 0% export VAT claim or refund mechanism exists. Exports of epoxy resin dice from Gibraltar are outside the scope of any Gibraltar consumption tax, while UK import VAT, if any, is accounted for by the UK buyer at the border. No Gibraltar indirect tax is chargeable on either local retail supply or outward export supply.

  • Customs Export Declaration (submitted via the UK Customs Declaration Service applicable to Gibraltar departures)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Commercial Invoice describing the sharp-edge epoxy resin liquid core dice and HS classification
  • Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT/MT103 evidence of export proceeds

None. Gibraltar does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction controls, certified e-invoicing, SII, or digital stamping. Export and import declarations are filed electronically through the UK Customs Declaration Service or CHIEF, while commercial B2B and B2C invoicing remains in standard free-format electronic or paper form.

No VAT clawback can arise as no VAT is charged. Customs audit scrutiny applies to export declarations, duty drawback, and transit reliefs; false or incomplete declarations may trigger forfeiture of goods, monetary penalties of up to three times the value of the goods, suspension of the customs trader code, and director personal liability under Gibraltar customs legislation. Where export proceeds are not remitted within the 180-day benchmark, the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit expects the payment institution to apply enhanced due diligence and potentially file a Suspicious Activity Report; trade mis-invoicing schemes may additionally attract prosecution under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility, current and capital account. No Gibraltar or UK exchange-control statute restricts purchases, sales, or remittances in GBP, USD, EUR, or GIP. Trade settlements, service fees, and marketplace sweeps move freely without pre-authorisation. Profit remittance (dividend, capital reduction, or repayable shareholder loan) faces no FX hurdle and no Gibraltar withholding tax; the bank will expect board minutes, CRS self-certification, and evidence that Bermuda tax-exempt status does not create a sham permanent-establishment exposure.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Gibraltar and the United Kingdom impose no export-proceeds surrender timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no requirement to repatriate export receipts. The company may retain 100% of proceeds in any currency, in any jurisdiction, indefinitely. The only binding obligations are documentary: retain underlying invoices, bills of lading, and marketplace settlement reports for at least five years under the GFSC AML/CTF Directions, and accept the transacting bank's ITRS aggregate statistical reporting.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the licensing and supervisory authority for Gibraltar banks; H.M. Government of Gibraltar (Financial Secretary, as Currency Commissioner) issues Gibraltar pounds at a statutory 1:1 peg to GBP; the Bank of England sets monetary policy for the underlying sterling; the Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) supervises the Bermuda Exempted Company and issues the Certificate of Compliance required for Gibraltar onboarding.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% (20% for regulated utilities, gaming and telecommunication providers)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Gibraltar does not impose dividend withholding tax, whether to residents, Bermuda, UK, or other non-resident shareholders.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 12 months after the end of the chargeable accounting period; for a period ending on 31 December, the statutory due date is 31 December of the following year.

Box 8 Adjusted assessable profit (GIP). Reconcile with the transfer pricing documentation; if the Bermuda parent has not adopted arm's-length remuneration for the PE, an upward assessment will be made. Box 9 Tax computed at 12.5% (GIP). Multiply adjusted profit by 12.5%; do not apply the 20% rate unless the branch derives regulated utility, telecom or gaming revenue. Late return: fixed GIP 100 penalty; daily surcharge of GIP 30 per day for up to 90 days; additional GIP 300 after 6 months and GIP 100 or 100% of tax due after 12 months; criminal prosecution for fraudulent returns under the Crimes Act 2011.

Apply any available unilateral foreign tax credit for income taxes paid in Bermuda, capped at the Gibraltar tax on the same income; pay the balance to the Commissioner. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 aggregate controlled transactions for mandatory Local File; Master File required when the Gibraltar group is part of an MNE group with consolidated group revenue above GIP 750,000,000.

Economic substance scope: 2019 Economic Substance Regulations apply to relevant activities (headquarters, distribution, service centre, pure holding, IP, financing, leasing and regulated financial activities). Manufacturing and sale of polyhedral dice do not constitute a relevant activity; however, the PE must still evidence regular board meetings, a physical notion of management in Gibraltar, and operational substance in line with OECD substance guidance.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Return

Statutory sanction: Fine not exceeding GIP 10,000 and/or imprisonment for up to 7 years under the Crimes Act 2011 s.190; civil penalty of up to 100% of the tax undercharged under Income Tax Act 2010.

Director personal liability: Directors of the Bermuda Exempted Company that knowingly cause the false filing are jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax; the Supreme Court may issue a travel ban, asset restraint and director disqualification until the liability is liquidated.
Shell Company / Artificial Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Compulsory strike-off of the Gibraltar overseas company registration; revocation of tax registration; reassessment under the 20% anti-abuse rate; penalty of up to GIP 500,000 under the Economic Substance Rules; possible court-ordered liquidation.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally assessed for the entire landmarked profit under section 96 of the Income Tax Act, with a 15-year restriction on leaving Gibraltar and extradition to the UK for willful default.
AML / Payment Rails Abuse

Statutory sanction: Forfeiture of the payment account, fine up to GIP 2,000,000 and/or 14 years imprisonment; suspension or revocation of the Gibraltar financial services permission; referral to the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit.

Director personal liability: Directors and shadow managers exiting Gibraltar may face personal criminal liability; a conviction triggers disqualification from directorships for 15 years, personal asset freezing, and denial of bail pending trial.
