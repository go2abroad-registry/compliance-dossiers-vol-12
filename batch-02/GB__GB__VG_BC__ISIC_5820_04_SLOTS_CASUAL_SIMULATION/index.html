Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) – GOV.UK tax administration portal; VAT registration via gov.uk or the VATEC71 non-established taxable person procedure.

E-government filing portal

A non-established BVI Business Company is not required to obtain a UK Corporation Tax Unique Taxpayer Reference unless it creates a UK permanent establishment. If it supplies digital services to UK consumers, it must register for UK VAT before the first supply. The application is made to HMRC using the online non-established VAT registration route or form VATEC71, supported by the BVI certificate of incorporation, proof of business address, power of attorney, and a description of the digital supplies. HMRC issues a 9-digit GB VAT number. A separate UK VAT Mini One Stop Shop (MOSS) registration may be used for non-UK suppliers of business-to-consumer electronic services.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone (the BVI Business Company is tax-exempt in its home jurisdiction; for UK tax purposes it is a non-resident company with a narrow territorial scope, taxable on UK-source profits only if attributable to a UK permanent establishment). — A non-established BVI Business Company is not required to obtain a UK Corporation Tax Unique Taxpayer Reference unless it creates a UK permanent establishment. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (the BVI Business Company is tax-exempt in its home jurisdiction; for UK tax purposes it is a non-resident company with a narrow territorial scope, taxable on UK-source profits only if attributable to a UK permanent establishment).

Available election forms

No UK check-the-box or entity classification election is available for a BVI BC. UK hybrid mismatch rules apply only to persons within the UK corporation tax net and are not applicable to an offshore non-resident vehicle without a UK PE. The company may elect into the UK VAT MOSS for digital services, but this is a filing mechanism, not a substantive tax classification election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GBP 90,000 annual taxable turnover for UK-established businesses. A non-established business supplying digital services to UK non-taxable consumers has no de minimis threshold and must register for UK VAT immediately upon making the first taxable supply.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

UK VAT zero-rating is available for physical exports of goods where statutory evidence is held. For simulated social casino digital services there is no physical export, so zero-rating does not apply. Where the customer belongs outside the UK, the place of supply is outside the UK and the supply is outside the scope of UK VAT; where the customer is a UK non-business consumer, UK VAT at 20% applies. The correct characterisation is therefore a place-of-supply analysis rather than an export zero-rating claim.

  • Not applicable to digital services; no physical customs declaration or bill of lading is required for cross-border digital gaming supplies.
  • If a physical export of goods were to be zero-rated, the standard HMRC evidence bundle would comprise: Customs Export Declaration (CDS/CHIEF), Bill of Lading or air waybill, commercial invoice, packing list, proof of shipment, and a bank inward remittance certificate or payment confirmation where applicable.

There is no real-time e-invoicing clearance, digital stamping, or fiscal pre-clearance API mandate in the UK. VAT-registered businesses must maintain digital records and submit VAT returns through software using HMRC's Making Tax Digital (MTD) application programming interface; no government authorisation of individual invoices is required before issuance.

HMRC may raise VAT assessments, deny input VAT recovery, and claw back any unlawfully zero-rated amounts with interest. Civil penalties under Schedule 24 Finance Act 2007 for inaccurate VAT returns are up to 100% of the potential lost revenue, with higher penalties for deliberate and concealed behaviour. Criminal prosecution may follow for fraudulent evasion of VAT, and dishonest claims for repayment or wrongful zero-rating expose directors and principals to personal liability, disqualification orders, and asset-freezing actions.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The UK operates full capital and current account convertibility under the Currency Act 1983 and general Bank of England practice. There is no legal distinction between current and capital account treatment for non-resident BVI companies; GBP, USD, EUR and all freely tradeable currencies may be converted without prior approval. Profit remittance and dividend repatriation by a BVI Business Company are unrestricted, subject only to bank-level AML/KYC due diligence, CRS/FATCA reporting on the controlling person, sanctions screening, and the risk that an FCA-regulated UK bank may treat the BVI entity as a higher-risk corporate structure requiring enhanced source-of-funds evidence. No withholding tax is imposed by the UK on outbound dividends to a BVI company under domestic law, though a UK permanent establishment of the BVI company would trigger UK corporation tax on trading profits attributable to that PE.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. The UK imposes no statutory surrender obligation, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no timeline for repatriation of export proceeds, including software publishing and virtual slots game licensing royalties. A BVI company may retain all export receipts in foreign currency in any bank account worldwide, or convert them freely at a UK-authorised bank. The only residual restrictions flow from UK/EU financial sanctions regulations (Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018) where a receiving counterparty is a designated person or a sanctioned jurisdiction, in which case funds must be frozen rather than converted.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (BoE), acting jointly with HM Treasury and the Prudential Regulation Authority; the United Kingdom maintains no dedicated Foreign Exchange Administration and no exchange-control licensing body under the Exchange Control Act 1947 (substantially repealed), with residual statutory powers vested in HM Treasury for sanctions and asset-freezing purposes.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol requires all capital injections to be routed via SWIFT MT103 (or ISO 20022 CBPR+ pain.001 / camt.054 where the correspondent bank supports it) from the shareholder's account in a well-regulated jurisdiction, with a purpose code reflecting direct investment equity, such as 'EQUI - Direct Investment Equity Capital' or the UK bank-required narrative 'Capital Contribution and Working Capital Injection into VG_BC'. The receiving bank will require a board resolution approving the capitalisation, an updated register of members showing the allotment, a FATCA/CRS self-certification (W-8BEN-E for US persons, CRS entity self-certification for the BVI company), and a source-of-funds package including the remitter's last six months of bank statements. Same-day GBP clearance should use CHAPS or Faster Payments where the receiving account is a UK clearing bank; USD and EUR injections should be cleared over intra-bank nostro/current accounts to avoid intermediary fees and ensure a clean MT103 trail. Cryptocurrency rails, personal accounts, cash deposits, and third-party payer funding are prohibited for first-in capital.
