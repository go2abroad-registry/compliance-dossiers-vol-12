Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office, official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation; corporate registry filing via Gibraltar Companies House portal

E-government filing portal

A Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) must apply to the Gibraltar Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN) using the partnership registration certificate, details of all general and limited partners, and confirmation of non-resident partner status. Non-resident foreign entities do not need a local registered agent tax nomination unless the ELP derives Gibraltar-source income or carries on a trade or business in Gibraltar; in such cases, a prescribed registration form must be filed and a TIN issued before any taxable activity begins.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Gibraltar generally treats limited partnerships as tax-transparent; the ELP may elect to be treated as a company for tax purposes under the Income Tax Act 2010 if it meets the legislated criteria, subject to approval by the Commissioner of Income Tax. Hybrid mismatch rules under Gibraltar's Income Tax (Hybrid Mismatch) Regulations require careful election documentation where an ELP is treated as transparent in Gibraltar but opaque in a foreign jurisdiction.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST; Gibraltar applies a limited system of import duties and customs excises under the Customs Ordinance; digital services and VPN subscriptions are outside the scope of indirect taxation unless physically imported goods are involved

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable; Gibraltar has no VAT registration threshold. The ELP has no indirect tax registration requirement for remote digital services; import duty registration is required only for physical imports into Gibraltar under the Customs Ordinance.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar does not operate a domestic VAT zero-rating mechanism. For cross-border digital services provided by an ELP to non-resident customers, the supply is treated as outside the scope of Gibraltar taxation, subject to the ELP demonstrating that the place of effective management and control is not in Gibraltar, the service is performed outside Gibraltar, and no UK or EU VAT is chargeable.

  • No customs export documentation is required for intangible digital VPN services; conservatively maintain documented customer contracts, IP destination evidence, payment gateway settlement reports, and partner identity records for tax audit purposes
  • For any physical servers or related equipment exported through Gibraltar customs, retain the Gibraltar Customs Entry/Export Notification, commercial invoice, packing list, and proof of departure to substantiate non-Gibraltar use

None. Gibraltar does not mandate real-time electronic fiscal clearance or digital stamping for software-as-a-service exports. Standard commercial invoicing suffices, and the Income Tax Office requires ordinary accounting records evidencing the supply of VPN network privacy services outside Gibraltar.

Because Gibraltar has no VAT, there is no export zero-rating refund regime to abuse. Conversely, inaccurate customs declarations on physical goods attract penalties under the Customs Ordinance, and deliberate misstatement of the ELP's tax-transparent status or Gibraltar-source income may trigger retrospective assessments, interest, and criminal penalties under the Income Tax Act 2010 and Proceeds of Crime Act 2015.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar is a fully convertible, no-exchange-control jurisdiction. Current account transactions (software fees, VPN subscription receipts) and capital account movements (partner capital contributions, IP licence imports, debt injections) are free from pre-approval. Profit remittance and partner distributions to the Cayman ELP owners are not subject to local FX gates or approval, provided the accounting trail and GFSC-mandated AML documents are retained. Practical friction is limited to the bank's correspondent network: GBP settles via UK CHAPS/Faster Payments, EUR via SEPA (through UK/EU correspondents), USD via London/New York clearing; GIP circulates only inside Gibraltar.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory obligation to surrender export proceeds or to convert VPN subscription receipts into GIP/GBP. The ELP can retain, convert or disburse USD/EUR/GBP revenue at its own discretion. No mandatory ratio or deadline applies at central bank level. The operative constraints are purely compliance-based: the bank will require a full source-of-funds mapping for PSP settlement flows (Stripe/Paymentwall) and a documented FX policy evidencing that each conversion leg is real, priced at arm's length and entered into the partnership statement.

Central bank supervision: No indigenous central bank exists in Gibraltar. Monetary policy is set by the Bank of England, which anchors the Gibraltar pound (GIP) at a fixed 1:1 parity with GBP. Banking supervision, Anti-Money Laundering (AML), Countering the Financing of Terrorism (CFT) and foreign exchange oversight are executed by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) under the Financial Services Act 2019 and the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015. GIP banknotes and coins are issued by the Government of Gibraltar under the Currency Notes Act 1934.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium and conditional. Wise Business, Airwallex and Payoneer can in principle serve the Gibraltar ELP as an overlay treasury rail, and the target PSP rails (Stripe/Paymentwall) may integrate with them efficiently. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Partner capital contributions and IP-licence fees are routed by SWIFT MT103 in GBP (CHAPS), EUR (SEPA/TARGET2) or USD (London/New York correspondent) to the ELP's treasury account at the Gibraltar clearing bank. Remittance information must state CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION or SUBSCRIPTION PRICE together with the partnership agreement date; the beneficiary account number must match the ELP's name exactly to avoid return or sanctions-screening issues. The bank will hold funds in suspense until it verifies the executed contribution resolution, the LP agreement abstract and the source-of-wealth questionnaire. No exchange control approval is required, but the bank will maintain an internal audit trail for each inbound leg.The ELP uses SWIFT MT103 for routine payments to cloud infrastructure, licensing and professional advisers; MT202 COV for large correspondent transfers between the Gibraltar head office and UK/EEA partner banks; and irrevocable commercial letters of credit (UCP 600) confirmed by UK or EU banks for any physical network-hardware procurement. Domestic GBP payroll and supplier runs clear via UK Faster Payments or CHAPS; EUR disbursements to EU co-licensors clear via SEPA Credit Transfer or SEPA Instant; high-volume PSP net settlements to Stripe and Paymentwall are executed in batched PAIN.001 ISO 20022 files rather than individual wires, preserving audit integrity and lower fees.Outright deliverable FX forward contracts on GIP/GBP, EUR/GBP, USD/GBP and EUR/USD with approved Tier 1 counterparties, tenors up to 12 months, documented under ISDA or a Gibraltar master agreement.Cash-settled non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for emerging-market subscription currencies (BRL, TRY, MXN, ZAR, PLN, IDR) to hedge PSP settlement translation risk without physical delivery.Cross-currency swaps (XCCY) converting USD and EUR revenue lines into GBP operating funding, including basis-hedged structures for partner distributions from the Cayman ELP to non-UK resident partners.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory where the aggregate value of connected-party transactions exceeds GBP 1,000,000 in a fiscal year. Master File is required for a multinational group with consolidated group revenue of at least EUR 750,000,000. Country-by-Country Report filing applies where the ultimate parent has consolidated revenue above EUR 750,000,000.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations 2019 (ESR) apply to Gibraltar tax-resident entities carrying on relevant activities. The development and provision of VPN/network privacy SaaS is treated as a 'distribution and service centre' or 'intellectual property holding' activity if the ELP earns royalties or supplies digital services. To satisfy ESR, the ELP must maintain a physical office in Gibraltar, employ a proportionate number of full-time qualified staff, incur adequate operating expenditure, and exercise senior management functions in Gibraltar. A pure passive shell or holding box is insufficient and will be struck off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Understatement

Statutory sanction: Penalty of up to 150% of the tax undercharged; statutory interest at 7.5% per annum; criminal fine of up to £100,000 per count on conviction; offence of fraudulent evasion of income tax punishable by imprisonment for up to 7 years.

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners who knowingly authorise the false return are jointly and severally liable for the tax, penalties and interest; travel bans may be imposed pending completion of tax inquiries; and convicted individuals face imprisonment and disqualification from acting as a director or manager for up to 15 years.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Forced strike-off and deregistration; administrative fine of up to £10,000; exchange of information to HM Treasury or foreign tax authorities under tax information sharing agreements; denial of tax residency benefits including patent box and participation exemption.

Director personal liability: General partners and shadow directors face civil restitution orders to restore diverted corporate assets; travel bans may be issued in respect of involvement in an unlawful shell arrangement; the courts may impose personal liability for unpaid corporate tax under a piercing-the-veil theory.
Anti-Money Laundering (AML/CFT) Breach

Statutory sanction: Fines of up to £100,000 per breach under the Proceeds of Crime Act and the AML Regulations; revocation of the ELP's AML registration; freezing of bank accounts; criminal prosecution for money laundering offences with penalties including forfeiture of assets and imprisonment of up to 5 years.

Director personal liability: Compliance officers, directors and general partners who participated in or knowingly permitted the breach are criminally liable; financial Regulatory Authority may issue prohibition orders and travel bans; individuals are subject to personal fines and imprisonment for up to 5 years.
Transfer Pricing Misstatement and Penalty

Statutory sanction: Transfer pricing adjustment to the arm's length amount, resulting in additional corporate tax at 12.5%; penalty of 50% of the additional tax liability for each failure; interest at 7.5% per annum from the original due date; file late additions of 5%.

Director personal liability: Directors of the ELP's general partner who approved the mispriced transactions are jointly and severally liable for the adjusted tax and penalties to the extent they knowingly participated; civil recovery proceedings may extend to personal assets; persistent offenders face director disqualification and travel bans in connection with exit proceedings.
