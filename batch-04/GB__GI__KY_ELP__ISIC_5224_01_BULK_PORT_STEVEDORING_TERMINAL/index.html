Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (Commissioner of Income Tax), 140 King Street, Gibraltar; digital filing and TIN handling via the official Government of Gibraltar Income Tax e-Services portal. Support authorities: Gibraltar Registrar of Companies (entity registration) and HM Customs Gibraltar (importer and port clearance registration).

E-government filing portal

For a non-resident owned Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP): 1) Incorporate the ELP at the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies and secure the Certificate of Registration; 2) file a Notice of Chargeability with the Income Tax Office within 30 days of commencing stevedoring operations to obtain the Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN); 3) lodge the partnership deed, registered office certificate, identity of the general partner, and ownership schedule; 4) for FATCA/CRS, register the ELP with the Income Tax Office to obtain a Global Intermediary Identification Number (GIIN) and CRS reference where financial accounts exist; 5) if importing dutiable goods (machinery, fuel, stores), obtain an HM Customs Gibraltar importer reference at the same time.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible - the ELP is fiscally transparent by default; the partnership itself is not a chargeable person, and tax attaches only at partner level. Non-resident limited partners are assessable only on Gibraltar-source income attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment; a resident general partner exercising management may be charged on world-wide income. — For a non-resident owned Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP): 1) Incorporate the ELP at the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies and secure the Certificate of Registration; 2) file a Notice of Chargeability with the Income Tax Office within 30 days of commencing stevedoring operations to obtain the Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN); 3) lodge the partnership deed, registered office certificate, identity of the general partner, and ownership schedule; 4) for FATCA/CRS, register the ELP with the Income Tax Office to obtain a Global Intermediary Identification Number (GIIN) and CRS reference where financial accounts exist; 5) if importing dutiable goods (machinery, fuel, stores), obtain an HM Customs Gibraltar importer reference at the same time.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible - the ELP is fiscally transparent by default; the partnership itself is not a chargeable person, and tax attaches only at partner level. Non-resident limited partners are assessable only on Gibraltar-source income attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment; a resident general partner exercising management may be charged on world-wide income.

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a US-style check-the-box election. Available statutory elections: (i) irrevocable aggregation election under the Income Tax Act to assess a partner on the partnership aggregate income; (ii) direct assessment election for non-resident limited partners acting without a Gibraltar agent; (iii) group relief and consortium elections for corporate partners; (iv) irrevocable election to apply the Gibraltar Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax under the Income Tax (Multinational Top-up Tax) Act 2024 for in-scope multinational groups; (v) elections under the UK-Gibraltar Double Taxation Agreement 2020 for residence and permanent establishment relief.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax regime. Gibraltar is outside the UK VAT territory and outside the EU VAT area; the only indirect levies are narrow customs/excise duties (alcohol, tobacco, hydrocarbon fuels) under Gibraltar law. Stevedoring and wharfage services supplied in Gibraltar are outside the scope of UK VAT and incur no Gibraltar turnover tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT/GST registration threshold exists in Gibraltar. For customs duty purposes the effective registration trigger is GBP 0; any person importing dutiable goods (port machinery, bunker fuel, ship stores) must hold an HM Customs Gibraltar importer reference before the first import.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is not applicable because Gibraltar imposes no VAT/GST; there is no output tax to zero-rate and no input tax refund mechanism. UK VAT zero-rating under VATA 1994 Schedule 8 does not extend to Gibraltar, and stevedoring services physically performed there are outside the UK VAT territory. Goods transiting Gibraltar are duty-neutral only while not released into free circulation; claims for customs duty relief on terminal equipment (temporary admission, transit, end-use) require proof of physical onward movement of cargo and commercial settlement of freight proceeds within 180 days.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

No continuous transaction control (CTC) mandate, no real-time fiscal clearance, and no digital stamping obligation. E-invoicing remains voluntary. Statutory digital obligations are limited to electronic filing of tax returns through the Gibraltar e-services portal and electronic lodgment of import/export declarations (Gibraltar SAD) with HM Customs Gibraltar; licensed port terminal operators may additionally use the Gibraltar Port Authority EDI/API channels for manifests and berth notifications.

No VAT exists to claw back, but false transit/export declarations and misuse of customs duty relief expose the ELP to: (i) Customs Ordinance penalties of up to treble the value of the goods plus forfeiture; (ii) imprisonment of up to 7 years and unlimited fines for fraudulent evasion; (iii) Income Tax Act penalties of up to 200% of undercharged tax with retrospective assessments for up to 6 years, unlimited in fraud; (iv) duty claw-back with statutory interest; and (v) cross-border audit discovery through UK-Gibraltar DTA 2020 and Gibraltar TIEAs. Non-resident partners relying on pass-through treatment must preserve the full 180-day settlement documentation suite or forfeit relief.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GIP is fully convertible into GBP at parity and GBP banknotes are accepted interchangeably throughout Gibraltar. Both the capital and current accounts are fully liberalised: there are no exchange controls, no prior-approval requirements for foreign-currency accounts, no residency-based constraints on USD/EUR holdings, and no restriction on repatriation of partnership profits, service proceeds, or capital to non-resident partners. The ELP may therefore operate a multi-currency treasury (GIP/GBP, EUR, USD) without FX authorisation; the only practical friction is the shallow local FX market, making GIP/EUR and GIP/USD conversions most efficient through GBP triangulation at a NatWest International or Gibraltar International Bank dealing desk.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or conversion obligation exists. The United Kingdom abolished exchange controls in 1979 and Gibraltar has never imposed a surrender regime: there is no mandatory timeline for repatriation of export receipts, no minimum conversion ratio, and no requirement to sell foreign currency to any state authority. Foreign-currency charter payments, stevedoring fees, and port service receipts may be retained indefinitely in external currency accounts, used directly for offshore disbursements, or hedged through forwards; voluntary conversion to GIP/GBP is at the ELP's sole discretion.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar does not maintain a domestic central bank. Note issuance is the statutory role of the Government of Gibraltar through the Gibraltar Currency Commissioner under the Currency Notes Act 1934; prudential supervision of licensed banks is exercised by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) under the Financial Services Act 2019. The Bank of England acts solely as the external monetary anchor for the 1:1 Gibraltar Pound (GIP) to Sterling (GBP) currency peg and holds no direct supervisory jurisdiction over GFSC-licensed banks. The Prudential Regulation Authority and Financial Conduct Authority of the United Kingdom have competence only at the group level for UK parent undertakings.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: A contemporaneous Local File is mandatory where the aggregate value of controlled transactions in an accounting period exceeds GIP£500,000; a Master File is required where the ELP forms part of an MNE group with consolidated group revenue of EUR 750,000,000 or more, aligning with Gibraltar's Country-by-Country Reporting notification thresholds.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar's Economic Substance Requirements (ESR) apply to tax-resident entities carrying on relevant geographically mobile activities and require adequate premises, qualified staff, and core income-generating activities in Gibraltar proportionate to the income derived. Bulk port stevedoring is inherently physical and location-bound; the ELP satisfies ESR by employing port operatives, holding cargo-handling equipment, contracting directly with the Port Authority, and making its strategic management decisions in Gibraltar. If the ELP merely books invoicing or routing profits without local operations, the Commissioner will deny tax residence, recharacterise transactions, and impose penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate Income Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of up to 100% of the tax undercharged; interest at the statutory rate; criminal prosecution on indictment with an unlimited fine and imprisonment for up to 5 years for fraudulent evasion of income tax; the Commissioner may extend the assessment window to 20 years where fraud is established.

Director personal liability: The general partner and any limited partner who participates in management become jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax; courts may pierce the ELP veil where the partnership is a mere façade, exposing individuals to civil restitution, travel bans, asset freezing orders, and imprisonment for up to 5 years.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Violation

Statutory sanction: Refusal or revocation of the Certificate of Tax Residence; re-characterisation and full re-assessment of profits at 10%; ESR non-compliance penalties of up to GIP£100,000; deregistration or strike-off from the Gibraltar Companies Register; cancellation of the Port Terminal licence.

Director personal liability: Personal assessment against the general partner's assets where the ELP is found to be an artificial entity; director disqualification orders for up to 15 years; publication of the individual on the public disqualification register; criminal liability for materially false substance declarations.
Money Laundering / AML-KYC Deficiency

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 (Gibraltar), money laundering is punishable on indictment by up to 14 years' imprisonment and an unlimited fine; the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit may freeze accounts; administrative penalties and suspension of the banking licence may also be imposed.

Director personal liability: Criminal liability attaches personally to the Money Laundering Reporting Officer, compliance officer, and general partner; travel bans and passport seizure are available to the Royal Gibraltar Police; the statutory AML regime overrides the limited liability shield, and directors may face personal asset forfeiture.
Transfer Pricing / Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: Commissioner's transfer pricing adjustment to the arm's-length price; additional tax at 10%; late-payment interest; penalties of up to 100% of the undercharged tax for failure to provide adequate contemporaneous documentation; inclusion of the ELP in the public defaulters' list.

Director personal liability: Directors and the general partner who approve mispricing owe a fiduciary duty to the partnership and may be ordered to pay civil restitution for the resulting tax liability and penalties; criminal sanctions for perjury or falsification of transfer pricing documentation; travel bans may be imposed during investigation.
