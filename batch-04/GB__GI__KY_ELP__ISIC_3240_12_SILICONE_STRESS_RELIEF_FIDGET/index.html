Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up capital is required for a Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership; no escrow bank account or capital deposit condition is imposed. Capital contributions and funding obligations are contractual and must be specified in the Limited Partnership Agreement.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certificate required from the Gibraltar Fire and Rescue Service covering production and storage areas; structural load inspection under BS EN 1991 and BS EN 13814; occupational health clearance from the Health and Safety at Work Authority, including noise monitoring below 85 dB(A) daily, safe raw material storage, and mandatory ventilation to prevent dust accumulation.

Paid-in capital rule: No minimum paid-up capital is required for a Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership; no escrow bank account or capital deposit condition is imposed. Capital contributions and funding obligations are contractual and must be specified in the Limited Partnership Agreement.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar - Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax), 6 Cathedral Square, Gibraltar; online filing portal: Gibraltar Income Tax e-Services (https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation).

E-government filing portal

Foreign-owned Exempted Limited Partnerships (ELPs) must (1) register with the Registrar of Companies under the Limited Partnerships Act to obtain the partnership certificate; (2) apply to the Income Tax Office for a partnership tax reference via Form PT1; (3) register with the General Consumption Tax (GCT) Office via Form GCT1 once the GBP 150,000 annual taxable turnover threshold is met or exceeded. Resident partners obtain individual Gibraltar TINs; non-resident partners deriving no Gibraltar-source income are not obliged to hold a Gibraltar TIN.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible; Partner-Level Territorial Scope — Foreign-owned Exempted Limited Partnerships (ELPs) must (1) register with the Registrar of Companies under the Limited Partnerships Act to obtain the partnership certificate; (2) apply to the Income Tax Office for a partnership tax reference via Form PT1; (3) register with the General Consumption Tax (GCT) Office via Form GCT1 once the GBP 150,000 annual taxable turnover threshold is met or exceeded. Resident partners obtain individual Gibraltar TINs; non-resident partners deriving no Gibraltar-source income are not obliged to hold a Gibraltar TIN.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; Partner-Level Territorial Scope

Available election forms

Partnership transparency is mandatory under the Income Tax Act 2010, and no check-the-box election regime exists. Partners may, however, elect profit-sharing ratios and capital versus revenue allocation in the partnership agreement, elect the territorial source exemption for non-resident partners with respect to foreign-source income, and the ELP may elect GCT group registration with related Gibraltar entities where the common-control conditions are satisfied.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

1%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GBP 150,000 per annum of taxable supplies in Gibraltar; importers of taxable goods are registrable irrespective of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero rating applies to (i) goods physically exported from Gibraltar to a destination outside Gibraltar within three months of the supply and (ii) services supplied to persons outside Gibraltar where the place of enjoyment is outside Gibraltar. The ELP must retain contemporaneous evidence of export, delivery outside Gibraltar, and consistent commercial payment terms.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration (SAD / Export Accompanying Document)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (or certified international courier receipt)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103 credit advice
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Purchase Order / Export Contract

No real-time fiscal clearance, API stamping, or continuous transaction control (CTC) e-invoicing mandate. Invoices are self-issued and must state the supplier's GCT reference and, for exports, the legend 'Zero Rated - Export'. GCT returns are submitted quarterly via the Income Tax Office e-portal with supporting schedules.

Fraudulent export zero-rating claims expose the ELP to a retrospective six-year clawback of suppressed GCT, a penalty of up to 100% of the tax evaded, statutory interest, misdeclaration penalties of up to GBP 50,000, and criminal prosecution on conviction. Given the transparent pass-through classification, assessment and penalty liability may extend to the individual partners.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar maintains full, unrestricted convertibility on both capital and current account. There are no exchange control laws, no licensing requirements for cross-border transfers, and no restrictions on dividend or profit repatriation. GIP circulates at parity with GBP and is freely interchangeable, while the ELP may hold operational liquidity wholly in USD, EUR, or GBP without prior central bank approval. The only practical hurdles are documentary: banks require a clear capital-contribution narrative, beneficial ownership disclosure, and standard AML-compliant wire references; profit distributions to non-resident limited partners are processed as ordinary SWIFT transactions free of Gibraltar withholding tax under the ELP pass-through regime.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Neither Gibraltar nor UK law imposes any statutory timeline, mandatory conversion ratio, or surrender obligation for export receipts. A Gibraltar ELP exporting food-grade silicone tactile fidgets may retain 100% of export proceeds in foreign currency accounts indefinitely, and conversion of USD or EUR receipts to GIP/GBP is at the sole commercial discretion of the partnership with no reporting requirement to any monetary authority.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (ultimate monetary authority for GBP clearing and settlement) together with the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the locally competent financial services regulator; the Government of Gibraltar issues the Gibraltar Pound (GIP) at a fixed statutory 1:1 parity to GBP. Gibraltar operates no independent central bank or foreign exchange administration.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium feasibility for operational liquidity, platform payouts, and Swift_TT settlement; Low feasibility for capital escrow. GFSC-licensed electronic money institutions under the Financial Services Act 2019 can legally support low-risk fidget-toy revenue collection and marketplace disbursements, but a fully licensed tier-1 bank is mandatory for segregated partner capital injections. Post-Brexit, UK and EU EMI passporting into Gibraltar has ceased; unlicensed offshore digital EMIs cannot lawfully accept deposits from Gibraltar-regulated entities.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant SWIFT MT103 transfers from each contributing partner's own bank account to the ELP's segregated capital escrow account at a Gibraltar tier-1 bank, with payment reference 'ELP capital contribution – no goods or services'. The remitting bank applies the standard capital-account purpose code (mapped internally as 'Equity Injection / Capital Contribution' under GFSC regulatory reporting; USD remittances clear via correspondent banking and are converted to SWIFT MT103). Verification requires the partner's source-of-funds documentation, the notarized partnership deed, and the general partner board resolution authorizing the injection. No Gibraltar central bank approval is required; only standard AML verification applies.Primary rail: SWIFT MT103 priority telegraphic transfer for supplier settlements, including USD or cross-border RMB payments to Chinese compression-molding factories and GBP/EUR payments to European raw-material vendors. Secondary rail: Commercial Letter of Credit on a sight basis, confirmed where the supplier requires bank endorsement, settled through the Gibraltar bank's correspondent network for large production runs. Platform-pay refunds, dropship fees, and marketplace disbursements clear through GFSC-licensed EMI accounts via Swift_TT and Platform_Pay APIs. Domestic GBP disbursements settle via CHAPS; spot or forward FX conversion is executed by the ELP's tier-1 bank at the point of disbursement.Vanilla FX forwards (GBP/USD, GBP/EUR, USD/CNY)FX swaps (spot-forward structures and swap-point rollovers)Cross-currency swaps (USD/GBP and CNY/GBP with an existing underlying trade commitment)Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on CNY/USD and other restricted currencies executed with a GFSC-authorized counterpartyNo GIP-specific NDF is required given the statutory GIP/GBP parity peg
