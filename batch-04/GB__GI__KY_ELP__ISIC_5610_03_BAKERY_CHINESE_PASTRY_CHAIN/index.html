Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar; official portal https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities must first register the Exempted Limited Partnership with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies to obtain a statutory Partnership Registration Number. Thereafter, file an application with the Income Tax Office on Form ELP/Exemption requesting tax-exempt status; submit certified copies of the partnership agreement, list of partners, and non-resident proof for each partner. Upon approval, the Income Tax Office issues an Exempt Status Certificate and a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number. Because Gibraltar has no VAT, no separate VAT registration ID is issued; import duty registration with Gibraltar Customs applies only when physical goods are imported.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — Foreign entities must first register the Exempted Limited Partnership with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies to obtain a statutory Partnership Registration Number. Thereafter, file an application with the Income Tax Office on Form ELP/Exemption requesting tax-exempt status; submit certified copies of the partnership agreement, list of partners, and non-resident proof for each partner. Upon approval, the Income Tax Office issues an Exempt Status Certificate and a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number. Because Gibraltar has no VAT, no separate VAT registration ID is issued; import duty registration with Gibraltar Customs applies only when physical goods are imported.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in Gibraltar because no VAT/GST is levied. Exports of bakery products and franchise royalties are outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect tax; no 0% rate or refund mechanism exists. For cross-border documentation, if the supply is exported to the UK or EU, the relevant zero-rating criteria and evidence of export under foreign VAT law must be satisfied.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

No real-time e-invoicing or digital clearance mandate; invoices may be paper or electronic. Customs clearance is filed electronically via the Gibraltar Customs ASYCUDA system, with digital stamp for goods import/export.

Gibraltar may impose penalties under the Customs Ordinance and Income Tax Act for false export declarations or fraudulent substantiation, including monetary penalties of up to GBP 500 per offence plus 200% of duty evaded and imprisonment for up to seven years. Although there is no VAT clawback, any statutory exemption obtained by fraudulent documentation may be retroactively revoked with interest at 8% per annum.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility on both current and capital accounts; no exchange-control statute applies in Gibraltar or the United Kingdom (Exchange Control Act repealed 1979). The GIP is freely convertible but circulates only domestically; all international settlements are effected in GBP, USD or EUR. ELP capital and profits may be repatriated without Gibraltar central-bank approval or withholding on non-resident limited-partner distributions; the only practical hurdles are the receiving bank's AML/EDD review of the distribution purpose and any home-jurisdiction compliance, such as Chinese SAFE/NDRC ODI registration for mainland-China investors.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender, mandatory conversion or repatriation timeline applies to export or trade receipts in Gibraltar or the UK; proceeds may be retained in any freely convertible currency. This requirement is not engaged for the bakery master-franchise ELP, whose receivables are predominantly franchise royalty wires and intercompany central-kitchen trade flows within the UK/EEA and Hong Kong.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) – primary banking and financial regulator of Gibraltar; Bank of England – monetary authority for the GBP currency zone; Government of Gibraltar – issuer of the Gibraltar pound (GIP) at statutory 1:1 parity with GBP.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Moderate-to-high for operational rails, low as a master-account substitute. FCA-regulated digital EMIs (Wise Business, Airwallex, Revolut Business, Currencycloud/Visa) are viable for FX conversion, supplier disbursements and royalty-collection sweeps, but they are e-money institutions rather than credit institutions: they cannot issue UCP600 commercial letters of credit, provide regulated escrow or act as the principal capital-receiving bank for an offshore ELP. A GFSC-regulated bank must remain the treasury spine; EMIs may be linked as sub-accounts subject to full UBO/BOX disclosure, sanctions screening and FATF travel-rule counterparty verification.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% flat corporate income tax rate on taxable profits for companies and, in the case of a fiscally transparent Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP), at the level of the resident partners on their Gibraltar-source share of profits.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by a Gibraltar company or partnership to residents or non-residents; distributions are made gross with no statutory reduction.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

No later than 12 months after the end of the partnership's accounting period, unless the Income Tax Office issues a notice requiring earlier filing; the Gibraltar year of assessment runs 1 July to 30 June.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 12 months of the end of the company's accounting period; self-assessed tax is due on or before the filing deadline.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Box 1 Partnership name, ELP registration number, principal place of business in Gibraltar, and registered general partner details. A non-resident-driven ELP must disclose the Gibraltar-resident general partner or authorised tax agent; absence of a Gibraltar address flags shell status. Late filing: initial penalty up to GBP 500 plus a continuing daily penalty of up to GBP 50 per day; incorrect or negligent returns: penalty up to 100% of the under-assessed tax; fraudulent returns: penalty up to 200% of the tax and prosecution; statutory interest accrues on unpaid tax.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Master File and Country-by-Country (CbC) Report mandatory where the multinational group's consolidated revenue equals or exceeds EUR 750 million (approximately GBP 645 million), per the Income Tax (Country-by-Country Reporting) Regulations 2017; Local File must be maintained contemporaneously for all material intercompany transactions (master franchise royalties, central kitchen management fees, and IP licensing) with no de-minimis threshold; the Income Tax Office may demand documentation within 30 days.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance (Definition of Substance) Regulations 2019 apply to every tax-resident Gibraltar entity and partnership carrying on a 'relevant activity', including distribution and service centres, intellectual property holding, financing, holding company, and headquarters operations. The ELP must demonstrate: (a) partner/board meetings and strategic management in Gibraltar; (b) adequate physical premises, plant and full-time employees in Gibraltar; and (c) core income-generating activities conducted and supervised in Gibraltar. An annual ESR declaration must be filed with the Commissioner of Income Tax. Failure attracts penalties up to GBP 100,000 plus daily default fines and automatic exchange of information with UK and EU tax authorities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Tax Return

Statutory sanction: On summary conviction: fine up to GBP 10,000 and/or imprisonment up to 6 months; on indictment: unlimited fine and/or imprisonment up to 7 years; additional penalty up to 200% of the tax evaded plus statutory interest; confiscation and asset freezing orders under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 (Gibraltar).

Director personal liability: Directors, general partners and shadow directors who connive in the fraud are personally liable as secondary parties, subject to criminal prosecution, disqualification as directors for up to 15 years, civil restitution orders, travel bans, and seizure of personal assets where the corporate veil is pierced for sham or fraud.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: ESR penalty up to GBP 100,000; continuing daily default fine of up to GBP 1,000; exchange of information with UK/EU tax authorities; striking off the partnership register; denial of treaty benefits; inclusion on public registers of non-compliant entities.

Director personal liability: General partner and directors face personal liability for wrongful trading, director disqualification, personal assessment for unpaid penalties, and travel restrictions; where the structure is found to be an artificial avoidance arrangement, the court may impose restitutionary awards against the individuals who orchestrated it.
AML / CTF and Beneficial Ownership Violations

Statutory sanction: Criminal liability under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 (Gibraltar) and the Beneficial Ownership (Register) Regulations: fines up to GBP 10,000 and imprisonment up to 14 years for money laundering; freezing orders; closure of bank and EMI accounts; significant regulatory penalties from the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission and a ban on holding a financial services licence.

Director personal liability: Directors, MLROs and compliance officers are personally criminally liable for failures in suspicious activity reporting; personal confiscation orders equal to the value of laundered funds; extradition arrangements with the UK; travel bans and disqualification from acting as a director of any regulated entity.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation / Artificial Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: Income Tax Office adjustment of profits to arm's length; recovery of underpaid tax with interest; penalty up to 100% of the additional tax; documentation-related penalties up to GBP 100,000; automatic exchange of information under CbC Reporting and potential joint UK/Gibraltar tax investigations.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign inaccurate TP documentation are exposed to penalties for negligent misstatement; in aggravated cases, the authorities may lift the corporate veil to recover unpaid tax from directors; professional disqualification and travel bans are available remedies.
Payroll and Employment Tax Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Recovery of all unremitted employee taxes plus statutory interest; penalty up to 100% of the unremitted amount; criminal prosecution for each employee where the default is deliberate; public naming of the employer; closure of the business pending compliance.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally and severally liable for unremitted PAYE and social insurance contributions; the State may issue a personal liability notice, impose a travel ban, and seek a court order charging personal assets for the unpaid liabilities.
Uk / Gibraltar Permanent Establishment Exposure

Statutory sanction: UK HMRC assessment of the ELP's attributable UK PE profits at 19%-25% UK corporation tax plus interest and penalties up to 100% of the tax; potential transfer pricing adjustments; double taxation where Gibraltar residence and UK PE documentation conflicts; mutual agreement procedure under the UK-Gibraltar DTA.

Director personal liability: Directors can be pursued under UK CFC and corporate criminal offence rules for failure to prevent tax evasion; personal liability for incorrect CTA2010 penalty assessments; director disqualification and, in serious cases, extradition proceedings.
