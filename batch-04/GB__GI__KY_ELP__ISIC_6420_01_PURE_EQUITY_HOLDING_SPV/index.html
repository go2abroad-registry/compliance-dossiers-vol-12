Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

The Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar; official e-services and tax return portal operated under the Gibraltar Government Tax Services platform (portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) must: (1) register at Gibraltar Companies House under the Limited Partnerships Act 1995 and obtain the certificate of registration; (2) file with the Income Tax Office the 'Application for Tax Registration (Partnership)' together with the certificate of registration, the executed limited partnership agreement, full particulars of the general and limited partners, registered office address in Gibraltar, and apostilled corporate/passport identity documents; (3) receive the irrevocable numeric Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN) from the Commissioner of Income Tax; (4) file the beneficial-ownership return under the Companies (Beneficial Ownership) Act 2017; and (5) maintain a licensed Gibraltar corporate service provider as registered office holder. Because the ELP is fiscally transparent, the taxpayer registration is limited to obtaining the TIN; no entity-level corporation tax file is opened and no withholding tax is operated by the partnership.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — the Gibraltar ELP is fiscally transparent: chargeable income is assessed in the hands of the partners, not the partnership. Foreign-source passive income of non-resident partners (dividends, capital gains on share disposals, interest) is outside the territorial scope of Gibraltar income tax; the partnership itself incurs no Gibraltar entity-level income tax, and distributions to non-resident partners are exempt from any Gibraltar withholding tax. — A foreign-owned Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) must: (1) register at Gibraltar Companies House under the Limited Partnerships Act 1995 and obtain the certificate of registration; (2) file with the Income Tax Office the 'Application for Tax Registration (Partnership)' together with the certificate of registration, the executed limited partnership agreement, full particulars of the general and limited partners, registered office address in Gibraltar, and apostilled corporate/passport identity documents; (3) receive the irrevocable numeric Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN) from the Commissioner of Income Tax; (4) file the beneficial-ownership return under the Companies (Beneficial Ownership) Act 2017; and (5) maintain a licensed Gibraltar corporate service provider as registered office holder. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible — the Gibraltar ELP is fiscally transparent: chargeable income is assessed in the hands of the partners, not the partnership. Foreign-source passive income of non-resident partners (dividends, capital gains on share disposals, interest) is outside the territorial scope of Gibraltar income tax; the partnership itself incurs no Gibraltar entity-level income tax, and distributions to non-resident partners are exempt from any Gibraltar withholding tax.

Available election forms

Election for Non-Resident Partnership status under the Income Tax Act where central management and control is located outside Gibraltar, with the effect that all non-Gibraltar-source income of the ELP is excluded from the charge; no US-style check-the-box election exists in Gibraltar; hybrid mismatch disclosure and classification elections are governed by the Tax (International Compliance) (Hybrid Mismatch Arrangements) Regulations requiring submission of Form HMMR; the ELP may file a transparency election for CRS/FATCA classification purposes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST — Gibraltar operates no value-added tax, goods and services tax, or general sales and use tax; indirect taxation is confined to customs import duty and excise duty on alcohol, tobacco and hydrocarbon oils, together with limited stamp duty on specific instruments.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — as there is no VAT/GST system, no economic turnover threshold can trigger indirect-tax registration. Importers of controlled goods into Gibraltar are registered on a per-consignment basis with HM Government of Gibraltar Customs under the Customs Duties Act 2015, with no de minimis registration exemption.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — because Gibraltar levies no VAT, there is no 0% export VAT rate to claim and no input-VAT refund mechanism. For customs purposes only, goods physically exported from Gibraltar are relieved from import duty under suspension/outward-processing arrangements. A pure equity holding SPV (ISIC 6420) deriving solely foreign-source dividends and share-disposal proceeds has no export transaction requiring substantiation. No statutory FX-repatriation deadline exists in Gibraltar; the 180-day parameter is retained solely as an investor covenant benchmark.

  • Gibraltar Customs electronic export declaration (SAD EXS/ENS safety-and-security dataset)
  • Commercial invoice / pro forma invoice for any physical goods movement
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export from Gibraltar
  • Certificate of Origin where preferential or UK-trade-agreement tariff treatment is claimed
  • Inward remittance certificate or SWIFT MT103 evidencing the correlated payment
  • ELP partner resolution confirming the transaction falls within the passive equity-holding mandate

None — Gibraltar imposes no real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, continuous transaction control, or QR-code e-invoicing mandate. Customs declarations are transmitted electronically via the Gibraltar customs clearance system using the Single Administrative Document (SAD) with ENS/EXS safety-and-security datasets; commercial invoicing is governed by ordinary contract and record-keeping requirements.

Although VAT zero-rating is inapplicable, Gibraltar Customs may retrospectively claw back any import-duty relief or suspension erroneously granted, recover unpaid duty for up to six years, apply penalties of up to three times the duty evaded, and effect forfeiture of goods for false or fraudulent declarations. The Income Tax Office may revoke non-resident partnership status where central management and control is misrepresented, retrospectively assess up to six years of foreign-source income with statutory interest, and impose civil penalties of up to 100% of the tax undercharged, plus daily default fines and criminal sanctions for fraudulent evasion.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar maintains full and unrestricted convertibility on both current and capital account transactions. GIP is freely convertible into GBP at par with zero friction, and there are no exchange controls, no prior approval requirements, and no limitations on dividend remittance, interest payments, loan repayments, or liquidation proceeds. The ELP is tax-transparent; distributions to non-resident partners incur zero Gibraltar withholding tax and can be repatriated freely. Practical note: GIP is not an internationally quoted ISO currency for market trading, so all treasury accounts should be maintained in GBP to eliminate conversion friction; EUR/USD balances require spot conversion at the receiving bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Gibraltar imposes no export proceeds surrender rules, no mandatory conversion ratios, no repatriation timelines, and no holding-period constraints on trade receipts. For this ISIC 6420 passive equity holding SPV, no export revenue stream exists; all inbound treasury flows are equity contributions and dividend/interest distributions, which are freely disposable subject only to AML/CFT verification and the terms of the Limited Partnership Agreement.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the integrated financial services regulator and AML/CFT supervisor for the jurisdiction. There is no separate central bank or foreign exchange administration; the Government of Gibraltar issues the Gibraltar pound (GIP) under the Currency Notes Act 1934, pegged at par 1:1 with GBP. The Bank of England acts as the anchor monetary authority for sterling, and all GIP clearing ultimately settles through UK banking infrastructure.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Moderate-to-Low for this ELP. UK FCA-authorised electronic money institutions (e.g., Revolut Business, Wise Platform, Airwallex) generally decline Gibraltar partnerships bearing offshore-style 'Exempted' features, unless the EMI maintains an explicit British Overseas Territory coverage policy; post-Brexit, no EEA EMI passport applies to Gibraltar, and a GFSC licence under the Financial Services (Electronic Money) Act 2011 would be required for full in-jurisdiction operation. No major digital EMI currently holds such a licence. Feasible secondary use: a UK-based EMI account may be opened for inbound EUR/USD collections if onboarding accepts a non-UK-permanent-establishment partnership, but expect enhanced due diligence, strict transaction velocity limits, and refusal risk under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015. The primary GBP treasury and capital escrow must remain with a tier-1 Gibraltar bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: parent group initiates a SWIFT MT103 in GBP from its UK/EU corporate account to the ELP's Gibraltar account using the GI IBAN and the receiving bank's SWIFT BIC; remittance narrative must state 'Capital Contribution - GELP Registration No. [XXX]' together with the ELP's legal name. Gibraltar imposes no statutory purpose codes, but all tier-1 banks will request the underlying executed contribution agreement and a compliance clearance memo for any single credit exceeding GBP 500,000. For injections originating in restricted currency jurisdictions, use an MT103 with purpose 'Capital Injection - Non-resident Holdings' and attach the GP's signed undertaking confirming the funds are not criminal property and that no exchange-control approval is outstanding. For USD/EUR injections, standard correspondent clearing through UK clearers or US/EU correspondents applies; conversion to GBP occurs at the receiving bank at its published spot rate unless a prior FX forward is booked. All escrow balances must remain segregated from operational balances.Primary clearing rails: (i) dividend and interest distributions to limited partners via SWIFT MT103 with narrative 'ELP distribution - no withholding under Gibraltar Income Tax Act'; (ii) intercompany loan on-lending via MT103/MT202 covering instructions supported by the loan agreement; (iii) acquisition payments for purchase of subsidiary shares settled by plain MT103 or, where the transaction involves trade-financed assets, a Commercial Letter of Credit under UCP600 with the ELP as applicant. GBP payments to UK counterparties may clear via Faster Payments (up to GBP 1 million) or CHAPS; EUR payments clear via TARGET2/SEPA using the counterparty's IBAN; USD payments clear via SWIFT over the receiving bank's Federal Reserve or correspondent account.
