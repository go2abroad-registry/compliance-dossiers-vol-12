Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar, Europort, Gibraltar, accessible via the Government of Gibraltar portal (www.gibraltar.gov.gi); for UK VAT and customs matters, HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) via GOV.UK (www.gov.uk); for import/export goods movements, the Gibraltar Customs and Excise Division.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the Exempted Limited Partnership with the Gibraltar Companies Registry and obtain the partnership registration number. Step 2: Notice the formation with the Gibraltar Income Tax Office and apply for a Tax Reference Number and a certificate of exemption confirming fiscal transparency and non-attribution of exempt offshore income. Step 3: Apply to the Gibraltar Customs and Excise Division for an EORI number if the ELP imports or exports goods physically. Step 4: For online retail into the UK, submit HMRC Form VAT1 as a non-established taxable person; HMRC will issue a GB VAT registration number for sales where the ELP is liable for UK VAT. Step 5: For EU distance sales, register under the OSS Non-Union scheme or the relevant Member State portal. No separate Gibraltar TIN is required for the transparent ELP itself; partners are assessed in their respective residence jurisdictions.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Gibraltar limited partnerships are fiscally transparent by default under the Income Tax Act; no US-style 'check-the-box' election exists and the ELP cannot unilaterally elect entity-level taxation. The operative mechanism is an administrative certificate of exemption confirming pass-through status. For hybrid mismatch purposes, UK TIOPA 2010 Part 6A and EU Anti-Tax Avoidance Directive rules require the partnership's classification to be mirrored with the tax treatment of the partners; no affirmative election is available, but the Gibraltar exemption certificate serves as the documentary basis for the pass-through treatment.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No Gibraltar VAT or general sales tax; indirect taxation is confined to customs and excise duties plus a narrow General Indirect Tax on enumerated local supplies. UK VAT at 20% applies to distance sales of goods delivered into the UK; EU VAT and OSS rules apply to intra-EU distance sales.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Gibraltar: no registration threshold because no local VAT/GST exists. UK: non-established sellers must register for UK VAT from the first taxable supply; the GBP 90,000 threshold applies only to UK-established taxable persons (threshold effective from 1 April 2024). EU: the EUR 10,000 intra-EU distance-selling threshold applies only to EU-established sellers; a Gibraltar non-EU seller has a nil threshold and must register in each Member State or use the Non-Union OSS. Platform facilitation via Stripe or Shopify Payments does not remove the ELP's statutory registration liability unless a platform assumes deemed-supplier status under UK or EU marketplace rules.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

UK VAT zero-rating applies to direct exports of goods only where the supplier retains valid documentary evidence and the goods leave the UK within three months of supply; related services to overseas recipients are generally outside scope or reverse-charged. Gibraltar levies no VAT, so exports from Gibraltar are not 'zero-rated' but are simply outside the scope of local indirect taxation. The ELP may claim a 0% rate only for sales physically exported from UK or EU inventory to a non-UK destination, supported by proof of movement; sales delivered to UK addresses are standard-rated at 20%. The 180-day FX repatriation deadline is adopted as an internal compliance benchmark for audit consistency; no separate Gibraltar or UK statutory deadline governs export proceeds.

  • Customs Export Declaration (UK CDS or Gibraltar equivalent showing export movement)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank payment confirmation evidencing FX repatriation

Gibraltar: no real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing, or digital stamping mandate. UK: Making Tax Digital requires VAT-registered businesses to maintain digital records and submit quarterly VAT returns through HMRC-compatible software and APIs; no transaction-level e-invoicing mandate applies to B2C online retail. EU: OSS returns are filed electronically via the OSS portal; transaction-level e-invoicing/clearance obligations (such as PEPPOL/UBL mandates in certain Member States) apply only where the ELP registers locally and not to Gibraltar-sourced supplies.

HMRC may retrospectively disallow zero-rating where export evidence is incomplete, with assessment of the unpaid output VAT plus default interest and civil inaccuracy penalties of up to 100% of the tax lost under FA 2007 Schedule 24. Fraudulent evasion of VAT is a criminal offence under VATA 1994 s.72, carrying an unlimited fine and up to seven years' imprisonment; customs fraud is separately sanctioned under CEMA 1979. Joint and several liability notices may be issued to connected parties. In Gibraltar, false declarations or misuse of the ELP exemption certificate attract penalties under the Income Tax Act and customs legislation, including revocation of exempt status and retrospective taxation with interest.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is legally pegged to GBP at a 1:1 rate, and GBP circulates freely as legal tender alongside GIP. Both current account and capital account payments are fully convertible; there are no exchange control or profit remittance approvals required by the GFSC or the Government of Gibraltar. For a pass-through eligible Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) with non-resident partners, distributions out of accumulated trading profits can be repatriated without withholding or hurdles, subject only to substance, beneficial ownership, and AML record-keeping obligations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Gibraltar does not impose any statutory surrender timeline, mandatory conversion ratio, or export receipt repatriation requirement for online retail proceeds. Export proceeds from desktop 3D printer and consumables sales may be retained in GIP, GBP, USD, EUR, or other freely convertible currencies at the partnership's discretion, provided that incoming wire narratives and clearing messages are retained for five years for AML/CFT evidence.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the financial services regulator; HM Government of Gibraltar's Currency Notes Issuing Authority is responsible for Gibraltar pound note issuance, with a currency board-style peg to Bank of England issued GBP.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium feasibility. Offshore digital EMI-only providers without a Gibraltar licence generally cannot onboard an ELP merely through remote corporate portals; GFSC-regulated e-money institutions located in Gibraltar are required for regulated payment activity in respect of the GIP/GBP corridor. A structurally compliant route is to combine a licensed local commercial bank account as a treasury anchor with a non-bank EMI for merchant API settlement, but only if the EMI accepts GIP/GBP and maintains an office or an authorised agent in Gibraltar or the UK that is able to perform non-face-to-face EDD.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections from non-resident limited partners into the Gibraltar ELP must be executed by SWIFT MT103 or an equivalent GBP Faster Payments / USD CHIPS / EUR TARGET2 wire. For GBP clearing, use a settlement code such as 'E011 General commerce' or clearly the narrative 'Limited Partner Capital Contribution' together with the ELP's registered reference; for USD use a professional 'Purpose of Payment' code equivalent to 'Equity injection - intercompany investment' maintained in the bank's reference data. For EU counterparties, SEPA TARGET2 payments are appropriate; for Chinese and other Asian suppliers, MT103 in USD/EUR with correct SWIFT BIC/IBAN or local clearing account numbers is used. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% (Gibraltar standard corporate income tax rate; the Exempted Limited Partnership is fiscally transparent, so the ELP itself is not chargeable and each partner is assessed on their distributive share)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on dividends or partnership profit distributions.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The Form P1 must be delivered on or before 30 November following the end of the accounting period (e.g., for a 31 December 2024 year-end the filing deadline is 30 November 2025); the e-Tax portal grants an automatic extension to the following 28/29 February on written application; the Comptroller may raise an estimated assessment if the return is not filed.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File: mandatory where aggregate controlled transactions in the accounting period exceed EUR 1,000,000 (approximately GIP/GBP 860,000); Master File: mandatory where the group consolidated turnover exceeds EUR 50,000,000; Country-by-Country Report: mandatory where group consolidated turnover exceeds EUR 750,000,000. Documentation must be contemporaneous and produced to the Commissioner within 30 days of notice (up to 90 days for CbCR).

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar Economic Substance Act 2020 and the Taxation (Economic Substance) Regulations 2021 apply to any entity, including an ELP, undertaking Distribution and Service Centre activity - the correct category for online retail of 3D printers and consumables. The ELP must: (i) be managed and directed in Gibraltar (majority of board meetings physically held in Gibraltar with quorum and minutes); (ii) have adequate physical offices and full-time employees proportionate to the turnover; (iii) perform core income-generating activities (sourcing, pricing, warehousing direction, contract negotiation and execution) in Gibraltar; and (iv) file the annual ESR-01 return. Failure automatically disqualifies the entity from preferential or exempt status and the Commissioner may retroactively assess the ELP profits at 12.5% with penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Evasion of Income Tax

Statutory sanction: Unlimited fine and/or imprisonment up to 14 years under the Income Tax Act 2010 s.113 and Criminal Justice Act; interest at 8% per annum; confiscation of assets under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 including merchant settlement balances and 3D printer inventory.

Director personal liability: The general partner and any Gibraltar-resident designated partner are personally liable for the full tax shortfall plus penalties; the court may issue travel bans and alienation prohibitions pending prosecution; practical effect: loss of the ELP limited liability shield.
Money Laundering / Payment Processor Fraud

Statutory sanction: Proceeds of Crime Act 2015: up to 14 years' imprisonment and unlimited fines for laundering; the Criminal Finances Act 2017 extends liability to the ELP for failing to prevent the facilitation of UK tax evasion by associated sales agents; Visa/Mastercard MATCH termination follows automatically.

Director personal liability: The general partner is a relevant person under the CSDML regulations and faces personal prosecution for failure to file Suspicious Activity Reports; personal fines up to GIP 250,000; director disqualification and freezing orders over UK and Gibraltar bank accounts.
