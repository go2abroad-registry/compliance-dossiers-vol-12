Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar; official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax

E-government filing portal

An Exempted Limited Partnership is registered with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies and must complete a tax registration application to the Income Tax Office. The application is submitted by the registered agent or a licensed local representative, accompanied by the ELP certificate of registration, a certified copy of the partnership agreement, and identification details of all partners. The Income Tax Office then issues a Gibraltar TIN. Where an overseas partnership seeks to carry on activity in Gibraltar, an ordinary non-resident partnership registration form must be filed, and the Commissioner determines whether the partnership is fiscally transparent or assessable in accordance with the Income Tax Act.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — An Exempted Limited Partnership is registered with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies and must complete a tax registration application to the Income Tax Office. The application is submitted by the registered agent or a licensed local representative, accompanied by the ELP certificate of registration, a certified copy of the partnership agreement, and identification details of all partners. The Income Tax Office then issues a Gibraltar TIN. Where an overseas partnership seeks to carry on activity in Gibraltar, an ordinary non-resident partnership registration form must be filed, and the Commissioner determines whether the partnership is fiscally transparent or assessable in accordance with the Income Tax Act.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no check-the-box election. The ELP is fiscally transparent by default; profits and losses flow through to partners. No statutory election is available to convert an ELP into an entity taxed as a company for Gibraltar tax purposes solely by shareholder choice. Hybrid mismatch rules (based on the EU ATAD framework) apply to payments and structures involving hybrid entities, with mechanisms to deny deductions or impose corresponding adjustments where there is double non-taxation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar does not operate a VAT, GST, or general sales and use tax; indirect taxation is limited to customs import duties, excise duties on specific goods, and stamp duty on particular transactions.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable. There is no VAT/GST registration threshold in Gibraltar. Businesses supplying digital services are not required to register for any Gibraltar indirect tax. Importers of goods subject to customs or excise duties must register with Her Majesty's Customs Gibraltar for each importation; this obligation arises from the first import rather than at a turnover threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Because Gibraltar does not levy VAT, exported goods and digital UI/UX design subscription services are outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect tax rather than zero-rated. For any physical goods element, the exporter must demonstrate that the goods were physically exported from Gibraltar and delivered outside Gibraltar. For digital services, the eligibility condition is that the supplier is established in Gibraltar, the customer is a non-Gibraltar resident, and the place of supply is outside Gibraltar. No prior tax authority approval or licence is required for intangible design services.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration (Geneva/SAD equivalent) — required only when physical goods cross the frontier
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure from Gibraltar and arrival abroad
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit confirmation showing cross-border payment receipt from an overseas customer

No mandatory electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API stamping, or government clearance platform is in force for Gibraltar invoices. B2B invoices may be issued electronically by mutual agreement. Records must be retained for tax audit purposes, but there is no digital fiscal authentication requirement.

Fraudulent or unsupported export exemption claims may trigger retrospective clawback of any duty relief granted, addition of interest from the original duty point, and penalties of up to 100% of the duty evaded. Criminal sanctions apply under Gibraltar's Customs and Excise Duties Act for knowingly making false declarations. Partners of the ELP may be held jointly and severally liable for any penalty attributable to false indirect-tax export claims, and the ELP's records and bank accounts may be the subject of tax authority inspection and restraint orders.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Capital and current account transactions are fully liberalised. The Gibraltar pound is pegged at par to GBP; there are no Gibraltar exchange controls and no restrictions on currency convertibility. Profit remittance, distribution, and capital repatriation may be made freely in GBP or any convertible currency, subject only to GFSC AML record-keeping and suspicious transaction reporting requirements.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender obligation, no conversion timeline, and no minimum conversion ratio apply to export receipts. Foreign currency export proceeds may be retained indefinitely in any convertible denomination. Only standard corporate record-keeping, tax documentation, and AML/CFT reporting thresholds govern cross-border receipt movements.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority) and Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as banking regulator and AML supervisor

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium. Regulated European Economic Area or UK EMI authorisations do not automatically passport to Gibraltar. Offshore digital EMIs may onboard a Gibraltar exempted limited partnership only where their internal country-approval matrix expressly covers British Overseas Territories. Feasibility is therefore conditional on EMI risk appetite, underlying clearing bank, and GFSC-licensed sponsor relationship.

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% flat corporate rate under the Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended). The Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is fiscally transparent: no tax is imposed at the partnership level; each partner is assessed on their allocable share.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% (Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on dividends, profit distributions, or partnership allocations to partners, whether resident or non-resident).

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within six months after the end of the ELP's chargeable period (accounting year-end). Where accounts coincide with the statutory Year of Assessment (1 July - 30 June), the filing deadline is 31 December following the year of assessment unless an extension is granted by the Commissioner of Income Tax.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Country-by-Country Report and Master File: MNE groups with consolidated group revenue equal to or exceeding EUR 750 million (reported to the GITO). Local File: required where the Gibraltar entity's controlled transactions exceed GBP 1 million in aggregate per accounting period; for the ELP, intercompany charges to/from UK affiliates must be contemporaneously documented. Lower thresholds apply where the Commissioner directs on risk-based grounds.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to all Gibraltar tax-resident entities and partnerships, including ELPs. UI/UX design subscription services constitute a 'distribution and service centre' relevant activity. The ELP must maintain in Gibraltar: (i) physical premises, (ii) local qualified staff proportionate to the scale of activity, (iii) operating expenditure, and (iv) board/management governance in Gibraltar. A 'pure equity holding' exemption is unavailable because the ELP carries an active service trade. Non-compliance attracts civil penalties up to GBP 100,000 per financial period, correction directions, strike-off, and the denial of a tax residence certificate (with consequential DTA exposure).

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Return

Statutory sanction: Financial penalty up to 100% of the tax undercharged plus daily interest; criminal prosecution before the Supreme Court of Gibraltar; fine and/or imprisonment for up to seven years; confiscation orders for proceeds of the evasion.

Director personal liability: Directors of the corporate general partner are personally liable for fraudulent trading and are subject to civil restitution orders, statutory disqualification, departure prohibition orders preventing them from leaving Gibraltar until tax is secured, and imprisonment in cases of deliberate concealment.
Shell Company / ESR Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty up to GBP 100,000 per relevant financial period; penalty for material false statement (fine and imprisonment up to two years); mandated correction direction requiring substance to be established within a fixed window; strike-off from the Gibraltar Register of Limited Partnerships; denial of tax residence status; adverse exchange-of-information findings under the OECD Forum on Harmful Tax Practices.

Director personal liability: Directors of the corporate GP face personal liability for the ESR penalty where the partnership was used as a vehicle to obtain an improper tax advantage; the Supreme Court may issue a travel ban and add penalties on the GP's assets; directors may be disqualified under the Companies Act for up to 15 years.
AML / Proceeds of Crime Act 2015

Statutory sanction: Money laundering: conviction on indictment carries imprisonment up to 14 years and an unlimited fine; failure to disclose suspicious activity: imprisonment up to five years and a fine; tipping-off offences carry imprisonment up to two years; civil penalties may be imposed by the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit.

Director personal liability: Directors of the corporate GP are treated as senior management and can be individually prosecuted for consenting to or conniving in the offence; personal confiscation orders, travel bans, and disqualification orders may be imposed; the corporate veil is pierced where the ELP is used as the laundering vehicle.
Permanent Establishment Exposure / Treaty Abuse

Statutory sanction: Reallocation of profits to a UK permanent establishment under UK corporate tax rules; UK corporation tax at up to 25% on attributable profits; application of UK GAAR (penalty up to 100% of the counteracted tax advantage); Gibraltar GAAR and transfer pricing adjustments with world-wide allocation of profits, plus interest and penalties up to 100% of the additional assessment.

Director personal liability: Directors of the corporate GP are personally involved if they knowingly signed returns reflecting the abusive structure; UK and Gibraltar authorities may issue cross-border enquiry notices, travel bans, and, in cases of deliberate concealment, extradition-eligible criminal proceedings; personal civil liability for the partnership's unpaid tax arises where the GP is a façade with no independent assets.
