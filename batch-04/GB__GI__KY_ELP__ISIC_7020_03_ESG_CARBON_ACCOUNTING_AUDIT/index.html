Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (official portal: https://tax.gov.gi; limited partnership registration: Gibraltar Companies House, Registrar of Companies; indirect tax administration: H.M. Gibraltar Customs & Excise Department, Gibraltar Trade Single Window).

E-government filing portal

A Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) first obtains a registration/reference number from the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under the Limited Partnerships Act. No separate income-tax file is opened unless the ELP has Gibraltar-source chargeable income or has partners resident in Gibraltar. Where a filing obligation exists, the general/designated partner submits the partnership deed, partner identification and proof of place of business to the Income Tax Office through the online portal, and the Commissioner issues a Gibraltar tax reference number to the ELP and, where applicable, payer codes to resident partners. Where the ELP has no Gibraltar-source income and no Gibraltar-resident partners, no Gibraltar TIN or VAT ID is required. A VAT ID is not available because Gibraltar is outside the UK VAT territory and does not operate a VAT/GST registration regime.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

The ELP is treated as tax-transparent by default: the partnership is not itself assessable to Gibraltar income tax, and each partner is assessed on their share of Gibraltar-source income. There is no US-style check-the-box or elective entity classification under Gibraltar law. In appropriate territorial circumstances, partners may elect to attribute chargeable income under the Income Tax (Categories and Rates) rules; hybrid mismatch elections are not provided for under Gibraltar domestic law, but UK hybrid mismatch/CFA transparency rules may apply to UK-resident partners under UK law where the ELP or a partner is treated differently in another jurisdiction.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales-and-Use Tax regime; Gibraltar applies only customs import duty, excise duties and transitional duties on specified goods.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable for VAT/GST because no such tax exists in Gibraltar. A business importing, removing or handling controlled or excise goods must register with H.M. Gibraltar Customs and obtain a customs/import-export code irrespective of turnover; there is no economic turnover threshold for VAT registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar has no VAT, so there is no VAT zero-rating or VAT refund mechanism. For customs purposes, goods being exported from Gibraltar to a destination outside Gibraltar are outside the scope of Gibraltar customs and excise duty, subject to a valid export declaration and physical removal. ESG carbon footprint auditing and offset brokerage services supplied to clients outside Gibraltar are treated as non-Gibraltar-source, offshore services and are outside the income-tax territorial charge when performed from Gibraltar for offshore recipients; no Gibraltar indirect tax is chargeable. Where the same services are supplied to a UK taxable person, UK VAT may apply under the UK reverse charge or normal place-of-supply rules, but this arises under UK VAT law and not under Gibraltar law.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic SAD/goods declaration lodged in the Gibraltar Trade Single Window)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill or CMR consignment note evidencing physical export movement
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT MT103/MT202 records evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Partnership deed and tax-transparency confirmation (beneficial ownership evidence for customs and licensing)
  • Statement of physical movement or end-use/onward-supply certificate where goods are transiting Gibraltar

None. Gibraltar does not operate real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction controls, digital stamping, or an API-based e-invoicing clearance platform. Import and export declarations are lodged electronically via the Gibraltar Trade Single Window or by paper at the customs frontier when electronic submission is unavailable. No post-fiscal validation of B2B invoices is required.

H.M. Gibraltar Customs and the Commissioner of Income Tax can reopen export declarations, shipping records and partner allocations retrospectively. Fraudulent export declarations, deliberate mis-statements and false claims to duty relief or offshore income status attract civil penalties, including monetary fines calculated by reference to tax or duty lost, interest on underdeclared amounts, forfeiture of goods, and criminal liability upon conviction, with imprisonment for serious customs evasion. In the absence of a VAT regime there is no anticipated retrospective VAT clawback; however, any false representation exploited to obtain a tax-transparency position or customs duty relief may be challenged and clawed back by the Commissioner, together with statutory late-payment interest and penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is fixed at parity with GBP. Gibraltar applies no exchange-control restrictions on current or capital account transactions. The ELP may receive non-resident capital contributions, hold multicurrency accounts, and remit profit shares abroad without prior central bank approval. Practical hurdles are limited to bank-level AML/KYC verification, CRS/FATCA classification, and source-of-funds documentation; no withholding or exchange-control permit is required.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion ratio exists in Gibraltar. Exporters and service providers may retain receivables in any freely convertible currency through GFSC-licensed banks. Bank reporting obligations remain limited to sanctions screening, suspicious transaction reporting, and tax transparency under CRS/FATCA where applicable.

Central bank supervision: Government of Gibraltar under the Currency Notes Act 1934, with banking and deposit-taking supervision exercised by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC); Gibraltar has no statutory central bank and the Bank of England has no direct regulatory authority over Gibraltar-licensed banks.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium. Offshore digital EMIs are legally constrained: a non-Gibraltar EMI must obtain GFSC authorisation to carry on e-money or payment services in or from Gibraltar, and UK/EU licences do not automatically passport post-Brexit. A regulated fintech EMI may support payment connectivity as a secondary rail, but the primary treasury account and clearing relationship must sit with a GFSC-licensed bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital contributions should be routed by SWIFT MT103 from the investor's or general partner's banking institution to the Gibraltar operational or escrow account. SEPA is not available from Gibraltar, so GBP/USD/EUR funding must use SWIFT, CHAPS, Fedwire, or CHIPS as applicable. In the MT103, the ISO 20022 purpose code 'INTC' (intra-company transfer) should be used where the payer and the ELP are within the same group; if the bank applies IMF/balance-of-payments coding, use the direct-investment equity code, and include the narrative 'Capital Contribution - KY_ELP - INTC'. Banks will verify source of funds, notify the GP of CRS/FATCA reporting obligations, and may require a capital account entry or audit confirmation.Operational disbursements, carbon-credit purchase settlements, and auditor fees should be paid by SWIFT MT103 through the GFSC-licensed bank. For commercial letter-of-credit settlements, use SWIFT MT700 for LC issuance, MT707 for amendments, and MT754/MT760 as required; final drawing payments are executed by MT103 via the confirming bank. GBP clearing is available through CHAPS and the UK Faster Payments channel via the Gibraltar bank's UK correspondent; EUR could be cleared through SEPA using an EU correspondent account, and USD through CHIPS/Fedwire.Spot GBP/EUR/USD transactions with a GFSC-licensed bank or an authorised EU/UK investment firm acting as agent.Plain-vanilla deliverable forwards under a bilateral ISDA 2002 master agreement with credit support annex, used only to hedge identified non-GBP trade or treasury exposures.Non-deliverable forwards on illiquid or restricted currencies (e.g., CNY, INR, BRL) via a licensed counterparty, subject to regulatory margin rules and board-approved hedging policy.Cross-currency swaps for matched long-term liability or asset-currency alignment, permitted when the underlying exposure is non-speculative and consistent with the ELP's commercial carbon-audit and brokerage operations.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory monetary threshold exists for Local File or Master File; the Comptroller may demand transfer pricing documentation for any controlled transaction under Income Tax Act 2010 section 25A. Country-by-Country Reporting applies only to multinational groups with consolidated group revenue of EUR 750,000,000 or more, a threshold the ELP does not meet.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar's Economic Substance Regulations 2019, as amended, require entities, including ELPs, conducting relevant activities to maintain adequate premises, full-time employees, and locally incurred operating expenditure. Pure third-party ESG carbon-audit and offset-brokerage services are not a listed relevant activity unless performed for connected affiliates, in which case the service-centre and headquarters categories apply. The ELP must nevertheless maintain a registered office, statutory registers, board minutes, and genuine economic presence in Gibraltar; otherwise the Comptroller may recharacterise profits as Gibraltar-source or impose substance penalties.
