Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar — Income Tax Office, Office of the Commissioner of Income Tax; e-filing via the 'iTax' portal linked to www.gibraltar.gov.gi.

E-government filing portal

A non-resident Cayman Islands Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) that carries on business through a Gibraltar permanent establishment (PE) must: (1) register the Gibraltar establishment at the Companies Registry and appoint a registered office/agent; (2) submit a tax registration application to the Income Tax Office via iTax within 30 days of commencing Gibraltar-source activities, attaching the ELP's Cayman certificate of registration, limited partnership agreement, partner register satisfying AML/KYC requirements, and evidence of the Gibraltar PE address; (3) obtain the Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN), being the statutory TIN; and (4) thereafter file annual partnership returns and issue partner profit-allocation statements.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — the ELP is fiscally transparent; no entity-level income tax is imposed. Each partner is assessed individually on its distributable share of Gibraltar-source partnership profits (attributable to the Gibraltar PE). Non-resident partners are chargeable only on Gibraltar-source income, consistent with the territorial source rules under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended). — A non-resident Cayman Islands Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) that carries on business through a Gibraltar permanent establishment (PE) must: (1) register the Gibraltar establishment at the Companies Registry and appoint a registered office/agent; (2) submit a tax registration application to the Income Tax Office via iTax within 30 days of commencing Gibraltar-source activities, attaching the ELP's Cayman certificate of registration, limited partnership agreement, partner register satisfying AML/KYC requirements, and evidence of the Gibraltar PE address; (3) obtain the Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN), being the statutory TIN; and (4) thereafter file annual partnership returns and issue partner profit-allocation statements.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible — the ELP is fiscally transparent; no entity-level income tax is imposed. Each partner is assessed individually on its distributable share of Gibraltar-source partnership profits (attributable to the Gibraltar PE). Non-resident partners are chargeable only on Gibraltar-source income, consistent with the territorial source rules under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended).

Available election forms

Gibraltar operates no check-the-box election; partnership transparency is mandatory. Available administrative options include: (i) an application to the Commissioner for an advance ruling/private clearance confirming PE profit attribution and partner-level treatment; (ii) for individual non-domiciled partners, an election for the remittance basis of assessment (where applicable); (iii) statutory treatment under Gibraltar's hybrid mismatch rules (BEPS Action 2-aligned), which allow no elective deferral or mismatch carve-out.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales-and-Use-Tax regime applies in Gibraltar; the territory sits outside the UK and EU VAT areas. Indirect taxation is confined to customs and excise import duties on physical goods (e.g., tobacco, alcohol, hydrocarbon oils). Pure B2B regulatory-consultancy services are outside the scope of any Gibraltar indirect tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — Gibraltar has no VAT/GST registration threshold; no indirect-tax registration or clearance requirement arises for the ELP's MedTech regulatory consultancy.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in Gibraltar: with no VAT levied, no export zero-rating claim, refund mechanism, or fiscal-exit certificate exists for services. If the ELP voluntarily registers for UK VAT (outside Gibraltar), supplies of FDA/CE regulatory consultancy to UK business customers would fall under the reverse-charge mechanism, and supplies to non-UK customers would be outside the scope of UK VAT.

  • Not applicable — intangible consultancy services attract no Gibraltar customs declaration or duty-refund pathway
  • Retain as fiscal evidence: executed client engagement agreements, itemized invoices, inward remittance confirmations, and transfer-pricing documentation; Gibraltar imposes no statutory FX repatriation deadline (180 days is treasury practice)

None — Gibraltar imposes no real-time fiscal clearance, API e-invoicing, or digital-stamping mandate. Electronic invoices are valid under the Electronic Commerce Act 2001 (as amended); underlying records must be retained for at least 7 years.

No VAT clawback risk exists for Gibraltar entities absent a VAT registration. On income-tax audits, negligent or fraudulent returns attract penalties up to 100% of understated tax; assessments may be reopened for up to 6 years (unlimited in fraud/willful default). Audit scrutiny centers on PE source attribution, arm's-length intercompany charging, partner-level transparency, and compliance with the Gibraltar Economic Substance filing obligations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound is fully convertible at a fixed 1:1 parity with pound sterling. Both current account and capital account convertibility are unrestricted, and profit remittances are not subject to exchange control approvals. Practical hurdles arise only from bank-level de-risking, which may require additional substance documentation and legal opinions before distributions to non-resident partners are cleared.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar imposes no statutory export proceeds surrender rule, mandatory repatriation timeline, or forced conversion ratio. The ELP may retain foreign currency proceeds offshore. For banking and tax compliance, the ELP must maintain documentary evidence that all export receipts are attributable to substantive medical-device regulatory consulting services, thereby preserving the pass-through and non-resident protection status.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority for the Gibraltar pound) and the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (banking and financial services regulator)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-to-Medium. Authorised UK or Gibraltar-licensed electronic money institutions can provide API-driven payment rails, IBAN provisioning, and multi-currency settlement, but they are not licensed banks and do not hold deposits. Cross-border EMIs are viable for inbound third-party payments by card and for small trade disbursements; statutory capital, capital escrow, and hedge collateral should remain with a Tier-1 licensed bank. Unregulated offshore EMIs must be rejected.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 aggregate annual controlled transactions require a contemporaneous Local File; a group Master File is mandatory where consolidated annual group revenue exceeds GIP 75,000,000 or where the Commissioner determines that transfer pricing documentation is necessary to verify the arm's length nature of connected-party transactions.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar's Economic Substance (Requirements) Regulations 2019 impose a real presence requirement on entities undertaking 'relevant activities' (e.g., financing, leasing, distribution, service centres, holding company functions). FDA/CE regulatory consulting is not expressly a relevant activity, but the Commissioner may nevertheless require evidence of economic substance where the ELP holds assets, claims treaty benefits, or carries out management and control functions in Gibraltar. An Exempted Limited Partnership should maintain a Gibraltar-registered office, retain original accounting and board/partner records, and demonstrate at least one physically present responsible person if any management activity is conducted in Gibraltar.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Filing

Statutory sanction: A fine of up to GIP 500,000, an additional assessment of up to 200% of the evaded tax, and imprisonment not exceeding 2 years under the Income Tax Act 2010 and the Criminal Procedure Code of Gibraltar.

Director personal liability: The general partner and controlling officers may be jointly and severally liable for the tax debt once the corporate veil is pierced; courts may impose a travel ban pending taxation of assets, an asset-freezing order, and disqualification from managing any Gibraltar entity for up to 10 years.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Penalty of GIP 5,000 for non-compliance with the Economic Substance Regulations, plus GIP 500 per day of continued failure; the Registrar may apply to strike off the entity, and the court may order the winding-up of the partnership with costs against the assets.

Director personal liability: Directors or the general partner who knowingly permitted the shell arrangement may be held personally liable for unpaid taxes and third-party creditor claims; they may also be disqualified as directors for a period of up to 15 years and, in the case of fraudulent trading, subject to imprisonment.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Register Failure

Statutory sanction: A contravention fine of up to GIP 10,000 per failure and imprisonment not exceeding 2 years; the Financial Intelligence Unit may also impose an administrative penalty of up to GIP 50,000 for systemic non-compliance.

Director personal liability: Officers of the general partner are personally accountable for false declarations on the beneficial ownership register and may be barred from leaving Gibraltar while an AML investigation is outstanding; severe non-compliance may trigger a ban and criminal prosecution in the Supreme Court of Gibraltar.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner may make an upward adjustment to taxable profits, deny deductions for undocumented fees, charge statutory interest from the original due date, and impose a penalty of up to 100% of the additional tax arising from the transfer pricing correction.

Director personal liability: Directors and the general partner may be personally liable where the transfer pricing adjustment is frustrated by a prior distribution or by the stripping of assets; the adjustment may be recovered by civil process against the officers who authorised the transaction.
Disguised Profit Repatriation

Statutory sanction: The payment will be reclassified as a dividend or partnership profit share; the deduction is disallowed, withholding obligations in the source jurisdiction are enforced, and penalties of up to GIP 50,000 plus interest are applied for misdeclaration.

Director personal liability: Officers who approved the disguised distribution without a solvency assessment are personally liable to restore the amount distributed and may be disqualified from acting as directors; criminal liability arises where the structure was designed to defeat a foreign revenue claim.
