VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST in Gibraltar (Gibraltar is outside the UK/EU VAT territory); indirect taxation limited to customs import duties and excise duties (alcohol, tobacco, energy products) administered by HM Customs Gibraltar

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT registration threshold exists; excise/customs duty licensing is required for manufacturers, importers and exporters of alcohol, tobacco, and fuel products, with sector-specific registration thresholds determined by HM Customs Gibraltar

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - Gibraltar levies no VAT/GST, therefore no export zero-rating refund mechanism exists. Exports of heparin sodium (HS 3001.90) from Gibraltar are outside indirect taxation. Duty suspension or customs drawback may apply under HM Customs Gibraltar regulations where imported duty-paid materials are subsequently re-exported; normal commercial export documentation must evidence the movement.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic SAD entry via HM Customs Gibraltar)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export
  • Commercial invoice (Incoterms 2020, HS code 3001.90, product description, quantity, value)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT wire / Letter of Credit credit advice evidencing payment receipt
  • Importer's customs entry or VAT acknowledgement from the destination jurisdiction (where applicable)

Not applicable under local law; no real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing API, or digital stamping mandate. Commercial invoices, customs declarations and transport documents accompany goods movements; import/export declarations are filed electronically via HM Customs Gibraltar using Single Administrative Document (SAD) procedures.

HM Customs Gibraltar may deny duty suspension/export drawback and claim retrospective customs duties where export substantiation is defective; the Commissioner of Income Tax may disallow export-related deductions and reclassify turnover taxable at 12.5% where documentation is insufficient. Evasion penalties under the Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended) include fines up to tax evaded, daily default penalties, interest, and criminal sanctions; Qualifying Company status can be revoked with retrospective clawback of the 0% tax benefit.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility on both current and capital account. GIP is freely exchangeable into GBP and, through the London market, into all major currencies. No exchange-control authorisation is required for inbound equity injections, outbound dividend/distribution remittances, or settlement of trade payables/receivables. The only practical hurdle is satisfying bank-source-of-funds and beneficial-ownership due-diligence under GFSC AML/CFT regulations. As the ELP is non-resident for Gibraltar tax purposes, profit remittance to foreign partners is unimpeded absent indictable activity.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No surrender requirement and no mandatory conversion ratio apply to an Exempted Limited Partnership holding foreign-currency proceeds from the export of pharmaceutical ingredients. Export receipts may be retained in any currency, either in on-shore GIP/GBP accounts or in permissible foreign-currency clearing accounts. Only standard retention of accounting records and economic-substance filing obligations apply under the Income Tax Act and Companies Act.

Central bank supervision: No indigenous central bank. Monetary and regulatory authority is exercised by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) for banking and FX licensing, while the issue of Gibraltar pound (GIP) banknotes is vested in HM Government of Gibraltar under the Gibraltar Currency Notes Act. GIP is pegged at 1:1 to GBP, and the convertibility of GBP is assured through Bank of England arrangements.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low for primary treasury and clearing rails; Medium for ancillary collection of small-value receipts. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Revolut Business, Wise Business, Mercury) cannot act as a GFSC-regulated credit or payments institution directly; a Gibraltar-licensed EMI or agent mandate is required to hold funds for the ELP. Most offshore digital platforms decline active pharmaceutical commodity flows due to enhanced due-diligence expectations, invoice-vetting requirements, and the need for documentary confirmation of physical trade. Consequently, fintech EMIs may be utilised only as limited reception gateways, never as the central liquidity or clearing infrastructure.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections must be initiated via a single SWIFT MT103 (member-to-member) or MT202 for cover, ensuring the clearing message from a fully licensed international bank contains the beneficiary partnership's full legal name and registered address in Gibraltar. The remittance instruction should specify Field 70 as 'CAPITAL INJECTION / LP CONTRIBUTION' and Field 50F with the remitter's regulatory authorisation reference. The bank's compliance unit will friction-check the amount against the capital contribution schedule in the partnership agreement, and will request a source-of-funds letter, the formation certificate, and a notarised copy of the deed. No prior exchange-control approval from the GFSC is required; however, the bank may invoke a one-to-three-business-day funding hold for verification purposes.For initial trade settlements, the ELP should use an irrevocable documentary letter of credit (SWIFT MT700) with a confirmed advising bank for purchases of crude heparin from China, and an unrestricted SWIFT MT103 for sales to regulated EU/UK pharmaceutical customers where open-account terms are already established. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File mandatory where related-party transactions exceed £500,000 aggregate per accounting period; Master File mandatory where worldwide group turnover exceeds £500 million or where the group is a qualifying CbC reporter; Country-by-Country notification threshold is consolidated group revenue of €750 million.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar enacted the Economic Substance (Requirements for Certain Companies) Act 2022. The ELP is treated as an exempted entity but must satisfy core income-generating activities (CIGAs) in Gibraltar for any 'relevant activity' it performs. For heparin sodium fractionation and wholesale, the CIGAs include managing risks, taking strategic decisions, employing qualified pharma staff, and maintaining physical oversight in Gibraltar. The ELP cannot be a shell company; it must maintain a physical office, qualified staff, and board minutes in Gibraltar. Failure to meet the substance test exposes the entity to sanctions under the Economic Substance Act, including financial penalties up to £250,000 and striking-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion – Deliberate Under-Declaration of Gibraltar-Source Profits

Statutory sanction: Under the Income Tax Act 2010, s.112, fraud or neglect attracts a penalty of up to 100% of the tax understated; criminal conviction for cheating the public revenue carries an unlimited fine and imprisonment not exceeding 7 years, together with interest accruing at 8% per annum.

Director personal liability: Directors of an ELP's corporate general partner may be pursued under director's responsibility provisions; directors can be retrospectively assessed for the entity's unpaid tax where they knowingly signed falsified returns. Courts can impose travel bans under the Civil Jurisdiction and Judgments Act 1993 (Gibraltar) and issue international arrest warrants, with confiscation orders for proceeds of crime.
Shell Company / Defeating Economic Substance Regulations

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance (Requirements for Certain Companies) Act 2022, a failing entity can be fined £100,000 plus daily default penalties of £5,000; the Registrar can strike off the ELP and deny its pass-through classification, causing all profits to be assessed in Gibraltar as a separate taxable person at 12.5%.

Director personal liability: Directors of the corporate general partner can be personally liable for aggregate penalties up to £250,000 if the substance failure was caused by conscious neglect; the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission may disqualify them from holding directorships for up to 15 years.
AML / Use of High-Risk Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and the AML/CFT Regulations 2018, non-reporting attracts a fine up to £2 million and/or 14 years' imprisonment for a director who knows or suspects money laundering; the ELP's bank accounts may be frozen under applicable criminal finance legislation and currency movement controls imposed.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers are personally liable for authorising money laundering; civil recovery orders can trace funds into the ELP's assets, and the Crown can forfeit the heparin stock and cash proceeds. Individuals can be barred from travel and extradition under international agreements.
