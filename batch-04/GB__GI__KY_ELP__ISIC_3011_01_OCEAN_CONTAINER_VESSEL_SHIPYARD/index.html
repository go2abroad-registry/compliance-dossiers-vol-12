Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is prescribed for a Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership. In the ISIC 3011 deep-sea vessel shipyard context, the Gibraltar Port Authority and maritime escrow banks ordinarily require an initial committed capital of at least USD 50,000 per vessel construction installment, with the first 10% of each installment held in a licensed Gibraltar escrow account pending issuance of the Certificate of Exempted Status.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar; filing is conducted by authorised tax agents through the Government of Gibraltar online tax portal.

E-government filing portal

A Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is first registered with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Limited Partnerships Act. The general partner then submits an application for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number to the Income Tax Office, supported by the partnership deed, certificate of registration, and confirmation of the registered agent in Gibraltar. The ELP itself is transparent for tax; the Income Tax Office issues the partnership number for filing and administrative purposes while profits are assessed at partner level. For exports and imports, the ELP must additionally obtain a Customs Trader Reference from the Gibraltar Customs and Excise Department before any commercial movement of vessels or shipyard goods.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible / Territorial_Scope — the ELP is fiscally transparent; non-resident partners are taxable only on Gibraltar-source income, while Gibraltar-resident partners are taxed on their allocated shares of Gibraltar-source profits. Shipbuilding income from a dockyard in Gibraltar will generally be treated as Gibraltar-source for resident and non-resident partners carrying on trade in Gibraltar through the ELP.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election is available in Gibraltar. The ELP is transparent by default. Where hybrid mismatch outcomes arise, Gibraltar's hybrid mismatch rules require alignment through documentation or an election to prevent double non-taxation, and the mixed-membership partnership anti-avoidance provisions may reallocate deductions or surrender to ensure a matching tax outcome.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar Customs and Excise Duties (no VAT/GST system)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A — Gibraltar does not impose VAT or GST and therefore has no registration threshold. A commercial shipyard must register with the Gibraltar Customs and Excise Department as an importer/exporter before the first import or export, irrespective of monetary turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating for export VAT is not applicable because Gibraltar does not levy VAT. Newly built ocean-going container vessels exported from a Gibraltar shipyard are outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect taxation. Where duty-free or duty-suspended marine components were imported for installation, the shipyard must prove physical export of the finished vessel to preserve the suspension or obtain any applicable duty repayment.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic movement record)
  • Bill of Lading evidencing ocean export from Gibraltar
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank FX confirmation for the export consideration
  • Builder's Certificate for the new ocean container vessel
  • Export cargo shipping instruction or carrier's receipt if freight forwarder used

No real-time tax-authority e-invoicing, digital stamping, or invoice-clearance mandate applies in Gibraltar. Import and export declarations are lodged electronically through the Gibraltar Customs and Excise Department's electronic data interchange system; commercial invoices and customs records must be retained for a minimum of six years.

False or reckless export declarations are prosecuted under Gibraltar's Customs and Excise Duties Act; penalties include forfeiture of the vessel or goods, retrospective duty assessment and clawback for up to six years, civil penalties of up to 300% of evaded duty, and imprisonment on conviction. Tax agents involved in fraudulent zero-rating or duty suspension claims may be struck off or debarred.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The GIP is fully and freely convertible into GBP at par and, through GBP, into any tradable currency. Both current-account transactions (export LC proceeds, maritime supplier payments, contractor disbursements, bunker and equipment imports) and capital-account transactions (capital contributions from the Cayman ELP holding layer, partner loan advances, profit distributions and partner redemptions) are unrestricted and require no exchange-control licence or central-bank approval. Profit remittance from the shipyard project is unencumbered: Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on distributions by a transparent limited partnership to non-resident partners; however, non-resident partners are taxable in Gibraltar at 12.5% on their share of trading profit attributable to the Gibraltar permanent establishment of the ELP, after which the net cash may be repatriated without FX permit, surrender duty or balance-of-payments reporting beyond normal GLMS statistical filings by the commercial bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Nil. There are no statutory export-proceeds surrender timelines and no mandatory conversion ratios applicable to shipbuilding export receipts in Gibraltar. All instalment proceeds from the sale of deep-sea container vessels may be retained in the original settlement currency (typically USD) in a designated offshore or domestic account for the purpose of matching USD procurement obligations for steel, engines and maritime electronics. Conversion into GIP/GBP, if required, is executed at commercial spot rates through the clearing bank without reference to the Government of Gibraltar. Export proceeds nevertheless remain subject to the GFSC's financial sanctions screening for OFAC, UK ONSI, EU and UN embargoes on vessels, shipowners and charterers.

Central bank supervision: Government of Gibraltar (Ministry of Finance) as sole issuer of the Gibraltar Pound (GIP) under the Currency Notes Act 1934; Bank of England as the monetary anchor of the pegged GIP-GBP exchange rate at 1:1 parity; Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as banking licensor, AML/CFT supervisor and conduct-of-business regulator for banks, fiduciary providers and DLT firms. Gibraltar has no independent standing central bank; its monetary sovereignty is circumscribed by full sterling convertibility.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium for ancillary treasury operations, Low for the primary banking of the shipyard. UK FCA-authorised e-money institutions (e.g., Wise Business, Revolut Business) and GFSC DLT-licensed fintechs (e.g., Xapo Bank) may lawfully provide GBP and EUR payment accounts for a Gibraltar-resident ELP under the Gibraltar (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2020 preserved access for UK-regulated firms, but such EMIs are not licensed to take deposits, issue documentary credits, or extend trade or discount facilities. Practical constraints include low per-transaction ceilings (commonly GBP 250,000 or USD 300,000 daily), standard exclusion clauses covering high-risk maritime and shipbuilding categories, and the absence of FSCS or Gibraltar deposit-guarantee protection. Consequently, the EMI rail is viable only for payroll sweeps, small supplier reimbursements and intercompany transfers under GBP 100,000; all escrow, LC and vessel-scale USD flows must clear through Tier-1 Gibraltar banks or a UK clearing bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All capital injections from the KY_ELP into the Gibraltar shipyard operating and escrow accounts must be remitted by SWIFT MT103 using ISO 20022 Purpose Code INVS (investment) or COUN (for partner loan contributions) with the narrative field carrying the exact partnership clause reference, e.g., 'CAP-CONT / LPA-CLAUSE-8.2 / KY_ELP-Q2-2025'. GBP transfers should use the UK Faster Payments or CHAPS rail via the Gibraltar bank's UK sorting-code membership; EUR contributions require the bank's correspondent Nostro arrangement in Frankfurt or Amsterdam because Gibraltar is outside the post-Brexit SEPA zone. For USD injections above USD 5,000,000 equivalent, the receiving Gibraltar bank requires three business days' prior notice to source correspondent liquidity through CHIPS or Fedwire New York. No GFSC foreign-exchange approval is required, but the bank's Financial Crime Unit must receive the underlying partnership agreements and proof of identity of the remitting partner before the first deposit, and each wire must include the full lexicographic name of the ELP to prevent clearing failures.Two-tier disbursement rail is mandatory for an ISIC 3011 container-vessel shipyard. Tier 1 - High-value maritime settlements: USD and EUR vendor payments for steel plate, engines, navigation electronics and auxiliary machinery via SWIFT MT103 with Purpose Code TRAD; imported critical equipment financed by Commercial Letters of Credit issued under SWIFT MT700, at sight or deferred up to 180 days, with the Gibraltar clearing bank offering an LC discount line against the underlying shipbuilding contracts. Tier 2 - Operating expenditures: GIP/GBP payroll, port dues and local utilities via Faster Payments or CHAPS within the UK-clearing framework; EUR payments to EEA suppliers must be settled through the bank's EU correspondent or an FCA-authorized EMI utilising its own lightweight IBAN-clearing access. Outbound partner distributions are made by MT103 with Purpose Code DIVI (profits) or DIST (capital return), always accompanied by a partner resolution, LC-backed cashflow projection and solvency certificate from the ELP's auditors.Vanilla GBP/USD and GBP/EUR Outright FX Forwards (deliverable, tenors up to 24 months) documented under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement with a Gibraltar or UK clearing bank, strictly adhering to the FX Global Code 2017.Cross-Currency Swaps (CCS) converting USD LC-backed long-term instalment receivables into GBP floating-rate liabilities, matching the shipyard's multi-year payroll and drydock overhead profile.Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDFs) on CNY and KRW offshore legs, permissible only against verifiable East-Asian steel, machinery and ship-equipment supply contracts, with 3 to 12 month tenors and no speculative position-taking.FX Swap/Rollover facilities converting EUR/USD export receipts into GIP/GBP working capital for short liquidity bridges between milestone payments and monthly cost payments.Zero-cost USD/EUR Option Collars used exclusively for EUR-denominated shipbuilding contracts where the production budget is USD/GBP based, subject to bank credit approval, independent valuation and quarterly hedge-effectiveness documentation.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory once aggregate controlled transactions exceed GIP 10 million per annum; Master File is mandatory where the ELP's ultimate parent group has consolidated turnover above GIP 50 million per annum.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar's Economic Substance Regime is directed at geographically mobile activities. Commercial shipbuilding with a physical drydock and operational workforce is treated as having substance; however, an ELP used merely as a procurement, IP-holding, or financing vehicle for vessel construction must demonstrate actual employees, premises, and decision-taking in Gibraltar. Failure to do so may result in profit reallocation to partners' residence jurisdictions and the imposition of anti-shell penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Deliberate Tax Evasion and False Returns

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of up to 200% of the tax evaded plus interest; criminal conviction is liable to imprisonment for up to 3 years and an unlimited fine before the Gibraltar Supreme Court.

Director personal liability: The general partner and any managing partner who signed the false return are jointly and severally liable for restitution of unpaid tax and criminal fines; courts may issue travel bans and asset-freezing orders.
Abusive Transfer Pricing / Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: Compulsory transfer-pricing adjustment, disallowance of intra-group cost deductions, late-payment interest of up to 3% per month, and penalties of up to 100% of the adjusted profit.

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners responsible for transfer-pricing documentation face personal assessment where the asset is stripped from the ELP; the court may order restoration of vessel proceeds, restitution, disqualification, and travel restrictions.
Shell Company / Substance Deficiency

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner may disregard the structure, attribute profits to the entity or persons with substance, and impose a compliance penalty of up to GIP 20,000; the Gibraltar Companies Registrar may strike off the ELP for false declarations.

Director personal liability: The general partner and beneficial owner face unlimited civil restitution to creditors, disqualification from acting as a shipyard manager or director for up to 10 years, and travel bans pending investigation.
AML / Suspicious Payment Handling

Statutory sanction: Penalties under the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act of up to GIP 1,000,000 per breach; confiscation of vessel proceeds; revocation of the statutory shipyard licence; referral to the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit.

Director personal liability: The Money Laundering Reporting Officer and general partner face imprisonment up to 14 years, personal confiscation orders, travel bans, and exclusion from regulated payment rails.
Trade Sanctions and Export Control Violations

Statutory sanction: An unlimited fine, forfeiture of the vessel and letter-of-credit proceeds, suspension of export privileges, and criminal sanctions under UK and Gibraltar trade sanctions legislation.

Director personal liability: Individual directors and general partners may be criminally prosecuted and imprisoned for up to 7 years; assets may be seized pending sanctions enforcement, and travel within the British Crown Dependencies may be barred.
