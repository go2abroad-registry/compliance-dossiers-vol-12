Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar operates a fully convertible currency regime with no exchange controls: both current-account and capital-account transactions are unrestricted, profit remittance and capital repatriation to non-resident ELP partners require no central-bank approval, and no withholding taxes apply to partnership distributions. GIP/GBP conversions are executed at the fixed 1:1 peg with zero FX margin; EUR and USD settlement is freely permitted through GFSC-licensed banks and their UK correspondent banking relationships.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Gibraltar does not impose any statutory export-proceeds surrender obligation, mandatory conversion ratio, or repatriation timeline for export of services. Web-scraping and API-gateway revenues may be retained offshore, net of operating reserves, in any freely convertible currency, including USDC treasury positions, subject only to contractual and AML record-keeping requirements.

Central bank supervision: The Bank of England is the ultimate monetary authority for the Gibraltar pound (GIP), which circulates at a statutory parity of 1:1 with the Great British pound (GBP); the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the statutory regulator for banking, payment services, e-money institutions, and distributed ledger technology (DLT) providers in Gibraltar.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Medium-High with legal constraints. Gibraltar is not in the EEA, so UK or EU e-money institution (EMI) passports do not automatically extend to the Territory. A Gibraltar-authorised EMI licence under the Financial Services (E-Money) Regulations or reliance on an FCA-registered UK EMI (e.g., BCB Group or Currencycloud) is required for USDC-to-fiat conversion. Stripe does not currently support Gibraltar as a merchant jurisdiction; the ELP should use a UK-incorporated settlement arm with a restricted-purpose EMI, or contract with a GFSC-licensed DLT payment provider offering card-acquiring via Worldpay/Adyen for card-not-present API revenues. Any regulated packaged USDC custody must be licensed under the Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT) Act 2019 and the GFSC DLT Guidance Notes.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Partner capital injections are routed via SWIFT MT103 in GBP/GIP from the UK settling bank to the Gibraltar beneficiary account using ISO 20022 purpose code CAPC (capital contribution), with reference to the ELP registration number and partner ID. Conversion from GBP to GIP occurs at the fixed 1:1 peg with no declaration or exchange-control filing. For USDC-seeded capital, convert through an FCA-registered UK EMI to GBP and remit MT103 to the Gibraltar operating bank, attaching source-of-funds evidence and the partnership capital schedule to the bank pre-advice. Beneficiary must match the exact legal name on the bank account mandate; a pre-account verification using the bank's Standard Settlement Instructions is required to avoid return.Supplier and trade disbursements settle via SWIFT MT103 priority payments for subscription invoices and infrastructure costs; GBP domestic obligations clear through CHAPS; EUR through TARGET2 via the nostro accounts of Gibraltar clearing banks in London; USD via Fedwire or correspondent SWIFT. Irrevocable commercial letters of credit may be issued by the Gibraltar clearing bank for hardware or cloud-procurement counterparties requiring bank intermediation. USDC payouts are first converted to fiat through a GFSC-DLT-licensed exchange, then executed as MT103, preserving a complete audit trail. Daily cash-pooling sweeps are consolidated at the primary Gibraltar operating account to support intraday liquidity and same-day disbursement cycles.Deliverable GBP/USD and GBP/EUR FX forwards with tenors from 7 days to 12 months offered by Banco de Sabadell (Gibraltar) or Gibraltar International Bank treasury desks in cooperation with London market-makers.FX swaps (GBP/USD rollovers) for short-term matching of USDC-fiat conversion timing with disbursement cycles.USD non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) permissible solely as a hedge for USDC-denominated invoices settled in fiat without physical USD delivery, executed through London-based NDF desks.Cross-currency swaps (GBP/USD and GBP/EUR) for matched-funding of multi-year operational disbursement liabilities; no GIP/GBP swap is required because of the statutory 1:1 parity peg.
