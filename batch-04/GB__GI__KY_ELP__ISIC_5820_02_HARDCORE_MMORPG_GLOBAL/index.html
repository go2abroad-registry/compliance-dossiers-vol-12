Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible - the ELP is treated as a non-transparent entity charged at the 12.5% corporation rate unless it elects qualifying-partnership transparency under section 114 ITA 2010; upon election, profits flow through to partners. Territorial_Scope applies: only Gibraltar-source profits are chargeable; non-Gibraltar-source app-store profits of non-resident partners are not taxed in Gibraltar. Entity_Level_Taxation is the fallback default for a non-qualifying partnership. Gibraltar is not a Tax_Exempt_Zone and grants no blanket exemption to resident entities.

Available election forms

(i) Qualifying partnership (tax transparency) election under section 114 Income Tax Act 2010 - available to limited partnerships satisfying transparency conditions; election is irrevocable once made. (ii) Statutory tax cap election for eligible IP-holding and group companies: annual corporation tax capped at the greater of GBP 15 million or a payroll-derived amount, subject to anti-avoidance and substance conditions. (iii) No Gibraltar 'check-the-box' regime exists; a US-member-owner may independently file US Form 8832 for US federal classification. (iv) Hybrid mismatch: Gibraltar is not an EU member state and has not transposed ATAD; mismatches are addressed only via UK CFC/anti-abuse rules where a UK nexus exists.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No general VAT, GST, or sales tax in Gibraltar; indirect taxation is limited to customs and excise duties on specified physical goods (alcohol, tobacco, hydrocarbon oils) and narrow import duties. Digital and software services, including MMORPG in-app purchases, are outside the scope of any Gibraltar indirect tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

None locally - no Gibraltar indirect-tax registration or threshold exists. For EU B2C e-service sales, the EU non-Union OSS scheme applies from the first euro with no registration threshold; the ELP must register for OSS in one EU member state immediately upon making any EU consumer supplies. UK VAT has no application to Gibraltar.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar levies no VAT/GST on any supply, so all digital exports (MMORPG IAP and virtual goods) are untaxed de facto with no zero-rating claim required. For EU B2C customers, the EU place-of-supply rule taxes e-services in the customer's member state and the ELP must charge EU VAT via non-Union OSS; for EU B2B customers, the recipient's reverse charge applies. No physical export movement occurs for pure digital deliveries, and no customs export procedure is engaged for core software sales.

  • Customs Export Declaration (required only where physical media, collector's editions, or merchandise are dispatched; for pure digital downloads, retain platform delivery logs and sales reports as functional substitute)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill (only for physical consignments; for digital sales, Apple App Store and Google Play payout reports and settlement statements evidence the transaction flow and value)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC is not issued in Gibraltar; substitute with bank/EMI account statements, SWIFT/SEPA credit advices, and FX conversion records evidencing repatriation of app-store proceeds into the ELP's bank accounts)

None. Gibraltar imposes no real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, API fiscalisation, or mandatory e-invoicing. Commercial invoices and Apple App Store / Google Play payout reports must be retained for six years under Gibraltar record-keeping rules; common-law invoicing standards apply with no B2B e-invoicing requirement.

No domestic VAT zero-rating clawback exists because Gibraltar imposes no VAT. Primary exposure is EU VAT non-compliance: EU member states may retroactively assess unpaid OSS VAT for up to 10 years, with interest, penalties, and potential fiscal representative requirements. Domestically, artificial structures exploiting ELP transparency or the GBP 15 million payroll-based cap trigger Gibraltar GAAR, transfer-pricing and source-rule adjustments under the Income Tax Act 2010, plus penalties up to 100% of unpaid tax, daily default fines, and criminal liability for tax fraud; Apple/Google settlement data are cross-matched to filed returns during audit.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar operates a fully liberalised no-exchange-control regime mirroring the UK post-1979. Both capital and current account transactions are freely convertible; the statutory 1:1 GIP/GBP peg eliminates sterling convertibility friction. Non-resident partners of the KY_ELP may repatriate profit shares, capital returns and loan repayments without exchange-control approval. Remittance hurdles are therefore exclusively documentary: GFSC AML/CFT source-of-wealth verification, CRS/FATCA classification, and Gibraltar Economic Substance documentation sufficient to avoid tax-residency reclassification. USD and EUR conversion is available through all tier-1 banks via correspondent nostro accounts at normal market spreads, with no central-bank levy, conversion quota or prior-approval filing. Apple App Store and Google Play proceeds in USD, EUR or GBP may be retained in any currency denomination of the multi-currency operating account.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender rules exist in Gibraltar: there is no mandatory conversion timeline, no minimum repatriation ratio, and no obligation to convert in-app purchase receivables into GIP or GBP. The ELP may retain USD, EUR, GBP and other settlement currencies indefinitely in designated multi-currency accounts. GFSC transaction-monitoring expectations nonetheless require documented reconciliation each financial period; best market practice is a 180-day rolling cycle matching App Store Connect and Play Console payout statements to bank credits. The licensing and distribution of the MMORPG is treated as an export of digital services, which falls outside any merchandise-based surrender or collection regime; all proceeds vest beneficially in the ELP and are taxable only in accordance with the partnership's pass-through classification.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) — integrated regulator for banking, electronic money and foreign exchange services; Government of Gibraltar Commissioner of Currency — statutory issuing authority for the Gibraltar Pound (GIP) under the Currency Notes Act 1934; Bank of England — the monetary anchor because the GIP is pegged at 1:1 to GBP and UK sterling circulates freely as legal tender in Gibraltar.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Constrained Medium-Low viability. UK FCA-authorised EMIs (Wise Business, Revolut Business) and EU EMIs generally rate a non-resident Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership as offshore-parasternal risk and either decline onboarding or impose restrictive turnover caps. Critically, no foreign EMI may lawfully take deposits or provide money transmission inside Gibraltar without a GFSC Electronic Money Institution licence or a post-Brexit passport equivalent, which has lapsed for UK and EU issuers. The compliant architecture is to keep the principal treasury and all capital accounts at a tier-1 Gibraltar bank, and use an FCA-regulated UK EMI only as a disbursement gateway for sub-£50,000 vendor and creator payments, never as the primary clearing house. A locally GFSC-licensed e-money institution is the only fully compliant domestic alternative and becomes viable once the ELP has sustained monthly payout volume above approximately GBP 250,000.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound GBP capital from UK investors must route by SWIFT MT103; UK Faster Payments and CHAPS terminate at the London gateway and cannot credit a Gibraltar BIC directly. Instruct the remitting bank to send an MT103 with beneficiary BIC of the tier-1 Gibraltar bank (e.g., GIBLGI GI XXX for Gibraltar International Bank), reference line 'GP capital contribution — KY_ELP', and SWIFT Business Application purpose code S400 (direct investment equity). GIP/GBP 1:1 pegging eliminates FX cost on sterling injections. For USD capital from US venture funds, route via the Gibraltar bank's New York correspondent (JP Morgan or Citibank grade) with an OFAC-compliant cover message, purpose code S400, and open the account as multi-currency USD so no forced conversion occurs. For EUR injections, use TARGET2 settlement through the tier-1 bank's Frankfurt or HSBC nostro, purpose code S301 or S400. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% flat corporation tax; the first GIP 50,000 of annual taxable profits of a qualifying small/medium enterprise is taxed at the reduced rate of 8% (Income Tax Act 2010, as amended).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on dividend distributions or ELP profit allocations paid to either resident or non-resident recipients.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

The ELP and its corporate general partner must file within nine months after the end of the accounting period; for a 31 December year-end the filing and payment date is 30 September of the following year. Payment on account and balancing payments are enforced by the Commissioner.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: A Local File is mandatory where the aggregate value of Gibraltar controlled transactions exceeds GIP 5,000,000 in an accounting period. A Master File and Country-by-Country Report alignment apply where the global MNE group exceeds EUR 750,000,000 in consolidated revenue or where local related-party turnover exceeds GIP 50,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Applicable. The Income Tax (Substance Requirements) Rules 2019 require any Gibraltar entity deriving geographically mobile income - including IP royalties, brand licensing, and finance income from MMORPG operations - to demonstrate premises, employees, direction and management in Gibraltar, and core income-generating activities. For the transparent ELP, the Commissioner looks through to the partners; non-resident partners must ensure that the corporate general partner maintains adequate substance, otherwise the ELP may be reclassified as a Gibraltar-resident taxable person on its worldwide relevant income.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Return

Statutory sanction: Financial penalty of up to three times the aggregate tax evaded; on conviction on indictment, imprisonment of up to seven years; asset confiscation under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015; publication orders identifying the offender.

Director personal liability: The court may pierce the ELP's limited liability veil where the partnership is used as a facade for fraud; general partners, investment managers, and directing minds are jointly and severally liable for the evaded tax together with criminal fines and custodial sentences of up to seven years.
Shell Company / Sham Substance

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner may disregard the ELP structure as a sham, re-assess all attributed profits at the 12.5% rate, impose a tax-geared penalty of up to 100%, strike the entity off the register, and withdraw the ELP's authority to carry on business.

Director personal liability: The general partner and any shadow director become personally liable for the assessed tax and penalties; travel bans, asset freezing orders, and disqualification orders under the Companies Act apply where substance abuse is used to evade tax or launder the proceeds of digital gameplay monetisation.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Fines of up to GIP 1,000,000 per breach under the Proceeds of Crime (Anti-Money Laundering) Order; daily default fines for overdue beneficial ownership filings; imprisonment of up to five years on conviction for anti-money laundering offences involving concealed partners.

Director personal liability: The general partner and each designated officer are personally liable for AML fines; directors of the corporate general partner may be disqualified and subjected to travel bans and confiscation proceedings where the ELP is used to obscure beneficial ownership of the global MMORPG revenue stream.
Abusive Transfer Pricing / Documentation Default

Statutory sanction: Adjustment of profits to the arm's-length standard; an additional penalty of up to 100% of the extra tax assessed; interest at 1% above the Bank of England base rate; denial of treaty benefits and, in aggravated cases, criminal prosecution for tax evasion.

Director personal liability: Tax-geared penalties attach personally to partners who sign the inaccurate computation; the Commissioner may revoke the ELP's registration under the Limited Partnerships Act 2017, and individuals responsible for the manipulation face fines, travel bans, and imprisonment of up to seven years.
