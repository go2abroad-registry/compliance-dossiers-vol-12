Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed on a Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership; the general partner must subscribe a nominal capital (market standard GBP 100) to evidence the contribution stated in the Statement of Particulars. No escrow account is mandated by the Registrar, and the contribution may remain credited to the partnership records.

Funding & unlock path: Not applicable for virtual operations. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Her Majesty's Government of Gibraltar — Commissioner of Income Tax (Income Tax Office), HM Customs & Excise Gibraltar; official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation; customs and indirect duties: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/customs

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Register the ELP with the Registrar of Companies (Companies House Gibraltar) under the Limited Partnerships Act, obtaining a Certificate of Registration. Step 2: Lodge the partnership deed and a completed tax registration application with the Commissioner of Income Tax (Income Tax Office), declaring the ELP to be a tax-transparent partnership and providing the name, address, and capital contribution of each general and limited partner. Step 3: Receive the Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TIN) for the ELP; each partner receives a separate partner reference and, being non-resident and deriving no Gibraltar-source income, is not subject to Gibraltar income tax. Step 4: If physical goods are ever imported or exported, separately apply to HM Customs & Excise for an EORI number; no VAT or GST registration ID is available because Gibraltar does not operate any VAT/GST regime.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

None — Gibraltar has no check-the-box or similar opacity election. Under the Income Tax Act 2010, an Exempted Limited Partnership is mandatorily transparent; the partners are taxed in their own hands and no entity-level tax charge arises. No hybrid mismatch election is available under Gibraltar domestic law; however, general anti-avoidance principles and applicable double taxation arrangements may recharacterise hybrid mismatch results.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT / No GST / No Sales-and-Use Tax — Gibraltar applies only customs and import duties (territorial excise-type levies); digital health and fitness app supply via Apple IAP and Google Play Billing is entirely outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect taxation.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — Gibraltar has no VAT/GST registration threshold; turnover from digital app sales does not trigger any indirect tax registration. A customs EORI registration (with no monetary threshold) is required solely if the ELP imports or exports physical goods into or from Gibraltar.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — because Gibraltar imposes no VAT or GST, pure digital supplies (including the fitness biometric tracking app sold through Apple IAP and Google Play Billing) are outside the scope of indirect tax and require no zero-rating claim or refund procedure. For any hypothetical physical exports of associated wellness hardware, duty-relief applies only where ownership passes to a non-resident buyer and the goods are physically removed from Gibraltar customs territory, supported by standard export evidence.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Gibraltar Single Administrative Document / Export Entry) — required only for physical goods leaving Gibraltar; not applicable to pure digital app transactions
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill — applicable to tangible consignments only
  • Commercial Invoice evidencing the nature, buyer, destination, and value of the exported goods or services
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank statement evidencing receipt of export proceeds (including App Store Connect and Google Play payout settlements) within the repatriation window

No real-time fiscal clearance, API-based e-invoicing, digital stamping, or transaction authorisation mandate exists in Gibraltar. Ordinary commercial invoices and English-language financial records maintained in GBP/GIP satisfy the record-keeping obligations under the Income Tax Act 2010 and HM Customs & Excise requirements.

There is no VAT zero-rating mechanism in Gibraltar and therefore no VAT clawback regime; however, HM Customs & Excise may retrospectively assess duty on goods falsely declared as exports, impose civil penalties of up to 100% of duty evaded, and pursue criminal fines and imprisonment for fraudulent evasion. For income tax purposes, a false declaration or deliberate concealment of export-related revenue or partner allocations attracts penalties of up to 200% of the tax understated, interest, and potential criminal prosecution under the Income Tax Act 2010.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is fully fungible with sterling (GBP) at par and is freely convertible for both current and capital account transactions. There are no exchange-control quotas or administrative approvals for profit remittance, partner capital returns, or trade payments. In practice, banks require documentary substantiation such as audited accounts, GFSC entity filings, source-of-funds evidence, and a compliant invoice/payment instruction before the first outbound wire. No exchange-control licence is required for a software-related exempted limited partnership operating as an app developer.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar imposes no statutory surrender of export proceeds and no mandatory conversion ratio. Subscription, in-app purchase, and B2B telemetry licence revenues may be retained in GBP, USD, EUR, or any commercially agreed currency. Receipts should be credited to the ELP's operational bank account within the normal settlement period of the payment facilitator (typically 30 to 45 days for Apple IAP and Google Play Billing) and must be traceable to original order records, platform settlement reports, and the partnership ledger for AML audit purposes.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the foreign-exchange administration and banking supervisor for Gibraltar; no Gibraltar central bank exists. Monetary sovereignty remains with HM Treasury / Bank of England, with the Gibraltar pound legally pegged at par to sterling.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility: Medium for a GFSC-authorised electronic money institution (EMI); Low for an unlicensed offshore digital EMI. A Gibraltar-regulated EMI may provide a segregated payment account, multi-currency settlement, and remote onboarding support, but the primary liquidity buffer and treasury concentration must remain with a licensed credit institution. An offshore EMI not authorised by the GFSC cannot lawfully operate a payment account for a Gibraltar ELP without breaching the Financial Services Act 2019; such providers may only be used as a settlement facilitator where the final clearing leg is executed through a regulated Gibraltar or UK bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound capital contributions must be made by SWIFT MT103 or MT202 from the partner’s own regulated bank account, with a unique end-to-end transaction reference (UETR) and a commercial purpose code identifying capital contribution or equity injection into the Gibraltar ELP. The receiving bank will run sanctions, PEP, adverse-media, and source-of-funds clearance before booking value. For USD capital, the clearing path is through the bank’s US correspondent; for EUR, through TARGET2 or SEPA where the remitter is in the EEA; for GIP/GBP, settlement occurs via the UK sterling clearing system at par. No GFSC or HM Treasury approval is required, but the ELP must retain the capital call notice, partner resolution, remitter bank confirmation, and an internal ledger entry mapping each inbound wire to the contributor’s capital account.Outbound vendor and trade payments should be executed by high-priority SWIFT MT103 for USD and EUR, and by Faster Payments or CHAPS for GBP where the settlement bank is a UK clearing participant. For supplier contracts involving software licences, cloud infrastructure, advertising, content platforms, or data hosting, the ELP may use irrevocable commercial letters of credit issued by a first-class UK or Gibraltar bank and confirmed through MT700/MT710; payment at sight under complying presentation is made by MT103. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory when the Gibraltar partnership or any partner undertaking exceeds GIP 10 million turnover in a fiscal year; Master File is required for groups with aggregate consolidated turnover above GIP 50 million. Contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation must also be maintained where related-party cross-border transactions exceed GIP 1 million annually.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar applies Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) to geographically mobile activities, including intellectual property, financing, and digital operations. The health-and-fitness app business must demonstrate that core income-generating activities (e.g., software development, server management, and user-data processing) are carried out in Gibraltar, with adequate local employees, premises, and annual expenditure; a mandatory annual NES (Notification of Economic Substance) filing is required.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Deliberate Understatement

Statutory sanction: Penalty of up to 200% of the tax underpaid, a fine not exceeding GIP 100,000, and criminal prosecution leading to imprisonment for up to three years.

Director personal liability: The General Partner and any director acting in wilful default may face civil recovery for the entire unpaid tax, personal asset forfeiture, travel bans, director disqualification, and imprisonment for up to three years; corporate veil piercing is available under the Gibraltar Companies (Vicarious Liability) framework.
Shell Company / No Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner may deny Gibraltar tax treatment, reclassify profits as chargeable in Gibraltar, impose a fine not exceeding GIP 10,000 for ESR failures, and strike the ELP off the register.

Director personal liability: The General Partner is treated as a shadow director; civil penalties may be recovered personally, and directors can be disqualified from holding Gibraltar company offices for up to five years, with worldwide assets subject to mutual legal assistance seizure.
Anti-Money Laundering (AML/CFT) Breach

Statutory sanction: A fine not exceeding GIP 1,000,000, suspension of the ELP's operating licence, and criminal prosecution against the partnership and its partners for breach of the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act.

Director personal liability: Responsible officers and the General Partner are exposed to civil and criminal liability, including custodial sentences of up to two years, compensation orders for defrauded parties, and public disqualification from directorial roles.
Transfer Pricing Misstatement / Artificial Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: The Gibraltar tax authority may reprice the transactions, adjust taxable income, apply a 10% corporation tax rate to the reallocated profits, and levy a penalty of 100% of the additional tax liability.

Director personal liability: Directors and the General Partner who knowingly authorise transfer-pricing misstatements may be held jointly and severally liable for the adjusted tax, subject to civil recovery orders, travel restrictions, and discretionary imprisonment for up to three years.
