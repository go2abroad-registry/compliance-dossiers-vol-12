Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for an Exempted Limited Partnership; capital contributions are fixed exclusively by the Limited Partnership Agreement. No escrow account or capital deposit requirement applies under Gibraltar law; bank confirmation of contributions is recommended for AML record-keeping.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Risk Assessment and Fire Certificate from the Gibraltar Fire and Rescue Service; structural load adequacy certificate for the registered office premises issued by an approved structural engineer; health and safety workplace clearance under the Health and Safety at Work (General) Regulations (Gibraltar); occupancy certificate evidencing compliance of the office premises with building control and means-of-escape requirements.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital for an Exempted Limited Partnership; capital contributions are fixed exclusively by the Limited Partnership Agreement. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Gibraltar ELPs are statutorily transparent; no check-the-box regime is available. An elective opaque treatment is permitted only for certain partnership arrangements under the Gibraltar Limited Partnerships (Taxation) Rules and must be made by the general partner in writing to the Commissioner of Income Tax. Hybrid mismatch disclosure rules require reporting of any such election or of any jurisdictional classification mismatch with a foreign CFC or hybrid entity.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT / GST / Sales and Use Tax (Gibraltar is outside the EU VAT area and has no general goods-and-services tax)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT/GST registration threshold exists for services; an Economic Operator Registration is required only for physical goods movements through Gibraltar Customs

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar imposes no VAT/GST on services, so all export supplies of App Store Optimization and multilingual localization services are outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect tax. There is consequently no zero-rating claim mechanism and no refund system for such supplies. If physical promotional materials are exported, relief from Customs import duty may be claimed on proof of export outside Gibraltar within three months, provided the exporter holds valid export documentation and retains evidence of dispatch.

  • Customs Export Declaration (for physical goods only)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill as proof of export
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank statement evidencing receipt of funds from the overseas purchaser

None. Gibraltar does not operate a real-time e-invoicing or fiscal clearance regime. Invoices for cross-border SEO and localization services must be issued and retained under the Income Tax Act and Companies Act record-keeping rules, but no API-based fiscal stamping or digital clearance is mandatory.

Although no indirect tax is chargeable in Gibraltar, wilful or negligent misstatements in any export relief or duty drawback claim attract penalties under the Customs and Excise Act 1988: fines of up to three times the duty value, forfeiture of goods, and imprisonment for fraud. For income tax purposes, failure to substantiate arm's-length consideration received for cross-border services within the 180-day documentary period may lead to an adjustment of assessable profits, a penalty of up to 100% of the tax evaded, and interest accrual. Retrospective assessments may extend to six years, or twenty years in cases of fraudulent conduct.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar operates with full current and capital account convertibility. There are no exchange controls restricting the conversion of GBP, EUR, USD or other currencies. Profits of an Exempted Limited Partnership may be freely remitted to non-resident partners after satisfying tax-filing, CRS/FATCA, anti-money-laundering and economic substance documentation requirements. No Gibraltar withholding tax applies on ELP distributions to partners.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. No statutory timeline exists for surrendering export/service proceeds and no mandatory conversion ratio applies. Receipts in foreign currency may be retained in any currency. However, to avoid bank de-risking and comply with GFSC AML/CFT guidance, the ELP should maintain a documented monthly reconciliation of all inbound receipts, client contracts and underlying app store/localization services evidence.

Central bank supervision: HM Government of Gibraltar (Currency Notes Act 1934); the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission supervises banks; the Bank of England does not act as Gibraltar's central bank and Gibraltar has no standalone central bank.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

High feasibility only as a supplementary non-bank payment corridor. Wise B2B and Payoneer are not Gibraltar-regulated banks and cannot be used as the principal treasury account for the ELP. They may lawfully receive app store settlement proceeds and distribute localization vendor payments in multiple currencies, but all significant inbound capital contributions and annual profit distributions must flow through a Gibraltar-regulated bank to preserve clean audit trails and satisfy GFSC AML/KYC substance expectations.

Root cause: Pass-through distributions to partners in jurisdictions outside the UK or Gibraltar

Defense evidence: FATCA and CRS registration files, partner tax residence certificates, annual ELP tax returns, board/distribution minutes, a legal opinion confirming no Gibraltar withholding tax, and a breakdown of distributable profits sourced from external audited financial statements.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All capital injections must be made by wire transfer through established banking channels. GBP contributions should use CHAPS or SWIFT MT103 with the ELP's Gibraltarian IBAN and SWIFT/BIC code. EUR contributions should use SEPA Credit Transfer or SWIFT MT103; USD contributions should use SWIFT MT103 via the correspondent network. Purpose references should state 'Capital Contribution' or 'Limited Partner Subscription' and must not reference crypto, personal spending or unrelated trade receipts. Commercial letters of credit under UCP600 are unnecessary for ISIC 7310 service agency activity but may be used if a major enterprise client insists on documentary settlement. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% (standard Gibraltar corporate income tax rate under the Income Tax Act 2010; the Exempted Limited Partnership itself is fiscally transparent and therefore not chargeable to income tax at entity level)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on dividends, partnership profit distributions, or other periodic distributions to non-resident partners under the Income Tax Act 2010. Such distributions are also deductible/non-taxable in the hands of the ELP because of its pass-through classification.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 6 months of the ELP accounting reference period end (for a 30 September year-end: by 31 March; for a 31 December year-end: by 30 June) or within 30 days of a formal notice to file, whichever is earlier.

Deadline: · Frequency:

No later than 42 days after the anniversary of the ELP's registration date, irrespective of accounting year-end.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Gibraltar TP rules are aligned with OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines (Income Tax Act 2010, as amended). Mandatory Local File: any accounting period in which the value of cross-border controlled transactions with related parties exceeds GBP 1,000,000; Master File: group consolidated turnover above GBP 65,000,000; Country-by-Country Report: group consolidated revenue of EUR 750,000,000 or more (local currency requiring filing in GIP/GBP equivalent).

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to relevant activities, including 'distribution and service centre' and 'headquarters' — which capture an intra-group ASO/SEO localization agency if services are rendered to related parties. The ELP must demonstrate, in Gibraltar: physical premises, strategic decision-making by local board or general partner meetings, sufficient qualified employees, and adequate operating expenditure proportionate to revenue. Pure third-party agency revenue outside any relevant activity still requires a low-key CIGA trace for CRS/FATCA purposes. Tax transparency does not exempt the ELP from ESR filing or verification.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Deliberate Misdeclaration

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty up to 200% of the tax understated; interest at 8% per annum; criminal prosecution for cheating the public revenue or fraudulent evasion of income tax carrying unlimited fines and/or imprisonment (up to 7 years per offence); the Commissioner may void loss carry-forward relief and reopen prior assessments.

Director personal liability: General partner and any managing partner who signs or procures a false return are personally liable for civil restitution of the tax evaded, subject to director disqualification orders (up to 15 years), and, upon criminal conviction, travel bans and custodial sentences.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance (ESR)

Statutory sanction: ESR financial penalties proportional to the failure (escalable up to the value of the tax advantage forgone), daily penalties for continued contravention, mandatory automatic exchange of information with UK/EU authorities, and strike-off of the ELP from the Gibraltar Register; the Commissioner may treat the ELP as a shell and deem distributed profits to taxable individuals.

Director personal liability: Directors and the General Partner risk personal fines for each day of non-compliance, disqualification, and, where the shell is deployed to defeat foreign tax claims, civil liability for the resulting tax shortfall and criminal conspiracy exposure.
AML / Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and GFSC Regime Breach

Statutory sanction: Unlimited fine and/or imprisonment up to 14 years for money laundering and failure to disclose; GFSC regulatory penalties, freezing orders, confiscation under POCA 2015; statutory debarment from Financial Services Act 2019 licensed activity.

Director personal liability: General Partners and appointed compliance officers face personal criminal liability for 'consent' failures and for unauthorized disclosures, including travel bans, asset restraint orders, and custodial sentences where predicate tax offences are proven.
UBO Register, CRS, and DAC6 Reporting Failures

Statutory sanction: Criminal fine up to GBP 5,000 per default plus up to 2 years' imprisonment for false or misleading statements; Registrar may strike off the ELP; late CRS/DAC6 penalties of up to GBP 1,000 per form plus daily increases; the non-resident partners become reportable to their home tax authorities.

Director personal liability: The General Partner remains personally responsible for registry accuracy; partners found to have structured ownership to conceal identity face personal criminal penalty, disqualification from managing Gibraltar entities, and civil restitution of any tax advantages obtained.
