Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office is not acceptable as the statutory registered office for an Exempted Limited Partnership. The ELP must maintain a physical registered office in Gibraltar at an address approved by the Registrar of Companies and occupied by the licensed registered agent. The GFSC and corporate service providers expect substantive commercial presence, including local records and availability for inspections; tax residence and VAT registration depend on the real registered office and management location.

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, Ministry of Finance, Her Majesty's Government of Gibraltar (Director of Income Tax); online portal: https://incometax.gi (iTax e-services). Incorporation registry: Companies House Gibraltar, Registrar of Companies, https://corporations.gov.gi.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Register the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) with Companies House Gibraltar under the Limited Partnerships Act 2010 and obtain the Registrar's official partnership number. Step 2: File the ELP exempted status election (non-resident partnership) with the Registrar, supported by a declaration of non-Gibraltarian ownership. Step 3: Submit a partnership information return to the Income Tax Office so that the ELP is assessed on a transparent basis; the Office issues a tax reference for the partnership's administrative file. Step 4: Non-resident partners rely on their own foreign TINs; a Gibraltar TIN is required only where Gibraltar-source income arises or a withholding obligation applies. Step 5: No Gibraltar VAT ID is obtainable because Gibraltar has no VAT; where UK VAT registration is triggered by digital services supplied to UK consumers, register online with HMRC via gov.uk/register-for-vat (no turnover threshold for non-established suppliers of e-services).

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Available elections: (a) ELP non-resident status election under the Limited Partnerships Act 2010; (b) partnership fiscal-transparency election under the Income Tax Act 2010, ensuring that the ELP is not chargeable to Gibraltar income tax and that partners bear tax on their shares; (c) hybrid mismatch neutralisation elections under Gibraltar's implementation of the OECD BEPS Action 2 framework, preventing double deduction and deduction-without-inclusion outcomes; (d) US federal Form 8832 check-the-box (matters only for US tax classification, not Gibraltar law).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar Purchase Tax and customs import duties on physical goods; no VAT and no GST are levied in Gibraltar. UK VAT (20%) may apply to the ELP only where a digital supply is treated as made in the United Kingdom to UK consumers.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Gibraltar: no turnover threshold for purchase tax; liability arises on importation or supply of dutiable physical goods. UK VAT: threshold GBP 90,000 for UK-established businesses; zero threshold for non-established suppliers of e-services (including royalty-free stock music subscriptions) to UK consumers; distance-selling threshold for goods from Gibraltar is GBP 90,000 per 12 months.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export zero-rating is inapplicable to the core activity because digitally delivered stock music is a service, not goods. A B2B license supplied to a UK business is outside the scope of UK VAT (reverse charge to the customer); a B2C supply to UK consumers is chargeable at 20% once registration is required; B2C supplies to buyers outside the UK are outside the scope. Zero-rating under section 30 VATA 1994 would arise only if physical media (e.g., pre-recorded audio hardware) is exported from Gibraltar, and then only with proof of physical removal from the UK/Gibraltar customs territory within three months.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Gibraltar Single Administrative Document or equivalent export entry)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical exportation from Gibraltar
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit confirmation evidencing receipt of export sale proceeds

Gibraltar imposes no e-invoicing mandate, real-time fiscal clearance, API reporting or digital stamping. UK VAT invoices, where required, must contain the statutory content under VATA 1994 s.23 and reg. 13 of the VAT Regulations 1995 and be issued within 30 days of supply; Making Tax Digital quarterly VAT filing applies to UK-established traders, while a Gibraltar-based ELP with a UK VAT registration files through HMRC's designated non-established-traders service. PEPPOL BIS 3.0 e-invoicing is compulsory only for UK public-sector procurement, not for private B2B or B2C audio licensing.

Fraudulent export zero-rating, misdescription or underdeclaration is prosecuted under the Gibraltar Customs and Purchase Tax fiscal offence legislation, carrying penalties up to three times the duty evaded and imprisonment up to 10 years; deliberate inaccuracies in Income Tax Office filings attract penalties up to 100% of the underpaid tax. For UK VAT, unsupported zero-rating or outside-scope claims by a Gibraltar-registered trader expose the ELP to HMRC assessment, interest, penalty of up to 100% of potential lost revenue for deliberate error, deregistration for persistent non-compliance, and criminal prosecution under VATA 1994 s.72; HMRC may claw back refunds for up to 4 years (6 years where deliberate error is established).

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GIP is fully convertible into GBP at par without restriction, and the territory imposes zero exchange controls on current or capital account transactions. Capital account convertibility is unrestricted for foreign-owned limited partnerships; distributions and profit remittances to non-resident LPs are free of FX approval, withholding, or repatriation tax. Practical constraints: first-time inbound wires may be held 1–5 business days for Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 screening, and bank FX spreads typically range 80–150 basis points for spot USD/GBP and EUR/GBP. Multi-currency accounts (GIP, GBP, USD, EUR) are standard, allowing the ELP to retain USD/EUR licensing receipts without mandatory conversion.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Gibraltar imposes no statutory surrender, mandatory conversion, or holding-period rule for export proceeds. Royalty and licensing income from international music-marketplace sales may be retained in original settlement currency (USD/EUR) or converted to GBP/GIP at the ELP's discretion. Deposits exceeding EUR 10,000 equivalent trigger a standard source-of-funds note, but no GFSC circularisation or conversion obligation arises.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the integrated prudential, conduct, and AML/CFT supervisor; HM Government of Gibraltar (Currency Section) issues the Gibraltar pound (GIP) at a currency-board-style 1:1 parity with GBP. No independent central bank exists; the Bank of England's policy rate anchors Gibraltar's monetary conditions de facto.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% standard Gibraltar corporation tax rate (Income Tax Act 2010 as amended). The Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is fiscally transparent: no entity-level tax is imposed in Gibraltar; taxation is levied solely at the partner level on each partner's share of chargeable profits.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Gibraltar domestic law imposes no withholding tax on distributions, dividends, or profit withdrawals made by a Gibraltar entity, including an Exempted Limited Partnership, regardless of the partner's residence.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Gibraltar's year of assessment runs from 1 July to 30 June. The return for a chargeable period ending on or before 30 June must be filed on or before 30 November immediately following the end of the relevant year of assessment. A later-issued notice of assessment extends the deadline to 30 days from the date of issue. Payment of any assessed tax is due by 30 November of the year of assessment; instalment arrangements are not available for transparent partnerships.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: The Income Tax (Transfer Pricing) Regulations 2019 apply the OECD arm's-length standard to all controlled transactions without a monetary de minimis threshold. A formal Local File and Master File are mandatory only when the Gibraltar entity, aggregated with its transparent partners, is part of a group with consolidated revenue above EUR 50 million and records Gibraltar turnover above EUR 10 million. Below those thresholds, the ELP must still maintain contemporaneous, proportionate TP documentation (financial model, pricing policy, comparability summary) and deliver it to the Commissioner within 30 days of a request. Functional analysis must document the ELP's ownership of the music catalogue, contract execution, and risk assumption distinct from the UK parent.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations 2020 (as amended) apply to every Gibraltar tax-resident entity and to any partnership carrying on a 'relevant activity'. Intellectual-property business, including the commercial holding and licensing of a stock-music audio catalogue, is a relevant activity. The ELP must meet a proportionality test: (i) physical premises in Gibraltar (leased office, not a virtual mailbox); (ii) qualified full-time employees in Gibraltar (minimum one for a catalogue of this size, ideally separate from the GP); (iii) minimum annual operating expenditure in Gibraltar (target at least 30% of gross licensing revenue); (iv) core income-generating activities performed in Gibraltar, including IP maintenance, sub-licence negotiation, compliance monitoring, and royalty collection oversight; and (v) all statutory books and records retained in Gibraltar. Outsourcing to the UK parent is permitted only on an arm's-length commercial basis and does not automatically satisfy the substance test; a pure 'passive IP shell' will fail ESR.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Misstatement

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty up to 100% of the under-assessed tax (increased to 200% where fraud is established under s.215 of the Income Tax Act 2010); interest at the official rate from the date the tax ought to have been paid; criminal indictment on conviction of cheating the public revenue with fines unlimited at the discretion of the Supreme Court and imprisonment up to 5 years.

Director personal liability: Directors of a corporate general partner are jointly and severally liable for the ELP's unpaid tax where the return was filed with their knowledge or consent; confiscation orders may be made under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 freezing personal and family assets; travel bans of up to 6 months renewable may be imposed; directors face disqualification from managing any Gibraltar company for up to 15 years.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance Regulations 2020: initial penalty up to GIP100,000; subsequent years up to GIP200,000; additional daily penalty up to GIP2,000 per day for continued non-compliance; the Registrar may strike the ELP off the Companies Register with automatic forfeiture of the protected 'ELP' name and disqualification from re-registration for 5 years.

Director personal liability: GP directors bear personal civil liability for ESR penalties where they knowingly caused the failure; the court can issue a cease-and-desist order and compel winding-up; personal liability extends to creditors and limited partners who relied on false substance declarations; in cases involving evaded tax above GBP 500,000 linked to UK licensors, the Attorney General may consent to extradition to the UK.
Anti-Money Laundering and Sanctions Breach

Statutory sanction: Fines up to GIP250,000 per breach under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 (Gibraltar) and associated Sanctions Regulations; termination of payment-rail access by Stripe, PayPal, or the referring EMI bank; immediate freezing of all funds connected to the tainted transaction; confiscation of cryptocurrency or fiat proceeds equal to the full value of the laundered sums.

Director personal liability: A GP director or Money Laundering Reporting Officer who turns a blind eye may be indicted for money laundering and imprisoned for up to 14 years; personal financial penalty of up to three times the laundered amount; civil freezing orders over the director's worldwide assets; prohibition from acting as a director in Gibraltar or the UK for 10 years; the corporate veil is pierced and the UK parent entity may be joined as anchor defendant in Gibraltar civil proceedings.
Transfer Pricing, Diverted Profits Tax, and Cross-Border Misalignment

Statutory sanction: UK Diverted Profits Tax at 25% of the diverted profit plus interest; from April 2025, an additional policy penalty of up to 10% of the excess profit notified following a TP adjustment; Gibraltar Revenue may also make an upward adjustment to 12.5% on the arm's-length profit with a penalty of up to 100% of the additional tax and 5% p.a. late-payment interest.

Director personal liability: A UK-resident director of the corporate GP or parent that knowingly participates in arrangements designed to obtain a tax advantage may be personal assessable for the DPT penalty, with a conduct penalty surcharge of 20% added; HMRC may certify a Senior Accounting Officer failure where reasonable tax accounting arrangements were not maintained, giving rise to a GBP 5,000 personal penalty and mandatory restriction on future tax clearances.
