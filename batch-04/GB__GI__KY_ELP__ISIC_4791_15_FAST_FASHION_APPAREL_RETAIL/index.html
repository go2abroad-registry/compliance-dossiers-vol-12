Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (Commissioner of Income Tax); official online services portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/

E-government filing portal

For an Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP): (1) incorporate/register the ELP with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under the Limited Partnerships Act to obtain the Gibraltar company number and certificate of registration; (2) appoint one or more general partners and, if required under the AML/CFT Code, a representative person in Gibraltar; (3) apply to the Income Tax Office for GCT registration using Form GCT-1, submitting a certified copy of the partnership deed, certificate of registration, registered office address, and expected annual turnover split between taxable and zero-rated supplies; (4) receive the GCT Registration Number (the indirect tax TIN) and the GCT filing schedule; (5) register for income tax to the extent any partner is Gibraltar-resident or where the ELP has Gibraltar-source taxable income, acting through the representative person where necessary.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible (Gibraltar ELPs are tax transparent for income tax and separately registrable as taxable persons for General Consumption Tax; entity-level taxation does not apply unless the ELP is incorporated or elects opaque treatment). — For an Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP): (1) incorporate/register the ELP with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under the Limited Partnerships Act to obtain the Gibraltar company number and certificate of registration; (2) appoint one or more general partners and, if required under the AML/CFT Code, a representative person in Gibraltar; (3) apply to the Income Tax Office for GCT registration using Form GCT-1, submitting a certified copy of the partnership deed, certificate of registration, registered office address, and expected annual turnover split between taxable and zero-rated supplies; (4) receive the GCT Registration Number (the indirect tax TIN) and the GCT filing schedule; (5) register for income tax to the extent any partner is Gibraltar-resident or where the ELP has Gibraltar-source taxable income, acting through the representative person where necessary.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible (Gibraltar ELPs are tax transparent for income tax and separately registrable as taxable persons for General Consumption Tax; entity-level taxation does not apply unless the ELP is incorporated or elects opaque treatment).

Available election forms

No check-the-box election for GCT. Taxation default is pass-through; no statutory election to be taxed as a company is available for a Gibraltar ELP except conversion to a Gibraltar company (e.g., Companies Act). Hybrid mismatch elections (for interest/royalty deductions) may be filed with the Commissioner of Income Tax to conform the ELP's treatment to GAAR/ATAD, with transparent status documented via the partnership deed and tax residence certificate.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

1%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GIP 150,000 per annum (compulsory registration for taxable supplies); export-only traders may register voluntarily to claim zero-rating/refund. There is no registration threshold for non-established suppliers of digital services.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Supplies of fast-fashion apparel and footwear are zero-rated for GCT if: (i) goods are physically delivered from Gibraltar to a customer outside Gibraltar (e.g., Great Britain, EU, US, Asia); (ii) the ELP retains written evidence of export/removal from Gibraltar within 3 months of supply; (iii) the ELP holds the general partner's inward remittance/payment for export-backed goods where consideration is in a foreign currency; and (iv) the customer is not a private individual in Gibraltar. Services such as delivery and storage supplied directly to export goods are also zero-rated.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Gibraltar Single Administrative Document / UK Customs Declaration System if exported through UK)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (or SWIFT/MT103 evidence from the purchasing customer's bank)
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List

No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or API pre-clearance mandate. GCT invoices must contain prescribed particulars (GCT registration number, quantity, amount, rate, and taxed amount) and be issued within 30 days. GCT returns (Form 2) are filed electronically on a quarterly basis via the Income Tax Office online portal, with automatic reconciliation of GCT collected to debit/credit card settlement data under the digital economy compliance programme.

Penalty regime under the General Consumption Tax Act and Income Tax Act: (a) retrospective GCT assessment up to 6 years, with the burden on the ELP to prove physical export; (b) tax-geared penalty up to 100% of evaded GCT and interest of up to 12% per annum; (c) specific penalty of GIP 300 for each return containing false evidence; (d) criminal offence if dishonestly claiming zero-rating, carrying up to GIP 5,000 fine on summary conviction or an unlimited fine and up to 6 years' imprisonment on conviction on indictment. Customs may also apply forfeiture of goods; the ELP's members and general partners are jointly and severally liable for such penalties. There is no immunity for Cayman ELP/members under Gibraltar law.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current-account and capital-account convertibility. Gibraltar maintains no exchange controls and imposes no prior approval requirement for the remittance of dividends, partnership distributions, capital repayments, or trade proceeds. The GIP circulates at par with GBP, is freely interchangeable with GBP at Gibraltar banks and is treated as a Sterling-zone currency. Non-resident partners of an ELP may repatriate profits without GFSC consent or BoE exchange-control filing. The only practical hurdles are commercial: correspondent banks and payment service providers may apply enhanced due diligence and sanctions screening to outbound flows destined for high-risk jurisdictions such as Bangladesh, Vietnam, or China. Klarna BNPL and Shopify Payments settlements arrive in GBP via UK acquiring banks and are freely sweepable to the ELP's Gibraltar operating account.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. The UK and Gibraltar abolished statutory exchange-control and export-proceeds surrender requirements in 1979. There is no mandatory timeline for repatriation, no required conversion ratio, and no obligation to convert online retail export receipts into GIP/GBP. Cross-border e-commerce sales proceeds from UK, EU, and non-EU consumers may be retained in any currency. The only technical requirement is accurate balance-of-payments classification by the reporting bank and normal corporate record-keeping; no surrender certificate or central-bank approval is required.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the licensing and supervisory authority for banks and payment institutions; Government of Gibraltar as the note-issuing authority for the Gibraltar Pound (GIP), which is 100% Sterling-backed and pegged at par to GBP; Bank of England as the underlying Sterling anchor and operator of UK large-value and retail clearing infrastructure; HM Treasury (UK) retaining overarching AML/CTF, sanctions, and financial crime policy oversight applicable to all Gibraltar-regulated firms.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: HIGH for a low-risk, KYC-transparent fast-fashion e-commerce ELP. UK-authorised E-Money Institutions such as Wise Ltd, Revolut Ltd, and Airwallex (UK) Ltd may onboard the Gibraltar ELP through the UK–Gibraltar financial services passporting bridge established under the Financial Services Act 2021, provided the General Partner is a GFSC-licensed corporate services provider and the limited-partner UBOs are individually verified. Legal constraints: (i) US-domiciled and certain Asia-Pacific digital banks commonly classify Gibraltar-licensed partnerships as restricted offshore structures, requiring Interposed UK or EU holding vehicles; (ii) GFSC-authorised Gibraltar payment institutions and EMIs can issue GBP IBANs but may require a UK agent bank for full Faster Payments membership; (iii) Klarna BNPL and Shopify Payments settle through UK acquiring banks and PSPs, so the ELP must maintain at least one GBP settlement account in the UK clearing zone to avoid payout rejection. The overall recommendation is a dual-rail structure: a Gibraltar bank account as treasury anchor plus a UK EMI account for PSP settlement, both subject to identical UBO disclosure.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing: subscription wires from non-resident LPs or parent entities must be sent via SWIFT MT103 to the Gibraltar bank's London correspondent using the ELP's GBP IBAN. Remittance Information (Field 72) must state 'ELP capital contribution — LP reference [name/ID]' and the ISO 20022 purpose code 'EQUI' (Equity capital) or 'TRAD' for trade-related working capital top-ups. For sterling from UK sources, use CHAPS for amounts above GBP 1 million or Faster Payments below that threshold; for USD use SWIFT MT103 over Fedwire with a London CHAPS conversion step. The receiving Gibraltar bank will classify the credit under the balance-of-payments 'Direct Investment — Equity Capital' category for statistical reporting. Mandatory Return Breakdown

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GBP 1,000,000 aggregate related-party transaction value triggers a mandatory Local File; a Master File is required where the wider multinational group has consolidated turnover above GBP 5,000,000.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations apply to Gibraltar tax-resident entities and permanent establishments conducting 'distribution and service centre' activity, which includes online fast-fashion apparel retail. The ELP must demonstrate adequate substance in Gibraltar through local premises, full-time local employees, board/partner meetings in Gibraltar, and proportionate expenditure. A non-resident ELP relying on pass-through treatment must file a substance notification and prove it is neither tax resident nor conducting a relevant activity in Gibraltar.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Deliberate Error

Statutory sanction: Criminal prosecution under the Income Tax Act; penalties of up to GBP 5,000 plus daily penalties, and imprisonment for up to 7 years; tax-geared civil penalties of up to 100% of omitted tax and disgorgement of any tax advantage.

Director personal liability: Directors and the general partner can be made personally liable for civil restitution, subjected to travel bans, disqualified as directors, and imprisoned where the evasion was committed with their consent or connivance.
Shell Company / Sham Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Penalties for ESR non-compliance: GBP 2,000 initial penalty, GBP 20,000 for continued failure; potential loss of tax-residence/exemption status, strike-off of the ELP, and joint liability for unpaid tax.

Director personal liability: The general partner and underlying directors who sign false substance declarations risk personal fines, disqualification, and criminal liability for making false statements to the Registrar.
Transfer Pricing / Thin Capitalisation Abuse

Statutory sanction: The GRA may adjust taxable profits at the 10% corporate rate, impose interest and late-payment surcharges, and levy penalties up to 100% of the additional tax assessed.

Director personal liability: The general partner and directors may be personally liable for reckless or fraudulent transfer-pricing misstatements; indemnity claims between partners may fail where the corporate veil is pierced for deliberate tax distortion.
AML / Proceeds of Crime

Statutory sanction: Under the Criminal Finances Act and Proceeds of Crime Act: unlimited fines, asset freezing and confiscation orders, and custodial sentences of up to 14 years for money laundering; regulators may also issue monetary penalties of up to GBP 1,000,000.

Director personal liability: Beneficial owners, general partners, and authorised signatories face civil confiscation, international travel bans, extradition, and personal criminal liability; the corporate/partnership veil offers no protection in cases of fraud.
