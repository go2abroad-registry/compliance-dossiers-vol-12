Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum capital requirement exists for an Exempted Limited Partnership; capital contributions are purely contractual under the Partnership Deed. The Partnership must at all times maintain at least one General Partner (typically a Gibraltar-incorporated or licensed company) and one Limited Partner; compliance is evidenced at registration and on any constitutional change.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax), Ministry of Finance, 6 Casino Point, Gibraltar; GCT administration is exercised by the GCT Unit within the same office, with import controls under HM Customs Gibraltar. Taxpayer portal and statutory guidance: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Register the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) under Gibraltar's Limited Partnerships Act 2010 with the Registrar of Companies; the Registrar issues the statutory registration number that serves as the corporate identifier. Step 2: Deliver the statutory registration statement and, where applicable, the exempted-partner declaration confirming all partners are non-resident, so the ELP is treated as non-resident for income tax purposes. Step 3: Register as a taxpayer with the Income Tax Office via the online taxpayer portal to obtain an income-tax file reference; a non-resident ELP with no Gibraltar-source income files returns only on demand. Step 4: Register as an employer for Tax at Source (PAYE) before paying wages to locally based employees or contractors on the EOR platform. Step 5: Register for GCT once actual or projected annual taxable turnover reaches the statutory threshold, or immediately if supplying taxable services into Gibraltar on an ongoing basis; the GCT Unit issues a GCT registration number. Step 6: If an opacity election is sought, file the prescribed election with the Commissioner before the first partnership return is due.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Partnerships are fiscally transparent by default under the Income Tax Act 2010; the ELP may make a statutory opacity election, subject to the Commissioner's consent, to be assessed as if it were a company, centralising tax at entity level. A transparent ELP allocates profits to partners for direct assessment (individual partners at progressive rates up to 28% effective from 1 July 2021; corporate partners at the flat 12.5% company rate). Exempted ELPs with all non-resident partners and no Gibraltar-source income enjoy automatic extraterritorial non-assessment without further election. Gibraltar is not a check-the-box jurisdiction; OECD Hybrid Mismatch Rules (Income Tax (Amendment) Regulations) apply to financial instrument and entity mismatches, require return disclosures, and afford access to private advance rulings from the Commissioner.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GBP 100,000 annual taxable turnover for resident and non-established suppliers of goods and services (GCT Act 2024), monitored on a rolling 12-month basis. A Global EOR agency recharging payroll administration and contractor payment fees to Gibraltar-based clients must register once the threshold is met or exceeded; non-established suppliers supplying taxable services into Gibraltar continuously are required to register on the first taxable supply and may also elect voluntary registration before the threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A supply qualifies for the 0% GCT export rate where the taxable person demonstrates that (a) goods are physically exported outside Gibraltar within 3 months of supply, or (b) services, including EOR employment administration and cross-border contractor payroll facilitation, are supplied to a recipient belonging outside Gibraltar and the effective benefit and use and enjoyment occur outside Gibraltar. Zero-rating also extends to international transport, ancillary transport services and supplies to qualifying international bodies. The burden of proof rests on the taxpayer; unless substantiated at the time of supply, the supply is re-characterised as standard-rated and charged at 10%.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic SAD filed via the Gibraltar Customs single window) bearing the export movement reference
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / CMR consignment note naming the Gibraltar consignor and final foreign destination
  • Commercial invoice and signed Global EOR / contractor services agreement evidencing the cross-border supply and payment terms
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT/MT103 evidencing receipt of foreign-currency consideration from the overseas client
  • Foreign-currency receivables ledger and proof of actual repatriation of funds to the ELP's Gibraltar bank account within 180 days

Gibraltar does not impose real-time fiscal clearance, API-connected cash-register stamping or continuous transaction controls (CTC) e-invoicing. GCT invoices must show the GCT registration number, invoice date, description of supply, value exclusive of GCT, applicable rate and GCT charge. GCT returns and Tax at Source (PAYE) statements are filed digitally through the Income Tax Office taxpayer portal; quarterly GCT returns and monthly/quarterly TAS employer remittances are mandated. Taxable services imported into Gibraltar are subject to reverse-charge accounting, and imported goods are cleared electronically through the Gibraltar Customs single window, with GCT payable at the border unless zero-rated or exempted.

The Commissioner and the GCT Unit conduct exporter-focused audits covering removal evidence, client-side 'use and enjoyment' records and foreign-exchange repatriation. Failure to substantiate zero-rating triggers retrospective clawback of the tax differential by reclassifying the supply as standard-rated at 10%, with interest running from the original due date. Civil default penalties range from 30% to 100% of understated tax, with 100% reserved for deliberate wrongdoing; criminal liability for fraudulent zero-rating claims carries a fine of up to GBP 250,000 and/or imprisonment of up to 7 years under the GCT Act 2024. Unfounded refund claims may be rejected, the exporter may be deregistered, and unpaid GCT constitutes a preferential debt in insolvency with potential joint and several liability for the general partner.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar operates a fully liberalised capital- and current-account convertibility regime. There are no exchange controls, no central-bank prior approval for remittances, and no profit-repatriation withholding imposed on the non-resident Exempted Limited Partnership. The ELP may invoice Global EOR fees in GBP, EUR, or USD, hold proceeds in unrestricted multi-currency accounts at GFSC-licensed banks, and remit cash-flow surpluses or capital distributions to foreign partners free of Gibraltar Income Tax. The only operative hurdles are GFSC-mandated source-of-funds verification, FATF-compliant embedded AML controls, and the practical requirement to clear GIP/GBP liquidity through London correspondent banks.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Under the repealed UK Exchange Control framework (abolished 1979), no export-proceeds surrender timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no retention obligation apply to the ELP. Cross-border service receipts comprising EOR placement fees, contractor payroll advances, and staffing-management royalties may remain indefinitely in the foreign currency of collection; voluntary conversion into GIP/GBP is executed at interbank mid-market rates through the GFSC clearing bank without regulatory filing.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (supreme monetary authority for sterling and the Gibraltar pound at fixed 1:1 parity); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the statutory prudential and AML/CFT banking regulator; and the Gibraltar Currency Commissioner, operating under H.M. Government of Gibraltar, as the sole issuer of GIP banknotes and coin at legal parity with GBP.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility: MEDIUM as an auxiliary disbursement rail, LOW as a primary treasury bank. Legal constraints: following Brexit, FCA-regulated UK electronic money institutions such as Wise Plc no longer possess passporting rights into Gibraltar and cannot hold GIP balances or onboard Gibraltar-resident counterparties without a GFSC e-money or payment-services licence under the Financial Services Act 2019. The ELP may nevertheless appoint Wise as a segregated contractual 'payroll engine' for contractor payouts in 40+ jurisdictions, provided: (i) the master treasury account remains at a GFSC-licensed bank; (ii) all funds are returned to the master account and disbursed under MT103 'SALA' instructions; (iii) the EMI executes all underlying settlements outside Gibraltar; and (iv) no GIP-denominated float is created outside the GFSC banking perimeter.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound route: originating bank executes SWIFT MT103 (or CHAPS for faster UK same-day value) to the GFSC clearing bank through the London GBP correspondent, quoting the ELP account number and remittance reference 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION — KY_ELP'. The ISO 20022 category purpose code must identify a treasury/equity transfer ('TREA' or 'EQUI'); trade, unclassified, or salary codes are rejected. SWIFT gpi tracking is required. On receipt, the GFSC bank verifies the signed capital schedule, the general partner resolution, FATF-compliant remitter due diligence, and the ELP's partnership agreement before crediting the Capital Escrow Account. 0% — Gibraltar does not levy dividend withholding tax on distributions by a Gibraltarian company or ELP; partnership allocations are profit shares and, in the absence of a taxable permanent establishment, no Gibraltar withholding is imposed.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 9 months and 1 day after the end of the ELP's accounting period, unless the Commissioner of Income Tax specifies an earlier date on the filing notice; calendar-year ELPs are administratively expected by 1 October of the following year.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 9 months and 1 day after the end of the chargeable accounting period; interim or provisional payments may be required quarterly for large taxpayers.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 12 months of the end of the relevant financial period, or by the date specified in the ESR compliance notice issued by the Registrar.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No separately gazetted domestic Local File monetary threshold in Gibraltar. For multinational groups, mandatory Country-by-Country Reporting applies where consolidated group revenue exceeds EUR 750,000,000 (approximately GIP 645,000,000). In practice, any Gibraltar EOR entity should prepare a Local File where connected-party payroll, management, licence or service transactions exceed GIP 1,000,000 in a year, and a Master File should accompany group transactions above the CbCR threshold.
