Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office and Customs & Excise Service; official tax portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation

E-government filing portal

Foreign-owned Gibraltarian Exempted Limited Partnerships are required to register at the Gibraltar Companies House and obtain a partnership tax reference (GIP TIN) from the Income Tax Office within 30 days of incorporation. Submit the filed partnership statement, registered-office certificate, partner registration schedule, and a non-residence declaration through a licensed tax representative or Gibraltar tax solicitor. For customs purposes, register with HM Customs as a Customs Trader, obtain a Customs Trader Identifier (CTID) and, if warehousing high-proof alcohol for export, apply for a Duty Suspension Warehouse Keeper approval. VAT ID is not applicable because Gibraltar does not operate a VAT/GST system.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Gibraltar ELPs are statutorily fiscally transparent; there is no check-the-box election. The Commissioner for Income Tax may issue a unilateral clearance on the application of Gibraltar's source rules to foreign-resident partners. Partners may elect between statutory accrual and actual-receipt apportionment for overseas income only with prior approval. Hybrid mismatch/anti-hybrid rules under the Income Tax (Tax Avoidance) Act apply, ensuring the ELP's pass-through treatment is respected for BEPS purposes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST; Gibraltar Customs and Excise Duty plus Consumer Tax regime under the Customs Ordinance

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GIP 0 – no VAT threshold exists; all commercial importers/exporters must hold a Customs Trader Identifier (CTID); income tax registration is required for a partnership carrying on trade through a permanent establishment in Gibraltar.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar does not levy VAT/GST on supplies, so zero-rating as understood in VAT jurisdictions does not apply. For consumer tax/duty purposes, an export of baijiu physically leaving Gibraltar to a third-country destination is outside the charge to import duty/tax. Eligibility requires the ELP to hold the goods in an approved duty-suspension or free-zone arrangement prior to shipment, and to provide proof of export to a customs officer within the prescribed period. Repayment of any consumer tax borne on importation is available where re-export occurs within 12 months and documentary evidence satisfies Regulation 12 of the Customs Ordinance (Exports).

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration (SAD/Exit Summary Declaration)
  • Commercial invoice with Incoterms 2020, HS 2208.90, and baijiu proof specification
  • Bill of Lading or Master Air Waybill evidencing export to the named destination
  • Packing list and cargo manifest
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Gibraltar banking institution) or SWIFT MT103 / Letter of Credit advice evidencing full and timely settlement
  • Excise certificate or T-19/T-2 movement document (if applicable) proving duty status

No real-time e-invoicing or fiscal stamping is mandated in Gibraltar for export transactions. Customs declarations are transmitted electronically via the Gibraltar Customs Single Administrative Document (SAD) interface; alcohol movements in duty suspension within EU/UK networks use the Electronic Excise Movement and Control System (EMCS). Shipments to third countries rely on exit-summary declarations and border export clearance, with no continuous invoice clearance obligation.

HM Customs and the Income Tax Office may conduct post-export audits for up to 6 years. Fraudulent export declarations, misdescription of spirit strength, or abuse of the duty-suspension regime attract penalties up to the greater of GIP 1,000,000 or 200% of the duty/tax savings, plus interest. The relevant export relief may be clawed back retroactively, and partners are jointly and severally liable. Criminal sanctions include seizure and forfeiture of the goods, director disqualification, and prosecution under the Criminal Financial Partnership Act and Forgery Act.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar imposes no exchange control statute and no prior-approval regime on either current-account or capital-account transactions. Trade settlements, profit remittances, capital injections, loan repayments, and partner distributions are freely convertible into GBP, USD, EUR, or any other deliverable currency. The only practical restriction is that GIP banknotes and balances are non-deliverable outside Gibraltar and must be settled through a UK clearable route; therefore treasury accounts should be held in GBP, USD, and EUR. The KY_ELP, being a tax-transparent Exempted Limited Partnership under the Limited Partnerships Act 2013 and the Limited Partnerships (Amendment) Act 2021, may remit profits to non-resident partners without Gibraltar withholding tax. The sole remittance hurdle is the receiving bank's standard enhanced due diligence requirement to verify that the distribution corresponds to audited or agreed partnership reserves and that no undisclosed third-party beneficiary exists.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Gibraltar law imposes no mandatory surrender period, no conversion ratio, and no notification requirement for export receipts. The wholesale Baijiu exporter may retain 100% of export proceeds in foreign currency indefinitely without GFSC or customs intervention. For financial reporting, non-GBP balances are translated to the partnership's functional currency (GBP or USD as elected) using the Bank of England closing rate or the annual average rate consistently applied. The exporter's only statutory treasury obligations are retention of underlying shipping, excise, and FX records for a minimum of five years under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 (Gibraltar) and availability of those records to the GFSC AML/CFT supervisor on request.

Central bank supervision: HM Government of Gibraltar - Commissioner of Currency (sole issuer of the Gibraltar Pound, GIP, at a 1:1 peg with GBP) and the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the integrated regulator of banks, electronic money institutions, and financial services. There is no separate Gibraltar central bank; monetary policy is effectively externalised to the Bank of England due to the fixed GIP/GBP parity. Banks licensed in Gibraltar are subject to the Financial Services Act 2019 (Gibraltar), Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 (Gibraltar), and GFSC AML/CFT Rulebooks implementing UK-derived and FATF standards.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM for collections and LOW for trade-definitive clearing. Post-Brexit, UK or EEA electronic money institution (EMI) licences do not automatically passport into Gibraltar; only GFSC-licensed EMIs under the Financial Services (Electronic Money) Act 2019 may issue e-money in or from Gibraltar. Leading digital EMIs (Wise, Revolut Business, Airwallex, Currenxie) generally restrict or heavily policy-guard alcoholic-beverage wholesale and cannot issue commercial letters of credit or documentary collection instruments. Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Deliberate Understatement

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of up to 100% of the tax evaded, plus daily interest; criminal prosecution under Gibraltar's Income Tax Act and Criminal Code may result in fines and imprisonment for up to 7 years.

Director personal liability: General partners and directors of the corporate general partner face joint and several liability for civil penalties. Gibraltar courts may impose director disqualification for up to 15 years, freeze personal assets, issue travel bans, and order personal confiscation where the veil of the ELP is pierced.
Shell Company and Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Registration strike-off, withdrawal of Gibraltar tax residence, administrative penalty up to GBP 100,000, and daily penalties for continuing non-compliance; tax authorities may recharacterise profits as taxable in Gibraltar or in the jurisdiction of actual management.

AML, Beneficial Ownership Concealment and Proceeds of Crime

Statutory sanction: Confiscation orders under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015, administrative penalties of up to GBP 500,000, and imprisonment of up to 14 years for money laundering offences.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced for deliberate money laundering. Directors face personal criminal liability, bank account freezes, travel bans, extradition risk, and personal confiscation of assets derived from the offence.
Misreporting Cross-Border Payment Rails and Foreign Accounts
