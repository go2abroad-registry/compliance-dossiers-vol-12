E-government filing portal

Foreign entities (Cayman ELP) obtain a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) by filing with the Income Tax Office: registration as a foreign/overseas partnership with the Registrar of Companies, certified Cayman formation documents, partnership deed/LPA, particulars of all partners, Gibraltar service address, and declaration of a Gibraltar representative. No minimum capital. Processing typically 5-10 business days; TRN is prerequisite for customs trader registration, banking, and payroll compliance.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists. Under the Income Tax Act, a foreign ELP is statutorily transparent: Gibraltar-source profits from suture needle assembly flow through to partners and are taxed at partner level (corporate partners 12.5%; individuals progressive up to 42%). A partnership may elect single-assessment-unit filing. Hybrid mismatch: Gibraltar has not adopted full EU ATAD; BEPS-aligned substance rules limit dual non-taxation. Qualifying Company 2% election is unavailable to medical manufacturing unless separately licensed.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax in Gibraltar; indirect taxation limited to Import Duty and Excise Duty (outside EU VAT area).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - Gibraltar levies no VAT; therefore no turnover registration threshold exists. Importers/exporters register as Customs Traders (no minimum turnover) and account for import/excise duty per customs entry.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - zero-rating is a VAT concept and Gibraltar imposes no VAT. Surgical sutures manufactured in Gibraltar and exported are outside the charge to Gibraltar import duty; medical sutures (HS 3006.10) are generally duty-free. Duty drawback and inward-processing relief apply where duty-paid inputs are subsequently exported.

  • ASYCUDA World electronic Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical export from Gibraltar
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT receipt evidencing collection of export sale proceeds

All imports and exports are filed electronically through ASYCUDA World single-window clearance; no fiscal e-invoicing or real-time digital stamping mandate applies domestically because no VAT is levied. Mandatory electronic record retention for 6 years under the Customs Ordinance.

Gibraltar imposes no exchange controls, but where customs duty drawback or relief was granted or contractual export-facility conditions require repatriation, the 180-day benchmark applies. HM Customs Gibraltar may audit export evidence for up to 6 years. False declarations attract civil penalties up to the full duty evaded (Customs Ordinance s.99), forfeiture of goods and conveyances, criminal prosecution (unlimited fine and/or imprisonment), retrospective clawback of duty relief plus 8% per annum penalty interest; the ELP general partner and Gibraltar representative are jointly and severally liable.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar has no operative exchange-control restrictions; the historical UK-era exchange-control regime has been repealed and is not administered by the GFSC. Current-account transactions (trade in goods and services, royalties, management fees, insurance) and capital-account transactions (capital contributions, partner loan advances, distributions, liquidation surplus) are fully convertible and freely transferable. Profit remittances and capital repatriation by non-resident limited partners require no foreign-exchange authority approval and incur no Gibraltar withholding tax on partnership distributions. GIP is pegged at par to GBP, so GBP is the functional treasury base; USD and EUR accounts may be held without mandatory conversion. Residual practical hurdle: all external transfers must flow through a GFSC-licensed credit institution or its EEA/UK correspondent so that the bank can complete transaction monitoring; there is no quantitative ceiling on foreign-currency holdings and no approval requirement for inbound or outbound wires.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Gibraltar imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender requirement, no minimum conversion ratio, no repatriation deadline and no requirement to sell export receipts into local currency. A GBP exporter of absorbable PGA braided sutures and surgical needles may hold 100% of USD and EUR receivables in multi-currency bank accounts indefinitely, use them for direct supplier settlements, or convert them at market rates. The absence of surrender obligations mirrors UK policy under the exchange-control repeal and supports a natural-hedge treasury policy whereby USD collections fund USD raw-material imports and EUR collections fund EU logistics.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (ultimate monetary authority; the Gibraltar pound is pegged at par 1:1 with GBP and circulates interchangeably with GBP in Gibraltar); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) (statutory financial regulator, AML/CFT supervisor and banking licensing authority under the Financial Services Act 2019); no separate Gibraltar central bank exists; the Government of Gibraltar issues GIP banknotes under the Currency Notes Act, and exchange-rate policy is effectively aligned with the Bank of England through the fixed peg.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: 2.5/5 (Low-Medium) as a primary treasury solution; 4/5 (High) as a supplementary FX-optimisation wallet. Wise Business, Revolut Business and Airwallex will generally review a Gibraltar ELP under enhanced due diligence, and Gibraltar is treated as an offshore high-risk territory by many UK EMIs, so account approval is discretionary. EMIs cannot issue documentary letters of credit, do not offer CHAPS real-time settlement finality and are not licensed by the GFSC unless separately registered; they should therefore carry only working-float balances below GBP 50,000 while the GFSC-licensed bank remains the sole custodian of escrowed capital and trade proceeds. Legal constraint: post-Brexit UK EMIs do not automatically passport payment services into Gibraltar, so cross-border EMI provision relies on reverse-solicitation or a GFSC-recognised arrangement; client-fund segregation and any deposit guarantee must be verified in the EMI terms before use.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Gibraltar law applies the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines as a direct standard; there is no statutory Local File/Master File monetary threshold, but the ITO requires contemporaneous comparability documentation for any controlled transaction of material value. For this manufacturing ELP, aggregate controlled turnover of GBP 1,000,000 per annum (intercompany purchases of PGA polymer inputs, toll-assembly services, and IP royalties) is treated as a threshold for enhanced TP review in ITO practice.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar applies Economic Substance Requirements aligned with the OECD Inclusive Framework and the EU Code of Conduct standard. An ELP carrying on surgical suture assembly must demonstrate physical premises, qualified production staff, core income-generating activities, and genuine board/partner management in Gibraltar proportionate to the business. A Cayman-managed shell with no Gibraltarian substance will be recharacterized as tax resident in Gibraltar and assessed on its worldwide trade profits, with penalties for non-declaration.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate Income Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Tax-geared penalty up to 100% of the understated tax under the Income Tax Act 2010 (ss.146-152); statutory interest; criminal prosecution on indictment with fines and imprisonment for serious tax fraud; confiscation and asset-recovery orders under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 (Gibraltar).

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners who knowingly participate are jointly and severally liable for the ELP's unpaid tax; the corporate veil is pierced on evidence of fraud or sham; personal financial restriction orders, travel bans, passport surrender, and imprisonment may be imposed; director disqualification for up to 15 years.
Shell Company / Sham Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Recharacterization of the ELP as fully taxable in Gibraltar with the entire manufacturing profit assessed at 10%; transfer-pricing adjustments with penalties up to 100% of the additional tax; strike-off from the Gibraltar Limited Partnerships Register; freezing of the ELP's Gibraltarian bank accounts by court order.

Director personal liability: General partner and its nominated directors face personal civil restitution for the tax shortfall; liability for partnership debts on insolvency under fraudulent trading rules; prohibition orders against acting as directors in Gibraltar; asset freezes and restrictions on leaving the jurisdiction.
AML / Payment-Rails Abuse

Statutory sanction: Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 (Gibraltar): imprisonment up to 14 years and/or unlimited fines for money laundering; civil recovery of criminal proceeds; regulatory suspension or revocation of the ELP's banking and EMI facilities; referral to the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission for license revocation.

Director personal liability: Directors and the designated Money Laundering Reporting Officer face personal criminal liability for failing to report suspicious transactions; international travel bans and free-standing freezing orders may be issued; permanent bar from holding regulated positions across Gibraltar and the United Kingdom.
Beneficial Ownership and Transparency Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Criminal fines and imprisonment for officers under the Gibraltar Companies Act 2014 and the Beneficial Ownership Regulations; administrative dissolution of the ELP; escalation of the entity's AML risk tier to High Risk, resulting in mandatory bank account closure and enhanced supervisory scrutiny; invocation of sanctions where a hidden UBO appears on a global sanctions list.

Director personal liability: Every general partner and responsible officer is jointly and severally exposed to personal fines and imprisonment; directors may be disqualified and travel-restricted; assets in Gibraltar and through correspondent UK banking channels may be seized pursuant to mutual legal assistance requests.
