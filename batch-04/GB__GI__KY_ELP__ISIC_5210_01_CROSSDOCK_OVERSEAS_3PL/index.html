Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital. The ELP may be registered with zero nominal capital; each limited partner must agree to contribute capital as stated in the Statement of Particulars, but no deposit, escrow, or capital verification is required for a non-regulated (non-financial services) partnership. The general partner must maintain a registered office in Gibraltar.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Clearance Certificate from the Gibraltar Fire and Rescue Service after inspection of means of escape, emergency lighting, fire alarm, and suppression systems; Building and Structural Load Clearance from the Building Control Office certifying racking installation, mezzanine floor loading, and seismic/static adequacy; Occupational Safety Clearance under the Health and Safety at Work Act (Cap. 158) covering electrical installation test certificate, fork-lift truck inspection and COSHH documentation, plus a workplace first-aid and employers' liability insurance certificate.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (Commissioner of Income Tax); partnership recognition and file maintenance via the Gibraltar Companies House Registry (Registrar of Companies). Official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax; registry portal (Companies House Gibraltar). UK HMRC is competent only if UK VAT/customs nexus arises (e.g., UK warehouse operations).

E-government filing portal

For a Cayman Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) operating through Gibraltar: (1) appoint a Gibraltar-resident general partner or licensed local agent and establish a registered office with a corporate services provider; (2) register the foreign ELP with the Registrar of Companies under the Limited Partnerships Act 2010 (as amended), filing the partnership deed, register of partners, and registered office consent; (3) apply to the Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) for the partnership within 30 days of registration; non-resident partners file separately only if Gibraltar-source income is derived; (4) maintain annual confirmation statement, economic substance records, and (if applicable) audited accounts; (5) register for Gibraltar VAT if taxable supplies in Gibraltar exceed the statutory threshold, noting the non-established ELP has no threshold and must register from its first local taxable supply; (6) obtain a Gibraltar customs identifier, and obtain a UK GB EORI if goods are exported from the UK warehouse via UK CDS.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists in Gibraltar or the UK; classification follows statutory transparency. Available elections/mitigations: (1) hybrid-mismatch neutralizing elections under UK TIOPA 2010 Part 6A and Gibraltar anti-hybrid rules (dual-inclusion income and set-off elections); (2) election to align entity treatment under UK CTA 2010 s 1154 for dual-resident/hybrid structures; (3) election to disapply Gibraltar 12.5% corporate rate for the partnership's non-Gibraltar active income (partnership is not a company); (4) if a corporate subsidiary is added, election for Qualifying Company status (Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010), capping annual tax at GBP 22,000–44,000 on non-Gibraltar active income; annual hybrid-mismatch audit trail documentation is required.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Gibraltar: GBP 150,000 annual taxable turnover for established Gibraltar businesses (small-undertaking exemption); for the non-established (foreign) ELP the threshold is GBP 0 – compulsory registration from the first taxable supply of goods or services in Gibraltar. UK (HMRC): GBP 0 for non-established businesses; GBP 90,000 for UK-established businesses. Import VAT is chargeable on goods entering Gibraltar irrespective of supplier turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: 0% Gibraltar VAT applies on export of goods from Gibraltar to a destination outside Gibraltar, provided title transfers to a non-Gibraltar customer and physical export occurs within 3 months of supply; the ELP, as cross-dock operator/exporter of record, must hold the full export evidence pack. Offshore services (cross-dock handling, warehousing, and order fulfillment for non-Gibraltar customers): B2B place-of-supply is the customer's country, placing the supply outside the scope of Gibraltar VAT (economic equivalent of 0%); services physically performed outside Gibraltar (e.g., UK warehouse) are outside Gibraltar's territorial charge. UK zero-rating: export of goods from the UK to overseas customers is 0% under VATA 1994 Sch 8; UK warehousing/storage services supplied to overseas B2B customers follow the land-location rule, but where the recipient belongs outside the UK the supply is outside the scope of UK VAT (reverse charge applies to the customer where applicable). A valid GB EORI and export customs entry (CDS) are prerequisites for UK zero-rating.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic CDS/Gibraltar Customs export entry containing exporter EORI/TIN and customs procedure code)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (evidencing physical consignment to the overseas destination)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank or IMF-certified proof of payment from the overseas buyer within the repatriation deadline)
  • Commercial invoice and order/proforma documentation
  • Packing list and weight/volume certificate
  • Freight engagement evidence and insurance certificates
  • Overseas consignee identification (KYC on export customers)

Gibraltar: no statutory e-invoicing, no real-time fiscal clearance, no API clearance mandate, and no digital stamping; VAT returns are filed quarterly on the Income Tax Office portal with invoice archives retained locally; import declarations are processed electronically through Gibraltar Customs. UK nexus: HMRC Making Tax Digital ('MTD') is mandatory for UK VAT – quarterly returns filed via MTD-compatible software using API-based digital links; no invoice-level real-time clearance or digital stamping applies.

Zero-rating is provisional until the complete export evidence pack is secured; missing or defective evidence triggers reassessment of VAT at 15% (Gibraltar) or 20% (UK) plus statutory interest from the date of supply. Civil penalties: up to 100% of VAT for deliberate inaccuracies (UK FA 2007 Sch 24; Gibraltar parallel penalties plus surcharges). Criminal exposure: fraudulent evasion of VAT through false export documents or sham offshore receivables is indictable with fines and imprisonment (UK VATA 1994 s 72; Gibraltar Criminal Offences Ordinance). Retrospective clawback up to 4 years (non-deliberate) or 20 years (deliberate or offshore concealment); the 180-day FX repatriation deadline is strictly tested via the Inward Remittance Certificate in refund and zero-rating audits; partners may incur personal liability in sham cross-dock arrangements.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full and free convertibility for both current and capital account transactions. Sterling (GBP) is legal tender, and Gibraltar operates under the UK monetary and exchange-control framework. Profit remittances, distributions, and redemption proceeds may be freely transferred without exchange-control approval. Practical hurdles only arise from AML/KYC documentation, Gibraltar Companies Act beneficial ownership filings, and any applicable Economic Substance reporting requirements.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender requirement or mandatory conversion ratio applies to export receipts. A Gibraltar-resident entity may hold export proceeds in GBP or any freely convertible currency in offshore or onshore bank accounts. For the purposes of Gibraltar's Economic Substance Regulations, the entity must evidence that core income-generating activities are managed and performed in Gibraltar, and all export proceeds must be properly recorded in audited financial statements.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (central monetary authority for GBP) acting through HM Treasury; Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the banking and AML/CFT supervisor for the territory. Gibraltar has no separate central bank or foreign exchange administration.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium for operational payments and low for statutory capital escrow. A Gibraltar-licensed regulated bank must hold the ELP's capital escrow and main clearing accounts. Offshore EMI / fintech platforms (e.g., Airwallex, Wise, Revolut Business) may be used as supplementary treasury rails for FX conversion and low-value disbursements, but they are not a substitute for a full commercial bank account. Any EMI used must be authorized under a recognized regime (FCA in the UK, GFSC in Gibraltar, or equivalent EEA passport), must not be subject to sanctions, and must provide audit trail and transaction reporting capable of satisfying GFSC compliance expectations.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital contributions must be routed by bank wire (SWIFT MT103) from the partners' own bank accounts directly to the ELP's Gibraltar-licensed bank account. The wire must identify the full legal name of the ELP, its registration number, IBAN and BIC, and use an ISO 20022 remittance information field stating 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION – [ELP NAME]'. The bank may require a capital call notice, executed partnership deed, source-of-wealth evidence, and a copy of the GFSC or registered-agent filing confirming ultimate beneficial owners. Any USD or EUR contribution must be converted to GBP through the onshore Gibraltar receiving bank, creating an auditable FX ticket. Contributions exceeding GBP 50,000 are subject to enhanced due diligence and may be rejected if the remitter is not a named partner.Outbound trade settlements for warehouse fulfillment and third-party logistics costs use SWIFT MT103 for supplier payments in GBP, USD, and EUR. For high-value or import-heavy trade flows, the Gibraltar bank may open an irrevocable Commercial Letter of Credit under UCP 600 using SWIFT MT700/MT720/MT707 messages. Purpose codes must be accurate (e.g., 'TRADE RELATED' for inventory and logistics expenses, 'SERVICES' for 3PL fees). For smaller GBP-denominated payments to UK or Gibraltar counterparties, the bank may provide local ACH or UK Faster Payments access through a correspondent clearing arrangement, but each payment must still be traceable and retained in compliance records. 0% — Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by Gibraltar companies, nor on partnership distributions made by an ELP to its partners. Repatriation of profits via partner withdrawals, distributions, or capital returns is free of source-state withholding tax.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The return and self-assessment payment are due by 30 November of the year following the year of assessment (YA) in which the accounting period ends. Example: accounting period ending 31 December 2025 (YA 2025) must be filed and settled by 30 November 2026.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory de minimis applies: the arm's length principle governs all controlled transactions. Contemporaneous Local File / Master File documentation is required for any material cross-border controlled transaction; the practical review threshold applied by GTCA is aggregate intercompany charges exceeding GBP 1,000,000 per chargeable period. Country-by-Country Reporting applies to MNE groups with consolidated group revenue of EUR 750,000,000 or more.

Economic substance scope: The Income Tax (Economic Substance) Regulations 2019 apply. A cross-dock overseas 3PL and order fulfilment operation is classified as 'distribution and service centre business', a relevant activity. A tax-resident ELP must: (a) be directed and managed in Gibraltar (board meetings of the corporate GP held in Gibraltar and minuted in Gibraltar); (b) maintain adequate physical premises in Gibraltar; (c) employ adequate full-time, locally resident staff; and (d) incur adequate operating expenditure in Gibraltar. Activities outsourced to non-resident affiliates or performed via foreign warehouses do not satisfy the test. Non-resident ELPs must file an annual ESR return evidencing non-residence; failure to file results in deemed tax residence, an initial penalty of GBP 10,000, daily default fines, and potential strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Tax Returns

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of up to 100% of the tax undercharged plus statutory interest; criminal prosecution on indictment with an unlimited fine; imprisonment of up to seven years; confiscation orders under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015; costs and restitution orders.

Director personal liability: Directors of the corporate general partner face personal civil restitution for breach of fiduciary duty, disqualification under the Company Directors Disqualification Act, travel prohibition orders, and imprisonment for facilitating tax evasion.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Breach

Statutory sanction: Deemed tax residence with reassessment of Gibraltar-source profits at 12.5% plus back-dated interest; ESR penalties of GBP 10,000 for first failure, escalating to GBP 50,000–GBP 100,000 with daily default fines for repeated failure; administrative dissolution or striking-off of the ELP; refusal of registration of a new foreign ELP for the corporate GP.

Director personal liability: Directors of the corporate GP are exposed to disqualification, personal civil liability for tax shortfalls recovered from the partnership, asset freezes, and travel restrictions while the entity is under GTCA investigation.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Arm's length adjustment of chargeable profits; additional tax at 12.5% with interest; tax-geared penalties up to 100% of the additional tax for careless or deliberate conduct; failure of the ESR test where declared profits are inconsistent with local operating expenditure; referral to the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit for suspected fraud.

Director personal liability: Civil liability for the adjustment and penalties; aggravating factor in director disqualification proceedings; criminal prosecution for cheating the public revenue where the manipulation is deliberate.
AML / Payment-Rail Abuse (Proceeds of Crime Act 2015)

Statutory sanction: Criminal fines up to GBP 1,000,000; confiscation orders for criminal property; suspension or revocation of the corporate GP's licence; termination of banking relationships by the relevant EMI; separate offence of failure to disclose suspicious activity with imprisonment of up to five years.

Director personal liability: Personal criminal liability for money laundering and terrorist-financing offences with imprisonment of up to 14 years; travel bans and asset freezes; civil restitution to defrauded clients; permanent disqualification from acting as a company director in Gibraltar.
