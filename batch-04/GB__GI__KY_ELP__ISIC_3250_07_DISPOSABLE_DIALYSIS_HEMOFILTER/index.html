Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office of HM Government of Gibraltar, administered by the Commissioner of Income Tax, 8 Europort, Gibraltar; official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation

E-government filing portal

The foreign-owned ELP must first register under the Limited Partnerships Act 2016 with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies. Upon issuance of the Limited Partnership Certificate, the partnership appoints a Gibraltar-resident tax agent and files the Partnership Taxpayer Registration Form (IT-REG/LP) with the Income Tax Office, attaching the deed of partnership, certificate of registration, register of partners, registered-office consent letter, and the tax agent appointment letter. The Income Tax Office then issues a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN) and any applicable partnership reference code; a separate Customs registration (Gibraltar Customs ID / UK EORI-equivalent) must be obtained from HM Customs Gibraltar before import of production machinery or export of dialysis filters. Standard processing time is 5 to 15 working days.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible: the Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership is fiscally transparent under the Income Tax Act 2010, as amended; the general partner is assessable on partnership profits, and each non-resident limited partner is chargeable only on profits attributable to the partnership that accrue in or are derived from Gibraltar (territorial basis). Foreign-source manufacturing and export profits remain outside the Gibraltar tax charge provided management and control of the revenue-earning activities occur outside Gibraltar.

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no US-style IRC Section 7701 check-the-box election. Available elections: (i) territoriality election under the Income Tax Act 2010 to treat the ELP as transparent and therefore not separately assessed; (ii) unanimous partner resolution to keep central management and control outside Gibraltar, preserving non-resident status of the ELP; (iii) grandfather-style exempt certificate for qualifying offshore income, strictly limited to pre-reform entities and unavailable to newly formed ELPs; (iv) anti-hybrid elections under the Gibraltar Hybrid Mismatch Regulations, which may recharacterise the ELP as opaque where the partner jurisdiction recognises it as an entity; any election must be documented in the partnership deed and filed with the Commissioner of Income Tax.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST; Gibraltar is expressly excluded from the United Kingdom VAT area and has no sales or use tax. The applicable indirect regime consists of Gibraltar customs/excise duties (Import Duty on selected inbound goods and excise duties on alcohol, tobacco and hydrocarbons) and Vehicle Registration Duty.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable for VAT. A customs registration and duty account are required upon the first import of any dutiable goods, irrespective of turnover; specific excise licences are required for alcohol, tobacco and fuel. The manufacturing ELP will normally register as a direct importer of polyethersulfone resin and medical-device components and as a periodic exporter of finished hemofilters, with no economic threshold triggering registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Since Gibraltar charges no VAT, the concept of 0% export VAT is replaced by two exemptions: (i) the customs exemption from Gibraltar Import Duty and excise duty for goods exported out of Gibraltar; and (ii) the territorial income-tax exclusion for profits of the ELP derived from exported dialysis consumables, provided the sale is concluded with a non-resident customer, title passes outside Gibraltar, and management of the manufacturing/sale function is not located in Gibraltar. The ELP must also hold the applicable Medical Device Regulation / ISO 13485 certificates to support customs classification of the hemofilters as ISIC 3250 medical consumables.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document, export/EORI reference)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing shipment from Gibraltar/Customs port to foreign destination
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List identifying ISIC 3250 hollow-fiber dialyzers and batch numbers
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 payment confirmation evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Certificate of Origin and relevant Movement Certificate for the UK-Gibraltar single customs corridor
  • CE/UKCA marking certificate and proof of medical-device classification to substantiate HS tariff heading

Gibraltar does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or government-authorised API-based e-invoicing. Commercial e-invoices issued in PDF or UBL format are accepted. Export and import clearances are filed electronically through HM Customs Gibraltar using the Single Administrative Document / Freeport system, with optional integration to the UK Customs Declaration Service for movements across the single UK-Gibraltar customs boundary. A summary declaration, manifest, and Bill of Lading are the operative clearance documents; no fiscal backend API is required.

Fraudulent export zero-rating or artificial foreign-source declarations are treated as tax evasion under the Income Tax Act 2010, as amended, with civil penalties of up to 100% of the evaded tax plus interest. HM Customs Gibraltar may impose duty-equivalent penalties, forfeit exported goods, and levy fines of up to GIP 3,000 per false declaration. Criminal conviction for deliberately false export or tax declarations carries imprisonment of up to 12 months and unlimited fines. Retrospective clawback of any exempted export profits or customs relief is available for up to six tax years from the end of the year of assessment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is issued at par against Bank of England sterling and is freely interchangeable with GBP. Both current-account and capital-account transactions are fully convertible; there are no exchange controls, no prior-approval requirements for capital injections, and no restrictions on profit or capital repatriation for non-resident partners of an Exempted Limited Partnership. Remittances are subject only to routine tax compliance, CRS/FATCA reporting, and anti-money-laundering documentation standards.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender timeline, mandatory conversion ratio, or forced repatriation period applies in Gibraltar or the United Kingdom. Export receipts may be retained in the original transaction currency or converted freely through licensed commercial banks. The only binding obligations are accurate record-keeping under anti-money-laundering rules and proper recognition of export income for tax purposes.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (United Kingdom) as the monetary anchor for sterling; Government of Gibraltar Currency Notes Issue as issuer of Gibraltar pound banknotes; Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the statutory financial regulator and foreign-exchange licensing authority in Gibraltar.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium. Offshore digital electronic-money institutions (EMIs) may be used only if they hold a GFSC, UK FCA, or recognised EEA authorisation that remains legally valid in Gibraltar post-Brexit; automatic EU/EEA passporting no longer applies. An EMI cannot accept deposits or hold client funds without a banking licence. Digital EMIs are viable as secondary payment and FX-execution channels for supplier disbursements, but the primary liquidity rail must remain a regulated Gibraltar bank account. GFSC will impose full CDD, source-of-funds verification, and sanctions screening on any EMI onboarding.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% standard Gibraltar corporation tax rate (Income Tax Act 2010, s.76); an 18% rate applies only to licensees in utilities, telecommunications and online gambling. The Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is fiscally transparent: profits are allocated to partners and taxed in their hands, with corporate partners chargeable at 12.5% on their Gibraltar-source distributive share.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Gibraltar does not impose withholding tax on dividends, distributions, or partnership profit remittances to non-resident partners. Distributions made by the ELP to its limited partners may be paid free of any Gibraltar deduction at source.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 (Gibraltar pounds) aggregate value of controlled transactions with related parties in a chargeable period triggers the mandatory Local File; Master File is required where the multinational enterprise group has consolidated group turnover above EUR 750 million. Below the GIP 1,000,000 threshold, contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation must still be prepared where an arrangement enjoys a tax advantage. Gibraltar follows the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines as implemented by the Income Tax (Transfer Pricing) Regulations 2019, with penalties for non-documentation.

Economic substance scope: The Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2019 and the Limited Partnerships (Economic Substance) Regulations 2020 apply to ELPs carrying on 'relevant activities' (banking, insurance, shipping, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, distribution and service centres, holding companies, and intellectual-property holding). Manufacturing of disposable dialysis hemofilters under ISIC 3250 is not a listed relevant activity; nevertheless, the ELP is subject to the general substance doctrine: it must maintain a registered office in Gibraltar, retain books and records locally, keep a Gibraltar bank account (the target rails being SWIFT LC and TT wire), and, if tax residence is claimed, exercise central management and control genuinely in Gibraltar. A shell ELP whose decisions are made entirely in the UK or Cayman Islands will be denied a TRC and its profits may be re-sourced offshore, negating Gibraltar treaty benefits.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Criminal prosecution under s.204 Income Tax Act 2010: fine of up to GIP 100,000 and/or imprisonment up to 7 years; civil penalty of 100% of the tax undercharged plus interest at 4% per annum; assessments reopened for 6 years (no time limit where fraud or wilful default is established).

Director personal liability: General partners and directors of the corporate partner are personally and severally liable for the penalty debt; court-ordered civil restitution of diverted profits; travel bans and passport seizure may be imposed pending payment; director disqualification in Gibraltar for up to 10 years and criminal imprisonment in egregious cases.
Shell Company / Artificial Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Denial of the Gibraltar Tax Residence Certificate; recharacterisation of profits as taxable in the partner's home jurisdiction; cancellation of treaty relief; additional tax assessed at 12.5% on deemed retained earnings; potential public designation as a shell entity under anti-BEPS measures.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign a false residence declaration or cause the ELP to claim treaty benefits without economic substance may be personally assessed for unpaid tax up to GIP 250,000; injunctions freezing personal assets; 5-year director disqualification; liability extends to the general partner as fiduciary.
