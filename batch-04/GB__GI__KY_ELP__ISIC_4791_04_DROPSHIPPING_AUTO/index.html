Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (Commissioner of Income Tax), 10 Tuckeys Lane, PO Box 601, Gibraltar GX11 1AA; corporate registry: Gibraltar Companies House (Registrar of Companies), 30-38 Engineer Lane, Gibraltar; official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi

E-government filing portal

Step 1: File the Exempted Limited Partnership formation documents with the Registrar of Companies and obtain the Certificate of Limited Partnership, whose statutory registration number serves as the tax reference. Step 2: Submit Partnership Particulars within 28 days of formation identifying the general partner, registered office, and all limited partners. Step 3: Register the ELP with the Income Tax Office for income tax purposes; each partner is separately assessed on their allocated share of Gibraltar-source income. Step 4: If the ELP has employees, register as an employer for Payroll Tax at the Income Tax Office. Step 5: For any physical goods movement through Gibraltar, obtain a customs registration code with HM Customs of Gibraltar and operate via ASYCUDA World. Where the general partner is not a regulated person, the Registrar requires a substance approval before registration. There is no minimum capital requirement and non-resident ownership is freely permitted.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Gibraltar law does not provide a check-the-box or fiscal transparency election; pass-through treatment is automatic under section 30 of the Income Tax Act 2010. The ELP is fiscally transparent and each partner is charged individually. Partners may elect special profit allocations through the partnership agreement provided they are commercially substantive and not predominantly tax-driven. Where a foreign jurisdiction treats the ELP as a taxable entity, Gibraltar's anti-hybrid rules implementing EU ATAD apply to prevent double non-taxation, and the Commissioner may invoke the General Anti-Abuse Rule to disregard artificial arrangements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar has no VAT or GST; it applies import duties only to selected goods (alcohol, tobacco, fuel, and motor vehicles) under the Customs Tariff Ordinance; UK VAT at 20% applies when the ELP acts as merchant of record for distance sales to consumers in Great Britain.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable in Gibraltar - there is no VAT registration or turnover threshold; import duty is limited to dutiable goods physically entered for free circulation in Gibraltar, and most dropshipped consumer goods are duty-exempt. For UK VAT, the ELP must register with HMRC when distance sales to UK consumers exceed GBP 70,000 annual taxable turnover, or immediately for goods supplied through an overseas seller arrangement under the low-value consignment rules.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in Gibraltar: no VAT exists to zero-rate and no export refund mechanism operates; goods in transit may be duty-suspended under the open-port regime. For UK VAT, zero-rating is available only when the registrant physically exports the goods from the UK within 3 months of supply; an automated dropshipping model generally fails this test because the goods ship directly from a third-country supplier to the consumer and never enter UK customs territory. Offshore services rendered by the ELP to overseas customers fall outside the scope of Gibraltar income tax by reason of the territorial basis.

  • Gibraltar Export Entry / Customs Declaration (ASYCUDA World)
  • Commercial Invoice identifying the exporter of record
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export movement
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank payment settlement evidence
  • UK Export Declaration (Customs Declaration Service) and export proof where UK zero-rating is asserted

Gibraltar: no electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping mandates; customs declarations are lodged through ASYCUDA World with standard electronic transmission. UK: if the ELP registers for UK VAT, Making Tax Digital (MTD) requires filing via HMRC-approved API-linked software; no fiscal e-invoicing mandate is in force.

Gibraltar: no statutory export zero-rating refund exists, so direct clawback is inapplicable; false declarations for duty relief attract penalties up to three times the duty and imprisonment up to 7 years under the Customs and Excise Duties Ordinances. UK: erroneous zero-rating claims are retrospectively clawed back with statutory interest; accuracy penalties range from 30% to 100% under Schedule 24 Finance Act 2007 for careless or deliberate concealment; fraudulent evasion of VAT is a criminal offence under section 72 VATA 1994 carrying imprisonment up to 7 years and director disqualification.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility applies for both current-account and capital-account transactions. Gibraltar does not maintain exchange controls. Profits of an Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) may be remitted freely without prior permission or convertibility restrictions, provided that the underlying transactions are documented and subject to GFSC/AML record-keeping, the Economic Substance Act 2020 reporting, and applicable UK/Gibraltar tax information exchange requirements.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Gibraltar imposes no statutory timeline, no mandatory reserve percentage, and no forced conversion ratio for export receipts. Dropshipping gross proceeds may be retained in GIP, GBP, EUR, or USD at the ELP's commercial discretion; however, banking partners may require a documented treasury policy and evidence of tax provision for withheld/dormant balances.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the banking and financial-services supervisor; no dedicated central bank exists in Gibraltar, monetary stability being anchored by the Bank of England through the Sterling currency parity of the Gibraltar pound (GIP)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium feasibility with documented limitations. UK-authorised and EEA-licensed electronic money institutions (EMIs) can generally accept Gibraltar-registered ELPs, but the combination of non-resident limited partners, automated dropshipping, and payment-processor volume may trigger enhanced due diligence declines. Legal constraints include the lack of a Gibraltar EMI licensing regime for pure pass-through entities, the requirement for all fund flows to remain traceable under the UK Money Laundering Regulations 2017 (as extended to Gibraltar), and the commercial practice of EMIs refusing high-velocity marketplace flows that cannot be reconciled to named counterparties. The recommended path is a licensed tier-1 bank core account plus an EMI as a forward-settlement sub-channel only for low-risk territories controlled by the bank's treasury.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital from a non-resident limited partner must be executed by SWIFT MT103 only (never MT202COV for entity treasury flows) from the sender's licensed bank to the ELP's Gibraltar International Bank account. The MT103 field 70 must read: 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION TO EXEMPTED LIMITED PARTNERSHIP GB__GI__KY_ELP IN ACCORDANCE WITH LPA'. The beneficiary account must be the designated capital escrow sub-account, not the operational VAT/trade account. Purpose codes and transaction descriptors must match the bank's AML questionnaire: use 'GIF' (general investment) or 'LFC' (intercompany loan) acceptable to UK correspondent banks; include the ELP GIIN/tax reference to avoid erroneous Common Reporting Standard classification. For UK sterling injections from the general partner, use CHAPS or Faster Payments with the narrative 'Intercompany funding to GI ELP, exempt from HM Treasury FATCA/CRS filing duplication', and retain the LPA capital account ledger entry, board minutes, and source-of-funds documentary evidence for six years.Supplier and logistics disbursements are cleared primarily over SWIFT MT103 for non-UK payees; for EU/EEA suppliers use single euro payments area (SEPA) credit transfers; for domestic Sterling suppliers use Faster Payments or CHAPS for same-day settlement. High-value automotive inventory procurement above GBP 50,000 per transaction should be backed by an irrevocable commercial letter of credit (LC 7.0) with SWIFT MT700 issuance and MT707 amendments, with inspection certificates, bills of lading, and commercial invoices submitted to the issuing bank. Payment-processor payouts from Stripe/PayPal are settled in daily batches into the operational account; do not allow the processors to pay suppliers directly, so that the treasury ledger retains the required audit trail. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GBP 1,000,000 aggregate controlled transactions per annum (or GBP 2,000,000 group revenue), subject to the Gibraltar Transfer Pricing Regulations 2019.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to ELPs carrying on relevant activities, typically including distribution and service businesses such as automated dropshipping. An ELP must demonstrate: (a) its core income-generating activities are undertaken in Gibraltar; (b) it has an adequate physical presence, staff, and expenditure in Gibraltar; and (c) its management decisions are made in Gibraltar. A pure pass-through ELP with no employees or office in Gibraltar will likely fail the ESR test and may be reclassified as a shell entity, triggering reporting obligations to the Commissioner and possible penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Deliberate Under-Reporting

Statutory sanction: Penalty of 100% of the evaded tax, plus interest up to 5% per annum. Criminal prosecution can lead to a fine of up to GBP 5,000 and/or imprisonment up to 5 years. Civil recovery orders under the Criminal Finances Act may also be obtained.

Director personal liability: Directors of the corporate general partner are personally liable for penalties if they knowingly cause the false filing. Individual partners who participate in tax evasion may face personal tax assessments, travel bans, and seizure of assets. Courts may pierce the ELP veil to hold the general partner jointly and severally liable.
Shell Company / Artificial Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Reclassification for tax purposes as non-resident, assessment of all Gibraltar-source income in the hands of the beneficial owners, and penalties under the ESR: GBP 10,000 per annum for failure to meet substance; GBP 20,000 plus daily penalties if non-compliance continues after an ESR review. Potential deregistration of the ELP.

Director personal liability: Directors of a corporate general partner who knowingly run a shell may be disqualified from acting as directors in Gibraltar for up to 5 years and held personally liable for unpaid taxes and ESR penalties. Shareholders can be required to repatriate undeclared profits and face criminal prosecution alongside the company.
Anti-Money Laundering (AML / CTF)

Statutory sanction: The Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) can impose fines of up to GBP 1 million or 5% of annual turnover, whichever is greater. For criminal breach under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015, individuals face up to 7 years' imprisonment and unlimited fines. Failure to file a SAR exposes the MLRO to separate penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors and MLROs are personally criminally liable for failing to comply with AML obligations. They can be disqualified, fined, and imprisoned. The ELP's assets and personal assets of the general partner can be subject to a serious crime prevention order (SCPO) and confiscation proceedings if money laundering is linked to dropshipping operations.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Tax adjustments to nil profitability, additional tax at the parent jurisdiction's rate, penalties up to 35% of the transfer pricing adjustment (under HMRC-style rules in Gibraltar), and interest. Tier 1 documentation penalties of GBP 3,000 per year for failure to keep contemporaneous records.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve below-market transfer pricing arrangements without valid economic analysis are personally liable for fines and may face personal tax indemnity claims. Partnership general partners are jointly liable for TP adjustments imposed on the ELP if they consented to the non-arm's length terms.
