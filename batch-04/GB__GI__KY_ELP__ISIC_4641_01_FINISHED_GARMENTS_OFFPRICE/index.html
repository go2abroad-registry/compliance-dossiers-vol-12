Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Her Majesty's Government of Gibraltar Income Tax Office (HMG Treasury and Taxation Department, Income Tax Division); online portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax; indirect tax functions are administered by the Gibraltar Customs and Excise Department (HM Government of Gibraltar Customs House).

E-government filing portal

A non-resident Exempted Limited Partnership must apply for a Gibraltar Income Tax File Number (GITIN) by submitting the prescribed ELP registration forms, partnership deed details, and non-resident declarations to the Income Tax Office. Because Gibraltar does not operate VAT, no VAT ID number is issued; for customs/export transactions the ELP may obtain a Gibraltar EORI-type customs identifier from the Customs and Excise Department to complete import and export entries.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — A non-resident Exempted Limited Partnership must apply for a Gibraltar Income Tax File Number (GITIN) by submitting the prescribed ELP registration forms, partnership deed details, and non-resident declarations to the Income Tax Office. Because Gibraltar does not operate VAT, no VAT ID number is issued; for customs/export transactions the ELP may obtain a Gibraltar EORI-type customs identifier from the Customs and Excise Department to complete import and export entries.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Gibraltar law does not provide a US-style check-the-box election. The ELP may elect or apply for non-resident taxation status under the Income Tax Act, ensuring all profits arise outside Gibraltar and no chargeable income accrues to the partnership. Hybrid mismatch rules consistent with the OECD/G20 BEPS project apply to any deductible/creditable mismatch where the ELP is treated transparently in one jurisdiction but opaque in another.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Customs and Excise Duties (No VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax regime applies in Gibraltar)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - Gibraltar has no value-added tax or general consumption tax; therefore no turnover-based registration threshold exists. Import duty and excise liability arise on the physical importation of goods into Gibraltar, while exported goods are not subject to a VAT/GST charge.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

N/A - there is no VAT/GST to zero-rate in Gibraltar. However, goods such as finished off-price garments may be imported into a customs-approved warehouse without payment of duty and subsequently exported outside Gibraltar under customs supervision, thereby achieving a full duty relief equivalent to zero-rating. The ELP must maintain evidence that goods never entered Gibraltar free circulation and left the territory within prescribed customs timelines.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Proof of Re-Export/Customs Warehouse Removement

No real-time e-invoicing or fiscal clearance mandate exists in Gibraltar. Customs declarations are filed electronically via the Gibraltar Customs and Excise Department's customs processing system; the system does not implement API-based national digital stamping or continuous transaction controls. Standard commercial invoices and customs entries must be preserved for audit purposes.

False or abusive reliance on customs warehousing/re-export relief is scrutinised by HM Government of Gibraltar Customs and Excise and Income Tax Office. Penalties include forfeiture of goods, retrospective duty recovery, financial penalties up to 200% of the evaded duty, interest, and prosecution. Misstatement of non-resident status to claim tax exemption may trigger income tax back assessments, late payment interest, penalties, and loss of pass-through treatment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is pegged 1:1 to GBP and is fully convertible; there is no restriction on capital account transactions, no exchange control approval for inward investment or profit remittance, and no differentiation between current and capital account. Repatriation of profits and liquidation proceeds from a Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is unrestricted and may be denominated in GBP, USD, or EUR subject to bank availability. The only operative constraints are UK/EU/UN sanctions screening, FATF-style AML checks, and the anti-money-laundering suspicious activity reporting regime, not currency convertibility.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory timeline or mandatory conversion ratio applies to export receipts. Export proceeds may be retained offshore in any freely convertible currency (USD, EUR, GBP/GIP). For the off-price apparel liquidation scope, receipts from UK/EU or third-country buyers require no surrender, no central bank settlement, and no repatriation license; standard commercial banking documentary requirements (invoice, shipping bill, proof of delivery) suffice to satisfy bank compliance.

Central bank supervision: Government of Gibraltar – Ministry of Finance (Currency Notes Act 1934) acting with the Bank of England (UK) for sterling monetary stability; prudential and conduct regulation of banks and currency service providers by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Low. Gibraltar applies its own Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licensing regime under the Financial Services (Electronic Money) Act 2011; only locally licensed EMIs may onboard a Gibraltar ELP for settlement. Cross-border offshore digital EMIs (e.g., non-EEA neobanks without GFSC license or UK FCA full authorization with Gibraltar eligibility) are barred from serving the ELP as principal treasury rail due to lack of regulatory reciprocity, restricted banking partner intermediation, and recurrent account holds on private foundation/partnership structures. A supervised Gibraltar EMI sub-account can be used only as a minor disbursement wallet, never as the primary clearing rail.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File required when the aggregate value of Gibraltar controlled transactions exceeds GIP 1,000,000 (or GIP 500,000 for individual transactions with tax-haven entities); Master File required where the worldwide group consolidated turnover exceeds GIP 50,000,000 or where related-party transactions exceed GIP 10,000,000. For the ELP pass-through structure, the General Partner must consolidate and disclose partner-level controlled transactions under the Income Tax (Transfer Pricing) Rules.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations 2019 apply to tax-resident entities and Gibraltar-sourced operations carrying on 'relevant activities'; wholesale distribution of finished garments is a 'distribution and service centre' activity. An ELP carrying on off-price apparel liquidation must demonstrate: (a) management and control exercised in Gibraltar; (b) adequate physical presence and staff in Gibraltar; (c) core income-generating activities in Gibraltar; (d) expenditure proportional to profit. A tax-transparent non-resident ELP must nonetheless file an annual ESR declaration (Form ESR-1) and, if no relevant activity in Gibraltar, evidence that it is not subject to tax in another jurisdiction or is a pure non-resident passive entity.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion/Fraudulent Non-Filing

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty up to 100% of understated tax plus interest at 8% p.a.; criminal conviction: fine up to GIP 50,000 and/or imprisonment up to 5 years under the Income Tax Act 2010 and Criminal Justice Act 2013

Director personal liability: Directors/General Partner can be personally assessed for the ELP's unpaid tax where assets were stripped; courts issue travel bans, asset freezing orders, and restoration orders; fraudulent trading leads to civil restitution without limitation period
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Denial of tax-residency benefits; recharacterization as Gibraltar-resident; 12.5% tax imposed on deemed profits; ESR declaration penalties: GIP 500 to GIP 20,000 per failed declaration plus daily default fines; possible compulsory strike-off under the Companies (Economic Substance) Regulations

Director personal liability: General Partner (and any shadow directors) incur joint, several liability for tax debts; court can disqualify directors for up to 15 years and prohibit alternative residence or travel
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Primary TP adjustment reallocating profits at 12.5%; secondary penalty of 20% of the adjustment amount; late payment interest; cancellation of tax refunds; potential 100% accuracy penalty for careless/inaccurate documentation

Director personal liability: Directors who sign inaccurate TP returns are personally liable for penalties if they knew of the mispricing; professional negligence claims by partners; disqualification orders if reckless
AML / Suspicious Transaction Reporting

Statutory sanction: Fines up to GIP 500,000 per breach under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015; confiscation proceedings of all profits deemed proceeds of crime; freezing of magnetic bank accounts; revocation of banking relationship

Director personal liability: Individual liability for money laundering offences: imprisonment up to 14 years; the General Partner is treated as a 'relevant person' under AML obligations and cannot hide behind the corporate veil
Improper Loss Utilization

Statutory sanction: Disallowance of carried-forward loss relief under change-of-ownership anti-avoidance provisions; tax recaptured with interest at 8% p.a.; penalty up to 100% of the disallowed relief

Director personal liability: Partners/shareholders who engineered the loss-buying scheme are jointly liable; professional advisors exposed to negligence claims; director disqualification for wrongful trading
Failure to Remit Payments / Provisional Tax Default

Statutory sanction: Late payment interest at 8% p.a. compounded daily from the due date; surcharge of 5% if defaulter receives a written notice and remains unpaid after 30 days; distress action and enforcement proceedings against the partnership assets

Director personal liability: Where the General Partner directed the ELP to prefer other creditors over the tax authority, personal liability for the unpaid amount; travel restrictions issued for taxpayers leaving Gibraltar with outstanding assessments exceeding GIP 20,000
