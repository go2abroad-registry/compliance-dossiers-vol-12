Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible. The Gibraltar ELP is fiscally transparent: no entity-level corporation tax is imposed; profits are attributed pro rata to general and limited partners under the partnership deed. Non-resident partners are taxable only on Gibraltar-source income (including profits of a Gibraltar permanent establishment) and on Gibraltar land/rental income. No withholding tax on distributions, interest, or royalties; no Gibraltar capital gains tax for non-residents. For OECD Pillar Two purposes the ELP is a pass-through entity and is not a constituent entity absent an MNE group inclusion election.

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no US-style check-the-box regime; partnership transparency is mandatory. Available elections: (a) election for separate legal personality under the Limited Partnerships Act 2019 (affects asset ownership, not tax status); (b) election to file a consolidated partnership information return through the general partner as tax agent in lieu of individual partner returns; (c) hybrid-mismatch matching elections under OECD BEPS Action 2 domestic anti-hybrid rules aligning the ELP as transparent for UK/US corporate partners; (d) election of non-residence where central management and control is exercised outside Gibraltar, limiting assessment to Gibraltar-source income only.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GIP 85,000 (Gibraltar Pound, pegged 1:1 to GBP) of taxable supplies in any rolling 12-month period for businesses established in Gibraltar; nil threshold for non-established foreign suppliers, who must register before making their first taxable supply. Voluntary registration is permitted.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

(a) Goods physically exported from Gibraltar to a non-Gibraltar destination are zero-rated where the supplier proves removal within the prescribed period (typically 3 months from supply). (b) B2B services supplied to a recipient belonging outside Gibraltar are zero-rated where the place of supply is outside Gibraltar. (c) In this ISIC 3240 triangular OEM model, plush dolls are manufactured and shipped by a third-country factory directly to an overseas customer without entering Gibraltar; the ELP acts as principal in a chain transaction and the supply is outside the scope of Gibraltar GST (no tax charge), provided complete commercial and transport evidence is retained. Zero-rating or out-of-scope treatment is denied where goods are entered into Gibraltar or where the arrangement results in actual domestic Gibraltar consumption.

  • Customs export declaration of the exporting/manufacturing country (e.g., China/Vietnam clearance record) showing the ELP or its customer as exporter/consignee
  • Bill of lading or airway bill, with container/waybill number and packing list, evidencing direct shipment from third-country factory to foreign customer
  • Gibraltar ELP commercial invoice (serialized) showing 0% GST/out-of-scope indicator, supplier GST number, and Incoterms
  • OEM/CMT manufacturing contract and factory purchase order proving the ELP's beneficial ownership of the plush-toy goods at time of supply
  • Foreign customer's inward remittance certificate or SWIFT MT103/credit confirmation matching invoice value
  • Certificate of origin or pre-shipment inspection certificate where required by the destination country

Gibraltar does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or API-based e-invoicing clearance. Tax invoices must be sequentially numbered and include the supplier's GSTN, invoice date, description, consideration, and GST amount; an electronic audit file must be retained for at least 6 years. PEPPOL/UBL, PDF/A, and UK-compatible e-invoice formats are accepted. Customs export declarations are obtained via the manufacturing jurisdiction's electronic single-window platform, not through any Gibraltar clearance system.

GST Act 2018: failure to register attracts a penalty up to 100% of the GST due; incorrect zero-rating/out-of-scope claims attract up to 100% of the evaded tax plus statutory interest and a maximum 5% daily default surcharge. Fraudulent evasion is criminal: on summary conviction a fine up to GBP 10,000 and/or up to 2 years' imprisonment; on indictment an unlimited fine and/or up to 7 years' imprisonment. The Commissioner may reopen returns for 4 years (12 years for fraud/neglect), claw back previously credited input GST as an immediate debt to the Crown, and apply the general anti-abuse rule of the GST Act and Income Tax Act 2010, with additional civil recovery powers under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 for circular offshore arrangements engineered solely to obtain export relief.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar maintains unrestricted current and capital account convertibility. There are no exchange-control permissions, no benefit of investment screening, and no profit remittance blocking for non-resident partners of an Exempted Limited Partnership. Cross-border capital contributions, trade settlement, royalty, and distribution payments may be freely effected in GIP, GBP, USD, or EUR. The GIP is pegged at parity to GBP, so practical settlement risk is limited to the correspondent banking chain rather than currency convertibility.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender requirement exists. Gibraltar imposes no mandatory timeline for repatriation of export proceeds and no mandatory conversion into GIP/GBP. Export receipts may be retained offshore or in the ELP’s operational account. However, accurate commercial books and records are required under the Limited Partnerships Act (Gibraltar); all trade receipts must be traceable to valid invoices and customs/import documentation for AML/CTF compliance.

Central bank supervision: Commissioner of Currency, Government of Gibraltar (Currency Notes Act 1934); prudential supervision and licensing of deposit-taking institutions are vested in the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC). The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is issued under a 100% backing mechanism using Bank of England banknotes.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium (supplementary). GFSC-licensed Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) and UK/EEA-authorized EMIs operating via passporting into Gibraltar can provide payment aggregation, virtual receipt points (e.g., commercial cards), and multi-currency wallets. They cannot be used for statutory capital escrow because an EMI is not a deposit-taking institution under the Financial Services (Banking) Act (Gibraltar). They are acceptable for low-value trade disbursements and FX conversion where the counterparty is a UK or EEA payment institution; however, the primary operating account must remain with a licensed Gibraltar bank to enable clean MT103 clearing and LC issuance.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound shareholder/partner capital contributions are routed by SWIFT MT103 from the remitting jurisdiction to the ELP's GBP or USD operational account. For SWIFT purposes, field 70 (Remittance Information) must include the ELP registry number and the exact phrase 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION – ELP PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT DATED [DD/MM/YYYY]'. The recommended BPM6 balance-of-payments purpose code is 'INVE' (investment in equity capital) or, for a loan from a partner, 'CCAB' (currency and deposits) in the supplementary information block. The receiving bank will require the source-of-funds to be evidenced by a signed partner resolution and the bank statement where the remitter's funds originated. As Gibraltar has no exchange control pre-approval, verification is limited to bank AML/KYC checks; clearing is immediate once authenticated.For settlement of raw-material purchase invoices (fabric, stuffing, trims) and service fees, use SWIFT MT103 bank-to-bank transfers. For high-value or first-time vendor shipments, the preferred rail is a confirmed Commercial Letter of Credit (L/C at sight) issued by the buyer's or ELP's banking counterpart, with MT700/MT701 messages, and the Gibraltar bank acting as advising or confirming institution. For low-value recurring platform payouts (pay-per-order to embroidery subcontractors), GBP Faster Payments (via the UK clearing system) or an EMI's SEPA/account-to-account rails may be used. USD-denominated disbursements should be routed through the bank's correspondent in New York on the CHIPS or Fedwire network. Incoterms must be stated on each invoice to allow customs and AML goods-matching; all disbursements must be mapped to relevant purchase orders and shipping documentation.Deliverable FX forward (GBP/EUR, GBP/USD, EUR/USD) with maturity up to 12 monthsNon-deliverable FX forward (NDF) on restricted/emerging-market currencies such as CNY, INR, or BRL when a full deliverable market is unavailable offshoreFX swap / cross-currency swap between GBP, USD, and EUR for working-capital smoothing and to manage mismatched funding currenciesEuropean-style vanilla FX option for contingent price protection on larger trade orders

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

10% standard Gibraltar corporate income tax rate; the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) itself is fiscally transparent and therefore no entity-level corporate tax arises in Gibraltar

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% statutory withholding tax on dividends paid by a Gibraltar resident company. A Gibraltar ELP is a pass-through vehicle; profit distributions to partners are not dividends and no entity-level withholding tax is imposed. The partner's allocable share of Gibraltar-source income is taxed directly at the partner level via assessment, unless the partner is a non-resident with no Gibraltar permanent establishment, in which case the distribution is normally free of Gibraltar tax.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory contemporaneous Transfer Pricing documentation (Local File and Master File) is triggered for a Gibraltar tax-resident ELP when the aggregate value of cross-border related-party transactions exceeds GBP 10,000,000 in a twelve-month period, or when the Commissioner issues a direction under the Transfer Pricing Rules. For a low-risk plush toy OEM manufacturing operation, a simplified Local File is recommended when intercompany charges exceed GBP 1,000,000; the documentation must be ready at the filing date and submitted to the Commissioner within 45 days of a written request.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar has codified Economic Substance Regulations aligned with the OECD's Base Erosion and Profit Shifting standards and the EU's harmful tax practices list. A tax-resident ELP carrying on a 'relevant activity' must demonstrate the following: physical presence in Gibraltar (exclusive or shared office), qualified staff engaged in the core income-generating activity, and board/partner management meetings held in Gibraltar. The plush toy OEM activity itself is not automatically a relevant activity, but if the ELP undertakes holding, financing, distribution, or leasing functions in relation to toy IP or trade receivables, it will be captured. A pure pass-through ELP with no Gibraltar tax residence (i.e., managed and controlled outside Gibraltar and with no local PE) is outside the scope of the Economic Substance Regulations but cannot claim Gibraltar tax residence or issue a Gibraltar tax certificate to its partners. Failure to meet the economic substance test for an in-scope, tax-resident ELP triggers penalty and possible strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Tax Avoidance

Statutory sanction: Immediate assessment to the full tax understated plus interest at the aggregate rate; deterrent penalty of up to 100% of the tax evaded; criminal prosecution in the Gibraltar Magistrates' Court or Supreme Court resulting in an unlimited fine and forfeiture of proceeds under the Proceeds of Crime Act

Director personal liability: Partners or designated managers are personally liable to civil restitution for the tax debt where they knowingly made or permitted a false return; disqualification from serving as a director/partner for up to 15 years; a travel ban may be imposed pending compliance; risk of imprisonment of up to 5 years on indictment
Sham Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner may denounce the ELP's purported tax residency status and treat it as a Gibraltar resident company under CFC legislation; entity-level tax will be reimposed at 10% of assessed profit; a penalty of the greater of GBP 50,000 and 5% of chargeable income is levied; the Registrar may administratively strike the ELP from the Gibraltar Limited Partnership Register

Director personal liability: Directors and partners who presented the ELP as tax-free despite actual Gibraltar management face liability as shadow directors; courts may lift the partnership veil and order personal restitution of all avoided taxes; a restricting order may be issued barring the individual from leaving the jurisdiction; custodial sentence of up to 7 years in aggravated cases of fraudulent incorporation
Anti-Money Laundering / Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Civil penalties up to GBP 1,000,000 per contravention; confiscation or forfeiture of the funds involved; freezing orders issued by the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit; referral for prosecution under the Proceeds of Crime Act carries an unlimited fine

Director personal liability: A manager who knew or suspected that the ELP was being used to launder money or to evade a third-party tax is personally guilty of an offence; criminal liability attaches irrespective of corporate status; statutory minimum imprisonment of 3 years on conviction; travel bans and extradition to the United Kingdom are available for offshore mischarges
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Administrative transfer pricing adjustment increasing the ELP's assessable income; accuracy penalty of 35% of the additional tax attributable to the mispricing; interest and daily surcharges are applied; the return may be reopened for six years after the filing date in the case of deliberate pricing errors

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners are jointly and severally liable for transfer pricing penalties where they were responsible for the false pricing assertions; the General Partner may be suspended under section 289(6) of the Companies Act; enforced dissolution and disqualification orders of up to 12 years are available in egregious cases
