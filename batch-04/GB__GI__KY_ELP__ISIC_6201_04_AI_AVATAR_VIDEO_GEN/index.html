Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (Commissioner of Income Tax). Administrative portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation (e-services via TaxPay, where available).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) must obtain a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN). The ELP or its general partner files an application with the Income Tax Office using Form P/F (registration) and submits the certified partnership deed, register of partners (including ultimate beneficial owners), registered office details, and a completed 'Non-Resident Partnership' declaration if the central management and control is outside Gibraltar. Upon approval, the ELP receives a unique tax ID. The ELP itself is classified as a non-resident partnership for income tax purposes when its control and management are abroad, and partners are assessed individually on their Gibraltar-source income only.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not offer a US-style 'check-the-box' election. Pursuant to the Income Tax (Partnerships) Rules, a partnership may elect to be treated as transparent by default, with each partner taxed on their share of Gibraltar-source profits. No elective entity-level tax designation exists for ELPs. Additionally, the partnership may elect to apply the special 'Economic Substance' reporting regime to affirm non-resident status, but no optional tax consolidation is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None – Gibraltar does not levy VAT, GST, or general sales and use tax. Limited import duties/excise duties apply to physical goods only.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable – no turnover threshold, no indirect tax registration requirement for AI video generation SaaS services. Physical imports may trigger customs duty registration with HM Customs if the ELP imports goods.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable – because Gibraltar has no VAT/GST system, exports of AI video generation software and cloud-based virtual avatar services are entirely outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect tax. There is no 0% rating to claim; no output tax is chargeable. Thus, no input VAT refund mechanism exists.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar Pound is fully and unconditionally convertible into Pound Sterling at a fixed 1:1 parity, creating de facto free convertibility for both current and capital account transactions. There are no exchange control restrictions, no prior approval requirements for foreign investment, and no quantitative limits on profit remittance, dividend distributions, or repatriation of capital. The jurisdiction operates an open capital account aligned with UK obligations and EU-derived free movement of capital legacy frameworks, subject only to standard AML/KYC documentation and sanctions screening. Profit remittance from the ELP to foreign partners is unrestricted and may be executed through any authorised bank without GFSC or central bank consent.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender requirement exists in Gibraltar. There is no mandated timeline for conversion of foreign currency export receipts into GIP/GBP, and no compulsory repatriation ratio applies to proceeds earned from cross-border SaaS subscriptions, licensing fees, or AI video generation services. Export receipts may be retained in foreign currency (USD, EUR, or other) in offshore or domestic accounts at the ELP's discretion, subject only to corporate accounting and transfer pricing documentation standards.

Central bank supervision: Government of Gibraltar (Currency Notes Act) as issuing authority for the Gibraltar Pound (GIP), with the Bank of England as the ultimate monetary anchor via the GIP/GBP 1:1 currency board arrangement; financial supervision and licensing conducted by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC), with HM Treasury and the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) exercising extraterritorial oversight for UK-connected activities

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM with legal constraints. Offshore digital EMI providers authorised in the UK (FCA) or EU/EEA (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Currenxie) may operate cross-border into Gibraltar under temporary permissions or equivalent third-country passporting regimes, but post-Brexit passporting rights have narrowed. A non-UK EMI requires GFSC authorisation under the Financial Services Act 2019 to hold funds for Gibraltar-incorporated entities; however, practical onboarding of a Gibraltar ELP at a UK-authorised EMI is achievable where the EMI's terms of service permit British Overseas Territory corporate clients. Key legal constraints: (i) EMI must not provide Gibraltar banking services without a GFSC licence if domiciled in a third country; (ii) the ELP must maintain a real economic nexus to Gibraltar or the UK to satisfy the EMI's jurisdictional acceptance criteria; (iii) funds held by EMIs are e-money float, not deposits, and may not be used for capital escrow or statutory capital verification. Recommendation: use a GFSC-licensed domestic bank as the primary treasury anchor and an FCA-authorised EMI for operational SaaS receivables collection only.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant capital injection into the Gibraltar ELP follows a two-stage wire protocol: (i) the foreign investing partner issues a SWIFT MT103 from their bank referencing the ELP's name and the GFSC-registered partnership number, with purpose coded as 'Direct Investment Capital Contribution — ISIC 6201 Software Development Partnership' using the harmonised BOP reason code 'M1000 Direct Investment — Equity Capital'; and (ii) funds settle into the ELP's designated capital escrow or operational account at a GFSC-licensed bank (e.g., Gibraltar International Bank) in GIP, GBP, EUR, or USD. Acceptance requires the bank's treasury team to verify the partnership agreement capital provision, the registered agent's authorisation, and the source-of-funds dossier. No FX approval is required in Gibraltar; however, if the remitting jurisdiction is subject to its own capital controls (e.g., non-UK high-risk countries), the remitter must obtain local central bank approval and present the partner's documentary approval to the receiving Gibraltar bank. Wire reference fields must include the six-character partnership ELP code (ELF_CUSTOM_KY_ELP) to avoid quarantine in sanctions screening.Trade settlements from the ELP's treasury are executed primarily through SWIFT MT103 for cross-border supplier, licensor, and developer payments; UK Faster Payments Service (FPS) and CHAPS are used for same-day GBP sub-ledger sweeps; SEPA Credit Transfer (SCT) in EUR via the Gibraltar bank's TARGET2 link handles EEA disbursements; card-based commercial disbursements (virtual corporate cards) are used for SaaS infrastructure spend at 1.5–3.5% fee economics; Letters of Credit (commercial LCs) are only required for hardware or colocation procurements exceeding GBP 250,000 and are issued under the bank's standard UCP 600 framework, with deferred payment at 90 days and a 10% performance margin. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: €750 million consolidated group revenue (triggering mandatory CbCR documentation), or GIP 1,000,000 of cross-border related-party transactions (triggering mandatory Master File and Local File preparation); lower-threshold ELPs must keep contemporaneous TP records.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to all tax-resident companies and commercial limited partnerships engaging in relevant activities, including software development, IP holding, distribution, and service provision; the AI avatar video generation SaaS must demonstrate adequate premises, local employed staff, board/partner governance, and core income-generating activities performed proportionately in Gibraltar.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (Deliberate Under-Reporting)

Statutory sanction: Under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act and Criminal Offences Act: civil penalty equal to 100% of additional tax, financial fines up to GIP 500,000, and imprisonment up to 7 years on conviction.

Director personal liability: Directors/general partners are jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax and penalties where they knew or agreed to the false filing; personal disqualification orders, travel bans, asset seizure, and imprisonment up to 7 years.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: ESR enforcement: fixed penalty of GIP 10,000 plus daily penalty of GIP 1,000 per day; eventual strike-off of the partnership from the Gibraltar Register, dissolution, and revocation of tax registration.

Director personal liability: General partners engaged in day-to-day management may incur direct penalties and disqualification orders; courts may lift the liability shield, order civil restitution, and impose travel restrictions pending tax clearance.
Money Laundering / AML Failure

Statutory sanction: Under the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and AML/CFT Regulations: fines up to GIP 3,000,000 per contravention, license or registration suspension, confiscation of proceeds, and corporate criminal conviction leading to debarment from payment rails.

Director personal liability: Directors and senior managers face personal criminal liability, including up to 14 years' imprisonment for money laundering, civil confiscation, travel bans, board disqualification, and personal financial restitution orders.
