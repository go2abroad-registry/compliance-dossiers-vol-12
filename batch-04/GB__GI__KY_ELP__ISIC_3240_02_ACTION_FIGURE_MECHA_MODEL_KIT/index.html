Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax), governed by the Income Tax Act 2010; official portal at https://www.taxation.gi; corporate registry interface via Gibraltar Companies House under the Companies Act 2014.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible – the Exempted Limited Partnership is fully tax transparent; income, gains, and losses flow through pro rata to partners, and non-resident partners are outside the charge to Gibraltar income tax unless trading through a Gibraltar permanent establishment. No entity-level corporation tax is imposed on the ELP itself.

Available election forms

Qualifying Category elections (Category 1 and Category 2) under the Income Tax Act 2010 are available for eligible trading activities and provide certainty of tax treatment for partners; Category 3 election applies to high-net-worth individuals. There is no US-style check-the-box regime; hybrid mismatch rules are implemented through the Taxation (Hybrid and Mismatch Arrangements) Act 2018, aligned with OECD BEPS Action 2 and the UK/Gibraltar Tax Information Exchange Agreement.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax – Gibraltar is outside the European Union VAT territory despite its UK Overseas Territory status; no general consumption tax, sales tax, use tax, or GST is levied on goods or services.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not Applicable – no VAT or GST registration regime exists in Gibraltar; there is no turnover threshold, no fiscal representative requirement, and no intra-community acquisition or distance-selling registration obligations.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not Applicable – because Gibraltar imposes no VAT, a 0% export VAT rate does not exist; export sales of action-figure mecha model kits manufactured by the ELP are outside the scope of any Gibraltarian indirect tax, and no VAT refund mechanism or input-VAT credit claim arises.

  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill

Not Applicable – no real-time fiscal clearance, B2B e-invoicing, SAF-T reporting, or digital stamping mandate exists in Gibraltar; commercial invoices must satisfy Income Tax Act record-keeping requirements with a 6-year retention period, but no tax-authority pre-clearance, API integration, or continuous transaction control regime applies. Excise duties are limited exclusively to fuel, alcohol, and tobacco products.

No VAT clawback applies given the absence of VAT; however, the Commissioner of Income Tax may reopen assessments within 4 years, extended to 6 years for fraudulent or negligent conduct, under the Income Tax Act 2010. False customs export declarations attract penalties of up to 200% of evaded duties and forfeiture of goods under the Customs Ordinance 2011. Failure to retain export documentation for the 6-year statutory period may result in disallowance of foreign-source income exemptions for partners and default assessments at the 12.5% corporate rate.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility applies. Gibraltar maintains no exchange control legislation and no capital or current account restrictions, mirroring the UK's post-1979 liberalised regime. The GIP/GBP peg is fixed at par, so conversion into and out of GBP carries negligible currency risk; USD, EUR, and CNH/CNY conversion is freely available through the licensed banking sector. Profit remittance by the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) to non-resident limited partners is unrestricted, and because the ELP is tax-transparent, distributions are not subject to Gibraltar withholding tax unless the partner is a Gibraltar resident or the profits are attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment. The only friction is bank-level source-of-funds verification under GFSC AML guidance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Neither the United Kingdom nor Gibraltar imposes statutory surrender timelines, mandatory conversion ratios, or repatriation windows for export receipts. The ELP may retain export proceeds in any freely convertible currency (GIP, GBP, USD, EUR, CNH) in designated foreign-currency accounts. Only statistical aggregate reporting obligations apply (GFSC monetary and balance-of-payments returns); there is no forced conversion or central-bank surrender requirement.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar has no independent central bank. Banking supervision, AML/CFT oversight, and licensing are vested in the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC); the note-issuing authority is the Gibraltar Currency Commission, a unit of HM Government of Gibraltar's Treasury; the Gibraltar pound (GIP) is legal tender and is fixed at par with the British pound sterling (GBP), with the Bank of England serving as the de facto monetary anchor through the 1:1 GIP/GBP peg.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Medium-High feasibility with material legal constraints. Gibraltar is outside the EEA and UK financial-services passporting perimeter, so a UK or EU licensed EMI (e.g., Wise, Revolut Business, Payoneer) cannot passport payments services into Gibraltar; it may only onboard the ELP as a customer under its proprietary safeguarding/custody model. Wise Business and Payoneer currently accept Gibraltar-registered corporate structures and are operationally well suited to sweep PayPal-sourced e-commerce proceeds; Revolut Business (UK EMI) onboards Gibraltar entities subject to FCA e-money safeguarding rules. Legal constraints: (i) funds held with an EMI are not Gibraltar bank deposits and are outside the Gibraltar Deposit Guarantee Scheme; (ii) EMIs may classify the ELP as higher risk due to the offshore-territory designation and non-resident partners, triggering enhanced due diligence, velocity monitoring, and payout caps; (iii) the EMI cannot provide trade finance or foreign-exchange hedging products. Recommended use is a collection-and-settlement sweep layer only, with core treasury liquidity parked in a GFSC tier-1 bank.

Recommended use is a collection-and-settlement sweep layer only, with core treasury liquidity parked in a GFSC tier-1 bank.
