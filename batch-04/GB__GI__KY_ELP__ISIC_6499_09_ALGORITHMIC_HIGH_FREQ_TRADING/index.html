Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, HM Government of Gibraltar, Income Tax Office, 6 New Harbours, Gibraltar GX11 1AA; official e-filing and taxpayer portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation. Parallel registrations are required with the Registrar of Companies, Companies House Gibraltar, for the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) under the Limited Partnerships Act 2016 (as amended), and, where the trading activities involve payment services or exchange membership, with the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC). The Income Tax Office and GFSC jointly administer the Gibraltar Tax Information Exchange Framework aligning with the UK–Gibraltar Taxation Co-operation Agreement.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: The General Partner files the ELP registration application with Companies House Gibraltar; the ELP receives a Certificate of Registration and a Companies House registration number which serves as the primary statutory identifier. Step 2: Within 30 calendar days of registration, the ELP files a Notification of Tax Registration with the Income Tax Office stating the registered office, principal place of business, general and limited partners, accounting reference date and address for service in Gibraltar. Step 3: The Commissioner allocates a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number / Assessment Reference Number (TIN/ARN) and enters the ELP into the self-assessment system under the Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended). Step 4: Where no partner maintains a physical presence or permanent establishment in Gibraltar, a tax representative with a Gibraltar address must be appointed and the signed power of attorney notarised and lodged with the Income Tax Office. Step 5: For any corporate partner that is tax-resident in the UK, EU or a Cayman-style jurisdiction, the ELP must submit the applicable hybrid-mismatch disclosure and, upon request, obtain a Certificate of Fiscal Transparency and Non-Residence from the Commissioner. Electronic lodgement is processed within 5–10 working days; paper filing within 15–20 working days.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible. A Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership is fiscally transparent by default: under the Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended) and the Limited Partnerships Act 2016, the ELP is not a taxable person; partnership income and gains are computed at partnership level and allocated to partners under the partnership agreement. Only the partners are chargeable, and a partner is taxed in Gibraltar solely on: (i) income arising in Gibraltar, or (ii) foreign income remitted to or enjoyed in Gibraltar by a Gibraltar-resident partner. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible. A Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership is fiscally transparent by default: under the Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended) and the Limited Partnerships Act 2016, the ELP is not a taxable person; partnership income and gains are computed at partnership level and allocated to partners under the partnership agreement. Only the partners are chargeable, and a partner is taxed in Gibraltar solely on: (i) income arising in Gibraltar, or (ii) foreign income remitted to or enjoyed in Gibraltar by a Gibraltar-resident partner. Non-resident partners trading through the ELP are taxable in Gibraltar only if the trade is carried on through a permanent establishment in Gibraltar, which for an ultra-low-latency proprietary trading operation requires a fixed place of business, a dependent agent, or substantive trading infrastructure physically located in Gibraltar (e.g., co-located matching-engine servers and exchange memberships operated from Gibraltar). A purely offshore book, with algorithmic order routing, execution and risk oversight conducted outside Gibraltar, remains outside Gibraltar's territorial scope, making the ELP a pure conduit.

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a US-style check-the-box regime; transparency is statutory and generally irrevocable for an ordinary ELP. Available elections include: (1) an election to adopt a particular accounting reference period (e.g., aligning the ELP year-end with UK partner taxation) under the Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended); (2) an elective profit-sharing and capital-allocation ratio election in the partnership agreement, determinative for the tax allocation of financing costs and trading profits among partners; (3) the reverse-hybrid election under the Gibraltar anti-hybrid rules implementing ATAD and BEPS Action 2, whereby the ELP is treated as a separate corporate person in respect of any investor that itself regards the ELP as opaque, to neutralise double non-taxation; and (4) an elective application to the Commissioner for advance confirmation of fiscal transparency and non-residence status, supported by a binding ruling under the Gibraltar taxation-ruling framework, which may be coordinated with HMRC under the UK–Gibraltar Taxation Co-operation Agreement.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales Tax Regime. Gibraltar is outside the EU VAT area and does not levy value added tax, goods and services tax, or a general retail sales tax. The indirect tax system consists of import duty and excise duty on physical goods, plus a single-stage Purchase Tax on imported and locally manufactured goods. Cross-border financial services, including proprietary algorithmic high-frequency trading and market-making (ISIC 6499 / NAICS 523130), are entirely outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect taxation.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not Applicable. No VAT/GST registration threshold operates in Gibraltar. Purchase Tax registration and a bonded-warehouse licence are required only where the ELP imports physical trading hardware (servers, network switches, fibre and co-location equipment) into Gibraltar; the customs de minimis threshold is GIP 100 per consignment for non-registered importers, above which an EORI-GI economic operator registration and a licensed customs broker are mandatory. As the ELP's HFT activities are services, no indirect-tax registration is triggered.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Because Gibraltar has no VAT, no legal category of 0% export VAT exists; the substantive neutrality mechanism is Gibraltar's territorial income tax rule. Proprietary HFT profits are treated as arising outside Gibraltar where: (i) the ELP has no Gibraltar permanent establishment; (ii) algorithmic order routing, execution and risk management are performed and supervised outside Gibraltar; (iii) no Gibraltar-resident personnel exercise trading discretion; and (iv) the registered office performs only clerical and administrative functions. The ELP may obtain a Certificate of Fiscal Transparency and Non-Residence from the Income Tax Office to evidence to HMRC and foreign tax authorities that the ELP is not Gibraltar-taxable, that distributions are not subject to Gibraltar withholding tax, and that partners are assessable exclusively in their own residence jurisdictions. UK-resident partners rely on the UK–Gibraltar Taxation Co-operation Agreement and the UK partnership tax rules to secure corresponding transparent treatment and foreign tax credit relief.

None. Gibraltar imposes no real-time fiscal clearance, no B2B e-invoicing API and no digital stamping obligation. Tax compliance is effected by electronic self-assessment returns filed through the GI-TAX portal, and import declarations, where relevant, are filed via the Gibraltar Customs Electronic System. The Income Tax Office and GFSC nevertheless require the ELP to retain trade records, algorithmic source-control logs, FIX session archives and Fedwire/Direct Exchange Clearing settlement records for six years and to produce them on demand; AML transaction reporting obligations apply under the GFSC rules where the ELP engages in regulated payment or exchange activity.

Under the Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended), the Commissioner may disregard the ELP's transparent form where the structure was adopted mainly to obtain a tax advantage or lacks commercial substance; the ELP can be recharacterised as an opaque company and assessed at the applicable corporate rate, including the OECD Pillar Two-aligned top-up rate for in-scope multinational groups. Retrospective assessments may extend six years for careless error and twenty years for fraudulent conduct, with penalties up to 100% of the tax under-assessed for failure to notify and up to 150% for deliberate withholding, plus interest at 8% per annum compounded quarterly. The Gibraltar anti-hybrid rules implementing ATAD and BEPS Action 2 require corresponding matching adjustments in the UK investor's hands, and any recharacterisation automatically triggers HMRC counter-action under the UK hybrid mismatch legislation. Additional consequences include denial of any Purchase Tax credit on imported hardware, GFSC fines of up to GIP 100,000 per regulatory breach, revocation of any trading or e-money authorisation, and proceedings under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 for confiscation of assets traceable to non-compliant arrangements.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is pegged at par to GBP and fully convertible. Both current and capital account transactions are unrestricted; profit remittance is free of exchange control authorisation. For a Cayman ELP operating with a Gibraltar tax/presence route, all trading profits may be repatriated after filing relevant GFSC and tax returns. There is no requirement to obtain central bank consent for dividends, loan repatriation, or equity distribution. Minimal compliance obligations are limited to AML, FATCA/CRS reporting, and economic substance filing.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable for financial services. No statutory surrender or mandatory conversion ratio exists for foreign exchange receipts under Gibraltar or UK law. All FX proceeds may be retained in the original currency, converted freely via the interbank market, or maintained in a multi-currency treasury account.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (central monetary authority for the UK and Gibraltar; local financial regulation is exercised by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission – GFSC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-HIGH for ancillary treasury operations, pre-funding, and operating-expense settlements. Legal constraints: EMIs (regulated by GFSC or FCA) cannot act as clearing brokers, hold segregated client money for derivative margin, or provide prime brokerage services. They may be used for commercial expense payments, currency conversion, and treasury liquidity pooling but must be backed by a tier-1 bank account in the name of the ELP. Rapid onboarding is often possible within 5–10 business days.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File threshold: aggregate cross-border related-party transactions exceeding GIP 10 million in an accounting period. Master File threshold: multinational enterprise group with cross-border revenue exceeding GIP 50 million. Country-by-Country Report threshold: group consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to entities conducting relevant activities, including banking and investment fund management. Algorithmic high-frequency trading and proprietary market-making, if licensed and managed from Gibraltar, constitute relevant activity requiring demonstrable economic substance: a physical office in Gibraltar, adequate staff with decision-making power, board or partner meetings held in Gibraltar, revenues generated by local activity, and no outsourcing of core profit-generating functions. The ELP must file an annual economic substance report; failure to meet the test may result in penalties, strike-off, and exchange of information with foreign tax authorities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion / fraudulent under-declaration

Statutory sanction: Criminal prosecution; fine up to £250,000; imprisonment up to 7 years; civil penalty up to 100% of the understated tax; confiscation orders under the Proceeds of Crime Act and publication of the taxpayer's details.

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners who caused or knowingly permitted the fraudulent return are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax; courts will pierce the corporate veil, impose restitution orders, travel bans, director disqualification, and imprisonment.
Shell company / no economic substance

Statutory sanction: Economic Substance Regulations penalty up to £100,000 for failure to meet the substance test; mandatory strike-off from the Gibraltar Companies/Partnerships Register; exchange of financial information with OECD and EU tax authorities; loss of tax benefits and treaty access with retroactive effect.

Director personal liability: The general partner and managing persons may be personally liable for the penalty where they are determined to be responsible for the entity's non-compliance; disqualification orders and restriction on holding Gibraltar directorships apply for up to 15 years.
Aggressive transfer pricing / base erosion

Statutory sanction: Tax authority adjustment increasing Gibraltar taxable profit; interest on underpaid tax at 6%; penalty up to 100% of the additional tax; late payment surcharges; downward adjustment not permitted; CFC attribution to Gibraltar parent entities.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed or approved non-arm's length transfer pricing documentation are liable for penalties up to £10,000 each; where the transfer pricing arrangement is a sham, directors are jointly liable for the underlying tax deficiency and may be subject to civil recovery and travel restrictions.
AML breach / unlicensed financial services

Statutory sanction: Unlicensed financial service activity: fine up to £250,000 and/or 2 years' imprisonment. AML violations: unlimited fines, public censure, asset freezing, revocation of any regulatory licence, and criminal money laundering penalties up to 14 years' imprisonment.

Director personal liability: General partners and directors in regulated activities are individually accountable; sanctions include civil fines, disqualification, travel bans, loss of fit-and-proper status, and imprisonment for up to 14 years for knowingly participating in money laundering or unlicensed investment business.
