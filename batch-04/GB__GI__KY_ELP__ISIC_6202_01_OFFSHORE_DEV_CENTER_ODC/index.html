Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar — official portal at www.veg.gi (or www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation)

E-government filing portal

A non-resident Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) seeking to establish a tax presence in Gibraltar must first appoint a Gibraltar-resident general partner/manager and file the ELP with the Registrar of Companies (Overseas Limited Partnerships Register). Thereafter, the ELP or its general partner must apply to the Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TIN) using Form PART-1 / online partnership registration. VAT registration is not applicable because Gibraltar does not levy VAT; if the entity imports or warehouses dutiable goods, it must separately apply to HM Customs for an Import Duty Account and relevant excise licences.

Default tax classification

Available election forms

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar Import Duty and Excise Duty (no VAT/GST system)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable for VAT; registration is mandatory for any person importing, producing, or warehousing excise goods or goods subject to import duty in Gibraltar; no economic turnover threshold applies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export of physical goods from Gibraltar is outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect taxation (effectively nil-rated) because no VAT is imposed; eligible exporters must retain proof of physical export to a third jurisdiction. Offshore services (software engineering and maintenance) supplied to non-Gibraltar recipients are treated as non-taxable and do not require a Gibraltar VAT registration or VAT zero-rating claim; no refund mechanism exists since no indirect tax is levied on the supply.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document / CDS submission)
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill / CMR consignment note
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank transfer receipt or FX proof from Wise/Payoneer/Wire)
  • Proof of Overseas Delivery (courier POD, customs stamped commercial invoice, or electronic delivery confirmation)

No real-time electronic invoicing or fiscal stamping mandate is in force in Gibraltar. Customs clearance is effected via electronic Single Administrative Document (SAD) filings using the CDS/CHIEF platform for goods moving between Gibraltar, the United Kingdom, and the EU; excise goods movements require electronic administrative documents pursuant to the Excise Movement and Control System (EMCS).

Fraudulent or negligent export declarations expose the taxpayer to civil penalties of up to 100% of the duty evaded plus interest; serious cases may attract criminal prosecution and seizure of goods. Clawbacks may also be imposed where export substantiation is not contemporaneous or where repatriation of consideration fails to occur within the prescribed 180-day window. Because Gibraltar imposes no VAT, export zero-rating clawback risk is minimal; the main audit focus is on inaccurate import duty classifications, customs valuation errors, and transfer pricing documentation for intercompany software services.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Both current and capital accounts of the balance of payments are fully convertible for GIP/GBP and all major reserve currencies. There are no Gibraltar exchange-control restrictions on profit remittance, dividend repatriation, or capital transfers to/from foreign resident investors. A formal banking, licensing, and tax-residency verification under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act and ELP partnership deed will be required to evidence commercial substance and to avoid misclassification as a pure shell vehicle.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar imposes no statutory surrender, forced conversion, or holding-period rule on export receipts. For the ISIC 6202 offshore software engineering service line, all export proceeds may be retained and freely deployed in any permitted currency; however, the FSC requires complete invoice, payment-flow, and bank-statement records to substantiate intra-company or third-party service revenue, beneficial ownership, and economic substance reporting.

Central bank supervision: Government of Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (FSC) as the integrated financial regulator; monetary policy and the Gibraltar pound are managed by the Government of Gibraltar under a Sterling peg to the Bank of England. There is no independent central bank with discretionary exchange controls; all banking licensing, AML/CFT, and payment system matters are supervised by the FSC.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: High for settlement-class payments in GBP/EUR/USD via UK-licensed electronic money institutions (Wise B2B, Payoneer, Revolut Business) and Medium as the primary operating treasury due to legal constraints. Offshore digital EMIs are not banking institutions in Gibraltar, do not hold a Gibraltar passported banking licence, and are required to hold client funds in segregated e-money accounts under the safeguarding rules of their home EEA/UK regulator. For this Gibraltar ELP, the optimal treasury stack is a Gibraltar tier-1 commercial bank as the settlement/gateway account and a UK/EU EMI as the headless, low-friction disbursement rail for recurring technology vendor payments.

Offshore software development revenues earned by non-residents are outside the Gibraltar charge and must be marked Not Applicable. Late filing incurs an initial penalty of GIP 150, rising by GIP 10 per day to a maximum of GIP 500; after 90 days, a further GIP 50 daily surcharge applies; maximum aggregate penalty is GIP 10,000; statutory interest of 8% per annum accrues on unpaid tax.
Box 4 / Deductions and Expenses Expenses incurred wholly and exclusively in producing taxable Gibraltar-source profits. Non-resident partners may deduct only expenses supported by vouchers and transfer pricing documentation; personal expenses are disallowed. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 2,000,000 in cross-border controlled transactions for mandatory Local File preparation; Master File documentation required where the consolidated group turnover exceeds GIP 50,000,000. The ELP must maintain contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation for any software development service transactions with its UK parent.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar applies the EU/Code of Conduct and OECD Economic Substance Regulations through domestic legislation. An ELP performing offshore software development and maintenance is a relevant entity when income is derived from geographically mobile activities. It must prove central management, qualified staff, physical premises, and core income-generating activities in Gibraltar. Tax-transparent status does not relieve the ELP from substance requirements; persistent failure leads to withdrawal of transparency status and reclassification as fully taxable.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Filing

Statutory sanction: On conviction: maximum fine of GIP 150,000 or 300% of tax evaded, imprisonment up to 7 years, civil penalty equal to 100% of the unpaid tax, and statutory interest at 8% per annum.

Director personal liability: The court may pierce the ELP veil; every general partner or managing director who consented to the fraud is jointly and severally liable. Asset freezing and travel bans are imposed under the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act 2015.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Substance failure penalty up to GIP 100,000 per annum; cancellation of tax-transparent status; the ELP becomes taxable on worldwide profits; UK HMRC may deny treaty benefits.

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners are liable for penalties up to GIP 100,000; disqualification from holding office for up to 15 years; limited liability protection is lost where a partner participates in management.
Transfer Pricing Misstatement

Statutory sanction: Potentially a tax adjustment plus interest; an accuracy penalty of up to 50% of the understated profits or GIP 100,000, whichever is greater, and submission of uncertain tax position disclosures where applicable.

Director personal liability: The CFO or responsible officer may be personally assessed where negligence or wilful default is established; civil recovery suits by the Tax Office; travel restrictions against such officers for up to 3 years.
Money Laundering / AML Breach

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment up to 14 years and/or an unlimited fine; GFSC administrative penalty up to EUR 5,000,000; confiscation of the ELP's property under the Criminal Proceeds Act.

Director personal liability: Partners who knowingly facilitate money laundering face personal confiscation orders, travel bans, and extradition proceedings; prohibition from holding regulated positions and civil liability to affected third parties.
