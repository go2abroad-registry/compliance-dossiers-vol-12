Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar direct tax: Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar, with the GibTax electronic filing portal (https://www.gibtax.gi). Gibraltar indirect tax and customs: Gibraltar Customs and Excise Department for General Consumption Tax (GCT). United Kingdom: HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) becomes relevant only if a UK permanent establishment or a UK taxable supply nexus is created.

E-government filing portal

A non-resident-owned Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) must first be incorporated or registered with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies and obtain its certificate of registration. The ELP must then appoint a Gibraltar-resident general partner or licensed tax agent and file the partnership deed, KYC/AML documents, and prescribed TIN application forms with the Gibraltar Income Tax Office to obtain the statutory Gibraltar Tax Identification Number. Where annual taxable turnover reaches or exceeds the GCT threshold, the ELP must also register for GCT with the Customs and Excise Department to obtain a GCT registration number. Both direct tax and GCT registrations may be initiated through the GibTax portal or the official paper forms, and all statutory filings must subsequently be made under the assigned TIN/GCT reference.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Under Gibraltar income tax law, an Exempted Limited Partnership is fiscally transparent by default: the partnership itself is not charged to tax and partners are assessed on their allocable share of profit. There is no domestic check-the-box or statutory entity-classification election. For US federal tax purposes, the ELP may elect entity classification on IRS Form 8832 to the extent it constitutes a foreign eligible entity. Any such election must be assessed against UK and Gibraltar hybrid mismatch rules (OECD BEPS Action 2) before reliance, particularly where the partners or the ELP are tax resident in different jurisdictions.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GBP 100,000 of annual taxable turnover; zero-rated and out-of-scope export turnover is disregarded for threshold purposes, and once the threshold is exceeded GCT registration is compulsory.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

GCT zero rating applies to physical exports of goods from Gibraltar and to qualifying services supplied to customers outside Gibraltar, including cross-border market research, consumer polling and analytics SaaS services delivered from the Gibraltar platform, provided the place of supply is outside Gibraltar. Zero rating or outside-scope treatment is conditional on the ELP obtaining and retaining sufficient commercial evidence of foreign supply, including a signed cross-border contract, GCT place-of-supply statement, digital delivery or platform access logs, IP geo-location evidence, and payment documentation showing consideration settled through the offshore counterparty's banking rails.

  • Customs export declaration (customs/SAD data or combined transport document) for physical goods; for services, the cross-border service agreement and GCT place-of-supply statement together with consumer panel platform delivery and server logs
  • Bill of lading / air waybill or equivalent transport evidence for goods; for SaaS and digital survey services, platform access records, API usage logs, and IP geo-location data demonstrating that the services are delivered to counterparties outside Gibraltar
  • Inward remittance certificate, FX transfer confirmation, or bank and payment-rail statements (PayPal Mass Payout or Wise) evidencing receipt of consideration from the foreign customer and settlement through the ELP's bank account

Gibraltar does not operate a real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control, or digital stamping mandate comparable to Italy's SdI or Spain's SII. Electronic GCT returns are filed periodically through the GibTax portal, and taxpayers must issue and retain commercial electronic invoices and export or place-of-supply supporting documents in the ordinary course of business. There is no mandatory API clearance obligation or government-issued digital invoice stamping requirement.

GCT and income tax audits may retrospectively disallow zero-rated treatment and claw back input tax plus interest where the export or foreign-supply documentation bundle is incomplete, internally inconsistent, backdated, or incapable of demonstrating an actual offshore supply. The Commissioner of Income Tax and the Customs and Excise Department may impose penalties under the GCT Act and the Income Tax Act for negligent or fraudulent claims, including penalties up to 100% of the tax at risk, and criminal liability may arise for deliberate falsification of export or foreign-supply documents. Records must be retained for the statutory period and produced promptly upon audit demand; failure to evidence foreign receipt of consideration within the applicable period may result in assessment at the standard GCT rate and adverse exchange-control findings.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GIP is fully convertible into GBP at par, and the jurisdiction imposes no exchange controls on current or capital account transactions. Inward capital contributions to the ELP and outward remittances of profits, distributions, or trade settlements are freely permitted without prior licensing or approval. Profit remittance from the Gibraltar ELP to its Cayman partners is free of Gibraltar withholding tax and of exchange-control barriers. The only operational caveat is that GIP is not traded internationally; therefore, major receivables and hedge flows should be denominated in GBP, USD, or EUR and settled through London clearing corridors.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no export-proceeds surrender rules in Gibraltar. Receipts from cross-border provision of market-research and consumer-panel services (ISIC 7320) may be retained in any currency, converted at the prevailing interbank rate, or applied in partial pre-payment of foreign suppliers. No statutory conversion timeline, mandatory surrender ratio, or repatriation declaration applies, other than ordinary CRS/FATCA account reporting on income balances.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar does not possess an independent central bank. The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is issued by the Government of Gibraltar under the Currency Notes Act and is pegged at 1:1 with pound sterling (GBP). Financial services supervision is vested in the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) under the Financial Services Act 2019. Monetary policy is effectively that of the UK, transmitted via the Bank of England; the GFSC and the Gibraltar Ministry of Finance jointly administer FX-related regulation and any transaction reporting obligations.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Feasibility: High for payout rails; Medium as a primary banking substitute. Wise (FCA-authorised UK EMI) and PayPal (Luxembourg-authorised financial institution) are operationally suited to mass consumer-panel disbursements and can be integrated through the ELP's API. Legal constraints: these channels must be ring-fenced to paying panelist consideration and vendor fees (service-based disbursements) and must not be presented as unlicensed money transmission to the public. An e-money or payment-services licence would be required if the ELP issued e-money or executed payment transactions for third parties on a commercial basis; the survey-panel payout model does not trigger this boundary if the ELP contracts as principal with panelists. Capital escrow, partnership liquidity reserves, and any credit or financing facilities must remain at a GFSC-licensed commercial or private bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

LP capital contributions are routed via SWIFT MT103 in GBP/USD/EUR to the ELP's Trusted Novus Bank or Gibraltar International Bank account. The remittance narrative must read 'Capital contribution under LPA [date], ELP ref [ID]' and must not contain money-service or brokerage references. The receiving bank obtains a dated subscription letter, six-month source-of-funds statements for each LP, passport/KYC copies of the LP signor, and confirmation that any controlling person owning 25% or more is disclosed for CRS/FATCA. USD wires clear via London/New York correspondent corridors in 2–3 business days; EUR flows should use SEPA CT/Instant where an IBAN-enabled account is held; GBP flows clear same day via CHAPS.Panelist and surveyor payouts below equivalent US$2,000 per transaction are executed via Wise Business batch API and PayPal Mass Payouts. All vendor, supplier, and sub-processor settlements above US$2,000 are paid by SWIFT MT103 from the primary GFSC-licensed bank with the remittance narrative 'market research service fees' and the commercial-services payment-purpose category required by the beneficiary bank (UK BoP classification 'Other Business Services'). Commercial and standby Letters of Credit are permissible but non-routine, reserved for performance undertakings demanded by institutional procurement clients.
