Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office (Office of the Commissioner of Income Tax), HM Government of Gibraltar — official portal www.gibraltar.gov.gi with Income Tax Office e-filing; partnership registration is effected through the Registrar of Companies (Companies House Gibraltar).

E-government filing portal

Foreign owners of a Gibraltar Limited Partnership: (1) file the Statement of Limited Partnership with the Registrar of Companies under the Limited Partnerships Act 2015 and obtain the Certificate of Registration; (2) appoint a Gibraltar-licensed corporate service provider; (3) apply to the Income Tax Office for the partnership Tax Reference/TIN using the post-registration return form; (4) register each chargeable partner separately for Income Tax (non-resident partners only if Gibraltar-source profits arise); (5) obtain a Gibraltar Customs code / Economic Operator identifier for any physical import/export activity; (6) no Gibraltar VAT ID is obtainable because no VAT regime exists — a separate UK VAT registration with HMRC is required only if UK taxable supplies arise.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — Foreign owners of a Gibraltar Limited Partnership: (1) file the Statement of Limited Partnership with the Registrar of Companies under the Limited Partnerships Act 2015 and obtain the Certificate of Registration; (2) appoint a Gibraltar-licensed corporate service provider; (3) apply to the Income Tax Office for the partnership Tax Reference/TIN using the post-registration return form; (4) register each chargeable partner separately for Income Tax (non-resident partners only if Gibraltar-source profits arise); (5) obtain a Gibraltar Customs code / Economic Operator identifier for any physical import/export activity; (6) no Gibraltar VAT ID is obtainable because no VAT regime exists — a separate UK VAT registration with HMRC is required only if UK taxable supplies arise.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no check-the-box or entity-classification election: the ELP is fiscally transparent by default and cannot elect to be taxed as a company. Available statutory elections are limited to the Category 2 individual regime and the private fund partnership designation under the Limited Partnerships Act 2015 (regulatory only). No Gibraltar hybrid-mismatch election exists; hybrid arrangements between the ELP, its partners and the UK are tested under UK TIOPA 2010 Part 6A by UK counterparties.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None (Gibraltar does not operate VAT, GST or Sales and Use Tax; only customs and excise duties at importation, in a territory outside both the EU and UK VAT systems)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no Gibraltar VAT/GST registration threshold exists; customs/excise duty is triggered by importation regardless of turnover. If UK taxable supplies generate more than GBP 90,000 (approximately GIP 90,000 fixed at 1:1) in a rolling 12-month period, the ELP must register for UK VAT with HMRC.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable under Gibraltar direct taxation: the territory has no VAT to zero-rate. Under Gibraltar customs law, goods exported from Gibraltar are relieved of customs/excise duty once the export declaration is accepted and the goods depart; international NVOCC ocean freight supplied by the ELP is outside the Gibraltar territorial charge for non-resident partners. Where the ELP holds a UK VAT registration, UK zero-rating is claimable for direct goods exports and freight transport services under VATA 1994 Sch. 8 Groups 7/4 subject to standard proof-of-export conditions.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Gibraltar Customs outward shipment entry)
  • Bill of Lading / Sea Waybill issued by the NVOCC as contractual carrier (master and house set)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103 evidencing settlement of export freight proceeds

No real-time fiscal clearance, API submission or digital stamping is required in Gibraltar. The Income Tax Office requires invoices and records to be kept for six years; ordinary commercial e-invoicing is permitted but no fiscal e-invoice clearance with the Commissioner is mandated.

Gibraltar: no statutory FX repatriation deadline applies (the 180 days is this pipeline's conservative compliance convention). The Income Tax Office can re-characterise freight income as Gibraltar-source where control/management or booking activity is exercised from Gibraltar; fraudulent or negligent returns attract penalties up to 200% of the tax under-assessed, default daily fines and interest, with unlimited reassessment periods for dishonesty. Customs infractions expose the ELP to forfeiture of goods and fines up to treble the evaded duty. Where the ELP is UK-VAT registered, participation in an abusive export chain (MTIC/Kittel scenario) enables HMRC to deny input VAT recovery, assess output VAT on the full value, impose civil penalties up to 100%, and pursue criminal sanctions for dishonest evasion under VATA 1994 s.72.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility on both current and capital account; Gibraltar maintains no exchange control legislation and no restrictions on the repatriation of profits, dividends, interest or partnership distributions. The GIP/GBP peg is irrevocably anchored at 1:1, with GBP legal tender in Gibraltar. A Gibraltar-resident Exempted Limited Partnership may hold multi-currency accounts (USD, EUR, GBP) and remit profits to non-resident partners without exchange control approval; the only operative frictions are GFSC AML/CTF verification, economic substance filing under the Income Tax (Substance Requirements) Regulations, and customary bank-level source-of-funds review.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Gibraltar and the United Kingdom impose no statutory timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts. An NVOCC ELP may retain gross freight-forwarding and brokerage receivables in the original settlement currency (typically USD) indefinitely. Statutory obligations are limited to balance-of-payments and economic substance reporting, anti-money-laundering record keeping under the Proceeds of Crime Act (Gibraltar), and transaction-level sanctions screening; there is no surrender to the central bank or designated local-currency conversion requirement.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (banking, payments and FX licensing) in conjunction with the Government of Gibraltar, which exercises note-issuing powers under the Currency Notes Act 1934; the Gibraltar pound is pegged at par to pound sterling, making HM Treasury and the Bank of England the de facto anchor monetary authorities for GIP/GBP convertibility.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium-High, subject to strict licensing limitation: only Gibraltar-licensed banks and e-money institutions (EMIs) may lawfully provide payment accounts to Gibraltar-incorporated ELPs. Xapo Bank (Gibraltar) Limited and similarly GFSC-authorized digital banks are viable for an NVOCC ELP offering dedicated multi-currency IBANs, SWIFT and local GBP clearing, with fully digital onboarding. UK FCA-authorised EMIs and EU-licensed EMIs lost passporting rights into Gibraltar upon the UK's exit from the European Union; an account opened with an unlicensed offshore EMI is not viable for regulated NVOCC settlement because it would breach Gibraltar's Financial Services Act 2019 and would not provide the compliance infrastructure required by ocean freight counterparties.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound capital injections are executed via SWIFT MT103 from the contributing partner's bank using the explicit purpose code 'Equity Capital Contribution to a Non-Resident Enterprise' (mapped to UK BOPS/ITRS category D.1.1 or Gibraltar equivalent), or 'Intercompany Operating Funding' where structured as partner loans. The receiving Gibraltar bank will complete source-of-funds verification against the capital call notice, the executed contribution agreement, the ELP's constitutional documents and the UBO file before crediting the multi-currency operational account. High-value sterling injections clear via CHAPS into the Gibraltar payment system; non-GBP injections settle in real time through the correspondent bank's nostro accounts, with the bank's FX desk converting only if the account is GIP-denominated.Outbound ocean freight disbursements are executed primarily by SWIFT MT103 for direct wire payments to carriers, inland hauliers and port authorities, with MT202 COV reserved for nostro funding movements between the ELP's bank and its correspondent network. Commercial Letters of Credit under UCP600 are the preferred clearing rail for large forwarder contracts and back-to-back transport procurement, providing title-flow and documentary compliance protection. Recurrent, low-value port charges are settled by local Sterling direct debit or ACH-style automated clearing where the counterparty maintains a UK or Gibraltar account, using the payment rails 'Swift_Wire' and 'ACH_Direct' in line with the pipeline target. Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The income tax return must be filed on or before 30 November following the end of the year of assessment. For a fiscal year ending 31 December, the filing deadline is 30 November of the following year, approximately 11 months after the reporting period close.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GBP 2,000,000 aggregate related-party turnover triggers mandatory Local File and Master File documentation for the ELP on a consolidated group basis, applying the Gibraltar Income Tax (Transfer Pricing) Rules.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations apply to entities carrying on geographically mobile activities, including shipping and freight forwarding. The ELP must demonstrate economic substance in Gibraltar through premises, employees, and board decisions. For NVOCC ocean freight forwarding, core income-generating activities (negotiating slot charters, coordinating cargo bookings, issuing house bills of lading, and managing logistics systems) must occur in Gibraltar to be tax resident and not be treated as a shell arrangement.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Filing

Statutory sanction: Civil penalties up to 100% of the tax understated, daily default penalties, and interest. Criminal prosecution may lead to imprisonment up to 7 years and unlimited fines.

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners may be personally liable where they knowingly assisted evasion. The courts may impose travel bans, freezing orders over personal assets, and custodial sentences. The corporate veil may be pierced where the ELP is used as a façade.
Shell Company Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: Denial of Gibraltar tax treatment, reassessment on the non-resident corporate partner in its home jurisdiction, economic substance penalty up to £100,000, and possible nullification of the ELP registration.

Director personal liability: The Gibraltar Registrar may strike off the ELP and disqualify partners from holding Gibraltar corporate positions. Personal liability can attach for unpaid taxes attributable to the ELP where the partner has received distributions without provision for tax.
AML Non-Compliance and Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: AML fines up to £1,000,000 per breach, in addition to confiscation orders. The Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit may impose civil penalties, and criminal charges can lead to imprisonment up to 14 years.

Director personal liability: Registered MLROs and partners may be criminally prosecuted. Directors who participate in or conceal suspicious transactions face personal fines, travel bans, and imprisonment.
Transfer Pricing and Thin Capitalisation Violations

Statutory sanction: Adjustment of chargeable profits, late payment interest, and penalties up to 100% of the additional tax. A nil document exemption is not automatic if turnover exceeds GBP 2,000,000.

Director personal liability: Where the directors authorise artificial pricing, they may be jointly and severally liable for tax penalties; if the ELP is insolvent, they face disqualification and personal contribution orders.
Failure to File and Persistent Default

Statutory sanction: Automatic penalties of £1,000 per return plus daily surcharges, escalating tax-geared penalties, automatic loss of administrative reliefs, and possible strike-off of the partnership.

Director personal liability: Persistent default leads to personal liability for unpaid ELP taxes and disqualification orders. The courts may issue personal summons and reasonable foreign travel restrictions where FISA obligations are triggered.
