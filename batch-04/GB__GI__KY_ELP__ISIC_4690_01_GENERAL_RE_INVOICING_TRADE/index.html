Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax acting through the Income Tax Office of Her Majesty's Government of Gibraltar (HMGoG); registration of companies and limited partnerships is administered by the Registrar of Companies at Companies House Gibraltar; customs and excise matters are handled by the Gibraltar Customs & Excise Department. Official issuance and filing portal: HMGoG Income Tax Office e-Tax self-service platform (accessible via gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax) supported by Companies House online registry filings.

E-government filing portal

(1) File the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) registration with the Registrar of Companies under Gibraltar's Limited Partnerships Act (as amended): lodge the statement of particulars, appoint at least one general partner (ordinarily a licensed Gibraltar-regulated entity or qualifying individual), maintain a registered office in Gibraltar, and apply for 'Exempted' status on the statutory ground that the partnership carries on business wholly outside Gibraltar. (2) Obtain the Certificate of Registration and ELP registry number. (3) File the same particulars with the Income Tax Office via the e-Tax portal to obtain the ELP's statutory Tax Reference Number (TRN) and partner-level TRNs for Gibraltar-resident partners; non-resident limited partners are not required to obtain a Gibraltar TIN unless they derive Gibraltar-source income. (4) Register for Purchase Tax with the Commissioner only if taxable supplies are made in Gibraltar; a pure offshore re-invoicing operation does not trigger such registration. (5) File the annual economic substance notification/return and the income tax return (including nil returns), and appoint a Gibraltar tax representative or licensed general partner where an overseas general partner is used.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — (1) File the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) registration with the Registrar of Companies under Gibraltar's Limited Partnerships Act (as amended): lodge the statement of particulars, appoint at least one general partner (ordinarily a licensed Gibraltar-regulated entity or qualifying individual), maintain a registered office in Gibraltar, and apply for 'Exempted' status on the statutory ground that the partnership carries on business wholly outside Gibraltar. (2) Obtain the Certificate of Registration and ELP registry number. (3) File the same particulars with the Income Tax Office via the e-Tax portal to obtain the ELP's statutory Tax Reference Number (TRN) and partner-level TRNs for Gibraltar-resident partners; non-resident limited partners are not required to obtain a Gibraltar TIN unless they derive Gibraltar-source income. (4) Register for Purchase Tax with the Commissioner only if taxable supplies are made in Gibraltar; a pure offshore re-invoicing operation does not trigger such registration. (5) File the annual economic substance notification/return and the income tax return (including nil returns), and appoint a Gibraltar tax representative or licensed general partner where an overseas general partner is used.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a US-style check-the-box election. The operative statutory mechanism is the ELP ('Exempt') election made at formation: the limited partnership is fiscally transparent, no entity-level income tax arises, and non-resident partners' distributive shares of non-Gibraltar-source trading profits fall outside the Gibraltar income tax charge, subject to economic substance compliance and Gibraltar's hybrid mismatch legislation implementing OECD BEPS Action 2/EU ATAD. Partners may elect, through the partnership agreement, a definitive profit-sharing and allocation formula; the Commissioner of Income Tax respects the commercial allocation to the extent it is supported by capital, services and risk assumption. A Gibraltar-resident corporate general partner remains taxable on its Gibraltar-source fee income at the standard 12.5% corporate rate; no 'opacity' or entity-level taxation election is available to the ELP itself.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

1.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GIP nil — no economic turnover threshold applies for Purchase Tax registration; any 'taxable person' carrying on a taxable activity in Gibraltar must register with the Commissioner of Income Tax. A pure offshore re-invoicing ELP transferring title outside Gibraltar, with no physical goods entering the Gibraltar customs territory, is outside the charge to Purchase Tax and is not registrable.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies where the ELP demonstrates that (i) goods are physically exported from Gibraltar to a destination outside Gibraltar within the statutory period; or (ii) in the case of offshore re-invoicing/merchanting trade, title, risk and beneficial ownership pass to a non-resident buyer while the goods never enter the Gibraltar customs territory, placing the supply outside the scope of Purchase Tax altogether. Services directly related to exported goods and services supplied to non-resident persons are zero-rated or outside the charge; input Purchase Tax attributable to zero-rated or exported supplies is creditable and repayable through the periodic return. The evidentiary burden lies on the ELP to prove export, offshore passage, or non-Gibraltar place of supply.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Gibraltar SAD/electronic export declaration where goods physically leave Gibraltar, or the exporting jurisdiction's equivalent export declaration where goods ship directly from a third country)
  • Bill of Lading / airway bill matching the commercial invoice in vessel, container, cargo description, weight, shipment date and consignee
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank SWIFT MT103 or letter-of-credit settlement advice evidencing that the gross sale consideration was credited to the ELP's bank account in Gibraltar or a permitted jurisdiction

None. Gibraltar does not mandate real-time electronic invoicing, continuous transaction controls, fiscal clearance APIs, or digital stamping. Invoices may be issued in any standard paper or electronic format; periodic Purchase Tax and income tax returns are the sole reporting vehicles. For physical movements through Gibraltar, the Customs & Excise Department requires electronic export/import declarations via its single-window (SAD-based) system, but that is a customs documentation obligation, not an e-invoicing continuous-controls mandate.

HMGoG conducts targeted audits of zero-rated and offshore re-invoicing claims, cross-referencing customs declarations, bills of lading, bank remittances, invoice flows and UBO registers. Fraudulent or unsupported export zero-rating claims under the Purchase Tax Act 2005 attract assessments with statutory interest, civil penalties up to and extending beyond 100% of the tax evaded, criminal fines and imprisonment for false declarations, and deregistration of the ELP exempt status with retrospective clawback of any Gibraltar tax benefits for the non-compliant period. Under the Income Tax Act, dishonest conduct triggers extended fraud-period back-assessments and adverse economic substance findings against the general partner.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current and capital account convertibility applies. Neither the United Kingdom nor Gibraltar maintains exchange controls: current-account transactions (trade settlements, commissions, service fees) and capital-account flows (equity injections, partner loans, profit distributions, capital repatriation) are unrestricted and require no regulatory approval. The Gibraltar Pound circulates freely alongside Bank of England notes at a fixed 1:1 parity; GIP is not internationally traded, but conversion into GBP is always at par, and USD/EUR positions are freely convertible at market rates without quantitative limits. Profit remittance from Gibraltar to the Cayman ELP or its non-resident partners is subject to no foreign-exchange levy, minimum retention, or prior-authorisation requirement. The only practical obstacles are documentary: banks will require evidence of the underlying trade, a CRS/FATCA self-certification, and confirmation of the ELP's economic substance in Gibraltar for tax-residency and anti-abuse review.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Gibraltar and the United Kingdom impose no statutory surrender ratio, repatriation deadline, or mandatory conversion requirement for export proceeds or re-invoicing collections. The UK Exchange Control Act 1947 was repealed in 1979, and Gibraltar has never enacted exchange-surrender legislation. A commercial re-invoicing hub may therefore retain 100 per cent of export-related receipts in any permitted currency indefinitely. What remains mandatory is documentary retention for AML, transfer pricing, and sanctions-compliance audits: original supplier invoices, sales contracts, bills of lading/CMR waybills, and bank statements must be archived for at least six years, and any receivable settlement above the equivalent of EUR 10,000 may trigger an enhanced due-diligence request from the beneficiary bank, but this is a compliance measure, not an exchange-control surrender obligation.

Central bank supervision: Government of Gibraltar under the Currency Notes Act 1934 as the sole issuing authority of the Gibraltar Pound (GIP), pegged at par to GBP; monetary policy and lender-of-last-resort functions are conducted by the Bank of England; prudential and AML/CFT supervision of banks and e-money institutions is exercised by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) under the Financial Services Act 2019; HM Treasury retains sovereign foreign-exchange control policy authority over the United Kingdom and Gibraltar.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium - operationally constrained but legally available. Gibraltar authorises e-money institutions under the Financial Services Act 2019, and the ELP may hold accounts with UK FCA-authorised EMIs (Wise Business, Revolut Business, Currencycloud) or EU-authorized EMIs passported into Gibraltar. Legal constraints: EMIs cannot issue or confirm letters of credit, provide trade loans, execute SWIFT MT202 COV cover payments, or offer documentary collection services; they are legally classified as payment institutions, not deposit-takers, so no insurance protection applies to balances. Practical constraints: EMIs universally classify a Cayman-domiciled ELP as high-risk, which triggers enhanced UBO disclosure, mandatory turnover forecasting, and hold periods of up to ten business days on inbound wires; single-transaction limits are commonly capped at GBP 250,000 and aggregate monthly ceilings may not exceed GBP 1 million without re-underwriting. Viable deployment: ancillary treasury accounts for low-value operating expenses, supplier micro-payments, and preferential-rate FX conversion, with balances never exceeding GBP 100,000; core settlement must remain on Tier-1 commercial bank rails.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GBP 1,000,000 aggregate controlled transaction value triggers mandatory Local File preparation; Master File is mandatory for a multinational group with consolidated turnover above GBP 50,000,000.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations 2019 (as amended) apply; a Gibraltar resident ELP conducting distribution, trading and re-invoicing activities must demonstrate core income-generating activities in Gibraltar, including staff of adequate skill and headcount, physical premises, and strategic management and direction exercised in Gibraltar. Filing of annual economic substance returns is mandatory, and the ELP must keep contemporaneous supporting records for six years.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Fine on conviction of up to GBP 250,000 and an increase in the assessed tax of up to 200%; the Gibraltar Income Tax Officer may issue a stop-notice suspending the ELP's ability to continue trading; public procurement disqualification applies while the unpaid tax liability exceeds 10% of annual turnover.

Director personal liability: A general partner or director may face imprisonment for a term up to 7 years, civil restitution orders of three times the evaded tax, and a travel ban order pending full settlement of the tax debt; asset tracing orders are commonly issued.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Breach

Statutory sanction: For each financial period the ELP is liable to a fine of GBP 100,000 and a daily penalty of GBP 1,000 until the substance filing is corrected; the Commissioner may order compulsory dissolution and strike-off from the Gibraltar Companies Register.

Director personal liability: Directors or the non-resident general partner may be served with a substituted person notice holding them personally responsible for the entity's unpaid penalties; a cross-border director disqualification order under the Gibraltar Company (Cancellation) Regulations may be registered in the UK, Gibraltar, Isle of Man and Crown Dependencies.
