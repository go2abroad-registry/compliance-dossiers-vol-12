Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

UK: HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC), Government Gateway portal (gov.uk). Gibraltar: Income Tax Office of HM Government of Gibraltar (gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation).

E-government filing portal

UK VAT: a non-UK Exempted Limited Partnership that makes taxable supplies with goods situated in the UK must register for VAT with HMRC before the supply occurs; the standard route is Form VAT1 / online VAT registration through Government Gateway, supported by ELP formation documents, evidence of business activity, identity of partners, and expected UK turnover. HMRC may require a VAT security deposit. Gibraltar: the ELP should apply to the Gibraltar Commissioner of Income Tax for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number / TIN if it has any Gibraltar-source income or a taxable presence; no VAT registration exists because Gibraltar does not levy VAT.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — UK VAT: a non-UK Exempted Limited Partnership that makes taxable supplies with goods situated in the UK must register for VAT with HMRC before the supply occurs; the standard route is Form VAT1 / online VAT registration through Government Gateway, supported by ELP formation documents, evidence of business activity, identity of partners, and expected UK turnover. HMRC may require a VAT security deposit. Gibraltar: the ELP should apply to the Gibraltar Commissioner of Income Tax for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number / TIN if it has any Gibraltar-source income or a taxable presence; no VAT registration exists because Gibraltar does not levy VAT.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no check-the-box election analogous to the US; an Exempted Limited Partnership is normally fiscally transparent and each partner is taxed on its share of partnership income. To the extent needed for UK/Gibraltar domestic law, a binding partnership classification election may be submitted with the Gibraltar Income Tax Office to confirm pass-through treatment for non-resident partners. The ELP remains a separate taxable person for UK VAT.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

UK: £90,000 annual taxable turnover threshold for UK-established businesses; nil threshold for non-established taxable persons whose FBA stock is physically in the UK at the time of sale. Gibraltar: not applicable, as no VAT/GST is levied.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

UK VAT zero-rating applies to exports of goods supplied to a destination outside the UK where the supplier retains valid commercial and customs export evidence within the prescribed time limits. Goods sold from FBA stock within the UK to UK consumers are standard-rated. Gibraltar has no VAT, so no separate Gibraltar zero-rating claim is available.

  • Customs Export Declaration (C88/SAD) or equivalent UK Customs Declaration System record
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / transport document proving goods physically left the UK
  • Commercial invoice and sales contract / purchase order
  • Packing list and customs consignment documents
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank transfer evidence of export payment
  • Proof of buyer identity and non-UK destination

UK: no real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping mandate; VAT-registered businesses must keep digital records and file VAT returns through HMRC-compatible Making Tax Digital software/APIs. Gibraltar: no e-invoicing, clearance, or stamping mandate.

HMRC may deny zero-rating, claw back input VAT, and assess undeclared UK VAT where export evidence is missing or inadequate. Penalties range from 30% of potential lost revenue for careless inaccuracies up to 100% for deliberate concealment, plus interest and retrospective assessments; investigations may extend 4 years for careless errors and up to 20 years where deliberate non-disclosure is established.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GIP is fully convertible at 1:1 into GBP and no exchange-control legislation is in force; the former UK Exchange Control Act regime was repealed in respect of Gibraltar. Current-account convertibility is unlimited: trade invoices, Amazon marketplace commissions, FBA warehouse charges, carrier fees, customs duties, royalties and professional fees may be settled in any currency without prior-authorisation duty. Capital-account convertibility is equally unrestricted: partner capital contributions, partner loan repayments, redemption of partnership interests, dissolution distributions and profit remittances to non-resident partners are free of exchange-control approval, quantum limits and currency surrender requirements. Gibraltar levies no withholding tax on distributions to non-resident partners of a tax-transparent Exempted Limited Partnership, and no stamp duty or capital duty applies to capital injections or equity transfers. Residual friction is documentary only: banks require source-of-funds evidence, CRS/FATCA classification of the ELP (generally a Non-Financial Entity), and internal transfer-pricing documentation where intercompany funding or intragroup treasury functions are deployed.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Nil statutory obligation. Gibraltar law imposes no surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, no repatriation window and no minimum retention of export or Amazon FBA sales proceeds in GIP. Receipts denominated in GBP, EUR or USD may be accumulated in any bank account inside or outside Gibraltar without reporting to a monetary authority. The only binding constraints are AML/CTF record-keeping obligations (minimum six-year retention of transaction and verification records under the AML Act 2015), CRS/FATCA account reporting, and UBO register filing duties under the Gibraltar Beneficial Ownership (Regulations) regime. No Economic Substance filing is required for a purely passive trading partnership where economic direction remains outside Gibraltar, though a substance notification may be expected if active management is conducted from Gibraltar.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar has no autonomous central bank or central exchange-control authority. Monetary issuance and financial supervision are allocated as follows: (i) Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) — the sole statutory licensing and prudential regulator for credit institutions, payment institutions and electronic money institutions under the Financial Services Act 2019; (ii) Currency Commissioner, HM Government of Gibraltar — statutory issuer of Gibraltar pound (GIP) notes and coin under the Currency Notes Act 1934, maintaining GIP at a hard 1:1 parity with GBP under the Currency (Amendment) Act 1995; (iii) HM Treasury (UK) — extraterritorial sanctions authority whose OFSI restrictions bind all Gibraltar-incorporated persons; and (iv) Bank of England — monetary anchor for the GIP/GBP parity, without direct supervisory mandate over Gibraltar-licensed banks. The GFSC maintains the credit-institution register and applies the EBA-equivalent AML/CTF rulebook through the Financial Services (Banking) Act 2019 and the AML Act 2015.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The return is due within 30 days of service of the Commissioner's notice to file; administratively, the annual deadline is 30 November following the 30 June year-end. Non-resident managed ELPs may apply for a one-month extension by written application through the local tax agent.

Deadline: · Frequency:
