Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (Ministry of Finance), 6-8 Bonavia House, Gibraltar. Electronic filing and taxpayer portal: Gibraltar Income Tax Office e-Services, accessible via www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation.

E-government filing portal

A Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) obtains its Tax Reference Number (TRN) by submitting the Partnership Registration Form (ELP attachment to the standard income tax registration form) executed by the general partner, together with the partnership deed, the Certificate of Registration issued by the Registrar of Companies, and particulars of each partner (name, registered address, corporate TIN/VAT ID from the partner's home jurisdiction). Non-resident general partners additionally file a declaration confirming the absence of Gibraltar-source activity. The Commissioner issues the TRN within 5-10 working days; no minimum capital or share allotment is required. Where the ELP generates no Gibraltar-source income, it may apply for a certificate of non-assessment / non-residence status to evidence its tax-transparent nil position to banks and counterparties.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible — The ELP is presumptively tax transparent so that income, gains, deductions and credits are attributed to the partners and taxed in their hands. Under Gibraltar's territoriality principle, only profits arising in or derived from Gibraltar are chargeable; foreign partners of an ELP with no Gibraltar-source income have no Gibraltar tax liability and are not treated as carrying on a trade in Gibraltar merely by holding an interest in the ELP.

Available election forms

(i) Category 2 Election under Income Tax Act 2010 s.50A and the Income Tax (Exempted Limited Partnerships) Regulations 2011: the ELP may elect to be taxed as a company at entity level at 12.5% (reduced to 10% for certain qualifying activities), thereby reversing the default pass-through treatment; (ii) Category 1 default transparency: no election is filed and each partner is assessed on their allocable share; (iii) Hybrid mismatch alignment: Gibraltar applies the UK-legislated OECD BEPS Action 2 concepts contractually — there is no domestic check-the-box form; the election is documented by the Registrar of Companies filing, notified to the Commissioner, and evidenced to agents via a partner resolution. The election is irrevocable for the ELP's lifetime except on conversion to a non-ELP.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current-account and capital-account convertibility without exchange-control licensing or profit-remittance approval. GIP is convertible into GBP at par without restriction; no surrender or repatriation requirements for export earnings; non-resident partners may receive distributions without withholding tax. The only practical hurdle is bank de-risking and demonstration of source of funds under enhanced due diligence.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. There is no statutory surrender timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio and no obligation to hold proceeds in GBP. Scrap-export receipts can be retained indefinitely in USD, EUR or any convertible currency; UK-resident partners must nonetheless preserve full audit trails for HMRC worldwide-income reporting.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (ultimate monetary authority for GBP; Gibraltar pound GIP pegged at 1:1) and the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as prudential and AML/CFT supervisor of banks and FX houses; HM Government of Gibraltar is the note-issuing authority for GIP banknotes.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility: LOW-MEDIUM, and only as an ancillary expense/FX wallet. Principal legal constraint: UK-authorised EMIs no longer passport automatically into Gibraltar post-Brexit; a Gibraltar top-up licence under local electronic-money regulations is required for regulated activity. EMIs also cannot operate documentary LCs or trade finance, use narrow correspondent networks, and commonly classify scrap-metal wholesaling as higher-risk trade, so account freezes on large supplier wires are frequent. Primary settlement must remain with a tier-1 bank.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% (standard corporation tax rate for companies in Gibraltar that do not carry on a qualifying activity); 10% preferential corporate rate applies to qualifying activities, namely banking, utilities, telecommunications and online gambling. The Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) itself is fiscally transparent and is not chargeable to corporation tax; taxation arises wholly at the level of the partners on allocated profits.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Gibraltar does not impose statutory withholding tax on dividends or partnership profit distributions paid to non-residents. Distributions by the ELP to limited partners are treated as allocable profit shares and are not subject to any Gibraltar dividend withholding tax.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Return and full payment are due within seven months of the end of the ELP's accounting period; for a 31 December year-end, the statutory deadline is 31 July of the following year. The Commissioner may extend filing on written application only where a sworn declaration of facts justifies the extension.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Box 1 Registered entity legal name and Limited Partnerships Register number, plus the ELP's Income Tax Office reference number. Enter the exact ELP name as registered in Gibraltar; the non-resident general partner must cite its form of identification in the partner schedule attached to the return. Late filing of the CITR-GI triggers an initial penalty of GIP 100 plus a daily default surcharge of GIP 25 per day from the date of default, capped at 365 days. Where the return remains outstanding for more than 12 months, an additional penalty of up to 100% of the tax shown or GIP 3,000 (whichever is higher) is imposed. Late payment interest accrues at 3.5% over the prevailing Gibraltar base rate compounded daily. Persistent default enables the Commissioner to issue a best-of-judgment assessment, revoke ELP exempt status, and strike the ELP from the Limited Partnerships Register.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: There is no codified statutory monetary threshold for a Local File or Master File in the Income Tax Act 2010; the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines are incorporated administratively by the Commissioner. As a practical benchmarking threshold, contemporaneous Local File and Master File documentation is mandatory in audit practice where aggregate cross-border related-party transactions of the ELP exceed GIP 1,000,000 per annum. Country-by-Country Reporting is compulsory for the multinational group where consolidated annual revenue is EUR 750,000,000 (approximately GIP 642,000,000), with the Gibraltar filing obligation registered with the Commissioner's international tax unit.

Economic substance scope: Applicable. Gibraltar applies the OECD Forum on Harmful Tax Practices economic substance framework. An ELP trading recycled scrap copper must demonstrate, in Gibraltar, a physical office or industrial premises genuinely used for the business, a locally resident general partner exercising real management, frequent and documented board or partnership management meetings in Gibraltar, competent employees commensurate with throughput, independent local bank accounts, and complete books and records prepared in Gibraltar. A 'letterbox' ELP lacking such substance forfeits pass-through exemption, becomes assessable at 12.5% on worldwide profits, and faces de-registration; economic substance is evaluated annually against actual operational evidence, not contractual assertions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Filing

Statutory sanction: Criminal conviction under the Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended): unlimited fine and imprisonment for up to 7 years. Civil penalties of up to 100% of the tax evaded are assessable for each fraudulent year, together with late-payment interest. The ELP's exempt status is retroactively revoked for the entire six-year audit window, exposing cumulative tax at 12.5%.

Director personal liability: The general partner of the ELP has unlimited personal liability for all tax, penalties and interest. Where directors of the corporate general partner authorise or knowingly participate in false filings, the corporate veil is pierced: they become personally and severally liable for civil restitution, are subject to travel bans, asset freezing and confiscation orders, may be extradited under the UK-Gibraltar mutual assistance framework, and face imprisonment. Director disqualification up to 15 years is concurrently imposed.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Revocation of ELP exempt status with retroactive effect; assessment on worldwide trading profit at 12.5%; administrative penalty of up to GIP 25,000; deregistration from the Limited Partnerships Register under the Limited Partnerships Act; and adverse exchange of information reports to foreign tax authorities via TIEAs.

Director personal liability: The unlimited general partner bears full liability for the assessed corporate debts. Directors who knowingly operate a shell general partner risk unlawful-trading personal liability, disqualification, civil restitution to creditors, and restrictions on leaving Gibraltar; in egregious cases criminal liability for assisting activities of an unlicensed financial or trading enterprise is asserted.
AML / Trade-Based Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Proceeds of Crime Act (Gibraltar): confiscation orders equal to the full benefit of the criminal property; imprisonment up to 14 years; financial penalties up to GIP 500,000 or three times the amount laundered, whichever is greater. The Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit may freeze all ELP bank accounts and compel production of originals of all shipping, insurance, weight and payment records.

Director personal liability: All partners and directors with knowledge or reckless indifference face personal criminal prosecution, confiscation of personal and corporate assets, travel bans pending extradition, and joint and several civil restitution for the full transaction value. The corporate veil of the general partner is pierced; director disqualification is automatic on conviction for money laundering offences.
Transfer Pricing / Cross-Border Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: Commissioner's adjustment under the Income Tax Act 2010: the transaction is recomputed at arm's length using the most reliable OECD method, additional tax at 12.5% is assessed for each open year, late-payment interest is compounded, and penalties of up to 100% of the under-declared tax are imposed. Non-filing of Country-by-Country Report where applicable carries fines of up to GIP 50,000 plus daily default penalties.

Director personal liability: The general partner's unlimited liability extends to all TP adjustments and penalties assessed on the ELP. Directors of the general partner who approved pricing without adequate benchmarking exposure personal liability for the tax shortfall; deliberate mispricing is treated as fraudulent revenue misconduct engaging disqualification, travel restrictions, personal surety orders, and criminal prosecution for tax evasion.
