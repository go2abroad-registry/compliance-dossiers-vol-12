Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

The Limited Partnerships Act 2005 imposes no statutory minimum paid-up capital; capital contributions are agreed in the Limited Partnership Agreement and may be in cash or kind. No escrow account is statutorily required, but prudent formation practice places the initial capital contribution in escrow with a licensed Gibraltar bank until the Certificate of Registration issues. For the regulated M&A-advisory licence, the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission expects an initial net-capital buffer of at least EUR 50,000 (or GBP equivalent) evidenced by a banker's confirmation or approved professional-indemnity cover.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Clearance under the Fire (Safety) Act 2020: annual fire-risk assessment, emergency lighting, fire-door ratings, means-of-escape plans, and a fire-safety certificate issued by the Gibraltar Fire and Rescue Service before staff occupation. Occupational clearance under the Occupational Safety and Health Act 2012: first-aid provisions, electrical-installation certificates, display-screen equipment assessments, and adequate sanitary facilities. Structural load inspection and occupancy certificate from the Buildings Department under the Building Act 1984 for the serviced office suite, including confirmation that floor-loading limits accommodate the intended filing and IT equipment.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (Office of the Commissioner of Income Tax), 142 Main Street, Gibraltar GX11 1AA; official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi (Income Tax Office section). Customs and excise matters are handled separately by Her Majesty's Customs, Gibraltar (Customs House). Entity registration is effected through the Registrar of Companies (Companies House Gibraltar) within the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission cluster.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: A Gibraltar-licensed formation agent files the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) application under the Limited Partnerships Act 1991 with the Registrar of Companies, drawing the Certificate of Registration. Step 2: Upon issuance, the general partner registers the ELP and its partners with the Income Tax Office using the prescribed partnership particulars and partner declaration forms. Step 3: The Income Tax Office assigns a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN) to the ELP, and a separate domestic TIN to each chargeable Gibraltar-resident partner; non-resident partners are recorded for transparency reporting but are generally outside Gibraltar charging provisions. Step 4: The general partner must file an annual partnership information return even where no Gibraltar tax is payable, disclosing each partner's share of profits and fees, thereby supporting issuance of a Certificate of Compliance for the partnership and its non-resident partners.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible: The ELP is statutorily tax-transparent under Gibraltar law; the partnership is not a taxpayer and each partner is charged in their own hands on their allocated share. Territorial scope applies: only partners resident in Gibraltar, or non-residents deriving Gibraltar-source income, are within the Gibraltar tax net. No entity-level corporation tax is imposed on ELP profits.

Available election forms

Gibraltar operates no US-style check-the-box regime. Under the Income Tax Act 2010, partnership transparency is mandatory; no election exists to tax the ELP as a company. Available elections and mechanisms: (i) partner-level ascertainment of income shares and, for qualifying non-resident partners, elections to be taxed under the gross income-based system (GIB) rather than the assessable income system; (ii) the general partner may elect, on behalf of the partnership, the method for allocating expenses where partners are subject to differing systems; (iii) hybrid mismatch elections under Gibraltar's anti-hybrid rules (Taxation – Anti-Hybrid Rules) to determine whether the ELP or a partner is treated as a reverse hybrid or transparent entity for OECD BEPS purposes; (iv) UK entities investing through the ELP may separately be subject to UK S. 248A hybrid-transparent election rules in their own filings, which do not alter the ELP's Gibraltar transparency.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST operates in Gibraltar. Gibraltar is outside the EU VAT area and does not apply UK VAT. Indirect taxation consists of customs import duties and excise duties on physical goods under the Gibraltar Customs Tariff. Cross-border M&A advisory services are entirely outside any Gibraltar indirect tax system.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable for services — Gibraltar imposes no VAT/GST and therefore no turnover-based registration threshold exists. Commercial importers of physical goods must register with Her Majesty's Customs (Gibraltar) and obtain a customs identifier before clearing goods; no de minimis threshold applies to commercial importers. The threshold concept is therefore confined to customs duty valuation bands expressed in Gibraltar Pounds (GIP), not to advisory service turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — Gibraltar levies no VAT/GST, so there is no 0% export rating or refund mechanism for advisory services. Success fees and retainer fees of the M&A advisory boutique are outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect tax and are not treated as exports for VAT purposes. If the ELP ever imported physical goods duty would arise at import under the Gibraltar Customs Tariff; no UK VAT, and no EU VAT, is chargeable or recoverable in Gibraltar.

Not applicable — Gibraltar has no e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or mandatory API reporting regime. Commercial electronic invoices and PDF invoices are acceptable. Retention is mandatory: under Income Tax Act 2010 record-keeping requirements and Gibraltar Financial Services Commission AML rules, invoices, engagement letters, and underlying fee records must be preserved for at least six years.

Gibraltar maintains no exchange-control rules and imposes no statutory foreign-exchange repatriation deadline; the 180-day parameter is adopted as best-practice fee-collection policy to ensure clean revenue recognition and document integrity. There is no export VAT clawback regime because no VAT zero-rating exists in Gibraltar. Scrutiny instead targets income tax and AML compliance: negligent or deliberately incorrect partnership returns attract civil penalties up to 100% of the tax undercharged plus default interest, with criminal fines and imprisonment for fraudulent statements under the Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended); cross-border success fees are additionally subject to Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 enforcement and GFSC disciplinary sanctions, including licence revocation and unlimited fines, if fee flows are connected to money laundering, bribery, or sanctioned counterparties.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% (flat Gibraltar Corporation Tax on chargeable profits; Income Tax Act 2010, s. 10A)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% (Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on distributions, whether to resident or non-resident partners; dividend distributions are not assessable income where paid out of taxed profits).

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Gibraltar assesses on a preceding-year basis: the year of assessment runs 1 January to 31 December. The Comptroller issues a notice requiring delivery of CTR-1; the return must be filed within 30 days of the date of the notice, and in all cases by no later than 30 June immediately following the calendar year in which the ELP's accounting period ended.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 aggregate value of controlled transactions per fiscal year triggers mandatory contemporaneous Gibraltar Local File documentation. A group Master File is required where the multinational group's consolidated turnover equals or exceeds EUR 50 million, aligned with OECD BEPS Action 13. Documentation must be submitted with CTR-1 or produced within 30 days of a formal notice to produce.

Economic substance scope: Mandatory. The Economic Substance (Companies and Limited Partnerships) Regulations 2019 apply because M&A advisory is an analysis, advisory and headquarters-type service relevant activity. The ELP must maintain in Gibraltar: a physical office or premises; a minimum of three qualifying full-time employees, including at least one senior executive; core income-generating activities actually performed in Gibraltar; and an annual ESR return filed with the GFSC. Pure holding ELPs may invoke the reduced substance test. Failure results in loss of tax residence certification, automatic information exchange to HMRC, financial penalties up to GIP 50,000, and ultimately removal from the Gibraltar register.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Tax Fraud

Statutory sanction: Tax-geared penalty up to 100% of the tax undercharged; court fine up to GIP 1,000,000; imprisonment up to 7 years under Income Tax Act 2010, ss. 115-117; forfeiture of assets.

Director personal liability: The general partner and controlling officers are jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax; civil restitution orders; worldwide asset-freezing orders; travel bans and passport surrender; fraudulent trading findings render personal liability unlimited.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Compensating transfer-pricing adjustment increasing chargeable profits; penalty up to GIP 20,000 for defective documentation; daily penalties for failure to produce records; interest on unpaid tax.

Director personal liability: Directors of the corporate general partner may be personally assessed where the ELP is insolvent; HMRC and GITO may pursue the senior accounting officer for certification failures; disqualification orders up to 15 years.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: GIP 50,000 automatic fine; continuing default attracts an additional GIP 50,000 every six months; cancellation of tax residence certificate; automatic exchange of information to HMRC; ultimate strike-off and dissolution.

Director personal liability: The general partner and Gibraltar administrator are criminally liable for false ESR declarations — up to 2 years' imprisonment; disqualification from acting as a Gibraltar officer; personal liability for unpaid penalties of the struck-off ELP.
Anti-Money Laundering / Sanctions Breach

Statutory sanction: Financial penalties up to GIP 200,000 per AML breach under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015; confiscation order equal to the value of proceeds; revocation of the GFSC M&A advisory licence; referral to the Gibraltar Criminal Court.

Director personal liability: MLRO and general partner face up to 14 years' imprisonment for money laundering; travel bans and asset restraint orders; personal civil liability to defrauded clients; prohibition from acting as MLRO or compliance officer anywhere in the British Overseas Territories.
Persistent Non-Filing / Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Automatic penalty of GIP 1,000 per return; daily penalties of GIP 100 up to GIP 30,000; tax-geared penalty up to 100%; compulsory strike-off of the ELP; publication of the non-compliant taxpayer's name; denial of Gibraltar tax residence certificates.

Director personal liability: General partner and managers disqualified from acting as officers or general partners in Gibraltar for up to 15 years; personal liability for restoration costs and all outstanding taxes; contempt of court powers permit committal to prison for breach of compliance orders.
