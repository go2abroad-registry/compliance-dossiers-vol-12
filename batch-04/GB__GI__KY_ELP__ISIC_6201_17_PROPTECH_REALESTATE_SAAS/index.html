Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital. The ELP issues no share capital; partners' contributions are fixed contractually in the Limited Partnership Agreement and may be as low as USD 1. No escrow or blocked-account requirement applies under Gibraltar law.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety compliance per the Fire Safety at Work (Commercial Premises) Regulations 2018 - annual fire-risk assessment, certificate of fire-safety measures, and quarterly evacuation drill record. Occupational clearance under the Safety at Work Act (Cap. 191) - workplace risk assessment, first-aid provision, and display-screen-equipment assessments for software staff. Building Act 2018 - the registered office lease must evidence structural load certification for office use at a maximum of 10-person occupancy; no zoning change or rezoning is required for a SaaS office.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital. The ELP issues no share capital; partners' contributions are fixed contractually in the Limited Partnership Agreement and may be as low as USD 1. No escrow or blocked-account requirement applies under Gibraltar law.

Pro-Forma First Board Resolution Draft
RESOLVED, that the General Partner approves and executes the Statement of Limited Partnership and the Limited Partnership Agreement for formation of the Exempted Limited Partnership under the Limited Partnerships Act 2019 (Gibraltar); RESOLVED FURTHER, that the initial capital contribution of USD 1 be subscribed and credited by the Initial Limited Partner in the partnership books, no share capital being issued; RESOLVED FURTHER, that the registered office be established at [Registered Office], Gibraltar, and that [Corporate Services Provider] be appointed registered agent and designated representative for service of process; RESOLVED FURTHER, that [Director A] and [Director B] be appointed authorized officers of the General Partner to file the registration application, execute banking mandates, and open the partnership's operating accounts.
Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Not applicable. No Gibraltar industrial licensing board exercises jurisdiction over ISIC 6201 (computer programming and cloud software services); the platform is outside the licensing perimeter of the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission and the Trade Licensing Act.

Category: No statutory operating or manufacturing permit required. The SaaS platform is not a regulated financial service; however, the operator must file a data-controller registration with the Gibraltar Regulatory Authority under the Data Protection Act 2019 (GDPR-equivalent), which is a ministerial compliance filing, not a commercial license.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (Commissioner of Income Tax); online portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation/income-tax

E-government filing portal

For a non-resident-owned Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP): (1) cause the ELP to be constituted and registered with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies/Companies Registry under the Limited Partnerships Act, with no minimum capital requirement; (2) file the partnership statement/register of partners and registered office particulars; (3) instruct a Gibraltar tax agent to notify the Income Tax Office of formation and file the partnership's initial tax notification; (4) obtain the Gibraltar tax reference number issued to the partnership by the Income Tax Office; and (5) if the ELP relies on the territorial source principle, apply for or record a nil/non-resident assessment position for foreign-source income and ensure non-resident partners provide tax residence certificates.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; Territorial_Scope; non-resident partners are not chargeable in Gibraltar on non-Gibraltar-source income, and the ELP itself is not subject to entity-level income tax on such income where the territorial exclusion applies.

Available election forms

No U.S.-style check-the-box election exists. The ELP is statutorily transparent; partners are taxed on their distributive shares. Available mechanisms include a formal territorial-source-only filing/application to the Income Tax Office claiming non-Gibraltar-source classification, and the ability to file a partnership return with nil/non-resident apportionments. No local hybrid-mismatch election exists; U.K./OECD hybrid mismatch rules may apply insofar as payment deductions are claimed in the United Kingdom.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Not Applicable — Gibraltar does not operate a VAT, GST, or sales/use tax system; only limited customs duties/import duties and excise taxes are imposed, not on SaaS exports.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

None — there is no turnover-based registration threshold for VAT/GST or sales/use tax because no such indirect tax is levied in Gibraltar.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable to VAT: Gibraltar levies no VAT, so there is no export VAT zero-rating or input VAT refund regime. For direct-tax territoriality, PropTech SaaS export income from non-resident customers may remain outside Gibraltar income tax if the services are effectively performed outside Gibraltar and do not give rise to a Gibraltar permanent establishment or Gibraltar-source income.

  • Customs Export Declaration (only for physical goods; not required for pure SaaS services)
  • Commercial invoice / SaaS subscription contract
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (where goods are exported)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank settlement record evidencing FX repatriation
  • Customer tax residence certificate / non-resident confirmation
  • Service delivery logs and IP address or hosted-server evidence

None. Gibraltar imposes no real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction controls, e-invoicing clearance, API filing, or digital stamping obligation. Invoices may be issued in ordinary PDF/paper form; accounting and AML records must be retained and produced to the Commissioner/Financial Intelligence Unit on demand.

No VAT clawback regime applies because Gibraltar does not administer VAT. For income-tax territorial/foreign-source claims, incorrect or fraudulent filings expose the ELP and partners to Income Tax Office assessments outside normal time limits, interest, tax-geared penalties, withdrawal of territorial-source treatment, and criminal liability for deliberate evasion under Gibraltar income tax law.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GIP and GBP are fully and freely convertible at the statutory 1:1 peg with zero capital account restrictions. The Gibraltar current account and capital account are completely open: profit remittances, partnership distributions, capital contributions and intra-group service payments to non-residents require no exchange control approval, no Central Bank filing and are not subject to withholding taxes. The Gibraltar ELP is fiscally transparent under the Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended); non-resident limited partners are not Gibraltar-taxable on distributions, and the ELP may maintain multi-currency accounts (USD, EUR, GBP/GIP) without restriction. Residual caution: if the ELP's place of effective management were ever determined to be in the UK, the UK CFC and mixed-membership anti-hybrid rules could recharacterise profits; this risk is mitigated by Gibraltar-based substance providers, a local general partner and board meetings held in Gibraltar.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Gibraltar has no statutory obligation to surrender, convert, or repatriate export proceeds, and no requirement to sell foreign currency earnings to the Government or to any authorised dealer. SaaS subscription receipts and PropTech platform revenues (ISIC 6201 / NAICS 531390) received in USD via Stripe Billing or ACH Direct may be retained offshore in USD, converted freely at market rates, or hedged as the ELP sees fit. The only applicable reporting obligation arises under the GFSC AML framework: accountable persons must record cross-border wire transfers and maintain transaction documentation; an automatic surveillance threshold applies at EUR 10,000, but no conversion ratio or timeline is imposed.

Central bank supervision: The Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the statutory prudential and conduct regulator for all credit institutions, payment institutions and electronic money institutions in Gibraltar, operating under the Financial Services Act 2019. The Government of Gibraltar (HM Government of Gibraltar) acts as the monetary authority with exclusive right to issue Gibraltar pound (GIP) banknotes and coins under the Legal Tender Notes Act, as amended. There is no domestic central bank; GIP is legally pegged at 1:1 to GBP, and the banking system relies on Bank of England settlement access through UK correspondent banks for CHAPS, Faster Payments and SWIFT clearing.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: HIGH for UK-authorised electronic money institutions (EMIs) such as Airwallex UK Ltd, Wise Payments Ltd, Currencycloud/Treasury Prime partners, or specialised corporate e-wallet providers that accept Gibraltar-registered partnerships as customers. These provide Faster Payments and CHAPS in GBP plus local US routing/ACH via partner banks. Legal constraints: (1) no Gibraltar-licensed EMI can passport into the UK post-Brexit, and a UK EMI onboarding the Gibraltar ELP must file a standard FCA AML/KYC file; (2) a Gibraltar-licensed EMI under the Payment Services Act 2019 has no direct settlement account at the Bank of England and must sponsor through a Gibraltar bank, which makes it redundant as the primary treasury rail; (3) for USD ACH Direct and Stripe Billing settlements, the EMI must demonstrate a US money-transmission licence and maintain segregated pooled accounts at an FDIC-insured sponsor bank. A dual-EMI architecture is advised: (a) UK EMI for GBP/Faster Payment operations and (b) US sponsor bank account for ACH; both sweep daily to the Gibraltar ELP's primary Barclays account, preserving a single point of liquidity control.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Cayman ELP (KY) will initiate a SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfer to the Gibraltar ELP's primary account at Barclays Bank PLC (Gibraltar Branch), SWIFT BARCGBGI, or at Jyske Bank (Gibraltar) Ltd, SWIFT JYSKGBGI. Field 23E must contain the transaction type code 'CRED' and field 70 (Remittance Information) must state: 'Capital contribution pursuant to Section [X] of the Limited Partnership Agreement — ISIC 6201 PropTech Software Activities — no goods shipped'. For capital movements exceeding USD 1,000,000, the sending bank should process a pre-advice via MT103 (field 33B in USD) and a separate MT199 for documentary compliance. No gilbraltar exchange control filing is needed; for UK statistical purposes, the correspondent will classify the entry as 'Other capital transfer' with the EU/UK BOP code 'OCTCIN' or equivalent, but no conversion or surrender is triggered. Verification obligations: the receiving bank will require (i) a notarised GP resolution authorising the capital injection, (ii) the capital call schedule, (iii) source of funds letter from the Cayman ELP's banker, and (iv) proof of the KY ELP's legal existence. The funds may be held in USD or converted to GIP/GBP at the prevailing interbank rate; conversion is settled SPOT (T+2) and requires no derivative documentation.Primary clearing rails for trade settlements are SWIFT MT103 for non-UK and non-US cross-border vendor payments (cloud infrastructure, data licences, subcontractors); MT202 for bank-to-bank cover payments; UK Faster Payments (FPS) for domestic UK suppliers up to GBP 1,000,000; CHAPS for immediate same-day UK high-value disbursements; and SEPA Credit Transfer (SCT) for EU-based vendors, using the bank's IBAN capability. For US-domiciled subcontractors and platform costs, the ELP will aggregate funds in the US bridge account and disburse via NACHA ACH (CCD/CTX) or a SWIFT MT103 with US routing; this is aligned with the 'ACH_Direct' rail in the target payment stack. Commercial Letters of Credit (MT700/MT707) are permitted but commercially discouraged for recurring SaaS subscriptions; standby LCs are acceptable only as performance guarantees for enterprise PropTech contracts where landlords require deposit security. All outbound payments must be matched to the underlying vendor invoice and screened against OFSI and EU sanctions lists; a daily cash sweep consolidates dollar balances into the GBP/GIP treasury account at the primary Gibraltar bank, keeping separation of duty between settlement, disbursement and FX execution.Outright GBP/USD spot and forward contracts up to 24 months with a GFSC-licensed counterparty, permitted without FCA leverage limits where the ELP is categorised as a professional client under MiFID II; documentation is a standard ISDA 2002 Master Agreement with CSA.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for exotic or restricted currencies (e.g., emerging-market local currencies if the PropTech platform later accepts tenant payments from non-convertible jurisdictions), permitted on a deliverable USD basis, subject to the bank's internal emerging-market limits and EMIR trade reporting.Cross-currency interest rate swaps (GBP/USD) to swap floating-rate USD SaaS receipts into fixed GBP liabilities, permissible where the bank confirms the ELP's underlying commercial exposure, with netting under a single ISDA netting set.European-style OTC currency options (puts and calls on GBP/USD) for risk management of the annual profit distribution to the Cayman ELP, with premium payable in GBP and no short option positions permitted; mandatory board approval memorialised in treasury minutes.Simple FX forwards on EUR/GBP for settlement of European hosting and staff costs, hedged using the standard ECB Euro FX fixing as reference, with no exotic barriers permitted.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility Rating: HIGH for UK-authorised electronic money institutions (EMIs) such as Airwallex UK Ltd, Wise Payments Ltd, Currencycloud/Treasury Prime partners, or specialised corporate e-wallet providers that accept Gibraltar-registered partnerships as customers. These provide Faster Payments and CHAPS in GBP plus local US routing/ACH via partner banks. Legal constraints: (1) no Gibraltar-licensed EMI can passport into the UK post-Brexit, and a UK EMI onboarding the Gibraltar ELP must file a standard FCA AML/KYC file; (2) a Gibraltar-licensed EMI under the Payment Services Act 2019 has no direct settlement account at the Bank of England and must sponsor through a Gibraltar bank, which makes it redundant as the primary treasury rail; (3) for USD ACH Direct and Stripe Billing settlements, the EMI must demonstrate a US money-transmission licence and maintain segregated pooled accounts at an FDIC-insured sponsor bank. A dual-EMI architecture is advised: (a) UK EMI for GBP/Faster Payment operations and (b) US sponsor bank account for ACH; both sweep daily to the Gibraltar ELP's primary Barclays account, preserving a single point of liquidity control.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File threshold: annual turnover exceeding GIP 10 million; Master File threshold: group consolidated turnover exceeding GIP 50 million; Country-by-Country Report threshold: group turnover exceeding GIP 750 million. The ELP is part of the KY_ELP group for these calculations.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar ESR Regulations under the Taxation (Compliance) Act 2019 require entities generating taxable income from relevant activities to demonstrate substance. For the PropTech SaaS and real estate rental management platform, the ELP must maintain a registered office in Gibraltar, employ at least one Gibraltar-resident decision-maker, have a physical office, retain adequate staff, and produce board minutes evidencing strategic control. The Cayman parent's holding of the ELP interest is classified as pure equity holding and requires only adequate personnel and premises in Gibraltar. Failure to satisfy ESR triggers reassessment, denial of treaty benefits, and monetary penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Deliberate Under-Declaration

Statutory sanction: Full reassessment of Gibraltar-source profits plus a tax-geared penalty up to 100%; criminal fine up to GIP 250,000; imprisonment up to 7 years under the Income Tax Act.

Director personal liability: Directors of the Gibraltar corporate general partner may be liable for fraudulent trading under Companies Act obligations; civil restitution orders for the full tax avoided; travel bans under the Proceeds of Crime Act; disqualification from holding directorships for up to 15 years.
Shell Company / ESR Evasion

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner may disregard the ELP's tax-transparent nature and attribute profits directly to the UK parent, imposing a penalty of GIP 150,000 plus a daily surcharge of GIP 1,000 until substance evidence is produced.

Director personal liability: Officers can be held personally liable for outstanding penalties up to GIP 100,000 each; restrictive measures such as a prohibition on leaving Gibraltar can be imposed where collection is at risk.
AML / CFT Compliance Breach

Statutory sanction: GFSC financial penalties up to GIP 5 million per breach; criminal prosecution under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 (Gibraltar) for money laundering; confiscation of proceeds; licence revocation if any payment-service threshold is exceeded.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers face personal criminal liability where they knew or suspected money laundering and failed to report; imprisonment up to 5 years; civil recovery orders against personal bank accounts.
Transfer Pricing / Profit Shifting Abuses

Statutory sanction: Adjustment of assessable profits to arm's-length level; application of the UK's Diverted Profits Tax at 25% on the shifted profit; Gibraltar secondary adjustment plus interest at 6% p.a.; cumulative penalties up to 100% of adjusted tax.

Director personal liability: After a TP adjustment, directors of the Gibraltar general partner may be made jointly and severally liable for the corporate partner's tax debt if they knowingly approved non-arm's-length pricing and caused insolvency.
