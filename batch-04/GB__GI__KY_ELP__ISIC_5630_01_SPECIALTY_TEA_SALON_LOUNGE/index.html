Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax), Ministry of Finance; Gibraltar Companies House for entity registration; Gibraltar Financial Services Commission for licensed business oversight; portal: HM Government of Gibraltar Income Tax Office website

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) must file the partnership registration statement with Companies House under the Limited Partnerships Act. Upon formation, the ELP must register with the Income Tax Office and obtain a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number. There is no separate VAT ID because Gibraltar does not levy VAT/GST. The ELP must provide its Tax Reference Number on the annual partnership tax return and on any Gibraltar withholding or reporting forms. For a pass-through partnership, the ELP itself is registered as a non-resident transparent entity, and each non-resident partner is not required to file a Gibraltar individual income tax return unless the partner derives Gibraltar-source income.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible; territorial scope: Gibraltar taxation applies only to profits or income sourced in Gibraltar; the ELP is transparent for income tax purposes and non-resident partners are outside the charge to Gibraltar tax on non-Gibraltar-source income — A foreign-owned Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) must file the partnership registration statement with Companies House under the Limited Partnerships Act. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; territorial scope: Gibraltar taxation applies only to profits or income sourced in Gibraltar; the ELP is transparent for income tax purposes and non-resident partners are outside the charge to Gibraltar tax on non-Gibraltar-source income

Available election forms

Under the Income Tax Act, a partnership may be treated as fiscally transparent by default; no equivalent to a US check-the-box election exists. The ELP may elect into the qualifying company / qualifying category provisions to access the 0% corporate rate only for profits arising in Gibraltar. No hybrid mismatch election is available because Gibraltar has no VAT and applies a territorial-income income tax; partners must be mindful of mismatch rules in the investor’s home jurisdiction. No separate entity-level vs partnership-tax election exists for the ELP.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Not Applicable – Gibraltar does not operate a VAT, GST, or general sales and use tax; indirect charges are limited to customs and excise duties on select imported goods and certain locally consumed products

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable; no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. There is no general turnover tax, and an ELP engaged in a Specialty Tea Salon Lounge does not trigger any consumption-tax registration. Customs-excise registration may be required only if the business imports dutiable goods; that obligation arises per transaction, not as a turnover threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Because Gibraltar has no VAT/GST, there is no export zero-rating mechanism, no VAT refund system, and no input VAT to recover. Exports of loose-leaf tea, ceramic products, and services from Gibraltar fall outside the scope of any indirect tax. The ELP should nonetheless maintain standard export documentation to evidence legitimate cross-border trading for income tax territoriality and for foreign customs authorities in destination jurisdictions.

  • Gibraltar Customs export declaration or Single Administrative Document (electronically lodged through the applicable customs inventory system)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (or other proof of export accepted by customs authorities)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank transfer evidence showing payment of export proceeds from the overseas buyer

Gibraltar does not impose mandatory electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping. No government-authorised e-invoicing API or clearance platform applies to business-to-consumer or business-to-business sales. The ELP is only required to maintain proper accounting records and invoices as substantiation for income tax and corporate records; electronic invoicing is permitted but not mandated.

Although export zero-rating does not exist, false statements to Gibraltar Customs in respect of export declarations, duty-relief classifications, or cash approximations may attract penalties up to £5,000 per offence, forfeiture of goods, retrospective assessments, and clawback of any improperly obtained duty relief. Persistent or fraudulent conduct may trigger criminal liability for the partnership and its general partner, including fines and imprisonment of responsible officers, under the Customs Ordinance and the Income Tax (Penalties and Interest) Regulations.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar imposes no statutory exchange-control restrictions on current-account or capital-account payments. GIP is not independently tradeable outside Gibraltar but is operationally fully convertible through licensed banks at the fixed 1:1 sterling peg. Current-account payments for trade, services and royalties are free, and capital injections by non-resident limited partners of an Exempted Limited Partnership require no central-bank approval. Profit remittances to non-resident partners attract no Gibraltar withholding tax on the ELP's pass-through profits. Practical hurdles are not legal convertibility restrictions but correspondent-bank AML/KYC, FATCA/CRS classification, source-of-wealth evidence and the ELP's tax transparency profile.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender regime exists in Gibraltar. There is no mandated timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio and no obligation to repatriate foreign-currency export receipts into GIP or GBP. Export proceeds may be retained in the currency of receipt, subject only to the entity's normal accounting and AML/CTF reporting obligations. If the operating bank account is base-currency GBP, the bank may auto-convert foreign-currency receipts at its commercial dealing rate, but this is a contractual bank feature rather than an exchange-control requirement.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the competent prudential and financial-crime banking regulator; the Government of Gibraltar issues Gibraltar Pound (GIP) currency under the Currency Notes Act 1934. Gibraltar has no separately constituted central bank; the monetary anchor is the pound sterling, with GIP pegged at 1:1 to GBP.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility is Low-to-Medium. UK and EEA electronic-money institutions cannot passport into Gibraltar after Brexit, and an offshore digital EMI cannot hold funds as a regulated deposit or provide bank-grade payment clearing in Gibraltar. EMIs may be used only as ancillary low-value card-collection or payment-initiation channels, with balances safeguarded as e-money, not bank deposits. For core treasury operations, supplier payments, escrow and FX execution, a Gibraltar-licensed commercial bank must be the anchor rail. Any EMI onboarding requires separate KYB checks and does not satisfy the statutory banking requirement for capital contributions or commercial letters of credit.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Partner capital contributions must be routed by SWIFT MT103 credit transfer from the contributor's regulated bank to the Gibraltar bank's UK correspondent nostro account, with final credit to the ELP's segregated capital escrow account. The payment instruction must include the full ELP legal name as beneficiary, ISO20022 purpose code CAPL, reference KY_ELP-ISIC5630-CONTRIB-001, and a short source-of-funds narrative in the cover/account details field. The remitter must exactly match the registered General Partner or Limited Partner in the ELP deed; any third-party payment requires a notarised authorization and source-of-funds opinion. Contributions exceeding USD 50,000 or GBP equivalent will trigger mandatory CRS/FATCA self-certification, UBO verification and enhanced due diligence prior to ledger credit.Supplier settlements for tea and ceramic imports are executed primarily by SWIFT MT103 directly to the supplier's bank account. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File and Master File are triggered when the ELP's connected-party transactions (tea and ceramic supply-chain purchases, management fees, intra-group financing and cost recharges) exceed GBP 1,000,000 in an accounting period; separate transactional documentation is required for any related-party financing balance above GBP 500,000. Country-by-Country reporting applies to multinational groups with consolidated revenue of EUR 750,000,000 or more.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) and the Income Tax (Substance Requirements) Act apply to Gibraltar tax-resident companies carrying on designated relevant activities. Retail food-and-beverage service under ISIC 5630 is not a designated relevant activity, so no statutory minimum-economic-substance test is triggered for the tea salon itself; however, the ELP must still evidence genuine physical premises, employed staff, and core income-generating activities (procurement, pricing, service delivery, treasury decisions) in the jurisdiction of tax attribution, and any Gibraltar-resident corporate general partner engaged in relevant activities remains fully subject to ESR compliance and notification.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion — Cash and Card Receipt Suppression

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of up to 100% of the tax understated plus statutory interest; criminal prosecution for fraudulent evasion with unlimited fines on indictment and confiscation of the proceeds of crime.

Director personal liability: The General Partner and any limited partner participating in management are jointly and severally liable; the court may pierce the ELP veil on evidence of sham trading, imposing personal civil restitution, asset-freezing orders, travel bans, and imprisonment of up to 5 years.
Shell Company / Sham ELP and ESR Evasion

Statutory sanction: Revocation of the ELP's non-residence exemption and re-characterisation as a Gibraltar tax-resident partnership assessed at 12.5% on worldwide profits; ESR penalties of GBP 10,000 fixed plus daily penalties up to GBP 5,000 for resident corporate partners carrying relevant activities; cancellation of the ELP exempt status by the Companies Registry.

Director personal liability: Limited partners who intervene in management forfeit their statutory limitation of liability; the General Partner faces disqualification as a fit-and-proper person, personal liability for the full assessed tax debt, and up to 5 years' disqualification for facilitating a shell arrangement.
AML / Financial Crime — Cash Handling without Customer Due Diligence

Statutory sanction: Offences under the Gibraltar Money Laundering Act 2018 and the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 attract unlimited fines, imprisonment of up to 14 years, confiscation orders, suspension of the salon's trading licence, and revocation of banking and card-acquiring facilities by the Financial Services Commission.

Director personal liability: Directors and the General Partner are personally liable for assisting money laundering; exposure includes asset seizure, travel bans, disqualification orders, and custodial sentences.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation — Related-Party Underpricing

Statutory sanction: Commissioner adjustment of the consideration to arm's length, additional assessments at 12.5%, late-payment interest, penalties of up to 100% of the additional tax for deliberate transfer-pricing errors, and denial of treaty relief where the arrangement lacks commercial rationale.

Director personal liability: A General Partner who knowingly certifies inaccurate transfer-pricing documentation incurs joint and several personal liability for the adjusted tax, professional disqualification, and criminal sanctions where fraud is established.
Treaty Relief Abuse — Dividend and Income Stripping

Statutory sanction: Denial of DTA relief, withdrawal of the Tax Residence Certificate, assessment at 12.5% with penalties of up to 100% of the tax avoided, referral to the HMRC Mutual Agreement Procedure, and public flagging of the arrangement under applicable anti-abuse rules.

Director personal liability: Partners implicated in the abusive conduit lose limited-liability protection; personal restitution, travel restrictions, and imprisonment for tax fraud apply where deliberate falsification of residency and ownership facts is proved.
