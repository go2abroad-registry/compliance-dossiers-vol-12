Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Commissioner of Income Tax (Income Tax Office, Ministry of Finance, Government of Gibraltar); official portal: www.gibraltar.gov.gi (Income Tax e-services section).

E-government filing portal

Step 1 — Incorporate the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under the Limited Partnerships Act; the Registrar issues a Partnership Registration Number. Step 2 — File the prescribed partnership notification and registration form with the Income Tax Office; the Commissioner issues a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number for the ELP as a transparent filing entity and, where assessable, separate Tax Reference Numbers for the general partner and each limited partner. Step 3 — For non-resident partners with no Gibraltar-source income or no remittances into Gibraltar, submit an annual territorial confirmation or nil return. No VAT identification number is obtainable or required because Gibraltar levies no VAT or GST.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — Step 1 — Incorporate the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under the Limited Partnerships Act; the Registrar issues a Partnership Registration Number. Step 2 — File the prescribed partnership notification and registration form with the Income Tax Office; the Commissioner issues a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number for the ELP as a transparent filing entity and, where assessable, separate Tax Reference Numbers for the general partner and each limited partner. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election and no hybrid-mismatch election exists under Gibraltar law; partnership tax transparency is mandatory under the Income Tax Act 2010. The only statutory election available to an ELP is the application to the Registrar of Companies for a Certificate of Exemption, which grants administrative relief from annual return filing and certain name-ending requirements but does not alter the partnership's substantive tax characterisation. Non-resident partners may additionally rely on territorial and remittance basis rules to exclude foreign-source income from the Gibraltar charge.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT / GST / Sales-and-Use Tax — Gibraltar is outside the EU VAT area; only a limited Purchase Tax plus customs and excise duty applies to specified goods at importation.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — there is no VAT or GST registration threshold in Gibraltar. Importers and dealers in goods subject to Purchase Tax or excise duty must nevertheless obtain a customs trader registration with HM Customs (Gibraltar), irrespective of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in VAT form: Gibraltar has no VAT, so no export zero-rating claim or VAT refund mechanism exists. Economic zero-rating is achieved through the territorial income tax boundary — foreign-source income of non-resident ELP partners is outside the charge to Gibraltar tax unless received or remitted into Gibraltar. Digital B2B marketplace platform fees invoiced abroad by the ELP therefore carry no Gibraltar indirect tax and require no refund filing.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration (electronic manifest / SAD-equivalent clearance record)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing the physical export route and foreign consignee
  • Inward remittance credit voucher or bank statement evidencing FX repatriation of export proceeds

None. Gibraltar does not impose real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing API, or digital stamping mandates. Standard commercial e-invoicing is unrestricted; electronic customs declarations are filed through the Gibraltar customs clearance system for controlled goods, and invoice records must evidence territorial-source profit for income tax purposes.

The Commissioner of Income Tax may raise assessments within six years of the filing date (twelve years in cases of fraud or negligent withholding), with civil penalties of up to 100% of the tax evaded plus interest. Deliberate misstatement of territorial-source facts, invoicing, or export documentation is a criminal offence punishable by unlimited fine and/or imprisonment. Abuse of ELP exemption status can trigger withdrawal of the Certificate of Exemption and retrospective taxation; because no VAT applies, no export-VAT clawback exists, but HM Customs (Gibraltar) may seize goods and impose separate excise and customs penalties for documentation irregularities.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar has full convertibility on both capital and current accounts; no foreign exchange control act or ECB-style approval gates apply. The Gibraltar Pound (GIP) is freely convertible into GBP and all major currencies. Profit remittance faces zero exchange-control hurdles; the only practical precondition is submission of the annual partnership tax return (for pass-through confirmation) and documentary support for dividend/distribution payments to the Cayman ELP partners.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender timeline or mandatory conversion ratios exist for export or marketplace sales proceeds in Gibraltar. A B2B marketplace operator may retain, convert, or repatriate trade receipts in any permitted currency without GFSC or Bank of England filing. Only FATF/GSC standard suspicious transaction reporting obligations apply to the payment flows.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority for GBP with Gibraltar Pound pegged at 1:1) and HM Government of Gibraltar (Currency Notes Act, local note issuance); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the statutory banking and AML/CFT supervisor for all licensed credit institutions and EMIs operating in or from Gibraltar.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Viability: HIGH for daily treasury operations only. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

10% Gibraltar corporate income tax rate on chargeable income; the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is tax transparent and therefore bears no entity-level corporate tax.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. Gibraltar does not impose a dividend withholding tax, and the ELP's profit distributions to non-resident limited partners are not subject to any Gibraltar WHT.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is required where aggregate controlled cross-border transactions attributable to the Gibraltar ELP exceed GBP 750,000 in an accounting period. Master File is required where the worldwide group revenue exceeds GBP 50,000,000 and the Gibraltar entity is a significant participant in the group's value chain.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to the ELP because the B2B marketplace platform constitutes the relevant activity of 'provision of other services'. The ELP must, on an annual basis, demonstrate sufficient physical presence and core income-generating activities in Gibraltar: a registered place of business, locally held board meetings with quorum of directors/partners, locally resident staff, and strategic decision-making regarding platform pricing, marketplace onboarding, payment processing via Alibaba_Pay and SWIFT, and IT infrastructure. Failure to meet the ESR test results in tax penalties, publicity orders, and potential strike-off of the Gibraltar entity.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Criminal conviction carries a fine up to GBP 100,000 per offence and imprisonment up to five years; civil assessment on the omitted income plus 100% penalty and compound interest at 10% per annum.

Director personal liability: The general partner or managing partner may be personally liable for the tax evaded where the partnership veil is pierced; courts may impose travel bans, freezing orders, disqualification of directors for up to 15 years, and restitution orders over the partners' personal assets.
Shell Company / Abuse of ELP Exemption

Statutory sanction: Cancellation of the ELP registration, strike-off under the Companies Act, penalty up to GBP 50,000, and reclassification of the entity as a taxable Gibraltar company on all worldwide profits at 10% for up to six years.

Director personal liability: Directors of the general partner are liable for civil restitution to tax authorities and third-party creditors; disqualification orders and a 15-year travel ban may be imposed for facilitating a sham tax structure.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing

Statutory sanction: Adjustment to arm's length profits, a penalty of 100% of the additional tax attributable to the adjustment, and interest on underpaid tax from the due date. Missing documentation triggers an automatic penalty of GBP 25,000.

Director personal liability: The general partner who knowingly signed incorrect transfer pricing documentation faces personal assessment for the penalty if the partnership has insufficient assets, as well as disqualification under Gibraltar's Companies (Disqualification) Act.
Failure to File and Late Remittance

Statutory sanction: Accumulated late filing penalties up to GBP 1,150; tax-geared late payment penalties up to 10%; interest at 10% per annum; seizure of bank accounts via collection warrant.

Director personal liability: Directors or the general partner are personally liable for unpaid tax where the ELP has been operated recklessly or where tax was deducted from third parties and not remitted to the Gibraltar Commissioner.
AML / Sanctions Violations

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and AML Regulations, fines up to GBP 250,000 for the partnership; serious or repeated breaches trigger asset forfeiture and seizure of payment processing accounts.

Director personal liability: Individual directors and the general partner face criminal liability up to 14 years' imprisonment for predicate money laundering, personal fines, assets freezing, and travel bans. Courts will pierce the ELP veil if the entity was used as a conduit for laundering commerce proceeds.
