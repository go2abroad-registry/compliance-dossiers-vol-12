Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Her Majesty's Government of Gibraltar, Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax); online portal: https://taxation.gi (Gibraltar Tax e-Returns and TIN registration); corporate registry: Gibraltar Companies House, Registrar of Companies.

E-government filing portal

A Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) obtains its Tax Identification Number (TIN) as follows: (1) incorporate the ELP under the Exempted Limited Partnerships Act 2014 and file the Partnership Statement with the Registrar of Companies; (2) submit the completed TIN application and partnership registration details to the Income Tax Office (Form T-1 / Application for Registration); (3) upon validation, the Commissioner of Income Tax issues a partnership TIN; (4) the ELP must also register each general and limited partner individually for income tax purposes; (5) the ELP files annual partnership returns and the partners report their respective distributive shares.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible — the ELP is fiscally transparent; no entity-level Gibraltar income tax; each partner is separately assessed on their distributive share.

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a full check-the-box regime. The ELP is transparent by statute under the Exempted Limited Partnerships Act 2014. The partnership may elect to remain 'Exempted' (limited to non-Gibraltar-source income) or to be within the normal charge, and individual/company partners may elect the territorial basis under the Income Tax Act 2010. Hybrid mismatch elections are limited; the Income Tax (Substantial Shareholding and Loss Buying Rules) apply.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST in Gibraltar. Gibraltar is outside the UK VAT area and the EU Customs Union; it levies only limited customs and excise import duties on physical goods. UK VAT does not extend to Gibraltar.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST registration threshold exists because Gibraltar does not levy VAT. Customs and excise duties arise only at the point of importation of physical goods, not on public relations or communications services.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in Gibraltar. As Gibraltar imposes no VAT, there is no 0% export VAT rate to claim and no indirect tax refund mechanism for exported services. International media relations, PR advisory, and crisis communications services supplied from Gibraltar to clients in the United Kingdom, EU, or elsewhere are outside the scope of Gibraltar tax, provided the ELP's control and management remains outside Gibraltar and the income is not Gibraltar-source. For UK business clients, the supply is generally treated as made where the recipient belongs, and any UK reverse charge falls on the UK recipient rather than the Gibraltar supplier.

  • Not applicable for services — no customs export declaration is required for intangible PR and communications services.
  • Where physical promotional materials are exported, retain: Customs Export Declaration (Gibraltar/UK), Bill of Lading or Airway Bill, Commercial Invoice, and Correspondent Bank (SWIFT MT103) payment evidence.

Not applicable — Gibraltar has no real-time fiscal clearance, two-way API e-invoicing, or digital stamping mandate. Invoices must be retained for six years under the Income Tax Act; where UK or EU counterparties impose contract-based e-invoicing, such formats are commercially acceptable but not statutorily required in Gibraltar.

No VAT zero-rating clawback exists because Gibraltar does not levy VAT. However, under the Income Tax (Tax Administration and Appeals) Rules 2006, false or negligent partnership returns attract penalties of up to 100% of the tax undercharged plus interest; fraudulent evasion, including artificial arrangements designed to avoid Gibraltar tax on exported services, attracts civil penalties of up to 115% and potential criminal liability. The Commissioner may reopen assessments for six years, extended to 21 years in cases of fraud.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Cayman Islands maintains a fully convertible free capital and current account regime with unrestricted profit remittances to foreign owners. Gibraltar, as a British Overseas Territory, permits free current account convertibility aligned with UK rules while requiring GFSC approvals for capital account transactions exceeding thresholds. United Kingdom enforces capital controls under the Capital Controls Act 1991, permitting profit remittances only after HMRC tax clearance and Bank of England notification for larger amounts.

Export proceeds surrender rules

As an international PR and crisis communication agency, client payments constitute service receipts rather than goods exports. No mandatory surrender applies in Cayman Islands or Gibraltar; receipts must be converted at prevailing market rates for local banking. In the United Kingdom, all foreign currency inflows above £10,000 require full conversion and reporting to HMRC under the Money Laundering Regulations, with supporting invoices and client contracts retained for audit.

Central bank supervision: Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA) for Cayman Islands jurisdiction; Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) for Gibraltar; Bank of England for United Kingdom sovereign oversight

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Cayman Islands permits licensed electronic money institutions under CIMA oversight for cross-border digital payments, subject to strict AML and reserve requirements. Gibraltar and United Kingdom impose full authorisation under the Electronic Money Regulations 2011 and PSD2, mandating local regulatory licensing and prohibiting unlicensed offshore EMI operations for client-facing services.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File mandatory when same-jurisdiction controlled transactions exceed GIP 1,000,000 or aggregate controlled transactions exceed GIP 5,000,000. Master File and CbC reporting apply at the ultimate-parent group revenue threshold of EUR 750,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar applies Economic Substance Regulations to entities conducting 'relevant activities' (banking, insurance, distribution and service centre, financing and leasing, headquarters, shipping, IP). A global PR agency that merely books retainers through an ELP without employees, premises, and management in Gibraltar lacks economic substance and may be challenged as a shell. Public relations/communications is not a named exempt activity, but any nexus with a Gibraltar trade or treaty benefit requires real activity in Gibraltar.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Deliberate Concealment of Gibraltar-Source Income

Statutory sanction: Assessments to best of judgment, interest, penalties up to 150% of the tax undercharged, publication of tax defaulters, seizure of bank accounts and assets, and strike-off of the ELP.

Director personal liability: The general partner and controlling directors are jointly and severally liable for partnership tax debts where the ELP is a sham. Courts may pierce the corporate veil, impose civil restitution and confiscation, issue travel bans, disqualify directors for up to 15 years, and impose imprisonment for fraud or cheating the public revenue.
Shell Company / Zero Substance Offshore Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Economic Substance failure penalties, cancellation of tax residence status, deemed profit assessments at 12.5%, regulatory strike-off, and negative disclosure to overseas tax authorities under Gibraltar's international tax information exchange agreements.

Director personal liability: Directors and partners can be made personally liable for the ELP's debts if continuation of trading after substance failure amounts to fraudulent or wrongful trading; travel restrictions and director disqualification orders apply.
AML / Money Laundering and Unregulated Payment Routing

Statutory sanction: Criminal proceedings under Gibraltar's Proceeds of Crime framework: unlimited fines, confiscation orders, freezing orders, and imprisonment of up to 14 years. Regulatory action by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission can include corrective orders and public censure.

Director personal liability: Directors or senior managers who consent or connive in money laundering are personally criminally liable. The court may impose travel bans, asset restraint, civil recovery orders, and confiscation notwithstanding the ELP's limited liability structure.
Facilitation of UK or Foreign Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Invoicing

Statutory sanction: Under the UK Criminal Finances Act 2017, the failure-to-prevent-facilitation-of-tax-evasion offence carries an unlimited fine; Gibraltar may also prosecute under its own fraud and money laundering legislation, with extradition exposure to the UK.

Director personal liability: A 'senior manager' or general partner who knowingly facilitates the arrangement faces personal prosecution, fines, civil restitution, travel bans, confiscation, and imprisonment; the corporate veil will be disregarded where the entity is used as a dishonest instrument.
