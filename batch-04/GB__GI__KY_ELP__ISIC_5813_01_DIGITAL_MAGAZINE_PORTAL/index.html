Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; Territorial_Scope applies - the Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership is fiscally transparent, is not chargeable to Gibraltar income tax on non-Gibraltar-source income once exempt status is approved, and its profits are attributed to partners for taxation in their respective home jurisdictions.

Available election forms

No Gibraltar check-the-box election exists; the ELP is automatically treated as pass-through for Gibraltar tax purposes. Exempt status is an annual elective application under the Income Tax Act. For U.S. federal purposes, the ELP may elect classification via IRS Form 8832. Gibraltar applies OECD BEPS-compliant anti-hybrid rules which restrict elections producing deduction-without-inclusion outcomes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

9%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GIP 90,000 annual taxable turnover (excluding GST); the threshold is set by the annual Budget Act and is aligned in substance with the UK VAT threshold

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating (or outside-scope treatment) applies to: (i) goods physically exported from Gibraltar where the exporter retains customs-approved export evidence; (ii) services, including digital magazine portal subscriptions and sponsored media placements, supplied to customers belonging in a country other than Gibraltar under place-of-supply rules; and (iii) international transport services. The ELP must evidence that the recipient belongs outside Gibraltar using normal residency indicators such as billing address, IP address, payment card country, and customer declarations.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Gibraltar Customs frontier submission)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical departure from Gibraltar
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank payment evidence from the overseas customer
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Supply agreement or purchase order with overseas delivery terms
  • Digital services substantiation pack (IP address, billing address, payment country, subscriber records)

No real-time electronic invoicing clearance, fiscal stamping, or API-based digital clearance mandate. GST-registered businesses may issue paper or electronic invoices containing the statutory particulars. GST returns are filed periodically through the Income Tax Office portal, and the Commissioner may require digital access to invoice records during audit.

Fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating claims trigger retrospective assessments, statutory interest, civil penalties up to 100% of the understated tax, and criminal prosecution (fines and imprisonment) under the GST Act 2006 and Income Tax Act. Input GST credits may be clawed back where exported goods are diverted back into Gibraltar or where export documentation is not produced within the statutory retention period (generally six years). Absence of repatriated consideration within 180 days is treated as evidence that the supply was not a genuine export and may invalidate zero-rating.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The GIP is pegged at par to GBP and fully convertible. Gibraltar maintains no exchange-control statute and imposes no capital-account restrictions. Current-account flows (subscription revenue, advertising payouts, royalties) and capital-account flows (partner capital subscriptions, profit distributions, loan repayments) are freely remittable in any major currency. Unrestricted profit remittance applies to the KY_ELP structure, provided the payor satisfies the bank's source-of-funds verification, beneficial-ownership disclosure to the Gibraltar Registry of Beneficial Ownership, and demonstrates economic substance in Gibraltar to mitigate any tax-transparency challenge.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No surrender, repatriation, or mandatory conversion rules exist in Gibraltar for digital publishing export receipts. Subscription fees, AdSense-programme income, sponsored-media placements, and affiliate commissions may be retained in USD, EUR, or GBP in multicurrency operating accounts. The ELP may invoice in GBP for domestic merchants, USD for programmatic ad inventory, and EUR for EU trade counterparties without conversion obligations. Statutory reporting applies only for suspicious-transaction reporting under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 (Gibraltar) and the GFSC AML Guidance Notes.

Central bank supervision: Financial Services Commission of Gibraltar (GFSC) as banking licensor, prudential supervisor and AML/CFT authority; the Government of Gibraltar issues the Gibraltar pound (GIP) under the Currency Notes Act 1934, with notes backed one-for-one by Bank of England sterling reserves. Exchange-control oversight is exercised jointly through the UK–Gibraltar customs and financial-services nexus, with HM Treasury retaining ultimate monetary and sanctions authority over the British Overseas Territory.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-Low for offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Revolut, Airwallex) because the post-Brexit UK/EU payment-passporting regimes do not extend to Gibraltar, and few non-GFSC-licensed EMIs onboard Gibraltar 'GI'-flagged legal entities. Feasibility rating: High for GFSC-licensed EMIs and licensed payment institutions, e.g., GC Partners and other Gibraltar-authorised forex-payment firms, which can provide multi-currency virtual accounts and SWIFT routing for USD/EUR/GBP settlement. The ELP should retain a tier-1 bank as the settlement anchor and use an EMI only as a treasury optionality layer, ensuring EMI accounts are titled in the ELP's exact legal name and covered by the partnership-deed signatory matrix.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File when the ELP's Gibraltar gross revenue exceeds GBP 1,000,000 in an accounting period or when any single connected-party transaction exceeds GBP 500,000; Master File required when the worldwide group turnover exceeds GBP 50,000,000. CbC Report triggers at GBP 750,000,000 group revenue.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to an ELP carrying on a 'relevant activity', which includes digital magazine publishing and sponsored media placement when the core income-generating activity is performed in Gibraltar; the ELP must maintain adequate physical presence, full-time employees, operational expenditure, and management board meetings in Gibraltar; a purely passive royalty/licensing ELP with no local decision-making or staff will be treated as a shell and denied tax transparency, with penalties under the ESR up to GBP 200,000 and potential strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Returns

Statutory sanction: Under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010, a tax-evasion penalty of up to 250% of the tax undercharged; criminal prosecution leading to a fine of £50,000 and/or imprisonment for up to seven years; the court may issue a confiscation order for the full value of hidden profits

Director personal liability: General partners of the ELP have unlimited liability for the entity's unpaid taxes; in tax-evasion prosecutions, directors and partners may face civil restitution orders, travel bans, asset-freezing orders, and disqualification from acting as a director for up to 15 years; limited partners who engage in management risk loss of limited-liability protection and direct personal liability.
Shell Company / Insufficient Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Violation of the Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations (2018) results in financial penalties of £50,000 to £200,000 per annum, automatic denial of DTA benefits, loss of its pass-through tax status, and eventual compulsory strike-off under the Companies Act; adverse information is shared automatically with the UK, EU, and OECD

Director personal liability: Directors of the corporate general partner may be personally disqualified, barred from holding office, subject to travel restrictions, jointly and severally liable for the ELP's penalty amounts, and may face a charge of false statutory declarations if the substance report is knowingly false.
AML / Counter-Terrorist Financing Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Act (Gibraltar), penalties reach £500,000 per foundation and imprisonment up to fourteen years; the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission may revoke the ELP's or its general partner's financial services authorisation, seize the digital platform's domain, and freeze bank accounts held at Gibraltar banks

Director personal liability: Any general partner or director knowingly concerned in money laundering is personally liable for the full amount laundered, loses the protection of limited liability, and may be jailed; travel bans and bank garnishee orders commonly follow; the UK court may enforce UK AML penalties if the offence affects a UK advertiser.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: Specific penalty of £25,000 per missing document, reduction of deductible expenses to the arm's length benchmark, and additional tax and interest of 8% per annum on the adjustment; in serious cases, the arrangement may be recharacterised as a deemed exit from Gibraltar

Director personal liability: Directors of the general partner may be jointly and severally liable for the additional tax arising from transfer-pricing adjustments if they signed the return knowing the pricings were non-arm's length; HMRC may issue a personal liability notice for up to £50,000 and a UK travel ban in cases of cross-border dividend stripping.
