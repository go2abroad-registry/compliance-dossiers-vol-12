Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office (Taxation Department), with the Gibraltar Customs Department for customs and excise matters; taxpayer portal is accessed via the Income Tax Office e-services on the official Government of Gibraltar portal at https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity establishing or registering an exempted limited partnership in Gibraltar must apply to the Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number. The application requires: (i) completed Form TP-OR-ELP registration declaration for non-resident limited partnerships; (ii) notarised/deed-certified partnership agreement and certificate of limited partnership registration issued by the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies; (iii) full particulars of general and limited partners including name, address, nationality, and residence status; (iv) certified copy of the registered office address and resident agent appointment; and (v) a statement classifying the ELP as tax-transparent in Gibraltar. The Income Tax Office issues a TIN within 5–10 working days via the registered agent; no physical attendance is required if a Gibraltar-registered agent handles the filing electronically.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — A foreign entity establishing or registering an exempted limited partnership in Gibraltar must apply to the Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number. The application requires: (i) completed Form TP-OR-ELP registration declaration for non-resident limited partnerships; (ii) notarised/deed-certified partnership agreement and certificate of limited partnership registration issued by the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies; (iii) full particulars of general and limited partners including name, address, nationality, and residence status; (iv) certified copy of the registered office address and resident agent appointment; and (v) a statement classifying the ELP as tax-transparent in Gibraltar. The Income Tax Office issues a TIN within 5–10 working days via the registered agent; no physical attendance is required if a Gibraltar-registered agent handles the filing electronically.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a US-style 'check-the-box' election. An Exempted Limited Partnership is treated as a transparent taxable arrangement by default under the Gibraltarian Income Tax Act and Partnership Law, with the general partner and limited partners taxed on their respective shares of income. No separate election is available for the partnership to be treated as an entity for Gibraltar income tax purposes; however, partners may vary their profit-sharing ratios in the partnership deed, subject to anti-avoidance reallocation powers of the Income Tax Commissioner. Hybrid instruments governed by the UK/Gibraltar double taxation arrangements may be affected by UK hybrid mismatch rules, but no Gibraltar statutory election equivalent to IRC 7701 exists.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar customs duty and excise duty system (no VAT/GST)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — Gibraltar does not levy value added tax (VAT) and therefore has no VAT registration threshold. Any person or partnership importing goods into Gibraltar, including for re-export, must register with the Gibraltar Customs Department to obtain an Economic Operator Registration and Identification (EORI) number before the first importation; the obligation is subject to no local-currency turnover threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in VAT terms because Gibraltar has no VAT. Export of dehydrated garlic flakes, crushed chilli pepper, and other dehydrated vegetable/spice products from Gibraltar to a destination outside Gibraltar is outside the scope of Gibraltar customs duty and excise duty, provided the customs export declaration is accepted and the goods physically leave Gibraltar within the required period. If imported goods are held under customs warehousing or end-use/removal relief, zero-rating is functionally achieved through duty suspension; no VAT zero-rating claim or refund mechanism is available in Gibraltar.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document / ASYCUDA export declaration)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical departure from Gibraltar
  • Commercial invoice listing HS codes 2003/0912 for dehydrated garlic and crushed chilli pepper
  • Packing list and container weight/count documents
  • Certificate of Origin (for dual-use/sanction/ anti-dumping verification only if requested by destination customs)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT/MT103 confirmation evidencing receipt of payment from the non-resident buyer

Gibraltar has no mandatory electronic VAT filing or fiscal clearance/ digital stamping regime for invoices. Import and export declarations are filed electronically through the Gibraltar Customs automated declaration system (ASYCUDA) or the legacy Customs declaration submission service. A business-to-business commercial invoice is not required to carry an electronic signature or QR code for tax purposes; however, a valid customs declaration must contain the exporter marker, invoices, packing list, transport documents, and any licences or certificates for dehydrated garlic and spice products.

Export documentation is subject to pre-clearance and post-clearance audit by the Gibraltar Customs Department and the Income Tax Office. The Commissioner for Customs and the Income Tax Assessor may jointly conduct a benefit-of-the-duty review; any false declaration, understated export value, fabricated outward shipment, or forged inward remittance certificate will be treated as a fraud offence. Penalties under the Customs and Excise Acts include seizure and confiscation of goods, civil penalty up to 100% of the duty/undertaxed amount, monetary fines up to GIP 50,000, and imprisonment of up to 7 years for officers/partners found guilty of fraudulent evasion. Retrospective assessments may be made for up to 6 years after the end of the relevant accounting/tax year, and the clawback may also reclassify the transaction as taxable income of the partners, triggering personal/corporate income tax assessments (currently up to 12.5%) plus interest and surcharges.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GIP is fully convertible to GBP on a one-for-one basis, and both GIP and GBP are legal tender in Gibraltar. There are no exchange controls restricting current account or capital account convertibility for residents or non-residents. An ELP engaged in bulk dehydrated vegetables and spices export may hold, convert, and remit GIP, GBP, USD, and EUR balances freely. Profit distribution and capital repatriation are not subject to exchange control approval, provided that the partnership maintains its non-resident tax-exempt status, files the required annual beneficial ownership returns, and demonstrates economic substance appropriate to the trading activity. The only practical hurdles are those imposed by counterparty banks under anti-money laundering, sanctions, and source-of-funds verification obligations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar and the United Kingdom impose no statutory export proceeds surrender rule. There is no mandatory conversion ratio and no timeline within which export receipts must be exchanged into local currency. Export proceeds may be retained in the invoicing currency, held in foreign currency accounts, or converted to GIP at the commercial bank's prevailing rate. As a matter of banking practice rather than law, the receiving bank may request the underlying sales invoice, bill of lading, and customs declaration within five business days of arrivals to confirm the commercial nature of the credit and to complete its regulatory reporting. The ELP must retain full trade documentation for at least five years to satisfy GFSC record-keeping expectations.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar has no independent central bank; monetary control is anchored to the Bank of England through the Gibraltar pound (GIP), which is issued under the Currency Notes Act (1934) as a distinct currency at a fixed 1:1 parity with GBP. The Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the statutory regulator for banking, capital markets, and payment services, while HM Government of Gibraltar administers exchange control records and financial sanctions. All banking and FX operations for the Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) will be overseen by the GFSC and, to the extent applicable, the UK Financial Conduct Authority for cross-border correspondent banking relationships.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility: Medium for ancillary treasury payments and low-value trade receipts; Low as the primary clearing rail for commercial letters of credit and bulk spice export settlements. Offshore and EU digital EMI platforms (e.g. UK or EEA-authorised e-money institutions) may be used only after confirmation that they are licensed to accept payment services in Gibraltar under a permissive approach or an overseas business introduction arrangement. The ELP may be rejected by some digital-only EMIs because of its non-resident, tax-exempt partnership classification. Legal constraints require the ELP to provide a registered agent in Gibraltar, a certified partnership deed, and full breakdown of ultimate beneficial owners. An EMI cannot offer a documentary letter of credit or acceptance of bills of exchange, so the entity must maintain at least one Tier 1 clearing account for export transactions.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Partner capital contributions should be transmitted by SWIFT MT103/MT202 to the Gibraltar bank using a clearly defined reconciliation reference such as 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION — ELP PARTNERSHIP' and the partner's individual capital account reference. The remitting bank should use the correct ISO 20022 bank transaction code corresponding to capital investment, in practice the receiving bank will map the transaction to the UK/Gibraltar balance of payments category 'Capital Participation and Investment'. Field 70 may cite the ELP registration number and the invoiced business scope. Verification requires a certified copy of the capital call notice, a partners' resolution, the bank's name and SWIFT/BIC code, and a signed deposit letter confirming that funds arise from the partner's own assets and are not connected to third-party lending.Trade settlements to overseas suppliers of dehydrated vegetables and spices will be executed primarily through SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers for amounts below the documentary credit threshold, and through commercial letters of credit (MT700) issued in favour of the supplier for bulk shipment values above USD 50,000. The ELP's issuing bank in Gibraltar will send the LC via SWIFT to the beneficiary's confirming bank if the supplier requires local confirmation. Swift field 50K must identify the ELP exactly; field 59A the beneficiary; field 71A charges must be pre-agreed; and field 44C/E contain the latest shipment date and expiry. For TT settlements, the payment instruction must include the referring invoice number, packing list number, and contract number in field 70. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GBP 1,000,000 aggregated related-party transactions per category (purchase, sale, services, finance, IP) requiring a Local File; Master File mandatory where the group's consolidated turnover exceeds GBP 50,000,000. A small-enterprise exemption applies where the ELP's annual turnover does not exceed GBP 10,000,000 and controlled transaction value is below GBP 1,000,000. The ITO may rely on benchmark studies from commercial databases (e.g., RoyaltyStat, TP Catalyst) to verify commodity trading margins on dehydrated garlic flakes and crushed chili pepper.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar does not apply the Caribbean-styled Economic Substance Regulations (ESR). Instead, substance is enforced through the territoriality rule in the Income Tax Act 2010, the management-and-control test for tax residence, and the Limitation on Benefits article in the UK–Gibraltar DTA. An ELP claiming Gibraltar residence must demonstrate real local presence: an occupied registered office, substantive board or general partner meetings held in Gibraltar, statutory records kept in the jurisdiction, and day-to-day administration exercised from Gibraltar. Pure offshore pass-through entities trading dehydrated vegetables through nominees without local management are not tax-resident and will be denied TRCs. The GFSC also applies the economic substance expectations of the OECD Forum on Harmful Tax Practices; the ITO may recharacterize transactions or require TP adjustments where substance is insufficient.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Wilful Under-Declaration

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of up to 200% of the undercharged tax, statutory interest at 8% per annum, and daily default fines. Criminal conviction for dishonest evasion under the Income Tax Act (Amendment) 2020 carries an unlimited fine and imprisonment for up to 7 years. The ITO may issue an assessment out of time without the usual 6-year limit where fraud is proven.

Director personal liability: The general partner's directors, the ELP administrator, and persons controlling the entity are jointly and severally liable for the civil penalty; the Commissioner may apply to the Supreme Court for charging orders over personal assets, freezing injunctions, and confiscation orders. Directors knowingly signing false returns face personal criminal conviction, disqualification from managing Gibraltar entities for up to 15 years, and travel bans to prevent absconding during extradition or mutual assistance proceedings under the UK–Gibraltar arrangements.
Shell Company and Sham Arrangement

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner can disregard the sham entity, strike the ELP off the Companies House Gibraltar register, invalidate tax returns, and assess the beneficial owners directly on the whole of the avoided Gibraltar tax. The Registrar may impose administrative fines of GBP 5,000 plus GBP 100 per day for continued non-compliance. The GFSC or ITO will refer the case to the Financial Intelligence Unit for a false-declaration investigation.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced: beneficial owners and directors are personally liable for the full economic under-declaration plus penal surcharge of up to 200%; they are excluded from acting as directors for up to 10 years; personal travel bans and asset restraint orders are obtainable under the Criminal Justice (Penalties) Rules; an adverse finding triggers automatic disqualification from all regulated financial services under the Financial Services Act 2019.
Trade-Based Money Laundering Abuse of Commodity Invoicing

Statutory sanction: Enforcement under the Criminal Justice Act 2015 and Proceeds of Crime Act 2015: GFSC penalties up to 10% of annual turnover or GBP 2,000,000, compliance monitoring orders, and revocation of the entity's registration as a specified non-financial business; banks may terminate the ELP's correspondent banking relationship and freeze transacted funds. An offence of fraud for dishonestly obtaining an exemption or relief is punishable by imprisonment for up to 14 years under the Crimes Act 2011.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers who knowingly structure invoices, approve false Letters of Credit, or give instructions to conceal the true counterparty face personal conviction, confiscation orders over personal and corporate assets, travel bans, and prohibition from acting as directors for 20 years; international mutual legal assistance under the UK–Gibraltar treaty enables extradition to prosecuting states.
AML Record-Keeping and Reporting Failures

Statutory sanction: GFSC supervision imposes public censure and financial penalties up to GBP 50,000 per offence, plus per-day penalties of GBP 2,500 for continuing non-compliance; the ELP's books may be seized and audited by appointed inspectors. The supervisor may apply to strike the entity from the register and communicate the finding to UK HMRC Customs for cross-border trade compliance review.

Director personal liability: The general partner's designated compliance officer and directors are personally criminally liable for each breach of the record-keeping regulations, with imprisonment up to 2 years per offence and fines at the court's discretion; personal civil liability extends to compensation for losses caused by non-compliance, and repeated breaches result in a statutory travel ban and permanent bar from holding any directorship in Gibraltar or UK-regulated sectors.
