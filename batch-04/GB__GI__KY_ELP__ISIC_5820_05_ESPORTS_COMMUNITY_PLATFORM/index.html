Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax), HM Government of Gibraltar; portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi; corporate registry: Gibraltar Companies House

E-government filing portal

Foreign investors register the ELP under the Limited Partnerships Act 2011 with the Registrar of Companies, obtaining the partnership registration number. Tax registration follows with the Income Tax Office, which issues the ELP's tax file reference; because the ELP is fiscally transparent, each partner's distributive share is reported on the partner's own return. Non-resident partners are assessable only on Gibraltar-source income under the territorial Income Tax Act 2010; no VAT registration is possible or required because Gibraltar has no VAT system.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible / Territorial_Scope: ELP is tax-transparent and not liable to entity-level tax; partners are taxed only on Gibraltar-source income. Income derived from overseas esports operations is outside the territorial charge.

Available election forms

Tax transparency is mandatory, not elective; no check-the-box exists in Gibraltar. A formal advance tax ruling may be applied for from the Commissioner of Income Tax. Hybrid mismatch rules (Income Tax (Hybrid and Arbitration) Regulations 2020, ATAD-aligned) may deny mismatches if the UK or another jurisdiction treats the ELP as opaque; partnership election forms under the Income Tax Act 2010 are used to allocate profits in accordance with the partnership deed.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/sales tax in Gibraltar; indirect taxation limited to Import Duty under the Customs Duty Act 2018 and stamp duties on limited instruments; the jurisdiction sits within EU customs territory but outside the EU VAT area.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no turnover-based indirect tax registration threshold exists in Gibraltar; Import Duty arises only on physical movement of goods into Gibraltar, not on digital services or platform turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - no VAT export zero-rating exists in Gibraltar because no VAT is charged on supplies. Cross-border esports services to overseas users are simply outside the territorial scope of taxation. For physical goods re-exported from Gibraltar, Import Duty may be suspended, relieved, or refunded under the customs warehousing, transit, and outward-processing provisions of the Customs Duty Act 2018.

  • For physical exports/re-exports only: Single Administrative Document, commercial invoice, bill of lading or airway bill, certificate of origin, and duty-suspension application; digital service exports require no customs documentation

Not applicable - Gibraltar has no electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or API clearance mandate for digital services; customs declarations for physical goods are submitted electronically through the integrated customs system.

Fraudulent duty suspension or export misdeclaration is punishable by penalties up to the full value of goods and imprisonment up to 7 years under the Customs Duty Act 2018. Erroneous partner-level tax reliefs are subject to retrospective clawback with interest and penalties up to 100% of underpaid tax, or 150% for fraud or willful neglect, under sections 99 and 100 of the Income Tax Act 2010. All export relief claims are audited by the Commissioner with a 6-year record retention requirement.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The GIP is fully convertible into GBP at par on both current and capital account. Gibraltar imposes no exchange controls, no restrictions on holding or transacting in foreign currency, and no approval requirements for profit remittances or dividend distributions to non-resident partners. An Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is fiscally transparent, so Gibraltar does not levy withholding taxes on income attributable to the Cayman partner. The UK-Gibraltar double taxation arrangement does not restrict capital flows, although substance and anti-abuse rules (OECD BEPS, CFC rules) must be observed at the group level.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Neither the UK nor Gibraltar imposes any statutory surrender timeline, mandatory conversion ratio, or repatriation obligation on export proceeds, tournament entry fees, service income, or intellectual property royalties. The ELP may retain balances in USD, EUR, GBP, or any freely convertible currency. The only compliance layer is the GFSC Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism Obligations under the Gibraltar AML Regulations, which require transaction monitoring and reporting without restricting convertibility.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary anchor) and the Government of Gibraltar acting as the currency board issuer of the Gibraltar Pound (GIP, pegged 1:1 to GBP); the Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the prudential and AML/CFT supervisory authority for all licensed banks and e-money institutions in Gibraltar.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 annual aggregate controlled transactions for mandatory Local File; a Gibraltar group with consolidated turnover above GIP 10,000,000 and cross-border related-party transactions above GIP 10,000,000 must prepare a Master File.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar does not maintain an EU-style Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) code, but the Income Tax Act and DTA jurisprudence require that taxable profits be determined by the location of central management and control and core income-generating activities. An ELP serving as the esports platform should maintain: (a) a registered office in Gibraltar; (b) local board or partner meeting minutes; (c) physical or secure digital infrastructure for matchmaking or tournament systems in Gibraltar; and (d) local accounting, HR, and IT functions commensurate with its turnover. A shell ELP without these elements is denied residency-based treaty benefits, and the Commissioner may re-characterise all global revenues as Gibraltar-source.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Return

Statutory sanction: Upon conviction under the Income Tax Act, fines of up to GIP 100,000, a surcharge of up to 55% of the unpaid tax, statutory interest, and imprisonment of up to seven years for individuals.

Director personal liability: Any director, general partner, or commercial partner who knowingly signs a false return is jointly and severally liable for the full unpaid liability. A court may order civil restitution, issue a travel ban, attach personal assets, and disqualify the individual from acting as a manager of any Gibraltar-registered entity.
Shell Company / Sham ELP

Statutory sanction: Re-characterisation of profits as Gibraltar-source and assessment at 12.5%, plus penalties of up to 100% of the tax and a fixed default penalty under the Companies Act. The Registrar may initiate strike-off proceedings.

Director personal liability: The general partner and any shadow director are jointly and severally liable for the shortfall if the arrangement has no commercial substance; the court may pierce the corporate veil and enforce judgments against the underlying beneficial owner and promoter.
AML / Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015, such conduct is punishable by up to fourteen years' imprisonment, unlimited fines, and confiscation orders covering all transacted funds held in the ELP's accounts.

Director personal liability: Officers who deliberately ignore AML red flags may be prosecuted personally. Conviction leads to personal criminal liability, travel bans, asset freezing orders, and a mandatory bar from Gibraltar Financial Services Commission licensing.
Transfer Pricing / Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner may make an upward adjustment of profits, charge interest on the additional tax, and impose a penalty of 35% of the additional tax, in addition to a fixed penalty of GIP 7,500 for non-production of contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation.

Director personal liability: Responsible officers who prepare or approve negligent transfer pricing documentation are liable for misstatement. Tax court proceedings may also impose a personal fine of GIP 2,000 per unfiled document and the director may be disqualified from board appointment in Gibraltar for a specified period.
DTA Abuse / Artificial Tax Avoidance

Statutory sanction: The Revenue, acting under the Finance (Gibraltar) Act 2020, may deny treaty relief and tax gross Gibraltar-source revenue at 12.5%, apply a tax-geared penalty of 5.5% of the benefit obtained, and publish the scheme in the General Anti-Abuse Rule disclosure list.

Director personal liability: Promoters and beneficial owners of such abusive schemes face personal fines, publication of their participation, and if they are UK residents, the UK may recapture tax under the DTA's limitation on benefits clause and seek enforcement through the mutual agreement procedure.
