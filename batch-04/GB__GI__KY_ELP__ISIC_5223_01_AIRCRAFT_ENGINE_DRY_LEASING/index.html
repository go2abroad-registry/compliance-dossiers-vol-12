Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, Government of Gibraltar (under the Income Tax Act 2010); separate registration arm: Registrar of Companies, Gibraltar Companies House (under the Limited Partnerships Act 2018). Official portals: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax and https://www.gibraltarcompanieshouse.gi

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP): (1) file the partnership registration application with the Registrar of Companies under the Limited Partnerships Act 2018, including the statutory declaration for exempted status, the general partner's details and the registered office in Gibraltar; (2) upon issue of the Gibraltar Partnership Registration Certificate, submit to the Income Tax Office a completed Tax Identification Number (TIN) application form together with the registration certificate, the partnership agreement, the general partner's identification documents and tax-residency declarations of each partner; (3) the Commissioner issues the Gibraltar TIN for the ELP; (4) the ELP then files annual tax returns (typically nil returns where no Gibraltar-source profits arise); (5) no minimum capital, no statutory sectoral licence and no tax clearance certification are required for the dry-leasing scope.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — statutory tax transparency: the ELP is not itself a taxable person; each partner is assessed on their respective share of the ELP's profits under the territorial/source-based rules of the Income Tax Act 2010; non-resident partners are taxed only on Gibraltar-source income, and cross-border aircraft engine dry-leasing income is not Gibraltar-source absent a Gibraltar trade or permanent establishment. — For a foreign-owned Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP): (1) file the partnership registration application with the Registrar of Companies under the Limited Partnerships Act 2018, including the statutory declaration for exempted status, the general partner's details and the registered office in Gibraltar; (2) upon issue of the Gibraltar Partnership Registration Certificate, submit to the Income Tax Office a completed Tax Identification Number (TIN) application form together with the registration certificate, the partnership agreement, the general partner's identification documents and tax-residency declarations of each partner; (3) the Commissioner issues the Gibraltar TIN for the ELP; (4) the ELP then files annual tax returns (typically nil returns where no Gibraltar-source profits arise); (5) no minimum capital, no statutory sectoral licence and no tax clearance certification are required for the dry-leasing scope.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible — statutory tax transparency: the ELP is not itself a taxable person; each partner is assessed on their respective share of the ELP's profits under the territorial/source-based rules of the Income Tax Act 2010; non-resident partners are taxed only on Gibraltar-source income, and cross-border aircraft engine dry-leasing income is not Gibraltar-source absent a Gibraltar trade or permanent establishment.

Available election forms

Gibraltar operates no US-style check-the-box election; ELP transparency is mandatory and non-elective. Available mechanisms: (i) an administrative election for partnership-level self-assessment to file consolidated computations without altering the transparent incidence; (ii) where UK-resident partners are involved, the UK hybrid-mismatch rules extended to Gibraltar (Taxation (International Tax Compliance) (Hybrid Mismatches) Regulations as applied to Gibraltar) may require alignment of the ELP's tax treatment with UK transparency or trigger counteraction; (iii) non-resident corporate partners may elect, where eligible, to be assessed under the territorial corporate regime at the standard 12.5% rate but only on Gibraltar-source profits.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST applies in Gibraltar. Gibraltar is expressly outside the UK VAT area (Value Added Tax Act 1994 does not extend to the territory) and outside the EU VAT territory; the only indirect taxes are Gibraltar Import Duty and excise duties under the Customs Duty Act on goods entering Gibraltar. Aircraft engine dry-lease rentals are a cross-border service and therefore fall outside the scope of any Gibraltar consumption tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable in Gibraltar pounds (GIP) — no VAT/GST system exists and therefore no VAT registration threshold applies. Import Duty and excise duties are collected at customs clearance without an economic-registration threshold; low-value consignments below the GIP 10 de minimis value are duty-free.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

VAT zero-rating is inapplicable because no VAT exists in Gibraltar. For goods physically exported from Gibraltar: eligibility for duty-free treatment requires (i) a valid export SAD declaration through ASYCUDA World, (ii) physical departure of the goods from Gibraltar within the prescribed statutory or temporary-export period, (iii) proof of export via Bill of Lading or Air Waybill, and (iv) that the goods did not enter Gibraltar home-use consumption. For services, specifically aircraft engine dry leasing to non-Gibraltar lessees: lease rentals are outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect tax and, for income-tax purposes, the engine is not situated in Gibraltar and no Gibraltar-source lease income arises absent a Gibraltar trade or permanent establishment of the ELP; non-resident partners' shares are therefore not subject to Gibraltar taxation.

  • Single Administrative Document (SAD) export declaration filed through ASYCUDA World (required for goods; not applicable to cross-border service lease payments)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export of the goods (required for goods only)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT/FX confirmation evidencing repatriation of engine lease rentals into a Gibraltar or designated partner account (required for service income substantiation)

No real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control or digital stamping mandate exists in Gibraltar. Import and export declarations are submitted electronically through the UNCTAD ASYCUDA World customs platform using the Single Administrative Document (SAD); the Importer/Exporter Reference Number linked to the TIN is required. Lease invoices may be issued electronically under ordinary commercial practice without government clearance.

Gibraltar has no export-VAT refund scheme and therefore no export zero-rating fraud regime; the enforceable equivalents are (i) customs duty evasion on goods, attracting a penalty equal to the duty evaded, seizure and forfeiture of the goods, criminal fines and/or imprisonment up to 7 years under the Customs Duty Act; (ii) income-tax fraud and materially false declarations, attracting penalties up to 100% of the tax undercharged plus statutory late-payment interest, and upon conviction fines and imprisonment up to 3 years under the Income Tax Act 2010 revenue-fraud provisions; (iii) retrospective assessments within the standard 6-year limitation period (extended to 20 years in cases of fraud or wilful default); (iv) the Commissioner's general anti-avoidance powers to recharacterise artificially diverted engine-lease income as Gibraltar-source; and (v) for UK-resident partners, UK hybrid-mismatch counteraction (denial of deduction or inclusion of income) notwithstanding the absence of any Gibraltar clawback.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The GIP is fully convertible at a fixed one-to-one parity with GBP, and both residents and non-residents may transact freely in GBP, USD, EUR and any other major currency. The current account is fully liberalised: aircraft-engine dry-lease rentals, maintenance reserves and insurance payments may be paid or received without prior approval. The capital account is equally open: partner contributions, capital distributions and intra-group loans are free from exchange-control licensing, and there is no Gibraltar withholding tax on profit distributions or interest paid to non-resident partners.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No surrender obligation or mandatory conversion ratio applies to export receipts or cross-border lease receivables. A Gibraltar-licensed bank may credit all foreign-currency proceeds to a commercial account in the currency of receipt; the entity may convert to GIP/GBP at free-market rates if required for local settlement. Only routine balance-of-payments reporting and anti-money-laundering record-keeping are triggered for payments above the GFSC transaction-reporting threshold, and no percentage of export proceeds must be repatriated.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar is a self-governing British Overseas Territory with no independent central bank. The monetary authority is the Government of Gibraltar, which issues the Gibraltar pound (GIP) at par with sterling under the Currency Notes Act 1934; banking and exchange-control supervision is delegated to the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC), and HM Treasury remains competent for UK sanctions and asset-freezing measures.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM. UK-authorised e-money institutions (EMIs) such as Revolut Business, Wise, and Airwallex will accept Gibraltar ELPs with remote onboarding, offering convenient funds-sweeping and multi-currency IBANs. However, digital EMIs are generally not licensed to provide escrow services, documentary LC facilities or aviation-finance-grade custody; they are also prone to compliance restrictions where the UBO chain contains non-EEA partnerships. Unlicensed offshore EMIs are rated LOW and are not acceptable for treasury operations because they lack GFSC/FCA authorisation and cannot issue ISO 20022 purpose-code trails.
