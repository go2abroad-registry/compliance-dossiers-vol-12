Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax); portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/en/taxation and tax portal https://tax.gov.gi

E-government filing portal

A foreign Exempted Limited Partnership that is registered in Gibraltar or carrying on activities in Gibraltar must file a partnership registration statement with the Income Tax Office, attaching the partnership agreement, certificate of incorporation/registration, general partner details, and non-resident ownership schedule. The Commissioner issues a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TIN/GRN) upon verification. The same TIN is used for tax returns, Customs transactions, and employer obligations.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible (fiscally transparent ELP; income/credits flow to partners under the partnership agreement; non-resident partners are taxed only on Gibraltar-source/accruing income, subject to territorial limits and treaty relief) — A foreign Exempted Limited Partnership that is registered in Gibraltar or carrying on activities in Gibraltar must file a partnership registration statement with the Income Tax Office, attaching the partnership agreement, certificate of incorporation/registration, general partner details, and non-resident ownership schedule. The Commissioner issues a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TIN/GRN) upon verification. The same TIN is used for tax returns, Customs transactions, and employer obligations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible (fiscally transparent ELP; income/credits flow to partners under the partnership agreement; non-resident partners are taxed only on Gibraltar-source/accruing income, subject to territorial limits and treaty relief)

Available election forms

Gibraltar law does not provide a US-style check-the-box election. Under the Income Tax Act 2010, the partnership itself may irrevocably elect to be treated as a taxable body corporate; alternatively, partners may elect for joint assessment in certain circumstances. Hybrid mismatch rules under OECD BEPS Action 2 apply to cross-border entities with differing classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales-and-Use Tax in Gibraltar; selective import and excise duties are levied under the Customs Ordinance and Excise Duties Act

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. Any person importing dutiable or controlled goods (alcohol, tobacco, hydrocarbons) must register with HM Customs as an importer; there is no economic turnover threshold for such registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable under Gibraltar law because there is no VAT/GST to zero-rate. For customs purposes, goods exported from Gibraltar that were previously imported at duty are eligible for duty relief only if the export is evidenced by a customs export entry and proof that the goods left Gibraltar. Services supplied to non-resident clients are outside the charge to Gibraltar direct tax unless the services are performed in Gibraltar and income accrues in or is derived from Gibraltar.

  • Customs Export Declaration / Single Administrative Document (as required)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export from Gibraltar
  • Inward remittance certificate or bank statement proving foreign exchange repatriation

No real-time fiscal clearance, no mandatory API clearance, and no digital stamping of invoices. Tax invoices may be issued in paper or electronic form. Invoices and underlying records must be retained for six years and produced on request of the Commissioner or HM Customs.

HM Customs can deny or withdraw duty relief, require immediate payment of previously relieved duty plus interest, and impose civil penalties of up to double the duty evaded for inaccurate export declarations. Fraudulent export claims are criminal offences punishable by fines and imprisonment. The Commissioner of Income Tax may make amended assessments, impose 100% penalty for negligent submissions, and invoke anti-avoidance rules to disregard artificial schemes failing to repatriate export proceeds within 180 days.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GIP is fully convertible into GBP at par and freely transferable in major currencies. There are no restrictions on current account, capital account, profit repatriation, or liquidation proceeds. Foreign exchange controls are absent; legal tender conversion is automatically available via the GBP peg.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or conversion requirements. Gibraltar has no exchange control legislation, and export receipts may be retained in any currency without derivational timelines or mandatory conversion ratios.

Central bank supervision: No separate central bank; the Government of Gibraltar through the Currency Commission issues the GIP, and the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the financial regulator. Monetary policy is formally exercised by the Bank of England.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium feasibility. Gibraltar is not in the EU/EEA, so EU/UK EMI passporting does not extend. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise, Revolut, Payoneer, and Airwallex can be used as auxiliary payment channels but are not a substitute for a licensed Gibraltar bank for escrow or high-value clearing. EMIs are accepted only after enhanced due diligence and may operate via a UK or EU licensed entity without local settlement capabilities.
