Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

None. An ELP has no statutory minimum paid-up capital and is not a body corporate; partners make contractual capital contributions as set out in the Limited Partnership Agreement. No escrow account is legally mandated for registration, but funds intended for operating expenses and banking onboarding should be held in an account at a GFSC-licensed bank or EMI in Gibraltar until Stripe and PayPal payment rails are activated.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certificate from the Gibraltar Fire and Rescue Service for the registered office and any leasehold premises; structural load certificate where server racks are installed; electrical installation certificate per the Electricity Supply Regulations; workplace risk assessment file under the Health and Safety at Work Act 1997 (Gibraltar), including a Display Screen Equipment (DSE) risk assessment for helpdesk operator workstations; public liability insurance cover with a minimum limit of GBP 1,000,000.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (Commissioner of Income Tax); fiscal administration is separate from HMRC; official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi (Income Tax Office e-services and e-filing); import/customs formalities: Gibraltar HM Customs.

E-government filing portal

The ELP, acting through its Gibraltar-registered agent, must (1) obtain partnership registration and Certificate of Registration under Gibraltar's limited partnership legislation; (2) file the prescribed taxpayer registration application with the Income Tax Office to obtain a Gibraltar TIN; (3) register for General Consumption Tax (GCT) only if it makes taxable supplies connected with Gibraltar, while an export-only helpdesk is outside the scope of GCT and needs no GCT registration; (4) evaluate whether a separate UK VAT registration is required only if services are supplied to UK-resident customers. Required supporting documents include the partnership deed, partner identity/KYB extracts, registered Gibraltar office address and authorised agent appointment.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible - the ELP is statutorily fiscally transparent; no entity-level Gibraltar income tax is charged; each partner is assessed on its agreed profit share; under Gibraltar's territorial rules, offshore helpdesk/cloud-service revenue of non-resident partners is not Gibraltar-source and is outside the charge to income tax.

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a US-style check-the-box election; transparent classification is mandatory. Recognised statutory mechanisms include: (i) election for each partner to be separately assessed on its agreed profit share; (ii) consolidated partnership return filing through the registered agent; (iii) UK hybrid-mismatch election/self-certification filings for UK-resident partners under CTA 2010 Part 6A; (iv) application for an advance tax ruling from the Commissioner of Income Tax to confirm non-Gibraltar-source treatment of export service revenue.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar has no VAT, GST or sales-and-use tax; a residual General Consumption Tax (GCT) applies only to Gibraltar-connected imports and local supplies of goods, while exported B2B helpdesk services are outside the scope of GCT / effectively 0%.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Nil for an export-only helpdesk ELP; GCT registration is triggered only when the entity makes taxable supplies connected with Gibraltar. Helpdesk SaaS fees from non-resident business customers are outside the scope of GCT and do not count toward any Gibraltar registration threshold. The ELP must nevertheless monitor its Gibraltar-connected turnover, which is denominated in GIP/GBP, because a supply consumed in Gibraltar would attract the statutory GCT rate.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating/outside-scope treatment for the multi-channel omni-helpdesk and conversational-bot cloud services is available where: (i) the customer belongs outside Gibraltar (KYB-verified B2B recipient); (ii) the service is delivered by cloud/SaaS and consumed offshore; (iii) the ELP has no Gibraltar permanent establishment from which the supply is made; (iv) contracts, e-invoices and delivery logs evidence the offshore place of supply; (v) consideration is settled through Stripe Billing/PayPal from overseas accounts, with export proceeds being received or repatriated within 180 days.

  • Executed SaaS/helpdesk services agreement identifying the offshore recipient and place of consumption
  • Dated e-invoice (Stripe Billing/PayPal) expressly endorsed 'Outside the scope of Gibraltar GCT / 0%'
  • Payment settlement evidence (SWIFT MT103, Stripe settlement report or PayPal transaction record)
  • Multi-channel delivery and usage logs evidencing offshore user consumption
  • Customer residence/place-of-belonging certificate or KYB extract confirming non-Gibraltar status
  • Customs Export Declaration, Bill of Lading and Inward Remittance Certificate where physical goods or prototypes are incidental to the helpdesk contract

No real-time fiscal clearance, government API clearance or digital stamping requirement applies in Gibraltar; the ELP may issue standard e-invoices (PDF/EDI) through Stripe Billing or PayPal; each invoice must identify the non-resident recipient, indicate 'outside the scope of Gibraltar GCT / 0%', and be supported by payment and service-delivery records retained for at least 6 years.

Fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating claims attract retrospective clawback of undercharged GCT/income tax, statutory interest, penalties of up to 100% of the tax undercharged, and criminal liability for dishonest evasion, with the ELP's exempt status capable of revocation. UK-resident partners and any UK-linked entity may also incur liability under the UK Criminal Finances Act 2017 for failure to prevent facilitation of tax evasion, and cross-border exchange of information with HMRC applies.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is pegged at par to the pound sterling (GBP) and is fully convertible. No foreign-exchange controls restrict capital or current account transactions; profit distributions and capital repatriation to non-resident limited partners are freely allowed once verified under anti-money-laundering and terrorist-fighting protocols. Convertibility risk is negligible, and the GFSC does not require exchange control approval for remitting funds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar imposes no statutory export proceeds surrender requirement. Export and SaaS-service receipts may be retained in any freely convertible currency, and there is no mandatory conversion ratio, repatriation deadline, or government-designated clearing account.

Central bank supervision: Government of Gibraltar through the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC); monetary policy is executed by the Bank of England and HM Treasury as Gibraltar has no independent central bank.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

High feasibility for low-risk helpdesk-SaaS treasury flows through licensed UK and EU electronic-money institutions (EMIs) such as Revolut Business, Wise, and Payoneer. Legal constraints: EMIs may not receive statutory capital escrow designated for a regulated entity, may not replace a GFSC-licensed bank for audit evidence, and must be registered under Gibraltar's anti-money-laundering framework or operate through a fully established branch of an EEA/UK EMI. The EMI can serve as a secondary clearing lace for operational receivables, not as the sole repository for partner capital or tax settlement.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound partner capital injections must be routed over SWIFT using secure MT103 messages with a Unique End Transaction Reference (UETR) allocated by the remitting bank. The remittance narrative must state 'Capital contribution to Exempted Limited Partnership' and reference the ELP registration number and partnership agreement date; every wire must be matched against the bank's internal GFSC-compliant customer ledger and source-of-funds record. There is no Gibraltar-specific BOP codes mandate, but when the capital comes from the EU, the selecting bank should use the EPC remittance architecture and include the beneficiary's account number plus the ELP legal entity identifier so the receiving GFSC-licensed bank can perform a compliance release. All injections are channelled into a segregated Capital Escrow Account before being formally moved to the operational account through a signed board resolution of the general partner.Operational trade disbursements for software subscriptions and outsourced helpdesk support are settled primarily by SWIFT MT103 with UETR for larger counterparty invoices; MT202 is used for interbank crossings when moving funds between partner banks. Inline with the low-risk SaaS scope, Commercial Letters of Credit are not common but remain permissible for physical-goods procurement related to infrastructure hardware, and must be documented with a true sale contract, clean commercial invoice, and transport or delivery evidence. For global customer collections, Stripe Billing and PayPal settlement reports are cleared through the operational bank account, and all disbursements follow a dual-approval matrix managed by the general partner and the fund administrator.Deliverable FX forward contracts with GFSC-licensed banks for GBP/EUR and GBP/USD exposuresPlain-vanilla over-the-counter FX options with regulated Gibraltar or UK counterpartiesCross-currency interest-rate swaps for any medium-term capital finance facilityNon-deliverable forwards for trades involving volatile or illiquid currencies when the underlying transaction is clearly documented and the hedge does not create speculative leverage

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% standard Gibraltar corporate income tax rate under the Income Tax Act 2010. A separate 15% top-up tax applies solely to multinational groups with consolidated revenue >= EUR 750 million (OECD Pillar Two). An Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is tax-transparent: the partnership itself pays no corporate tax; partners are taxed on their allocated share only.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on dividends, profit distributions or partnership capital withdrawals paid by an ELP to resident or non-resident partners.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The ELP partnership return is due 5 months after the end of the accounting period, and in all cases by 30 November immediately following the 30 June year of assessment. Partner-level returns follow the same statutory timetable.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Controlled transactions with associated enterprises must be priced at arm's length where the aggregate annual value exceeds GIP 100,000. Formal Local File and Master File documentation is mandatory only for multinational groups with consolidated group revenue of at least EUR 750 million (Country-by-Country Reporting threshold). Small and medium enterprises (fewer than 250 employees and turnover up to GIP 50 million) are exempt from routine transfer pricing documentation, although a contemporaneous benchmark summary is still recommended for intercompany SaaS licensing and management fee recharges.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar is not subject to the EU Economic Substance Regulations, but a parallel substance requirement applies under Gibraltar's Income Tax Act and the UK-Gibraltar DTA. The ELP must maintain a registered office in Gibraltar, a resident registered agent, adequate accounting records and evidence that strategic decisions are made in Gibraltar. A foreign-managed ELP that is a pure conduit risks DTA denial, loss of non-resident exemption, reclassification as a taxable Gibraltar resident, and a 15% Pillar Two top-up charge if part of a large MNE group.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Returns

Statutory sanction: Financial penalty of up to 100% of the tax undercharged plus interest; daily default penalties continue until the correct return is filed. Upon criminal conviction on indictment: an unlimited court fine and imprisonment for up to 3 years. The Commissioner may also raise estimated assessments and issue a travel restriction order pending settlement.

Director personal liability: The general partner is jointly and severally liable for the ELP's tax debts and penalties. Where the general partner is a corporate entity, its directors can be pursued personally for civil restitution if they authorised or connived in the fraudulent filing, and may face disqualification orders under the Companies Act 2014.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Denial of DTA benefits; the entire profit distribution is recharacterised as Gibraltar-source and taxed at 12.5%; application of the 15% Pillar Two top-up tax if within an MNE group; the Registrar of Companies may strike off the ELP under the Exempted Limited Partnerships Act 2014 after a 90-day compliance notice.

Director personal liability: Directors of a corporate general partner face disqualification and personal assessment for unpaid tax where the entity is a facade. The Commissioner may impose a travel ban and asset freezing order. Limited partners who participate in management lose their statutory limited-liability shield and become personally liable for all partnership obligations.
AML / CFT Breach (Proceeds of Crime Act 2015)

Statutory sanction: On conviction for money laundering: a fine of up to GIP 500,000 and/or imprisonment for up to 10 years. Regulatory fines under the GFSC AML regime can reach GIP 2 million for the registered agent and the general partner. Offshore PSP contractual termination and permanent account closure are automatic consequences.

Director personal liability: Relevant officers, including the registered agent, are personally liable where the offence was committed with their consent or connivance. The Supreme Court of Gibraltar may issue civil confiscation orders against the directors' personal assets under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and may impose travel bans pending international mutual assistance requests.
Transfer Pricing Misstatement / Thin Capitalisation

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner has statutory power to adjust taxable profits to the arm's length standard and to disallow excessive interest deductions. A misdeclaration penalty of up to 100% of the additional tax assessed is imposed for deliberate concealment, with the statutory interest running from the original due date. Continued non-compliance triggers a formal transfer pricing audit and potential public naming by the Income Tax Office.

Director personal liability: Because an ELP has no minimum capital, the general partner's personal assets are exposed to the full amount of the TP adjustment. A parent-company indemnity is unenforceable unless executed as a Gibraltar-law deed. Directors of the corporate general partner may be personally liable for penalty amounts if they signed or approved the transfer pricing documentation knowing it to be false.
