Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar; official portal: www.taxation.gi / Gibraltar Government Taxation Portal; corporate registry administered through the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) acting as Registrar of Companies and Limited Partnerships.

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity forms an Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) by filing a registration statement and partnership deed with the GFSC Registrar under the Limited Partnerships Act 2015; the Registrar transmits the registration file to the Income Tax Office, which automatically issues a Tax Reference Number (TRN) to the ELP for administrative reporting. The ELP is fiscally transparent, so each non-resident partner is assessed individually on Gibraltar-source profits only; no Gibraltar VAT ID is obtainable or required because Gibraltar imposes no VAT. If UK taxable supplies arise, the ELP must separately register with HMRC under the Non-Established Taxable Person procedure using Form VAT1 or the HMRC online VAT registration service.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible / Territorial_Scope - Gibraltar law treats exempt limited partnerships as statutorily transparent; the partnership is not a taxable person and the partners are subject to income tax only on profits attributable to a permanent establishment or source in Gibraltar (Income Tax Act 2010). Non-resident partners deriving no Gibraltar-source income are outside the charge to Gibraltar tax, and no withholding or entity-level corporate tax is levied on the ELP.

Available election forms

No Gibraltar check-the-box election exists; pass-through treatment is mandatory under the Income Tax Act 2010 and the Limited Partnerships Act 2015. A foreign corporate investor may itself make a US check-the-box election (IRS Form 8832) to classify the ELP as a corporation or as a disregarded entity; the UK hybrid mismatch rules (Finance Act 2019, TIOPA 2010 Part 6A) then apply automatically and can recharacterise the ELP as a taxable entity if the investor jurisdiction treats it as a corporation and the resulting mismatched deduction arises in the UK. No separate Gibraltar entity classification election is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar Import Duty (no VAT, GST or sales tax in Gibraltar); UK VAT applies only when the ELP makes taxable supplies into the United Kingdom under the place-of-supply rules for B2B and B2C digital services.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Gibraltar: no turnover-based registration threshold and no VAT system; import duty is levied transactionally on goods entering Gibraltar. UK VAT: £90,000 standard taxable turnover threshold for established UK businesses; £8,818 (GBP) statutory threshold for non-established suppliers of B2C electronic and digital services into the UK, including programmatic advertising, DSP and SSP platform services, above which UK VAT registration is mandatory.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar: no VAT zero-rating regime exists because Gibraltar levies no VAT; goods physically exported from Gibraltar are outside the charge to Gibraltar import duty. For UK VAT: zero-rating (0%) is available for exports of goods where the exporter holds valid evidence of export within the statutory time frame; programmatic advertising, DSP and SSP services supplied to customers established outside the UK are outside the scope of UK VAT under the B2B place-of-supply rules, provided the supplier holds proof of the customer's business establishment and non-UK status.

  • Gibraltar Customs export entry / UK Customs Export Declaration (CDS/CHIEF)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FX repatriation evidence)
  • HMRC VAT Notice 703 export evidence pack comprising commercial contracts, correspondence, and banking records
  • Customer B2B status and establishment evidence for zero-rating or outside-scope treatment of DSP/SSP digital services

Gibraltar: no mandatory e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API integration or digital stamping regime; customs import and export entries are submitted electronically through the Gibraltar Customs Automated System (SAD-based declarations). If the ELP registers for UK VAT, it must comply with HMRC Making Tax Digital for VAT by using MTD-compatible software and APIs for VAT return submission; non-established digital services suppliers must register and file through HMRC's online Non-Resident Taxable Person service.

For UK VAT: failure to obtain export evidence by the relevant VAT return period invalidates zero-rating; HMRC may claw back the uncharged VAT by assessment with statutory interest extending up to 4 years (6 years for deliberate behaviour), and fraudulent export claims attract penalties up to 100% of the involved VAT plus possible criminal prosecution under the Finance Act 2007 and Value Added Tax Act 1994. For Gibraltar customs: false export or import declarations engage penalties under the Customs Duties Ordinance, including fines of up to three times the duty at stake, forfeiture of goods, and suspension or permanent cancellation of customs authorisations; deliberate evasion may result in imprisonment of responsible officers.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar applies a fully liberalised exchange-control regime: there is no exchange control legislation and no central-bank authorisation is required for any currency conversion. GIP and GBP are freely interchangeable at par, and capital-account as well as current-account operations are fully convertible. The Exempted Limited Partnership (KY_ELP), if granted non-resident tax status under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act and applicable regulations, faces no profit-remittance hurdles; dividends, partnership distributions, interest and royalty-style payments are transferable without withholding, subject only to the bank's AML/KYC verification of the underlying commercial substance and the provision of non-residence confirmation from the Gibraltar Commissioner of Income Tax.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar imposes no statutory surrender, conversion ratio or timeline for export/operating receipts. A qualifying non-resident ELP may retain 100% of invoice proceeds in any currency, hold multi-currency balances in Gibraltar or abroad, and remit or convert funds at commercial rates without central-bank reporting. The only practical impediment is the ELP's internal treasury policy to demonstrate that AdTech revenue streams are genuine trade settlements supported by dated DSP/SSP contracts and transaction audit trails.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (Sterling monetary anchor) and the Government of Gibraltar Currency Board issuing the Gibraltar Pound (GIP) at a fixed 1:1 parity to GBP; prudential and AML/CFT supervision for banking and payments is exercised by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) under the Financial Services Act 2019, the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and the AML/CFT Regulations 2019.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-Medium for use as the principal treasury account. Gibraltar is not an EEA state and does not issue UK-style EMI licences; consequently, the Gibraltar fintech bank Xapo Bank Ltd can provide a regulated deposit and FX wrapper, while UK-licensed EMIs (Revolut Business, Wise, Airwallex) may open IBANs for a Gibraltar-registered ELP if UK substance and UBO disclosures satisfy their AML rules. However, UK EMIs are e-money institutions and do not hold funds as bank deposits, creating counterparty and commingling risk on the 'pass-through' house accounts. Recommended deployment is a sub-limit operational float of no more than 5% of monthly treasury volume; all core liquidity and clearing must sit with a fully licensed tier-1 Gibraltar bank.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

13.5% standard corporate income tax rate in Gibraltar for chargeable periods beginning on or after 1 January 2025 (previously a flat 12.5%); an 18% rate applies to in-scope multinational groups with consolidated revenue above EUR 750 million; lower sector bands continue for utilities/retail and licensed operators. The ELP itself is fiscally transparent and bears no entity-level corporate income tax; chargeable partners are taxed individually on their share of Gibraltar-source profits.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Gibraltar does not impose any withholding tax on distributions or profit allocations made by an ELP to its partners, whether resident or non-resident; the distribution itself is not subject to Gibraltar tax.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Return and full payment due by 30 November immediately following the calendar year in which the accounting period ends; a 31 December year end therefore requires filing and payment by 30 November of the following year. Nil/repayment cases may rely on the 12-month filing window, but unpaid tax carries interest from the 30 November due date.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File (contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation) where the aggregate value of controlled transactions in a chargeable period exceeds GBP 250,000; a Master File applies to multinational groups with consolidated annual turnover exceeding EUR 50 million; Country-by-Country Report applies at EUR 750 million group revenue. Small and medium-sized enterprises (fewer than 50 employees and turnover below GBP 7.5 million) are exempt from the documentation requirement but remain bound by the arm's length principle.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance Rules require any ELP undertaking a relevant activity to maintain adequate personnel, premises, and core income-generating activities in Gibraltar. Programmatic real-time ad exchange and SSP platform operations are not categorised as a designated relevant activity in their own right, but the exploitation of proprietary bidding algorithms, DSP/SSP software, or IP licences brings the IP-related income within the nexus requirements, and an annual substance declaration is mandatory; failure to evidence substance exposes the ELP to penalty, registry strike-off, and partner-level recharacterisation.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate income tax evasion and fraudulent filings

Statutory sanction: Penalty up to 100% of the tax understated plus statutory interest at 7.5% per annum; on indictment, unlimited fines and imprisonment of up to 5 years for tax fraud.

Director personal liability: The General Partner and its directors are jointly and severally liable for the ELP's tax debts; fraudulent conduct pierces the corporate veil, enabling civil recovery orders against personal assets, travel bans pending extradition, and disqualification from acting as a director in Gibraltar.
