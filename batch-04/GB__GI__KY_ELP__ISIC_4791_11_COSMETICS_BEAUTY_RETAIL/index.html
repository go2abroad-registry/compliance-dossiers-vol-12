Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax), operating the eTax portal at https://egov.gibraltar.gov.gi; registration of the Exempted Limited Partnership is through the Gibraltar Registry of Companies; HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) in the United Kingdom is the separate competent authority for UK VAT and direct-distance digital trade compliance via the Government Gateway portal.

E-government filing portal

For a Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership, engage a recognised Gibraltar regulated agent to file Form ELP-1 with the Registry of Companies, obtain the Partnership Registration Number, then submit an application to the Gibraltar Income Tax Office for a Tax Reference Number (TRN) using the standard partnership registration and financial projection forms. Non-resident partners are not assessed on foreign-source income, and no physical presence is required for registration, provided a 'designated resident individual' is appointed for process-servicing purposes. A separate non-established taxable person registration arises with HMRC for UK VAT purposes: if the ELP makes taxable supplies in the UK or exceeds £90,000 of UK distance-sales in a rolling 12-month period, it must register for UK VAT online via Government Gateway, obtain a UK VAT number and Economic Operators Registration and Identification (EORI) number. For EU consignment-based sales, VAT One-Stop Shop (OSS) registration can be made in the relevant EU Member State or via the UK OSS scheme in limited post-Brexit circumstances.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible: A Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership is treated as fiscally transparent for Gibraltar income tax purposes. The partnership is not chargeable to tax at entity level; profits and losses flow through to partners in accordance with the partnership deed. Non-resident limited partners are not within the charge to Gibraltarian taxation on non-Gibraltarian-source profits under the territorial scope, provided no local permanent establishment or local income arises. The ELP is expressly eligible for pass-through classification unless an election is made to apply entity-level taxation.

Available election forms

Under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act and the Exempted Limited Partnerships Act, the ELP may elect to be treated as a taxable company for certain fiscal purposes, including where all partners consent and the election is filed with the Commissioner of Income Tax. Partnerships may also elect to apply the 'Fruit and Control' test to allocate income to the partners genuinely exercising control and ownership, rather than to the nominal limited partners. The ELP may further elect financially transparent treatment under Gibraltar domestic law, consistent with UK Hybrid Mismatch Legislation and Gibraltar's equivalent rules, thereby preventing mismatch deductions and ensuring that a partner resident in the UK or EU is treated as receiving the partnership income directly. No direct 'check-the-box' election exists in Gibraltar, but US partners may elect to apply US Treas. Reg. §301.7701-3 to treat the ELP as a flow-through entity or an association taxable as a corporation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound is fully convertible and redeemable at a fixed one-to-one rate with the pound sterling. There are no Gibraltar exchange controls on current account transactions, capital account movements, or profit remittances. Cosmetic retail e-commerce receipts in GBP, EUR, or USD may be held in designated bank accounts without mandatory conversion. Profit distributions by an Exempted Limited Partnership to non-resident partners are not subject to foreign-exchange approval; the practical remittance hurdles are exclusively commercial and compliance-driven, principally bank onboarding due diligence, FATCA/CRS beneficial ownership reporting, sanctions screening, and verification of underlying sales and source of funds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. No statutory timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio apply to export proceeds in Gibraltar. The exporter and partnership may retain receipts in GIP/GBP, EUR, USD, or any freely convertible currency. The treasury policy should nevertheless require repatriation of Shopify Payments and PayPal settlement balances into the licensed Gibraltar operating account within a regular liquidity cycle, supported by platform settlement reports, to satisfy bank AML monitoring and to provide clean audit trail for the underlying beauty retail sales.

Central bank supervision: No domestic central bank in Gibraltar. Foreign exchange administration and monetary oversight are exercised through HM Treasury (ultimate monetary sovereignty), Government of Gibraltar Currency Commissioners (issuance of the Gibraltar pound, GIP, at strict parity with GBP), and Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the licensing, conduct, and AML/CTF supervisor of banks and payment service providers.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Feasible at a medium confidence level. UK FCA-authorised fintech EMIs such as Wise and Revolut Business, alongside Shopify Payments and PayPal, can be used as front-end collection or settlement rails for low-risk beauty DTC e-commerce. Legal constraints include the loss of EU/EEA passporting into Gibraltar for UK EMIs post-Brexit, the reluctance of some pure digital EMIs to onboard Gibraltar-domiciled limited partnerships with non-resident partners, and the absence of local GFSC licensing for many offshore digital EMIs. The ELP should therefore treat EMIs as supplementary transaction rails only, never as the primary store-of-value layer; settlement balances must flow to a fully licensed Gibraltar commercial bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Partner capital contributions must be wired through SWIFT MT103 from well-regulated overseas banks into the dedicated capital escrow or initial operational account at a licensed Gibraltar bank. The MT103 narrative should clearly state 'Capital Contribution to [ELP legal name] — Partnership Deed dated [date]' and, where required, a supplementary bank reference such as 'CAPINJ/GB__GI__KY_ELP'. No exchange-control permission is required from HM Treasury or GFSC. The receiving bank will verify the remitting bank, source of funds, ultimate beneficial ownership, and sanctions status before accepting funds. High-value contributions should be preceded by a capital introduction pack containing the partnership deed, register of partners, source of wealth statement, and a commercial explanation of the online cosmetics retail roll-out.Supplier and trade settlement disbursements should use SWIFT MT103 for international open-account payments, with clear payment purpose codes in field 70 such as 'Goods Purchase', 'Trade Settlement', or 'Inventory Supply'.
