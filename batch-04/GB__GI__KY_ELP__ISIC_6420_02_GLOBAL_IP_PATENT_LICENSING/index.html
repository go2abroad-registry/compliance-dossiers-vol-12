Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar - Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax), Admiralty House, Gibraltar; official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation; FATCA/CRS registration through the Gibraltar Competent Authority (Tax Information Exchange Office).

E-government filing portal

Foreign-owned Gibraltar ELP must obtain a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) by filing an Application for Registration (Partnership) with the Income Tax Office, attaching the Registrar of Companies' certificate, partnership deed, details of all partners (name, jurisdiction of residence, foreign TIN) and intended activities. Submission by post/email through the registered agent; no minimum capital, no notarization, no in-person visit; processing 3-10 business days. TRN is mandatory for all returns, CRS/FATCA reporting and any withholding declarations.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible / Territorial_Scope. The ELP is fiscally transparent under Income Tax Act 2010 (s.6/s.50): no entity-level charge; each partner is taxed on their profit share. Non-resident partners are chargeable only on Gibraltar-source income; foreign-source patent royalties are outside Gibraltar's territorial charge. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible / Territorial_Scope. The ELP is fiscally transparent under Income Tax Act 2010 (s.6/s.50): no entity-level charge; each partner is taxed on their profit share. Non-resident partners are chargeable only on Gibraltar-source income; foreign-source patent royalties are outside Gibraltar's territorial charge. No designated tax-exempt zone applies to the ELP.

Available election forms

A partnership may elect (with Commissioner consent) to be treated as a taxable company, thereby accessing Gibraltar's Qualifying IP/patent box regime (modified nexus, effective rate reduced below the 10% headline) and corporation tax. Available elections also include: (i) deduction of IP amortization on audited accounts basis; (ii) OECD Pillar Two safe-harbour elections (Transitional CbCR and Permanent Safe Harbour) for MNE groups with euro 750m+ global revenue; (iii) hybrid mismatch notification election under UK/GI anti-hybrid rules where the ELP is treated as opaque in the UK - avoid mismatch.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar Purchase Tax only - Gibraltar is outside the EU VAT Directive and EU Customs Union; no general VAT/GST or sales tax applies to services. Purchase Tax applies narrowly to imported selected chargeable goods (alcohol, tobacco, hydrocarbon oil, motor vehicles and certain luxury items).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A - no turnover threshold for services; no registration for IP royalties. Persons importing/manufacturing chargeable goods (alcohol, tobacco, fuel, vehicles) must register for Purchase Tax with the Commissioner of Purchase Tax. Local currency: Gibraltar Pound (GIP).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - no VAT/GST in Gibraltar, so no 0% export VAT rate or refund mechanism exists for cross-border IP patent licensing royalties. Purchase Tax is not levied on services; exports of goods are outside the purchase-tax base. For UK VAT, the Gibraltar ELP is not within the UK VAT net unless it has a UK fixed establishment; royalties to UK customers are outside the scope of UK VAT with no registration threshold. No statutory FX repatriation deadline applies (field set to 0).

  • Not applicable to royalty services (no customs declaration). Where goods exports are bundled, retain: Gibraltar Customs Single Administrative Document (export declaration), Bill of Lading / Air Waybill, commercial invoice, and foreign import entry as evidence of export.

None. Gibraltar imposes no continuous transaction controls, real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing API or digital stamping mandate. Standard invoice-keeping obligations apply under the Income Tax Act 2010 with 6-year record retention; records must be made available to the Income Tax Office on request.

No export-VAT clawback risk arises. Direct-tax scrutiny: (i) General Anti-Abuse Rule (Income Tax Act 2010 s.9) allows the Commissioner to counteract tax advantages lacking economic substance; (ii) transfer-pricing rules per OECD TP Guidelines require arm's-length royalties; (iii) Gibraltarian substance guidance requires business premises, full-time employees and management in Gibraltar for IP HoldCo, else returns may be recharacterized/excluded from treaty benefits; (iv) penalties for deliberate understatement are up to 100% of tax plus daily default penalties of GIP 60; fraud can attract criminal prosecution.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar has full current and capital account convertibility. The Gibraltar pound is pegged at par to GBP sterling. There are no exchange controls, no repatriation restrictions, and no licensing or approval requirements for dividends, royalties, capital injections, loan repayments, or any other inbound or outbound capital movements. Foreign currency accounts (USD, EUR, GBP, CHF) are freely permitted. The jurisdiction’s offshore status does not limit monetization of IP income.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or conversion rules apply to export proceeds, royalty receipts, or other forms of legal tender in Gibraltar. A Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership may retain all foreign currency receipts offshore or convert them to GIP/GBP at the prevailing market rate without central bank intervention. No mandatory holding period is imposed.

Central bank supervision: The Bank of England (as the monetary policy authority anchoring the sterling area) combined with the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the prudential and banking regulator; the Government of Gibraltar retains note-issuing authority for the Gibraltar pound (GIP).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility: MEDIUM-LOW. Gibraltar sits outside the EEA/UK single market; EU-authorised e-money institutions (e.g. Revolut, Wise) do not offer licensed corporate onboarding for Gibraltar ELPs without a UK/EEA branch passport. UK-regulated EMIs may accept a Gibraltar entity only under a GFSC cross-border reciprocal exemption or through a licensed Gibraltar introducer, but most will decline structures with Cayman-linked general partners or pure IP holding profiles. Offshore digital EMIs (Mercury, Banked, etc.) do not serve Gibraltar. A GFSC-licensed EMI, if available, is the only viable fintech route, but current selection is extremely limited.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound capital injection route: (1) originating bank sends a SWIFT MT103 or Fedwire/SEPA-equivalent to the Gibraltar-licensed bank’s correspondent (e.g. Barclays UK, HSBC London, or JPMorgan New York); (2) instruction must use the partnership’s full legal name and registered address, include the purpose code 'CAPT' (capital transfer) in field 70, and reference the capital contribution clause of the ELP deed; (3) if the contributor is a Cayman ELP, the remittance must attach a notarised general partner resolution and a capital contribution certificate from the Cayman registered office; (4) the receiving Gibraltar bank will apply standard sanctions screening and only require exchange control authorisation if the transaction triggers GFSC or Bank of England instructions (none typically).Outbound royalty and trade settlement disbursements clear via (a) SWIFT MT103 for single cross-border payments with payment purpose code 'ROYL' (royalty) or 'INGT' (intra-group transfer) as appropriate; (b) commercial letters of credit issued on MT700/MT710 (UCP600-compliant) when trade settlement involves acquisition of IP collateral or physical media prototypes; (c) SEPA XML (ISO 20022) for euro payments to EEA beneficiaries using the partnership’s EU IBAN equivalent (available through the Gibraltar bank's courtesy IBAN); (d) Correspondent banking rails via London clearing (CHAPS) for urgent same-day GBP transfers. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 aggregate cross-border controlled transactions for mandatory Local File; Master File required where the multinational group consolidated revenue exceeds GIP 50,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply. A pure equity holding entity may satisfy reduced substance requirements, but an IP HoldCo engaged in patent licensing is treated as carrying on an 'Intellectual Property Business' and must demonstrate core income-generating activities in Gibraltar: strategic development and control of the patents, maintenance and protection of the IP, exploitation and licensing decisions, and assumption of economic risk. The ELP must maintain adequate physical premises, number of employees, and operating expenditure proportionate to the IP activity. Mere post-box arrangements are prohibited; failure is referred to the Income Tax Office and may trigger enhanced disclosure to foreign tax authorities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer Pricing Manipulation / IP Migration Abuse

Statutory sanction: Re-computation of taxable profits on an arm's-length basis; additional tax charged; penalty of up to 100% of the under-assessed tax; interest at the statutory rate; potential disallowance of royalty deductions in the payer jurisdiction and exchange of information under the Country-by-Country framework.

Director personal liability: Directors and designated partners are jointly and severally liable for tax when they cause or permit a false return or deliberately omit chargeable receipts. Civil restitution can be claimed, travel bans may be imposed under Gibraltar's Tax Collection (Enforcement) Act, and deliberate evasion carries imprisonment.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Economic Substance penalty regime: fixed penalty of GIP 1,000; monthly penalty of GIP 1,000 for continued default up to a maximum of GIP 15,000; possible application for judicial strike-off and revocation of the ELP's registration; mandatory notification to the Gibraltar Income Tax Office for onward tax information exchange.

Director personal liability: Designated members and general partners face personal liability for unpaid taxes if the ELP is struck off while owing amounts; director disqualification and, in flagrant cases, a statutory travel ban and contempt proceedings.
Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Non-Declaration

Statutory sanction: Criminal prosecution under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act and Criminal Justice Act; maximum financial penalty of GIP 1,000,000; unpaid taxes plus daily interest; additional civil penalty of 100% of evaded tax; in egregious cases imprisonment of up to seven years.

Director personal liability: Directors, partners, and statutory representatives may be made respondents in penalty proceedings. The court can order personal restitution, seize personal assets, and issue a travel ban to prevent departure pending resolution.
AML / Financial Crime

Statutory sanction: Proceeds of Crime Act and Sanctions Act violation: unlimited fine, confiscation orders over the entire benefit derived, asset freezing, and imprisonment of up to 14 years for money laundering; the financial promoter may be suspended and struck off by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission.

Director personal liability: Designated partners and beneficial owners face direct criminal liability regardless of corporate form; civil asset forfeiture can reach equitable interests in the ELP; travel bans and extradition procedures are available.
