Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar — official website: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax; indirect taxes (customs/excise) administered by the Gibraltar Customs and Immigration Department, Customs Division — official website: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/customs

E-government filing portal

A foreign (Cayman) Exempted Limited Partnership must file an Application for Registration as a Partnership (Form 2) with the Income Tax Office and obtain a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN). Required: certified copy of the Cayman certificate of limited partnership, register of partners, ultimate beneficial owner declarations, local registered office or tax representative letter in Gibraltar, and a declaration of anticipated income. Processing typically 10–15 working days. No VAT ID is obtainable because Gibraltar does not impose VAT; the TRN serves as the sole tax identity. The partnership must also register with the Customs Department for export/import purposes if it intends to move goods through Gibraltar.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists under Gibraltar law. A foreign partnership is ipso facto transparent; partners are taxed personally on their share of profits. Hybrid mismatch alignment is not selected by the taxpayer but follows the statutory anti-hybrid rules in the Income Tax Act for cross-border arrangements. A partnership may, with the prior written consent of the Commissioner of Income Tax, irrevocably elect to be treated as a corporation for Gibraltar tax purposes (rare, non-resident ELPs).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None — no VAT/GST in Gibraltar; limited import duty and excise tax under the Customs and Consumption Tax Ordinance

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A (no VAT registration threshold). Import duty registration is mandatory for any person importing goods into Gibraltar, irrespective of turnover; duty rates depend on the HS classification.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — Gibraltar has no VAT; therefore no zero-rating claim can be made. Exports are outside the scope of Gibraltar consumption tax by default. For corporate tax purposes, export income is treated as falling outside the charge to Gibraltar tax if title passes outside Gibraltar and the partnership has no permanent establishment in Gibraltar; an export declaration and supporting transport documents evidence this territorial approach.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration (SAD / EUR1 if applicable)
  • Original Bill of Lading (or Sea Waybill for dry bulk cargo)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103 credit advice evidencing payment
  • Commercial Invoice issued to the overseas buyer
  • Packing List and Weight List
  • Certificate of Origin (if required by the destination customs)
  • Marine Insurance Certificate

Not applicable for VAT/GST. Customs filings must be made electronically through the Gibraltar Customs Automated System (GCAS) for import and export clearances. No digital stamping or real-time fiscal clearance is required.

Fraudulent export declarations are prosecuted under the Customs Ordinance with penalties of up to 150% of the duty evaded, plus seizure of goods and potential imprisonment (up to 14 years). For corporate tax, misclaimed offshore treatment results in retrospective assessment for up to 6 years, interest at the official rate, and penalties of up to 100% of additional tax due; deliberate concealment carries higher penalties and potential director disqualification.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility on both current and capital accounts. The GIP is a fixed local denomination of the pound sterling and is freely interchangeable with GBP, EUR, USD and other major currencies through licensed banks. There are no profit-remittance restrictions, no prior authorisation for dividend or partnership distributions, and no gatekeeping on liquidation proceeds payable to the Cayman ELP or its non-resident partners. The ELP is tax-transparent and, if non-resident in Gibraltar, no Gibraltar withholding tax applies to distributions; remittances offshore are processed after standard AML and sanctions screening. Capital-account transactions, shareholder loans, intercompany funding and hedging margin flows are unrestricted. The only practical hurdles arise from bank internal policy on offshore partnerships and from OFAC/EU/UN sanctions screening on counterparties, particularly for USD-cleared wires routed through New York correspondents.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Neither Gibraltar nor the United Kingdom imposes statutory export-proceeds surrender, mandatory conversion ratios, or repatriation deadlines. The ELP may retain sulfur trade receivables freely in USD, EUR, GBP or other convertible currencies and may deploy them for onward purchases, broker commissions, demurrage, insurance, freight and hedging margin without any clearing authority approval. The compliance duties are limited to record-keeping under the AML/CFT and sanctions legislation, accurate CRS/FATCA reporting, and, where applicable, an annual Economic Substance return under Gibraltar's Economic Substance Act 2019/2020; wholesale bulk trading of granular elemental sulfur is not a relevant activity for that purpose, but the ELP should document its commercial substance and business rationale contemporaneously to avoid challenge.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar does not maintain an independent central bank; the Gibraltar Currency Commission (a function of Her Majesty's Government of Gibraltar) issues the Gibraltar Pound (GIP) under a statutory peg of 1 GIP = 1 GBP, with Bank of England notes and coins also legal tender. Monetary policy for the pound sterling is set by the Bank of England (United Kingdom). Prudential regulation, conduct supervision and licensing of banks, payment institutions, e-money institutions and DLT providers are administered by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) under the Financial Services Act 2019, the Banking Act 2015 and the AML/CFT regime (AML Act 2019 and Sanctions Regulations). No exchange-control authority or Ministry-level FX approval exists for trade, capital or current transactions.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound capital must arrive by SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 cover payment for high-value amounts) into the Gibraltar International Bank operational account, from an account in the name of the Cayman ELP or the injecting limited partner, using ISO 20022 purpose code 'REMB' (remittance of capital) or 'ECAP' (equity capital increase) in the payment narrative, followed by the text 'Contribution to KY_ELP capital account per Partnership Agreement dated [date]'. The receiving bank's compliance team must receive a pre-notification of the inward transfer with a chain-of-funds statement, the underlying capital-account ledger and, where the payer is not the ELP, a declaration of no tax evasion purpose. For USD clearing, the sending bank should select a correspondent that avoids OFAC-adjacent rejections; for GBP, prefer CHAPS settlement into the GIB account; for EUR, use SEPA CT or TARGET2 via the Gibraltar IBAN. The injection may be split into a capital escrow sub-account and an operational current account to preserve audit clarity; no statutory approval is required.Primary clearing rails: (1) Commercial Letters of Credit - an issuing bank (often the buyer's bank) sends an MT700; the ELP's Gibraltar bank or a confirming UK bank adds confirmation under UCP 600; settlement occurs at sight or usance 30-90 days in USD or EUR against a compliant document package (clean on-board bill of lading, commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, quality/weight certificates, insurance policy). (2) Direct bank wires via SWIFT MT103 for brokerage commissions, demurrage, agency fees, inspection costs and smaller supplier settlements, using purpose codes 'TRAD' or 'PURC'. (3) Documentary collections (D/P or D/A) for established counterparties where full L/C cover is not commercially required. (4) High-value GBP payments via CHAPS and Faster Payments for local Gibraltarian costs; USD via the correspondent network of the Gibraltar bank through a New York clearing bank; EUR via TARGET2/SEPA. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low-Medium. Post-Brexit, EEA-authorized EMIs do not benefit from passporting into Gibraltar; a non-Gibraltar EMI must obtain separate GFSC authorisation for payment or e-money services under the Financial Services Act 2019, and the GFSC has granted only a small number of such licences. Global digital EMIs (Wise, Revolut, Airwallex, etc.) generally classify a Cayman exempted limited partnership as an offshore high-risk structure, frequently reject onboarding or impose low transaction caps, and none can issue, advise on, or confirm commercial letters of credit. For ISIC 4669 bulk sulfur trade with SWIFT LC target rails, an EMI is not a viable treasury backbone. It may only support minor operating expenses when the core trade settlement account sits with a Tier-1 licensed bank and the EMI is GFSC- or FCA-authorised and linked to an approved introduction route.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GBP 1,000,000: aggregate related-party transactions (goods, freight, brokerage, and management fees) in a chargeable period trigger a mandatory contemporaneous arm's length documentation file. MNE groups with consolidated revenue exceeding EUR 750,000,000 must prepare an OECD-aligned Master File and Local File and submit a Country-by-Country Report.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations 2019 apply to ELPs carrying on a relevant activity, including distribution and service centre activities. A sulfur ore brokerage deriving income from purchase-and-resale logistics with related parties must demonstrate premises, full-time employees, direction, and core income-generating activities in Gibraltar. Pure third-party brokerage and shipping agency activities are generally outside the scope, but an annual ESR self-assessment return must still be filed to preserve the low-risk status.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Deliberate Under-Declaration

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of up to 100% of the tax understated plus interest; criminal conviction for tax evasion carries an unlimited fine and a term of imprisonment. Continuing failure to file returns exposes the ELP to daily default surcharges and potential revocation of the transparency election.

Director personal liability: The general partner and any controlling corporate partner may be assessed personally on a joint and several basis where the ELP is deployed as a facade; Gibraltar courts may impose director disqualification for up to 15 years, travel and prohibition orders, civil restitution of evaded tax, and personal bankruptcy exposure.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Financial penalty per infraction plus daily default penalties for continued failure; the Commissioner may apply to the Supreme Court for strike-off. Adverse ESR findings are automatically exchanged with UK and EU tax authorities, triggering blacklisting, CFC attribution abroad, and enhanced AML scrutiny.

Director personal liability: Liability pierces through to the general partner and any Gibraltar-resident designated officer; the court may order compulsory winding-up, asset freezing, director disqualification, and personal liability for penalty amounts where the entity is dissolved or insolvent.
AML / Counter-Terrorist Financing Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and AML Regulations, conviction for money laundering carries an unlimited fine and imprisonment of up to 14 years; the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit and Financial Services Commission may impose regulatory fines and suspend the ELP's bank and EMI payment rails.

Director personal liability: The Money Laundering Reporting Officer, general partner, and beneficial owners face criminal prosecution, confiscation orders under POCA, travel bans, and personal civil liability; veil-piercing is applied readily where the ELP is found to be a mere instrument of money transmission.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing and False Documentation

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner may adjust chargeable income to the arm's length position, impose a penalty of up to 100% of the underassessment, and charge interest. Serious mispricing with fabricated documentation is prosecuted as fraudulent tax evasion.

Director personal liability: Directors and partners who knowingly sign false transfer pricing documentation are jointly and severally liable for adjusted taxes and penalties; the court may grant worldwide asset disclosure orders, sequestration, and travel restrictions pending full recovery.
