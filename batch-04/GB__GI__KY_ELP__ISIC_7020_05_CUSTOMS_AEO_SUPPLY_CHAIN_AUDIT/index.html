Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar; portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax

E-government filing portal

A Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) obtains a statutory Income Tax Reference Number (ITN) by filing the relevant partnership registration documents, including the Limited Partnership Registration Form and partner particulars, with the Gibraltar Companies House and the Income Tax Office. Non-resident partners electing for Gibraltar source-only taxation may be required to file annual declarations to evidence non-resident status and to obtain a partner-level ITN for any Gibraltar-source income assessment.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

ELPs may elect to be treated as transparent for income tax purposes under the Limited Partnerships Act 2015 and the Gibraltar Income Tax Act. No check-the-box system exists; however, partner-level elections and declarations may be used to characterise non-resident partners as outside Gibraltar territorial tax jurisdiction. VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST system; Customs and Excise Duty regime administered by HM Customs Gibraltar

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no general VAT/GST registration threshold. Persons importing or exporting commercial goods, or holding customs authorisations (including AEO), must register with HM Customs Gibraltar under the Customs and Excise Duties Act, irrespective of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable for VAT purposes because Gibraltar does not levy VAT. Customs duty is, however, charged at importation; exportation of goods is outside the charge to duty, and duty-paid imported goods subsequently exported may be eligible for remission or repayment under the re-export provisions of the Customs and Excise Duties Act, provided the export is properly declared and evidence is retained.

  • Customs export declaration (electronic declaration lodged with HM Customs Gibraltar)
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Inward remittance certificate or proof of export payment received in Gibraltar (where exchange control evidence is required)
  • Authorized Economic Operator (AEO) certificate number and security-related documentation where applicable

None. There is no statutory electronic invoicing or real-time fiscal clearance mandate. Customs declarations are submitted electronically through the Gibraltar customs declaration processing system, but no digital stamping or fiscal inventory requiring a third-party fiscal stamp is imposed.

HM Customs Gibraltar has authority to conduct retrospective audits of export declarations. The making of false statements for export duty relief may result in forfeiture of goods, monetary penalties up to 200% of the duty at stake, and criminal prosecution. Clawback of any duty remission is retrospective if the exporter fails to maintain supporting export evidence for at least six years or if the goods are not exported within statutory time limits.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar operates a fixed-peg currency board system under which the Gibraltar pound (GIP) is fully convertible at parity with GBP. There are no exchange control restrictions on current or capital account transactions, and non-resident partners may freely remit profits, distributions, and management fees without pre-approval or withholding taxation. The only practical hurdles are bank-level CDD requirements, economic-substance inquiry by licensed banks, and UK financial sanctions screening; no statutory convertibility or remittance bottleneck exists for the ELP's AEO audit service receipts.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion regime applies in Gibraltar. Service revenue from cross-border AEO certification and supply-chain audit engagements may be retained indefinitely in GIP, GBP, USD, or EUR with no required conversion ratio, no holding-period limit, and no repatriation deadline. Banks may nevertheless require underlying engagement contracts to evidence the commercial purpose of incoming service fees.

Central bank supervision: Government of Gibraltar - Ministry of Finance (Commissioner of Currency) in conjunction with the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the bank user; the Bank of England (UK) acts as the anchor central bank of the sterling area for GBP/GIP clearing and monetary stability reference.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: HIGH for operational cash-flow rails, including client fee collection and small-vendor disbursements, but MEDIUM-LOW for escrow, partner-capital parking, or large milestone settlements. Legal constraints: a Gibraltar-registered ELP may open UK/EEA EMI accounts, notably Wise Business, using the entity's registered address, UBO declarations, and partnership documents; however, EMIs frequently reject accounts lacking economic-substance evidence and may terminate relationships if the account is used as a shadow treasury. Wise is expressly viable as the named payment rail for sub-threshold AEO consulting fees, with monthly reconciliation to the tier-1 bank account.

Late filing: fixed penalty of GBP 100, increasing to GBP 500 where the return remains outstanding for more than three months; daily penalty of GBP 10 per day after six months, capped at GBP 1,200. Negligent or fraudulent zero declaration: civil penalty up to 100% of the tax underpaid, plus interest at the rate of 8% per annum; criminal fines and imprisonment apply where evasion is proved.
Box A Relevant activity classification: headquarters, distribution and service centre, financing and leasing, intellectual property, or other ESR category. An exempt ELP must mark 'None – exempt non-resident ELP' and confirm the absence of Gibraltar-based relevant activity. Incorrect classification draws ESR scrutiny and may attract penalties. Failure to file: GBP 25,000 fixed penalty. Continued substantive failure after assessment: additional monthly penalty of GBP 25,000 or 10% of Gibraltar turnover, up to a maximum of GBP 100,000 per month. The general partner and any person responsible for the false filing are jointly and severally liable for such penalties.
Box B Management and control: where are board/partnership management decisions taken? State that all decision-making occurs outside Gibraltar for a non-resident exempt ELP; if any management meeting is held in Gibraltar, tax residence may be triggered and the exemption may be invalidated. Failure to file: GBP 25,000 fixed penalty. Continued substantive failure after assessment: additional monthly penalty of GBP 25,000 or 10% of Gibraltar turnover, up to a maximum of GBP 100,000 per month. The general partner and any person responsible for the false filing are jointly and severally liable for such penalties.
Box C Employees and physical premises in Gibraltar. For exempt ELPs, state nil employees and no premises. Substantive commercial activity in Gibraltar without an adequate local workforce or place of business indicates shell-company abuse and ESR failure. Failure to file: GBP 25,000 fixed penalty. Continued substantive failure after assessment: additional monthly penalty of GBP 25,000 or 10% of Gibraltar turnover, up to a maximum of GBP 100,000 per month. The general partner and any person responsible for the false filing are jointly and severally liable for such penalties.
Box D Core income-generating activities performed in Gibraltar. Exempt ELP: insert 'None.' If any AEO audit or supply-chain-consulting activity is actually performed from Gibraltar, the ELP must be treated as tax-resident and liable to 12.5% tax. Failure to file: GBP 25,000 fixed penalty. Continued substantive failure after assessment: additional monthly penalty of GBP 25,000 or 10% of Gibraltar turnover, up to a maximum of GBP 100,000 per month. The general partner and any person responsible for the false filing are jointly and severally liable for such penalties.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Contemporaneous Local File documentation is mandatory where the aggregate annual value of controlled transactions exceeds GBP 2,000,000, or where the Gibraltar entity records turnover above GBP 20,000,000; a Master File is required when the multinational group's consolidated annual revenue exceeds GBP 200,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar's Economic Substance Regulations under the Income Tax Act 2010 apply to entities and partnerships that are tax-resident in Gibraltar and that carry on relevant activities. An Exempted Limited Partnership that remains non-resident and performs no Gibraltar business activity is outside the scope; however, if it engages in AEO certification, security auditing, or supply-chain consultancy through a Gibraltar permanent establishment, it must demonstrate adequate employment, premises, local decision-making, and core income-generating activities in Gibraltar. The ELP must file the annual Economic Substance Return and may not rely on the exemption certificate to shield substantive local operations.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Nil Return

Statutory sanction: Additional assessment equal to 100% of underpaid tax; civil penalty up to 300% of the evaded tax; criminal conviction for tax fraud carrying fines up to GBP 250,000 and a prison term of up to seven years under the Income Tax Act 2010.

Director personal liability: The general partner and any senior individual knowingly involved are jointly and severally liable for the full tax and penalties; the Gibraltar court may issue travel bans, asset freezing orders, and manager disqualification orders for up to fifteen years.
Transfer Pricing / Thin Capitalisation Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: The Income Tax Office may adjust taxable profits; failure to produce documentation attracts a penalty of up to 50% of the underpaid tax, plus interest at 8% per annum; a separate late-documentation penalty of GBP 3,000 per accounting period may apply.

Director personal liability: The general partner who signs inaccurate transfer pricing schedules is personally liable for culpable penalties and may be prohibited from acting in a fiduciary or AEO-accredited official capacity; an administrative disqualification of GBP 10,000 per individual may be imposed.
Economic Substance Failure / Shell Company Abuse

Statutory sanction: Revocation of exemption, retroactive taxation of profits at 12.5% for up to four prior years; ESR penalty of GBP 25,000 for the first substantive failure; recurring monthly penalties of GBP 25,000; cancellation of the Gibraltar trade licence and removal from the register of exempt partnerships.

Director personal liability: General partners and shadow directors are jointly and severally liable for back taxes and penalties; the court may impose a five-year travel ban, order winding up of the ELP, and hold the general partner personally liable for the entity's unpaid liabilities.
AML / AEO Supply Chain Fraud

Statutory sanction: Criminal fines under the Criminal Justice (Money Laundering) Act and Proceeds of Crime Act: up to GBP 250,000 or twice the value of the laundered benefit; confiscation orders; revocation of AEO accreditation and removal from the Approved Economic Operator register; additional customs penalties under the Customs (Criminal Penalties) Regulations.

Director personal liability: Individuals involved in processing illicit payments are chargeable for money laundering offences; directors and general partners face civil liability for damage to third parties, travel bans, and imprisonment of up to fourteen years for serious offences.
