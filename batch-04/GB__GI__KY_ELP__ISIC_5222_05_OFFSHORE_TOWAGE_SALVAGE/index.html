Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar, Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax), 6-10 Bayside Road, Gibraltar; e-Portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership obtains a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN) through the statutory registration of the ELP with the Gibraltar Companies Registry and the filing of partnership particulars with the Income Tax Office. The application must nominate a Gibraltar-resident general partner and a registered office. There is no minimum capital requirement and non-resident partners need not obtain individual Gibraltar TINs unless they have Gibraltar-source taxable income. The Income Tax Office issues the TIN after completeness check, usually within one to three business days.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible; the ELP is statutorily transparent. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; the ELP is statutorily transparent. Income is taxed in the hands of partners only, and non-resident partners are outside the charge to Gibraltar tax on non-Gibraltar source income (territorial scope).

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists under Gibraltar law; transparency is mandatory for limited partnerships. The partnership deed may elect allocation of taxable profits and losses among partners. Gibraltar tax law applies hybrid mismatch limitation rules comparable to the OECD BEPS recommendations, requiring affected ELPs to make disclosure where dual classification produces a deduction/no-inclusion or double-deduction outcome.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax. Gibraltar imposes only customs and excise duties on imported and locally manufactured dutiable goods; services, including offshore towage and salvage, are outside any general indirect tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. A person who imports goods into Gibraltar must obtain customs clearance for each import declaration regardless of turnover. Gibraltar Pound (GIP) is equal to GBP and is the settlement currency.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable for VAT purposes because Gibraltar has no VAT/GST. For customs duty purposes, goods exported from Gibraltar are relieved from duty only where they have not been released for free circulation and are covered by an approved export/re-export procedure. Offshore towage and salvage services performed by a Gibraltar ELP for non-resident clients are outside the Gibraltar indirect tax net and, under the territorial income tax system, are not Gibraltar source income absent a Gibraltar permanent establishment.

  • Customs export declaration (Gibraltar export/re-export entry) for tug, towed unit, spares, stores and bunkers
  • Bill of Lading / Sea Waybill and port clearance certificate
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Towage/salvage contract (BIMCO TOWCON/TOWHIRE or Lloyd's Open Form)
  • Lloyd's salvage security or escrow settlement statement
  • Inward remittance certificate / SWIFT MT103 evidencing settlement
  • Vessel certificate of registry and flag state documentation

No real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or API reporting mandate applies. Tax invoices may be issued in paper or electronic form. Customs declarations must be filed electronically through the Gibraltar Customs declaration portal before export/import clearance.

Gibraltar Customs and Income Tax Office conduct risk-based audits of any duty relief, re-export, and foreign income claims. False or fraudulent documentation triggers forfeiture of goods/equipment, immediate retrospective clawback of any duty or tax relief, penalties up to 100% of duty/tax evaded, personal liability on the general partner where the ELP is insolvent, and criminal fines and imprisonment under the Customs and Excise Act and Income Tax Act. Persistent breach can also expose the ELP to deregistration and sanctions under AML/CFT legislation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar maintains full convertibility on both current and capital account. There are no exchange-control laws, no advance-approval screens on foreign investment, and no restrictions on repatriation of dividends, partnership distributions, interest, or salvage and towage fee remittances. The GIP trades at par with GBP; banks freely operate multi-currency accounts in GIP, GBP, USD and EUR. Residual friction arises solely from bank-level anti-money-laundering (AML) source-of-funds and source-of-wealth due diligence, not from regulatory currency controls.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Neither the United Kingdom nor Gibraltar imposes statutory export-proceeds surrender, mandatory conversion, or holding-period requirements for service exports. Deep-sea towage and maritime salvage service revenues may be retained in any convertible currency and netted against operating expenses without central-bank approval; a bank obtaining a signed salvage or towage contract, commercial invoice and vessel ownership extract is sufficient to process onward remittance.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England as monetary anchor for the GBP peg, together with the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the licensing and supervisory authority for banking, payments, investment and fiduciary activity under the Financial Services Act 2019 and the Banking Act 2020. The Government of Gibraltar issues Gibraltar pound (GIP) banknotes under the Currency Notes Act (Gibraltar) 1934 at a fixed 1:1 parity with GBP.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium-Low viability, subject to material legal constraints. Gibraltar-licensed electronic money institutions (EMIs) under the Financial Services (Electronic Money) Act 2020 may support limited operational disbursements, but most do not accommodate offshore Cayman ELP structures, high-risk maritime salvage payment corridors, or Commercial Letter of Credit issuance. UK-authorized EMIs benefit from temporary-permissions equivalence via the Gibraltar (Miscellaneous Amendments) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, yet they commonly cap turnover, restrict towage or salvage SIC codes, and refuse escrow or LC roles. Core treasury functions should therefore remain with tier-1 banks; EMIs may be used only for petty disbursements and crew welfare payments.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound routing requires SWIFT MT103 wires from the Cayman GP's own bank account or a regulated equity bridge in GIP, GBP, or USD. The wire must state an ISO 20022 purpose code reflecting equity contribution ('EQUI') or partner loan ('LOAN') in field 70; for maritime operations, 'MAR3' may be used where the injection is linked to a specific towage or salvage contract. The receiving Gibraltar bank requires the capital commitment schedule, amended partnership filing, and source-of-wealth evidence to avoid a funds hold. Credits are then applied to the capital escrow account and recorded contemporaneously in the ELP capital ledger. No Gibraltar reserve-bank authorization or advance declaration is required because no exchange controls exist.Operational outflows for salvage and towage are executed via SWIFT MT103 and MT202 for crew wages, bunkering, port fees, subcontractor settlements, and insurance premiums. Larger contract disbursements, particularly post-salvage awards or vessel-repair payments, are structured through a Commercial Letter of Credit issued by the Gibraltar bank under UCP 600, or as standby LCs and escrow releases aligned to Lloyd's Open Form salvage award clauses. Payments to non-EU providers use the ordinary MT103 corridor; payments to UK and EU suppliers may also clear via UK Faster Payments or SEPA where both counterparts hold eligible accounts. All wire messages must carry the correct ISO 20022 purpose code and reference the underlying salvage or towage contract to preserve audit continuity.FX Forwards (GIP/GBP, GBP/USD, EUR/USD) up to 12 months tenor, permitted under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement without central-bank approvalNon-Deliverable Forwards (NDFs) in USD/EUR against GIP/GBP where a convertibility or settlement-currency mismatch exists and the bank confirms hedge-leg eligibility by internal policyPlain-vanilla Cross-Currency Swaps (GBP/USD and USD/EUR) to match salvage-award receipts with bunker-fuel and repair cost liabilitiesCase-by-case currency options permitted under the bank's derivatives eligibility policy, restricted to hedging an underlying commercial towage or salvage exposure; speculative transactions are prohibited
