Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar Income Tax Office (official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation); Registrar of Companies, Gibraltar (Companies House Gibraltar) under the Limited Partnerships Act; HM Government of Gibraltar Customs Department for customs registration of goods flows.

E-government filing portal

1) File the partnership registration statement with the Registrar of Companies under the Limited Partnerships Act to obtain the Gibraltar Limited Partnership number; 2) submit the partnership registration form to the Income Tax Office to obtain the partnership Tax Reference Number (TRN); 3) disclose each partner's name, address, capital contribution and profit share; 4) non-resident partners without Gibraltar-source income are not required to file an individual return but must be disclosed; 5) where the CRO ships clinical-trial materials, obtain a Customs Business Authorisation; 6) the ELP itself is transparent and holds the TRN only for compliance information purposes.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — 1) File the partnership registration statement with the Registrar of Companies under the Limited Partnerships Act to obtain the Gibraltar Limited Partnership number; 2) submit the partnership registration form to the Income Tax Office to obtain the partnership Tax Reference Number (TRN); 3) disclose each partner's name, address, capital contribution and profit share; 4) non-resident partners without Gibraltar-source income are not required to file an individual return but must be disclosed; 5) where the CRO ships clinical-trial materials, obtain a Customs Business Authorisation; 6) the ELP itself is transparent and holds the TRN only for compliance information purposes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act, partnerships are fiscally transparent by default; the partners may jointly elect for the partnership to be treated as a separate taxable person subject to Income Tax Officer approval; no US-style check-the-box election exists; hybrid-mismatch and OECD BEPS Action 2 principles apply where an election would produce double non-taxation; the medical-trial CRO licence does not alter partnership transparency.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST applies in Gibraltar because Gibraltar is outside the EU VAT territory; the only indirect levy is customs duty and excise duty under the Customs Duty Ordinance for physical goods crossing the frontier.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A - Gibraltar has no VAT or GST registration threshold; importers and exporters of goods must obtain a Customs Business Authorisation with no minimum economic turnover; clinical-trial CRO services are outside any turnover-based indirect tax registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

N/A under indirect tax law because Gibraltar imposes no VAT and therefore no 0% export zero-rating is claimable; for income tax purposes, CRO management fees derived from multinational drug trials are excluded from Gibraltar taxation only if the services are performed wholly outside Gibraltar, contracts are concluded outside Gibraltar, no Gibraltar permanent establishment exists, and the partnership's management and control, or the relevant partner's activity, are carried on outside Gibraltar.

  • Customs Export Declaration where clinical-trial materials cross the customs frontier
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill covering trial supplies
  • Signed Global Clinical Trial Master Services Agreement and each trial work order
  • Detailed offshore service-delivery report evidencing performance outside Gibraltar
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT credit confirmation evidencing FX repatriation of milestone fees
  • Statutory declaration of non-residence or offshore management for each ELP partner

N/A - no real-time fiscal clearance, API fiscalisation, digital stamping or mandatory e-invoicing regime exists in Gibraltar; customs declarations are filed through the Gibraltar Customs electronic interface; CRO trial milestone invoices may be issued electronically without government pre-clearance.

The Income Tax Office may reopen assessments up to six years and without time limit in cases of fraud; fabricating offshore-performance evidence attracts penalties up to 100% of the tax understated, daily default penalties, interest and criminal prosecution for tax evasion; application of the general anti-abuse rule or hybrid-mismatch rules permits retrospective clawback of tax relief together with interest on the entire partnership income misattributed to Gibraltar.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar operates a broadly open foreign-exchange regime with no general exchange-control system, capital-control approval process, or statutory restriction on conversion between Gibraltar pounds, sterling, euros, United States dollars, and other major currencies. Current-account receipts and payments, including payments for CRO services, are generally transferable subject to bank compliance, sanctions screening, source-of-funds controls, and documentary evidence. Capital-account transactions, shareholder funding, intercompany loans, distributions, and repatriation of profits generally do not require a separate foreign-exchange licence, but must be supported by valid corporate approvals, accounting records, tax analysis, beneficial-ownership information, and evidence that the payment is not a disguised dividend, prohibited distribution, sham loan, sanctions breach, or transaction involving an unlicensed financial service. The proposed Cayman exempted limited partnership structure is not a standard Gibraltar entity form; the entity's legal existence, governing law, registration status, partner authority, tax classification, and ability to maintain Gibraltar accounts must be confirmed before onboarding. A Gibraltar bank may require a registered Gibraltar entity, a recognised foreign partnership, or a Gibraltar branch or manager with demonstrable local nexus.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no general Gibraltar statutory requirement to surrender export proceeds, convert a prescribed percentage of foreign-currency receipts, or repatriate receipts within a fixed statutory period. CRO service receipts should nevertheless be collected into an account held by the contracting entity or an expressly authorised collection agent, reconciled to invoices and contracts, and converted or retained in the operating currency under a documented treasury policy. Banks may impose contractual settlement deadlines, require invoice and service-delivery evidence, and apply enhanced review to large, unusual, third-party, pass-through, or high-risk-country receipts. UK and Gibraltar sanctions, anti-money-laundering, tax-reporting, transfer-pricing, and accounting obligations remain applicable notwithstanding the absence of exchange controls.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar has no independent central bank. Banking and payment-services supervision is principally conducted by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission. The Government of Gibraltar administers local currency legislation, while the Gibraltar pound is fully interchangeable with sterling at par and Gibraltar banks commonly access sterling clearing through UK banking relationships. The Bank of England is not the general prudential regulator of Gibraltar-incorporated banks.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium for supplementary payment operations and Low to Medium as the primary treasury bank. A UK or Gibraltar electronic-money institution may provide payment accounts, virtual IBANs, foreign-exchange services, and cross-border transfers, but an EMI account is not a bank deposit account and may not provide deposit protection, lending, commercial letters of credit, overdrafts, or reliable access to every correspondent rail. A non-Gibraltar offshore EMI cannot be assumed to be authorised to provide payment services to a Gibraltar entity. UK market-access rights must be verified after the end of automatic UK-EU passporting, and Gibraltar authorisation, local rules, agent arrangements, safeguarding structure, customer-money terms, and permitted-currency coverage must be checked. Use an EMI only after confirming its licence, safeguarding bank, settlement geography, sanctions policy, legal contracting entity, virtual-IBAN ownership, account-closure procedures, and ability to process clinical-trial-related payments.
