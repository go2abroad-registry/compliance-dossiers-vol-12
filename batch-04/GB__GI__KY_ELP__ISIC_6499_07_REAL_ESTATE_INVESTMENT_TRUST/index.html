Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, Taxation Division, HM Government of Gibraltar; official portal: the Income Tax Office e-Services platform (tax.gi) and the HM Government of Gibraltar taxation pages (gibraltar.gov.gi).

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity such as an Exempted Limited Partnership must first complete registration with the Gibraltar Companies House / Registrar of Companies and obtain its Gibraltar company/partnership number and Certificate of Registration. It must next submit the prescribed registration forms and partnership details (including names, addresses, registered office and tax representative, if any) to the Income Tax Office. The Commissioner then issues a Gibraltarian Tax Identification Number (TIN). If the ELP has a permanent establishment or Gibraltar-source activity, it must additionally register for payroll/Social Insurance and, if importing or exporting goods, obtain a customs registration with HM Customs Gibraltar. No VAT ID is required or issued because Gibraltar does not levy VAT/GST.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible / Territorial_Scope. The ELP is fiscally transparent under Gibraltar income tax law; partners are taxed on their share of Gibraltar-source income. There is no entity-level corporation tax on the ELP unless a specific statutory election or the Economic Substance (for Companies) regime applies to re-characterise it as an opaque company. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible / Territorial_Scope. The ELP is fiscally transparent under Gibraltar income tax law; partners are taxed on their share of Gibraltar-source income. There is no entity-level corporation tax on the ELP unless a specific statutory election or the Economic Substance (for Companies) regime applies to re-characterise it as an opaque company. Non-resident partners with no Gibraltar-source income or no permanent establishment have no Gibraltar tax liability.

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no U.S.-style check-the-box election. Classification as transparent or opaque follows from legal form. An ELP may, to the extent permitted by the Income Tax Act, elect for treatment as a qualifying fund/passive investment vehicle or a definitive tax-status certificate where applicable; hybrid mismatch elections and notifications required under the OECD BEPS Action 2 framework must be considered where a payment is deductible in one jurisdiction and not included in another (deduction / no-inclusion, double inclusion / non-inclusion and imported mismatch situations). No broad hybrid election is available to make a transparent ELP taxable as a company solely at the entity's option.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT / GST / Sales and Use Tax. Gibraltar levies customs import duties and excise duties on selected products, and stamp duties on certain transactions; it is outside the UK/VAT EU area.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

None (GIP 0). Gibraltar imposes no VAT/GST and therefore no economic turnover threshold triggers an indirect-tax registration. Importers/exporters needing to clear goods through Gibraltar customs must register with HM Customs separately.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in Gibraltar: exports are outside the scope of any Gibraltar indirect tax and no VAT zero-rating or refund mechanism exists. If goods are exported from the United Kingdom to Gibraltar or overseas, UK VAT zero-rating may be claimed by the UK supplier under VAT Act 1994, Schedule 8, Group 13 provided the statutory export evidence is held within the permitted time limit. For an ELP holding real estate in Gibraltar, the sale of a capital asset is not an export zero-rated supply; Gibraltar stamp duty may apply to the transfer, and income/property taxes remain territorial.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List

No real-time fiscal clearance, API mandate or digital stamping for invoices exists in Gibraltar. Commercial invoices may be issued electronically or on paper. For customs purposes electronic declarations are required for export/import, but no continuous invoice clearance system applies; records must be retained for at least six years to support income tax and customs compliance.

Gibraltar has no VAT zero-rating claim to claw back. However, the Commissioner of Income Tax may raise assessments within six years of the chargeable date (unlimited in cases of fraud/neglect), charge late-payment interest, and impose penalties of up to 100% of the understated tax for careless or deliberate inaccuracies. False or abusive UK export VAT claims are audited with retrospective assessment and clawback of input VAT, penalties under Finance Act 2007 / Value Added Tax Act 1994, and potential criminal investigation. Fraudulent customs declarations in Gibraltar expose the exporter to seizure and forfeiture of goods, monetary penalties under the Customs and Excise Duties Act, and prosecution for fraudulent evasion of duty with imprisonment and unlimited fines.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current-account and capital-account convertibility in both the United Kingdom and Gibraltar. There are no exchange-control authorizations, central bank permits, or reserve requirements applicable to inward capital contributions, rental income, refinancing proceeds, or profit remittances of a Cayman Islands Exempted Limited Partnership operating a real estate investment trust through the UK/Gibraltar corridor. Dividend and interest distributions may be denominated and remitted in GIP, GBP, EUR, or USD without conversion mandate. The only residual obligations are GFSC/BoE AML transaction reporting thresholds and UK ONS balance-of-payments classification for significant direct-investment flows; no profit-repatriation tax clearance or conversion certificate is required.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. The United Kingdom abolished statutory surrender and conversion requirements for export and foreign-currency proceeds upon the repeal of exchange control legislation in 1979, and Gibraltar imposes no statutory timeline, mandatory conversion ratio, or surrender obligation on any foreign receipts. REIT rental income and partnership distributions are not classified as export proceeds under any UK or Gibraltar instrument; the ELP may retain, convert, or remit one hundred percent of foreign-currency balances without authorization.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (BoE) as the United Kingdom's central bank and sole authority for sterling clearing and RTGS settlement used by Gibraltar banks; HM Treasury holds residual foreign-exchange control powers, with the Exchange Control Act 1947 repealed in 1979; Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the licensing and AML/CFT supervisor for all Gibraltar-incorporated or branch banks and e-money institutions. Gibraltar maintains no separate central bank; the Gibraltar pound (GIP) is issued by the Government of Gibraltar at a fixed 1:1 peg to GBP and is freely interchangeable.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low for treasury-critical functions; Medium for ancillary operational flows. Gibraltar-licensed electronic money institutions under the Financial Services (Electronic Money) Regulations 2020, and UK-authorized EMIs operating through the UK-Gibraltar financial-services bridge, may support low-value tenant collections, utility payments, expense reimbursements, and cross-currency expense settlements. EMIs are not viable as the primary escrow, clearing, or dividend-distribution rail because e-money balances are not deposits, attract no statutory deposit-guarantee protection equivalent to that enjoyed by Gibraltar-licensed banks, and cannot hold client money or issue guarantees in the manner required by GFSC guidance for an income-generating property trust with statutory reserve obligations.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

Flat 12.5% Gibraltar corporation tax on taxable profits of a resident company or resident partnership; a non-resident Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is chargeable only on Gibraltar-source income, leaving foreign rental profits from the real estate portfolio outside the Gibraltar tax net.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Gibraltar does not impose withholding tax on dividends paid by a Gibraltar resident company or by an ELP, whether to resident or non-resident partners; no Gibraltar source tax is levied on distributions from a non-resident ELP because the underlying rental income has no Gibraltar source.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The return must be filed within 9 months of the ELP's accounting reference date (e.g., 30 September for a 31 December fiscal year end); the Gibraltar tax assessment, if any, becomes payable 30 days after notice of assessment.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File in GIP (Gibraltar pounds) if the aggregate value of controlled transactions between the ELP and related parties exceeds GIP 750,000 in any fiscal year; Master File documentation is required where the ELP belongs to a multinational group with consolidated turnover of at least GIP 750 million and more than 5,000 employees globally; a contemporaneous TP policy summary must be retained for all lower-value transactions.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations 2019 (subsequently amended in 2021) apply to all relevant activities conducted by a Gibraltar-resident ELP or a non-resident ELP with a Gibraltar permanent establishment. A REIT will be classified as a 'Pure Equity Holding Company' if it only holds equity interests in property-owning SPVs and receives only dividends/capital gains; that classification carries a reduced substance requirement of filing an annual ESR return, providing a registered office in Gibraltar, and demonstrating adequate control and staff for the holding activity. If the ELP additionally conducts financing, leasing, or treasury functions for the property SPVs, the jurisdiction applies the full 'Finance & Leasing' ESR test: physical premises in Gibraltar, full-time employees in Gibraltar, and the core income-generating decision-making performed in Gibraltar, all evidenced by board minutes, payroll records, and expense allocations.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Penalty under Section 159 of the Income Tax Act 2018 equal to 100% of the additional tax due and payable, plus prejudicial interest accruing at 8% per annum; criminal prosecution on conviction carries a fine of up to GIP 50,000 and imprisonment not exceeding 12 months; where the tax withheld from partners' distributions is not accounted for, a separate penalty of up to GIP 10,000 per failure applies.

Director personal liability: The General Partner (GP) and any managing partner who knowingly signed a false return are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax under the Partnership Act (Cap 168); civil restitution orders can attach to the GP's personal assets, travel bans are imposed through the Criminal Court under the Criminal Justice Act 2015, and senior officers face imprisonment for up to 14 years where the same conduct constitutes money laundering under Section 342 of the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Initial penalty of GIP 5,000 for failure to meet the economic substance test; continuation penalty of GIP 50,000 for each subsequent month; for the final stage the Commissioner may assess an additional penalty of up to GIP 200,000 or 10% of the ELP's turnover, whichever is greater; the entity is liable to strike-off from the Gibraltar Register under the Companies Act with compulsory liquidation of any Gibraltar property assets.

Director personal liability: The GP and shadow directors are jointly and severally liable for the penalties, and the court may issue a disqualification order barring them from acting as a director or GP in Gibraltar for up to 10 years; where the ESR failure involves the manufacture of false invoices to demonstrate substance, criminal liability attaches under Section 6 of the Fraud Act 2011 with up to 10 years of imprisonment.
AML / Economic Crime

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and the AML Regulations 2020, the GP and the Gibraltar licensed administrator face a fine of up to GIP 100,000 per offence plus confiscation orders representing the totality of the laundered distributions; the Financial Services Commission may revoke the REIT's statutory license and require immediate cessation of distribution activities.

Director personal liability: Directors and GP officers who knowingly execute or permit the suspicious wire transfers are guilty of an offence carrying a maximum of 14 years' imprisonment and an unlimited fine; civil restitution follows the Partnership's assets, including foreign real estate owned through SPVs, and travel bans are issued proactively to prevent flight; the refusal of a director to pay civil restitution results in the seizure of personal property under the Enforcement of Judgments Act 2008.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure (by Category: Statutory Mismatch)

Statutory sanction: The hybrid mismatch rules under the Income Tax (Hybrid Mismatch) Regulations 2019 disallow deductions and reclassify arrear distributions as taxable Gibraltar-source profits; the assessment is recomputed at 12.5%, plus penalty of GIP 3,000 per mis-stated return and daily fines of GIP 100; corrective interest of 8% compound is charged from the original due date; a repeat offense triggers the cancellation of the ELP's non-residence certificate.

Director personal liability: The GP is personally compelled to pay the recomputed corporate-level tax out of his own funds on the ground that he extended the corporate veil by filing inconsistent declarations; in addition, any accountant or legal advisor who certified the contradictory filing is liable for regulatory sanctions before the Gibraltar Chartered Institute of Tax Advisers, including a fine of GIP 500,000 and withdrawal of practising certificate; the court can remove the GP and appoint a judicial receiver for the ELP's foreign property portfolio.
