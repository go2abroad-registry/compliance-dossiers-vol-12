Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, HM Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office; filing portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/tax-office

E-government filing portal

A foreign investor must first incorporate or register the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) under Gibraltar's Limited Partnerships Act at Companies House Gibraltar. After issuance of the partnership registration certificate, the ELP's general partner or designated agent must apply to the Income Tax Office on the prescribed partnership registration or annual tax return form, submitting the certificate of registration, partnership agreement, list of partners, and non-resident declarations. The Income Tax Office issues a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (GTRN) for the partnership and individual GTRNs to resident partners where required; non-resident partners are not separately registered unless they derive Gibraltar-source income through a permanent establishment in Gibraltar.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — A foreign investor must first incorporate or register the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) under Gibraltar's Limited Partnerships Act at Companies House Gibraltar. After issuance of the partnership registration certificate, the ELP's general partner or designated agent must apply to the Income Tax Office on the prescribed partnership registration or annual tax return form, submitting the certificate of registration, partnership agreement, list of partners, and non-resident declarations. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Not a check-the-box jurisdiction. The ELP is transparent as a matter of law; the partners bear tax on their share of profit. Available elections: (i) approval as an Exempted Limited Partnership with confirmation from the Minister for Finance where all partners are non-resident and income arises outside Gibraltar; (ii) annual election between gross-income-based assessment and self-assessment for resident partners; (iii) hybrid mismatch disclosure and election mechanisms under Gibraltar's Anti-Hybrid Rules within the Income Tax Act 2010.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST; import/customs duty and excise duty levied at importation under the Customs & Excise Duties Act

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable – Gibraltar does not operate a VAT/GST registration threshold; no VAT registration obligation arises for reverse-logistics services. Customs registration as an authorized economic operator is voluntary and does not depend on turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

As no VAT applies, a 0% VAT zero-rating mechanism does not exist. Instead, import/customs duty relief is available on goods entered to a customs warehousing or inward/outward processing regime and subsequently re-exported; cross-border return-merchandise processing and grading services performed for non-resident customers are outside the charge to Gibraltar customs duty and income tax (for non-resident partners under territorial rules). To obtain the duty relief, the ELP must demonstrate that the goods are in transit/temporary admission or re-exported from Gibraltar without home-use entry.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Gibraltar/UK SAD for outward goods)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill evidencing overseas consignee
  • Inward Remittance Certificate showing settlement in foreign currency
  • Goods Movement Reference from Gibraltar Customs
  • Returned Goods Relief Declaration (if goods previously exported from the EU/UK)

No real-time fiscal clearance/API e-invoicing mandate for services. Goods entering Gibraltar must be cleared through GibTrade/SEAHS electronic customs submission; electronic export declarations and shipping bills are lodged via the UK-Gibraltar community system. Digital stamping is not required.

Fraudulent claims for duty relief or customs re-export status are subject to Customs & Excise Duties Act penalties, including duty recovery plus interest, penalties up to 175% of evaded duty, seizure of goods, and criminal sanctions up to imprisonment; the Income Tax Office may reopen assessments and claw back expenses/reliefs with interest and upwards of 100% penalty if tax returns are demonstrated to be based on false documentation.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current-account and capital-account convertibility without exchange-control approvals. The Gibraltar pound is freely interchangeable with GBP at a fixed 1:1 peg, and all UK sterling-denominated clearing is fungible. There are no statutory barriers to inbound capital contributions, external debt servicing, or profit remittance/partnership distributions to non-resident partners. The Exempted Limited Partnership qualifies as fiscally pass-through, and no withholding or repatriation tax is imposed at source, making dividend and distribution flows straightforward subject to standard bank KYC verification.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender of export proceeds applies. Gibraltar maintains no exchange-control regime, no statutory timeline for repatriation, and no forced conversion ratio for receipts arising from cross-border reverse-logistics or returned-goods settlement flows. Foreign-currency balances may be retained freely in any denominated account, and conversion to GBP is executed at commercial interbank rates without central-bank intervention.

Central bank supervision: Government of Gibraltar (Central Treasury, issuing authority for the Gibraltar pound under the Currency Notes Act 1934, as amended) with monetary anchoring via the Bank of England given the Gibraltarian currency peg at par to GBP; prudential conduct and foreign exchange supervision delegated to the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

High viability as an operational overlay for the targeted Local_ACH and Airwallex rails, but not a substitute for a GFSC-regulated local bank account. Airwallex UK Ltd operates under an FCA e-money institution authorization in the United Kingdom; it is not licensed by the GFSC in Gibraltar. The ELP may lawfully use its safeguarding account for multi-currency collections and fast FX settlement, but statutory escrow functions, capital verification, and high-value trade settlement should remain with Gibraltar International Bank or another locally licensed institution. Legal constraint: funds held with Airwallex are not covered by the Gibraltar Deposit Guarantee Scheme and must be ring-fenced in safeguarding accounts per UK EMI regulations, creating a counterparty-tiering requirement.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital contributions must be routed via SWIFT MT103/MT202 from the contributing partner's own verified bank account to the ELP's designated Gibraltar International Bank capital escrow account. Recommended purpose codes are 'Capital Contribution to Limited Partnership' (BOP category: direct investment) or 'Returned-Goods Settlement Reimbursement' where the transfer relates to intercompany reverse-logistics cost recovery. Compliance verification requires a signed contribution schedule, the relevant LPA clause, source-of-funds documentation, and an escrow release instruction counter-signed by the Gibraltar registered agent. Incorrect or incomplete returns: penalty up to 200% of the tax undercharged; criminal prosecution for fraudulent or negligent returns.

Non-resident corporate partners must include their share in their home jurisdiction tax filings and may argue worldwide taxation is nil in Gibraltar only if no Gibraltar source trade exists. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 (annual aggregate value of cross-border transactions between the ELP and its connected enterprises); below this threshold, a contemporaneous documentary record must still be maintained on request where the Commissioner considers tax risk to exist.

Economic substance scope: The Income Tax (Economic Substance) Regulations 2019 apply to any Gibraltar entity carrying on 'relevant activities' (banking, insurance, fund management, finance and leasing, headquarters, shipping, or pure holding). Cross-border reverse logistics and returned goods quality grading is an operating trade and is not categorised as a relevant activity; nevertheless, the ELP must maintain real economic presence in Gibraltar (employees, offices, board or partner meetings, and expenditure) to avoid reclassification as a shell and to preserve statutory tax benefits.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Tax Fraud

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty up to 200% of the tax undercharged; daily penalties for continued non-disclosure; criminal conviction can result in fines without upper limit and imprisonment of up to 10 years.

Director personal liability: Directors or designated member partners who knowingly participate in evasion are jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax and penalties; court may issue asset freezing orders, travel bans, director disqualification for up to 15 years, and custodial sentences up to 10 years.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Economic substance assessment; penalty of up to GIP 200,000; failure to correct may lead to compulsory strike-off, forfeiture of tax exemptions and deemed director / partner liability for tax arrears; the Gibraltar Commissioner may also issue a notice requiring cessation of the commercial arrangement.

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners may be personally liable for the full shortfall of Gibraltar taxes if the corporate entity is struck off; individual partners can be prevented from leaving the jurisdiction while asset recovery investigations are pending.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Transfer pricing adjustment eliminating the artificial deduction; interest from the date the return should have been filed; penalty of 50% of the additional tax liability (or 75% if the transaction was concealed).

Director personal liability: Directors approving fraudulent intercompany invoices can be pursued for restitution and are subject to the general fraud penalties; civil recovery proceedings may attach their personal assets.
AML / Counter-Terrorist Financing Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Criminal Justice Act 2015 (as extended to Gibraltar) and Proceeds of Crime Act, penalties include unlimited fines, confiscation of assets, and imprisonment of up to 14 years for money laundering; the Financial Intelligence Unit can issue fines up to GIP 250,000 for non-reporting.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers are personally subject to civil claims by victims (restitution), disqualification or travel restrictions, and criminal liability of up to 10 years; where a director turns a blind eye, the court may impose a personal contribution order equal to the laundered amount.
