Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

None — Gibraltar law imposes no minimum paid-up capital and no escrow or capital-locking requirement for an Exempted Limited Partnership; initial capital contributions are fixed solely by the Limited Partnership Agreement (Schedule of Capital Commitments).

Funding & unlock path: Civil Contingencies (Gibraltar) obligations apply only upon opening a physical office: fire-safety certificate from HM Civil Protection and Fire Services (annual inspection), structural clearance under the Gibraltar Building Regulations, electrical installation certification under the Electricity Regulations, and a workplace risk assessment under the Health and Safety at Work Act 1984 (Gibraltar). For a fully remote ELP with no employees in Gibraltar, these consents are conditionally waived.

Paid-in capital rule: None — Gibraltar law imposes no minimum paid-up capital and no escrow or capital-locking requirement for an Exempted Limited Partnership; initial capital contributions are fixed solely by the Limited Partnership Agreement (Schedule of Capital Commitments).

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax); official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities without Gibraltar-source income or a permanent establishment are not required to obtain a Gibraltar tax ID. If a registration duty arises, the appointed general partner or local agent files an application with the Income Tax Office using the relevant tax registration form, accompanied by the ELP's certificate of registration, executed partnership agreement, particulars of partners, and proof of registered office address. Upon acceptance, the Income Tax Office issues a unique taxpayer reference number for the ELP's tax file; partners are separately identified for pass-through reporting.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible - Gibraltar law generally treats exempted limited partnerships as fiscally transparent for income tax purposes; income and gains are attributed to partners. A non-resident partner without a Gibraltar permanent establishment or Gibraltar-source income is outside the charge to Gibraltar income tax.

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a US-style check-the-box regime. Partnerships are treated as transparent unless a contrary election is specifically available under the Income Tax Act or the Taxation (Jurisdiction), Exchange of Information, and other OECD-aligned statutory instruments. Hybrid mismatch elections and aligning entity classifications with the UK or another jurisdiction may be made where permitted by the relevant double tax treaty and the Gibraltar hybrid mismatch rules; advance tax treatment confirmation from the Commissioner may be sought through the established ruling practice.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar has no VAT/GST or general sales-and-use tax; indirect taxation is limited to customs and excise duties on imported dutiable goods. The UK VAT regime does not apply in Gibraltar.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A - Gibraltar does not impose VAT/GST and therefore no turnover threshold exists for indirect tax registration. Separate import duty registration arises only for traders importing dutiable goods into Gibraltar.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in Gibraltar: because Gibraltar does not levy VAT, there is no domestic VAT zero-rating mechanism for exported goods or offshore services. An ELP selling a mobile utility app through Apple and Google app stores will not receive a Gibraltar VAT zero-rating benefit; any non-Gibraltar VAT (e.g., UK VAT on business-to-consumer digital services if registration is triggered) is governed by the overseas jurisdiction's own rules.

  • N/A for intangible app supply - Gibraltar customs export documentation is not engaged for digital downloads
  • App-store transaction records and remittance files showing counterparty app-store proceeds are used if foreign VAT zero-rating is claimed outside Gibraltar
  • Statutory declaration or certificate of digital export may be supplied to foreign tax authorities as evidence of supply location

None under Gibraltar law. No real-time fiscal clearance, API-based e-invoicing, or digital stamping mandate is imposed by HM Government of Gibraltar. Any invoicing requirements arise contractually or under overseas VAT laws (e.g., UK or EU place-of-supply rules for digital services).

No Gibraltar VAT zero-rating penalty can arise. General anti-avoidance rules and incomplete-return penalties under the Income Tax Act apply to fabricated or artificial transactions involving purported export or offshore supply arrangements; fraudulent misrepresentation may attract fines up to 100% of under-declared tax and criminal penalties, while foreign VAT authorities may impose retrospective VAT assessments and penalty interest where export evidence is falsified.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current-account and capital-account convertibility. The Gibraltar pound is issued at a one-to-one parity with GBP sterling and freely exchangeable without permission. There are no exchange controls, no surrender obligations, and no limits on non-resident profit remittances. Gibraltar has no withholding tax on ELP distributions because of the pass-through tax treatment; taxation is applied only in the partners' residence jurisdictions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Gibraltar law does not require surrender, mandatory conversion, or any repatriation timeline for export or service-export receipts. Inbound bank credits are settled immediately, subject only to ordinary AML/KYC verification.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary anchor); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the supervisory authority; no central bank or exchange-control administration exists in Gibraltar.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM. The GFSC has a proportionate EMI registration route, but most UK/EU digital EMIs treat Gibraltar as non-EEA and decline pure offshore entities. A Gibraltar-regulated EMI under the Financial Services Act 2019 is the legally viable path, whereas a purely foreign digital EMI without a local licence should not be relied upon for fiat treasury operations.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: A contemporaneous Local File must be maintained when aggregate connected-party transactions exceed GBP 1,000,000 in the accounting period, or where intercompany financing exceeds GBP 1,000,000. A Master File is required when the consolidated group turnover exceeds GBP 50,000,000. The Commissioner may require full OECD-compliant documentation below these thresholds where an obvious mispricing or treaty abuse risk exists.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar's Economic Substance Regulations apply to relevant activities including 'IP Business' and pure holding company activity. The mobile utility app's monetization through Apple IAP and Google Play Billing is treated as IP Business if the ELP owns or exploits software intangibles, trademark, or platform rights. The ELP must demonstrate management and direction in Gibraltar, adequate employees, physical premises, and operating expenditure proportionate to the CIGA (app development, IP maintenance, customer support, and billing coordination). A pure holding ELP is subject to a lighter reduced substance test. Failure to file the annual ESR return or satisfy the substance test exposes the ELP to fines, automatic exchange of tax rulings information, and possible strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Returns

Statutory sanction: Criminal liability under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act and Proceeds of Crime Act. Financial penalties may reach 200% of tax evaded, plus unlimited pecuniary fines. A conviction for fraudulent evasion of tax carries imprisonment for up to 7 years and mandatory confiscation of assets derived from the offence.

Director personal liability: The statutory 'veil piercing' rule applies where fraud or dishonesty is established. Directors and the general partner may be assessed jointly and severally for the evaded tax; travel bans, exit controls, asset freezing orders, and director disqualification for up to 15 years may be imposed.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Penalty under the Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations of up to £10,000 per infringement plus a continuing penalty of £1,000 per month; the Registrar may strike off the ELP and file an adverse report with the Commissioner. Conduit arrangements may also be disregarded, producing 12.5% corporation tax on all income plus a 200% surcharge.

Director personal liability: The general partner has unlimited liability for the ELP's obligations. Directors of a corporate general partner who knowingly permit a sham substance arrangement may be held personally liable for penalties, disqualified, and subjected to travel restrictions when unpaid tax exceeds the serious default threshold.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner may re-determine chargeable income under the arm's length principle; the adjustment carries an additional 200% surcharge on the underpaid corporation tax and interest at 8% per annum. Where the inaccurate documentation is fraudulent or negligent, the penalty is criminal, including a fine up to £10,000 and imprisonment for up to 2 years.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or approve false transfer pricing certificates are personally liable for the surcharge; indemnification clauses in the ELP agreement are void in cases of deliberate misconduct. Persistent transfer pricing failure triggers disqualification orders and personal restitution proceedings.
AML and Beneficial Ownership Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Breach of the Gibraltar AML legislation and the Proceeds of Crime Act carries an unlimited fine, confiscation orders, and imprisonment up to 14 years for money laundering. Administrative penalties of at least €1,000 per breach may be imposed by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission; repeated breaches lead to cancellation of the ELP's registration.

Director personal liability: The MLRO, directors, and the general partner may be prosecuted in their personal capacity. Courts may issue travel bans, freeze personal bank accounts, and impose disqualification where the compliance failure enabled a money laundering transaction. The corporate veil is disregarded when beneficial ownership filings are intentionally false.
