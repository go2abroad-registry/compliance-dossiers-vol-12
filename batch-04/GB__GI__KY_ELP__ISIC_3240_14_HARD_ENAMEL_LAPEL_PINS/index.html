E-government filing portal

For a Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) with non-resident partners: (1) obtain the Certificate of Limited Partnership from the Registrar; (2) submit Form GP1 to the Income Tax Office together with the partnership deed and registered office address; (3) obtain a partnership Income Tax Reference Number (IT number); (4) each non-resident partner must deliver proof of tax residence and its own foreign tax ID/NIF; (5) registration with Gibraltar Customs and Excise is required only if physical imports will occur; no VAT/TIN registration is required because Gibraltar is outside the EU VAT territory.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

The ELP may elect for tax-exempt status under the Income Tax Act 2010 where all income is derived from outside Gibraltar (non-resident income exemption). Gibraltar has no check-the-box electoral regime comparable to the US; however, under Gibraltar's anti-hybrid rules (EU ATAD transposition), the taxpayer may make hybrid mismatch adjustments to disregard payments or duplication offset in cross-border structures.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Sales and Use Tax (Gibraltar Purchase Tax)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A - no VAT/GST threshold; any person importing goods into Gibraltar must hold a Customs Trader Code and file an import declaration irrespective of turnover. The official local currency is the Gibraltar pound (GIP), pegged 1:1 with GBP, with no separate economic registration threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically dispatched or transported from Gibraltar to a foreign destination are outside the scope of Gibraltar purchase tax and qualify for zero-rating. The Exempted Limited Partnership must substantiate that title, transport, and delivery occur outside Gibraltar and that full legal ownership is transferred to the overseas buyer; services ancillary to offshore goods are also zero-rated if supplied to a non-resident business and used wholly outside Gibraltar. Exports to EU private consumers may be subject to EU distance selling rules only if dispatched from a VAT-registered EU place of supply.

  • Customs Export Declaration, Bill of Lading, Inward Remittance Certificate

No real-time fiscal clearance mandate for B2B invoices; invoices must be issued in a traceable format (PDF/A-3 or paper) and retained for at least 6 years. All physical exports require an electronic outward entry through the Gibraltar Customs Declaration System (CDS) prior to departure; digital stamping and continuous e-invoicing are not implemented.

Fraudulent zero-rating claims attract retrospective assessment for up to 6 years, a penalty of up to 200% of the tax evaded, and civil penalties under the Income Tax Act 2010. False customs export declarations expose the ELP, its general partner and officers to prosecution under the Customs and Excise Ordinance with potential confiscation of goods and equipment, debarrement from further CDS filings, and mandatory repayment of any refund obtained together with compound interest.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar maintains full current-account and capital-account convertibility. There are no exchange-control approvals, quantitative limits or profit-remittance approval requirements for an Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) paying distributions or profits to non-resident limited partners. GIP is not separately listed on global FX markets; practical settlement occurs in GBP at the statutory 1:1 peg through UK correspondent clearing. Capital contributions, trade proceeds and financing flows may be freely denominated and settled in GBP, USD, EUR or other major currencies, subject only to GFSC AML/CFT, sanctions screening and banking-contract documentation. No Gibraltar withholding tax is imposed on partnership distributions, because the ELP is fiscally transparent; tax is attributed to partners at their respective residency levels.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Nil. Gibraltar imposes no statutory timeline for export receipt repatriation and no mandatory surrender/conversion ratio. Export proceeds in hard currency may be retained indefinitely in foreign-currency accounts, converted at commercial rates, or remitted to the ELP's partners after satisfying GFSC-regulated AML/CFT evidence requirements. An ELP exporter of die-struck enamel lapel pins is therefore free to manage USD/EUR export receivables without central-bank conversion obligations.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) — integrated regulator for banking, investment services and electronic money; no separate central bank or foreign-exchange administration exists in Gibraltar. The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is issued by the Government of Gibraltar under the Currency Notes Act 1934, is fully Sterling-backed and is pegged at par to GBP. HM Treasury and UK Sanctions authorities retain jurisdiction over UK-wide sanctions and financial sanctions, but not over ordinary Gibraltar exchange controls.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Medium — conditional viability. A Gibraltar-licensed electronic money institution (EMI) or a recognised EEA/UK EMI with existing cross-border arrangements can be used as a secondary corridor for low-value Etsy Direct Pay, Stripe, PayPal or card-settlement flows. It cannot act as the primary treasury bank or as the depositary of partner capital subscriptions, because EMI accounts are not bank accounts, do not provide SWIFT documentary-trade capabilities, and are not treated by GFSC as adequate safeguarding accounts for capital-escrow balances. Legal constraints: (i) the EMI must be authorised in Gibraltar under the Financial Services (Electronic Money) Act/regulations or in the EEA/UK with valid passporting (post-Brexit UK passports into Gibraltar no longer apply automatically); (ii) Gibraltar-sourced e-money safeguarding must be maintained through a licensed credit institution; (iii) the ELP must not use an EMI to receive large capital injections or to execute trade-payment instructions requiring MT103/LC documentation.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All capital injections into the Gibraltar ELP must be made by SWIFT MT103 to the ELP's segregated capital-escrow or operational bank account in Gibraltar. The remitter name must be the exact legal name of the contributing limited partner; any third-party, cash or crypto funding is presumptively unacceptable. Remittance information should include the partnership name, capital-call reference and purpose of payment, expressed as ISO 20022 purpose code 'EQUI' (equity) or 'FINC' (funding of investment) where XML/ISO payment formats are used, or an unambiguous free-format reference 'COLLATERAL CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION — LP SUBSCRIPTION / CALL NO. X / PARTNER REF Y'. For GBP from UK clearing, CHAPS or Faster Payments may be operationally used but SWIFT MT103 is preferred for audit trail; EUR can be remitted via SEPA if the sending institution participates, otherwise via SWIFT. The receiving bank must complete CDD on the remitter before credit, verify the injection against the capital call, and maintain a partnership capital register. No borrowing from unrelated third parties may be labelled as capital unless a genuine loan agreement and source-of-funds documentation are separately provided.For settlement of overseas supplier invoices, raw-material purchases and outsourced manufacturing costs, use SWIFT MT103 payment transfers for open-account trade, with the commercial invoice, packing list and beneficial-ownership declaration retained by the dealing bank. Where letter-of-credit (LC) terms are required, use SWIFT MT700 to issue a commercial documentary credit, MT707 for amendments, MT720/MT721 for transferable LC transfers, and MT202COV or MT103 for LC reimbursement/settlement. Key controls: (i) the ELP must state the trade purpose in the payment reference (e.g., INVOICE NO., PURCHASE ORDER NO.); (ii) the paying bank must review the underlying trade documents to avoid goods/services divergence; (iii) USD payments settle via Fedwire/CHIPS or SWIFT through the Gibraltar bank's US correspondent; EUR via TARGET2/SEPA through an EU correspondent; GBP via UK CHAPS or SWIFT; (iv) Etsy Direct Pay and Stripe revenues are not SWIFT rails and must be received as card/ACH settlement currencies into the designated revenue account, then periodically converted to GBP/GIP through the treasury bank's FX desk using spot or forward contracts.Outright FX spot contracts for immediate conversion of USD/EUR trade receipts and payments into GBP/GIP.Deliverable FX forward contracts (typically up to 12 months) in GBP/USD, EUR/GBP, GBP/EUR and USD/EUR covering genuine receivables, payables and partner-capital exposure.FX swaps for matched near and far legs where the ELP has a treasury cycle of recurring export proceeds and supplier disbursements.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) limited to currencies that are not freely deliverable in Gibraltar (e.g., CNY, INR, BRL) and cash-settled in GBP or USD against a licensed bank counterparty, used only where the underlying trade invoice is denominated in that restricted currency.Cross-currency interest-rate swaps (CCIRS) for hedging long-term partner loans or multi-currency borrowing, matched to the notional amount of the underlying loan and not for speculative profit-taking.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% of chargeable business profits under the Income Tax Act 2010, as amended; no separate capital gains tax is imposed on the sale of business assets.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Gibraltar does not impose withholding tax on dividends or profit distributions, including ELP distributions to non-resident limited partners.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The return and final tax payment are due no later than 9 months after the end of the accounting period. For a 31 December year-end, the filing and payment deadline is 30 September of the following year. No automatic extension is granted for non-resident partners or offshore administrators.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 aggregate annual controlled transactions with associated enterprises triggers mandatory Local File preparation. Master File and Country-by-Country reporting obligations apply only to multinational groups with consolidated group revenue exceeding GIP/EUR 750,000,000. Contemporaneous documentation is also required where related-party transactions are individually large or not commercially rational at arm's length.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations apply to ELPs carrying on a relevant activity in Gibraltar. The online sale of hard enamel lapel pins through Etsy and Stripe, including order management, inventory holding, payment settlement, distribution, and customer service, is treated as a distribution and service-centre activity. The ELP must maintain adequate physical premises, employees, equipment, and locally executed core income-generating activities. Because the ELP is tax-transparent, the substance assessment may look through to the general partner and limited partners; an ESR annual return must still be filed even when all partners are non-resident and no Gibraltar income tax is payable.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion and fraudulent filing

Statutory sanction: Criminal prosecution under the Income Tax Act 2010 and the Criminal Justice Act: penalty up to treble the tax evaded, unlimited fines, confiscation orders, and imprisonment; civil penalties of up to 100% of the understated tax plus statutory interest.

Director personal liability: Directors, the general partner, and any manager who knowingly signs a false return or participates in concealment are jointly and severally liable for the tax and penalties, subject to director disqualification, travel bans, and imprisonment.
Shell company or sham economic substance

Statutory sanction: Failure to file the Economic Substance Return attracts financial penalties; failure to satisfy the economic substance test attracts escalated monetary penalties and possible court-ordered strike-off. The Income Tax Office may also deny treaty benefits and reclassify the profits to a controlled foreign entity.

Director personal liability: General partners and directors can be personally assessed for penalties after strike-off, and continued trading while struck off exposes them to civil restitution and criminal liability for fraudulent trading.
Anti-money laundering and payment-rail misconduct

Statutory sanction: Penalties under Gibraltar's Proceeds of Crime Act and AML framework: imprisonment up to 14 years, unlimited fines, confiscation and asset-forfeiture orders, and revocation of payment-processing privileges by Stripe and Etsy.

Director personal liability: Directors and the general partner are treated as senior management for AML purposes and may face personal criminal liability, travel restrictions, asset freezing, and disqualification if the ELP is used as a laundering vehicle.
Transfer pricing misstatement and artificial profit stripping

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner may adjust profits to an arm's-length level, disallow excess deductions, impose secondary penalties up to 100% of the underpaid tax, and charge statutory interest; contemporaneous transfer-pricing documentation must be produced on enquiry.

Director personal liability: Controlling partners and directors who implement non-arm's-length terms risk joint liability for the adjustment and interest; if the profit-stripping structure is fraudulent, criminal sanctions for tax evasion apply.
