Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax (Income Tax Office) and Commissioner of HM Customs, jointly administered by HM Customs and Tax Division, HM Government of Gibraltar. Official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi (tax guidance and e-filing); corporate and partnership registration is lodged with the Registrar of Companies at the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC), portal: https://www.fsc.gi.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-sponsored Gibraltar ELP obtains its statutory identifier as follows: (i) the foreign principals appoint a Gibraltar-licensed registered agent, who files the Exempted Limited Partnership statement under the Limited Partnerships Act 2020 with the Gibraltar Companies Registry (GFSC), electing ELP status and, if desired, separate legal personality; (ii) the Registry issues the Certificate of Registration and Registered Number, typically within 1-3 business days; (iii) the registered agent then files the ELP tax registration with the Income Tax Office, obtaining a Tax Reference Number (TRN), which is the sole statutory tax identifier; Gibraltar has no VAT ID and no separate indirect-tax registration for digital advertisers; (iv) a Customs Business Indicator is additionally required only if physical goods are imported, which is not triggered by AdMob/AppLovin digital settlements. No minimum capital is prescribed and non-resident ownership is unrestricted.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible - A Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership is fiscally transparent under the Income Tax Act 2010: the ELP is not a chargeable person; each partner is assessed on their distributive share of ELP profits. Territorial scope: Gibraltar-resident partners are liable on worldwide income at 12.5% for corporate partners (standard rate) or applicable personal tax bands for individuals; non-resident partners are chargeable only on Gibraltar-source income. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible - A Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership is fiscally transparent under the Income Tax Act 2010: the ELP is not a chargeable person; each partner is assessed on their distributive share of ELP profits. Territorial scope: Gibraltar-resident partners are liable on worldwide income at 12.5% for corporate partners (standard rate) or applicable personal tax bands for individuals; non-resident partners are chargeable only on Gibraltar-source income. A hyper-casual gaming ELP whose management and control are located outside Gibraltar, whose publishing contracts are executed and performed abroad, and whose ad revenues are receivable offshore, is outside the charge to Gibraltar tax at both entity and non-resident-partner level, producing structurally zero Gibraltar tax liability.

Available election forms

No Gibraltar statutory 'check-the-box' or partnership opacity election exists under the Income Tax Act 2010; tax transparency is mandatory for all limited partnerships. Available elections: (i) election under section 30 of the Limited Partnerships Act 2020 to confer separate legal personality on the ELP, which is fiscally neutral and does not alter transparency; (ii) for US federal tax purposes only, a multi-member ELP may file IRS Form 8832 to be classified as a partnership (or as a disregarded entity if single-member), independent of Gibraltar classification; (iii) hybrid mismatch consequences, if the ELP is dual-filed in the UK, US, or Cayman, are governed by the Income Tax (Anti-Tax Avoidance) Regulations 2021, which transpose EU ATAD-aligned hybrid rules; no election under Gibraltar law can make the ELP opaque for Gibraltar income tax.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales-and-Use-Tax; Gibraltar operates only Import Duty and Excise Duty under the Customs Duties Act and Customs Ordinance. Digital advertising and hyper-casual mobile-game publishing revenues are outside the scope of all Gibraltar indirect taxes.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - Gibraltar has no VAT/GST system, so no economic turnover threshold for indirect-tax registration exists; registration for customs purposes (Customs Business Indicator) is triggered from the first GIP/GBP 1 of physical imports, while digital-only publishing services incur no indirect registration. For income tax, a TRN is required from the first GIP/GBP 1 of Gibraltar-source revenue, but pure foreign-source ad settlements received by non-resident partners, absent a Gibraltar trade, are outside that requirement.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - no VAT zero-rating claim can arise in Gibraltar because no VAT is imposed. Exports of goods from Gibraltar are outside the scope of Gibraltar import duty, and offshore digital services are automatically outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect taxation. For income-tax foreign-source substantiation, records should evidence place of effective management outside Gibraltar, contracts executed through AdMob and AppLovin outside Gibraltar, services performed outside Gibraltar, and offshore receipt of settlement funds. Gibraltar maintains no exchange-control or statutory FX-repatriation regime; the 180-day inward-remittance window is retained as a prudent substantiation benchmark.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Gibraltar HM Customs electronic export lodgement; required only for physical exports)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / electronic proof of despatch (required only for physical exports)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank MT103/SWIFT credit confirmation evidencing AdMob/AppLovin settlement receipts)

Gibraltar imposes no statutory real-time e-invoicing, fiscal-clearance API, or digital-stamping mandate. Commercial invoices and platform settlement statements issued by AdMob and AppLovin constitute sufficient documentation. Only physical imports and exports require electronic customs lodgement through HM Customs' frontier declaration gateway; these clearance procedures are not engaged by hyper-casual digital publishing and offshore advertising revenue.

HM Customs and Tax Division may audit the ELP's non-residence, foreign-source, and outside-scope positions. Fraudulent or negligent under-declaration attracts civil penalties of up to 100% of the additional tax assessed, statutory interest, and, upon conviction for fraudulent evasion under the Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended), imprisonment of up to 7 years and fines. Retrospective reassessments can extend six years from the end of the charge year and are unlimited in cases of fraud or wilful default; clawback of the offshore/outside-scope position results in partner-level assessments with interest running from the original due date. In cross-border gaming structures, sham offshore management, dual-claim abuse, or inconsistent US/Gibraltar classification may disqualify the zero-tax profile and trigger penalties against both the ELP and its registered agent; anti-money-laundering and record-keeping duties under the Proceeds of Crime Act (Gibraltar) 2015 remain applicable to the licensed introducer.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar operates a fully convertible, no-exchange-control regime. The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is pegged at parity to pound sterling (GBP) and is freely interchangeable with GBP, which is also legal tender within Gibraltar. Both current account and capital account transactions are unrestricted, including non-resident ownership of capital, cross-border dividend/revenue remittances, and return of capital. No prior central bank approval, licensing, or declaration is required for profit remittance, loan repayments, or capital injections. The GFSC maintains only standard anti-money laundering (AML) monitoring and transaction reporting obligations, not foreign exchange restrictions. For a pass-through exempted limited partnership, no additional convertibility barrier exists for repatriation of gaming revenues or partner distributions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender obligation applies to Gibraltar residents or entities. Export receipts, including advertising payouts from ad networks, may be retained offshore in any permitted currency without mandatory conversion to GIP/GBP. Under Gibraltar's AML framework (Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and AML Regulations 2019), only record-keeping and source-of-funds verification obligations apply for a 5-year period. There is no Central Bank mandated timeline for conversion, no minimum conversion ratio, and no exchange control surrender regime.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary anchor for Gibraltar pound and pound sterling); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the prudential and market conduct regulator; Government of Gibraltar as issuer of Gibraltar pound banknotes and coins under the Currency Notes Act.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Cross-border electronic money institution (EMI) viability is moderate and requires enhanced due diligence. UK-authorised EMIs do not have automatic passporting rights into Gibraltar because Gibraltar is not part of the EEA; however, several tier-1 UK EMIs and EEA EMIs accept Gibraltar entities on a case-by-case basis under their commercial client acceptance policies. Onboarding may involve lengthier KYC subject to the ELP's beneficial ownership disclosure and a physical or quasi-physical verification step. Fully digital offshore EMIs offering multi-currency IBANs are viable for high-volume, low-value ad-network flows, but settlement finality is weaker compared to licensed local banks. Non-EEA EMIs with no regulated status in the UK/EEA are generally not acceptable for treasury-grade clearing rails.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections from partners into the Gibraltar ELP must be executed via high-value SWIFT MT103 wire transfers denominated in GBP, EUR, or USD. Recommended purpose codes: 'CARE' (Capital, payments of earnings and profits of a direct investment enterprise) or 'CALC' (Capital, credit between related enterprises). For partner loans, use 'CALC' or 'CANT' (capital, cancellation or repayment of credit between related enterprises) as appropriate. Banks will require the name of the foreign remitter, the ELP full legal name, the partnership's GFSC reference, and a capital account reference to segregate contributed funds. Funds must route from a regulated bank account bearing the partner's identity; crypto or third-party funding are blocked under GFSC AML guidance. Verification includes remitting bank confirmation, an internal credit memo, and reconciliation against the partnership's capital ledger.Operational disbursements for trade and gaming infrastructure (server costs, subcontractors, app developers) clear via: (i) SWIFT MT103 for cross-border, high-value payments with full adherence to purpose codes 'PROC' (payments for trade in goods and services) or 'INTC' (payments for the international trade in services); (ii) GBP Faster Payments or CHAPS for UK clearing needs; (iii) SEPA SCT credit transfers for euro-denominated EU expenses; (iv) commercial letters of credit (UCP600) for any physical or high-value procurement, established via Gibraltar licensed banks through correspondent banking relationships. All outbound wires must be supported by underlying contracts, invoices, and beneficial owner identification to satisfy GFSC transaction monitoring.
