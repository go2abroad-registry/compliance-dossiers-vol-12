Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A purely virtual office is not permissible for an Exempted Limited Partnership acting as a venture capital general partner. The ELP must maintain at all times a physical registered office in Gibraltar at a non-PO-box street address at which the register of partners and register of beneficial owners are available for inspection by competent authorities. A licensed Gibraltar trust and company service provider may supply the registered office as a physical presence service (premises, name plate, mail handling, and statutory filing point); however, the GFSC will assess commercial substance, and a bare virtual address without physical administrative presence will fail the licensing and substance assessment.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for the ELP itself; capital contributions are fixed solely in the Limited Partnership Agreement. However, if the General Partner is licensed as a full-scope Alternative Investment Fund Manager (AIFM) under the Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2018 (Gibraltar), minimum initial capital of EUR 125,000 applies (EUR 50,000 for internally managed AIFs); a sub-threshold AIFM must register with the GFSC without mandatory initial capital.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certificate and means-of-escape inspection from the Gibraltar Fire and Rescue Service for office premises; building control and occupancy permit from the Building Control Office under the Building Act 2019 (Gibraltar) covering structural load, electrical certification and disabled access; occupational health and safety compliance under the Health and Safety at Work Act 1992 (Gibraltar) including workplace risk assessment, first-aid provision and employers' liability insurance; no hazardous-process civil defence clearance required for the licensed fund management use

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital for the ELP itself; capital contributions are fixed solely in the Limited Partnership Agreement. However, if the General Partner is licensed as a full-scope Alternative Investment Fund Manager (AIFM) under the Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2018 (Gibraltar), minimum initial capital of EUR 125,000 applies (EUR 50,000 for internally managed AIFs); a sub-threshold AIFM must register with the GFSC without mandatory initial capital.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (6-8 Castle Street, Gibraltar GX11 1AA); online portal GOSS (Gibraltar Online Self-Service) via www.gibraltar.gov.gi

E-government filing portal

Non-resident ELPs obtain a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number by (i) incorporating/registering as an Exempted Limited Partnership with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies; (ii) filing an annual tax return with the Income Tax Office claiming non-resident/territorial treatment; and (iii) for customs purposes, applying via the Gibraltar Customs Port for an Economic Operator Registration (GIB EORI). No VAT ID is available because Gibraltar has no VAT/GST regime.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible - partnership transparent for Gibraltar income tax; non-resident partners' offshore-source income generally outside charge; territorial scope limited to Gibraltar-source income — Non-resident ELPs obtain a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number by (i) incorporating/registering as an Exempted Limited Partnership with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies; (ii) filing an annual tax return with the Income Tax Office claiming non-resident/territorial treatment; and (iii) for customs purposes, applying via the Gibraltar Customs Port for an Economic Operator Registration (GIB EORI). No VAT ID is available because Gibraltar has no VAT/GST regime.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible - partnership transparent for Gibraltar income tax; non-resident partners' offshore-source income generally outside charge; territorial scope limited to Gibraltar-source income

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box. Under Income Tax Act 2010, the ELP may elect to be treated as exempt from partnership-level registration and accounting for tax on non-Gibraltar source income; partners may elect for deduction/credit treatment on hybrid mismatch arrangements in accordance with OECD Action 2 rules.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST - Gibraltar imposes only selective Customs and Excise Duties on imported goods; services are outside the special territory indirect tax net

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT/GST; for customs duty, every importer must hold a Gibraltar EORI registration irrespective of turnover (GIP-denominated no threshold), with a separate liquor/tobacco excise licence where required

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in Gibraltar: because there is no value-added tax, there is no '0% export rating'. Export shipments are exempt from domestic customs duty subject to completing movement records; venture-capital GP management services rendered by a Gibraltar ELP to non-resident fund vehicles are treated as outside the charge to Gibraltar income tax rather than zero-rated supplies.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • GP Accounts Confirming Waiver of VAT/Zero-Rating Status

No statutory e-invoicing, continuous clearance or digital stamping mandate for sales invoices. Customs declarations are submitted electronically through the Gibraltar Trade Statistics/ Import Export system (eg TARGA/Customs Declaration Service); approved XML API clearance for full containerized cargo.

There is no export-VAT refund to claw back. However, false customs declarations, duty-relief misstatements, or artificial arrangements to shift partnership income abroad attract penalties under the Customs Ordinance and Income Tax Act 2010 (up to 200% of the tax/duty at stake, plus interest and revocation of non-resident tax status).

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is pegged at par to GBP. Both current-account and capital-account transactions are fully convertible with no exchange-control restrictions. Profit remittance, dividend distributions, and capital withdrawals are unrestricted provided that underlying transactions satisfy AML/KYC and beneficial-ownership verification. Non-resident partners or shareholders may freely receive their distributive share without state or withholding taxes in Gibraltar, subject to documentary evidence of source and arm's-length substance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar imposes no statutory surrender or mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds. Receipts from cross-border trade in services, including management fees, carried interest, and disposal proceeds, may be retained in any freely convertible currency. There is no statutory time limit for repatriation or retention of export receipts; the only obligation is to record the receipt in the audited financial statements and to remain compliant with CFC or substance accounting rules per the parent UK regime where applicable.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) and the Bank of England (monetary anchor), together with HM Government of Gibraltar's Currency Unit

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium – Cross-border fintech EMIs (e.g., Wise, Revolut, Airwallex) can provide rapid remote onboarding and multi-currency IBANs. They may be used as auxiliary disbursement rails for small-value operational expenses. However, they cannot serve as the sole treasury counterparty because: (i) they lack a full Gibraltar banking licence under the Financial Services (Banking) Act 1996; (ii) client funds must be safeguarded with duly authorised credit institutions; and (iii) post-Brexit restrictions have limited EEA pass-through into Gibraltar for e-money institutions (with certain grandfathering exceptions). EMIs should be restricted to sinking-fund transactions and never hold capital-call proceeds of regulated investors.

Defense evidence: Execution copies of the ELP deed and partnership certificate; full register of partners with beneficial-ownership percentages; GFSC or DLT-provider licences (if any); registered-agent certificate; audited financial statements; and a substance/economic-activity report.

Venture-capital early-stage asset class containing only unquoted equity interests, which deviates from normal commercial asset liquidity

Root cause: Venture-capital early-stage asset class containing only unquoted equity interests, which deviates from normal commercial asset liquidity

Defense evidence: Investment management agreements; capital-call notices describing committed capital; valuation and deployment memoranda; and financial statements showing only management fees, carried interest, and expenses rather than customer deposits or unusual retail money flows.
Presence of directors or shareholders with politically-exposed-person (PEP) or negative-news flags from the commercially operated register

Defense evidence: Full legal-name and date-of-birth records, national identification, residential proof, professional licences, external legal opinion on the ownership chain, and a beneficial-ownership declaration pursuant to the Beneficial Ownership (Companies) Regulations 2017.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound LP capital calls are routed via SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfer with transaction-type purpose code 'G105' (venture-capital capital contribution) or 'SF05' (securities/investment transaction). For GBP transfers from UK/EU, the UK Faster Payments Service in combination with CHAPS settlement is acceptable. Every inbound transfer must be accompanied by a digitally signed capital-call notice containing the LP identity, committed-capital reference, and benefit-of-position statement. Verification includes matching the paying name to the commercial register, linking the remitter's bank BIC and IBAN to the investor file, conducting real-time sanctions screening, and confirming that the remittance amount matches the capital-call schedule to within tolerance.Portfolio-investment disbursements use SWIFT MT103 with purpose code 'P0153' (private equity investment) and, where the paying bank employs a correspondent model, an MT202 cover payment. Domestic GBP settlement is cleared via CHAPS or the UK Faster Payments Service for sub-£1m transfers; cross-currency USD disbursements are routed through SWIFT to a US clearing agent using FedWire. Commercial letters of credit (MT700) are not typical for pure equity venture-capital transactions but may be structured for venture-debt or convertible-note instruments. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

10% standard Gibraltar corporation tax rate; 12.5% applies to large companies (250+ employees and annual turnover exceeding EUR 50 million or balance sheet exceeding EUR 43 million) on chargeable profits above GIP 10 million

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on dividends or partnership distributions, regardless of the recipient partner's residence

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Returns must be filed by 30 November following the end of the relevant year of assessment, i.e. within five months of the 30 June year-end; established entities are assessed on the previous-year basis, newly incorporated entities on the current-year basis; the Commissioner may grant a written extension upon application

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GBP/GIP 1,000,000 aggregate value of controlled transactions in a year of assessment triggers mandatory contemporaneous Local File and group-level Master File preparation; a summary transfer pricing disclosure is required on Form CT-1 Box 4 regardless of threshold

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance (Companies and Partnerships) Regulations 2019 apply to the ELP as a relevant entity engaged in fund management, a relevant activity; the ELP must be directed and managed in Gibraltar, perform core income-generating activities locally (investment decision-making, risk management, and monitoring), maintain proportionate premises and full-time employees, incur adequate local expenditure, and file an annual ESR return; failure to satisfy the substance test may trigger reclassification of the ELP as tax-resident and application of the 12.5% large-company rate

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Under-Declaration

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty up to 100% of undercharged tax plus statutory interest; upon conviction, a fine up to GIP 100,000 and/or imprisonment up to 7 years; seizure and forfeiture of partnership assets

Director personal liability: The general partner and any directors are jointly and severally liable to restitution; criminal asset-freezing orders and travel bans are imposed pending trial; confiscation orders may extend to carried interest and personal bank accounts
Shell Company / ESR Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: ESR penalty of GIP 10,000 per relevant activity, increased by GIP 500 per day of continuing default; strike-off from the Gibraltar register; retroactive assessment of Gibraltar-source or worldwide income at the 10%/12.5% rate

Director personal liability: The general partner as signatory is the responsible person for ESR compliance; directors may be disqualified for up to 15 years and held personally liable for unpaid ESR penalties where the partnership has been struck off
AML / Sanctions Due Diligence Failures

Statutory sanction: Unlimited fine or imprisonment up to 14 years for unlawful money transmission or possession of criminal property under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015; regulatory fines up to GIP 1,000,000 and suspension of the licensed general partner designation

Director personal liability: The designated AML compliance officer and directors face personal fines up to GIP 250,000; travel bans and passport surrender during investigation; confiscation orders extending to the general partner's personal wealth
Transfer Pricing / Connected Party Mis-Pricing

Statutory sanction: Arm's-length adjustment of Gibraltar chargeable profits with interest; penalty up to 100% of additional tax where documentation is absent or fraudulent; referral to HMRC for a joint cross-border audit

Director personal liability: Directors face personal assessment for deliberately arranging connected-party loss of tax; civil restitution orders; disqualification and international enforcement through the UK-Gibraltar mutual assistance framework
