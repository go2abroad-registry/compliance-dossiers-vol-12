Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar Customs & Excise Department, HM Government of Gibraltar (VAT and indirect tax administration; e-services via the HM Government of Gibraltar tax portal) and the Gibraltar Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (direct tax, Tax Reference Number and partnership registration).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Register the Exempted Limited Partnership with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under the Limited Partnerships Act 2016; obtain the Companies House Gibraltar registration number (no minimum capital requirement). Step 2: File the Income Tax Office partnership registration application (Form GIB-ELP-TR) to obtain the partnership's Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) and to record the identities of all general and limited partners. Step 3: Confirm with the Income Tax Office the non-resident and non-Gibraltar-source status of the ELP to establish the exempt or tax-transparent position. Step 5: Register for PAYES and Social Security contributions only if the ELP creates a local employment nexus; otherwise no payroll registration is required. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Under the Limited Partnerships Act 2016 and the Income Tax Act, an Exempted Limited Partnership may elect to be fiscally transparent, causing profits to be allocated to and assessed upon the partners directly in their own jurisdictions, instead of being taxed at entity level. Alternatively, the ELP may leave the default entity-level treatment in place or, where eligible, confirm full exemption from Gibraltar income tax for non-Gibraltar-source income. For United States-connected partners, a US federal check-the-box election (Form 8832) may classify the ELP as a partnership or disregarded entity for US tax purposes. Hybrid mismatch elections and notification requirements under Gibraltar's ATAD and OECD BEPS-aligned rules must also be assessed for any cross-border hybrid instruments used by the ELP.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GIP 150,000 annual taxable turnover; compulsory registration applies as soon as the threshold is exceeded or will be exceeded in the next 30 days, and voluntary registration is permitted below the threshold where input VAT recovery is sought.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% G-VAT rate applies to direct exports of physical goods to destinations outside Gibraltar and to cross-border supplies of services where the place of supply is treated as outside Gibraltar. For B2B supplies, the 3D game art and character rigging services supplied to overseas game developers, publishers or platform operators are zero-rated or outside the scope of Gibraltar VAT when the recipient is established in the United Kingdom or another non-Gibraltar jurisdiction, provided the recipient supplies a valid foreign VAT/TIN and the ELP holds contemporaneous evidence of the customer's overseas establishment; B2C supplies to private individuals resident outside Gibraltar are zero-rated where the service is effectively used and enjoyed abroad.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic SAD/EX1 where physical media or deliverables cross the customs frontier)
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill or courier consignment note evidencing physical export from Gibraltar
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT/bank credit advice evidencing receipt of foreign-currency settlement from the overseas client
  • Executed master service agreement and per-project work orders with the overseas game developer or publisher
  • Foreign customer VAT registration certificate or equivalent tax identification document confirming non-Gibraltar business establishment
  • Digital handover or delivery acceptance record substantiating the provision of 3D art, sculpting or rigging services
  • Zero-rated supply declaration signed by the overseas recipient where required by the Customs & Excise Department

Gibraltar does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or a mandatory B2B e-invoicing clearance regime; VAT invoices may be issued electronically or on paper. Invoices must contain the statutory content prescribed by the VAT Act 2020, including supplier name, address and TRN, recipient identification, description of supply, rate, and VAT amount. G-VAT returns must be filed quarterly through the Customs & Excise Department's online filing system, and supporting records, contracts and bank documents must be retained for at least six years.

Unsupported or fraudulent export zero-rating claims expose the ELP to a civil penalty of up to 100% of the VAT undercharged together with interest, a fixed penalty for overstated input VAT recovery, and retrospective assessments of up to six years under the VAT Act 2020. Dishonest evasion may be prosecuted criminally with fines and imprisonment. The Customs & Excise Department and the Income Tax Office may jointly claw back input VAT, issue disqualification orders against the general partner, strike the ELP from the registry, and render general partners jointly and severally liable for the fraudulent claim.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar operates a fully convertible offshore financial centre. There is no exchange-control legislation and no regulatory distinction between current-account and capital-account convertibility: export revenues from AAA-game 3D art outsourcing, partner capital contributions, intra-group loans, and profit distributions are all freely convertible between GIP, GBP, USD, and EUR. The GIP is pegged at par to GBP, and both currencies are legal tender in Gibraltar, materially reducing cross-currency settlement friction. Profit-remittance hurdles are practical rather than statutory: (i) banks apply enhanced due diligence to distributions flowing to a Cayman-domiciled ELP and will require audited accounts, CRS/FATCA returns, and a UBO declaration; (ii) the ELP must avoid being characterised as carrying on unlicensed deposit-taking or FX-dealing for third parties when converting client receipts; (iii) recurring high-value USD conversions require a documented hedging mandate to rebut any suggestion of speculative intent; and (iv) ultimate beneficial owners must be recorded in the Gibraltar Beneficial Ownership Register. No Gibraltar withholding tax is imposed on ELP profit distributions, and no exchange-control certificate or central-bank approval is required for any remittance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Zero statutory surrender requirement, zero mandatory conversion ratio, and no fixed repatriation timeline for export proceeds. The ELP may hold export receipts indefinitely in USD, EUR, or GBP in multi-currency operational accounts, or in PayPal business balances within platform limits. As a service exporter under ISIC 7490, the ELP is outside any trade-related FX surrender regime. Prudent compliance practice, consistent with the Low_Risk_Standard AML tier, requires that all receipts above GBP 10,000 be traceable to signed client master service agreements and invoice schedules; that client-receivable accounts remain segregated from partner-capital accounts; and that USD-denominated export balances are marked to market monthly with any hedge instruments recorded at fair value in the ELP's pass-through accounts.

Central bank supervision: Government of Gibraltar - Currency Commission (the sole issuing authority for the Gibraltar Pound (GIP), which circulates at par with GBP). Prudential, conduct, and AML/CFT supervision of licensed banks and foreign-exchange counterparties is exercised by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC). There is no separate Gibraltar central bank; monetary stability is anchored by the Bank of England and HM Treasury, and the GFSC administers foreign-exchange licensing, sanctions screening, and cross-border payment oversight.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM - permissible as an ancillary treasury rail but never as the primary banking anchor. UK-authorised electronic-money institutions (EMIs) may onboard the Gibraltar ELP remotely for GBP/EUR receipt collection and low-value contractor payouts, provided the ELP supplies a complete look-through of all partners along with certified partnership documents. Legal constraints: (i) post-Brexit, an FCA-authorised EMI does not automatically hold passporting rights into Gibraltar, so the service must be serviced from the UK without representing itself as a Gibraltar-licensed bank; (ii) most EMIs are structurally unwilling to service a Cayman-owned partnership as the sole bank, citing UBO opacity and sanctioned-jurisdiction exposure; (iii) EMI e-money balances are protected by safeguarding obligations, not by the Gibraltar Deposit Guarantee Scheme, so balances should be capped and swept daily to a regulated Gibraltar bank; and (iv) EMIs are unsuitable for commercial letters of credit, CHAPS same-day clearance, or high-value FX forward execution. A GFSC-licensed tier-1 bank remains mandatory for all regulatory and treasury-critical flows.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant primary route: the Cayman KY-ELP instructs its Cayman bank to issue a SWIFT MT103 wire in GBP or USD to the Gibraltar ELP's tier-1 Gibraltar bank account. The remittance information field must recite exactly: CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION - KY_ELP TO GIBRALTAR ELP - PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT [DATE] - FISCALLY TRANSPARENT ELECTION - NO EXCHANGE CONTROL APPLICABLE. The receiving bank will require, before credit release, a certified copy of the Gibraltar ELP registration certificate, the executed limited partnership agreement, a GP capital-call resolution, the Cayman ELP's certificate of good standing, and UBO declarations from all natural-person partners. For statistical reporting, the transaction is classified as a capital-account transfer and, where a UK reporting bank is in the chain, as direct-investment other capital within the Bank of England survey. The injection must trace exclusively to partner reserve funds or a documented shareholder loan; commingling PayPal or client-receivable balances with the capital injection will trigger automatic enhanced due diligence. GBP injections are clearing-optimal because Gibraltar banks settle GBP via London nostro accounts using CHAPS and Faster Payments, whereas USD injections require US correspondent routing with higher fees and a 2-3 business-day clearing lag; USD amounts above GBP 1,000,000 equivalent should be split into tranches to remain below bank observation thresholds. No Gibraltar central-bank approval is required, but substance documentation must be retained to rebut any artificiality challenge.Disbursement hierarchy for trade settlements: (i) SWIFT MT103 priority wires in GIP/GBP, USD, or EUR for corporate vendors, tax authorities, and high-value subcontractors, with purpose narrative TRADE SETTLEMENT - 3D ART OUTSOURCING - INVOICE [REF] - ISIC 7490; (ii) UK CHAPS via the Gibraltar bank's London nostro for same-day GBP payments to HMRC, the corporate GP management fee, and UK-based artists; (iii) UK Faster Payments for urgent sub-GBP 1,000,000 disbursements, subject to recipient pre-validation and bank caps; (iv) SEPA Credit Transfers for EU partner studios and independent contractors, converting GBP to EUR at T+2; (v) irrevocable confirmed Commercial Letters of Credit issued by the tier-1 Gibraltar bank for enterprise infrastructure or game-engine licensing contracts above GBP 250,000, and for receivable settlement where a US client requires LC terms; and (vi) PayPal Business mass payouts for micro-royalties to individual 3D modelers and riggers, capped at GBP 50,000 equivalent per weekly batch and supported by a payout manifest. Every outbound wire must be backed by an invoice-and-acceptance evidence file; the GFSC AML/CFT rules require the bank to identify the commercial purpose of each charge, including recurring management fees to the Cayman partner, which must be underpinned by an executed inter-company services agreement and robust transfer-pricing documentation.Clean/vanilla FX Forward (tenor 1M-12M, GBP/USD and GBP/EUR), fully matched to invoiced export exposure and the ELP's functional-currency requirements.Cross-Currency FX Swap (GBP/USD and GBP/EUR) used solely for short-term liquidity rebalancing, with zero speculative overnight open positions.GIP/USD Non-Deliverable Forward (NDF) as an offshore net-settlement-only proxy for the Gibraltar-pound peg, permitted exclusively with a GFSC-licensed counterparty.Zero-Cost Collar / Participating Forward, expressly non-speculative, requiring prior written approval of the bank's treasury desk and the ELP's audit committee.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: MEDIUM - permissible as an ancillary treasury rail but never as the primary banking anchor. UK-authorised electronic-money institutions (EMIs) may onboard the Gibraltar ELP remotely for GBP/EUR receipt collection and low-value contractor payouts, provided the ELP supplies a complete look-through of all partners along with certified partnership documents. Legal constraints: (i) post-Brexit, an FCA-authorised EMI does not automatically hold passporting rights into Gibraltar, so the service must be serviced from the UK without representing itself as a Gibraltar-licensed bank; (ii) most EMIs are structurally unwilling to service a Cayman-owned partnership as the sole bank, citing UBO opacity and sanctioned-jurisdiction exposure; (iii) EMI e-money balances are protected by safeguarding obligations, not by the Gibraltar Deposit Guarantee Scheme, so balances should be capped and swept daily to a regulated Gibraltar bank; and (iv) EMIs are unsuitable for commercial letters of credit, CHAPS same-day clearance, or high-value FX forward execution. A GFSC-licensed tier-1 bank remains mandatory for all regulatory and treasury-critical flows.
