Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Register the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) with the Gibraltar Companies House by filing Form ELP01, statement of particulars, registered office consent and general partner details, and pay the registration fee/stamp duty. Step 2: Submit an application to the Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (GRN/TRN) in the partnership name, attaching the executed partnership agreement, full register of limited partners, certified KYC/AML identification for each partner, and the certificate of registered office. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible - The Gibraltar ELP is statutorily tax-transparent: income, gains and expenses flow through to partners per the partnership agreement; the ELP files an information return but pays no entity-level income tax. Non-resident partners are within the charge to Gibraltar income tax only if they carry on a trade in Gibraltar through a permanent establishment; a non-resident partner of a pure offshore digital-licensing and stock-photo marketplace ELP is generally outside the scope of Gibraltar taxation.

Available election forms

Gibraltar law contains no US-style check-the-box election; transparency is automatic under the Limited Partnerships Act 1990 as amended. Available elections and mechanisms: (a) election in the partnership deed regarding allocation of receipts, profits and losses among partners; (b) application of the Income Tax (Non-Resident Companies and Partnerships) Rules limiting charge to Gibraltar-source trade income attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment; (c) conduit treatment election under Gibraltar's Taxation (Conduit Companies and Partnerships) Regulations for EU/UK parent-subsidiary style relief where applicable; (d) hybrid mismatch analysis and elections under Gibraltar's Taxation (International Tax Compliance) (Hybrid and Other Mismatches) Regulations; (e) for US federal tax purposes, the ELP may file IRS Form 8832 (check-the-box) to elect classification as a partnership or association taxable as a corporation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None - Gibraltar levies no VAT, GST or general sales and use tax. Gibraltar is outside the EU VAT area; excise duty applies only to alcohol, tobacco and hydrocarbon fuels. Digital stock-photo licences and generative-visual-asset services are not subject to any Gibraltar indirect tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable in Gibraltar: there is no indirect-tax registration, threshold or fiscal mark. Destination-market triggers may apply to this cross-border digital business: UK VAT registration for a non-established supplier of digital services is required from the first supply to UK consumers (no threshold); EU One-Stop-Shop (OSS) registration applies for supplies to EU consumers (no EUR 10,000 intra-EU threshold available because Gibraltar is a non-EU supplier); other jurisdictions may impose GST/sales-tax registration based on their economic-nexus rules.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - because Gibraltar levies no VAT, there is no export zero-rating claim, no VAT refund mechanism and no statutory requirement to substantiate a 0% rate. The stock-photo licence and generative-visual-asset deliverables are intangible digital services supplied from Gibraltar; liability, if any, arises under the destination jurisdiction's VAT/GST rules (UK, EU OSS, Australia GST, New Zealand GST, US state sales tax under economic nexus, etc.).

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound is pegged at par to the pound sterling. The jurisdiction operates a fully liberalised current and capital account regime inherited from the UK's post-1979 abolition of exchange controls. There is no central bank approval requirement for converting GIP, GBP, USD, or EUR for profit remittance, capital distributions, or trade settlement. An exempted limited partnership structured as pass-through will not typically attract Gibraltar tax on distributions to non-resident partners, provided no Gibraltar-source income is derived; remittance of such distributions is not subject to exchange control approval. Practical hurdles are limited to anti-money-laundering source-of-funds verification, beneficial ownership disclosure, and financial sanction screening. Availability of deep GIP/GBP liquidity is high because GIP is interchangeable at 1:1 with GBP through Gibraltar banking channels.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export proceeds surrender rule and no mandatory conversion ratio in Gibraltar or the United Kingdom. The Exchange Control Act 1979 was repealed, and no subsequent legislation reintroduced surrender obligations. Export receipts from licensing stock photography and generative visual assets may be retained in the original currency, freely credited to operational accounts, and used directly for operating expenditures without a conversion timeline. The only regulatory constraints are standard transaction reporting under the Terrorism Act and EU-derived anti-money-laundering frameworks, plus the need to ensure payment routing does not breach OFAC, HMT, or UN financial sanctions. Mandatory conversion requirement: 0%; statutory timeline: not applicable.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England as the monetary authority and final settlement agent for sterling; HM Treasury as the sovereign authority for exchange control, sanctions, and financial sanctions; Gibraltar Financial Services Commission as the licensing and conduct regulator for banks, payment institutions, and electronic money institutions in Gibraltar. Gibraltar has no separate central bank; the Government of Gibraltar issues the Gibraltar pound under the Currency Notes Act, with the note issue backed by Bank of England assets.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections must be routed through SWIFT MT103 or, for UK sterling payments, CHAPS/UK Faster Payment, to a segregated Capital Escrow Account in the ELP's name at a Gibraltar-licensed bank. The remitter field 50K must state the legal entity making the contribution; field 59 must identify the ELP exactly; field 70 must state the purpose code and description, for example 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION - PARTNERSHIP SUBSCRIPTION' and, where applicable, the relevant ISO 20022 purpose code such as 'INVP' or 'CMTC'. The bank should receive a pre-advice email attaching the partnership resolution, contribution certificate, and source-of-funds documentation. Funds must not commingle operational revenue and capital contributions; all inbound investment wires should pass a sanctions screening and a beneficial ownership match before credit. If the contribution is in non-GIP currency, the conversion to GIP/GBP must be executed only on the ELP's instruction and documented with a deal ticket. Where a legal opinion is required, an English counsel and Gibraltar counsel joint opinion confirming that the contribution is a true equity-like capital subscription and does not create debt should be uploaded to the bank's compliance portal.Outbound treasury disbursements should use SWIFT MT103 for international wire payments; MT202 for interbank/financial institution transfers; CHAPS for high-value sterling wires; UK Faster Payments for low-value sterling vendor payments; and SEPA/SEPA Instant for euro-denominated contributor payments. For acquisitions of stock image libraries or enterprise media licences, irrevocable documentary letters of credit may be issued under UCP600 using an MT700/MT710 chain through a Gibraltar/UK correspondent bank. Marketplaces such as Stripe and PayPal should be used as final-mile payout rails for small-value royalty distributions, with a central treasury rule that balances above an established threshold are swept to the principal bank account daily. The ELP should maintain a treasury calendar for outbound disbursements, separate counterparty payee verification, positive-pay fraud controls, and reconciliation of digital payout reports against the internal ledger. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Moderate as an ancillary collection rail, low as a primary treasury custodian. Following Brexit, UK and EEA electronic money institutions do not enjoy automatic passporting rights into Gibraltar; a non-Gibraltar EMI must either obtain a Gibraltar e-money licence from the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission, establish a regulated branch or agent arrangement, or rely on a Gibraltar-licensed bank sponsor. Digital EMIs such as Wise, Payoneer, and similar platforms can be used for merchant payouts, currency conversion, and low-value vendor disbursements, but the ELP should maintain its principal segregated treasury and capital escrow accounts at a Gibraltar-licensed bank. The targeted rails Stripe and PayPal remain acceptable for marketplace settlement but should be configured as pass-through collection arrangements with daily sweep settlement to the licensed bank account.
