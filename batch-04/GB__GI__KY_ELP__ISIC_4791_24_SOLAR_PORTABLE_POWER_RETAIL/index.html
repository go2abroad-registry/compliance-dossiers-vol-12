Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is prescribed for an Exempted Limited Partnership under the Limited Partnerships Act 2016. Capital contributions are deferred per the Partnership Agreement, may be satisfied in cash or in kind, and no escrow or statutory bank deposit is required before registration; however, the General Partner must maintain a registered office and authorised Recognised Agent in Gibraltar at the time of filing.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar Income Tax Office and Gibraltar Customs & Excise Division, HM Government of Gibraltar; taxpayer portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity must appoint a Gibraltar-registered tax representative and file Form TRFN-1 with the Income Tax Office, submitting the certificate of incorporation or partnership deed, registered office address, proof of address of the entity and its principals, a description of the trade, and an application for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (GTRN). For indirect tax purposes, the same form triggers a GCT registration number where taxable turnover exceeds or is expected to exceed the statutory threshold.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — A foreign entity must appoint a Gibraltar-registered tax representative and file Form TRFN-1 with the Income Tax Office, submitting the certificate of incorporation or partnership deed, registered office address, proof of address of the entity and its principals, a description of the trade, and an application for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (GTRN). For indirect tax purposes, the same form triggers a GCT registration number where taxable turnover exceeds or is expected to exceed the statutory threshold.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

An ELP is by default fiscally transparent and may elect under the Income Tax (Partnership Elections) Rules to be treated as an entity-level taxpayer for gross income tax purposes. Hybrid mismatch elections are permitted only where the transaction structure does not create a deduction without a corresponding income inclusion, and must be notified to the Income Tax Office within 60 days of the first filing date.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GBP 100,000 of taxable supplies in any rolling 12-month period

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export zero-rating is available under GCT law where the seller retains proof that portable solar power goods or related services are supplied to an off-territory customer, the goods physically leave Gibraltar or are exported through an EU/UK exit point within 90 days of invoicing, and the payment is received in foreign currency through a Gibraltar licensed bank. Services are zero-rated where the recipient is a non-Gibraltar resident and the effective use and enjoyment of the service occurs outside Gibraltar.

  • Customs Export Declaration (EX-1) with Movement Reference Number (MRN)
  • Commercial Invoice with Incoterms and detailed goods description
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing export
  • Certificate of Origin (where required by the importing jurisdiction)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate from a licensed Gibraltar bank
  • Monthly GCT return schedule listing all zero-rated export transactions

GCT requires electronic invoices to be submitted via the GCT e-returns portal on a monthly or quarterly basis; B2B invoices must include a GCT registration number and be archived for 10 years. Real-time clearance is not mandatory; no digital stamping or API-based fiscal data circling is imposed. Simplified e-receipts are acceptable for online consumer sales executed through Stripe and Affirm payment rails.

Fraudulent or substantively defective export zero-rating claims are subject to retrospective clawback of all input GCT recovered, a tax penalty of 100% of the tax evaded, interest at 1.5% per month, and cancellation of the GCT registration. In cases of reckless misdeclaration, partners or directors may be pursued personally under the Gibraltar Customs and Excise (General) Regulations and the GCT Act 2022. The statutory audit look-back period is six years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar maintains full current and capital account convertibility with no foreign exchange controls or restrictions. The GIP circulates interchangeably with GBP and is exchangeable at par with no administrative impediment. Profit remittances from a Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) are unrestricted, though banks apply standard anti-money-laundering (AML) checks to distributions. There are no exchange control formalities, no approval requirements for inward or outward transfers, and no requirement to repatriate export proceeds. The ELP may hold and transact in GBP, EUR, USD, and RMB freely.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or conversion obligations exist. Exporters in Gibraltar are not required to convert foreign currency receipts into GIP or GBP, nor are there prescribed timeframes for selling them. A business may retain export proceeds in any currency to manage procurement and market exposures freely, subject only to the bank's risk-based monitoring.

Central bank supervision: The Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the prudential bank supervisor, with the Bank of England (BoE) serving as the ultimate monetary authority for the Gibraltar pound (GIP) which is pegged 1:1 to the British pound sterling (GBP).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: High, with legal constraints. The ELP may open online accounts with UK-authorized Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) such as Wise Business, Revolut Business, and Currencycloud under the UK-Gibraltar financial services passporting provisions. These EMIs support multi-currency, low-cost settlement and direct connectivity to Stripe and similar payment aggregators. However, they are not authorized as Gibraltar deposit-taking institutions, so they should be used for operational float, not as the primary treasury vault. The GFSC holds a conservative view: regulatory finality and maximum creditor protection require a minimum core balance held with a licensed Gibraltar bank. Legal limitation: UK EMIs cannot provide isolated-investment-grade custody or serve as custodian for capital escrow funds.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant wire transfer protocols and purpose codes: Capital contributions must be routed from the investor's bank to the ELP's Gibraltar operational account using SWIFT MT103 or, for European SEPA partners, a SEPA CT. The message must include (i) in field 50K, the remitter's investor name, (ii) in field 59, the ELP name and account number, (iii) in field 70, a purpose code of 'S020' (capital contribution) or 'B110' (business transfer) and the reference to the Capital Call Notice, and (iv) in field 72, the account of the registered agent. For US-dollar transfers above USD 100,000, the bank may require an additional pre-arrival compliance screening and Form MoF-1 declaration. A funds-source verification pack (recent bank statements, contract of limited partnership, etc.) is to be pre-cleared with the bank's onboarding team.Clearing rails for trade settlements: Supplier payments to China, Hong Kong, and Southeast Asia are to be executed via SWIFT MT103 with full documentary attachments; for orders exceeding GBP 25,000, use a sight Commercial Letter of Credit (LC) issued by the Gibraltar bank in favour of the supplier and confirmed by a prime UK or Singapore bank. In addition, the partnership should configure a batch-payment API through the bank's online cash management system (using SWIFT gpi) to support daily clearing of Stripe and Affirm merchant settlement payables.
