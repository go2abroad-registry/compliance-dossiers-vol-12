Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax), with HM Customs Gibraltar for indirect taxes; portals: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation and https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/customs. For UK residual VAT/CT matters: HM Revenue & Customs, https://www.gov.uk/hmrc.

E-government filing portal

A non-resident Cayman Islands Exempted Limited Partnership is not automatically registered for Gibraltar tax. Procedure: (1) appoint a resident tax representative/accountant if the ELP has no Gibraltar establishment; (2) file an application with the Commissioner of Income Tax using the partnership constitution, Cayman certificate of registration, certificate of good standing, partnership agreement, identity of the general partner and controllers, and a projection of Gibraltar-source income; (3) obtain a Gibraltar Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN); (4) register for Payroll Tax (Class 1/2) if the ELP employs staff and for Social Insurance contributions; (5) register with HM Customs Gibraltar only if importing dutiable goods. No Gibraltar VAT ID is required because Gibraltar is not an EU VAT territory and does not levy VAT.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — A non-resident Cayman Islands Exempted Limited Partnership is not automatically registered for Gibraltar tax. Procedure: (1) appoint a resident tax representative/accountant if the ELP has no Gibraltar establishment; (2) file an application with the Commissioner of Income Tax using the partnership constitution, Cayman certificate of registration, certificate of good standing, partnership agreement, identity of the general partner and controllers, and a projection of Gibraltar-source income; (3) obtain a Gibraltar Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN); (4) register for Payroll Tax (Class 1/2) if the ELP employs staff and for Social Insurance contributions; (5) register with HM Customs Gibraltar only if importing dutiable goods. No Gibraltar VAT ID is required because Gibraltar is not an EU VAT territory and does not levy VAT.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Gibraltar and the UK do not operate a US-style 'check-the-box' election. The Cayman ELP is transparent by default; a copy of the partnership allocation election should be filed with the Gibraltar Income Tax Office so that partners are assessed directly on their shares of Gibraltar-source income. For UK hybrid mismatch purposes, the partners may file an election or consistency statement under TIOPA 2010 Part 6A where the ELP is treated as a separate taxable person in another jurisdiction; the Commissioner of Income Tax may be asked to issue a ruling confirming the transparent treatment of the ELP for Gibraltar income tax.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar has no general VAT or GST. Indirect taxation consists of Gibraltar customs and excise duties, stamp duties, financial services levy and social/payroll-type taxes. UK VAT (VATA 1994) applies in Great Britain and Northern Ireland, not in Gibraltar.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable: Gibraltar has no VAT turnover threshold because no VAT exists. Customs/excise registration arises only upon importation of dutiable goods. If a separate UK VAT registration is required (e.g., a UK branch supplies taxable services), the registration threshold for UK-established persons is GBP 90,000 of taxable turnover; non-established persons with UK-taxable supplies are registerable from the first pound.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For Gibraltar indirect tax purposes, there is no VAT zero-rating scheme or VAT refund mechanism. Export of goods from Gibraltar is outside the scope of Gibraltar customs duty if no duty is incurred, but any previously relieved/received excise or duty may be remitted only with proof of exportation. For UK VAT, zero-rating of exported goods is available if the supplier supplies goods for export to an address outside the UK and retains satisfactory commercial evidence of export within standard time limits. Cross-border commercial litigation services (ISIC 6910) supplied by a Gibraltarian supplier to an overseas business are generally outside the scope of UK/Gibraltar VAT under B2B place-of-supply rules; where the recipient is outside the UK, no VAT is charged and no refund is required.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Gibraltar Customs declaration or UK Customs Declaration Service entry), including commodity code and valuation
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill showing shipment from Gibraltar/UK port to overseas destination
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT/Payment confirmation proving export consideration and FX repatriation
  • Proof of export destination and final buyer (delivery/order correspondence and customs proof)
  • Legal services engagement letter and billing detail for offshore services, if claiming outside-the-scope treatment

Gibraltar does not mandate real-time e-invoicing or continuous controls. Customs declarations are submitted electronically through HM Customs Gibraltar's electronic clearance facility; excise goods (alcohol and tobacco) must carry fiscal mark/banderole. In the UK, HMRC operates the Customs Declaration Service (CDS) for export/import declarations and the Making Tax Digital VAT reporting framework; e-invoicing is voluntary and no digital stamping is required.

Fraudulent or unsupported export zero-rating claims are subject to retrospective clawback of the full VAT or duty relieved, statutory interest, and penalties up to 100% of the tax amount. In the UK, HMRC may issue assessment and penalties for deliberate inaccuracies (Finance Act 2007 Schedule 24) with a 20-year assessment time limit for deliberate concealment; dishonest facilitation of VAT evasion may lead to criminal prosecution. In Gibraltar, false customs declarations and attempted duty avoidance are actionable under the Customs Ordinance, with monetary penalties, forfeiture of goods, and imprisonment; the Commissioner of Income Tax may also issue a best-of-judgment assessment where the underlying profit attribution is mis-stated.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Sterling and the Gibraltar pound are fully convertible. Gibraltar levies no general exchange controls on current or capital account transactions, and the Gibraltar pound trades at par with GBP. Profit remittance, capital distributions, legal-fee repatriation, and repayment of partner loans are not subject to FX licensing or approval. In practice, counterparty banks and the GFSC-licensed clearing banks apply source-of-wealth and source-of-funds checks to satisfy UK sanctions, reporting, and anti-money laundering standards.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export-proceeds surrender requirement or mandatory conversion ratio for receipts derived from cross-border legal services, litigation funding, or arbitration settlements. Fee and settlement funds may be retained in the original currency. However, any payment subject to UK or Gibraltar financial sanctions must be frozen and promptly reported to the Gibraltar Sanctions Office or Office of Financial Sanctions Implementation where the underlying dispute touches sanctioned regimes.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as integrated financial supervisor; monetary and foreign-exchange authority is exercised through the UK-Gibraltar currency and customs union with HM Treasury and the Bank of England within the wider sterling monetary framework.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File mandatory where annual related-party transactions (including shared legal-fee revenue, management or referral fees, joint retainer reimbursements and rent allocations) exceed GBP 1,000,000; Master File mandatory if the consolidated group turnover of all jurisdictions exceeds GBP 50,000,000; simplified documentation threshold for low-risk service ELPs: GBP 500,000.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar Economic Substance (Companies and Limited Partnerships) Regulations 2019 apply to the ELP. Cross-border commercial litigation, arbitration, and asset tracing is treated as a mobile business requiring economic substance in Gibraltar. The ELP must maintain a registered office, a local licensed manager/general partner, adequate physical premises, qualified staff (as appropriate to the litigation practice), and conduct core-income-generating activities and strategic decisions in Gibraltar. An annual ESR-01 return must be filed with Companies House Gibraltar; failure is punished by financial penalties (up to GBP 250,000) and potential striking-off; non-resident ELPs must still appoint a Gibraltar tax representative and an authorised ESR filing agent.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Misdeclaration

Statutory sanction: Best-judgment assessment plus penalty up to 200% of the tax evaded; unlimited criminal fine; imprisonment up to 10 years under the Income Tax Act 2010 and the Proceeds of Crime Law; asset forfeiture for instrumentalities of the offence.

Director personal liability: The general partner and any corporate director who signs the fraudulent return is jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax and penalties; the court may pierce the ELP’s veil, issue civil restitution orders, impose a travel ban, and disqualify the director for up to 15 years; imprisonment is available for controlling officers.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Financial penalty of up to GBP 250,000; daily default penalty of GBP 2,500 per day; strike-off and dissolution by the Gibraltar Registrar; disqualification orders against the general partner; referral to the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit for further AML investigation.

Director personal liability: The general partner is personally liable for penalties where partnership assets are insufficient; corporate directors are susceptible to director disqualification, worldwide asset freezing orders, and travel bans; in serious cases, the court will order civil restitution equal to benefits derived from the shell arrangement.
