Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital; no escrow or capital deposit requirement. Each initial Limited Partner must make at least one contribution (nominal GBP 1 or higher as set by the Partnership Agreement), and the General Partner must file a Statement of Particulars confirming contribution amounts.

Funding & unlock path: Not required for pure e-commerce operations with no physical premises. If a small commercial office is later leased: (i) obtain a Fire Safety Certificate from the Gibraltar Fire & Rescue Service upon completion of an annual fire-risk assessment; (ii) obtain a Certificate of Occupancy from the Buildings Control Office, HM Government of Gibraltar, for structural load compliance; and (iii) comply with the Safety at Work Regulations 2018 for workstation ergonomics and first-aid kits. No civil-defense or workplace clearance is statutorily triggered for a virtual-only online retail operation.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital; no escrow or capital deposit requirement. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, H.M. Government of Gibraltar — Income Tax Office (portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation); indirect/customs authority: Gibraltar Customs and Excise Department.

E-government filing portal

For an ELP formed in Gibraltar, the Registrar of Companies automatically notifies the Income Tax Office, which issues the Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) without separate filing. A foreign-formed ELP carrying on trade in Gibraltar must apply to the Commissioner of Income Tax for a TRN, submitting the partnership agreement, certificate of good standing, and registered-office evidence. Gibraltar levies no VAT, so there is no VAT ID; an ELP that imports or exports goods must separately register with the Gibraltar Customs and Excise Department for a customs registration/identification number.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — For an ELP formed in Gibraltar, the Registrar of Companies automatically notifies the Income Tax Office, which issues the Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) without separate filing. A foreign-formed ELP carrying on trade in Gibraltar must apply to the Commissioner of Income Tax for a TRN, submitting the partnership agreement, certificate of good standing, and registered-office evidence. Gibraltar levies no VAT, so there is no VAT ID; an ELP that imports or exports goods must separately register with the Gibraltar Customs and Excise Department for a customs registration/identification number.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists under Gibraltar law. An ELP is fiscally transparent by default, with partners taxed on their profit shares. There is no statutory election for entity-level taxation; however, the Commissioner may assess a partnership as a body of persons where exceptional anti-avoidance conditions apply. Hybrid mismatch rules have not been separately enacted in Gibraltar; UK hybrid mismatch rules may apply to UK-resident partners.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax — Gibraltar imposes customs duty on importation and excise duty on specified goods; domestic sale of electronics is outside any local consumption tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A – no VAT/GST registration threshold; any person importing or exporting goods must register with the Gibraltar Customs and Excise Department from the first customs declaration. Excise registration applies only to producers or importers of excisable goods.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — Gibraltar has no VAT or GST, so no zero-rating claim, refund mechanism, or export VAT schedule exists. Goods exported from Gibraltar are simply outside the scope of Gibraltar consumption tax. Customs-duty relief may be available for goods imported for re-export under the Customs Ordinance, subject to prescribed conditions and export evidence.

  • Customs export declaration (electronic, via Gibraltar Customs processing system)
  • Commercial invoice / sales contract
  • Packing list
  • Bill of lading or air waybill
  • Delivery confirmation or foreign customs evidence of arrival
  • Copy of inward remittance / SWIFT payment confirmation (where required by anti-money-laundering or customs-valuation rules)

Gibraltar does not mandate electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or API fiscalisation for retail sales. Customs entries are lodged electronically through the Gibraltar Customs and Excise Department's customs processing system; no separate sales-data transmission obligation applies.

In the absence of VAT, no VAT refund is claimable; audit scrutiny focuses on customs-duty relief or drawback claims supported by export documentation. False, overstated, or unsupported export claims are retrospectively clawed back for up to six years from the date of relief. Under the Customs Ordinance, penalties include a fine of up to GIP 10,000 plus treble the duty evaded and imprisonment of up to three years; negligent errors attract administrative penalties of 10%–30% of the duty involved.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The GIP is fully and freely convertible into GBP at statutory parity; GBP is accepted as legal tender alongside GIP in Gibraltar. There are no exchange-control restrictions applicable to the ELP: current-account transactions (e-commerce receivables, supplier payments) and capital-account transactions (partner capital contributions, profit distributions) are fully unrestricted. Because the ELP is tax-transparent and Gibraltar levies no withholding tax on distributions to non-resident partners, profit remittance is subject only to bank-level AML/KYC verification of ultimate beneficial ownership, not to regulatory approval.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Gibraltar imposes no mandatory surrender, repatriation deadline, or conversion ratio for export, e-commerce, or retail proceeds. The ELP may retain USD, EUR, or other major currencies in designated multi-currency accounts, and may convert at commercial unregulated rates without notification to the GFSC.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar has no independent central bank; monetary authority is effectively vested in the Bank of England, with the Gibraltar pound (GIP) issued by HM Government of Gibraltar under the Currency Notes Act 1934 at a fixed 1:1 parity to GBP. Banking prudential and conduct regulation is exercised by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) under the Financial Services Act 2019 and the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Exempted Limited Partnership with undisclosed/silent non-resident limited partners, creating ambiguity over beneficial ownership and control.

Root cause: Exempted Limited Partnership with undisclosed/silent non-resident limited partners, creating ambiguity over beneficial ownership and control.

Defense evidence: Original/notarised partnership deed and certificate of registration; GP's statutory declaration identifying all partners and control; up-to-date register of partners; UBOS diagram with passport copies; confirmation that the ELP is not a collective investment scheme under GFSC guidance; partnership tax transparency statement from a licensed professional.
Large lump-sum capital contribution from overseas partners immediately ahead of upstream payment activity, triggering source-of-funds concerns.

Root cause: Large lump-sum capital contribution from overseas partners immediately ahead of upstream payment activity, triggering source-of-funds concerns.

Defense evidence: Personal and corporate bank statements covering 6-12 months before injection; tax returns or tax clearance; source-of-wealth documentation (sale agreements, dividend vouchers, inheritance/court documents, or regulated exchange statements); sworn letter citing the capital in the partnership agreement and specifying the commercial purpose (inventory purchase, deposits on platforms/trade creditors).
High-velocity cross-border B2C e-commerce flows via Stripe/PayPal with chargeback and refund cycles, raising laundering and consumer-protection risk flags.

Root cause: High-velocity cross-border B2C e-commerce flows via Stripe/PayPal with chargeback and refund cycles, raising laundering and consumer-protection risk flags.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Partners remit via SWIFT MT103 from their personal/corporate accounts to the ELP's designated GIP/GBP operational account at a GFSC-licensed bank. The remittance must be accompanied by the beneficiary's full legal name, ELP account number, and a remittance-information line reading 'Capital Contribution — [Partner Name] — [ELP Name]' together with the ELP's registration number. Gibraltar and the UK operate no mandatory national purpose-code system; where the intermediary bank requests a purpose code, select 'Capital Investment / Equity Injection' or the statistical direct-investment code applicable to partnership capital, and provide the bank's source-of-funds form and the signed partnership deed at onboarding to prevent return. The funds must be in GBP, USD, or EUR and, if above the bank's defined threshold, a completion source-of-funds report is required before crediting.Trade settlements use a layered clearing structure: (i) SWIFT MT103 covers open-account payments to APAC suppliers, cleared over USD via the bank's correspondent (CHIPS/Fedwire) or in EUR via TARGET2; (ii) commercial letters of credit (sight or usance, up to 90 days) are established for bulk inventory purchases from Shenzhen/Korea/Taiwan suppliers, confirmed/advised by a Hong Kong or Shenzhen bank and routed via the Gibraltar bank's trade-services unit; (iii) documentary collections (D/P, D/A) handle mid-value consignments; and (iv) CNH/USD payments to mainland suppliers are executed through offshore RMB clearing in Hong Kong via the MT103 route or an NDF-converted USD payment, depending on bank limits.
