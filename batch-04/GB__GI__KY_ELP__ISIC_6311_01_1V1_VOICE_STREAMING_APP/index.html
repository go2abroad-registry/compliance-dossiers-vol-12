Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for an Exempted Limited Partnership; capital contributions are fixed solely by the Limited Partnership Agreement and may be cash, in kind or nil at formation; no escrow or paid-up capital deposit is required. The General Partner must maintain a registered office in Gibraltar and the ELP must appoint an authorised representative for service of process.

Funding & unlock path: No physical plant exists at formation: the registered office occupies serviced premises under a landlord certificate of occupancy and fire safety compliance certificate. If a Gibraltar-staffed office is opened later, the entity must complete a workplace risk assessment under the Health and Safety at Work Act and obtain a fire inspection clearance from the Gibraltar Fire and Rescue Service before occupation; remote-only operations require only employer liability insurance and the registered office provider's fire compliance confirmation.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital for an Exempted Limited Partnership; capital contributions are fixed solely by the Limited Partnership Agreement and may be cash, in kind or nil at formation; no escrow or paid-up capital deposit is required. The General Partner must maintain a registered office in Gibraltar and the ELP must appoint an authorised representative for service of process.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility for both current and capital account transactions in GBP and GIP (pegged at par to GBP). No exchange controls, no prior approval requirements, no profit remittance restrictions for the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP), and no barriers on dividend or capital distributions to non-resident partners. Voice streaming app revenues via Apple IAP and Google Play Billing are fully remittable subject only to standard AML/KYC verification and beneficial ownership disclosure.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no timeline for repatriation of export proceeds or cross-border digital service receipts within the UK or Gibraltar. Retention of documents for five years is required under the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and UK Money Laundering Regulations 2017.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England and HM Treasury; for Gibraltar-specific banking supervision, the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Medium-High, subject to restrictive use. Digital EMIs (including Wise, Revolut, and MultiPass) are permissible for low-value operational disbursements and FX conversion, but not as the primary settlement account for Apple IAP and Google Play Billing proceeds. The ELP must maintain a GFSC-supervised commercial banking relationship for capital escrow and to meet enhanced due diligence expectations for a Medium_Risk_Enhanced voice streaming application. EMIs may be added for supplier payments only after the tier-1 banking relationship is active.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 annual cross-border related-party transactions for Local File preparation; GIP 50,000,000 group turnover triggers a Gibraltar Master File; GIP 750,000,000 consolidated group turnover triggers country-by-country reporting where the ELP or a Gibraltar partner is the reporting entity.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to tax-resident companies and partnerships undertaking relevant activities. For the voice streaming app, the relevant activity is either 'intellectual property' (where proprietary AI, audio models, or exclusive app IP is held) or 'distribution and service center business' (for in-app payment, marketing, and platform management functions). The ELP must have an adequate physical presence in Gibraltar, full-time staff with decision-making authority, sufficient operational expenditure, and core income-generating activities performed locally. A passive shell with a registered agent only, or a structure that merely invoices Apple/Google proceeds without local substance, will fail ESR and be subject to penalty, information exchange, and potential strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Returns

Statutory sanction: Under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act and the Criminal Code, offenders face civil penalties up to 100% of the tax undercharged, daily default interest, monetary fines, and imprisonment for a term of up to 14 years where fraudulent evasion is proved. The court may also order confiscation of the tax avoided.

Director personal liability: A general partner or director who signs a false return may be jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax if the ELP is treated as a facade for the defendant; civil recovery actions, travel bans to prevent departure from Gibraltar, and revocation of the individual's directorship or professional standing can be imposed.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Failure of the Economic Substance Regulations subjects the entity to financial penalties of GIP 10,000 to GIP 100,000 per relevant activity, mandatory correction notices, penalty increases for continued non-compliance, and ultimately an application to strike off the ELP from the Registry of Companies. A default certificate may be sent to foreign tax authorities.

Director personal liability: The general partner cannot invoke limited liability for the ELP's substance default; if the entity is a mere shell, courts may pierce the partnership veil, hold the general partner personally liable for unpaid penalties, and issue director disqualification orders preventing the individual from managing Gibraltar companies or trusts.
AML and Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: The Proceeds of Crime Act 2011 and the Gibraltar Anti-Money Laundering Act impose fines at an unlimited level, rigorous confiscation orders over connected property, asset freezing orders, and terms of imprisonment up to 14 years. The Financial Services Commission may also exercise civil penalties and revoke the ELP's integrity rating.

Director personal liability: Managers, beneficial owners, and anyone directing the processing of proceeds face personal criminal liability for money laundering; travel bans, stringent financial conduct disqualifications, and extradition cooperation with EU and UK authorities are routine. The corporate veil is pierced where the ELP is used as a conduit for laundering the financial benefits of unlawful conduct.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Anti-Avoidance Assessment

Statutory sanction: The Gibraltar Tax Office may make a transfer pricing adjustment, increase the taxable profits, apply interest of 8% per annum, and levy a penalty of up to 100% of the tax avoided; a General Anti-Abuse Rule assessment may be issued for artificial arrangements lacking commercial substance.

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners who knowingly authorize sham pricing agreements can be assessed jointly and severally for the adjusted tax, subjected to civil claw-back proceedings, and, where bad faith pricing is used to evade tax, prosecuted criminally with potential imprisonment and prohibition from acting as a manager of any partnership in Gibraltar.
