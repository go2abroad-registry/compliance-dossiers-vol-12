Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

There is no statutory minimum paid-up capital for a Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership. Capital contribution is fixed solely in the Partnership Agreement. No escrow account is legally mandated; however, if a paid-in capital deposit is used for banking compliance, it must be held in a Gibraltar account in the name of the partnership and be supported by standard KYC evidence.

Funding & unlock path: For the administrative office, an Occupancy Approval under the Buildings Ordinance is required, including completion of a fire-risk assessment and structural load certification, and a civil defense clearance from the Gibraltar Fire and Rescue Service confirming emergency exit routes, fire alarms, extinguishers and personnel marshalling. For any airside delegate at Gibraltar International Airport, a Restricted Area Security Pass must be obtained from the Gibraltar International Airport Security Coordination Unit, subject to background vetting and Security Training awareness.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (official portal: https://www.income-tax.gi; Companies House Gibraltar for entity registration; eTax filing portal: https://portal.gibraltar.gov.gi)

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is constituted under the Gibraltar Limited Partnerships Act 2013 via the Registrar of Companies. Because the ELP is fiscally transparent, it does not receive a separate corporation tax reference; the Income Tax Office allocates a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) to the partnership for administrative filing, and each partner is separately assessed on Gibraltar-source income. For General Consumption Tax (GCT) purposes, the ELP must submit Form GCT-REG to the Income Tax Office within 30 days of first taxable supply to Gibraltar recipients; non-resident ELPs with no physical presence must appoint a resident tax representative. No VAT/TIN registration is available or mandatory because Gibraltar is outside the EU VAT area.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible (ELP default classification is a fiscally transparent partnership; no entity-level income tax under Gibraltar Income Tax Act unless election made) — A foreign-owned Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is constituted under the Gibraltar Limited Partnerships Act 2013 via the Registrar of Companies. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible (ELP default classification is a fiscally transparent partnership; no entity-level income tax under Gibraltar Income Tax Act unless election made)

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not provide a US-style check-the-box election. Under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act, a partnership may elect to be treated as a body corporate for tax purposes, subject to Commissioner approval. The ELP may also choose non-transparent treatment for specific categories of partners provided the arrangement has commercial substance; hybrid mismatch filings are required if a tax deduction in a non-Gibraltar jurisdiction is not matched with income inclusion in Gibraltar.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No registration threshold is prescribed under the General Consumption Tax Act 2015; any person making a taxable supply of a service in Gibraltar, including a non-resident supplier, must register and account for GCT unless an express exemption or zero-rating applies. Air cargo charter brokerage services supplied to non-resident counterparties as exported services are zero-rated and therefore do not create a practical GCT registration obligation.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

GCT zero-rating applies to exports of services where the recipient belongs outside Gibraltar and the benefit of the service is not enjoyed in Gibraltar. Air cargo charter brokerage services qualify where the ELP contracts with a non-resident principal, cargo owner, or foreign airline, negotiates and books air cargo aircraft capacity, and does not perform flight operations within Gibraltar. Tangible cargo transiting Gibraltar remains outside the scope of GCT. Substantiation requires a written zero-rating declaration from the recipient and commercial evidence showing the foreign place of belonging of the recipient.

  • Signed air cargo charter agreement or broker engagement letter identifying the non-resident principal
  • Commercial invoice with GCT zero-rating code and place-of-supply statement
  • Master Air Waybill or House Air Waybill number and cargo manifest for the underlying charter flight
  • Customs Transit Declaration or Export Customs Declaration where the aircraft moves goods through Gibraltar customs territory
  • SWIFT/Fedwire inward remittance evidence showing settlement from a non-resident counterparty
  • GCT return extract reconciling zero-rated export revenue

No real-time e-invoicing clearance, fiscal stamping, or tax authority API pre-approval regime is currently operative in Gibraltar. The Income Tax Office mandates retention of full accounting records and issuance of invoices containing the GCT registration number, date, service description, consideration, and GCT charged. Quarterly GCT returns are filed online through the Gibraltar eTax portal; digital records must be maintainable on an automated accounting system for audit inspection.

Gibraltar applies anti-avoidance sanctions for fictitious or unsupported zero-rated export supplies. Assessments may be reopened for up to six years, or twenty years in cases of fraud. Penalties include clawback of GCT with interest, a penalty of 100 percent of the tax evaded, and fixed civil penalties up to GIP 50,000. Criminal prosecution under the GCT Act 2015 and the Gibraltar Criminal Code may result in imprisonment, disqualification of the ELP and its general partner, and suspension or revocation of the freight-forwarding license required under Gibraltar's financial services and industry licensing legislation. A broker knowingly issuing false zero-rating certificates is personally liable.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GIP and GBP are fully convertible at par under the Currency Notes Act 1934; both capital-account and current-account convertibility are unrestricted; no prior regulatory approval is required for inbound partner capital contributions, profit distributions, or gain repatriations by an exempted limited partnership; the residual Exchange Control Act 1947 does not restrict ordinary trade-related transfers or treasury operations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender regime applies in Gibraltar; there is zero mandatory conversion ratio and no required repatriation timeline; freight brokerage fees, charter income, and service receipts may be retained in any convertible currency and repatriated freely, subject only to standard GFIU threshold and suspicious transaction reporting.

Central bank supervision: Financial Services Commission (GFSC) of Gibraltar as the statutory financial regulator; HM Treasury and Bank of England as the sterling monetary anchor; no independent central bank exists in Gibraltar, with the Gibraltar pound being a domestic issue pegged at par to GBP.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium-High, conditional: UK-authorised electronic money institutions may onboard a Gibraltar ELP utilising the UK-Gibraltar regulatory coherence framework, but most restrict air-cargo charter brokers absent a GFSC licence or a regulated partner; the cleanest route is a GFSC-licensed EMI under the Financial Services Act 2019; EU-only passporting no longer extends to Gibraltar post-Brexit.
