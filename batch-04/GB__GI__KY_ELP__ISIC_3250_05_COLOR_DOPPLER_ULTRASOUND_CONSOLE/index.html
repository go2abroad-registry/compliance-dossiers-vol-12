Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Commissioner of Income Tax (Gibraltar), Income Tax Office, Ministry of Finance, 8 Secretary's Lane, Gibraltar; online portal: Gibraltar Tax e-Services / Government of Gibraltar official website (taxation section); customs administration: HM Customs Gibraltar.

E-government filing portal

A foreign/offshore Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) seeking to operate through Gibraltar or open a Gibraltar EMI bank account must: (i) register with the Gibraltar Companies Registry or file a foreign/overseas limited partnership notice, as applicable; (ii) submit the Income Tax Office partnership registration form, accompanied by the certified partnership deed, certificate of formation, registered office certificate, schedule of general and limited partners, notarised passports of individuals, and tax residence certificates of entity partners; (iii) be issued a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (GTRN), normally within 5-10 business days; (iv) if importing or exporting goods, obtain a Gibraltar Customs EORI code through HM Customs Gibraltar. Gibraltar does not levy VAT/GST, so no separate VAT registration number or VAT ID is issued; the GTRN is the sole fiscal identifier for tax, customs, and banking purposes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

No check-the-box election or partnership-to-company election exists under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010; partnership transparency is mandatory for Gibraltar tax purposes and the ELP is not an entity-level taxpayer. For U.S. federal tax purposes, the ELP may file Form 8832 to elect classification as a partnership or association. UK/Gibraltar hybrid mismatch rules apply where a deductible payment is made by a transparent entity without corresponding taxable income inclusion in the payee jurisdiction. A Gibraltar tax residence certificate may be obtained on application for non-resident partner status and for banking/EMI onboarding.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST; Gibraltar Customs Duties and Excise Duties only

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - Gibraltar does not levy VAT/GST and imposes no economic turnover threshold for indirect tax registration. Operators importing or exporting goods require a Gibraltar Customs EORI, which is an activity-based registration with no turnover threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar has no VAT/GST, so there is no Gibraltarian 0% export VAT rate and no VAT refund procedure. Exports of medical imaging equipment, including the Color Doppler Ultrasound Console, from Gibraltar are outside the scope of any Gibraltarian indirect tax. If the export flow is routed through a UK VAT-registered affiliate in Great Britain, UK VATA 1994 Schedule 8 Group 1 permits zero-rating for goods exported outside the UK, provided the UK supplier retains satisfactory export evidence within three months; that zero-rating claim belongs to the UK registrant and not to the transparent Gibraltar ELP.

  • Gibraltar export declaration (GD/EXP) or electronic customs entry lodged through HM Customs Gibraltar before departure from the jurisdiction
  • Clean on-board Bill of Lading or airway bill, or equivalent transport document, evidencing physical export from Gibraltar to the destination country
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103 or telegraphic transfer credit advice demonstrating the export proceeds are repatriated to the ELP's EMI bank account within the contractual 180-day window
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, and UK CDS export declaration where goods originate from or are dispatched from Great Britain
  • CE-MDR / ISO 13485 certificate and medical device listing evidencing lawful export of the Class IIb phased-array ultrasound console

None for Gibraltar domestic tax purposes: no real-time fiscal clearance, no digital stamping, no B2B e-invoicing mandate. Sales invoices may be issued in paper or electronic form; records must be retained for six years. If goods are physically dispatched from Great Britain by an associated UK VAT-registered entity, the UK Making Tax Digital for VAT rules and Customs Declaration Service (CDS) export requirements apply to that UK registrant.

Gibraltar has no VAT clawback because no VAT is levied. However, false customs declarations or fabricated export documentation may be prosecuted as tax fraud under the Income Tax Act 2010 and Gibraltar Customs Ordinance, attracting tax-geared penalties of 100% of the tax/duty loss plus interest, confiscation orders of up to three times the loss, and imprisonment of up to 14 years. Where the same factual documentation supports a UK export zero-rating claim by an associated UK entity, HMRC may impose civil penalties under VATA 1994 sections 60-61 of up to 100% of the VAT claimed, with criminal liability for dishonest evasion. Retrospective assessments or clawbacks generally extend to six years, extended to twenty years where fraud is established.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar jurisdiction maintains a fully liberalised capital and current account, with no exchange-control registration or prior approval required for capital movement. The Gibraltar pound is pegged at par to GBP, and GBP itself is fully convertible on the global market. Profit remittance, unlimited-partner capital returns, and liquidation proceeds can be freely repatriated to any jurisdiction, including the United States, subject solely to source-of-funds verification and AML/CFT obligations. For the underlying medical-imaging export trade, there are no restrictions on invoicing in USD, EUR, GBP or any other currency.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar applies no mandatory export-proceeds surrender, no statutory conversion timeline, and no minimum conversion ratio for export receipts. All export revenue derived from the sale of colour Doppler ultrasound consoles and spare parts can be retained in any convertible currency, held in a multi-currency operational account, or sold at commercial market rates as treasury policy dictates. The only statutory requirement is that the exporter maintain verifiable trade documentation and supporting transfer records for GFSC AML record-keeping and, where relevant, HM Revenue & Customs / Gibraltar tax reporting.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (for United Kingdom monetary policy and overall Sterling framework); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) (for Gibraltarian financial-services supervision and local note issuance under the Gibraltar Currency Notes Act and Financial Services Act 2019). No separate Central Bank of Gibraltar exercises exchange-control authority.

Most large digital/neobank EMIs, including Revolut, Wise, or Airwallex, either decline British-Overseas-Territory partnerships or impose enhanced due-diligence requirements, physical-director interviews, and KYC documentation equivalent to a full banking file. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections must be routed through SWIFT MT103/MT202 wire-transfer protocols using a properly formatted BIC and IBAN (Gibraltar IBAN begins with 'GI') and must clearly designate the economic purpose as 'capital contribution to exempted limited partnership'. The remitter must include the ELP registered name, the general partner’s name, the partnership registration number, and a reference to the capital call or injection agreement. The receiving bank will verify the source of funds before crediting the Capital Escrow Account, and once compliance clearance is obtained, the funds may be swept to the Operational Account. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% — the standard Gibraltar corporate income tax rate applicable to non-SME companies; a concessional 10% rate applies to qualifying small and medium-sized enterprises. The exempted limited partnership is fiscally transparent, so the ELP itself bears no corporate tax; only the partners are assessable on their respective shares of partnership profits.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Gibraltar has no domestic withholding tax on cash or in-kind distributions made to resident or non-resident partners or shareholders.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Interim instalments of estimated tax are payable on 1 January and 1 July of the year of assessment.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Contemporaneous Local File documentation is compulsory where the aggregate value of controlled transactions with Gibraltar-resident or non-resident connected enterprises exceeds GIP 500,000 in any fiscal year; a Master File is required where the ultimate parent’s consolidated group revenue exceeds EUR 750,000,000. The arm’s length principle is codified in the Income Tax (Amendment) Act 2018 and follows the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines 2017, including the requirement to perform a comparability analysis and to apply the most appropriate method.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations (2019, as amended) apply to tax-resident ELPs carrying out relevant activities. For a medical imaging manufacturer-exporter, the mapped categories are distribution and service centre business and, where the ELP holds the ultrasound console IP, holding company and IP business. The ELP must demonstrate: (1) management and partnership meetings held physically in Gibraltar; (2) a physical office or premises in Gibraltar; (3) qualified full-time employees proportionate to activity; and (4) core income-generating activities actually performed in Gibraltar. An annual ESR notification and income test report must be filed with the Registrar of Companies, and non-compliant entities are reported to EEA tax authorities and the UK HMRC under the mandatory exchange of information provisions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Returns

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of up to 150% of the tax undercharged, interest at 8% per annum, revocation of the statutory medical device manufacturing licence, and criminal prosecution resulting in unlimited fines and imprisonment of up to five years under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010.

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners who knowingly sign false returns or cause the ELP to act as a mere conduit may be personally and jointly liable for the evaded tax. The Supreme Court of Gibraltar may pierce the corporate veil, issue asset-freezing orders, impose travel bans, and commit directors to prison where fraud is established.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Administrative penalties of up to GIP 200,000, daily default penalties of GIP 2,000 for continuing ESR non-compliance, mandatory exchange of information with EEA authorities and the UK HMRC, and compulsory strike-off or dissolution of the ELP from the Gibraltar Register.

Director personal liability: Directors and the general partner bear personal responsibility for accurate ESR declarations; the Commissioner may seek disqualification orders for up to 15 years, travel bans, and custodial sentences where ESR evasion was deliberate and used to abet tax evasion in another jurisdiction.
AML and Beneficial Ownership Violations

Statutory sanction: AML penalties of up to GIP 1,000,000 per breach, confiscation orders over the proceeds of the supply chain, suspension of the medical devices licence, and revocation of the ELP’s status as a compliant Gibraltar partnership.

Director personal liability: Compliance officers and directors who consent to or neglect AML obligations face criminal prosecution with imprisonment up to fourteen years for money laundering, personal fines, confiscation of personal assets, and travel bans preventing departure from Gibraltar; the corporate veil will be pierced where the ELP was used as a façade for illicit fund movements.
Treaty Abuse and Improper Claims of Tax Residence

Statutory sanction: Recovery of the foreign tax under assessment, penalties of up to 200% of the foreign tax avoided, automatic exchange of information notifications to the source state, and cancellation of the ELP’s tax residence certificate, triggering loss of treaty benefits and Gibraltar good standing.

Director personal liability: Partners and directors who orchestrate treaty-shopping arrangements may be sued for restitution by the affected revenue authorities, barred as directors of Gibraltar entities, subjected to travel bans, and extradited or prosecuted where the arrangement constitutes criminal conspiracy to defraud the revenue.
