Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (Ministry of Finance); online e-Tax portal at https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation; entity registration with Companies House Gibraltar.

E-government filing portal

A Gibraltar-recognised Exempted Limited Partnership, once registered with Companies House Gibraltar under the Limited Partnerships Act 1991, must apply to the Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number. The partnership is not separately taxable; each general and limited partner must obtain an individual Tax Reference Number and file a tax return in respect of their allocated share of partnership income. Foreign partners with no Gibraltar place of residence or business must appoint a Gibraltar tax-resident representative or agent authorised to receive assessments and service of documents. The application must include the registered partnership certificate, partnership agreement, beneficial ownership schedule, certified constitutional documents, and evidence of economic substance in Gibraltar if Gibraltar-source income is expected. Since Gibraltar does not impose VAT/GST, no separate VAT registration TIN is required; however, a Gibraltar EORI number must be obtained from the Gibraltar Customs and Excise Department if goods will be imported or exported.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — A Gibraltar-recognised Exempted Limited Partnership, once registered with Companies House Gibraltar under the Limited Partnerships Act 1991, must apply to the Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a US-style check-the-box regime. A limited partnership is mandatorily tax transparent under the Income Tax Act, with partners assessed individually on their agreed profit shares. Statutory allocations and special profit-sharing elections in the partnership agreement are respected for tax purposes. There is no elective corporate classification for an ELP. Hybrid mismatch rules under the OECD/G20 Base Erosion and Profit Shifting framework are implemented in Gibraltar, and an ELP participating in a mixed-status structure must comply with any required recharacterisation of deductions or income; no elective opt-out is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT / No GST / No General Sales Tax — Gibraltar operates a limited customs and excise duty system on specified goods (tobacco, alcohol, fuel) and imposes no broad-based indirect tax on goods or services.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — Gibraltar imposes no VAT/GST, so there is no economic turnover threshold triggering indirect tax registration. Any person importing goods into or exporting goods from Gibraltar must obtain a Gibraltar EORI number and register with the Customs and Excise Department before the first dutiable transaction; there is no monetary threshold for that registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar has no VAT/GST, therefore exports are outside the scope of indirect taxation rather than zero-rated. A taxable person may obtain relief or drawback of any customs and excise duties paid at import where goods are subsequently physically exported from Gibraltar to a non-Gibraltar destination. The exporter must demonstrate that the goods left Gibraltar, that consideration was received from a non-resident customer, and that the transaction was not part of a circular or artificial arrangement. Services supplied to non-residents for use wholly outside Gibraltar are not subject to any Gibraltar indirect tax and require no refund mechanism because no input VAT or GST is chargeable.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Entry / Single Administrative Document (SAD)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / CMR carrier proof of export
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Customs Duty Drawback Claim Form (where applicable)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank SWIFT payment statement evidencing receipt of funds from the overseas customer

None — Gibraltar does not mandate real-time electronic invoicing, fiscal data clearance, API transmission, or digital stamping. Customs entries are filed electronically through the Gibraltar Customs Single Administrative Document (SAD) portal, and standard commercial invoices are sufficient for customs and tax purposes.

Gibraltar Customs and Excise Department may audit all export and drawback documentation. False statements, forged export proofs, or circular transactions give rise to forfeiture of goods, a monetary penalty of up to the greater of GIP 5,000 or 150% of the duty evaded, retrospective clawback of all relief or drawback obtained, and potential criminal liability for fraudulent evasion of customs duty. In parallel, the Income Tax Office may disallow associated deductions, impose interest and penalties for under-declared taxable income, and apply Gibraltar’s economic substance and anti-abuse rules to recharacterise sham transactions.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GIP is pegged at par to GBP and is unconditionally convertible; Gibraltar maintains no exchange-control regime and imposes no restrictions on current-account or capital-account transactions. For a charitable endowment ELP, inbound donations, investment income, grant distributions and capital remittances do not require central bank approval. Convertibility friction arises only from sanctions screening, anti-money laundering verification and CRS/FATCA tax-transparency reporting, not from statutory currency barriers.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar has no statutory export-proceeds surrender, repatriation or mandatory conversion requirements. The charitable endowment is free to hold, convert and disburse inbound donation flows in GIP, GBP or any freely convertible currency; no exchange control mandate applies to grant receipts or to US/UK/EU source funds.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as prudential banking regulator; monetary policy and exchange-control competence retained by the Bank of England and the Government of Gibraltar, with the Government of Gibraltar issuing the Gibraltar pound (GIP) under the Currency Notes Act 1934.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low-to-Medium feasibility as a secondary, non-custodial rail only. Revolut Business and Wise Business accept Gibraltar-registered companies but reject fiduciary, escrow, and charitable-endowment retention accounts and impose material caps on charitable payout flows. Gibraltar does not maintain a distinct EMI licensing class; following Brexit, EEA EMIs no longer passport automatically into Gibraltar and any e-money service must obtain authorisation under the Financial Services Act 2019 (Gibraltar). The ELP must not hold endowment principal, donor capital or escrowed grant funds in an EMI.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Pass-through ELP structure with non-resident limited partners and undisclosed ultimate beneficial owners

Root cause: Pass-through ELP structure with non-resident limited partners and undisclosed ultimate beneficial owners

Defense evidence: Notarised register of beneficial owners filed under the Gibraltar Companies (Beneficial Ownership) Act 2017; complete limited partnership agreement; UBO chart identifying every 25% or greater economic-interest holder; passports and proof-of-address for all partners and controllers; GFSC-accredited anti-money laundering legal opinion; statutory declaration that no nominee or bearer shareholding exists.
Ambiguous charitable status for a private foundation conducting endowment management

Root cause: Ambiguous charitable status for a private foundation conducting endowment management

Defense evidence: Registration certificate from the Gibraltar Charities Commissioner under the Charities Act 1961 (as amended); foundation deed and endowment trust deed demonstrating exclusively charitable objects; investment-management policy; annual budget and five-year cash-flow projections; independent Gibraltar legal opinion confirming no private profit motive; explicit confirmation that the ISIC 6430 grant-making activity does not constitute commercial trading.
Large cross-border donations originating from foreign trusts, private foundations or high-risk jurisdictions

Root cause: Large cross-border donations originating from foreign trusts, private foundations or high-risk jurisdictions

Defense evidence: Donor gift letters and trust instruments; source-of-wealth files with audited financial statements or public tax records; UK Gift Aid direction or US 501(c)(3) equivalency letter where applicable; banker's reference from the donor's bank; independent reputation and sanctions database searches covering the donor, its principals and its jurisdiction.
Immediate onward grant distribution to unvetted third-country grantees immediately after account opening

Root cause: Immediate onward grant distribution to unvetted third-country grantees immediately after account opening

Defense evidence: Approved grantee KYC file for each beneficiary comprising registration or charity status certificate, director and officer list, bank confirmation and sanctions screening; signed grant agreements referencing disbursement milestones; annual grant-risk matrix; pre-negotiated monthly disbursement threshold with the bank; written commitment to file suspicious transaction reports under the Gibraltar AML Act 2021 and a prohibition on cash or bearer-instrument payments.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound donations and endowment capital injections must be received into the named Capital Escrow Account via SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 COV where a correspondent bank handles cover). Each message must use the ISO 20022 purpose code 'CHAR' (charity) or 'GIFT' and carry the ELP's Gibraltar Companies House registration number and permanent-endowment reference in fields 70/72. The remitter and payee names must exactly match the KYC profile with no third-party interposition; donor banks must be licensed in the UK, EEA, Gibraltar or another FATF-equivalent jurisdiction. Any single donation exceeding GBP 250,000 triggers enhanced due diligence, CRS-relevant tax disclosure certificates and confirmation from the donor's bank of the lawful origin of funds; all receipts must be reconciled in a segregated grant-receivable ledger before any investment or disbursement.Grant distributions settle through SWIFT MT103 with MT202 COV covering for USD and EUR wires; UK grantees are paid via CHAPS or Faster Payments; EEA grantees via SEPA with the grant agreement identifier in the end-to-end reference field; USD grantees via the bank's New York correspondent through CHIPS or Fedwire. Procurement-linked scientific-grant contracts may additionally be secured by an MT700 documentary letter of credit issued by Jyske Bank where a grantee requires trade-settled payment against milestones. Every outbound payment must cite the grant agreement number, apply the ISO 20022 purpose code 'CHAR', 'GIFT' or 'COMC' as appropriate, and be screened against OFAC, EU and UN sanctions lists; grantees resident in restricted or high-risk jurisdictions require prior board and GFSC-relevant approval together with enhanced counterparty KYC.Outright FX forward contracts (GIP/USD, GIP/EUR, GBP/USD, GBP/EUR) with tenors up to 24 months executed with Jyske Bank or Turicum Private Bank as counterparty.Cross-currency interest-rate swaps (GBP/USD and GBP/EUR) used solely for duration-matching and currency alignment of the endowment portfolio, capped at 10% of net asset value.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) in non-convertible grant-currency exposures such as CNY, INR and NGN executed through Jyske's London desk; strictly no leverage and no extension beyond 12 months.European-style FX options with upfront premium paid for protection on USD/EUR-denominated grant pipelines; leveraged, exotic or speculative derivative instruments are prohibited by the foundation's investment policy and the GFSC suitability requirements.
