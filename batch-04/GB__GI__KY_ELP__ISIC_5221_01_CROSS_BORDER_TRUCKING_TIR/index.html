Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for an Exempted Limited Partnership; capital contributions and commitments are fixed exclusively by the Limited Partnership Agreement; no escrow deposit or capital verification requirement imposed by the Registrar.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certificate for any depot, marshalling yard or vehicle maintenance bay issued by the Gibraltar Fire and Rescue Service; structural load inspection of maintenance bays and workshops under the Building Act 2012; occupational health and safety clearance from the Health and Safety at Work Inspectorate covering manual handling, vehicle movement areas, and driver welfare; annual operating inspection by the Development and Planning Commission where fixed depot infrastructure exists.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital for an Exempted Limited Partnership; capital contributions and commitments are fixed exclusively by the Limited Partnership Agreement; no escrow deposit or capital verification requirement imposed by the Registrar.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar - Income Tax Office and Customs Division; official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation

E-government filing portal

Foreign general partners must engage a Gibraltar resident agent and file an application with the Commissioner of Income Tax (Form ELP-1) together with the registered office address and underlying partner particulars. The Income Tax Office issues a Tax Reference Number (TRN) within 15 working days. Application documents require certified copies of the partnership deed, register of partners, and bank confirmation.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — Foreign general partners must engage a Gibraltar resident agent and file an application with the Commissioner of Income Tax (Form ELP-1) together with the registered office address and underlying partner particulars. The Income Tax Office issues a Tax Reference Number (TRN) within 15 working days. Application documents require certified copies of the partnership deed, register of partners, and bank confirmation.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

No IRC-style check-the-box; however the ELP is transparent by default. A joint election by all partners can convert an ELP into a taxable entity under the Income Tax Act 2010. Under the Partnerships (Tax) Rules, an election to apply the 'foreign profit exemption' may be made for non-Gibraltar-source income. Hybrid mismatch regulations require filing a counterparty classification statement within six months of the first transaction.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax in Gibraltar; limited customs excise and consumption duties apply only to specified categories such as fuel, alcohol, and tobacco

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT/GST registration threshold. For customs warehousing and TIR operations, the enterprises must register with the Customs Division for an Economic Operator Registration (EORI-GI) without any turnover threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Because Gibraltar has no VAT, there is no VAT export zero-rating refund claim. Instead, exports are relieved from customs duty by physical exit of goods; service export under the ISIC 5221 model is treated as outside-Gibraltar source and must satisfy the 'outside Gibraltar' test through contracts signed and performed outside territorial jurisdiction with no recipient in Gibraltar. Required instruments include the supply agreement, CMR waybill, TIR carnet, and proof of payment from a non-Gibraltar bank.

  • Customs Export Declaration (NCTS/EMCS) and TIR Carnet (or eTIR discharge)
  • Signed CMR waybill or equivalent bill of lading evidencing cross-border carriage
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by a licensed Gibraltar or UK bank, or SWIFT MT103 credit confirmation

No real-time fiscal clearance, fiscal grid, or digital stamping regime. Electronic invoicing is not mandated for B2B. For TIR operations, the NCTS (New Computerised Transit System) requires the electronic transit declaration to be lodged at the office of departure, and the TIR carnet or eTIR must be discharged at the destination.

The Customs and Excise Division may recover any duty avoided within six years. Penalties for fraudulent zero-rating or false customs declarations under Sections 57 and 58 of the Customs Duties Ordinance include a civil fine of up to three times the value of the goods plus a surcharge of 20% per annum, and criminal imprisonment of up to seven years. If audit reveals false statements in tax returns, the Income Tax Office may claw back credits, uncapped, with compound interest at the maximum rate plus 15% per annum.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GIP is fully convertible into GBP at par and the jurisdiction maintains no general exchange-control restrictions on current or capital transactions. Residents and non-residents may freely convert profits, dividends, and capital into GIP, GBP, EUR, USD, or other major currencies. Profit remittance from a Gibraltar entity is legally unhindered, subject only to corporate tax filing, economic substance documentation, and standard anti-money-laundering verification of beneficial ownership.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory requirement in Gibraltar for export proceeds to be surrendered, converted, or repatriated within a prescribed timeline. TIR-related freight receivables may be retained in the invoiced currency, routed through any GFSC-licensed bank, and settled as trade receivables. For tax purposes, all revenue must be recorded in GIP/GBP using the prevailing exchange rate on the transaction date, and the ELP must maintain an audit trail connecting each freight invoice to its corresponding clearing or SWIFT credit.

Central bank supervision: No central bank is established in Gibraltar. Currency issuance is vested in the Government of Gibraltar under the Currency Notes Act, while prudential regulation of banks and financial services is exercised by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC). The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is pegged at par to the British pound sterling (GBP), which anchors monetary policy through the Bank of England.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium for unlicensed offshore electronic money institutions. Gibraltar is outside the European Economic Area, so PSD2 passporting is unavailable; any EMI must hold a Gibraltar e-money licence or be a UK-authorised repayment institution with a local agency arrangement. Pure offshore EMIs without a GFSC licence are not viable for corporate treasury, high-value TIR border guarantees, or fuel-card clearing. A licensed Gibraltar EMI or UK-licensed EMI using Gibraltar sponsor-bank accounts is feasible only for low-value operational payments, not as a primary settlement rail.

Defense evidence: Full chain-of-title ownership schedule to natural persons; individual passports and proof of address; source-of-wealth statements and bank references for each ultimate beneficial owner; tax-residency certificates; signed declaration of beneficial ownership under Gibraltar AML legislation; independent AML risk assessment by a licensed Gibraltar compliance consultant.
TIR trucking involves frequent cross-border cash-equivalent fuel payments and third-party clearing cards, which can appear as unexplained cash movements.

Root cause: TIR trucking involves frequent cross-border cash-equivalent fuel payments and third-party clearing cards, which can appear as unexplained cash movements.

Defense evidence: Executed TIR carnets and load manifests; invoices from commercial fuel-card issuers (e.g., DKV, UTA, Shell); GPS telematics logs matching route history to fuel draws; transport contracts with consignors; periodic reconciliation statements between the ELP's bank account and fuel-card clearing statements; escalated AML decision log from a senior compliance officer.
Large inbound capital injections are disproportionate to the stated issue of partnership units.

Root cause: Large inbound capital injections are disproportionate to the stated issue of partnership units.

Defense evidence: Capital subscription agreement; board minutes or partnership resolution authorising capital calls; source of funds and source of wealth for all limited partners; bank-to-bank dealing tickets showing FX path; explanation of the capital required to secure new rolling-stock assets and TIR guarantees.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound capital injections should be executed through a GFSC-licensed bank using SWIFT MT103/MT202 messages with an explicit purpose code such as 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION' or 'PARTNER LOAN' and a narrative that cites the ELP deed and the ISIC 5221 cross-border trucking business plan. The financing entity must concurrently provide a board resolution, the executed capital subscription, and a certificate of identity for the bank's KYC file. No Gibraltar exchange-control approval is required, but the bank may request the ultimate beneficial-owner structure and beneficial-ownership filing reference before releasing funds for TIR operations.TIR route settlements should use dual rails: (1) SWIFT MT103 for major counterparties such as fuel-card consolidators, toll authorities, insurance brokers, and truck maintenance vendors; and (2) commercial fuel-card clearing networks (e.g., DKV/UTA) for high-frequency low-value payments to fuel stations. For large cross-border transactions with European consignors, an irrevocable commercial letter of credit may be established with the Gibraltar bank, with the TIR carnet acting as a customs-bonded transport document.
