Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar - Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax), acting through the Government of Gibraltar eServices portal; customs and excise matters are administered by HM Customs Gibraltar, with import/export declarations linked to the UK Customs Declaration Service where Gibraltar is treated as a customs territory separate from Great Britain.

E-government filing portal

A foreign owned Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) obtains its statutory corporate identity from Gibraltar Companies House at incorporation, which issues the Gibraltar Companies House Number. For direct tax, the ELP must within 30 days of incorporation or commencement of business file a Partnership Return / Income Tax registration declaration with the Gibraltar Income Tax Office, appointing a tax representative approved by the Commissioner where any partner is non-resident. The Income Tax Office will issue the ELP a Gibraltar Tax File Number (TFN). As Gibraltar imposes no VAT/GST, no VAT identification number can be obtained; the TFN is the sole tax identifier. For imports and/or exports, the ELP must register with HM Customs Gibraltar as a declarant and may be issued a customs economic operator registration and identification code for goods declaration purposes. The TFN is also used for employer registration if the ELP has Gibraltar-resident employees.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — A foreign owned Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) obtains its statutory corporate identity from Gibraltar Companies House at incorporation, which issues the Gibraltar Companies House Number. For direct tax, the ELP must within 30 days of incorporation or commencement of business file a Partnership Return / Income Tax registration declaration with the Gibraltar Income Tax Office, appointing a tax representative approved by the Commissioner where any partner is non-resident. The Income Tax Office will issue the ELP a Gibraltar Tax File Number (TFN). As Gibraltar imposes no VAT/GST, no VAT identification number can be obtained; the TFN is the sole tax identifier. The TFN is also used for employer registration if the ELP has Gibraltar-resident employees.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not provide a US-style check-the-box election. The ELP is statutorily tax transparent under the Income Tax Act: the partners are assessable on their respective shares of Gibraltar-source profits. There is no unilateral hybrid mismatch election; the Income Tax (Amendment) Act 2020 incorporates OECD Hybrid Mismatch Rules, which automatically neutralise double-non-taxation by denying deductions or crediting amounts. An ELP may, however, apply for a formal advance tax ruling under the Taxation (Advance Rulings) Regulations to confirm treatment, and may elect into the Qualifying Fund or Eligible Commercial Activity regime only if the partnership actually carries on permitted activity with sufficient substance.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales-and-Use-Tax - Gibraltar does not operate a general ad valorem indirect tax; only customs duty, excise duty on alcohol/tobacco/hydrocarbons, and stamp duty apply to specified transactions and goods.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable: no economic turnover threshold triggers VAT/GST registration. For customs/excise, every importer of regulated excise goods (alcohol, tobacco, hydrocarbon oils) must hold a current trading licence and lodge a security bond with HM Customs Gibraltar. There is no de minimis turnover exemption; a customs declaration is mandatory for all commercial import/export consignments.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar zero-rating is not applicable because Gibraltar does not levy VAT/GST. The export of 1:18 scale die-cast automotive replicas (HS 9503.00.75) from Gibraltar to any foreign destination is outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect tax, and no output tax is charged. The exporter does not receive a Gibraltar VAT refund because no input VAT can arise. If the die-cast models are subsequently imported into the United Kingdom, European Union, or another VAT jurisdiction, the consignee will pay or postpone any import VAT under the importing country's rules; Gibraltar is not entitled to issue a VAT invoice. Therefore the zero-rating eligibility criterion is simply the provision of genuine evidence that the goods have physically departed Gibraltar and that title and price support the export transaction.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Gibraltar exit summary declaration submitted through HM Customs Gibraltar electronic system, referencing ELP TFN)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill / courier waybill demonstrating physical departure from Gibraltar
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or equivalent automated SWIFT wire advice / PayPal payout confirmation evidencing receipt of export sale proceeds

Not applicable: Gibraltar has no virtual fiscal clearance regime, no continuous transaction controls, no e-invoicing mandate, and no real-time digital stamping for general goods. Excise goods require duty stamping/digital marks for alcohol and tobacco as directed by HM Customs Gibraltar. Commercial invoices must satisfy the Evidence Act and general record-keeping requirements but may be issued as paper, PDF, or electronic files. Export clearance is executed through the HM Customs electronic declaration system, not through a dedicated local tax stamp.

Because no VAT/GST applies, there is no direct zero-rating clawback mechanism. Penalties arise mainly under customs and income tax law: a false export declaration intended to obtain a duty relief, excise drawback, or fraudulent avoidance of import duty is prosecutable as fraudulent evasion of customs duty under the Customs and Excise Management Act as applied in Gibraltar, carrying fines, seizure of goods, and imprisonment up to seven years. If goods declared for export are diverted back into Gibraltar free circulation or to a prohibited destination, HM Customs Gibraltar may issue a best-judgment assessment for unpaid excise or customs duty, add interest, and impose civil penalties of up to 100% of the revenue at risk. For income tax purposes, export proceeds not repatriated within 180 days may be treated as unexplained remittances and assessable if the partnership is carrying on a trade in Gibraltar, with penalties for deliberate non-disclosure up to 100% of tax and publication in the defaulters list. UK/HMRC or EU authorities may likewise assess import VAT and customs duty against the exporter or importer if goods are ultimately found to have entered their territory without full customs and VAT compliance.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full and unrestricted convertibility applies on both current and capital account. Gibraltar has no foreign-exchange control law, no exchange-control licensing requirement, and no prior-approval regime for inward or outward payments; the UK Exchange Control Act 1947 was repealed in 1979 and has no application to Gibraltar. Profit repatriation and capital distributions from an Exempted Limited Partnership to UK or non-resident partners are subject to no withholding tax at any point of conversion, although the paying bank must complete source-of-funds verification under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 (Gibraltar) and the Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018 (UK as extended to Gibraltar). There is no barrier to holding USD or EUR settlement balances.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. The statutory framework imposes no surrender timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no requirement to repatriate export proceeds into GIP/GBP. Receipts from the export of die-cast scale automotive models may be retained in the currency of collection (USD, EUR, or GBP), held in foreign-currency settlement accounts, or converted freely at the bank's dealing desk. The only administrative duties are the statutory record-keeping obligations under the Gibraltar Beneficial Ownership Register, the Common Reporting Standard, and the partnership's annual returns.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar maintains no domestic central bank. The issuance of Gibraltar pound banknotes and coin is administered by the Currency Note Issuing Authority of HM Government of Gibraltar (Currency Commissioner), while banking prudential regulation, conduct supervision, and AML/CFT oversight are discharged by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC). The Gibraltar pound (GIP) circulates at a fixed 1:1 parity with GBP, leaving the Bank of England as the effective monetary anchor for the jurisdiction.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Medium. UK FCA-authorised e-money institutions (Wise, Airwallex, Currencycloud) and CSSF-regulated PayPal (Luxembourg) can support the operational cash cycle, but none can substitute for a GFSC-regulated bank. Legal constraints: (i) client funds are held under the EMI's safeguarding regime rather than the Gibraltar Deposit Guarantee Scheme; (ii) most EMIs classify Gibraltar-incorporated partnerships as non-EEA and impose enhanced KYC and lower settlement caps; (iii) capital-escrow mechanics and documentary-credit features are unavailable in EMI accounts; (iv) PayPal is viable for low-value B2C collections but carries account-freeze risk when consumer refunds are blended with trade disbursements, so a designated bank collection account must sit beneath the PayPal business account; (v) the domestic licensed-bank sector is small and relationship-based, making the Gibraltar International Bank route the primary treasury core.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The remittance from the UK parent to the Gibraltar ELP is executed by SWIFT MT103, with no Bank of England or HMRC permission required because the Exchange Control Act 1947 was repealed in 1979. Recommended protocol: Field 33B in GBP; Field 50K for the UK parent; Field 59 for KY_ELP at Gibraltar International Bank; Field 70 referencing 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION - NON-COMMERCIAL DIRECT INVESTMENT EQUITY - BPM6 5E2 - ELP AGREEMENT CLAUSE 8.1'. Verification bundle: the executed ELP capital-account schedule, the Gibraltar registry filing receipt, and the bank's source-of-funds confirmation. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% flat rate of Gibraltar corporate income tax levied on the taxable profits of every Gibraltar-incorporated taxable entity; for a transparent Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP), no entity-level CIT is charged and the 12.5% rate applies to the allocable Gibraltar-source profits of the partners in their hands.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Gibraltar does not levy any withholding tax on dividends, profit distributions, partnership profit allocations, or other distributions of a Gibraltar entity to either resident or non-resident recipients; no advance corporation tax or notional withholding applies at the ELP level.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

All returns must be submitted electronically; paper filing is accepted only with a formal waiver.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is compulsorily required where the annual aggregate value of the ELP's controlled transactions (purchase of zinc alloy components, die-casting tooling, tampo-printing services from connected suppliers, management charges from the GP, or loans and royalties with partners) exceeds GIP 250,000, and in all cases where gross receipts of the ELP exceed GIP 5,000,000. A Master File is required where the worldwide multinational group to which the ELP belongs reports consolidated turnover of EUR 750,000,000 or more. Below these thresholds, a contemporaneous one-page transactional summary and arm's-length benchmark note (usually applying TNMM or CUP) must still be prepared for every accounting period.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar's Economic Substance Requirements (ESR), enacted to meet EU Code of Conduct Group standards, apply only to 'relevant activities'—banking, insurance, fund management, financing, intellectual property holding, distribution and service centres, shipping, and headquarters. Zinc alloy die-casting and tampo-printing manufacturing is NOT a relevant activity, so the formal ESR filing and economic substance test do not operate. However, the ELP must still maintain proportionate substance in Gibraltar—premises, bank account, decision-making, and the practical capacity to carry on the manufacturing/commercial trade—where it claims that its profits are not Gibraltar-source or where treaty benefits are sought. A purely passive mailbox ELP with all functions in mainland China or the UK will be re-characterised and fully taxed in Gibraltar at 12.5%.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion — Deliberate Under-Reporting of Sales

Statutory sanction: Penalty of up to 100% of the tax evaded, plus monthly interest at 1.5%, and criminal prosecution under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act with an unlimited fine and imprisonment up to 7 years; the ELP's registration may be struck off under the Limited Partnerships Act for filing false statements.

Director personal liability: The corporate General Partner's directors and any individual GP cannot hide behind the limited liability shield: the ITO may pierce the partnership veil, demand civil restitution of the evaded tax and penalties from personal assets, obtain a Supreme Court travel ban on the GP and its directors, and seek imprisonment for fraudulent evasion.
False Return Certification and Forgery of Residence Documents

Statutory sanction: Tax-geared penalty of 100% of the understated tax, automatic daily penalties, criminal conviction for forgery and perjury with imprisonment up to 7 years, and mandatory disqualification of the tax representative from future appointments.

Director personal liability: Directors of a corporate GP and the Gibraltar tax representative are jointly and severally liable; civil restitution extends to legal fees and receiver costs; travel bans may be issued pending full repayment; imprisonment applies upon conviction for perjury or forgery.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Recharacterisation of the entire profit as Gibraltar-source taxable at 12.5%; penalty of up to 100% of the tax sought to be avoided; ESR-style penalty of up to GIP 10,000 where the activity is re-labelled as distribution/services; compulsory dissolution or strike-off of the ELP; adverse information exchange with HMRC and Chinese tax authorities under the OECD exchange framework.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil of the GP is pierced: its directors are personally liable for the ELP's tax debts on the recharacterised profit, face civil restitution, travel bans, and disqualification from managing any Gibraltar company for up to 15 years; criminal liability arises if the arrangement is promoted with deliberate deception.
Anti-Money Laundering and Proceeds of Crime Violations

Statutory sanction: Under the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act, money laundering is punishable by imprisonment up to 14 years, unlimited fines, asset confiscation via civil forfeiture, reporting to the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit, and freezing of bank accounts; the ELP's banking relationship will be terminated and its registration reviewed.

Director personal liability: Directors of the GP and any partner who 'consents' to the laundering are treated as principals: they face personal criminal conviction, civil restitution of the funds laundered, travel bans, and loss of limited liability for the full tainted amount; confiscation orders are enforceable against worldwide assets.
Persistent Non-Filing and Failure to Appoint a Tax Representative

Statutory sanction: Automatic penalties of GIP 100 per return with daily escalation, best-of-judgment assessments with a 100% uplift, immediate loss of the non-resident partner election, interest compounding monthly at 1.5%, and deregistration of the ELP under the Limited Partnerships Act with notification to the Registrar.

Director personal liability: The GP's directors and the tax representative are personally liable for all penalties and extra tax; the Commissioner may apply to the Supreme Court for an exit ban against the directors, travel ban and passport surrender, and imprisonment up to 3 years for contempt of a tax summons; civil restitution is collectable by seizure of the directors' assets.
