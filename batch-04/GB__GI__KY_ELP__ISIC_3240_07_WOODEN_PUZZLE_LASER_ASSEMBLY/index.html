Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar - Income Tax Office (ITO, under the Commissioner of Income Tax) for direct tax and partnership registration; Customs & VAT Division of HM Customs Gibraltar for indirect tax administration and export control. Official guidance and e-filing are published via the Government of Gibraltar tax portal at www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxes.

E-government filing portal

For the Cayman-incorporated Exempted Limited Partnership establishing a taxable or supply presence in Gibraltar: (1) appoint a Licensed Fiduciary / registered agent and maintain a registered office in Gibraltar; (2) file the Form LP-INT (Notice of Place of Business / Branch Registration) with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies to obtain a local registration number; (3) submit the Certificate of Limited Partnership, partnership agreement, register of partners and Ultimate Beneficial Owner declarations to the Income Tax Office together with Form IT-PR to obtain a Partnership Tax Reference Number and confirm the ELP's transparent classification; (4) if actual or anticipated taxable supplies exceed the VAT registration threshold, file Form VAT-1 with the Customs & VAT Division, disclosing estimated export turnover and the identity of the general partner, and obtain a Gibraltar VAT identification number; (5) where individual non-resident partners will derive Gibraltar-source trading income, each partner must obtain an individual Tax Identification Number (TIN) for self-assessment purposes. The full registration sequence is normally executable within 7-10 working days through a licensed Gibraltar professional.

The full registration sequence is normally executable within 7-10 working days through a licensed Gibraltar professional.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible (the ELP is statutorily transparent for Gibraltar income tax; profits are assessed in the hands of the partners on a territorial basis - Gibraltar-source trading income is taxable, foreign-source income and non-Gibraltar management and control profits remain outside the charge absent a permanent establishment in Gibraltar).

Available election forms

Gibraltar's Income Tax Act confers no direct 'check-the-box' election on partnerships; transparency is statutorily mandated. Available mechanisms: (i) for US federal tax purposes the ELP (Cayman-incorporated) is not a per se corporation and may file US Form 8832 to elect corporate (opaque) or disgregarded/partnership classification; (ii) UK-resident partners must apply the UK anti-hybrid rules (Part 6TA TIOPA 2010) and may be required to make a deemed-election disclosure where the ELP's transparent classification produces a hybrid mismatch; (iii) the ELP may elect under Gibraltar law to be treated as non-Gibraltar-resident where its central management and control is exercised outside Gibraltar, thereby limiting the domestic tax charge to Gibraltar-source income; (iv) no VAT fiscal-unity or group registration election is available to partnerships in Gibraltar - each partnership is a distinct VAT person.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GBP 85,000 of annual taxable supplies (the Gibraltar VAT registration threshold, aligned to the UK threshold and reviewable by Treasury Order). Registration is mandatory where taxable turnover exceeds GBP 85,000 in any 12-month rolling period, or where there is an objective expectation of exceeding that amount within 30 days. Reverse-charge and acquisition VAT may also trigger registration for goods imported into Gibraltar from outside its VAT territory.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies under the VAT Act 2018, Schedule 1 to: (i) supplies of goods physically removed from Gibraltar to a destination outside Gibraltar within three months of the time of supply, supported by retained documentary proof of export; (ii) services performed directly in connection with exported goods where the recipient belongs outside Gibraltar; and (iii) international transport and ancillary handling services. The ELP's product (ISIC 3240 / HS 9503 - wooden puzzles) qualifies when shipped under B2C or B2B contracts with genuine removal from Gibraltar. Where online marketplaces (Amazon, Stripe, Amazon Pay) are used for UK/EU destination sales, the marketplace or deemed-supplier may account for import/destination VAT, but this does not invalidate the Gibraltar zero-rating of the underlying dispatch provided the ELP evidences actual movement of goods out of Gibraltar.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration (electronic declaration of dispatch through the Gibraltar Customs system)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (transport document evidencing physical removal from Gibraltar)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List describing the goods as wooden puzzles (ISIC 3240 / HS 9503) with quantities, unit values and incoterms
  • Proof of payment via the target payment rails (Amazon Pay / Stripe) including bank credit advice or settlement report evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Carrier tracking and delivery confirmation (UPS / DHL / FedEx track-and-trace report) confirming delivery to the destination country

No real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control, fiscal stamping, or mandatory API tax clearance regime applies in Gibraltar. VAT-registered suppliers must issue standard VAT invoices within 30 days of each supply; electronic invoices (PDF, XML, EDI) are legally admissible provided authenticity, integrity and legibility are preserved. Quarterly VAT returns must be filed electronically through the Gibraltar e-filing portal, with payment due one month after the end of the VAT period. Export declarations are processed electronically through the Gibraltar Customs electronic declaration system under the Customs Act 1992.

The Customs & VAT Division may audit zero-rated export claims for up to six years after the end of the relevant VAT accounting period. If any prescribed export evidence is missing, defective or inconsistent, the zero-rating is retrospectively reversed and VAT is assessed at the standard rate (10%) on the full consideration, with statutory interest from the original due date. Additional consequences include: (i) a penalty of up to 50% of the VAT understated for careless inaccuracy, rising to 100% for deliberate concealment (VAT Act 2018, Schedule 7); (ii) mandatory clawback of any input VAT reclaimed and repayment of any refund previously issued; (iii) criminal liability for fraudulent evasion of VAT or for knowingly furnishing false export evidence, punishable by an unlimited fine and/or up to seven years' imprisonment; and (iv) joint and several liability of the general partner and any knowingly-participating partner for the assessment, penalties and interest, notwithstanding any limitation of liability in the ELP agreement.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar maintains full convertibility on both current and capital account for all currencies, including the Gibraltar pound (GIP), British pound (GBP), US dollar (USD), and euro (EUR). There are no exchange-control laws, no advance approval requirements for commercial remittances, and no restrictions on the repatriation of dividends, interest, partnership profit distributions, or capital reduction proceeds, provided the underlying transactions are bona fide and compliant with the Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended), the Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018 (as extended to Gibraltar), and the Gibraltar Sanctions Regulations 2020. A non-resident exempted limited partner is not subject to Gibraltar tax on passive profits provided the partnership does not carry on a taxable activity in Gibraltar and the limited partner does not hold a permanent establishment there.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory surrender, conversion, or repatriation timeline for export proceeds in Gibraltar. Exporters may freely retain receivables in the invoice currency, switch settlement currencies, and direct payments to any authorised account. The only affirmative obligations are documentary: maintain full transaction records, issue valid invoices, preserve underlying shipping evidence (bill of lading, airway bill, customs declaration), and retain all books and records for a minimum of seven years. Transactions with sanctioned persons or related-party sales at non-arm's-length prices must be avoided.

Central bank supervision: The Government of Gibraltar acting through the Commissioner of Currency (responsible for Gibraltar pound note issue) and the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the integrated banking and anti-money laundering regulator; the Bank of England serves as the anchor monetary authority for the Gibraltar pound's parity peg to sterling and provides the ultimate correspondent clearing relationship.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low-Medium. Gibraltar recognises licensed electronic money institutions (EMIs) only for firms authorised under the Financial Services Act 2019 and the Electronic Money Regulations 2021 with a physical place of business in Gibraltar. Most UK or EU-authorized EMIs (e.g., Stripe, Airwallex, Currencycloud, Wise) do not passport into Gibraltar post-Brexit and cannot be used as a primary liquidity clearing rail for the partnership's capital escrow account. Such EMIs may be engaged solely as e-commerce payment and collection rails for Amazon Pay and Stripe merchant settlement, with subsequent automatic sweep or manual transfer into a Gibraltar-licensed bank. A segregated safeguarding account at a GFSC-licensed bank is mandatory if any EMI is used.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound partnership capital and shareholder/limited-partner loans should be routed via SWIFT MT103 / ISO 20022 credit transfer to the beneficiary bank in Gibraltar, with the originator and beneficiary fields exactly matching the ELP's registered name and account title. The SWIFT message must include a valid purpose code: use 'EQUI' where the transfer is a genuine equity-like partnership contribution, 'MALA' for shareholder/master limited-partner loans, and 'TRDP' for third-party trade payments. Furthermore, the message reference field should carry 'KY_ELP-ISIC3240' to identify the project. For contributions in USD or EUR, settlement will occur through London or New York corresponding clearers, converted to GIP/GBP at parity where required. The bank must receive, before settlement, a general partner resolution, a capital contribution certificate, and a source-of-funds declaration from the remitting limited partner. No commingling of personal and partnership funds is permitted.Disbursements for wood materials, laser optics, engineering parts, packaging, and logistics should be executed by SWIFT MT103 for international beneficiaries or local Faster Payments / CHAPS for UK counterparts. For large capital equipment purchases from the EU or Asia, use an irrevocable documentary letter of credit (UCP600) advised through a UK correspondent bank, with the partnership as applicant and the machinery supplier as beneficiary. Settlement is in either GBP, USD, or EUR depending on the supplier's invoice currency; USD settlement occurs via CHIPS/Fedwire and EUR via SEPA, using correspondent banking chains that route through major clearing banks with GFSC-established relationships. Each payment must be supported by an invoice, packing list, transport document, and, where relevant, EU customs declaration to enable automated AML filtering and audit trailing.Outright deliverable FX forwards (GBP/USD/GIP/EUR) with a maximum tenor of 12 months under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement or a local FX Master Agreement with a GFSC-regulated bank.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) in exotic currencies (e.g., CNY, AED, INR) with a maximum tenor of six months, settled in cleared USD or GBP, used only where full convertibility in the local jurisdiction is unavailable.Cross-currency swaps with tenors of one to five years used to align USD-denominated limited-partner capital into GBP/GIP operational cash flows, executed only after an ALM policy has been approved by the general partner.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% — Gibraltar flat corporate income tax rate (Income Tax Act 2010, s. 39H(1)). The ELP is fiscally transparent; this rate applies only to the corporate general partner on its Gibraltar-source profit share.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on dividend distributions or ELP profit distributions to non-resident partners (territorial source-based regime with no distributed profits tax).

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Within 6 months after the end of the ELP's accounting period (e.g. 30 June following a 31 December year-end). Gibraltar's year of assessment runs 1 July to 30 June; the Commissioner may grant a filing extension only on written application submitted before the statutory due date.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File documentation is triggered when the ELP's aggregate controlled cross-border transactions with associated enterprises exceed £1,000,000 per annum; a Master File is required when the ultimate parent group's consolidated turnover equals or exceeds €750,000,000 (Gibraltar TP rules aligned with OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines and BEPS Actions 8-13).

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar ESR/economic substance doctrine: manufacturing (ISIC 3240 — precision CO2 laser cutting and assembly of 3D mechanical wooden puzzles) is NOT a 'relevant activity' (it is not banking, insurance, financing, headquarters, shipping, holding, IP-holding, or distribution/service centre), so the ELP avoids the strict ESR substance tests; nevertheless, the Commissioner applies OECD/GAAR-style anti-abuse doctrine — the ELP must maintain real commercial substance (premises, CO2 laser equipment, employees, board-level decision-making) and cannot claim pure offshore status while physical production is performed in another jurisdiction without proper profit attribution and documentation.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Returns

Statutory sanction: Civil: penalty up to 150% of the tax evaded plus interest under Income Tax Act 2010, s. 34; criminal prosecution for fraudulent evasion with unlimited court fines; in a UK-Gibraltar cross-border fraud, HMRC may join the investigation and the undeclared profits become additionally taxable on the UK-resident partner with UK surcharges and offshore asset penalties.

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil: the ELP's general partner and its directors are jointly and severally liable for ELP tax debts where intent to defraud is proven; directors face travel bans (PSP/search orders), disqualification up to 15 years, and imprisonment up to 7 years under the Gibraltar Criminal Justice Offences Act and UK Fraud Act 2006, s. 2.
Artificial Shell / Sham Arrangement (Substance Abuse)

Statutory sanction: Recharacterisation: the Commissioner may disregard the ELP for tax purposes and assess profits directly to the beneficial owners; Gibraltar GAAR/anti-avoidance doctrines apply; for a UK-resident partner, the UK GAAR can impose a 60% serious tax evasion penalty and accelerated payment notices; the ELP registration may be struck off for failure of substance declarations.

Director personal liability: Directors of the corporate general partner are exposed to restitution orders for dividends/withdrawals linked to the sham scheme; courts may impose director disqualification and, in cross-border cases, a 12-month overseas travel ban; fraud-by-abuse cases routinely result in joint and several civil liability for the full principal tax, interest, and costs.
AML & Beneficial Ownership Compliance Breach

Statutory sanction: Financial penalties up to £500,000 under Gibraltar's Criminal Justice (Money Laundering) Act 2015 and Companies (Beneficial Ownership) Act 2017; the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission may issue administrative penalties up to £2 million or twice the benefit obtained; a lack-of-economic-substance notification can result in the beneficial ownership information becoming publicly exposed on the Gibraltar register.

Director personal liability: Directors/general partners who knowingly assist in money laundering commit an indictable offence carrying imprisonment up to 14 years and personal fines; the GFSC or HMRC may apply to court for a confiscation order under the Proceeds of Crime Act against directors' personal assets; professional indemnity insurance on the ELP becomes void.
Personal Liability of Directors / General Partner (Commercial & Veil Piercing)

Statutory sanction: Wrongful/fraudulent trading liability under the Gibraltar Companies Act 2014: the court can order directors to contribute to the ELP's assets for the shortfall; the Commissioner of Income Tax may apply for a declaration of personal liability against the general partner and its directors for the entire tax unpaid.

Director personal liability: Civil restitution covering tax, interest, penalties, and legal costs; travel bans of 6-12 months preventing departure from Gibraltar pending inquiry; imprisonment for wrongful trading or fraud up to 7 years; automatic disqualification as director for up to 15 years; HMRC may enforce UK tax liabilities directly against the UK-resident partner through personal assessment.
