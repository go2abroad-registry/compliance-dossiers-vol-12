Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Customs and Income Tax Department (CITD), HM Government of Gibraltar, 24 Kingsway, Gibraltar; official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation (tax filings via the Gibraltar e-Tax Portal: https://etax.gibraltar.gov.gi).

E-government filing portal

Upon incorporation of the Gibraltar Exempt Limited Partnership, the General Partner must file the ELP registration with the Gibraltar Companies Registrar. Thereafter, apply to the CITD for a tax reference number (TRN) using Form IR-01 (Partnerships), attaching the ELP certificate, the partnership agreement, a list of partners showing non-resident status where applicable, and a declaration on intended exempt status. The CITD will issue a TRN with income-tax classification. If the ELP is to rely on the Income Tax Act exemption for Exempt Limited Partnerships, submit the Exempt Partnership Election form and any supporting Certificate of Resume; no minimum capital or shareholding is required for foreign ownership.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — Upon incorporation of the Gibraltar Exempt Limited Partnership, the General Partner must file the ELP registration with the Gibraltar Companies Registrar. Thereafter, apply to the CITD for a tax reference number (TRN) using Form IR-01 (Partnerships), attaching the ELP certificate, the partnership agreement, a list of partners showing non-resident status where applicable, and a declaration on intended exempt status. The CITD will issue a TRN with income-tax classification. If the ELP is to rely on the Income Tax Act exemption for Exempt Limited Partnerships, submit the Exempt Partnership Election form and any supporting Certificate of Resume; no minimum capital or shareholding is required for foreign ownership.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not apply a US-style check-the-box regime. By statute, an ELP is fiscally transparent: income and gains are taxed at partner level. The ELP may elect, by written notice to the CITD within 12 months of incorporation, to be taxed on an entity-level basis as if it were a company under the Gibraltar Companies (Income Tax) regulations. Hybrid mismatch elections are not separately available; anti-hybrid rules under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act cross-reference the OECD template and require reporting on the intended tax treatment of cross-border payments and the location of tax transparency in each partner's jurisdiction.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GIP 100,000 (or GBP 100,000) annual taxable turnover of goods and services supplied in Gibraltar; no threshold applies to non-resident suppliers with a local establishment or to importers from non-Gibraltar territories; foreign entities undertaking taxable supplies of implantable medical-grade titanium devices under remote invoicing may be required to register on their first taxable supply unless fully zero-rated.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Supplies of orthopedic titanium implant devices are zero-rated when physical goods leave Gibraltar and the supplier holds evidence of export to a destination outside Gibraltar (including the UK, which is outside the Gibraltar GST area) within 90 days of invoice. Offshore services relating to those goods, such as supply-chain management, quality certification, and IP royalties received by the ELP for manufacturing processes outside Gibraltar, may be classified as international services and zero-rated if supplied to a non-resident business customer and consumed wholly outside Gibraltar. The exporter must show the goods were actually exported and the recipient is a taxable business in their own territory.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Gibraltar Single Administrative Document, Gt-3)
  • Bill of Lading or airway bill naming the producer or seller as shipper and evidencing export destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT advice showing receipt of payment from the overseas buyer

Gibraltar does not currently mandate a real-time fiscal clearance or digital-stamping e-invoicing framework. Invoicing must satisfy GST Act invoice-content requirements, and electronic invoicing via EDI or PDF is accepted. Import and export cargo declarations are electronically cleared through HM Customs Gibraltar's Customs Declarations System (CDS) and the Gibraltar Trade System; there are no API-based per-invoice e-approval or digital-stamping requirements for B2B sales from offshore manufacturers.

Export zero-rating claims are closely audited under GST sections 22 and 30. If a claim is fraudulent, unsupported, or fails the documentation and 180-day repatriation conditions, the CITD may issue a protective assessment and claw back the refunded credit, adding interest at the statutory rate and a penalty of up to 150% of the amount evaded. Where dishonesty or criminal intent is established, the exported party commits an offence carrying unlimited fines and up to five years' imprisonment on indictment. Retrospective clawback extends up to 20 years in cases of fraud or deliberate misdeclaration, and up to 4 years in negligence cases.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GIP is fully convertible at an unconditional 1:1 peg to GBP. Neither Gibraltar nor the United Kingdom operates exchange controls on current-account or capital-account items. Profit remittances, partner distributions, repayment of shareholder loans and repatriation of contributed capital pass freely and require no FX licence. The only practical constraints are bank-level AML/KYB and source-of-funds verification, not statutory FX convertibility restrictions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory surrender obligation, no mandatory conversion ratio and no required repatriation timeline for export proceeds in Gibraltar or the UK. The ELP may retain 100% of USD/EUR export receipts in foreign-currency accounts indefinitely and convert to GBP at its commercial discretion; only statistical balance-of-payments reporting may be required by the GFSC/Government of Gibraltar for aggregate cross-border flows.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC), acting jointly with the Government of Gibraltar / Commissioner of Currency for Gibraltar pound (GIP) note issuance; monetary alignment and sterling settlement finality are provided through the Bank of England (CHAPS/SWIFT) because GIP is pegged at a fixed 1:1 ratio to GBP.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Moderate. UK-authorised EMIs serve as the most practical post-Brexit non-bank corridor into Gibraltar; providers such as Paytrix, Currencycloud/Barclays and Mercuryo can on-board a Gibraltar-incorporated ELP under enhanced KYB. EEA EMI passports do not extend to Gibraltar, so EU EMIs must clear via correspondent bank partners. Because medical-device manufacturing with physical trade is rated Medium-Risk Enhanced, non-bank EMIs typically cap individual payments below GBP 20k and generally refuse advance payments, LC negotiation and high-ticket disbursements; the recommended architecture is a Tier-1 bank primary relationship with the EMI used only for low-value operational expense and e-commerce collections.

Strike-off trigger

Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: Mandatory independent audit applies where the ELP satisfies two or more of the Gibraltar small-enterprise thresholds: annual turnover exceeding 10.2 million GBP; gross assets exceeding 5.1 million GBP; or average headcount exceeding 50 employees. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GBP 2,000,000 aggregate annual value of controlled transactions attributable to the Gibraltar ELP (de minimis); Master File and Local File are mandatory where this threshold is exceeded and the MNE group has consolidated revenue exceeding EUR 50,000,000. A simplified compliance file documenting the arm's-length result must in any case be retained.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance (Relevant Activities) Regulations 2019 impose a substance test on 'Relevant Entities' carrying on 'Relevant Activities' (banking, insurance, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, shipping, distribution, and service centres, and pure equity-holding or IP businesses). Electron-beam 3D melting of porous titanium hip prosthetics (ISIC 3250) is not itself a listed relevant activity, so no separate ESR annual return is triggered on the manufacturing line; however, the ELP must still demonstrate real economic presence (local registered office, authorised representative, genuine board/management decision-making, and, where applicable, non-resident partners must not create a permanent establishment in Gibraltar), and any 'pure equity holding entity' limb or financing limb of the group is subject to the ESR return, which is filed annually with the Commissioner of Income Tax and carries penalties for non-compliance.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion / fraudulent understatement of partnership profits

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty equal to 100% of the evaded tax, plus daily default surcharges and interest; criminal prosecution for fraudulent evasion is punishable by a fine not exceeding GBP 50,000 and/or imprisonment for a term not exceeding 5 years; conviction also triggers disqualification from holding a regulated licence under the Financial Services Act.

Director personal liability: The corporate general partner's directors and the individual general partners are jointly liable where the structure is used as a facade; the courts may pierce the partnership veil to impose personal restitution for underpaid tax, make a confiscation order under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015, and impose a travel ban (court-imposed restriction on leaving Gibraltar) pending settlement of the tax debt.
Shell company / sham economic substance

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner of Income Tax may disregard the ELP's non-residence claim and assess undistributed profits at 10%/12.5%; ESR non-compliance attracts a financial penalty of up to GBP 10,000 per offence (and GBP 1,000 per day for continuing default) plus an obligation to file corrected returns; in egregious cases the Registrar of Companies may strike off the ELP and refer directors to the Financial Services Commission.

Director personal liability: Directors of the general partner and the general partner itself face civil restitution for the full tax shortfall; the court may also issue a disqualification order against the directors (up to 15 years), a travel ban, and a sentence of imprisonment for up to 5 years for knowingly signing false economic substance declarations.
AML / enhanced due-diligence failure (banking_aml_risk_tier: Medium_Risk_Enhanced)

Statutory sanction: Under the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and the Criminal Finances (Amendments and Modification) Regulations 2019, a contravention is an indictable offence punishable by imprisonment for up to 7 years and/or unlimited fine; the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit and Commissioner for Financial Services may impose administrative penalties of up to EUR 2,000,000 or 10% of turnover for regulated entities; the bank is contractually entitled to freeze the account and file a suspicious activity report.

Director personal liability: A director who knowingly facilitates money laundering through the ELP bank account is criminally liable as principal offender; courts may freeze personal and corporate assets, impose a travel ban, and order personal confiscation equal to the value of the tainted funds; imprisonment term may be increased to up to 10 years in cross-border laundering cases.
Transfer pricing manipulation and base erosion

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner is empowered to substitute an arm's-length consideration and adjust the partnership's chargeable profits, with interest and a penalty of up to 100% of the additional tax; failure to prepare or retain the Local File / compliance file when the GBP 2,000,000 threshold is met triggers a further fixed penalty of GBP 25,000.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign transfer-pricing declarations without reasonable care face personal financial penalties (up to GBP 50,000 per declaration); if the adjustment exposes the ELP as a sham, directors may be joined to the assessment under the piercing doctrine, be made subject to civil restitution of the diverted profits, and be prohibited from leaving Gibraltar pending full compliance.
