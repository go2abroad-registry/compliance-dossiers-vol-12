Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registrar of Companies, Gibraltar Companies Registry, Suite 771, Europort, Gibraltar (electronic filing via the Gibraltar Connect portal).

Governing statute

Limited Partnerships Act 2017 (Gibraltar), ss. 4-8 (Registration, Exempted Status, Partnership Name, Registered Office), read with the Limited Partnerships (Exempted Limited Partnerships) Regulations 2017; the corporate general partner is additionally governed by the Companies Act 2014 (Gibraltar).

Formation / constitutional filings

Form LP1 — Application for Registration of a Limited Partnership, together with a written application for exempted status under the Limited Partnerships (Exempted Limited Partnerships) Regulations 2017.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 21 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital; no escrow, deposit or bank-confirmation requirement. Capital contributions are fixed exclusively by the Limited Partnership Agreement and may be satisfied in cash or in kind.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certificate under the Fire Safety Act (Gibraltar); structural and occupancy clearance from the Gibraltar Building Control Authority; workplace minimum standards, Employer's Liability insurance, and occupational health clearance under the Health and Safety at Work Act (Gibraltar) before commencement of services.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital; no escrow, deposit or bank-confirmation requirement. Capital contributions are fixed exclusively by the Limited Partnership Agreement and may be satisfied in cash or in kind.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Her Majesty's Government of Gibraltar, Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax); official online portal: Gibraltar Tax e-Services (accessible through the Income Tax Office website).

E-government filing portal

Where the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is a foreign (Cayman) exempted limited partnership seeking to carry on business in Gibraltar: (1) appoint a Gibraltar-resident tax representative and register the foreign partnership with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under the Companies (Foreign Companies) Act or equivalent limited partnership filing procedure; (2) submit to the Income Tax Office the Cayman certificate of registration, partnership agreement, declaration of place of effective management, and details of the Gibraltar representative; (3) obtain the Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN); (4) register with the Gibraltar Customs Department only if importing or exporting physical goods; (5) file annual partnership returns and maintain nil or actual tax status. No VAT ID or VAT registration is required because Gibraltar has no VAT.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible: The ELP is fiscally transparent for Gibraltar income tax purposes; profits and losses flow through to the partners. Non-resident partners are taxable in Gibraltar only on Gibraltar-source income, and no entity-level income tax is imposed on the partnership itself. — Where the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is a foreign (Cayman) exempted limited partnership seeking to carry on business in Gibraltar: (1) appoint a Gibraltar-resident tax representative and register the foreign partnership with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under the Companies (Foreign Companies) Act or equivalent limited partnership filing procedure; (2) submit to the Income Tax Office the Cayman certificate of registration, partnership agreement, declaration of place of effective management, and details of the Gibraltar representative; (3) obtain the Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN); (4) register with the Gibraltar Customs Department only if importing or exporting physical goods; (5) file annual partnership returns and maintain nil or actual tax status. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible: The ELP is fiscally transparent for Gibraltar income tax purposes; profits and losses flow through to the partners. Non-resident partners are taxable in Gibraltar only on Gibraltar-source income, and no entity-level income tax is imposed on the partnership itself.

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not apply the US check-the-box election. Under domestic law, the ELP is transparent by default. Hybrid mismatch rules under OECD BEPS are incorporated into Gibraltar tax law, so an ELP that is transparent in Gibraltar but opaque in the partner's jurisdiction may be recharacterised to deny mismatched deductions. No discretionary entity-classification election is generally available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar has no general VAT or GST; indirect taxation comprises import/customs duties and selective taxes on certain services (e.g., electricity, fuel, gambling) under the Tax on Certain Services Act.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable: Gibraltar has no VAT/GST and therefore no turnover-based registration threshold for indirect taxation. Any business carrying on taxable activity must register with the Income Tax Office, and importers must comply with Gibraltar Customs from the first importation. Professional consultancy services are generally outside the scope of the selective services tax.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable: Gibraltar does not levy VAT or GST, so exports of goods or offshore services cannot be zero-rated and no input VAT refund mechanism exists. Customs duties may be relieved on goods re-exported from Gibraltar under warehousing, inward processing, or temporary admission procedures. For income tax purposes, consultancy services supplied by the ELP to non-Gibraltar clients are outside the charge to Gibraltar tax if the services are neither performed in Gibraltar nor attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment; no export VAT filing is required.

  • Customs Export Declaration (only where physical goods are exported from Gibraltar)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill (for goods exports; not applicable to pure consultancy)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 evidencing receipt of export proceeds in foreign currency
  • Executed consultancy agreement with the non-resident client
  • Service completion or market-entry roadmap reports evidencing performance outside Gibraltar
  • Bank statement showing final settlement of the service fee
  • Gibraltar Income Tax Office correspondence confirming tax status, where requested

Gibraltar has not introduced mandatory real-time electronic invoicing, fiscal clearance, API reporting, or digital stamping for consultancy services. Electronic invoices are accepted for tax record-keeping if authentic, intact, and legible. Customs declarations are transmitted electronically via the applicable Gibraltar Customs electronic system; no continuous transaction-control or fiscal-clearance mechanism applies to services.

Gibraltar law strictly penalises abuse of tax and customs relief. If an exporter falsely claims duty relief or export status and goods are diverted into domestic consumption, the Customs and Excise Duties Act imposes repayment of the relieved duty, civil surcharges up to 100% of the duty, and criminal fines or imprisonment. Misstatement of the source of consultancy fees may trigger income tax assessments on non-resident partners with interest and penalties up to 100% of the tax underpaid. Although no VAT zero-rating exists, cross-border transactions remain subject to transfer pricing, anti-avoidance, and beneficial-ownership scrutiny.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar operates a liberalised foreign exchange regime aligned with United Kingdom practices. Capital account transactions are freely convertible without prior approval or quantitative restrictions. Current account transactions, including profit remittances to non-resident partners, are permitted without prior authorisation provided full AML/CFT compliance and tax clearance are evidenced. Profit remittance hurdles are minimal for qualifying low-risk entities; no capital controls apply to standard corporate distributions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory requirement exists for mandatory surrender of export proceeds. Entities may retain foreign currency receipts in offshore accounts as required for commercial operations. Voluntary surrender to local banks is encouraged but not mandated. No fixed conversion ratios or surrender timelines are prescribed by law.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC)
