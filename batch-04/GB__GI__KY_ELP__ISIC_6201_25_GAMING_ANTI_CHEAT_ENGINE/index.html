Competent corporate registry

Companies House Gibraltar — Office of the Registrar of Companies and Limited Partnerships, HM Government of Gibraltar

Governing statute

Exempted Limited Partnerships Act 2019 (Gibraltar), Part 2 (registration, statement of particulars and registered office), read with the Limited Partnerships Act 2019 (Gibraltar) formation provisions; Companies Act 2014 (Gibraltar) applies to the corporate General Partner; Income Tax Act 2010 (Gibraltar) partnership-transparency provisions govern the pass-through taxation result.

Formation / constitutional filings

Form LP-1 (Application for Registration of a Limited Partnership) together with Form ELP-1 (Application for Registration of an Exempted Limited Partnership), comprising the Statement of Particulars, the Statement of Initial Limited Partner, and the General Partner's statutory declaration.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 14 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed on an Exempted Limited Partnership; the General Partner customarily subscribes a nominal GBP 1 contribution and the Limited Partner's contribution is fixed solely by the Partnership Agreement in cash or in kind. No escrow account or compulsory capital deposit is required at formation; if the ELP is later authorised as a private fund under the Financial Services (Alternative Investment Funds) Act 2014, subscription monies are held only by a licensed custodian once investors commit.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office (ITO); official public portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation/. HM Customs Gibraltar separately administers import, export, and excise duties.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) obtains a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN), not a VAT ID, because Gibraltar does not operate a VAT/GST system. Procedure: (1) ensure the ELP is validly registered with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies and has a registered office in Gibraltar; (2) submit the Income Tax Office registration application together with the certificate of registration, limited partnership deed, details of general and limited partners, registered office evidence, and a declaration of expected activities; (3) state whether the ELP will carry on business in Gibraltar and whether any Gibraltar-source income will arise; (4) the ITO issues the TRN. If no Gibraltar-source income arises, the ELP is generally outside the charge to Gibraltar income tax, but a TRN is routinely required for banking, EMI onboarding, invoicing platforms, and counterparty due diligence.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible – The Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership is fiscally transparent. The ELP itself is not charged to Gibraltar income tax; partners are charged individually only on their allocable share of Gibraltar-source income, and non-resident partners are within the scope of Gibraltar tax only where the ELP carries on business in Gibraltar through a taxable presence.

Available election forms

No Gibraltar 'check-the-box' election exists. Partnership transparency is statutory and mandatory under Gibraltar income tax law and limited partnership legislation. For UK tax purposes, the UK hybrid mismatch rules (TIOPA 2010 Part 6A) may recharacterise an entity only where double non-taxation arises; both Gibraltar and the UK generally treat a Gibraltar common-law partnership as transparent, so no statutory election is required. Taxpayers should document the partnership's transparency position in UK and Gibraltar tax filings and in any bank or commercial onboarding questionnaire.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or general sales and use tax in Gibraltar; indirect taxation is limited to customs import duties and excise duties. The export of kernel-level anti-cheat software middleware and related offshore services is outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable – Gibraltar has no VAT/GST registration regime and therefore imposes no economic turnover threshold for indirect tax registration. The local currency is the Gibraltar pound (GIP), pegged 1:1 to the pound sterling.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable as a Gibraltar VAT matter because Gibraltar operates no VAT/GST regime. A Gibraltar ELP selling anti-cheat software, integrity middleware, and offshore services to non-resident customers does not charge Gibraltar indirect tax and has no export VAT zero-rating claim to make. If separate UK VAT obligations arise on supplies to UK consumers, that is a UK VAT rule, not a Gibraltar export zero-rating mechanism.

  • Bill of Lading or other transport document (where applicable)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank/FX confirmation showing export consideration repatriated
  • Executed software licence or middleware service agreement identifying recipient, place of supply, and service description

None. Gibraltar does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, government-certified e-invoicing APIs, digital tax stamping, or continuous transaction controls. Commercial invoices and standard accounting records are sufficient for tax and customs purposes. Where physical goods are imported or exported, electronic customs declarations must be submitted to HM Customs Gibraltar through the applicable customs electronic entry system.

Gibraltar has no VAT zero-rating clawback because no VAT/GST system exists. However, fraudulent or negligent customs declarations seeking duty relief or export benefits can trigger HM Customs Investigations assessments, monetary penalties proportional to evaded duty, seizure or forfeiture of goods, criminal prosecution, and revocation of customs authorisations. For income tax and transfer-pricing audit purposes, the ITO may review export revenue documentation and the 180-day FX repatriation benchmark as supporting evidence of genuineness; failure to substantiate cross-border receipts can lead to adjusted profits, interest, and penalties on under-declared Gibraltar-source income.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Capital and current account transactions are fully convertible in Gibraltar; there is no exchange-control law requiring Government approval for inward capital subscriptions, dividend/profit remittances, or royalty payments. The Exchange Control Act regime was repealed in the United Kingdom in 1979 and analogous Gibraltar restrictions are not enforced. GIP is non-international in the sense that offshore counterparties generally settle in GBP, USD, EUR; local bank accounts may be held in GIP/GBP/USD/EUR without restriction. Profit remittance by the Exempted Limited Partnership to foreign partners is unrestricted, subject only to tax ruling confirmation and standard anti-money-laundering verification.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. There is no statutory timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds in Gibraltar. Receivables from the anti-cheat software licence (ISIC 6201) may be retained in the original currency (for example USD proceeds from Stripe, or EUR from EU studio customers) or converted to GIP/GBP at prevailing commercial rates. Export proceeds surrender reporting is not required, but the receiving bank may ask for invoice evidence under GFSC AML Regulations. A zero-percent mandatory conversion applies.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the licensing and AML/CFT conduct authority; no in-territory central bank exists. The Gibraltar Pound (GIP) is issued by the Government of Gibraltar and is pegged at par with GBP, with banknote convertibility ultimately backed by sterling reserves under Her Majesty's Treasury auspices; Bank of England operational settlements are used for GBP clearing.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-to-Moderate. Offshore digital EMIs authorised by the UK FCA (Wise, Revolut Business, Airwallex, etc.) generally do not passport into Gibraltar post-Brexit and will often decline an Exempted Limited Partnership whose ownership contains non-resident partners and pass-through tax status. A Gibraltar-licensed EMI or payment institution under the Financial Services Act 2019 is viable, but these institutions are onshore and require the same substance and AML files as banks. If the ELP can establish a UK branch/registered place of business, a UK EMI may board the entity, but this risks UK permanent establishment tax exposure and contradicts the offshore treasury efficiency. Therefore the preferred route is a tier-1 Gibraltar commercial bank, with Stripe as a restricted merchant acquiring rail on top of the bank account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital subscriptions from the UK/Cayman partners must arrive by SWIFT MT103, not via courier, with the ELP receiving bank and account number pre-cleared by the AML team 48 hours in advance. The remitter field must show a partner name matching the partnership register; field 72 should contain 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION UNDER LIMITED PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT DATED [DATE]; NO EXCHANGE CONTROL APPROVAL REQUIRED.' The recommended settlement currency is GBP to avoid conversion leakage; USD/EUR settlements are permitted but should be accompanied by a currency conversion order to GIP/GBP only if operating expenses require it. For UK payment reporting consistency, use the inward direct investment purpose code equivalent to BPM6 'equity and investment fund shares' (M7NE), and in UK-CHAPS interbank messages include the same code. The bank must receive at closing: executed subscription schedule, certificates of contributions, partners' source-of-wealth file, and prior board authorisation.Primary rail is SWIFT MT103 for operational trade and royalty disbursements, with the payee BIC/IBAN pre-validated and purpose codes set as 'software licence fee', 'royalty payment', or 'independent contractor remuneration' depending on recipient. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 640 million (approx. EUR 750 million) consolidated group revenue; mandatory Local File and Master File above this threshold.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to tax-resident entities undertaking relevant activities. The anti-cheat middleware business falls under 'service centre' and 'IP business'. The ELP must meet the core income generation test: be managed and directed in Gibraltar, have qualified employees, physical premises, and incur adequate operating expenditure. Failing that results in automatic denial of tax residency and substantial fines.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty: up to 100% of the tax avoided plus interest (5% per annum). Criminal sanction: unlimited fine and imprisonment up to 5 years for responsible officers.

Director personal liability: Under the Gibraltar Companies (Partnership) Act, directors and designated partners face personal liability if the evasion is attributable to their consent or neglect. Court may issue travel bans and confiscation of personal assets.
Shell Company with No Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: ESR penalty: GIP 10,000 for first year, GIP 20,000 for second, GIP 35,000 for third, and up to GIP 200,000 for repeated default; plus refusal of tax residence certificate.

Director personal liability: Directors and partners may be personally assessed for the penalties up to GIP 50,000; disgorgement of profits; cancellation of Gibraltar registration and exclusion from trading.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Tax authority may adjust profits retrospectively; penalty of 100% of the tax underpaid (minimum GIP 5,000), plus late-payment interest charged at 5% per annum.

Director personal liability: Designated partner or director can be held jointly liable for adjustment amount if they signed inaccurate transfer pricing documentation knowingly. Travel restrictions may be imposed for non-payment.
AML/Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Fine up to GIP 1,000,000; daily default fine of GIP 500; criminal prosecution for the designated partner leads to imprisonment up to 3 years.

Director personal liability: Personal criminal liability attaches to every director and designated partner who fails to take reasonable steps. Court may order civil restitution, issue asset freezing orders, and impose a 5-year travel ban.
