Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital; capital contributions are fixed solely by the Limited Partnership Agreement; no escrow account deposit requirement imposed by the Registrar; exempt ELP must pay the prescribed annual levy (approx. GBP 400) to maintain tax-exempt status.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certificate required from the Gibraltar Fire and Rescue Service for the registered office premises. Structural and load-capacity verification via the landlord's building certificate. Compliance with the Health and Safety at Work Act (Gibraltar), including a workplace risk assessment, employers' liability insurance, and certified accessibility of office premises.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital; capital contributions are fixed solely by the Limited Partnership Agreement; no escrow account deposit requirement imposed by the Registrar; exempt ELP must pay the prescribed annual levy (approx. GBP 400) to maintain tax-exempt status.

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

H.M. Government of Gibraltar — Income Tax Office (Office of the Commissioner of Income Tax), established under the Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended); official portal: (https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax). Gibraltar is a distinct tax jurisdiction within the United Kingdom sovereign framework (Composite Key: GB > GI) and is outside the UK VAT and EU customs/VAT areas.

E-government filing portal

For a Cayman Islands Exempted Limited Partnership (KY_ELP): (1) appoint a Gibraltarian tax representative/registered agent; (2) deliver to the Income Tax Office a certified copy of the ELP's Certificate of Registration issued by the Cayman Islands Registrar of Exempted Limited Partnerships, the executed Limited Partnership Agreement, and a completed foreign entity/partnership registration application; (3) obtain the allocated Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (Assessment Reference Number) and a partnership income-tax file; (4) if staff are engaged in Gibraltar, register with the Employment & Social Security Department for Social Insurance; (5) no general licence is required for ISIC 7310, but the tax representative must file annual partnership returns (nil returns if no Gibraltar-source income) to preserve the tax file. There is no minimum capital requirement and non-resident partners may hold 100% of the ELP.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible / Territorial_Scope / Tax_Exempt_Zone — For a Cayman Islands Exempted Limited Partnership (KY_ELP): (1) appoint a Gibraltarian tax representative/registered agent; (2) deliver to the Income Tax Office a certified copy of the ELP's Certificate of Registration issued by the Cayman Islands Registrar of Exempted Limited Partnerships, the executed Limited Partnership Agreement, and a completed foreign entity/partnership registration application; (3) obtain the allocated Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (Assessment Reference Number) and a partnership income-tax file; (4) if staff are engaged in Gibraltar, register with the Employment & Social Security Department for Social Insurance; (5) no general licence is required for ISIC 7310, but the tax representative must file annual partnership returns (nil returns if no Gibraltar-source income) to preserve the tax file. There is no minimum capital requirement and non-resident partners may hold 100% of the ELP.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible / Territorial_Scope / Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No Gibraltar check-the-box regime exists. The ELP relies on: (i) the Cayman Tax Concessions Law irrevocable 20-year tax-exemption undertaking upon formation (Cayman election); (ii) statutory transparency of partnerships under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010, whereby the ELP's income is computed at partnership level but assessed in the hands of partners according to their shares (pure pass-through); (iii) for any UK-resident partner, a protective election under TIOPA 2010 s.259DA (UK hybrid-mismatch election) may be made to neutralise a dual-status (transparent/non-transparent) mismatch; Gibraltar itself offers no hybrid-mismatch elections, and Gibraltarian-source income of a Cayman ELP is territorial, so offshore advertising income is exempt absent a Gibraltar trade.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT / GST / Sales and Use Tax — Gibraltar is excluded from the EU VAT area, the UK VAT area and the UK customs union for fiscal purposes; the only indirect impost is the Gibraltar Customs and Excise Duty (import duty) on physical goods entered for consumption, which is immaterial to pure digital advertising services.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no Gibraltarian turnover threshold because no VAT/GST system is in force. Caution: B2B supplies of affiliate media-buying services to EU/UK customers fall under customer reverse-charge (customer self-assesses), so no EU/UK VAT registration is required for the ELP; B2C supplies of digital services to EU end-users require the non-Union OSS scheme registration from the first euro (no threshold) in the member states of consumption, a compliance obligation of the ELP where its affiliate model generates consumer-side revenue.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is not a Gibraltarian VAT concept because Gibraltar has no VAT. Supplies of digital advertising, affiliate lead generation and media arbitrage services by the KY_ELP to non-Gibraltarian customers are treated as outside the scope of Gibraltar Customs & Excise Duty. Where a UK affiliate reseller is interposed, UK VAT zero-rating of exported advertising services (VATA 1994 Sch.8 Group 9) is available to that UK entity, and the ELP must consequently supply the UK entity with an invoices bundle evidencing that the services are wholly used for the UK entity's onward export supplies.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD/CHIEF or CDS export entry) — required only where physical media/hardware is shipped; not required for pure digital services
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill — required only for physical goods; not applicable to the affiliate media-buying services scope
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank settlement evidence (Mercury ACH, Wire Transfer, Wise statements) matching customer invoices to inbound USD/GBP/EUR receipts
  • Master Services Agreement and insertion-order/IO log evidencing the place of supply and the foreign principal's jurisdiction
  • Affiliate publisher payout ledger and lead-source substantiation (IMR/attribution reports) evidencing the services actually rendered

Not applicable in Gibraltar: no real-time fiscal clearance, no ObTiMA/Orchestra/Peppol mandate, no digital stamping, and no Making Tax Digital obligation for this territory. If the ELP voluntarily registers for VAT in an EU member state (e.g., under non-Union OSS), that member state's e-invoicing/fiscal-clearance rules may apply only to domestic B2B invoices; standard international commercial e-invoicing (PDF/XML) remains sufficient for the Cayman/Gibraltar nexus.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar operates under full current and capital account convertibility through the Gibraltar pound (GIP), pegged at par to GBP and freely exchangeable. No statutory exchange controls or profit remittance hurdles exist; however, bank-level AML/KYC due diligence on source of funds and ultimate beneficial ownership constitutes the only practical gateway for profit repatriation from the Exempted Limited Partnership to non-resident partners.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no mandatory surrender of export proceeds. All receipts may be retained in any currency, converted at will, and remitted without approval. Statutory timeline: none; mandatory conversion ratio: 0%; proceeds are fully retainable in GIP/GBP or freely convertible foreign currencies.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary policy and GBP parity oversight) and Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (banking supervision and foreign exchange licensing)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility: Medium-High with constraints. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., non-Gibraltar, non-UK FCA-regulated neobanks) are not acceptable for capital escrow or initial partnership funding due to Gibraltar's Banking Act and E-Money Regulations requiring local authorisation or an EEA passport. UK-licensed EMIs and EEA-licensed e-money institutions passporting into Gibraltar can serve as ancillary clearing rails for low-value operational float and affiliate payouts, but not as primary treasury accounts.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant wire transfer protocols use SWIFT MT103/MT202 with purpose code 'CAPC' (Capital Contribution) or 'INVS' (Investment in Securities) combined with a clear narrative: 'Capital injection to Exempted Limited Partnership — ERTF/ELP contribution'. For UK domestic, Faster Payments and CHAPS are used with payment reference matching partnership capital call notices. Verification requires source-of-funds documentation per limited partner: prior tax returns, shareholder distribution records, liquidation statements, sale/purchase contracts, signed promissory notes (if debt-over-equity), and independent bank references. The receiving bank may request a capital call certificate signed by the general partner.Primary clearing rails are SWIFT MT103 for cross-border affiliate and publisher settlements; Commercial Letters of Credit (c) for high-value physical inventory transactions, if any; UK domestic GBP payments through Faster Payments and BACS; SEPA Instant for EUR-denominated inflows and outflows; and ACH/NACHA rails through embedded banking providers (Mercury, Wise) for US-origin media buying. All outbound disbursements require appropriate purpose code, e.g., '221' (Business Travel) is avoided; codes such as 'PURG' (Purchase of digital media assets) or 'TRIP' (Service payment to non-resident) are applied together with ripened reconciliation data from affiliate dashboards.Deliverable GBP/USD forward contractsDeliverable GBP/EUR forward contractsFX swaps (GBP/USD and GBP/EUR)Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for emerging-market currencies used in targeted media inventoryCross-currency swaps limited to investment-grade counterparties, subject to ELP partnership agreement and GFSC waiver if required
