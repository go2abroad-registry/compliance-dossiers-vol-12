Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A Gibraltar registered office is mandatory for all Exempted Limited Partnerships. The Companies Act 2014 and the Limited Partnerships Act 1991 require a registered office situated in Gibraltar, which may be the office of a licensed registered office service provider. A virtual office service, where the provider supplies an address and removes/receives mail, is permissible in principle for the registered-address requirement. However, for the ISIC 3250 scope of manufacture of ultra-HD 4K minimally invasive rigid endoscopes, a virtual office alone will not satisfy prima facie commercial substance expectations under Gibraltar's economic substance rules if tax residence or any regulated license is sought; a physical operational production location, staff, and equipment must exist, whether in Gibraltar or in the offshore manufacturing jurisdiction. The ELP structure, operating in pass-through mode, is generally considered eligible to maintain a virtual registered office where it has genuine outsourced manufacturing locations outside Gibraltar, but the registered agent must document the actual global substance chain to avoid sham-office inference.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar Income Tax Office (ITO) of HM Government of Gibraltar, administered through the official HM Government of Gibraltar portal (www.gibraltar.gov.gi, Income Tax Office section); HM Customs (Gibraltar) exercises customs and excise functions; the Registrar of Companies operates the Gibraltar Companies Registry for entity registration.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Register the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) with the Gibraltar Companies Registry under the Limited Partnerships Act 2019 and obtain the partnership registration number. Step 2: File the registered office address and the identity of the Gibraltar-resident general partner/registered agent. Step 3: Apply to the Gibraltar Income Tax Office (ITO) for a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN/assessment number) using the ITO registration form for partnerships, attaching the Companies Registry certificate, the partnership agreement, and the names, addresses, and tax residences of the general and limited partners. Step 4: Where the ELP is non-resident owned and carries on no trade in Gibraltar, submit the ITO non-residence declaration/clearance form so that the ITO records a nil/declaratory tax file. Step 5: Register with HM Customs (Gibraltar) for a customs EORI number before any importation/exportation of the laparoscopy towers. Step 6: If the ELP employs staff in Gibraltar, register for PAYE. The ELP remains outside HMRC and UK VAT registration because Gibraltar is a distinct tax jurisdiction; UK registration is triggered only if a UK branch/permanent establishment is created.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible — the Gibraltar ELP is statutorily tax-transparent under the Income Tax Act 2010: the partnership itself is not a taxable person, files a nil/declaratory return, and each partner is assessed in his own hands on his share of Gibraltar-source profits; the territorial scope rule means a non-resident partner with no Gibraltar-source income has no Gibraltar income tax liability, and the ELP has no entity-level tax exposure so long as it is not carrying on a trade in Gibraltar through a permanent establishment.

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no US-style check-the-box election; partnership pass-through treatment is mandatory and cannot be waived. The material statutory mechanisms are: (i) the gross-assessable-income alternative in the Income Tax Act, under which a qualifying company is charged by reference to a percentage of gross revenue rather than computed profits; (ii) Qualifying Company status for non-resident-owned companies that do not trade in Gibraltar; (iii) the ITO non-residence clearance election available to partnerships and their partners; and (iv) post-BEPS anti-hybrid and General Anti-Abuse Rule (GAAR) provisions that override transactions designed to create double non-taxation. No hybrid-mismatch election is available to the ELP other than ensuring that its structure does not trigger the anti-hybrid rules of the UK or another counterparty jurisdiction.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None_No_VAT_GST — Gibraltar does not impose Value Added Tax, Goods and Services Tax, or any general sales and use tax; the only indirect impositions are customs and excise duties levied at importation by HM Customs (Gibraltar), with rates aligned, outside the EU Customs Union, to the UK's external tariff structure where applicable.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — there is no VAT/GST registration threshold because no VAT/GST is levied; the only mandatory indirect-tax infrastructure registration is a customs EORI number obtained from HM Customs (Gibraltar), which is required for any commercial import/export movement regardless of the ELP's turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in VAT terms because Gibraltar has no VAT to zero-rate. At customs level, exports of the 4K surgical laparoscopy towers from Gibraltar to foreign destinations are outside the Gibraltar tariff and attract no Gibraltar export duty. To preserve this zero-duty export treatment and to demonstrate that no Gibraltar domestic supply has occurred, the ELP must evidence: (i) physical export of the goods from Gibraltar with a lodged customs export declaration; (ii) transfer of title and risk to the overseas buyer; and (iii) receipt of the sale consideration into a non-Gibraltar bank account or another documented form of payment. Import VAT and customs duty in the destination state (e.g., UK import VAT, EU import VAT and the Common Customs Tariff) are the obligation of the importer of record, not the Gibraltar ELP.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document) lodged with HM Customs Gibraltar
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical export from Gibraltar
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank SWIFT MT103 or TT credit advice) evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Certificate of Origin and CE Declaration of Conformity / technical file summary for the 4K laparoscopy tower

Not applicable — Gibraltar operates no real-time fiscal clearance system, no mandatory B2B/B2G e-invoicing platform, no digital stamping, and no continuous transaction controls. The ITO nevertheless requires complete commercial records, serialized invoices and proof of export in accordance with Income Tax Act record-keeping rules; the ELP should maintain its medical-device commercial files and CE technical documentation as supporting evidence.

Because Gibraltar has no VAT, there is no VAT zero-rating refund or clawback mechanism; instead, HM Customs (Gibraltar) and the ITO exercise scrutiny. HM Customs may audit export declarations and recover duty or value by forfeiture or penalty where export evidence is false; penalties can be severe, including goods forfeiture, monetary penalties of up to three times the duty/value evaded, cancellation of EORI status, and criminal prosecution under the Customs and Excise Act. The ITO may reopen assessments within the statutory assessment window (or without limit for fraud or gross negligence), and misdeclared export turnover attracts additional assessments, statutory interest, and penalties of up to 100% of the tax under-assessed (or higher where deliberate fraud is established), with referral for revenue-fraud prosecution by the Attorney General's Chambers. All export documentation must be retained for at least six years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar maintains no exchange-control regime. The Gibraltar pound is pegged one-for-one to the British pound and is freely convertible. Current-account payments, capital-account transfers, profit/dividend remittances, loan repayments, and partnership capital distributions are unrestricted and do not require central-bank consent. Convertibility is not administratively constrained, but all settlement flows remain subject to GFSC AML/CFT obligations, sanctions screening, and source-of-funds verification by the receiving bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory export-proceeds surrender rule and no statutory conversion ratio apply to Gibraltar entities. The ELP may retain export proceeds in foreign currency, maintain multi-currency bank accounts, or convert receipts at commercial rates without a prescribed repatriation timeline. However, all trade-related receipts must be supported by commercial invoices, transport documents, customs declarations, and internal accounting records satisfying Gibraltar anti-money-laundering and customs requirements.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (Prudential Regulation Authority) as the UK monetary and prudential authority; H.M. Government of Gibraltar as note-issuing authority for the Gibraltar pound; Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the integrated regulator of Gibraltar-licensed banks and foreign-exchange businesses.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility: Medium as a secondary collection and disbursement channel; Low as the primary treasury or clearing channel. Offshore digital EMIs licensed under the UK Electronic Money Regulations 2011 or under reputable EEA e-money regimes may offer faster account opening for customer receivables, distributor rebates, and smaller operational expenses. Legal constraints are significant: EMIs cannot issue commercial letters of credit, cannot accept capital escrow as regulated deposits, and cannot provide the same SWIFT MT103 clearing assurance as a GFSC-licensed bank. The ELP should keep the capital escrow account, trade-clearing account, and any FX hedging relationship at a regulated UK or Gibraltar bank, with the EMI limited to low-value receivables and a documented aggregate balance cap.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: A Gibraltar Local File is required when aggregate controlled transactions attributable to the ELP (or a partner's Gibraltar PE) exceed £1,000,000 in the accounting period. A Master File is required for a multinational group with consolidated revenue of £200,000,000 or more. Transactions below £1,000,000 are exempt from contemporaneous documentation.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance (Companies and Partnerships) Act 2018 applies to any Gibraltar tax-resident entity carrying on a 'relevant activity' (banking, insurance, shipping, fund management, financing/leasing, HQ, distribution/service center, and IP). Manufacturing of surgical endoscopes is not a prescribed relevant activity, so the ELP is not subject to the full economic substance test; however, if the ELP operates from Gibraltar it must maintain adequate staff, premises, and expenditure. Non-resident partners with no PE trigger no substance requirement.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (intentional suppression of taxable profits)

Statutory sanction: A fine of up to £500,000 on the partnership; on conviction in the Gibraltar Supreme Court, imprisonment for up to 7 years for the general partner or responsible partner; further penalty equal to 100% of evaded tax; default interest at 12% per annum.

Director personal liability: The court may lift the partnership veil and hold every participating partner jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax. Travel restriction orders and asset seizure are routinely applied, and the general partner may be disqualified from managing entities in Gibraltar for 10 years.
Sham Partnership / Shell Company (abuse of limited partnership form)

Statutory sanction: Revocation of the ELP's transparent/exempt status; immediate assessment of all worldwide profits at 12.5%; penalty of up to 150% of tax avoided; referral for criminal investigation of related foreign tax evasion.

Director personal liability: The general partner is personally liable for the entire assessed tax as the partnership is treated as a mere agent. Personal civil liability of up to twice the tax undercharged may be imposed; travel bans are issued until full restitution.
Transfer Pricing Misdeclaration / Unsupported Intercompany Pricing

Statutory sanction: The Gibraltar Commissioner will adjust profits under the Income Tax Act 2010 s.29; a 50% penalty on the underpayment (up to 150% for deliberate failure) and interest at 12% per annum.

Director personal liability: Where artificial arrangements are orchestrated by a named partner/director, the corporate veil is pierced; director disqualification and personal restitution orders are made; in cross-border cases extradition may be pursued.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Non-Disclosure

Statutory sanction: Civil fine of £50,000 per year of non-compliance; cancellation of the ELP's registration under the Limited Partnerships Act; criminal penalties include imprisonment for up to 5 years for the general partner.

Director personal liability: The general partner and any compliance officer are jointly and severally liable for the fine. Failure to implement adequate AML/CFT procedures gives rise to a private right of action for creditors, and asset freezing orders may be imposed.
