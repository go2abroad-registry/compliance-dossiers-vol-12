Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office, acting through the Commissioner of Income Tax (official taxpayer portal: https://www.taxation.gi); HM Customs and Excise, Gibraltar, is the competent authority for customs, excise and export-control registrations.

E-government filing portal

An Exempted Limited Partnership comprised of foreign partners is fiscally transparent. No Gibraltar TIN/VAT ID is required unless the ELP carries on a taxable activity in Gibraltar or employs staff in Gibraltar. If a taxable presence arises, the ELP must appoint a Gibraltar-resident tax representative or registered agent and file the partnership particulars with the Registrar of Companies; the Income Tax Office then issues a TIN in the name of the ELP as agent and each assessable partner. Customs-facing foreign ELPs obtain a Gibraltar customs EORI number by filing Form EORI-1 with proof of identity, formation certificate, registered office evidence and non-resident partner declarations. No VAT ID can be issued because Gibraltar imposes no VAT/GST.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — An Exempted Limited Partnership comprised of foreign partners is fiscally transparent. No Gibraltar TIN/VAT ID is required unless the ELP carries on a taxable activity in Gibraltar or employs staff in Gibraltar. If a taxable presence arises, the ELP must appoint a Gibraltar-resident tax representative or registered agent and file the partnership particulars with the Registrar of Companies; the Income Tax Office then issues a TIN in the name of the ELP as agent and each assessable partner. Customs-facing foreign ELPs obtain a Gibraltar customs EORI number by filing Form EORI-1 with proof of identity, formation certificate, registered office evidence and non-resident partner declarations. No VAT ID can be issued because Gibraltar imposes no VAT/GST.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a check-the-box election. The ELP is statutorily tax-transparent; profits are allocated to the partners under the partnership deed and assessed on them only if those profits arise in Gibraltar or are attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment. A partner may, outside Gibraltar, make an entity-classification election in its own tax jurisdiction, for example a US Form 8832 election; Gibraltar hybrid-mismatch rules under the Income Tax Act will then restrict double-non-taxation or deduction-without-inclusion outcomes. The Limited Partnerships Act election to register as a private fund or exempted limited partnership is legal only and does not change tax transparency.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales-and-Use Tax; indirect taxation is limited to Gibraltar customs and excise duties imposed at importation and on manufactured goods.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable: no turnover-based indirect-tax registration threshold exists in Gibraltar. A customs EORI is required for all commercial imports/exports; de minimis and low-value consignment thresholds apply only to relief from customs declaration procedures, not to income-tax/GST registration. The local currency is the Gibraltar pound, pegged at parity with GBP.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar has no VAT zero-rating or VAT refund regime. The equivalent benefit is total exemption or duty relief on physical exportation from the Gibraltar customs territory: goods must leave Gibraltar to a third territory, the exporter must hold a valid customs export declaration, and the supply must be a genuine freight-forwarding or logistics service to an offshore destination. The ELP must be the lawful exporter or co-loader on the shipping records; supplying only re-invoicing or call-centre services without control of the goods will not support export relief.

  • Customs Export Declaration with Gibraltar entry/export reference and customs clearance validation
  • Bill of Lading or equivalent Air Waybill / CMR transport document for each leg
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103 showing export proceeds received by the ELP within 180 days
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Proof of export/arrival, including customs exit stamp, port/airport receipt or foreign customs entry proof
  • Pharmaceutical GDP consignment file, including shipper's letter of instruction, temperature-excursion record, qualified-person or GDP compliance certificate
  • Valid general or individual pharmaceutical export authorisation or marketing-authorisation evidence for the drug products handled

No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping or B2B e-invoicing mandate applies. Electronic invoices are acceptable for income-tax substantiation if secured and auditable. Goods declarations and export clearances must be lodged electronically through the Gibraltar Customs single-window/electronic customs clearance system; a customs validation and unique movement reference is generated before goods are released for exit. APIs for third-party customs software are available but are not mandatory for a low-volume GDP freight validator.

Because no VAT zero-rating exists, any claim presented as a VAT zero-rating or refund is a false claim and will be prosecuted as tax fraud. For customs duty relief, post-clearance audit may claw back any duty relief where export is not substantiated, together with statutory interest, customs penalties geared to lost duty, monetary penalties up to the amount of duty or tax evaded, forfeiture of goods and means of transport, revocation of the GDP logistics licence, and criminal liability for the persons who signed the declaration. Inward remittances failing the 180-day deadline or being converted to non-trade balances will rebut the export presumption and trigger full duty and penalty assessment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The United Kingdom and Gibraltar operate under a currency board system pegged to the Pound Sterling, providing full capital account convertibility. Current account transactions, including profit remittances from offshore structures such as Exempted Limited Partnerships, are freely convertible with no significant regulatory hurdles. Post-Brexit, UK entities benefit from streamlined FX processes, while Cayman Islands structures maintain USD pegged convertibility via the Cayman Islands Dollar. Profit remittance to non-resident partners is permitted subject to tax filings and beneficial ownership verification.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender of export proceeds required for GBP transactions. Export receipts must be repatriated within statutory timelines, typically 30 days for reporting to tax authorities. No mandatory conversion ratios apply as the currency is freely tradable. Voluntary surrender to central bank possible but not obligatory for compliant entities.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Digital EMIs are feasible for GBP transactions with high viability in low-risk pharma logistics. Legal constraints include adherence to Gibraltar FSC and Cayman CIMA regulations, plus AML/KYC for cross-border flows. Offshore digital wallets require robust compliance programs to avoid sanctions screening failures.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory monetary threshold is prescribed for mandatory Local File or Master File preparation; however, the Income Tax Office expects contemporaneous arm's length documentation for all controlled transactions, and tax audits typically require a transfer pricing analysis where aggregate related-party transactions exceed GIP 1,000,000 per accounting period.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to relevant activities including distribution, transportation and logistics under ISIC 5229. The ELP must demonstrate a physical presence in Gibraltar, full-time staff, core income-generating activities performed locally, and adequate records. Failure to comply attracts penalty of GBP 10,000 per relevant activity, daily penalties for continued default, and potential strike-off and information exchange.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Tax Return

Statutory sanction: Penalty up to GBP 5,000 for deliberate inaccuracy plus 100% of the additional tax evaded; criminal prosecution for fraudulent evasion carrying imprisonment of up to 7 years.

Director personal liability: Directors or managing partners who authorize or participate in fraudulent misdeclarations are jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax and penalties; courts may issue travel bans, disqualification orders, and custodial sentences up to 7 years.
Economic Substance Failure / Shell Company Abuse

Statutory sanction: Penalty of GBP 10,000 per relevant activity, daily escalation up to GBP 500, mandatory exchange of information with tax authorities, and strike-off from the Gibraltar Register if unresolved.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held personally liable for penalties and compliance failure costs; a court can make a disqualification order prohibiting management of any Gibraltar entity for up to 15 years, and the Financial Intelligence Unit may impose travel bans.
AML Compliance Default / Misrepresentation of Beneficial Ownership

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of up to GBP 5,000 per breach under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015; criminal fines and imprisonment of up to 14 years for money laundering, plus asset forfeiture proceedings.

Director personal liability: Directors involved in money laundering or predicate tax offences can be personally disgorged of profits, have travel restricted pending investigation, and may be imprisoned for up to 14 years even where the partnership form is used to veil control.
