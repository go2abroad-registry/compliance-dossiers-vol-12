Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar Income Tax Office (Income Tax Act 2010) is the competent tax authority for income and corporate tax matters; HM Customs Gibraltar administers customs, excise and trade controls. The official portals are www.tax.gov.gi and the Gibraltar Companies House portal at www.companieshouse.gi.

E-government filing portal

A Cayman Exempted Limited Partnership is not a domestic Gibraltar statutory entity, so it must first be registered as an external/foreign partnership with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies. The registrar will reserve the name and issue a Gibraltar registration number after submission of the Cayman certificate of registration, exemption certificate, partnership agreement, notarised constitutional documents, and the identity and address details of the general partner. After registration, the partnership must file the prescribed Income Tax Office registration form to obtain its Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN), disclosing the place of effective management, Gibraltar principal address, territorial source of income, and partner details. Non-resident partners do not require a Gibraltar TRN unless they are separately chargeable. A non-resident ELP with no Gibraltar permanent establishment and no Gibraltar-source income has no active Income Tax Office registration requirement beyond the foreign partnership registration with the Companies House.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; partnership income, gains and losses are attributed to the partners under Gibraltar income tax principles. The charge to Gibraltar tax is territorial-scope limited: only income arising in Gibraltar, or income derived from activities carried on through a Gibraltar permanent establishment, is within the tax net.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election is available in Gibraltar. Limited partnerships are fiscally transparent by default and cannot unilaterally elect entity-level taxation, although an entity that is separately incorporated or that obtains body-corporate status may fall within corporate taxation rules. Gibraltar applies domestic anti-avoidance provisions and OECD hybrid-mismatch-analysis principles where inconsistent tax treatment arises between Gibraltar and the United Kingdom or the Cayman Islands.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No Value Added Tax, goods and services tax, or general sales and use tax applies in Gibraltar. Gibraltar is outside the UK/EU VAT territory; indirect taxation is limited to customs duties and excise duties under Gibraltar customs and excise legislation.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A — there is no VAT-registration threshold because Gibraltar does not operate VAT. A person importing commercial goods or dealing in excise goods must register with HM Customs Gibraltar and obtain a customs economic operator reference number from the first import regardless of transaction value; de minimis concessions may apply only to expressly exempted low-value consignments.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — zero-rated export VAT is not a concept available in Gibraltar because no VAT is imposed. No VAT refund is claimable upon export and no output tax is charged. Relief from customs or excise duty may be available for goods placed under temporary export, external transit, outward processing, or similar duty-suspension arrangements, provided the goods physically depart Gibraltar within the prescribed period and the exporter substantiates the exportation.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration and approved departure certificate
  • Commercial invoice and packing list describing the goods and export classification
  • Transport document evidencing movement outside Gibraltar (Bill of Lading, Sea Waybill, or Air Waybill)
  • Customs duty or excise waiver, end-use authorisation, or duty-relief authorisation where relief is claimed
  • Bank or payment-rail evidence of export proceeds, including an inward remittance certificate or equivalent, where foreign-exchange repatriation is required or contractually stipulated
  • Proof of the applicant's Gibraltar Tax Reference Number and customs registration reference

No real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing clearance, API stamping, or digital stamping regime applies to commercial invoices in Gibraltar. Electronic invoices are recognised for recordkeeping if they preserve authenticity, integrity, and legibility and contain the supplier’s Gibraltar TRN and the information required under normal tax record-keeping rules. Customs declarations must be lodged electronically through the Gibraltar customs clearance system.

Because no VAT zero-rating exists, customs-duty relief claims are closely scrutinised by HM Customs Gibraltar. Penalties for false declarations, diversion of goods inland, or unproven export include forfeiture or seizure of goods, monetary penalties of up to 100% of the duty or trade value, retrospective clawback of any wrongly obtained relief with interest, and criminal prosecution for fraudulent conduct. For a digital crypto-tax-accounting SaaS service there is no VAT clawback risk, but incorrect export documentation may nonetheless lead to customs penalties and reputational regulatory consequences.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar operates a liberalised exchange-control regime with full convertibility for both current account and capital account transactions. Inward investment, capital injections, and outward profit remittances do not require prior exchange-control approval. Practical friction arises from bank anti-money-laundering, sanctions screening, and source-of-wealth obligations rather than from statutory currency convertibility limits. Profit or capital distributions to non-resident partners are not subject to exchange-control hurdles, provided the partnership maintains clear capital accounts, audited financial statements, and documentary evidence of the economic substance of each distribution.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar law imposes no export-proceeds surrender obligation and no mandatory conversion ratio. Export receipts may be retained in any freely convertible currency, held in onshore bank accounts, or used offshore. The only binding requirements are record-keeping and reporting obligations imposed under AML/CFT legislation, requiring the underlying trade invoices, proof of receipt, and reconciliation against the relevant corporate bank account.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar does not maintain a separate central bank. The monetary anchor is the Bank of England as issuer of sterling, with the Gibraltar pound exchangeable at par. The Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) functions as the statutory regulator for banking, payments, and financial services within Gibraltar.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium for operational floats and Low as a substitute for licensed banking. Digital EMIs incorporated outside Gibraltar may provide faster remote onboarding, multi-currency virtual IBANs, and card acceptance, but they are not licensed banks and cannot accept deposits, extend credit, or provide protected client money custody. Offshore EMIs are generally unsuitable for capital escrow or settlement of tax-related disbursements because GFSC-regulated correspondent banks may reject EMI-sourced funds as high-risk. Use of an offshore digital EMI should be limited to low-balance operating expenses and must be supported by separate bank records and a licensed Gibraltar payment institution or UK/EEA-authorised EMI if customers pay in USDC or card rails.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital contributions should be routed by SWIFT MT103 or MT202 from the parent company, a UK regulated financial institution, or a partner's verified bank account into the ELP's Gibraltar Tier-1 operational or escrow account. The payment must carry a clear remittance information field structured as KYELP-CAP-YYYYMMDD and the appropriate ISO 20022 purpose code for capital contribution (normally EQUI or CAPI as accepted by the receiving bank). Pre-clearing documentation includes the ELP deed, executed subscription schedule, source-of-funds questionnaire, UBO chart, FATCA and CRS self-certifications, and a written capital-account instruction to the bank.Disbursements for trade settlements, professional fees, software infrastructure, and partner distributions are executed over SWIFT MT103 with a global payment-innovation overlay for track-and-trace; GBP payments are cleared through UK CHAPS/Faster Payments via a UK settlement account; EUR payments through TARGET2 via the bank's EU correspondent network; USD payments through Fedwire/CHIPS. Commercial letters of credit may be used where a counterparty requires documentary title or performance security, though this structure is unlikely to be needed for a SaaS digital-asset tax bookkeeping application. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GBP 1,000,000 - contemporaneous Local File required where aggregate cross-border controlled transactions exceed GBP 1 million; a Master File is required where the consolidated group revenue exceeds GBP 50 million or where the transaction involves a low-taxed IP holding entity. Scope of required documentation: functional analysis, benchmark study, transfer pricing policy, and annual TP declaration; penalty for non-compliance is 10% of the adjusted profit, plus deficiency interest and potential criminal sanctions for fraudulent records.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to every Gibraltar tax resident entity and non-resident entity with Gibraltar-source income carrying on a relevant activity: banking, insurance, fund management, financing/leasing, headquarters, shipping, intellectual property holding, distribution, and service centre. A pure equity holding company and a family office function may be subject to reduced substance requirements. The crypto tax SaaS application does not inherently constitute a relevant activity, unless it is contractually structured as a distribution and service centre for a parent or affiliate, in which case the ELP must demonstrate physical presence, employees, expenditure, and direction and control in Gibraltar. Failure to satisfy ESR results in financial penalties, a declaration of non-compliance, or the entity being struck off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Recovery of the full underpaid tax, interest at up to 9% per annum, a tax-geared penalty of 100% of the tax understated, and criminal liability for fraudulent evasion of income tax. Conviction may result in a fine of up to GBP 30,000 and imprisonment for up to 7 years.

Director personal liability: The Commissioner can lift the ELP veil where the general partner or beneficial partner exercises control. Directors and the general partner may incur joint and several civil liability for the limited partnership's tax arrears if assets were extracted by way of unauthorised distribution or on a sham basis; a travel ban can be imposed on the responsible individual, and expatriation of the founder is prohibited pending settlement.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Obligation to file under a substantive residence test; denial of treaty benefits; up to GBP 10,000 per failure under the Economic Substance Regulations; referral to the Registrar of Companies for strike-off; the entity may be reclassified as UK tax resident under domestic tie-breaker and subjected to UK corporation tax at 25%.

Director personal liability: Directors of the general partner or the UBEO are liable to civil penalties for allowing the ELP to continue to trade without economic substance. A civil recovery order may be made for the entire deferred tax, plus interest, and the directors may be disqualified for up to 15 years.
AML / Sanctions Violation

Statutory sanction: Civil penalties up to GBP 2,000,000 or 5% of annual worldwide turnover, plus unlimited fines on conviction; confiscation orders; public reprimands; suspension of the financial establishment licence; criminal liability for money laundering activities with imprisonment up to 14 years.

Director personal liability: Individual officers and general partners face criminal liability where they consented to or were wilfully blind to AML breaches. Travel bans and freezing orders against their personal assets (including crypto assets) are routinely granted; the corporate veil is pierced where non-resident partners actively direct the compliance failures.
Transfer Pricing Misstatement

Statutory sanction: Transfer pricing adjustment to the arm's-length consideration, additional tax up to 10% of the adjusted amount, and a 20% documentation penalty under the Income Tax (Transfer Pricing) Regulations if the required Local File or Master File is not produced. Repeat non-compliance can lead to an assessment based on anti-avoidance provisions, plus interest from the original tax due date.

Director personal liability: General partners who sign or cause the TP misstatement are liable for the additional tax as 'responsible officers' if they knew or should have known that the pricing was unsupported. Civil proceedings may recover the adjusted tax and penalties from the individual; in egregious cases the court will order sequestration of the partner's on-chain assets and impose a custodial sentence for perjury in relation to the submitted TP declaration.
