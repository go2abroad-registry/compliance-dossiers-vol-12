Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office (ITO) of HM Government of Gibraltar, acting through the Commissioner of Income Tax, part of the Ministry of Finance; registrations and filings are submitted via the Gibraltar tax e-services portal (gibraltar.gov.gi) and customs declarations through the ASYCUDA World single-window.

E-government filing portal

A non-resident investor forming a Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) must: (1) file the partnership registration statement with the Registrar of Companies (Companies House Gibraltar) under the Limited Partnerships Act 2011, through a licensed registered agent, and submit a statutory declaration that the partnership qualifies for exempted status; (2) after receiving the partnership registration number, apply to the Commissioner of Income Tax on the prescribed taxpayer registration application, attaching the registration certificate, the partnership agreement (LPA), the register of partners, and the identity/KYC documentation of the general and limited partners; (3) obtain the ELP's own Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN) and a TIN or confirmed exemption certificate for each partner; (4) register the beneficial ownership particulars of all ultimate beneficial owners in the Gibraltar Beneficial Ownership Register maintained by the Registrar; and (5) maintain a Gibraltar-registered office and a resident tax representative or registered agent who will receive official correspondence and file the annual partnership return. Physical presence in Gibraltar is not required. Registration is completed within 10 to 15 working days on a standard non-expedited filing.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Gibraltar law contains no statutory 'check-the-box' election like the United States. The Income Tax Act 2010 and the Limited Partnerships Act 2011 provide that an ELP is fiscally transparent by default: the partnership itself is not chargeable to Gibraltar income tax; profits are computed at partnership level under the normal income tax rules and then assessed on each general and limited partner in their share. The Commissioner of Income Tax may, however, by written agreement, accept entity-level characterisation where a double taxation treaty requires the partnership to be treated as a taxable resident person in Gibraltar, provided this does not create a hybrid mismatch. Partners may further elect to settle the tax due on non-residence-derived profits by way of a fixed withholding or deduction-at-source arrangement, and an ELP carrying on a collective investment scheme may elect into the qualifying fund regime. Any election or structuring step causing a deduction without a corresponding income inclusion in another jurisdiction is disregarded under the Gibraltar hybrid mismatch rules aligned with the OECD BEPS and EU ATAD framework.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT and no GST or general sales tax in Gibraltar. Gibraltar is excluded from the EU VAT territory, so supplies of goods and services, including the sale of robotic AI pet toys, are not subject to Gibraltar VAT/GST. The only indirect taxes are Import Duty (Gibraltar Tariff, aligned with the EU/UK Common Customs Tariff nomenclature) and Excise Duty on alcohol, tobacco and hydrocarbon oils. Services and digital supplies are wholly outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect taxation.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT/GST registration threshold exists in Gibraltar because no consumption tax is levied. For import duty purposes, a regular importer must obtain an economic operator licence from HM Customs; casual, low-value consignments benefit from a de minimis duty-free allowance, and goods of UK origin imported directly from the United Kingdom are relieved from duty under the UK-Gibraltar customs arrangements.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable as a recoverable claim. Because Gibraltar does not operate a VAT system, there is no zero-rating machinery and no input VAT refund procedure. Exports of AI pet robot hardware from Gibraltar into a foreign market leave the scope of Gibraltar taxation by operation of law, without the need for a registered exporter to file for a zero rate. Where the customer is in the EU or the UK, the relevant import VAT is accounted for in the destination jurisdiction (for example, postponed VAT accounting in the UK or reverse charge in the EU depending on the buyer). The Gibraltar ELP is neither liable to add nor entitled to reclaim Gibraltar VAT on such transactions, as no Gibraltar VAT registration number is issued or exists.

  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill issued by the carrier
  • Commercial invoice fully describing the AI pet robot, quantity, sale price and applicable harmonised system classification
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by a Gibraltar-licensed bank confirmation that the export consideration has been received in Gibraltar from the foreign buyer

There is no electronic invoicing mandate, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping regime in Gibraltar. The operative electronic control is customs clearance: goods imported into Gibraltar must be declared through the ASYCUDA World platform (web or EDI/API) before arrival, with duty assessed and settled electronically prior to release. The customs declaration reference and release number must be quoted on the commercial invoice and retained with the accounting records for reconciliation with HM Customs.

Although no VAT zero-rating claim can be fabricated in Gibraltar, the Customs Duties Act imposes severe sanctions on false export or import declarations, duty evasion, and the misuse of duty reliefs: civil penalties of up to three times the evaded duty, forfeiture of the goods, suspension or revocation of the importer/exporter licence, and on conviction on indictment fines and imprisonment of up to seven years. Separately, under the Income Tax Act 2010, an exporter that misstates sales invoices or misdirects export proceeds to a foreign shell entity to avoid Gibraltar tax is liable to interest at 7% per annum, a penalty up to the amount of the tax undercharged, and retrospective clawback of reliefs or treaty benefits for all open tax years, being not less than five years from the date the assessment is raised. Failure to repatriate export proceeds within the 180-day period is treated as an indicator of suppressed turnover and can itself trigger a best-of-judgment assessment on the ELP's partners, plus AML reporting to the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar operates a fully liberalised currency system. The Gibraltar Pound is freely convertible at fixed 1:1 parity with GBP, with no separate convertibility controls on either current or capital account transactions. Both trade-related current account payments and capital account flows (equity injections, partnership distributions, loan repayments) are unrestricted and require no prior exchange-control approval. Profit remittance and partnership distributions to the ultimate Cayman Islands partner are subject to no Gibraltar withholding tax, no dividend tax, and no beneficial-ownership reporting gateway; the only practical hurdles are bank-level source-of-funds verification, CRS/FATCA self-certifications, and documentary evidence of economic purpose. Because GIP/GBP are interchangeable at par, the ELP's residual FX exposure is effectively GBP-based, while USD and EUR exposures arising from robotics trade flows are fully hedgeable through onshore and offshore deliverable and non-deliverable markets.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Gibraltar imposes no statutory obligation to surrender export proceeds, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation timeline for foreign-currency receipts. The ELP may retain, convert, or reinvest export collections (including Stripe card settlement batched in GBP, EUR, and USD) at its discretion. The only compliance duties are accurate accounting records, POCA 2015 record-keeping for a minimum of five years, and availability of underlying commercial documentation to evidence the genuine trade substance of all inward receipts.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (sovereign monetary authority for sterling), Government of Gibraltar (issuer of Gibraltar Pound banknotes at statutory parity of GIP 1 = GBP 1), and Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) (integrated prudential, conduct, and AML/CFT supervisor for licensed banks and payment/e-money institutions; no exchange-control statute exists)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM — permitted strictly as a secondary collections/settlement rail, never as the primary treasury repository. UK-authorised EMIs (Stripe, Wise, Revolut Business) can provide GBP IBANs and route payouts by SWIFT to the Gibraltar bank account, but the UK post-Brexit passport terminated, so a UK EMI cannot provide regulated payment services into Gibraltar without GFSC authorisation or an agent arrangement. E-money balances are not covered by the Gibraltar Deposit Guarantee Scheme, and Stripe does not list Gibraltar as a supported country. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% flat rate on assessable profits of Gibraltar-resident companies; the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is fiscally transparent and taxable only at partner level.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% – Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on dividends or on profit distributions made by an Exempted Limited Partnership, irrespective of the residence of the recipient partner.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The return is due on or before the later of 30 June following the close of the YoA or six months after the end of the ELP's accounting period, unless the Commissioner of Income Tax grants a written extension. Self-assessed tax is payable within 30 days of the notice of assessment; instalment arrangements are available only with prior written approval.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: The Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended) and the Transfer Pricing Regulations 2019 apply to controlled transactions between associated enterprises; no de minimis threshold is codified in Gibraltarian legislation. In practice, contemporaneous Local File and Master File documentation aligned with OECD BEPS Action 13 is mandatory for ELPs belonging to a multinational group whose consolidated revenue exceeds EUR 750 million. Smaller taxpayers must retain a functional analysis, a budgeted cost sheet, and contractual evidence proportionate to the controlled transactions; the Commissioner may require documentation where annual controlled turnover exceeds a commercially material level (approximately GIP 1,000,000 per connected counterparty).

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar applies OECD-consistent Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) to entities and partnerships carrying on 'relevant activities': banking, insurance, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, shipping, holding-company, intellectual-property exploitation, and distribution/service centres. The assembly of interactive AI pet robots is a manufacturing activity and is not per se a relevant activity; however, where the ELP derives income from licensing the embedded AI speech-synthesizer software or operates a distribution hub, it must satisfy the full substance test: direction and management in Gibraltar, adequate premises and employees, and core income-generating activities performed locally (nexus approach). The corporate general partner must demonstrate a payroll and physical presence proportionate to the activity, and all board decisions must be taken in Gibraltar with minutes retained at the registered office.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Returns

Statutory sanction: Penalty of up to 200% of the under-assessed tax; daily surcharges; criminal fines and imprisonment pursuant to the Income Tax Act 2010 and the Criminal Code; forfeiture of connected proceeds; publication of the taxpayer's identity in the Official Gazette.

Director personal liability: Directors of the corporate general partner and limited partners who participate in management face joint and several liability for the penalty and unpaid tax, director disqualification orders, civil restitution proceedings, court-imposed travel bans and surrender of passports, and custodial sentences for fraudulent evasion.
Shell Company / Artificial Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Denial or withdrawal of the Tax Residence Certificate, refusal of DTA benefits, reassessment of profits on a 12.5% entity basis, strike-off from the Register of Partnerships, and voidance of artificial tax avoidance arrangements under general anti-abuse rules; the Commissioner may also impose a penalty equal to the tax advantage obtained.

Director personal liability: The general partner's directors risk personal civil liability for the tax debts of the partnership where they have knowingly permitted the artificial structure; asset-freezing injunctions, travel bans, and disqualification orders may be issued during the investigation; limited partners who take part in management forfeit the statutory limitation of liability and become personally liable for all partnership debts.
AML / KYC and Record-Keeping Breach

Statutory sanction: Fines for each day of default, aggregate penalties up to GIP 500,000, suspension or cancellation of the AML registration of the corporate general partner, confiscation orders under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015, and loss of the privilege to maintain a Gibraltar bank account (destroying the Stripe / credit-card settlement rails).

Director personal liability: Directors who approved transactions without CDD or who laundered proceeds face criminal prosecution, civil confiscation of personal assets, travel bans pending extradition, and imprisonment; the AML supervisory authority may also seek a director-level prohibition order.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance and Improper Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: Reassessment on an arm's-length basis with upward adjustment to Gibraltar profits; penalty of up to 100% of the resulting tax shortfall; late-payment interest at the statutory rate; and denial of the corresponding deduction at the distributor level where the arrangement is characterised as a sham.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held personally liable for the tax shortfall where the corporate general partner is stripped of assets or where the directors executed fictitious transfer pricing agreements; joint and several liability follows fraudulent trading and misfeasance under the Gibraltar Companies Act 2014.
Wrongful Trading and Insolvency of the General Partner

Statutory sanction: Civil wrongful-trading remedy requiring the general partner's directors to contribute such sums as the court thinks proper to the partnership assets; disqualification of directors for up to 15 years; criminal sanctions for fraudulent trading where the intent to defraud creditors is proved.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal civil restitution for the full amount of partnership debts incurred during the period of insolvency, court-approved travel bans pending the contribution hearing, and, in the case of fraudulent trading, custodial sentences.
