Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax); corporate/partnership registration via Gibraltar Companies House. Online portal: www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax-office.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership is first registered under the Gibraltar Limited Partnerships Act (or Companies Act for registration of the partnership as a composite body). The Registrar of Companies issues a Gibraltar Entity Number. Within 30 days of registration, form IT-ELP must be filed with the Income Tax Office requesting a Tax Reference Number (TRN). As the ELP is fiscally transparent, the TRN is a filing reference only; each partner's share of income is assessed directly. Where all partners are non-resident and no Gibraltar-source income arises, the ELP remains taxable on nil profit and no VAT ID is available because Gibraltar is not a VAT territory.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — A foreign-owned Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership is first registered under the Gibraltar Limited Partnerships Act (or Companies Act for registration of the partnership as a composite body). The Registrar of Companies issues a Gibraltar Entity Number. Within 30 days of registration, form IT-ELP must be filed with the Income Tax Office requesting a Tax Reference Number (TRN). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Gibraltar imposes automatic fiscal transparency for ELPs; no check-the-box style election is offered. A limited partnership may only elect to be taxed as a company if it is incorporated as a limited liability company (not available for an ELP). Partners may however make protective hybrid mismatch elections under UK CTA 2010 Part 17A and the UK/Gibraltar mutual tax information agreement to allocate mismatched tax results, provided the election is documented prior to the tax return filing date.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar does not maintain a VAT or general GST system. The relevant indirect levies are Customs & Excise duties, a motoring consumption tax, and a Financial Services Levy. For export services, the system is one of no indirect taxation.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT/GST registration threshold exists. Any person importing goods into Gibraltar from the UK/third countries must register with HM Customs Gibraltar under the Customs Ordinance (NO_APP: for services, no registration). Excise operators require a specific warehouse licence, regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Since Gibraltar does not levy VAT, there is no zero-rating claim; exported services are 'outside the scope' of any Gibraltar indirect tax. With respect to UK VAT, where the ELP supplies PCT patent prosecution or trademark registration services to a UK business customer, the customer accounts for UK VAT under the reverse charge; supplies to non-UK entities remain outside UK VAT if the place of supply rules (VAT Act 1994 s.7A) so determine. No refund of input VAT is claimable in Gibraltar for service exporters as there is no taxable VAT input.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration (SAD/ENS) where physical documents are shipped; otherwise an electronic departure record via GACCS
  • Commercial invoice describing the IP service, WIPO/PCT fee remittances and client billing
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (for any physical delivery of priority documents or paper dossiers)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT/confirmation evidencing receipt of export proceeds to a Gibraltar bank account
  • Written reverse-charge statement from the UK recipient (if UK VAT reverse charge applies), supported by the customer's VAT number and a copy of the contract

No e-invoicing or fiscal stamping mandate applies. Goods movements are cleared through the Gibraltar Automated Customs Clearance System (GACCS) using the SAD/ENS; service invoices are governed by ordinary commercial law and do not require government clearance or API stamping.

Although no VAT clawback is applicable, misuse of customs reliefs (e.g., temporary admission of patent priority documents) triggers forfeiture and penalties under the Gibraltar Customs Ordinance (up to £5,000 plus treble duty). UK HMRC may assess import VAT evasion penalties of up to 100% of the VAT required where UK reverse charge is misapplied, with a six-year assessment window. Mis-declared intangible service exports are also subject to director/partner liability under Gibraltar Companies Act 2014 and the Income Tax (Fraud) provisions.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility for both current and capital account transactions; Gibraltar maintains no exchange-control approval mechanisms or profit-remittance restrictions for non-resident partners of exempted limited partnerships, and no withholding taxes apply.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender rules exist in Gibraltar; exporters and service providers may retain all eligible receipts in any convertible currency, with no mandatory conversion ratio or time-based repatriation requirement.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary policy) / Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) (supervisory authority over banking and foreign-exchange intermediaries in Gibraltar)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Wise is operationally viable as a secondary rail for low-value, multi-currency official fee disbursements and client refunds, but it is not GFSC-regulated as a bank and cannot hold client money in a mandatory escrow arrangement. Revolut Business and Payoneer may serve similarly, but their UK EMI status post-Brexit requires careful legal review to ensure no regulated activity in Gibraltar is triggered without a local licence.
