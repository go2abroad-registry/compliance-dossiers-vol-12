Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax), HM Government of Gibraltar; official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/ and iTax electronic filing portal. Customs matters: Gibraltar Customs Department, HM Government of Gibraltar.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Register the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under the Limited Partnerships Act 2018; the Certificate of Registration and registration number are the primary corporate identifiers. Step 2: Through a Gibraltar licensed registered agent, file an application with the Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) for the ELP whenever the ELP carries on business in Gibraltar or files an annual return; the TRN is the statutory tax identifier. Step 3: Non-resident partners need no Gibraltar TRN; their home-jurisdiction TINs are declared for CRS and transfer pricing purposes. Step 4: No VAT/GST registration exists; customs registration is required only if the ELP directly imports, exports or warehouses controlled goods.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

No check-the-box election is available. The ELP is statutorily tax transparent under the Income Tax Act 2010: profits are assessed on partners in their own hands as allocated by the partnership agreement, with no entity-level Income Tax charge. No election exists to tax the ELP as a company; a Gibraltar company can be interposed if entity-level taxation is desired. Gibraltar's hybrid mismatch rules (BEPS Action 2) apply automatically to prevent deduction/non-inclusion where another jurisdiction treats the ELP as opaque; no elective disapplication is permitted.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST or sales-and-use tax system in force (Gibraltar is outside the EU VAT territory). A separate Customs Duties and Excise Duties regime applies only to specified goods physically entering Gibraltar; exports and offshore services are outside its scope.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable: no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. Registration with Gibraltar Customs is activity-based (import/export/warehouse dealer), not turnover-based, and carries no de minimis turnover threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable as a VAT concept. Gibraltar has no VAT, so the ELP cannot claim a 0% export VAT rate or VAT refund. Exports of raw cotton bales physically leaving Gibraltar, and international brokerage services performed for non-resident buyers, are simply outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect taxation; exports are duty-free upon proof of genuine export, and third-country goods in transit/transshipment may similarly move duty-free under customs supervision.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic export entry in SAD format lodged with Gibraltar Customs)
  • Bill of Lading (negotiable ocean/multimodal bill of lading evidencing dispatch from Gibraltar)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank SWIFT MT103 or documentary collection evidence of foreign currency receipt)

No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping or government API e-invoicing mandate is in force. Invoices must be issued under commercial practice and retained by the ELP for at least six years. Customs export entries must be lodged electronically in Single Administrative Document (SAD) format and are screened by risk-based selectivity; no physical fiscal stamp is required.

Gibraltar Customs may audit export evidence within six years. False declarations, misclassification, or failure to export goods declared as duty-free trigger forfeiture of goods, retrospective clawback of duty/drawback, interest, penalties of up to three times the duty or value involved, and criminal liability (fines and imprisonment) for the ELP and its partners. As no VAT exists there is no VAT zero-rating clawback; however, overstated export turnover or mis-statements in Income Tax returns attract Income Tax Office penalties of up to 200% of underdeclared tax plus prosecution for fraud.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GIP has full current-account and capital-account convertibility; there are no exchange-control restrictions on capital contributions, trade settlements, profit distributions, or partnership allocations. An Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is fiscally transparent, and profit allocations to non-resident partners do not require central bank approval or Gibraltar withholding where valid partner residence certificates and the register of partners are maintained. Practical hurdles are limited to bank-level anti-money-laundering and source-of-funds due diligence, not statutory convertibility constraints.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Gibraltar imposes no statutory requirement to surrender export proceeds, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no prescribed repatriation timeline. The ELP may retain 100% of cotton-bale export receivables in USD, EUR, or other permitted currencies indefinitely, and may use those balances as collateral for trade facilities or hedge execution.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the statutory banking supervisor; the Government of Gibraltar's Currency Board is the authority responsible for issuing the Gibraltar pound (GIP) at a fixed one-to-one peg with pound sterling. The Bank of England and HM Treasury are not direct supervisors of Gibraltar banks but are the de facto monetary anchor through the sterling peg and the application of UK financial sanctions in Gibraltar.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium feasibility. Offshore digital EMIs may be used only as supplementary, pre-funded settlement conduits, not as the primary treasury account. The EMI must be fully licensed by the FCA or an EEA regulator, hold client funds in segregated safeguarding accounts with an EU/UK credit institution, apply FATF-compliant customer due diligence, and contractually submit to Gibraltar or English law. Because Gibraltar is outside the EEA, nonelectronic-money passport rights automatically extend to Gibraltar; explicit written acceptance of the Gibraltar ELP as a client is required. Unregulated crypto or unreviewed fintech rails are not permissible for cotton trade settlement.

For a transparent ELP, treaty relief must be claimed at partner level. Attach a valid Gibraltar Tax Residence Certificate or partner-level residence certificate and a one-page beneficial-ownership declaration for each non-resident partner. Late filing triggers an automatic penalty of GBP 100, a daily default surcharge of GBP 10 per day, and a tax-geared penalty of up to 50% of the unpaid tax for deliberate non-filing. Interest accrues at 2% above the reference rate. The Commissioner may raise a best-of-judgment assessment, strike off the ELP from the registry, and, in fraud cases, impose a 100% penalty and refer the matter for criminal prosecution.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: A Gibraltar ELP engaged in related-party raw-cotton or yarn brokerage transactions exceeding GBP 1,000,000 in aggregate during a tax period must prepare and retain a Local File. A Master File is required where the consolidated group revenue of the Gibraltar group exceeds GBP 50,000,000. Transactions below these thresholds remain subject to arm's-length pricing records under the Income Tax Act 2010 and OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines, but full contemporaneous documentation may be replaced by a concise benchmarking memorandum.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance regime applies to resident ELPs carrying on relevant activities. If the ELP is centrally managed and controlled in Gibraltar, it must maintain a physical office in Gibraltar, employ adequate full-time staff, hold board meetings in Gibraltar, and ensure the core income-generating activities of the raw-cotton brokerage are performed in Gibraltar. A transparent non-resident ELP with no Gibraltar management and no Gibraltar-source income is not subject to the substance test. However, any activity involving contract conclusion, invoice issuance, banking decisions, or risk-bearing functions in Gibraltar will establish a taxable presence and trigger the Economic Substance filing and substance verification.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion Through Sham Cotton Brokerage

Statutory sanction: Fraudulent evasion of tax under the Income Tax Act 2010: monetary penalty up to 200% of the tax evaded, interest, and criminal fines; conviction may result in imprisonment of up to 7 years. The tax authority may reopen tax years without a time limit in cases of deliberate concealment.

Director personal liability: The general partner of an ELP is jointly and severally liable for partnership tax debts. Where a general partner or shadow director causes the ELP to issue false invoices or conceal foreign management and control, the court may order civil restitution, freeze personal assets, impose travel bans where unpaid tax exceeds GBP 10,000, and order disqualification as a director for up to 15 years.
Shell Company or Letterbox ELP

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance Regulations, the entity may be struck off the Gibraltar registry, its tax registration cancelled, and the Commissioner may issue a tax assessment on all invoice amounts net of zero allowable expenses. Fixed penalties up to GBP 8,000 per year plus daily penalties up to GBP 100 per day may be imposed, with total penalties not exceeding GBP 100,000 per year.

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil results in civil liability of the general partner for tax, penalties, and interest. Officers who knowingly sign false substance declarations may face criminal liability for perverting the course of law, freezing of bank accounts, disqualification orders, and a travel ban pending final civil judgment.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation on Cotton Yard Sales

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner may make a transfer pricing adjustment to the arm's-length price, disallow the overstated cost, and reallocate the profit to the ELP, giving rise to additional tax at 12.5%, late payment interest, and a tax-geared penalty of 100% of the understated tax. Failure to produce the Local File upon request triggers a separate penalty of GBP 5,000 plus GBP 50 per day.

Director personal liability: Directors and the general partner are personally liable where the transfer pricing adjustment is based on false or misleading documents. Penalties include civil restitution of diverted profits, joint and several liability for the whole tax debt, disqualification from acting as director or partner, and imprisonment where forged contracts or false invoices are used to support the reported price.
AML/CFT Failure in Documentary Collection and Wire Settlement

Statutory sanction: Breach of the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and AML/CFT regulations: fines of up to GBP 10,000 per violation for the ELP, revocation of the ELP's banking license, removal of the money-laundering reporting officer, and cancellation of the partnership registration. Criminal money-laundering penalties attract imprisonment of up to 14 years and confiscation of the trade proceeds.

Director personal liability: The general partner and any partner engaged in management face personal criminal liability if they knew or suspected the cotton-export proceeds were criminally obtained. The court may order asset freezing, forfeiture of the underlying LC proceeds, travel bans pending criminal trial, and shareholder/partner personal recovery actions by the liquidator.
