Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar Income Tax Office (ITO), Secretary's Lane, Gibraltar; official portal accessible via www.gibraltar.gov.gi

E-government filing portal

The Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is first registered with the Gibraltar Companies House as a limited partnership. Once the registration certificate and partnership deed are issued, the general partner files the income tax registration application with the Income Tax Office, together with the certificate of registration, partnership agreement, identity details of all partners, and confirmation of non-resident status. The ITO then issues a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN). Because Gibraltar has no VAT/GST, no VAT identification number is required; an optional customs registration may be obtained only if physical goods will be imported or exported through Gibraltar.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

A qualifying limited partnership may apply for Exempted Limited Partnership status under the Income Tax (Exempted Limited Partnership) Rules, achieving tax exemption for the partnership and its non-resident partners on foreign-source income. There is no US-style check-the-box election in Gibraltar. Hybrid mismatch reporting and substantiation may be required under the Tax (Exchange of Information) framework for cross-border payments to related entities.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST; Gibraltar applies import duty on a limited range of physical goods and stamp duty on instruments; digital services are outside indirect tax scope

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — there is no VAT registration threshold. Businesses trading in dutiable physical goods must register with Gibraltar Customs, but no threshold applies to digital subtitle and AI voice services

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar has no VAT zero-rating regime. Automated subtitle transcription and AI voice-cloning services supplied to clients outside Gibraltar are outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect tax because they are not goods and no local consumption occurs. For direct tax, foreign-source income of an Exempted Limited Partnership is exempt provided the partnership does not carry on a taxable trade in Gibraltar and all partners are non-resident.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD/Form C88) — required only for physical exports; not applicable to digital services
  • Bill of Lading/Airway Bill — required only for physical consignments
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / Bank credit advice confirming receipt of export consideration

No real-time e-invoicing or fiscal clearance mandate exists in Gibraltar. Commercial invoices serve as ordinary tax records. Physical goods are cleared through Gibraltar Customs using the Single Administrative Document (SAD/C88) and the electronic customs management system; digital services require no clearance.

No VAT clawback applies under Gibraltar law. Material misstatements or fraudulent claims of the ELP exemption can result in penalties of up to 200% of the tax or import duty evaded, late-payment interest, retrospective withdrawal of exempt status, and criminal liability under the Income Tax Act and Customs Ordinance.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is pegged at a fixed parity of 1.00 GIP = 1.00 GBP and circulates freely alongside GBP banknotes. Both capital and current account transactions are fully convertible. No exchange control permission is required for capital injections, profit repatriation, or trade payments. Non-resident partners may receive remittances in any freely convertible currency without exit friction, subject only to standard AML/KYC and tax compliance records. The absence of current-account restrictions means subscription revenue and service fees from post-production and subtitle AI clients can be retained or converted without specific central bank approvals.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar does not impose any statutory surrender, repatriation, or conversion obligation on export proceeds. Invoice payments for AI subtitle transcription, audio dubbing, and post-production services may be held in the currency of receipt or converted through any GFSC-licensed bank or authorized FX provider. Although no surrender rule applies, the partnership must maintain underlying commercial contracts and proof of service delivery to support the legitimacy of inbound payments and to satisfy reporting obligations under the GFSC’s anti-money laundering framework.

Central bank supervision: The Financial Services Commission (Gibraltar) (GFSC) is the principal banking and monetary licensing authority, with the Government of Gibraltar acting as the issuer of the Gibraltar pound through the Currency Notes Act, 1934 and the Coinage Act, 1934. HM Government of Gibraltar’s Ministry of Finance provides institutional supervision over monetary stability; the Bank of England does not have regulatory oversight of GIP but the peg to GBP is supported by the Government of Gibraltar’s fiscal policy.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Fintech EMIs (e-money institutions) are viable only if they hold an authorization that is valid for serving Gibraltar residents. Post-Brexit, EU/EEA EMIs cannot passport directly into Gibraltar in the absence of a reciprocal equivalence agreement. UK EMIs licensed by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) may, in practice, serve the Gibraltar market only where the EMI has obtained recognition or a branch licence from the GFSC under the Financial Services (Electronic Money) Regulations. A GFSC-registered EMI or a fully licensed Gibraltar bank is the preferred low-risk route. Stripe and Wise can be used as payment collection rails, but settlement must ultimately be received into a GFSC-authorised banking account to avoid unlawful deposit-taking or unlicensed payment services. The overall feasibility for this entity is Medium; the route is acceptable only under a regulated EMI sponsorship or when using banks that act as settlement sponsors.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The initial capital injection from the Cayman ELP partners must flow through conventional SWIFT MT103 wires. The receiving bank is the Gibraltar International Bank or Santander Gibraltar branch, and the transfer instructions must clearly state the beneficiary as the Gibraltar ELP with reference „Capital Contribution – ELP Partners – GIB0001234“. The purpose code should be „CAPT“ (capital contribution) or the equivalent ISO 20022 phrase „Capital injection for partnership purposes“. All inbound wires must be remitted from an account in the name of the actual partner; third-party or split-total wires are prohibited. The bank will require a capital contribution resolution, updated extract of the register of partners, and a cash-flow explanation. Inbound transfers above EUR 10,000 are subject to continuous bank transaction monitoring and may trigger a suspicious activity report if the source is inconsistent with the partnership's declared profile.For trade settlements to international AI compute vendors, subtitle software houses, and dubbing talent agencies, the primary clearing rail is SWIFT MT103 for direct bank-to-bank settlement in GBP, EUR, or USD. Commercial Letters of Credit (MT700) are permissible only when a licensor requires irrevocable payment security for software or infrastructure commitments above GBP 50,000. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 in aggregate annual intercompany transactions, or GIP 250,000 for any single controlled transaction, triggers mandatory contemporaneous Local File documentation for a Gibraltar ELP or its corporate partners. Below threshold, the ELP should still maintain a one-page arm's-length summary for all subtitle AI licence transactions.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar's Economic Substance for Companies Policy (2019) applies to entities conducting 'relevant activities'. Automated subtitle transcription and AI voice dubbing are not ordinarily within the core IP regime unless the ELP derives brand royalty or licensing income from the AI voice models/IP. If the ELP is classified as a pure IP holding vehicle, it must demonstrate: (a) commercial substance in Gibraltar, (b) senior employees with decision-making capacity in Gibraltar, and (c) local spending proportionate to rental/licensing income. Failure triggers administrative penalties and possible loss of tax residence.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Determination to the best of information, additional assessments up to 200% of undeclared profits, financial penalty up to GIP 100,000, and on criminal conviction an unlimited fine and imprisonment up to seven years.

Director personal liability: The general partner or director who signed a false return may be prosecuted personally. Courts may pierce the ELP veil where the ELP is a mere facade, enabling civil restitution, travel bans, and confiscation orders under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Economic Substance non-compliance penalty of GIP 5,000 plus GIP 500 per day, up to a maximum of GIP 100,000; revocation of tax residence, annual return strike-off, and possible director disqualification.

Director personal liability: Liability attaches to the general partner where capital contribution has never been paid or the partnership acts with sham intent. Personal asset freezes and 15-year director disqualification orders are available in the Gibraltar courts.
Transfer Pricing Misstatement

Statutory sanction: Mandatory transfer pricing adjustment, interest at 1.5% monthly, penalty up to 100% of under-declared tax, and refusal to allow the related-party deduction for the Gibraltar payer.

Director personal liability: Directors and partners who materially participated in the mispricing may be jointly assessed; criminal liability for fraudulent evasion of corporate tax attaches where such participation is proved beyond reasonable doubt.
AML / Financial Sanctions Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Gibraltar AML/CFT legislation, fines up to GIP 1,000,000; separate sanctions breaches carry imprisonment up to five years; affected funds are subject to freezing orders and forfeiture.

Director personal liability: AML/CFT obligations run to every partner and director with managerial control; personal civil restitution orders and custodial penalties apply without the need to prove personal enrichment when the ELP is used as a conduit.
