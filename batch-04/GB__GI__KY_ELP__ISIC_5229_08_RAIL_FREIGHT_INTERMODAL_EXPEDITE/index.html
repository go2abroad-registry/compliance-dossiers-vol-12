Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum capital or paid-up capital is required. Contributions are fixed by the Limited Partnership Agreement and may be cash, in kind, or deferred. No escrow account is required for an ordinary exempted limited partnership; if the ELP acts as a regulated private fund, Gibraltar Financial Services Commission rules may require segregation and safeguarding of investor money.

Funding & unlock path: Not triggered for pure intermediary activities. If a physical office is occupied, the ELP must obtain fire safety certification under the Gibraltar Fire Safety Act, secure emergency lighting and means-of-escape inspection, obtain structural load clearance from Gibraltar Building Control for any racking, and prepare a workplace health and safety risk assessment under the Health and Safety at Work Act.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum capital or paid-up capital is required. Contributions are fixed by the Limited Partnership Agreement and may be cash, in kind, or deferred. No escrow account is required for an ordinary exempted limited partnership; if the ELP acts as a regulated private fund, Gibraltar Financial Services Commission rules may require segregation and safeguarding of investor money.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office of HM Government of Gibraltar (Commissioner of Income Tax), 132-140 Main Street, Gibraltar; official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi and Income Tax e-services at https://etax.gibraltar.gov.gi. Customs administration: HM Customs Gibraltar (Customs Department, HM Government of Gibraltar).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is formed under the Gibraltar Limited Partnerships Act 1995. The ELP must be registered with the Registrar of Companies, which issues the Registration Number. The ELP then applies to the Commissioner of Income Tax for a Certificate of Exemption under Section 37 of the Limited Partnerships Act, certifying that the partnership is an exempted limited partnership that does not carry on business within Gibraltar and cannot be assessed to Gibraltar income tax. Simultaneously, the ELP and each non-resident partner must apply through Form TRN / Entity Taxpayer Registration to obtain a Tax Reference Number (TRN) for correspondence, compliance screening, and bank account opening. A Certificate of Exemption and each partner's TRN are issued once statutory declarations, registered office details, and partner information are filed; there are no minimum capital requirements and no capital duty.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a US-style check-the-box election. The principal statutory election is the irrevocable application for Exempted Limited Partnership status under Section 37 of the Limited Partnerships Act 1995, conditioned on the partnership not carrying on any business in Gibraltar and on the filing of an annual exemption certificate and non-resident partner return. Partners may also elect individual tax treatment under Gibraltar's Income Tax Act, and hybrid mismatch avoidance elections (including for UK-Gibraltar cross-border partnership structures) are available under the UK Hybrid and Other Mismatches (Prevention of Avoidance) Rules as extended to Gibraltar, to prevent double non-taxation or deduction/non-inclusion mismatches.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST applicable. Gibraltar is outside the EU VAT area and imposes no value added tax, sales tax, or use tax. Indirect levies are limited to customs duties, excise duties, and a small consumption duty on specified imported goods (spirits, tobacco, mineral hydrocarbons) chargeable under the Customs Tariffs Act 2016 and the Customs, Excise and Taxes Act.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable for VAT/GST because no such tax exists. A customs registration obligation applies to any importer/exporter of goods irrespective of turnover; the economic operator must register with HM Customs Gibraltar as an importer/exporter of record and obtain a customs agent/declarant code to file SAD and NCTS transit declarations. There is no turnover threshold for customs registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in Gibraltar because no VAT/GST regime exists and thus no 0% export VAT rate or VAT refund mechanism is available. The Gibraltar ELP's rail freight forwarding income is outside the scope of Gibraltar income tax under the exemption certificate, provided no business is carried on in Gibraltar; export freight services are therefore not subject to tax and require no export VAT refund claim. For customs duty purposes, goods exported from Gibraltar, including those moving under transit from a warehouse or freezone, are duty-free, and inward processing relief or temporary admission is available for goods subsequently exported.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document / SAD Export) lodged electronically through the Gibraltar Integrated Customs System or under NCTS transit procedure
  • Bill of Lading, multimodal transport bill, or Fiata FBL evidencing international movement of the export container block train
  • CIM rail consignment note for the continental rail leg under COTIF/CIM rules
  • Inward/Outward Remittance Certificate or bank/FCA-conformant Foreign Exchange Certificate from a Gibraltar bank evidencing freight settlement and inward funds receipt
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and weight certificate
  • Freight forwarding service contract, cargo insurance certificate, and the letter of credit instrument where payment is routed via Swift/Wire

Gibraltar imposes no real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing, or digital stamping mandate. Invoices may be issued in paper form subject to standard record-keeping requirements (retention for a minimum of 6 years). Customs clearances are processed through the Gibraltar Integrated Customs System, which supports electronic submission of Single Administrative Documents and New Computerised Transit System (NCTS) declarations for transit movements across the European Union and third countries; API-based machine-to-machine integration is accepted for licensed customs agents, but a human-validated digital stamp is not required.

Fraudulent claims for export duty relief or misrepresentations intended to preserve ELP exemption attract retrospective revocation of the Certificate of Exemption by the Commissioner of Income Tax, with the partnership re-assessed for all past Gibraltar-source profits as if exempt status had never existed, plus interest from the original due date. Penalties under the Income Tax Act include amounts up to 100 percent of the tax evaded and monetary penalties per uncorrected failure. False customs declarations attract fines of up to GBP 10,000 per declaration and forfeiture of the goods under the Customs Tariffs Act 2016. Where rail transit documents, bills of lading, or remittance certificates are fabricated or backdated, criminal liability for fraud, money laundering, or customs evasion may attach, and clawback of any tax refund, duty relief, or bank-facility benefit is ordered. The ELP's non-resident partners additionally remain exposed to their own residence-jurisdiction CFC/transparency rules; HM Revenue & Customs and HM Customs Gibraltar cooperate on joint audit scrutiny of Eurasian rail freight flows routed through the port of Gibraltar.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar maintains no separate exchange control regime. The Gibraltar pound is fully convertible into pound sterling at par, and both current account and capital account transactions are free from exchange control restrictions. Profit remittance, partnership distributions, capital repatriation, and loan repayments to non-residents may be effected without Central Bank approval, subject only to standard AML/CFT due diligence and documentary requirements.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or conversion requirements apply. There is no mandatory timeline, minimum conversion ratio, or obligation to repatriate export proceeds into Gibraltar pound sterling. Freight-forwarding and logistics receipts may be held in any freely convertible currency in any GFSC-licensed bank or a permitted offshore counterparty, provided supporting trade documentation is retained for a minimum of six years under AML/CFT obligations.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (acting as monetary anchor for the Gibraltar pound) and the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC), the competent authority for licensing, AML/CFT supervision, and foreign exchange activity regulation

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-High for operational disbursement support; Low for principal capital escrow and service of high-value letters of credit. Legal constraints: following Brexit, EEA EMI passporting rights do not extend to Gibraltar; any offshore digital EMI must be licensed or otherwise registered with the GFSC to hold funds or provide payment services in or from Gibraltar. Non-licensed EMIs may be used only as receiving sub-accounts for permitted outbound cash management if the main treasury relationship remains with a fully GFSC-licensed tier-1 bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All capital injections must be routed via SWIFT MT103 from the general partner's or limited partner's own bank account to the ELP's GFSC-licensed operational account. The wire message must contain the partnership's legal name and account IBAN, the partner's creditor reference, and a clearly stated purpose code such as 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION' or 'SHAREHOLDER LOAN' in the payment narrative, matching the capital call letter. Supporting verification must include a signed capital contribution letter, the underlying partnership agreement clause authorising the injection, source of funds evidence (such as bank statements or audited statements of wealth), and a partnership capital account reconciliation. Third-party fund remittance is prohibited; each injection must be traceable from the same beneficial owner or regulated intermediary named in the KYC file.Outbound disbursements for rail freight and intermodal transport settlements should be made through SWIFT MT103 for direct payments to rail operators, container terminal operators, inland hauliers, and customs agents, and through MT700 commercial letters of credit for high-value intermodal service contracts or when the supplier requires a documentary payment guarantee. Each payment must be matched to an underlying transport order supported by a rail waybill, CMR/e-CMR, supplier invoice, and customs declaration. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 – aggregate cross-border controlled transactions threshold requiring preparation of a Local File and Master File. A Country-by-Country Report is separately required where ultimate group revenue exceeds EUR 750,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) 2019 apply to entities deriving gross income from relevant activities including distribution and service centre business. A rail freight and intermodal expediting ELP (ISIC 5229) will likely be classed as a distribution and service centre where it earns fees from logistics coordination, contract procurement, cargo documentation, and shipment scheduling. The ELP must therefore exercise management and control in Gibraltar, maintain proportionate staff and premises, and perform core income-generating activities in Gibraltar such as negotiating freight rates, issuing transport documents, managing intermodal connections, and administering collection/payment flows. A pure pass-through ELP with no physical substance in Gibraltar will fail the ESR unless it can demonstrate that all gross income arises outside the scope of relevant activities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Misuse of ELP Exemption

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner will re-assess the under-declared profits at the 12.5% corporate rate, add interest from the due date, and impose a penalty of up to 100% of the undeclared tax. Criminal prosecution for tax evasion may lead to an unlimited fine and imprisonment for up to 7 years on indictment.

Director personal liability: Courts will pierce the ELP veil where the general partner and its directors signed false exemption declarations. Directors are jointly and severally liable for restitution of tax, subject to confiscation orders, travel bans, and disqualification from holding directorships in Gibraltar for up to 15 years.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: An adjustment to taxable profits plus interest and a tax-geared penalty of up to 100% of the corrective assessment. Failure to produce mandatory transfer pricing documentation also attracts a fixed penalty of GIP 10,000 per controlled transaction, with automatic escalation to daily penalties for continued obstruction.

Director personal liability: Directors who directed or approved unwarranted transfer pricing adjustments can be sued for the resulting tax penalties and are personally exposed to civil fraud claims. In cross-border cases, the UK-Gibraltar exchange-of-information provisions may lead to joint liability proceedings between the associated enterprises and their directors.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Breach

Statutory sanction: Non-compliance with the Economic Substance Regulations triggers a fine of up to GIP 100,000 per relevant activity per financial year and daily default penalties. Persistent breaches lead to strike-off from the Gibraltar Register of Companies and denial of the ELP's exempt tax status.

Director personal liability: The general partner and responsible officers face personal fines of up to GIP 500,000, disqualification orders, and travel bans issued by the Supreme Court. Where unpaid tax follows loss of exemption, shareholders and directors may be held liable for the corporate tax debt under the veil-piercing provisions of the Gibraltar Companies Act.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Criminal fines up to GIP 200,000 and imprisonment up to 10 years for money laundering; failure to submit a suspicious activity report attracts separate criminal liability. Civil penalties of up to GIP 250,000 and confiscation of freight-related gains may be imposed under the Proceeds of Crime Act.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors and the ELP's general partner cannot hide behind the corporate form. Individuals involved in facilitating mis-invoicing or transporting sanctioned cargo are personally liable for civil restitution, subject to worldwide freezing orders and travel bans, and may be sentenced to up to 14 years' imprisonment for serious money laundering offences.
