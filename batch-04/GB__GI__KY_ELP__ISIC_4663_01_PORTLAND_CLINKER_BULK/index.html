Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax, HM Government of Gibraltar) - e-Services portal via gov.gi for direct taxation; HM Customs Gibraltar (Customs Department) for import/export duties, excise and customs registration. The jurisdiction applies its own Income Tax Act 2010 and is not part of the UK VAT system.

E-government filing portal

1) Form the ELP with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under the Limited Partnerships Act and obtain the partnership registration number. 2) Within 30 days of commencement, submit the statutory tax registration form to the Income Tax Office to obtain the ELP Tax Reference Number (TRN). 3) Each non-resident/corporate partner applies separately for a partner TRN. 4) Register a Customs Trader Account with HM Customs Gibraltar and, for EU/UK goods movement, a GB EORI number via HMRC. 5) File the annual partnership return (even if nil) and maintain partner allocation schedules. No minimum capital required; processing typically 5-10 business days.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible - the ELP is fiscally transparent by default; profits are allocated directly to partners and no entity-level Gibraltar corporate/income tax is charged on the ELP. Non-resident partners are charged to Gibraltar tax only on trading profits attributable to a permanent establishment in Gibraltar; ocean-freight and offshore wholesale trading ('managed and controlled abroad') generally remains outside charge. — 1) Form the ELP with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under the Limited Partnerships Act and obtain the partnership registration number. 2) Within 30 days of commencement, submit the statutory tax registration form to the Income Tax Office to obtain the ELP Tax Reference Number (TRN). 3) Each non-resident/corporate partner applies separately for a partner TRN. 4) Register a Customs Trader Account with HM Customs Gibraltar and, for EU/UK goods movement, a GB EORI number via HMRC. 5) File the annual partnership return (even if nil) and maintain partner allocation schedules. No minimum capital required; processing typically 5-10 business days.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible - the ELP is fiscally transparent by default; profits are allocated directly to partners and no entity-level Gibraltar corporate/income tax is charged on the ELP. Non-resident partners are charged to Gibraltar tax only on trading profits attributable to a permanent establishment in Gibraltar; ocean-freight and offshore wholesale trading ('managed and controlled abroad') generally remains outside charge.

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a US-style check-the-box regime. The ELP may: (i) define capital/profit-sharing in the partnership deed to tax-transparent effect; (ii) rely on automatic transparent treatment under Income Tax Act 2010; (iii) claim treaty relief (e.g. UK-Gibraltar DTCDTA) at partner level; (iv) elect suspension customs arrangements (inward processing/warehousing) for imported clinker pending re-export; and (v) apply OECD BEPS Action 2 / hybrid mismatch rules to prevent double non-taxation of deductible cross-border payments.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST in Gibraltar. The only indirect taxes are (i) Customs Import Duty under the Customs Ordinance (Cap. 53) on goods imported for home use, and (ii) Excise Duty on alcohol, hydrocarbons and tobacco. Exports of goods (including bulk portland clinker) and international freight services are outside the scope of any Gibraltar indirect tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A - there is no turnover-based registration threshold as no VAT/GST exists. Registration with HM Customs Gibraltar (Customs Trader Account) is mandatory from the first import/export transaction; a UK GB EORI number is required for goods moving between Gibraltar and the EU/UK. No minimum capital or economic presence threshold applies to the ELP.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - there is no VAT/export zero-rating mechanism in Gibraltar; exports are simply outside the indirect tax charge. Duty relief on imported clinker inputs can be accessed only through customs suspension regimes (inward processing, customs warehousing) with re-export within the approved period. For direct-tax purposes, export profits are reportable only at partner level and only where the trade is carried on through a Gibraltar permanent establishment; documentation of off-shore administration supports 'managed and controlled abroad' treatment.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic SAD via ASYCUDA World)
  • Bill of Lading / ocean waybill evidencing vessel freight
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103 or bank advice of export proceeds
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List (bulk weight certificate)
  • Certificate of Origin (where preferential market access is claimed)
  • End-Use / Re-Export Declaration (where a duty suspension regime is used)

None. Gibraltar does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, API-based e-invoicing, continuous transaction control or digital stamping. B2B bulk commodity invoices are electronic (PDF/EDIFACT compatible); each export requires a customs declaration lodged electronically via the ASYCUDA-based Gibraltar Customs system, supported by digital commercial invoice and packing list, bill of lading, and weight certificate.

HM Customs Gibraltar may audit export and suspension records for up to 6 years. False declarations, misdescription or non-export attract forfeiture of goods, customs penalties up to three times the evaded duty, and criminal sanctions (imprisonment up to 7 years) under Cap. 53. The Income Tax Office may assess within 6 years (unlimited for fraud), with penalties up to 100% of the tax undercharged plus statutory interest, and retrospective clawback of any duty or tax relief. Abusive use of pass-through/hybrid arrangements in the clinker supply chain is counteracted under OECD BEPS Action 2 and Gibraltar anti-avoidance rules.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar maintains full capital and current account convertibility without exchange-control restrictions. The Gibraltar pound is pegged at parity to the British pound. No statutory hurdles apply to profit remittances or distributions for non-resident partners; however, funds flowing to or from sanctioned jurisdictions require compliance with HM Treasury financial sanctions as implemented by the Gibraltar Sanctions Act.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No export proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion applies in Gibraltar. UN, UK, and Gibraltar exchange-control restrictions are absent; all export receipts may be retained in the original currency, credited to any permitted foreign-currency account, or converted through authorised Gibraltar banks at freely negotiated rates.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (as central monetary authority for the pound sterling) and Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (as licensing, AML/CFT, and payments supervisor)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium. Only entities holding a Gibraltar e-money or payment-services licence may lawfully onboard a Gibraltar ELP. UK or EEA EMIs without current Gibraltar passported authorisation cannot offer accounts to a Gibraltar entity. Practical viability requires a Gibraltar-licensed EMI subsidiary or a Tier-1 bank relationship, with enhanced due diligence attached to the offshore tax transparency position.

Where the late return results from negligence or fraud, a tax-geared penalty of up to 100% of the understated tax may be levied, with statutory interest running on all unpaid tax.
Box 8 Declaration, date, and signature of the general partner or authorised tax agent The declaration must be signed by a natural person with authority; non-resident partners may appoint a Gibraltar tax agent with a filed power of attorney. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory Local File or Master File threshold is enacted in Gibraltar. As prudent market practice, a contemporaneous Local File should be maintained where annual cross-border related-party turnover exceeds GBP 5,000,000, and Master File documentation is recommended for multinational groups with consolidated revenue above EUR 750,000,000 (OECD benchmark).

Economic substance scope: The Companies (Economic Substance) (Requirements) Act (Gibraltar) applies to relevant activities, expressly including distribution and service centre operations. An ELP booking ocean-vessel freight and wholesale distribution of portland cement/clinker must demonstrate physical presence in Gibraltar, qualified employees, and core income-generating activities performed in Gibraltar in proportion to the income derived, failing which the tax authority may recharacterise or attribute the income and apply penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Return

Statutory sanction: Upon conviction, fines up to 100% of the tax evaded plus statutory interest; imprisonment of up to 2 years in the Summary Court and up to 7 years in the Supreme Court on indictment; forfeiture of unlawfully avoided tax and assets traceable to the evasion.

Director personal liability: Directors and general partners of the ELP may have the corporate veil pierced where fraud is proved: personal civil restitution of evaded tax, director disqualification, travel bans, seizure of personal assets, and custodial sentences.
Economic Substance Failure (Shell Company)

Statutory sanction: Annual financial penalty of up to GBP 10,000 for each failure to satisfy the economic substance test; the Registrar may strike the ELP off the Gibraltar register and the Income Tax Office may reattribute profits subject to tax at 12.5%; automatic exchange of information with the United Kingdom tax authority follows.

Director personal liability: Where the ELP is struck off, the general partner and its directors face personal liability for unpaid penalties and taxes, together with restrictions on acting in the management of any Gibraltar entity and potential travel restrictions pending settlement.
Anti-Money Laundering and Sanctions Violation

Statutory sanction: Fines under the Proceeds of Crime Act and the Gibraltar AML regulatory framework of up to GBP 500,000 per breach; freezing of funds and confiscation orders; imprisonment of up to 14 years for money laundering offences involving tainted trade proceeds.

Director personal liability: The nominated Money Laundering Reporting Officer, directors, and general partners are individually prosecutable; sanctions include personal fines, confiscation of profits, director disqualification, travel bans, and long custodial sentences for knowingly facilitating illicit trade finance.
