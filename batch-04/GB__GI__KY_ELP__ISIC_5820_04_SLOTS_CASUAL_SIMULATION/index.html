Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar - Income Tax Office (ITO), Ministry of Finance; official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax and associated Income Tax e-services.

E-government filing portal

The ELP must be registered with the Gibraltar Income Tax Office through a recognized tax/company registration agent. For a Gibraltar-registered ELP, the agent files the registration application together with the partnership certificate, partnership deed, beneficial ownership information, registered office confirmation, and details of the general and limited partners. The ITO then assigns a Tax Reference Number (TRN). For a Cayman ELP seeking Gibraltar fiscal registration, the foreign partnership must provide its Cayman certificate of limited partnership and filing details, appoint a Gibraltar tax representative if no partner is tax-resident in Gibraltar, and apply for a Gibraltar TRN before opening a Gibraltar payment account or onboarding with Apple/Google merchant services. The procedure requires KYC documentation for all partners and confirmation of the ELP's taxable transparency status.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible - The ELP is fiscally transparent for Gibraltar tax purposes; the partnership itself is not chargeable to income tax. Each partner is assessed on its respective share of partnership profits. Non-resident partners are generally within the territorial scope only in respect of income accruing in or derived from Gibraltar; capital gains and foreign-source receipt are outside the Gibraltar charge unless specifically remitted or sourced from Gibraltar.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists under Gibraltar law, and no general hybrid-mismatch election is available. The ELP is transparent by default and cannot elect to be treated as a Gibraltar-resident corporation merely by filing. The only administrative option is a reporting/collection arrangement whereby the general partner is jointly responsible for filing and payment on behalf of the partners, which does not change the pass-through classification. Any attempt to elect corporate treatment requires forming a separate Gibraltar private limited company.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT / GST / Sales and Use Tax - Gibraltar does not operate a value added tax or broad-based consumption tax; limited customs and excise duties apply to physical goods imported into Gibraltar.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - there is no turnover-based VAT/GST registration threshold in Gibraltar because no VAT/GST system is in force. Customs EORI-type registration is only required where the business physically imports into or exports from Gibraltar; this is not a revenue tax threshold and does not apply to digital social casino service provision.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - Gibraltar has no VAT/GST and therefore no statutory 0% export VAT rate and no input VAT refund mechanism. Cross-border digital supplies of simulated social casino and virtual slots content via Apple IAP and Google Play Billing are outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect tax. The ELP should nonetheless maintain substance documentation and app-store payout evidence to support the treatment of foreign-source revenue in the hands of the partners.

  • Customs Export Declaration (required only if physical media or goods are exported; N/A for pure digital app-store supply)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill (N/A for intangible services; substitute with Apple IAP and Google Play Billing transaction records)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank SWIFT credit confirmation (retained as evidence of 180-day foreign-exchange repatriation)

No electronic invoicing or real-time fiscal clearance mandate applies in Gibraltar. There is no API-based tax clearance, no continuous transaction control, and no digital tax stamping for invoices. Ordinary electronic invoices, Apple/Google app-store commercial reports, and bank records are acceptable. Tax records must be retained for a minimum of six years from the end of the relevant tax year.

Gibraltar has no VAT refund or export zero-rating clawback because no such mechanism exists. However, any false declaration to the Income Tax Office or customs authorities regarding the territorial source or dual nature of receipts may trigger civil penalties up to 100% of the tax undercharged plus statutory interest. The Income Tax Office can reopen assessments for up to six years, increasing to an unlimited period in cases of fraud or deliberate concealment. Customs-related penalties under the Customs Ordinance include fines and forfeiture where goods declarations are falsified. The ELP must maintain robust transfer-pricing and economic-substance records to withstand audit scrutiny.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is fully convertible to GBP at a 1:1 fixed peg with no exchange-control restrictions on current or capital account transactions. Profit remittance and capital repatriation are free, but receiving banks may require tax residence evidence and an up-to-date beneficial ownership certificate. Non-resident ELPs may hold GIP, GBP, USD and EUR multi-currency accounts; no central bank approval is required for conversion between GIP and any foreign currency.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory surrender requirement, mandatory conversion timeline, or conversion ratio for export receipts in Gibraltar; exporters may retain 100% of foreign currency proceeds offshore, in GIP, or in any GIP/GBP current account, provided the financial institution's AML policies have been satisfied.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority for the GBP/GIP peg) and Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the local prudential and conduct regulator; the Government of Gibraltar issues GIP currency under the Currency Notes Act, and there is no separate Gibraltar central bank.

Stripe Fit: High

Low. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File: required when the ELP's aggregated related-party transactions exceed GBP 1,000,000 in any fiscal year, or when the ELP is part of a multinational group with consolidated revenue above GBP 50,000,000. Master File and Country-by-Country Report: required for groups with consolidated revenue ≥ EUR 750,000,000.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance (Implementation) Regulations 2019 apply to entities carrying on 'relevant activities' as defined by EU Code of Conduct Group. Publishing simulated social casino games and operating a digital platform is classified as a 'trade or business', requiring the ELP to maintain in Gibraltar: (i) core income-generating activities (CIGA) including performance of digital platform management and content creation; (ii) an adequate physical office; (iii) at least two part-time employees with decision-making responsibility; and (iv) sufficient operational expenditure proportionate to the ELP's activity. Non-compliance leads to penalties and strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (Understatement of Gaming Revenue)

Statutory sanction: Under the Gibraltar Income Tax (Amendment) Act 2: civil penalty up to 200% of additional tax due; criminal prosecution with fines up to £5,000,000; publication of taxpayer details in the official gazette.

Director personal liability: General partner's directors and any shadow director can be held jointly and severally for unpaid tax plus interest; travel bans under the proceeds of Crime Act; imprisonment up to 7 years for deliberate tax evasion; disqualification as director for up to 15 years under the Companies Act.
Shell / Substance-Less ELP

Statutory sanction: Under the ESR Regulations 2019, a financial penalty of £5,000 for the entity and daily default penalties up to £500/day; failing to satisfy the economic substance test following an enforcement notice can lead to strike-off and dissolution; taxation at 10% on deemed profits of the ELP.

Director personal liability: Directors personally liable for the ESR penalty and any deemed tax if they caused the ELP to trade without substance; travel restrictions imposed by a Gibraltar court; disqualification order under Section 183 of the Companies Act 2014.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar revenue authority applies 'small penalty' under the Transfer Pricing Rules 2018: adjustment of taxable profit; simple interest at 5% per annum; a penalty of 15% of adjusted amount if transfer pricing documentation is not prepared; in serious cases a penalty up to 100% of tax gain.

Director personal liability: Directors of the general partner may incur civil penalties if they knowingly signed a defective transfer-pricing return; for a deliberate breach causing a tax loss to a foreign affiliate, extradition or Mutual Legal Assistance can be used; director disqualification up to 10 years.
Money Laundering / KYC Failures

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime (Gibraltar) Act 2022 and the Financial Services (AML) Regulations: administrative fine up to £2,000,000 or 5% of annual turnover (whichever greater); revocation of any EMI or payment institution licence; confiscation of proceeds; criminal prosecution for money laundering with penalties of 14 years' imprisonment.

Director personal liability: The General Partner's board (especially the Money Laundering Reporting Officer) is personally criminally liable if they fail to report suspicious activity; travel bans and asset freezing orders; personal confiscation orders; dishonour-based disqualification as trustee or director.
Unlicensed Gambling Cross-Border

Statutory sanction: Not a Gibraltar tax matter, but can trigger UK and foreign licensing fines: in the UK, a break of the Gambling Act 2005 may expose the operating group to unlimited fines and censure by the Gambling Commission; in Gibraltar, despite not requiring a gaming product licence for social casino, the Commissioner can impose a public regulatory penalty of up to 10% of Gibraltar GGY if found to intermediate unlicensed remote gambling.

Director personal liability: Directors of the Gibraltar ELP can be prosecuted in jurisdictions where customers reside; extradition possible under the Gibraltar–UK treaties; personal liability for unpaid licence fees and compensation to players; in the EU, individual cross-border penalties may pass to directors under EU anti-fraud provisions.
