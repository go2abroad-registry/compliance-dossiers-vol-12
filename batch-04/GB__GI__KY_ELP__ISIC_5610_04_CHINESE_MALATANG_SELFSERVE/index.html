Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax); official administration portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation and electronic filing via the Income Tax Office 'MyTax' digital service.

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities and exempted limited partnerships must obtain a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN). Procedure: (1) incorporate or register the ELP with the Gibraltar Companies House under the Limited Partnerships Act; (2) engage a Gibraltar-registered agent or tax representative; (3) file Application for Tax Reference Number (form TRN/ELP) with certified constitutional documents, registered office certificate, and partner particulars; (4) receive TRN within 15 working days; only required if the ELP or any partner has Gibraltar-source income or is liable to register for import duty.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar Pound (GIP) is pegged at 1:1 to GBP and circulates interchangeably with GBP coinage; no statutory exchange controls, no capital account approval regime, no profit remittance licensing, and no restrictions on distributions or liquidation proceeds apply to a non-resident Exempted Limited Partnership. Current account convertibility is unrestricted, meaning franchise royalty remittances and trade payments may be made in any currency; the only operational constraint is the standard AML/CFT and sanctions-screening obligation imposed by the receiving and correspondent bank rather than any central bank authorisation. For a pass-through ELP holding the master licensing rights of a Chinese Malatang fast-casual brand, the practical FX profile is fully free, with GIP/GBP as the functional treasury currency and CNY revenues convertible at whatever price the market offers.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. The Bank of England and the Government of Gibraltar impose no statutory surrender timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no onshore repatriation deadline for export or licensing proceeds earned by Gibraltar entities. Franchise and master-licensing royalty invoices issued by the ELP to foreign franchisees, including Chinese sub-licensees, may be retained offshore in the original invoicing currency or converted into GBP at the ELP's discretion; any bank-level FX conversion requires only the underlying royalty schedule, the executed franchise agreement, and a standard FX instruction ticket, with no filing to the GFSC or its prudential desk for amounts below the licence thresholds applicable to financial-services activity.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary anchor for the Gibraltar Pound); Government of Gibraltar Treasury (GIP note issuance and reserve management under the Currency Notes Act 1934); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) (licensing, conduct and AML/CFT supervision of banks, electronic money institutions and investment firms operating in Gibraltar).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Ramp Fit: High

Rating: Medium-High for locally licensed Gibraltar EMIs; Low for offshore digital EMIs. Gibraltar maintains a dedicated Electronic Money Institution licensing regime preserved from the EMD framework and administered by the GFSC under the Financial Services Act 2019 and the Financial Services (Electronic Money) Regulations; only a GFSC-authorised EMI may lawfully hold funds or issue e-money for a Gibraltar-resident ELP, and such an EMI may also passport to other UK Overseas Territories only where bilateral arrangements exist. Post-Brexit, UK FCA-authorised EMIs no longer enjoy passporting into Gibraltar, and EEA-authorised EMIs similarly cannot passport, so a Lithuanian, German or Polish EMI without a Gibraltar branch will usually provide at best an unregulated virtual IBAN that tier-1 Gibraltarian banks will treat as a high-risk inflow source. A reputable Gibraltar-licensed EMI specialising in corporate treasury, SWIFT connectivity and multi-currency wallets is a viable fast on-ramp for royalty collection, partner distributions and off-cycle supplier disbursements, provided the EMI's own KYC file mirrors the ELP's complete ownership and commercial documentation.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol for partner capital contributions: remit via SWIFT MT103 from the partner's home bank to the ELP's settlement account at Gibraltar International Bank or Banco Sabadell Gibraltar, crediting the ELP's nominated GIP/GBP account. Use the ISO 20022/SEPA payment-purpose code 'CAPH' (Capital Increase) in the structured remittance information, or, where the originating bank does not support purpose codes, insert the free-text narrative 'Direct Investment - Equity Capital (BPM6 code 511)' together with the ELP partnership deed number and the contributing partner's full name. Verification bundle to accompany the wire: executed partnership deed with contribution schedule; bank remittance instruction clearly earmarking the payer as a named limited partner; current source-of-funds letter signed by the partner; and the bank's internal client classification form. No Bank of England or GFSC capital import licence is required, but the receiving bank requires pre-notification of any inbound amount exceeding GBP 50,000, and payments originating from a PRC SAFE-regulated entity must reference the SAFE foreign exchange approval or the appropriate SAFE contracted-payment filing number to avoid refusal by the US or UK correspondent clearing chain.Clearing rail matrix for operating and trade payments: (i) GBP disbursements to UK and Gibraltar suppliers and franchisee-support vendors settle via Faster Payments (real-time, sub-GBP 1 million) or CHAPS (same-day final, above GBP 1 million); (ii) cross-border USD and CNY payments to Chinese equipment, packaging and raw-food suppliers settle through SWIFT MT103 over Banco Sabadell Gibraltar or Trusted Novus Bank correspondent networks, filling the reference field with the purchase order, commercial invoice number and HS code; (iii) card-scheme acquirer settlements from the franchisees' POS terminals are collected into the operational account, while royalty remittances to the master licence-owners are executed by MT103 using purpose code 'ROYA' (Royalty) plus a full royalty calculation statement in the narrative; (iv) commercial letters of credit for inventory import financing are available as SWIFT MT700 instruments issued by Banco Sabadell Gibraltar with the ELP as applicant and a Chinese or Hong Kong bank as beneficiary, using USD or EUR reimbursement lines when equipment originates from EMEA suppliers; (v) EUR trade disbursements to continental European sub-licensees can use the SEPA CT scheme with the 'ROYA' or 'SUPP' purpose code and the ELP's IBAN at the Gibraltar licensed bank.Plain-vanilla deliverable GBP/CNY forward contracts with Banco Sabadell Gibraltar or Trusted Novus Bank, tenor up to 12 months, matched against monthly royalty receipts under an executed underlying commercial licence agreement.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on CNY/USD and CNY/EUR referencing the offshore CNH fixing, permissible where a PRC counterparty cannot deliver CNY onshore due to SAFE restrictions, with residual GBP-leg rebooking into the ELP's treasury account.Cross-currency swaps GBP/USD and GBP/EUR for tenor-matching of capital contributions against sub-licence fee inflows, executed as asset-only OTC derivatives under the bank's ISDA-style master agreement and subject to the ELP board-approved treasury risk policy.Contingent rolling FX forwards on the CNY/GBP cross covering projected fixtures, equipment and franchisor inventory purchases, limited to 100% of the board-approved notional disbursement forecast and documented in the treasury operating manual.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: In Gibraltar, mandatory Local File and Master File documentation is triggered once aggregate controlled transactions (including intra-group royalties, management fees and finance costs) exceed GBP 1,000,000 per annum or total group turnover exceeds GBP 10,000,000. Where no threshold is crossed, a summary transfer pricing policy note must still be attached to the tax return.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar applies the Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) enacted under the Income Tax (Economic Substance) Act. A wholly-owned ELP earning IP royalties must demonstrate independent board management, adequate staff, a registered office in Gibraltar and a level of expenditure proportionate to the royalty income. For the fast-casual Malatang master licensing model, the founder's commercial decisions, franchise IT systems and brand-management functions must be physically exercised in Gibraltar; otherwise the ELP is deemed to lack substance and is subject to annual aggregate penalties of up to GBP 10,000 or cancellation of registration.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate Income Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner of Income Tax may raise an assessment on the full omitted amount, apply a penalty of 100% of the tax involved, add statutory interest at 3% above base rate, and initiate criminal prosecution for evasion.

Director personal liability: Where profits have been diverted, a general partner or director is personally liable for civil restitution equal to the tax loss. The Supreme Court of Gibraltar may grant a freezing order, impose a travel ban, and an individual can face up to seven years' imprisonment for fraudulent evasion.
Shell Company / Artificial Substance Arrangement

Statutory sanction: The Registrar may strike off the ELP, impose a regulatory fine of GBP 20,000, disallow any tax treaty benefits, and apply the controlled foreign company rules to attribute the licensing profits to the foreign shareholder.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly subscribe to a report confirming Gibraltar substance can receive a disqualification order for 15 years and a restitution order requiring repayment of all sums withdrawn from the ELP's bank accounts during the period of sham activity.
Anti-Money Laundering / Financial Crime

Statutory sanction: The Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit and Proceeds of Crime (Seizure and Confiscation) Act impose a civil penalty of up to GBP 50,000, confiscation of the licensed assets, and cancellation of the firm's AML registration under the Financial Services Act.

Director personal liability: The general partner is personally criminally liable for failure to prevent money laundering; on conviction an individual may be imprisoned for five years and be ordered to make a confiscation order amounting to the criminal benefit derived from the Malatang franchise operation.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing and False Documentation

Statutory sanction: An TP adjustment by the Commissioner will recalculate profits at the arm's length price, impose late payment interest and a penalty of 70% of the additional tax; deliberate evasion can lead to criminal prosecution with an unlimited fine.

Director personal liability: Where the TP adjustment results from dishonesty or gross negligence, the director is jointly and severally liable for the underpaid tax up to a cap equal to twice the director's annual remuneration. Where a profit split is artificially engineered, a UK anti-avoidance charge may also arise, and the director may be surcharged personally.
