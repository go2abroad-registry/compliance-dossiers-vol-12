Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax); official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) must file Form GIB-IT-REG through the Gibraltar Income Tax Office. The ELP is tax-transparent, so the partnership obtains a Partnership Reference Number for administrative and withholding purposes; each partner not resident in Gibraltar must provide a certified foreign tax residency certificate. Non-resident partners are not required to obtain a Gibraltar TIN unless they have Gibraltar-source income or trigger withholding obligations. For customs/excise, the importer of record registers for a Customs Registration and Identification Number (CRN) via the Gibraltar Customs single window.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Under the Gibraltar Income Tax (Transparent Entities) Regulations, an ELP may irrevocably elect to be treated as a corporate entity for Gibraltar tax purposes (check-the-box). A private company may also elect to be treated as a transparent partnership. Hybrid mismatch elections are governed by the Taxes (Hybrid Mismatch) Rules 2018, which follow OECD BEPS Action 2.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar does not levy VAT or GST; indirect taxation consists solely of customs import duties and excise duties on specified goods.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable for VAT/GST; no general consumption tax registration threshold exists. Commercial importers must obtain a Customs Registration Number before the first importation of goods, regardless of volume.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

As Gibraltar has no VAT/GST, there is no export zero-rating mechanism. Exported goods and offshore B2B services are outside the scope of Gibraltar consumption tax. For customs duty drawback relief, the exporter must demonstrate physical removal of the goods from Gibraltar to a non-Gibraltar destination within 90 days, supported by transport and customs evidence.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD/EX1)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank SWIFT confirmation)

Gibraltar does not operate a real-time e-invoicing or fiscal clearance mandate. Importers must lodge electronic customs declarations through the Gibraltar Trade and Customs System (GTCS) using Union Customs Code format. No digital stamping or API pre-clearance is required. A separate Entry Summary Declaration (ENS) is required for pre-arrival risk assessment under safety and security rules.

HM Customs Gibraltar may audit any customs duty drawback or relief claim. A false or fraudulent declaration triggers retrospective clawback of the duty relieved, a penalty of up to £5,000 per declaration or 100% of the duty involved, seizure of goods, and criminal prosecution in the Gibraltar Magistrates' Court.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The GIP is fully convertible at a fixed 1:1 parity into GBP, which is itself a freely convertible global reserve currency. Gibraltar maintains no exchange-control or capital-control legislation; current-account payments (testing fees, supplier invoices, salaries) and capital-account transactions (LP capital contributions, profit distributions, loan repayments) are freely permitted. Profit remittance by the ELP to non-resident limited partners is unrestricted, subject only to standard banking AML narrative review and automatic CRS/FATCA reporting of the pass-through income allocation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No export-proceeds surrender obligation exists in Gibraltar. Hardwaare safety testing services invoiced in GBP, EUR or USD can be retained offshore, remitted abroad, netted against payables, or hedged without any requirement to convert into GIP or to sell foreign currency receipts to a licensed bank within a statutory timeline.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the financial services regulator; the Government of Gibraltar Currency Commission exercises currency issue under the Currency Notes Act 1934; monetary policy and sterling convertibility are de facto anchored to the Bank of England because the Gibraltar pound (GIP) is legally pegged at par with GBP.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Medium viability subject to enhanced due diligence. UK-FCA regulated EMIs (Wise, Revolut Business) will generally onboard a Gibraltar exempted limited partnership because Gibraltar is a British Overseas Territory, but they classify the account as a cross-border GB-Wide business account rather than a domestic UK account. Wise multi-currency GBP/USD/EUR accounts are feasible for receiving testing-lab invoices; however, EMIs cannot issue a Gibraltar IBAN or a GIP-denominated ledger, and the ELP must evidence valid UBO declaration, operating substance and a UK/Gibraltar physical service address. Legal constraints are structural: post-Brexit the EU passport is lost for Gibraltar-licensed EMIs, while UK-FCA EMIs may rely on the 2021 UK-Gibraltar financial services MoU; simultaneously the GFSC does not regulate foreign EMI activity in Gibraltar if the service is provided cross-border.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Gibraltar imposes no exchange control, so limited partners remit directly via SWIFT MT103 using the descriptive standards of the payee bank, typically LP capital contribution, unsecured, no invoice. The receiving Gibraltar bank performs ATLAS screening, source-of-funds verification and, where triggered, FATCA/CRS self-certification. Wires exceeding USD 100,000 or GBP-equivalent should be pre-advised to the relationship manager with the capital commitment clause reference. The UK Bank of England statistical reporting and Gibraltar RASSL rules do not apply to GIP sterling flows, so the routing is unrestricted outside EU capital-movement reporting. EUR or USD contributions are converted at the bank-client forward rate of the day or held in multi-currency suspense until allocated.Suppliers in the United Kingdom are paid through the local NatWest clearing using CHAPS for T+0 and Faster Payments for T+1. International laboratory consumable and courier invoices are settled via SWIFT MT103 through the London correspondent of the Gibraltar bank. For larger equipment purchases, commercial letters of credit are issued on SWIFT MT700 with MT710 confirmation through NatWest International or Banco Santander Totta. EUR-denominated flows are cleared via SEPA Credit Transfer once a euro IBAN is available at the Santander branch; USD flows are cleared over the New York CHIPS/CHATS system through the bank's office.
