Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up or subscribed capital applies to an Exempted Limited Partnership; partner contributions are contractual and are recorded in the capital statement. Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (tax authority); e-tax portal: eTax (https://etax.gibraltar.gov.gi). Customs matters are handled by HM Customs Gibraltar under the Ministry of Finance.

E-government filing portal

Non-resident entities and partnerships first obtain a Companies Registry number (Limited Partnerships Act). They then register for tax at the Income Tax Office on the prescribed registration form (Form 1) via eTax, submitting the partnership deed, registered office certificate, details of partners and a declaration of non-residence. The Income Tax Office issues the Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) which serves as the statutory tax identity. The ELP must annually file a nil/exemption return or otherwise confirm that no Gibraltar-source chargeable profits arise.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible (territorial scope: the ELP is fiscally transparent; each partner is taxed only on Gibraltar-source income; non-resident partners deriving no Gibraltar-source income are outside charge) — Non-resident entities and partnerships first obtain a Companies Registry number (Limited Partnerships Act). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible (territorial scope: the ELP is fiscally transparent; each partner is taxed only on Gibraltar-source income; non-resident partners deriving no Gibraltar-source income are outside charge)

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no US-style check-the-box election. The ELP's transparency is statutory; partners may submit a non-residence/exemption certificate confirming the partnership has no Gibraltar-source profits. There are no hybrid mismatch elections under Gibraltar domestic law, but relevant UK-Gibraltar treaty / OECD hybrid rules may apply where dual-resident partners or permanent establishments exist.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None - Gibraltar has no VAT, GST or sales and use tax. Imported physical goods may attract Gibraltar customs tariffs/excise duties, unless relieved. Digital B2B services (EMR SaaS) are outside any Gibraltar indirect tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable. No VAT/GST registration threshold exists because the applicable indirect tax rate is zero. Trade imports are systemically reported through the Gibraltar Customs declaration process (SAD/import entry) when goods physically enter Gibraltar.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable as no VAT is leviable. The EMR SaaS export by the ELP to non-Gibraltar clients is outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect taxation. For physical exports of goods, a 'proof of export' can exempt or duty-relieve goods that were previously imported into Gibraltar; this is governed by HM Customs Gibraltar procedures, not VAT zero-rating.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD/export entry, only for physical goods departing Gibraltar)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / Proof of Exit
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank transfer evidence (for commercial substantiation of service exports)

None. Gibraltar does not mandate RTFD (real-time fiscal data) or digital stamping for invoicing. Commercial invoices, tax invoices, or agreements in electronic form are acceptable. Customs declarations, where relevant, are processed through HM Customs' electronic import/export systems and are not linked to a VAT clearance regime.

Any false or fraudulent declaration designed to secure an export duty relief or non-residence/tax exemption entitles the Income Tax Office or HM Customs to reopen prior periods, withdraw the relief/exemption and impose penalties up to 100% of the tax or duty evaded, plus interest, in addition to criminal prosecution for tax fraud and potential disqualification/removal of the ELP's exempt status.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is pegged at parity to pound sterling and is fully convertible. Gibraltar maintains no exchange controls for current-account or capital-account transactions. Cross-border profit remittances by non-resident partners of an Exempted Limited Partnership are unrestricted, provided the remitting bank completes the usual source-of-funds and anti-money-laundering verification. Economic substance requirements applicable to certain Gibraltar entities must be satisfied but do not restrict currency convertibility.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no statutory export-proceeds surrender rules or mandatory conversion ratios in Gibraltar. Export receipts and trade receivables may be retained in GIP, GBP, or any freely convertible foreign currency. When settlements are routed through UK clearing systems, the underlying commercial invoice and service contract serve as the source-of-funds explanation and no forced FX conversion is applied.

Central bank supervision: Government of Gibraltar Currency Commissioners (note issuance and statutory backing) and Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) (banking and anti-money-laundering supervision)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise, Revolut Business, or MultiPass may be used for limited non-core collections and disbursements, but they cannot replace a GFSC-licensed banking relationship. These EMIs often restrict GIP-denominated IBANs, may not accept health-sector or regulated clinical software payments, and are subject to group-level AML restrictions. Retail-facing payment collection for EMR SaaS subscriptions should be channelled through Stripe Billing or equivalent card/bank-transfer gateways, while treasury liquidity remains in a Gibraltar-licensed bank account to satisfy local substance and audit expectations.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital contributions should be executed as a single GBP-denominated SWIFT MT103 wire from the partner's own banking account to the GIP/GBP operational account held at a GFSC-licensed bank. The beneficiary reference must state: 'Contribution pursuant to Clause [X] of ELP Partnership Agreement No. [ID]'. The remitter name must exactly match the partner name in the partnership deed. The onboarding banker must receive a certified copy of the partnership agreement and a source-of-funds statement. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Gibraltar levies no withholding tax on dividends, partnership profit distributions, or other profit repatriations by Gibraltar entities to non-residents. A Gibraltar ELP distributing EMR SaaS profits to its foreign corporate partners incurs no Gibraltar WHT at source.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP £1,000,000 — mandatory Local File (Transfer Pricing Disclosure) is required for any Gibraltar-resident partner where controlled transactions with associated enterprises exceed GIP £1,000,000 in a chargeable period; a Master File and Country-by-Country Report are required where the worldwide group has consolidated revenue exceeding EUR 750,000,000.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations 2019 (as amended) apply to entities carrying on relevant activities (IP holding, distribution and service centres, financing, banking, insurance, HQ and shipping). An ELP is covered to the extent it has a Gibraltar tax residence or PE. For EMR SaaS, the principal exposure arises if the partnership is managed and controlled in Gibraltar while owning software IP; it must then demonstrate: (i) board/committee direction and control in Gibraltar, (ii) a sufficient number of qualified employees physically present in Gibraltar, (iii) premises in Gibraltar, and (iv) operating expenditure in Gibraltar proportional to the derived income. If instead the ELP is genuinely administered from the UK/US with foreign cloud infrastructure and the partnership merely uses the Gibraltar registry, the relevant activity is accordingly outsourced and the economic substance test can be satisfied by the offshore substance of the parent. Non-compliance triggers financial penalties and, importantly, repudiation of any reliance on treaty or residency claims.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion — Deliberate Understatement and Concealment

Statutory sanction: A tax-geared penalty of up to 100% of the tax evaded, interest at 5% per annum, and criminal conviction in the Gibraltar Magistrates Court or Supreme Court punishable by a fine of up to £500,000 and/or imprisonment of up to 7 years for the general partner and wilful participants.

Director personal liability: Limited partnership veil is pierced only on fraud or wrongful trading; where material concealment is deliberate, courts may order the general partner to restore unpaid tax plus civil restitution of the distributed EMR SaaS profits. Individuals face asset freezes, travel bans while extradition or mutual legal assistance is pending, disqualification from managing any Gibraltar or UK company for up to 15 years, and personal confiscation orders under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015.
Empty Shell / Sham Economic Substance and False DTA Claims

Statutory sanction: Economic Substance Regulations penalties escalate from £5,000 to £100,000 per annum for continued failure; the ITO can disallow residency status, recast the arrangement and charge Gibraltar tax on the profit, and the registered agent may be struck off the Limited Partnership register. A false TRC certification is additionally an offence of false statement punishable on conviction by a fine up to £10,000.

Director personal liability: Directors of the general partner are personally liable where they knowingly approve false substance declarations. Remedies include personal liability for the ESR fines, disqualification as director or general partner for 10 years, international travel bans, and if the sham was used to launder proceeds of the clinic payments, imprisonment up to 14 years.
AML/Financial Crime — Mandatory Reporting and Sanctions Breaches

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and the Sanctions Regulations, failure to report exposes sanctions-infringement penalties up to £1,000,000 for companies and imprisonment up to 2 years for individuals; money laundering carries custodial sentences up to 14 years and confiscation orders equal to the whole benefit stream.

Director personal liability: General partner directors are treated as relevant persons; personal liability attaches for authorising suspicious inflows. Remedies: personal fine, confiscation of personal assets representing the benefit, travel bans pending criminal proceedings, and cross-border prosecution under Gibraltar-UK/EU extradition cooperation with up to 14 years of imprisonment.
Register, Filing and Payroll Compliance Failure

Statutory sanction: Failure to satisfy a beneficial ownership requirement attracts a daily default penalty of £10 per day plus criminal exposure on repeated default to a level 5 fine (up to £20,000) and up to 6 months imprisonment; payroll misclassification exposes arrears of income tax, social insurance contributions, and penalties up to £100 per month per employee plus interest.

Director personal liability: The general partner and its directors are jointly and severally liable for payroll deductions withheld but not accounted; courts can pierce the ELP veil to recover unpaid contributions and issue conduct orders restricting the director from acting as a general partner in any British Overseas Territory.
