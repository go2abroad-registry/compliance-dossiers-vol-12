Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, HM Government of Gibraltar (Income Tax Office), 72 College Lane, Gibraltar; official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation. Customs and excise administration is under HM Customs Gibraltar (https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/customs).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership must first be registered with the Gibraltar Companies Registry (Form ELP-1). Within 30 days of establishment, the ELP must submit an income tax registration application to the Income Tax Office to obtain a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (GTRN). Partners' names, addresses, and capital contributions are filed as part of the registration. No VAT ID is available or required because Gibraltar does not levy VAT. If the ELP imports or exports goods, it must separately apply to HM Customs Gibraltar for a Gibraltar EORI number before the first customs declaration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a US-style check-the-box regime. An ELP is fiscally transparent by default, but the partners may make an irrevocable statutory election under the Income Tax Act 2010 for the partnership to be taxed as a company. The ELP may also apply for exempted status as a qualifying non-resident partnership, effectively limiting Gibraltar income tax to Gibraltar-source income. OECD hybrid mismatch rules are incorporated into Gibraltar law, and partners may make protective elections to align the partnership's classification between Gibraltar and the partner jurisdiction.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Not applicable — Gibraltar has no VAT, GST, or sales and use tax. The applicable indirect tax regime consists of Gibraltar Import Duties and Excise Duties on specific goods.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No monetary turnover threshold applies because no VAT/GST registration is required. For customs purposes, any legal person importing or exporting goods in the course of business must obtain a Gibraltar EORI number before first declaration; no economic turnover threshold applies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

No VAT zero-rating can be claimed because Gibraltar imposes no VAT. The equivalent relief is zero-rate customs duty on goods physically exported from Gibraltar to a destination outside the Gibraltar customs territory, provided the goods leave within 90 days of release, the exporter is an EORI-registered entity, and the ELP holds valid evidence of export. Offshore services supplied from Gibraltar to non-resident customers are outside the scope of Gibraltar taxation and effectively bear a 0% rate.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Gibraltar EX Form / SAD export declaration)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical dispatch from Gibraltar
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (or SWIFT / letters of credit payment evidence)

Gibraltar does not mandate real-time digital stamping, electronic fiscal invoicing, or point-of-sale clearance for domestic transactions. Customs import and export declarations must be filed electronically through HM Customs Gibraltar's electronic single window, either by the declarant or via an accredited customs agent. Excise goods movements require electronic administrative documents in accordance with the UK/EU movement control system. API integration is available for approved high-volume declarants.

HM Customs Gibraltar may conduct post-clearance audits for up to four years, or twenty years in cases of serious irregularity. Failure to substantiate an export duty relief results in recovery of unpaid duty, interest at 1.5% per month, and a civil penalty of up to 100% of the evaded amount. Fraudulent representations may lead to unlimited fines, forfeiture of goods, and imprisonment for up to seven years. The Income Tax Office may also reopen partnership returns within twenty years where export-related income has been misstated, impose back-taxes on Gibraltar-source trading income, and apply a penalty of up to 100% of the additional tax due.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Sterling (GBP) is fully convertible in Gibraltar; there is no exchange-control statute and no IMF Article VIII restriction. Both current-account trade payments and capital-account movements are unrestricted. The payable CS/ELP is structured as a non-resident exempted limited partnership, so Gibraltar profits are not taxed, and distributions or capital repatriations to non-resident partners can be made gross, without withholding tax. The only practical remittance hurdles are documentary: proof of economic substance in Gibraltar, an updated beneficial-ownership register, bank-source-of-funds confirmation, and a non-resident tax-status declaration for the partners.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar imposes no statutory surrender of export proceeds, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no fixed timeline for repatriation of export receipts. Foreign-currency receipts may be retained indefinitely in licensed bank accounts. Nevertheless, Gibraltar-licensed banks apply AML/CTF transaction-reporting thresholds of EUR 10,000 or equivalent and may request a full trade-file bundle for any receipt that does not match the declared commercial invoice or purchase order.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority for Gibraltar), HM Treasury (sanctions and financial crime policy), and the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the licensed-bank regulator and foreign-exchange transaction monitor.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium with material constraints. Gibraltar is a British Overseas Territory and is not an EU/EEA member after Brexit; therefore, a UK FCA-authorised electronic money institution (EMI) does not automatically passport into Gibraltar. Digital offshore EMIs may be used as a secondary collection or disbursement node, but most will reject onboarding for a Gibraltar-registered partnership with non-EU non-resident partners due to correspondent-bank de-risking. The primary liquidity infrastructure must remain a fully-regulated Gibraltar-licensed bank; an EMI is acceptable only for small-value operational floats and must never hold the ELP's capital, trade proceeds, or client funds.

Defense evidence: Notarised ELP deed / partnership agreement; Gibraltar Companies House or GFSC registration certificate; full register of beneficial owners; passports, proof of residential address, and professional CVs of all natural-person controllers; certified bank reference from the existing banking institution; source-of-wealth statement; source-of-funds statement; legal-structure chart with commercial rationale for the Gibraltar nexus.
Open-account cross-border commodity arbitrage with intercompany purchases and sales lacking physical-movement evidence.

Root cause: Open-account cross-border commodity arbitrage with intercompany purchases and sales lacking physical-movement evidence.

Defense evidence: Master purchase and sale contracts with Incoterms 2020 rules; commercial invoices; packing lists; warehouse receipts; third-party inspection certificates (SGS, Intertek, or similar); transport bills of lading; customs declarations; end-use certificate from the lithium converter or battery-materials buyer; bank trade-flow narrative showing each payment leg of the arbitrage.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory where aggregate cross-border controlled transactions exceed EUR 10m per tax period or where the Gibraltar entity recognises gross turnover above EUR 20m; Master File is required only for MNE groups with consolidated revenue equal to or greater than EUR 750m; in all cases, contemporaneous evidence must be retained on the battery-grade lithium-carbonate price benchmark (e.g., FOB Shanghai / GBL battery-grade index), the gross margin split and the LC commercial terms

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar ESR 2020 applies because cross-border trade in battery metals is a relevant activity. The ELP must demonstrate core income-generating activities in Gibraltar: procurement contracts negotiated and approved locally, linked inventories managed, storage and insurance decisions taken, LC and invoice administration performed, adequate premises with at least one locally resident officer or employee holding delegated decision-making power, and proportionate operating expenditure. Paper-only substance or UK-desk controlled decision-making will fail ESR and constitutes a material false filing offence.
