Step 2: File Form TIN-101 'Application for Tax Identification Number – Non-Resident/Transparent Partnership' with the ITO, enclosing the Cayman Islands ELP registration certificate, exempted limited partnership deed, register of partners, and a certified general-partner resolution appointing a Gibraltar tax representative. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no check-the-box election. Transparent treatment of the Cayman ELP follows Income Tax Act 2010 ss.53–56 (partnerships assessed on partners). The ELP may elect (Form ELP-ELEC-1) an arm's-length apportionment of Gibraltar-source versus foreign-source profit allocation; advance classification rulings are available from the ITO (Form GR-ADR). Hybrid-mismatch neutralisation under OECD BEPS Action 2 / UK-GI ATAD-aligned rules applies to arrangements that would otherwise produce double non-taxation; no unilateral entity-classification election is available in Gibraltar.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales Tax – Gibraltar Import Duty and customs-union regime (Gibraltar outside EU VAT area; UK-Gibraltar customs union post-Brexit)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A – no VAT or turnover-tax registration threshold. Mandatory customs EORI registration (GI prefix) from the first import/export transaction; no de minimis turnover exemption.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in a VAT context because Gibraltar levies no VAT/GST. For duty purposes, goods in the international wholesale supply chain may be exported duty-free under External Transit (T1/UK transit) or under a duty-suspension warehouse regime; where frames have been entered into free circulation, a duty drawback claim requires legal exportation evidenced by the customs export declaration, commercial invoice, and packing list. For direct taxes, only profits with a Gibraltar source (local procurement, marketing, management activity) are chargeable; foreign wholesale margins are apportioned on an arm's-length basis, and the Cayman ELP must document that attribution.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration (electronic SAD/EXS) naming the ELP as exporter
  • Bill of Lading (or multimodal/air waybill) evidencing physical dispatch from Gibraltar
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank SWIFT credit advice evidencing receipt of sales proceeds

None. Gibraltar imposes no real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction controls, or mandatory e-invoicing/API clearance. Traders may issue paper or electronic invoices. Customs declarations must be filed electronically through the Gibraltar Customs single-window (UK-Gibraltar integrated declaration system); no digital stamping requirement.

Duty relief, suspension claims, or export zero-rating are retrospectively clawed back with interest where export documentation is not produced or goods are re-supplied into the Gibraltar market. False declarations attract customs penalties of up to 100% of the evaded duty plus forfeiture of goods and, in fraud cases, criminal prosecution with fines and imprisonment. Income-tax evasion penalties can reach 100% of the tax charge plus daily default interest. Under Gibraltar's AML/CFT framework, fabricated export invoices designed to move funds offshore constitute a predicate money-laundering offence, triggering independent FSC and GFSC enforcement action.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar maintains full current and capital account convertibility under its Offshore Special Territory framework. There are no exchange control regulations restricting the conversion of Gibraltar pounds into foreign currencies. Profit remittances, dividends, and partnership distributions may be transferred freely without prior central bank approval, subject only to routine AML/KYC reporting by the instructing bank. No surrender or repatriation requirements exist for non-resident partners. The GB/Gi post-Brexit arrangement preserves access to UK payment channels but not automatically to EU passporting.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender regime applies in Gibraltar. Export receipts may be retained in any currency, and there is no mandatory conversion ratio or regulatory timeline for repatriation or conversion. Banking practice may require submission of underlying commercial documents (e.g., invoice, bill of lading) for transactions exceeding threshold amounts, solely for Sanctions and AML screening.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Gibraltar (functionally exercised through Her Majesty's Government of Gibraltar and the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission; the Bank of England serves as the monetary authority for the Gibraltar pound, which is pegged at parity to GBP)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Medium with material legal constraints. Gibraltar is not an EU Member State; EEA-authorised Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) cannot passport into Gibraltar. A limited number of licensed Gibraltar payment institutions (under the Financial Services (Payment Services) Act) may provide cross-border clearing, but they are not full retail EMIs. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise or Revolut generally do not offer Gibraltar-resident business accounts or local GIP clearing. They may be used for outbound USD/EUR settlements as an ancillary rail, but cannot serve as the primary treasury account holder due to KYC restrictions on Gibraltar-licensed partnerships and the absence of GIP balance support. Any EMI onboarding must be assessed on a case-by-case basis for legal capacity to onboard an exempted limited partnership whose partners reside in third countries.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol requires an international wire in freely convertible currency (USD, EUR, or GBP) to the ELP's segregated capital escrow account at the tier-1 Gibraltar bank. The wire must state purpose code 'GIB CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION ELP' or, under current SWIFT practice, narrative field 70 'CAP CONTRIBUTION -- NON-REFUNDABLE'. Banks will require a copy of the registered limited partnership agreement, the capital call resolution, and the legal entity identifier (LEI) where available. All inbound wires must be pre-notified to the receiving bank with a completed source-of-funds questionnaire. The bank will verify against the AML/KYC file and, for amounts above FGP 50,000, enhanced due diligence will apply. No exchange control approval is required, but the remitter must be a named signatory or a recognised contributor to the ELP's capital structure.Clearing rails for trade settlement are principally (i) SWIFT MT103 for direct correspondent-bank payments in USD/EUR/GBP; (ii) SWIFT MT202 for bank-to-bank covering of inter-bank transfers; and (iii) Commercial Letters of Credit (L/C) governed by UCP 600 issued for high-value ophthalmic frame shipments. For low-value disbursements (< USD 10,000), the bank may offer local payment gateways (e.g., Faster Payments within UK, SEPA for EUR when originating from an EEA hub). The ELP must maintain clearly defined approved payee lists and sub-limits. All outgoing payments require underlying supporting invoices, shipment tracking references, and a declaration that the goods are not controlled or dual-use items. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% for Gibraltar-resident companies subject to Corporation Tax; an Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is income-tax transparent and each partner is assessed on its distributive share, resulting in no entity-level Gibraltar corporation tax.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Gibraltar does not impose dividend withholding tax. Distributions from an ELP to non-resident partners are paid free of Gibraltar statutory deduction.

Deadline: · Frequency:

On or before the 31st day of May following the end of the partner's year of assessment, or within 9 months of the accounting period end for a corporate partner, whichever is earlier; payment of any partner-level assessment falls due on 31 January immediately following the assessment year.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File and Master File obligations arise where Gibraltar gross consolidated revenue exceeds GBP 10 million and the group crosses the OECD ALP international threshold of EUR 50 million; each controlled cross-border transaction with value above GBP 100,000 must be individually supported by contemporaneous documentation.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) adopt OECD/EOIR standards; a wholesale distribution of optical frames is classified as a 'distribution and service centre' activity requiring core income-generating activities (sourcing, pricing, warehousing, customer contracting, and risk management) to be effectively directed and performed in Gibraltar with adequate premises, employees and board-level expenditure; for a low-risk wholesale reseller, the requirement is satisfied by booking all sale revenue in Gibraltar, maintaining strict risk control over acetate eyewear inventory, and ensuring at least one substantive board meeting is conducted there annually.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Criminal tax evasion carries a maximum sentence of 7 years' imprisonment, an unlimited fine, or both; civil tax penalties commence at 100% of the evaded tax and can rise to 200% with interest at 8% per annum; the Gibraltar Income Tax Office may proceed with criminal asset recovery under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015.

Director personal liability: The general partner or director who signed the false return is personally and jointly and severally liable, the corporate veil is pierced under the Misrepresentation and Fraudulent Trading provisions of Gibraltar Companies Act 2014; a travel ban may be issued preventing departure until all outstanding taxes are settled and a confiscation order may be enforced against the director's personal bank accounts and property.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Under the Income Tax (Economic Substance) Regulations 2020/2022, failure to pass the substance test attracts an annual fine of GBP 10,000 plus a daily penalty of GBP 500 for continued default; the Commissioner may issue a formal strike-off notice and remove the ELP from the Gibraltar Companies Register; tax advantages earlier granted are rescinded and unpaid tax becomes immediately assessable.

Director personal liability: Directors, shadow directors, and the general partner are personally liable for penalties if they knowingly operate without substance; the Supreme Court may make a disqualification order (up to 15 years), impose a travel restriction order, and attach the director's local and foreign-held real estate for failure to liquidate the economic substance shortfall.
AML / Correspondent Banking Abuse

Statutory sanction: Money laundering and terrorist financing are punishable under Gibraltar's Criminal Procedure and Evidence Act and the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 with up to 14 years' imprisonment and confiscation orders; Financial Services Commission penalties for failures in AML/KYC controls reach GBP 2 million or 10% of annual turnover; a financial crime suspicion requires a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) to the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit.

Director personal liability: Senior officers and the money laundering reporting officer may be personally targeted for civil fines and imprisonment if they are complicit; asset freezing orders, international cooperation with Interpol red notices, and personal travel bans are routinely imposed.
Transfer Pricing Mismatch and DTA Abuse

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner may make transfer pricing adjustments plus interest and restitutionary penalties of 100% of the underpaid chargeable profit; statutory late payment penalties apply from the due date of the adjustment; a public statement can be issued naming the entity whether separate failure documentation penalties of GBP 5,000 per 48-hour non-compliance window in the case of formal requests.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable to pay the transfer pricing adjustment if they signed or approved the prevailing returns and the foreign affiliate is in a jurisdiction with no enforcement mechanism; a director may be banned from holding a Gibraltar office and made subject to an injunction on international flight movements until restitution.
