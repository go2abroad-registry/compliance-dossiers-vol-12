Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar: Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, H.M. Government of Gibraltar (official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/tax-customs). For any optional UK connection, HMRC remains a separate competent authority; no HMRC registration is triggered by Gibraltar tax registration alone.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Register the ELP under the Gibraltar Limited Partnerships Act 2019 with Companies House Gibraltar; obtain the Gibraltar Limited Partnership number. Step 2: File any liability notification with the Income Tax Office only if the ELP or its partners carry on a taxable activity deriving Gibraltar-source income; the general partner normally files. Step 3: Receive an Income Tax Office Assessment/Tax Reference number for the chargeable partners. Step 4: Non-resident foreign partners with no Gibraltar activity will not receive a Gibraltar TIN; retain the ELP registration certificate, partnership deed, and foreign tax residence certificate. No Gibraltar VAT ID exists because Gibraltar has no VAT.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; Territorial_Scope. The ELP is statutorily tax-transparent for Gibraltar Income Tax Act purposes; income is allocated to partners. Non-resident partners are taxable in Gibraltar only on Gibraltar-source income or a trade carried on in Gibraltar; no entity-level Gibraltar income tax is imposed on the ELP.

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no check-the-box or entity-classification election; transparency is statutory. For U.S. federal tax purposes, eligible ELPs may be classified under Treasury Reg. section 301.7701-3 by Form 8832, but that election has no Gibraltar tax effect. Hybrid mismatch risks are to be analyzed under the UK TIOPA 2010 Part 6A rules; no Gibraltar-specific mitigation election exists.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No_General_Indirect_Tax_System; Gibraltar is outside the EU VAT area and applies only selective customs/import duty and excise on specific physical goods.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable for SaaS: no VAT/GST registration threshold in Gibraltar. A physical-goods importer/excise operator may need a customs/excise registration, but this does not apply to the micro-SaaS subscription service.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in Gibraltar: no positive VAT/GST is chargeable, so there is no 0% export VAT claim or refund mechanism. Cross-border SaaS supplies are outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect tax. In a separate UK/EU VAT context, the supplier would apply place-of-supply rules; that is not a Gibraltar export zero-rating event.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full liberalisation applies to both current-account and capital-account transactions. GIP is redeemable at par into GBP on demand and functions as a fully convertible sterling-zone token currency. Non-resident partners of the Exempted Limited Partnership may inject capital, receive distributions, and repatriate profits without Gibraltarian withholding tax or exchange-control approval, because the ELP is fiscally transparent and profits are taxable only at partner level. Residual hurdles are documentary only: CRS/FATCA self-certifications, source-of-wealth evidence, and, where tax-transparent treatment is sought, a Commissioner of Income Tax (CFM) non-residency ruling confirming that the ELP has no Gibraltar-sourced profits and no permanent establishment in Gibraltar. No central-bank consent is required for dividends, capital withdrawals, or intra-group funding.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender or conversion obligation exists in Gibraltar or the UK. There is no repatriation time window, no mandatory conversion ratio (effectively 0 percent), and no central-bank settlement requirement for software subscription revenue received via Paddle or Lemon Squeezy. Settlement payouts in GBP, EUR, or USD may be held indefinitely in the ELP's multi-currency accounts; banks may simply tag settlement receipts by provenance for GFSC AML transparency purposes.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) acts as the integrated banking, AML and financial-services supervisor and as the de facto monetary-regime administrator for Gibraltar; HM Government of Gibraltar is the issuing authority of the Gibraltar pound (GIP), which is pegged at par (1:1) with GBP; the Bank of England and HM Treasury remain the anchor monetary and exchange-control authorities for the sterling zone, with UK exchange controls repealed since 1979.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility: HIGH for FCA-licensed UK EMIs and neo-banks (Wise Business, Revolut Business, Currencycloud) and for merchant-of-record PSPs (Paddle Ltd, Lemon Squeezy), which routinely on-board Gibraltar-registered ELPs using a UK-standard KYC pack. Feasibility: MEDIUM for EEA-licensed EMIs (e.g., Payoneer and EU providers) because Gibraltar's automatic EEA financial-services passporting ceased after the Brexit transition and most EU EMIs require a UK branch or treat Gibraltar as a restricted registration territory. Feasibility: LOW for non-EEA offshore digital EMIs and crypto-based payment platforms, which are either unsupported by GFSC-licensed banks or create an unacceptable AML counterparty mismatch. Legal constraint: any entity issuing e-money or holding payments for Gibraltar persons on a commercial basis must be authorised under the Gibraltar Financial Services Act 2019 or rely on an applicable UK/EU transitional passport; using an unlicensed offshore EMI as the ELP's sole operating account would violate GFSC AML expectations and jeopardise the primary bank relationship.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: the partner's bank sends a SWIFT MT103 to the Gibraltar receiving bank (or its UK correspondent at parity) with ISO 20022 purpose code CABB (capital injection) and the narrative CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION - ELP GB__GI__KY_ELP__ISIC_6201_02_CONSUMER_MICRO_SAAS. The wire must reference the ELP name exactly as registered to permit automated posting to the Capital Escrow Account. The receiving bank verifies against the onboarding mandate and may request the capital-contribution resolution and source-of-funds pack for any single transfer above GBP 10,000 or for aggregated linked flows. USD and EUR contributions are credited to the multi-currency incoming account and may be converted into GIP at the interbank 1:1 peg via the bank's FX desk. No central-bank validation or import/export authorisation is required; the bank's compliance check and UBO documentation are the only gatekeepers.For SaaS suppliers the ELP will use: (i) UK Faster Payments or CHAPS for GBP vendors and recurring disbursements; (ii) SEPA Credit Transfer with IBAN for EU vendors (cloud, media, marketing); (iii) US ACH, Fedwire, or MT103 via correspondent for USD vendors (US data centres and ad platforms); (iv) SWIFT MT103 with purpose code MULT or services rendered plus a commercial invoice for residual cross-border vendor payments; and (v) a Commercial Letter of Credit (MT700/MT400) only where a vendor contractually requires bank-backed settlement, which is atypical for a micro-SaaS.
