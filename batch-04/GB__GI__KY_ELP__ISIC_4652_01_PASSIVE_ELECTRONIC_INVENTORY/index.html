Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax under the Income Tax Act 2010, Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (primary portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi; entity registration and annual statutory filings via the Gibraltar Companies House, https://www.companieshouse.gi); customs and import duty administration is vested in HM Customs Gibraltar.

E-government filing portal

On incorporation, the Gibraltar Companies House issues the ELP a statutory registration number. For income tax purposes, the ELP applies to the Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number where it has Gibraltar-source income; non-resident partners are not required to obtain a Gibraltar TIN and may rely on their home-state TIN where the ELP derives no Gibraltar-source income. The ELP must maintain a registered office in Gibraltar, file an annual confirmation statement listing its partners and their non-residence status, and may be required to make an economic substance declaration where it carries on a relevant activity.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists in Gibraltar; fiscal transparency of the ELP is statutory. Available elections: (i) partners may jointly elect an alternative income apportionment under s.84(2) of the Income Tax Act 2010; (ii) the ELP may elect simplified record-keeping and nil-filing treatment where all partners are non-resident and no Gibraltar-source income arises; (iii) for UK-owned structures, transparency is respected for hybrid mismatch purposes under TIOPA 2010 Part 6TA provided the partnership is not itself a taxable person; (iv) an ELP carrying on a relevant activity (including distribution and service centre activity) must elect an economic substance reporting pathway under the Gibraltar Economic Substance Code.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales Use Tax in Gibraltar; the territory is outside the EU VAT area. The applicable regime is the Gibraltar customs import duty system administered by HM Customs Gibraltar, supplemented by excise duties on limited goods (alcohol, tobacco, fuels).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - Gibraltar imposes no turnover-based indirect tax registration threshold because no VAT/GST/sales tax is levied. Import duty is transactional and arises per consignment at the border; a customs declarant authorisation is required for commercial consignments, and electronic components (MLCCs and resistors under Chapter 85) falling under ITA-equivalent tariff lines enter duty-free at 0%.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable as a VAT concept - Gibraltar levies no VAT. Exports of the ELP's passive electronic component inventory are outside the charge to Gibraltar import duty and no zero-rating is required. Where goods are imported into Gibraltar duty-free under Information Technology Agreement-equivalent tariff lines, warehousing and re-export may be conducted under customs supervision without duty payment, with duty relieved on proof of exit.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export/Exit Declaration (electronic transit or re-export message)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill
  • Inward/Outward stock record evidencing duty-free warehousing
  • ITA-equivalent tariff-line certificate where duty relief is claimed

None. Gibraltar does not mandate electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction controls, or digital stamping. Invoices must satisfy the ordinary record-keeping requirements of the Income Tax Act 2010; import and export clearance is lodged electronically through the Gibraltar Customs manifest/single-window system without a fiscal API cryptographic stamping mandate.

Gibraltar law imposes no statutory FX-repatriation deadline and no export VAT clawback mechanism; the stated 180-day benchmark is applied as a prudential AML/tax-transparency covenant. False customs declarations attract penalties under the Customs Duties legislation, including forfeiture of goods and civil penalties of up to 100% of the unpaid duty. Fraudulent claims to territorial non-assessability before the Income Tax Office expose the ELP and its partners to penalties of up to £50,000 plus 100% of the tax sought to be avoided, and may give rise to criminal prosecution under the Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended).

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GIP/GBP enjoy full convertibility with zero exchange controls for non-resident exempted limited partnerships; both current and capital accounts are unrestricted. Profit remittance and partnership distributions flow freely subject to Overseas Territories Sanctions Regulations and GFSC AML reporting. The ELP is outside the charge to Gibraltar income tax on external trading profits (territoriality principle), removing withholding and remittance hurdles; FATF and CRS filing obligations remain.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender timelines or mandatory conversion ratios exist under Gibraltar or United Kingdom law; the entity may retain USD/EUR/GBP receipts in defined multi-currency accounts. Documentary record-keeping under the GFSC AML (Client Due Diligence) Code and CRS/FATCA reporting is required for audit-trail purposes only.

Central bank supervision: Her Majesty's Government of Gibraltar (Commissioner of Currency) issues the Gibraltar pound (GIP) at a statutory 1:1 parity with GBP; bank licensing and prudential supervision are vested in the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC). The Bank of England acts as the de facto monetary anchor; no separate Gibraltar central bank is established.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility: MEDIUM-HIGH with significant caveats. UK-regulated EMIs (Wise Business, Airwallex, Ebury, ClearBank) may onboard a Gibraltar-flag ELP, but generally reject a Cayman Islands exempted limited partnership with 100% external business unless an intrusive UBO pack is provided. Post-Brexit, UK EMIs cannot passport into Gibraltar and may act only as white-label FX/liquidity channels; a GFSC-licensed e-money institution or licensed PSP is required for primary settlement. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% standard Gibraltar corporation tax rate; the Exempted Limited Partnership is tax-transparent, so the rate is applied at the level of partners rather than at the ELP level.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% statutory withholding tax on dividends paid by a Gibraltar-resident company; for the ELP, profit distributions are not dividends and are not subject to Gibraltar withholding tax, regardless of the partner's country of residence.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Return due no later than 12 months after the end of the chargeable period. For a 31 December fiscal year-end, the statutory filing deadline is 31 December of the immediately following year; where the ELP is voluntarily registered with the Gibraltar Income Tax Office, an earlier nil-return deadline may be imposed.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall

Late filing attracts a fixed penalty of GIP 100 per return, plus a daily surcharge of GIP 10 per day after the first six months. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Gibraltar legislation does not prescribe a bright-line Local File / Master File threshold. For this whitepaper, GIP 500,000 of annual cross-border controlled transactions is treated as the prudent threshold triggering a contemporaneous Local File; a Master File is recommended where group turnover exceeds GIP 50,000,000 or controlled intangible dealings exceed GIP 2,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar has implemented Economic Substance Regulations mirroring OECD BEPS Action 5. A passive electronic inventory distribution business is classified as a distribution and service centre and must demonstrate the following in Gibraltar: staff employed in Gibraltar with appropriate qualifications, physical premises from which distribution activities are conducted, active decision-making by the board of the vehicle, and sufficient expenditure incurred within Gibraltar. An ELP that merely holds inventory risk without performing core income-generating activities in Gibraltar will fail the economic substance test and may be deemed a shell company.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion - Misdeclared Partnership Income

Statutory sanction: On conviction, a fine of up to GIP 1,000,000 and imprisonment for up to 7 years; the tax authority may also assess tax on the full net profit for 6 years with a penalty of up to 100% of the tax evaded.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced for fraudulent trading or dishonest evasion. Directors, general partners and shadow managers may be jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax, civil restitution, confiscation orders and international travel bans, in addition to custodial sentences.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: The tax authority may re-determine taxable profits under the arm's-length principle, impose a 100% adjustment penalty, and charge interest from the original due date. Persistently deficient documentation attracts additional daily penalties of GIP 200 per day after written request.

Director personal liability: Directors involved in preparing false transfer pricing evidence may be found personally liable for restitution of taxes; travel bans and director disqualification orders may be imposed where the manipulation is part of a structured evasion arrangement.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Failure to pass the Gibraltar economic substance test triggers an annual financial penalty of up to GIP 100,000, a strike-off direction upon continued failure, and compulsory deregistration with the Companies Registry; the tax office may additionally re-characterise the ELP as a resident corporate taxpayer.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for civil penalties, removal from the Gibraltar register, restrictions on acting as directors for up to 5 years, and criminal liability where a false economic substance declaration was knowingly made.
AML / Sanctions / Dual-Use Export Violation

Statutory sanction: Breaches of the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and Sanctions Regulations carry fines of up to GIP 500,000 and imprisonment for up to 14 years; the assets of the ELP and gains from sanctioned deals are subject to civil forfeiture.

Director personal liability: Directors and the ELP's general partner bear individual criminal responsibility for authorising or not preventing the transaction. Personal liability includes criminal fines, confiscation of personal assets, international travel bans, and exclusion from the financial services sector.
Opaque Beneficial Ownership / False Nominee Arrangement

Statutory sanction: A criminal fine of up to GIP 250,000 and up to 5 years' imprisonment; the ELP's bank accounts may be frozen and the limited partnership struck off without a striking-off order.

Director personal liability: The underlying beneficial owner and any general partner who knowingly permits the arrangement are personally liable for the entire tax charge, civil restitution of all hidden profits, and statutory minimum penalties; travel restrictions are routinely imposed to secure enforcement.
