Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, HM Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office, 6-8 Harbourside, Gibraltar; official portal: www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax

E-government filing portal

An Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) incorporated in Gibraltar must apply to the Registrar of Companies for registration under the Limited Partnerships Act and obtain a Certificate of Registration; thereafter the ELP must register with the Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN/TRN). The application requires: (i) certificate of registration of the ELP; (ii) deed of partnership or limited partnership agreement; (iii) full particulars of all general and limited partners (name, address, jurisdiction of residence and tax residence); (iv) registered office address in Gibraltar; and (v) application form IR1 (or equivalent). No VAT ID is available because Gibraltar is not part of the EU VAT area.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible: the ELP is fiscally transparent under Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010; the partnership itself is not chargeable to income tax; partners are taxable in their own hands. Non-resident partners are within territorial scope only if they carry on a trade in Gibraltar through a permanent establishment; otherwise they are not subject to Gibraltar tax. — An Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) incorporated in Gibraltar must apply to the Registrar of Companies for registration under the Limited Partnerships Act and obtain a Certificate of Registration; thereafter the ELP must register with the Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN/TRN). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible: the ELP is fiscally transparent under Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010; the partnership itself is not chargeable to income tax; partners are taxable in their own hands. Non-resident partners are within territorial scope only if they carry on a trade in Gibraltar through a permanent establishment; otherwise they are not subject to Gibraltar tax.

Available election forms

There is no check-the-box regime in Gibraltar; pass-through treatment is the statutory default. An ELP may nevertheless apply for an Exempt Limited Partnership certificate under s.74 Limited Partnerships Act, which provides certainty that the partnership is treated as transparent and not as a separate taxable unit. For hybrid mismatch purposes, the Taxes (Hybrid Mismatch) Regulations 2019 apply; a partner may file a formal transparency election with the Commissioner to align the Gibraltar treatment with the jurisdiction of any partner where the partnership is treated as opaque there.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Not applicable (Gibraltar has no VAT/GST/sales or use tax; excise and import duties apply to physical goods under the Customs Ordinance 2016)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT/GST registration threshold exists in Gibraltar. A person importing taxable goods may require customs-warehouse or importer registration with HM Customs (Gibraltar) regardless of value; service suppliers are not required to register for indirect tax because no indirect tax is levied on services in Gibraltar.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable: exports of goods and offshore services from Gibraltar are wholly outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect tax because there is no VAT or GST. Consequently there is no zero-rating regime to claim. Suppliers are not required to substantiate zero-rating refunds; however, to evidence non-establishment and export status for foreign regulators, the supplier should retain the customary export documents.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

No real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing or digital stamping mandate in force. Commercial invoices issued in accordance with the Companies Act and Income Tax (Record Keeping) Rules satisfy compliance. If physical goods are imported/exported, the HM Customs Single Administrative Document declaration is filed electronically via customs clearance systems.

In the absence of VAT/GST, there is no export VAT clawback; however, false or misleading customs declarations, or arrangements contrived to obtain a fiscal advantage, may be investigated by the Commissioner and HM Customs. Penalties include fines up to £10,000 or 200% of the duty evaded, criminal liability, and for tax matters retrospective adjustments for up to 6 years (unlimited period in cases of fraud). In addition, treaty abuse and permanent establishment reconstruction may give rise to partner-level tax assessments with clawback of any foreign tax credits erroneously claimed.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The GIP is unconditionally convertible into GBP at parity. Gibraltar maintains no foreign-exchange controls or approval mechanisms on either current account or capital account transactions. A Cayman Islands Exempted Limited Partnership (KY_ELP) may freely inject capital, receive revenue in USD/GBP/EUR, and remit profits to non-resident partners without any convertibility barrier. No exchange control approval is required for profit distribution, loan repayment, or capital repatriation, and Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on distributions to non-resident partners under its territorial tax system.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Gibraltar imposes no statutory requirement to surrender export proceeds, no minimum conversion ratio, and no timeline within which export or digital-service receipts must be repatriated or converted into local currency. Inbound USD proceeds from Apple IAP and Paymentwall settlement may be retained in foreign currency indefinitely.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC); there is no independent Central Bank of Gibraltar – the Government of Gibraltar issues local currency notes under the Currency Notes Act 2011, and the UK's HM Treasury retains ultimate monetary authority. The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is pegged at parity with GBP.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Moderate-to-High. A Gibraltar-licensed Electronic Money Institution (EMI), regulated by the GFSC under the Financial Services (Electronic Money) Regulations 2011, may offer multi-currency virtual IBANs and faster payment rails to a KY_ELP without requiring full commercial bank relationship. However, offshore EMIs incorporated outside Gibraltar cannot passport into Gibraltar post-Brexit; a GFSC-authorised EMI or a UK/EEA EMI with GFSC reverse recognition is required. The GFSC will apply enhanced due diligence because the ELP is a Cayman vehicle, and will require evidence of economic substance, source of funds, and the app's transactional monitoring framework.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound capital injections are executed through SWIFT MT103 wire transfers from the investor's bank to the ELP's Gibraltar-licensed bank account. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% (standard Gibraltar corporate income tax rate; the Exempted Limited Partnership itself is fiscally transparent, and each corporate partner is taxable at 12.5% on its allocable profit share).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Gibraltar imposes no corporate withholding tax on dividends, profit distributions, or dissolution proceeds of an ELP. Because the ELP is transparent, distributions are treated as tax-free returns of capital or previously taxed profits.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

On or before 30 November following the fiscal year-end (Gibraltar tax year aligns to 31 December; a specially approved accounting year may be used for the ELP, with the return due no later than 9 months after the approved year-end).

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Box 1 — Partnership Identification ELP registered name, registration number, registered office in Gibraltar, Gibraltar tax reference, UK TIN if claiming DTA relief, and foreign tax identifier of each general and limited partner. Non-resident limited partners must enter their jurisdiction tax reference, country code, and, where applicable, a UK or Gibraltar treaty certificate. If no Gibraltar-source profits are reported, the Schedule NRP must still be annexed with a declaration that the ELP is not carrying on a trade through a Gibraltar permanent establishment. Late filing triggers an initial penalty of GBP 200 plus a daily surcharge of GBP 40 per day, capped at GBP 3,600 per return. In addition, the Comptroller may issue a determination based on estimated profits and impose a further penalty of up to 100% of the tax under-assessed for failure to produce records. Deliberate withholding of a return after written notice is treated as tax evasion and referred to the Gibraltar Attorney General for criminal prosecution.
Box 2 — Accounting Period and Profits Approved accounting period, turnover, deductible operating expenses, capital allowances claimed, and chargeable profits computed under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act. For non-resident partners, only the share arising from Gibraltar operations or the Gibraltar permanent establishment of the ELP is entered. Foreign-source income allocated but not received or derived through Gibraltar must be excluded and shown in the reconciliation schedule. Late filing triggers an initial penalty of GBP 200 plus a daily surcharge of GBP 40 per day, capped at GBP 3,600 per return. In addition, the Comptroller may issue a determination based on estimated profits and impose a further penalty of up to 100% of the tax under-assessed for failure to produce records. Deliberate withholding of a return after written notice is treated as tax evasion and referred to the Gibraltar Attorney General for criminal prosecution.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GBP 2 million aggregate controlled transaction value per fiscal year, or GBP 1 million for intangible-related payments such as software licensing royalties, triggers mandatory Gibraltar Master File and Local File contemporaneous documentation. A Country-by-Country reporting obligation arises for an ultimate parent group with consolidated revenue above EUR 750 million.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance (Companies and Partnerships) Regulations 2019 apply. The ISIC 6311 video-match operation is assessed as a 'distribution and service centre business' or 'IP business' if brand/software value is stored in Gibraltar. If a relevant activity is carried out, the ELP must prove: (a) management decisions are taken in Gibraltar, (b) full-time employees and physical premises are adequate, (c) core income-generating activities — server administration, user-onboarding, and cross-border payment routing — are performed in Gibraltar, and (d) the ELP is not a shell, mailbox, or conduit. A non-resident ELP engaged solely in passive investment may be treated as a pure equity holding company with a reduced substance test.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporation Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Civil fraud penalties of up to 200% of the unpaid tax; interest at 8% per annum compounded; seizure of operating bank accounts; disqualification of the audit sponsor; criminal fine of GBP 100,000 and up to 7 years’ imprisonment under the Income Tax Act (Gibraltar).

Director personal liability: The Inland Revenue may pierce the ELP veil where the general partner acts as a façade and the UK directors personally operate the app; directors become jointly and severally liable for the entire tax undercharge, face travel bans under the Income Tax (Enforcement) Regulations, and in aggravated cases receive custodial sentence of up to 7 years.
False or Deceptive Filing

Statutory sanction: Recomputation of profit against the Gibraltar general partner; increased liability at 12.5% plus late payment interest; financial penalty of GBP 10,000 per false declaration; referral to the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission for administrative revocation and the issuance of a 'Red Alert' for money-laundering.

Director personal liability: The court may treat the ELP as the alter ego of the controlling UK board, making unpaid corporate tax a personal debt; directors cannot leave Gibraltar while assessment under appeal; intentional concealment carries imprisonment of up to 5 years and an order prohibiting them from acting as a director without prior court consent.
Economic Substance Deficiency

Statutory sanction: Removal of Gibraltar tax residence by the Commissioner; imposition of a penalty equal to 20% of gross profit attributable to the relevant activity; notification to the UK HMRC under the exchange-of-information framework; cost-recovery surcharge of GBP 50,000 for administrative investigation.

Director personal liability: Directors who certify false economic substance declarations commit offences under the Economic Substance Regulations and may be fined up to GBP 15,000 and imprisoned for 3 years; where the company is deemed to be UK resident, UK director disqualification orders and extradition to the UK may also be pursued.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing

Statutory sanction: Primary adjustment restating taxable profits at the 12.5% Gibraltar rate; secondary adjustments treated as additional distributions subject to no WHT but reclassified as capital; penalty of 40% of the avoided tax amount; denial of treaty relief for the related-party royalty flow.

Director personal liability: The legislation attributes responsibility for the ELP’s controlled documentation to the general partner’s board; directors knowingly signing distorted TP returns are civilly liable for the full excess tax plus 10% interest, and may be subject to a 4-year travel restriction barring offshore personal spending allocation.
Anti-Money Laundering and Payment-Rails Abuse

Statutory sanction: Proceeds of Crime (Gibraltar) Act penalties of up to GBP 250,000 or 14 years’ imprisonment per transaction; money-laundering reporting fine of GBP 100,000; suspension of the ELP’s external payment reconciliation licence; freezing orders over all worldwide payment processing accounts.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally accountable under AML obligations as statutory 'relevant persons'; culpability includes imprisonment of 14 years, confiscation orders over app revenues, overseas travel bans, and extradition to the United Kingdom; the general partner may be personally sued for third-party merchant-funding losses.
Shell Company and Beneficial Ownership Non-Disclosure

Statutory sanction: Civil penalties of GBP 100,000 for failure to maintain a natural-person beneficial ownership register in Gibraltar; the Registrar may strike off the ELP and require the general partner to assign profits to the Crown after 3 months; aggravated facts are reported to the Gibraltar Police Financial Intelligence Unit as a shell-company predicate offence.

Director personal liability: Courts in Gibraltar and the UK are prepared to pierce the veil where the ELP is merely a façade for the UK founders; directors and the nominee general partner become jointly liable for all commercial debts of the app, lose any cap on liability under the limited partnership agreement, and may be jailed for up to 10 years for conspiracy to fabricate ownership.
