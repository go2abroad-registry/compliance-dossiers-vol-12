Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (eTax portal); HM Customs Gibraltar for customs and excise matters. UK HMRC is not the direct tax authority for Gibraltar unless a UK permanent establishment or VAT nexus arises.

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity that has no Gibraltar permanent establishment or taxable presence is generally not required to obtain a Gibraltar tax ID. Where a foreign Exempted Limited Partnership establishes a permanent establishment, gains Gibraltar tax residence, or carries on taxable activity in Gibraltar, it must apply to the Gibraltar Income Tax Office for a Tax Reference Number and, if engaged in import/export transactions, register with HM Customs Gibraltar for a customs economic-operator identifier. No VAT registration number exists in Gibraltar because no VAT regime is in force.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — A foreign entity that has no Gibraltar permanent establishment or taxable presence is generally not required to obtain a Gibraltar tax ID. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a check-the-box election; partnership tax transparency is statutory. An Exempted Limited Partnership may elect, through its partnership agreement or by filing a notice with the Income Tax Office, to allocate tax transparent profits among partners. If the ELP is regarded as a hybrid under UK or third-country rules, the relevant UK hybrid mismatch provisions under CTA 2010 Part 6TA may require an election or adjustment to avoid mismatch outcomes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST; limited customs and excise duties administered by HM Customs Gibraltar

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — Gibraltar has no general consumption tax and thus no VAT/GST registration threshold. Importers of dutiable goods must register with HM Customs Gibraltar; no turnover threshold applies for customs registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — Gibraltar does not levy VAT, so no export VAT zero-rating claim can be made. Exports of virgin polyethylene and polypropylene granules are outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect tax. For customs duty purposes, goods held under customs warehousing or inward-processing relief may be exported with no duty charge, and re-exported goods may qualify for duty relief.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration (electronic customs declaration)
  • Bill of Lading or sea waybill (or air waybill where applicable)
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Certificate of Origin where required by the import country or letter of credit
  • Authenticated bank credit or inward remittance certificate evidencing receipt of export proceeds

No real-time fiscal e-invoicing, digital stamping, or API clearance mandate exists in Gibraltar. Commercial paper or electronic invoices are acceptable for business records. Import and export declarations must be lodged through the Gibraltar Customs electronic clearance system, with customs declarations filed electronically and supporting documentation retained for audit.

False declarations, abusive use of customs relief, or misstatement of export proceeds may result in the withdrawal of relief, recovery of unpaid customs duty, civil penalties up to 100% of the duty evaded, forfeiture of goods, and criminal prosecution. From an income tax perspective, a non-resident ELP asserting no taxable presence in Gibraltar may face audit scrutiny if operational substance, management, or effective control indicators are present, leading to retrospective assessment, interest, and penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar applies unrestricted current and capital account convertibility with no exchange-control legislation. The GIP is pegged at par to GBP and is fully interchangeable; all G7, EEA, and major Asian settlement currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, CHF, JPY, CNY) are freely convertible for trade and capital transactions. Profit remittance and partnership distribution hurdles are nil: an Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) may repatriate trading profits, partner capital repayments, and loan repayments without prior approval, withholding taxes, or exchange-control consents. The ELP's pass-through tax classification further ensures that no Gibraltar income-tax barrier attaches to outward distributions, provided the partnership retains economic substance in Gibraltar.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender rule and no mandatory conversion ratio exist in Gibraltar. A bulk petrochemical polymer wholesaler may retain export receipts in any freely convertible currency without converting into GIP or GBP. Nevertheless, prudent treasury practice requires the ELP to maintain segregated foreign-currency receivable accounts for USD and EUR, periodically convert surplus USD into GBP/GIP only to satisfy Gibraltar operating expenditure, substance costs, and partner distribution cash-flow planning, and to evidence such conversions with board-approved treasury policies.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the integrated financial services supervisor; HM Government of Gibraltar as the Gibraltar pound (GIP) issuing authority under the Currency Notes Act; Bank of England as the monetary policy anchor through the GIP/GBP 1:1 currency-board peg. There is no separate foreign-exchange administration because Gibraltar maintains full exchange-control freedom.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW for primary treasury, LC issuance, and high-value bulk settlement; MEDIUM for ancillary operational disbursements below EUR/GBP 50,000. Legal constraint: post-Brexit, no European Economic Area electronic-money institution can passport into Gibraltar; any EMI must obtain separate authorisation from the GFSC as a Payment Institution under the Financial Services (Payment Services) Regulations 2019. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% standard Gibraltar corporate income tax rate under the Income Tax Act 2010; the Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is fiscally transparent, so the charge is levied on partners, each at 12.5% if Gibraltar resident, or at nil on non-resident partners absent a Gibraltar permanent establishment or Gibraltar-source trading income.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on distributions made by a partnership or company to resident or non-resident partners/shareholders.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Returns for a calendar year of assessment are due on or before 30 September of the following year; an ELP with a 31 December accounting period end must file by 30 September of the next calendar year. Estimated payments for Gibraltar-resident partners follow quarterly instalment rules.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Gibraltar statute prescribes no minimum GIP threshold for Local File and Master File; documentation obligations arise when the domestic tax authority determines a prima facie risk of non-arm's length pricing. CbC reporting and notification obligations apply to multinational groups, including the UK parent of the ELP, with consolidated group revenue equal to or exceeding EUR 750 million, aligned with OECD BEPS Action 13. In practice, the ELP should maintain contemporaneous TP documentation for all intercompany polymer purchase and sale transactions wherever group turnover exceeds GIP 10 million.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance (Information and Validation) Regulations 2020 apply. Wholesale distribution of virgin polyethylene and polypropylene granules constitutes a Distribution and Service Centre relevant activity. The ELP must demonstrate that its core income-generating activities — negotiation with suppliers, purchase order execution, inventory risk management, LC financing decisions, and logistics co-ordination — are performed in Gibraltar, with sufficient physical presence, qualified employees, and operating expenditure proportionate to the trading volumes. Outsourcing to the UK parent is permitted for purely auxiliary functions only, and the outsourced functions must be directed and managed from Gibraltar by ELP partners or their authorised delegates.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Under-Declaration

Statutory sanction: Penalty up to 200% of the tax evaded, interest at 8% per annum, and a fine at the discretion of the Supreme Court of Gibraltar; imprisonment of up to 7 years under the Income Tax Act 2010.

Director personal liability: The general partner and any managing partner acting 'knowingly and wilfully' are jointly and severally liable for the full civil tax debt, subject to confiscation orders under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015, travel bans, asset freezing, and criminal imprisonment of up to 7 years.
Shell Company and Abusive Economic Substance Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Failure to satisfy the Economic Substance (Information and Validation) Regulations 2020 attracts a financial penalty of up to GIP 100,000 per relevant activity, plus GIP 10,000 for each month of continued non-compliance; automatic exchange of information with HMRC and potential strike-off of the ELP.

Director personal liability: The Commissioner may apply to the Supreme Court for disqualification orders, and partners may be held personally liable for penalties where the entity is insolvent, with travel restrictions issued under the Court's enforcement jurisdiction.
Anti-Money Laundering / Terrorist Financing Breach

Statutory sanction: Unlimited fine and imprisonment of up to 7 years under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and the AML Regulations 2020; civil penalties levied by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission, public censure, and revocation of banking facilities.

Director personal liability: The general partner is a Relevant Individual under the AML regime, with personal criminal liability for failing to discharge MLRO obligations; personal confiscation of assets, director disqualification up to 15 years, and travel bans pending criminal proceedings.
Transfer Pricing Misstatement and Base Erosion

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner may adjust the chargeable income to the arm's length price, assess additional tax at 12.5% on the adjusted amount, apply interest at 8% per annum, and impose a penalty of up to 200% of the additional tax liability.

Director personal liability: Where the transfer-pricing adjustment renders the ELP unable to meet its liabilities, the general partner is liable for the tax debt; deliberate mispricing attracts fraudulent trading liability and imprisonment under the Companies Act 2014.
Late Filing, Record-Keeping and Information Disclosure Failure

Statutory sanction: Fixed penalty of GIP 200, increasing by GIP 50 per day up to GIP 1,500; any subsequent year of non-compliance escalates the fixed penalty to GIP 1,000; adverse inference may be drawn against the ELP in the Commissioner's assessment.

Director personal liability: The general partner who caused the default is jointly and severally liable for all penalties incurred, and the Commissioner may seek a magistrate's warrant to impose per diem custodial sanction for continuing non-compliance with a court-ordered information notice.
