Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office is not permissible for registered office purposes under the Companies Act 2014 and Limited Partnerships Act 2017 of Gibraltar, unless a licensed registered office provider maintains a physical address in Gibraltar that can receive service of process and official mail. The remote/virtual arrangement is only acceptable at the level of a 'service office' for mail forwarding, but the statutory registered office must be a physical street address in Gibraltar. For an exempted limited partnership formed under the Limited Partnerships Act 2017, the registered office must be maintained at the address of its licensed registered office holder or a corporate services provider regulated by the GFSC. Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar (jurisdiction GI): Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar, 42 Parliament Lane, Gibraltar. Official portal: www.gibraltar.gov.gi (Taxation section; e-services for registered taxpayers). The UK's HMRC is not the registration authority for this jurisdiction; HMRC interaction is limited to any future UK VAT trigger for B2C digital supplies, which the B2B newswire API model avoids under the UK reverse charge.

E-government filing portal

Step 1 - Incorporate the ELP with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under the Exempted Limited Partnerships Act 2014 and obtain the Certificate of Registration (no minimum capital; non-resident ownership permitted). Step 2 - Submit the Income Tax Office registration application along with the ELP deed, certificate of limited partnership, and particulars of general partners in order to obtain a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number for the ELP. Step 3 - File the partnership's initial tax status declaration confirming whether the partners are non-Gibraltar-resident and whether the ELP derives any Gibraltar-source income; this preserves pass-through/territorial treatment. Step 4 - For each foreign partner, record the partner's home-jurisdiction TIN for CRS/FATCA and DAC6 reporting. Foreign partners with no Gibraltar-source income have no withholding obligation; the ELP does not require a tax representative unless the Income Tax Office requests one for a transparent vehicle.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible with Territorial_Scope: the ELP is not a taxpayer in its own right; each partner is taxed on its share of profit. Non-resident partners are chargeable only on Gibraltar-source or Gibraltar-attributable income, so an ELP serving overseas syndicated finance clients has a 0% effective Gibraltar tax rate on export subscription revenue.

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not offer a US-style check-the-box election; fiscal transparency is statutory and mandatory for exempted limited partnerships. The ELP deed may allocate profit shares, which governs the transparent attribution of income among partners, subject to general anti-abuse and transfer-pricing rules. There is no Gibraltar hybrid mismatch election; however, UK-resident partners must apply TIOPA 2010 Part 6 hybrid mismatch rules, and the ELP contract should include a partner tax-indemnity clause. Grandfathered exempt-company-style elections are not available for newly registered entities; the ELP simply relies on territorial sourcing and transparency.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST or general Sales and Use Tax. Gibraltar levies only border-type import duties, excise duties (alcohol, tobacco, fuel) and a customs primage surcharge under the Gibraltar Customs Tariff. The syndicated finance newswire API is an electronically supplied service outside the indirect tax base; the applicable structural rate is 0%.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable in GIP; no consumption-tax registration threshold exists. Only importers of dutiable physical goods must register with HM Customs of Gibraltar; no turnover-based registration, remote-seller threshold, or distance-selling rule applies to the API service.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For pure Gibraltar purposes, export income is effectively zero-rated by territoriality: (i) the ELP as a pass-through entity is not chargeable; (ii) non-resident partners are chargeable only on profits from a Gibraltar permanent establishment or Gibraltar-source services; (iii) subscription fees from overseas banks and institutional customers for the ISIC 6391 real-time financial newswire API are sourced outside Gibraltar where contracting, performance and delivery infrastructure are located offshore. A 0% effective rate applies and no VAT refund claim is needed because no output tax is ever charged. If the ELP were to interact with UK VAT, B2B supplies to UK customers fall under the UK reverse charge (VATA 1994 s7A), so the ELP must not charge VAT; a UK VAT registration would be needed only for B2C digital sales. Customer VAT registration IDs and the API licence contract should expressly state the B2B place-of-supply basis.

None for Gibraltar. There is no real-time fiscal clearance, SAF-T reporting, government e-invoicing network, or digital stamping mandate. Commercial PDF/XML invoices and underlying bank evidence are sufficient. If the ELP ever triggers a UK VAT registration (B2C digital supplies only), HMRC Making Tax Digital record-keeping would apply, but no invoice pre-clearance API is required.

Gibraltar: incorrect or fraudulent returns trigger penalties up to 100% of the tax undercharged, daily default penalties, interest, and a six-year assessment window (twenty years for fraud or negligent concealment); a clawback of territorial/transparent treatment occurs if the Commissioner finds a Gibraltar-source trade or permanent establishment. UK interface: any false zero-rating or VAT refund claim exposes the ELP to recovery of VAT with interest, FA 2007 Schedule 24 inaccuracy penalties of up to 100% of lost VAT, Schedule 41 registration penalties, and potential criminal prosecution for dishonest evasion. Documentation must be retained for six years from the relevant year of assessment; the 180-day FX benchmark is a documentary substantiation standard rather than a statutory Gibraltar remittance deadline.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar maintains no separate exchange control regime; the United Kingdom's post-1979 liberalised framework applies. Sterling, US dollars, and euros are fully convertible for both current and capital account transactions. Profit remittance from the Cayman Islands Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) to non-resident partners is unrestricted, subject to evidence of economic substance or tax transparency classification and anti-money laundering verification. No Gibraltar withholding tax applies to distributions of a non-Uk domiciled partnership, and no prior regulatory approval is required for dividend, interest, or fee payments to overseas beneficiaries. Practical friction may arise from counterparty risk-based due diligence on the Cayman vehicle, but no statutory currency controls or convertibility hurdles exist.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory requirement in Gibraltar or the United Kingdom to surrender export proceeds, repatriate foreign currency, or convert export receipts into local currency. Mandatory conversion ratios are nil. For the financial newswire API subscription business, foreign currency receipts may be retained in multicurrency treasury accounts, and the ELP may freely allocate proceeds to operating expenses, investments, or partner distributions. Banking onboarding, however, requires a documented source-of-funds trail, and any unusually large inbound settlement from a foreign payment processor (e.g., Stripe payouts) must be supported by subscription contracts and transaction reports.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC); Bank of England (as ultimate monetary and exchange control authority for Gibraltar, which forms part of the United Kingdom exchange control territory)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: High for UK Financial Conduct Authority-authorised electronic money institutions (EMIs) and Gibraltar-licensed payment institutions, but Moderate for EU/EEA EMIs post-Brexit because Gibraltar is not in the EEA and passporting rights no longer apply. Legal constraints: (1) An offshore EMI must be authorised by the GFSC or operate through an authorised agent to service Gibraltar-based accounts; (2) UK FCA-authorised EMIs may onboard the Cayman ELP as a low-risk commercial client if the beneficial owners meet enhanced due diligence; (3) The ELP's business model of syndicated financial news delivery is permissible without a payment licence, but the EMI must not be used for unlicensed credit, lending, or speculative FX dealing; (4) The use of Stripe as a payment facilitation rail is viable, but Stripe payouts must be routed to a settlement bank account maintained under a GFSC-regulated institution to avoid unregulated money service business exposure.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections into the Cayman ELP's Gibraltar treasury account must be initiated via SWIFT MT103 from the partner's funding bank or the ELP's fund administrator. The payment instruction should clearly reference the underlining capital contribution, using purpose code 'ECIC' (Enterprise/Investment Capital) or 'B040' (Investment in a non-resident partnership) where required by the remitting institution. The receiving bank may request a capital contribution certificate, the executing subscription agreement, and a source-of-wealth questionnaire for each limited partner. All funds must originate from an account in the name of the contributor; third-party funding or third-party deposits are prohibited. The ELP should pre-clear the expected inbound amount with the Gibraltar bank to ensure compliance with internal fraud thresholds and to avoid the escalations for unexplained incomings.Outbound trade settlements from the ELP's Gibraltar account will be executed through: (1) SWIFT MT103 for low-value and mid-value supplier payments, use of the 'CRED' instruction code and a full telegraphic transfer narrative; (2) Commercial Letters of Credit (LC) for high-value syndicated content licensing or infrastructure procurement, issued by a tier-1 Gibraltar bank or confirmed by a London clearing bank; (3) SEPA Credit Transfer for euro-denominated disbursements to European content providers, leveraging the ELP's Gibraltar bank's SEPA membership; (4) Faster Payment Service (FPS) for urgent domestic GBP payments to UK-based technology vendors; (5) Stripe Connect or Stripe Treasury for API-driven payout distributions to the ELP's sub-licensees, provided the underlying activity remains non-regulated. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: High for UK Financial Conduct Authority-authorised electronic money institutions (EMIs) and Gibraltar-licensed payment institutions, but Moderate for EU/EEA EMIs post-Brexit because Gibraltar is not in the EEA and passporting rights no longer apply. Legal constraints: (1) An offshore EMI must be authorised by the GFSC or operate through an authorised agent to service Gibraltar-based accounts; (2) UK FCA-authorised EMIs may onboard the Cayman ELP as a low-risk commercial client if the beneficial owners meet enhanced due diligence; (3) The ELP's business model of syndicated financial news delivery is permissible without a payment licence, but the EMI must not be used for unlicensed credit, lending, or speculative FX dealing; (4) The use of Stripe as a payment facilitation rail is viable, but Stripe payouts must be routed to a settlement bank account maintained under a GFSC-regulated institution to avoid unregulated money service business exposure.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

10% standard corporate rate under the Income Tax Act 2010; the Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is fiscally transparent and is not itself chargeable to corporate income tax.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% domestic rate: Gibraltar does not levy withholding tax on dividends, partnership distributions, or profit remittances.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory where gross controlled transactions exceed GBP 1,000,000 per accounting period or related-party interest-bearing debt exceeds GBP 600,000. Master File and Country-by-Country Report thresholds apply to multinational groups with consolidated turnover of EUR 750,000,000 or more.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations apply exclusively to 'relevant activities': banking, insurance, fund management, financing, leasing, headquarters, distribution, service centres, shipping, and IP holding. Financial news aggregation and API syndication (ISIC 6391) is not a relevant activity; the ELP is therefore outside the enhanced ESR filing net. Nevertheless, the General Partner must substantiate a minimum economic presence in Gibraltar (physical premises, resolvable employees, or qualifying outsourcing) to preserve the non-resident partners' 0% effective tax outcome and to avoid characterization as a shell.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Concealment

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of up to 200% of the tax evaded; interest at 8% per annum; criminal conviction on indictment leading to an unlimited fine and up to 7 years' imprisonment and forfeiture of the proceeds of the newswire syndication.

Director personal liability: The General Partner is jointly and severally liable for civil restitution and penalties out of personal assets; courts may impose a travel ban, sequestration of assets, and disqualification from managing any Gibraltar partnership.
Shell Entity / Sham Substance

Statutory sanction: Substance denial: recharacterization of all profits as Gibraltar-source, assessment at the 10% corporate rate, penalty for material misdeclaration of up to GBP 100,000, and possible strike-off from the Register of Limited Partnerships.

Director personal liability: General Partner liable for the full reassessed tax; civil restitution to the Crown; travel bans under tax-debt enforcement; disqualification of the relevant directors for up to 3 years.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing / Thin Capitalization

Statutory sanction: Compensatory adjustment of taxable profits, disallowance of excess interest, penalty of up to 100% of the additional tax, and referral to the UK-Gibraltar tax information exchange mechanism.

Director personal liability: The General Partner is liable for the TP adjustment and penalties; individuals who knowingly certified false Box 8 declarations may be prosecuted for tax fraud and banned from serving as officers.
Anti-Money Laundering Reporting Failure

Statutory sanction: Regulated-sector penalties under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 of up to GBP 1,000,000; confiscation orders on the revenue stream; imprisonment of up to 5 years for the MLRO or General Partner who knowingly fails to report.

Director personal liability: Directors and the General Partner face personal criminal liability, unlimited personal fines, and confiscation of personal property where wilful money laundering is established.
Unauthorized Registration / Return Invalidity

Statutory sanction: Invalidation of the filing, loss of the statutory due date, restoration costs, pecuniary penalties per violation, and refusal to issue a tax clearance certificate for the ELP.

Director personal liability: Officers are personally liable for continuing penalties; the Commissioner may collect from personal bank accounts and impose a travel restriction until all filings and payments are remediated.
