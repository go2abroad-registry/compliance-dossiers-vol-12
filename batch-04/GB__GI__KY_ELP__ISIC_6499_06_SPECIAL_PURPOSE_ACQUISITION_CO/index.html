Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (3 Marina View, Gibraltar) - principal direct-tax authority and TIN issuer; Gibraltar Customs and Excise Department (Customs House, Midtown, Gibraltar) - competent authority for indirect taxes, import/export duties, and trader registration; Registrar of Companies, Companies House Gibraltar - partnership incorporation registry feeding tax registration. Digital e-portal: the Income Tax Office MyGOV e-filing portal (https://portal.gibraltar.gov.gi) for direct-tax filings, and the UK Customs Declaration Service (CDS) for customs clearance.

E-government filing portal

For a non-resident SPAC sponsor forming a Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership: (1) Incorporate the ELP under the Limited Partnerships Act 2015 via the Registrar of Companies; obtain the partnership registration number and Certificate of Registration. (2) Register the ELP with the Income Tax Office within 30 days of formation using the Partnership Income Tax Registration Form (CFP-1), supplying the partnership deed, subscribers' particulars, and profit-share ratios, to receive a Gibraltar Tax Reference (partnership TIN). (3) Each non-resident partner must separately evidence their own foreign TIN; the ELP is transparent and does not consolidate partners' tax identifications. (4) Apply to the Customs and Excise Department for a UK-recognised EORI number (GB or XI series) before undertaking any import or export transaction. (5) For SPAC escrow wiring, the ELP TIN and each partner's TIN must be appended to SWIFT MT103 instructions and the trust/custody account documentation. (6) Where the ELP performs regulated activity, submit the relevant Gibraltar Financial Services Commission licence application concurrently, as the GFSL licence number becomes part of the tax file supporting any deemed collective investment vehicle election.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible - Under the Income Tax Act 2010, a partnership is fiscally transparent: each partner is assessed directly on their share of Gibraltar-source income, and the ELP bears no entity-level income tax. Territorial Scope - only profits derived from or in Gibraltar are chargeable; SPAC escrow interest, warrant premium, custody income, and deal-advance fees sourced and executed outside Gibraltar fall outside the charge but remain reportable on informative returns with partner attribution. Tax_Exempt_Zone - not applicable; the former exempt status regime has been abolished and replaced by the territorial basis of assessment.

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no US-style check-the-box election; however, the Income Tax Act 2010 and the Taxation (Hybrid and Mismatch) (Gibraltar) Regulations 2020 permit: (i) a transparent/opaque consistency election under which the ELP may, with Commissioner approval, be treated as a separate taxable person for domestic administrative purposes where a partner's home jurisdiction (e.g., the US for federal check-the-box purposes) classifies the ELP as opaque, thereby neutralising double non-taxation; (ii) a deemed Collective Investment Vehicle (CIV) election allowing pooled treatment of SPAC investor escrow income and consolidated FATCA/CRS reporting; and (iii) a designated hybrid mismatch election providing that any deduction claimed in a foreign jurisdiction without a corresponding income inclusion in Gibraltar triggers primary adjustment in Gibraltar and secondary responding-party adjustment in the partner's jurisdiction.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar Customs and Excise Duties Regime (no VAT, GST, or sales/use tax is imposed in Gibraltar; taxation is limited to customs import duty, excise duty on specific goods, and duties under the UK/Gibraltar customs union arrangements in force from 31 December 2020)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable: no VAT or GST registration threshold exists in Gibraltar because no such tax is levied. Mandatory customs registration for an EORI number applies from the first import or export transaction, with zero economic threshold. Duty liability is calculated on the customs value of each declaration; tariffs average 0% on most industrial goods under the UK Global Tariff applied to Gibraltar but can reach 25% on sensitive agricultural products; excise duty applies to alcohol, tobacco, and hydrocarbons. The minimum accounting floor for periodic duty payment is GIP 1,500 per declaration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Strict VAT export zero-rating is inapplicable as Gibraltar has no VAT. The equivalent reliefs are: (1) full duty suspension or exemption for goods physically exported from Gibraltar to a third-country destination outside the UK/Gibraltar customs territory within 30 days of release; (2) outward processing relief and duty drawback on previously imported goods re-exported in an altered state; and (3) for the SPAC's exported financial services, the ELP may claim that profits are outside the charge to Gibraltar income tax as non-Gibraltar-source income, provided that no Gibraltar-resident counterparty exists, central management and control are exercised abroad, the escrow interest arises from non-Gibraltar financial institutions, and all contract negotiation, execution, and custody instructions occur outside Gibraltar.

  • Customs Export Declaration (CDS Entry / Single Administrative Document C88 lodged electronically with the National Clearance Hub using the ELP's UK-recognised EORI)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical loading, transport routing, and exit of goods from the Gibraltar customs territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 bank confirmation evidencing receipt of export consideration into the ELP's escrow or operating account
  • Commercial invoice and packing list in duty-compliant format (or, for exported financial services, the trust/escrow custody statement evidencing offshore performance)
  • Certificate of Origin or EUR.1/ex-1 movement certificate where preferential tariff treatment is claimed

Real-time electronic clearance is mandatory for all goods movements: each import/export declaration must be lodged electronically on the UK Customs Declaration Service (CDS) via the National Clearance Hub (Leeds) using a UK-recognised EORI, with pre-arrival and pre-departure notification completed before frontier crossing; commercial invoices, packing lists, and transport documents must be attached in digital format. There is no national fiscal e-invoicing, digital stamping, or real-time sales-reporting regime for domestic services because Gibraltar levies no VAT/GST; however, SPAC custody and management invoices must be retained as digitally signed, time-stamped records satisfying anti-money laundering audit requirements.

Export duty-relief and outside-the-charge claims are audited by the Compliance and Anti-Avoidance units of the Income Tax Office under the Income Tax Act 2010, and customs claims by the Customs and Excise Department. Penalties: (1) negligent misdeclaration attracts a penalty of up to 100% of the duty avoided; (2) fraudulent misdeclaration or counterfeit documentation attracts a penalty of up to 200% of the duty avoided, criminal fines up to GIP 1,000,000, and imprisonment up to 10 years under the Crimes Act 2011 and applicable customs legislation; (3) retrospective clawback applies to all partnership profits attributed to the misclaimed years with interest at 4% per annum and a 20% surcharge, enforced jointly and severally against the general partner; and (4) for a SPAC vehicle, adverse findings trigger revocation of tax classification, disqualification of the listed entity, mandatory disclosure to the acquisition exchange, and potential civil liability to escrow investors for negligent misrepresentation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility for all current and capital account transactions. GIP is pegged at parity to GBP (1 GIP = 1.0000 GBP) and both currencies circulate interchangeably in a currency-board style arrangement, eliminating redemption friction for SPAC proceeds, capital contributions, and profit remittances. The Exchange Control Act 1972 is not in force, and there are no exchange control permits, no restrictions on the import/export of capital, no prior approval requirements for the transfer of trust/escrow proceeds to non-resident limited partners, and no statutory withholding tax on partnership distributions or interest payments to foreign investors. Capital account transactions, including the subscription and redemption of partnership interests in the Exempted Limited Partnership, are fully liberalized; the SPAC may issue units and hold restricted cash in USD within Gibraltar banks without exchange control registration. The only practical constraint is the absence of a dedicated GIP offshore derivative market; hedging must be executed in the underlying GBP or USD deliverable book.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No surrender obligation, no statutory mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation timeline for export proceeds or any receivable entering the ELP's books. Gibraltar law (including the Financial Services Act 2019 and the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015) does not impose currency conversion requirements; commercial parties may invoice and settle in GIP, GBP, USD, EUR, or any freely convertible counterpart. For the SPAC treasury, IPO proceeds remitted as USD from the US trustee may be retained in USD indefinitely without mandatory conversion to GIP or GBP, and the ELP may choose its functional treasury currency (recommended USD) for accounting clarity in a future business combination.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the prudential and conduct authority for banking, securities, and fiduciary services; Government of Gibraltar Currency Commission as the statutory issuer of Gibraltar pound (GIP) banknotes; Bank of England as the monetary policy anchor supplying sterling (GBP) as parallel legal tender; HM Treasury retaining ultimate currency sovereignty for the British Overseas Territory of Gibraltar. No separate Gibraltar central bank exists; the GFSC administers licensing, AML/CFT supervision, and systemic oversight under the Financial Services Act 2019 and the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Medium for operational treasury and payment flows, Low for the statutory SPAC escrow. Under the Financial Services Act 2019, certain Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) passported or locally licensed in Gibraltar—including Wise (Gibraltar) and regulated Euro-EEA EMIs operating under temporary permissions—can provide multi-currency operating accounts, low-cost outward SWIFT-equivalent transfers, and FX conversion for ordinary SPAC working expenses (legal, audit, due diligence fees, and per-diem expenses). However, EMIs cannot serve as the custodian of the SPAC's public offering proceeds, because the blank-check business mandate requires a banking-licensed institution maintaining segregated trust/escrow accounts in compliance with the Financial Services (Capital Adequacy) Rules and the Investment Services Act 2019 investor-asset protection requirements. The de facto construction is a dual-rail treasury: (1) a Tier 1 bank trust account for all equity proceeds, and (2) an EMI account capped at the lesser of £50,000 or 20% of total operating budget for disbursement agility, specifically avoiding any mingling of restricted and unrestricted funds.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound protocol for the ELP receiving IPO or private-placement proceeds: (1) remittance via SWIFT MT103 with field 70 value line 'SPAC ESCROW RELEASE/ GBSPACUS / ELP PARTNERSHIP NO. GI-XXXX / CUSIP XXXX' (alternative purpose codes: T51 for settlement of public offering, S103 for securities operations) to the Gibraltar International Bank Ltd or Trusted Novus Bank account; (2) wire must originate from the named US trustee bank account (e.g., Wilmington Trust, N.A.) and remitter must be pre-notified to the Gibraltar bank to match expected flow; (3) include ISIN or CUSIP, the ELP's legal entity identifier (LEI) in field 50K or 72, and a PDF copy of the escrow release instruction letter; (4) the receiving Gibraltar bank verifies the source against the sponsor's approved fiat limit, and for wires exceeding GBP 10,000,000, it requires a pre-received authenticated SWIFT MT199 or a tested telex from the sending institution. For equity injections by limited partners not linked to the IPO, applicable purpose code is CAP (capital contribution) with full UBO package.Trade settlement and business-combination clearing rails: (1) Standard cross-border vendor payments - SWIFT MT103 using SEPA or CHAPS/EBA clearing for EUR/GBP legs, USD via CHIPS or Fedwire for US counterparties; (2) Acquisition consideration at closing - SWIFT MT202 COV for interbank cover transfer, with MT103 for beneficiary notification, TARGET2 for EUR leg, and SWIFT gpi for end-to-end tracking; (3) Commercial Letters of Credit - issued under UCP600 (ICC Uniform Customs and Practice), typically MT700 form, for deferred earnout or post-closing working capital contributions where the acquisition target requires bank-undertaking security; (4) Intra-Gibraltar clearing - daily net settlement via the Gibraltar Settlement System (operated by the Ministry of Finance) for same-day GIP/GBP transfers between local banks; (5) Multi-currency treasury sweeps - automated sweep from USD operating account to GIP/GBP overnight deposit using pre-arranged FX standing instruction with a limit of GBP 5,000,000 per day and settlement T+1, avoiding GIP/USD market illiquidity. All outbound payments above GPB 25,000 require the bank's sanction screening and same-day compliance approval; payments in excess of GBP 250,000 require supporting acquisition documentation acceptance by the bank's transaction monitoring unit.Vanilla GBP/USD Foreign Exchange Forward (up to 12 month tenor, deliverable, cleared via CLS) - the primary hedge for the SPAC's GBP-denominated operating expenses against USD proceeds held in the trust account.Cross-Currency Swap (GBP/USD and EUR/USD) - permitted for interest-rate/currency overlays when the SPAC assumes fixed-rate debt in an acquisition target operating in EUR or GBP.FX Options and Zero-Cost Collars (GBP/USD and USD/EUR) - permissible for protecting the minimum trust release value against material sterling depreciation; premiums paid from the unrestricted fee account.Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDF) - permitted for exposure to non-deliverable target-market currencies (e.g., Nigerian Naira, Kazakhstan Tenge, emerging Asian currencies) where no GIP/USD deliverable book exists; settlement against a published reference rate and ISDA 2002 Master Agreement framework.Currency Swap with the Government of Gibraltar Currency Commission - not a market instrument, but in practice GIP/GBP conversion at par is available for unlimited amounts, rendering the GIP conversion risk effectively zero; the ELP may utilize this statutory peg for treasury bookings.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility Rating: Medium for operational treasury and payment flows, Low for the statutory SPAC escrow. Under the Financial Services Act 2019, certain Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) passported or locally licensed in Gibraltar—including Wise (Gibraltar) and regulated Euro-EEA EMIs operating under temporary permissions—can provide multi-currency operating accounts, low-cost outward SWIFT-equivalent transfers, and FX conversion for ordinary SPAC working expenses (legal, audit, due diligence fees, and per-diem expenses). However, EMIs cannot serve as the custodian of the SPAC's public offering proceeds, because the blank-check business mandate requires a banking-licensed institution maintaining segregated trust/escrow accounts in compliance with the Financial Services (Capital Adequacy) Rules and the Investment Services Act 2019 investor-asset protection requirements. The de facto construction is a dual-rail treasury: (1) a Tier 1 bank trust account for all equity proceeds, and (2) an EMI account capped at the lesser of £50,000 or 20% of total operating budget for disbursement agility, specifically avoiding any mingling of restricted and unrestricted funds.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File documentation is required where the aggregate value of controlled transactions exceeds GIP 1,000,000; Master File documentation is required for a group with consolidated turnover above EUR 750,000,000. An exempt ELP that is tax transparent is generally outside the corporate transfer pricing rules unless it holds a taxable Gibraltar source activity.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance Act 2019 and associated regulations require the filing of an annual Economic Substance Return for an entity carrying on 'relevant activities'. A SPAC/blank-check vehicle is not a pure holding company when it actively directs acquisitions and treasury management; consequently, if classified as 'financing' or 'fund management', it must demonstrate adequate physical premises, employees, board decision-making and expenditure proportionality in Gibraltar, and file ESR Form ESR-1 annually.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty equal to 100% of the tax evaded plus interest; criminal fine up to GBP 50,000; Commissioner may issue a further GBP 100-per-day default notice; the ELP's exemption is retrospectively revoked.

Director personal liability: The General Partner and its directors incur joint and several liability for the partnership's unpaid tax; personal assets can be seized in civil enforcement; travel bans may be imposed pending prosecution; imprisonment for up to 14 years on fraud conviction.
Shell Company / No Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Compulsory strike-off and deregistration; forfeiture of the exempt status; penalty of GIP 10,000 per omitted ESR return; denial of tax advantages for a period of 5 years.

Director personal liability: Directors may be disqualified for up to 15 years; each director is personally liable for the wind‑up costs of the Commissioner and for civil damages to minority investors who relied on Gibraltarian substance representations.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Red Flag

Statutory sanction: Unlimited fine under the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act and FSC regulations; freezing and confiscation order over the entire SPAC trust account; withdrawal of any trust or company service provider licence.

Director personal liability: Directors and the General Partner face imprisonment up to 14 years; confiscation orders may attach to personal bank accounts and pension funds; re-entry bans and travel restrictions apply while proceedings are pending.
Misreporting and GAAR Abuse

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar General Anti-Abuse Rule (s.96A) recharacterises the transaction; penalty of 100% of the tax advantage is imposed; disclosure penalties up to GIP 20,000; the Finance Centre Director can revoke the partnership's registration with immediate effect.

Director personal liability: The General Partner and shadow directors are jointly and severally liable for the entire tax advantage plus penal costs; courts may issue a 10-year civil prohibition order; custodial sentences of up to 7 years apply for forgery and fraudulent filing.
