Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for an Exempted Limited Partnership. The partnership agreement governs capital contributions, which may be paid in cash, promissory note, or in-kind contribution. No escrow account requirement is imposed by the Registrar; however, if banking facilities are sought, the ELP's bank may require a minimum capital deposit as a matter of internal AML/KYC policy.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar; 12 Bishop's Court, Bayside Business Park, Gibraltar (portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi; filings and TIN applications submitted through registered tax agents).

E-government filing portal

An Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) must first be registered with the Registrar of Companies under the Limited Partnerships Act 2010. Within 21 days of registration, the ELP files a tax registration application with the Income Tax Office to obtain a Tax Identification Number (TIN). Required documents: (1) Certificate of Registration of the ELP; (2) particulars of the Gibraltar-resident general partner; (3) particulars of the licensed management company acting as agent; (4) written confirmation of tax-transparent treatment under s.50 Income Tax Act 2010; (5) for each non-resident limited partner, a Certificate of Tax Residence from the foreign jurisdiction to substantiate territorial exemption. TIN issuance typically completes within 5-10 business days.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible — the ELP is fiscally transparent; no entity-level income tax is chargeable on the partnership. Each partner is taxed on their distributive share of ELP profits. Non-resident partners benefit from territorial scope: only Gibraltar-source income is within the charge, while income from international bonded trucking, airport feeder services, and cross-border freight operations performed outside Gibraltar is generally exempt.

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists under Gibraltar law; tax transparency for ELPs is automatic and statutory under the Income Tax Act 2010 and the Limited Partnerships Act 2010. The ELP may seek an advance ruling from the Commissioner of Income Tax on source attribution of freight income. Hybrid mismatch rules (OECD BEPS Action 2, implemented by Regulation) require a partner whose home jurisdiction treats the ELP as opaque to align tax character; failure to disclose may result in denial of deductions or withholding relief in the partner's jurisdiction.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT system — Gibraltar is outside the UK VAT area and the EU VAT Directive. Indirect taxation comprises Customs and Excise Duties under the Customs Ordinance (Cap. 52) and duties on fuel, alcohol, and tobacco.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — Gibraltar imposes no VAT/GST turnover threshold. The customs-bonded truck fleet operator must register with Gibraltar Customs Duty Office as an authorised transit operator (AEO status or customs transitor approval) and obtain an Income Tax TIN irrespective of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Rate of 0% VAT is legally inapplicable in Gibraltar as no VAT system exists. For customs duty purposes, goods moving under customs transit (CTC/TIR) in the bonded truck fleet are subject to duty suspension: no import duty is levied where goods remain in temporary storage or transit and are re-exported within prescribed customs timelines. Cross-border logistics services supplied by the ELP to non-resident partners fall outside the Gibraltar income tax charge under territorial sourcing rules.

  • Customs Transit Declaration (NCTS/TIR electronic accompanying document) or Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading or CMR International Consignment Note
  • Inward Remittance Certificate evidencing settlement of freight fees
  • GPS-Seal Event Log for customs-bonded fleet audit integrity

Gibraltar imposes no real-time e-invoicing clearance, API fiscal stamping, or e-invoicing mandate. Paper and electronic invoicing are both permitted; records must be retained for 6 years under the Income Tax Act 2010 and Companies Act 2014. Customs transit movements require electronic filing via the NCTS (New Computerised Transit System) under the Common Transit Convention and TIR procedure, including a Transit Accompanying Document (TAD) and prior electronic TIR declaration at the point of departure.

Under the Customs Ordinance (Cap. 52), false transit declarations and export misdeclarations attract forfeiture of the goods or conveyance, a penalty of up to three times the duty value, and imprisonment up to 2 years on conviction. Under the Income Tax Act 2010, incorrect returns attract penalties up to 200% of the tax underpaid plus late-payment interest; fraudulent evasion is indictable. Gibraltar Customs and HM Revenue and Customs (UK) cooperate under the post-Brexit Trade, Cooperation and Economic Arrangement, enabling retrospective clawback audits of transit irregularities and exchange of fiscal information for export substantiation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GIP is fully convertible with GBP at parity, and Gibraltar applies the same IMF Article VIII current-account and capital-account liberalization regime as the United Kingdom. There are no exchange-control approvals, no FDI screening thresholds, and no profit remittance restrictions for non-resident partners of a Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership; however, prudence requires an Economic Substance filing, an ELP tax treatment certificate from the Gibraltar Income Tax Office, and a bankable distribution policy to avoid re-characterization as a passive foreign investment arrangement. EUR and USD trade receipts may be held in separate currency accounts without forced conversion into GIP or GBP.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Gibraltar imposes no statutory timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no surrender obligation on export receipts; the customs bonded truck fleet may retain USD/EUR balances freely. Notwithstanding this, the ELP must maintain operative cash-flow records for GFSC AML/TF supervision, and any cash receipt or physical import/export transfer exceeding EUR 10,000 equivalent must be reported through the GFSC Financial Intelligence Unit reporting gateway as part of standard record-keeping obligations.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC), Banking Supervision Division, and the Government of Gibraltar Commissioner of Currency acting under the Currency Notes Act 1934; the Bank of England is the sovereign issuer anchoring the Gibraltar pound (GIP) at statutory 1:1 parity with GBP. There is no independent Gibraltar central bank, and all exchange-control authority is effectively delegated to Her Majesty's Treasury through the UK-Gibraltar monetary arrangements.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-LOW as a primary clearing rail and HIGH only as an auxiliary sub-ledger. GFSC regulates e-money institutions and payment services under the Financial Services (Electronic Money) Act 2021 and Payment Services Act 2019; UK-regulated EMIs such as Revolut Business, Wise, or Tide cannot passport into Gibraltar post-Brexit and are legally barred from holding customs bond guarantees or bonded duty escrow. A GFSC-licensed EMI may be used for minor ACH (US) vendor payouts and SEPA small-value disbursements if it accepts the Gibraltar-licensed GP KYC pack and reconciles daily to the tier-1 operating bank; any such arrangement must be disclosed to the principal banker as a shadow sub-account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant capital injections use SWIFT MT103 from the partner's home bank to the ELP's GBP/USD account at Gibraltar International Bank or NatWest International. SWIFT field 50K must disclose the true originating partner; field 59 must name the ELP with its registered office and GFSC-licensed GP; narrative must read 'Capital contribution to ELP - ISIC 5222 bonded trucking operations' with no goods/purpose code conflict. USD injections are settled via NatWest's US correspondent (e.g., Bank of America or Citibank New York) and EUR injections via UK or EU correspondents with full pre-screening against UK, UN, EU, and US sanctions lists. Any physical cash component above EUR 10,000 requires FIU-G declaration; electronic contributions are not subject to HMRC import declaration.Two clearing classes apply: (a) high-value and customs-duty-critical vendor settlements via SWIFT MT103 directly from the tier-1 Gibraltar bank using IBAN beneficiary verification; EU subcontract hauler payments additionally use SEPA Instant Credit or SEPA Direct Debit for recurring toll and road-charge liabilities; (b) US-denominated ground-handling, fuel, and airport landing-fee payouts via ACH Direct in NACHA CCD/CTX format through the bank's US clearing partner, with a treasury policy capping ACH per-transaction value at USD 10,000. Commercial Letters of Credit are permissible for customs bond providers: an irrevocable standby letter of credit issued by Gibraltar International Bank or NatWest International in favour of the HM Customs Gibraltar bond guarantor or an HMRC-approved surety, supported by the underlying CMR contract and airport Road Feeder Service agreement.Deliverable GBP/GIP-USD FX forward (tenor up to 12 months) with a GFSC-licensed bank counterparty; the 1:1 GIP/GBP peg eliminates domestic settlement risk.EUR/USD non-deliverable forward (NDF) capped at 12-month tenor, used solely for projected non-contractual EUR revenues in airport feeder corridors.EUR/USD and GBP/USD cross-currency swaps matched on a 1:1 notional basis to underlying ISIC 5222 bonded trucking service contracts.Plain-vanilla FX swap or rollover for intra-month conversion of USD surplus into GBP operational liquidity at same-day value.
