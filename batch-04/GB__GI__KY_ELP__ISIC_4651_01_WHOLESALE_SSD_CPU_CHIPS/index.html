Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Her Majesty's Government of Gibraltar, Income Tax Office (ITO) — Department of the Civil Service, HM Government of Gibraltar; online portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation; administered alongside the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission for regulatory matters.

E-government filing portal

A non-resident Exempted Limited Partnership must appoint a Gibraltar-resident general partner or licensed management company, then file Form ELP-1 with the Gibraltar Companies House together with the limited partnership agreement and registered office consent. Following certificate of registration, the Income Tax Office issues a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (Gibraltar TIN) for the ELP; the ELP may also apply for a Gibraltar VAT registration number via the Customs and Excise Department if making taxable supplies in Gibraltar or acquiring goods from the EU/UK for business purposes.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — A non-resident Exempted Limited Partnership must appoint a Gibraltar-resident general partner or licensed management company, then file Form ELP-1 with the Gibraltar Companies House together with the limited partnership agreement and registered office consent. Following certificate of registration, the Income Tax Office issues a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (Gibraltar TIN) for the ELP; the ELP may also apply for a Gibraltar VAT registration number via the Customs and Excise Department if making taxable supplies in Gibraltar or acquiring goods from the EU/UK for business purposes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

An Exempted Limited Partnership is treated as fiscally transparent under Gibraltar income tax law; no Gibraltar corporation tax election is available because the ELP is not a body corporate. The partners may elect to have the ELP treated as an offshore 'exempt company' style vehicle by way of an application for exemption status under the Income Tax (Exempt Companies) Act if the vehicle is instead incorporated as a company; for ELP structures, partners may elect to allocate Gibraltar-source income and chargeable gains to a Gibraltar-resident general partner, subject to the ITO's anti-avoidance and hybrid transfer pricing rules. The UK-Gibraltar Double Taxation Agreement and UK hybrid mismatch rules (part of the OECD BEPS Action 2 implementation) may require a 'payee tax deduction' or 'dual inclusion income' election where the ELP is fiscally transparent in Gibraltar but treated as opaque in the United Kingdom.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

VAT (Gibraltar applies the UK VAT system by virtue of the EU/Gibraltar arrangements; no separate local GST).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GBP 85,000 for the standard taxable supplies threshold set by HM Revenue & Customs and applied in Gibraltar, subject to any local provisions; however for a pure offshore ELP carrying on wholesale brokerage outside Gibraltar with no UK taxable establishment, no UK VAT registration is triggered unless the ELP imports goods into the UK or supplies digital services to UK consumers.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exported from the UK/Gibraltar to a destination outside the UK and EU may be zero-rated for VAT if the ELP retains evidence of export within specified time limits, provided the goods are removed from the UK/Gibraltar in the course of a supply to a person who is not in the UK/EU; offshore services, including brokerage and intermediation of SSD/CPU chips between non-UK parties, are generally outside the scope of UK VAT rather than zero-rated unless connected with land or goods. A Gibraltarian ELP that has no UK VAT registration cannot reclaim UK input VAT unless it registers and satisfies the normal rules.

  • Customs Export Declaration (UK Customs Declaration Service CDS or Gibraltarian import/export declaration, customs procedure code 10 00 00 00)
  • Bill of Lading (or Air Waybill for air cargo shipments)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank transfer evidence showing payment for the goods or services
  • Commercial invoice evidencing the exporter as principal and beneficiary of the supply
  • Supplementary evidence: contract of sale, packing list, CMR/Sea Waybill, or shipping instructions

Gibraltar does not currently mandate real-time e-invoicing or fiscal clearance; UK MTD (Making Tax Digital) applies only to UK VAT-registered businesses for VAT return filing via software and APIs. If the ELP is registered for UK VAT in respect of UK imports or UK supplies, it must use MTD-compatible software; no digital stamping or tax authority pre-clearance is imposed for Gibraltar wholesale transactions.

HM Revenue & Customs and the Gibraltar Customs and Excise Department may impose penalties under the UK Value Added Tax Act 1994 (as extended to Gibraltar) equal to the evaded VAT plus interest, civil penalties up to 100% of the VAT evaded for dishonest conduct, and criminal prosecution including imprisonment for fraudulent evasion of VAT (VATA 1994 s.72). A claim for zero-rating may be disallowed and issued as a VAT assessment for up to 4 years (extended to 20 years for deliberate error); the ELP and its partners may also be denied input VAT deductions and subjected to retrospective clawback and disqualification from exporting regimes.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility applies on both current and capital accounts. Gibraltar imposes no exchange controls, no prior authorisation for capital movement, no restrictions on foreign-currency account holding, and no limits on cross-border transfers. GIP circulates solely within Gibraltar, but GBP is also legal tender and is interchanged at parity without charge; USD, EUR, CNY/CNH, JPY, KRW, TWD, and SGD are freely available through licensed banks and can be held as operational or settlement currencies. Profit and capital remittances from a Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership to its non-resident UK parent and limited partners are unrestricted; no withholding tax, dividend permit, or exit approval is applicable to pass-through distributions. The UK side likewise imposes no exchange-control restrictions on outbound equity injections into a Gibraltar ELP.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Neither Gibraltar nor the United Kingdom operates statutory export-proceeds surrender rules, mandatory conversion ratios, or minimum repatriation timelines. The ELP may retain SSD/CPU trade receipts in USD, EUR, or Asian currencies in offshore operational accounts or convert them internally at commercial interbank rates without GFSC or HM Treasury approval. There is no obligation to convert export proceeds into GIP or GBP at any point.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar does not maintain an independent central bank or foreign-exchange administration. The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is issued by the Government of Gibraltar under the Currency Notes Act 1934, is pegged at a fixed 1:1 parity with GBP, and monetary policy is effectively determined by the Bank of England. Financial regulation of deposit-taking, payments, and investment business is exclusively supervised by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) under the Financial Services Act 2019 and the Banking Act 2015.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low-to-Medium for offshore digital EMIs. UK-authorised EMIs (e.g., Revolut Business, Wise) lost passporting into Gibraltar post-Brexit and frequently decline the electronics distribution sector on policy grounds; they cannot provide GIP domestic accounts or facilitate trade settlement for a Gibraltar ELP. The only compliant fintech route is a GFSC-licensed EMI or payment institution under the Financial Services Act 2019, but such institutions generally lack the Asian correspondent banking network required for high-value CPU/SSD trade wires or LC confirmations. Digital EMIs are therefore confined to minor operating expenditure (e.g., subcontractor payments, subscriptions) and must never be used for escrow, capital escrow, or supplier settlement flows.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections flow from the UK parent to the ELP's NatWest International or Gibraltar International Bank account via SWIFT MT103 in GBP/GIP at parity. The UK remitter should reference purpose code 'Investment in overseas partnership' under BoE/OFS returns, with narrative 'ELP CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION'; no Gibraltar exchange-control or GFSC approval is required. For inbound amounts exceeding GBP 10,000,000, the UK parent files an HMRC Form A statistical return only. Funds are first credited to a dedicated Capital Escrow account and subsequently swept to the operational account upon verification of the partnership deed, capital account schedule, and bank compliance sign-off.Trade settlements use three rails: (i) SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfers for open-account spot brokerage to tier-1 supplier banks in Taiwan, Korea, Japan, Singapore, and China (offshore CNH), with mandatory UETR tracking and purchase-order matching; (ii) irrevocable, at-sight Commercial Letters of Credit issued under UCP600, confirmed through Hong Kong or Singapore banks, for high-value repetitive CPU/SSD lots; and (iii) Escrow Inspection Wires whereby trade funds are segregated in the ELP's escrow sub-account and released only upon presentation of an SGS/TUV inspection certificate and signed warehouse receipt. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

10% (Gibraltar standard corporate tax rate; the Exempted Limited Partnership itself is fiscally transparent and not chargeable at entity level)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Gibraltar does not impose withholding tax on dividends or on profit allocations made by an Exempted Limited Partnership to its partners.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Return and self-assessment are due no later than nine months after the end of the accounting period; for a 31 December ELP year-end, the fixed due date is 30 September of the following calendar year. Where the Income Tax Office serves a formal notice to file, the deadline stated in the notice prevails if earlier.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 in aggregate controlled transactions with connected persons in an accounting period triggers mandatory preparation of a contemporaneous Local File. A Master File is expected for multinational groups with consolidated turnover above EUR 750,000,000 under the global minimum tax framework; controlled semiconductor transactions above GIP 250,000 additionally require a written internal pricing memorandum.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar applies economic substance requirements to geographically mobile activities, including wholesale distribution and service brokerage. A non-resident ELP dealing in SSD and CPU chips may not rely on territorial exemption unless it can demonstrate substantive decision-making, negotiation, invoicing, logistics and risk control in Gibraltar. A registered-agent-only shell with no local premises, employees or operating expenditure will be treated as Gibraltar-resident and subjected to 10% worldwide tax, with treaty benefits denied.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Non-Declaration

Statutory sanction: Tax-geared penalty of up to 100% for negligent incorrect returns and up to 300% for deliberate concealment; criminal conviction punishable by a fine of up to GIP 100,000 and imprisonment for up to seven years; court-ordered restitution of evaded tax, interest and costs.

Director personal liability: General partners and directors who knowingly caused the ELP to evade tax are jointly and severally liable. On proof of fraudulent trading, the corporate veil is pierced, personal assessments are issued, and the court may impose travel bans, asset freezing orders and director disqualification.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Abuse

Statutory sanction: Recharacterisation as a Gibraltar-resident entity; worldwide trading profits assessed at 10%; loss relief and substantial shareholder exemption denied; penalty of up to GIP 10,000 for non-disclosure of substance changes; registration struck off where non-compliance is persistent.

Director personal liability: Limited partners who take part in management lose their limited liability shield. Directors of the general partner are liable to civil restitution for benefits extracted, and travel bans and disqualification orders may be issued if the substance failure is designed to avoid tax.
AML/CFT and Offshore Payment-Rail Abuse

Statutory sanction: Under Gibraltar's Proceeds of Crime legislation, failure to report suspicious activity and tipping-off are imprisonable offences; money laundering is punishable by an unlimited fine and up to fourteen years' imprisonment; confiscation orders may attach partnership assets.

Director personal liability: The appointed money laundering reporting officer and any director who directs a suspect transaction may be personally prosecuted. Courts may order confiscation of personal assets, impose travel bans and prohibit the individual from acting as a partnership or company officer.
