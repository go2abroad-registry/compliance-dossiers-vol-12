Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registrar of Companies, Companies House Gibraltar, HM Government of Gibraltar

Governing statute

Limited Partnerships Act 1991 (Gibraltar), ss. 4-9 (formation and registration of exempted limited partnerships) and s. 12 (registered office); read with the Companies House (Fees) Regulations and the Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010 passages on non-resident partnership pass-through treatment.

Formation / constitutional filings

Form 1 — Statement of Registration of a Limited Partnership, accompanied by the executed Partnership Agreement and the Declaration of the General Partner.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 25 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital; an ELP may be registered with zero issued capital. Capital contributions are purely contractual and are recorded in the Partnership Agreement. No escrow account requirement is imposed by Gibraltar law.

Funding & unlock path: Not required for pure digital service delivery. If a physical office is occupied, the following clearances must be secured from the local authorities: (i) a Fire Safety Certificate under the Gibraltar Fire Services Act; (ii) compliance with the Workplace (Health, Safety and Welfare) Regulations governing workstation ergonomics and emergency egress; (iii) structural load clearance is not applicable to a desk-based office occupancy and no civil defense permit is triggered.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital; an ELP may be registered with zero issued capital. Capital contributions are purely contractual and are recorded in the Partnership Agreement. No escrow account requirement is imposed by Gibraltar law.

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

No industrial licensing authority applies. The offshore CAD/BIM service activity does not trigger the Gibraltar Factories and Machinery Act or any manufacturing establishment permit. The appropriate regulatory interface for incidental business operations is the Financial Services Commission only where regulated payment activity is undertaken.

Category: No statutory operating or manufacturing permit is required for remote Building Information Modeling and Structural Drafting services. If the ELP elects to maintain a physical desk space in Gibraltar, only a standard commercial lease and, where applicable, registration under the Gibraltar Data Protection Act (as data processor) are required.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Primary competent authority: Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (Ministry of Finance), official portal: www.gibraltar.gov.gi (Tax Office section). Entity registry: Registrar of Companies, Companies House Gibraltar, for issuance of the ELP registered number under the Limited Partnerships Act (Cap. 211, as amended for Exempted Limited Partnerships). Customs and indirect duties: HM Customs, Gibraltar. UK overlay: HMRC (www.gov.uk/hmrc) is engaged only if the ELP or its general partner creates a UK permanent establishment/fixed base or if central management and control of the partnership shifts to the UK.

E-government filing portal

1) Incorporate the ELP: file the statement of limited partnership with the Gibraltar Companies House, appoint a Gibraltar registered office address and a licensed registered agent, and maintain at least one general partner and one limited partner; obtain the Certificate of Limited Partnership and the statutory registered number. 2) Register for tax: submit the partnership notification and opening return to the Income Tax Office; the Commissioner assigns a Gibraltar Income Tax reference (functional TIN) to the ELP, distinct from the Companies House number. 3) Social insurance: if the ELP employs Gibraltar-based BIM/CAD staff, register as an employer with the Gibraltar Social Security (GSSC) and establish payroll references. 4) Economic substance: prepare and file the annual economic substance return evidencing that the BIM/CAD activities are directed, managed, and performed from Gibraltar (a relevant activity for territorial-scope purposes). 5) Banking/treasury: open the operating account with a Gibraltar bank or licensed EMI (Wise Business) presenting the Certificate of Limited Partnership, partnership agreement, and tax reference before accepting client remittances. Non-resident partners are not automatically registered; a partner-level filing obligation arises only where Gibraltar-source income exists.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; Territorial_Scope (the ELP is fiscally transparent and files information returns only; partners are the taxable persons. Non-resident partners are chargeable to Gibraltar income tax solely on Gibraltar-source income; profits from offshore BIM/CAD and structural engineering consultancy supplied to non-Gibraltar business clients are outside the Gibraltar tax charge, provided management and control and economic substance remain in Gibraltar).

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no check-the-box regime: partnership transparency is mandatory and no election to be taxed as a corporation exists. Available flexibilities: (a) election under the Limited Partnerships Act for the ELP to acquire separate legal personality (contracting convenience only, with no income tax consequence); (b) partnership agreement elections governing profit-sharing ratios and the scope of the limited partners' management participation; (c) for US federal tax purposes, default classification as a partnership, with partners able to file IRS Form 8832 if entity reclassification is ever desired; (d) for UK-resident partners, no pass-through election is available and the transfer of assets abroad rules in ITA 2007, Part 13, Chapter 2 apply; (e) for MNE groups with consolidated revenue exceeding EUR 750 million, the Gibraltar multinational top-up tax (GloBE/Pillar Two) legislation, effective from 2024, requires designation of ELP status and, where relevant, elections on the income inclusion rule and the qualified domestic minimum top-up tax.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar Goods and Services Tax (GST), a narrow, import-oriented consumption duty; Gibraltar is outside the VAT territory of the United Kingdom and outside the EU VAT system. BIM/CAD and structural drafting services supplied digitally to non-Gibraltar business customers are outside the scope of Gibraltar GST; in the recipient's jurisdiction the B2B reverse charge applies (UK: section 7 VATA 1994 and the Place of Supply of Services Order 1992; EU: Article 44 of the VAT Directive). Any tangible imports (hardware, printed deliverables) into Gibraltar are subject to Gibraltar customs duties and category-based GST at the point of importation.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No GST or VAT registration threshold applies to a services-only ELP because service supplies are outside the scope of Gibraltar GST. Registration with HM Customs, Gibraltar is required only for physical imports above the low-value consignment de minimis. The UK VAT registration threshold of GBP 85,000 is not engaged absent a UK fixed establishment. In customer jurisdictions, a local registration liability may arise where the service is connected with immovable property situated there (on-site civil-infrastructure engineering) or where digital B2C supplies are made to consumers.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Formal export zero-rating is inapplicable because Gibraltar imposes no positive-rate VAT on services. The ELP instead substantiates 'outside the scope of Gibraltar GST' treatment and the customer's reverse-charge liability. Eligibility conditions: (a) the customer is a business established outside Gibraltar holding a valid VAT or registration ID; (b) the service is not connected with immovable property in Gibraltar and is not physically performed in Gibraltar for a Gibraltar consumer; (c) invoices are marked 'reverse charge; place of supply: [customer jurisdiction]' and carry both supplier and customer IDs; (d) for UK clients, the service is received for business purposes so the general place-of-supply rule situates the supply in the UK and the customer self-accounts for UK VAT. Retention of the contract, BIM/CDE delivery logs, and client acceptance certificates is mandatory to defeat recharacterization.

  • Customs Export Declaration (not applicable to digital service exports; for any physical consignments, the Gibraltar customs entry or ATA carnet is the operative record)
  • Bill of Lading / Delivery Certificate (for services, the functional substitute is the signed BIM execution and acceptance certificate, the CDE audit-trail extract with SHA-256 file hashes, and the 'issued for construction' transmittal register)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (the SWIFT or Wise Business credit statement, the client's payment reference, the currency conversion record, and the aged-receivables reconciliation evidencing repatriation within the internal treasury window)

None in Gibraltar. There is no real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or mandatory e-invoicing platform (no PEPPOL, SAF-T, ZATCA, or Chorus Pro equivalent). Standard PDF/EDI invoices are sufficient. Contractual compliance with the customer's domestic e-invoicing and digital reporting rules is recommended; the invoice data set must include the ELP legal name and Gibraltar registered address, the Gibraltar tax reference, invoice date, description of BIM/CAD deliverables, amount in the agreed currency, the customer's registration ID, and a statement that the place of supply is the customer's jurisdiction with the reverse charge applying.

The Income Tax Office may issue discovery assessments on the ELP where Gibraltar-source income is understated; fraudulent or negligent filings attract civil penalties of up to 100% of the tax understated, statutory interest, and criminal prosecution for deliberate evasion. Incorrect reliance on reverse-charge or outside-scope treatment on UK invoices exposes the ELP to HMRC inaccuracy penalties of up to 100% of potential lost revenue, joint and several liability in self-billing arrangements, and retrospective input-tax clawback where the customer's recovery is denied. For UK-resident partners, HMRC may re-attribute ELP profits under the transfer of assets abroad provisions (ITA 2007, Part 13, Chapter 2), with penalties and interest if the motive defence fails; a year of UK-based management can render the ELP UK-resident and retroactively unwind the Gibraltar territorial exclusion. In infrastructure-host states, cross-border service fees may be recharacterized as royalties or immovable-property-linked consideration, triggering local withholding tax, permanent establishment claims, interest, and collection enforcement.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GIP is fully convertible at par with GBP; GBP notes circulate interchangeably and no exchange-control authorization is required for current-account or capital-account transactions. The ELP, as an unregulated non-financial commercial vehicle rendering CAD/BIM engineering services (ISIC 7110), may hold, receive, and remit USD, EUR, and GBP freely. Profit shares, capital returns, and management distributions remitted to the non-resident Cayman partner (KY_ELP) are not subject to FSC approval, withholding, or surrender requirements. The only limitations arise from EU/UK sanctions screening and the GFSC AML/CFT reporting framework under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 (Gibraltar), which require accurate remittance narratives but impose no restriction on convertibility.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or mandatory conversion ratio exists in Gibraltar. All export service proceeds for infrastructure BIM deliverables may be retained in the original invoicing currency (GBP/USD/EUR) at any GFSC-licensed bank or DFS-enabled EMI. There is no time limit for repatriation or liquidation of foreign-currency receivables. The ELP is nonetheless advised to maintain a documented treasury policy demonstrating that invoice proceeds correspond to the underlying civil-infrastructure service contracts, thereby satisfying source-of-funds verification without triggering regulatory scrutiny.

Central bank supervision: Government of Gibraltar – Banking Department (Currency Note Act 1934) is the statutory issuer of Gibraltar pound (GIP) notes, maintaining a full 1:1 reserve peg against GBP. The Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) exercises prudential and AML/CFT supervision of all licensed banks, payment institutions, and fiduciary providers. The Bank of England anchors monetary policy for the currency union; post-Brexit cooperation is governed by the UK–Gibraltar Financial Services Memorandum of Understanding.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Medium – Wise (Wise Payments Ltd, UK FCA EMI licence reference 900507) is an operationally acceptable secondary disbursement rail for sub-GBP 10,000 supplier payments and initial fee receipts; however, Wise does not support GIP balances or issue a Gibraltar IBAN, so it cannot serve as the principal treasury account. Legal constraint: under the Financial Services Act 2019 (Gibraltar), a UK/EEA EMI licence does not confer a Gibraltar passport; Wise may only transact on behalf of the ELP by routing funds to/from the ELP's GFSC-licensed bank account. The primary account must remain at a licensed tier-1 bank to ensure a GFSC-supervised audit trail for economic substance and AML compliance.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Non-resident Cayman ELP owning a Gibraltar ELP with no apparent substance on the Rock

Root cause: Non-resident Cayman ELP owning a Gibraltar ELP with no apparent substance on the Rock

Defense evidence: Provide (1) Gibraltar ELP registration certificate and GFSC Economic Substance return confirming the outsourced engineering-scope; (2) certified Limited Partnership Agreement proving a genuine Gibraltar general partner with fiduciary duties; (3) certificate of good standing and registered-agent letter for the Cayman ELP; (4) the master CAD/BIM service contract and a staffing/substance plan for Gibraltar; (5) MLRO cover letter from the registered agent under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015.
Multi-currency inbound flows from government-linked overseas infrastructure principals triggering enhanced due diligence

Root cause: Multi-currency inbound flows from government-linked overseas infrastructure principals triggering enhanced due diligence

Defense evidence: Provide (1) executed master service agreement with anti-bribery/anti-corruption clauses; (2) sanctions screening report (UK consolidated list, OFAC, EU) of the counterparty and project site; (3) remitting bank's source-of-funds reference for the initial 12-month pipeline; (4) project award letter or tender contract cross-referencing the relevant infrastructure authority; (5) the ELP's forecasted volume declaration to enable accurate transaction monitoring.
Immediate forwarding of client funds to the Cayman parent (pass-through pattern) flagged as potential wealth laundering

Root cause: Immediate forwarding of client funds to the Cayman parent (pass-through pattern) flagged as potential wealth laundering

Defense evidence: Provide the ELP's audited profit-and-loss waterfall demonstrating legitimate value creation; the inter-partnership distribution clause; the Cayman parent's regulatory filings and GAAP statements; and a tax/legal opinion confirming the pass-through, Non-Hybrid, treatment under Gibraltar and Cayman law, plus invoices and timesheets for each distributed milestone.
Use of Wise or another EMI as the first receiving account creating an 'unlicensed payment institution' red flag

Root cause: Use of Wise or another EMI as the first receiving account creating an 'unlicensed payment institution' red flag

Defense evidence: Submit the FCA EMI licence certificate, a bank confirmation letter from the primary GFSC-licensed bank, and a 30-day reconciliation ledger proving automatic same-day sweeping of EMI receipts into the licensed account. This demonstrates that the EMI is a pure disbursement utility, not a custodial treasury.

NO. XXXX'. In the absence of Gibraltar-specific purpose-code classification, the MT103 field 70 should also include the service-scope reference 'ISIC 7110 CAD/BIM' and a unique funding identifier. For amounts over USD 200,000, a copy of the bank credit/debit advice from the Cayman remitter and an executed contribution certificate (signed by the general partner) must be sent to the compliance officer prior to value date. Late filing: fixed penalty of up to GIP 1,500 plus a daily surcharge of up to GIP 100 per day, capped at GIP 50,000. Inaccurate or fraudulent returns: penalty of 30% to 200% of the understated tax. Box 5 (Foreign-source turnover) Gross income arising outside Gibraltar from exported engineering design, BIM coordination, and structural drafting services. Foreign-source income is taxable only if received in or remitted to Gibraltar. Payments collected through Wise or overseas bank accounts and retained offshore fall outside the Gibraltar tax charge; retain contracts, invoices, and payment routing evidence. Late filing: fixed penalty of up to GIP 1,500 plus a daily surcharge of up to GIP 100 per day, capped at GIP 50,000. Inaccurate or fraudulent returns: penalty of 30% to 200% of the understated tax. Late payment interest accrues at 6% per annum. Persistent default triggers compulsory strike-off and referral to the Criminal Investigation Unit.
Box 6 (Deductible expenditure) Allowable costs including software subscriptions, BIM licenses, subcontract labor, professional fees, insurance, depreciation, and ordinary overheads attributable to chargeable income. Allocate expenses pro rata between Gibraltar-source and foreign-source income. Related-party recharges must satisfy the arm's length principle and be supported by a transfer pricing documentation file. Late filing: fixed penalty of up to GIP 1,500 plus a daily surcharge of up to GIP 100 per day, capped at GIP 50,000. Inaccurate or fraudulent returns: penalty of 30% to 200% of the understated tax. Late payment interest accrues at 6% per annum. Persistent default triggers compulsory strike-off and referral to the Criminal Investigation Unit.
Box 7 (Adjusted profit / loss before loss relief) Net assessable profit or loss of the partnership after statutory adjustments, shown separately for Gibraltar-source and foreign-source categories. As a pass-through entity, the ELP must attach an allocation schedule (Form TI-11) showing each partner's profit share, country of tax residence, and foreign tax reference number. Late filing: fixed penalty of up to GIP 1,500 plus a daily surcharge of up to GIP 100 per day, capped at GIP 50,000. Inaccurate or fraudulent returns: penalty of 30% to 200% of the understated tax. Late payment interest accrues at 6% per annum. Persistent default triggers compulsory strike-off and referral to the Criminal Investigation Unit.
Box 8 (Loss relief and tax payable) Offset of eligible losses carried forward (maximum 5 years) and computation of tax at 12.5% on the partner-allocation of Gibraltar-source chargeable profits. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 aggregate cross-border related-party transactions in an accounting period triggers preparation of the Local File. A Master File is required where the ultimate parent group has consolidated revenue exceeding EUR 750 million. Contemporaneous TP records are strongly recommended for any ELP with intra-group service revenue above GIP 250,000. Country-by-Country Reporting is required at the GIP equivalent of the EUR 750 million threshold.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance regime (Income Tax Act 2010 and Income Tax Office Economic Substance Guidance) applies to entities carrying on relevant activities, including distribution and service centres. An ELP engaged in CAD/BIM and structural drafting outsourcing must demonstrate: (i) physical office premises in Gibraltar; (ii) an adequate number of full-time engineering and management employees in Gibraltar with discretion over core functions; (iii) strategic decision-making and risk assumption conducted in Gibraltar; and (iv) proportionate operating expenditure. Non-compliance results in recharacterization of profits as Gibraltar-source, denial of treaty benefits under the UK-Gibraltar DTA, and GAAR counteraction.
