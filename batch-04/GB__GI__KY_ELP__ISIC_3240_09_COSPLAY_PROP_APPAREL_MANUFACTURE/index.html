Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office (ITO) of HM Government of Gibraltar; e-services via the official Government of Gibraltar portal at www.gibraltar.gov.gi; entity registry: Gibraltar Companies House (Companies House Gibraltar).

E-government filing portal

For a non-resident-owned Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP): (1) Register the ELP with the Gibraltar Companies House under the Limited Partnerships Act 2015; obtain the partnership number (no minimum capital, 100% non-resident ownership permitted); (2) file the partnership registration particulars with the Income Tax Office to obtain the Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN/TIN); the ITO assesses partners individually on their allocable shares; (3) no VAT identification number is issued because Gibraltar does not impose VAT/GST; (4) if trading in goods, register with HM Customs Gibraltar for a customs trader identification code (EORI-style) to lodge export declarations; (5) a Gibraltar tax agent may be appointed to satisfy local filing formalities where the ELP has no physical presence.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible / Territorial_Scope: the ELP is statutorily fiscally transparent; the partnership is not a taxable person, and each partner is taxed on their share of Gibraltar-source profits only. Individual partners are taxed at progressive rates up to 36.5%; corporate partners are taxed at the 10% corporate rate. Non-resident partners are outside the charge to Gibraltar income tax when the ELP carries on no trade in Gibraltar and derives no Gibraltar-source income. — For a non-resident-owned Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP): (1) Register the ELP with the Gibraltar Companies House under the Limited Partnerships Act 2015; obtain the partnership number (no minimum capital, 100% non-resident ownership permitted); (2) file the partnership registration particulars with the Income Tax Office to obtain the Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN/TIN); the ITO assesses partners individually on their allocable shares; (3) no VAT identification number is issued because Gibraltar does not impose VAT/GST; (4) if trading in goods, register with HM Customs Gibraltar for a customs trader identification code (EORI-style) to lodge export declarations; (5) a Gibraltar tax agent may be appointed to satisfy local filing formalities where the ELP has no physical presence.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible / Territorial_Scope: the ELP is statutorily fiscally transparent; the partnership is not a taxable person, and each partner is taxed on their share of Gibraltar-source profits only. Individual partners are taxed at progressive rates up to 36.5%; corporate partners are taxed at the 10% corporate rate. Non-resident partners are outside the charge to Gibraltar income tax when the ELP carries on no trade in Gibraltar and derives no Gibraltar-source income.

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists under Gibraltar law; fiscal transparency is mandatory for limited partnerships. Elective measures available: (a) application of the EU ATAD anti-hybrid rules, as transposed by the Taxation (Anti-Hybrid Rules) Regulations, to elect a single-taxpayer treatment for cross-border hybrid mismatches; (b) an election under the Limited Partnerships Act 2015 for the ELP to acquire separate legal personality (civil-law effect, not tax effect); (c) an election in the partnership return to allocate profits to partners in accordance with the partnership agreement; and (d) a voluntary UK VAT registration election if the ELP establishes a UK establishment or exceeds UK distance-selling thresholds.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar has no VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax; the applicable indirect system comprises selective import duties and excise duties under Gibraltar customs legislation. Post-Brexit, Gibraltar forms part of the UK customs-territory arrangements; cosplay apparel and costume goods falling within HS Chapters 61, 62, and 95 may attract UK Trade Tariff duties at the point of import into the UK or EU, while no Gibraltar indirect tax is charged on local supply or export.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable: there is no VAT/GST registration threshold in Gibraltar. Any person importing or exporting commercial goods must obtain a Gibraltar customs trader registration from the first shipment (nil turnover threshold). Excise dealing licences are required only for alcohol, tobacco, and fuel.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar VAT zero-rating is not applicable because no VAT system exists in Gibraltar; exports from Gibraltar are outside the scope of any Gibraltar indirect tax, and no Gibraltar VAT refund mechanism is available. If the ELP elects UK VAT registration (e.g., via a UK establishment), exports of cosplay apparel from the UK to overseas customers may be zero-rated under the Value Added Tax Act 1994, s.30(6), provided the goods are exported within the statutory time limits and the export evidence bundle is retained. Sales to EU consumers may remain subject to EU import VAT at destination, with OSS/IOSS registration for distance sellers.

  • Customs export declaration (SAD/CDS electronic declaration with movement reference number) evidencing the Gibraltar/UK export movement
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or courier consignment note evidencing physical exportation from the jurisdiction
  • Commercial invoice and packing list describing the goods (HS Chapters 9505, 6114, 6104), quantity, value, and country of origin
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT MT103 / payment gateway settlement report proving receipt of foreign currency proceeds
  • Carrier departure evidence such as port/airport stamp, manifest extract, or electronic arrival notice from the destination country

No real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, API tax-reporting, or digital stamping regime is mandated in Gibraltar. Export clearances are submitted electronically through the UK/Gibraltar Customs Declaration Service (CDS) using the Single Administrative Document format; invoices and supporting records must be retained for six years under the Income Tax Act 2010.

Gibraltar Customs and the Income Tax Office may reopen assessments within six years (unlimited in cases of fraud). False export declarations, unsupported zero-rating claims, or misdescribed goods trigger retrospective duty assessment, interest, and civil penalties of up to 100% of the duty shortfall, with power to seize and forfeit goods. Fraudulent conduct attracts criminal fines up to £5,000 and/or imprisonment for up to two years, together with suspension or cancellation of the customs trader registration.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Both current and capital account transactions are fully convertible. Gibraltar maintains no exchange control restrictions under the Financial Services (Capital Requirements) Act and the UK Exchange Control Act regime was abolished in 1979. Profit remittances, dividend distributions, and capital repatriations from an Exempted Limited Partnership are freely permitted without exchange control approval. The GIP is not a separate floating currency; its legal tender parity to GBP eliminates currency transfer risk within the UK/GB clearing orbit.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender obligation exists for export proceeds in Gibraltar or the United Kingdom. Enterprises may retain export receipts in any currency, hold them in external accounts, and deploy them without mandatory conversion or time-bound repatriation. Banking custom requires a declared trade purpose code only for statistical reporting by bona fide commercial banks.

Central bank supervision: HM Government of Gibraltar (Finance Centre Gibraltar, Financial Services Commission) and Bank of England as sovereign monetary anchor; Gibraltar pound is pegged at 1:1 to GBP and issued by the Gibraltar Commissioner of Currency.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Income Tax (Transfer Pricing) Regulations 2017: Local File required where controlled transactions exceed GBP 500,000 in aggregate per category in the chargeable period; Master File required where the multinational group's consolidated turnover exceeds GBP 50,000,000 (GIP pegged 1:1 to GBP)

Economic substance scope: Economic Substance Act 2020 and Economic Substance Regulations 2021 apply to a relevant entity (including a corporate general partner and any ELP treated as a company) carrying on a relevant activity. For cosplay costume manufacture and retail, the core income generating activities (managing operational risk, staffing, strategic decision-making, physical production and logistics) must be performed in Gibraltar with adequate premises, full-time employees and expenditure. Pure equity-holding ELPs face reduced substance requirements. An annual economic substance return is mandatory; failure triggers financial penalties, strike-off, and disqualification of the general partner

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Tax Fraud

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty up to 100% of underpaid tax plus 8% interest; criminal conviction for fraudulent evasion (Income Tax Act 2010): unlimited fine and imprisonment up to 7 years

Director personal liability: Directors of the corporate general partner may be personally assessed for the unpaid tax where they knowingly signed false returns; restitution orders, travel bans, and custodial sentences apply
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Economic Substance Act 2020 penalties up to GBP 150,000, daily default fines, loss of QRO status, strike-off or dissolution, and adverse reporting to HMRC under the UK-Gibraltar exchange of information provisions

Director personal liability: General partner directors face disqualification orders under the Companies Act 2014 and personal responsibility for penalties if economic substance returns are falsely certified
AML / Sanctions Compliance

Statutory sanction: Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and AML/CFT Regulations 2019: unlimited fines, confiscation orders, and imprisonment up to 14 years for money laundering; GFSC enforcement actions against the appointed MLRO

Director personal liability: Directors and the MLRO are personally criminally liable for failing to file suspicious activity reports; travel bans and freezing orders may be imposed pending investigation
Transfer Pricing Mispricing / Profit Stripping

Statutory sanction: Transfer pricing adjustment to arm's-length results, penalty up to 100% of the additional tax, 8% interest, and daily surcharges for failure to produce the Local File on demand

Director personal liability: Directors who approved the mispriced transactions may be jointly and severally liable for the adjusted tax and penalties if the ELP is insolvent or liquidated
