An Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) must first be registered with the Gibraltar Companies Registry under the Limited Partnerships Act. Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar imposes no exchange-control regime on current or capital account transactions, and the UK Exchange Control Act 1947 has been repealed, leaving no residual statutory restrictions on the purchase, sale, or holding of foreign currency. Current account convertibility is unrestricted, including trade remittances and service payments; capital account convertibility is likewise unrestricted for non-resident investors, with the sole material condition being adherence to GFSC-approved AML/CFT and source-of-funds protocols. Profit remittances by an Exempted Limited Partnership where the general partner is tax-resident outside Gibraltar are typically free of withholding and can be settled in GIP, GBP, USD, or EUR at the partner's instruction, subject only to bank-level documentation and any relevant double-taxation treaty position.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory surrender or mandatory conversion obligation on export proceeds in Gibraltar; exporters, including e-commerce and POD operators, may lawfully retain, disburse, or convert export receipts in any currency without a minimum retention ratio or repatriation timeline. In practice, the receiving bank will require the underlying invoice or platform payout statement to satisfy CDD, and any unusually delayed conversion of repatriated GIP or GBP may trigger an economic-substance or tax-residency enquiry under Gibraltar's statutory residence rules.

Central bank supervision: HM Government of Gibraltar, acting through the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) for banking and AML/CFT supervision, and the Ministry of Finance for currency issuance under the Currency Notes Act 1934; there is no separate central bank with conventional monetary policy functions, and the Gibraltar Pound (GIP) is a sterling-dominated currency issued at a fixed 1:1 parity with the UK Pound Sterling.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Medium for a GFSC-licensed or UK-FCA-authorized EMI operating through a Gibraltar-licensed settlement partner, and Low for an unlicensed offshore digital EMI lacking a physical or regulatory nexus to Gibraltar. The principal constraint after Brexit is that EEA EMIs can no longer passport directly into Gibraltar; a foreign EMI must either hold a Gibraltar e-money or payment-institution authorization, or alternatively settle through a GFSC-licensed bank, because the unlicensed collection of customer funds or execution of payment transactions in Gibraltar constitutes regulated activity. For the POD business using predefined retailer marketplaces and Stripe/PayPal rails, an EMI can be commercially viable only if the embedded payout account is opened in the ELP's full legal name and the receiving Gibraltar bank will accept the EMI's originator references.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All qualifying capital injections are transmitted by SWIFT MT103 from the investor's/non-registered partner's bank to the ELP's operational account at GB Bank Limited or Barclays Bank PLC, Gibraltar Branch. The MT103 must carry the full legal name of the Exempted Limited Partnership as beneficiary, the partnership's reference code, and an unambiguous payment purpose code equivalent to 'CAPI' for capital contribution or 'A030' for capital/repatriation-related transfers where UK economic-statistic reporting applies. The receiving bank requires a prefilled source-of-funds questionnaire, evidence of the capital commitment in the partnership deed, and confirmation of the contributor's identity; once cleared, the funds become freely available because GIP is pegged to GBP and no exchange control conversion is required, although the bank may offer a conversion to USD or EUR where the POD trade cycle demands it.Settlements for POD suppliers, print vendors, courier services, and merchant fees are cleared primarily by SWIFT MT103 from the Gibraltar bank account, with UK or EEA payees able to accept SEPA Direct Credit when an IBAN is available; for non-EEA manufacturers, MT103 via a UK correspondent bank is the standard clearing rail, with same-day GBP clearing via US or internal bank networks where applicable. Commercial Letters of Credit are used only where a supplier or marketplace requires documentary security, and in such cases the issuing bank is the Gibraltar relationship bank with the GW (documentary credit) clause; ordinary recurring disbursements to Stripe, PayPal, and payment gateways are executed through bank-initiated card-advance or direct-debit transfers using the platform's published settlement instructions. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File: required where aggregate related-party cross-border transactions in a fiscal year exceed GIP 1,000,000. Master File and Country-by-Country Report: required where the ultimate parent group's consolidated turnover exceeds EUR 750,000,000.

Economic substance scope: The Taxation (Economic Substance) Act 2019 requires every Gibraltar company and partnership carrying on a 'relevant activity' to maintain adequate physical presence, full-time employees, and direction and management in Gibraltar proportionate to its income generation. POD retail is classified as a 'Distribution and Service Centre'. An annual substance declaration (Form ESR-1) is mandatory. Failure attracts penalties up to GIP 100,000, repeated-failure strike-off, and compulsory exchange of information with UK HMRC and other tax authorities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion — deliberate under-declaration of POD sales

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty up to 100% of the tax understated plus interest at 8% per annum; criminal offence under the Income Tax Act (s.149) with unlimited fine and imprisonment up to 7 years.

Director personal liability: Directors of the general partner may be prosecuted jointly with the ELP; on conviction the court may impose travel bans, passport surrender, confiscation orders over personal assets, and director disqualification for up to 15 years.
Shell company / sham economic substance

Statutory sanction: ESR financial penalty up to GIP 100,000; strike-off from the Gibraltar Companies Register; mandatory information exchange to HMRC and EU member states; re-assessment of profits at 12.5% plus interest.

Director personal liability: General partner directors face personal liability for trading while the ELP is struck off; personal fines up to GIP 50,000; travel restrictions and potential extradition under UK-Gibraltar mutual legal assistance arrangements.
AML/CFT violations — payment rails (Stripe/PayPal)

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and the AML, CTF and Sanctions Act: imprisonment up to 14 years, unlimited fines, and asset forfeiture orders.

Director personal liability: AML duty-bearers (MLRO, Compliance Officer, GP directors) face personal criminal liability; travel bans, passport surrender, and confiscation of personal assets traceable to the offending funds.
Fraudulent or wrongful trading by the general partner

Statutory sanction: Personal liability for all ELP debts under the Companies Act 2014 (fraudulent trading provisions); criminal penalty of unlimited fine and imprisonment up to 14 years; civil compensation orders.

Director personal liability: The court may bar the GP's directors from managing any entity for up to 15 years, impose travel bans, and authorise extradition to the UK where cross-border creditor harm is established.
