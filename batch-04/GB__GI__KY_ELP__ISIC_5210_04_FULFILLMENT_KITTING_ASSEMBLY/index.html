Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax); official portal: Gibraltar Tax Online Services (https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation). HM Customs Gibraltar (Customs House, Waterport) administers import/export duty and the Gibraltar Customs Declaration System.

E-government filing portal

Step 1 – File the foreign ELP's particulars with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under the Business Names Act 1938 (if trading under a name in Gibraltar) and file the Cayman exempted limited partnership (LPA) deed plus Cayman registration certificate. Step 2 – Submit Income Tax Registration Form T1 to the Commissioner of Income Tax with the executed ELP agreement, partner register, identity documents of the general partner, and the Gibraltar service address/agent appointment. Step 3 – Obtain the ELP Tax Reference Number (TRN) and, for each partner in the Gibraltar tax net (Gibraltar-resident or with Gibraltar PE profits), a partner TRN; the ELP is placed on the partnerships return register. Step 4 – Register with HM Customs Gibraltar as the importer/exporter of record to obtain the GIB economic operator/customs account for clearing kitting materials and finished goods. Step 5 – Register for Gibraltar PAYE/social insurance if the ELP employs warehouse staff. Timeline: TRN in 5–10 working days; customs account in 2–5 working days. No VAT identification number is issued because Gibraltar imposes no VAT.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible | Territorial_Scope. The Cayman ELP is transparent under the Income Tax Act 2010; the partnership itself is not a chargeable person. Each partner is taxed on its share of Gibraltar-source income and PE-attributable profits. No entity-level taxation applies to the ELP; Tax-Exempt-Zone status is not applicable, although duty-suspended warehousing relief is available for transit/kitting operations.

Available election forms

Available elections under Gibraltar law: (a) group relief election under the Income Tax Act 2010 for corporate partners (loss surrender within a 75% group); (b) capital-asset rollover election on assets contributed to the ELP; (c) hybrid mismatch notification/election under the Income Tax (Hybrid and Other Mismatch Arrangements) Rules 2019, including substitute-payment and reporting elections to neutralise reverse hybrid or imported mismatch outcomes; (d) advance clearance/private ruling application to the Commissioner of Income Tax to confirm partnership transparency and profit attribution; (e) UK-Gibraltar double taxation agreement competent-authority mutual agreement procedure (Articles 23-24) to resolve classification conflicts with HMRC. No U.S. check-the-box election applies; classification is governed by Gibraltar statute.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Import Duty / Customs Duty under the Customs and Excise Duties Act 2010 (as amended); Gibraltar is outside the UK VAT area—no VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax is levied. The UK VAT rate (20%) does not extend to Gibraltar.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT/GST registration threshold exists (no VAT in Gibraltar). Customs importer-of-record registration is mandatory from the first import—nil or GIP 0 de minimis threshold. Under the Income Tax Act 2010, the ELP must file an annual partnership return (even where profits are nil); an individual partner becomes chargeable above the GIP 10,000 personal allowance, while corporate partners are chargeable at 12.5% on Gibraltar/PE profits regardless of profit level.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable to Gibraltar VAT (no VAT system). Duty-relief equivalent: imported materials for the 3PL/kitting operation that are re-exported outside the Gibraltar customs territory qualify for customs duty relief under the duty-suspension/warehousing procedure (free-zone or customs warehousing), provided goods are not released for free circulation. Offshore services (warehousing, kitting, order fulfillment) supplied to non-resident principals are outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect taxation. The ELP cannot claim UK VAT export zero-rating because it holds no UK VAT registration or UK establishment.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD/C88 with exit summary declaration filed in the Gibraltar Customs Declaration System)
  • Bill of Lading or House Airway Bill evidencing physical removal from Gibraltar customs territory
  • Commercial Invoice and detailed Kitting/Packing List (ISIC 5210 scope)
  • Customs transit/warehouse movement record (T1 or NCTS) evidencing duty-suspended status and re-export
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank FX credit confirmation evidencing repatriation of export proceeds into the ELP's Gibraltar bank account)

No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, SAF-T, or state API e-invoicing mandate exists in Gibraltar. Annual tax returns are filed electronically through the Income Tax Office online self-assessment platform. Customs clearances are submitted as electronic SAD (Single Administrative Document) messages through the Gibraltar Customs Declaration System using encrypted file transfer/API; e-invoices (UBL/eIDAS-compliant) are accepted as supporting commercial documents but do not require state validation.

HM Customs Gibraltar and the Income Tax Office may retrospectively audit export/duty-relief claims for up to six years (unlimited in fraud cases). Deficient export proof or diversion into free circulation triggers clawback of suspended duty at the applicable rate, civil penalties of up to GBP 10,000 per false declaration and/or up to treble the duty evaded, and criminal prosecution for fraudulent evasion with imprisonment up to seven years. The ELP's partners are jointly and severally liable for any clawback, penalty, and interest where the ELP is treated as a sham or conduit; the general anti-abuse rule (section 66A, Income Tax Act 2010) may counter artificial arrangements intended to obtain a tax or duty advantage.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Both the current and capital accounts are fully convertible. The Gibraltar Pound (GIP) is freely convertible into pound sterling at par; there are no prior approvals, surrender requirements, or repatriation limits on profits, dividends, partner drawings, loan repayments, or liquidation proceeds. GIP is not internationally traded, so GBP is the effective functional treasury currency. Non-resident partners may hold, use, and remit in foreign currency without restriction, subject only to standard payment-transparency information accompanying wire transfers.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio exist under Gibraltar law for export, service, or trade proceeds received by an exempted limited partnership. GFSC AML/CFT Guidance Notes nevertheless require a complete invoice-and-remittance matching trail for all trade receipts to deter trade-based money laundering; source documents must be retained for at least five years and produced to the Financial Intelligence Unit upon demand.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Ministry of Finance (Currency Notes Act 1934) as the note-issuing authority under a currency-board arrangement pegged at 1:1 to GBP; the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the prudential, market-conduct, and AML/CFT supervisor; there is no separate foreign-exchange administration, and exchange-control policy follows the UK's fully liberalised post-1979 regime, with OFSI sanctions applicability.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

High for Airwallex (UK) Limited, an FCA-authorised Electronic Money Institution, and comparable regulated EMIs; the Gibraltar ELP may be onboarded fully remotely with a certified KYC pack and CSP introducer. Legal constraints: (i) Airwallex cannot issue GIP-denominated e-money, so balances are held in GBP/EUR/USD e-money with UK partner banks; (ii) the EMI is not a GFSC-licensed bank and cannot hold the ELP's capital-escrow float or confirm GIP currency-board parity; (iii) GFSC AML classification treats unaffiliated e-money aggregators as higher-risk third-party processors, requiring the primary bank's prior written approval before channelling trade payables through Airwallex.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The return is due within nine months of the ELP's accounting reference date; for a standard 31 December year-end the filing deadline is 30 September of the following calendar year. The annual exemption fee is payable on or before 1 July of each tax year.

Box 3 - Gibraltar-Source Income Total profits arising in or derived from Gibraltar from the kitting, packaging, assembly, and fulfilment trade. Enter 'NIL' where the statutory ELP exemption is claimed and all partners are non-resident; any Gibraltar-source income not covered by the exemption must be declared and will be assessed at 12.5%. Late filing of Form ELP-ITR and non-payment of the exemption fee attract a fixed penalty up to GIP 1,000 plus a daily surcharge of GIP 10; persistent default triggers revocation of exempt status, retroactive assessment at the 12.5% statutory rate, monthly interest of 2%, and, for deliberate misdeclaration, criminal fines up to GIP 10,000 and imprisonment.
Box 4 - Cross-Border Payments Dividends, profit distributions, fees, interest, and royalties paid or credited to partners or connected enterprises during the accounting period. Declare all distributions notwithstanding the 0% domestic withholding rate; disclose settlement through ACH Direct or Airwallex and identify each beneficial owner for exchange-of-information purposes. Late filing of Form ELP-ITR and non-payment of the exemption fee attract a fixed penalty up to GIP 1,000 plus a daily surcharge of GIP 10; persistent default triggers revocation of exempt status, retroactive assessment at the 12.5% statutory rate, monthly interest of 2%, and, for deliberate misdeclaration, criminal fines up to GIP 10,000 and imprisonment.
Box 5 - Exemption Claim Statutory declaration that the ELP is entitled to exempt status because no partner is resident of Gibraltar for tax purposes. Execute the joint exemption declaration; the exemption is lost automatically if any partner becomes Gibraltar-resident or if the ELP fails to pay the annual exemption fee. Late filing of Form ELP-ITR and non-payment of the exemption fee attract a fixed penalty up to GIP 1,000 plus a daily surcharge of GIP 10; persistent default triggers revocation of exempt status, retroactive assessment at the 12.5% statutory rate, monthly interest of 2%, and, for deliberate misdeclaration, criminal fines up to GIP 10,000 and imprisonment.
Box 6 - Substance and Transfer Pricing Statement Number of Gibraltar-resident employees, premises occupied, core income-generating activities performed in Gibraltar, and aggregate value of controlled transactions with associated enterprises. Complete even when all taxable income is nil; a valid Economic Substance (ESR) declaration and a transfer pricing arm's length confirmation must accompany the return to avoid penalty review. Late filing of Form ELP-ITR and non-payment of the exemption fee attract a fixed penalty up to GIP 1,000 plus a daily surcharge of GIP 10; persistent default triggers revocation of exempt status, retroactive assessment at the 12.5% statutory rate, monthly interest of 2%, and, for deliberate misdeclaration, criminal fines up to GIP 10,000 and imprisonment.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: The Gibraltar Income Tax (Transfer Pricing) Regulations 2019 require a contemporaneous Local File and Master File where the aggregate value of controlled transactions equals or exceeds GIP 1,000,000 per accounting period, or where the ELP is part of a multinational group with consolidated group revenue exceeding EUR 750,000,000 (Country-by-Country Reporting threshold); below the GIP 1,000,000 threshold, a simplified arm's length declaration in Box 6 suffices.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar's Economic Substance Regulations apply to 'relevant activities', which include distribution and service-centre functions such as third-party logistics, kitting, assembly, and fulfilment. Despite the ELP's full tax exemption, the partnership must demonstrate adequate premises in Gibraltar, a sufficient Gibraltar-resident employee base, minimum operating expenditure, and locally exercised management control; an annual ESR declaration (Form ESR-1) must be filed and supporting records retained for six years. Non-compliance attracts financial penalties, administrative strike-off, revocation of exempt status, and referral to the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Exemption Fraud

Statutory sanction: Income Tax Act 2010 penalties of up to 200% of the tax evaded, back-dated assessments covering seven years, monthly interest of 2%, and criminal fines up to GIP 10,000; the ELP exemption is void ab initio and all profits become chargeable at 12.5%.

Director personal liability: The general partner and controlling beneficial owners are jointly and severally liable for the evaded tax and penalties; criminal conviction carries imprisonment of up to two years on summary conviction or seven years on indictment, together with asset tracing, freezing orders, and travel bans pending recovery proceedings.
Shell Company / Sham Substance

Statutory sanction: Economic Substance Regulations penalties of up to GIP 200,000 per failure, strike-off from the Gibraltar Companies Registry, immediate revocation of exempt status, and disallowance of the 0% effective tax rate with reassessment at 12.5%.

Director personal liability: The general partner is personally liable for ESR fines, restoration costs, and Revenue assessments; the court may pierce the partnership veil, rendering partners personally answerable to creditors and the Revenue, with travel bans and prohibition on outward transfers imposed during formal investigation.
Anti-Money Laundering Breach

Statutory sanction: Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and AML/CFT Regulations: fines up to GIP 1,000,000, confiscation and freezing orders, enforcement action by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission, and termination of all banking relationships.

Director personal liability: Senior management and the general partner face personal prosecution for money laundering with imprisonment of up to 14 years, disqualification as partnership officers, civil restitution to the Crown, and travel bans pending extradition and asset recovery proceedings.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Transfer pricing adjustment to the arm's length standard, additional assessment at 12.5% where any chargeable profit remains, penalties of up to 100% of the adjusted amount, and cross-border enforcement penalties via the UK-Gibraltar information exchange framework.

Director personal liability: Directors and the general partner who knowingly sign false transfer pricing declarations incur personal fines; where the adjusted profit is recharacterised as a distribution to partners, each partner's home jurisdiction may pursue collection personally from those partners, and culpable officers are subject to director disqualification orders, civil restitution, and travel bans.
