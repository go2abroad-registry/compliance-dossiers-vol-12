Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible - the ELP is fiscally transparent under s.9 Income Tax Act 2010 read with the Limited Partnerships Act 1991; profits, gains and losses are allocated to partners per the partnership deed. The ELP has no entity-level income tax liability; partners are assessed only on Gibraltar-source income or, if Gibraltar-resident, on their worldwide income. Non-resident partners with no Gibraltar-source trading profit have no Gibraltar tax exposure.

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not offer a US-style check-the-box election. Statutory mechanisms affecting classification: (i) the Commissioner may direct opaque treatment where a partnership effectively functions as a collective investment vehicle, but this is an administrative direction, not a taxpayer election; (ii) the ELP may elect Private Fund Limited Partnership (PFLP) status under the Limited Partnerships (Private Fund Limited Partnership) Regulations 2018, confirming transparent, non-collective-investment-scheme treatment; (iii) partners may apply the Gibraltar Hybrid Mismatch Regulations (ATAD-aligned) to resolve double-deduction or deduction-without-inclusion positions, with the effective classification of the ELP governed by the partner's tax residence jurisdiction; (iv) small partnerships may elect cash-basis accounting where annual turnover is below the statutory small-traders limit.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GBP 50,000 (Gibraltar pounds) of annual taxable turnover for established businesses with a place of business in Gibraltar; nil-threshold registration for non-established foreign entities from their first taxable supply in Gibraltar; import VAT is chargeable on all commercial imports regardless of turnover; voluntary registration permitted for suppliers making exclusively zero-rated supplies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to exports of whole and skimmed milk powder (HS 0402.10 / 0402.21 / 0402.29) from Gibraltar where: (i) the goods are physically removed from Gibraltar to a destination outside the fiscal territory of Gibraltar within 3 months of the time of supply; (ii) legal title and risk pass to the non-Gibraltar customer; (iii) the ELP retains contemporaneous proof of export, transport and payment; (iv) the milk powder is genuinely classified for human consumption (no mis-declared feed-grade or return-to-factory classification); and (v) the supply is a supply of goods, not services. Offshore B2B brokerage and trading services are outside the scope of Gibraltar VAT under the place-of-supply rules rather than zero-rated; input VAT is recoverable pro rata where used for the export trading activity.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Gibraltar electronic declaration or UK CDS entry with movement reference number and prior export notification where applicable)
  • Bill of Lading (negotiable ocean Bill of Lading or Sea Waybill) evidencing physical departure from Gibraltar to a non-Gibraltar destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103 credit confirmation from the collecting bank evidencing receipt of export proceeds within the statutory repatriation window
  • Commercial Invoice and Detailed Packing List with HS 0402 nomenclature, net weight and dairy batch/lot identifiers
  • Certificate of Free Sale / Health Certificate and Certificate of Origin (preferential or non-preferential) where required by the destination customs authority

Gibraltar does not operate a real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control, or digital stamping mandate. A valid VAT invoice must be issued within 30 days of the time of supply in paper or PDF form showing the partnership name, TRN, VAT registration number, tax point, description, quantity, net value, VAT rate and VAT amount. VAT returns are filed electronically at www.taxation.gi as the sole clearance mechanism. Export declarations are lodged electronically through HM Customs Gibraltar ASYS and, for UK-bound goods, the UK Customs Declaration System (CDS). No API-based clearance certification or pre-stamping of invoices is required.

HM Customs Gibraltar and the Income Tax Office jointly audit zero-rating claims, and the ELP bears the burden of proving both physical export and receipt of foreign consideration. On failed, fabricated or overstated export claims: (i) the zero-rated VAT credit is clawed back with statutory interest from the date of the return; (ii) a civil penalty of up to 100% of the VAT lost applies, mitigable to 40% for unprompted disclosure; (iii) knowingly furnishing false documents is a criminal offence punishable by fines and imprisonment of up to 7 years; (iv) the general partner and, where applicable, the partners are jointly and severally liable for assessed VAT, penalties and interest; (v) repeat offenders face publication on the tax defaulters list, continuous customs stock surveillance, and mandatory bank-guarantee security requirements for future export declarations.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Sterling is fully convertible in Gibraltar on both the current account and the capital account. There are no Ghanaian-style surrender requirements, no exchange rate bands, and no profit-remittance withholding for a tax-transparent Exempted Limited Partnership. Dividend or partnership profit distributions to non-resident owners are freely remittable, provided the payor bank obtains standard source-of-funds and beneficial-owner documentation. In practice, banks apply enhanced due diligence for remittances routed to high-risk territories, but there is no regulatory delay or cap.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or mandatory conversion ratio exists in either the United Kingdom or Gibraltar. Export receipts denominated in any foreign currency may be retained in the ELP's operational account, converted at market rates at the bank's discretion, or used directly for trade settlement. The only relevant obligations are record-keeping and suspicious transaction reporting under the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act and the UK Proceeds of Crime Act 2002; receipts that are inconsistent with the ELP's normal trade pattern must be reported to the financial intelligence unit by the bank.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar does not maintain a separate central bank. The Bank of England acts as the monetary authority for the sterling currency, while the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) regulates all Gibraltar-licensed banks. HM Treasury and the Office of Financial Sanctions Implementation (OFSI) remain responsible for UK-wide sanctions and foreign exchange control policy affecting Gibraltar.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Conditional Medium for ancillary payments, Low for primary treasury clearing. Offshore digital EMIs such as UK or EU-authorised platforms are not licensed as banks in Gibraltar and therefore cannot issue letters of credit, provide overdraft facilities, or hold deposits as core banking services. They may be used for low-value supplier disbursements and FX conversion, but the ELP must ensure that the EMI safeguards client funds with a GFSC-licensed or UK-authorised tier-1 bank. Post-Brexit, an EEA EMI has no automatic passport into Gibraltar; a GFSC authorisation, an appointed-representative arrangement, or a Gibraltar subsidiary may be required before the EMI can hold a material treasury balance.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital contributions from foreign partners, parents or investors must be routed by SWIFT MT103 wire from the remitter's licensed bank to the ELP's GFSC-licensed bank. The instruction should include the full legal partnership name as beneficiary, the Gibraltar IBAN, the purpose code mapping to reverse investment or capital contribution under the UK Overseas Transaction Reporting framework, and a clear reference to the capital call notice and the ELP registration number. The remitter must also supply a certificate of title or declaration of source of funds to the beneficiary bank. For amounts above the bank's normal monitoring threshold, the ELP should pre-notify the receiving bank, provide the signed GP resolution, the capital call document, and a consolidated organisational chart showing the entire ownership chain. The bank will screen all parties against OFSI, UN, EU and GFSC lists before crediting the account.Trade disbursements for milk powder purchases should be executed through either a SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfer or a documentary letter of credit issued under UCP600. For direct supplier payments, the ELP's bank will require an original purchase invoice, shipment and transport documents, and any certificates of origin or dairy quality. For documentary credits, the ELP issues an MT700 to the supplier's bank, with the credit available at sight, against presentation of a clean bill of lading, commercial invoice, packing list, phytosanitary certificate, insurance certificate and delivery acknowledgement. Reimbursement of a confirming bank is made through SWIFT MT202 or MT103. Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Return and full payment due within 9 months after the accounting period end. Example: FYE 30 June 2025 is due by 31 March 2026. The Gibraltar year of assessment runs from 1 July to 30 June, and the return is filed in the year of assessment in which the accounting period ends.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Filing deadline of 31 March following the end of the relevant calendar year; assessments and evidence requests are issued by the Commissioner of Income Tax.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: SME exemption: fewer than 250 employees AND (annual turnover ≤ EUR 50,000,000 or balance sheet ≤ EUR 43,000,000). Non-SME entities must prepare and maintain a Local File; groups with consolidated revenue ≥ EUR 750,000,000 must also maintain a Master File. Controlled transactions above GBP 10,000,000 annually require a contemporaneous transfer pricing register and benchmarking study.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar enforces OECD/EU-aligned economic substance requirements. Distribution and trading of goods is a relevant activity: the ELP must demonstrate adequate physical premises in Gibraltar, qualified full-time employees, core income-generating activities performed in Gibraltar, and appropriate annual expenditure. An annual ESR return is mandatory; failure to satisfy the substance test results in penalties, reassessment, compulsory deregistration, and reporting of the deficiency to foreign tax authorities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Penalty up to 100% of the understated tax, statutory interest, asset seizure, criminal fines, and prosecution by the Crown.

Director personal liability: The general partner and directors of any corporate general partner face personal civil restitution, travel restrictions, and imprisonment of up to 3 years under Gibraltar criminal tax law.
Shell Company / Sham Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: ESR non-compliance penalties, compulsory strike-off of the ELP, CFC attribution of profits to UK or foreign controllers, denial of tax treaty benefits, and enhanced CRS reporting to partner residence jurisdictions.

Director personal liability: Personal CFC tax assessments against UK-resident controllers, asset freezing orders, travel bans under AML legislation, and director disqualification for up to 15 years under the Companies Act 2014.
AML / Proceeds of Crime Deficiency

Statutory sanction: Unlimited criminal fines or fines up to GBP 1,000,000 per contravention, suspension of the ELP bank mandate, confiscation orders, and asset freezing.

Director personal liability: General partner criminally liable under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 (Gibraltar) with imprisonment up to 14 years; personal confiscation orders; restriction orders preventing travel or dealing with property; extradition exposure for serious cross-border fraud.
Unlicensed Trading / Regulatory Breach

Statutory sanction: Cessation orders, daily regulatory fines up to GBP 5,000, forfeiture of consignments, revocation of food business registration, and customs penalties for misdescription of dairy consignments.

Director personal liability: Officers and the general partner personally liable for fines and regulatory costs; disqualification as directors under the Companies Act 2014; potential personal liability to third-party creditors for losses caused by unlawful trading.
