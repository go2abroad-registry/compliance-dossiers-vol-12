Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A registered office in Gibraltar is statutorily mandatory for an Exempted Limited Partnership. Gibraltar permits the registered office to be situated at the premises of a licensed Corporate Service Provider (CSP), which may operate as a serviced or virtual registered office address, provided the address is a genuine physical location in Gibraltar capable of accepting legal process and statutory notices. A purely postal drop-box or a non-physical virtual mailbox without an associated Gibraltar station is not accepted. For the ISIC 3240 low-risk resin statue manufacturing entity, no statutory licence is required and the virtual registered office route via an FSC-licensed CSP will satisfy the registration mandate; however, if the ELP asserts Gibraltar tax residence or operational substance for income tax purposes, the economic substance assessment may require a physical office, local payroll and presence commensurate with the income-generating activity.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (Ministry of Finance); official filing portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi (Income Tax e-filing portal and authorised tax agent gateway)

E-government filing portal

The Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) does not possess a separate legal personality for tax purposes and is therefore registered by the Income Tax Office by reference to the partnership itself. The appointed Gibraltar-resident tax agent or the general partner files the partnership registration application (Form PT1) with the Income Tax Office, accompanied by the certified certificate of limited partnership, the executed limited partnership deed, particulars of all general and limited partners, and the ELP's registered address in Gibraltar. The Income Tax Office issues a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) for the ELP within 10–15 working days as a prerequisite to filing the annual partnership return. Non-resident partners without Gibraltar-source income are not required to obtain an individual TRN or file a Gibratar return, but their identity, capital contribution, and profit-share ratios must be disclosed in the partnership registration and annual return.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible / Territorial_Scope — the ELP is fiscally transparent under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010; profits are assessed in the hands of the partners in accordance with their profit-sharing ratio. Territorial limitation applies: non-resident partners are chargeable to Gibraltar tax only on income arising in Gibraltar or on profits attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment, while the ELP itself is not a taxable person.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or elective entity classification regime exists in Gibraltar. The Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended) prescribes mandatory pass-through treatment for partnerships, including ELPs. A composite assessment election is available under the Income Tax (Partnerships) Regulations only where all partners are Gibraltar-resident and the Commissioner approves the application. Hybrid mismatch rules (aligned with OECD BEPS Action 2 under the Income Tax (Amendment) Regulations) apply to neutralise double non-taxation where a foreign jurisdiction (e.g., the United Kingdom or a Cayman parent jurisdiction) treats the ELP as an opaque entity. The ELP may also apply to adopt an accounting reference date other than 31 December, subject to Commissioner discretion.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None — Gibraltar is outside the UK VAT and EU VAT areas and imposes no VAT, GST, sales, or use tax. Only selective customs and excise duties apply on restricted categories of goods (alcoholic beverages, tobacco, hydrocarbon oils, and certain imported consumables); exports of manufactured goods such as polyresin statues are outside the scope of any Gibraltar indirect tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST registration exists in Gibraltar. An importer of dutiable goods must obtain a Customs Trader authorisation from the Gibraltar Customs and Excise Department, which is a licensing and security arrangement rather than a turnover-based registration threshold; no economic turnover trigger is prescribed.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Within Gibraltar: no VAT is chargeable on exports; resin statue consignments are outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect taxation, and no 0% claim or refund mechanism is required. For goods shipped via the United Kingdom or the EU: the exporter (including the Gibraltar ELP where it holds UK or EU VAT registration and is the supplier of the movement) may zero-rate the export pursuant to UK VAT Act 1994 s.30 and VAT Regulations 1995 reg. 129 only if all of the following cumulative criteria are met: (i) the goods are consigned to a place outside the UK/EU; (ii) the supplier is the exporter of record on the customs export declaration; (iii) the departure is evidenced by authenticated transport documentation; (iv) the supply is made to a non-UK non-EU customer for consideration; and (v) the goods physically leave the territory within three months of supply, with input VAT on resin raw materials reclaimed only to the extent supported by this documentation set.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration (electronic export entry or summary declaration when the goods depart the Gibraltar or transit customs territory)
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or global courier consignment note evidencing physical export of the polyresin statues and identifying the exporter of record and final destination address
  • Commercial invoice and packing list stating the polyresin statue type, unit value, country of origin, and HS 3926.40 / ISIC 3240 classification
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT/MT103/Stripe-PayPal settlement report proving the purchase consideration has been received in the ELP's bank account
  • Proof of delivery (signed delivery note or courier tracking terminal event) matching the consignee to the invoiced customer
  • Certificate of origin (where any preferential origin claim is made on the destination customs declaration)

None — Gibraltar has no real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction controls, SAF-T filing, or digital stamping/e-invoicing mandate for B2C preorder sales of polyresin garage kits. Commercial invoices and settlement receipts from Stripe and PayPal satisfy statutory record-keeping requirements. Where statue shipments physically transit or are fulfilled from the UK or EU, the destination or transit jurisdiction's invoicing and reporting mandate (e.g., UK MTD for VAT, Italian SDI, French Factur-X, Spanish SII) applies only to the taxable supplier of the relevant intra-UK/EU movement leg and not to the Gibraltar ELP as a pure offshore exporter.

Gibraltar: false or fraudulent export declarations render the goods liable to forfeiture and expose the exporter to a penal assessment of up to three times the value of the goods, a court-imposed fine at the discretion of the sentencing court, and on conviction for customs fraud a custodial sentence of up to seven years. United Kingdom/EU transit leg: where zero-rating is supported by forged, backdated, or misdescribed export documentation and statues are diverted for domestic sale or remain within the UK/EU, HMRC or the relevant EU VAT authority may raise a retrospective VAT assessment, disallow corresponding input tax recovery, impose a civil penalty of up to 100% of the evaded VAT (Finance Act 2007 Schedule 24 deliberate concealed inaccuracy), charge default interest from the date of the original return, apply joint and several liability in a missing-trader chain, and suspend or revoke the ELP's UK VAT registration and customs CIEL status, with referral for criminal prosecution under VATA 1994 s.72.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GIP is legal tender in Gibraltar, fixed at parity with GBP and fully backed by sterling. The UK and Gibraltar both operate under no exchange-control regime: current-account payments, capital-account transfers, foreign-currency bank accounts, and profit repatriation are fully liberalised for bona fide trade and investment. No Gibraltar exchange-control approval, blocked-account regime, or non-resident repatriation restriction applies. The only practical conversion hurdles are documentary: source-of-funds evidence, sanctions/PEP screening, and Gibraltar economic-substance/tax compliance for an ELP.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no statutory export-proceeds surrender rules, mandatory conversion ratios, or prescribed repatriation timelines under UK or Gibraltar law. An ISIC 3240 exporter may retain 100% of resin-statue preorder receipts in GIP/GBP, EUR, USD, or other cleared currencies. For cash-equivalent settlements above GBP/EUR 10,000, the receiving bank requires commercial invoices, platform settlement reports, shipping evidence, and a coherent trade narrative under the AML/CTF regime; no conversion into local currency is required.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (sterling anchor and systemic stability) with HM Government of Gibraltar as issuer of the Gibraltar pound (GIP); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) exercises financial conduct and financial-crime supervision over Gibraltar-licensed banks.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low-to-moderate. A non-Gibraltar fintech EMI, such as a UK-authorised or Baltic-authorised e-money institution, is viable only as a front-end merchant collection rail and not as a primary treasury settlement bank. Post-Brexit UK EMIs no longer carry Gibraltar passporting rights; any EMI holding customer funds for the ELP would require its own GFSC e-money or payment-services licences, or a GFSC-regulated bank must be the ultimate settlement account. Stripe and PayPal can be used as payment acquirers, but daily settlement sweeps must be made into a Gibraltar-licensed or UK-licensed bank account subject to the GFSC's expectations.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inward routing is via SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfer from the non-resident investor's own bank account to the Gibraltar ELP's operational or capital-escrow account. The wire must state a clear purpose: 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION TO GIBRALTAR EXEMPTED LIMITED PARTNERSHIP'; no exchange-control certificate is required. The receiving GFSC-regulated bank will perform sanctions screening, verify the investor's identity, and assess the contribution against the ELP's capital schedule and beneficial-ownership register. For contributions above GBP 100,000, provide an accountant's source-of-funds letter, a partnership resolution, and a signed capital account ledger to evidence the underlying ownership and to avoid recharacterisation as taxable income.Trade settlement is executed through SWIFT MT103 for ordinary supplier payments, with MT202 for correspondent interbank cover where separate routing is required. Commercial Letters of Credit are appropriate for high-value mould and bulk-resin purchase orders: the issuing bank in Gibraltar or UK opens an irrevocable documentary credit, with a confirming bank in the supplier's jurisdiction; required documents include commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading/airway bill, inspection certificate, and certificate of origin.
