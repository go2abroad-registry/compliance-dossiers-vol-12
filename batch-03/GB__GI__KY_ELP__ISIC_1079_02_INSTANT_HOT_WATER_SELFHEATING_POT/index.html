Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (Commissioner of Income Tax) for direct taxation; Registrar of Companies / Gibraltar Companies House for statutory entity registration; HM Customs Gibraltar (Customs and Excise Department) for VAT, import and export controls. Primary official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi, supplemented by the Income Tax Office and HM Customs Gibraltar e-services modules.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-held Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership obtains its tax identity by the following sequential procedure: (1) register the ELP with the Registrar of Companies under the Limited Partnerships Act 1995, appoint a Gibraltar-licensed registered office provider, and obtain the partnership registration number; (2) file the prescribed partnership registration return with the Income Tax Office, disclosing the general partner, all limited partners, profit-sharing ratios, business description and place of effective management; (3) obtain or confirm a Gibraltar tax reference number for every Gibraltar-resident partner and furnish a certificate of tax residence for every non-resident partner; (4) if taxable supplies in Gibraltar exceed the statutory threshold, apply to HM Customs Gibraltar for a Gibraltar VAT registration number; (5) establish and maintain statutory books, registers, bank account records and supporting export documentation at the registered office for a minimum retention period of six years.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible / Territorial_Scope. Under the Income Tax Act 2010, a Gibraltar limited partnership is statutorily transparent: the ELP is not a taxable person and bears no entity-level income tax. Each general and limited partner is assessed on the agreed distributive share of partnership chargeable profits. Non-resident partners deriving income not arising in Gibraltar and not received or remitted in Gibraltar fall outside the scope of Gibraltar income tax. An ELP whose business is carried on wholly outside Gibraltar may additionally be constituted as an Exempted Limited Partnership, reinforcing territorial non-liability and exemption from local filing of partnership accounts.

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a check-the-box election because partnership transparency is fixed by statute. Available elective mechanisms include: (a) allocation of profits, losses and capital through the partnership agreement; (b) election of a non-standard accounting period with the Commissioner's prior approval; (c) for corporate limited partners, election of group taxation treatment under the Gibraltar corporate tax regime where the statutory group criteria are met; and (d) alignment under the OECD BEPS Action 2 hybrid-mismatch rules incorporated into Gibraltar Income Tax (Hybrid Mismatch Arrangements) legislation, including joint elective adjustments to neutralize deduction/non-inclusion mismatches where an overseas jurisdiction fiscally classifies the Gibraltar ELP as an opaque corporation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

£100,000 (Gibraltar pound sterling) of annual taxable turnover in Gibraltar. Voluntary registration is permitted below the threshold; any unregistered importer is required to register and account for VAT on goods imported into Gibraltar irrespective of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

An export supply of self-heating instant meal products qualifies for 0% VAT where: (i) the ELP transfers ownership of the goods to a customer established outside Gibraltar; (ii) the goods are physically removed from Gibraltar as a result of the supply, by the supplier or on the supplier's behalf, within the statutory time limit (normally three months from the time of supply, or an extended period authorized by the Commissioner); (iii) the goods are not intended to return to Gibraltar; and (iv) the ELP retains and produces on demand the prescribed evidence of export (customs declaration and transport documentation). Internationally supplied services, including services physically performed outside Gibraltar and received by a non-resident trader for business purposes, are zero-rated where the direct benefit of the service is outside Gibraltar. For the composite food/heating-device product, the domestic liability depends on the principal-element test, but export zero-rating remains available regardless of domestic classification provided the export evidence is complete. Excess input VAT attributable to zero-rated exports is reclaimable through the quarterly VAT return and the ordinary refund/offset procedure.

  • Customs export declaration lodged electronically with HM Customs Gibraltar, including the unique movement reference number
  • Commercial invoice or proforma invoice stating the exporter, overseas buyer, goods description, quantities, unit values and Incoterms
  • Bill of Lading (ocean), Air Waybill (air) or CMR consignment note (road) evidencing physical removal from Gibraltar
  • Packing list and certificate of origin where required under the applicable trade agreement
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT/telegraphic transfer confirmation evidencing receipt of payment
  • Transporter's signed receipt and the ELP's export evidence register maintained under the Gibraltar VAT (Export) Rules

Gibraltar does not currently impose real-time e-invoicing, continuous transaction controls, digital fiscal clearance or government digital stamping. VAT invoices may be issued electronically and must contain the prescribed particulars under the Gibraltar VAT invoicing rules (full tax invoice, simplified invoice below the value threshold, and self-billed invoice where agreed). VAT returns are lodged quarterly through the HM Customs Gibraltar e-filing service; import and export declarations are submitted electronically through the national customs electronic declaration system. No SAF-T or PEPPOL mandate exists at present, although the Government of Gibraltar is progressively digitizing customs procedures through its 'One Government' paperless platform.

HM Customs Gibraltar and the Income Tax Office may audit export evidence retrospectively for up to six years. Missing, deficient or contradictory export evidence empowers the Commissioner to issue a protective or jeopardy assessment clawing back the zero-rating and charging VAT at the standard rate of 15% on the export value, together with statutory interest on the unpaid tax. Civil penalties apply by reference to culpability: carelessness attracts up to 30% of the tax, deliberate inaccuracies up to 70%, and deliberate and concealed inaccuracies up to 100%. Fraudulent evasion of VAT carries a fine of up to £50,000 and/or imprisonment for up to seven years in the Supreme Court of Gibraltar. Failure to repatriate export proceeds within the 180-day foreign-exchange window and to produce the inward remittance certificate materially weakens the economic-substance evidence and invites re-characterization of the transaction as a sham or circular scheme. The ELP and its general and limited partners are jointly and severally liable for the partnership's VAT obligations, exposing them to clawbacks, penalties, interest and, on proof of fraud, criminal prosecution.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GIP is pegged at parity to GBP and is fully convertible with zero legal restriction; both GIP and GBP circulate freely. Gibraltar has no exchange controls on current or capital account (Exchange Control Act 1972 disapplied) and imposes no withholding taxes on dividends, interest, royalties or partnership distributions made by an Exempted Limited Partnership to non-resident partners. Profit remittance and capital repatriation hurdles are purely documentary: the clearing bank requires a certificate of good standing, an up-to-date UBO register maintained at the registered office, a substance-related declaration where applicable, and standard source-of-funds evidence.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Neither Gibraltar nor the United Kingdom imposes any mandatory surrender ratio, conversion timeline, or repatriation window for export proceeds. The ELP may lawfully hold 100% of receipts in any freely convertible currency, with SWIFT purpose codes reflecting trade settlement for transaction-monitoring purposes only.

Central bank supervision: The Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the sole prudential banking regulator in Gibraltar; HM Government of Gibraltar's Commissioner of Currency issues Gibraltar Pound (GIP) notes and coin under a currency-board system fully backed by Bank of England sterling. The Bank of England is the ultimate monetary anchor for the GIP peg, but has no direct supervisory authority over GFSC-licensed banks; Gibraltar maintains no separate central bank or foreign-exchange administration.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Medium. A Gibraltar-incorporated ELP may onboard with UK FCA-authorised EMIs (Wise Business, Revolut Business, Tide) under the UK-Gibraltar financial services framework; however, EMIs are not viable as the primary treasury institution. They lack GFSC licensing, cannot host documentary trade instruments (letters of credit), and are risk-averse toward offshore ELP structures. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All partner capital injections must flow by SWIFT MT103 from the contributor's bank to the ELP's GFSC-licensed account. Use purpose code 'CAPC' (Capital Contribution) or 'INVC' for equity-like contributions, with the payment reference 'ELP Capital Injection — [ELP Name] — [Partnership Agreement date]'. GBP contributions should clear via UK CHAPS where the remitting bank is UK-connected; EUR transfers require SWIFT EUR via a London or Frankfurt correspondent because Gibraltar is outside SEPA; USD transfers clear via Fedwire/CHIPS using the receiving bank's New York correspondent. The bank-side verification bundle comprises: the capital contribution clause of the LP agreement, GP board resolution authorising the account and the capital call, the ELP's capital-account ledger, and the remitting partner's bank statement evidencing source of funds.Supplier payments below GBP 250,000 equivalent are executed via SWIFT MT103 with purpose code 'TRDP' (trade payment) and matched on a 3-way basis (purchase order, commercial invoice, goods-receipt documentation). Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GBP 500,000 (aggregate related-party transactions per chargeable period)

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar's Economic Substance (Implementation) Ordinance 2019 requires an ELP carrying on a relevant activity to demonstrate management and control in Gibraltar, adequate physical presence and employees, core income-generating activities performed in Gibraltar, and filing of confidential economic substance information annually. Active manufacturing of exothermic self-heating instant meals is a tangible business activity; the ELP must substantiate production activities, staff, and premises in Gibraltar where any profits are claimed as Gibraltar-resident.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Penalties up to 100% of the tax underpaid plus interest at the prevailing rate; criminal liability under the Income Tax Act includes a fine not exceeding three times the tax evaded and imprisonment of up to three years; surrender of assets may be ordered.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced for fraudulent trading under the Companies Act. Directors may be subject to restitution orders, asset freezes, travel bans, and disqualification as directors for up to 15 years.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance (Implementation) Ordinance 2019, non-compliant entities face a GBP 10,000 penalty in the first year and GBP 100,000 for continued non-compliance; the Registrar may strike off the ELP, resulting in loss of tax residence and ineligibility for treaty benefits.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false substance declarations face criminal penalties of up to GBP 50,000 and imprisonment of up to two years; directors may be banned from managing Gibraltar entities and are personally liable for civil penalties.
Anti-Money Laundering / Beneficial Ownership Failure

Statutory sanction: Financial penalties up to GBP 250,000 per breach under the Proceeds of Crime Act, with additional civil penalties for each day of non-compliance; the Registrar may suspend the ELP's registration and block profit repatriation.

Director personal liability: Directors face criminal liability including imprisonment for up to two years and personal fines; travel bans may be issued under the Court's crime proceeds enforcement powers.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing

Statutory sanction: Tax adjustment under the arm's-length principle; penalties of up to 100% of the undercharged tax plus late payment interest; separate fixed penalty of GBP 20,000 for failing to maintain transfer pricing documentation.

Director personal liability: Directors knowingly approving artificial pricing may be jointly and severally liable for tax adjustments and subject to civil restitution claims; repeat offenders face travel restrictions and disqualification orders.
