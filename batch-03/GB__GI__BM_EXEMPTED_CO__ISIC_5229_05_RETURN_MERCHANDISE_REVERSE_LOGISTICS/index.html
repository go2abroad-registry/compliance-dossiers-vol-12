Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (Commissioner of Income Tax); official portal: www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation. Customs and indirect tax administration: HM Customs Gibraltar, Gibraltar Border and Coastguard Agency.

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company establishing a Gibraltar presence must: (1) register as an external company with the Registrar of Companies under the Companies Act 2014, submitting certified Bermuda certificate of incorporation, exemption certificate, constitutional documents, and appointment of a Gibraltar-resident corporate service provider; (2) apply to the Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) with a residence declaration (central management and control); (3) obtain a Gibraltar EORI identifier from HM Customs Gibraltar before lodging any import/export declaration; and (4) appoint a local tax/customs correspondence agent. The TRN must be quoted on corporate tax returns, annual filings, and customs declarations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone / Territorial_Scope: The Bermuda Exempted Company benefits from a Bermuda statutory exemption certificate (no Bermuda tax for up to 50 years). In Gibraltar, the entity is classified as non-resident unless central management and control is exercised in Gibraltar; a non-resident company is chargeable only on Gibraltar-source income (territorial scope), while foreign-source income and dividends are outside the Gibraltar tax net. If Gibraltar resident status arises, entity-level income tax applies at the standard corporate rate of 12.5%.

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate US-style check-the-box elections. Entity classification follows residence (place of effective management) and territorial source rules under the Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended). Available statutory mechanisms include: non-resident status filings evidenced by board minutes and meeting locations; group relief or group election between Gibraltar-resident companies; Returned Goods Relief applications for customs purposes; and ATAD-aligned anti-hybrid and controlled foreign company rule filings. Hybrid mismatch check-the-box elections are not permitted.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar Import Duty and Excise Regime (No VAT/GST; Gibraltar is outside the EU VAT and Customs Union)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable (no VAT/GST in Gibraltar). A Gibraltar EORI number and HM Customs registration are mandatory from the first import of goods, with no economic turnover de minimis threshold. A non-resident Bermuda Exempted Company providing cross-border reverse-logistics services without importing goods into Gibraltar has no indirect tax registration obligation.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar levies no VAT, so the EU/UK-style 0% export rating does not apply. For customs purposes, goods exported from Gibraltar are outside the charge to Gibraltar import and excise duty. Returned-goods reverse logistics may claim Returned Goods Relief (RGR) where goods previously exported from Gibraltar are re-imported unchanged after grading, repair, or quality inspection within the statutory relief period (generally six months). Goods in transit may use duty suspension procedures. The Bermuda Exempted Company must evidence that no Gibraltar trade presence exists other than non-resident offshore services.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration (electronic lodgement with HM Customs Gibraltar)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List reflecting return-merchandise grading or repair
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill evidencing export and re-import movement
  • Returned Goods Relief application and duty suspension evidence
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or FX repatriation confirmation
  • Bermuda Exemption Certificate, Gibraltar TRN and EORI references

Gibraltar does not mandate electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping. Paper or electronic invoices are acceptable subject to statutory record-keeping periods. Customs declarations must be lodged electronically through HM Customs Gibraltar; no real-time tax clearance API or fiscal stamping regime applies.

HM Customs Gibraltar may forfeit goods and impose penalties of up to three times the duty evaded for false export declarations or misuse of Returned Goods Relief. The Commissioner of Income Tax may raise assessments to claw back reliefs within four years (six years for fraud or negligent conduct), with penalties up to 100% of the tax understated plus daily default fines; criminal prosecution may result in imprisonment up to two years. Although no VAT zero-rating exists, cross-border reverse logistics remains a high fiscal-transparency review category, requiring a complete audit trail of contracts, customs declarations, transport records, and payment settlement.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Capital and current-account convertibility are de jure unrestricted in both Gibraltar and Bermuda; GIP is freely convertible into GBP, EUR, and USD at the pegged rate through licensed commercial banks without central-bank authorisation. Profit remittance, dividends, intercompany loan repayments, and liquidation distributions face no exchange-control hurdle and no Gibraltarian or Bermudian withholding tax, but practical blockers are: (i) enhanced customer due diligence triggered by a zero-tax Bermuda domicile, (ii) potential Gibraltar corporate tax at the standard 10% rate where management and control are exerted from Gibraltar, and (iii) UK controlled-foreign-company and transfer-pricing scrutiny if the Bermuda parent holds commercial risk while the UK and Gibraltar entities execute reverse-logistics operations. The treasury blueprint therefore preserves the tax-exempt character by contracting through a Gibraltar or UK operational service provider under an arm's-length arrangement and by keeping the group treasury ledger at the Bermuda Exempted Company layer.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender obligation exists in Gibraltar, and the Bermuda Exchange Control Act 1972 applies only to resident persons; the exempted company's Non-Resident designation exempts it from any mandatory conversion of foreign-currency receipts. Export proceeds, salvage-sale proceeds, and service fees may therefore be retained indefinitely in GBP, USD, or EUR. Statutory conversion timeline: not applicable; optional conversion at the bank's prevailing rate; mandatory conversion ratio: 0%. For reverse-logistics flows, maintained customs entry records (UK import and export declarations), RMA documentation, and salvage-sale invoices must substantiate the underlying trade and avoid recharacterisation of the offshore balances as unexplained credits.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the statutory regulator and prudential supervisor of banks, investment firms, and FX intermediaries in Gibraltar; the Government of Gibraltar Treasury issues the Gibraltar pound (GIP) under the Currency Notes Act 1934 at a fixed 1:1 parity with UK sterling, so Gibraltar has no fully autonomous central bank. The Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) is the home-jurisdiction central bank and exchange-control administrator for the Bermuda Exempted Company, but an exempted company holding a Non-Resident designation under the Bermuda Exchange Control Act 1972 is exempt from per-transaction exchange-control approval. Ultimate monetary policy and sterling liquidity support flow from the Bank of England through the GIP peg.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: HIGH for operational collections and disbursements; LOW as the sole capital-escrow home. Airwallex (UK) Limited, an FCA-authorised Electronic Money Institution, is pre-selected as the target rail and provides a UK GBP sort-code account enabling Local_ACH (Faster Payments and BACS), multi-currency wallets (GBP/EUR/USD), and SWIFT MT103 outbound payments through a single API. Legal constraints: (i) funds are safeguarded under the E-Money Regulations 2011, not FSCS-protected deposits; (ii) the EMI cannot issue a bank reference letter or operate a true escrow under Gibraltar law; (iii) certain Gibraltar clearing banks will reject an initial credit arriving from an EMI as unverified third-party funds, so the initial capital injection should first clear through Jyske Bank (Gibraltar) or Butterfield Bermuda and only then be swept into Airwallex; (iv) the EMI's digital onboarding is entirely remote and does not satisfy the physical-appearance condition where a commercial bank requires it, but it remains acceptable for sub-threshold refund disbursements.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File required where controlled cross-border reverse-logistics transactions exceed GBP 5,000,000 in an accounting period; Master File triggered when group consolidated revenue exceeds GBP 50,000,000 and controlled transactions exceed GBP 5,000,000; simplified record keeping applies to transactions below GBP 1,000,000.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to all companies tax-resident in Gibraltar conducting relevant activities, including reverse logistics and distribution. The company must demonstrate that its core income-generating activities (strategic sourcing, return handling, quality inspection, staffing, and risk management) are performed in Gibraltar with an equipped warehouse, qualified employees, sufficient revenue expenditure, and board meetings held in Gibraltar. Failure to comply results in ESR fines, deeming provisions, denial of DTA benefits and possible strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Understatement

Statutory sanction: Penalty equal to 100% of underpaid tax, fixed fines up to GBP 3,000, and imprisonment for up to seven years under the Taxes (Offences) Act; the tax office may reopen the previous six years of returns and strike the company off.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly signed a false return are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax, may be served travel bans, and face personal criminal conviction with imprisonment of up to seven years.
Transfer Pricing Mismatching

Statutory sanction: Transfer pricing adjustment to arm's-length profit, interest on the under-reported tax, and a tax-geared penalty of up to 25% of the resulting liability; continued breach leads to a formal TP audit and uplift of all cross-border transactions.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally accountable for the grossed-up tax adjustment where they signed or implemented the mismatched invoices; disqualification from serving as a director for up to 15 years may be imposed.
Shell Company / No Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Economic Substance Regulations penalties of GBP 10,000 plus GBP 500 per day for continuing failure; GDP-based penalty of proportion to profits; deeming of the income as Gibraltar-source subject to 12.5% tax; striking off from the Companies Registry and loss of treaty access.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly liable for ESR penalties and restored tax; deliberate shell misuse leads to removal of the corporate veil, civil recovery orders, and personal tax residence assessments.
AML / Disguised Distribution

Statutory sanction: AML offence attracts a fine up to GBP 2,000,000 or imprisonment for up to eight years under the Proceeds of Crime Act; the disguised distribution is recharacterized and taxed at 12.5% plus surcharge; the Financial Intelligence Unit may freeze bank accounts.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for confiscation of the distribution value, automatic travel bans, and imprisonment of up to eight years; piercing of the corporate veil is presumed where the funds were routed through a related BVI or Cayman account.
