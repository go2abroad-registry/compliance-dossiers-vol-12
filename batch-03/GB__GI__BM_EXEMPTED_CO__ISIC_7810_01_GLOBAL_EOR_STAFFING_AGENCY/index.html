Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope - the Bermuda Exempted Company is tax-exempt in Bermuda by exempted-company election, but its Gibraltar branch/activity is taxed by Gibraltar only on chargeable income accruing in or derived from Gibraltar (Income Tax Act 2010). EOR and payroll service fees generated through Gibraltar-based activity are chargeable at 12.5% corporation tax; foreign-source income not arising or received in Gibraltar is outside the Gibraltar charge.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists in Gibraltar. Available mechanisms: (i) Qualifying Company / finance-centre income exemption application under the Income Tax Act 2010 for specified qualifying categories, subject to economic substance conditions; (ii) Gibraltar group relief elections; (iii) capital allowance pooling and transfer elections; (iv) hybrid mismatch alignment/reporting elections under OECD/EU ATAD-aligned hybrid mismatch rules (effective 1 January 2020); (v) Bermuda exempted-status election with the Bermuda Registrar (no impact on Gibraltar tax).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax in Gibraltar; the territory is outside the EU VAT area and outside UK VAT (Value Added Tax Act 1994, Schedule 1). Gibraltar imposes only narrow customs and excise duties on specified physical goods under the Customs Ordinance 2012 (alcohol, tobacco, fuel, certain imports). Cross-border EOR and contractor payroll services are outside the scope of any Gibraltar indirect tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT/GST registration threshold exists because no VAT/GST system is in force. No indirect tax registration is required for pure service/EOR activities. Importers of physical goods must obtain an importer code from HM Customs Gibraltar (no de minimis threshold; duty arises on first importation; reliefs exist for temporary admission and re-export).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - Gibraltar has no VAT to zero-rate. Cross-border EOR/payroll services rendered to non-Gibraltar clients are not subject to any Gibraltar indirect tax and require no 0% VAT claim or refund mechanism. For physical goods, customs-duty relief may be achieved by export-processing, temporary-admission, or duty-drawback regimes under the Customs Ordinance 2012; no VAT refund exists.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full de jure and de facto convertibility for GIP against GBP, EUR, and USD. There are no current-account or capital-account exchange restrictions applicable to a Bermuda Exempted Company operating a global EOR payroll platform with treasury functions routed through Gibraltar. Client fee receipts, payroll funding remittances, intercompany shareholder loans, and profit remittances may flow freely in any convertible currency without exchange-control approval. The only practical hurdles are GFSC/AML source-of-funds verification, CRS/FATCA filing classification, and standard banking confirmation of the commercial purpose of each flow. Profit remittance to non-resident shareholders is unrestricted, subject only to beneficial-ownership identification and tax-transparency reporting.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. The United Kingdom abolished mandatory surrender of export proceeds in 1979, and Gibraltar applies the same deregulated framework. There is no statutory timeline for repatriation, no minimum conversion ratio, and no designation into state-controlled clearing accounts for export-of-services receipts such as EOR client fees and payroll margins. Export proceeds may be retained indefinitely in any freely convertible currency; only standard GFSC record-keeping, accounting, and AML/CFT transaction-monitoring obligations apply.

Central bank supervision: There is no independent central bank of Gibraltar. The Bank of England anchors monetary policy for the Sterling area, the Government of Gibraltar issues the Gibraltar Pound (GIP) at a fixed 1:1 parity with GBP, and the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the competent supervisor for banks, payment institutions, and electronic-money institutions. The operative exchange-control framework is the residual and suspended UK Exchange Control Act 1947 (as extended to Gibraltar), under which no exchange-control permits, consents, or licensing are required for bona fide current or capital transactions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

MEDIUM-HIGH viability with mandatory structural safeguards. Gibraltar has no unlicensed EMI sector: electronic-money institutions require GFSC authorisation under the Financial Services (Electronic Money) Regulations 2011 and the Payment Services Act, and may passport into the UK under the preserved UK-Gibraltar financial-services arrangement. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise Business, Airwallex, Revolut Business) can onboard this Bermuda Exempted Co for bulk contractor payroll disbursement and virtual-IBAN segregation, but they cannot take deposits or hold the treasury's capital escrow beyond safeguarding thresholds. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: MEDIUM-HIGH viability with mandatory structural safeguards. Gibraltar has no unlicensed EMI sector: electronic-money institutions require GFSC authorisation under the Financial Services (Electronic Money) Regulations 2011 and the Payment Services Act, and may passport into the UK under the preserved UK-Gibraltar financial-services arrangement. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise Business, Airwallex, Revolut Business) can onboard this Bermuda Exempted Co for bulk contractor payroll disbursement and virtual-IBAN segregation, but they cannot take deposits or hold the treasury's capital escrow beyond safeguarding thresholds. The blueprint therefore mandates a tier-1 Gibraltar authorised bank as master-account custodian, with the EMI deployed solely as a disintermediating, low-value payroll clearing rail. GFSC-regulated EMIs will also apply enhanced sector-level due-diligence to staffing and EOR platforms.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

10% flat corporate income tax rate on assessable profits (Income Tax Act 2010, as amended); no progressive corporate bands and no surtax.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by a Gibraltar company to any shareholder, resident or non-resident.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Within nine months after the end of the year of assessment (1 July to 30 June), i.e. no later than 31 March immediately following the 30 June tax-year end; the Comptroller of Income Tax may issue an estimated assessment where a return is not received.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory Gibraltar monetary threshold is published; the Income Tax Office applies an OECD-aligned materiality test. Practical safe harbor: prepare a Local File when controlled cross-border EOR transactions exceed GIP 1,000,000 per year and a Master File when group consolidated revenue exceeds GIP 50,000,000; maintain a benchmarking study where an intercompany service margin falls outside the interquartile range (typically 3%-7% for low-risk EOR administration).

Economic substance scope: The Income Tax (Economic Substance) Regulations 2019 apply to a Gibraltar-resident company carrying on a Relevant Activity; a global EOR and contractor payroll platform falls within the headquarters/service-centre category. The company must in Gibraltar: (i) hold and document board meetings directing the EOR trade; (ii) employ staff adequate in number and seniority relative to the payroll headcount administered; (iii) maintain a physical office; (iv) incur expenditure proportionate to gross revenue; and (v) perform core income-generating activities (client contracting, payroll computation and disbursement, statutory reporting to each host jurisdiction) locally. Delegation to non-Gibraltar affiliates is permitted only if supervised from Gibraltar; failure triggers financial penalties, exchange of information with HMRC, and adjustment of the territorial basis.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Interest at 8% per annum on unpaid tax, tax-geared penalties of up to 100% of the under-assessed tax plus a 50% concealment uplift; on conviction a court fine at discretion and/or imprisonment up to 7 years under the Income Tax Act 2010 and Criminal Justice Act 2011.

Director personal liability: Directors and shadow directors may be personally assessed for the tax where assets were dissipated, disqualified for up to 15 years, and subject to travel restraints during mutual legal assistance and confiscation proceedings; fraudulent trading triggers unlimited personal contribution under the Companies Act 2014.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Penalties of up to GIP 10,000 per substance category plus continuing daily default penalties; the Income Tax Office will cancel territorial-remittance treatment, re-open assessments, and exchange the file with HMRC under the UK-Gibraltar exchange of information provisions.

Director personal liability: Directors face civil restitution for the tax shortfall, formal disqualification from office, and exposure to Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 confiscation orders; courts may impose travel bans pending determination.
Anti-Money Laundering Breach (EOR Payroll Pass-Through)

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and Gibraltar AML Regulations, up to GIP 250,000 fine and/or 14 years' imprisonment for money laundering; the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission may suspend payment operations and impose daily compounding penalties.

Director personal liability: Personal criminal liability for consenting directors; trading disqualification; confiscation and freezing orders over personal assets; travel bans and surrender of passports are available where flight risk is shown.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: Adjustment of profits to the arm's-length result, 100% tax-geared penalties on the uplifted amount, daily late-documentation surcharges, and exposure to aligned UK HMRC inquiries through the exchange mechanism.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed inaccurate TP declarations may be personally liable for the penalty uplift, disqualified as directors, and named in mutual tax assistance requests limiting international travel and banking.
