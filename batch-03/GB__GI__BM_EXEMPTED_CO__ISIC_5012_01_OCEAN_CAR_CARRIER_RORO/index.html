Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, Customs & Income Tax Department, HM Government of Gibraltar, 4th Floor, Eurotowers, Gibraltar; official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi (tax forms and services via the Customs & Income Tax Department).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-incorporated Bermuda Exempted Company must register with the Gibraltar Income Tax Office only if it becomes Gibraltar tax resident (central management and control in Gibraltar) or derives Gibraltar-source income through a permanent establishment. Step 1: file a non-residence / status declaration with the Income Tax Office supported by the Bermuda certificate of incorporation, certificate of good standing, constitutional documents, registers of directors and shareholders, registered office details and the Bermuda Tax Exemption Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966. Step 2: if any Gibraltar activity exists, complete the standard corporate registration form with certified copies of the Bermuda documents and obtain the Gibraltar Corporation Tax Reference Number (generally issued within 10 working days). Step 3: if a branch or place of business is established in Gibraltar, register the branch with the Gibraltar Companies Registry. Step 4: file any required notification under the Gibraltar Economic Substance (Companies and Foreign Partnerships) Act 2019 where the company is Gibraltar tax resident.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone - The Bermuda Exempted Company is statutorily exempt from Bermuda income tax under its tax exemption certificate; it is not Gibraltar tax resident absent local management and control, and international RoRo freight income is treated as foreign-source and excluded from Gibraltar corporation tax.

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not maintain a US-style check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch election regime. Applicable statutory elections: (i) Bermuda Tax Exemption Certificate election under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, granting immunity from Bermuda tax until 2035 or 2050 (extendable); (ii) Gibraltar non-resident status election by filing a formal status declaration with the Income Tax Office; (iii) if Gibraltar tax residence is nonetheless accepted, election for participation exemption for qualifying dividends and capital gains under the Income Tax Act 2010; no tonnage-tax election is currently available for shipping companies in Gibraltar.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST in Gibraltar; indirect taxation is limited to customs and excise import duties, transit and transshipment controls, and port dues (non-resident RoRo shipping services remain outside the indirect tax net).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - Gibraltar has no VAT/GST registration regime or economic turnover threshold triggering registration. Commercial importers of dutiable goods must obtain customs and excise registration from H.M. Customs Gibraltar; RoRo cargo in transit or transshipment through the Port of Gibraltar is exempt from customs duty with no registration threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is not applicable because Gibraltar imposes no VAT/GST. The equivalent relief is the customs transit/transshipment exemption: vehicles carried by the RoRo vessel and discharged at foreign ports are not chargeable to Gibraltar customs duty, and the international ocean transport service is outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect taxation. To preserve non-taxation, the company must demonstrate that the vessel's effective management is outside Gibraltar and that the freight income is foreign-source.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export or Transit Declaration (or UK CDS Export Declaration where applicable)
  • RoRo Bill of Lading (or seaway bill) evidencing port of loading and port of discharge
  • Vehicle and freight manifest and Gibraltar Port Authority sailing / port clearance certificate
  • Bank inward remittance certificate or SWIFT MT103 trail evidencing repatriation of freight income within 180 days

No real-time fiscal clearance, API clearance or digital stamping mandate applies in Gibraltar. Customs declarations for imported or transshipped vehicles are filed electronically through Gibraltar Customs (or the UK Customs Declaration Service where the applicable UK-Gibraltar customs union route is used); invoices and supporting transport documents must be retained for at least six years to sustain transshipment relief and foreign-source income treatment.

The Gibraltar Income Tax Office may audit status rulings, transshipment files and economic substance documentation. If central management and control is found to be in Gibraltar, or the export/transshipment documentation is incomplete or fraudulent, the shipping income is reclassified as Gibraltar-source and assessed at the 10% corporate rate, with statutory interest and penalties up to 100% of the tax undercharged (or greater in aggravated cases), as well as criminal fines and imprisonment for fraudulent evasion; retrospective reassessments generally extend to a period of six years, and the Bermuda tax exemption certificate does not preclude Gibraltar assessment where economic substance and actual control are in Gibraltar.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar utilises the Gibraltar pound (GIP), which is maintained at parity with, and is freely convertible into, pound sterling (GBP). Both current-account and capital-account transactions are fully convertible; there are no general exchange controls, central-bank approval gates, or repatriation restrictions on dividends, interest, management fees, loan principal, or sale proceeds. Internal AML/KYC documentation and economic-substance records remain the practical hurdles to profit remittance. A Bermuda-Exempted Company can remit funds to shareholders and creditors provided the underlying treasury activity is supported by board minutes, invoices, and factual substance evidence.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar and the United Kingdom impose no statutory export-proceeds surrender requirements. There is no mandatory timeline for repatriating export receipts and no compulsory conversion ratio. The Ro-Ro vessel operator may retain foreign-currency earnings offshore, elect to invoice in USD or EUR, and settle liabilities in the traded currency, subject only to bank onboarding and anti-money-laundering narrative requirements.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England, acting as the United Kingdom monetary authority, with the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission as the local banking regulator for the British Overseas Territory of Gibraltar. No separate foreign-exchange administration is established in Gibraltar.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-Medium. Offshore digital e-money institutions and non-resident EMIs are not an acceptable primary treasury rail for a Gibraltar-regulated vessel operator. They generally lack a Gibraltar banking licence, cannot hold client funds as regulated deposits, may not offer credit or complex trade-finance instruments, and are not integrated into SWIFT-compatible commercial letter-of-credit issuance. Such EMIs may be used only as ancillary collection channels, subject to prior bank consent and enhanced due-diligence review; no EMI should hold the main operating liquidity for the Ro-Ro shipping business.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound shareholder capital should be routed by SWIFT MT103 international wire transfer using ISO 20022 payment-message standards where possible. The remitter must include a valid UETR, the Gibraltar bank's BIC and account number, and narrative identifying the payment as 'Bermuda Exempted Company equity capital contribution'. Recommended ISO 20022 purpose code for shareholder funding is 'INTC' for intercompany transfer, with supplementary narrative 'CAP' or 'CAPP' where the receiving bank requires a capital marker. For high-value GBP transfers, use the parent bank's London settlement account and CHAPS or Faster Payment only where the beneficiary Gibraltar bank maintains a UK clearing sort code; otherwise standard SWIFT MT103 is the most reliable rail. All received funds must be reconciled against a board-approved capital injection resolution and entered into the exempted company's capital register.Operational disbursements for the Ro-Ro vessel, including bunker purchases, port fees, canal tolls, crew wages, repairs, spare parts, and charter-hire payments, should be cleared through SWIFT MT103 wires with UETR and appropriate ISO 20022 trade/commercial purpose codes such as 'TRAD' or 'COMC'. For acquisition and large-scale vessel supply contracts, an irrevocable commercial letter of credit governed by UCP600 should be issued by the Gibraltar relationship bank to the seller's bank, payable at sight or by deferred payment, depending on the shipyard contract. The LC route should be used only where title documents, build contracts, and inspection certificates are compliant.
