Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Her Majesty's Government of Gibraltar Income Tax Office (ITO) and Companies House Gibraltar; online portal: www.ito.gov.gi and www.companieshouse.gov.gi

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-incorporated Bermuda Exempted Company, the following procedure applies: (1) appoint a Gibraltar resident company manager or tax agent; (2) file an application for a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN) with the ITO using Form GI-TIN-NR, accompanied by certified certificate of incorporation and certificate of good standing from the Bermuda Registrar of Companies; (3) register for Value Added Tax (VAT) via the GRA (Gibraltar Revenue Authority) online e-postbox using VAT Form 1 if taxable supplies above the threshold or if the entity intends to recover input tax on local purchases; (4) obtain a Gibraltar business registration certificate from Companies House Gibraltar for local trading or a certificate of non-resident status if the entity merely renders offshore services without a physical establishment; (5) after issuance, file annual returns and VAT returns electronically. No notarization or apostille is required for corporate documents if submitted via a licensed agent.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Under the Income Tax Act 2010 (Gibraltar), qualifying companies may elect to be taxed under the 'qualifying company' regime. The Bermudian Exempted Company may also elect to be treated as non-resident for Gibraltar tax purposes on foreign-source income, subject to hybrid mismatch rules under OECD BEPS (Section 3 Income Tax Act 2010). There is no analogous 'check-the-box' election; however, the entity may apply for an advanced tax ruling from the ITO to confirm pass-through treatment of offshore subscription revenue.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

VAT (Gibraltar Value Added Tax Act 2004)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GBP 85,000 annual taxable turnover for goods and services supplied in Gibraltar; non-resident suppliers of digital design subscription services are required to register if they exceed this threshold through B2C supplies to Gibraltar consumers, but B2B cross-border supplies are generally zero-rated and may be exempt from local VAT registration if the place of supply is outside Gibraltar.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to: (1) goods physically exported from Gibraltar to a non-Gibraltar destination; (2) services supplied to a business customer located outside Gibraltar where the place of supply is outside Gibraltar; (3) UI/UX digital design subscription services supplied on an offshore basis to non-Gibraltar clients, provided the recipient is outside Gibraltar and the contract and invoices evidence the offshore nature. For B2C digital services, proof of customer residence outside Gibraltar is required to avoid reverse charge assessment.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document / SAD)
  • Bill of Lading, Sea Waybill or Airway Bill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank receipt evidencing foreign currency receipt
  • Contract and invoice evidencing offshore UI/UX design subscription services
  • Non-residence certificate of the overseas customer for B2C supplies

Gibraltar does not operate a real-time e-invoicing or fiscal clearance system. VAT returns must be submitted electronically via the GRA e-postbox portal. Electronic invoices are permitted and must be retained digitally for six years. There is no digital stamping, SAF-T, or mandatory clearance API; however, the GRA may require access to transactional data upon audit.

The Gibraltar Revenue Authority may audit any zero-rated export claim within 6 years. Penalties for deliberate or negligent overstatement of zero-rated exports include a fine of up to 200% of the tax that would have been chargeable, interest applied retrospectively, and clawback of any input VAT recovered. Criminal prosecution may apply for fraudulent documentation, with custodial sentences possible. Amounts wrongly zero-rated are assessed as output tax with late-payment penalties and interest.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Current account and capital account transactions are fully convertible. The Gibraltar pound is pegged at parity to GBP, with free convertibility into GBP, USD, EUR and other major currencies. There are no exchange control restrictions, advance approval requirements, or profit remittance barriers for a Bermuda exempted company. Funds must merely satisfy AML/KYC and beneficial-ownership verification procedures.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender rules or mandatory conversion ratios apply to export receipts in Gibraltar. There is no prescribed timeline for converting foreign-currency trade proceeds into local currency; companies may retain or reinvest proceeds in foreign currency as treasury policy permits. Record-keeping must comply with the Proceeds of Crime Act and Gibraltar tax regulations.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (United Kingdom) as ultimate monetary authority for the sterling-pegged Gibraltar pound; Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the operating foreign exchange and banking regulator.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

High feasibility for using regulated electronic money institutions (EMIs) such as Wise and Revolut, and for Stripe Billing to support the subscription model. Legal constraints: the EMI must be authorised in an appropriate jurisdiction (FCA UK or GFSC Gibraltar) and must hold safeguarding accounts at a credit institution. Many EMIs require evidence of a genuine operational link to Gibraltar/UK rather than a pure Bermuda offshore shell; an administered office, professional directorship, and demonstrable substance are therefore critical. Overall viability: High subject to substance and AML/KYC acceptance.
