Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

H.M. Commissioner of Income Tax, Gibraltar Civil Service (Income Tax Office, Suite 771, Europort, Gibraltar); administrative portal: Government of Gibraltar Digital Services / Self-Assessment filing via the Gibraltar Tax e-Services system; parent UK authority: HM Revenue & Customs (non-resident rules only, not applicable to Gibraltar-registered ELPs).

E-government filing portal

A foreign beneficial owner of a Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership must file a GP01 / ELP registration return with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies (Companies House Gibraltar, under the Limited Partnerships Act 2016). The Income Tax Office then issues a Gibraltar Tax Number (Martriculation Number) upon first filing of the Partnership Return (Form ELP-T) within 9 months of the accounting period end. Non-resident foreign partners without a Gibraltar permanent establishment are not individually taxed but the partnership must file a transparency declaration. TIN format: GIB plus 8-digit reference for partners requiring a cross-border Certificate of Residence.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible; under Section 40 of the Income Tax Act 2010 (Gibraltar), an Exempted Limited Partnership is fiscally transparent; all income, gains, losses and credits flow through to partners. Territorial scope: taxation is on a territorial basis within Gibraltar; income arising outside Gibraltar to a non-resident partner is outside the charge to Gibraltar income tax. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible; under Section 40 of the Income Tax Act 2010 (Gibraltar), an Exempted Limited Partnership is fiscally transparent; all income, gains, losses and credits flow through to partners. Territorial scope: taxation is on a territorial basis within Gibraltar; income arising outside Gibraltar to a non-resident partner is outside the charge to Gibraltar income tax. Tax-Exempt Zone: no tax treaty network applies to the ELP itself; the partnership is not a commercial trading taxpayer in Gibraltar unless it derives local-source income from immovable property or a trade wholly carried on in Gibraltar.

Available election forms

Election for partnership-level taxation: an ELP may irrevocably elect under the Income Tax (Partnerships) Rules 2017 to be treated as an entity for tax purposes if all partners are tax-resident in Gibraltar. Foreign partners may also make a hybrid mismatch election under Finance Act (Gibraltar) provisions aligning with OECD BEPS Action 2; however, dual-registration (check-the-box) is not available because Gibraltar does not adopt US federal classification; instead, the ELP obtains a Gibraltar ruling letter confirming transparency. A further election can be made to treat the ELP as a 'Qualifying Investment Partnership' exempt from registration if it holds only passive investments.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No_Indirect_Tax_Regime (Nil VAT, Nil GST, Nil Sales and Use Tax in force in Gibraltar since the abolition of the temporary 'Bosman' VAT with effect from April 1997; replaced by selective excise duties on alcohol, tobacco, fuel and hydrocarbon oils only).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable; Gibraltar levies no VAT/GST and thus there is no economic turnover threshold triggering indirect tax registration. Excise duty registration applies to importers of dutiable goods irrespective of turnover (good for licensing, without threshold). For sales into mainland UK, the UK VAT threshold of GBP 90,000 (2024/25) will apply to the importer of record in the UK, not to the Gibraltar ELP.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in Gibraltar because there is no VAT regime to zero-rate. Exports of canned boba tapioca pearls and aseptic-tubed ambient products from Gibraltar to the UK and EU are treated as outside the scope of any Gibraltar indirect tax. For UK VAT purposes, if the ELP registers voluntarily in the UK, goods exported from Gibraltar to non-UK customers may qualify for zero-rating as exports under UK VAT Section 30 Value Added Tax Act 1994, provided export evidence is retained. Service exports qualify as outside the scope of UK VAT under the place-of-supply rules only if the ELP operates from a UK presence.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Entry (pre-departure summary declaration via the Northern Ireland and UK/Union Customs Code systems, including the UK-Gibraltar Trade Agreement rules of origin certificate EUR-GIB when shipping to the EU)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical shipment from Gibraltar Port (Harbours and Ports Act) to the destination market
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List with ISIC 1030 classification and HS Code 2005/2106 declaration for tapioca pearls
  • Certificate of Origin (for preferential tariff treatment under the UK-Gibraltar FTA with the EU or third countries)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate for goods exported to EU/UK receivables, issued by the receiving Gibraltar bank for payment settlement trails

No real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing (B2B/B2C) clearance, API, or digital stamping regime operates within Gibraltar. The Government of Gibraltar uses a standard self-assessment e-filing system (Gibraltar Tax e-Services) for income tax only, not for invoices. Any electronic invoicing to UK customers falls under UK HMRC Making Tax Digital (MTD) requirements for VAT-registered UK entities when the goods cross the UK border; the Gibraltar ELP itself is not subject to UK MTD unless separately VAT-registered in the UK.

Since no VAT zero-rating claim exists in Gibraltar, the direct fraud penalty framework applies to income tax self-assessment: failure to file a partnership return triggers a fixed penalty of GBP 100 plus GBP 60 per day (Income Tax Act 2010, s. 114). Fraudulent misrepresentation of the ELP as tax-transparent or misreporting of offshore income to claim effective 0% taxation carries a penalty of up to 100% of the tax undercharged plus interest, and criminal sanction under s. 121 (tax evasion offences) with imprisonment up to 7 years. If UK VAT registration is separately obtained to zero-rate exports, UK HMRC clawback under Schedule 24 Finance Act 2007 imposes a penalty up to 100% of the VAT amount improperly zero-rated; simultaneous retrospective clawback, import VAT, and criminal penalties under CEMA 1979 apply for fraudulent export declarations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current and capital account convertibility. Gibraltar has no exchange-control regime; GIP and GBP are fully interchangeable, and profit distributions from the partnership to non-resident partners are freely remittable. No surrender or intensification for trade receipts. Remittance hurdles are limited to AML/KYC documentation, including source-of-funds and economic-substance declarations under the Gibraltar Economic Substance Act 2020.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. There is no statutory timeline, conversion ratio, or mandatory surrender of export proceeds. Exporters may hold USD, EUR, GBP, or GIP balances; banks may request export documents such as electronic customs data, commercial invoices, and bills of lading for high-value settlements.

Central bank supervision: Government of Gibraltar (Currency Notes Issuance); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (banking and AML/CFT supervision); Bank of England (sterling monetary anchor for GIP at 1:1 parity)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Moderate to Low for specialized trade financing; Medium for low-value operational spending. UK-licensed EMIs such as Wise Business and Revolut Business accept Gibraltar-incorporated limited partnerships but typically require a full UK/OVOT economic-substance file; they do not support GIP clearing (only GBP) and impose aggregate USD/GIP caps. Offshore EMIs outside FCA/GFSC scope cannot provide a true SWIFT domestic clearing number (GB sort code) and are unsuitable for LC/draft-based trade rails. An EMI may serve as an auxiliary liquidity buffer, not as a primary treasury rail.
