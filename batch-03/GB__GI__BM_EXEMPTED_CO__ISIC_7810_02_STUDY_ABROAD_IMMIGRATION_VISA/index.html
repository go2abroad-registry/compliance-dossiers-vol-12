Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office, administered by the Commissioner of Income Tax; online GCT filing and registration at www.tax.gov.gi; corporate registry filings at Gibraltar Companies House.

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity (Bermuda Exempted Company) seeking to register for Gibraltar tax purposes must submit the GCT1 registration form to the Income Tax Office prior to making the first taxable supply or, if not registered, within 30 days of the day on which the GCT registration threshold is exceeded. The application requires: certified apostilled certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, proof of registered address in Bermuda, a Gibraltar service address, appointment of a Gibraltar-resident agent for service of process, particulars of directors and ultimate beneficial owners, and evidence of any physical fixed establishment in Gibraltar. After approval the Income Tax Office issues a Gibraltar tax reference number and GCT registration certificate. The entity is also required to register with Gibraltar Social Security as an employer if it employs personnel in Gibraltar.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists under Gibraltar tax law. Classification follows the legal personality of the Bermuda Exempted Company under Bermudian corporate law. For Gibraltar income tax purposes, a foreign-incorporated company is resident in Gibraltar only if its central management and control is exercised in Gibraltar; a non-resident company carrying on a trade in Gibraltar through a permanent establishment is chargeable only on Gibraltar-source profits. For GCT purposes, a business may voluntarily elect to register before reaching the mandatory threshold. The GCT Act also provides optional elections to use the cash accounting scheme and the annual accounting scheme, subject to conditions.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GIP 84,000 in annual taxable turnover (other than zero-rated or exempt supplies); threshold may be adjusted by Regulations.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exported from Gibraltar to any destination outside Gibraltar are zero-rated provided the goods are physically exported within three months of the supply and satisfactory evidence of export is preserved. For services, a supply of services is zero-rated as an exported service where the recipient is a person not belonging in Gibraltar, the services are not performed directly in Gibraltar for a benefit received in Gibraltar, and the supply is to a taxable person in a foreign jurisdiction (B2B) or otherwise satisfies the GCT export-of-services conditions. For the study abroad and immigration visa advisory business, fees receivable from non-resident students and visa applicants in respect of overseas educational placements and immigration services are treated as exported services when the advice is prepared and delivered outside Gibraltar or to a foreign-resident client.

  • Customs Export Declaration, Bill of Lading, Inward Remittance Certificate

Gibraltar has no real-time fiscal clearance mechanism. GCT invoices must be issued within 30 days of the tax point and must contain specified particulars under the GCT Act. An electronic invoice is acceptable, and all invoices must be retained in digital or paper form for audit. There is no government digital stamping or API integration mandate; periodic GCT returns (normally quarterly) are filed online through the Income Tax Office portal, and assessments and refunds are processed in that portal.

The Commissioner of Income Tax applies heightened scrutiny to zero-rated export claims. If the goods are not actually exported, if services do not satisfy the external-supply conditions, or if export documents are false, the zero-rating is clawed back retroactively, GCT becomes payable at 12.5% together with default interest and late-payment surcharges of up to 100% of the tax due. Deliberate or fraudulent evasion exposes the company and directors to civil penalties up to 150% of the tax loss and criminal prosecution with fines and imprisonment (up to 7 years). Failure to evidence receipt of offshore funds within the 180-day inward remittance deadline, or absence of a valid customs declaration/Bill of Lading, is treated as strong evidence of a non-export and may trigger full assessment, disqualification of directors and cancellation of the GCT registration.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar is a full-convertibility jurisdiction with no current or capital account exchange restrictions. The Gibraltar pound (GIP) circulates alongside GBP at a fixed 1:1 parity, and unrestricted sterling flows exist within the UK-Gibraltar currency area. Profit remittances and dividend distributions from the Gibraltar treasury platform of the Bermuda Exempted Company to Bermudian ultimate shareholders are unrestricted, subject to no Gibraltar withholding tax, and require no central bank approval. Capital account transactions, including share subscription, intercompany loans, and foreign direct investment injections from Bermuda, are fully convertible without licensing, subject only to standard CRS/FATCA reporting and GFSC AML/CFT record-keeping for transactions above the EUR 10,000 equivalent reporting threshold.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Gibraltar imposes no statutory export proceeds surrender rule, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation timeline. Receipts from study-abroad placement fees, immigration/visa advisory services, and other consultancy income may be retained in their original currency (GBP, EUR, USD, CNH) at the tier-1 bank of choice. The only relevant requirement is GFSC regulatory reporting of large cash or wire receipts under the Criminal Justice Act money-laundering provisions; no surrender or exchange-control filing to the Bank of England or GFSC is required.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority for the pound sterling and the Gibraltar pound, which is pegged at par to GBP); HM Government of Gibraltar Ministry for Financial Services and the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the licensing, prudential, and conduct-of-business authority for banking, payment services, and FX activities; exchange controls formally abolished for Gibraltar under the UK Exchange Control Act 1979 framework.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility: Medium, with material legal constraints. Digital EMI onboarding is acceptable only where the EMI holds a GFSC payment-services licence under the Gibraltar Payment Services Act 2019 or a UK FCA e-money authorisation with a Gibraltar-compatible passported arrangement; post-Brexit, the EEA passport is not automatic for Gibraltar. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise Business or Revolut Business may be used for low-value operational disbursements but cannot serve as the custodial Escrow_Account rail because the GFSC Client Money and Assets regime requires safeguarding of client funds with a bank. Recommended structure: EMI for sub-consolidated tuition top-ups and petty advisory expenses; tier-1 bank for capital injection, escrow custody, and large disbursements.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital from the Bermudian parent or ultimate UBOs is routed via SWIFT MT103 wire transfer to the GIB Capital Escrow Account using UK balance-of-payments purpose code 'J002' (equity capital / direct investment) with the narrative 'SHARE CAPITAL INJECTION – BERMUDA EXEMPTED CO'. The receiving-bank verification package must include the board resolution approving the capital call, executed share subscription and allotment resolutions, source-of-funds statements from the remitting bank, and confirmation that no Gibraltar exchange-control authorisation is required because the capital account is fully convertible. If funds arrive in USD or EUR, the bank executes spot conversion to GIP/GBP on a T+2 basis using the operational FX overlay; no surrender, Bank of England approval, or GFSC notification is required.Tuition and study-abroad disbursements are executed over SWIFT MT103/R-message rails with purpose text 'TUITION / STUDY-FEE REMITTANCE'; same-day sterling clearing uses CHAPS, for which Gibraltar banks are direct members; euro-denominated payments to EU universities use SEPA Credit Transfer or SEPA Instant. Large multi-year enrolment contracts may be secured by a Commercial Letter of Credit issued by GIB against the escrow balance, with the beneficiary university drawing against verified enrolment attestations and stage-completion certificates. Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The completed Form GIB-100 must be filed within 12 months after the end of the chargeable accounting period. For a Bermuda Exempted Company using a 31 December accounting year-end, the return is due no later than 31 December of the succeeding year. Interest accrues on any unpaid corporation tax from the original due date.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: A local file must be maintained where aggregate controlled transactions with connected persons exceed GIP 2,000,000 in a chargeable period. A master file is required where the multinational group's consolidated turnover exceeds GIP 750,000,000 equivalent. All intra-group management fees, commission sharing arrangements, escrow service charges, and visa consulting fees must satisfy the arm's-length standard even where no documentation threshold is triggered.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance (Companies and Foreign Partnerships) Act 2020 applies. A Bermuda Exempted Company claiming tax residence in Gibraltar or seeking treaty benefits must demonstrate genuine economic substance. The international student placement and golden visa advisory business is classified as a 'services business'; therefore the company must maintain in Gibraltar: (i) employees with appropriate qualifications and authority, (ii) physical premises, (iii) core income-generating activities performed in Gibraltar, and (iv) operating expenditure proportionate to income. An annual Economic Substance Declaration must be filed. A pure Bermuda shell company with no substantive presence in Gibraltar will be denied Gibraltar tax residence and treaty benefits.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Tax Return

Statutory sanction: Under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010, the Commissioner may impose a penalty of up to 200% of the tax under-assessed, plus statutory interest. Criminal prosecution on indictment may result in an unlimited fine and imprisonment for up to seven years. The court must order payment of the unpaid tax and may confiscate assets equal to the tax evaded.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or direct the submission of a false return are personally liable to civil restitution and criminal sanction. The court may pierce the corporate veil, impose travel bans, issue director disqualification orders of up to 15 years, and order imprisonment for fraudulent evasion.
Shell Company and Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The Gibraltar Commissioner may disregard the corporate residence claim, recharacterise the company as a non-resident shell, and assess all Gibraltar-source profits directly. False substance declarations attract penalties of up to GIP 20,000 per declaration, potential strike-off of the Gibraltar registration, and denial of all DTA benefits.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held personally liable for taxes arising if the shell company is found to be a facade or sham. Courts can impose personal contribution orders, asset restraints, international travel bans, and prohibition from acting as a company director in Gibraltar for up to 15 years.
Anti-Money Laundering and Proceeds of Crime Violations

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and Gibraltar AML Regulations, penalties include fines of up to GIP 500,000 or 10% of annual turnover, confiscation of assets, imprisonment for up to 14 years for money laundering, and mandatory revocation of the firm's DNFBP registration with the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers responsible for AML failures are personally prosecutable. The court may order personal fines, freezing orders over personal assets, seizure of travel documents, and custodial sentences. Disqualification from operating in any regulated activity in Gibraltar is mandatory after a serious AML conviction.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing and Artificial Profit Stripping

Statutory sanction: The Gibraltar Income Tax Office will adjust the taxable profits to the arm's-length level, disallow the excessive deductions, and assess additional corporation tax. Penalties of up to 100% of the additional tax are imposed, together with statutory interest at 2% above the Bank of England base rate. Deliberate documentation fabrication may escalate to criminal tax evasion charges.

Director personal liability: Directors who approved the artificial arrangements can be jointly and severally liable for the adjusted tax deficiency. Civil recovery proceedings may be brought against personal assets, and the court may issue travel bans, director disqualification, and custodial sentences for fraudulent financial arrangements.
