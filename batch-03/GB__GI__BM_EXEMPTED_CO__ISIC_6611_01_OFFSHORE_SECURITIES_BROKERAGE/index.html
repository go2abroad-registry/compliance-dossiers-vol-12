Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar - Income Tax Office (ITO), tax e-services portal at https://www.income-tax.gi; entity registration via the Gibraltar Companies Registry; financial services authorisation via the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC). HMRC (UK) has no administrative authority over Gibraltar tax matters.

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company conducting retail securities brokerage in or from Gibraltar must: (1) register as an External Company with the Gibraltar Companies Registry; (2) file the ITO branch/foreign company registration form to obtain a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN); (3) submit the Bermuda tax-exemption certificate and statutory declaration of place of management and control; (4) obtain GFSC authorisation under the Financial Services Act 2013; and (5) register for PAYE and Gibraltar social insurance (GIPS) if it has a local establishment or employees.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone: the Bermuda Exempted Company is exempt from Bermuda corporate income tax, subject only to the annual Bermuda Government Fee; in Gibraltar, the company is taxable at the 12.5% corporation tax rate only where profits are attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment or where strategic management and control are exercised in Gibraltar; a pure offshore brokerage with foreign-source income and no Gibraltar PE is outside the Gibraltar tax charge. — A Bermuda Exempted Company conducting retail securities brokerage in or from Gibraltar must: (1) register as an External Company with the Gibraltar Companies Registry; (2) file the ITO branch/foreign company registration form to obtain a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN); (3) submit the Bermuda tax-exemption certificate and statutory declaration of place of management and control; (4) obtain GFSC authorisation under the Financial Services Act 2013; and (5) register for PAYE and Gibraltar social insurance (GIPS) if it has a local establishment or employees.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: the Bermuda Exempted Company is exempt from Bermuda corporate income tax, subject only to the annual Bermuda Government Fee; in Gibraltar, the company is taxable at the 12.5% corporation tax rate only where profits are attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment or where strategic management and control are exercised in Gibraltar; a pure offshore brokerage with foreign-source income and no Gibraltar PE is outside the Gibraltar tax charge.

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no US-style check-the-box election regime. Available mechanisms: (i) application for an advance tax ruling/clearance from the ITO on residence, PE and profit attribution; (ii) election by a Bermuda Exempted Company for a long-term tax exemption certificate under Bermuda law; (iii) alignment elections under OECD/ATAD hybrid mismatch rules where a Gibraltar nexus exists; (iv) application for GFSC 'permission for overseas firm' status. No deeming election can convert a Gibraltar-managed entity into a tax-exempt company.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST: Gibraltar is outside the UK VAT area and the EU common VAT region; indirect taxation consists solely of Gibraltar Import Duty and selective Purchase Tax on enumerated consumables; securities brokerage and financial services are outside the Gibraltar indirect tax base.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT/GST registration threshold exists in Gibraltar. A GFSC-licensed broker must register with the ITO for corporation tax regardless of turnover and with the GIPS/PAYE regime if it employs staff in Gibraltar.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable because Gibraltar levies no VAT/GST. Cross-border offshore securities brokerage services are outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect tax: no output tax is charged and no input VAT recovery mechanism exists. A separately incorporated UK affiliate could rely on UK VAT zero-rating only for actual exported goods, which is irrelevant to this brokerage licence.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration (required only for physical exports; not applicable to pure securities brokerage services)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill (for goods movements only; the services equivalent is the executed trade confirmation and contract note)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice evidencing receipt of brokerage commission in the designated client/company account

No real-time electronic invoicing, fiscal clearance, SAF-T or digital stamping mandate applies in Gibraltar. Invoices must comply with the Income Tax Act record-keeping rules (6-year retention), GFSC Systems and Controls requirements, and MiFID/IFD transaction reporting where the broker passport services into the EEA.

Gibraltar has no export-VAT clawback because zero-rating does not exist. However, the ITO may open criminal proceedings for false declarations of non-residence or PE status; penalties reach up to 100% of underpaid tax plus interest and potential imprisonment. The GFSC may impose financial penalties, suspend or revoke the brokerage licence. Artificial profit-shifting or abuse of the Bermudian exemption triggers OECD/ATAD anti-avoidance rules, retrospective assessments, and collective clawback of any prior approvals or concessions.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GIP is fully convertible at par with GBP under the UK-Gibraltar currency board arrangement, and both GIP and GBP are freely circulating within the jurisdiction. No exchange controls exist on current-account flows (dividends, fees, commissions, client settlements, intra-group services) or capital-account flows (equity injections, debt, acquisitions, liquidation proceeds) from Gibraltar to Bermuda or to any other jurisdiction. Profit repatriation from the brokerage to its Bermuda shareholders is unrestricted, provided the firm maintains audited distributable reserves, satisfies GFSC own-funds requirements, and preserves segregation of client money under the GFSC Client Money Rules.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Neither Gibraltar nor the United Kingdom imposes any statutory timeline or mandatory conversion ratio for the surrender of export proceeds. Brokerage commissions, CFD and equities settlement receipts and ancillary fee income derived from cross-border clients may be retained indefinitely in USD, EUR, GBP or GIP and converted at the firm's discretion. Only statistical reporting obligations apply, such as Bank of England BOP/ETF returns for UK-resident financial corporations, and GFSC prudential returns for licensed investment firms.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar does not maintain an independent central bank. Monetary, currency issuance and financial-stability functions are exercised by HM Government of Gibraltar in coordination with the Bank of England (the United Kingdom's central bank), while prudential and conduct regulation of licensed banks and investment firms is performed by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) under the Financial Services Act 2019 and the Banking Act. The Gibraltar pound (GIP) circulates at a fixed 1:1 parity with GBP.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium feasibility. UK-FCA-authorised e-money institutions and offshore digital EMIs are generally viable only for ancillary treasury disbursements, not for primary client-money segregation. Most mainstream EMIs explicitly exclude high-risk FX/CFD/retail-equities brokers and any Bermuda-incorporated offshore entity, rejecting onboarding at sector and jurisdiction screening stages. However, specialised EMIs serving the regulated Tier-1 gaming and brokerage sector in Gibraltar may provide GBP/EUR/USD settlement accounts and MT103 rails where the broker holds a valid GFSC investment-services licence, provides a clean sanctions/UW profile and accepts a multi-bank liquidity structure. Post-Brexit EEA EMI passporting is unavailable in Gibraltar; the EMI must either hold a GFSC licence or service the entity strictly on a reverse-solicit basis, and client money may not be held via an EMI unless an express trust and sub-ledger segregation regime is contractually established.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing is via SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 COV where the remitter is a financial intermediary) from the ultimate shareholder's bank account into the Bermuda Exempted Co's Gibraltar capital escrow account. The payment must be clearly labelled with the UK/Gibraltar standard purpose code for direct investment or equity capital ('Capital contribution — subscription for ordinary shares, no consideration; corporate act of the Bermuda Exempted Company'), the Unique End-to-End Transaction Reference (UETR) for SWIFT gpi tracking, and the reference to the Bermuda board resolution authorising the share issuance. The bank will require, prior to credit release, a certified copy of the board resolution, the CRS/FATCA self-certifications of the contributor, source-of-funds and source-of-wealth evidence for the UBO, and a confirmation from the Gibraltar registered office of receipt of statutory books. No GFSC prior approval is required, but transfers above the bank's internal threshold (typically GBP 500,000) attract enhanced due diligence and a 2-3 business day clearing hold pending compliance release.Retail client withdrawals are executed via SWIFT MT103 (SEPA/TARGET2 for EUR, CHAPS/Faster Payments for GBP domestic, Fedwire/CHIPS through the US correspondent for USD), always from the segregated client-money account, never from the operational account. Equities, CFD and derivative trade settlements to prime brokers, clearing houses or liquidity providers are effected via MT202/MT202 COV for own-account transfers, accompanied by MT300/MT304 FX confirmations, with central counterparty clearing where applicable through LCH or equivalent. Commercial letters of credit (MT700, with MT707 amendments and MT799 pre-advices) are permissible for principal-position corporate acquisitions or trade finance collateralisation of the brokerage inventory, provided the underlying instrument is within the GFSC licence scope. Interim payments on account may be required on 30 June and 31 December in the year of assessment.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: A Gibraltar Local File must be prepared and maintained when the Gibraltar permanent establishment books annual related-party cross-border transactions exceeding £500,000 or total Gibraltar-sourced brokerage turnover exceeds £2,500,000. A Master File is required when the ultimate Bermuda parent group has consolidated turnover above £750,000,000. Documentation must be submitted to the Commissioner within 30 days of a formal notice.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar's Economic Substance Regulations apply to tax-resident companies carrying on relevant activities, including securities brokerage. The entity must demonstrate an adequate and proportionate premise in Gibraltar, full-time qualified staff, actual decision-making and board meetings in Gibraltar, and that core income-generating activities are performed in Gibraltar. A Bermuda Exempted Company that is not managed and controlled in Gibraltar is outside the Gibraltar ESR only if it can prove genuine management and control in Bermuda; a mere letterbox arrangement in either jurisdiction is treated as non-substantive and triggers a substance deficiency notice.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Assessment of the underpaid tax plus penalty up to 200% of the evaded amount, criminal fines up to £250,000, and imprisonment for up to seven years under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act and Criminal Justice Act. The company may also be struck off and its investment services licence revoked.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or cause a false return to be filed are jointly and severally liable for civil restitution of the evaded tax; a court may issue travel bans, freezing orders, and disqualification from acting as a director for up to 15 years. Imprisonment applies to individuals convicted of knowing participation in tax evasion.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Economic Substance Regulations penalty of £100,000 for a substance deficiency; continued non-compliance may result in a second penalty, striking off, and an order that the company be wound up. The tax authority may reclassify the entity as a Gibraltar resident and assess 12.5% tax on all worldwide brokerage income.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced where the company is a mere façade; directors become personally liable for unpaid taxes and regulatory penalties, are subject to travel bans, and are disqualified for fraudulent trading under the Gibraltar Companies Act.
AML / CTF Breach

Statutory sanction: Financial penalty imposed by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission of up to £3,000,000 or 10% of annual turnover; suspension or revocation of the investment services licence under the Financial Services (Investments) Act; criminal liability for money laundering with imprisonment up to 14 years.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and MLROs are personally accountable and may face imprisonment, separate fines, travel bans, and orders for restitution of commissions derived from illicit funds; corporate liability does not shield officers where they consented or connived.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing / Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner is empowered to re-characterise or adjust the transaction, compute the taxable profit at arm's length, and levy an additional assessment plus a 100% accuracy penalty and interest on unpaid tax from the original due date.

Director personal liability: Directors who approved or participated in the mispriced transaction may be joined in collection proceedings where assets have been stripped from the company; in an insolvency, the court may make an order for contribution and disqualification, and travel bans may be imposed pending repayment.
Improper Profit Repatriation / Hidden Distribution

Statutory sanction: Tax authority reclassification as a deemed dividend distribution and disallowance of the related deduction; penalty of 100% of the tax under-assessed plus interest; unlawful distribution under the Companies Act renders the distribution void and recoverable.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable to repay or contribute the amount of an unlawful distribution where they knew or ought reasonably to have known of the company's insolvency; they face disqualification orders, travel bans, and are liable to account for civil restitution to creditors.
