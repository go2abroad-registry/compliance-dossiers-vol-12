Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Trilateral fiscal authority framework: (1) Bermuda Ministry of Finance, Office of the Tax Commissioner (https://www.gov.bm) - administers the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 tax-exemption certificate and the Economic Substance (International Tax Compliance) Act 2018 filing regime; (2) HM Government of Gibraltar, Income Tax Office (https://www.tax.gov.gi) - issues the Gibraltar Tax Reference Number and assesses 12.5% corporation tax only on Gibraltar-source income attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment; (3) HM Revenue & Customs of the United Kingdom (https://www.gov.uk/hmrc) - governs UK VAT registration, Import One-Stop Shop (IOSS), and customs/EORI obligations for B2C e-commerce supplies of 3D printers and resin filaments into the UK.

E-government filing portal

Step 1 - Bermuda incorporation: file the Notice of Registration (Form BR/1) with the Registrar of Companies and submit Form 2 to obtain the Tax Exemption Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, guaranteeing zero Bermuda taxes, duties, levies and capital controls until 31 March 2035 (renewable to 2050). Step 2 - Bermuda economic substance: file the annual economic substance declaration; if the entity conducts a relevant activity (distribution and service centre is a relevant activity), file the full Economic Substance Return with the Bermuda Minister of Finance; alternatively elect the pure equity holding entity exemption where the entity merely holds shares and earns only dividends and capital gains. Step 3 - Gibraltar: if a place of business is established in Gibraltar, register the foreign company with Gibraltar Companies House (Form FC-1 within one month) and file Form 1 with the Income Tax Office to obtain a Tax Reference Number; if no permanent establishment and no Gibraltar-source income, no Gibraltar corporate tax return is required. Step 4 - United Kingdom: obtain a UK EORI number and register for UK VAT (Form VAT1) from the first remote sale of goods located in the UK to UK consumers, irrespective of turnover; register for IOSS if goods are imported from outside the UK into the UK with intrinsic value not exceeding GBP 135.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (primary classification - Bermuda Exempted Company holds a statutory tax exemption certificate guaranteeing zero Bermuda corporate income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax and dividends tax until 2035/2050). Secondary classifications: Entity_Level_Taxation in Gibraltar (12.5% corporation tax confined to Gibraltar-source income or profits attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment; no taxation of foreign-source income in the absence of a Gibraltar PE). For UK VAT purposes, the entity is a non-established taxable person required to account for UK VAT on UK-destination supplies of goods physically located in the UK.

Available election forms

(1) Election for tax exemption protection under Section 6 of the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, renewable for a further 15 years to 2050; (2) Economic substance compliance election - elect to file as a pure equity holding entity (reduced substance obligations) or as a full relevant activity entity (distribution and service centre) requiring physical presence, employees and expenditure in Bermuda; (3) Hybrid mismatch elections under the OECD/G20 BEPS Action 2 framework for cross-border financing, royalty or intercompany arrangements between the Bermuda entity and its Gibraltar or UK affiliates; (4) UK VAT elections - IOSS registration for imported goods not exceeding GBP 135 and optional use of the UK VAT margin scheme or principal-to-principal drop-shipping arrangements where applicable.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST or general sales tax is imposed in Bermuda or Gibraltar. Bermuda levies only customs/import duties (generally up to 4.75% plus a 3% removals tax in the City of Hamilton) and payroll tax on employers. Gibraltar levies import duty and excise on specific goods and is outside the EU and UK VAT territories. The UK Value Added Tax Act 1994 system (standard rate 20%) constitutes the de facto destination-based indirect tax regime for B2C distance sales of 3D printers and resin filaments delivered into the United Kingdom.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable in Bermuda (no VAT/GST; only customs registration, Bermuda dollar pegged 1:1 to USD). Not applicable in Gibraltar (no VAT/GST; import duty is collected at customs, Gibraltar pound pegged 1:1 to GBP). United Kingdom threshold: GBP 90,000 annual taxable turnover for UK-established businesses; for non-UK-established online sellers the threshold is nil and UK VAT registration is mandatory from the first supply of goods physically located in the UK.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is inapplicable in Bermuda and Gibraltar because neither territory imposes VAT/GST. Under UK VAT, supplies of goods exported from the United Kingdom to a destination outside the UK (including Gibraltar) qualify for zero-rating pursuant to Section 30 VATA 1994 and HMRC VAT Notice 703, provided: (a) the goods are exported within 3 months of the time of supply; (b) the supplier retains official proof of export (customs exit validation or electronic departure confirmation); (c) commercial documents evidence an export contract and final foreign destination; and (d) no diversion back into the UK occurs. Supplies of 3D printers and materials dispatched from a UK warehouse to non-UK customers are zero-rated; supplies to UK consumers are subject to 20% VAT unless the IOSS import scheme election applies.

  • Customs Export Declaration (UK Single Administrative Document with National Export System exit validation)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (signed transport document evidencing physical export)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List (HS codes, quantities, unit values, ultimate destination)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT MT103 credit confirmation evidencing receipt of foreign sale proceeds

No real-time fiscal clearance, government API clearance or digital stamping mandate exists in Bermuda or Gibraltar; ordinary commercial invoices and customs single administrative documents suffice. Gibraltar customs declarations are filed via the Gibraltar Trade System. For the UK, HMRC Making Tax Digital (MTD) mandates digital record-keeping and quarterly VAT return filing through HMRC-recognized software APIs; however, no continuous transaction control (CTC), real-time clearance or pre-clearance e-invoice stamping regime is imposed in the UK.

HMRC may retrospectively claw back zero-rated VAT with statutory interest and impose penalties up to 100% of the tax under Finance Act 2007 Schedule 24 where export evidence is missing, forged, defective, or where goods are diverted back to the UK. Bermuda: the economic substance regime imposes fines up to BMD 250,000 per offense and imprisonment up to 7 years for knowingly false declarations, and the Minister of Finance may revoke the tax exemption certificate upon fraud. Gibraltar: the Commissioner of Income Tax may reassess up to 6 years of back taxes with compound interest and penalties up to 100% of underpaid tax for fraudulent or negligent returns; directors face personal liability and disqualification for falsifying export or customs documentation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current-account and capital-account convertibility. Gibraltar and the United Kingdom impose no exchange controls, no profit-remittance restrictions, no prior approval requirements for cross-border dividends, capital repatriation or intercompany funding, and no restrictions on holding multi-currency balances. GIP is freely interchangeable with GBP at par; USD, EUR and CNY positions may be maintained without regulatory permission. The practical limitation is not convertibility but the banking due-diligence burden of demonstrating economic substance for the Bermuda Exempted Company's Gibraltar-based sales-support activity.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. There is no statutory surrender timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio and no repatriation deadline for export or e-commerce proceeds in Gibraltar or the UK. The entity may retain 100% of USD/EUR/GBP settlement receipts in offshore accounts. As a treasury control, net USD receipts should be converted in weekly tranches with a rolling 30-day hedge horizon to optimise USD/CNY supplier funding costs.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority for sterling); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the licensing and prudential supervisor for all banks, payment institutions and EMIs in Gibraltar; Government of Gibraltar as issuer of the Gibraltar pound (GIP) at parity with GBP; HM Treasury and the Office of Financial Sanctions Implementation (OFSI) for sanctions alignment. No standalone exchange-control administration exists in Gibraltar.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: A Gibraltar Local File must be prepared and filed with the annual corporate return when the aggregate value of controlled transactions exceeds GIP 1,000,000; a Master File is required where the consolidated group turnover exceeds GIP 50,000,000 or where cross-border related-party transactions involve intellectual property, financing, or distribution activities.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar applies the Economic Substance Regulations to companies carrying on relevant activities, including distribution and service centres. The online retail sale of desktop 3D printers, resin filaments, and consumables falls within the distribution and service centre category; the company must demonstrate strategic management in Gibraltar, adequate employees, premises, operating expenditure, and core income-generating activities. A Bermuda Exempted Company cannot rely on its offshore exempt status to avoid Gibraltar economic substance obligations.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Accounting

Statutory sanction: A tax-geared penalty of up to 100% of the tax understated, daily interest on unpaid tax, publication in the tax defaulters list, and criminal prosecution punishable by an unlimited fine and up to seven years' imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly participate in false accounting are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax; a Gibraltar court may impose director disqualification for up to 15 years, a travel restriction order, and a custodial sentence for fraudulent trading.
Shell Company / Sham Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner may reclassify profits as Gibraltar-source, deny expense deductions, issue an assessment with interest and a 100% non-disclosure penalty, and report the entity to the Gibraltar Registrar; the company may be struck off for failing to demonstrate real presence.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal civil restitution to the tax authority for distributions made while insolvent, travel bans pending criminal investigation, and imprisonment for fraud where the sham structure is used to evade tax.
Transfer Pricing Avoidance

Statutory sanction: The Income Tax Office will adjust the controlled transaction to the arm's-length result, disallow the excess deduction, apply interest, and impose a TP penalty of between 100% and 200% of the resultant underpaid tax; failure to submit contemporaneous Local File documentation triggers an automatic fixed penalty.

Director personal liability: The finance director and any controlling director who signed the incorrect transfer pricing return may be held personally liable for the underpaid tax under Gibraltar wrongful-trading principles, face a ban from acting as a company director, and be prosecuted for making misleading statements to the Commissioner.
Anti-Money Laundering / Payment Processing Failure

Statutory sanction: The Gibraltar Financial Services Commission may impose a financial penalty up to GBP 2,000,000 or 5% of annual turnover, suspend or revoke the payment facilitation permission, and issue a public censure under the Proceeds of Crime Act.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers may be prosecuted for money laundering offences punishable by imprisonment of up to 14 years; personal confiscation orders, disqualification orders, and travel bans apply where funds derived from fraudulent activity are settled through the corporate accounts.
Failure to File / Persistent Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: A late-filing penalty of GBP 100, escalating daily surcharges up to GBP 1,000, tax-geared penalties for unreasonable non-compliance, and compulsory strike-off of the Gibraltar-registered company after repeated default.

Director personal liability: Directors remain personally liable for penalties and unpaid tax where they caused the company's default; the Gibraltar court may impose a travel ban during proceedings, a disqualification order, and custodial penalties for contempt of court or willful obstruction of the tax authority.
