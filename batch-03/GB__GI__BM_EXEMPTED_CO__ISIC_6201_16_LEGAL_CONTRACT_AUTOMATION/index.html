Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (Commissioner of Income Tax); GCT on imports is administered by Gibraltar Customs and Ports Authority. Official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi; income tax e-filing via the eTax portal (etax.gibraltar.gov.gi).

        
E-government filing portal
1) Register the Bermuda Exempted Company as an overseas company with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under the Companies Act 2014, filing certified Bermuda certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles, directors' register, Gibraltar registered office, and a Gibraltar-resident process agent. 2) Submit the corporate tax registration form to the Income Tax Office (via eTax) with a Bermuda certificate of good standing, a written declaration of place of effective management and control, and the appointed Gibraltar tax agent mandate. 3) The Commissioner issues a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN); non-resident status is acknowledged only after the non-residence declaration is accepted with supporting board-minute and management evidence. 4) Register for GCT with Gibraltar Customs only if physical import of chargeable goods occurs (not triggered by cloud SaaS). 5) If B2C digital services are supplied to UK or EU consumers, additionally register with HMRC as a non-established taxable person for UK VAT and under an EU Member State OSS non-Union scheme.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Territorial_Scope

        
Available election forms
Gibraltar has no check-the-box regime; classification follows statutory management-and-control (POEM) rules. Available mechanisms: (a) non-resident company election by filing a declaration evidencing board control seated in Bermuda, restricting Gibraltar tax to Gibraltar-source income; (b) accounting period election under the Income Tax Act 2010; (c) Gibraltar group relief/surrender election where a GI-resident subsidiary exists; (d) UK-Gibraltar Double Taxation Arrangement (2020) tie-breaker and exchange-of-information procedure; (e) Bermuda Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 opt-in certificate guaranteeing tax exemption in Bermuda until 2035; (f) OSS non-Union and UK VAT registration elections for remote digital supplies.

      

    

    

      
VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

      

        
Standard VAT / GST rate
12%

        
Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)
Current GCT Act: mandatory registration for any person importing chargeable goods in the course of business (no turnover de minimis); proposed GIP 100,000 taxable-supplies threshold under the 2026 reform; UK VAT: zero threshold for non-established suppliers of B2C digital services (NETP registration required from the first onward sale to UK consumers); EU OSS non-Union: no registration threshold.

      

      
      

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
For the core cloud document automation and e-signature SaaS, no GCT export zero-rating claim is required because exported and offshore digital services fall wholly outside the current GCT charge. For UK and EU VAT, B2B supplies are outside the scope of the supplier with the recipient applying reverse charge; B2C digital supplies require supplier VAT registration and output VAT at destination rates (UK 20% via HMRC NETP; EU rates via OSS non-Union). The 2026 GCT reform intends to keep exported services and cross-border B2B services outside charge or zero-rated.

No real-time fiscal clearance, API e-invoicing or digital stamping mandate is currently in force in Gibraltar. GCT is collected via customs import entries (SAD/GDG documentation). Banking & Treasury Architecture

      
Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
The Bermuda Exempted Company, when designated non-resident under Bermuda's Exchange Control Act 1972, enjoys unrestricted convertibility for both capital and current account transactions. Gibraltar operates a free capital movement environment; the Gibraltar pound (GIP) is pegged 1:1 with GBP and freely convertible. Profit remittance, dividend distribution, or capital repatriation require no exchange-control approval, but intermediaries apply standard AML/CFT transaction verification and source-of-wealth checks.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
No statutory foreign-exchange surrender timeline or mandatory conversion ratio applies. As a non-resident designated Bermuda Exempted Company, the entity may retain export proceeds in any permitted currency and is not subject to the 6-month repatriation requirement reserved for resident entities. In Gibraltar, there are no surrender rules; funds may be kept, converted, or swept offshore at the holder's discretion.

      

      
Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) for exchange-control oversight of the Bermuda Exempted Company; Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) for licensing and FX conduct supervision within Gibraltar.

    

    

      
Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

      

        
        

          

            Wise
            Fit: High
          

          
High feasibility for a GFSC-licensed Electronic Money Institution (EMI) offering multi-currency accounts and local IBANs. Legal constraints: a non-EEA EMI (e.g., UK, Malta or Cyprus authorised) cannot passport automatically into Gibraltar post-Brexit; a Gibraltar branch or top-up licence is required to hold client funds locally. The Bermuda Exempted Company may board on an offshore EMI (e.g., Wise Platform or Torchange) only if the EMI's entity-acceptance policy tolerates holding offshore companies in low-risk jurisdictions; most require physical substance evidence and a 'no negative news' historical profile. Overall viability: Medium-High, with regulatory-minimum EMI onboarding.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

      
      

        Offshore jurisdiction nexus — Bermuda Exempted Company with no tax residency and low effective substance in Gibraltar.
        
Root cause: Offshore jurisdiction nexus — Bermuda Exempted Company with no tax residency and low effective substance in Gibraltar.

        
Defense evidence: Provide a substance profile evidencing (i) a physical office in Gibraltar with lease; (ii) two or more Gibraltar-resident directors; (iii) board meeting minutes held in Gibraltar; (iv) outsourcing agreements proving local management of sales, support, and treasury functions; (v) UK/Gibraltar tax residence assessment (for the company or its management) and economic substance return filings.

      

      
      

        Nominee shareholders or bearer shares; beneficial ownership opacity.
        
Root cause: Nominee shareholders or bearer shares; beneficial ownership opacity.

        
Defense evidence: Deliver a registered beneficial ownership register with ultimate natural persons, trust deed (where any corporate shareholder is a trust), beneficial ownership declaration form, and source-of-wealth statements supported by audited investment or business income documentation.

      

      
      

        High-volume cross-border subscription receipts from unrelated SaaS consumers without a physical local merchant presence.
        
Root cause: High-volume cross-border subscription receipts from unrelated SaaS consumers without a physical local merchant presence.

        
Defense evidence: Provide signed master subscription agreements, Stripe standard merchant agreement, payment processor board resolution, ISAE 3402 SOC 2 Type II report, and a documented business model note explaining digital-only sales, local and overseas sales channels, and absence of cash-intensive operations.
