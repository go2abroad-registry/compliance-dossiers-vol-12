Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office (ITO); portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company seeking a Gibraltar tax nexus must first be registered as a foreign company under the Gibraltar Companies Act or otherwise maintain a registered office in Gibraltar. The Income Tax Office issues a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) upon submission of Form CT1 (non-resident/foreign entity registration), certified copy of the Bermuda certificate of incorporation, certificate of good standing, constitutional documents, proof of registered office in Gibraltar, and AML/KYC files of the ultimate beneficial owners. The application is processed after verification of economic substance and physical presence indicators required under the Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations (ESR). If the entity has no permanent establishment or taxable Gibraltar-source activity, a TRN may still be required for nil-return filing or for Purchase Tax purposes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Election for exempt status under the Income Tax Act for non-resident companies; election to be treated as a territorial-scope company for non-Gibraltar-source income; and the applicable economic substance elections mandated by the Gibraltar ESR. Hybrid mismatch elections under Gibraltar's controlled foreign company and territorial tax rules are not generally available to exempted companies but may be considered by election for financing structures.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Purchase Tax

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No economic turnover threshold; any person importing or exporting chargeable goods into or out of Gibraltar is required to register with the Gibraltar Customs and Excise Department and obtain a Purchase Tax registration number. Registration is mandatory regardless of turnover if the person carries on a taxable import/export business or makes taxable purchases of restricted goods (GIP).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating of Purchase Tax applies to the physical export of goods from Gibraltar to a destination outside Gibraltar, provided the goods are exported within a prescribed period (generally within 12 weeks of purchase) and are not consumed in Gibraltar. For digital services, Web3 foundation protocol services are outside the scope of Gibraltar Purchase Tax because Purchase Tax only applies to goods brought into Gibraltar; offshore services supplied to non-Gibraltar persons do not attract the tax. Claims for zero-rated export treatment require proof of physical removal from Gibraltar, the exporter's Purchase Tax registration, and retention of compliant evidence.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration (ENS via ASYCUDA World)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill showing Gibraltar as departure point and foreign destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or equivalent bank remittance evidence demonstrating consideration received from outside Gibraltar

Gibraltar does not operate a real-time national e-invoicing or fiscal clearance system for Purchase Tax. Import declarations must be lodged electronically via the ASYCUDA World customs management system. Digital stamping or authenticated electronic invoices are not currently imposed. For supply-chain transparency in the Web3 context, the customs clearance platform requires electronic filing of import/export declarations, commodity codes, valuation data, and origin documentation; approval is granted through system-generated Customs Entry Numbers.

HM Government of Gibraltar – Customs and Excise may audit export claims within six years. A false zero-rating claim or failure to substantiate export within the required deadline results in clawback of the Purchase Tax previously relieved, plus interest. Penalties range from 50% to 300% of the tax evaded, with contempt-type fines, forfeiture of goods, and criminal liability for officers of the entity. For Web3 entities, fraudulent offshore service or export declarations can also trigger suspension of the Gibraltar TRN and referral to the Financial Intelligence Unit.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar is a British Overseas Territory with no independent central bank and no exchange-control statute. The Gibraltar pound is fully backed by and convertible at par with pound sterling. Both capital-account and current-account movements are free from exchange-control approval; profit, grant, dividend, licensing and token-treasury remittances may be repatriated without surrender or conversion obligations. Practical convertibility constraints arise from correspondent-bank de-risking, GFSC anti-money-laundering due-diligence and sanctions screening rather than from statutory currency controls. A Bermuda Exempted Company using Gibraltar as its treasury venue should maintain a board-approved currency policy and evidence of source of funds for all conversions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender period and no mandatory conversion ratio apply in Gibraltar. Export proceeds, service fees, grant receipts and digital-asset proceeds may be retained in the original receipt currency, held in USDC or converted at the entity's commercial discretion in GIP, GBP, EUR or USD. For evidential compliance, the treasury should maintain a foreign-currency receipts ledger matching each invoice, grant agreement or smart-contract transaction with bank statements and, where applicable, an on-chain audit trail.

Central bank supervision: HM Government of Gibraltar through the Commissioner of Currency issues the Gibraltar pound; the Bank of England is the sovereign monetary authority and lender of last resort; the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the conduct, AML/CFT and foreign-exchange supervision authority.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low to Medium as a primary banking rail. Gibraltar has a GFSC-administered electronic-money framework under the Financial Services Act, but most offshore digital EMI providers are not authorised in Gibraltar and post-Brexit passporting from the UK or EEA does not extend to Gibraltar. Mainstream EMIs generally classify token foundations as prohibited or high-risk and reject non-profit Web3 entities. A more viable substitute is a GFSC-licensed DLT provider or an authorised investment-firm custody arrangement holding segregated funds, with commercial banking at a tier-1 Gibraltar bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound treasury injections should be routed over SWIFT MT103 for USD and, where available, via CHAPS or SEPA for GBP and EUR into the Bermuda Exempted Company's dedicated Gibraltar operating account or a segregated capital escrow account. The remittance narrative must state the true purpose, for example 'equity capital injection', 'founder loan' or 'grant for open-source protocol development', and the remitting bank must map that narrative to the applicable balance-of-payments purpose code used by the sending jurisdiction. For a capital contribution the correct category is a capital-account code, not a services-code. Verification must include the SWIFT ACK or MT199 acknowledgement, the bank credit advice, a matching board-approved capital budget entry, and confirmation that the inflow has been included in the entity's accounting and AML source-of-funds file. Stablecoin or token injections should first be liquidated by a regulated counterparty and then moved by fiat wire; direct exchange-wallet transfers to the main operating account should be avoided unless supported by a complete transaction-level audit trail.Fiat disbursements for grants, development services, legal fees, accounting fees and operational suppliers are cleared through SWIFT MT103 with the payee's full legal name, bank identifier and invoice reference embedded in the remittance field. If physical goods, hardware procurement or documentary services are involved, a commercial letter of credit may be opened under the foundation's banking facility, supported by the underlying commercial invoice, packing list and shipping documents. For final settlement involving the foundation's Web3 treasury, disbursements should be executed as native USDC transfers from a Gnosis Safe multisig vault with a pre-specified signer quorum, on-chain remittance metadata, and the same invoice or grant-agreement reference. No outbound instruction, whether fiat or stablecoin, may be released without a positive sanctions-screen, beneficiary KYC check and dual-authorisation within the board-approved treasury policy.Deliverable FX forward contracts in GIP, GBP, EUR and USD with tenors up to 24 months, executed under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement with an authorised Gibraltar or UK bank or broker.FX swap transactions converting stablecoin-denominated treasury receipts into operating currencies on a spot-and-forward basis.Cross-currency swaps matching the foundation's USDC-native assets against GBP or EUR operating liabilities, limited to treasury hedging and prohibited for speculative open positions.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) only for illiquid or non-convertible currencies where physical delivery is legally impossible, with settlement in USDC, GBP or EUR and no physical exchange.
