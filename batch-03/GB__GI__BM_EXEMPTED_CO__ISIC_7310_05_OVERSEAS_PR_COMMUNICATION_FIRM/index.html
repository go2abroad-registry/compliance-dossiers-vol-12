Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (Commissioner of Income Tax) and HM Customs Gibraltar for customs/indirect duty matters. Principal portals: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax and https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/customs

E-government filing portal

The Bermuda exempted company must first file as a foreign company with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under the Companies (Foreign Companies in Gibraltar) Act, obtain a foreign company registration number, appoint a Gibraltar resident agent, and file its constitutional documents. It then applies to the Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar corporate Tax Identification Number (TIN) using the prescribed application form. If the company imports or exports goods, it must additionally register with HM Customs Gibraltar for a customs reference/declaration code. No part of this procedure requires minimum capital or a statutory licence for a PR agency.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists in Bermuda or Gibraltar for an exempted company. Gibraltar law does not provide a US-style entity classification election; however, a limited election may be made under the Gibraltar group relief and pooling provisions once the company is chargeable to income tax, and the company may elect which Gibraltar representative is responsible for tax filings. Non-taxable status in Bermuda is automatic; no election is required.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Customs and Excise Duty Regime (no VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A – Gibraltar does not operate a VAT, sales tax or GST regime; no turnover threshold triggers VAT registration. A customs registration is only required on a per-declaration basis for dutiable imports/exports.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. As Gibraltar has no VAT, there is no 0% export VAT rate to claim. Cross-border PR/communication services supplied by a Gibraltar branch of a Bermuda exempted company are outside the scope of Gibraltarian indirect tax. If the company occasionally exports physical promotional materials, such exports are duty-free as outward transfers, but no VAT zero-rating certificate is issued by the tax authority.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Gibraltar Export Entry, if physical goods leave Gibraltar)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / CMR consignment note
  • Proof of Direct Payment or Inward Remittance Certificate where the underlying PR contract is evidenced
  • Incoterms evidence and export packing material proof for duty-relief claims

No real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing, digital stamping, or SAF-T/B2B clearance regime is operative in Gibraltar for an exempted service company. Invoice issuance is governed by ordinary commercial law and the market practice of the PR client contract; any invoice should still carry a clear description, date, amount, and supplier identity. For HM Customs, goods entries are lodged electronically via the Gibraltar Electronic Customs Handling System.

For customs, a false export claim or mis-description can lead to seizure of goods, reassessment of duty, penalty up to £3,000 plus goods liable to forfeiture under the Customs Ordinance, and criminal proceedings where fraud is established. For income tax, finance bill penalties apply for incorrect returns (up to 100% of the tax undercharged). There is no statutory FX repatriation clawback regime in Gibraltar, but the Inward Remittance Certificate will be scrutinised by the Income Tax Office to trace service income.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar operates a fully convertible current and capital account regime. GIP is freely convertible to GBP and other major currencies subject only to standard AML/KYC onboarding and customer due diligence. Profits and capital may be remitted to beneficial owners without exchange control approval or withholding taxes, provided the Bermuda Exempted Company maintains its non-resident tax status and does not transact in prohibited sectors. Financial statements and UBO declarations must support the provenance of any dividend or shareholder loan repayment.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar imposes no statutory export proceeds surrender rules and no mandatory conversion ratios. The entity's scope is cross-border public-relations and media advisory services (ISIC 7310), which constitute an export of services rather than physical goods. Consequently, all service invoice proceeds may be retained in GIP, GBP, USD, EUR or other convertible currencies at the election of the treasury function.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (ultimate monetary anchor for the Gibraltar Pound sterling peg); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as statutory banking conduct regulator; HM Government of Gibraltar as note-issuing authority for Gibraltar Pounds (GIP) at a compulsory 1:1 peg to GBP.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium feasibility as a supplementary digital clearing rail, not as a replacement for a licensed Gibraltar bank. Fintech and EMI providers (UK- or EU-regulated) can onboard the Bermuda Exempted Company remotely where the UBO owns a standard passport in an OECD jurisdiction and the corporate package includes a registered Gibraltar address. Key legal constraints: the EMI must hold a permission allowing business corporate clients, must be able to accept a non-operating holding structure, and will typically reject the account if any crypto dealing, inward FX-only treasury arbitrage, or unverifiable IP payments are detected. Onboarding is rapid, but operational limits and frozen-fund risk are materially higher than with tier-1 Gibraltar banks.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital contributions and shareholder loans must be wired via SWIFT MT103 from the private banking account of the ultimate beneficial owner or the Bermuda holding entity to the Gibraltar operational account. The wire reference field (Field 70) must state either 'Share capital contribution per Board Resolution dated [date]' or 'Shareholder loan facility dated [date] and signed by all beneficial owners'. Supporting documentation pre-cleared before transaction includes a certified board resolution, source-of-funds questionnaires, evidence of availability of funds, and a full corporate registry extract of the Bermuda Exempted Company. Funds should be denominated in GIP or GBP first to minimise conversion cost, with USD/EUR accepted only where the originating lender/bank requires a specific trade jurisdiction.Trade settlements relating to media purchases and subcontract PR deliverables are cleared primarily through SWIFT MT103 payments with purpose code 'SRV' (General Services) or 'BOD' (Business-related services) in the correspondent banking message. For UK domestic GBP settlements, the bank may route through the UK Faster Payments Service or CHAPS using the GIP/GBP parity, provided the receiving entity has a UK or Gibraltar bank account.
