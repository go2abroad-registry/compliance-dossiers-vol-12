Competent tax authority

HMRC (UK) is not the competent authority for Gibraltar local taxation; Gibraltar is a separate tax jurisdiction outside the UK VAT and direct tax systems.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Register the Bermuda Exempted Company with Companies House Gibraltar as an overseas company under the Companies Act 2014, filing certified certificate of incorporation, memorandum/articles, board resolution, registered office address and local process agent details. Step 2: Submit the Income Tax Office company registration form, beneficial ownership declaration, Gibraltar registered business address and constitutional documents; the Commissioner will issue a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN) for the foreign entity. Step 3: File the annual corporate tax return on territorial profits; if central management and control remains outside Gibraltar, a non-resident declaration should be filed to avoid Gibraltar tax residency while retaining the Gibraltar TIN. Step 4: Maintain Bermuda exempted status through annual declarations to the Bermuda Registrar of Companies via the registered resident representative.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope — the Bermuda Exempted Company is tax-exempt in Bermuda (no Bermuda income tax, capital gains tax or withholding tax) but, when registered to carry on business in Gibraltar, becomes chargeable to Gibraltar corporation tax at 12.5% only on profits accruing in or derived from Gibraltar (territorial attribution). If central management and control is exercised in Gibraltar, the company is treated as a Gibraltar tax resident under section 82 of the Income Tax Act 2010, with the same territorial charge; foreign-source profits remain outside the Gibraltar tax base.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or entity classification election exists in Gibraltar; residence is determined factually by central management and control. The former Qualifying Company (Category 2) regime is closed to new entrants (phased out by 2010). Hybrid mismatch rules (Taxes (Hybrid Mismatch) Regulations 2017, aligned with OECD BEPS Action 2) apply without elective carve-outs; no statutory election is available to treat the Bermuda Exempted Company as pass-through or disregarded for Gibraltar tax purposes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales-and-Use-Tax in Gibraltar; Gibraltar is outside the UK VAT area and indirect taxation is limited to customs import duties and excise on a narrow list of physical goods. Software-as-a-service (ISIC 6201) exports and payroll automation subscription revenues are outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect tax; UK VAT (20%) may apply to UK customers under reverse-charge or overseas supplier registration rules, but that is an HMRC regime, not a Gibraltar charge.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no registration threshold exists because Gibraltar does not levy VAT/GST on services; import duty is generally zero-rated on entry of goods into Gibraltar, with excise only on alcohol, tobacco and fuel.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — Gibraltar has no VAT/GST zero-rating mechanism; cross-border SaaS and payroll-automation services invoiced from Gibraltar are outside the scope of any Gibraltar indirect tax, so no zero-rating claim or export refund procedure is required. If the company voluntarily registers for UK VAT to supply UK customers, UK zero-rating for exported services may apply under VATA 1994, but that is administratively outside Gibraltar's regime.

  • N/A — no Bill of Lading or airway bill exists for electronically supplied services; retain the executed MSA, SaaS subscription order, service delivery logs and IP right evidence instead.
  • Inward remittance evidence (SWIFT MT103, bank credit advice or statement) should be retained for territorial profit-attribution and transfer-pricing audits; no statutory FX repatriation certificate is mandatory in Gibraltar.

Not applicable — Gibraltar has no real-time fiscal clearance, no mandatory e-invoicing/API requirement, no digital stamping regime and no clearance portal for invoices; standard electronic or paper invoicing with a complete audit trail is sufficient for Gibraltar tax compliance.

Gibraltar has no VAT zero-rating regime, so no indirect-tax clawback is applicable. However, misstatement of territorial profits in Gibraltar corporate tax returns attracts penalties under the Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended): up to 100% of the tax underpaid for negligence and up to 150% for fraudulent or wilful default, plus daily default penalties, interest and potential criminal prosecution. If the company voluntarily registers for UK VAT and makes a false zero-rating claim, UK Finance Act 2007 Schedule 24 penalties (up to 100% of lost revenue) and interest would apply. Gibraltar has no statutory FX repatriation deadline; the 180-day benchmark is a documentary and transfer-pricing convention for export proceeds realization in cross-border SaaS operations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The GIP is pegged at par to GBP, and both current-account and capital-account transactions are fully convertible. Neither Gibraltar nor Bermuda maintains exchange-control permit regimes, capital import licensing, or profit-remittance restrictions. A Bermuda Exempted Company benefits from statutory exemption from Bermuda tax; dividends and capital repatriations to shareholders are free of Bermuda and Gibraltar withholding tax provided the company is not managed and controlled in Gibraltar. Treasury discipline requires that central management, board meetings, and strategic decision-making remain outside Gibraltar to preserve the tax-exempt and non-resident treasury status.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Neither Bermuda nor Gibraltar imposes statutory export-proceeds surrender timelines, mandatory conversion ratios, or repatriation windows. Receipts for ISIC 6201 payroll-software and automation services may be retained in GBP, USD, EUR, or other freely convertible currencies and settled according to commercial terms. Post-Brexit, EU capital-movement restrictions do not extend to Gibraltar, and no Bank of England or HM Treasury clearance is required for trade or service receipts.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC). Gibraltar has no central bank; the Government of Gibraltar issues Gibraltar pound (GIP) notes, legal tender only in Gibraltar, fully backed at par by pound sterling reserves held with the Bank of England. The GFSC regulates banking, anti-money laundering, and payment services under the Financial Services Act 2019 and the AML Act 2019. The Bermuda Exempted Company is supervised at entity level by the Bermuda Registrar of Companies and the Bermuda Monetary Authority; the UK Prudential Regulation Authority has no direct supervisory mandate over Gibraltar-licensed banks.
