Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Her Majesty's Revenue & Customs (HMRC) for UK VAT administration; Gibraltar Income Tax Office (GITO) for Gibraltar local tax identity. HMRC portal: https://www.gov.uk/vat; Gibraltar portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/tax

E-government filing portal

For UK VAT: Non-Established Taxable Person (NETP) applies to HMRC on Form VAT1; non-established suppliers of digital services to UK consumers may register via the VAT One Stop Shop (OSS) portal. For Gibraltar: a foreign company (including a Bermuda Exempted Company) registers at the Gibraltar Companies Registry under the Foreign Companies Act, obtains a certificate of registration, and files Form TAX1 with the Income Tax Office to secure a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN). No VAT registration in Gibraltar is required because Gibraltar does not operate a VAT regime.

Default tax classification

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GBP 90,000 for UK-established businesses; no de minimis threshold for non-established businesses supplying digital services to UK consumers (mandatory registration); Gibraltar: no VAT threshold (not applicable)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods may be zero-rated if exported to a non-UK destination within 3 months, with contemporaneous transport and customs evidence. Services, including digital advertising and hyper-casual game publishing services, are outside the scope of UK VAT (and thus effectively zero-rated) when supplied to a customer who belongs outside the UK, subject to evidence of the customer's identity and country of residence. For B2B services the customer's VAT registration number or equivalent must be obtained; for B2C services the location of the recipient is established by payment/billing address and IP geo-location.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD / C78 / CDS electronic acknowledgment)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (or bank / fintech payment evidence for FX repatriation)
  • Export invoice and purchase order / contract
  • For digital services: customer confirmation of non-UK status, IP address validation, payment receipt and internal contract file

UK Making Tax Digital for VAT requires digital record-keeping and API-based submission of VAT returns; e-invoicing is not currently mandated; no real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping is required, except for VAT OSS digital services periodic returns. Gibraltar has no e-invoicing or fiscal clearance mandate.

HMRC may issue a protective VAT assessment up to 20 years for deliberate misstatement; zero-rating can be retrospectively revoked with interest; penalties of up to 100% of the VAT due apply for fraudulent evasion; criminal prosecution may be brought under the Customs and Excise Management Act 1979. Gibraltar Customs may apply forfeiture and value-based fines for export declaration falsehoods or misclassification.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current-account and capital-account convertibility. The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is pegged at par to GBP and circulates interchangeably with sterling in the banking system; EUR and USD are freely accepted for settlement. No exchange-control approvals are required for inward investment, dividend distribution, licensing royalties, or intercompany funding. Profit remittance from a Gibraltar-registered foreign company of a Bermuda Exempted Company is unrestricted, with zero withholding tax. The regime complies with IMF Article VIII obligations and retains EU-derived free-movement-of-capital principles.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. No statutory timeline, surrender obligation, or mandatory conversion ratio applies to export proceeds or advertising receivables in Gibraltar or the UK. USD, EUR, and GBP receipts from AdMob/AppLovin may be retained in original currency or converted at treasury discretion. The only compliance overlay is internal AML/CFT record-keeping under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 (Gibraltar) and the Sanctions Act 2020 (Gibraltar), requiring retention of underlying invoices, platform payout statements, and commercial contracts.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the sole prudential and conduct banking regulator; Government of Gibraltar (Ministry of Finance) as issuer of Gibraltar pound banknotes and manager of the statutory 1:1 GBP peg; Bank of England as the de facto anchor monetary authority for the pegged currency. Gibraltar has no independent central bank.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: HIGH as an auxiliary collection and FX-conversion spoke, but MEDIUM-LOW as a standalone principal treasury rail. UK FCA-licensed electronic money institutions (Wise Business, Revolut Business, Payoneer) — or EMIs holding a direct GFSC e-money licence — may lawfully service Gibraltar entities under the UK-Gibraltar financial services equivalence framework; EEA EMIs cannot rely on EU passporting into Gibraltar. Payoneer and Wise payout collection for Google AdMob and AppLovin is available. Legal constraints: (i) e-money is not a bank deposit and is not protected by the Gibraltar deposit compensation scheme or UK FSCS, (ii) safeguarding accounts are held at tier-1 banks and are vulnerable to third-party insolvency, and (iii) high-velocity micro-payout settlement or mismatched branding between the EMI account name and the AdMob/AppLovin payee name triggers duplicate onboarding and frequent compliance interventions. Recommended architecture: EMIs operate only as collection spokes for platform payouts; the GFSC-regulated bank account remains the consolidated treasury hub.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For a zero-minimum-capital Exempted Company, injections are routed as SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's licensed bank to the Gibraltar settlement account using ISO 20022 purpose code SUBS (share subscription) or CINL (capital increase), with field 70 stating 'Share capital subscription per board resolution [date]' and the company CRS/GIIN reference. The complying file pack comprises: executed board resolution, allotment form, updated register of members, source-of-wealth documentation of the UBO, and CRS self-certification; injections exceeding GBP 100,000 equivalent trigger EDD and may require a confirmation letter from the remitting bank. No exchange-control or GFSC approval is required, but the receiving bank must complete customer due diligence under the Gibraltar AML/CFT framework before credit; capital received is cleared on a T+1 basis and may be held in any currency without surrender.Primary clearing rails: SWIFT MT103/MT202 COV for international trade settlements and USD payouts via the Jyske/Gibraltar International Bank correspondent network; UK Faster Payments (FPS) for GBP disbursements to suppliers and contractors; SEPA Instant for EUR-region payouts; ACH/SWIFT for US payor routes. Commercial Letters of Credit are not applicable to digital advertising transactions, the operative documentary rail being the cross-border MT103 supported by pro-forma invoices and, where required, on-demand payment guarantees under URDG 758. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 500,000 in aggregate related-party transactions triggers a mandatory contemporaneous Local File. Master File documentation is required for Gibraltar group members whose consolidated group revenue exceeds EUR 750,000,000 (approximately GIP 675,000,000).

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar does not currently operate a standalone Economic Substance Regulation regime, but the ultimate substance obligation sits with the Bermuda Exempted Company under the Bermuda Economic Substance (Corporate Tax) Act 2018. A company holding IP for hyper-casual games must demonstrate a physical presence, employees, and expenditure in Bermuda unless it qualifies for a pure equity holding exemption. If central management and control is found to be in Gibraltar, Gibraltar will deem the company resident and demand tax on worldwide profits. The 'Tax_Exempt_Zone' classification is upheld only where a genuine Bermuda substance core exists and no Gibraltar PE is created.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (false non-resident claim)

Statutory sanction: Reassessment as a Gibraltar-resident company on worldwide advertising profits at 10%; penalty of up to 100% of the tax due; fine of up to GIP 50,000.

Director personal liability: Directors signing false residency declarations are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax, subject to disqualification for up to 5 years, and can face a travel ban. Criminal fraud carries imprisonment of up to 2 years.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Bermuda ESR penalties range from $10,000 to $250,000 and can result in striking off the company. Gibraltar can issue a winding-up order for non-filing of Form C and late tax penalties up to GIP 25,000.

Director personal liability: Directors must certify economic substance; continuing to sign false certificates can trigger personal restitution of avoided tax and a travel ban under the Foreign Tax Enforcement Act, in addition to court-imposed director disqualification.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: The Income Tax (Transfer Pricing) Act grants the Commissioner power to reallocate profits to reflect arm's-length outcomes; the adjustment is taxed at 10% with monthly interest at 0.5% and a penalty of 25% of the understatement.

Director personal liability: The TP return requires officer certification. False documentation or failure to provide the Local File triggers a penalty up to GIP 10,000 per document. Where the scheme is proved fraudulent, directors are personally liable for the tax and may be prosecuted for theft of revenue.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Concealment

Statutory sanction: Under the Gibraltar Beneficial Ownership (Information) Act, non-disclosure of UBO information is a criminal offence punishable with a fine of up to GIP 500,000 and/or imprisonment for up to 2 years. PSPs may freeze accounts and block payouts.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors can be disqualified from acting as directors in Gibraltar for 10 years and fined personally up to GIP 20,000. If funds are laundered, personal accounts may be frozen under the Proceeds of Crime Act, and travel bans are available where the director is outside the jurisdiction.
Illegal Dividend Re-Patriation

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar Companies Act prohibits distributions unless the directors issue a solvency statement. An illegal distribution is a criminal offence; the company remains liable for unpaid tax plus interest, and shareholders may be ordered to repay the dividend.

Director personal liability: Directors who cosign a solvency statement without reasonable grounds are personally liable for the company's debts and any tax shortfall. The Commissioner may sue for wrongful trading, and a travel ban may be imposed under the Foreign Tax Enforcement Act if assets are moved out of Gibraltar.
