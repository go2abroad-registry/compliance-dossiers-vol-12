E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company (BM_EXEMPTED_CO) establishing a branch or place of business in Gibraltar must: (1) register as a 'foreign company' with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under the Companies Act 2014 (Part XXIII) within one month of establishment, filing certified constitutional documents, register of directors/secretary, local registered-office agreement and proof of a local Process Agent, obtaining a Gibraltar FC-company reference number; (2) file a Notification of Liability to Tax (Form NLT) with the Income Tax Office within 30 days of commencing the Gibraltarian business activity, using the FC reference to obtain a Gibraltar Identification Number (GIN/Tax Reference) from ITO; (3) if claiming territorial exemption on foreign-source matcha-trade income, apply for the foreign-source income exemption at ITO with board resolutions, substance documentation (physical office, local secretary, management minutes in Gibraltar) and a forecast of non-Gibraltar revenue; (4) register as a customs declarant/importer with the Gibraltar Customs & Excise Department and appoint a licensed customs agent where goods will physically transit or be warehoused in Gibraltar. Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current-account and capital-account convertibility. GIP is legally pegged to GBP, eliminating intra-GIP convertibility risk. No exchange-control legislation exists in either Gibraltar or Bermuda; no statutory restrictions apply to dividend distribution, loan repayment, capital repatriation, or profit remittance, and no withholding taxes are levied by either jurisdiction. Trading proceeds in USD and JPY are freely convertible into GIP/GBP or may be retained in multicurrency accounts.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Gibraltar and Bermuda impose no mandatory surrender, conversion, or repatriation timeline for export proceeds. A Bermuda Exempted Company may hold export receivables indefinitely in any freely convertible currency without GFSC or Bermuda Monetary Authority conversion obligations.

Central bank supervision: Government of Gibraltar (Currency Notes Act 1934) is the issuing authority for the Gibraltar Pound (GIP), pegged at 1:1 to GBP; regulatory supervision of banking and payment institutions is exercised by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC); the Bank of England provides the underlying GBP backing. Bermuda imposes no central bank exchange-control mandate over exempted companies.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW-TO-MEDIUM. GFSC-licensed Electronic Money Institutions are few and typically require Gibraltar substance through a regulated fiduciary; post-Brexit, UK and EU EMI passporting no longer covers Gibraltar. Most UK FCA-authorised EMIs (e.g., Wise Business, Currencycloud) will decline a Bermuda Exempted Company with non-resident nominee directors under UK Money Laundering Regulations 2017 unless the entity is introduced by an approved Gibraltar TCSP and maintains a Gibraltar-resident signatory.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Return must be filed no later than 30 November following the end of the year of assessment in which the accounting period ends; for a 31 December year-end the filing and final payment deadline is 30 November of the following calendar year. Non-calendar-year accounting periods: due date is the following 30 November unless the accounting period ends after 30 September, in which case an extension to the next 30 November may apply.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Box 1 – Company Identity Legal name, Gibraltar registration number, ITO tax reference, registered office address, date of incorporation. List all non-resident shareholders and directors; state the 'Bermuda Exempted Company' structural status and the jurisdiction of tax residence (Bermuda or Gibraltar). Late filing: fixed penalty of GIP 100, increasing to GIP 1,000 after three months, plus daily surcharge of GIP 60 (maximum GIP 3,600); negligent or fraudulent returns: penalty up to 100% of the tax understated; criminal liability for deliberate evasion with fines of up to GIP 500,000.
Box 2 – Accounting Period Start and end dates of the chargeable accounting period. First return may cover up to 18 months from incorporation; quarterly instalment scheme entries to be completed for large companies. Late filing: fixed penalty of GIP 100, increasing to GIP 1,000 after three months, plus daily surcharge of GIP 60 (maximum GIP 3,600); negligent or fraudulent returns: penalty up to 100% of the tax understated; criminal liability for deliberate evasion with fines of up to GIP 500,000.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File: mandatory where aggregate controlled transactions exceed GIP 1,000,000 per annum; Master File: mandatory where the multinational group's consolidated turnover exceeds GIP 50,000,000; Country-by-Country Report follows the OECD threshold of EUR 750,000,000.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations 2019 (Schedule 2 of the Income Tax Act 2010) classify 'distribution and service business' as a relevant activity; the matcha trading company must maintain (i) board meetings and strategic direction in Gibraltar, (ii) adequate physical premises and employees, and (iii) performance of core income-generating activities in Gibraltar. The Bermuda Exempted Company classification does not eliminate ESR duties: if control and management are found to be in Bermuda, the entity falls under Bermuda's Economic Substance Act 2018, requiring equivalent CIGA presence in Bermuda and annual ESR reporting to the Registrar.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Filing

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of up to 100% of the tax evaded plus statutory interest; criminal fines up to GIP 500,000; confiscation of the proceeds of the matcha trade under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015.

Director personal liability: Nominee and executive directors who sign false returns are personally liable for civil restitution of the evaded tax; courts may impose travel bans, exclusion from directorships for up to 15 years, and imprisonment of up to 7 years.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Failure of the ESR test: penalty of GIP 10,000 rising to GIP 1,000 per day (capped at GIP 50,000) and automatic exchange of information with HMRC and Bermuda; the company may be struck off the Gibraltar register and lose tax-residence status.

Director personal liability: Directors who fail to exercise real management and control are jointly and severally assessed for the company's unpaid tax on piercing of the corporate veil; disqualification orders under the Companies Act 2014 and liability for ESR penalties.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Adjustment of taxable profits at 12.5% with statutory interest; penalty up to 100% of the tax shortfall for failure to maintain the Local File/Master File; CbC-related penalties for non-filing.

Director personal liability: Directors who approved the intercompany pricing and failed to commission an OECD-compliant benchmarking study are jointly and severally liable for the tax adjustment and face director disqualification proceedings.
AML / Unlicensed Financial Activity

Statutory sanction: Corporate fines of up to GIP 1,000,000; confiscation orders over trade proceeds; revocation of the company's exempted status and banking facilities; regulatory censure by the GFSC.

Director personal liability: Individual liability: imprisonment of up to 14 years for money-laundering; travel bans and asset-freezing orders; personal confiscation orders and permanent disqualification from acting as a director of any Gibraltar company.
