Formation / constitutional filings

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a Bermuda Exempted Company. The founding subscriber declares the issued capital in the Memorandum of Association; funds may be placed in escrow with a licensed overnight bank or fiduciary pending commencement of business; any share premium above par must be credited to a non-distributable share premium reserve account.

Funding & unlock path: A Fire Safety Risk Assessment compliant with the Gibraltar Fire and Rescue Services Act and the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2006 (as applied) is required before any physical premises are occupied. A structural load inspection certificate from a certified engineer is required if racking or mezzanine storage exceeding 3 meters is installed. Occupational clearance under the Gibraltar Health and Safety at Work Act 2018 must be obtained for manual handling of COD parcels, electrical safety of retail IT equipment, and emergency evacuation plans.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a Bermuda Exempted Company. Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Not Applicable — ISIC 4791 (mail-order retail with cash-on-delivery settlement) does not require an industrial license in Bermuda or Gibraltar. Should a physical warehouse or order-fulfillment center be established in Gibraltar, the competent authority would be the Department of the Environment, Heritage and Climate Change for permitting and the Gibraltar Trade Licensing Authority for a trading license.

Category: Not Applicable for pure e-commerce operations. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office (HM Government of Gibraltar), with the e-Tax online portal administered for income tax, VAT and customs duties; indirect tax and customs enforcement is administered by HM Customs Gibraltar (Gibraltar Tax and Customs).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Register the Bermuda Exempted Company or its Gibraltar branch at the Gibraltar Registry of Companies (Companies House Gibraltar) under the Companies Act 2014. Step 2: Apply to the Income Tax Office for a Tax Reference Number (TRN) using the enterprise registration form, submitting the certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles, register of directors and shareholders, registered-office confirmation and a declaration of whether central management and control will be exercised from Gibraltar. Step 3: On issuance of the TRN, configure payroll, accounting and payment-rail onboarding (including Airwallex and local logistics remittance accounts). Step 4: Where taxable supplies are made in Gibraltar, file the VAT registration application with HM Customs Gibraltar before the effective date of the first taxable supply, disclosing the TRN, premises, the mail-order retail activity and estimated annual taxable turnover. Step 5: Obtain a UK/Gibraltar EORI number for customs movements and appoint a fiscal representative if the foreign company lacks a Gibraltar establishment but makes distance sales or imports goods into Gibraltar.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not provide a domestic US-style check-the-box election; a company incorporated under the Gibraltar Companies Act 2014 is resident on incorporation, while a foreign-incorporated Bermuda Exempted Company is Gibraltar-tax-resident only if its central management and control is exercised from Gibraltar. Available election and designation mechanisms include: (1) applying to the Commissioner for a tax clearance or advance ruling confirming absence of Gibraltar-source liability for a non-resident offshore company; (2) maintaining Bermuda exempted-company status under the Bermuda Exempted Companies Act 1981 to support a no-permanent-establishment position; (3) making an IRS Form 8832 check-the-box election for US federal classification where US-connected investors are present; and (4) applying hybrid mismatch elections or substitutional adjustments under Gibraltar's alignment with OECD/G20 BEPS Action 2 to neutralise double non-taxation in cross-border payment and logistics arrangements with the UK and emerging-market jurisdictions.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

18%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GBP 85,000 (Gibraltar pound at parity) of annual taxable supplies for suppliers established in Gibraltar, in line with the UK-style small-business registration threshold; a foreign taxable person with no Gibraltar establishment must register when its distance sales or taxable supplies into Gibraltar meet the same threshold, and any person importing goods into Gibraltar must register for customs and import VAT regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A supply of mail-order goods by the Bermuda Exempted Company is zero-rated under the Gibraltar Value Added Tax Act 2010 where: (a) the goods are physically exported from Gibraltar to a destination outside Gibraltar and outside the UK; (b) removal occurs within the statutory time limit, normally three months from the supply; (c) the contract of carriage is made with the consignee or a freight forwarder acting for the exporter and names the final foreign destination; (d) the exporter retains the complete export evidence chain including customs declaration, Bill of Lading, delivery proof and remittance certificate; (e) for ancillary cross-border services (platform, fulfilment, payment processing), the recipient is a taxable person outside Gibraltar and the place of supply is outside Gibraltar; and (f) no part of the goods, logistics chain or settlement proceeds is diverted back into Gibraltar.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic CDS/SAD export declaration lodged through the UK Customs Declaration Service or Gibraltar-designated customs gateway)
  • Bill of Lading, Airway Bill, CMR or postal dispatch record evidencing physical export movement
  • Commercial invoice and packing list issued to the overseas purchaser
  • Signed proof of delivery (POD) from the final logistics operator at the foreign destination
  • Bank SWIFT MT103 credit advice or inward remittance certificate confirming settlement of export proceeds through Airwallex or local logistics remittance rails
  • Insurance certificate and contract of carriage naming the consignee
  • Certificate of Origin (EUR.1, ATR or non-preferential origin certificate) where preferential tariff treatment is claimed
  • VAT export listing schedule submitted with the relevant VAT return

Gibraltar does not currently mandate real-time B2B e-invoicing clearance, fiscal APIs or digital stamping. Taxable persons must issue VAT invoices electronically or in paper form within 30 days of supply and retain complete invoice-level transactional data for the statutory six-year record-keeping period. VAT returns are filed through the e-Tax portal, while customs export and import declarations must be lodged electronically through the UK Customs Declaration Service (CDS) and related common-transit procedures. Payment-rail data from Airwallex and local logistics remittance providers must be reconcilable to invoices for audit purposes, but no state-level transaction clearance platform applies.

HM Customs Gibraltar and the Income Tax Office perform post-export verification, risk profiling and cross-border mutual assistance with HMRC and Bermuda authorities. Incomplete, contradictory or fraudulent export documentation, including falsified COD settlement evidence or circular remittance arrangements through Airwallex or local logistics rails, triggers retrospective assessment at the standard VAT rate of 18%, disallowance of input VAT recovery and interest on arrears; the ordinary assessment period is six years, extended to twelve years in cases of fraud or neglect. Civil penalties may reach up to 100% of the evaded VAT. Criminal sanctions on conviction include unlimited fines and imprisonment of up to seven years for fraudulent evasion, together with director disqualification and forfeiture of goods, vehicles and banking assets. The Gibraltar Financial Services Commission may also revoke EMI or payment-permission approvals and impose conduct fines where AML/CFT controls have been bypassed in settlement routing.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound is pegged at par with GBP and is fully convertible for both current and capital account transactions. Bermuda Exempted Companies are subject to no exchange-control approval for capital inflows, operational disbursements, or distributable profits. The only practical hurdles are bank-level enhanced due diligence and source-of-funds verification, particularly for cross-border flows routed through fintech EMIs or emerging-market logistics providers. A non-resident Bermuda Exempted Company will not face Gibraltar corporation tax or withholding tax on profit remittances, but a Gibraltar tax residency or substance assessment may be triggered if central management and control is exercised from Gibraltar.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender requirements and no mandatory conversion ratio for receipts denominated in GBP, USD, or EUR. Export proceeds may be retained in any freely convertible currency. Under the GFSC AML/CFT framework, the beneficiary company must maintain auditable records of all incoming cross-border trade proceeds for at least five years, including underlying contracts, logistics confirmations, customer identification, and traceable settlement trails.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) in its capacity as the integrated financial regulator and foreign-exchange licensing authority; monetary authority for the Gibraltar pound (GIP) rests with HM Government of Gibraltar.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Airwallex, Wise Business, and Revolut Business are feasible as supplementary inbound collection and payment-rail escrow solutions, rated High for distribution-side liquidity, but they are not licensed as Gibraltar deposit-taking institutions and cannot substitute for a tier-1 local banking relationship. Legal constraints include potential recovery limitations, frozen-funds exposure under sanctions screening, and refusal to onboard end-customers in high-FATF-risk emerging markets unless the Bermuda Exempted Company can demonstrate a full KYB/KYC packet, real contracts with logistics partners, and transparent treasury reporting.
