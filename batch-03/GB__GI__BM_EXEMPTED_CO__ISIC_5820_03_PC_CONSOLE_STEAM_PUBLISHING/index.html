Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GIP is fully convertible into GBP at a fixed 1:1 rate and is freely usable in the UK and Gibraltar; no foreign-exchange restrictions apply to current or capital account transactions under Gibraltar law. The Bermuda Exempted Company, when designated non-resident by the BMA, may receive and retain foreign currency without limitation; profit remittances to shareholders are permitted without statutory approval provided the exempted company is not classified as resident for exchange-control purposes. Remittance hurdles exist only contractually, e.g. board-approved solvency certification, CRS/FATCA self-certification, and the availability of distributable reserves.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar imposes no statutory surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds or digital publishing royalties; Steam and PlayStation remittances may be retained in USD or converted to GBP/GIP on a discretionary treasury basis. Bermuda exchange-control regulations for exempted companies generally do not require mandatory conversion or time-bound surrender; any authorisation conditions imposed by the BMA or appointed authorised dealer must be respected, but in standard non-resident exempted company operations export receipts are freely retainable.

Central bank supervision: For Gibraltar operations: the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the licensing and supervisory authority for banking and cross-border payment institutions; monetary authority functions for the Gibraltar pound (GIP) are exercised by the Government of Gibraltar. For the Bermuda Exempted Company itself: the Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) administers exchange control and capital transfer permissions. The Bank of England is the de facto monetary anchor because GIP is pegged at par to GBP and the UK forms the ultimate sovereign framework.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: High for low-value treasury clearing and payout-collection as a secondary rail; Low for primary account-holding of statutory capital or long-term treasury reserves. Legal constraints under Gibraltar Financial Services (Electronic Money) Regulations and the UK Electronic Money Regulations 2011: an offshore digital EMI may open a segregated customer account for the Bermuda Exempted Company, but it is not a bank and cannot provide deposit protection, lending, or escrow of shareholder capital. EMI balances are safeguarded but not covered by deposit guarantee regimes; therefore EMIs such as Wise, Revolut Business, or Airwallex may be used only for receivable collection and small disbursements, subject to a GFSC/UK FCA-authorised EMI having a passported or branch arrangement valid for Gibraltar.

Late filing of Form CT-1 attracts an automatic penalty of GIP 100 plus a daily default fine of GIP 10 for up to 90 days; a further tax-geared penalty of up to 5% of the final tax liability applies. Inaccurate returns are subject to a penalty up to 100% of the underpaid tax and interest at 8% per annum.
Box 4 - Chargeable Profits Total taxable profits attributable to Gibraltar sources or, for residents, worldwide income. Include only Gibraltar-source trading income and income receivable in Gibraltar from the Steam/PlayStation publishing activities; exclude foreign-source royalties not received in Gibraltar. Late filing of Form CT-1 attracts an automatic penalty of GIP 100 plus a daily default fine of GIP 10 for up to 90 days; a further tax-geared penalty of up to 5% of the final tax liability applies. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 in aggregate controlled transactions during the period triggers mandatory Local File preparation; groups with GIP 20,000,000 group turnover trigger Master File preparation. Below those thresholds a high-level arm's length policy and tolerance analysis must still be retained.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar applies the Economic Substance (Companies) Act 2019 to entities carrying on relevant activities including distribution and service centres, headquarters and holding companies. A Bermuda exempted company operating a Steam/PlayStation publishing hub in Gibraltar must show premises, full-time employees, operating expenditure and decision-making proportionate to the digital distribution functions. A non-resident company without a Gibraltar permanent establishment is outside the ESR, but a mere shell or conduit arrangement will be disregarded for tax and treaty purposes.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Misrepresentation of Non-Residence

Statutory sanction: Additional assessment on the true profits at 10%; a penalty of up to 200% of the underpaid tax; daily interest at 8% per annum; public disclosure of the default and seizure of assets.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign a false return may be personally and jointly liable for unpaid tax under the corporate veil piercing doctrine; a criminal conviction carries imprisonment of up to 3 years, a fine of GIP 50,000, a travel ban and restitution of all avoided tax.
Shell Company / Artificial Arrangement / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Disregard of the corporate personality, reallocation of profits to the true place of management, denial of tax exemption, a penalty of up to GIP 150,000 under the Economic Substance Act, and administrative struck-off.

Director personal liability: Directors can be disqualified for up to 5 years, ordered to make restitution for the costs of formal winding up, and made personally liable for the tax equal to the income routed through the fictional structure.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Full arm's length recomputation of chargeable profits, a secondary adjustment treating excess payments as distributions, a penalty of up to 35% of the additional tax payable, plus 8% per annum interest from the due date.

Director personal liability: The finance director and the tax compliance officer are personally exposed to civil penalties for negligent misstatement if they approve the return without a reasonable investigation; a punitive award can be recovered from their personal estate.
AML and Money Laundering / Unlicensed Payment Routing

Statutory sanction: Freezing orders, forfeiture under the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act, fines up to GIP 500,000, imprisonment for money laundering up to 14 years and revocation of any EMI or financial licence connected to the operation.

Director personal liability: Directors as relevant persons are personally liable for failing to file a Suspicious Activity Report; the corporate veil will be pierced for concealment, leading to a civil recovery order, asset freeze, surrender of passports and a travel ban pending trial.
