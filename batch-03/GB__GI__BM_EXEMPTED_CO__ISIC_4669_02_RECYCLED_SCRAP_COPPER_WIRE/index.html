Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar – Taxation Department (Income Tax Office) and HM Customs of Gibraltar (General Consumption Tax); official digital filing portal: tax.gov.gi

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company operating through Gibraltar as a non-resident vehicle must be registered for tax purposes only when it derives Gibraltar-source income, maintains an establishment/place of business in Gibraltar, or engages in import/export activities physically passing through Gibraltar. For General Consumption Tax (GCT): file Form GCT-1 with HM Customs, provide Bermuda certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, registered address, local trading address if any, and expected annual taxable turnover; a GCT registration number is issued within 15 working days. For corporate tax: file Form T1 with the Taxation Department, submit the Bermuda registration documentation, state the place of central management and control, and obtain a Gibraltarian Tax File Number (TFN). Inbound foreign vehicles may also obtain a voluntary tax reference for banking/EMI onboarding without creating tax residency.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (Bermuda Exempted Company treated as an offshore non-resident vehicle; any income attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment or sourced in Gibraltar remains subject to entity-level territorial taxation at the prevailing Gibraltarian corporate rate if managed and controlled from Gibraltar)

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no US-style check-the-box election. For GCT, registration is mandatory once the statutory threshold is crossed; no fiscal-transparency election is available for companies. For income tax, resident companies may make limited elections under the Income Tax Act regarding source and remittance treatment; hybrid mismatch adjustments are mandatory under local OECD-aligned rules and no opt-out election exists.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

1%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GIP 100,000 annual taxable turnover; separate compulsory registration applies for persons importing goods into Gibraltar or receiving goods from outside Gibraltar, regardless of value, where the import is not an exempt consignment

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

GCT zero-rating applies to the export of recycled scrap copper wire and other shredded non-ferrous scrap from Gibraltar where (i) the supply is made to a customer outside Gibraltar, (ii) the goods physically leave Gibraltar to a destination outside the customs/consumption territory of Gibraltar, (iii) removal occurs within three months of the supply date, and (iv) the exporter retains the complete customs export set. The supplier may also zero-rate qualifying offshore services supplied to a non-resident business under cross-border service rules.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

No real-time fiscal clearance, API-linked clearance, or digital stamping mandate is currently in force in Gibraltar. GCT invoices must be issued in a standard format within 30 days and retained for six years. Quarterly GCT returns and payment summaries are filed electronically through HM Customs' online portal; B2B e-reporting obligations may arrive only through future EU/UK digital reporting alignment.

HM Customs applies vigorous trade-based audits to GCT zero-rating claims on scrap metal consignments. If the documentary evidence is incomplete, unverified, or inconsistent with the physical shipment, the zero-rating benefit is clawed back together with accrued interest; fixed penalties may reach 200% of the tax understated for deliberate or fraudulent conduct. Criminal prosecution is available for misdeclaration of scrap weight, grade, export destination, or forged inward remittance evidence, with personal liability extending to directors and compliance officers of the exempted company.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

UK and Gibraltar are fully convertible on both current and capital account. No exchange-control permit is required for capital injection, profit remittance, dividend distribution, or trade settlement. The Gibraltar pound is pegged at parity with GBP. Remittance hurdles are therefore not exchange-control based, but arise from GFSC AML/KYC obligations, economic substance review, and documentary proof of the underlying trade or corporate purpose.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Neither Gibraltar, the United Kingdom, nor Bermuda imposes any statutory timeline, mandatory conversion ratio, or surrender obligation on export proceeds. The Bermuda Exempted Company may retain proceeds in any freely convertible currency. Banks may nonetheless require export contracts, customs declarations, invoices, and inspection certificates to substantiate the source of funds for compliance purposes.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (United Kingdom) is the sovereign central-monetary anchor for the sterling zone; Gibraltar does not maintain a domestic central bank or exchange-control authority. The local prudential and licensing authority is the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

High for operational liquidity and treasury pooling, but Medium for Documentary LC and large trade escrow because an EMI is not a licensed credit institution and cannot issue or confirm letters of credit. Legal constraints require the offshore EMI to be authorised under UK FCA regulations or GFSC licensing, with segregated client funds and no protected deposit guarantee. Scrap-copper trading flows will attract enhanced due diligence, so only established EMIs with clear UBO governance and sanctions screening are viable.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 5,000,000 for the Local File (annual value of Gibraltar controlled-party transactions) and GIP 1,500,000,000 for the Master File (worldwide group turnover, aligned with the OECD/EU documentation thresholds). Even below these thresholds, the Gibraltar Income Tax Office may require contemporaneous TP documentation where connected-party pricing materially affects the Gibraltar tax base.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar applies the Economic Substance (Companies) Regulations and the Gibraltar Economic Substance Office regime. Trading, distribution, and service-centre activities, including cross-border consignment trading of recycled scrap metal, are relevant activities. A Gibraltar-resident company must maintain adequate premises, full-time employees, appropriate expenditure, and core income-generating activities in Gibraltar, and must file an annual ESR return with CIGA/GESO. A non-resident Bermuda Exempted Company must demonstrate the absence of Gibraltar management and control; if board decisions, trading contracts, stock management, or bank sign-off authority are exercised in Gibraltar, the company will be reclassified as resident, subjected to ESR, and taxed at 12.5%.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Return

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of up to 200% of the tax underpaid, interest at 8% per annum, unlimited fine upon criminal conviction, and imprisonment of up to 7 years under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act and Crimes Act.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false returns or cause the company to evade tax are jointly and severally liable for the corporate tax deficiency, penalties, and investigation costs. Courts may impose travel bans, asset-freezing orders, director disqualification of up to 15 years, and imprisonment.
Shell Company / Sham Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Reclassification as a Gibraltar-resident company, disallowance of the claimed non-resident tax treatment, strike-off or administrative dissolution, and a penalty of up to GIP 250,000 for ESR non-compliance.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for the unpaid corporate tax and false substance declarations. The Gibraltar court may order a public examination, impose travel bans, and issue custodial sentences for contempt or fraudulent evasion connected to sham arrangements.
AML / Money Laundering in Scrap-Metal Trade

Statutory sanction: Freezing and confiscation orders under the Proceeds of Crime Law 2015, revocation of banking/EMI facilities, regulatory fines of up to GIP 1,000,000 or 5% of turnover, and criminal prosecution with imprisonment of up to 14 years.

Director personal liability: Directors, officers, and the nominated Money Laundering Reporting Officer face individual criminal liability, personal fines, disgorgement of ill-gotten gains, travel restrictions, and imprisonment. Beneficial owners are identified through the Gibraltar beneficial ownership register and may be named in confiscation proceedings.
Transfer Pricing and Thin Capitalisation

Statutory sanction: Transfer pricing adjustment to taxable income, disallowance of deductions, penalty of up to 100% of the additional tax liability, interest accrual at 8% per annum, and potential denial of relief under the Gibraltar-UK treaty where substance is absent.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly approve artificial related-party pricing or fail to maintain required TP documentation are jointly and severally liable for the adjusted tax and penalties. Courts may make director compensation orders, impose disqualification, and issue travel bans pending satisfaction of the liability.
