Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (portal: gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax); Commissioner of Customs and Excise, Customs & Excise Department for GST (portal: gibraltar.gov.gi/customs); Companies House Gibraltar (companieshouse.gi) for foreign company registry filing.

E-government filing portal

For the Bermuda Exempted Company trading through Gibraltar: (1) Register the foreign company with the Gibraltar Companies Registry under the Companies Act 2014, filing the certified Bermuda certificate of incorporation, memorandum/articles, directors and shareholders register, and a Gibraltar registered office address, to obtain the Gibraltar foreign company number; (2) Apply to the Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TIN) using the corporate registration form, disclosing central management and control location and expected Gibraltar-source income; (3) File Form GST1 with the Customs & Excise Department for GST registration; a non-established company must appoint a Gibraltar-resident GST fiscal representative with joint and several liability; (4) Obtain the GST registration certificate, update the Companies Registry record, and open a Gibraltar banking account to receive SWIFT remittances. Timeline: 5-10 working days; GST registration is effective before the first taxable supply.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation; Territorial_Scope; Tax_Exempt_Zone (only at Bermuda level). The Bermuda Exempted Company is a separate taxable body corporate, not pass-through eligible. Under Bermuda law it enjoys exemption from Bermuda future taxes to 2035, but in Gibraltar it is taxed on a territorial basis: chargeable to 12.5% corporation tax only on Gibraltar-source profits or profits attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment. If central management and control remains outside Gibraltar and no Gibraltar PE exists, offshore wholesale trading income from SSD/CPU chips is outside Gibraltar income tax. — For the Bermuda Exempted Company trading through Gibraltar: (1) Register the foreign company with the Gibraltar Companies Registry under the Companies Act 2014, filing the certified Bermuda certificate of incorporation, memorandum/articles, directors and shareholders register, and a Gibraltar registered office address, to obtain the Gibraltar foreign company number; (2) Apply to the Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TIN) using the corporate registration form, disclosing central management and control location and expected Gibraltar-source income; (3) File Form GST1 with the Customs & Excise Department for GST registration; a non-established company must appoint a Gibraltar-resident GST fiscal representative with joint and several liability; (4) Obtain the GST registration certificate, update the Companies Registry record, and open a Gibraltar banking account to receive SWIFT remittances. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; Territorial_Scope; Tax_Exempt_Zone (only at Bermuda level). The Bermuda Exempted Company is a separate taxable body corporate, not pass-through eligible. Under Bermuda law it enjoys exemption from Bermuda future taxes to 2035, but in Gibraltar it is taxed on a territorial basis: chargeable to 12.5% corporation tax only on Gibraltar-source profits or profits attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment. If central management and control remains outside Gibraltar and no Gibraltar PE exists, offshore wholesale trading income from SSD/CPU chips is outside Gibraltar income tax.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists under Gibraltar law. Available elections: (a) certified holding company election under the Income Tax Act 2010 to exempt qualifying dividends and substantial-shareholding capital gains; (b) written election to the Commissioner of Income Tax to be treated as non-resident where central management and control is outside Gibraltar; (c) GST group registration election for connected companies to file as a single unit; (d) election of GST quarterly/annual accounting periods. Elections are made on prescribed forms and bind the taxpayer.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GIP 100,000 (annual taxable turnover, rolling 12-month test) for businesses established in Gibraltar; no threshold for non-established businesses, which must register before the first taxable supply; voluntary registration permitted below the threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating of 0% applies to supplies of goods (SSD drives, server processors) exported by the supplier from Gibraltar to a non-resident customer where title and risk pass to the buyer and the goods physically depart Gibraltar. Related brokerage/commission services are zero-rated only when supplied to a non-resident customer and directly linked to the exported goods; freight/logistics services may also be zero-rated under place-of-supply rules. The supply must be evidenced on a zero-rated tax invoice; no export licence is required for these goods where dual-use and export-control clearances are separately satisfied.

  • Gibraltar customs export declaration (automated export system acceptance) evidencing physical departure from Gibraltar
  • Zero-rated commercial tax invoice with supplier GST registration number and the annotation 'Zero-rated export supply'
  • Bill of lading or air waybill naming Gibraltar as port/airport of loading and the foreign destination
  • Packing list
  • Foreign buyer purchase order or supply contract with overseas delivery address
  • Freight forwarder or port authority certificate of actual export
  • SWIFT MT103 / Inward Remittance Certificate from the Gibraltar receiving bank evidencing FX receipt of export proceeds
  • Warehouse release or delivery confirmation proving transfer of title to the non-resident buyer

No real-time fiscal clearance, API clearance, or digital stamping mandate applies. GST invoices must be issued within 30 days of the tax point, sequentially numbered, and display the GST registration number, description, quantity, tax point date, and either the GST charged at 12.5% or a zero-rating annotation for exports. E-invoicing is permitted subject to authenticity and integrity controls. GST returns are submitted electronically via the Customs & Excise online portal on an elected quarterly or annual basis; no SAF-T or XML exchange format is required.

The Customs & Excise Department applies post-clearance risk audits to export zero-rating claims, especially in high-frequency semiconductor brokerage. If supporting documentation is missing or false, the zero-rating is reversed and the full 12.5% GST is clawed back from the tax point date with statutory interest, plus civil penalties up to 100% of tax evaded; the GST fiscal representative is jointly and severally liable. Fraudulent evasion is a criminal offence under the Crimes Act 2011, punishable by imprisonment up to 7 years and unlimited fines, with asset forfeiture under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015. Failure to repatriate export proceeds within 180 days is treated by the authorities as an incomplete export, triggering full de-zero-rating, penalty assessments, and enhanced AML/CTF review of the Gibraltar banking relationship; directors may be subject to disqualification proceedings.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full and unrestricted convertibility. Gibraltar applies no exchange-control laws to current-account trade transactions or capital-account movements; GIP is freely redeemable into GBP at par, and operating accounts may be held in USD, EUR, GIP, or GBP without regulatory approval. Profits and capital may be remitted from a Gibraltar-registered branch/subsidiary to the Bermuda parent without prior central-bank clearance, subject only to distributable-reserve requirements under the Gibraltar Companies Act 2014 and to bank-level AML/FATCA/CRS screening. Bermuda exempted companies enjoy BMA General Permission to maintain USD accounts and transfer funds globally, so profit repatriation and capital injections encounter no statutory hurdle. No withholding taxes apply on outbound interest, dividends, or royalties in either Gibraltar or Bermuda.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Statutory surrender timeline: None. Neither Gibraltar nor Bermuda imposes any deadline for the repatriation or surrender of export proceeds. Mandatory conversion ratio: 0% — the wholesale SSD/CPU trading entity may retain 100% of USD receipts in its Gibraltar operational account indefinitely. Practical bank-side duties are limited to GFSC Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism Regulations 2019 reporting (large-cash and suspicious-transaction reports), CRS/FATCA automatic information exchange, and export-control scrutiny for dual-use semiconductor goods; these are transparency obligations, not currency-surrender requirements.

Central bank supervision: Financial Services Commission (Gibraltar) (GFSC) is the banking and foreign-exchange supervisor; the Bank of England (United Kingdom) is the monetary anchor because the Gibraltar pound (GIP) is issued by the Government of Gibraltar at a fixed 1:1 parity with GBP. For the Bermuda Exempted Company parent, the Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) administers the Exchange Control Act 1972 and grants automatic exchange-control permission for non-resident USD treasury operations.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Limited-Medium feasibility. GFSC/UK-regulated digital EMIs (e.g., Wise Business, Revolut Business, Payoneer) allow fully remote onboarding for operational float in USD/GBP and are useful for small supplier payments and FX conversion. They are NOT viable for: (i) capital escrow or escrow-inspection wires (EMI safeguarding is not equivalent to bank balance-sheet protection; escrow accounts require a licensed bank/trustee), (ii) commercial letters of credit or documentary collections (EMIs cannot issue MT700/MT754), and (iii) high-value trade settlements above approximately USD 250,000 due to correspondent-bank routing constraints. Legal constraint: post-Brexit, a UK FCA-authorised EMI cannot passport payment services into Gibraltar; a GFSC payment-services licence (or a Gibraltar-licensed branch) is required for inbound EUR/GBP payment acceptance in Gibraltar. Many digital EMIs also auto-decline ISIC 4651 semiconductor-brokerage profiles under internal dual-use/export-control risk policies, so a written end-use compliance statement and a minimum-volume commitment are required to avoid closure.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: Bermuda parent treasury board resolution authorizes a USD capital contribution via SWIFT MT103 to the Gibraltar operating account (USD). MT103 field 70 must state 'EQUITY CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION / SHARE PREMIUM' (or 'SUBORDINATED SHAREHOLDER LOAN — IDA' if intragroup debt). Beneficiary must be the exact account title at the Gibraltar bank; the remitter is the Bermuda Exempted Company holding BMA General Permission. Pre-funding verification: notarized board resolutions of both entities, audited Bermuda treasury financials, source-of-wealth report, and confirmation of the Gibraltar entity's CRS/FATCA status as an Active NFE (non-financial entity) to avoid automatic-exchange opacity. The receiving Gibraltar bank will validate the purpose code against the corporate object of ISIC 4651 wholesale trading and may require a post-injection funds-utilisation report; no GFSC exchange-control approval is required.Tiered clearing-rail stack: (1) SWIFT MT103 (bank-to-bank telegraphic transfer) for spot SSD/CPU purchases below USD 500,000, each referencing the invoice and purchase-order number to preserve the audit trail; (2) Commercial Letter of Credit issued by the Gibraltar bank via SWIFT MT700 and confirmed by a London/New York top-tier bank for batch allocations of server processors, payable at sight against commercial invoice, packing list, airway bill/Bill of Lading, beneficiary certificate, and independent inspection certificate (SGS/TUV); (3) Escrow-Inspection Wire: buyer's funds are remitted to a GFSC/FCA-licensed escrow agent's segregated collection account; the escrow release instruction is triggered by the inspection certificate and the buyer's written acceptance, then wired to the supplier via MT103; (4) Nostro/Vostro clearing mechanics: Gibraltar USD accounts clear through correspondent accounts at JPMorgan Chase and Bank of New York Mellon in New York, while GIP/GBP clear at par through the UK interbank settlement system; any correspondent bank must use MT202 COV to guarantee the originator/beneficiary trail under FATF Recommendation 16. For trades settled in RMB with Chinese foundry suppliers, use the offshore CNH market via a clearing bank in Hong Kong, not onshore CNY rails.Deliverable Outright FX Forwards — onshore GIP/USD and GBP/USD forwards with GFSC-licensed bank counterparties under standard ISDA/CSA master agreements.FX Swaps — spot/forward rolling structures used to match recurring USD inventory purchases and quarterly repatriation cycles to the Bermuda parent.Cross-Currency Swaps — USD/GBP (or USD/GIP) fixed-for-floating swaps hedging long-term intragroup financing between the Bermuda treasury parent and the Gibraltar operating entity, including principal exchange at maturity.Non-Deliverable Forwards — permitted exclusively for USD/CNH or USD/CNY hedges to cover Renminbi-denominated supplier costs from Taiwanese/Korean/Chinese foundry channels; GIP/GBP NDFs are unnecessary and not market-standard because GIP is pegged at par with GBP and fully deliverable.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Limited-Medium feasibility. GFSC/UK-regulated digital EMIs (e.g., Wise Business, Revolut Business, Payoneer) allow fully remote onboarding for operational float in USD/GBP and are useful for small supplier payments and FX conversion. They are NOT viable for: (i) capital escrow or escrow-inspection wires (EMI safeguarding is not equivalent to bank balance-sheet protection; escrow accounts require a licensed bank/trustee), (ii) commercial letters of credit or documentary collections (EMIs cannot issue MT700/MT754), and (iii) high-value trade settlements above approximately USD 250,000 due to correspondent-bank routing constraints. Legal constraint: post-Brexit, a UK FCA-authorised EMI cannot passport payment services into Gibraltar; a GFSC payment-services licence (or a Gibraltar-licensed branch) is required for inbound EUR/GBP payment acceptance in Gibraltar. Many digital EMIs also auto-decline ISIC 4651 semiconductor-brokerage profiles under internal dual-use/export-control risk policies, so a written end-use compliance statement and a minimum-volume commitment are required to avoid closure.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 5,000,000 in annual related-party turnover triggers mandatory Local File preparation; Master File required for groups with consolidated worldwide turnover exceeding GIP 50,000,000

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance (Companies and Partnerships) Act 2019 applies to resident companies conducting relevant activities, including wholesale and distribution of semiconductors. The entity must demonstrate management and control, staff, premises and equipment in Gibraltar commensurate with its income; failure to meet substance tests may result in penalties and forced dissolution.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Declarations

Statutory sanction: Upon conviction, a fine of up to GIP 50,000 and additional tax equal to 200% of the underpaid tax may be imposed; aggravated cases may lead to forfeiture of assets.

Director personal liability: Directors or shadow directors who knowingly participate in fraudulent evasion become personally liable for the company's unpaid taxes and fines; they may face imprisonment for up to 7 years and a criminal restitution order requiring repayment of all evaded sums.
Shell Company Without Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The Gibraltar Economic Substance Act provides for financial penalties from GIP 10,000 to GIP 100,000; persistent failure subjects the company to strike-off and deregistration; revenue authorities may recharacterise profits and deny treaty benefits.

Director personal liability: Directors may be fined individually up to GIP 50,000 and disqualified from serving as directors for up to 10 years; where the shell arrangement is used for abusive tax purposes, the directors could be prosecuted for fraudulent evasion and face custodial sentences.
Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Violations

Statutory sanction: Unlicensed money service activity or AML compliance breaches carry fines up to GIP 250,000 per violation; failure to report suspicious transactions can lead to asset freezing orders and criminal confiscation.

Director personal liability: Directors can be held personally criminally liable for knowingly permitting AML breaches, with imprisonment up to 5 years and seizure of personal assets equivalent to the laundered amount; travel bans may be imposed pending investigation.
