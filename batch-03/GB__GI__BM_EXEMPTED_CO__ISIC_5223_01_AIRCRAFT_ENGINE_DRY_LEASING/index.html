Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

      
Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

    

    

      
Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
Gibraltar Tax & Customs Duty Department, HM Government of Gibraltar, comprising the Commissioner of Income Tax and the Commissioner of Customs & Excise; official registration and filing portal: https://www.gibraltartax.gov.gi; companies registry portal: https://www.egov.gi.

        
E-government filing portal
For a Bermuda Exempted Company (the 'Bermuda Co' or 'Exempted Co') establishing a taxable or registrable presence in Gibraltar: (1) register the Bermuda Co as an overseas company with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under Section 428 of the Gibraltar Companies Act 2014, including appointment of a Gibraltar registered office and a resident process agent; (2) obtain a Gibraltar company registration number and a 'Local Business Notification' certificate if a physical office is opened; (3) apply to the Income Tax Office for a Tax Reference Number (TRN) by submitting the prescribed company tax registration form together with the Bermuda Certificate of Incorporation, certificate of exempted status, constitutional documents, and a written declaration as to whether central management and control is or will be exercised from Gibraltar; (4) register for GST with HM Customs and Excise using the GST registration form (GST/APP) where expected annual taxable supplies in Gibraltar exceed the statutory threshold or where input VAT recovery is intended; (5) if employees are located in Gibraltar, register as an employer for PAYE social insurance purposes; (6) receive the Gibraltar TIN (TRN) and a separate GST registration number. No statutory financial licence is required for aircraft engine dry leasing in Gibraltar.

No statutory financial licence is required for aircraft engine dry leasing in Gibraltar.

    

    

      
Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Tax_Exempt_Zone (as a Bermuda Exempted Company). For Gibraltar tax purposes, the classification is not a blanket exemption; the entity is treated as a non-resident company subject to territorial taxation on Gibraltar-source income only, unless central management and control is found in Gibraltar, in which case entity-level worldwide taxation would apply at the prevailing Gibraltar corporate rate.

        
Available election forms
Gibraltar does not operate a U.S.-style check-the-box election. The Bermuda Exempted Company's tax-exempt status in Bermuda has no direct legal effect in Gibraltar. Available elections include: (a) an election for a non-resident company not trading in Gibraltar to be assessed on a deemed basis under the Income Tax Act where applicable; (b) a group company election allowing a consolidated return at the discretion of the Commissioner; (c) GST elections such as the annual accounting scheme and cash accounting scheme for eligible small businesses; and (d) limited election mechanisms under the Income Tax (Hybrid Mismatch Rules) Regulations 2020 for aligning accounting periods and applying defensive adjustments where hybrid financial instruments or hybrid entities are present in the structure. All elections must be made in writing to the Commissioner before the statutory return deadline.

      

    

    

      
VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

      

        
Standard VAT / GST rate
12.5%

        
Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)
£85,000 (GBP) annual value of taxable supplies made in Gibraltar; supplies that are exported or otherwise outside the scope of Gibraltar GST are disregarded for the threshold. A person making taxable supplies in Gibraltar must register for GST if the threshold is exceeded or if the person expects to exceed it within the next 30 days.

      

      
      

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
A dry lease of a turbofan aircraft engine may be zero-rated or treated as outside the scope of Gibraltar GST where: (a) the engine is leased to a lessee that belongs in a jurisdiction outside Gibraltar and is not a Gibraltar-established recipient for GST purposes; (b) the supply is an export of goods from Gibraltar, evidenced by a valid customs export declaration and movement out of Gibraltar; (c) the supply is a service the place of supply of which is outside Gibraltar under the B2B place-of-supply rules (i.e., the customer belongs in a third country), with no effective use and enjoyment in Gibraltar; (d) the lease is directly related to or for international transport operations and the engine is not operated in Gibraltar airspace or stationed in Gibraltar; and (e) the lessor holds contemporaneous commercial evidence that the engine was received abroad and remained abroad. The zero-rating claim is available to a GST-registered Bermuda Exempted Company only where input VAT recovery is being claimed in Gibraltar; otherwise the supply is simply outside the scope of Gibraltar GST.

Gibraltar does not impose a real-time fiscal clearance, mandatory e-invoicing network, continuous transaction control, or digital stamping regime. Taxpayers must issue a valid tax invoice, in paper or electronic form, containing prescribed particulars: invoice number, date, supplier name and GST registration number, recipient name and GST registration number (where applicable), description and quantity of goods or services supplied, the amount payable, the GST rate, and the GST amount charged. Filing is periodic under self-assessment; customs exports of goods are cleared through a Single Administrative Document (SAD) using standard customs procedures without API-based integration into accounting systems.

      
HM Customs can open a post-clearance audit for up to six years from the end of the relevant accounting period. Where the zero-rating or outside-scope treatment is disallowed due to insufficient or fraudulent evidence, the input GST claimed in respect of that export is clawed back, interest is charged at the statutory rate, and a civil penalty of up to 100% of the tax evaded may be imposed. Knowingly fraudulent claims, including false declarations of foreign use or false bills of lading, attract additional penalties of at least £250 per invoice, summary conviction fines up to £10,000 per offence, and possible imprisonment for up to two years. Retrospective clawbacks may also be accompanied by a disallowance of aircraft lease expense deductions in the hands of any Gibraltar resident user, and participation in a hybrid mismatch arrangement involving the Bermuda tax-exempt entity may give rise to separate adjustments under the Gibraltar Income Tax (Hybrid Mismatch Rules) Regulations 2020.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

      
Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
Gibraltar applies no exchange-control restrictions. The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is pegged at 1:1 to GBP and fully convertible; free market convertibility exists for GBP, EUR, USD and all major currencies. Capital account and current account transactions, including dividends, management fees, lease rentals, and liquidation proceeds, can be freely remitted without central bank approval, subject only to AML/CFT reporting and sanctions screening.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
There is no statutory surrender requirement in Gibraltar. All export or cross-border service proceeds, including aircraft engine dry-lease rental streams, may be retained in the currency of receipt; mandatory conversion ratios do not exist. Entities may denominate lease agreements in USD/EUR/GBP and hold any foreign currency without being required to repatriate or exchange proceeds.

      

      
Central bank supervision: Bank of England is the ultimate monetary anchor for the Gibraltar pound; the Government of Gibraltar's Currency Authority acts as issuing and exchange-control administrator, while the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission supervises banks and FX firms.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

      
Master / Local File threshold: Local File mandatory where aggregate related-party transactions exceed GBP 10,000,000 in the accounting period; Master File mandatory where group consolidated revenue exceeds GBP 500,000,000; a concise TP self-assessment record must be retained regardless of threshold

      
Economic substance scope: Applicable. Dry-leasing of aircraft engines falls within 'Finance and Leasing' relevant activity. Under Bermuda's Economic Substance (Amendment) Act and Gibraltar's Economic Substance Regulations, the exempted company must evidence in the relevant tax-resident jurisdiction: local board meetings, local strategic decision-making, adequate physical premises, proportionate staff and local expenditure. An annual economic substance return must be filed; failure can trigger revocation of exempt status and penalties up to BMD/GIP 250,000, plus director disqualification

      

    

    

      
Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

      
      

        Tax Evasion via False Nil Return
        
Statutory sanction: Additional assessment to tax at 12.5%, penalty up to 100% of undercharged tax, criminal fine up to GBP 500,000, forfeiture of lease income under proceeds of crime legislation

        
Director personal liability: Directors who signed or authorised the false return are personally liable for civil restitution; travel bans and passport surrender can be imposed; custodial sentence up to seven years

      

      
      

        Shell Company / No Economic Substance
        
Statutory sanction: Revocation of tax-exempt certificate, striking-off under the Gibraltar Companies Act, deemed profit assessments, daily penalties, and an adverse regulatory finding causing de-risking by banks and escrow providers

        
Director personal liability: Directors may be jointly and severally liable for all tax and penalties arising after corporate veil-piercing; disqualification for up to ten years; travel restrictions and compulsory attendance before the tax authority

      

      
      

        Transfer Pricing Mispricing
        
Statutory sanction: Arm's-length adjustment to taxable income, interest on unpaid tax, penalty of 40 percent of the adjusted amount for deliberate mispricing, and penalty of up to 100 percent where documentation is fabricated

        
Director personal liability: Directors knowingly approving the transfer pricing policy are jointly and severally liable for the additional tax and harsher penalties; civil recovery may attach to personal assets outside Gibraltar

      

      
      

        AML and Beneficial Ownership Evasion
        
Statutory sanction: Fines up to GBP 100,000 per breach under Gibraltar AML/PoC legislation, confiscation orders, revocation of the corporate services and EMI banking relationship, and criminal money-laundering charges carrying fourteen years' imprisonment

        
Director personal liability: The Money Laundering Reporting Officer and directors are personally exposed where wilful blindness is proven; travel bans, extradition, and civil liability to compensate victims may follow

      

      
      

        Treaty Abuse and False Residence Claims
        
Statutory sanction: Treaty benefits denied, retroactive withholding applied at up to 20%, principal-purpose-test penalty up to 50% of gross payment, and referral to the Joint International Tax Compliance and Enforcement authorities

        
Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners who sign false certificates may be prosecuted for perjury; they are personally liable to indemnify the payer for all withheld and unpaid tax; passport seizure and imprisonment up to seven years are available
