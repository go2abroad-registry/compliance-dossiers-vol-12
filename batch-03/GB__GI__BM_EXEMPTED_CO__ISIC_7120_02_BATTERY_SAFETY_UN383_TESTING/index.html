Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar; portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity, including a Bermuda Exempted Company, that establishes a branch or begins taxable activity in Gibraltar must apply to the Income Tax Office within 30 days of commencement using the Foreign Company Registration Application. The applicant must submit the Bermuda certificate of incorporation, certificate of exemption, memorandum and articles of association, director and shareholder lists, and a Gibraltar registered office or branch address. Upon approval, the Income Tax Office issues a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (Gibraltar TIN) in electronic form; a separate exemption notification may be filed for companies claiming qualifying or tax-exempt status under the Income Tax Ordinance.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign entity, including a Bermuda Exempted Company, that establishes a branch or begins taxable activity in Gibraltar must apply to the Income Tax Office within 30 days of commencement using the Foreign Company Registration Application. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not have a check-the-box election. Eligible companies may elect to be treated as a Qualifying Company under the Gibraltar Income Tax Ordinance, which permits certain income to be taxed at 0%, subject to economic substance requirements. Companies may also elect application of the International Participation Exemptions for capital gains and dividends. Hybrid mismatch outcomes are addressed under Gibraltar's implementation of OECD BEPS rules through the Taxes (Base Erosion – Hybrid Mismatch) Rules, with no elective hybrid mismatch regime comparable to US or UK check-the-box.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or general GST; indirect taxation comprises Gibraltar customs import duties, excise duties on goods entering Gibraltar, and no sales/use tax

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable for VAT/GST; any person importing or exporting goods from Gibraltar must register with the Gibraltar Customs Department regardless of turnover (no minimum threshold). Registration is conducted through the Gibraltar Customs Electronic System and the UK-Gibraltar Customs Declaration Service.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar levies no VAT, therefore a VAT zero-rating regime does not apply. For customs purposes, goods that are physically exported from Gibraltar to a non-Gibraltar destination are relieved of import duties and excise duties, provided the exporter proves legal removal from Gibraltar through an accepted export declaration. Offshore services supplied by a Bermuda Exempted Company from Gibraltar, without local economic activity, remain outside the scope of Gibraltar taxation under the territorial tax framework.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document via CDS, including Movement Reference Number)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (carrier-validated proof of physical export)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank transfer record evidencing repatriation of export sale proceeds
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Dangerous Goods Declaration and UN38.3 Battery Test Summary Certificate

Electronic customs declarations are mandatory via the UK-Gibraltar Customs Declaration Service (CDS) for import and export clearance. There is no real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or API stamping regime for invoices. E-invoicing is not statutorily required; standard commercial invoicing is accepted, subject to customs record-keeping requirements.

False or unsupported export declarations, misdescription of goods, failure to substantiate physical export, or abuse of duty relief may be treated as customs fraud under the Customs Ordinance 2015. Penalties include forfeiture of goods, denial of export relief, retrospective clawback of duty relief granted within a six-year audit window, monetary penalties up to three times the value of evaded duties, fines up to GIP 10,000 per false declaration, and imprisonment of up to seven years for serious criminal cases.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar pound is pegged at par to GBP and is freely convertible. There are no currency controls on current account transactions, trade related settlements, or profit remittance. Capital account movements are unrestricted but are subject to standard AML/CTF, FATCA, CRS and economic substance documentary checks. A Bermuda Exempted Company resident for tax purposes in Gibraltar may remit test laboratory profits to its direct shareholders via intercompany loan repayment, dividend, or management fee without exchange control approval or withholding tax.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender of export proceeds applies in Gibraltar. There is no statutory timeline or required conversion ratio. Export receipts arising from battery safety testing and UN38.3 certification work may be retained in any convertible currency (GBP, GIP, USD, EUR) in an operational account, or partially converted to hedge currency exposure, subject only to bank-specific internal reporting duties and suspicious transaction reporting.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England / HM Treasury (monetary authority for the Gibraltar pound peg); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the competent banking and FX regulatory authority under the Banking Act 2019 and the Financial Services Act 2019.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-HIGH. Gibraltar-licensed E-Money Institutions (EMIs) and UK FCA-authorized payment institutions are viable rails for cross-border laboratory fee collection and procurement settlement, provided they hold permission to serve B2B hardware safety testing entities. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise or Airwallex are operationally feasible for inbound receipts and outbound vendor payments; however, their legal viability under Gibraltar payment services regulation depends on a demonstrable KYC/EDD record, transactional flow transparency, and the avoidance of any 'pass-through' treatment that would render the EMI a bank without a Gibraltar deposit-taking license. The Gibraltar entity should maintain at least one licensed relationship bank in the tier1 list alongside any EMI to ensure clearing continuity.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity and capital contributions from the Bermuda exempted parent must be sent via SWIFT MT103 or MT202 from the parent bank to the Gibraltar operational account, using the purpose code designated by the receiving bank for direct capital injection (commonly cited as 'CAPI-INJ' or 'GFSC-CAPITAL-LAB'). The physical remittance must be accompanied by a board resolution, updated share register, written capital contribution schedule, and a signed declaration confirming no third-party funds are commingled. The reference line must read: 'Capital Contribution - GB__GI__BM_EXEMPTED_CO__ISIC_7120_02_BATTERY_SAFETY_UN383_TESTING' to support GFSC scrutiny and to avoid misclassification as trading income.Trade disbursements for test chamber procurement, calibration services, laboratory consumables, and external certification fees should be settled through the correspondent banking network using SWIFT MT103 direct credit transfers, and for large repeatable supplies, a commercial letter of credit (LC payable against presentation of calibrated equipment delivery certificate). For payments to UK and EEA vendors, CHAPS/SEPA via a Gibraltar bank's indirect participation through UK clearers is acceptable. For hazardous goods logistics and equipment customs clearance, the payment instruction must include the invoice number, HS code reference, and the laboratory's consistency code to satisfy trade compliance and anti-proliferation controls.
