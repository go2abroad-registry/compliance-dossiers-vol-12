Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not provide a US-style check-the-box election for classifying a foreign or domestic entity. VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or general sales and use tax in Gibraltar. Import duties, customs charges, and other product-specific charges may apply.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable: Gibraltar has no VAT/GST registration threshold or general sales-tax registration regime. Income-tax registration and liability are separate and do not depend on a VAT turnover threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in Gibraltar: there is no VAT system and therefore no export zero-rating, input VAT recovery, or VAT refund regime. Export treatment for customs purposes depends on the goods, destination, applicable customs arrangements, and the exporter’s legal establishment. The manufacturing activity may separately require food-safety, product, customs, and other regulatory approvals.

  • Export declaration or other customs export evidence required for the relevant customs territory and route; Gibraltar does not prescribe a VAT zero-rating document bundle.
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and transport evidence such as a bill of lading or airway bill, as applicable to the transaction and customs procedure.
  • Proof of payment or inward-remittance evidence only where required for accounting, banking, sanctions, or other applicable compliance purposes; no Gibraltar export-VAT rule requires an Inward Remittance Certificate.

No Gibraltar VAT real-time clearance, fiscal-stamping, or VAT e-invoicing mandate applies. Ordinary commercial, accounting, customs, and record-retention requirements remain relevant. No Gibraltar VAT API registration is available or required.

No Gibraltar export-VAT zero-rating claims or associated VAT clawbacks exist. False customs declarations, evasion of applicable customs duties, or inaccurate income-tax reporting may result in assessments, interest, civil penalties, or prosecution under the applicable Gibraltar legislation. No general Gibraltar foreign-exchange repatriation deadline applies.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The United Kingdom and Gibraltar maintain full current and capital account convertibility under Articles VIII and XIV of the IMF Articles of Agreement. No exchange controls impede profit remittances, dividend distributions, partnership withdrawals, or the conversion of retained earnings. The Gibraltar pound is freely exchangeable with pound sterling at a fixed 1:1 rate, and foreign-currency bank accounts are available without prior regulatory approval. Capital account transactions such as partner capital injections, loan subordination, and equity buybacks are not subject to central bank licensing, but must satisfy source-of-funds and economic-substance documentation standards under anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing (AML/CTF) legislation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory surrender requirement for export proceeds in the United Kingdom or Gibraltar. Exporters may retain receivables in any convertible currency, maintain offshore nostro/vostro accounts, and remit funds back to Gibraltar without a mandatory timeline or conversion ratio. Failing to repatriate proceeds, however, may be scrutinised by HMRC and the GFSC for transfer-pricing and substance purposes. For an Exempted Limited Partnership, any foreign-currency receipts should be documented in audited or management financial statements and test clearly to commercial transactions in retort rice noodle manufacturing and canning.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (United Kingdom) together with HM Treasury; for Gibraltar, the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) regulates banking and financial services, while the Government of Gibraltar issues the Gibraltar pound (GIP) at parity with GBP under the Currency Notes Act. No separate exchange control authority sits in Gibraltar; UK exchange control restrictions have been fully lifted.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low to Medium. Offshore digital EMIs incorporated as Electronic Money Institutions in Lithuania, Ireland, Malta, or the United Kingdom may open sponsor-backed settlement accounts for a Gibraltar ELP, but direct EMI licences issued in Gibraltar are subject to GFSC approval and do not benefit from EEA passporting post-Brexit. Such EMIs are useful for low-value collections and supplier payments through virtual IBANs, but they are not a substitute for a licensed tier-1 Gibraltarian or UK bank when establishing a capital escrow account, confirming commercial letters of credit, or using Swift MT103 clearing rails. Legal constraints include the need to file a beneficial-ownership declaration with the Gibraltar Registry, maintain substance in Gibraltar, and avoid unauthorised deposit-taking activity under the Financial Services Act 2019.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Incoming capital contributions must be wired via SWIFT MT103 from the contributing partner's bank account to the Gibraltar ELP's designated escrow account. The instruction must use a clear payment narrative such as 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION - INSTANT RICE NOODLE MANUFACTURING - PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT [DATE]'. The remitting bank should be instructed to include a purpose-of-payment code reflecting equity subscription or partnership contribution; where the UK BOP system is used, the code 'P32' (other capital transfers) or 'P60' (partner contribution) is acceptable. The receiving bank in Gibraltar will require a completed funds-receipt questionnaire, a copy of the partnership agreement, the beneficial-ownership declaration, and evidence of the source of wealth. All transfers must be cleared through the SWIFT network; the bank may request a notarised source-of-funds certificate prior to releasing funds for working capital disbursements.Clearing rails for trade settlements must prioritise the SWIFT MT103 wire for direct TT payments to suppliers, and SWIFT MT700/MT707 documentary letters of credit for high-value commodity purchases of rice, packaging, and retort canning equipment. For immediate RMB or Southeast Asian settlement, a correspondent bank with offshore CNY clearing capability or a standard SWIFT EUR/GBP leg is preferred. Within Gibraltar or the UK, domestic sterling payments may be cleared using Faster Payments or CHAPS in GBP, while euro-denominated disbursements should use SWIFT via SEPA Instant or regular SEPA Credit Transfer. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) require any tax-resident company carrying on relevant activities (distribution, financing, IP holding, head office, etc.) to maintain premises, full-time staff and direction in Gibraltar. A manufacturing partnership as such is not a prescribed 'relevant activity' unless it operates as a shell that simply holds the noodle-processing IP: then it must demonstrate real R&D and operational substance in Gibraltar. If the ELP is fiscally transparent, individual non-resident partners' ordinary overseas manufacturing does not create Gibraltar ESR exposure, but should the ELP file as an onshore tax resident, substance evidence is mandatory.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Filing

Statutory sanction: Assessed understatement penalty of up to 100% of the additional tax; criminal liability upon indictment to a fine of up to GIP 50,000 and imprisonment for up to seven years, plus confiscation of the pecuniary benefit.

Director personal liability: General partners and any de facto director-managers are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax, interest and penalties; the court may impose travel bans, charging orders over personal assets and a custodial sentence for the controlling mind.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Abuse

Statutory sanction: Re-categorisation of the ELP as a centrally managed-and-controlled company, resulting in an assessment at 10% on the whole profit; additional penalty of GIP 100 per day of non-compliance with the Economic Substance Regulations; deregistration of the partnership may also be ordered.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil will be pierced against the general partner; the partner receives civil restitution orders, a travel ban pending final resolution, and, in the case of unlawful distributions, criminal liability for fraudulent trading with imprisonment up to seven years.
AML / Counter-Financing of Terrorism Breach

Statutory sanction: Statutory fine of up to GIP 2,000,000 or twice the benefit derived; the Financial Services Commission may suspend, restrict or revoke the ELP's banking licence; freeze all bank accounts and appoint a receiver.

Director personal liability: Officers and general partners are personally liable for the monetary penalty; they may be disqualified from managing any Gibraltar limited partnership or company, subjected to confiscation proceedings, and imprisoned for up to seven years.
