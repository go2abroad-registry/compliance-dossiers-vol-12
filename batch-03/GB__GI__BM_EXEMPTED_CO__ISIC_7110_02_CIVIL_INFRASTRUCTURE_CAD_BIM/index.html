Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registrar of Companies, Bermuda — 43 Church Street, Hamilton HM 12, Bermuda (incorporation); Gibraltar Companies Registry, Registrar of Companies, Government of Gibraltar (branch registration).

Governing statute

Bermuda Companies Act 1981, as amended: Part II (Incorporation) and Part III (Exempted Companies), notably ss. 14-20 and s. 18 (exempted company filing, memorandum registration, registered office, curbs on local business); Economic Substance Act 2018 (declaration); Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966, s. 6 (tax guarantee). For the Gibraltar physical branch: Gibraltar Companies Act 2014, Part 19 (Overseas Companies).

Formation / constitutional filings

Bermuda: Memorandum of Association of an Exempted Company filed with the Registrar of Companies, followed by issuance of the Certificate of Incorporation; Gibraltar: Overseas Company — Place of Business Notice under the Companies Act 2014.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a Bermuda Exempted Company; at least one share must be issued and fully paid (par value commonly US$1) and the Memorandum must state an authorized capital (usual minimum US$12,000). No escrow/deposit account is required by Bermuda law. In Gibraltar the overseas branch is likewise subject to no minimum capital.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety: fire alarm and evacuation certificate issued by the Gibraltar Fire and Rescue Service prior to occupation; structural load: approved building regulations sign-off for the office floor (design live load 2.5 kPa typical office); occupational health: risk assessment certified by a Gibraltar-accredited health and safety consultant, employers' liability insurance, and a clean workplace clearance from the Environmental Health Department.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a Bermuda Exempted Company; at least one share must be issued and fully paid (par value commonly US$1) and the Memorandum must state an authorized capital (usual minimum US$12,000). No escrow/deposit account is required by Bermuda law. In Gibraltar the overseas branch is likewise subject to no minimum capital.

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar Taxation Office, Ministry of Finance, Gibraltar; official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation

E-government filing portal

A foreign-incorporated entity, including a Bermuda Exempted Company, that opens a branch or permanent establishment, carries on trade in Gibraltar, or derives Gibraltar-source income must register for Income Tax by filing the prescribed application with the Gibraltar Taxation Office. The filing must include certified constitutional documents, a certificate of good standing or certificate of exemption from Bermuda, particulars of directors, shareholders and ultimate beneficial owners, details of the Gibraltar branch representative or registered address, and a signed declaration of the entity's central management and control location. The Commissioner issues a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN) once the application is verified. Non-resident entities with no Gibraltar permanent establishment and no Gibraltar-source income are not obliged to obtain a TIN and may alternatively request a Certificate of Non-Residence.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign-incorporated entity, including a Bermuda Exempted Company, that opens a branch or permanent establishment, carries on trade in Gibraltar, or derives Gibraltar-source income must register for Income Tax by filing the prescribed application with the Gibraltar Taxation Office. The filing must include certified constitutional documents, a certificate of good standing or certificate of exemption from Bermuda, particulars of directors, shareholders and ultimate beneficial owners, details of the Gibraltar branch representative or registered address, and a signed declaration of the entity's central management and control location. The Commissioner issues a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN) once the application is verified. Non-resident entities with no Gibraltar permanent establishment and no Gibraltar-source income are not obliged to obtain a TIN and may alternatively request a Certificate of Non-Residence.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a US-style check-the-box regime. A Bermuda Exempted Company qualifies for tax-exempt-zone treatment by establishing and maintaining central management and control outside Gibraltar and by having no permanent establishment in Gibraltar. Qualifying Companies and Exempt Companies may nevertheless elect to be assessed under the Income Tax Act 2010 preferential categories where applicable; hybrid mismatch and ATAD anti-avoidance rules apply and may override elective characterisation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None — Gibraltar does not operate VAT or GST; imports of goods are subject to Customs and Excise Duties, and certain local supplies may bear Purchase Duty.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A — no VAT/GST threshold applies. Any person or entity importing goods into Gibraltar must register with the Gibraltar Customs and Excise Department irrespective of annual value.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable because Gibraltar has no VAT/GST. Exported goods fall outside the scope of Gibraltar Customs Duty, and exported services fall outside the scope of Gibraltar Income Tax and Purchase Duty. A supplier of offshore BIM/CAD services may substantiate zero tax treatment by demonstrating that the services are performed on behalf of and delivered to a non-resident recipient, that consideration is received in foreign currency or via international banking channels, and that the entity has no Gibraltar permanent establishment.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Gibraltar electronic export submission or Single Administrative Document) — required for tangible goods only
  • Commercial Invoice and executed Statement of Work for engineering CAD/BIM services
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill or electronic proof of delivery — for tangible goods only
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 credit evidence showing receipt of export proceeds
  • Certificate of Non-Residence or a technical memorandum confirming the absence of a Gibraltar permanent establishment

No real-time fiscal clearance, electronic invoicing mandate, or digital stamping regime applies in Gibraltar. Customs declarations must be submitted electronically through the Gibraltar Customs electronic data interchange system; services exports require no fiscal clearance.

Gibraltar imposes penalties for fraudulent customs declarations or materially false export substantiation, including fines of up to GBP 50,000 per declaration, forfeiture of goods, and criminal liability for directors or service providers. Where a purported export transaction is recharacterised as a domestic supply or as generating Gibraltar-source income, the general anti-abuse rule allows the Taxation Office to claw back unpaid tax plus interest and a surcharge of up to 100%; assessments may be issued retrospectively for a six-year period, extended to ten years in cases of deliberate non-disclosure.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility on both current and capital accounts. The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is pegged at par to pound sterling and is redeemable at parity with UK sterling. There are no exchange controls restricting current-account trade settlement, dividend distributions, loan repayments, or capital repatriation for a Bermuda Exempted Company operated from Gibraltar. Profit remittance hurdles are limited to normal corporate governance and tax-residency evidence; no Gibraltar withholding tax applies to non-resident shareholders on dividends or royalties, provided no Gibraltar-source income is attributed to a Gibraltar permanent establishment.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Gibraltar has no statutory export-proceeds surrender regime, and there is no mandatory conversion requirement for service export receipts. BIM/structural drafting and civil-infrastructure CAD consultancy are classified as services under ISIC 7110, therefore all export invoices may be retained in the invoiced currency, typically GBP or USD or EUR, with no forced conversion or repatriation deadline.

Central bank supervision: No central bank of Gibraltar; the monetary anchor is HM Treasury of the United Kingdom / Bank of England, with banking conduct and AML supervision under the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Medium. Offshore digital EMI accounts cannot serve as the primary escrow or treasury clearing rail for a Gibraltar-domiciled corporate entity, because GFSC-regulated banks require licensed banking counterparties for capital escrow and settlement accounts. A Gibraltar-licensed e-money institution or a UK-authorized EMI with Gibraltar passported permission may provide operational float and supplier-disbursement accounts, but such accounts are not viable for tier-1 capital injections or large trade settlements. Wise and similar fintech rails are acceptable only for low-value operational disbursements and cannot substitute for SWIFT clearing.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

SWIFT MT103/T103 wire transfers are the primary compliant routing for inbound capital injection from the Bermuda parent or shareholders into the Gibraltar GIP/GBP account. The wire purpose description must state 'Intercompany capital injection for BIM engineering services working capital' to avoid being misclassified as trade proceeds. Banks require the underlying shareholding structure, board resolution for the capital contribution, and a completed source-of-funds form. For GIP-denominated injections, sterling funds are converted at par with no FX cost. Large injections should be settled via CHAPS or a same-day SWIFT transfer to the bank's clearing account to ensure value-dated availability. The bank will issue a credit advice referencing the capital contribution, and the funds are immediately available for licensed disbursements once KYC/onboarding is completed.Outbound trade disbursements for civil-infrastructure BIM/CAD service delivery are executed either by SWIFT MT103 for supplier invoices and subcontractor payments, or by commercial letter of credit through SWIFT MT700 where the transaction involves physical procurement of hardware, software licences, or engineering equipment. The preferred clearing rail is GBP/HKD via SWIFT for Hong Kong or Singaporean CAD subcontractors, and GBP/USD via SWIFT for US cloud rendering and BIM server costs. Commercial LCs are used only for large capital equipment purchases tied to infrastructure projects. All outbound payments must be covered by a matching inbound invoice, disbursement instruction, and internal expenditure approval; SEPA EUR transfers may be used for EU suppliers. Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Gibraltar imposes no statutory withholding tax on dividends paid by a Gibraltar-resident company, including a Bermuda Exempted Company, to non-resident shareholders. The nil rate is final and requires no prior tax authority approval.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory contemporaneous Local File documentation is required where aggregate cross-border related-party transactions exceed GIP 1,000,000 per chargeable period; a Master File is required where the multinational group's global consolidated turnover exceeds €750,000,000 (OECD-aligned threshold). Below the Local File threshold, a retained transfer pricing policy with a functional analysis and gross margin study is compulsory for audit readiness. All TP documentation must be filed within 30 days of the Income Tax Office request.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR), enacted under the Income Tax (Economic Substance) rules, apply to companies conducting a relevant activity. ISIC 7110 offshore BIM/structural drafting services constitute a 'service centre business' when rendered to group companies or connected parties. The entity must maintain: (I) a physical registered office and actual premises in Gibraltar; (II) at least one director and one employee exercising independent decision-making in Gibraltar, with staffing proportionate to the revenue; and (III) annual operating expenditure in Gibraltar of between GIP 100,000 and GIP 500,000 depending on the volume of activities. A pure equity holding entity with no commercial service activity is fully exempt. Failure to satisfy the test causes reassessment of the entire revenue at 12.5% and triggers the ESR penalty regime.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

UK Tax Evasion — Central Management and Control Misdeclaration

Statutory sanction: HMRC applies the central management and control test to reclassify the entity as UK-resident; all worldwide profits, including the Gibraltar-excluded offshore CAD/BIM revenue, are reassessed at the UK 19% corporation tax rate with interest; deliberate non-disclosure penalties up to 100% of tax avoided and criminal prosecution under the Fraud Act 2006 and CTA 2010 false-statement provisions carry fines and imprisonment up to 7 years.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for restitution of unpaid group tax; UK and Gibraltar courts may issue disqualification orders up to 15 years, travel bans, and custodial sentences of up to 7 years for dishonestly filing misleading CT-1 declarations.
Gibraltar Shell-Company and ESR Substance Abuse

Statutory sanction: The Gibraltar Companies Registry and Income Tax Office impose financial penalties up to GIP 200,000 on first offence, daily penalties of GIP 1,000 for continued infringement, compulsory strike-off of the company, revocation of Qualifying Company status, and retrospective taxation at 12.5% on the gross infrastructure design revenues of the preceding six years.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for the resultant unpaid tax through civil restitution orders, ban from acting as a director for 10 years, travel bans preventing departure from Gibraltar during investigation, and exclusion from financial services sectors in Gibraltar, Jersey, and the Isle of Man.
Transfer Pricing and Profit Shifting in CAD-BIM Outsourcing

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar transfer pricing audit re-determines an arm's-length net contribution margin of 18-25%; the resulting profit is taxed at 12.5% in Gibraltar or 19% in the UK through the joint competent-authority procedure, plus late-payment interest; mispricing penalties up to 200% of the adjustment apply and DTA relief on related-party royalties is denied where economic substance fails.

Director personal liability: Directors signing non-arm's-length agreements face penal surcharges, disqualification under the Gibraltar Companies Act, personal civil liability for the group's lost tax, and joint and several liability in cross-border avoidance proceedings initiated by HMRC and the Gibraltar Income Tax Office.
AML and Economic Crime Failure on Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Under the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and AML/CTF regulations, fines up to GIP 10,000,000 and custodial penalties up to 14 years for money laundering by arrangement apply; the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit may freeze accounts, revoke the company's registration, and list the entity on the sanctions register.

Director personal liability: Directors and the Money Laundering Reporting Officer are individually criminally liable; the Crown may obtain confiscation orders, travel bans, asset seizure, and a permanent bar from Gibraltar banking infrastructure; extradition to the UK is available where the laundered funds enter the UK financial system.
Repatriation and Earnings Stripping — Disguised Distributions

Director personal liability: Directors responsible for the re-characterised distributions are personally liable for the shortfall in UK withholding tax deductions, subject to civil restitution, monthly travel restriction notices, and extradition proceedings between Gibraltar and the UK in severe circumstances.
