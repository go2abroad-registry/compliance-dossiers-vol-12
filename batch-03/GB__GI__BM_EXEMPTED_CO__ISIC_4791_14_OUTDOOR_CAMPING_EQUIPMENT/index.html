Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A Bermuda exempted company must maintain a registered office in Bermuda at the physical address of a licensed registered agent or corporate-services provider; a pure virtual or mail-forwarding address is not permissible for the registered office. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar – Commissioner of Income Tax and Commissioner of Goods and Services Tax (GST), Ministry of Finance, Gibraltar; e-services portal: https://eservices.gibraltar.gov.gi

E-government filing portal

A non-Gibraltar company (including a Bermuda Exempted Company) that establishes a taxable presence in Gibraltar must file a Foreign Company Registration application with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies and the Income Tax Office, appointing a Gibraltar-resident agent and obtaining a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN). Where annual taxable turnover will exceed the statutory GST threshold (GBP 80,000), or where voluntary registration is sought for input GST recovery, the company must also file Form GST-REG with the Commissioner of GST. Both the TRN and the GST registration number must be quoted on tax invoices, customs declarations, and banking instructions; registration must be completed within 30 days of commencing economic activity in Gibraltar.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A non-Gibraltar company (including a Bermuda Exempted Company) that establishes a taxable presence in Gibraltar must file a Foreign Company Registration application with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies and the Income Tax Office, appointing a Gibraltar-resident agent and obtaining a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN). Where annual taxable turnover will exceed the statutory GST threshold (GBP 80,000), or where voluntary registration is sought for input GST recovery, the company must also file Form GST-REG with the Commissioner of GST. Both the TRN and the GST registration number must be quoted on tax invoices, customs declarations, and banking instructions; registration must be completed within 30 days of commencing economic activity in Gibraltar.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a US-style check-the-box election. A qualifying foreign-owned company may (i) apply to the Commissioner for a no-tax-liability ruling under the territoriality provisions of the Income Tax Act where central management and control are located outside Gibraltar, (ii) elect territorial limitation of assessment so that only Gibraltar-source profits are subject to corporation tax, and (iii) invoke the Bermuda Exempted Companies grandfathering election under the Bermuda Economic Substance regime to confirm exemption from Bermuda income tax. OECD/G20 hybrid mismatch adjustments are handled by the Commissioner on a case-by-case basis with no statutory hybrid election form available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GBP 80,000 (annual value of taxable supplies made in Gibraltar in a rolling 12-month period; registration is compulsory within 30 days of breach, and voluntary registration is permitted for export-only businesses seeking input GST credit)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating at 0% GST is claimable for (a) goods sold online and physically exported from Gibraltar to a destination outside Gibraltar, provided the exporter holds documentary evidence of removal from Gibraltar within three months of the supply; (b) services supplied to a person established outside Gibraltar for the benefit and use wholly outside Gibraltar, including digital marketplace facilitation and remote retailing services; and (c) goods re-exported unchanged from a licensed bond or customs warehousing facility. The exporter may claim full input GST credit on purchases attributable to zero-rated export supplies; partial exemption rules apply where input GST relates to both taxable and exempt supplies.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document / AES entry, or CN22/CN23 customs declaration for low-value loose parcels)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill, or courier consignment note evidencing departure from Gibraltar
  • Commercial invoice and pro-forma purchase order
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or non-Gibraltar bank statement evidencing receipt of export payment from the foreign buyer
  • Proof of freight, insurance, and on-forwarding arrangements to the foreign destination

No statutory real-time fiscal clearance, API clearance, or digital stamping regime is in force. GST registrants must issue standard tax invoices displaying the supplier's name, address, TRN and GST registration number, description and quantity of supplies, GST rate applied, and total in GBP equivalent; file quarterly electronic GST returns on the Gibraltar e-Services portal, including Box 6 for zero-rated export supplies; and retain machine-readable invoicing and export records for six years. The Commissioner may issue an estimated direct assessment where electronic return filing is omitted or inaccurate.

Fraudulent, unsupported, or prematurely claimed zero-rating is assessed as GST evaded, attracting a civil penalty of up to 100% of tax evaded, statutory interest, clawback of all input GST credits attributable to the export, and upward estimated assessments for up to six years. Criminal conviction for GST fraud and export documentation falsehoods carries fines and imprisonment of up to seven years under Gibraltar's Criminal Offences Act; customs and tax authorities may suspend the exporter's registration, require a financial guarantee, and impose retrospective denial of zero-rating for all open assessments.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar operates a free convertibility regime for both the current account and capital account; there are no nominal exchange controls, usury limits, or central bank prior-approval gates imposed on profit remittance, dividends, or intercompany capital flows. GIP is exchangeable at par with GBP, and EUR/USD current accounts are widely permitted. A Bermuda Exempted Company structured as the Gibraltar tax-exempt conduit faces no incremental non-resident withholding taxes on outbound dividends or interest under Gibraltar's 0% corporate regime, subject only to standard GFSC AML record-keeping and the statutory filing of annual returns and audited financial statements prepared in accordance with GAPSE.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender obligation, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation timeline on export receipts. Outdoor camping equipment e-commerce sales proceeds may be retained in GIP, GBP, EUR, or USD in any licensed Gibraltar bank without obligatory inward remittance documentation; however, all payment flows above EUR 10,000 are subject to GFSC AML/JMLIT reporting thresholds, and cross-border wire transfers are reported under the UK/Gibraltar framework to the Joint Intelligence Team for tracking balance-of-payments movements.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (BoE) as the ultimate monetary sovereign for GBP, acting in coordination with the Government of Gibraltar's Financial Services Commission (GFSC) which licenses and supervises Gibraltar banks; the Gibraltar Pound (GIP) is issued by the Government of Gibraltar and is pegged at par (1:1) to GBP, with no separate monetary policy instrument.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium feasibility. UK-regulated E-money Institutions (EMIs) under the Financial Conduct Authority with valid e-money permission may onboard a Gibraltar-incorporated Bermuda Exempted Company via fully remote digital KYC where the company risk rating is Low_Risk_Standard under the GFSC AML regime. Legal constraints apply: EMIs cannot lend, cannot take client deposits (only safeguarded e-money), cannot provide full open-credit facilities, and their virtual IBAN rails are typically not treated as acceptable settlement accounts by regulated merchant acquirers for outbound trade Letters of Credit; consequently EMIs serve strictly as a fronting liquidity conduit, with final treasury settlement to be consolidated in a licensed Gibraltar or UK correspondent bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Share capital and working capital injections are effected via SWIFT MT103 corporate transfers to the Gibraltar International Bank Capital Escrow Account using purpose code S010 (issue of share capital) under the Gibraltar Companies Act Chapter 138. The complete compliance packet comprises the board written resolution authorising the transfer, certificate of incorporation, certified copy of the register of directors, register of charges, and the remitter's source-of-funds letter from the parent Bermuda Exempted Company; funds are subsequently cleared to the operational current account via CHAPS in GBP or through the Bank of England's settlement system, with EUR/USD cleared via the local FX desk.Supplier settlements for outdoor camping equipment inventory use SWIFT MT103 for straight telegraphic transfers at contract confirmation; for larger or higher-risk purchase orders, a commercial Letter of Credit at sight is opened by the Gibraltar corporate bank and advised through a Far East advising bank to secure title documents.
