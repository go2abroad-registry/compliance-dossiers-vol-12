Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for an exempt private company; the authorised capital is fixed in the Articles and may be unpaid. No escrow deposit or blocked capital account is required by the Registrar of Companies.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification from the Gibraltar Fire and Rescue Service covering emergency exits, fire alarms and extinguishers; structural load inspection and building control clearance from the Development and Planning Authority for the retail floor; electrical installation certificate; occupational health and safety workplace clearance for staff areas and refrigeration rooms.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital for an exempt private company; the authorised capital is fixed in the Articles and may be unpaid. No escrow deposit or blocked capital account is required by the Registrar of Companies.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (Commissioner of Income Tax), with e-filing via the Income Tax Office eTax portal; Purchase Tax and import controls are administered by HM Customs Gibraltar.

E-government filing portal

STEP 1 - Companies Registry: within one month of establishing a place of business in Gibraltar, the Bermuda Exempted Company must file the particulars of a foreign/overseas company with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies and obtain a local company number. STEP 2 - Income Tax registration: the Income Tax Office issues a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) upon Companies Registry enrolment; if not issued automatically, file the Company TRN application with the Income Tax Office, identifying the Bermuda Exempted Company, the Gibraltar registered office/resident agent, and the trade commencement date. STEP 3 - Direct tax filing: submit annual self-assessment corporation tax returns within nine months of the chargeable period end and pay the assessed corporation tax within 12 months; profits of the Asian supermarket branch are assessable under Case I of Schedule D on Gibraltar-source trading profits. STEP 4 - Customs registration: apply to HM Customs Gibraltar for a Business Number (BN) and importer-of-record status before first commercial import; Purchase Tax is payable at importation on the duty value. STEP 5 - Retail licence: obtain the mandatory store licence under the Trade, Business Licences and General Provisions Act 2011 before commencing grocery sales. STEP 6 - Substance notification: if the company elects to be treated as tax resident elsewhere, lodge the annual Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2019 notification; any such election does not exempt the Gibraltar store's permanent establishment from Gibraltar corporation tax.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - the Bermuda Tax_Exempt_Zone status is not recognised in Gibraltar; the Exempted Co branch trading as a grocery store is an autonomous corporate taxpayer chargeable under the Income Tax Act 2010 on the supermarket's trading profits, regardless of the Bermuda exemption from corporate income tax.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch pass-through elections exist. Available elections: (i) group relief/fiscal unity election by Gibraltar-resident companies with 75% group ownership; (ii) election to align the company's chargeable period with its accounting year-end (default basis: 31 March); (iii) annual election under the Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2019 to certify tax residence in another jurisdiction - ineffective against the Gibraltar permanent establishment's profits. The pre-2011 'Exempt Company' certificates were abolished; grandfathering expired on 30 June 2019.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

15%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No turnover threshold triggers purchase tax registration; all commercial importers must hold a HM Customs Gibraltar Business Number (BN) before effecting any import. Purchase Tax is levied on the duty value (CIF basis) at the rates in the Purchase Tax Ordinance - fresh vegetables and the majority of basic foodstuffs are exempt/zero-rated, whereas processed Asian condiments and prepared grocery items generally attract the 15% standard rate.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - Gibraltar has no VAT law, so the 0% export VAT zero-rating concept does not exist. Purchase Tax is charged only on importation into Gibraltar; goods delivered to non-Gibraltar markets leave the fiscal territory outside the tax net. A grocery store selling solely within Gibraltar has no export refund entitlement; however, the Purchase Tax Drawback Rules allow recovery of import duty previously paid where goods are later re-exported unused and within the prescribed drawback period. No statutory FX repatriation or 180-day deadline applies to export proceeds in Gibraltar; exchange controls have been abolished.

  • Commercial invoice and packing list stating quantity, origin, and duty value
  • Gibraltar Customs electronic Goods Declaration (import entry evidencing Purchase Tax/Import Duty paid)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing outward shipment (required for re-export drawback)
  • Original Purchase Tax duty receipt or Customs entry reference
  • Certificate of origin (only where preferential tariff/drawback rates are claimed)

No real-time fiscal clearance, no digital stamping, no SAF-T reporting, and no mandatory API e-invoicing gateway is in force. Purchase Tax collection occurs exclusively at the border via the customs goods-declaration system; retail transactions may be evidenced by ordinary paper or electronic invoices without transactional government clearance.

HM Customs Gibraltar may audit import/drawback entries for up to six years and claw back exempted or refunded Purchase Tax/Import Duty with interest; fraudulent claims attract forfeiture of goods, a civil penalty of up to treble the duty evaded, and criminal prosecution with up to two years' imprisonment. The Income Tax Office may similarly raise discovery assessments on the supermarket's branch file within the statutory time limits and levy penalties of up to 100% of the tax understated, plus daily default fines for false or incomplete records.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Capital and current account transactions are fully convertible in both Bermuda and Gibraltar. The Gibraltar Pound is pegged 1:1 to GBP and freely exchangeable; there is no exchange-control law, no prior-authorisation mechanism for dividends, intercompany loan repayments, profit remittances or liquidation distributions. Hurdles are purely documentary: the receiving bank requires proof of management-and-control location, a Gibraltar tax-residency certificate or Income Tax Office clearance if the company is managed and controlled from Gibraltar (corporate rate 12.5% on Gibraltar-source income), and a Bermuda economic-substance status letter to prevent CRS/FATCA misclassification. Dividends paid by the Gibraltar-registered overseas company to a Bermuda Exempted Company shareholder incur no Gibraltar withholding tax when the beneficial owner is non-resident.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Gibraltar imposes no export-proceeds surrender duty, no mandatory conversion ratio, no repatriation timeline and no maximum offshore-retention period for export receipts. The only obligation is GFSC-driven AML record-keeping: all cross-border credits must be traceable to the underlying invoice and retained for six years. For this grocery hub, which is structurally a net importer, the same rule applies to any incidental re-export proceeds, which may remain in the settlement currency at treasury discretion.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar has no independent central bank. The Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the integrated banking and prudential regulator under the Banking Act 2015 and the Financial Services Act 2019. Monetary authority and ultimate GBP liability rest with the Bank of England; the Government of Gibraltar issues the Gibraltar Pound (GIP) through the Commissioner of Currency at a fixed 1:1 parity with GBP.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Medium as a secondary rail, Low as a bank of record. GFSC-licensed or UK FCA-authorised EMIs (Wise Platform, Revolut Business, Currencycloud, Payoneer) offer multi-currency wallets, tight FX spreads and cheap MT103 batch disbursements, but none provides GIP-denominated local POS/Interac acquiring, Commercial LC issuance, or full safeguarding of imported-commodity trade flows. Post-Brexit EEA passporting has lapsed; an offshore EMI must hold a GFSC licence or a UK FCA EMI licence recognised in Gibraltar and must maintain a safeguarding ring-fence with a GFSC-licensed bank. Legal use is therefore limited to operational disbursements and FX conversion; the capital escrow and primary clearing must stay at a Tier-1 Gibraltar bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity is routed as SWIFT MT103 from the named shareholder's account in Bermuda, Hong Kong or Singapore to the Gibraltar Tier-1 operating or capital-escrow account. No Gibraltar exchange-control permit is required. The receiving bank must see, before the value date, a board resolution approving the capital call, source-of-wealth statements for the shareholder, the Bermuda Certificate of Exemption, the Gibraltar overseas-company registration, and a tax agent's confirmation of the GIP/GBP conversion treatment. Beneficiary details must match the registered name and overseas-company number; field 72 must carry 'EQUITY INJECTION / WORKING CAPITAL FOR GROCERY TRADE'. USD- or CNY-denominated injections are credited to a segregated multi-currency account and converted only under a signed FX authority letter. Amounts above GBP 50,000 require treasury second signature and may be subject to the bank's independent capital-verification review.Three rails are used for procurement: (1) SWIFT MT103 for prepayments and open-account supplier invoices, with exact invoice enumeration and no third-party private-wallet payments; (2) irrevocable documentary Commercial Letter of Credit (UCP600, with URDG758 where bank guarantees are required) issued by the Gibraltar bank for bulk containerised condiments and fresh vegetables, advised or confirmed through a Hong Kong, Shanghai, Singapore, Bangkok or Dubai correspondent, against clean-on-board bills of lading, phytosanitary certificates, packing lists and commercial invoices; (3) GIP/GBP MT103 or instant clearing for authorised Gibraltar and UK distributors, and USD/EUR correspondent clearing for Spanish and Portuguese wholesalers. In-store receipts are batched daily from POS/Interac and credit-card acquirers into the operational account under a T+1 merchant settlement schedule. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar's Economic Substance Regulations apply to resident and PE-taxable entities undertaking 'relevant activities' such as distribution and service centres. A physical Asian supermarket and grocery distribution hub in Gibraltar maintains a fixed place of business, local staff, board decision-making and warehouse management, which satisfies the adequate substance test. The Bermuda Exempted Company must file the annual economic substance declaration with the Gibraltar Registrar and retain contemporaneous board minutes, payroll and lease records proving real activity in Gibraltar; failure to do so triggers the presumption of an artificial shell arrangement.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Criminal conviction for tax evasion under Gibraltar law: fine up to GIP 1,000,000, full restitution of evaded tax with interest, and imprisonment for up to 7 years.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for tax cheated where they signed false returns or knowingly caused the company to suppress income; courts may impose confiscation orders, travel bans and director disqualification for up to 15 years.
Shell Company / Artificial Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar's general anti-avoidance doctrine recharacterises the arrangement, disallows any purported tax-exempt benefit, assesses the true Gibraltar-source profits at 12.5% plus interest and a civil penalty of up to 150% of the additional tax.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil will be pierced where there is no substantive business in Bermuda; directors are jointly and severally liable for the tax and penalties, and may be subject to asset freezing orders and supervisory disqualification.
Transfer Pricing Abuse & Foreign Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar transfer pricing adjustment restores arms-length remuneration, imposes additional 12.5% tax, adds late payment interest, and levies a negligence penalty up to 100% of the underpaid tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve back-to-back invoices without contemporaneous comparables or economic substance are treated as conduct participants; personal restitution, disqualification and, in aggravated cases, imprisonment of up to 5 years may follow.
AML / Counter-Terrorist Financing Failure

Statutory sanction: Enforcement action under Gibraltar AML legislation: unlimited financial penalties (in practice up to GIP 1,000,000), confiscation of proceeds, suspension of the store's constitutional licence, and criminal prosecution of the entity.

Director personal liability: A director who fails to appoint a Money Laundering Reporting Officer or who permits anonymous cash forks is personally fined, banned from holding public and corporate office, and may be sentenced to up to 5 years' imprisonment.
