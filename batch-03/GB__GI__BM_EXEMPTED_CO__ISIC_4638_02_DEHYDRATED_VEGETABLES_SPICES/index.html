Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (Commissioner of Income Tax), 132-138 Main Street, Gibraltar; official portal www.gibraltar.gov.gi and e-services at egov.gibraltar.gov.gi

E-government filing portal

Step 1: File Part XI 'Registration of Foreign Company' application with the Gibraltar Companies House (Registrar of Companies) if the Bermuda Exempted Company establishes a place of business or branch in Gibraltar; accompanying forms include a certified copy of the Bermuda certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles, a certified list of directors and shareholders, and a Gibraltar registered office consent. Step 2: Submit the 'Application for Taxation Reference Number' (Form TRN) to the Income Tax Office, attaching the registration certificate, proof of registered office, certified passport copies of directors/ultimate beneficial owners, and a declaration of non-residence (management and control outside Gibraltar). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no 'check-the-box' look-through election; entity-level taxation is determined by statutory residence rules. Relevant elections: (1) Bermuda Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 election — the Bermuda Minister of Finance issues a written assurance (extendable to 31 March 2035) that the exempted company will not be subject to any Bermuda income, capital, or profits tax, dividends, or withholding tax; (2) Non-residence election in Gibraltar — by maintaining central management and control outside Gibraltar (e.g., board meetings held in Bermuda or another treaty jurisdiction), the company elects to be treated as non-resident and avoids Gibraltar corporation tax (10% headline rate) on non-Gibraltar-source trading profits; (3) Group relief election under the Income Tax Act 2010 (ss. 41A-41D) for Gibraltar-resident fellow group companies to surrender losses; (4) Hybrid mismatch neutralization — no elective mechanism; the Income Tax (Hybrid Mismatch) Regulations 2018 apply mandatory defensive rules that deny deductions or impose inclusions on transactions structured to exploit double-non-taxation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales Tax — Gibraltar applies Import Duty (Customs Tariff) under the Customs Ordinance 2010 and Excise Duty on specified goods (alcohol, tobacco, fuel); goods exported are outside the charge

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — there is no VAT/GST in Gibraltar and therefore no turnover-based registration threshold. Import duty arises automatically on physical importation of goods into Gibraltar; a customs broker/importer registration with the Customs & Excise Department (Annual Import/Export Registration) is required for all commercial importers regardless of turnover. Dehydrated vegetables and spices classified under heading 0712 (dried vegetables) attract 0% import duty in Gibraltar's tariff schedule, provided the goods are not produced in or consumed in Gibraltar.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in Gibraltar as no VAT/GST is levied; there is no export VAT zero-rating or input VAT refund mechanism. For customs purposes, dehydrated garlic flakes and crushed chili pepper exported from Gibraltar are outside the charge to Gibraltar import duty. If goods are imported into Gibraltar and subsequently re-exported (bonded warehouse, transit, transshipment, or temporary importation for processing), a claim for relief from import duty may be made under the Customs Ordinance 2010 (ss. 55-60) by demonstrating that the goods were not released for free circulation in Gibraltar. Substantiation requires a fixed export declaration filed with the Gibraltar Customs & Excise Department, proof that the goods physically left Gibraltar, and proof of payment of the proceeds in foreign currency.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is fully convertible at par with GBP. Both current-account and capital-account flows are unrestricted; profit remittance, dividend distribution and repatriation of liquidation proceeds require no prior administrative consent. No hard rationing, dual-exchange-rate regime or discriminatory FX taxes apply. Capital injection and trade payments can be freely routed via domestic or international banks.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar imposes no statutory export proceeds surrender requirement. Exporters may retain, convert and remit foreign currency receipts freely. The only compliance overlay is standard anti-money-laundering record-keeping and interim suspicious-transaction reporting in accordance with the GFSC AML/CFT rules.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the statutory banking and FX-instrument regulator; there is no autonomous central bank. The Bank of England (UK) supports the GIP/GBP peg, and the Government of Gibraltar retains note-issuance authority.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-moderate. Non-Gibraltar EMIs cannot accept commercial clients without a GFSC licence or an applicable UK/Gibraltar passport. No pure-play fintech EMI in Gibraltar currently offers reliable trade clearing for commodity letters of credit and SWIFT wires. Use of an unlicensed offshore e-money provider for this corporate structure is not advised.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 aggregate annual controlled transactions with related parties; additionally, an MNE group with consolidated group turnover of EUR 750 million or more must prepare Master File, Local File and Country-by-Country Report in accordance with OECD BEPS Action 13.

Economic substance scope: Applicable. Gibraltar’s Economic Substance for Companies Act requires every Gibraltar-incorporated company carrying on a relevant activity to maintain economic substance in Gibraltar. Bulk trading and distribution of dehydrated vegetables and spices is a geographically mobile relevant activity. The company must hold board meetings in Gibraltar, maintain a physical office, employ substantive qualified staff, and perform core income-generating activities (contract negotiation, inventory management, logistics, invoicing, credit control) in Gibraltar. An annual economic substance filing is required with the Registrar. Failure to satisfy the substance test results in denial of tax residence, financial penalties up to GIP 100,000, administrative strike-off, and reporting to the Gibraltar competent authority.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of up to 100% of tax evaded, plus criminal fines up to GIP 250,000 and imprisonment for up to 7 years. The Commissioner may issue a protective assessment and seize bank accounts.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or cause a false return are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax and civil restitution. Courts may issue travel bans, asset freezing orders, disqualification orders up to 15 years, and custodial sentences for fraudulent evasion.
Shell company / sham economic substance

Statutory sanction: Cancellation of tax residence certificate, deemed assessment on a full arm's length mark-up against the non-compliant entity, economic substance penalty up to GIP 100,000 per failure, and compulsory strike-off under the Companies Act.

Director personal liability: Personal liability for penalties if directors authorized the sham structure. The court may pierce the corporate veil, impose disqualification, freeze personal property, and issue travel bans pending full restitution.
AML / trade-based money laundering

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Act, up to 14 years' imprisonment, unlimited fines, confiscation of assets, and civil penalties levied by the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit up to GIP 500,000.

Director personal liability: Directors personally exposed to confiscation, travel bans, freezing orders, and prosecution for money laundering. The corporate veil provides no protection where directors have actual knowledge or active involvement.
Transfer pricing non-compliance

Statutory sanction: Transfer pricing adjustment by the Commissioner, additional tax at 10%, interest at 8% from the due date, and a penalty of up to 100% of the additional tax. Repeated offenses trigger enhanced financial examinations.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for tax recovered where assets are stripped from the company or where they knowingly signed false transfer pricing documentation. Civil restitution and disqualification are routinely sought.
Failure to file corporation tax return

Statutory sanction: Fixed penalty of GIP 100; daily surcharge of GIP 10 up to a further GIP 300; additional GIP 300 if six months late; then automatic estimated assessment and company strike-off proceedings.

Director personal liability: Directors personally liable for outstanding penalties if the company is dissolved without settling its tax liabilities. Travel restriction and disqualification orders can be obtained under the Tax Collection (Enforcement) Act for serious non-compliance.
