Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: Bermuda Exempted Company is statutorily exempt from Bermuda income, corporate, and withholding taxes under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 (guarantee extended to 2050). In Gibraltar, the entity is non-resident unless central management and control (CMC) is exercised in Gibraltar; non-resident companies are taxed only on Gibraltar-source income (territorial scope). If CMC is present in Gibraltar, the entity becomes Gibraltar-resident and subject to entity-level tax at 12.5% standard rate (20% for utilities), with participation exemption for foreign dividends and capital gains. No pass-through classification applies.

Available election forms

(1) Bermuda: application for Tax Exemption Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 (valid to 2050). (2) Gibraltar: Qualifying Company election under the Qualifying Companies Act 1991 (legacy regime closed to new entrants; grandfathered only). (3) Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010 s.48: group relief loss-surrender election within a Gibraltar tax group. (4) UK-Gibraltar DTA (2019, in force 2021): residence tie-breaker and PE attribution. (5) UK hybrid mismatch rules (TIOPA 2010 Part 6A): no US-style check-the-box; mandatory and elective adjustments prevent double non-taxation from Bermuda exemption and Gibraltar territoriality differentials. (6) UK VAT: online marketplace deeming via VATA 1994 s.5C may shift VAT liability to the marketplace operator for goods up to £135.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The GIP/GBP regime imposes no exchange controls on either the current or capital account. There is full convertibility for trade settlements, dividend repatriation, and capital injections. Profit remittance and repatriation of invested capital are unrestricted; a Gibraltar-resident company or a Bermuda Exempted Company that is managed and controlled from Gibraltar may be subject to Gibraltar corporation tax at an effective rate of up to 12.5%, but no withholding tax is levied on dividends paid to non-resident shareholders, and no FX approval is required for outbound remittance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export-proceeds surrender requirement and no mandatory conversion ratio in Gibraltar or the UK. Exporters may retain 100% of receipts in any currency. Nonetheless, the Bank of England's International Transactions Reporting System (ITRS) and the Gibraltar Statistical Office require periodic reporting of transactions above the designated notification threshold, and marine/air cargo documentation must be retained for audit trail and AML compliance.

Central bank supervision: Monetary authority for Gibraltar is the Bank of England (GIP pegged 1:1 to GBP). Banking and foreign-exchange supervision is exercised by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) under the Financial Services Act 2019; the Government of Gibraltar Currency Unit handles GIP issuance.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Moderate-to-Low. GFSC-licensed electronic money institutions (EMIs) registered under Gibraltar's E-Money Regulations 2019 may onboard the structure, but a Bermuda Exempted Company with a Gibraltar operations desk is classified as an opaque offshore structure, triggering enhanced due diligence. Post-Brexit, no UK FCA EMI passport applies to Gibraltar without separate GFSC recognition; EEA-licensed EMIs (e.g., Malta, Lithuania) may be recognized on a transitional basis. Most digital EMIs will reject the merchant category code (456191 nutraceuticals/substances) unless a valid pharmacy/supplement retail license, negative result of product due diligence, and a 3-month processing history with a reputable acquirer are provided.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File preparation applies where related-party transactions exceed GIP 1,000,000; a Master File is mandatory where group consolidated revenue exceeds EUR 750,000,000; documentation must be filed or made available to the Comptroller of Income Tax within 30 days of a formal notice.

Economic substance scope: Where a Bermuda Exempted Company is managed and controlled from Gibraltar, both Gibraltar's International Tax Co-operation (Economic Substance) Act 2018 and Bermuda's Economic Substance Requirements apply to the same entity. The company must demonstrate in the relevant jurisdiction: a physical office, full-time employees proportionate to activity, and performance of core income-generating activities for the e-commerce distribution and service centre business. Failure to meet substance tests exposes the company to penalties up to GIP 100,000, compulsory strike-off, and automatic exchange of information with Bermuda and the UK tax authorities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Deliberate Understatement

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty up to 200% of the understated tax; criminal conviction attracts up to GIP 10,000 fine or imprisonment up to 5 years (or both) per charge, plus recovery of evaded tax.

Director personal liability: The Gibraltar courts will pierce the corporate veil where fraud is established; directors face joint and several civil liability for the tax loss, an unlimited restitution order, travel bans pending criminal proceedings, and extradition exposure through UK mutual legal assistance.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Penalty of up to GIP 100,000 under the Gibraltar Economic Substance regime, mandatory information exchange with Bermuda, potential two-year strike-off or dissolution of the company, and loss of Bermuda exempt-company tax guarantees.

Director personal liability: Directors may be disqualified for up to 10 years under the Gibraltar Companies Act; personal liability is imposed for trading while insolvent and for misrepresenting substance, with civil recovery orders and an overseas travel restriction until the penalty and costs are settled.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Adjustment of taxable profit to the arm's length result, penalty of up to GIP 3,000 for documentation failure, tax-geared penalties up to 100% of the primary adjustment, and interest at 8% per annum.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign incorrect transfer pricing returns without reasonable excuse expose themselves to personal penalties and, on proven cross-border evasion, joint liability with the company for the restored tax; serious cases extend to director disqualification and international asset-traced restraint orders.
AML / High-Risk Merchant Account Breach

Statutory sanction: Fines up to GIP 20,000 per contravention under the Proceeds of Crime Act; the Financial Services Commission may also require compensation, suspend the payment licence, and initiate criminal money laundering proceedings.

Director personal liability: Senior managers face personal criminal liability for consenting or conniving: imprisonment up to 5 years, confiscation orders under the PCA, and, for nominees, liability for restitution of the full payment volume, with travel bans pending asset recovery.
Unlicensed Pharmaceutical Supplement Distribution

Statutory sanction: Cease-and-desist from the Gibraltar Directorate of Public Health, product seizure and recall, criminal fines up to GIP 50,000, and removal of the entity's licence to operate the online pharmacy-adjacent business.

Director personal liability: Directors can be personally prosecuted for public health offences; liability includes all recall and compensation costs, disqualification from acting as company officers, and enforcement of fines against personal UK and Gibraltar assets.
