Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar, Gibraltar; online portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax (eTax)

E-government filing portal

Foreign-incorporated entities (including a Bermuda Exempted Company) must apply for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) within one month of commencing taxable activity. Procedure: (1) file Form ATG (Application for Tax Registration) with the Income Tax Office, providing certified copy of certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, and details of local representative (if any); (2) submit evidence of Bermuda exempt-company status (Certificate of Exemption) to support tax residency analysis; (3) receive TRN upon verification. The entity must also register with the Gibraltar Companies House if establishing a branch or place of business.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility is available for current-account and capital-account transactions in GBP, USD, and EUR; no exchange-control approval, surrender obligation, or repatriation licence applies. Gibraltar imposes no withholding taxes on dividends, interest, or royalties, and profit remittance from a Gibraltar-managed Bermuda Exempted Company is legally unrestricted. The only practical hurdles are bank-level AML/KYC disclosure, CRS/AEOI reporting, and satisfaction of the Gibraltar Economic Substance (Companies and Partnerships) Act 2019 for a 'distribution and service centre' relevant activity; none of these limits currency convertibility or profit remittance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. There is no statutory timeline for surrender, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no requirement to repatriate export proceeds. All surplus commercial aircraft parts sales receipts may be retained indefinitely and in full in a USD or multi-currency Gibraltar clearing account. For audit and economic-substance records, the enterprise should maintain a documented ledger of retained export proceeds matched to Gibraltar payroll, premises, and governance expenditure.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority for the pound sterling); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) (banking and investment firm supervisor); HM Treasury (financial sanctions and legislative authority); Government of Gibraltar Commissioners of Currency (issuer of Gibraltar banknotes). No dedicated foreign exchange administration exists because UK and Gibraltar exchange controls were abolished in October 1979.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium-Low feasibility rating for this mandate. UK FCA-authorised EMIs (Wise Business, Revolut Business, Airwallex, Ebury, Currencycloud) offer fast multi-currency collection and supplier payout accounts and may onboard a Bermuda Exempted Company where economic-substance documents are supplied. Legal constraints: post-Brexit there is no EU passport into Gibraltar, so an EMI may serve the structure only through a UK-registered entity, and EMIs cannot provide commercial letters of credit, committed FX credit lines, title escrow, or aviation settlement safekeeping because safeguarding accounts must be segregated and EMIs are prohibited from granting credit. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity and working-capital injections are routed by SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's bank to the designated capital escrow or operational account at the Gibraltar clearing bank. The credit narrative must state the ISO 20022 purpose code 'EQUI' or 'CAPT', the Bermuda Exempted Company registration number, and the phrase 'share capital contribution'; the remitter must be the named shareholder on the statutory register. Prior to the first inflow the bank requires: board resolution authorising allotment; updated register of members showing allotted shares; Bermuda Monetary Authority consent (if applicable); source-of-wealth statement for the shareholder; and proof that the injection does not constitute a restricted financial promotion. Any inbound USD wire of USD 100,000 or more should be pre-advised to the relationship manager to avoid AML suspension. Trade receipts must use purpose code 'GOOD', be matched to the invoice and air waybill, and must never be labelled as equity.Supplier settlements in the aviation spares trade use three clearing rails: (1) open-account disbursements via SWIFT MT103 with purpose code 'GOOD' or 'TRDC', cleared in USD through CHIPS/Fedwire correspondent chains and in GBP/EUR through CHAPS/TARGET2; (2) commercial letters of credit issued through SWIFT MT700 and confirmed by a top-tier global bank, with MT740 reimbursement and MT754 availability advice, used where the supplier requires payment against presentation of EASA or FAA release documentation; and (3) Escrow_Aviation settlement from a dual-signatory segregated escrow sub-account, released only against signed proof-of-delivery and the certified airworthiness release, thereby protecting buyer and lender from title fraud. Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Gibraltar has no statutory local-file or master-file turnover threshold; all related-party transactions must be priced at arm's length and contemporaneous documentation must be produced on request. Mandatory Country-by-Country Reporting applies to multinational groups with consolidated group revenue of EUR 750,000,000, equivalent to approximately GIP 645,000,000. Below this threshold, transfer pricing documentation should nevertheless be maintained to support distribution, financing, management fees and aircraft inventory pricing.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar's Economic Substance Regulations 2020 apply to tax-resident companies carrying on relevant activities, including distribution and service centres. A Bermuda Exempted Company trading in surplus commercial aircraft parts and avionics must maintain core income-generating activities in Gibraltar, including inventory purchasing decisions, warehousing or logistics coordination, credit and risk management, invoicing, and periodic management reporting. The company must employ a proportionate number of qualified full-time staff, maintain a physical office in Gibraltar, and submit the annual Economic Substance Return. If the entity lacks substance in Gibraltar but also claims Bermuda tax exemption, it becomes a stateless shell company in the eyes of both jurisdictions and may be denied treaty benefits.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion / fraudulent return

Statutory sanction: Criminal prosecution for willful tax evasion and penalties of 100% to 200% of the tax understated; monthly interest on unpaid tax; court fines of up to GIP 25,000 per offence; imprisonment up to 2 years; confiscation of aircraft inventory and escrow balances under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly cause the false Bermuda-only filing are jointly and severally liable for the tax penalty; courts may issue travel restriction orders, asset freezes, directorships disqualification and, where the fraud is deliberate, custodial sentences.
Economic substance / shell company abuse

Statutory sanction: Failure to meet Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations 2020 attracts penalties of up to GIP 25,000 per return and further GIP 10,000 for missing information. Repeated non-compliance may lead to strike-off, a declaration of shell company status in the beneficial ownership register, and disqualification orders. Bermuda-side Economic Substance Rules may also result in penalty assessments and revocation of exempted status.

Director personal liability: Directors risk personal disqualification under the Gibraltar Companies Act 2014, financial penalties, travel bans and a five-year prohibition on acting as a director where they knowingly sign false economic substance declarations or continue trading through an empty conduit.
AML / sanctions non-compliance

Statutory sanction: Under the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act 2015, Money Laundering Regulations 2019 and Sanctions Act 2019, failure to submit a Suspicious Activity Report can result in unlimited fines, imprisonment up to 14 years, regulatory fines up to 4% of annual turnover, and confiscation of the entire transaction value and aircraft parts inventory.

Director personal liability: The Money Laundering Reporting Officer, compliance personnel and directors may be personally criminally liable for tipping-off or negligent omission of SARs. Directors may also be subject to travel bans and asset restraint orders pending prosecution.
Transfer pricing manipulation

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner of Income Tax will make an arm's-length re-adjustment, assess additional tax at 10%, charge monthly interest, and impose a penalty up to 100% of the underpaid tax where contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation is absent. Sham transactions may be recharacterised and distributions treated as deemed dividends with no defensive protection.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign related-party agreements without a functional analysis may be liable for civil damages, contribution orders in insolvency proceedings, and director disqualification. Fraudulent invoices that misstate part-number valuations may also support money laundering and false accounting charges.
