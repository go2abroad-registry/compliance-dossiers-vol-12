Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office of Her Majesty's Government of Gibraltar (Commissioner of Income Tax); official online portal: www.gibraltar.gov.gi and eTax filing system

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company obtains a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) only after establishing a permanent establishment or deriving Gibraltar-source income. Submit Form T(PR), certified Bermuda Exemption Certificate, constitutional documents, register of directors and members, and evidence of registered office to the Income Tax Office. Issuance occurs within 5-10 business days. A VAT ID is unavailable because Gibraltar does not impose VAT or GST.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A Bermuda Exempted Company obtains a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) only after establishing a permanent establishment or deriving Gibraltar-source income. Submit Form T(PR), certified Bermuda Exemption Certificate, constitutional documents, register of directors and members, and evidence of registered office to the Income Tax Office. Issuance occurs within 5-10 business days. A VAT ID is unavailable because Gibraltar does not impose VAT or GST.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no check-the-box regime. The Bermuda Exempted Company may elect to renew its Bermuda exemption annually with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies. For Gibraltar tax purposes, the entity may elect territorial-source profit assessment if a permanent establishment is present, and may apply for non-residence status where central management and control remains outside Gibraltar. No hybrid mismatch elections are recognised under Gibraltar law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST; Gibraltar indirect taxation is limited to customs and excise duties on physical imports

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. An importer code under Gibraltar's Import Duty Act 1959 is required only where physical goods are imported into Gibraltar.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar has no VAT/GST regime, so export zero-rating is not applicable. Logistics SaaS fees charged to non-resident customers are outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect taxation and may be invoiced without local tax. Physical goods re-exported from Gibraltar may qualify for customs duty relief under customs warehousing or outward processing procedures.

  • Customs export declaration (electronic Single Administrative Document or equivalent frontier declaration)
  • Bill of lading or air waybill for physical goods; for SaaS, signed service agreement and cloud service delivery log
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank settlement evidence confirming receipt of export proceeds

None — Gibraltar does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing APIs, digital stamping, or SAF-T reporting; ordinary commercial invoices together with electronic customs entries (Single Administrative Document) satisfy clearance requirements.

No VAT clawback operates because no VAT was charged. False physical-export declarations attract penalties of up to 200% of duty evaded, forfeiture of goods, and prosecution under Gibraltar customs law. Where export treatment is knowingly misrepresented in Gibraltar income tax filings, the Income Tax Office may recover unpaid tax, statutory interest, and penalties of up to 150% under the Income Tax Act 2010, with criminal sanction available for intentional fraud.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar shares the United Kingdom's liberalised convertible currency regime: GBP is fully convertible with zero exchange-control restrictions. Current-account payments (SaaS licensing fees, software subscription charges, logistics telematics service fees, operational disbursements) are free from any licence or registration requirement. Capital-account transactions (Bermuda shareholder equity injection, intercompany shareholder loans, capital repatriation, profit distributions to the Bermuda Exempted Company) are likewise unrestricted and require no central bank approval or profit-remittance permit. As a special offshore territory of the UK, Gibraltar applies OECD-compatible capital-movement liberalisation, and non-resident-owned exempted structures face no mandatory FX conversion, no split-currency account regime, and no dividend-remittance hurdles; retained hard-currency balances can be held indefinitely in multi-currency treasury accounts.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. There is no statutory surrender timeline for export proceeds, no mandatory conversion ratio into GBP, and no obligation to repatriate foreign-currency SaaS subscription revenue into the jurisdiction. Receipts from logistics-fleet telematics exports may be retained in USD, EUR or GBP in designated multi-currency accounts, and only UK/Gibraltar financial-sanctions screening obligations (e.g., UK Global Human Rights Sanctions Regulations and Russia-related sanctions) apply at settlement-clearing level.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (central monetary authority for GBP; Gibraltar operates as a sterling territory with no autonomous central bank) and Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) (banking and AML/CFT supervisory authority for Gibraltar-licensed banks and payment institutions)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility: Medium-High for UK-authorised E-Money Institutions (EMIs) such as Modulr, Airwallex, Currencycloud and ClearBank-enabled platforms; Low for non-UK offshore digital EMIs without FCA authorisation or GFSC licensing. Legal constraints: (a) a Gibraltar-registered company is not automatically eligible for UK EMI accounts because of FCA 'nexus' onboarding policies and the cessation of the EEA passporting regime; UK EMIs may engage Gibraltar clients only via their own UK establishment or through the Temporary Permissions Regime (TPR); (b) GFSC-licensed EMIs operating in Gibraltar are few in number and often restrict 'tax-exempt offshore' structures under their AML risk appetite; (c) digital-only EMI platforms generally refuse Bermuda Exempted Companies with nominee directors absent a full economic-substance dossier, so the treasury blueprint requires a Gibraltar operating presence, local payroll evidence, and a licensed fiduciary introducer in parallel with any EMI onboarding application.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Bermuda Exempted Company with registered office only in Bermuda and no visible economic nexus in Gibraltar or the UK

Root cause: Bermuda Exempted Company with registered office only in Bermuda and no visible economic nexus in Gibraltar or the UK

Defense evidence: Provide: (i) Bermuda Registrar's Certificate of Good Standing; (ii) Certificate of Incumbency listing all directors, shareholders and final beneficial owners; (iii) a rolling 12-24 month Gibraltar substance file comprising payroll records, a service agreement with the Gibraltar operating subsidiary, office lease, local director appointments and management-meeting minutes; (iv) a legal opinion from a Gibraltar-licensed advocate confirming the entity's commercial purpose in the logistics-telematics SaaS vertical.
Legacy bearer-share or share-warrant provisions in the Bermuda Exempted Company charter triggering adverse media and enhanced due diligence

Root cause: Legacy bearer-share or share-warrant provisions in the Bermuda Exempted Company charter triggering adverse media and enhanced due diligence

Defense evidence: Submit: (i) Bermuda Companies Act 1981 compliance confirmation that no bearer shares are outstanding; (ii) a certified true extract of the Register of Members; (iii) board resolution irrevocably renouncing any bearer-share facility and adopting electronic share registration; (iv) confirmation from the Bermuda corporate administrator that the share register is fully digital, auditable and sanction-screenable.
AML classification of ISIC 6201 real-time telematics SaaS as crypto-asset or blockchain-adjacent activity by generic underwriting filters

Root cause: AML classification of ISIC 6201 real-time telematics SaaS as crypto-asset or blockchain-adjacent activity by generic underwriting filters

Defense evidence: Provide: (i) signed enterprise SaaS subscription agreement and sample invoices evidencing logistics-fleet end-customers; (ii) ISO 27001 certificate and SOC 2 Type II report; (iii) ISIC 6201 / NAICS 488490 industry-mapping certificate from an accepted KYC classification vendor; (iv) twelve-month clean payment-trail report via Stripe Billing and regulated merchant-acquiring banks.
Zero minimum capital and absence of audited historical financial statements in the exempted-company file

Root cause: Zero minimum capital and absence of audited historical financial statements in the exempted-company file

Defense evidence: Deliver: (i) 12-month audited management accounts prepared by a recognised Gibraltar/Bermuda CPA firm; (ii) shareholder source-of-funds evidence comprising original SWIFT MT103 credit notifications from the ultimate shareholder's UK or EU bank; (iii) a capital-injection resolution and asset-management declaration confirming the subscription consideration was wired exclusively through regulated banking channels.
CRS/FATCA tax-residency ambiguity for a Bermuda tax-exempt entity with management and control exercised from Gibraltar or the UK

Root cause: CRS/FATCA tax-residency ambiguity for a Bermuda tax-exempt entity with management and control exercised from Gibraltar or the UK

Defense evidence: Produce: (i) Gibraltar tax-residency certificate and CRS self-certification (Form CRS 612); (ii) FATCA GIIN registration or a 'no US threshold persons' declaration; (iii) Bermuda Tax Exemption Certificate issued under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act; (iv) a UBO register extract showing each individual with >25% ownership together with UK HMRC and Gibraltar Income Tax Office tax reference numbers.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing: equity injection or subordinated shareholder loan from the Bermuda ultimate parent to the Gibraltar treasury entity through SWIFT MT103. Wire protocol: sending bank must complete field 56A (Intermediary: a UK clearer such as Barclays London or HSBC London branch), field 57A (Account With: the Gibraltar beneficiary bank), full beneficiary legal name, IBAN/account number, and the payment narrative referencing the corporate resolution and share-certificate number. Recommended purpose information: UK/GB remittance code F0001 for share capital or a lowercase 'C' category for capital transfers; for euro or dollar injections use SWIFT narrative 'CAPITAL SUBSCRIPTION' or 'SHAREHOLDER LOAN DISBURSEMENT' with the underlying subscription agreement identifier. Verification package: board resolution approving the injection, executed subscription agreement, updated Register of Members, SWIFT MT103 confirmation, and proof-of-electronic-transfer (PDE) from the corresponding bank dated consistently with the CRS self-certification and the Bermuda corporate filings.
