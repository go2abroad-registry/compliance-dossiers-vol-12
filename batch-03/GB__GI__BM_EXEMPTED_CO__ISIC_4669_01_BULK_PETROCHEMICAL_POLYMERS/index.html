Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office; customs competence: HM Customs Gibraltar. Official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation; electronic filing portal: https://ehq.gibraltar.gov.gi. UK HMRC is not competent for Gibraltar domestic taxes.

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda exempted company is not a Gibraltar-incorporated entity and is not automatically required to obtain a Gibraltar TIN. Mandatory registration arises only if the company establishes a place of business, a branch, or exercises central management and control in Gibraltar. Procedure: (1) register as an overseas company with the Gibraltar Companies Registry; (2) submit a notice of chargeability to the Income Tax Office and obtain an Income Tax Reference Number/Assessment Number; (3) if physical imports or exports are cleared through Gibraltar, register with HM Customs Gibraltar as a customs trader and obtain a Customs Trader Identification Number. No VAT ID is available because Gibraltar does not operate a VAT/GST regime.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone (offshore non-resident Bermuda exempted company holding a Bermuda Tax Exemption Certificate); the entity is entity-level rather than pass-through but, absent Gibraltar management/control or a Gibraltar permanent establishment, it has no Gibraltar-source taxable income. If Gibraltar tax residence were established, the classification would become Entity_Level_Taxation on Gibraltar-source profits. — A Bermuda exempted company is not a Gibraltar-incorporated entity and is not automatically required to obtain a Gibraltar TIN. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (offshore non-resident Bermuda exempted company holding a Bermuda Tax Exemption Certificate); the entity is entity-level rather than pass-through but, absent Gibraltar management/control or a Gibraltar permanent establishment, it has no Gibraltar-source taxable income. If Gibraltar tax residence were established, the classification would become Entity_Level_Taxation on Gibraltar-source profits.

Available election forms

No United States-style check-the-box election exists in Gibraltar or Bermuda. Available elections/protections include: (i) application by the Bermuda exempted company to the Bermuda Minister of Finance for a Tax Exemption Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966; (ii) for a Gibraltar branch, election of applicable treaty treatment where Gibraltar treaty protection is available; and (iii) use of OECD-consistent accounting/tax elections under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act. Hybrid mismatch arrangements must be disclosed and are subject to counteraction under Gibraltar anti-hybrid rules.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar does not operate VAT, GST or sales/use tax. The only indirect levies are customs and excise duties applied at importation to specified categories of goods, principally alcohol, tobacco, fuel and vehicles.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable. There is no Gibraltar VAT/GST turnover threshold. A customs trader/declarant registration is required for any commercial import/export clearance; no turnover threshold applies for general excise registration except de-minimis non-commercial consignments.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

There is no Gibraltar VAT zero-rating because Gibraltar does not impose VAT/GST. Goods physically exported from Gibraltar are outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect tax and may be relieved from applicable customs/excise duty upon proof of export. Cross-border supplies of virgin polyethylene and polypropylene granules to non-Gibraltar buyers are not subject to Gibraltar indirect tax; the functional equivalent of zero-rating is the 'outside the scope of Gibraltar tax' treatment for exports and offshore services.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Gibraltar Single Administrative Document / customs declaration)
  • Bill of Lading or air waybill evidencing export from Gibraltar
  • Commercial invoice and packing list matching the customs declaration
  • Bank settlement evidence (SWIFT MT103 or letter-of-credit negotiation record) serving as the inbound remittance certificate
  • Product specification and HS classification documentation for virgin polyethylene/polypropylene granules (HS 3901/3902) supporting the tariff declaration

Gibraltar has no real-time electronic invoice clearance, SII-type reporting, continuous transaction controls, or digital fiscal stamping for ordinary B2B invoices. Customs import/export declarations are lodged electronically using the Single Administrative Document (SAD) through HM Customs Gibraltar or a licensed customs agent; no government-fiscal API endorsement of invoices is imposed.

HM Customs Gibraltar and the Income Tax Office may review export declarations for up to six years. False or misleading export declarations made to obtain export relief can lead to seizure of goods, penalties of up to three times the duty evaded, interest, and prosecution in the Gibraltar Magisterial or Supreme Court. A company found to have in substance maintained Gibraltar management and control may be assessed retrospectively on the whole profits, and abusive tax-avoidance arrangements may be counteracted under Gibraltar anti-avoidance/GAAR rules with fines and clawback of unavailable exemption and treaty benefits.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility applies for both capital and current account transactions in Gibraltar. There are no exchange controls, no surrender requirements, no withholding taxes on shareholder dividends, interest, royalties or management fees, and profits of a Bermuda Exempted Company designated non-resident are freely remittable. The GIP is tightly pegged at 1:1 to GBP, while USD and EUR clearing is freely available through the international banking system. The practical hurdles are regulatory rather than FX-technical: (i) obtaining the BMA non-resident exchange-control designation on the Bermuda registry to permit unrestricted external-currency accounts; (ii) managing the economic substance and management-and-control question if the exempted company is directed from Gibraltar, where a foreign-company registration under Gibraltar Companies Act and a GFSC-regulated local presence will be expected; and (iii) overcoming residual correspondent-bank de-risking sensitivity caused by the offshore exempted-company label and the prior FATF grey-listing of Gibraltar (June 2022 to June 2023). No profit-repatriation lock-in, capitalisation consent or advance exchange-control approval is required for routine trade flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Nil under Gibraltar law: the Financial Services Act and Banking Act impose no statutory timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts; exporters may retain 100% of USD, EUR or any other foreign-currency proceeds in on-island or offshore accounts. Under Bermuda law, an exempted company that has obtained non-resident exchange-control status may retain all foreign-currency export proceeds in unrestricted external accounts and faces no obligation to convert into BMD or to repatriate funds within any prescribed period. The operative compliance requirement is documentary: each export settlement must be matched to the underlying trade documents (commercial invoice, bill of lading, packing list, LC documentation, quality certificate) in order to evidence a genuine goods movement and satisfy trade-based money laundering controls.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the integrated regulator for banking, investment services and e-money in Gibraltar. The Government of Gibraltar, acting through the Ministry of Finance under the Currency Notes Act 1934, is the issuing monetary authority for the Gibraltar pound (GIP), which circulates at a fixed 1:1 parity with Bank of England sterling. The Bank of England and UK CHAPS infrastructure operate as the de facto clearing and settlement authority for GBP-denominated payment legs. For the Bermuda Exempted Company's home registry, the Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) administers the residual Exchange Control Act 1972 regime and grants the non-resident exchange-control designation that permits unrestricted foreign-currency accounts.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low-to-Medium for primary treasury use. Gibraltar maintains its own GFSC-authorised electronic-money regime under the Electronic Money (Gibraltar) Regulations 2019; post-Brexit, EEA e-money licences no longer passport into Gibraltar, and a non-Gibraltar EMI cannot lawfully provide a live settlement account to a Gibraltar-managed company without either a GFSC authorisation or an arrangement under the UK/Gibraltar close financial-services relationship. In practice, offshore digital EMIs such as Wise, Revolut Business or Modulr are acceptable only for low-value operational expenses, staff reimbursements and contingency payments; they cannot provide the SWIFT MT103/MT700 documentary-letter-of-credit rails required for bulk petrochemical polymer settlement. In addition, most EMIs classify wholesale commodity trading as a high-risk TBML sector and will decline full-stream treasury flows. Recommended treasury architecture: use EMIs strictly as a supplementary ancillary rail; hold the primary clearing and LC relationship at a GFSC-licensed commercial bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder capital is injected by SWIFT MT103, with an MT202 cover leg through the London or New York correspondent, from the Bermuda parent or ultimate beneficial owner account to the Gibraltar bank account. Field 70 must carry the exact remittance reference SHAREHOLDER CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION - [ENTITY NAME] - MEMORANDUM OF ASSOCIATION. The beneficiary Gibraltar bank verifies the originator against the approved mandate and source-of-funds file and credits funds value-date T+1. For USD legs cleared via New York, complete OFAC and US-AML originator-beneficiary screening and use the US Federal BPA code applicable to inward direct investment in equity. For GBP legs use CHAPS where the counterpart is UK-based, and for EUR use SEPA or SWIFT with an ICC trade-purpose reference. The capital injection must be accompanied by: a board resolution of the Bermuda Exempted Company; an updated register of members; BMA non-resident exchange-control confirmation; CRS/FATCA tax classification; and a funds-request certificate signed by two authorised directors. Funds may first be credited to a separately designated Capital Escrow Account and deployed to the operational trading account only after the bank releases them against the approved trading plan.Supplier payments for virgin polyethylene and polypropylene granules are executed through three coordinated rails. First, SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers for open-account and advance-payment terms, with the remittance field citing the commercial invoice and contract number. Second, irrevocable documentary letters of credit (sight or usance 90/120 days), issued by the Gibraltar bank via SWIFT MT700 and negotiated through the seller's advising bank under UCP 600, with a full document set comprising commercial invoice, bill of lading, packing list, certificate of origin and quality certificate on a documents-against-payment basis. Third, standby letters of credit issued via SWIFT MT760 as performance bonds or advance-payment guarantees under URDG 758. For low-value high-frequency disbursements, a multi-currency operational account using same-day CHAPS, SEPA Instant and Fedwire rails is maintained. The documentary LC remains the trade backbone for bulk petrochemical settlements because it provides title control, supplier performance security and a bank-grade audit trail. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Gibraltar does not prescribe a general Local File/Master File monetary threshold; however the Commissioner of Income Tax may require OECD-compliant transfer pricing documentation where controlled transactions exceed GBP 1,000,000 in an accounting period, particularly for intra-group procurement and distribution of petrochemical polymers. Bermuda ESR substance records and annual economic substance declarations are mandatory for any Bermuda exempted company conducting distribution and service centre activity.

Economic substance scope: Both the Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations 2019 and the Bermuda Economic Substance Amendment Act 2018 apply. The wholesale distribution of virgin polyethylene and polypropylene granules is a relevant activity where goods are distributed from or through the jurisdiction or purchased from related parties for distribution. The Bermuda Exempted Company must demonstrate adequate physical premises, full-time employees, board meetings, and core income-generating activities in the jurisdiction where substance is claimed, and must file the relevant annual economic substance declaration. Failure to satisfy substance requirements exposes the entity to information requests, penalties, and possible strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Avoidance

Statutory sanction: Penalties of up to 100% of the tax undercharged, daily default penalties, late-payment interest at 8% per annum, criminal fines, production orders, and imprisonment on indictment for fraudulent evasion.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly participate in or instruct tax evasion are personally liable for civil restitution and penalties; the corporate veil will be pierced. Fraudulent trading or deliberate misrepresentation may result in director disqualification, travel bans, asset freezes, and imprisonment.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Bermuda ESR penalties for non-compliance with the annual substance declaration and information obligations; Gibraltar ESR penalties and information-exchange measures; possible striking-off by the Registrar in Bermuda or enforcement action by the Gibraltar Income Tax Office.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for false annual declarations, subject to director disqualification, restriction orders, and civil claims by liquidators for wrongful trading where the entity operates as an artificial shell.
AML and Trade-Based Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Criminal money laundering offences punishable by substantial fines and imprisonment; civil asset forfeiture; regulatory enforcement against the entity for breaches of Gibraltar anti-money laundering legislation; freezing orders on trade proceeds.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers who fail to report suspicious transactions or who facilitate trade-based money laundering face personal criminal liability, travel bans, restraining orders, and director disqualification; the corporate veil will be pierced where the entity is used as a sham instrument.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing and Hidden Permanent Establishment

Statutory sanction: Arm's length re-adjustment of profit, disallowance of excessive deductions, interest on underpaid tax, tax-geared penalties up to 100% of the understatement, and denial of treaty benefits for lack of beneficial ownership or substance.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve or execute sham transfer pricing structures are liable for resulting corporate tax liabilities and penalties where they acted knowingly; they may also face disqualification, travel restrictions, and civil damages for breach of fiduciary duty in insolvency.
