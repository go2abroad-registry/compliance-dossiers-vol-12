Gibraltar Companies Act 2014 (ss. 5-8, 60, 112); Income Tax Act 2010 (territorial exemption for income arising and derived outside Gibraltar); legacy exempt-company principles under the repealed Companies Act 1930 s. 5, applied by equivalence to replicate the Bermuda Exempted Company form (limited liability, non-resident ownership, tax-neutral international status).

Formation / constitutional filings

Form CO-1 (Application for Incorporation) together with the Memorandum and Articles of Association; Form CO-1A for name pre-clearance.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office (ITO) of HM Government of Gibraltar, 6-8 Whitfield House, 20 Cathedral Square, Gibraltar, operating the ITO e-Return customer portal under www.gibraltar.gov.gi. Corporate branch registration is administered by the Gibraltar Companies Registry under the Companies Act 2014. Customs registrations and electronic declarations are administered by HM Customs Gibraltar through the ASYCUDA World single window.

E-government filing portal

(1) Register the Bermuda Exempted Company as an External Company with the Gibraltar Companies Registry within one month of establishing a Gibraltar place of business, filing a certified copy of the Bermuda Certificate of Incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, a Gibraltar registered office address, and details of the local authorised representative. (2) Following issuance of the Companies Registry external company number, file Form for company tax registration with the ITO to obtain a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN). (3) Obtain a Gibraltar Customs registration code from HM Customs (no economic threshold) before the first import or export declaration. (4) If employing personnel, register as an employer with the ITO for PAYE-type instalments and with the Gibraltar Social Insurance Authority. No minimum capital applies to the branch; typical processing time is 3 to 10 business days; non-resident ownership is permitted and no licence is required for ISIC 5229 project cargo logistics.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope - Gibraltar applies a territorial corporate tax system: a non-resident Bermuda Exempted Company operating through a Gibraltar branch is chargeable to Gibraltar income tax at 12.5% only on profits accruing in or derived from Gibraltar. The Bermuda parent itself remains within the Tax_Exempt_Zone category in Bermuda (0% corporate tax, annual government fee only), and foreign-source profits that are not attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment are outside the Gibraltar tax charge. The entity is not eligible for pass-through treatment in Gibraltar.

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no US-style check-the-box or entity classification election. Available mechanism: an election under the Income Tax Act 2010 for qualifying overseas income treatment and group loss offset, an application under the Economic Substance (Company and Private Foundations) Act 2019 for geographically mobile activities (including headquarters and distribution and service centre business) subject to substance tests, a closed 'Exempt Company' election unavailable to new entrants, and customs elections such as inward processing relief and temporary admission for project cargo. No hybrid mismatch election exists; the Income Tax (Hybrid) Regulations 2019 apply OECD hybrid mismatch rules and may neutralise mismatches involving the Bermudian entity. All elections require contemporaneous substance documentation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST applies in Gibraltar. Indirect taxation comprises Gibraltar import duties under the Customs Ordinance and the Consumption Tax Act 2010 applied only to alcohol, tobacco and mineral oils. OOG heavy-haul project cargo logistics services performed in or from Gibraltar are not subject to any Gibraltar output VAT, service tax, or sales and use tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT/GST registration threshold exists because Gibraltar levies no VAT/GST. Any person importing or exporting goods must hold a Gibraltar Customs registration code with no economic turnover threshold before the first declaration. Consumption Tax registration is mandatory for importers of excisable goods without a threshold. No de minimis turnover relief applies; simplified declarations are available only for low-value non-controlled consignments.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

No VAT zero-rating mechanism applies because Gibraltar imposes no VAT on any supply. Exported goods are outside the scope of Gibraltar import duty and Consumption Tax, and export of excisable goods qualifies for Consumption Tax drawback. OOG heavy-haul services rendered for non-resident clients are outside Gibraltar indirect tax. For income tax purposes, export service profits remain prima facie taxable at 12.5% if the profit accrues in or derives from Gibraltar; to rely on territorial exclusion the taxpayer must evidence that contracts, service performance, and decision-making occur without a Gibraltar source and that proceeds are received outside Gibraltar or only remitted under qualifying foreign-source rules. No fiscal refund algorithm exists for exports; import duty overpayments and Consumption Tax drawbacks are recovered through customs error and drawback procedures verified by HM Customs.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World electronic export entry)
  • Bill of Lading or Sea Waybill evidencing out-of-gauge project cargo shipment
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT credit advice evidencing repatriation of export proceeds to the Gibraltar branch account
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List with HS classification, Incoterms, dimensions, and lashing details
  • Project contract, milestone schedule, or Letter of Credit substantiating service scope and price

Gibraltar does not mandate continuous transaction controls (CTC), real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or government-authorised e-invoicing for logistics invoices. Customs clearance is fully electronic through the ASYCUDA World single window requiring API/e-file declaration submission with trader reference numbers, prescribed data sets (HS classification, weight, value, Incoterms, counterparty identifiers) and, for controlled goods, pre-arrival lodging. Commercial invoices must substantiate the customs declaration dataset, and Authorised Economic Operator (AEO) status is optional for expedited clearance.

HM Customs and the ITO may open audits on export and income records for up to 6 years, extended to 15 years in cases of fraud or wilful default. Failure to substantiate the export nexus triggers retrospective assessment with import duty and Consumption Tax clawed back from the original due date plus interest. Misdeclaration penalties reach 100% of the evaded duty, increasing to 150% and criminal fines with imprisonment (up to 7 years) for fraudulent evasion or false declarations. For income tax, unsubstantiated export services are assessed on Gibraltar-source profits at 12.5% with penalties up to 120% of the additional tax, daily continuing penalties, and public naming of defaulters. The Bermuda Exempted Company parent may also face Economic Substance Act penalties on geographically mobile activities, including fines up to GBP 10,000 and information exchange with Bermuda and HMRC.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar operates a fully liberalised exchange-control regime. Both current-account and capital-account transactions are freely convertible; there are no restrictions on profit remittances, dividend distributions, or intercompany settlements. The Gibraltar pound is fully backed by sterling reserves and redeemable at par, eliminating convertibility risk between GIP and GBP. Funds held in USD, EUR, or GBP may be repatriated without central-bank approval, subject solely to standard AML onboarding documentation and any applicable Gibraltar tax clearance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender requirement exists. There is no mandatory conversion ratio and no specified timeline for repatriating export proceeds. Inbound freight and project-cargo logistics revenues may be retained indefinitely in any permitted current-account currency, and offshore earnings of the Bermuda exempted company are not subject to Gibraltar exchange-control intervention.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England exercises monetary authority for the sterling zone; the Government of Gibraltar issues Gibraltar pound banknotes/coins at a fixed 1:1 parity with UK sterling. Prudential oversight of Gibraltar-licensed banks is conducted by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC), which functions as the de facto central supervisory authority for banking, anti-money laundering, and financial stability within the territory.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: HIGH for ancillary collection and disbursement rails via UK-FCA-authorized EMIs (Wise Business, Revolut Business, Airwallex) that are recognized in Gibraltar for electronic-money services. Legal constraint: EMIs are not licensed deposit-taking banks; they cannot issue letters of credit, provide performance-bond guarantees, or hold settlement float as bank deposits. The EMI channels should therefore be structured as secondary clearing rails only, with the primary LC-issuance and milestone-wire capacity anchored at a licensed Gibraltar commercial bank.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000

Economic substance scope: Applicable. The Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations apply to any tax-resident company carrying on relevant activities. Project cargo logistics is treated as a distribution and service centre activity where the company performs core revenue-generating activities in Gibraltar. The Bermuda Exempted Company must demonstrate that it is directed and managed in Gibraltar, maintains adequate physical premises and full-time employees, and incurs expenditure proportionate to the logistics income booked in Gibraltar.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Unlimited fine and seizure of assets upon conviction; in addition to the full assessment of evaded tax, the court may impose a penalty of up to 200% of the evaded amount and disqualify the offender from acting as a director for up to 15 years.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax and penalties where they knowingly participate in or consent to the evasion. A travel ban preventing exit from Gibraltar may be imposed pending criminal restitution, and personal civil liability extends to losses suffered by the tax authority.
Abuse of Exempt Company Status

Statutory sanction: A protective assessment plus tax at 10% on the audited profits; penalties of up to £100,000 for failure to provide information and fines for incorrect returns under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act, with possible strike-off and disqualification of the company.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal penalties for causing the company to rely on a sham exempt status. Where the company is adjudged to be a facade, the court will pierce the corporate veil, render directors personally liable for the company's tax liabilities, and may impose overseas travel restrictions pending settlement.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Non-compliance

Statutory sanction: Enforcement action under the Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations: financial penalty of up to £100,000, mandatory disclosure to the UK HMRC through exchange of information, and recharacterisation of profits to a deemed Gibraltar permanent establishment with a 10% tax charge and late-payment interest.

Director personal liability: Directors can be personally liable if the company is insolvent and continued trading without economic substance amounts to fraudulent trading under Gibraltar law. Liability may include civil restitution, asset attachment, and a court-imposed travel ban during the investigation.
AML and Sanctions Violation

Statutory sanction: Civil penalties under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 (Gibraltar) up to £5 million, plus criminal conviction on indictment with imprisonment for up to 14 years and confiscation of the proceeds of the offending transactions.

Director personal liability: Directors who consent to or connive in money laundering are individually criminally liable, may be subject to travel bans, have their personal assets restrained by court order, and be prohibited from managing any Gibraltar-regulated business.
