Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar: H.M. Government of Gibraltar, Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax), 6-8 Bonavia House, Cathedral Square, Gibraltar; online portal https://www.tax.gov.gi. Companies registration: Gibraltar Companies House (Registrar of Companies), https://www.companieshouse.gi. Bermuda (home state): Bermuda Registrar of Companies and Ministry of Finance, https://www.roc.gov.bm.

E-government filing portal

Step 1 - Maintain Bermuda Exempted Company status: file Annual Return and Economic Substance Declaration with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies and pay the annual Government fee (circa USD 2,640); no income tax registration arises in Bermuda. Step 2 - If managed and controlled from Gibraltar, apply to the Gibraltar Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (Form GITA company registration) within 90 days of commencing Gibraltar-source business; non-resident companies managed outside Gibraltar are not obliged to hold a Gibraltar TIN. Step 3 - If physical goods cross the UK frontier, obtain a GB EORI number via the HMRC portal. Step 4 - If UK taxable supplies exceed GBP 90,000 in a rolling 12-month period, complete HMRC VAT registration at https://www.gov.uk/vat-registration. Step 5 - For LME copper warrant trading, open a documentary LC/trading account at an approved bank; no additional tax identifier is required for warrant transfers.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone - The Bermuda Exempted Company holds a statutory tax-exemption certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 (sections 5-6), which guarantees exemption from Bermuda income tax until 31 December 2035 (extendable to 2050). In Gibraltar the classification is territorial: a company not managed and controlled in Gibraltar is non-resident and liable only on Gibraltar-source income, leaving foreign trading profits (LME physical arbitrage) outside Gibraltar tax. If management and control is exercised in Gibraltar, the entity becomes Gibraltar-resident and is taxable at 12.5% on worldwide trading income, with a 15% Pillar Two top-up only if the group's annual consolidated revenue exceeds EUR 750 million.

Available election forms

Bermuda: irrevocable election to remain an exempted undertaking with an annual Economic Substance filing for distribution and service centre activities (ISIC 4662). Gibraltar: residence election by notice to the Income Tax Office based on central management and control; a certificate of non-residence can be issued where control is exercised abroad. No check-the-box partnership passthrough election exists under Gibraltar or Bermuda law; corporations are opaque taxpayers. OECD Global Minimum Tax (15%) applies mandatorily to in-scope MNE groups from 1 January 2024 and cannot be opted out; hybrid mismatch rules are embedded in the Gibraltar Income Tax Act.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or sales and use tax in Gibraltar (outside EU VAT/customs territory) or Bermuda. Gibraltar levies only customs duties and excise on importation through its Border Agency; UK VAT may arise solely where physical goods are imported into Great Britain or where UK taxable supplies are made.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable in Gibraltar or Bermuda (no VAT regime). UK VAT compulsory registration threshold: GBP 90,000 of taxable turnover in a rolling 12-month period (2024/25); voluntary registration is available to copper cathode traders seeking input VAT recovery. A UK EORI number is mandatory for any GB customs declaration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

UK VAT: 0% applies under VATA 1994 s.30(6) and Schedule 8 Groups 1-2 to physically exported goods, provided the supply is made to another country, the goods leave the UK within statutory time limits (normally three months), and the supplier holds valid official HMRC export evidence (CDS/CHIEF declaration). For copper cathode arbitrage sold via LME warrant where the metal never enters the UK, the supply is outside the scope of UK VAT and no zero-rating relief is required. Gibraltar: exports are outside the Gibraltar indirect tax system and profits of a non-resident company from such exports remain foreign-source and non-taxable; the 0% rate concept does not arise because Gibraltar has no VAT. Where UK zero-rating is claimed, the cargo must be physically dispatched from the UK to a third-country destination, and the destination named on the bill of lading must match the contractual delivery terms.

  • HMRC/CDS Customs Export Declaration with Movement Reference Number or equivalent CHIEF declaration
  • Bill of Lading, Multimodal Transport Document or Air Waybill evidencing third-country arrival
  • Commercial Invoice, Packing List and weight/assay certificate for Grade A refined copper cathodes
  • LME warrant transfer / load-out order evidencing warehouse location outside the UK
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 confirming repatriation of export sale proceeds

None in Gibraltar or Bermuda - no real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or government API e-invoicing mandate exists. If UK VAT registered, HMRC Making Tax Digital (MTD) requires VAT returns to be submitted via API using MTD-compatible software; B2B e-invoicing is not yet compulsory in the UK. LME warrant transfers follow LME electronic warrant delivery and LME Clear settlement protocols, which are commercial procedures rather than fiscal clearance mechanisms.

HMRC may assess under-declared VAT under VATA 1994 s.73 with penalties up to 100% of the tax (Schedule 24 FA 2007), interest, and criminal prosecution under s.72; deliberate and concealed errors extend the assessment window to 20 years. Copper and metal trading are high-risk categories for MTIC/going-Rate fraud, enabling HMRC to require security, withhold refunds, and cancel EORI numbers. In Gibraltar, fraudulent evasion of corporation tax attracts penalties of up to 100% of the tax plus interest and fines. Bermuda revocation risk: a shell entity holding an unused exemption may lose its tax protection certificate; under the Economic Substance (Companies and Limited Partnerships) Act 2018, a first offence penalty can reach BMD 250,000 (repeat offences BMD 500,000), and false compliance declarations attract penalties up to BMD 1 million, with retrospective clawback of all tax benefits.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility on both current and capital accounts is maintained for GIP (pegged 1:1 to GBP) and all major floating currencies (USD, EUR, JPY, GBP). No exchange-control authorisation or profit-remittance licence is required. A Bermuda Exempted Company registered in Gibraltar may move capital and dividends freely, subject to standard GFSC/HMRC reporting thresholds and financial-crime screening. Tax-exempt status does not impede ordinary banking convertibility.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. No statutory export-receipts surrender timeline or mandatory conversion ratio is imposed on copper cathode export proceeds. Settlements may be received and held in any convertible currency. However, for deposits of USD/EUR export receipts exceeding the Enhanced Due Diligence threshold, banks require LME warrant, warehouse receipt, LC, or commercial invoice evidence to clear trade-circularity alerts.

Central bank supervision: Government of Gibraltar (Ministry of Finance) and Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC); the Bank of England (United Kingdom) provides the sterling monetary backstop. There is no separate foreign-exchange administration for Gibraltar.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: CONDITIONAL / MEDIUM-LOW for core LME and LC flows, but ACCEPTABLE for ancillary FX and payroll payments. Post-Brexit passporting constraints mean any EMI must hold a GFSC licence or authorised UK/EEA-equivalent e-money permission. Major incumbent trading counterparties and LME Clear will not accept EMI settlement as final clearing rails, so EMIs should be used only as a shadow treasury channel for small working-capital movements.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

10% Gibraltar Corporation Tax under the Corporate Tax Act 2021; the Bermuda parent entity holds a Tax Exemption Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 (protected through 31 March 2035) sustaining a 0% Bermuda tax rate.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% – Gibraltar levies no withholding tax on dividends paid to non-residents, and Bermuda imposes no withholding on distributions by an Exempted Company, permitting fully tax-free upstream remittance of copper trading profits to the ultimate UK/GIP ownership layer.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Nine months after the end of the chargeable accounting period; the Gibraltar tax year runs 1 July to 30 June and the trading company may elect an aligned fiscal period; quarterly instalment payments are required for companies with taxable profits above the large-payor threshold.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due on or before 31 January each year for the preceding calendar year; the annual government fee calculated by authorised share capital band must be paid to preserve the Tax Exemption Certificate.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Gibraltar Local File obligations are triggered when aggregate related-party transactions in a chargeable period exceed GIP 1,000,000; the Master File is mandatory where consolidated group revenue equals or exceeds GIP 750,000,000; Country-by-Country notification applies at the GIP 850,000,000 consolidated revenue threshold.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance (EU Exit) Regulations 2020 apply to any Gibraltar tax resident company undertaking a relevant activity; wholesale physical trading in non-ferrous copper cathodes is treated as a distribution and services activity requiring (i) direction and management exercised in Gibraltar, (ii) a physical office, locally resident staff and proportionate operating expenditure, and (iii) core income-generating activities including contract negotiation, logistics management, price risk hedging and LME warrant settlement performed in Gibraltar. The Bermuda Exempted Company may not rely solely on its Bermuda tax-exempt status: a board that merely ratifies UK-origin trading decisions creates a Gibraltar permanent establishment and subjects the entire arbitrage margin to Gibraltar corporation tax at 10%.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Deliberate Understatement

Statutory sanction: GTCA assessment to 10% tax on the concealed global arbitrage profit, a deliberate-conduct penalty of 100% of the tax, interest at 8% per annum, and criminal prosecution on indictment carrying an unlimited fine and imprisonment of up to 7 years under Gibraltar's Income Tax Act 2010 (Offences).

Director personal liability: Directors who directed the concealment are jointly and severally liable for the corporate tax shortfall; civil restitution orders attach to personal estates; Gibraltar's Director Disqualification Unit may obtain a disqualification order of up to 15 years and a travel ban pending criminal proceedings.
Shell Company / Artificial Substance Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Reclassification of the entity as fully Gibraltar tax resident, retrospective taxation of seven years of profits at 10%, annulment of the Bermuda Tax Exemption Certificate, a penalty of up to 200% of the recomputed tax, and an Economic Substance Regulations direction requiring immediate local substance or strike-off.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced where the exempted company is a mere façade; directors face civil liability for the recomputed tax, confiscation proceedings under the Proceeds of Crime Act, and imprisonment of up to 14 years for fraudulent trading contrary to the Gibraltar Companies Act.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: GTCA transfer pricing adjustment to the arm's-length price, disallowance of inflated cost deductions, an underpayment penalty of 50% of the adjusted tax, daily interest, a documentation penalty of GIP 250,000 where the local file is absent, and a secondary adjustment taxing the shifted margin as a deemed distribution.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed false contemporaneous pricing records are liable for negligent misstatement, jointly and severally liable for the adjusted tax, and exposed to disqualification and a five-year ban from acting as officers of any Gibraltar-regulated metals or commodities firm.
AML / Trade-Based Money Laundering (Copper Cathode Invoicing Fraud)

Statutory sanction: Forfeiture of trading assets, an unlimited fine on indictment, and imprisonment of up to 14 years under the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act 2015; the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit may freeze correspondent accounts and the company's EMI banking rails; failure to file a Suspicious Activity Report attracts a supervised penalty of GIP 2,000,000.

Director personal liability: Directors and the appointed Money Laundering Reporting Officer bear personal criminal liability for each undisclosed reportable transaction; travel bans and INTERPOL red notices follow any attempted departure; personal assets and shareholder distributions are clawed back through civil forfeiture.
