Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar Income Tax Office (H.M. Government of Gibraltar, Ministry of Finance) - issuing authority for the Income Tax Reference Number (TRN), tax residence certificates, and exemption confirmations; corporate registration is handled by the Registrar of Companies, Gibraltar Companies House, under the Companies Act 2014; electronic filing portal: iTax, accessible via https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax.

E-government filing portal

1) Incorporate the exempted vehicle as a private limited company under the Gibraltar Companies Act 2014, or if the Bermuda parent entity is pre-incorporated, register it as an External Company under Part 21 of that Act upon establishing a branch or place of business in Gibraltar. 2) Companies House transmits incorporation data to the Income Tax Office, which issues the Gibraltar TRN within 5-10 working days. 3) File the certified constitutional documents (certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles), registered office confirmation, and beneficial ownership particulars under the Gibraltar Beneficial Ownership Register before the TRN is issued. 4) Complete the iTax tax registration, including the non-residence declaration, the first accounting period, and the industry classification (ISIC 6201 - computer programming / software publishing; NAICS 511210). 5) Where treaty relief or contemporary banking/EMI onboarding requires proof of status, obtain an official Tax Residence Certificate or exemption letter from the Income Tax Office. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone - the Bermuda Exempted Company operating from Gibraltar is taxed under the territorial scope of the Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010, under which only income accruing in or derived from Gibraltar is chargeable. A non-resident-owned exempted company deriving all revenue from offshore AI avatar video generation SaaS customers, with no Gibraltar employees, no premises beyond the registered office, no Gibraltar-source trading activity, and no local economically significant presence is outside the Gibraltar income tax charge; Gibraltar imposes no capital gains tax, no wealth tax, no inheritance tax, and no withholding tax on dividends or royalties. Where the substance criteria are not met, the standard Gibraltar entity-level corporate rate of 12.5% applies to Gibraltar-source profits.

Available election forms

Gibraltar law has no US-style check-the-box election. Available statutory elections and confirmations: (1) Qualifying Company election under the Income Tax (Qualifying Companies) Regulations 2019, granting a reduced 2% effective rate on trading profits from approved Qualifying Categories (including information and communication technology, software development and head-office service activities), conditional on Economic Substance Requirements such as core income generating activities performed in Gibraltar, local employees, and local operating expenditure; (2) application for confirmation of non-residence / exemption for a non-resident-owned company carrying on no trade in Gibraltar; (3) election to compute liability on the territorial-source basis for foreign-source business income as provided in the Income Tax Act 2010; (4) hybrid mismatch outcomes are not elective - UK TIOPA 2010 Part 6A rules apply automatically where Gibraltar, Bermuda and the United Kingdom classify the entity differently, and the Gibraltar General Anti-Abuse Rule (Income Tax (Amendment) Act 2018) counteracts artificial mismatch schemes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST or Sales-and-Use Tax. Gibraltar is outside the EU VAT area and imposes no VAT or sales tax on digital services; its indirect tax system consists solely of Import Duty under the Customs and Excise Act 2015 (charged on physical goods at rates generally aligned with the UK Global Tariff) and stamp duty on limited transactions. AI avatar video generation SaaS exports fall entirely outside the indirect tax net.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT/GST registration threshold exists in Gibraltar pounds (GIP) or GBP because no VAT/GST is levied. For physical imports only, the trader must obtain a Gibraltar customs / EORI registration before the first import; there is no turnover threshold for that registration and no local-currency economic threshold triggering any indirect tax registration for digital services.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

VAT zero-rating is inapplicable because Gibraltar imposes no VAT and no export duty on digital services. The effective 0% tax outcome for ISIC 6201 cross-border AI avatar video generation is achieved through the territorial income rule. Eligibility conditions: (1) all customers are tax resident outside Gibraltar; (2) contracts are negotiated, executed and performed offshore, with board control exercised from Bermuda or the UK parent; (3) no Gibraltar fixed establishment, local staff or local data processing; (4) no Gibraltar-source marketing or sales enablement; (5) a complete substance and non-residence file is maintained; and (6) platform revenue settled through Stripe or Paddle is received into offshore or Gibraltar EMI bank accounts as foreign-source receipts. If any condition fails, the receipts are reclassified as Gibraltar-source and taxed at the standard 12.5% rate.

  • Customs Export Declaration - not applicable for digital services (no tangible goods movement; an N/A rationale is retained in the compliance file where EU or UK edge-node infrastructure is used)
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill - replaced by SaaS platform delivery logs, usage analytics and AI generation audit records evidencing offshore service delivery
  • Inward Remittance Certificate - bank or EMI settlement statements evidencing inbound export proceeds from Stripe and Paddle in GBP, USD or EUR
  • AI SaaS licensing agreement, order forms and commercial invoices for each export customer
  • Gibraltar substance and non-residence file, including registered office certificate, board minutes, non-residence declaration and core income generating activity records, substantiating the foreign-source classification

No real-time fiscal clearance, mandatory e-invoicing API, continuous transaction control or digital stamping regime applies to Gibraltar B2B or B2C digital services. Ordinary electronic invoices in GBP, USD or EUR satisfy tax record requirements. Physical goods declarations are lodged electronically through the Gibraltar Customs Management System, with the UK Customs Declaration Service used for UK-Gibraltar movements; no fiscal receipting interface, cryptographic stamping or government API clearance is required for the AIGC video platform.

The Gibraltar Income Tax Office may reopen assessments within six years, extended to twelve years where fraud or negligent conduct is established. Where the 0% / foreign-source classification is disallowed, the Commissioner retroactively claws back tax at the full 12.5% rate on gross service receipts, charges interest from the original due date, and may impose penalties of up to 100% of the tax evaded under the Income Tax Act 2010. Misused exempted company or Qualifying Company elections are revoked with retrospective effect, triggering daily default fines, director disqualification, and criminal prosecution for fraudulent declarations or tax evasion with fines and imprisonment. The Gibraltar General Anti-Abuse Rule and the UK-Gibraltar double taxation arrangement permit counteraction of artificial export schemes, and the OECD / EU substance review procedure can compel the winding up of the protected exempted entity where economic substance requirements are not satisfied.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility on both current and capital account. Gibraltar has maintained no exchange-control restrictions since the United Kingdom abolished exchange controls in 1979; the GIP is freely exchangeable for GBP at parity. Dividends, profit remittances, intercompany loans, royalties, and capital repatriation by a Bermuda Exempted Company require no GFSC or Government of Gibraltar approval. Multi-currency accounts (USD, EUR, GBP, GIP) are unrestricted. The Bermuda Exempted Company's 'exempted' designation under Bermuda's Exchange Control Act 1972 confers non-resident status for Bermuda currency-control purposes, freeing the treasury to hold GIP, GBP, USD, and EUR balances without restriction in either jurisdiction. The only practical hurdles are AML/CFT source-of-funds verification and counterparty due diligence by the receiving financial institution.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Gibraltar imposes no statutory timeline for repatriation, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no surrender obligation for export, SaaS licensing, or AIGC software subscription proceeds. USD and EUR revenue may remain indefinitely in foreign-currency nostro or settlement accounts. Reporting obligations are limited to standard AML/CFT thresholds, the wire-transfer travel rule (full originator/beneficiary data on every SWIFT MT103), and suspicious-activity reporting under the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and financial sanctions regulations.

Central bank supervision: The Government of Gibraltar (Minister for Finance) is the sole note-issuing authority under the Currency Notes Act 1934, operating through the Gibraltar Currency Note Fund, which holds sterling reserves at the Bank of England. Gibraltar has no independent central bank. The Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC), acting under the Financial Services Act 2019, is the prudential regulator and licensing authority for all credit institutions and e-money issuers in Gibraltar. The Gibraltar Pound (GIP) is fixed at parity with GBP (1 GIP = 1 GBP), and sterling is legal tender alongside GIP.

Stripe Fit: High

High feasibility with licensing caveats. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Country-by-Country Report: mandatory for Gibraltar constituent entities of groups with consolidated group revenue of EUR 750m or more; Local File and Master File: required for such groups and for smaller groups generally where controlled transactions exceed EUR 1m in aggregate per fiscal year; SMEs (fewer than 250 employees and either turnover not exceeding EUR 50m or balance sheet total not exceeding EUR 43m) are exempt from formal TP documentation provided the arm's length price is still applied and evidenced.

Economic substance scope: The Income Tax (Economic Substance) Regulations 2019 apply to tax-resident companies carrying on relevant activities, including IP holding, distribution and service centres and headquarters. For an AI video generation business holding proprietary code, models and brand IP, the company must perform core income-generating activities (strategic IP development, exploitation, risk management and alteration decisions) in Gibraltar, maintain a physical office, employ adequate full-time staff, and incur proportionate operating expenditure. Board meetings must be held in Gibraltar with real quorum and minutes. Failure to satisfy ESR results in penalty up to GBP 10,000 per annum, automatic exchange of information with EU/UK tax authorities, re-characterisation of profits and possible strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Deliberate Under-Declaration

Statutory sanction: Financial penalty up to 100% of the tax understated plus interest at 1.5% per month; on indictment, an unlimited fine and imprisonment for up to 7 years under s.196 of the Income Tax Act 2010.

Director personal liability: Directors who directed or consented to the evasion are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax and penalties; courts may impose travel bans, passport surrender and asset freezing orders as bail conditions, and a disqualification order of up to 15 years.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: ESR penalty up to GBP 10,000 per relevant activity per year, mandatory data exchange with the UK and EU, re-assessment of profits at the 12.5% rate by disregarding the s.44GA election, and strike-off for non-filing.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal liability for penalties where the company is struck off with assets distributed, civil restitution for unpaid taxes, disqualification under the Company Directors Disqualification Act, and exclusion orders restricting re-entry to Gibraltar.
AML / Proceeds of Crime Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and Criminal Finances Act (Gibraltar): fine up to GBP 500,000 and imprisonment up to 10 years for money laundering and failure to report suspicious activity.

Director personal liability: Individual criminal liability attaches where the director knew or suspected money laundering and failed to make an internal report; confiscation orders, travel bans, and disqualification from financial-services roles apply on conviction.
Withholding Tax Evasion and False DTA Claim

Statutory sanction: Fraudulent DTA claim penalties of up to 200% of the tax evaded, publication of the company's name in the Official Gazette, and criminal prosecution carrying imprisonment for up to 7 years.

Director personal liability: Directors are liable as trustees de son tort for withholding taxes deducted or payable and not remitted; civil restitution, interest at the statutory rate, and travel restrictions apply pending settlement.
