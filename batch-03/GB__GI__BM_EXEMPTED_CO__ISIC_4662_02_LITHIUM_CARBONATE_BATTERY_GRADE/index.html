Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office (direct taxation) and HM Customs Gibraltar (indirect/customs). Official e-portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/tax

E-government filing portal

The Bermuda Exempted Company is not automatically registered in Gibraltar. To obtain a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN), it must file the prescribed income tax registration application with the Income Tax Office, supported by its Bermuda incorporation certificate, constitutional documents, register of directors/shareholders, and evidence of intended Gibraltar activities. If the entity establishes a registrable presence in Gibraltar, branch registration with Companies House Gibraltar is required before the Income Tax Office issues the TRN. No VAT ID is issued because Gibraltar does not impose value added tax; an importer/exporter account with HM Customs Gibraltar is obtained separately.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No elective check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch opt-out is available under Gibraltar law. A Bermuda exempted company remains outside Gibraltar tax only to the extent it is not managed/controlled in Gibraltar and derives no Gibraltar-source income. Conversely, central management and control exercised in Gibraltar would subject it to entity-level Gibraltar corporation tax. Mandatory hybrid mismatch and anti-avoidance rules may recharacterise the entity or disallow deductions where an arrangement results in double non-taxation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST; Gibraltar applies customs duties and excise duties as the principal indirect taxes on goods

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable. There is no VAT/GST registration threshold because Gibraltar has no VAT/GST. Importers/exporters require a customs registration/account with HM Customs Gibraltar; the local currency is the Gibraltar pound (GIP).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Exports from Gibraltar are outside the scope of any VAT/GST charge, and there is no VAT export zero-rating or input VAT refund mechanism. Documentary proof of export and destination is nonetheless required for customs relief, suspension, or drawback and to establish foreign-source treatment for direct tax purposes.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Certificate of analysis showing battery-grade lithium carbonate (HS 2836.91)

No mandatory real-time fiscal clearance, API e-invoicing, or digital stamping is imposed. Commercial electronic invoices are accepted. Goods declarations for customs clearance must be filed electronically with HM Customs Gibraltar, and records must be retained for the period required under Gibraltar tax and customs legislation.

Fraudulent export declarations or failure to substantiate the export and repatriation of proceeds may trigger HM Customs and Income Tax Office investigations, retrospective withdrawal of customs relief or duty drawbacks, forfeiture, monetary penalties calculated on a duty-geared basis, and prosecution. Misdescription of battery-grade lithium carbonate (HS 2836.91) or diversion of goods within Gibraltar after claiming export relief is treated as an abusive transaction and may result in full duty clawback plus penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar maintains full current and capital account convertibility. There are no statutory exchange controls, no prior approval requirement for FX conversion, no limits on non-resident payment flows, and no profit remittance hurdles. The Bermuda Exempted Company may hold, convert, and remit GBP, USD, EUR, CNY, or any freely traded currency without central bank licence. Remittance of dividends, capital repayments, or shareholder-loan repayments from Gibraltar is unrestricted and free of withholding. Practical constraints exist only at bank internal AML policy level and at the counterparty jurisdiction level, notably onshore CNY convertibility which requires settlement through synthetic/CNH mechanisms or NDF structures.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Gibraltar and the UK sterling zone impose no mandatory surrender, repatriation timeline, or conversion ratio on export receipts. Export proceeds from resale of battery-grade lithium carbonate may be retained offshore in any currency and in full, without statutory timing duties. The only administrative obligations are bank-level balance-of-payments statistical reporting and demonstration of Gibraltar economic substance and proper booking of trading income, which the bank will assess to prevent de-risking and to satisfy GFSC anti-money-laundering expectations.

Central bank supervision: Financial Services Commission (GFSC) of Gibraltar, exercising prudential and conduct supervision under the Financial Services Act 2019 and the Banking Act 2019; note issuance authority rests with HM Government of Gibraltar under the Currency Notes Act 1934; the monetary anchor is the Bank of England as the Gibraltar pound circulates at a fixed 1:1 parity with pound sterling within the UK sterling zone.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Rating: Medium, with material constraints. GFSC-licensed electronic-money institutions provide fast onboarding, efficient GBP/USD/EUR clearing, and lower friction for operational disbursements, but most will decline letter-of-credit or documentary-credit work streams and may impose monthly throughput caps on high-value physical commodity flows. EMIs with direct SWIFT membership are viable for inbound capital injections and outbound open-account wires below approximately GBP 250,000 per transaction; they are not a substitute for a tier-1 commercial bank for the LC-based lithium-carbonate procurement corridor. Legal constraint: a cross-border EMI must hold a GFSC e-money licence or operate through a validly authorised Gibraltar agent; EMIs cannot offer credit, FX derivatives, or trade finance absent a further GFSC investment-services or banking authorisation.

0% - Bermuda imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by an exempted company to non-resident shareholders; Gibraltar likewise imposes no withholding tax on dividend distributions made by a Gibraltar-arranged entity. The dividend repatriation channel from the Bermuda Exempted Company is fully tax-neutral at source.
