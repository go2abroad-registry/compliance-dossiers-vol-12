Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar Income Tax Office (tax.gov.gi) and Gibraltar Customs & Excise (customs.gov.gi); Bermuda Registrar of Companies acts as the corporate registry for the Bermuda Exempted Company, though no Bermuda income tax authority applies to exempted companies.

E-government filing portal

For a Bermuda Exempted Company operating through Gibraltar: (1) maintain the Bermuda Certificate of Incorporation and Exempted Company certificate as the underlying corporate identity; (2) before substantive Gibraltar activities, file a foreign-company registration with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies if the entity is establishing a place of business; (3) apply to the Gibraltar Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number through a licensed tax agent, submitting the certificate of incorporation, registered-office and director details, and board resolutions confirming place of central management and control; (4) if no Gibraltar-source income is anticipated, obtain a letter of non-assessment from the Income Tax Office; (5) register with Gibraltar Customs & Excise for a customs trader code when importing raw material or exporting finished clear-aligner sheets. No Gibraltar VAT ID exists because Gibraltar imposes no VAT/GST.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — For a Bermuda Exempted Company operating through Gibraltar: (1) maintain the Bermuda Certificate of Incorporation and Exempted Company certificate as the underlying corporate identity; (2) before substantive Gibraltar activities, file a foreign-company registration with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies if the entity is establishing a place of business; (3) apply to the Gibraltar Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number through a licensed tax agent, submitting the certificate of incorporation, registered-office and director details, and board resolutions confirming place of central management and control; (4) if no Gibraltar-source income is anticipated, obtain a letter of non-assessment from the Income Tax Office; (5) register with Gibraltar Customs & Excise for a customs trader code when importing raw material or exporting finished clear-aligner sheets. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists in Gibraltar or Bermuda. A Bermuda Exempted Company can elect and receive the Bermuda exempted-undertaking certificate, which shelters the entity from Bermuda income tax until the statutory deadline. In Gibraltar, a foreign-incorporated company may elect to be treated as non-resident for Gibraltar tax purposes where central management and control is outside Gibraltar. Hybrid mismatch rules under UK/Gibraltar tax principles may apply where the entity is treated as transparent in another jurisdiction, and supplementary disclosure elections may be required to neutralise those mismatches. Gibraltar does not recognise pass-through classification for general trading companies.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar General Indirect Tax (Customs and Excise Duties; no VAT/GST system)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - Gibraltar does not operate a VAT/GST registration threshold; customs registration is required for all physical imports and exports irrespective of turnover, and no minimum Gibraltar pound (GIP) threshold applies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar has no VAT, so no 0% VAT export rating or VAT refund mechanism applies. For customs-duty purposes, export of dental clear-aligner sheets is relieved from Gibraltar import and excise liability where the goods physically leave Gibraltar to a non-Gibraltar destination, supported by an export declaration, contract, transport documentation, and proof of foreign payment. No Gibraltar indirect tax refund is available because no indirect tax is charged on the supply.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document or electronic equivalent filed with Gibraltar Customs & Excise)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure from Gibraltar
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 evidencing receipt of export payment
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List describing the clear-aligner sheets and HS classification
  • Certificate of Origin or company manufacturer declaration where requested by the destination jurisdiction

No real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control, or digital stamping mandate applies to Gibraltar B2B invoices. Export declarations must be filed electronically through Gibraltar Customs & Excise using the standard customs declaration framework; high-volume traders and customs brokers use API-integrated declaration software, but there is no statutory national e-invoicing clearance platform.

Gibraltar Customs & Excise may conduct post-export verification audits and claw back any duty relief or suspension if the goods do not leave Gibraltar within the mandatory 180-day period or are diverted back into Gibraltar consumption. Fraudulent export declarations or misdescribed clear-aligner sheets attract monetary penalties up to GBP 50,000, forfeiture of goods, additional customs penalties of up to 100% of the goods value, and criminal liability including imprisonment for up to 7 years for evasion. The Gibraltar Income Tax Office may also reopen non-resident status within six years and assess undeclared Gibraltar-source profits with interest where export documentation is shown to be false.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is legal tender only in Gibraltar, pegged at 1:1 to pound sterling and 100% backed by sterling. There are no exchange controls on current-account or capital-account transactions; dividends, interest, intra-group loans and sale proceeds may be freely remitted. The Bermuda exempted company's tax-exempt status does not impair convertibility, and no BMA exchange-control permission is engaged because no Bermuda bank account is maintained.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Gibraltar law imposes no export-proceeds surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio. Export receipts from clear-aligner sheet sales may be retained in USD, EUR, GBP or GIP and freely applied to operating costs, supplier payments or distributions.

Central bank supervision: Government of Gibraltar – Currency Commission (Commissioner of Currency) under the Currency Notes Act 1934; banking and AML supervision by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC). No standalone central bank and no exchange-control department.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The Company Tax Return and any balance of tax payable are due on or before the first day of the tenth month following the end of the accounting period (i.e., nine months and one day after the fiscal year-end); a calendar-year company with a 31 December year-end must file and pay by 1 October of the following year. Where the ITO issues a notice to file, the return is due within forty-two days of the issue of that notice. Large companies may be directed to make two interim instalment payments on account during the year.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File and Master File are mandatory where the aggregate value of controlled (related-party) transactions of the Gibraltar entity in an accounting period exceeds GBP 2,000,000, or where gross revenue exceeds GBP 10,000,000 and any controlled transaction exists. Country-by-Country Reportability applies where the ultimate parent group's consolidated revenue exceeds GBP 750,000,000. Documentation must be contemporaneous and produced to the ITO within thirty days of a formal notice.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar applies OECD substance-over-form doctrine through the Income Tax (Economic Substance) Regulations, requiring any tax-resident company to demonstrate direction and management, physical premises, employees, and core income-generating activities in Gibraltar when engaged in relevant activities (holding, IP, financing, distribution, headquarters, and service centres). Pure thermoforming manufacture of dental clear aligner sheets is outside the enumerated relevant-activities list; nevertheless, a Bermuda Exempted Company that asserts Gibraltar tax residence must still satisfy the general management-and-control test, maintain board meetings in Gibraltar, and ensure that the Bermudian 'tax-exempt zone' status is not used to deny Gibraltar taxing rights over profits attributable to the Gibraltar operation.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Deliberate Concealment

Statutory sanction: Assessment period extended to twenty years; penalty of up to 100% of the tax understated (minimum 50% where concealment is deliberate); statutory interest on unpaid tax; criminal fine up to GBP 5,000 per offence and imprisonment for up to five years on summary conviction.

Director personal liability: Directors who cause or permit the false return are jointly and severally liable for restitution of the evaded tax under the Income Tax Act 2010 and the Companies Act 2014; courts may pierce the corporate veil, impose travel bans pending tax clearance, freeze personal assets, and disqualify the director for up to fifteen years.
Shell Company and Sham Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Recharacterisation of the entity as Gibraltar tax resident; assessment of worldwide profits at 12.5% with interest and penalties of up to 100%; administrative strike-off and dissolution for non-compliance with annual return and substance filing obligations; denial of treaty benefits under the UK-Gibraltar DTA.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for the underlying tax shortfall when the corporate form is used as a mere sham; imposition of overseas travel restrictions, seizure of personal bank accounts, disqualification orders, and criminal prosecution for knowingly making false statements to the Companies Registry and the ITO.
Transfer Pricing Fraudulent Under-Pricing

Statutory sanction: ITO transfer-pricing adjustment to the arm's-length price with secondary adjustment (deemed dividend or loan interest), penalty of up to 100% of the adjusted tax, a six-year reassessment window (twenty years for undisclosed arrangements), and fixed penalties for failure to maintain the Local File or Master File.

Director personal liability: A director who signs artificial underpricing documentation knowing it is not at arm's length faces personal civil restitution of the diverted group profits, personal liability for the adjusted tax under veil-piercing principles, criminal sanctions for tax evasion, and disqualification as a director of any Gibraltar company.
AML and Sanctions Abuse on Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Freezing of merchant accounts and civil forfeiture of proceeds under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015; fines of up to GBP 1,000,000 per breach under the Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2019; revocation of the entity's registration and of any financial-services permission required for the card payment channel.

Director personal liability: Directors knowingly involved are exposed to personal confiscation orders, travel bans, extradition risk, and imprisonment of up to fourteen years for money laundering; professional indemnity coverage is voided and the director is permanently barred from holding compliance or fiduciary office in Gibraltar.
