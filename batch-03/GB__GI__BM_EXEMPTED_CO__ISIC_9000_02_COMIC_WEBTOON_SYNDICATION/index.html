Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax), HM Government of Gibraltar; official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation. Corporate registration is handled by the Registrar of Companies, Companies House Gibraltar.

E-government filing portal

For a foreign vehicle (Bermuda Exempted Company) with Gibraltar nexus: (1) file for registration as an overseas company at Companies House Gibraltar if it intends to maintain a branch, place of business or bank account in Gibraltar; (2) submit the Companies House-issued registration details together with a tax registration declaration to the Gibraltar Income Tax Office; (3) obtain a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) and, where payroll is present, a Gibraltar Social Security employer number; (4) register with HM Customs of Gibraltar only when physical goods are imported; (5) do not obtain a Gibraltar VAT ID because Gibraltar has no VAT/GST; (6) the company will not normally require UK VAT registration if Apple IAP or Google Play Billing are deemed suppliers under HMRC marketplace rules, but must apply directly to HMRC for UK VAT registration for UK consumer digital supplies if no deemed-supplier arrangement applies.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not administer a check-the-box election (a US concept) and the Bermuda Exempted Company does not elect Gibraltar tax status by default. The entity is classified as an opaque body corporate under Gibraltar and UK tax law; a transparent classification is possible only if the entity is a partnership or trust under its constitutive documents. Where effective management and control is centralised in Gibraltar, a unilateral residence election may be filed with the Commissioner of Income Tax with prior approval, converting status to Gibraltar tax resident for income tax. Hybrid mismatch outcomes (double deductions) are blocked under UK Finance Act 2016 / Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010 anti-hybrid rules. No election affects Bermudian exemption, other than maintenance of exempt status by annual government filing.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax — none in force in Gibraltar; Gibraltar is outside the EU VAT area. HM Customs of Gibraltar collects customs and excise duties on physical goods imported into Gibraltar.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT registration threshold applies because no VAT/GST system exists in Gibraltar. Any person importing goods into Gibraltar must register as an importer with HM Customs of Gibraltar on importation, irrespective of value; alcohol, tobacco, fuel and motor vehicles additionally require excise licensing.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A zero rate is not available in Gibraltar because Gibraltar has no output VAT. If the company later registers for UK VAT or is treated as UK VAT-established, physical export zero-rating under VATA 1994, Schedule 8 requires: (i) a taxable supply by a UK VAT-registered person; (ii) goods removed from the UK to a non-UK destination within three months; (iii) HMRC-prescribed export evidence retained. Offshore B2B services may be zero-rated under Schedule 4A where the business customer belongs outside the UK and the supply is not excluded (reverse charge applies). Pure B2C digital webtoon sales to UK consumers are taxable in the UK (except qualifying e-books at 0% on concession) and Apple IAP / Google Play Billing deemed-supplier rules generally apply.

  • Customs Export Declaration (C88/SAD Single Administrative Document) for physical merchandise exported from the UK or Gibraltar
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing goods leaving the UK or Gibraltar territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT MT103 evidencing export proceeds received in the seller's account within 180 days
  • Commercial invoice, packing list and proof of the foreign buyer's identity and address matching the export declaration

Gibraltar has no real-time fiscal clearance API, e-invoicing clearance or digital stamping mandate; taxpayers must keep proper books and records under the Gibraltar Companies Act and Income Tax Act, and invoices may be issued electronically. For digital webtoon syndication via Apple IAP or Google Play Billing, the store platform is usually the deemed supplier for UK/EU VAT; the Bermuda Exempted Company does not operate a Gibraltar fiscal gateway.

Although the Bermuda Exempted Company sits outside the Gibraltar VAT net, HMRC and Gibraltar authorities will attack artificial structures intended to defer or avoid UK VAT. Abuse-of-law and UK GAAR principles may recharacterise a scheme that routes digital webtoon-derived revenue through a tax-exempt non-UK entity while UK benefit is received. Consequential penalties include: (i) VAT assessment on full proceeds with default interest; (ii) 100% VAT civil penalty for dishonest evasion (VATA 1994, s 60/61); (iii) criminal prosecution for fraudulent evasion of VAT; (iv) HMRC MTIC/ECSL export-fraud clawback of any previously zero-rated exports plus penalty up to 100%; (v) director disqualification and de-registration under POCA. Export refunds are withheld where the 180-day evidence of remittance requirement is not met.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The UK and Gibraltar operate a fully liberalised foreign exchange regime under Article 8 of the IMF Articles of Agreement. Both current and capital account transactions are fully convertible with no exchange control restrictions or quantitative limits. Profit remittances and dividend distributions to shareholders are unrestricted, subject to the corporate law solvency test in the Gibraltar Companies Act and adequate documentary compliance under the Economic Substance (Companies) Act 2020. Withholding tax on distributions is nil for Gibraltar tax-resident payers, and no central bank approval certificate or exchange license is required for currency conversion.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no statutory export proceeds surrender rules or mandatory conversion ratios for export receipts in UK or Gibraltar. A company may retain export proceeds in any permitted currency, repatriate at its own discretion, and use such funds for trade settlement or reinvestment. Only statistical reporting obligations under the UK Office for National Statistics (ONS) International Trade Surveys and, where applicable, the Gibraltar Companies Act annual financial statement disclosure require accurate recording of export income.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (UK monetary authority) and Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the Gibraltar-licensed banking regulator; HM Treasury sets financial sanctions and money-laundering regulations. Gibraltar applies the UK's Exchange Control legislation framework which has been repealed and maintains no separate central bank issuance of currency beyond the Gibraltar pound pegged at par to GBP.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low to medium feasibility. Offshore digital EMI providers (e.g., Wise Business, Revolut Business, Payoneer, or Malta/Lithuania-licensed EMIs) typically reject or heavily restrict onboarding of Bermuda Exempted Companies due to the offshore jurisdiction flag and lack of trailing economic substance in the EMI's regulatory zone. Legal constraints include the EMI's inability to accept inbound wire transfers with certain purpose codes and automatic restriction of high-risk jurisdiction transactions. Gibraltar does not have a domestic EMI licensing regime under PSD2, and UK FCA-regulated EMIs may treat the Gibraltar entity as a third-country offshore company requiring enhanced due diligence. Therefore, a tier-1 Gibraltar commercial bank is recommended as the primary clearing house, with a UK EMI used only as a supplementary treasury conduit for low-value webtoon royalty payments where permissible under the EMI's terms.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity and working capital injections should be remitted using standard SWIFT MT103 wire with a clear beneficial-owner narrative 'Capital contribution to [COMPANY NAME] pursuant to board resolution dated ...' and a suitable purpose code under the OECD BPM6/FDI classification, e.g. 'E001 – Equity Capital' or 'E003 – Investment in Own Funds'. The remitting bank must be a regulated financial institution in an FATF-compliant jurisdiction, and the receiving Gibraltar bank will require a pre-delivery SWIFT MT199 from the remitting bank confirming the sender's authority, the form of the injection, and the matching board resolution. Amounts above GBP 50,000 or EUR 100,000 trigger an automatic enhanced due diligence referral; the company should pre-submit a certified UBO register, passport copies, and a notarised declaration of source of wealth to avoid clearing delays.Outbound trade settlements for webtoon licensing, creator advances, and distribution royalties should be executed via SWIFT MT103 for single cross-border payments, using SPF (Single European Payment Format) or SEPA Credit Transfer for euro-denominated payments within the EEA. For high-value intellectual property licensing agreements, a commercial irrevocable documentary credit issued under UCP 600 (MT700) can be established, with a deferred-payment feature to enable secure delivery of chapter unlock bundles. Recurring recurring royalty distributions may be automated as batch SWIFT MT104 or via API banking rails such as the UK Faster Payments Service (FPS) and SEPA Instant, provided the payee bank is connected.
