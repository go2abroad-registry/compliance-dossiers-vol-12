Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone. The Bermuda Exempted Company is, in Bermuda, expressly excluded from Bermuda income tax by the Exempted Companies Act 1981 and the Income Tax Act 1986; it is neither pass-through nor ordinarily subject to entity-level income tax in its home jurisdiction, and it may hold any class of owner with non-resident ownership permitted. Available election forms

Gibraltar does not provide a US-style generic 'check-the-box' election; entity classification is determined by the foreign legal form and by place of effective management. Available statutory elections and designations include: (i) an election under the Income Tax Act 2010 for the company to be taxed on a territorial basis, limiting charge to profits arising in or derived from Gibraltar; (ii) application for 'Qualifying Company' status under the Income Tax (Qualifying Companies) Regulations 2012 for eligible economic-substance activities (not generally applicable to sulfur mineral ore brokerage); (iii) group relief and loss-surrender elections under section 37 of the Income Tax Act 2010 where a 75% group relationship exists; (iv) in the United States, the Bermuda entity may file IRS Form 8832 to elect classification as a corporation, partnership, or disregarded entity for US federal tax purposes, a creature of US tax law that does not alter Gibraltar treatment; (v) hybrid mismatch and reverse-hybrid statements or elections required under the Gibraltar Income Tax (Hybrid and Other Mismatches) Regulations 2018 and, where relevant, the UK CTA 2010 Part 6A rules; no election may be used to avoid the application of those anti-mismatch provisions.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The GIP is fully convertible and legally pegged to GBP at par (1 GIP = 1 GBP) and circulates interchangeably with sterling within Gibraltar. Capital and current account transactions are comprehensively liberalised: there are no exchange-control statutes, no foreign-exchange licensing, no prior approval for trade payments, loan servicing, dividend or profit remittance, or liquidation distributions, and no restrictions on non-resident participation. The only practical barriers to conversion are AML/CFT-derived: source-of-funds verification under the GFSC AML Guidance Notes, sanctions screening against OFSI, OFAC, EU, and UN lists, and FATCA/CRS counterparty classification. For commercial treasury execution, GIP functions primarily as local legal tender; substantive liquidity should be held in GBP (local cost clearing) and USD (global sulfur commodity settlement), since GIP is not a tradeable offshore deliverable instrument beyond the Rock.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no prescribed repatriation window. Gross export proceeds in USD, EUR, or any other freely convertible currency may be retained indefinitely offshore, held on deposit, or netted against import and commodity payment obligations. For a Bermuda Exempted Company trading through Gibraltar the operative obligation is documentary: retention of a complete trade file (LCs, bills of lading, inspection certificates, invoices) to substantiate the economic-substance profile and CRS/FATCA reporting position, and, to the extent the entity is managed and controlled from Gibraltar so as to become Gibraltar tax-resident, correct recognition of attributable trading profits. In the present structure the Bermuda Exempted Company is not Gibraltar tax-resident provided central management and control is exercised from Bermuda and no Gibraltar permanent establishment arises.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar is a British Overseas Territory without an autonomous central bank: the Government of Gibraltar exercises the note-issuing function for the Gibraltar pound (GIP) under the Currency Notes Act 1934; the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) performs prudential, conduct, and AML/CFT supervision of all authorised banks and electronic-money institutions; the Bank of England anchors the Sterling Zone and underpins the reserve-asset framework supporting GIP convertibility; HM Treasury retains ministerial oversight of Gibraltar's international financial obligations and UK equivalence arrangements.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium with material constraints. Gibraltar is outside the EU/EEA, so no UK FCA or EU electronic-money institution may passport into Gibraltar; an EMI must hold a GFSC licence under the Financial Services (Electronic Money) Regulations 2016 or operate through a Gibraltar-authorised payment agent. Global digital EMIs (Revolut Business, Wise Business, Airwallex) commonly refuse or suspend onboarding for Bermuda Exempted Companies presenting a tax-exempt offshore profile, regardless of lawful substance; those that accept such vehicles typically impose throughput caps and prohibit commodity-trading business lines. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Route all inbound equity or shareholder-loan injections from the investor or Bermuda treasury via SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfer or MT202 COV interbank cover into the Gibraltar Capital Escrow Account, using SWIFT GPI with UETR propagation for end-to-end traceability. No exchange-control or central-bank approval is required along the Bermuda-Gibraltar Sterling corridor, but every inward remittance must state an explicit purpose narrative in field 70 ('Capital contribution for issued share capital', 'Shareholder subordinated loan for trade working capital', or 'Advance against future export invoices') and mirror UBO and beneficiary names identically across sending and receiving banks. The receiving Gibraltar bank will demand corroborating source-of-funds evidence: investor audited financials, dividend vouchers, share-sale agreements, or trust instruments. For USD-funded injections, provide the correct USD correspondent routing (commonly JPMorgan Chase, Citibank, or BNY Mellon in New York) with full legal name and BBAN/IBAN of the beneficiary. Under FATCA/CRS the injection is reportable as capital or equity contribution with no Gibraltar tax withholding; injected funds must not be commingled with trade proceeds before formal capitalisation is recorded in the Bermuda share register.For granular elemental sulfur settlement the preeminent rail is a Commercial Letter of Credit (UCP 600) transmitted via SWIFT MT700, payable upon presentation of the complete documentary bundle: commercial invoice, full set of clean-on-board bills of lading, certificate of analysis from SGS/Intertek, weight certificate, certificate of origin, and insurance policy. Settlement variants include at-sight LC for prime suppliers, usance (30-120 day) or deferred-payment LC for repeat counterparties, and LC confirmation by a top-tier bank (HSBC, Barclays, JPMorgan) to mitigate issuing-bank risk. Standalone trade disbursements (freight, port, survey, brokerage, insurance) are cleared via SWIFT MT103 customer transfers and MT202 COV cover payments under SWIFT GPI tracking, with each payment mapped to the ETP and contract file. GBP-denominated local disbursements (Gibraltar port charges, agent fees, insurance premiums) settle through the London CHAPS or Gibraltar Faster Payments equivalent via the relationship bank's UK settlement arrangement. No cash payments, bearer instruments, or third-country unbanked reimbursement corridors are permissible under the GFSC AML regime; every outbound flow must preserve a complete audit trail to the underlying trade documentation.Deliverable FX forward contracts in USD/GBP and USD/EUR (tenor up to 12 months) executed with the relationship bank under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement or a unilateral FX trade ticket, used to fix conversion of USD-denominated sulfur sales proceeds into GBP operating liquidity.FX swaps (spot/forward rollovers) converting USD trade receipts into GBP for working-capital deployment without realising spot-market timing risk.Cross-currency swaps (USD/GBP and USD/EUR) matching USD-denominated purchase obligations against GBP-denominated equity and capital-injection funding, including non-deliverable variants for restricted emerging-market currencies.Vanilla FX options, zero-cost collars, and barrier options on USD/GBP to protect the profit-conversion budget for periodic dividend or treasury repatriation flows.USD interest-rate swaps and forward-rate agreements to hedge LC discounting and usance-financing costs where the underlying commodity pricing is USD-denominated.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on restricted currencies (offshore CNY, IDR, INR, and other non-convertible Asian settlement currencies) where physical delivery is statutorily constrained, to hedge inbound receivable legs from Asian buyers or suppliers in the sulfur supply chain; NDFs against GIP/GBP are unnecessary and unavailable, as GIP maintains a hard currency-board parity with GBP.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Medium with material constraints. Gibraltar is outside the EU/EEA, so no UK FCA or EU electronic-money institution may passport into Gibraltar; an EMI must hold a GFSC licence under the Financial Services (Electronic Money) Regulations 2016 or operate through a Gibraltar-authorised payment agent. Global digital EMIs (Revolut Business, Wise Business, Airwallex) commonly refuse or suspend onboarding for Bermuda Exempted Companies presenting a tax-exempt offshore profile, regardless of lawful substance; those that accept such vehicles typically impose throughput caps and prohibit commodity-trading business lines. A GFSC-licensed EMI is therefore viable only as a secondary disbursement rail for minor GBP/EUR payables (port disbursements, surveyor fees, agent commissions), never as the primary custody, LC, or treasury settlement mechanism.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File and Master File must be prepared and submitted when aggregate related-party transactions equal or exceed GBP 1,000,000 per accounting period, or when total gross revenue exceeds GBP 10,000,000 and related-party transactions exceed GBP 250,000.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar applies the Income Tax (Economic Substance) Regulations 2020. A company carrying on 'distribution and service' business or 'shipping' activities (applicable to dry bulk sulfur transport brokerage) must demonstrate in Gibraltar or EEA: (a) board meetings held in Gibraltar, (b) adequate employees and premises, and (c) core income-generating activities (vessel charter negotiation, cargo risk management, purchase/sale contracting) performed locally. Failure to do so may deny tax residence for exemption claims and attract ESR enforcement penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Return

Statutory sanction: Penalty of up to 100% of undercharged tax plus a fine of up to GBP 5,000 per offence; criminal prosecution under Gibraltar Income Tax Act and Proceeds of Crime Act; maximum imprisonment of 7 years.

Director personal liability: Director officers are personally liable for civil restitution of the undercharged tax and interest; travel bans may be imposed pending inquiry; imprisonment up to 7 years on conviction.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Breach

Statutory sanction: ESR monetary penalties of up to GBP 10,000 per category; publication of non-compliant entity on GITO register; potential strike-off of the Gibraltar registration; reclassification of income as Gibraltar-sourced with 12.5% tax applied on deemed profits.

Director personal liability: Directors face fines up to GBP 5,000 each, disqualification from serving as director of any Gibraltar company for 3 years, and personal financial liability if they knowingly caused the substance failure.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Transfer pricing adjustment to arm's length profits, additional tax at 12.5%, interest at 5% per annum, and a penalty of up to 100% of the additional tax; separate documentation penalty up to GBP 2,000 if no Local/Master File is maintained.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for the additional liability if they authorised or were reckless in allowing the mispricing; a Director Disqualification Order may be issued for up to 5 years; criminal liability exists if the concealment is intentional.
AML / Counter-Terrorist Financing Breach

Statutory sanction: Criminal fine up to 10% of gross turnover or GBP 2 million, forfeiture of funds connected to the suspected transaction, and revocation of the entity's financial services licence; court order freezing all bank accounts in Gibraltar.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers may be held personally liable for gross negligence in AML oversight; travel impediments (court-ordered passport surrender) and confiscation orders on personal assets can be made; imprisonment up to 14 years for money laundering.
