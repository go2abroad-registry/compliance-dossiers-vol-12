Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar Revenue, HM Government of Gibraltar (Commissioner of Income Tax for direct tax; Customs and Excise Division for VAT), official digital portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation and Gibraltar Revenue e-Return / GibTax electronic filing platform.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Register the Bermuda Exempted Company with the Gibraltar Companies House (Registrar of Companies) as an external or non-resident company under the Companies Act 2014, obtaining a Gibraltar company number. Step 2: File the statutory 'Application for Tax Reference Number (TRN)' with the Gibraltar Commissioner of Income Tax, attaching certified certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, Gibraltar registered-office address, board resolution, and expected source of income. Step 3: Obtain the Gibraltar TRN, which is the single statutory tax identifier for corporate income tax, payroll (PAYE), and social insurance. Step 4: Apply separately for Gibraltar VAT registration (Form VAT 1) only if taxable supplies within Gibraltar exceed the registration threshold; a non-resident entity supplying clinical-trial management services wholly to non-Gibraltar sponsors is generally not required or eligible to register for VAT.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010 elections: (a) deemed-profit / estimated-assessment election for small companies with simplified computation of taxable profits; (b) non-resident status election for a company managed and controlled outside Gibraltar and without a permanent establishment, limiting charge to Gibraltar-source income only; (c) hybrid-mismatch alignment where group entities may elect to align Bermuda, Gibraltar, and UK entity classification under the UK Finance Act 2016 hybrid mismatch rules as applicable to Gibraltar cross-border arrangements; (d) Gibraltar does not operate a US-style 'check-the-box' regime, so classification follows statutory entity form save where express transparency elections apply to partnerships and investment vehicles.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

1%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GBP 14,000 of taxable supplies in any consecutive 12-month period (Gibraltar VAT Act 1993); a foreign Bermuda Exempted Company without a fixed establishment in Gibraltar and supplying cross-border CRO services to non-Gibraltar recipients has no Gibraltar VAT nexus and is below the registration threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: zero-rating applies where investigational medicinal products or trial supplies are directly exported from Gibraltar customs territory, evidenced by a valid customs export declaration and physical departure within statutory timelines. Services: B2B supply of clinical trial CRO management services (ISIC 7490 / NAICS 541714) to sponsors established outside Gibraltar is outside the scope of Gibraltar VAT under place-of-supply rules, qualifying as zero-rated export services; eligibility requires the supplier to hold the customer's foreign VAT registration number or equivalent proof of non-Gibraltar establishment, to deliver services wholly offshore, and to maintain contractual and activity evidence demonstrating no Gibraltar situs.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Gibraltar SAD / MRN), Bill of Lading or Air Waybill, Commercial Invoice and Packing List, Inward Remittance Certificate
  • Master Clinical Trial CRO Services Agreement, Clinical Trial Authorization (CTA) and ethics approval, sponsor VAT ID / CRN certificate
  • Milestone invoices, SWIFT incoming credit confirmations, trial-site activity logs, and customs transit documents where applicable

Gibraltar imposes no mandatory real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoice clearance, or digital stamping regime; B2B electronic invoicing is permitted provided that authenticity, integrity, and legibility are preserved via EDI or qualified electronic signature; Gibraltar Revenue retains audit access to electronic records, which must be retained for six years.

Gibraltar Revenue and Customs may conduct post-clearance and indirect-tax audits for up to four to six years; incorrect or fraudulent zero-rating claims result in VAT assessments, default surcharges of up to 30% for careless error and up to 100% for deliberate conduct, interest from the original due date, and retrospective clawback of refunds; fraudulent evasion of Gibraltar VAT and customs duty is a criminal offence punishable by fines and imprisonment of up to seven years under the VAT Act 1993 and Customs and Excise legislation, with public disclosure of the taxpayer's identity and disqualification from future export repayment schemes.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar operates a fully liberalised exchange regime with no exchange controls: current-account payments (CRO service fees, investigator grants, sub-CRO invoices) and capital-account flows (share capital injections, dividends, intercompany loans) are freely convertible without prior approval, with GIP interchangeable at par with GBP; USD and EUR clearing is unrestricted via the commercial banking system. Profit remittance to the Bermuda parent is unobstructed but banks impose documentary hurdles: audited financial statements or interim management accounts, a board-approved dividend or management-fee resolution, and UBO consent. Euro payment access is indirect through the bank's SEPA participation and UK/Spanish correspondents; the practical constraint is documentation and compliance sign-off, not regulatory authorisation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Neither Gibraltar nor Bermuda imposes any statutory timeline, mandatory conversion ratio, or surrender obligation on export proceeds. Clinical-trial service revenue received in USD, EUR or GBP may be retained in the original currency indefinitely or converted at commercially negotiated rates without foreign-exchange administration approval; no surrender certificate or BMA/GFSC filing is required.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) — the integrated regulator for banking, AML supervision and financial-services licensing in Gibraltar; there is no standalone central bank, as the Gibraltar Pound (GIP) is issued by H.M. Government of Gibraltar under the Currency Notes Act at a fixed 1:1 peg to GBP with sterling legal tender. For the Bermuda Exempted Company, the Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) supervises the home entity, while the GFSC supervises the Gibraltar treasury banking operations; HM Treasury retains ultimate monetary authority over the territory.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-to-Low for operational perimeter services only; offshore digital EMIs are expressly unsuitable for the capital escrow account or primary treasury relationship. Legal constraints: post-Brexit, EEA electronic-money passporting no longer binds Gibraltar, and an EMI must hold a GFSC licence under the Financial Services (Electronic Money) Regulations to service Gibraltar clients; most offshore digital EMIs (UK, Irish, or Baltic-state licensed) decline entities lacking local substance, particularly Bermuda Exempted Companies with non-resident shareholders. Where an EMI is accepted, it will be restricted to segregated collection of sponsor milestone wires with low transaction volumes, no negative FX balances, no interest-bearing treasury functions and no credit facilities. The GFSC-licensed full-reserve digital bank Xapo Bank offers remote onboarding but is not a substitute for the tier-1 commercial banking relationship.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Share-capital injections must be credited to the designated capital escrow account in the company's exact legal name via SWIFT MT103 with charge code SHA, remitted by the registered shareholder from the shareholder's own account; the remittance must state 'Share Capital Contribution' plus the board-resolution reference, with UK BOP purpose code E020 or ISO 20022 purpose 'SCBR'. The beneficiary bank will hold the credit pending a compliance pack comprising the notarised board resolution approving the share issue, the share certificate, the updated Bermuda register of members, and UBO consent; funds remain escrowed until transaction-monitoring sign-off, and onward transfer to the operational account requires a second authorised signatory's countersignature on the escrow release instruction.Operational trial disbursements are executed via SWIFT MT103 with ISO 20022 remittance references: investigator-grant and site payments are classified under UK BOP purpose code S120 (research and development / CRO services) or standard service narratives; sub-CRO invoices, central-lab fees and patient stipends carry the contract reference and CTIS or MHRA trial identifier. GBP payments clear through CHAPS or Faster Payments via the bank's UK correspondent; EUR through SEPA Credit Transfer; USD through Fedwire or CHIPS correspondents. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Gibraltar Local File threshold: GBP 1,000,000 in related-party transactions per accounting period; Master File threshold: GBP 50,000,000 aggregate group turnover; Bermuda ESR reporting threshold: gross income of BSD 1,000,000 from relevant activities (clinical trial management is classified as relevant activity).

Economic substance scope: Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations apply to exempted companies carrying on relevant activity, requiring core income-generating activities (clinical trial contract negotiation, medical protocol review, milestone assessment, global patient data risk management) be undertaken in Bermuda. Gibraltar separately applies economic substance principles to non-resident companies claiming non-residency, and will tax undeclared profits if management and control is exercised from Gibraltar. Adequate qualified employees, physical premises, and board minutes in Bermuda are mandatory; pure pass-through invoicing without substance will be disregarded.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Undisclosed Permanent Establishment

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar Income Tax Act assessment at 12.5% with a fiscal penalty of up to 200% of tax understated; criminal prosecution for fraudulent evasion under the Income Tax Act; Bermuda penalties for false statutory declarations, including BDA 10,000 fine; confiscation of assets; strike-off.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for unpaid taxes under a 'closer connection' test; disqualification orders for up to 15 years; travel bans; civil restitution covering unpaid tax and penalties; imprisonment of up to 7 years for tax evasion; joint and several liability where corporate veil is pierced for fraud or sham substance.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Registrar of Companies may strike off and dissolve the company; monetary penalty of up to USD 200,000 under the Economic Substance (Amendment) Act 2022; Gibraltar may treat the entity as tax resident and charge 12.5% on all worldwide CRO revenue plus a 30% surcharge; disgorgement of profits.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal penalty orders of up to USD 500,000; exclusion from acting as director in Bermuda, Gibraltar, and HM Courts; civil restitution of all undistributed treaty/fee income; a 5-year travel ban; custodial sentence of up to 5 years for making false substance declarations.
AML / Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Seizure of all funds under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2018 (Gibraltar); financial penalty up to EUR 5 million; AMR (Anti-Money Laundering) penalty of up to 10% of annual turnover; criminal forfeiture of the exemption certificate; revocation of the company's Bermuda status; fines to the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission.

Director personal liability: Travel bans preventing departure from Gibraltar or Bermuda; personal civil penalty of EUR 750,000 per director; confiscation of director's private assets to the value of laundered funds; imprisonment up to 14 years; permanent disqualification from banking, fiduciary, or CRO management; personal service of criminal proceedings under the foreign corrupt practices analogy.
