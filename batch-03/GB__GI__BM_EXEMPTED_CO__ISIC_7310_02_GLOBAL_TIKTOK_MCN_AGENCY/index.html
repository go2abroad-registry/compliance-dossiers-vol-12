Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (Bermuda Exempted Company; exempt from Bermuda income, capital gains, and withholding taxes until 2035 under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966). In Gibraltar: classified as a non-resident company unless central management and control is exercised in Gibraltar; non-resident companies are taxable only on Gibraltar-source income attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment at the 12.5% corporate rate. Territory scope: worldwide taxation only if Gibraltar-resident; territorial (Gibraltar-source) taxation if non-resident.

Available election forms

Gibraltar: (1) Participation Exemption election under Part XX of the Income Tax Act 2010 for dividends and capital gains on substantial shareholdings (≥10% for ≥12 months in a qualifying company subject to a qualifying tax); (2) Notional Interest Deduction (NID) election on qualifying new equity; (3) Group relief election for Gibraltar-resident group companies. No US-style check-the-box election exists in Gibraltar or Bermuda; entity classification follows statutory legal form, tax residency (central management and control), and PE principles. Hybrid mismatch: OECD BEPS Action 2-compliant hybrid mismatch rules restrict deductions for payments deductible in Gibraltar but not included in the counterparty's taxable income; a Bermuda tax-exempt parent is not treated as 'taxable' for participation exemption purposes unless it is a qualifying company.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST in Gibraltar; Gibraltar levies customs and excise duties at importation only. UK VAT (20%) may apply to supplies of digital/intermediary services to UK consumers if the Bermuda/Gibraltar entity is a non-UK supplier of electronically supplied services (VAT Digital Services regime).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Gibraltar: no VAT-registration threshold (no VAT regime); customs excise registration required for taxable imports of goods. UK: £85,000 VAT annual registration threshold for UK-established businesses; £0 mandatory threshold for non-established suppliers of digital services to UK consumers (HMRC VAT digital services/OSS regime).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar: N/A - Gibraltar has no VAT, therefore no export zero-rating regime; cross-border services (TikTok MCN agency management, creator incubation, talent representation and commission income) supplied by a Gibraltar-established Bermuda exempted company are outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect tax. UK VAT zero-rating: if the entity becomes UK VAT-registered, supplies of agency services to non-UK business customers are outside the scope (B2B place of supply = customer's country, reverse charge applies); supplies of genuine digital services to non-UK consumers may be zero-rated/outside scope where the customer belongs outside the UK; the MCN commission from TikTok payouts must be documented as consideration for intermediary services under a written Creator Representation Agreement to support place-of-supply analysis.

  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill (applicable to goods only; for services: executed Creator Representation Agreement and TikTok Agency Terms, performance reports, and payout statements)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank-inward payment evidence from TikTok_Creator_Payout / Wise_B2B rails; FX conversion and repatriation records)

Gibraltar: no e-invoicing or real-time fiscal clearance mandate. Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar operates under a fully liberalised exchange-control regime. Both current-account items (creator commissions, licensing fees, service payments) and capital-account items (equity contributions, shareholder loans, intercompany transfers) are freely convertible into GBP, USD, EUR or other globally convertible currencies without central-bank approval. Profit remittance is unrestricted as a matter of exchange control; however, a Bermuda Exempted Company must satisfy the appropriate solvency test and maintain good standing under Bermuda law before declaring dividends or making capital distributions. No Gibraltar withholding tax is levied on ordinary dividends or branch profit remittances.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No export-proceeds surrender requirement applies in Gibraltar. A Bermuda Exempted Company treasury may retain all export and service receipts in any freely convertible currency, may convert them to GBP/GIP at commercial market rates, and is not obliged to repatriate or convert to the local currency. Statutory timeline: not applicable; mandatory conversion ratio: 0%.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC), acting under the Government of Gibraltar; currency issuance and monetary administration are discharged by HM Government of Gibraltar's Treasury, with the Gibraltar pound pegged at 1:1 to the UK pound. There is no separate autonomous central bank.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-High for cross-border digital EMIs utilising the Wise B2B or Revolut Business rails, provided they are engaged on a principal-to-principal basis and not as the sole treasury depository. Legal constraints: (i) an EMI that is not licensed by the GFSC or does not benefit from an applicable UK/EEA post-Brexit permission may not establish a local payment service in Gibraltar, though service on a reverse-solicitation basis to a non-resident Bermuda Exempted Company remains possible; (ii) the EMI must comply with the principal bank's client-money safeguarding rules and cannot lawfully provide assignable credit, escrow-grade settlement, margin FX, or extendable overdraft facilities; (iii) Gibraltar AML legislation requires full identification of underlying beneficiaries and source-of-funds verification for every payment-account deposit; (iv) EMIs should be considered a disbursement application rail only, not a substitute for tier-1 bank clearing and treasury risk management.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing for equity or loan capital from the parent or subscription shareholders is executed by SWIFT MT103 into the Bermuda Exempted Company's Gibraltar operational account. The payment reference must explicitly state the purpose (e.g., 'Capital Contribution', 'Shareholder Loan', 'Subordination Agreement') and must be supported by a board resolution, an updated shareholders register, an investment declaration, source-of-funds documentation and, if applicable, a Bermuda tax-exemption certificate. For smaller GBP-denominated injections, UK Faster Payments is acceptable only where the payor is a pre-screened group entity and the amount is immaterial; USD and EUR capital flows should use SWIFT with full nostro details. Verification includes SDN and consolidated sanctions screening, jurisdiction risk scoring, legal-person beneficial-ownership identification, and transaction monitoring uplift for the specified purpose code.Primary clearing rail for creator and talent settlements is SWIFT MT103 with the appropriate outward-service payment purpose code, accompanied by the underlying service agreement and commission calculation sheet. EUR-denominated payments to SEPA-connected countries settle through SCT or SCT Inst; GBP-denominated domestic and Gibraltar creator payments utilise UK Faster Payments; high-value USD payouts are cleared over SWIFT with finality via Fedwire/CHIPS. For small-value cross-border off-cycle disbursements, the Wise B2B API is acceptable but must be used only as a supplementary rail. 0% - Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax on the Bermuda Exempted Company; a flat 12.5% Gibraltar Corporation Tax applies only to profits attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment or to worldwide profits if central management and control is in Gibraltar.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due within 10 months after the company's fiscal year-end; for a 31 December year-end this falls on 31 October of the following year; extension of up to 6 months may be requested from the Registrar.

Within 6 months after the end of each financial period; for a 31 December year-end the ESD is due by 30 June of the following year.
