Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office (operational tax authority for Gibraltar); online fiscal portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation and customs clearance portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/customs

E-government filing portal

Non-resident foreign entities, including a Bermuda Exempted Company, are required to register with the Gibraltar Income Tax Office only if they commence a taxable trade or business in Gibraltar, acquire a permanent establishment, or become tax resident through central management and control being exercised in Gibraltar. The procedure is: (1) file Form A (Application for a Tax Reference Number) within 30 days of the relevant event; (2) submit certified copies of the certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, board minutes approving Gibraltar activity, and notarised proof of registered office; (3) receive a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (GIB TIN). There is no VAT registration because Gibraltar levies no VAT/GST. If the entity has no Gibraltar taxable presence, no registration is required, but a voluntary registration may be advisable for banking or treaty self-certification.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — Non-resident foreign entities, including a Bermuda Exempted Company, are required to register with the Gibraltar Income Tax Office only if they commence a taxable trade or business in Gibraltar, acquire a permanent establishment, or become tax resident through central management and control being exercised in Gibraltar. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a check-the-box classification regime. A foreign-incorporated entity may, through evidence of central management and control outside Gibraltar, be treated as non-resident and therefore taxable only on Gibraltar-source income. No hybrid mismatch elections are available under Gibraltar domestic law; UK hybrid mismatch rules in CTA 2010 Part VI may apply only where the Bermuda Exempted Company forms part of a UK-headquartered group with UK-resident members.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST in Gibraltar; indirect taxation is limited to customs and excise duties, stamp duty, and environmental taxes levied at territorial level

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A – Gibraltar imposes no VAT/GST and therefore has no turnover registration threshold. Any person or entity engaging in import/export activity must register separately with HM Customs for a Customs Processing Number, regardless of turnover; such registration does not attract VAT/GST liability.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar has no VAT/GST, so there is no domestic export zero-rating mechanism for goods or services. If the Bermuda Exempted Company were separately registered for UK VAT in mainland UK (which is not automatic for Gibraltar-incorporated or offshore companies), consulting services supplied to overseas business customers would fall outside the scope of UK VAT under the general B2B place-of-supply rule, and exports of goods to non-UK destinations could be zero-rated provided the goods physically leave the UK within three months and appropriate evidence is retained.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

No real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping is required for VAT/GST purposes. Import and export declarations must be lodged electronically through Gibraltar's customs administration system (GibSAM / Customs Management System), with API integration for authorised customs declarants. Commercial invoices and packing lists accompany customs entries; no fiscal e-invoicing regime is currently mandated.

In Gibraltar, incorrect customs declarations can lead to seizure of goods, monetary penalties up to £5,000 per declaration, and criminal sanction for fraudulent evasion. For any UK VAT export zero-rating claim made from a UK VAT registration, HMRC may apply penalties up to 100% of the VAT claimed, issue a retrospective assessment from the date of discovery plus interest, and refer the matter for criminal prosecution where there is dishonest misrepresentation. The tax-exempt status of the Bermuda Exempted Company does not shield it from indirect-tax penalties in Gibraltar or from UK VAT clawback where a UK VAT registration is lawfully required.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound is fully convertible into GBP at par, and there are no statutory restrictions on current account or capital account convertibility. All currencies may be freely held, transferred, and remitted. Merchant and corporate accounts denominate in GIP, GBP, EUR, or USD. Profit remittance to Bermuda or the United Kingdom requires no central bank approval, but banks may require a tax-status opinion and a board resolution evidencing distribution legality.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no export proceeds surrender requirement in Gibraltar. Exporters and service providers, including market-entry consultancies, may retain 100% of foreign currency receipts in any designated account or convert them to GIP, GBP, EUR, USD, or other freely convertible currencies at negotiated exchange rates. No mandatory conversion ratio or statutory holding period applies.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) under the Financial Services Act 2019; the Government of Gibraltar issues the Gibraltar pound (GIP) at a fixed 1:1 parity with the British pound sterling (GBP). HM Treasury and the Bank of England exercise residual monetary oversight for GBP interoperability. No separate Gibraltar central bank or foreign exchange administration exists.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium-high feasibility: Gibraltar participates in the UK-broader payment ecosystem and recognises regulated e-money institutions under the Financial Services (Electronic Money) Regulations and the GFSC's Distributed Ledger Technology framework. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise Business, Airwallex, and Payoneer can be accessed for inbound EUR/GBP/USD liquidity, but a Bermudian exempted company without substance in Gibraltar may be subjected to enhanced due diligence or an address-director requirement. Specific EMIs may reject GIP-denominated treasury rails and may mandate UK or EU bank settlement accounts; such restrictions must be mitigated via a local omnibus account or a dedicated Gibraltar corporate bank account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant capital injections are routed via SWIFT MT103 for client-deposit wires, MT202/202COV for interbank credit transfers, and SEPA-Instant in EUR when the Gibraltar bank enables it. A board resolution approving the capital contribution is mandatory; the payment must be tagged with a defined purpose code such as 'EB01' (equity injection) or 'LO01' (shareholder loan). Verification includes source-of-funds declaration, the Bermuda exempted company's capital-allocation minutes, and an earn-out or subscription agreement showing the payment's corporate treasury purpose.
