Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Her Majesty's Government of Gibraltar Commissioner of Income Tax (Income Tax Office) — principal tax authority and portal at https://www.income-tax.gi; corporate registry is Gibraltar Companies House (https://www.csgi.gi); regulatory licensing for venture-capital general partner activity is administered by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (https://www.fsc.gi). Direct income tax, General Indirect Tax (GIT), and social insurance contributions are administered through the Income Tax Office and its eTax filing portal.

E-government filing portal

For a Bermuda Exempted Company establishing a taxable presence in Gibraltar, the mandatory sequence is: (1) file an overseas-company registration with Gibraltar Companies House under the Companies Act 2014 (Bermuda certificate of incorporation, notarized memorandum and articles, registered-agent appointment and Gibraltar service address) within 30 days of establishing a place of business; (2) submit the prescribed Company Particulars Return to the Income Tax Office together with the Bermuda certificate of good standing and obtain a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN); the TIN is issued in the standard Gibraltar reference format and is cross-referenced to the company's Companies House number; (3) concurrently apply to the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission for the requisite investment-services authorisation or Experienced Investor Fund reliance under the Financial Services Act 2019, since a venture-capital GP is a statutorily licenced activity and the GFSC reference is shared with the Income Tax Office; (4) file the GIT registration annex with the Income Tax Office before issuing the first management-fee invoice so that GIT can be charged or treated as out-of-scope correctly; (5) open a Gibraltar banking account with a licensed bank and provide the final tax-residence documentation for CRS/FATCA due diligence. Registration failure attracts daily penalties, loss of input GIT recovery rights, and disqualification from GFSC licensing.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation_with_Territorial_Scope — the Bermuda Exempted Company is tax-exempt in Bermuda but is treated in Gibraltar as a separate taxable corporate entity: it becomes resident in Gibraltar, and taxable at entity level, if its central management and control (board and investment-committee decision-making) is exercised in Gibraltar. Gibraltar applies a strictly territorial income tax system: only income accruing in or derived from Gibraltar, such as management fees charged to a Gibraltar-administered fund or carried interest attributable to Gibraltar services, is assessable. No pass-through election is available for an exempted company; however, the entity's Bermuda tax-exempt-zone status is respected only to the extent that the income has no Gibraltar source. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation_with_Territorial_Scope — the Bermuda Exempted Company is tax-exempt in Bermuda but is treated in Gibraltar as a separate taxable corporate entity: it becomes resident in Gibraltar, and taxable at entity level, if its central management and control (board and investment-committee decision-making) is exercised in Gibraltar. Gibraltar applies a strictly territorial income tax system: only income accruing in or derived from Gibraltar, such as management fees charged to a Gibraltar-administered fund or carried interest attributable to Gibraltar services, is assessable. No pass-through election is available for an exempted company; however, the entity's Bermuda tax-exempt-zone status is respected only to the extent that the income has no Gibraltar source. Taxable profits are charged at the flat 12.5% corporate rate, subject to Gibraltar's minimum corporate tax for low-profit companies, and the participation exemption may exempt qualifying dividends and capital gains.

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a US-style check-the-box regime; entity classification follows legal form. Available statutory elections and mechanisms relevant to a venture-capital GP include: (i) election under the Income Tax (Participation Exemption) Regulations to exempt qualifying dividend income and capital gains from qualifying participations, subject to holding-period, percentage-ownership and substance conditions; (ii) irrevocable election for voluntary GIT registration before any taxable supply is made, which preserves input GIT recovery; (iii) invocation of the UK–Gibraltar Double Taxation Agreement for residence tie-breaker and mutual-agreement relief where management is split between the UK and Gibraltar; (iv) anti-hybrid ordering under the Gibraltar Finance Centre legislation, which applies OECD BEPS Action 2 hybrid-mismatch and anti-reverse-hybrid rules so that no entity may elect into a tax-transparent or hybrid status that creates double non-taxation; the Bermuda exempted company must therefore be operated as a pure corporate issuer with substantive equity and debt documentation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GBP — GIT registration is mandatory from the first taxable supply for a person carrying on a taxable activity in Gibraltar in the specified sectors, which include financial services, professional services, insurance, utilities, telecommunications, travel and hospitality. No monetary exemption applies to financial services: a venture-capital general partner invoicing management or advisory fees from a Gibraltar establishment must be GIT-registered and charge GIT at 12.5% from inception. An administrative de minimis of approximately GBP 20,000 annual turnover applies only to incidental non-core supplies, and a non-established supplier may be required to register where the recipient is established in Gibraltar and the supply is enjoyed in Gibraltar.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

GIT is territorial, so supplies of services by a Gibraltar GP to a recipient belonging and established outside Gibraltar, such as a non-Gibraltar fund vehicle and its non-resident investors, are exported services: they fall outside the scope of GIT with a 0% effective rate, provided the place of supply is outside Gibraltar, the recipient has no Gibraltar establishment, and the benefit and contract performance are enjoyed abroad. For goods, physical exports from Gibraltar are outside the scope of GIT, since Gibraltar levies no VAT on goods, and such exports are zero-rated for customs purposes. The GP must establish and document the non-Gibraltar place of supply in its GIT returns and maintain out-of-scope supply schedules; mischaracterised intra-Gibraltar services are not eligible for zero-rating.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document / SAD) confirming physical export of any goods from Gibraltar
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing outbound movement and destination outside Gibraltar
  • Commercial Invoice and executed Investment Management / Advisory Services Agreements between the GP and the non-resident fund
  • Certificate of Tax Residence or fund incorporation documentation of the non-Gibraltar fund evidencing that the beneficial recipient belongs outside Gibraltar
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT credit advice proving receipt of the management fee from abroad for foreign-exchange repatriation records
  • GIT out-of-scope supply schedule and exemption certificate filed with the Gibraltar Income Tax Office through eTax

No real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction controls (CTC), digital stamping, or API-based invoice clearance mandate is currently enforced in Gibraltar. The Income Tax Office eTax platform requires electronic filing of GIT periodic returns, online settlement, and annual reconciliation. GIT invoices must contain the supplier's name, GIT registration number, description of the taxable supply, date, consideration, GIT rate and tax amount, must be issued within 30 days of the supply, and must be retained for six years for audit. Digital record-keeping and iXBRL-style tagging are progressively required for corporate returns, but no pre-clearance of individual invoices by a fiscal device is imposed.

Export and out-of-scope zero-rating claims are subject to enhanced audit review by the Income Tax Office compliance unit and to the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission's AML and fit-and-proper supervision of fund GPs. Fraudulent or negligent claims trigger: (i) a penalty of up to 100% of the GIT avoided plus statutory late-payment interest at 8% per annum under the General Indirect Tax Act; (ii) parallel income-tax penalties of up to 100% of the tax lost, plus daily default penalties, under the Income Tax Act 2010 and the Finance Centre legislation; (iii) retrospective clawback of input credits, refunds and zero-rating relief for up to six years, extended to twenty years where fraud or gross negligence is established; (iv) criminal prosecution and director disqualification under the Crimes Act 2011 and the Companies Act 2014; and (v) GFSC disciplinary sanctions, financial penalties, revocation of the GP's investment-services licence, and consequential reputational exclusion from the global venture-capital market.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current-account and capital-account convertibility exists. The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is pegged at a fixed 1:1 parity with the British pound sterling (GBP) and is freely exchangeable at par within the jurisdiction; outside Gibraltar it is not legal tender, therefore all major treasury operations are conducted in GBP, USD, and EUR. The UK abolished exchange controls in 1979, and Gibraltar applies no exchange-control or capital-movement restrictions. Profit remittance, dividend distribution, management-fee repatriation, and carried-interest distributions from a Bermuda Exempted Company operating as a Gibraltar-registered VC GP face zero statutory FX hurdles, zero withholding taxes, and no prior-authorisation regime. Residual frictions are limited to (i) GIP banknotes being non-convertible outside Gibraltar beyond par conversion via a licensed bank, (ii) CRS/FATCA reporting thresholds, (iii) enhanced EDD flagging of the Bermudian offshore classification, and (iv) potential UK CFC or permanent-establishment analysis if principal management and control is exercised from London; no material convertibility or remittance blockage exists.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable — zero statutory surrender obligation. Gibraltar and the United Kingdom impose no mandatory export-proceeds repatriation timeline, no minimum conversion ratio, and no forced sale of foreign currency. A licensed VC GP receiving carry, management fees, or divestment proceeds in USD, EUR, or other foreign currencies may lawfully retain such proceeds in any jurisdiction and in any currency denomination, subject to (i) complete audit-trail documentation under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and the Criminal Finances Act 2017 (Gibraltar), (ii) CRS and FATCA transparency declarations, and (iii) the bank's internal ongoing monitoring rules. If the entity elects Gibraltar tax-resident status, all retained proceeds remain outside scope of Gibraltar income tax unless attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority, lender of last resort, and GBP monetary policy settler for the Crown Dependency-type sterling peg); HM Treasury (ultimate sovereign responsibility for financial stability and international sanctions alignment); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (statutory regulator and AML/CFT supervisor for all licensed banks and investment firms in Gibraltar); Government of Gibraltar Treasury (sole issuer of Gibraltar pound banknotes and coinage at par with GBP).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium, with legal constraints. UK FCA-authorised Electronic Money Institutions (e.g., Ebury, Airwallex, Wise Business) and EU/EEA-licensed EMIs may provide multi-currency GBP/EUR/USD accounts, same-day GBP settlement via Faster Payments, and SEPA-compliant EUR disbursement through their EU passporting entities. Hard legal constraint: Gibraltar is outside the EEA and the SEPA scheme; EMIs cannot issue Gibraltar IBANs or GIP-denominated accounts, and post-Brexit EEA passporting rights do not extend to Gibraltar. Consequently, an EMI can serve only as an auxiliary FX-conversion and disbursement rail, never as the primary escrow/concentration bank for LP capital calls. Additional viability factors: (i) EMI corporate T&Cs frequently exclude investment-vehicle, fund, and pass-through capital-call flows — a full legal review is mandatory before routing; (ii) EMI onboarding of a Bermuda Exempted Company with Gibraltar regulation is typically held for enhanced due diligence and requires the same BO registry, source-of-wealth, and licensing evidence as a bank; (iii) no EMI provides escrow-grade account protection or GFSC-level licensing, so any balance held must be capped and swept daily to a Tier-1 licensed bank; (iv) practical recommendation: use an FCA EMI for standalone FX conversions (GBP-USD-EUR) on deal-by-deal disbursements, not for investor commitment concentration.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

LP commitment funding must follow a strict four-rail protocol. Rail 1 (preferred: cross-border wire) — SWIFT MT103 with ISO 20022 remittance field containing 'CAPITAL CALL — [FUND NAME] — [LP NAME] — [COMMITMENT REF]', purpose code 'CAPP' (capital payments) or narrative equivalent supplied in the bank's remittance-data structure, and full UETR tracking. Rail 2 (UK sterling, domestic) — Faster Payment Service (FPS) for GBP subscriptions ≤£1 million per instruction, with reference prefix 'CC/' to enable automated matching. Rail 3 (EU euro) — SEPA CT (Single Euro Payments Area Credit Transfer) only where the LP holds a euro-denominated account and the receiving Gibraltar bank maintains a euro nostro. Rail 4 (US dollar, US LPs) — Fedwire or CHIPS transfer to the bank's USD correspondent, ensuring finality and no intermediary-hold risk. Mandatory pre-credit controls: bank must receive a witness copy of the capital-call notice 24 hours before settlement; the account-operating agreement must restrict credit confirmation to matched commitment-schedule entries; all inbound funds are subject to sanctions/PEP screening with a UETR-based reconciliation; daily sprint reconciliation against the LP capital-contribution ledger; any currency mismatch (GBP/EUR/USD versus portfolio target currency) is immediately converted only under a pre-audited forward-limit instruction. No cash, cheques, cryptocurrency, or non-bank payment instruments may be accepted for capital calls.Primary clearing rail for all VC investment disbursements is SWIFT MT103 and MT103 STP (same-day priority) from the Gibraltar escrow account to the portfolio company's SPV account, with remittance narrative 'PORTFOLIO [NAME] — SHARE SUBSCRIPTION' or 'PORTFOLIO [NAME] — CONVERTIBLE LOAN ADVANCE', payment type 'BEN' (beneficiary pays all charges), and dual-authorisation execution via a 2-of-3 approved-signatory matrix. Commercial Letters of Credit under ICC UCP 600 are used only in exceptional structured transactions — e.g., a milestone-tranche disbursement into an escrow-receivable structure or a debt-co-investment tranche — where an issuing bank in Gibraltar or a London clearer provides a confirmed LC to the beneficiary SPV and the required transport/delivery documents are limited to material-adverse-change waivers or milestone certificates. ACH, Faster Payments, and EMI internal ledgers are expressly disallowed as exclusive settlement rails for equity subscriptions or convertible-loan disbursements. All outbound flows must clear the pre-registered beneficiary whitelist, carry UETR traceability, and be matched to the executed investment resolution and board-approved side letter. For portfolio companies in latency or restricted currency zones (CNH, INR, KRW, IDR), conversion and settlement proceed through a corresponding-bank NDF lock or a regulated local-currency clearing partner, never through unlicensed parallel channels.Plain-vanilla FX forwards (tenor ≤ 12 months) with a Tier-1 GFSC-authorised or UK-PRA-authorised counterparty, used strictly for committed-capital currency conversion of GBP-USD, GBP-EUR, and EUR-USD exposures arising from capital-call timing mismatches; hedge ratio capped at 100% of the next 12-month forecast disbursement exposure.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) in latency currencies (CNH, INR, KRW, IDR, TRY) permitted solely for hedging known portfolio-company subscription amounts denominated in non-convertible currencies, settled against Bloomberg/Refinitiv fixing rates with licensed counterparties; no speculative NDF trading.Cross-currency swaps (dual-currency or EUR-USD-GBP basis swaps) permitted for long-dated capital-commitment hedging where the underlying LP commitment stream is multi-currency for periods exceeding 12 months, subject to ISDA/CSA documentation and bilateral collateral arrangements.Vanilla FX options (European-style puts/calls) permitted for downside protection of projected carried-interest distributions or management-fee income, purchased only (no short option-writing) with premiums capped at 2% of annual projected management-fee revenue.Prohibited instruments: leveraged or speculative CFD/forex trading, binary options, barrier/exotic options, cryptocurrency derivatives, and any hedge exceeding the documented forecast commitment exposure; all hedging activity must be disclosed in the GP's periodic AML/regulatory return and audited annually.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Medium, with legal constraints. UK FCA-authorised Electronic Money Institutions (e.g., Ebury, Airwallex, Wise Business) and EU/EEA-licensed EMIs may provide multi-currency GBP/EUR/USD accounts, same-day GBP settlement via Faster Payments, and SEPA-compliant EUR disbursement through their EU passporting entities. Hard legal constraint: Gibraltar is outside the EEA and the SEPA scheme; EMIs cannot issue Gibraltar IBANs or GIP-denominated accounts, and post-Brexit EEA passporting rights do not extend to Gibraltar. Consequently, an EMI can serve only as an auxiliary FX-conversion and disbursement rail, never as the primary escrow/concentration bank for LP capital calls. Additional viability factors: (i) EMI corporate T&Cs frequently exclude investment-vehicle, fund, and pass-through capital-call flows — a full legal review is mandatory before routing; (ii) EMI onboarding of a Bermuda Exempted Company with Gibraltar regulation is typically held for enhanced due diligence and requires the same BO registry, source-of-wealth, and licensing evidence as a bank; (iii) no EMI provides escrow-grade account protection or GFSC-level licensing, so any balance held must be capped and swept daily to a Tier-1 licensed bank; (iv) practical recommendation: use an FCA EMI for standalone FX conversions (GBP-USD-EUR) on deal-by-deal disbursements, not for investor commitment concentration.
