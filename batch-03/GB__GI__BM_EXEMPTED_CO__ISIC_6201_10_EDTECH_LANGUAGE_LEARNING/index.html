Competent corporate registry

Registrar of Companies, Companies House Gibraltar, HM Government of Gibraltar, 5 Secretary's Lane, Gibraltar.

Governing statute

Companies Act 2014 (Gibraltar), ss. 5–10 (Incorporation), s. 33 (Memorandum and Articles), s. 57 (Registered Office), ss. 101–105 (Directors and Secretary); combined with the Income Tax (Qualifying Companies) Regulations 2011 for tax-exempt status, mirroring the Bermuda Exempted Company template; Data Protection Act 2019 (Gibraltar) for EdTech data processing.

Formation / constitutional filings

Form G (Application for Registration of a Company) together with the Memorandum and Articles of Association and Form N (Statutory Declaration of Compliance).

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 20 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital; a private company limited by shares may adopt a nominal capital of GIP 1.00 or any stated currency; subscriber shares may be issued at par with no escrow deposit requirement.

Funding & unlock path: For the leased Gibraltar office: obtain a Fire Safety Certificate and annual fire risk assessment under the Fire Safety Act (Gibraltar) from the Gibraltar Fire and Rescue Service; secure structural and electrical inspection approval via the Building Control Department; register the workplace under the Health and Safety at Work Act (Gibraltar) and appoint a Health and Safety Officer if more than five employees; obtain an Occupancy Certificate before staff take residence.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital; a private company limited by shares may adopt a nominal capital of GIP 1.00 or any stated currency; subscriber shares may be issued at par with no escrow deposit requirement.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation); UK VAT authority where applicable: HM Revenue & Customs, VAT Registration Service (https://www.gov.uk/vat-registration)

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company (BM_EXEMPTED_CO) receives no automatic Gibraltar tax ID. If it has no Gibraltar establishment or Gibraltar-source income, no Gibraltar tax registration is required. If a Gibraltar nexus arises, the entity must register as a foreign/overseas company with the Gibraltar Companies Registry, then apply to the Gibraltar Income Tax Office for a tax reference by submitting the Bermuda certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, registered office certificate, director/shareholder registers, and evidence of substance. For direct UK B2C digital sales, register with HMRC as a non-established taxable person through the Non-Union VAT Mini One-Stop Shop (VATOSS) or standard VAT registration; HMRC issues a GB VAT number and may require platform transaction records and payment evidence.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists in Gibraltar or Bermuda for entity classification. Bermuda exempted status is irrevocably elected by incorporation under the Bermuda Companies Act 1981 and maintained with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies. For indirect taxes, the entity may elect UK Non-Union VATOSS registration for B2C digital services, or rely on Apple IAP/Google Play Billing as merchant-of-record arrangements. No hybrid mismatch or offshore exempt-company election is available under Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010; tax treatment is determined by actual residence and economic substance.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar: no VAT/GST/sales or use tax. UK VAT (20%) is destination-based and may apply to B2C digital education supplies to UK consumers when the Bermuda/Gibraltar entity is the supplier; Apple IAP and Google Play Billing normally act as merchant-of-record and collect applicable UK VAT on platform sales.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Gibraltar: nil threshold because no VAT registration exists. UK VAT: no turnover threshold for a non-established supplier making B2C supplies of digital services to UK consumers; the UK GBP 90,000 threshold applicable to UK-established businesses does not govern a foreign entity supplying digital services to UK consumers.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar zero-rating is not applicable because Gibraltar imposes no VAT. Under UK VAT, digital language-learning software supplied to overseas business customers is outside the scope of UK VAT when the recipient belongs outside the UK and reverse-charge or local tax rules apply; B2C supplies to non-UK consumers are not subject to UK VAT because the place of supply is the consumer's country. UK export zero-rating is limited to qualifying physical exported goods, not pure digital subscriptions.

  • Not applicable: pure EdTech subscription services are delivered digitally, with no physical export of goods from Gibraltar or the UK.
  • If physical course materials were exported, the required bundle would be the Customs Export Declaration, Bill of Lading or air waybill, commercial invoice, proof of payment, and bank inward-remittance evidence.
  • No customs or Bill of Lading evidence is required for SaaS or online tutoring under Gibraltar or UK VAT rules.

Gibraltar has no real-time fiscal clearance, no digital stamping, and no mandatory e-invoicing/API clearance regime. UK VAT compliance is electronic under HMRC Making Tax Digital (MTD) when registration applies, with VAT returns filed via MTD-compatible software or the VATOSS portal; there is no government invoice stamping or real-time transaction clearance.

Gibraltar has no VAT clawback mechanism because no VAT is assessed. UK VAT inaccuracies, fraudulent zero-rating, or inappropriate treatment of UK digital B2C supplies as outside scope expose the entity to HMRC assessments, interest, and penalty exposure up to 100% under VATA 1994 and FA 2007 Schedule 24. Refund or zero-rating claims without customer contracts, payment evidence, IP logs, and platform merchant records will be rejected and clawed back. The 180-day period is treated as the internal compliance ceiling for repatriating export revenue; neither Gibraltar nor Bermuda imposes a statutory FX repatriation deadline, but a Bermuda exempted company must keep its exempted status by avoiding domestic Bermuda business activity.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar maintains full currency convertibility for both current-account and capital-account transactions. There are no exchange-control laws, no central bank approval gates, and no restrictions on the repatriation of capital, dividends, or profits for a non-resident Bermuda Exempted Company operating through Gibraltar. Practical limitations arise only from bank-level AML/KYC due diligence, HM Treasury sanctions compliance, and the absence of a domestic interbank FX market, requiring settlement through major London or European correspondent banks.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Gibraltarian law imposes no statutory timeline, no mandatory repayment of export proceeds, and no minimum conversion ratio into Gibraltar pounds. Export-related receipts, including EdTech subscription fees, may be held indefinitely in any convertible currency at a GFSC-licensed bank. However, a licensed bank may request supporting invoices, contracts, and payment evidence for any credit exceeding approximately GBP 100,000 to satisfy its AML/CTF reporting obligations.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the prudential and AML/CTF supervisor for licensed banks; HM Government of Gibraltar as the monetary authority for Gibraltar pound note issuance under the Currency Notes Act 1934; policy alignment with HM Treasury sanctions and financial restrictions; no separate Gibraltar central bank exists.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Moderate. A GFSC-licensed or recognised EEA/UK electronic money institution (EMI) can be used as an auxiliary payment-composition facility for digital settlement with Apple and Google, online card acquirers, and low-value vendor payouts. Legal constraints are decisive: EMIs are not licensed to take deposits, issue commercial letters of credit, open statutory capital escrow accounts, or deliver bilateral FX hedging products. Therefore, the Bermuda Exempted Company must retain at least one full-reserve corporate bank account with a tier-1 GFSC-licensed bank for capital injections, escrow, and FX trade. The offshore EMI may only act as a pass-through collection account, and any funds held in an EMI must remain segregated from the EMI's own balance sheet.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inward routing is by SWIFT MT103 from the Bermuda parent or investor bank into the Gibraltar tier-1 bank's operational or escrow account. The MT103 must carry a purpose narrative such as 'Capital Injection -- Equity Contribution for EdTech Operations' and should reference: (i) the beneficiary account at the Gibraltar bank using BIC and IBAN-like local account number; (ii) the corporate resolution date authorising the injection; and (iii) the ultimate beneficial owner's source-of-funds details. Verification on receipt follows GFSC AML/CTF controls: the bank will match the remitter against approved KYC records, confirm that the Bermuda entity is not subject to HM Treasury sanctions, and request a board resolution and capital account entry for any inbound amount exceeding a bank-defined threshold, typically GBP 100,000. No cash flows, third-party intermediary transfers, or cryptocurrency-funded injections are acceptable.Standard operational disbursements, license fees, vendor payments, and profit distributions are executed as SWIFT MT103 messages cleared through London correspondent banks via CHAPS for GBP or through the New York clearing system for USD. For larger infrastructure or content-licensing procurements, the Gibraltar bank may issue an irrevocable at-sight commercial Letter of Credit under UCP600, with the beneficiary bank confirming or advising the LC; settlement is against presentation of commercial invoice, transport or delivery document, inspection certificate, and beneficiary certificate where necessary. EdTech platform payouts from Apple and Google are received first into the operating account via a local clearing or acquirer settlement and then onward-dispatched by MT103 to the Bermuda entity or operating subsidiaries, with each outward transfer supported by contract references and, where relevant, intercompany invoicing. Profit repatriation is permitted in any convertible currency and should be narrated as 'Profit Distribution' with audited financials and board minutes available on demand.Outright deliverable FX forwards in GBP/USD/EUR with a GFSC-licensed bank or an FCA-regulated counterparty, limited to notional amounts backed by forecast EdTech subscriptions and known trade payables.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for emerging or restricted currencies where physical delivery into Gibraltar is not possible, documented under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement, and subject to the bank's credit risk approval.Cross-currency interest rate swaps (CCIRS) to hedge long-term intercompany loan exposure arising from the Bermuda parent's capital injection into the Gibraltar operational entity.Plain-vanilla FX swaps for managing same-week liquidity between subscription revenue, vendor payments, and dividend repatriation, without speculative leverage or unapproved complex option structures.

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% standard Gibraltar corporate income tax rate applicable to the Bermuda Exempted Company's chargeable profits derived through Gibraltar trading activity

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Gibraltar does not impose withholding tax on dividends paid by a Gibraltar-resident company to any shareholder, whether resident or non-resident, and Bermuda levies no dividend withholding tax on the Bermuda Exempted Company; repatriation of profits by dividend to a Bermuda or United Kingdom beneficial owner is therefore free of source-state dividend withholding tax

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The return must be delivered on or before 30 November in the year following the year of assessment in which the accounting period ends; for a December year-end the return is due by 30 November of the following year, with the related self-assessed tax payable by the same date

Deadline: · Frequency:

No later than 30 June in the year following the end of the Bermuda exempted company's financial year; for a 31 December financial year-end the EC Declaration is due by 30 June of the following year
