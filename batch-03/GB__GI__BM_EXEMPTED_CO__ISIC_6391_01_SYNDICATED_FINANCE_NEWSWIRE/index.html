Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar - Commissioner of Income Tax (Income Tax Office); official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi - e-services and tax filing via the Gibraltar Tax Office online portal (no separate VAT portal exists because Gibraltar levies no VAT).

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company is required to obtain a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) only if it creates a taxable presence in Gibraltar through a permanent establishment, generates Gibraltar-source income, or becomes managed and controlled from Gibraltar. Procedure for foreign entities: (1) determine non-residency by documenting that central management and control are exercised outside Gibraltar; (2) file a written application with the Commissioner of Income Tax enclosing the Bermuda Certificate of Incorporation, Bermuda Tax Exemption Certificate, constitutional documents, board minutes on control/management location, and an explanation of the financial newswire activity; (3) upon acceptance, the Commissioner issues a TRN. No separate VAT ID exists. When the entity remains non-resident and tax-exempt in Bermuda, registration is voluntary rather than mandatory.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no US-style check-the-box election. The relevant statutory election is between resident and non-resident status based on central management and control, as provided under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act. A foreign tax-exempt company may elect to be assessed only on Gibraltar-source income under a territorial basis, attributing profits to a Gibraltar permanent establishment where one exists. Hybrid mismatch elections are not available in domestic law; the General Anti-Abuse Rule (GAAR) and controlled foreign company rules apply to prevent arbitrage with Bermuda or UK tax treatment.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

VAT (Not Applicable - Gibraltar has no VAT, GST, or general sales and use tax regime)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No turnover threshold applies. Gibraltar does not impose value added tax or GST on supplies of goods or services. A Bermuda Exempted Company providing syndicated finance newswire API services to non-resident clients incurs no indirect-tax registration obligation. Import duty may arise on physical importation of goods under the Gibraltar Integrated Tariff, but this is a customs duty payable per transaction, not a turnover-based registration threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable because Gibraltar does not operate a VAT regime. There is no 0% export VAT rate to claim for goods or offshore services. The supply of financial market news aggregation and syndicated finance newswire services by a Bermuda Exempted Company is outside the scope of any Gibraltar indirect tax. If physical goods were exported from Gibraltar, they would be outside the scope of import duty rather than zero-rated for VAT, and export documentation would need to evidence removal from Gibraltar.

  • Customs Export Declaration (for any physical goods exported from Gibraltar; not required for digital newswire services)
  • Commercial invoice and packing list (where goods are involved; for services a billing invoice suffices)
  • Bermuda Tax Exemption Certificate and Gibraltar non-resident TRN certificate (to evidence tax status and support any customs or duty-relief application)

No electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or B2B/B2G e-invoicing mandate is enforced in Gibraltar. The entity may issue invoices, Stripe receipts, or API billing records without government clearance. If goods are physically imported into Gibraltar, a customs declaration must be submitted through Gibraltar's electronic customs system, but this does not apply to the export of financial data services.

There is no VAT export zero-rating mechanism in Gibraltar, so classical VAT clawback exposure does not arise. However, misrepresentation of residence or artificially arranging Gibraltar operations to avoid tax may trigger the Gibraltar GAAR, resulting in denial of tax advantage, retrospective counteraction for up to 7 years, interest at 8% per annum, and penalties up to 100% of the tax avoided. False customs declarations on any goods movement carry criminal penalties including fines up to £50,000 and imprisonment for up to 5 years, plus forfeiture of goods and revocation of customs privileges.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound is fully convertible at a fixed 1:1 peg with sterling; no exchange-control or prior-approval regime applies to capital or current account transactions. Profit remittances and dividend distributions to the Bermuda parent are unrestricted, provided the board has authorized the distribution, distributable reserves are positive, and standard anti-money-laundering source-of-funds documentation is supplied to the corridor banks. The realistic constraints on convertibility are UK and international financial sanctions, beneficial-ownership transparency, and correspondent-bank de-risking policies rather than statutory exchange controls.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender requirement exists in Gibraltar; export receipts may be retained in any permitted currency with an authorized credit institution. If proceeds are received in Gibraltar pounds, the fixed 1:1 sterling peg applies. There is no mandatory conversion percentage and no prescribed repatriation timeline, but all foreign-exchange transactions must be recorded and retained under AML/CFT record-keeping rules.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the financial-services and AML/CTF regulator; Commissioner of Currency (Gibraltar) as the note-issuing authority; no separate exchange-control central bank, with HM Treasury (United Kingdom) retaining overarching monetary and sanctions oversight.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Moderate-to-high for receiving treasury API flows, subscription receivables, and low-value disbursements; low for capital escrow and fiduciary deposit. A GFSC-licensed e-money institution or authorized credit institution can onboard the Bermuda exempted company remotely under a compliant digital identity process. Unlicensed offshore digital EMIs are not permitted to conduct e-money services in or from Gibraltar unless they operate through a locally licensed sponsor or licensed Gibraltar branch. UK FCA e-money passporting does not automatically extend to Gibraltar, so a legal opinion and a local sponsorship agreement are required before routing live liquidity through an offshore EMI.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% (Gibraltar standard Corporation Tax rate on taxable profits; no separate capital gains tax and no VAT).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid to residents or non-residents; Bermuda also imposes no dividend withholding tax on exempted companies.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The return must be filed no later than 12 months after the end of the accounting period (for a 31 December year-end, by 31 December of the following year). A notice of assessment may be raised by the Commissioner of Income Tax if the return is not delivered by that date.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Box 1: Company identity particulars Registered name, Gibraltar company number, registered office, principal place of business, date of incorporation and Gibraltar tax reference number. For a Bermuda Exempted Company, the Bermuda certificate of incorporation and exempted status are also recorded in the working papers. Non-resident companies must state the jurisdiction of tax residence and produce a tax residence certificate or equivalent from Bermuda; if no Gibraltar-source income is present, a nil territoriality statement must be attached. Late filing: fixed penalty of GIP£ 500 plus a daily surcharge of GIP£ 50 per day, capped at GIP£ 5,000. Inaccurate tax returns: tax-geared penalty up to 100% of the tax understated (200% for deliberate and concealed errors), interest at 5% per annum; criminal prosecution can attract a fine up to GIP£ 50,000 and/or imprisonment up to 7 years.
Box 2: Central management and control (CMC) declaration Location where board meetings are held, strategic decisions are adopted, key employees act and bank accounts are operated. If CMC is exercised in Gibraltar, the company is treated as Gibraltar tax resident and its worldwide assessable profits enter the Gibraltar computation. A Bermuda Exempted Company should answer 'No' to Gibraltar CMC only where management and control are genuinely exercised outside Gibraltar. A false declaration is a criminal offence and does not prevent the Commissioner from determining residence on factual grounds. Late filing: fixed penalty of GIP£ 500 plus a daily surcharge of GIP£ 50 per day, capped at GIP£ 5,000. Inaccurate tax returns: tax-geared penalty up to 100% of the tax understated (200% for deliberate and concealed errors), interest at 5% per annum; criminal prosecution can attract a fine up to GIP£ 50,000 and/or imprisonment up to 7 years.
Box 3: Accounting period and profit and loss details Accounting start and end dates; turnover from syndicated financial news, data feed licences, API access fees, subscription revenue and related services; deductible operating expenses; depreciation and capital allowances. Only report Gibraltar-source trading income or profits attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment. Foreign subscription fees from overseas subscribers may be outside the Gibraltar tax base if they are neither accrued in, derived from, nor received in Gibraltar. Late filing: fixed penalty of GIP£ 500 plus a daily surcharge of GIP£ 50 per day, capped at GIP£ 5,000. Inaccurate tax returns: tax-geared penalty up to 100% of the tax understated (200% for deliberate and concealed errors), interest at 5% per annum; criminal prosecution can attract a fine up to GIP£ 50,000 and/or imprisonment up to 7 years.
Box 4: Related-party transactions and transfer pricing schedule Aggregate values, counterparty names, nature and commercial terms of all controlled transactions with affiliates, including a Bermuda parent or financing vehicle. The selected arm's length methodology and supporting benchmark must be referenced. Non-resident filers must complete this schedule when Gibraltar connected-party transactions exceed GIP£ 1,000,000 per accounting period; otherwise a nil schedule and entity structure statement is required. Late filing: fixed penalty of GIP£ 500 plus a daily surcharge of GIP£ 50 per day, capped at GIP£ 5,000. Inaccurate tax returns: tax-geared penalty up to 100% of the tax understated (200% for deliberate and concealed errors), interest at 5% per annum; criminal prosecution can attract a fine up to GIP£ 50,000 and/or imprisonment up to 7 years.
Box 5: Loss relief and deductions Current-year trading losses, losses brought forward within the five-year restriction, deductible interest, statutory deductions and any double taxation relief credits. Non-residents may claim deductions only against Gibraltar-source profits. Losses of the Bermuda exempted company or of foreign affiliates cannot be surrendered into the Gibraltar return. Late filing: fixed penalty of GIP£ 500 plus a daily surcharge of GIP£ 50 per day, capped at GIP£ 5,000. Inaccurate tax returns: tax-geared penalty up to 100% of the tax understated (200% for deliberate and concealed errors), interest at 5% per annum; criminal prosecution can attract a fine up to GIP£ 50,000 and/or imprisonment up to 7 years.

Where no profits are chargeable in Gibraltar, enter nil and attach the exemption or territoriality statement to prevent an automatic estimated assessment. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File: GIP£ 1,000,000 aggregate value of controlled transactions per accounting period; Master File: GIP£ 50,000,000 consolidated group turnover and GIP£ 1,000,000 Gibraltar controlled transactions. Documentation must be contemporaneous and provided to the Commissioner within 30 days of demand.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance Code 2019 applies to any company deriving income from a 'relevant activity'. For a financial newswire aggregation and syndication feed, the relevant activities are 'distribution and service centre' (if services are provided to a connected person) and, where applicable, 'headquarters'. The company must demonstrate in Gibraltar: (i) physical premises and operating expenditure proportionate to the activity; (ii) qualified employees with authority; (iii) board meetings and strategic management in Gibraltar; and (iv) the core income-generating activities are undertaken in Gibraltar. Annual economic substance reporting is mandatory to the Gibraltar Companies House. A 'pure equity holding' exemption is available only to holding companies and cannot be used by an active commercial newswire business.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Dishonest Tax Return Filing

Statutory sanction: Penalty equal to 100% of the tax undercharged (200% for deliberate concealment), late payment interest at 5% per annum, maximum financial fine of GIP£ 50,000 and imprisonment for up to 7 years on conviction for tax fraud under the Income Tax Act 2010.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced; directors, shadow directors and compliance officers who knowingly participate in the fraud are jointly and severally liable for unpaid taxes and civil restitution, and may face director disqualification for up to 15 years, freezing orders, travel bans and a custodial sentence.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Under the Gibraltar Economic Substance regime: fixed penalty of GIP£ 25,000 plus daily penalty of GIP£ 1,000 per day of continuing breach; on court application, financial penalty up to GIP£ 250,000; failure to comply may lead to strike-off and cancellation of the Gibraltar company's registration.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for penalties if they authorise non-filing or provide false declarations; court may order director disqualification and travel bans, require restitution of the tax benefit, and sanction imprisonment for knowingly making a false statement.
Transfer Pricing Misstatement / Disguised Profit Repatriation

Statutory sanction: Arm's length adjustment by the Commissioner with additional tax at 12.5%, disallowance of excessive deductions, late payment interest at 5% per annum, and tax-geared penalties up to 200% of the additional tax for deliberate and concealed transfer pricing misstatements.

Director personal liability: Directors causing the company to file false transfer pricing documentation may be personally liable for fraudulent trading; the court can unwind the transactions, order civil restitution, impose freezing orders and travel restrictions, and hold directors liable for the company's tax deficiency in insolvency.
AML/CTF and Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Fine up to GIP£ 100,000 per breach plus daily ongoing penalty of GIP£ 2,000; for money laundering offences, imprisonment up to 5 years and confiscation orders; for false AML declarations, up to 2 years' imprisonment and an unlimited fine under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015.

Director personal liability: Directors and the nominated officer are personally liable where non-compliance was intentional or reckless; civil liability extends to traced laundered assets, directors may be banned from leaving Gibraltar, subject to asset seizure, and disqualified permanently from serving as directors.
