Governing statute

Gibraltar Companies Act 2014 (Cap. 100), as amended — Sections 1–85 (formation, memorandum, articles, directors, registered office and registers); Companies (Forms and Fees) Regulations 2014; Beneficial Ownership (Companies) Regulations 2017.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up share capital. The exempted company may be incorporated with nil issued shares, or with any nominal share capital denominated in any currency, and no escrow or capital deposit account is required by the Registrar of Companies. Share consideration may be satisfied by cash or by a non-cash contribution such as an intercompany IP assignment.

Funding & unlock path: No fire safety or structural load directly applies to an IP HoldCo without physical plant. If the company occupies a registered office in Gibraltar (whether serviced or leased), the premises must hold a current building completion certificate and a fire safety certificate in accordance with the Building Control (Gibraltar) Regulations; any office staff activities are subject to general Health and Safety at Work (Gibraltar) duties, and a baseline fire risk assessment at the registered office is recommended within 60 days of occupancy.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up share capital. The exempted company may be incorporated with nil issued shares, or with any nominal share capital denominated in any currency, and no escrow or capital deposit account is required by the Registrar of Companies. Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax; e-services via https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/); HM Customs Gibraltar for indirect taxes (https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/customs); Bermuda Registrar of Companies for entity registration and exempt-company annual fee (https://www.roc.gov.bm). No filing is made to HMRC unless the entity separately creates a UK VAT establishment.

E-government filing portal

(1) Incorporate as a Bermuda Exempted Company with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies; obtain Bermuda company registration number; pay Bermuda Government annual fee (no Bermuda income tax registration exists). (2) If central management and control are exercised from Gibraltar, complete Gibraltar income tax registration with the Commissioner of Income Tax within 30 days of commencement; obtain a Gibraltar Reference Number (GRN). (3) File annual corporation tax return, financial statements, and the Economic Substance Return (Income Tax (Substance Requirements) Act 2020) for the IP licensing activity. (4) For customs purposes, register as importer/exporter with HM Customs Gibraltar; no VAT ID exists because Gibraltar levies no VAT/GST. (5) Optional UK VAT registration with HMRC at the GBP 90,000 threshold applies only if the entity makes taxable B2C/B2B supplies through a UK establishment.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box election is available in Bermuda or Gibraltar. Bermuda Exempted Company status is an administrative exemption, not an elective taxation regime. Gibraltar has no elective tax classification for companies; applicable mechanisms are limited to (a) group relief/grouping elections under the Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended), and (b) statutory hybrid mismatch rules applied mandatorily without election. Tax residence must therefore be managed by factual control: an IP HoldCo resident in Gibraltar is taxed at entity level at 12.5% on profits, while retaining Tax_Exempt_Zone status in Bermuda.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax is levied in Gibraltar; indirect taxation is limited to Gibraltar Import Duty, Customs Duty, and Excise Duty under the post-Brexit Gibraltar Customs Tariff. UK VAT (20%) can apply to a UK-established branch or UK B2B supplies only if the entity voluntarily registers with HMRC.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No turnover threshold triggers indirect tax registration in Gibraltar for the IP licensing business (royalties are intangible services outside the charge to customs duty). Any importer of goods into Gibraltar must obtain a Customs Importer Code; there is no de minimis exemption for commercial goods. UK VAT registration threshold (where a separate UK establishment is created): GBP 90,000 (1 April 2024).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

N/A in Gibraltar: exported IP licensing services (offshore royalty and patent sub-licensing supplies) are simply outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect tax, rather than zero-rated, because Gibraltar levies no VAT. Under any separate UK VAT registration, B2B licensing supplies to non-UK customers are outside the scope of UK VAT under the place-of-supply rules, and zero-rating applies to physical goods exported from a UK establishment; no VAT refund arises on a Gibraltar-only flow.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank FX credit advice
  • Executed global IP patent and trademark licence agreement with registered grant and sub-licence chain
  • Commercial invoice evidencing royalty or sub-licence consideration

Gibraltar mandates no fiscal e-invoicing, continuous transaction control, or digital stamping. Customs declarations for any physical goods move electronically via the Gibraltar/UK Customs Declaration Service (CDS); royalty and sub-licence invoices are ordinary commercial invoices retained with the licence agreement. No real-time API clearance or government approval of invoices is required.

Gibraltar/Bermuda customs abuse attracts forfeiture of goods, penalties up to 100% of duty and criminal prosecution under the Customs Duties Ordinance. For any fraudulent UK VAT export claim: 100% penalty plus interest, civil penalties up to GBP 9,000, imprisonment up to 7 years, and six-year retrospective clawback. Gibraltar income tax fraud on IP royalty flows attracts tax-geared penalties up to 100%, interest, and economic-substance failure penalties up to GBP 10,000 with potential strike-off from the Companies Register and recharacterisation of IP income.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Current-account convertibility is effectively unlimited for IP royalty flows, sub-license fee remittances, and trade-related payments in GIP, GBP, EUR, and USD. Capital-account convertibility is also fully open, subject only to UK (OFSI), EU/UN, and US (OFAC) sanctions screening and to CRS/FATCA reporting obligations. The Bermuda Exempted Company is a non-resident enterprise under Bermuda's Exchange Control Act 1972 and is exempt from BMA approval for outward remittances; profit extraction and shareholder distributions are free of conversion restrictions. Practical friction is limited to enhanced due diligence by the receiving bank where the ultimate beneficial owner resides outside low-risk jurisdictions; no pre-approval or central bank permit is required for ordinary royalty transactions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender timeline, mandatory conversion ratio, or hard-currency repatriation obligation applies in Gibraltar, Bermuda, or the United Kingdom. Royalty proceeds derived from cross-border patent and trademark sub-licensing may be retained in the currency of receipt (GIP, GBP, EUR, USD) in full and without conversion to local currency. The unitary peg of GIP to GBP obviates any reserve-surrender mechanism, and the Bermuda Exempted Company's exemption certificate eliminates BMA exchange-control filing for export proceeds.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar has no independent central bank. Monetary sovereignty for sterling is vested in the Bank of England (United Kingdom), while the Government of Gibraltar issues the Gibraltar pound (GIP) at a 1:1 peg to GBP under the Currency Notes Act 1934. The Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the integrated financial regulator licensing and supervising banks, payment institutions, and electronic money institutions in Gibraltar. For the Bermuda Exempted Company constituent, the Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) is the exchange-control and licensing authority for the Bermudian parent, acting as the foreign-exchange administration for any Bermuda-side capital matters.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium. UK/FCA-authorised electronic money institutions (EMIs) and payment institutions passported or recognized in Gibraltar (e.g., Wise Business, Revolut Business, Airwallex) are viable as secondary collection gateways for royalty receipts in GBP/EUR/USD and for low-value intra-group disbursements. They are not acceptable as the primary treasury or capital-escrow account for a Bermuda Exempted Company classified as Medium_Risk_Enhanced because most EMIs lack lending authority, have volatile account-term policies, and provide no committed liquidity or FX hedging documentation under ISDA. Offshore EMIs domiciled outside the UK/EEA are rated Low and are generally rejected by GFSC-regulated banks as recognized payment counterparties for a tax-exempt IP HoldCo. Any EMI balances must be swept daily into the Gibraltar International Bank operational account to preserve audit trail integrity.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Paid-in capital from ultimate shareholders shall enter via SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's private or corporate account to the Gibraltar International Bank operational account (GIP or GBP denomination). The compulsory purpose code for equity capital injection must be the OECD/UK BOP code 'T0100' (Equity capital – shares) or, if structured as shareholder loan, 'C0300' (Intercompany lending). The remittance must reference the Bermuda exempted company number and the executed subscription letter or loan agreement. Sums above the equivalent of GBP 1,000,000 require a pre-notification to the bank's compliance team with the board resolution, updated UBO register, and source-of-wealth confirmations delivered via authenticated courier before first credit.Outbound disbursements are executed through SWIFT MT103 for all intercompany royalty sub-license fees and external licensee settlements; Commercial Letters of Credit (LC at sight) are reserved for bundled or high-value patent-package transactions exceeding EUR 250,000 where documentary collection is contractually required. GIP-denominated instruments are cleared via the issuing bank's GBP Nostro in the UK; EUR payments settle through TARGET2; and USD payments are cleared via a US correspondent through CHIPS/Fedwire. Each outbound leg must be accompanied by a commercial invoice or fee note, the contract reference, and intercompany arm's-length evidence. For same-day treasury concentration, SEPA Instant is permissible for euro-denominated beneficiary accounts within the SEPA zone.Outright deliverable FX forward contracts on GIP/GBP–EUR and GIP/GBP–USD pairs with tenors up to 12 months, executed with a GFSC-licensed bank.FX swap (rollover) agreements converting USD royalty receipts into GBP or GIP through the bank's dealing desk.Cross-currency interest rate swaps (CCIRS) between GBP and EUR, or GBP and USD, under an ISDA Master Agreement with a UK or Gibraltar authorized credit institution.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on USD–GBP or EUR–GBP solely where an offshore counterparty cannot settle onshore; the GIP hard peg to GBP renders the NDF economically equivalent to a deliverable forward.European-style FX options (purchased only) and zero-cost collars on EUR–GBP and USD–GBP, with premium paid upfront, to cap translation risk on forecast royalty streams.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Filing deadline is 30 November following the end of the Gibraltar Year of Assessment (YOA runs 1 July to 30 June). For a 31 December financial year-end falling within the YOA, the return is due by 30 November of the same calendar year, i.e. not later than 11 months after the accounting period end. Outstanding tax is payable by the assessment date, with self-assessment instalments no later than 31 December of the YOA; audited accounts are required where turnover exceeds GBP 1 million.

Deadline: · Frequency:

The ESR is due within 12 months of the end of the financial year; for a 31 December year-end, on or before 31 December of the following year.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Gibraltar pound (GIP, pegged to GBP). Country-by-Country Report threshold: consolidated group revenue exceeding GBP 700,000,000 (OECD BEPS Action 13). Local File/Master File: no statutory monetary threshold is codified, but the Commissioner administratively requires a full OECD-compliant TP study where annual controlled cross-border transactions (intercompany royalties, patent licence fees) exceed GBP 1,000,000; TP documentation must be produced within 45 days of a formal notice.

Economic substance scope: The Income Tax (Economic Substance) Regulations 2019 (as amended) require any entity carrying on a Relevant Activity in Gibraltar - including high-risk IP holding and patent licensing - to demonstrate adequate substance in Gibraltar. The company must be directed and managed in Gibraltar: at least two board meetings per year in Gibraltar, board minutes kept in Gibraltar, qualified resident directors, physical premises, proportionate employees, and the carrying on of CIGAs in Gibraltar. High-risk IP (IP acquired from connected parties or funded intra-group) raises a presumption of non-compliance; the taxpayer bears the onus of rebutting it with contemporaneous evidence. A Bermuda Exempted Company using Gibraltar solely as a treaty gateway and pure-IP mailbox is treated as a shell for economic substance purposes, triggering penalties, information exchange, and CFC reattribution to the UK shareholder.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Cheating the Public Revenue

Statutory sanction: Under Sections 100-112 of the Income Tax Act 2010, the Commissioner imposes the full under-assessed tax, penalties of up to 225% of the understated tax, and interest at 7% per annum. Criminal prosecution for cheating the public revenue carries an unlimited fine and imprisonment of up to 7 years, with confiscation of gross hidden profits.

Director personal liability: Directors are treated as controlling officers and are jointly and severally liable for the corporate tax debt where the evasion resulted from their fault or privity; personal penalties, travel bans for unpaid assessments, and imprisonment up to 7 years apply; corporate veil piercing is automatic under the joint liability provisions.
Treaty Abuse / Royalty Stripping through Hybrid Exempted Company

Statutory sanction: General Anti-Abuse Rule (GAAR) and Income Tax (Anti-Avoidance) Regulations counteraction: the tax advantage is denied, the royalty is re-sourced to the UK/Bermuda, and penalties of 100%-150% of the counteracted tax are applied; the TRC is withdrawn retroactively.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorised the artificial structure face disqualification under the Companies Act 2014 (up to 15 years), personal liability for the counteracted tax under joint liability powers, and criminal sanctions for knowingly making false TRC declarations.
Economic Substance Failure / Shell Company Red Flag

Statutory sanction: Tiered penalties up to GBP 300,000 under the Economic Substance Regulations 2019; the Registrar publicly lists the non-compliant company, exchanges information with HM Revenue & Customs, and strikes the company off the register; the entity loses treaty residence and becomes exposed to full UK CFC attribution.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign a false ESR commit an offence carrying fines and up to 2 years imprisonment; directors of a struck-off company face automatic disqualification up to 15 years and personal restitution for liabilities incurred after the substance failure is known.
Transfer Pricing Violation / Aggressive IP Migration

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner makes a TP adjustment to the arm's length royalty, reattributes profit to the UK licensee, imposes primary tax of 12.5% on the adjusted Gibraltar profit, interest at 7%, and penalties of up to 100% of the additional assessment where the taxpayer failed to exercise reasonable care.

Director personal liability: Where the pricing arrangement is found to be a sham stripping UK taxable profit, the corporate veil is pierced; directors are personally liable for the diverted profits and face disqualification, and the controlling shareholder may be subject to a UK CFC charge equal to the full diverted royalty income.
AML / UBO Red Flag and Sanctions Evasion

Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act, the entity and directors face unlimited fines, confiscation orders equal to the value of the laundered royalty flows, and AML regulatory penalties up to GBP 500,000 where the conduit is deemed a relevant financial business; the company is also exposed to immediate licence suspension.

Director personal liability: Directors with knowledge or suspicion of money laundering face personal criminal liability carrying up to 14 years imprisonment; travel bans and freezing orders are issued against the directors; civil restitution and compensation to the defrauded licensor are enforced by private action on the basis of knowing receipt.
