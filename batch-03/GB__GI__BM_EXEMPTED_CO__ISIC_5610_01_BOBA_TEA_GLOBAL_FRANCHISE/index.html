Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (Commissioner of Income Tax; e-filing via the Government of Gibraltar portal at https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi); Gibraltar Customs and Excise Department for indirect/customs taxes; Gibraltar Companies Registry (operated within the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission) for external company branch registration.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: File an external company registration with the Gibraltar Companies Registry within one month of establishing a place of business, submitting certified Bermuda Certificate of Incorporation, constitutional documents, director register, and a Gibraltar registered-office address. Step 2: Apply through the Government of Gibraltar portal for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) using the non-resident company with permanent establishment profile; the Commissioner of Income Tax issues the TRN and, where staff are engaged, an employer PAYE and Social Security reference simultaneously. Step 3: Register with Gibraltar Customs and Excise for a Customs Operator Code if goods are to be imported or exported. Step 4: File annual income tax returns and audited branch financial statements; the Gibraltar branch is taxed on a territorial basis while the Bermuda parent remains tax-exempt in Bermuda.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: the Bermuda Exempted Company is exempt from Bermuda income tax (except for in-scope multinational groups subject to the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 at 15% for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025). Its Gibraltar branch is an opaque permanent establishment of an external company chargeable at Gibraltar's standard 12.5% corporate rate solely on Gibraltar-source trading profits, with participation exemption, IP box, and territorial sourcing reliefs available; no pass-through treatment exists at entity level.

Available election forms

No check-the-box election is available because the Bermuda Exempted Company is a per-se corporation under U.S. Treasury Regulation §301.7701-2(b)(8) and is opaque for Gibraltar tax purposes. Gibraltar does, however, permit elections under the Income Tax Act 2010 to apply the participation exemption, the 50% partial exemption for intra-group financing income, and the patent-box regime to qualifying IP. Hybrid mismatch alignment elections under the Gibraltar Income Tax (Hybrid Mismatches) Rules 2017 may also be filed to pre-empt UK anti-hybrid counteraction under Finance Act 2016, Part VI, where the structure would otherwise produce double-deduction or deduction/non-inclusion outcomes.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility for both current and capital account transactions under Gibraltar law and UK monetary policy. There is no exchange-control permission, no restriction on profit remittance, and no residual requirement to seek Bank of England consent. Corporate distributions, royalty remittances, and capital repatriation from a Gibraltar-resident or Bermuda-exempted company are lawful and unrestricted subject to anti-money laundering (JMLSG/GFSC circulars) and the UK Economic Crime (Anti-Money Laundering) Levy reporting thresholds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Gibraltar and the United Kingdom impose no mandatory surrender or conversion ratio for export proceeds. Exporters may retain proceeds in any permitted currency; however, banking reporting obligations under UK/GFSC AML Regulations and EU Sanctions/UNFSF asset-freeze regimes require purpose-of-payment coding and automatic suspicious transaction reporting for cash or wire amounts above EUR 10,000 equivalent.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England and HM Treasury hold monetary jurisdiction; the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) supervises licensed banks, the Government of Gibraltar maintains note-issuing authority under the Currency Notes Act, and the Gibraltar pound (GIP) is fixed at par with GBP (1:1) with full interchangeability. No distinct central bank control over FX beyond UK/EU sanctions and AML obligations.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Medium-Low. Leading UK/Gibraltar digital EMIs (e.g., Revolut Business, Wise, Mettle) accept onboarding for a Bermuda Exempted Company with a clean registry profile, but restrict high-frequency franchise royalty collections, bulk syrup supplier settlements, and use of funds for persistent B2B trade. Offshore/boutique EMIs are viable only for overflow multi-currency balances and pre-funded 'chest' accounts; the primary treasury rail must be a Tier 1 clearing bank account in Gibraltar or the UK with SWIFT correspondent access.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Root cause: Bermuda Exempted Company structure utilises nominee shareholders and a corporate service provider as registered agent, creating a veil over ultimate beneficial ownership.

Defense evidence: Provide (i) certified copy of the Certificate of Exemption and Memorandum of Association from the Bermuda Registrar of Companies; (ii) completed Bermuda Beneficial Ownership (BO) Register return with encrypted UBO IDs; (iii) up-to-date register of directors and members; (iv) each UBO's notarised passport and proof of address (<3 months); (v) signed declaration of non-nominee status or nominee trust deed with full underlying disclosure.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 5,000,000 annual related-party gross turnover, or GIP 1,000,000 for a single controlled transaction, triggers Local File maintenance; Master File required where group turnover exceeds GIP 25,000,000; Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations require records for all 'relevant activities' regardless of threshold

Economic substance scope: Bermuda Exempted Company is subject to Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) and must file an annual Economic Substance Declaration; franchise licensing and distribution activities are 'relevant activities'; annual board meetings, physical presence, and core income-generating activity in Bermuda required unless a tax-resident non-Bermuda exemption is separately certified; Gibraltar also imposes economic substance through EU-inspired rules, requiring sufficient local substance for pass-through income treatment

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Return

Statutory sanction: Fine up to GIP 1,000,000 and/or imprisonment up to 5 years under Gibraltar Income Tax Act; daily principal sum equal to double the tax evaded; loss of exempt status; assessment at 10% standard rate with back interest

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners can be personally assessed for the tax, penalties, and costs; travel bans may be imposed by the Gibraltar Court; confiscation orders under Proceeds of Crime Act; prison time up to 5 years
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Bermuda Economic Substance Regulations: penalty of BMD 50,000–BMD 350,000, plus BMD 10,000 per day for continued breach; Gibraltar equivalent failure may trigger strike-off and deregistration; assessments are made on income (10% on rev'd chargeable income) plus interest

Director personal liability: Directors have exposure to disqualification, travel bans, and civil restitution; failure to file economic substance declaration triggers personal criminal liability in Bermuda; director travel bans are judicially available in Gibraltar for economic substance non-compliance
AML / Money Laundering (Royalty Wire & Syrup Trade)

Statutory sanction: Under Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act: fines up to GIP 500,000 or 10% of annual turnover; asset freezing orders; AML registration suspension; possible revocation of Gibraltar commercial licence; Bermuda counterpart sanctions apply under Proceeds of Crime Act 1997 (Bermuda)

Director personal liability: Directors/officers personally liable for facilitating money laundering: imprisonment up to 14 years; unlimited financial penalties; confiscation of personal and corporate assets; travel bans as condition of bail or court order
Transfer Pricing / Permanent Establishment Risk

Statutory sanction: Transfer pricing adjustment to arm's length; penalty 20% of the revised profit adjustment; interest at 2% monthly; possible additional assessment of Gibraltar-source profit at 10%; UK-HMRC recharacterisation of PE could generate substantial UK corporate tax + surcharges

Director personal liability: Directors who signed incorrect TP documentation can be liable for civil penalties up to GIP 100,000; if dishonesty proved, personal criminal liability; UK director disqualification and travel bans may apply if the PE issue is litigated
Failure to Maintain DTA / Beneficial Ownership Compliance

Statutory sanction: DTA relief denied; assessed at 10% full rate plus back taxes and interest; administrative penalties of GIP 500,000; potential exposure to EU ATAD/DAC6 reporting and cross-border arrangement penalties

Director personal liability: Civil liability for unpaid taxes caused by failure to register beneficial owners; travel restrictions from Gibraltar; personal fines under Gibraltar Companies (Beneficial Ownership) Regulations up to GIP 500,000; disqualification orders
