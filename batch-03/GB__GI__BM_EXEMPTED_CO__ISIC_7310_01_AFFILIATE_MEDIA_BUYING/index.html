Available election forms

Bermuda election: application for the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection undertaking (Companies Act 1981, s.115) confirming tax-exempt status to 31 March 2035. Gibraltar elections: there is no check-the-box and no hybrid-mismatch election; a foreign entity may choose to operate through a non-resident branch (taxed only on Gibraltar-attributable profits) or a Gibraltar-incorporated resident subsidiary (12.5% on Gibraltar-source trading profits); statutory group relief is available by election for 75% Gibraltar group companies; the former 'Qualifying Company' 50% tax reduction is closed to new entrants.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No_General_VAT_GST: Gibraltar is outside the EU VAT area and does not operate VAT or GST. The only import-related levies are Gibraltar customs import duties under the Import Duties Act 1961 plus selected duties on alcohol, tobacco and fuel. Digital advertising and affiliate lead-generation services are entirely outside any Gibraltar indirect tax charge.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable in GIP — no VAT registration threshold exists because there is no VAT. Customs registration and a Gibraltar EORI are required only where physical goods are imported, with no monetary threshold. The services-only affiliate media-buying business carries no indirect-tax registration obligation.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — Gibraltar does not levy VAT/GST, so there is no 0% export rating and no refund mechanism. Cross-border affiliate media buying and digital advertising services are outside Gibraltar indirect taxation regardless of customer location. For direct tax, export service income is Gibraltar-source only where the trade is carried on in Gibraltar; revenue earned from non-Gibraltar advertisers and affiliate networks is foreign-source and excluded from the territorial charge absent a Gibraltar permanent establishment.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration (electronic SAD/free-format export entry via ASYCUDA World) for any physical goods movements
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing export from Gibraltar or Bermuda
  • Inward Remittance Certificate and bank SWIFT/AFT credit evidence showing foreign-exchange repatriation of export proceeds

No real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, API clearance or digital stamping regime applies to services in Gibraltar. Physical-goods customs declarations are filed electronically through the Gibraltar Customs Service ASYCUDA World platform; commercial invoicing follows English common law, and no VAT line item is permitted or required.

There is no VAT clawback because no VAT can be zero-rated. The Income Tax Office may nonetheless open enquiries under the Income Tax Act 2010 into residence, central management and control, or permanent-establishment assertions: failure to notify chargeability, late filing or inaccurate returns attract penalties of up to 100% of understated tax plus interest, increasing to 200% in cases of fraud or neglect; the Gibraltar general anti-abuse rule permits retrospective recharacterization of offshore arrangements, and assessments may be extended to 6 years (21 years for fraud). Losing the Bermuda exempted status, failing to pay Bermuda annual government fees, or dissolving the Exempted Company terminates the tax-protection undertaking and exposes the structure to full tax.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GBP is freely convertible on both current and capital account under UK law, and Gibraltar maintains a fully liberalized exchange-control regime with no restrictions on profit remittance, dividend distribution, or repatriation of invested capital. The Bermuda Exempted Company, having been granted 'non-resident' status by the BMA under the Exchange Control Act 1972, may transact wholly in foreign currency (USD/GBP/EUR) without exchange-control consent. Residual compliance obligations arise solely from: (i) AML/CFT and sanctions-screening under the UK Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018; (ii) CRS/FATCA reporting through both Bermuda and Gibraltar registries; and (iii) Gibraltar beneficial-ownership registration. There are no withholding taxes on dividends or interest in Gibraltar or the UK, and Bermuda levies no income tax or withholding tax for a non-resident exempted company — thus after-tax cash is freely convertible into any settlement currency.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion ratio exists in the UK, Gibraltar, or Bermuda for non-resident exempted companies. Receipts from digital advertising services may be retained indefinitely in their original currency. Nevertheless, the UK's financial sanctions regime requires funds touching sanctioned jurisdictions or persons to be frozen and reported to OFSI, and the BMA requires the company to maintain its foreign-currency accounts under a designated 'non-resident' account status, evidenced by the standard exchange-control letter issued upon incorporation.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (sovereign monetary authority for the British Pound); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the Gibraltar prudential and AML/CFT supervisor; Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) as the home-state regulator of the Bermuda Exempted Company, administering its non-resident exchange-control status.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For the Bermuda Exempted Company's capital subscription, route inbound equity via SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 COV if intermediated through a parent account) with ISO 20022 purpose code EQUI (equity capital) or the bilateral-specific code 'CAPITAL SUBSCRIPTION' in field 70. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Gibraltar: contemporaneous Local File documentation (including entity overview, controlled transaction schedule, comparability analysis, selection of the most appropriate method, and arm's length conclusion) is required where connected-party transactions exceed approximately GIP 500,000 in aggregate per annum; a Master File is required where the wider MNE group has consolidated revenue above EUR 750 million and Gibraltar operations exceed the BEPS Action 13 country-by-country thresholds. Bermuda: no statutory transfer pricing filing exists for exempted companies below the EUR 750 million MNE CIT scope, but the ESR return requires arm's length substantiation of all intercompany allocations to the Bermuda entity; currency denomination in Bermuda is BMD (USD pegged, BMD 1: USD 1), and Gibraltar uses GIP at par with GBP.

Economic substance scope: Mandatory. Bermuda: the Economic Substance Act 2021 requires each exempted company to demonstrate, for every relevant activity, substantial economic presence in Bermuda evidenced by core income-generating activities (CIGA), qualified full-time employees physically present, adequate physical premises, and board meetings with actual management and control exercised in Bermuda. ISIC 7310 digital advertising media arbitrage and affiliate lead generation is ordinarily examined as an 'intellectual property business' if the company owns campaign software, algorithms or brand IP, or as a 'distribution and service centre' if it provides tied advertising services to affiliated networks; a nil-revenue 'pure equity holding' designation is disallowed where operating income demonstrably flows through bank accounts. Gibraltar: since the 2019 tax reform, the Economic Substance Code (Income Tax Act 2010, as amended) requires Gibraltar tax-resident companies earning Gibraltar-source relevant income to satisfy proportionality tests on management and control, employee headcount and premises expenditure; the prior 0% offshore label no longer exists, making management location the decisive factor.
