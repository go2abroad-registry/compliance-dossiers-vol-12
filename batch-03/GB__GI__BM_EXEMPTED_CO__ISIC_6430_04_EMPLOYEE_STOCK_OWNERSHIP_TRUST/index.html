Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A pure virtual office (mail-forwarding address without physical presence in Gibraltar) is NOT permitted as the sole registered office for this exempted-company and ESOP-trust structure. The Companies Act 2014 (Gibraltar) requires every company to maintain a registered office and a registered agent in Gibraltar; for a Bermuda-style exempted company, this must be the physical office of a licensed corporate service provider where the statutory registers, beneficial-ownership ledger and minute books are held and where statutory service of process can be effected. A 'managed office' with on-site representatives, meeting rooms and mail handling is the accepted minimum; complete absence of physical footprint would impair economic-substance compliance and materially increase inspection risk.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (Bermuda): the exempted company enjoys zero corporate income tax, zero capital gains tax, zero withholding tax and zero wealth taxes until at least 2035; only Bermuda payroll tax (for local employees) and the OECD Pillar Two global minimum top-up for in-scope groups apply. Gibraltar overlay: a Bermuda company managed and controlled from Gibraltar is treated as a Gibraltar tax resident subject to territorial corporation tax at the standard headline rate of 10%, with a 15% Pillar Two top-up for in-scope groups; no Gibraltar PE taxation applies for administration conducted offshore.

Available election forms

Bermuda has no 'check-the-box' regime; exemption is statutory. Elections available under the CIT Act 2023 include the Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax Safe Harbour election and, for pure equity holding entities, the election to be treated outside the relevant activity test of the Economic Substance Act 2018; GloBE rule elections may also be documented by the ultimate parent. Gibraltar likewise has no pass-through election; the UK-Gibraltar Double Taxation Arrangement (2019) permits residence tie-break and mutual agreement procedure access, and hybrid mismatch alignment elections must be documented in Gibraltar CIT filings.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar applies a fully liberalised exchange control regime; both capital and current account transactions are freely convertible and no prior exchange control approval is required for inbound capital injections, dividend distributions, profit remittances, or ESOP trust settlements. The Gibraltar pound is fully transferable and movable at par with the UK pound sterling. Banks generally require only standard customer due diligence and, for larger remittances, documentary evidence of the underlying employment plan or trust purpose. Repatriation of trust capital to Bermuda or the UK is unrestricted, though tax-residency certificates and underlying plan documentation should be maintained to prevent involuntary de-risking by banking counterparties.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender obligation exists in Gibraltar. Following UK exchange control liberalisation (post-1979), Gibraltar imposes no mandatory conversion ratio or timeline for export receipts. A Gibraltar-resident entity or ESOP trust may retain 100% of foreign currency proceeds indefinitely, subject only to applicable anti-money laundering record-keeping requirements and statistical balance-of-payments reporting to the GFSC where relevant.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC), acting as the local financial services and foreign exchange administration; the Government of Gibraltar retains statutory currency issuance authority, and the Bank of England serves as the anchor central bank for the Gibraltar pound's 1:1 GBP peg.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium-High as auxiliary rail, Low for custody and principal trust capital. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise Platform, Airwallex, and Currencycloud can be onboarded for low-value employee net-pay distributions, FX conversion services, and ancillary payment routing. Legal constraints require that any EEA or UK-authorised EMI operating in Gibraltar be recognised or passported via the GFSC; otherwise, unlicensed EMI use is impermissible. Safeguarded funds with an EMI are generally pooled and not protected under trust fiduciary rings, so the ESOP trust deed's banking covenant must prohibit use of an EMI for settlement of equity transfers or custody of capital. The primary tier-1 bank cannot be replaced by an EMI for clearing high-value inbound or outbound wires.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Root cause: Remotely managed Bermuda Exempted Company with no substantive bookkeeping presence in Gibraltar.

Defense evidence: Bermuda Certificate of Good Standing, Bermuda Beneficial Ownership Registry confirmation, registered office certificate, substance declaration for tax-exempt status, and a board resolution designating Gibraltar as the centre of management and control for trust administration.
Employee benefit trust with a discretionary class of beneficiaries or unnamed ultimate beneficial owners.

Root cause: Employee benefit trust with a discretionary class of beneficiaries or unnamed ultimate beneficial owners.

Defense evidence: The formal ESOP trust deed, plan rules, participant allocation schedule, an employee eligibility matrix, a class-of-beneficiaries description, and written confirmation that the trust is a bare commercial plan rather than an offshore personal asset holding vehicle.
Cross-border transfers from connected Bermuda/offshore entities and anticipated outbound equity settlements touching US securities.

Root cause: Cross-border transfers from connected Bermuda/offshore entities and anticipated outbound equity settlements touching US securities.

Defense evidence: Transaction flow diagram, source-of-wealth memorandum for contributed shares/cash, FATCA/CRS classification forms, a legal opinion confirming the trust is neither a tax-evasion arrangement nor a circumvention of securities law, and an enforceable undertaking to provide sanctions screening of all participant payments.
