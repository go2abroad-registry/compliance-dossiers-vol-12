Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a Gibraltar private company limited by shares; a nominal issued capital of GBP 100 (10,000 ordinary shares of GBP 0.01 each) is customary and sufficient. The Statement of Capital must be filed at incorporation but no escrow account or deposit of funds is required by the Registry. Should the M&A boutique later hold client money or seek a financial-services licence, the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission will impose client-money segregation rules and, for an electronic-money institution, a minimum capital-escrow requirement of EUR 350,000.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Commissioner of Income Tax, Gibraltar Income Tax Office, 6 Grange Road, PO Box 884, Gibraltar; official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation

E-government filing portal

A non-Gibraltar incorporated entity (e.g., a Bermuda Exempted Company) carrying on business in Gibraltar must register with the Gibraltar Companies Registry as an overseas company and then apply to the Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar Direct Tax Account. The application requires certified constitutional documents, a registered address in Gibraltar, details of officers/directors, and a statement of expected activities. Upon registration, the Income Tax Office issues a tax identification reference used for annual self-assessment returns. Filing is mandatory even if no taxable profit arises.

Tax ID type & application path: Territorial_Scope — A non-Gibraltar incorporated entity (e.g., a Bermuda Exempted Company) carrying on business in Gibraltar must register with the Gibraltar Companies Registry as an overseas company and then apply to the Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar Direct Tax Account. The application requires certified constitutional documents, a registered address in Gibraltar, details of officers/directors, and a statement of expected activities. Upon registration, the Income Tax Office issues a tax identification reference used for annual self-assessment returns. Filing is mandatory even if no taxable profit arises.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a check-the-box regime; classification follows legal form. However, the Income Tax Act 2010 permits elections such as group relief for loss transfer, the election to be taxed as a small company at reduced rates (subject to qualifying activity), and transparent treatment for certain partnerships. Hybrid mismatch rules apply under domestic law to neutralise tax arbitrage.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST - Gibraltar applies import duties and excise taxes only

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - Gibraltar levies no VAT/GST and has no turnover-based registration threshold. Import duty becomes chargeable upon the physical importation of goods into Gibraltar.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - Gibraltar is outside the EU VAT area and imposes no VAT/GST on exported goods or services. Therefore, there is no domestic zero-rating claim procedure. Reliefs are limited to customs duty suspension mechanisms such as warehousing or inward processing for physical goods.

  • Customs Export Declaration (EX form or electronic customs log) - only required when physical goods are exported from Gibraltar for duty/trade statistics purposes
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill / CMR - commercial evidence of cross-border movement if goods leave Gibraltar
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT/bank confirmation - proof of foreign receipts where a refund or exemption claim is made in another jurisdiction

No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or government e-invoicing mandate exists in Gibraltar. Commercial invoices suffice for corporate tax records; electronic records are acceptable but not transmitted to tax authorities in real time.

Although no VAT zero-rating applies, fraudulent understatement of taxable profits, false invoicing, or deliberate non-disclosure in Gibraltar can trigger extended assessments (up to six years, longer on fraud), interest, and penalties up to 100% of the tax under-assessed. Serious cases risk criminal prosecution under the Income Tax Act 2010 and the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar imposes no exchange controls and maintains full convertibility across both current and capital account items. Funds may be freely repatriated or remitted in any major currency, subject to compliance with GFSC AML/CFT regulations, HM Treasury sanctions, and enhanced source-of-funds verifications. For a Bermuda Exempted Company managed outside Gibraltar, profit remittance is unrestricted, though dividend distributions and capital repatriation must be accompanied by audited financial statements and appropriate tax clearance certificates where applicable.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable – Gibraltar does not impose statutory currency surrender or mandatory conversion timelines for export proceeds. Exporters and service providers may retain receipts in any currency and remit at commercial discretion, subject only to record-keeping obligations under the AML/CFT framework.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) – the integrated financial regulator for banking, investment, and payments; the Government of Gibraltar exercises currency board functions for the Gibraltar pound (pegged at par to GBP). There is no separate central bank or dedicated foreign-exchange administration.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility: Medium. A Gibraltar-licensed electronic money institution (authorised under the Financial Services (E-Money) Act 2019) can facilitate multi-currency accounts and payment clearing; however, a pure offshore digital EMI without a Gibraltar or UK/EEA licence may only act as a supporting payment agent and cannot serve as the primary clearing rail. Legal constraints include mandatory client money safeguarding, GFSC authorisation for regulated payment services, and a physical or independent liaison presence to satisfy onboarding and transaction monitoring.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound routing requires an MT103/MT202 SWIFT wire from the ultimate shareholder's bank to the Gibraltar operating or escrow account. The remittance must stipulate purpose code 'DI01' (Foreign Direct Investment) for equity capital, or 'TXI1' (Quasi-Capital) for shareholder loans, together with the company's Gibraltar BRN and the underlying board resolution. Sterling transfers are cleared via CHAPS, euro transfers via TARGET2, and US-dollar transfers via Fedwire/CHIPS, using the corporate bank's correspondent network; the bank will verify KYC against the source and route funds through the designated nostro agent.For advisory success fees, deal consideration, and escrow releases, settlement is achieved over SWIFT MT103 with the remittance information field mirroring the engagement letter, or via a commercial letter of credit issued by the Gibraltar corporate bank and advised through a confirming bank in the target jurisdiction. Intercompany treasury transfers should be consolidated through a concentration account and cleared via GB ICS or local higher-value payment system (Gibraltar has direct CHAPS membership via the Bank of England).
