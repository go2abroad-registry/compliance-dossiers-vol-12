Stage 03
Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

      
Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

    

    

      
Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
Gibraltar (principal place of business): Commissioner of Income Tax, Tax and Customs Duty Department, HM Government of Gibraltar, 179 Queen's Road, Gibraltar GX11 1AA, acting through the official Gibraltar Government taxation portal (www.gibraltar.gov.gi). Bermuda (entity home jurisdiction): Bermuda Registrar of Companies and the Bermuda Minister of Finance (Tax Administration), Hamilton, Bermuda, administering the Bermuda statutory tax-exemption certificate regime.

        
E-government filing portal
Step 1 - Bermuda incorporation: file the Memorandum and Articles of Association with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies, pay the incorporation fee, and obtain the Certificate of Incorporation. Step 2 - Bermuda tax exemption: within 30 days of incorporation, apply for the statutory exemption certificate under the Bermuda Companies Act 1981 and the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966; the Exempted Company receives an irrevocable statutory guarantee, until 2035, of no Bermuda income, capital, withholding or stamp taxes on its non-Bermuda operations, subject to annual filing and the annual government fee. Step 3 - Gibraltar external registration: register the Bermuda company as an External Company with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under the external companies provisions of the Companies Act 2014 within 30 days of opening any Gibraltar establishment. Step 4 - Licensing: obtain the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) Payment Services Licence for cross-border merchant acquiring and payment gateway services under the Gibraltar Financial Services Act 2019 and the applicable Payment Services Regulations implementing PSD2. Step 5 - Income tax registration: apply to the Commissioner of Income Tax for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (GTRN) providing the Bermuda Certificate of Incorporation, the exemption certificate, the Gibraltar External Company registration certificate, the GFSC licence, the ultimate beneficial ownership register, and the identity of the appointed Gibraltar-resident tax representative; the GTRN is issued within 10-15 working days. No VAT registration is required because Gibraltar has no VAT/GST system.

      

      
Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone: the Bermuda Exempted Company is statutorily exempt in Bermuda from all local taxation on foreign-sourced income, capital gains, dividends, interest, royalties and distributions until 2035, provided the annual exemption declaration and any required economic substance return are filed. The Gibraltar External Company branch constitutes a Gibraltar permanent establishment; profits economically attributable to that establishment are taxable in Gibraltar at the 10% corporate rate (Income Tax Act 2010), while payment gateway profits derived from services performed and received entirely outside Gibraltar and not booked through the branch remain outside Gibraltar territorial taxation. The entity is not eligible for pass-through treatment in Gibraltar and is not subject to UK corporation tax unless UK-source income or a UK permanent establishment exists. — Step 1 - Bermuda incorporation: file the Memorandum and Articles of Association with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies, pay the incorporation fee, and obtain the Certificate of Incorporation. Step 2 - Bermuda tax exemption: within 30 days of incorporation, apply for the statutory exemption certificate under the Bermuda Companies Act 1981 and the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966; the Exempted Company receives an irrevocable statutory guarantee, until 2035, of no Bermuda income, capital, withholding or stamp taxes on its non-Bermuda operations, subject to annual filing and the annual government fee. Step 3 - Gibraltar external registration: register the Bermuda company as an External Company with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under the external companies provisions of the Companies Act 2014 within 30 days of opening any Gibraltar establishment. Step 4 - Licensing: obtain the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) Payment Services Licence for cross-border merchant acquiring and payment gateway services under the Gibraltar Financial Services Act 2019 and the applicable Payment Services Regulations implementing PSD2. Step 5 - Income tax registration: apply to the Commissioner of Income Tax for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (GTRN) providing the Bermuda Certificate of Incorporation, the exemption certificate, the Gibraltar External Company registration certificate, the GFSC licence, the ultimate beneficial ownership register, and the identity of the appointed Gibraltar-resident tax representative; the GTRN is issued within 10-15 working days. No VAT registration is required because Gibraltar has no VAT/GST system.

    

    

      
Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Tax_Exempt_Zone: the Bermuda Exempted Company is statutorily exempt in Bermuda from all local taxation on foreign-sourced income, capital gains, dividends, interest, royalties and distributions until 2035, provided the annual exemption declaration and any required economic substance return are filed. The Gibraltar External Company branch constitutes a Gibraltar permanent establishment; profits economically attributable to that establishment are taxable in Gibraltar at the 10% corporate rate (Income Tax Act 2010), while payment gateway profits derived from services performed and received entirely outside Gibraltar and not booked through the branch remain outside Gibraltar territorial taxation. The entity is not eligible for pass-through treatment in Gibraltar and is not subject to UK corporation tax unless UK-source income or a UK permanent establishment exists.

        
Available election forms
Gibraltar: (i) group relief election - the Bermudian exempted company may join with its Gibraltar branch and any Gibraltar-resident subsidiaries in a joint election to compute combined profits for Gibraltar corporate tax, enabling intra-group loss relief (Income Tax Act 2010, group provisions); (ii) Qualifying Company election - the exempted company or a Gibraltar subsidiary may elect, for a renewable four-year term, Qualifying Company status conferring a zero-percent Gibraltar rate on qualifying investment, dividend and treasury income, with an annual flat substance fee; (iii) Bermuda: at formation the board may elect to constitute the company as an Exempted rather than Local company and elect the 2035 tax-extension guarantee; (iv) US elections: if the ultimate shareholder is US-taxable, the Bermuda Exempted Company (not a per se corporation) is an eligible entity for US check-the-box purposes and may be classified as a disregarded entity or partnership on Form 8832, provided the election does not conflict with the Gibraltar branch accounting; (v) hybrid mismatch elections: not applicable - Gibraltar domestic law does not provide check-the-box or hybrid-branch elections, and the UK hybrid mismatch rules (TIOPA 2010 Part 6A) have not been extended to Gibraltar.

      

    

    

      
VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

      

        
Standard VAT / GST rate
No VAT / GST / sales-and-use tax regime in Gibraltar. Gibraltar is outside the European Union VAT territory and has no general consumption tax. Selective excise duties apply only to alcohol, tobacco, fuel and motor vehicles under the Customs and Excise Duties Act; these duties do not attach to merchant acquiring, clearing, gateway, interchange or settlement services.

        
Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)
Not applicable - Gibraltar law establishes no indirect tax registration process, no economic-activity turnover threshold, and no VAT return filing obligation. The cross-border payment gateway is therefore outside the scope of any Gibraltar indirect tax; no Gibraltar pound (GIP), pound sterling (GBP) or euro (EUR) denominated turnover threshold triggers a registration requirement. Only physical importers of excisable commodities must register with the Customs and Excise Duty Department.

      

      
      

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
Not applicable as a VAT/indirect-tax claim - Gibraltar has no VAT, so there is no 0% export rating, no input tax credit and no export-refund claim mechanism. The functional substitute is the territorial-source exemption in Gibraltar corporation tax: profits from cross-border payment gateway services are outside Gibraltar taxation when the substantive underwriting, merchant acceptance, clearing and settlement decisions are performed outside Gibraltar (e.g., in the Bermuda head office, UK scheme offices or US clearing infrastructure), the services are rendered to foreign acquirers and merchants, and the associated receivables are booked offshore rather than through the Gibraltar licensed branch. To preserve this position the exempted company must maintain full transfer pricing documentation, demonstrate commercial substance and decision-making outside Gibraltar, and ensure the Gibraltar branch's taxable profit is limited to an arm's-length routine service margin subject to the 10% corporate rate.

Misapplication of the territorial exemption (i.e., under-declaring Gibraltar-source gateway fees) exposes the entity to: (i) a Gibraltar assessment within the statutory limitation period of six years (no time limit in cases of fraud or negligent misrepresentation), with late-payment interest and a penalty of up to 100% of the tax under-assessed; (ii) GFSC enforcement under the Financial Services Act 2019, including financial penalties of up to GIP 300,000 per contravention, licence suspension, cancellation or individual prohibition orders; (iii) clawback of settlement revenue in civil proceedings if the offshore booking has defrauded card scheme rules; (iv) if a UK corporation is ultimately involved, exposure to the UK Criminal Finances Act 2017 corporate offence of failure to prevent the facilitation of UK tax evasion, carrying an unlimited fine; and (v) for US-sourced Fedwire income, improper entity documentation triggers 30% US withholding tax under IRC chapter 3, with retroactive assessment authority for the US Internal Revenue Service; FATCA and CRS reporting obligations remain unaffected by the Gibraltar indirect-tax exemption.

Stage 04
Banking & Treasury Architecture

      
Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
Gibraltar maintains full convertibility on both current and capital account transactions. No exchange control approval or surrender requirements apply to non-resident owned exempted companies. Profit remittances, dividend distributions, and inter-company loan repayments are freely transferable in any convertible currency (GIP, GBP, EUR, USD) subject only to standard AML/KYC wire verification and tax clearance (if any).

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
No statutory mandatory surrender or holding period applies to export receipts in Gibraltar. All proceeds may be held in any foreign currency or converted freely. The only compliance obligations are record-keeping, beneficial ownership disclosure, and suspicious transaction reporting under the AML/CFT legislation (Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and relevant FSC guidance).

      

      
Central bank supervision: Financial Services Commission (FSC) of Gibraltar; monetary policy governed by Bank of England through the Gibraltar pound (GIP) peg to GBP, with the UK Treasury retaining ultimate sovereign responsibility.

    

    

      
Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

      

        
        

          

            Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails
            Fit: High
          

          
Moderate feasibility. Licensed Gibraltar Electronic Money Institutions and Payment Institutions under the Financial Services Act 2019 can operate cross-border for acquiring and clearing; however, EU passporting post-Brexit is no longer automatic and requires reverse solicitation or separate national licensing. A UK EMI (FCA-regulated) or EU EMI may be used for SEPA and UK Faster Payments capabilities, but such providers often face elevated compliance scrutiny when processing for a Bermuda exempted company with a Gibraltar nexus. Best to pair a licensed Gibraltar EMI with a UK/EU correspondent acquirer for diversified rail access.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

      
      

        Ultimate beneficial owner (UBO) nested through a Bermuda exempted company with bearer share or nominee share risk
        
Root cause: Ultimate beneficial owner (UBO) nested through a Bermuda exempted company with bearer share or nominee share risk

        
Defense evidence: Certificate of Incumbency from Bermuda registered agent, full registered shareholder ledger, signed declaration of trust/ nominee disclaimers, UBO registry extract (COMPLETE), and corporate registry search along with chain-of-ownership schematic.

      

      
      

        Cross-border payment gateway business with high aggregate turnover and multiple acquiring merchant counterparties
        
Root cause: Cross-border payment gateway business with high aggregate turnover and multiple acquiring merchant counterparties

        
Defense evidence: Fully executed merchant service agreements, Know-Your-Merchant due diligence files (including mCC classification), transaction-liability matrix, IP address and terminal location audit trail, and third-party AML/CFT risk assessment covering all acquiring channels.

      

      
      

        Material sanctions exposure due to clearing through UK or EU correspondent banks into high-risk jurisdictions
        
Root cause: Material sanctions exposure due to clearing through UK or EU correspondent banks into high-risk jurisdictions

        
Defense evidence: Sanction policy and screening screenshots (including UN, OFAC, HMT, EU lists), geoblocking configuration on payment gateway, list of approved merchant jurisdictions, and written confirmation that no sanctioned persons or vessels / aircraft are involved.

      

      
      

        Unexplained source of seed capital or treasury injection into the operating bank account
        
Root cause: Unexplained source of seed capital or treasury injection into the operating bank account

        
Defense evidence: Audited financial statements of the Bermuda holding company, bank statements showing affinity of initial capital, signed share issuance redemption forms, foreign exchange contract confirmations, and a letter from the Gibraltar certified public accountant confirming source of funds.

      

      
    

    

      
Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

      

        Inbound capital injections (shareholder loans, paid-in capital, receivables) must route via formal SWIFT wire with a purpose code of '39000 — equity capital / intercompany financing' (or GIP equivalent), including a clear narrative of 'Increase in paid-up capital — Bermuda Exempted Company'. Verification requires share register entry, board minute approving issuance, and the Bermuda Economic Substance (BES) report confirming placement in Gibraltar. Use a Gibraltar correspondent bank (e.g., GIB for GBP clearing, and an EU bank for EUR via TARGET2) to avoid intermediary delays.Trade payables and clearing settlements are executed via SWIFT MT103 for non-documentary transfers, while commercial letters of credit (LCs) are used for high-value or counterparty-risky trade settlements. For merchant acquiring payouts, use the bank's sweep filing / payout bank account (multi-currency) with detailed reconciliation against the PSP's generated REMIT records. Ensure overseas disbursements include the proper ICC code (e.g., '1090 — services rendered' / '4100 — payments for merchant services') and the entity's LEI, when required, to reduce FX fees and compliance holds.Exchange-traded and OTC FX Forwards (vanilla forwards up to 24 months, settled GIP/GBP/EUR/USD)Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDFs) for GIP (pegged) or other restricted currenciesCross-currency interest rate swaps (CCIRS) for matching long-term intercompany loan assetsFX options (European style) as an overlay for forecast cash flows, subject to FSC risk governance

Stage 06
Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

      
Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

    

    

      
Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)
12.5% standard corporation tax rate on chargeable trading profits for Gibraltar-resident companies and for non-resident companies carrying on business through a Gibraltar permanent establishment (PE).

      
Dividend withholding tax (WHT)
0%: Gibraltar does not impose withholding tax on dividends paid by a Gibraltar company or by the Gibraltar branch of a Bermuda Exempted Company, whether the shareholder is a Bermuda parent, a UK investor, or any other non-resident.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

      
Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 aggregate controlled transactions in a chargeable period. This threshold captures intercompany merchant fees, processing charges, management fees, software royalties, and settlement payments between the Gibraltar PE, the Bermuda Exempted Company, and any affiliated payment or intellectual property entities. A full Local File must be prepared when the threshold is exceeded; a Master File is required where the multinational group has consolidated turnover above GIP 75,000,000, regardless of the local controlled transaction amount.

      
Economic substance scope: Gibraltar's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR), issued under the Income Tax Act 2010, apply to every Gibraltar tax-resident entity and to non-resident companies with a Gibraltar PE carrying on a relevant activity. A cross-border payment gateway performing merchant acquiring, clearing, and settlement is treated as carrying on a finance or service business. The Bermuda Exempted Company must therefore demonstrate, for each accounting period, that its core income-generating activities are performed in Gibraltar: local merchant onboarding decisions, local risk management, local payment network contracts, local finance and compliance functions, and locally resident employees. Outsourcing those functions to Bermuda or the UK does not satisfy ESR. Failure to maintain substance can result in profit reattribution, a denial of tax residence certificates, and designation as a shell entity.

      

    

    

      
Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

      
      

        Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Returns
        
Statutory sanction: Civil: tax-geared penalty of up to 100% of the understated tax, late payment interest at commercial rates, daily penalties, and confiscation proceedings under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015. Criminal: on certification, a fine of up to GIP 100,000 and/or imprisonment for up to 7 years; on indictment, an unlimited fine and imprisonment for up to 10 years for deliberate evasion.

        
Director personal liability: Courts pierce the corporate veil where the Bermuda Exempted Company is a mere facade. Directors who knowingly cause or permit evasion become jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax and penalties; director disqualification up to 15 years; asset freezes and travel bans may be imposed; extradition arrangements with the United Kingdom are available for serious tax fraud.

      

      
      

        Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure
        
Statutory sanction: An ESR determination triggers financial penalties of up to GIP 250,000 for the first year and GIP 500,000 for subsequent years, plus daily penalties of GIP 10,000 for each day of continued failure; the ITO may exchange information with UK HMRC, deny the Certificate of Tax Residence, and reattribute profits to Gibraltar. Reputational de-risking and bank account termination are consequential sanctions.

        
Director personal liability: Directors may be disqualified under Gibraltar's Companies Act for reckless trading; the corporate veil is pierced for sham reliance on the Bermuda exempt company; directors are personally liable for ESR penalties if they instructed outsourcing to avoid substance; travel bans and banking blacklisting are common enforcement actions.
