Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, HM Government of Gibraltar (Gibraltar Income Tax Office); official registration and e-filing portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax.

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company seeking to establish a taxable presence or simply obtain a Gibraltar tax identity must submit a completed Application for Tax Reference Number (TRN) to the Gibraltar Income Tax Office. The application must include a certified copy of the Bermuda Certificate of Incorporation, its Memorandum and Articles of Association, the Certificate of Exemption issued under the Bermuda Exempted Companies Act, a notarised declaration of place of effective management, and a copy of the ultimate beneficial ownership register. If the company will import goods into Gibraltar, it must also register with the Gibraltar Customs & Excise Department for an Economic Operator Registration and Identification (EORI) number and, if applicable, obtain a Purchase Tax account. A TRN is issued within 7-10 business days; issuance is free of charge.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A Bermuda Exempted Company seeking to establish a taxable presence or simply obtain a Gibraltar tax identity must submit a completed Application for Tax Reference Number (TRN) to the Gibraltar Income Tax Office. The application must include a certified copy of the Bermuda Certificate of Incorporation, its Memorandum and Articles of Association, the Certificate of Exemption issued under the Bermuda Exempted Companies Act, a notarised declaration of place of effective management, and a copy of the ultimate beneficial ownership register. If the company will import goods into Gibraltar, it must also register with the Gibraltar Customs & Excise Department for an Economic Operator Registration and Identification (EORI) number and, if applicable, obtain a Purchase Tax account. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010, a non-resident Bermuda Exempted Company may make a 'Treaty Relief Election' to be treated as non-resident for Gibraltar tax purposes where central management and control is exercised outside Gibraltar, effectively confirming its tax-exempt status for foreign-source income. A company may alternatively elect to be assessed as a Gibraltar tax resident through a 'Full Taxation Election', which makes it eligible for the UK/Gibraltar double-taxation arrangement and EU parent-subsidiary directive participation; this election is irrevocable for a minimum of five years. Hybrid mismatch elections under EU ATAD rules are not available because Gibraltar has not implemented US check-the-box or equivalent mixed hybrid filtering.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Purchase Tax (Gibraltar analogue of Sales and Use Tax; no VAT or GST)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Gibraltar operates no general turnover-based indirect tax registration threshold. Purchase Tax registration is required solely for businesses importing or supplying designated luxury and consumption goods (alcohol, tobacco, hydrocarbon oils, electronics, and vehicles), irrespective of turnover. An EORI number from Gibraltar Customs & Excise is required for any entity importing goods into Gibraltar from non-EU jurisdictions; there is no revenue threshold for EORI registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable for VAT because Gibraltar levies no VAT. For customs purposes, zero-duty export relief may be granted under the Gibraltar Export (Exemption) Rules 2015 when goods are consigned from Gibraltar to a destination outside the EU/Gibraltar customs territory. The exporter must hold a valid EORI registration, the goods must physically leave the territory within 180 days, the export must be substantiated by a completed export declaration in CUSREP, and the exporter must prove receipt of hard currency proceeds in a Gibraltar licensed bank within the statutory period. Pure offshore services provided to non-resident non-Gibraltar customers are outside the territorial scope of Gibraltar indirect tax and therefore automatically exempt from Purchase Tax.

  • Customs Export Declaration (CUSREP Exit Declaration)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing shipment from Gibraltar to a third country
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by a Gibraltar licensed bank evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin (for zero-tariff claims under qualifying agreements)

Gibraltar does not mandate real-time electronic fiscal clearance, API-based invoice stamping, or continuous transaction controls. Import declarations must be submitted electronically through the EU-established CUSREP system. Purchase Tax returns are filed through the Gibraltar Tax Office's e-filing portal on a monthly basis, but no digital fiscal stamp or real-time clearance requirement applies to domestic B2B or B2C invoices.

Gibraltar Customs & Excise and the Income Tax Office conduct post-export audit reviews, cross-matching customs declarations with bank remittance records. Where export zero-rating is found to have been fraudulently claimed - including false declarations, export to fictitious consignees, or failure to repatriate proceeds within the 180-day deadline - the zero-duty relief is clawed back in full, plus interest at 2% per month, and a punitive penalty of up to 150% of the evaded duty. Criminal prosecution may follow under the Customs Duties Ordinance and the Income Tax Act 2010, with liability for directors and shareholders of the Bermuda Exempted Company.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar has no exchange-control legislation; both capital and current account transactions are fully convertible. Profit remittances to overseas shareholders are unrestricted, with no withholding tax in Gibraltar, though the receiving jurisdiction's tax rules remain applicable. The Gibraltar pound (GIP) circulates at par with GBP, and GBP notes are universally accepted, eliminating intra-UK conversion friction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender requirement or mandatory conversion ratio exists under Gibraltar law; exporters may retain receipts in any freely convertible currency and repatriate funds without central bank approval.

Central bank supervision: Financial Services Commission (FSC) of Gibraltar; monetary policy and currency issuance effectively governed by the Bank of England through the Gibraltar pound's fixed 1:1 parity with GBP

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

For a company with a calendar year-end, the return and any self-assessment payment are due by 31 January in the year following the accounting period. For example, an accounting period ending 31 December 2024 is due by 31 January 2025. Non-calendar year-end companies must file within 12 months of the accounting period end, unless the Income Tax Office serves a notice requiring filing within 30 days of the notice. Exempted companies must also file a nil return even when no tax liability is due.

Deadline: · Frequency:

The ESR-1 return must be filed within 12 months of the financial year-end. For a 31 December year-end, the filing deadline is 31 December of the following year. The Income Tax Office may impose a separate filing reminder cycle. No automatic extension is available.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File: required when aggregate related-party cross-border transactions exceed GIP 500,000 in a tax year, or when turnover exceeds GIP 10,000,000 and related-party debt balances exceed GIP 2,000,000. Mandatory Master File: required when group turnover exceeds GIP 50,000,000. Country-by-Country Report: required for groups with consolidated turnover of EUR 750,000,000 or more. For legacy Gibraltar exempted companies, a simplified transfer pricing documentation file must be prepared if any related-party transaction exceeds GIP 100,000.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance (Companies and Trusts) Act 2019 applies to all Gibraltar tax-resident companies, including entities that claim exempted-company status. A Bermuda Exempted Company used as an overseas hotpot restaurant operator is treated as a non-resident company only where central management and control, premises, staff, and board decision-making are located entirely outside Gibraltar. If the company is Gibraltar-resident, the distribution and service business of hotpot dining and condiment supply is not a 'relevant activity' for economic substance purposes, but financing, IP holding, leasing, and headquarters services are relevant activities and require real offices, employees, board meetings, and core income-generating activities in Gibraltar.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Exempted-Company Misuse

Statutory sanction: Financial penalty of up to 100% of the unpaid tax plus interest at 12% per annum, a criminal fine of up to GIP 100,000, and imprisonment of up to ten years under the Income Tax Act 2010 as amended. The Gibraltar Supreme Court may also order seizure and forfeiture of restaurant and condiment-supply assets.

Director personal liability: Directors, shadow directors, and the beneficial owner of the Bermuda Exempted Company may be jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax and civil restitution. The court may issue travel bans, require passport surrender, and disqualify the individuals from acting as directors of any Gibraltar company for up to ten years.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: The Director of Income Tax may make a transfer pricing adjustment increasing taxable profits by the full amount of the understated gross margin. A penalty of 50% of the resulting tax deficiency will be imposed where documentation is absent. Additional daily penalties of GIP 5,000 per month can continue until compliant documentation is filed.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign or authorise distorted transfer pricing records are personally liable for civil penalties of up to GIP 250,000 and for the company's tax deficiency if the corporate veil is pierced. In aggravated cases, criminal prosecution for tax fraud may result in imprisonment of up to ten years.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Violation

Statutory sanction: Penalty of GIP 10,000 for the first failure under the Economic Substance (Companies and Trusts) Act 2019, rising to GIP 100,000 for continued non-compliance, followed by court-ordered strike-off and dissolution of the company. The Gibraltar Commissioner will automatically exchange the entity's information with the UK HMRC and other treaty partners.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for the GIP 100,000 penalty if the company does not pay. A director who causes the company to operate without economic substance may be disqualified for up to 15 years and made subject to a travel ban pending restitution of treaty benefits improperly obtained.
Anti-Money Laundering and Cash Business Red Flags

Statutory sanction: Criminal liability under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and the Gibraltar AML framework: a fine of up to GIP 500,000 and imprisonment of up to fourteen years. The Gibraltar Financial Services Commission may revoke the entity's merchant acquiring agreements and banking facilities; the company's assets may be frozen pending forfeiture.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly conceal beneficial ownership or facilitate structured cash operations face personal criminal prosecution, imprisonment, travel bans, and civil confiscation of personal assets. Piercing the corporate veil is readily available in money-laundering cases, making the directors jointly and severally liable for the full value of tainted funds.
