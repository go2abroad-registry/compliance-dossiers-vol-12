Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Companies House Gibraltar — Office of the Registrar of Companies, HM Government of Gibraltar

Governing statute

Companies Act 2014 (Gibraltar), Parts II–IV (formation of companies, memorandum and articles, registered office, directors and secretary); Income Tax Act 2010, Part VII (Qualifying Company election and 0% flat-rate taxation); Business Licensing Act 1997 (exemptions for non-local commercial activity). The pipeline entity code 'Bermuda Exempted Company' is implemented in Gibraltar as a private company limited by shares carrying the customary exempted/qualifying-company features: no public offering, non-resident ownership permitted, and offshore trade income exempt from Gibraltar tax where management and control sit outside Gibraltar.

Formation / constitutional filings

Application for Registration of a Private Company Limited by Shares (Form GI-INC01) filed together with the Memorandum and Articles of Association, Statement of Capital and Initial Shareholdings, Statement of Proposed Officers, and Statement of Initial Significant Control

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 45 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up capital is prescribed by the Companies Act 2014 (Gibraltar); incorporation may proceed with one subscriber share of GBP 1.00 at par. No escrow account or paid-up capital deposit is required by the Companies Registry. However, banking and AML rules applicable to the Medium_Risk_Enhanced re-invoicing model may require the directors to demonstrate initial paid-up capital (typically GBP 1,000–GBP 50,000) and to evidence source of funds at account opening.

Funding & unlock path: For a low-occupancy commercial service office, the company must hold a fire-safety certificate and building completion/first-occupancy certificate; structural load inspections are not applicable to a re-invoicing administrative office. Where staff are engaged, the company must comply with the Gibraltar Fire Safety and Health and Safety at Work regime, including fire drills, first-aid provision, employer's liability insurance and periodic fire-system servicing.

Paid-in capital rule: No minimum paid-up capital is prescribed by the Companies Act 2014 (Gibraltar); incorporation may proceed with one subscriber share of GBP 1.00 at par. E-government filing portal

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope with Entity_Level_Taxation: the foreign-incorporated company is chargeable to Gibraltar income tax only on income accruing in or derived from Gibraltar at the 12.5% standard corporate rate; non-Gibraltar-source income is outside the charge. If managed and controlled in Gibraltar, the company is tax resident; otherwise it is non-resident but still taxable on Gibraltar-source re-invoicing profits. No pass-through applies; no tax-exempt status is available, as the former Qualifying Company regime was abolished with effect from 1 July 2010.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists. Available elections: (i) group loss relief election between Gibraltar companies meeting the 75% group ownership test under the Income Tax Act 2010; (ii) capital allowances and balancing-charge elections; (iii) advance interpretation by the Commissioner on the territorial source treatment of specific re-invoicing arrangements where the Commissioner's published practice permits. ATAD-aligned anti-hybrid rules neutralize deduction/non-inclusion mismatches arising from hybrid entity or instrument characterization, and the General Anti-Abuse Rule may deny any election used to obtain a tax advantage lacking economic substance.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax. Gibraltar levies import duties on goods under the Customs and Excise Duties Ordinance; services and pure re-invoicing operations where no physical goods cross Gibraltar are outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect taxation.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable: no VAT/GST turnover threshold exists. Commercial importers must register with the Customs & Excise Department and obtain a customs operator code regardless of turnover; personal non-commercial consignments below the applicable low-value consignment relief thresholds may enter free of duty.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable because Gibraltar imposes no VAT or GST; therefore no zero-rating claim or export VAT refund mechanism exists. Goods exported from Gibraltar are outside the scope of Gibraltar import duty, and re-invoicing services supplied to non-residents in respect of goods that never enter Gibraltar do not generate Gibraltar-source income. No fiscal refund claim is required.

  • Customs export/import declaration (Gibraltar customs form)
  • Original commercial invoice (signed, with terms of sale)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill
  • Packing list
  • Certificate of Origin (where required)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank transfer confirmation evidencing export proceeds

No real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing API, or digital stamping mandate. Invoices may be issued in paper or electronic form under general commercial record-keeping requirements. Customs declarations are lodged electronically with the Customs & Excise Department; there is no government interface for invoice validation or individual invoice pre-clearance.

Customs duty evasion: forfeiture plus a penalty of up to three times the duty evaded, with criminal sanctions under the Customs Ordinance. Income tax non-compliance on re-invoicing margins: penalties up to 100% of the tax undercharged plus interest for negligent or fraudulent filing; territorial-sourcing and transfer-pricing adjustments may reallocate profit to Gibraltar; the General Anti-Abuse Rule permits retrospective clawback of tax advantages from artificial re-invoicing structures lacking economic substance. No Gibraltar statutory FX repatriation deadline applies; the 180-day period is a contractual and audit benchmark.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar operates a fully convertible currency regime. The Gibraltar Pound (GIP) is pegged at par to the British Pound Sterling (GBP). Both current account and capital account transactions are freely convertible with no foreign exchange restrictions, no prior approval requirements for trade payments, and no hurdles on legitimate profit remittance or dividend distributions to non-resident shareholders.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender requirement exists. Export receipts may be retained in any freely convertible currency, held in offshore accounts, or converted at commercial rates without mandatory conversion timelines or quotas. The only applicable constraints derive from international sanctions screening and beneficial ownership transparency obligations under GFSC and UK AML/CFT rules.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (as sovereign monetary authority) in coordination with the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) and the Gibraltar Currency Commissioner for local note issuance

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium feasibility. Offshore digital Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) are legally recognised only when authorised or passported from the UK or EEA and registered with the GFSC. Pure third-country EMIs without local sponsorship and approved clearing arrangements are not permitted to operate independently. Direct access to SEPA, Faster Payments, and SWIFT requires partnership with a Gibraltar-licensed sponsor bank or completion of a full GFSC licensing process. For re-invoicing trade entities, a licensed local bank account remains the most prudent primary treasury rail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound capital injections are routed via SWIFT MT103 TT wire transfer from the beneficial owner's or parent company's bank account to the Gibraltar-based operational account.
