E-government filing portal

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Available mechanisms: (a) Bermuda Exempted Company status, granting exemption from Bermuda income, capital gains and withholding taxes, subject to annual economic substance filings and government fees; (b) election for non-resident status in Gibraltar under the Income Tax Act 2010 where central management and control is exercised outside Gibraltar, confining Gibraltar corporate income tax at 10% to Gibraltar-source profits (no US-style check-the-box); (c) election, if eligible, for Qualifying Company (Category 2) status in Gibraltar with a fixed annual levy in lieu of the standard 10% corporate tax on non-resident-owned assets; (d) application of the UK-Gibraltar taxation agreement (2019) for treaty allocation and double-tax relief where UK-resident beneficial owners exist; (e) compliance with hybrid-mismatch rules under UK TIOPA 2010 Part 6A and Gibraltar BEPS Action 2 measures, including any required election or avoidance statement where the Bermuda exempt company is fiscally transparent in an investor jurisdiction; (f) for indirect taxes, formal election of the UK/EU One-Stop-Shop (OSS) for B2C digital supplies, or reliance on the app-store deemed-supplier mechanism whereby Apple and Google account for VAT without requiring a statutory election by the developer.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

1%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GBP 5,000 annual turnover of taxable supplies in Gibraltar (Purchase Tax small-supplier threshold); UK VAT registration threshold GBP 85,000 for UK digital-distance sales; EU OSS no turnover threshold (non-EU supplier must register in an OSS Member State from the first euro of B2C digital supply); Apple IAP and Google Play Billing execution is not subject to any threshold because the app stores act as deemed suppliers.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exports: zero-rating is available to a Gibraltar-registered supplier where goods are physically removed from Gibraltar within 30 days of supply and supported by Gibraltar Customs export documentation. Services/WebNovel subscriptions: for B2C electronic services the place of supply is the customer's country of residence, so subscriptions to non-Gibraltar customers are outside the scope of Gibraltar Purchase Tax and automatically attract a 0% Gibraltar rate without any refund mechanism; where Apple IAP or Google Play Billing is used, the app store is deemed supplier for UK/EU B2C digital supplies and accounts for VAT/GST at the consumer's rate, remitting net-of-tax proceeds to the Bermuda Exempted Company, so no Gibraltar/UK zero-rating claim is required. B2B digital sales to a UK/EU taxable customer may be zero-rated or outside scope only with a valid customer VAT registration number and appropriate place-of-supply evidence.

  • Gibraltar Customs export entry/declaration (equivalent of SAD/AES) with evidence of physical removal of goods from Gibraltar, where goods are exported
  • Executed WebNovel content-licensing and platform-services contract plus Apple/Google developer-agreement pages evidencing the deemed-supplier appointment
  • Apple IAP and Google Play Billing transaction-level settlement reports showing gross consumer subscriptions, store-collected VAT/GST, commissions and net amounts remitted
  • Customer location evidence retained under OSS/IOSS and EU eVAT rules (GeoIP, device-country, billing address, two non-conflicting pieces of evidence) for place-of-supply substantiation
  • Inward remittance certificates (SWIFT MT103 or EMI account statements) evidencing repatriation of net subscription proceeds to the Bermuda Exempted Company's bank account

Gibraltar has no mandatory real-time e-invoicing, fiscal-clearance API or digital-stamping requirement; Purchase Tax is declared on ordinary invoices via periodic returns to HM Customs. Where UK VAT applies, Making Tax Digital mandates digital records and HMRC-approved MTD-compatible software with API filing; EU OSS demands periodic XML/portal declarations but no national e-invoicing clearance for B2C digital services. Apple/Google settlement APIs and customer receipts serve as the tax data of record; the platform should preserve transaction-level store reports and country-of-consumption logs.

Gibraltar HM Customs may revisit returns for up to six years, claw back misapplied zero-rating or out-of-scope treatment with interest, and impose penalties of up to 100% of the tax for careless or inaccurate certificates, with criminal prosecution (unlimited fine and imprisonment) for fraudulent evasion; failure to file Bermuda or Gibraltar economic substance returns can revoke the exempt-zone status. HMRC may assess under four-year standard, six-year careless or twenty-year deliberate windows, with FA 2007 Sch. 24 penalties up to 100% (up to 200% for deliberate and concealed conduct), and exclude the supplier from OSS/IOSS with cross-border audit under EU Regulation 904/2010. Apple and Google retain contractual clawback rights to recover uncollected VAT/GST, withhold future settlements, and suspend App Store or Play Billing integration for any incorrect seller-of-record or tax-fraud indicator.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar has full current-account and capital-account convertibility with no exchange-control legislation. The Gibraltar pound is freely exchangeable into sterling and other major currencies through licensed banks. Profit remittance and dividend repatriation by a Bermuda Exempted Company registered or branch-qualifying in Gibraltar are not subject to withholding tax or exchange-control authorisation. The only practical hurdles are GFSC-sanctioned AML/CFT screening, source-of-wealth verification for remittances exceeding enumerated thresholds, and CRS/FATCA reporting on ultimate beneficial owners. Routine dividend and intercompany loan repayment flows are treated as capital-account transactions and are executable via SWIFT with appropriate purpose codes.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar imposes no statutory surrender, mandatory conversion ratio, or prescribed timeline for export or service-delivery proceeds. A Gibraltar-licensed corporate account may retain receipts in the original currency of settlement, including USD and EUR. In line with standard AML/CFT practice, banks may require that export and digital-service receipts be promptly reconciled and, if the account receives foreign currency, that trade documentation (e.g., merchant agreements, invoices, app-store payout statements) be presented within five business days of receipt. No conversion to Gibraltar pounds or sterling is required.

Central bank supervision: Government of Gibraltar (issuing authority for the Gibraltar pound) together with the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the financial services and banking regulator; the Bank of England operates as the central monetary authority for pound sterling clearing and settlement. Gibraltar maintains a 1:1 peg between the Gibraltar pound and the United Kingdom pound sterling.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

The use of offshore digital EMI accounts is feasible only through a GFSC-recognised e-money institution, a UK-authorised EMI with a passport or a registered branch into Gibraltar, or an EEA EMI operating under transitional arrangements. Feasibility rating is Medium. Core legal constraints include: no anonymous accounts, mandatory strong customer authentication, refusal by many EMIs of zero-tax offshore parent structures, and the need to demonstrate that app-store settlement flows are not client-money pass-throughs but non-refundable merchant revenues. For a Bermuda Exempted Company, a fully licensed tier-1 Gibraltar bank account is operationally preferable to an EMI because of the greater availability of escrow, FX forwards, and documentary trade services.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The compliant route is a SWIFT MT103 from the offshore parent or investor to the Gibraltar bank using the receiving bank's unique BIC and IBAN. The payment must be accompanied by the creditor reference matching the obligatory capital increase notice and the issuer's corporate action pack. Purpose code must be 'EQUITY/CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION' (or 'C01' under the joint bank payment purpose code standard). For injections above GBP 50,000, the bank will require a Funds Intake Form, source-of-wealth statement and the capital injection board resolution. Inbound sterling payments from the United Kingdom may also be cleared via CHAPS or Faster Payments if the remitting bank participates in those systems.
