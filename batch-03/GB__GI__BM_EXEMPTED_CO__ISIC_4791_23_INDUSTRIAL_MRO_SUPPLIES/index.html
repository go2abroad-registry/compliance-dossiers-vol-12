Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is prescribed for a private exempted company limited by shares in Gibraltar; at least one share must be subscribed, and no escrow account or capital deposit is required for an unregulated electronic-catalogue MRO retail business.

Funding & unlock path: The premises must hold a final fire-safety and building-control certificate from the Office of the Chief Fire Officer and Building Control, confirming structural floor loading, means of escape, emergency lighting, fire detection, and extinguishing equipment. The company must maintain a valid occupancy certificate and complete an occupational health and safety assessment for all MRO product handling, including supply of safety data sheets where chemical risks are present.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital is prescribed for a private exempted company limited by shares in Gibraltar; at least one share must be subscribed, and no escrow account or capital deposit is required for an unregulated electronic-catalogue MRO retail business.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current- and capital-account convertibility. The Gibraltar pound (GIP) circulates at hard parity 1:1 with GBP; no exchange controls, prior approvals, surrender obligations, or remittance taxes are imposed on bons fide profit distributions. The Bermuda Exempted Company itself is tax-exempt under Bermuda law but remains subject to BMA oversight; fund flows from the Gibraltar treasury to shareholders are unrestricted. Structural caveat: HMRC and OECD substance rules apply to UK-sourced e-commerce profits routed through Gibraltar; convertibility is unconditional, but tax efficiency requires demonstrated operational substance in the jurisdiction (catalog sales support, inventory contracting, treasury risk management, procurement control).

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. The United Kingdom, Gibraltar, and Bermuda impose no statutory export-proceeds surrender timeline, minimum conversion ratio, or repatriation quota. Industrial MRO sales proceeds may be retained indefinitely in GBP, USD, EUR, or other freely convertible currencies. Regulatory duty: all export receipts must be routed through the licensed Gibraltar clearing bank and remain fully traceable under the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing Regulations 2019; no central bank conversion certificate or FX filing is required.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (United Kingdom, monetary anchor for GBP); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as banking conduct and prudential supervisor; Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) as licensing and regulatory authority of the Bermuda Exempted Company.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% standard Gibraltar corporation tax on chargeable profits of a tax-resident company.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid to non-residents, to residents, or between Gibraltar companies; no advance corporation tax or dividend distribution tax applies.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 9 months after the end of the accounting period (fiscal year-end); the self-assessed final tax balance is payable on the same date; the Commissioner may only extend the deadline by written consent.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 12 months after the end of the financial period to which the return relates.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Box 1 - Status and Identification Company name, Gibraltar company or overseas registration number, income tax reference number, registered office address, compliance officer details. A Bermuda-incorporated Exempted Company is registered in Gibraltar as an overseas company under Part 21 of the Companies Act 2014; the return must disclose that registration number and the Gibraltar tax reference. Do not file a Bermuda-exempt-only return. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory Gibraltar pound (GIP/GBP) monetary threshold is codified in the Income Tax Act 2010; the arm's length principle applies to all controlled cross-border transactions between the Bermuda Exempted Company and any connected person. In practice, the Commissioner may require a Local File or Master File for any material controlled transaction, and contemporaneous TP documentation should be maintained from the first GIP of intercompany turnover. Mandatory Country-by-Country (CbC) reporting notification applies where the multinational group has consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more (approximately GIP 750 million), filed with the Gibraltar Competent Authority, with Local and Master File obligations following OECD BEPS Action 13 in such cases.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations 2019 (as amended) apply because the entity performs a relevant activity. A B2B MRO catalogue e-commerce distribution business is classified as a Distribution and Service Centre, with potential secondary classification as a Headquarters. The entity must demonstrate in Gibraltar: (i) the performance of CIGA (purchasing, inventory management, pricing, marketing, distribution, and risk management decisions); (ii) adequate senior and operating employees; (iii) adequate physical premises; and (iv) adequate operating expenditure proportionate to the activity. An annual ESR Return is due within 12 months of the financial year-end. The Ber muda Exempted Company's tax-exempt status in Bermuda does not satisfy Gibraltar ESR; failure attracts penalties up to GBP 100,000 plus daily penalties, remedial directions, information exchange with HMRC and EEA authorities, and potential strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Declarations

Statutory sanction: Under the Income Tax Act 2010: civil tax-geared penalties up to 100% of understated tax, fines up to GBP 10,000, statutory interest on unpaid tax; criminal conviction for tax evasion carries imprisonment of up to 2 years, and making a false statement carries imprisonment of up to 2 years with unlimited fines.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign false returns are personally exposed to criminal prosecution, disqualification as directors for up to 15 years, travel bans pending trial, asset freezes and confiscation orders under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015, and civil restitution of the unpaid tax through fraudulent-trading proceedings under the Companies Act 2014.
Shell Company and Sham Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: ESR financial penalty up to GBP 100,000 plus daily penalties up to GBP 500 per day; remedial directions from the Gibraltar Competent Authority; strike-off from the Companies Registry under the Companies Act 2014; re-characterization of profits with disallowance of deductions and exposure to foreign tax assessments where the entity is treated as resident elsewhere.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for wrongful trading and for signing false ESR returns; disqualification orders of up to 15 years; personal fines for making misleading statements to the Income Tax Office or the ESR regulator; where assets are dissipated, the court may pierce the corporate veil and hold directors liable for the company's unpaid taxes and creditors' losses.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner may make arm's length adjustments: upward adjustment of chargeable profits, denial of deduction for excessive intercompany fees, statutory interest on underpaid tax, and tax-geared penalties of up to 100% of the under-reported tax where negligence or deliberate behavior is established; CbC non-notification attracts separate administrative fines.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign returns knowing the intercompany pricing is not arm's length are jointly and severally liable for the penalty amounts; deliberate mispricing constitutes fraudulent evasion leading to criminal prosecution, disqualification, and a personal liability to restitution; the Revenue may also seek security bonds over personal assets of directors where travel is contemplated.
AML and Trade-Based Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Criminal liability under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and the Criminal Justice Act 2011: money laundering carries imprisonment of up to 14 years and unlimited fines; the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit may freeze accounts and assets; the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission may impose regulatory penalties, revoke the merchant facility, and cancel the entity's registration.

Director personal liability: Directors who consent to or connive in laundering transactions are personally criminally liable and may face imprisonment of up to 14 years; travel bans and international asset-freeze orders may be imposed; directors may be disqualified and subject to confiscation of personal assets acquired with the proceeds of crime; card scheme rules additionally permit direct personal claims by acquirers against principals for chargeback liabilities.
Treaty Abuse and False Tax Residence Certificate

Statutory sanction: Refusal or revocation of the TRC; denial of UK DTA relief leading to imposition of UK withholding tax and interest; exchange of information with HMRC; penalties up to GBP 10,000 for false statements; restitution of the UK tax that should have been withheld, plus interest; the Gibraltar Commissioner may also re-assess profits on a look-through basis.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly furnish false statements to obtain a TRC face imprisonment of up to 2 years and personal fines; the court may impose a personal restitution order equal to the tax advantage obtained; directors may be disqualified and barred from acting as directors of any Gibraltar company; travel restrictions may be imposed while restitution remains unpaid.
