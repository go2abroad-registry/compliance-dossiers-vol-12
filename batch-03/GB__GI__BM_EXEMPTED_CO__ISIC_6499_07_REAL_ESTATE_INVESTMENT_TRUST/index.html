Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar Income Tax Office (Customs and Income Tax Department, HM Government of Gibraltar); Companies House Gibraltar; HM Customs Gibraltar. Portals: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation/income-tax and https://eservices.gibraltar.gov.gi

E-government filing portal

For BM_EXEMPTED_CO (Bermuda Exempted Company) establishing a footprint in Gibraltar: (1) File as an overseas company under Part VII, Companies Act 2014, at Companies House Gibraltar within 21 days of opening a place of business, submitting certified Bermuda incorporation certificate, memorandum and articles, directors/beneficial owners register, and a Gibraltar-resident process agent appointment; receive a Gibraltar Overseas Company Number. (2) If central management and control (POEM) is exercised from Gibraltar or Gibraltar-source income is derived, apply to the Gibraltar Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN) via the foreign company registration form; each director otherwise also requires a personal Gibraltar TIN. (3) For goods movements, obtain a Trader Unique Identification Number (TUID) from HM Customs Gibraltar to lodge declarations through the Gibraltar Automated Customs and Excise System (GACES). (4) File an annual Economic Substance Return through Companies House e-services, even if tax-resident nowhere in Gibraltar. A company that remains managed and controlled in Bermuda with no Gibraltar-source income need not register for income tax, but must file a non-residence declaration if requested.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone - BM_EXEMPTED_CO is a Bermuda Exempted Company automatically exempt from Bermudian income tax, capital gains tax, and withholding tax (annual government fee applies). In Gibraltar, the entity retains Tax_Exempt_Zone treatment only while central management and control sits outside Gibraltar and no Gibraltar-source income arises; a Gibraltar POEM shift reclassifies it to Entity_Level_Taxation under the 10% Gibraltar corporate income tax.

Available election forms

(i) Bermuda Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 election: obtain a Tax Protection Certificate guaranteeing continued exemption from any future Bermudian income, capital, dividend, or withholding tax until at least 2035. (ii) Gibraltar Income Tax Act election on locus of central management and control: a written notice to the Income Tax Commissioner enables treatment as a non-resident company, limiting Gibraltar tax to Gibraltar-source income. (iii) Taxes (Economic Substance) Regulations 2018 election: in the annual Economic Substance Return, elect 'Pure Equity Holding Company' to reduce substance obligations. (iv) No Gibraltar check-the-box election exists; UK hybrid mismatch elections under TIOPA 2010 Part VII are unavailable to a Bermuda/Gibraltar non-UK-resident entity.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar Customs and Excise Duties Regime - no VAT, no GST, no general sales tax; Bermuda Customs Duties Regime - no VAT/GST/sales tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - Gibraltar imposes no VAT/GST and therefore no economic turnover threshold triggers indirect tax registration. However, any importer must register with HM Customs Gibraltar for a TUID regardless of turnover. Import duty applies at ad valorem rates up to 25% plus excise on alcohol, tobacco, and fuel. Bermuda similarly lacks VAT/GST and levies customs duty under the Bermuda Customs Tariff.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar has no VAT system, so no statutory zero-rating or refund mechanism applies to exports; goods exported from Gibraltar are outside the scope of Gibraltar import/excise duties, and no Gibraltar export duty is levied. Bermuda also imposes no export tax or VAT zero-rating. For separate UK VAT-exposed activities of the REIT (e.g., management services supplied by a UK establishment to non-UK customers), zero-rating or outside-the-scope treatment requires: (i) the recipient belongs outside the UK; (ii) the supply is not an excepted supply connected to UK land; (iii) documentary evidence of the recipient's business status and supply is retained under regulation 16, VAT Regulations 1995; and (iv) for exported goods, proof of physical departure from the UK within three months of the supply.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration (EX1/SAD generated via GACES single window)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill naming the Bermuda Exempted Company as exporter
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin (where preferential tariff treatment is claimed)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate evidencing repatriation of export proceeds to the entity's Gibraltar or Bermuda bank account
  • Transport and insurance documentation for audit substantiation

No mandatory domestic e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API clearance mandate, or digital stamping exists in Gibraltar. Standard commercial invoices suffice. Customs clearance must be executed electronically through GACES, the Gibraltar single window; export and import declarations (EX1/SAD, IM4) are lodged via the GACES portal or machine-to-machine API with scanned transport and commercial documents attached. Bermuda imposes no e-invoicing mandate.

In Gibraltar, fraudulent or mis-declared exports are liable to seizure and forfeiture, customs penalties up to GBP 5,000 and/or 100% of evaded duty, and criminal prosecution for false documentation under the Customs Ordinance ss.66-72; failure to repatriate export proceeds within 180 days may trigger Exchange Control Act 1972 enforcement. In the UK VAT context, HMRC may make a retrospective assessment up to 20 years for deliberate non-compliance, apply penalties up to 100% of VAT lost under FA 2007 Schedule 24, and prosecute for cheating the public revenue or fraudulent evasion under VATA 1994 s.72. Bermuda imposes no export or income tax clawback, but false customs declarations attract fines and imprisonment under the Bermuda Customs (Offences) Regulations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar pound (GIP) is fully convertible at a 1:1 parity to sterling. No capital or current account exchange restrictions exist, but Gibraltar applies the UK's money laundering and financial sanctions framework, so transfers are legitimate only when commercial documents and KYC are present in the bank file. Profit remittance of a Bermuda-exempt REIT resident in Gibraltar is unencumbered by exchange controls; however, banks may delay distribution wires pending an annual AML review.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No surrender or conversion obligation exists. All export proceeds are freely retainable in GIP, GBP, EUR, or USD. There is no statutory timeline for conversion or repatriation; only transaction reporting thresholds apply under normal AML/CTF rules.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority) and Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the banking and foreign exchange administration for Gibraltar

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility: Low-Medium. Gibraltar does not currently issue a dedicated domestic EMI licence under the Financial Services Act 2019; any payment institution must obtain a GFSC payment services licence or rely on EEA/UK passporting rights that expired post-Brexit. UK-regulated EMI licensing does not automatically cover Gibraltar, and offshore digital EMIs present complex AML and source-of-fund issues for real estate transactions. Recommended approach: use a licensed Gibraltar bank as the primary escrow and clearing agent; a compliant outbound EMI (e.g. Czech or Irish authorised EMI) may be used only for low-value operational payments, not for trust subscriptions or property settlements.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound capital for the REIT must be routed through a licensed Gibraltar bank using a SWIFT MT103 with a recognised purpose code (e.g. K10 Investment, OPRT Operations, or PROP Trust Subscription). The GB-based correspondent bank should be included to avoid direct high-risk routing from Bermuda. A dedicated capital escrow account at Banco Sabadell (Gibraltar) or Gibraltar International Bank is recommended, with pre-agreed instruction formats for subscription letters, board resolutions, and latest audited balance sheet of the investor. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

10% flat corporate income tax on chargeable profits of every Gibraltar tax-resident company and on profits attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment of a non-resident company.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Gibraltar does not impose a withholding tax on dividends paid by a Gibraltar-resident company to resident or non-resident shareholders.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The return must be delivered within 12 months after the end of the accounting period; for a calendar-year company, the statutory filing deadline is 31 December of the following year unless the Commissioner grants an extension.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Box 1 (Company Particulars) Full legal name, company registration number, registered office, e-mail address, and contact details of the Gibraltar tax representative. Enter the Bermuda exempted company name, the Bermuda registration number, the Gibraltar branch establishment details, and the appointment of a Gibraltar tax agent; do not insert a Gibraltarian company number unless one has been issued. Late filing incurs a fixed penalty of GBP 100, a daily surcharge of GBP 10 per day up to a maximum of GBP 600, and, for deliberate or negligent under-declaration, a tax-geared penalty of up to 100% of the underpaid tax. State the Gibraltar branch accounting period that corresponds to the real estate investment trust's financial year. Box 3 (Chargeable Profits) Taxable profits before loss relief and deductions, computed under Gibraltar Income Tax Act principles. Include only profits attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment or property located in Gibraltar. Foreign rental income must be excluded unless derived through a Gibraltarian branch. Late filing incurs a fixed penalty of GBP 100, a daily surcharge of GBP 10 per day up to a maximum of GBP 600, and, for deliberate or negligent under-declaration, a tax-geared penalty of up to 100% of the underpaid tax. Persistent non-filing can also result in estimation assessments, enforcement proceedings, and strike-off of the company.
Box 4 (Loss Relief Claimed) Amount of carry-forward trading losses or other available relief deducted against chargeable profits. Only losses attributable to the Gibraltar PE may be carried forward within the 5-year statutory limit; losses incurred by the Bermuda company outside Gibraltar are not deductible. Late filing incurs a fixed penalty of GBP 100, a daily surcharge of GBP 10 per day up to a maximum of GBP 600, and, for deliberate or negligent under-declaration, a tax-geared penalty of up to 100% of the underpaid tax. Persistent non-filing can also result in estimation assessments, enforcement proceedings, and strike-off of the company.
Box 5 (Tax Chargeable) Tax computed at 10% on the chargeable profits after relief. Applicable rate remains 10%; no REIT-specific rate is available in Gibraltar. Enter the computed amount in Gibraltar pounds (GIP). Late filing incurs a fixed penalty of GBP 100, a daily surcharge of GBP 10 per day up to a maximum of GBP 600, and, for deliberate or negligent under-declaration, a tax-geared penalty of up to 100% of the underpaid tax. Persistent non-filing can also result in estimation assessments, enforcement proceedings, and strike-off of the company.
Box 6 (Double Taxation Relief) Credit for foreign tax paid on income also taxed in Gibraltar, limited to the Gibraltar tax on that income. Attach Form TRC and supporting computations where a withholding tax has been suffered on cross-border interest, royalties, or property income. Unilateral relief is granted even in the absence of a comprehensive double taxation agreement. Late filing incurs a fixed penalty of GBP 100, a daily surcharge of GBP 10 per day up to a maximum of GBP 600, and, for deliberate or negligent under-declaration, a tax-geared penalty of up to 100% of the underpaid tax. Persistent non-filing can also result in estimation assessments, enforcement proceedings, and strike-off of the company.
Box 7 (Tax Payable or Repayable) Net tax due after payments on account or the amount repayable to the taxpayer. Settle any balance via bank transfer to the Income Tax Office before the deadline. For non-resident entities without a Gibraltarian bank account, arrange a branch payment account or a regulated payment agent. Late filing incurs a fixed penalty of GBP 100, a daily surcharge of GBP 10 per day up to a maximum of GBP 600, and, for deliberate or negligent under-declaration, a tax-geared penalty of up to 100% of the underpaid tax. Persistent non-filing can also result in estimation assessments, enforcement proceedings, and strike-off of the company.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 10,000,000 annual group turnover or GIP 1,000,000 aggregate value of cross-border controlled transactions per accounting period, above which contemporaneous Local File documentation is mandatory. A Master File is additionally required where the consolidated group revenue exceeds GIP 50,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar's Economic Substance Regulations apply only to designated geographically mobile activities. Letting of physical real estate is not a designated relevant activity, so the REIT activity itself is outside the mandatory ESR substance test. Nevertheless, a Bermuda Exempted Company that is managed or directed from Gibraltar while remaining Bermuda-exempt will be treated as a Gibraltar tax resident on the basis of central management and control, and must demonstrate local presence, premises, and decision-making in Gibraltar to avoid classification as a shell company under general anti-abuse principles.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Deliberate Under-Reporting

Statutory sanction: Penalty up to 100% of the tax evaded, daily default surcharge of GBP 60 per day, criminal fine up to GBP 10,000 plus a sum equal to double the evaded tax, forfeiture of the asset, and civil assessment of unpaid tax plus interest.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly participate are jointly and severally liable for the corporate tax arrears and penalties. Travel bans may be imposed to prevent departure from Gibraltar pending payment, and imprisonment of up to 7 years can be imposed for fraudulent evasion of the public revenue.
Shell Company and Sham Substance Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Reclassification as a Gibraltar tax-resident company, assessment on worldwide profits at 10%, a substitutional shell-company penalty of up to GIP 50,000, and disqualification of any Gibraltar registration stamp or commencement certificate.

Director personal liability: The court may pierce the corporate veil to hold directors personally liable for unpaid tax and penalties. Restriction orders and travel bans can be issued, and a director who fails to comply with disclosure orders can be committed to prison for up to 18 months for contempt.
AML/CFT and Beneficial Ownership Failure

Statutory sanction: Corporate fines up to GIP 500,000, additional GIP 1,000,000 for regulated financial institutions or licensed REIT managers, revocation of the statutory real estate trust licence, and strike-off of the company from the Gibraltar register.

Director personal liability: Responsible officers face personal fines of up to GIP 100,000 per breach and imprisonment of up to 5 years for money laundering predicate offences. Confiscation orders and travel bans may be issued under the Proceeds of Crime Act, and directors can be barred from future directorships.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing and Artificial Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: Mandatory tax adjustment to the arm's length outcome, secondary adjustment recharacterising excess payments as dividends, statutory late-payment interest, and a transfer pricing documentation penalty of GIP 20,000 for non-production of a Local File.

Director personal liability: Directors of the taxpayer and related finance entities can be held personally liable for tax-geared penalties up to 100% of the underpaid tax. Publication in the serious defaulters list, travel bans, disqualification from acting as a director, and imprisonment of up to 7 years for fraudulent evasion are all applicable.
