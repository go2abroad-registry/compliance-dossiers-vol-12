Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar, Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax); official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi (Tax section). For customs and excise matters the competent body is HM Customs Gibraltar.

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda exempted company not incorporated in Gibraltar is treated as a foreign (non-resident) company. If it carries on activity through a branch, permanent establishment, or other taxable presence in Gibraltar, it must: (1) register the branch or place of business with the Gibraltar Companies Registry under the Companies (Foreign Companies) Act, submitting certified copies of the certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, register of directors, and a Gibraltar registered office address; (2) apply for a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN) by lodging the Registration of Company form with the Income Tax Office, together with board resolution, constitutional documents, and evidence of local substance (e.g., registered office, staff); (3) receive the TIN and then file annual income tax returns and pay corporate income tax at the standard rate on assessable Gibraltar-source profits. There is no VAT registration because Gibraltar has no VAT system.

Tax ID type & application path: Territorial_Scope — A Bermuda exempted company not incorporated in Gibraltar is treated as a foreign (non-resident) company. If it carries on activity through a branch, permanent establishment, or other taxable presence in Gibraltar, it must: (1) register the branch or place of business with the Gibraltar Companies Registry under the Companies (Foreign Companies) Act, submitting certified copies of the certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, register of directors, and a Gibraltar registered office address; (2) apply for a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN) by lodging the Registration of Company form with the Income Tax Office, together with board resolution, constitutional documents, and evidence of local substance (e.g., registered office, staff); (3) receive the TIN and then file annual income tax returns and pay corporate income tax at the standard rate on assessable Gibraltar-source profits. There is no VAT registration because Gibraltar has no VAT system.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

None available. Gibraltar does not operate a U.S.-style check-the-box election, notional-transparency election, or hybrid-mismatch election. Entity classification follows strictly the Gibraltar Companies Act and the Income Tax Act 2010, as amended. A non-resident company may be required to appoint a local tax representative for procedural compliance, but this does not alter its entity-level tax classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST — Gibraltar levies selective import, customs, and excise duties (e.g., on alcohol, tobacco, fuel) under the Customs Ordinance and similar legislation. It does not maintain a general sales, services, or value-added tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable – there is no VAT/GST and therefore no turnover-based registration threshold. For physical imports, HM Customs Gibraltar requires an import declaration on a per-transaction basis; no local currency registration threshold applies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable – Gibraltar does not impose VAT or GST, so there is no export zero-rating relief or export VAT refund mechanism. Supplies of cloud vulnerability scanning and penetration testing SaaS are intangible and fall outside Gibraltar’s indirect taxation. Such income is only subject to direct corporate tax under the territorial source rules if attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment or linked to Gibraltar-source trading operations.

  • Not applicable to cross-border SaaS services – no customs export declaration is required for intangible supplies.
  • If physical goods are ever in scope: Gibraltar customs export declaration or Single Administrative Document (SAD).
  • Commercial or tax invoice (stating no VAT applicable in Gibraltar).
  • Signed services contract and statement of work for the cybersecurity SaaS arrangement.
  • Bank SWIFT confirmation or inward remittance certificate evidencing receipt of payment.

No electronic invoicing mandate, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping regime is in force in Gibraltar. Taxpayers may issue paper or electronic invoices. For customs/excise imports, a standard customs declaration is lodged with HM Customs Gibraltar; continuous transaction controls (CTC) and real-time fiscal clearance are not implemented.

Gibraltar has no VAT clawback regime because no VAT exists. However, false declarations, deliberate underreporting of Gibraltar-source profits, or artificial exploitation of the Bermuda exempt company status can trigger civil and criminal sanctions under the Income Tax Act 2010 and general criminal law. The Income Tax Office may audit for up to six years from the normal filing date. Civil penalties range from 30% (careless) to 100% (deliberate concealment) of the tax undercharged, plus interest. Criminal convictions may lead to fines and imprisonment of up to seven years. Additionally, UK anti-abuse (GAAR), OECD substance, and transfer pricing principles apply to any arrangement aimed at gaining an improper tax benefit.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar maintains full current and capital account convertibility with no profit-remittance, dividend, or capital-flow restrictions. GIP circulates interchangeably with GBP; regulated banks offer multi-currency (GBP, USD, EUR) accounts. The Bermuda Exempted Company should obtain non-resident Exchange Control classification ('permit') from the BMA so that all foreign-currency transactions, share capital, and dividends remain free of local conversion and BMD-denominated constraints.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Neither Gibraltar nor the United Kingdom imposes statutory surrender or mandatory conversion of export proceeds. Bermuda non-resident Exempted Companies may retain SaaS subscription receipts in the original invoicing currency (USD/EUR/GBP) indefinitely, with no prescribed timeline or conversion ratio to BMD.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar has no central bank; banking supervision is vested in the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC), while the Government of Gibraltar issues the Gibraltar Pound (GIP) under the Currency Notes Act 1934 at a hard 1:1 peg to the Bank of England's sterling. For the Bermuda-incorporated parent, the Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) administers Exchange Control under the Exchange Control Act 1972.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Medium viability, with legal constraints. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory contemporaneous Local File documentation is triggered where the Gibraltar-resident entity's aggregate controlled transactions exceed GIP 7,500,000 in annual turnover or where intercompany services and licence recharges exceed GIP 500,000; a Master File and Country-by-Country Report are required where the consolidated group revenue of the ultimate parent (including the Bermuda Exempted Company) exceeds EUR 750,000,000.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance (Information Exchange) Act and Regulations (effective 1 January 2019) apply to companies carrying on relevant activities, including provision of services and IP holding. A Bermuda Exempted Company that is Gibraltar tax-resident, or that has a permanent establishment in Gibraltar, must demonstrate genuine economic substance by maintaining senior employees, board decision-making in Gibraltar, an operating premises, and adequate expenditure proportionate to the cybersecurity SaaS activity. Failure to meet the substance test triggers periodic financial penalties, compulsory exchange of information with HMRC and EU tax authorities, potential loss of Gibraltar tax residence, and eventual strike-off under the Gibraltar Companies Act 2014.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Tax Filing

Statutory sanction: Financial penalty up to 100% of the tax underpaid plus interest at 8% per annum; on criminal conviction, an unlimited fine and imprisonment for up to seven years; seizure of assets, court-ordered restitution, and disqualification from holding a Gibraltar directorship for up to fifteen years.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced for fraudulent trading: directors face civil liability for the full amount of the company's debts, worldwide freezing orders, travel bans pending criminal proceedings, and imprisonment for up to seven years where the false filing is proven to be knowing and intentional.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Breach

Statutory sanction: Periodic penalty of GIP 100,000 per breach, cumulative to GIP 500,000; exchange of information with HMRC and EU Member State authorities; revocation of Gibraltar tax residence; compulsory strike-off and dissolution of the Gibraltar registration; prohibition on reopening a financial institution account in Gibraltar.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false economic substance compliance declarations are personally liable to fines up to GIP 500,000, disqualification for up to fifteen years, and imprisonment for up to two years; the Bermuda Exempted Company's shareholders may be compelled to repatriate profits and pay the shortfall tax personally.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Commissioner's adjustment of the chargeable profit under the Gibraltar Income Tax (Transfer Pricing) Regulations, with a penalty of up to 100% of the additional tax arising from the adjustment plus interest at 8% per annum; secondary adjustment treated as a deemed distribution or contribution; publication of adverse determination in serious cases.

Director personal liability: Directors who instructed or approved the manipulated pricing are jointly and severally liable for the adjusted tax and penalties where the company is unable to pay; criminal referral for forgery applies where fabricated TP documentation is submitted, carrying imprisonment for up to seven years and travel bans pending extradition proceedings.
AML / Sanctions Infringement in SaaS Payment Flows

Statutory sanction: Under the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act and the Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering framework: unlimited fines, confiscation of the entire account balance, imprisonment for up to fourteen years, freezing of bank accounts by court order, and revocation of any financial services licence held by the entity or its principals.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers face personal criminal liability where they knew or suspected money laundering and failed to file a suspicious activity report; penalties include imprisonment for up to fourteen years, director disqualification, and travel bans imposed to secure prosecution jurisdiction.
Director Personal Liability / Corporate Veil-Piercing

Statutory sanction: Civil restitution under the Gibraltar Companies Act 2014 for fraudulent or wrongful trading up to the amount of the company's debts; disqualification orders for up to fifteen years; personal liability for the company's unpaid corporation tax, penalties, and contributions; in egregious cases, imprisonment for up to ten years.

Director personal liability: The limited liability shield is set aside: directors incur joint and several liability with the company and any shadow shareholders for the whole of the shortfall; courts may impose travel bans, asset freezes, and extradition orders, and may order the worldwide tracing and repatriation of diverted cryptographic and fiat assets held through the Bermuda structure.
