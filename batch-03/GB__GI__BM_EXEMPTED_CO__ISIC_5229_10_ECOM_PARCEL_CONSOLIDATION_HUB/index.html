Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar operates the UK's post-1979 fully liberalised exchange-control regime: both current and capital account items are freely convertible and no exchange-control approval exists for inbound or outbound flows. The GIP is fixed at 1:1 to GBP and circulates interchangeably with sterling at par. The Bermuda Exempted Company is tax-exempt in Bermuda and non-resident for Gibraltar income tax purposes; dividends, intercompany loan repayments, management fees, and liquidation proceeds may be remitted from Gibraltar accounts without withholding tax, BMA consent, or FX allocation. Residual friction is limited to bank-level CDD: large profit remittances require source-of-funds and source-of-wealth evidence plus a substance attestation to distinguish genuine parcel-consolidation trade cash flows from round-tripped capital.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Neither Gibraltar nor the United Kingdom imposes repatriation, surrender, holding-period, or mandatory conversion ratios on export receipts. For the ISIC 5229 parcel-consolidation hub, 100% of export proceeds may be retained offshore in any designated foreign currency (USD, EUR, GBP) and economically merged with working capital. The only non-prudential duties are statistical (Bank of England / HMRC C6 overseas-transactions return where the account is UK-linked) and GFSC anti-money-laundering reporting (suspicious transaction reports triggered by typology flags such as rapid round-tripping or settlement through unvetted counterparties).

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the integrated prudential and conduct regulator for banks and payment institutions; HM Government of Gibraltar (Commissioner of Currency) issues the Gibraltar pound (GIP) under a 100% sterling-backed currency-board arrangement; Bank of England as the de facto monetary anchor given the 1:1 GIP/GBP peg; Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) as the upstream regulator of the Bermuda Exempted Company.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

HIGH for payment-collection and pay-out rails, but LOW as a substitute for a GFSC-licensed bank account. Airwallex UK Ltd (authorised by the UK Financial Conduct Authority as a payment/e-money institution) can onboard the Bermuda Exempted Company remotely and provide multi-currency virtual accounts, API-driven settlement to parcel-carrier counterparties, and FX conversion at interbank rates. Legal constraint: post-Brexit Gibraltar is not an EEA state, so Airwallex's UK authorisation does not passport into Gibraltar; GIP settlement is effected via UK GBP clearing (Faster Payments / CHAPS) at 1:1 parity. The EMI must still perform enhanced due diligence equivalent to GFSC Guidance Notes on the offshore structure; the absence of statutory minimum capital in Bermuda is acceptable if the UBO register, economic-substance declaration, and source-of-funds file are complete. FCA CASS client-money safeguarding rules apply to all balances held with Airwallex.
