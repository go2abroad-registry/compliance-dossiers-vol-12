Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum issued or paid-up share capital for a private company under s. 5 of the Gibraltar Companies Act 2014; the company may be incorporated with two subscriber shares of USD 1 each. No escrow/deposit is legally required at formation; however, a board-approved working capital buffer of USD 250,000 (or EUR 125,000 MiFID-equivalent base capital) must be escrowed with a licensed bank as a precondition of the GFSC investment-services licence, with the escrow account opened within 10 business days of incorporation.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar – Office of the Commissioner of Income Tax (Income Tax Office); Companies House Gibraltar; Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC). Official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi

E-government filing portal

For a foreign entity such as a Bermuda Exempted Company operating an institutional OTC crypto desk through Gibraltar, the registration sequence is: (1) file Form Overseas Company Registration with the Registrar of Companies at Companies House Gibraltar to obtain a Gibraltar Company Number (statutory company identifier); (2) submit the Income Tax Office Registration Application to the Commissioner of Income Tax to receive a Gibraltar Reference Number (GRN), being the tax file reference; (3) if engaging in regulated DLT activity, obtain a GFSC DLT Provider Licence, whose regulatory reference must be linked to the GRN; (4) if no Gibraltar-source income is expected, file a clearance application to be categorised as a Tax Exempt Zone entity and deliver a nil tax return on an annual basis.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — For a foreign entity such as a Bermuda Exempted Company operating an institutional OTC crypto desk through Gibraltar, the registration sequence is: (1) file Form Overseas Company Registration with the Registrar of Companies at Companies House Gibraltar to obtain a Gibraltar Company Number (statutory company identifier); (2) submit the Income Tax Office Registration Application to the Commissioner of Income Tax to receive a Gibraltar Reference Number (GRN), being the tax file reference; (3) if engaging in regulated DLT activity, obtain a GFSC DLT Provider Licence, whose regulatory reference must be linked to the GRN; (4) if no Gibraltar-source income is expected, file a clearance application to be categorised as a Tax Exempt Zone entity and deliver a nil tax return on an annual basis.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Available elections under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act include election for group treatment and group loss relief; election for a non-resident classification where central management and control is located outside Gibraltar; and election to apply UK anti-hybrid mismatch treatment under SI 2018/1230 where the Bermuda Exempted Company is transparent in Bermuda but opaque for Gibraltar tax purposes. Gibraltar does not operate a US-style check-the-box election; classification follows the entity's constitutional form subject to clearances issued by the Commissioner.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales and Use Tax – selective Gibraltar Purchase Tax applies only to physical imported/consumed goods

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT/GST registration threshold exists. For customs purposes, importers must register with the Gibraltar Customs and Excise Department at the point of first importation; the threshold is not turnover-based.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Since Gibraltar has no VAT/GST on services, the institutional crypto brokering fee is not subject to indirect tax and is treated as an outside-scope 0% supply when rendered to non-resident institutional counterparties. Qualifying conditions: the provider holds an active GRN tax file in Gibraltar; the OTC service is arranged by a Gibraltar-licensed entity; settlement occurs through vaulted digital assets or mirroring bank rails without physical goods entering Gibraltar; and the beneficiary of the service is resident outside Gibraltar. There is no VAT refund mechanism because no input VAT is charged; for physical goods, refunds of Gibraltar Purchase Tax may only arise on re-export under the Customs and Purchase Tax Ordinance, supported by customs supervision.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration (C88/SAD) or a nil declaration for pure digital asset trades
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing cross-border movement if the settlement involves allocated physical vaulted commodities
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103/MT202 confirming receipt of institutional client funds
  • GFSC transaction trail comprising order ticket, trade confirmation, chain analytics report, KYC/CDD records, and licence reference

No real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing API, digital stamping, or continuous transaction control mandate applies. OTC desk invoices and transaction tickets are issued in English B2B format and must be retained for ten years to satisfy GFSC AML/CFT, economic substance, and Income Tax Office record-keeping rules.

The Income Tax Office may reopen the tax file up to six years for reasonable cause and without a time limit in cases of fraud. A false export/zero-rating claim or sham economic substance exposes the entity to clawback of tax-exempt status, reassessment on the full service fee, interest at statutory rates, penalties up to 100% of tax evaded under the Income Tax Act, and regulatory fines by the GFSC. Directors may be disqualified and held jointly and severally liable where the misrepresentation is shown to be intentional.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar maintains a fully liberalized exchange-control regime with complete current-account and capital-account convertibility for GIP, GBP, USD, and EUR. There are no statutory approval gates, no capital-export authorization procedures, and no restriction on repatriation of profits, dividends, interest, or Gibraltar-branch remittances to the Bermuda Exempted Company. The only practical hurdles are bank-level documentary controls: the OTC crypto desk is classified as a High-Risk Special License client, so every conversion or remittance must be supported by a coherent commercial rationale (executed OTC trade tickets, Fireblocks audit trail, counterparty due-diligence file) to satisfy the GFSC AML/CFT code. Tax-residence dislocation must also be avoided: treasury functions must not operate in a manner that creates unintended Gibraltar permanent-establishment risk beyond the licensed broker-dealer presence.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion rule exists in Gibraltar. Institutional OTC crypto brokering receipts (ISIC 6619), including brokerage fees, bid/ask spreads, and settlement proceeds, may be retained indefinitely in USD, EUR, GBP, or GIP with no conversion ratio, no repatriation deadline, and no transaction-level BOP filing obligation. Records of all foreign receipts must nonetheless be retained for at least five years under the Crimes Act 2011, as amended, and the DLT Provider record-keeping schedule, and must be capable of being reconciled in real time to the Fireblocks Vault ledger and the bank's SWIFT statements.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar has no independent central bank or standalone monetary authority. The de facto central regulator for corporate banking, FX execution, and payment clearing is the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC), acting under the Financial Services Act 2019 and the Financial Services (Distributed Ledger Technology) Act 2019. Gibraltar pound (GIP) banknotes are issued by the Government of Gibraltar under the Currency Notes Act 1934 at a fixed 1:1 parity with GBP; effective monetary stability is anchored to the Bank of England through the GIP/GBP peg, while USD and EUR clearing is delivered through GFSC-authorized banks using their New York and Frankfurt/TARGET2 correspondent relationships.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Cross-border EMI and fintech onboarding is feasible but constrained. UK FCA-authorised e-money institutions (ClearBank, Modulr, Railsr, BCB Group, Clear Junction) and Gibraltar-licensed e-money and payment institutions will onboard the Bermuda Exempted Company only when (i) the GFSC DLT Provider license (or equivalent regulatory authorization) is evidenced, (ii) the sponsor bank accepts virtual-asset-adjacent flows, and (iii) the EMI integrates with Fireblocks Vault so that wallet-provenance checks and Travel Rule messaging are automated. Overall viability is Medium-Low for generic EMIs; BCB Group and Clear Junction are the most crypto-native correspondents and can provide segregated fiat omnibus accounts, but subject to monthly velocity caps, per-transaction blockchain analytics, and transaction-level compliance fees. EMIs should be used as secondary disbursement rails, never as the sole treasury bank for the OTC desk.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder capital and subordinated loan inflows are credited to the Capital Escrow Account through SWIFT MT103 wires from Bermuda (or the ultimate investor's home bank) or, if the payer is inside the UK/EEA, via SEPA/TARGET2. The SWIFT field 50K must mirror the exact UBO/entity name registered in the bank KYC file; field 72 must read 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION - BERMUDA EXEMPTED CO - ISIC 6619 OTC DESK - GFSC DLT'. For USD inbound, the receiving Gibraltar bank draws its New York correspondent (e.g., Bank of New York Mellon, Citibank N.A., or J.P. Morgan) and settles over Fedwire or CHIPS; the remitter should use purpose code 'D0001/DIRECT INVESTMENT' for equity or 'SUBSCRIPTION/ACCOUNT FUNDING' for treasury cash. All capital injections above GBP 50,000 equivalent must be pre-notified to the bank's AML team 24 hours in advance with a wiring schedule, supporting board resolution, and source-of-funds pack; no same-day unannounced funding is permitted.Settlement disbursements flow from the Operational Account over the following rails: (1) USD to institutional counterparties via SWIFT MT103, cleared over Fedwire/CHIPS through the bank's New York correspondent; (2) GBP via Faster Payments Service for amounts below GBP 1 million and CHAPS for same-day critical transfers; (3) EUR via TARGET2/SEPA Instant to EU-based settlement banks; (4) digital-asset legs are settled exclusively through the Fireblocks Vault to whitelisted institutional wallet addresses, with wallet-permission policy enforcement at both policy and transaction level. Commercial Letters of Credit are not used for core crypto-delivery settlement; they may be structured only as ancillary fee-guarantee instruments for institutional counterparties purchasing optional physical-backed products. Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% – Gibraltar does not impose dividend withholding tax on distributions made by a Gibraltar-resident company to either residents or non-residents.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due within nine months after the end of the accounting period. For a standard calendar-year period, the return must be filed by 30 September of the following year.

Report only Gibraltar-source income unless the non-resident is deemed managed and controlled in Gibraltar due to excessive board activity on the Rock. Late filing attracts an initial penalty of GBP 1,000, plus a daily default surcharge of GBP 10 per day for each day outstanding (maximum 200 days). Box 5 – Deductible Operating Expenses Trade expenses wholly and exclusively incurred for the production of the reported income, including staff costs, rent, and professional fees. Deduct only expenses attributable to the Gibraltar-source activity and substantiated by contemporaneous invoices. Late filing attracts an initial penalty of GBP 1,000, plus a daily default surcharge of GBP 10 per day for each day outstanding (maximum 200 days). Inaccurate returns trigger a further penalty of up to 100% of the tax understated, and interest applies on unpaid tax at the statutory rate.
Box 6 – Net Taxable Profits Gross income less allowable deductions, before any reliefs. Calculate the profit on a standalone basis for the Gibraltar activity using arm's length transfer prices from related entities. Late filing attracts an initial penalty of GBP 1,000, plus a daily default surcharge of GBP 10 per day for each day outstanding (maximum 200 days). Inaccurate returns trigger a further penalty of up to 100% of the tax understated, and interest applies on unpaid tax at the statutory rate.
Box 7 – Tax at 12.5% The headline gross tax computed by applying 12.5% to net taxable profits. Apply the same 12.5% rate to Gibraltar-source taxable profits; no preferential rate for non-residents. Late filing attracts an initial penalty of GBP 1,000, plus a daily default surcharge of GBP 10 per day for each day outstanding (maximum 200 days). Inaccurate returns trigger a further penalty of up to 100% of the tax understated, and interest applies on unpaid tax at the statutory rate.
Box 8 – Reliefs and Credits Deductions for patent box relief, unilateral foreign-trade relief, and any available double-tax relief. Claim only reliefs authorised under Gibraltar law; attach a schedule computing any treaty credit. Late filing attracts an initial penalty of GBP 1,000, plus a daily default surcharge of GBP 10 per day for each day outstanding (maximum 200 days). Inaccurate returns trigger a further penalty of up to 100% of the tax understated, and interest applies on unpaid tax at the statutory rate.
Box 9 – Final Tax Payable or Repayable Remaining tax liability after credits, or net refund if estimates were remitted. Ensure any interim payments, if applicable, are declared to avoid erroneous refund claims. Late filing attracts an initial penalty of GBP 1,000, plus a daily default surcharge of GBP 10 per day for each day outstanding (maximum 200 days). Inaccurate returns trigger a further penalty of up to 100% of the tax understated, and interest applies on unpaid tax at the statutory rate.
Box 10 – Transfer Pricing Declaration Confirmation that all cross-border related-party transactions are priced at arm's length and that TP documentation has been prepared. Non-residents with Gibraltar branches must confirm that intra-group funding and fee arrangements are supported by an economic substance analysis. Late filing attracts an initial penalty of GBP 1,000, plus a daily default surcharge of GBP 10 per day for each day outstanding (maximum 200 days). Inaccurate returns trigger a further penalty of up to 100% of the tax understated, and interest applies on unpaid tax at the statutory rate.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GBP 5,000,000 total turnover of the Gibraltar entity triggers the need for contemporaneous Local File and Master File documentation. Additionally, multinational groups with consolidated revenue equal to or exceeding EUR 750,000,000 must file a Country-by-Country report, irrespective of Gibraltar turnover.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations apply to every Gibraltar-resident entity engaged in 'relevant activities', which include distribution and service centres, financing, leasing, intellectual property holding, and banking/funds management. An OTC crypto desk performing institutional bilateral brokering will normally fall within 'banking and financing' or 'investment services', thus requiring physical substance in Gibraltar: a local office, employees with decision-making authority, and core income-generating activities performed in Gibraltar. A Bermuda Exempted Company that is managed and controlled in Gibraltar but incorporated offshore is not excluded from the ESR; its substance profile must be evidenced with payroll, premises, and board minutes.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Civil penalties up to 150% of the evaded tax, plus criminal fines of up to GBP 100,000 and imprisonment for up to 7 years under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act and the Criminal Code.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign false returns are personally liable for the unpaid taxes and penalties; travel bans may be imposed by the Supreme Court pending settlement, and imprisonment is available for egregious fraud.
Economic Substance (Shell Company) Failure

Statutory sanction: Administrative penalties of up to GBP 250,000 per accounting period for absence of economic substance; the Commissioner may also strike the company off the register and deny tax residence certificates.

Director personal liability: Directors are subject to director disqualification orders, individual fines up to GBP 50,000, and asset freezing/investigation orders; travel restrictions may be enforced where the restructuring is seen as an abusive vehicle.
AML / KYC Breach in Digital Asset Handling

Statutory sanction: A fine of up to GBP 500,000 and revocation of the financial services licence under the Gibraltar Financial Services (AML) Regulations; the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit may also issue civil penalties for each breach.

Director personal liability: Directors and the Money Laundering Reporting Officer may face criminal prosecution personally, with custodial sentences of up to 8 years and personal monetary fines; involvement in illicit finance can trigger a worldwide asset freeze and denial of travel.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Default

Statutory sanction: Unsupported adjustments impose additional tax plus an accuracy penalty of up to 100% of the additional tax. Repeated non-compliance can attract a default filing penalty of GBP 20,000 per year.

Director personal liability: Directors are individually liable for penalties arising from wilful failure to supply documentation; the Commissioner may seek a court order requiring reimbursement by the company, with the director exposed for the resulting deficit if liquidation ensues.
