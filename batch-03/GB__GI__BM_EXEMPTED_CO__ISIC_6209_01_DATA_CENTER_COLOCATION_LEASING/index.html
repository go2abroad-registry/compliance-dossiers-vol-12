Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar: Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (Ministry of Finance; portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation). Sovereign-UK context: HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC; portal: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc). Domicile (Bermuda): Bermuda Ministry of Finance / Registrar of Companies.

E-government filing portal

(1) Incorporate the Bermuda Exempted Company and obtain EUTPA tax-protection guarantee; no minimum capital is required. (2) Register as an overseas company under the Gibraltar Companies Act 2014 (Part 10) via a licensed resident agent. (3) File the prescribed income-tax application with the Gibraltar Income Tax Office, attaching the certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, registered-office proof and director/manager particulars; the Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TIN) is issued within 3-5 working days. (4) If a UK filing nexus later arises, apply to HMRC for a UK UTR/VAT number under standard overseas-company procedures.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone - the Bermuda Exempted Company is exempt from Bermuda income tax; however, once a Gibraltar permanent establishment is operated (the data centre), the Gibraltar branch income falls under Entity_Level_Taxation on a Territorial_Scope basis: Gibraltar-source profits are taxable at 12.5% under the Income Tax Act 2010, while the offshore parent remains exempt in Bermuda.

Available election forms

Bermuda: election for an Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act (EUTPA) certificate guarantees exemption from future Bermuda tax on income/capital gains until 2035, extendable to 2055. Gibraltar: no check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election; the taxpayer may agree with the Income Tax Office a deemed-profit computation for non-resident PE income (ITA 2010 s.7) instead of detailed deduction proof. UK CTA 2010 Part 6A hybrid-mismatch rules should be screened for any intended double-non-taxation outcome.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar Purchase Tax - a single-stage duty on imported/local goods; Gibraltar is excluded from the UK VAT area, so no VAT, GST or sales-and-use tax arises. Data-centre colocation and bare-metal hosting are outside-scope services and bear 0% indirect tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable: Gibraltar Purchase Tax is accounted at import clearance, with no economic-turnover registration threshold for service suppliers. For comparison only, a UK VAT registration obligation (GBP 90,000 annual threshold) would exist only if a separate UK taxable business presence were established.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Colocation and hosting supplied B2B to customers established outside Gibraltar are automatically outside the scope of Gibraltar Purchase Tax (the tax attaches only to goods at import); no zero-rating claim or VAT refund mechanism exists locally. In a UK VAT scenario, the B2B place-of-supply rule (VATA 1994 s.7A) would site the supply in the customer's country, producing a 0% UK VAT outcome for non-UK business customers, evidenced by customer VAT/TIN or business-status proof.

  • Executed data-centre colocation service agreement (customer business status and foreign establishment evidenced)
  • Outside-scope service invoice (no Purchase Tax; rack units, power and bandwidth specified)
  • Inward remittance certificate / SWIFT-ACH credit advice (payment received within 180 days)
  • SAD/C88 customs export declaration and bill of lading or air waybill for any client-owned hardware physically exported at contract termination
  • Customer self-certification of foreign business status (corporate extract or VAT/TIN certificate)

None - Gibraltar has no real-time fiscal-clearance, API e-invoicing or digital-stamping system; ordinary service invoices are sufficient and must be retained for six years. If voluntary UK VAT registration is added, HMRC Making Tax Digital for VAT requires API-compatible software and digital record filing.

Gibraltar: negligent outside-scope treatment attracts penalties up to 100% of tax lost plus interest (Income Tax Act 2010 ss.113-114); fraudulent statements carry fines up to GBP 5,000 and imprisonment for revenue fraud; goods released without accounting for Purchase Tax are forfeit and subject to penalties up to treble value. UK (if registered): FA 2007 Sch.24 penalties (0-100%), VATA 1994 s.72 criminal liability (up to 7 years imprisonment), retrospective clawback of overclaimed input VAT plus default surcharge.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Capital and current account transactions are fully convertible with no material profit-remittance hurdles. Gibraltar retains no residual exchange-control restrictions for non-resident-owned exempted companies; dividends, management fees, intercompany loan repayments, and liquidation surpluses may be distributed offshore without GFSC or central-bank approval, provided the paying bank's AML/EDD unit can source the funds to verified commercial contracts and audited financial records. As GIP is pegged at par to GBP and clearing is executed on UK infrastructure, the practical currency risk is GBP settlement risk only, not convertibility or repatriation risk.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or conversion obligation exists. Data-center colocation and bare-metal hosting revenues may be retained 100% in any permitted foreign currency, held offshore, or converted freely. There is no mandated conversion ratio, no 30/60/90-day surrender window, and no central-bank monitoring threshold other than aggregated suspicious-transaction reporting (STR) obligations under the Proceeds of Crime Act (Gibraltar) and the GFSC AML/CFT framework.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) acting as the financial regulatory and anti-money-laundering authority; the Bank of England serves as the ultimate monetary backstop, and the Gibraltar Currency Commissioner issues the Gibraltar pound (GIP) at a statutory 1:1 parity with GBP. No separate exchange-control department exists or operates in the territory.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM with legal caveats. FCA-licensed fintech EMIs (e.g., Wise Business, Revolut Business, or comparable digital treasury platforms) can open accounts for the Bermuda Exempted Company using remote KYC with video-verified directors and notarized board resolutions, but they are not licensed deposit-takers; funds are held as safeguarded e-money and are not protected by deposit-guarantee schemes. Post-Brexit, Gibraltar is outside the EEA financial-services passporting zone, so EMIs must rely on restricted authorisation or own-initiative cross-border servicing. Such accounts are acceptable as low-value collections and disbursement rails, but the primary treasury balance must sit in a domestically licensed GFSC-regulated bank to preserve capital certainty, grant-clearance credibility, and clean audit trails.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File and Master File are mandatory when the aggregate value of controlled transactions exceeds GIP 1,000,000 per accounting period.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar has adopted Economic Substance Regulations requiring an entity carrying on a relevant activity (including distribution and service centre for colocation and hosting) to demonstrate adequate employees, premises, and expenditure within the territory. Data center colocation leasing qualifies as a relevant activity where the company generates core income from providing server hosting and rack space services; failure to meet substance tests triggers financial penalties up to GIP 200,000 and potential strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Penalty up to 100% of the tax not paid, compound penalty for fraudulent behavior, criminal prosecution with fines at the court's discretion, and public registry of tax defaulters.

Director personal liability: Director signatures on a false return expose directors to personal civil restitution of the tax lost, court-imposed travel bans pending settlement, and imprisonment for up to 2 years for aiding tax evasion.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Financial penalty of GIP 500 to GIP 200,000 per violation; mandatory strike-off of the company; repayment of any tax benefits obtained; referral to the Intelligence Service.

Director personal liability: Directors and corporate service providers incur personal liability for annual penalties, disqualification from directorship for 10 years, and travel bans imposed by the Registrar to secure attendance at proceedings.
Anti-Money Laundering (AML)

Statutory sanction: Criminal fine up to GIP 1,000,000, confiscation of tainted lease proceeds, and revocation of any required registration under Gibraltar's Proceeds of Crime Act; if the entity is a crypto/mining service provider, a further AML penalty of GIP 5,000 per day is accrued.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally prosecutable for money laundering and financing terrorism; applicable custodial sentence of up to 14 years, civil recovery of benefit, and a permanent travel ban is generally requested on indictment.
Profit Repatriation Abuse

Statutory sanction: Imposition of a 100% reverse adjustment on the artificially shifted profit, non-deductibility of the intercompany fees, and annual interest at 8% on the deficient tax.

Director personal liability: Directors may be jointly and severally liable for the tax not deducted or withheld, required remediation under a director disqualification order, and if deliberate, imprisonment for up to 7 years under anti-avoidance law.
