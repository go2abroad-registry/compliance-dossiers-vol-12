Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar: Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax), H.M. Government of Gibraltar — official portal via www.gibraltar.gov.gi; Companies House Gibraltar for entity registration. Bermuda: Office of the Registrar of Companies, H.M. Government of Bermuda (www.gov.bm). UK HMRC (www.gov.uk/hmrc) relevant only where UK VAT registration arises.

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company obtains (a) a Bermuda registration number and Certificate of Exempted Status from the Bermuda Registrar of Companies — no income-tax TIN is issued in Bermuda because exempted companies are tax-exempt, and the annual government-fee receipt and Tax Assurance Certificate constitute the tax identity; and (b) where managed-and-controlled in Gibraltar or maintaining a Gibraltar branch, a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number via the tax registration form filed with the Gibraltar Income Tax Office, accompanied by certified Bermuda incorporation documents, a Gibraltar registered office, local officer details and any applicable Economic Substance Form ESD. TIN issuance normally takes 5-10 working days. If UK taxable supplies arise, Form VAT1 to HMRC issues a UK VAT number and MTD-compatible software is required. Bermuda Corporate Income Tax registration applies only if the entity is a constituent entity of an MNE group with consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (Bermuda exempted-company statutory tax protection under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966; exempted from Bermuda income and capital gains tax; Gibraltar territorial taxation applies only to Gibraltar-source profits or profits attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment; no US-LLC-type pass-through).

Available election forms

(i) Bermuda exempted undertaking election and Tax Assurance Certificate under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966; (ii) Gibraltar non-residence determination through documented management-and-control outside Gibraltar to confine taxation to Gibraltar-source income; (iii) US Form 8832 check-the-box election relevant to FDA 510(k) contractor payments, subject to per-se corporation analysis; (iv) Gibraltar anti-hybrid and OECD BEPS Action 2 compliant filings to prevent deduction/no-inclusion outcomes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

1%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GIP 0 — Gibraltar Purchase Tax has no turnover-based registration threshold; any importation of dutiable goods into Gibraltar triggers the duty irrespective of turnover. UK VAT registration threshold is currently GBP 90,000 taxable turnover, with deregistration at GBP 88,000.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar does not levy VAT or GST and therefore has no export zero-rating mechanism; exports of goods from Gibraltar are outside the scope of Purchase Tax upon evidence of physical departure. For a UK-VAT-registered entity, goods exported from the UK to a third country are zero-rated at 0% under VATA 1994 s.30 provided export occurs within 3 months of supply. FDA 510(k) and CE-marking B2B consultancy services supplied to non-UK business customers are outside the scope of UK VAT, with the customer applying the reverse charge; input VAT on UK and Gibraltar business costs remains recoverable where the entity holds UK VAT registration.

  • Customs Export Declaration — UK Customs Declaration Service (CDS) or Gibraltar Single Administrative Document (SAD) evidencing physical export
  • Commercial invoice and packing list describing the exported goods and linking them to the FDA 510(k) / CE Mark consultancy service deliverables
  • Bill of Lading (sea) or Air Waybill (air) proving the goods left the customs territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103 or bank statement evidencing receipt of export proceeds from the foreign buyer
  • Underlying export contract, purchase order and FDA/CE regulatory engagement agreement connecting the service to the exported goods

None in Gibraltar or Bermuda. There is no continuous transaction control, no real-time fiscal clearance API, and no mandatory digital stamping or QR coding of invoices. The Gibraltar Income Tax Office accepts ordinary commercial invoices. If UK VAT applies, HMRC Making Tax Digital requires digital record-keeping and functional-compatible software for VAT returns, but there is no real-time HMRC invoice clearance.

Gibraltar and UK authorities rigorously audit export evidence and refund claims. Fraudulent zero-rating or export claims attract criminal sanctions under UK CEMA 1979 s.170 — unlimited fine and imprisonment up to 7 years — and civil penalties under FA 2007 Schedule 24 (30%-100% of tax lost) plus interest. Gibraltar Customs Ordinance penalties include forfeiture of goods, treble-value penalties and criminal fines for false declarations. Retrospective clawback of claimed 0% rates and input VAT is enforced where export evidence is missing, forged, or the goods are diverted; documentary retention of at least 6 years is mandatory.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is fully convertible into sterling at a fixed 1:1 parity, and no exchange controls apply to current account or capital account transactions. Profit remittance is unrestricted for non-resident shareholders and no prior authorization is required; however, corporate transfers must comply with the Gibraltar Companies Act record-keeping, beneficial ownership registry disclosure, and anti-money laundering verification standards.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar imposes no statutory export proceeds surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation timeline for export receipts. Medical device regulatory consulting fees may be retained in any freely convertible currency, subject only to the firm's own treasury liquidity management and tax filing obligations.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as banking and fiduciary supervisor, with the Commissioner of Currency (Government of Gibraltar) acting as the note-issuing and currency authority; monetary framework is pegged to the Bank of England's sterling policy.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-to-Medium under the post-Brexit framework. An offshore digital EMI (outside the UK or EEA) cannot rely on Gibraltar passporting rights and must obtain a separate e-money authorization from the GFSC if it solicits Gibraltar-licensed banking clients. Holding operational liquidity in an unauthorized EMI may breach GFSC safeguarding expectations and deprive the account holder of any deposit compensation coverage.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity or working capital is routed as a SWIFT MT103 wire to the entity's Gibraltar bank account (GIP or GBP), citing purpose code 'General operating expenses for professional services' or 'Capital injection of a non-resident corporate shareholder.' The bank verifies the corporate name, the Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO) registry listing, and the economic substance of the transaction. Any amount above the bank's internal threshold must be accompanied by a capital injection resolution, evidence of foreign exchange conversion via the bank's spot desk, and a detailed breakdown of the shareholder loan or equity account.
