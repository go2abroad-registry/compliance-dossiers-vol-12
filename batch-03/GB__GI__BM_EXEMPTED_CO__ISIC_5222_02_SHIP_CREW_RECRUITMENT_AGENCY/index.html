Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (Commissioner of Income Tax); online corporate tax portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company operating from Gibraltar must (1) register as an overseas company with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under the Companies Act 2014, filing certified constitutional documents and a registered office address; (2) apply to the Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) on Form C10, attaching certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, proof of registered office in Gibraltar, details of directors and shareholders, and (if electing non-residence) a declaration of central management and control outside Gibraltar; (3) obtain an EORI number from Gibraltar Customs for any import/export activity; the TRN is issued within 15 working days, and GIT registration is simultaneous for importers.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A Bermuda Exempted Company operating from Gibraltar must (1) register as an overseas company with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under the Companies Act 2014, filing certified constitutional documents and a registered office address; (2) apply to the Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) on Form C10, attaching certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, proof of registered office in Gibraltar, details of directors and shareholders, and (if electing non-residence) a declaration of central management and control outside Gibraltar; (3) obtain an EORI number from Gibraltar Customs for any import/export activity; the TRN is issued within 15 working days, and GIT registration is simultaneous for importers.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no check-the-box election. Tax residence is determined by central management and control. A Bermuda Exempted Company may elect to be treated as non-Gibraltar tax resident by proving its directors and strategic decisions remain in Bermuda, subject to anti-avoidance rules. No hybrid mismatch election is separately available under Gibraltar domestic law, but UK/Gibraltar tie-breaker and UK hybrid mismatch rules may be relevant for funding structures.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GIP 0 – no turnover threshold; registration mandatory for any person importing commercial goods into Gibraltar or supplying goods/services within scope of GIT. Crew recruitment services supplied to non-resident ship owners are outside the territorial scope of GIT.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar applies a territorial exemption (not zero-rating) to exports of goods and certain offshore services. A crew recruitment agency may treat its seafarer supply services as outside the scope of GIT if the vessel is non-Gibraltar flagged and the ship owner is resident/established outside Gibraltar. Eligibility requires contemporaneous evidence that the benefit of the service is received outside Gibraltar; no GIT is chargeable on the fee or the payroll disbursement.

  • Customs Export Declaration (only for physical exports of equipment/provisions)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill (for goods exports)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT credit confirmation for the foreign-sourced fee
  • Crewing services agreement with non-resident shipowner
  • Crew embarkation/disembarkation manifests and seafarer passes

No real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing, or digital stamping regime exists in Gibraltar. Import GIT is declared through the Gibraltar Customs declaration system (GCS) using EORI and standard EU-compliant Single Administrative Document (SAD) procedures; VAT-accounting is manual; no API-mandated invoicing.

Fraudulent claims for GIT export relief are subject to penalty of up to 150% of the tax evaded, interest, and disqualification of directors; retrospective clawback may extend to six years (no time limit in cases of deliberate wrongdoing). Retention of the aforementioned documentation for at least six years is mandatory to satisfy the Income Tax Office and Customs audit scrutiny.

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% headline corporate income tax in Bermuda for an Exempted Company holding an Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 (EUTPA) certificate; 12.5% Gibraltar Corporation Tax applies to profits of a Gibraltar branch or managed-and-controlled presence under the Gibraltar Income Tax (Amendment) Act 2011. Sector rates of 14.5% (banking, insurance, utilities) and 19.25% (retail, wholesale, property development) do not apply to ISIC 5222 ship crew recruitment.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% in Bermuda and 0% in Gibraltar. A Bermuda Exempted Company may distribute dividends to non-resident shareholders free of Bermuda withholding tax, and the Gibraltar branch remittance to the Bermuda parent is likewise subject to no Gibraltar dividend withholding tax, provided dividends are declared from after-tax Gibraltar profits and documented in board minutes.

Form CT must be filed no later than 12 months after the end of the accounting period; self-assessed corporation tax is payable in two equal instalments, the first within 6 months of the accounting period end and the balance within 12 months.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 0.00. Contemporaneous transfer-pricing documentation, including a Local File and, where the MNE group exceeds EUR 750 million consolidated revenue, a Gibraltar Country-by-Country Report notification, is mandatory for every Gibraltar tax-resident entity engaged in controlled transactions. For an ISIC 5222 crew-management agency, intra-group payroll recharges, crew-fee settlements, and management charges above GIP 500,000 annually must be supported by an OECD-compliant transfer-pricing file.

Economic substance scope: Applicable in both layers. Bermuda: the Exempted Company must satisfy the Economic Substance (International Tax Compliance) Act 2018 unless it is tax-resident in Gibraltar, in which case annual filing relief is available. Gibraltar: the Income Tax Act (Economic Substance) Guidelines treat crew recruitment and vessel crew administration as a shipping-support relevant activity, requiring the Gibraltar operation to maintain a licensed office, employ maritime staffing professionals such as marine superintendents, crewing officers and payroll officers, and perform the core income-generating activities of sourcing, vetting, contracting, dispatch and payroll disbursement in Gibraltar. A letterbox or bare-invoice operation fails both regimes.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate tax evasion on Gibraltar branch profits

Statutory sanction: Under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010: penalty up to 100% of the tax shortfall, daily default surcharges, statutory interest, criminal fines up to GIP 100,000 per return, and forfeiture of the aircraft? No, forfeiture of the vessels involved in the payroll scheme.

Director personal liability: A director who knowingly signs a false return or omits a material revenue line is personally liable for the civil penalty, faces up to 3 years imprisonment, and may be subjected to a travel ban and disqualification from acting as a Gibraltar company director.
