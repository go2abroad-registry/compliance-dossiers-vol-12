Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is prescribed under Bermuda law for an exempted company; nominal issued capital of one ordinary share of US$1.00 is sufficient. No escrow deposit or blocked capital account is required at incorporation. Any subsequent regulated lending or deposit-taking activity would trigger separate prudential licensing and capital/escrow requirements.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification: the tenant of any Gibraltar office must apply to the Gibraltar Fire and Rescue Service for a fire-safety certificate and maintain a fire-risk assessment. Occupational clearance: a workplace risk assessment and fire drill log must be kept in accordance with Gibraltar’s Health and Safety at Work Act; no heavy industrial civil-defence clearance is required.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office (ITO), HM Government of Gibraltar, administered by the Commissioner of Income Tax under the Income Tax Act 2010; official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi (Tax e-Services). Supporting authorities: Gibraltar Registrar of Companies (Companies Act 2014); Gibraltar Customs & Excise Department; Bermuda Registrar of Companies (home-jurisdiction registration of the Bermuda Exempted Co); UK HMRC only if a UK permanent establishment or UK VAT registration materialises.

E-government filing portal

Step 1 - Register the Bermuda-incorporated SPV as an overseas company with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies and obtain the Gibraltar Company Number. Step 2 - Register with the Gibraltar ITO under the Income Tax Act 2010 to obtain the Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (G-TIN); demonstrate central management and control to define territorial scope; no VAT ID exists in Gibraltar. Step 3 - Open a customs/import-export account with Gibraltar Customs & Excise only if physical goods are traded. Step 4 - Preserve Bermuda exempt status by filing the annual exempted-company application and paying the Bermuda annual government fee. Step 5 - If a UK taxable presence arises, register separately with HMRC for UK Corporation Tax and UK VAT.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Available election forms

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar Non-VAT regime - Import Duties, Excise Duties and limited statutory service taxes; no VAT/GST/Sales-and-Use-Tax (Gibraltar lies outside the EU VAT area)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable: Gibraltar levies no VAT/GST, so no economic turnover threshold triggers indirect-tax registration. All companies must register for direct tax with the ITO regardless of turnover; importers/exporters of dutiable goods must obtain a customs account in Gibraltar Pounds (GIP) without a monetary threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable as a VAT zero-rating concept because Gibraltar imposes no VAT/GST. Exports of goods from Gibraltar are outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect taxation and carry no VAT refund mechanism. Customs-duty relief is available for goods consigned to a foreign destination with a valid export declaration. The PE buyout SPV (ISIC 6499) deals predominantly in shares, debt, and fund flows, which are entirely outside the Gibraltar indirect tax base.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current- and capital-account convertibility across GIP, GBP, EUR, and USD. No GFSC, Bank of England, or BMA approval is required for dividend upstreaming, capital distributions, or buyout disbursements. Profit repatriation from Gibraltar to Bermuda or to UK limited partners is unrestricted, subject only to the Gibraltar Companies Act 2014 solvency test and the Bermuda Economic Substance (ES) filing demonstrating a non-conduit economic presence. Multi-currency SPV accounts may be opened and operated without any exchange-control authorisation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable: the ISIC 6499 PE-buyout SPV generates no trading export proceeds. Neither Gibraltar nor Bermuda imposes surrender, conversion, or retention deadlines on inbound receipts or disposal proceeds; such funds may be held indefinitely in the original currency. Only standard GFSC suspicious-transaction and threshold reporting duties apply.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the statutory banking and AML/CFT regulator under the Financial Services Act 2019. Gibraltar has no central bank; the Bank of England anchors the Sterling monetary zone, and the Government of Gibraltar issues Gibraltar Pound (GIP) banknotes via the Currency Commission at a statutory 1:1 GBP parity. For the Bermuda parent, the Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) exercises prudential and regulatory oversight but imposes no exchange controls.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low-to-Medium feasibility. Gibraltar has no domestic EMI deposit-taking licence roster and no post-Brexit passporting equivalence for UK FCA or EU EBA e-money firms. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Each limited partner remits via SWIFT MT103 to the SPV's Gibraltar account in GBP, USD, or EUR. The payment must be flagged as a capital subscription with field 70 narrative 'PE BUYOUT SPV - CAPITAL CALL NO. [X] - [LP NAME]', and the beneficiary bank may map the transaction to BPM6 category 'direct investment - equity capital'. Drawdown notices signed by the GP must accompany contributions above EUR 50,000. USD flows clear via a US correspondent (JPMorgan, Citi, or Bank of New York) to the bank's USD nostro; EUR flows above EUR 100,000 require TARGET2/SEPA same-day value. Funds remain in the operational account until the SPV issues a formal equity drawdown certificate; no commingling with operating expenses is permitted.Share-purchase closings settle via SWIFT MT103, with GBP-denominated closings using CHAPS for same-day completion, and remittance narrative 'ACQUISITION CONSIDERATION - [TARGET NAME] - SPA CLOSING'. Syndicated-loan drawdowns are initiated by the facility agent via MT103/MT202 cover payments, with the Gibraltar bank as receiving bank; funds are released only against the SPV's dual-signature mandate and the executed SPA. Escrow release mechanics are available for conditional payments. Commercial Letters of Credit (SWIFT MT700) are reserved for deferred-consideration or warranty escrow structures only, not as the primary M&A settlement rail. All outbound wires require dual-signature authorisation (two directors, or director plus CFO) and daily facility limits aligned to the capital-call schedule.Vanilla FX forwards (up to 24-month tenor) under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement or short-form FX Master Confirmation executed with the Gibraltar bank or its London booking centre; used to hedge GBP/USD or GBP/EUR exposure between capital-call drawdown and acquisition close.Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDFs) for restricted-currency target jurisdictions (CNH, INR, BRL, TRY) where no deliverable onshore market exists for the Bermuda SPV; settled in USD or GBP via the bank's offshore NDF desk, with the underlying acquisition contract retained as hedge documentation.Cross-currency swaps (GBP/USD and EUR/USD) for medium-term hedging of leveraged-loan or bridge-facility currency mismatches; executed under an ISDA/CSA credit-support annex and cleared through LCH, or retained internally where below clearing threshold.FX options (buy-side only, no short options) documented under ISDA 2002; permitted within the SPV treasury policy and subject to GFSC professional-client conduct rules governing derivative transactions.Currency futures through a UK- or EU-authorised futures commission merchant for known-dated capital-call hedging, subject to additional clearing-member KYC onboarding, margin collateral, and position-limit documentation.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 aggregate value of controlled transactions in a financial year triggers mandatory preparation of a Gibraltar Local File. A Master File is required when the worldwide group consolidated revenue exceeds GIP 750,000,000. The Bermuda Exempted Company is treated as a tax-haven counterparty, so any controlled transaction with it is subject to enhanced scrutiny even below the monetary threshold.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance (Companies and Limited Partnerships) Act 2020 applies because the SPV is a company resident in Gibraltar carrying on a relevant 'holding business'. It must satisfy the full or reduced substance test by having a fixed premises in Gibraltar, local employees, local board meetings, adequate expenditure, and corporate records kept in Gibraltar. The SPV must file an annual economic substance return with its tax return and retain all evidence for six years; failure to do so rebuts treaty benefits and triggers immediate sanctions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Deliberate Incorrect Returns

Statutory sanction: 100% tax-geared penalty on the additional liability, interest of up to 2% per month on unpaid tax, and upon criminal conviction an unlimited fine and imprisonment of up to 7 years; the court may also issue a confiscation order

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly signed or caused the false return are jointly personally liable for civil restitution of tax lost; travel bans, asset freezing orders, director disqualification, and imprisonment are available.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Fixed financial penalty of up to GIP 10,000 per offence plus GIP 100 per day for continuing non-compliance; the GRS may strike the company off the Register of Companies, revoke any tax status, and disallow treaty benefits

Director personal liability: Directors and ultimate shareholders are jointly and severally liable for the penalties and unpaid notional tax; courts may impose travel restrictions, disqualification orders, and forced liquidation of the SPV.
Money Laundering and Concealment of Beneficial Ownership

Statutory sanction: AML/CFT civil penalties of up to GIP 1,000,000, daily fines for information failures, and criminal prosecution for money laundering punishable upon indictment by imprisonment of up to 14 years and an unlimited fine

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and shareholders face criminal liability, personal confiscation, asset restraint, and disqualification; travel bans and international extradition may be invoked under mutual legal assistance arrangements.
Transfer Pricing, Thin Capitalisation, and Earnings Stripping

Statutory sanction: The GRS will adjust taxable profits, disallow the excessive interest or fee deduction, recharacterise surplus debt as equity, impose interest and a tax-geared penalty of up to 100%, and may prosecute if false documentation is supplied

Director personal liability: Finance directors and group treasurers are personally liable for penalties where the incorrect return is signed with knowledge of the non-arm's-length pricing; the court may require contribution and issue travel bans pending payment.
Dividend Stripping and Treaty Abuse

Statutory sanction: Application of Gibraltar GAAR and DTA limitation of benefits clauses; the claimed relief is denied, underpaid tax is collected with interest, and a penalty of up to 100% of the denied benefit may be imposed

Director personal liability: Directors who structured or approved the abusive scheme are personally liable for restitution to the revenue; criminal courts may impose imprisonment, community service, or a travel ban and require personal confiscation of the tax advantage obtained.
