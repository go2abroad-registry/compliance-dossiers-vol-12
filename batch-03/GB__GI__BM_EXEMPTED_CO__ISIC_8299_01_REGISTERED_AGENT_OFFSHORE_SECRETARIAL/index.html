Competent corporate registry

Governing statute

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

The Companies Act 2014 imposes no statutory minimum paid-up capital for a private company limited by shares; a single share of GBP 0.01 may be issued and no escrow or deposit-account rule applies. The GFSC licensing conditions for a Registered Agent and Company Manager nevertheless require adequate financial resources, expressed as issued capital plus regulatory reserves determined in the GFSC authorization letter on a risk-based basis; such capital is not escrowed but must remain unimpaired.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar - Commissioner of Income Tax (Income Tax Office, Duke of Kent House, Cathedral Square, Gibraltar) is the competent tax authority for all Gibraltar-sourced profits; corporate and external-company registration is administered by the Gibraltar Companies Registry (Registry of Companies). Tax filings and e-services are accessible through the official HM Government of Gibraltar taxation portal at www.gibraltar.gov.gi.

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company (BM Exempted Co) that establishes a place of business in Gibraltar must first register with the Gibraltar Companies Registry as an external/overseas company under the Gibraltar Companies Act, delivering its Bermuda certificate of incorporation, certificate of exempted status, constitutional documents, Gibraltar registered-office details, and particulars of its licensed registered agent. On commencement of any Gibraltar taxable activity, the company files the prescribed registration application with the Commissioner of Income Tax and receives a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN); no VAT identification number is obtainable because Gibraltar imposes no VAT and its GST is statutorily zero. The company must also obtain a Business Registration Certificate from the Director of Business Registration for the ISIC 8299 corporate-secretarial trade (medium-risk AML category) before providing registered-agent services.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone - the Bermuda Exempted Company is exempt from Bermuda income, capital gains, and withholding tax under the Companies Act 1981 and the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 (tax assurance protection generally secured until 2035); Gibraltar applies a territorial charging rule, so profits not accrued in or derived from Gibraltar remain outside Gibraltar tax, while Gibraltar-source profits of the branch are assessable at the flat corporate rate despite the Bermuda exempt status.

Available election forms

No check-the-box election or statutory hybrid-mismatch election is available in Bermuda or Gibraltar. Available elections include: (i) the Bermuda Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act assurance election fixing the exemption milestone; (ii) under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act, an election to be charged on the territorial basis as a non-resident company trading through a Gibraltar permanent establishment, with qualifying pre-2011 companies retaining grandfathered coverage under the Co-operation Agreement safeguards; and (iii) where UK persons hold the company, UK CFC and hybrid-mismatch rules may reclassify the structure, so a full OECD BEPS-style analysis must accompany the election to avoid unexpected UK apportionment.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar Goods and Services Tax (GST) at a statutory zero rate plus customs and excise duty; no VAT applies because Gibraltar is a separate British Overseas Territory outside the UK VAT system and the EU Customs Union.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No economic turnover threshold applies because the Gibraltar GST rate is fixed at 0%; all persons carrying on business in Gibraltar must nonetheless register with the Director of Business Registration under the Business Names Registration Act (nil turnover threshold). Where goods are imported or exported, the business must hold a valid UK or Gibraltar EORI number, as applicable, for customs declarations.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Because Gibraltar GST is 0% on all supplies, the offshore registered-agent and company-secretarial services (ISIC 8299) supplied to non-resident clients are automatically effective at 0% with no separate export election. To preserve the zero-rate and rebut any Gibraltar-source income assertion, the service provider must evidence non-resident billing addresses, place-of-performance of services outside Gibraltar, and the absence of Gibraltar real estate, payroll, or management activity. For goods, a physical export from Gibraltar obtains relief from customs and excise duty upon lodgement of the export declaration; no GST refund claim is available because zero-rating leaves no residual input tax to recover.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

Gibraltar has no real-time tax clearance, continuous-transaction-control, e-invoicing, digital stamping, or API-based fiscal device mandate. The registered agent must, however, issue compliant invoices evidencing the zero-rated or territorial nature of offshore secretarial fees and retain books, records, and invoices for at least six years (ten years under AML/CFT rules administered by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission).

Misuse of the exemption or zero-rated treatment attracts rigorous audit scrutiny by the Commissioner of Income Tax and the Gibraltar Customs; assessments may be reopened and tax clawed back with statutory interest, and fraudulent evasion or false declarations expose the company and its registered agent to financial penalties, forfeiture of goods, licence suspension or revocation by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission, and criminal prosecution. Where UK-connected investors are present, HMRC may additionally assert UK CFC or diverted-profits charges if the Gibraltar/Bermuda structure creates a tax outcome contrary to UK anti-avoidance legislation.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Bermudian dollar is pegged at 1:1 to the United States dollar and is fully convertible for current-account and capital-account transactions; the Gibraltar pound is freely convertible to pound sterling at parity with no exchange-control authorisation required. Profit remittances from Gibraltar to Bermuda or any OECD jurisdiction are unrestricted, but the company must maintain demonstrable economic substance in its actual place of management to avoid challenge under Gibraltar income tax, UK controlled-foreign-company rules, and general anti-abuse doctrines. Dividend and service-fee remittances are generally free of Gibraltar withholding tax, while the BMA expects an exempted company to file annual returns and maintain a registered office in Bermuda.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender requirement, mandatory conversion ratio, or fixed timeline exists for export proceeds in either Gibraltar or Bermuda. An enterprise may retain foreign-currency export receipts in designated foreign-currency bank accounts indefinitely, and conversion into local currency is commercially driven, not legally compelled. The applicable mandatory conversion ratio is 0%, meaning no portion of export proceeds is required to be surrendered by law; nevertheless, all receipts exceeding the equivalent of USD 10,000 or EUR 10,000 must be captured in the transaction-monitoring records and reported under Gibraltar AML/CTF and Bermuda Anti-Money Laundering (Financial Institutions) Regulations.

Central bank supervision: Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) is the primary central bank and exchange-control authority for the Bermuda Exempted Company; Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) regulates banks and licensed financial-services providers in Gibraltar; HM Treasury and the Bank of England are the monetary anchor for the Gibraltar pound sterling currency board, which issues Gibraltar banknotes at a fixed 1:1 parity to the British pound.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore digital EMI banking is feasible only as a supplementary collection rail, not as a principal treasury core. FCA-authorised e-money institutions in the United Kingdom and GFSC/EEA-authorised e-money firms may be accepted for payment routing, but unlicensed offshore EMIs will be rejected because they are not recognised as credit institutions for the custody of client money and cannot clear commercial CAP or other treasury payments through Gibraltar. A Bermuda Exempted Company with little local substance will be categorised as medium-high risk by digital EMIs, requiring enhanced KYC and, in many cases, a funded operational account at a licensed bank before the EMI account is opened. The legal constraint is that Gibraltar statutory registered-agent services must hold client monies only with a licensed financial institution; therefore the use of offshore EMI accounts must be limited to non-client operating balances.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity or subordinated-loan capital from the Bermuda parent or external investors should be routed as a SWIFT MT103 wire from the remitting bank to the Gibraltar beneficiary bank using the beneficiary BIC and a Gibraltar-issued IBAN. Field 70 of the MT103 must clearly state 'Capital Contribution / Subscription for Ordinary Shares of a Bermuda Exempted Company' and include the client reference for straight-through processing. The bank will require the purpose code for equity capital, typically 'C510' under international balance-of-payments classifications or the bank's internal 'Capital Injection' code, together with a certified board resolution approving the issue of shares, an updated register of members, the certificate of incorporation, a CRS/FATCA self-certification, and a signed source-of-funds declaration. The funds should be credited to a capital escrow or operational account and held in the receipt currency until the bank completes sanctions and conflict-of-interest screening; a clean compliance clearance must be obtained before any FX conversion or outbound disbursement is executed.Trade payments and business disbursements should be settled through counter-to-counter SWIFT MT103 messages for non-documentary payments, while high-value or contractually conditional trade settlements should be executed by an irrevocable Commercial Letter of Credit issued via SWIFT MT700/MT720. For euro-denominated disbursements, the transaction may use the Single Euro Payments Area (SEPA) clearing rail if both banks participate in SEPA; for sterling-denominated payments, CHAPS or Faster Payments is the domestic clearing rail; for US dollars, standard SWIFT correspondent banking through a US clearing network is required. Each disbursement must be supported by an original invoice, bill of lading or service-proving evidence, purchase order, packing list, and, where applicable, inspection certificate. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% (standard Gibraltar corporate income tax rate, effective for accounting periods commencing on or after 1 January 2022; previously 10%). The tax is territorial: charge is imposed only on profits accruing in or derived from Gibraltar.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Gibraltar imposes no statutory withholding tax on dividend distributions, regardless of shareholder residence. A Bermuda exempted company may therefore remit dividends, service profits, or capital returns to its ultimate shareholders free of Gibraltar withholding, provided the underlying profits were properly taxed in Gibraltar or are exempt foreign-source profits.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The return must be delivered within 9 months after the end of the accounting period (e.g., a 31 December year-end is due on or before 30 September of the following year); the Commissioner may grant extensions, and a return demanded by notice must be filed within 21 days of the notice unless an extended time is directed.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GBP: Gibraltar has not enacted a statutory monetary threshold, but the Commissioner of Income Tax administratively requires contemporaneous Local File documentation where associated-enterprise transactions (including management-fee or expense recharges to the Bermuda head office) equal or exceed GBP 1,000,000 per accounting period; a Master File is expected where worldwide group consolidated turnover exceeds GBP 50,000,000. All intra-group transactions must comply with the arm's length principle under the Income Tax Act 2010, applied per the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar has not enacted an Economic Substance Regulations statute parallel to Caribbean ESR regimes; substance is enforced instead through the Financial Services Act 2019 licensing of the registered-agent and company-management activity, which requires a licensed physical office, a resident compliance officer, a resident MLRO, and real staff in Gibraltar, and through OECD BEPS Action 5 and EU listing criteria. A Bermuda exempted company cannot rely on its exempted character to avoid Gibraltar permanent establishment attribution; a registered-agent office with nominal employees and no local decision-making will be recharacterized as a letterbox, stripped of treaty access, and assessed on attributable Gibraltar-source profits.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Nil Return

Statutory sanction: Civil penalties of up to 100% of the tax evaded plus interest at 8% per annum and a 10% late-payment surcharge; on summary conviction, a fine of up to GBP 10,000 and imprisonment up to 3 years; on indictment, an unlimited fine and imprisonment up to 7 years, together with forfeiture of the illicit fee income.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false returns are jointly and severally liable for the underpaid tax and penalties; the court may issue director disqualification orders of up to 15 years, asset freezes, travel bans, and custodial sentences for fraudulent trading.
Unlicensed Corporate Service Provider / Registered Agent Activity

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of up to GBP 50,000 per contravention, daily default penalties of GBP 1,000, prohibition orders, revocation of the agent licence, and compulsory strike-off of the Gibraltar branch; aggravated breaches are prosecuted as criminal offences with fines and imprisonment up to 2 years.

Director personal liability: Directors and the compliance officer face personal fines and disqualification, prohibition orders barring them from acting as company managers, personal liability for client restitution, and referral for UK travel bans in cross-border enforcement.
