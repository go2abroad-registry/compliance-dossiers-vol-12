Available election forms

Gibraltar has no check-the-box entity classification election; legal-form classification controls. Available elections and claims relevant to this structure: (a) Gibraltar Participation Exemption election to exempt qualifying dividends and capital gains on qualifying shareholdings; (b) election to allocate attributable profits to a Gibraltar branch or agency on an arm's-length basis rather than a broader worldwide assessment; (c) application for an advance clearance or ruling from the Commissioner of Income Tax on permanent establishment threshold, economic substance, and territorial scope; (d) hybrid mismatch awareness and elections under UK CTA 2010 Part 6A and corresponding Gibraltar provisions; (e) for B2C app-store sales, no indirect tax election is available because Apple and Google act as deemed suppliers for VAT collection.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No_General_Indirect_Tax (Gibraltar imposes no VAT, GST, or retail sales tax; indirect levies are limited to customs and import duties and excise duties under the Customs Tariff (Gibraltar) Order and the Excise Duty Ordinance)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - Gibraltar has no VAT regime and therefore no VAT registration turnover threshold. Customs registration is required for any importer or exporter of goods in Gibraltar regardless of turnover. A non-resident supplier of digital voice-streaming services has no Gibraltar indirect tax registration obligation; EU and UK consumer VAT is administered through the app-store deemed-supplier mechanism or, where applicable, through an EU Non-Union OSS registration in an EU Member State.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar has no VAT zero-rating regime, so no 0% export VAT certificate or input VAT refund mechanism exists in Gibraltar. The voice-streaming supply is intangible: when sold to end users through Apple App Store (Apple IAP) and Google Play Billing, Apple and Google are the deemed suppliers for indirect tax purposes and are accountable for EU/UK VAT or other digital taxes. The Bermuda Exempted Company's contractual supply to those platforms is a B2B service outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect tax. Any physical goods exported from Gibraltar would remain subject to customs declaration but would not attract Gibraltar VAT.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound is fully convertible at parity with UK sterling. Current-account and capital-account convertibility are unrestricted under Gibraltar and relevant UK law, and there are no exchange-control permits or profit-repatriation approval gates governing the Bermuda Exempted Company. Dividend and capital remittances may be made freely in GIP, GBP, EUR, or USD, subject only to bank-level source-of-funds verification, sanctions screening, and documentary evidence that the payment is consistent with Bermuda corporate law and the entity's tax-exempt status. Gibraltar operates no withholding-tax barrier on dividends paid to non-resident shareholders under prevailing territorial income tax principles.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender timeline or mandatory conversion ratio applies in Gibraltar. Export receipts from the voice-streaming application, including Apple IAP and Google Play settlement flows, may be retained in USD, EUR, GBP, or GIP indefinitely. The only administrative obligations are standard AML/CTF transaction reporting, accurate record-keeping for economic substance filings, and preservation of merchant-settlement and digital royalty ledgers for tax transparency and audit purposes.

Central bank supervision: The monetary authority for Gibraltar is the Government of Gibraltar acting through the Gibraltar Currency Commissioner, which issues the Gibraltar pound (GIP) on a one-to-one backing by Bank of England banknotes; prudential banking regulation and foreign-exchange licensing are administered by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (FSC). There is no autonomous Gibraltar central bank, and Bank of England monetary policy forms the effective anchor for the Gibraltar pound.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium-to-Low as a primary treasury rail; high as a settlement overlay only. Gibraltar does not currently passport UK/EEA EMI licences, and pure-play fintech electronic money institutions cannot provide the same safeguarding certainty, FX hedging capacity, or FSC regulatory protection as a licensed Gibraltar bank. The Bermuda Exempted Company may use an authorised UK/EEA EMI as a short-term collection sweep for marketplace channels, but must maintain a substantive Gibraltar or UK clearing account and must not hold core treasury liquidity in an offshore EMI.

Stage 06

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% (Gibraltar standard); 0% effective for a Bermuda Exempted Company preserving non-resident status with no Gibraltar-source profits.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Gibraltar imposes no dividend withholding tax on distributions to a Bermuda parent; Bermuda levies no tax on dividends received.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Gibraltar: return due within nine months of the year of assessment end (e.g., YA ending 31 December by 30 September next year). Bermuda: ESR declaration due within six months of the financial year-end.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GBP 1,000,000 of annual related-party transaction value (Local File threshold); Master File and Country-by-Country report where consolidated group revenue exceeds GBP 50,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar ESR 2019 (and Bermuda ESR 2018) apply to relevant activities; a voice streaming app is treated as a digital services / distribution and service centre / IP exploitation activity, requiring: (i) local board meetings with strategic decisions; (ii) qualified employees performing core income-generating activities; (iii) physical premises in the jurisdiction; and (iv) adequate annual operating expenditure. Annual ESR declaration and a local representative are mandatory; non-compliance is a criminal/revenue offence.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Dishonest Filing

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010: tax-geared penalty up to 100% of the understated tax, interest at 8%, and criminal fine with imprisonment up to 7 years for fraudulent evasion. Bermuda: no income tax, but the UK Criminal Finances Act 2017 offence of failure to prevent facilitation of tax evasion applies to the Bermuda entity's UK/Gibraltar operations (unlimited fine).

Director personal liability: Directors face personal criminal liability, civil restitution, disqualification as directors (up to 15 years), asset freezing and travel bans; a director who signed a false CT-1 is jointly and severally liable for the additional tax and penalties.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing / Inadequate Documentation

Statutory sanction: GITO may adjust profits to arm's length, imposing additional 12.5% tax, 100% penalty for deliberate errors, and statutory interest; failure to produce documentation attracts a separate documentation penalty and, under ESR, fines up to GBP 100,000 per relevant activity.

Director personal liability: Directors who approved the non-arm's-length royalty face joint and several liability for the adjusted tax; disqualification orders and personal monetary penalties for knowing participation; overseas travel bans pending resolution.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Bermuda ESR: initial fine BD$10,000, subsequent-year fine BD$100,000, potential strike-off and referral to the Registrar for dissolution. Gibraltar ESR: fine up to GBP 100,000 per activity; profits can be re-characterised and attributed to the beneficial owner under targeted anti-avoidance rules; the company may be struck off.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for corporate fines where failure is attributable to their neglect; personal civil restitution, disqualification for up to 15 years, travel bans, and non-issuance of tax residence certificates may prevent the directors from leaving the jurisdiction.
AML/CFT Breach and Beneficial Ownership Non-Disclosure

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and Companies Act 2014: fine up to GBP 2,000,000 per offence and imprisonment up to 14 years for money laundering; confiscation orders; regulatory censure and revocation of the EMI banking relationship; the Financial Services Commission may also impose public statement penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors are individually prosecutable for consenting to or facilitating ML, face personal confiscation of benefits, disqualification, travel bans, and prohibition from acting as a 'fit and proper' officer of any regulated payment institution.
Fraudulent Trading and Director Duty Breach

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar Companies Act 2014 (fraudulent trading): court order for personal contribution to the company's assets; criminal conviction attracts a fine and imprisonment up to 2 years (summary) or 10 years (indictment); director disqualification for up to 15 years.

Director personal liability: Personal liability for all unpaid corporate obligations and taxes; civil restitution orders requiring compensation to creditors and the Revenue; travel bans and contingent custodial sentences for wilful default in filing.
