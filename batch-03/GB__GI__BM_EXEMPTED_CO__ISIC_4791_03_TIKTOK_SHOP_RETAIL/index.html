Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office; Gibraltar Customs Department (Customs); portal: www.taxation.gi (Income Tax) and www.gibraltar.gov.gi/customs

E-government filing portal

A foreign-incorporated entity, including a Bermuda Exempted Company, is required to obtain a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) only if it becomes tax resident in Gibraltar under the 'central management and control' test or receives Gibraltar-source income attributable to a permanent establishment (PE). Application is made to the Income Tax Office on the relevant registration form, attaching (i) certified copy of the Bermuda Certificate of Incorporation and Certificate of Exemption; (ii) constitutional documents (Memorandum and Articles of Association); (iii) board minutes evidencing offshore management and control; (iv) details of any Gibraltar representative or agent; and (v) estimated Gibraltar-source profit statement. Non-resident entities without a PE do not require income-tax registration. If the Bermuda Exempted Company maintains an office or registers as an overseas company in Gibraltar, it must also register with the Gibraltar Companies Registry. For import/export of physical goods, the entity must register with the Gibraltar Customs Department and obtain a Customs Economic Operator Registration and Identification (EORI) code.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone – the Bermuda Exempted Company is tax-exempt in Bermuda; for Gibraltar income-tax purposes, a non-resident foreign entity without a Gibraltar PE remains outside the charge to Gibraltar tax. If it were to become tax resident or establish a PE, Gibraltar would tax the attributable profits at the standard 10% corporate tax rate.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid entity election exists under Gibraltar law. Entity classification follows the Income Tax Act (Gibraltar) and applicable domestic tax rules, with taxability determined by central management and control and PE analysis rather than by election. A Bermuda Exempted Company seeking zero Gibraltar tax should ensure no Gibraltar management and control, maintain offshore board meetings and decisions, and avoid creating a Gibraltar PE. OECD hybrid mismatch rules are recognised through the UK-Gibraltar tax cooperation framework, but no positive election mechanism is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None – Gibraltar does not levy VAT, GST, Sales and Use Tax, or any general consumption tax. Indirect taxation is limited to customs duties, excise duties, and import-related charges administered by the Gibraltar Customs Department.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable – there is no VAT registration threshold or VAT number. For physical import/export activity, an importer or exporter must register with the Gibraltar Customs Department and obtain a Customs EORI code. No specific turnover threshold exists for customs registration; customs duty is assessed on the value of each imported consignment.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable – Gibraltar has no VAT zero-rating regime because no VAT/GST is levied on Gibraltar supplies or exports. A zero-rating claim, refund mechanism, or export VAT relief does not arise in Gibraltar. If the Bermuda Exempted Company exports goods from the United Kingdom mainland, the UK VAT zero-rating may apply, provided the entity is registered for UK VAT, retains valid export evidence, and satisfies HMRC time limits; however, Gibraltar is outside the UK VAT territorial area and this stage is Gibraltar-specific.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration lodged via GECS
  • Commercial invoice / equivalent proof of sale
  • Packing list / cargo manifest
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (transport document)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank wire evidence (where required to prove export proceeds repatriation under AML/FX procedures; Gibraltar maintains no exchange controls)

No real-time fiscal clearance, mandatory e-invoicing, or digital stamping regime applies to TikTok Shop retail sales. For physical imports and exports, Gibraltar operates the Gibraltar Electronic Clearance System (GECS) for lodging customs declarations, calculating duties, and facilitating clearance. There is no API-based tax clearance requirement; commercial invoices must be issued for record-keeping and customs purposes but are not subject to electronic fiscal stamping.

There is no VAT clawback in Gibraltar because no VAT applies. Gibraltar Customs may audit export declarations for up to six years; false statements, misdeclared goods, or abuse of customs reliefs can result in assessments for unpaid duties, interest, civil penalties up to 100% of the duty under the Customs Ordinance, and criminal sanctions in cases of fraudulent evasion. If UK VAT zero-rating is invoked in parallel, HMRC can raise UK VAT assessments, impose penalties of up to 100% of the VAT, and require full repayment of incorrectly claimed refunds; intentional fraud may lead to prosecution.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Current-account and capital-account convertibility is unrestricted. The GIP trades at par with GBP; commercial banks may exchange GIP and GBP freely, and hard currencies such as USD and EUR can be held without quota, maturity, or central-bank approval limitations. No exchange-control permission is required for dividend/profit repatriation, repayment of intra-group loans, or capital distributions to the Bermuda parent or ultimate shareholders; funds may be routed directly from a GFSC-authorised bank to foreign banks. The only friction is normal correspondent-bank KYC, anti-money-laundering (AML), and sanctions screening.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory surrender, conversion, or repatriation timeline for export proceeds in Gibraltar. All amounts earned from TikTok Shop sales may be retained in any currency, held offshore, or converted at commercial bank rates. The compliance obligation is limited to retaining an audit trail of the underlying commercial export transaction and reporting suspicious or unusual activity to the GFSC Financial Intelligence Unit under the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act 2015. No percentage of export receipts must be converted into GIP or GBP.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar has no indigenous central bank. The regulatory authority for banking and foreign-exchange institutions is the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC), operating under the Financial Services Act 2019 and the Money Laundering Regulations 2019. Gibraltar pound (GIP) notes are issued by HM Government of Gibraltar and are pegged at a 1:1 exchange rate to pound sterling; the Bank of England provides the ultimate sterling settlement and RTGS infrastructure for systemic liquidity.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

High viability only where the EMI is itself authorised under Gibraltar's Financial Services Act 2019 / Electronic Money Act 2011. Post-Brexit, UK FCA-licensed or EEA-passported electronic money institutions no longer have automatic access to Gibraltar; offshore digital EMIs such as Wise, Airwallex, or Revolut may be used as payment-service gateways through a Gibraltar-licensed partner or as a non-core disbursement account. Legal constraints: EMIs may not grant credit or take traditional client deposits; TikTok Shop payouts accumulated in an EMI must be auto-swept daily to a GFSC-licensed bank to ensure safeguarding compliance, capital protection, and insolvency-remote treasury treatment.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Gibraltar law does not prescribe a statutory monetary threshold for mandatory Local File or Master File preparation. However, in alignment with BEPS Action 13, a Gibraltar entity should maintain a full transfer pricing documentation file whenever aggregate controlled transactions exceed GIP 500,000 per annum, and Country-by-Country Reporting is required for multinational groups with consolidated revenue of EUR 750,000,000 or more.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar itself does not maintain a separate Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) code. Nevertheless, because the underlying entity is a Bermuda Exempted Company, Bermuda's Economic Substance Regulations 2020 apply. A TikTok Shop retail and live-commerce operation constitutes a 'distribution and service center' and, at group-assistance level, possibly a 'headquarters' regime. The Bermuda exempted company must show adequate physical presence, employees, and local board or management in Bermuda, or alternatively demonstrate that it is tax resident in a qualifying jurisdiction such as Gibraltar with substantive activities, payroll, and locally managed operations there. Failing such proof, a nil-tax foreign exempt company may be treated as a shell and subjected to enforcement under both Bermuda ESR and Gibraltar anti-avoidance rules.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Revenue Declaration

Statutory sanction: Under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010, civil tax-geared penalties can reach 100% of the evaded tax plus default interest. Criminal prosecution on indictment is punishable by a fine of up to triple the evaded tax and imprisonment for up to five years; summary conviction carries imprisonment of up to twelve months.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly cause or agree to the company's tax evasion are jointly and severally liable for restitution of evaded tax and penalties. Personal consequences include director disqualification for up to fifteen years, travel bans pending criminal proceedings, and custodial sentences where the director is personally culpable.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Bermuda's Economic Substance Regulations impose escalating fines from BMD 50,000 to BMD 100,000 per relevant entity and provide for the striking off of the company from the Bermuda Registry. Gibraltar may also raise a deemed assessment and strike off the company under the Gibraltar Companies Act for non-filing or sham trading.

Director personal liability: Directors risk disqualification and personal liability if they fail to establish or disclose economic substance. The corporate veil is pierced on evidence of sham or illicit incorporation; directors may be ordered to pay restitution, subjected to travel bans, and barred from holding directorships in other international structures.
AML / Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing

Statutory sanction: Under the Gibraltar Crime (Money Laundering and Proceeds) Ordinance, offences are punishable on indictment by imprisonment up to fourteen years and an unlimited fine. Payment institutions involved in the scheme may have their licences revoked, and the proceeds of unlawful activity are subject to freezing, seizure, and confiscation orders.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers are treated as principal officers; they face separate criminal prosecution under anti-money-laundering law, confiscation orders covering proceeds, travel bans pending forfeiture proceedings, and, where found guilty, custodial sentences.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation / Inadequate TP Documentation

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner of Income Tax may adjust the company's Gibraltar profits to the arm's-length standard, charging tax at 10% on the upward adjustment, with penalties of up to 50% of the additional tax. In cases of fraud, the assessment window is unlimited rather than the standard six-year discovery period.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or direct false transfer pricing returns may be held personally liable for the additional tax where deliberate falsity is shown. They may also face director disqualification, financial restitution orders, and travel restrictions if cross-border mispricing investigations extend into UK or international anti-fraud enforcement.
