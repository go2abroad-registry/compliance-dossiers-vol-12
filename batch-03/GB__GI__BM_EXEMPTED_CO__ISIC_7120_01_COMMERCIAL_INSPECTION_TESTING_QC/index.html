Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed under the Bermuda Companies Act 1981; the incorporator ordinarily authorizes share capital of USD 12,000 split into 12,000 ordinary shares (par value USD 1.00 or USD 0.01) and allots a minimum of one fully paid share on incorporation. There is no escrow deposit or bank-capital verification requirement; the Gibraltar overseas-company registration requires a registered office address and a Gibraltar resident representative, not additional capitalization.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, Her Majesty's Government of Gibraltar; indirect tax and customs authority: HM Customs Gibraltar. Online portal: www.gibraltar.gov.gi/tax

E-government filing portal

For a Bermuda Exempted Company operating in Gibraltar (but not incorporated there), no standalone Gibraltar tax ID is required unless tax nexus is established. Step 1: Register or file notification with the Gibraltar Companies Registry if a branch or local representative office is established. Step 2: If tax residence is triggered by central management and control in Gibraltar, file Form CT1 with the Income Tax Office within 30 days of commencement. Step 3: For import/customs purposes, obtain a Customs Identification Number by registering with HM Customs Gibraltar under the Customs Ordinance; VAT ID is not applicable as Gibraltar does not operate a VAT system. Step 4: Maintain the Bermuda Certificate of Exemption and proof of non-residence in Gibraltar.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Gibraltar law does not provide a US-style check-the-box election. A foreign-incorporated entity can make a factual election to be treated as non-resident by demonstrating absence of central management and control in Gibraltar; a resident company may apply for ring-fenced qualifying company treatment under the Income Tax Act, subject to EU State Aid grandfathering and OECD economic substance requirements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST (Gibraltar levies only customs and excise duties on imported goods; no value-added or sales tax regime is in force)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A - no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. Customs registration is mandatory for all commercial importers regardless of turnover; there is no currency-denominated economic threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable because Gibraltar imposes no VAT/GST on the supply of goods or services. Exported goods are outside the scope of Gibraltar import duties when properly documented; cross-border inspection and quality-control services supplied to non-residents are not subject to any Gibraltar indirect tax, so no zero-rating claim or refund mechanism arises.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

No real-time electronic fiscal clearance or digital stamping mandate applies in Gibraltar. Electronic invoicing is permitted but not systematically validated by tax authorities; record-keeping must satisfy the Income Tax Act and Companies Act retention rules.

As there is no zero-rated VAT mechanism, penalties are enforced under the Customs Ordinance and Income Tax Act. False declarations or forged export documents attract seizure/forfeiture of goods, penalties of up to 300% of evaded duties, imprisonment up to 2 years, and denial of treaty or income tax reliefs. Any duty drawback or relief erroneously granted is subject to retrospective clawback within 6 years from the date of assessment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Fully convertible. Gibraltar's exchange-control restrictions were suspended in 1979 and no capital-account controls, profit-remittance approvals, or dividend/interest withholding taxes apply to non-resident corporations. GIP and GBP circulate concurrently at par, so a GBP-denominated treasury position has zero conversion friction. The only restrictive overlay is the UK/Gibraltar Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act framework, which prohibits financial services to sanctioned states (e.g., Russia, Belarus, DPR/LPR) and imposes enhanced due diligence on crypto-adjacent flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Nil surrender obligations. Gibraltar law does not impose mandatory conversion ratios, repatriation timelines, or surrender of export or service proceeds. A commercial inspection/QC agency may retain 100% of foreign-currency receipts in any GFSC-regulated account without prior approval. Record-keeping: invoices, contracts, inspection certificates and factory-audit logs must be retained for at least 5 years and produced to the bank on request under the AML/CFT framework.

Central bank supervision: No independent central bank exists in Gibraltar. The Government of Gibraltar (HM Gibraltar Treasury, issuing authority under the Currency Notes Act 1934) issues Gibraltar Pounds (GIP) at a statutory 1:1 parity with GBP. The Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the integrated regulatory authority for licensed banks, foreign-exchange firms and payment institutions. Monetary-policy and lender-of-last-resort functions are effectively anchored by the UK's Bank of England through the sterling-area parity relationship.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

HIGH for operational floating payments through Wise Business (UK FCA-authorised EMI), because GIP:GBP parity enables seamless GBP/EUR transfers between Gibraltar bank accounts and the EMI's UK clearing network. LEGAL CONSTRAINT: an EMI cannot provide capital-escrow depository services, documentary credits, or investment-grade clearing; the licensed bank account must remain the treasury anchor. GFSC treats inbound credits from unlicensed offshore EMIs cautiously, so restrict Wise usage to low-value per-inspection disbursements (not exceeding GBP 15,000 per transaction), and ensure the Wise business profile exactly matches the registered Bermuda corporate name.
