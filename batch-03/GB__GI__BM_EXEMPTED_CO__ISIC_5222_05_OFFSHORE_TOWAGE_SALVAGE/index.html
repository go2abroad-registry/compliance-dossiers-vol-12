Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (Ministry of Finance); official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation (Income Tax Administration System - ITAS).

E-government filing portal

The Bermuda Exempted Company must file Form IT-101 (Non-Resident Company Registration) with the Gibraltar Income Tax Office within 30 days of commencing local activity. The application requires a certified Bermuda Certificate of Incorporation, current Certificate of Good Standing, constitutional documents, appointment of a Gibraltar resident agent and auditor, and a written declaration of the place of effective management. Upon approval, the company obtains a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) with the GI prefix.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone (the Bermuda Exempted Company retains its Bermuda tax-exempt status; a Gibraltar branch or permanent establishment is taxable on Gibraltar-source profits at 12.5% if managed and controlled from Gibraltar, subject to qualifying-activity reductions and the UK-Gibraltar double taxation arrangement). — The Bermuda Exempted Company must file Form IT-101 (Non-Resident Company Registration) with the Gibraltar Income Tax Office within 30 days of commencing local activity. The application requires a certified Bermuda Certificate of Incorporation, current Certificate of Good Standing, constitutional documents, appointment of a Gibraltar resident agent and auditor, and a written declaration of the place of effective management. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (the Bermuda Exempted Company retains its Bermuda tax-exempt status; a Gibraltar branch or permanent establishment is taxable on Gibraltar-source profits at 12.5% if managed and controlled from Gibraltar, subject to qualifying-activity reductions and the UK-Gibraltar double taxation arrangement).

Available election forms

Gibraltar law does not provide a US-style check-the-box election; however, the company may elect application of the qualifying-source regime under the Income Tax Act 2010, may elect for treaty relief under the UK-Gibraltar Double Taxation Arrangement, and must comply with Gibraltar's hybrid mismatch rules which restrict elective tax arbitrage on hybrid entities and instruments.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or general sales tax in Gibraltar; indirect taxation is limited to customs, excise and import duties. Gibraltar is outside the UK VAT zone and the EU VAT regime.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable to services; physical goods importers must obtain a Gibraltar Customs Trader Registration with no minimum turnover threshold. General personal import duty exemption applies to consignments valued at or below GBP 150.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is not applicable because Gibraltar does not levy VAT or GST on offshore towage and salvage services supplied to non-residents; such supplies are outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect taxation. Where a UK VAT exposure is separately identified, services are generally outside the scope of UK VAT or subject to reverse charge rather than zero-rated.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration / Entry (electronic Single Trade Document)
  • Original Bill of Lading or Sea Waybill evidencing cross-border tug movement and salvage delivery
  • Lloyd's Open Form (LOF 2000) or BIMCO TOWCON/TOWHIRE executed towage/salvage contract
  • Commercial invoice and Lloyds Standard Form wire transfer confirmation
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103 evidencing receipt of foreign currency proceeds

Gibraltar does not impose mandatory real-time e-invoicing or fiscal clearance for B2B invoices. Customs declarations are submitted electronically through the Gibraltar Customs e-Processing portal using the Single Trade Document format; no digital stamping or continuous tax control (CTC) clearance is required.

As no zero-rating refund scheme exists in Gibraltar, principal risks are customs duty fraud and income tax evasion: civil penalties up to 100% of underdeclared tax or duty, interest at 8% per annum, criminal fines up to GBP 25,000 and imprisonment up to 7 years for falsified export or customs records, and retrospective reassessment extended to six years (or twelve years for fraudulent or careless conduct).

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility on both current and capital account. Gibraltar has no exchange-control statute restricting inbound or outbound transfers; GIP is pegged at 1:1 to GBP and circulates interchangeably with UK banknotes. Profit repatriation, dividend remittance, and cross-border debt servicing are unrestricted. The Bermuda Exempted Company is subject only to BMA registration-based exchange-control assent (a formality), not to quantitative limits. Practical hurdles are limited to GFSC/UK anti-money-laundering and economic-substance documentation rather than legal convertibility constraints.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender or conversion ratio applies. Neither Gibraltar nor the United Kingdom requires export proceeds, salvage remuneration, or towage receivables to be converted into local currency or remitted within a statutory window. Receipts in USD, EUR, or GBP may be held indefinitely in foreign-currency accounts. BMA rules likewise permit retention of foreign-currency proceeds for exempted companies, subject only to annual financial-return disclosure.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (BoE) and HM Treasury as sovereign monetary and exchange-control authority; Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the licensed banking and AML/CFT supervisor in Gibraltar; Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) as the exchange-control administrator for the Bermuda Exempted Company. Gibraltar issues its own Gibraltar Pound (GIP) at par with GBP under the Currency Act 1934, but the territory operates as a British Overseas Territory without an independent central bank.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW-MEDIUM. Gibraltar does not issue e-money institution authorisations; post-Brexit, UK FCA-authorised EMIs (Airwallex, Revolut, Wise Business, Currenxie) do not passport into Gibraltar, and most classify Bermuda-exempted offshore entities plus marine salvage/towage as a negative merchant sector due to AML/CTF sensitivity. Where an EMI accepts the file (e.g., Currenxie or Airwallex with strict underwriting), it is viable only as a secondary disbursement rail for subcontractor payments, never for escrow, hostage-release settlement, or commercial LC operations. EMIs must be kept below regulatory-relevant thresholds and cannot substitute for the GFSC-licensed tier-1 bank relationship.
