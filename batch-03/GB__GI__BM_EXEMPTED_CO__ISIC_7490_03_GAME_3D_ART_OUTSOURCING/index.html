Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope — the Bermuda Exempted Company is a Tax_Exempt_Zone entity in Bermuda (annual government fee only). In Gibraltar, absent central management and control in Gibraltar, it is a non-resident company whose chargeable income is limited to profits attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment or branch (the in-Gibraltar sculpting and rigging studio) and is taxed at the 12.5% standard corporate rate. If central management and control is in fact exercised in Gibraltar, the company becomes a Gibraltar tax resident and is assessed on worldwide income at 12.5%, with a credit for foreign taxes actually borne (effectively zero for the Bermuda arm).

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no check-the-box election. The applicable mechanisms are: (i) a status election evidenced to the Income Tax Office by board minutes and contemporaneous management-and-control documentation, electing non-residence and confining assessment to Gibraltar-source branch profits; (ii) group-relief elections under the Income Tax Act for 75%-or-more group members (loss surrender); (iii) hybrid-mismatch elections or alignments required by Gibraltar's ATAD-compliant hybrid rules to avoid double-deduction or deduction/non-inclusion outcomes involving the Bermuda exempt entity; and (iv) no pass-through election exists for a company — the branch remains an entity-level taxpayer.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is fixed at par with sterling (GBP) and is fully convertible to GBP. Both current-account and capital-account items are freely convertible with no exchange-control approvals or licences required. Profit remittances, dividends, and capital repatriations from the Bermuda Exempted Company's Gibraltar banking hub to the Bermuda parent or foreign shareholders are unrestricted and are not subject to Gibraltar withholding tax. The only practical hurdles are bank-level source-of-wealth verification and the commercial-substance evidence required to satisfy UK/Gibraltar anti-money-laundering and CRS obligations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar and the UK impose no statutory surrender requirements, no mandatory conversion ratios, and no repatriation timelines for export proceeds. USD, EUR, and GBP receipts generated from the 3D art outsourcing contracts may be retained in full in the operational account without conversion into GIP. Voluntary conversion into GBP or EUR may be executed solely for treasury optimisation.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority for the Gibraltar pound under the Currency of Gibraltar Act) and the Financial Services Commission, Gibraltar (GFSC) as the local banking and e-money regulator. Gibraltar maintains no separate central bank or dedicated foreign-exchange administration.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant wire protocol for inbound capital: GBP, USD, or EUR via SWIFT MT103 to the Gibraltar account using ISO 20022 purpose code 'INTC' for intercompany transfers or 'EQUI' for equity injection, depending on the underlying capitalisation instrument. UK domestic legs use FPS or CHAPS for same-day settlement. Verification documentation comprises the remitting bank's payment reference containing the full legal name of the Bermuda Exempted Company, the board-approved intercompany loan or equity contribution statement, and the standard CRS/FATCA self-certification. Commercial Letters of Credit are not a standard requirement for low-risk intangible service flows; where a publisher's procurement terms mandate bank intermediation, an unconfirmed sight Commercial LC is the most cost-effective documentary instrument. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% in Bermuda and 0% in Gibraltar; neither jurisdiction imposes statutory withholding tax on dividend distributions to non-resident shareholders.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Gibraltar: mandatory Local File documentation is triggered where annual turnover exceeds GBP 10,000,000 or where controlled transactions with non-Gibraltar related parties exceed GBP 1,000,000. Bermuda: no local file requirement for an ordinary exempted company below the Bermuda CIT threshold; if the entity is within an MNE group with consolidated revenue above EUR 750,000,000, OECD-compliant TP documentation and Bermuda CIT filings become mandatory.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda's Economic Substance Amendment Act 2018 applies to exempted companies conducting a 'relevant activity'. A 3D game art outsourcing studio may be classified as a 'distribution and service centre business' if it provides services to affiliates, or as an 'IP business' if it holds valuable game-art IP and earns royalty-like revenue. Such entities must demonstrate physical presence, qualified employees, operating expenditure, and core income-generating activities in Bermuda. Gibraltar applies a parallel substance test for tax residence and treaty access, requiring board meetings, business decisions, and operational risk management to occur in Gibraltar where treaty benefits are sought.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Sham Economic Substance / Shell Company Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: Economic substance penalties up to BMD 10,000 per offence and continuing daily penalties; in egregious cases, BMD 250,000 criminal fines, compulsory strike-off, and exchange of information with the ultimate parent jurisdiction. Gibraltar may also deny CIT treaty benefits and impose penalties for deliberate non-disclosure.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners can be made personally liable for unpaid fines, compliance costs, and the cost of striking off. Travel bans and asset freezing orders may issue, and criminal conviction can result in imprisonment of up to 2 years in Bermuda and up to 7 years in Gibraltar for fraudulent misrepresentation.
Tax Evasion Through Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar will adjust taxable profits at the full 12.5% rate, charge interest from the due date, and impose penalties up to 100% of the understated tax. In Bermuda, the CIT Act 2023 imposes a 15% top-up tax for large MNE groups; failure to file economic substance schedules attracts civil penalties and potential criminal indictment.

Director personal liability: Officers who sign false returns are jointly and severally liable for penalties; they may be subject to personal travel bans, seizure of passports, disqualification orders, and imprisonment for up to 7 years for deliberate tax evasion.
AML Failures in Cross-Border Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 enforcement can produce fines up to GBP 500,000, forfeiture of funds, and withdrawal of AML/supervisory permissions. Bermuda's Proceeds of Crime Act imposes parallel civil and criminal sanctions including asset confiscation.

Director personal liability: Directors can be held personally criminally liable for money laundering offences with imprisonment of up to 10 years, personal asset confiscation, and prohibition from holding director or trust-related positions. Regulators may issue travel bans pending investigation and require personal restitution to victims.
Failure to Register and File Gibraltar Returns

Statutory sanction: A default assessment may be issued for the full Gibraltar turnover, with daily late-filing penalties and a 5% surcharge on unpaid tax for every six-month period of delay. Striking-off proceedings may be initiated against the Gibraltar branch, and the company may be banned from contracting with Gibraltar-based studios or using Gibraltar EMI bank accounts.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for unremitted taxes; the Gibraltar Tax Office may impose a personal tax clearance block, travel restriction against the director, and obtain a personal civil judgment for unpaid corporate tax where the company is dissolved or stripped of assets.
