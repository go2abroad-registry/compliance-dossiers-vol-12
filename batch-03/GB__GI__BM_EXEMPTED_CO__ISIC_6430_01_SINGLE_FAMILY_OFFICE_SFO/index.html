Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Gibraltar Companies Registry, Registry of Companies, HM Government of Gibraltar, Europort Building, Gibraltar (online portal: www.registry.gov.gi)

Governing statute

Gibraltar Companies Act 2014 (Act 24/2014, as amended), ss. 4-6 (incorporation), ss. 15-21 (Memorandum and Articles), s. 12 (registered office), ss. 181-201 (officers), s. 98 (beneficial ownership register); complemented by the Income Tax (Economic Substance) Regulations 2020 and the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015. The entity is constituted as a private company limited by shares drafted on the Bermuda Exempted Company template (ELF_CUSTOM_BM_EX), permitting denominated foreign-currency share capital and broad investment-object powers.

Formation / constitutional filings

Form CO-01 Application for Incorporation of a Private Company Limited by Shares together with the executed Memorandum and Articles of Association, filed under Gibraltar Companies Act 2014, ss. 4-6

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 17 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital (minimum_capital_requirement: false). Share capital may be denominated in USD, GBP or EUR with nil/low par value; shares may remain unpaid or uncalled. No escrow deposit or pre-funding bank account rule is imposed by the Registrar for a single-family office exempted company.

Funding & unlock path: Not applicable to industrial processes, but the leased office premises must obtain a Fire Safety Certificate from the Gibraltar Fire and Rescue Service under the Fire Safety (Gibraltar) Act 2017, and a Certificate of Occupancy or structural load confirmation from the Building Control Office under the Building Act 2019 before staff occupation. The registered agent shall verify both certificates and the adequacy of emergency evacuation arrangements prior to executing the lease.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar - Income Tax Office, Office of the Commissioner of Income Tax (portal: www.gibraltar.gov.gi); customs and indirect duties: Gibraltar Customs and Excise Department. Home registration of the Bermuda Exempted Company: Bermuda Registrar of Companies (portal: www.roc.gov.bm) and Bermuda Ministry of Finance (portal: www.gov.bm). UK VAT reach-through (if triggered): HM Revenue and Customs, portal: www.gov.uk/hmrc.

E-government filing portal

Step 1 - Incorporate the Bermuda Exempted Company with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies to obtain the Certificate of Incorporation and Certificate of Exempted Status; Bermuda issues no income-tax assessment because exempted companies are outside Bermuda's income, capital gains, and withholding tax net. Step 2 - Register the Bermuda body corporate as an external company in Gibraltar under the Gibraltar Companies Act 2014; obtain the Gibraltar company registration number and a certificate of registration for the external company. Step 3 - File the company's initial status return with the Gibraltar Income Tax Office to obtain the Gibraltar tax reference number; as a Tax_Exempt_Zone vehicle the company is recorded as exempt-assessed with a standing nil-tax status letter. Step 4 - If the Single Family Office employs staff, register the payroll with the Gibraltar Income Tax Office for PAYE withholding. Step 5 - Register with the Gibraltar Customs and Excise Department only if physical goods are imported or exported; no VAT identification number is obtainable or required because Gibraltar does not operate a VAT. The procedure is fully electronic and is completed within 3 to 5 business days.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

The Bermuda Exempted Company may elect to be treated as a pure equity-holding entity under Bermuda's Economic Substance Regulations 2020, thereby satisfying reduced substance requirements and retaining its zero-tax status. For United States federal tax purposes, if the entity is not a per se corporation, it may file IRS Form 8832 to elect corporate or disregarded-entity classification (check-the-box); such election does not alter Bermuda or Gibraltar classification. Gibraltar law provides no check-the-box mechanism but permits the company to elect a fiscal year end and to apply for formal confirmation that central management and control is located outside Gibraltar, producing non-resident status for Gibraltar tax. The structure contains no targetable hybrid mismatch because income is not subject to tax at entity level in either Bermuda or Gibraltar; if the multinational group exceeds EUR 750 million consolidated revenue, the global minimum top-up rules and any relevant transitional elections must be assessed under the Bermuda Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 and the Gibraltar equivalent GloBE implementing regulations.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

VAT - Not applicable in Gibraltar (Gibraltar is outside the UK and EU VAT areas and levies no VAT, GST, or sales and use tax); indirect taxation is limited to Gibraltar customs import duties and excise duties on alcohol, tobacco, hydrocarbons, and a narrow set of luxury goods. UK VAT applies only if the entity makes taxable supplies of goods or services in the United Kingdom.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No Gibraltar VAT registration threshold exists because there is no Gibraltar VAT regime. If a UK VAT liability is triggered by UK taxable activities, the statutory registration threshold is GBP 90,000 of taxable turnover in a 12-month period; a passive Single Family Office will normally remain below this threshold and outside the UK VAT registration net.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar and Bermuda impose no VAT or GST, so the Single Family Office has no domestic export zero-rating claim for its passive investment and custody activities. If the entity voluntarily registers for UK VAT, exports of goods from the United Kingdom to non-UK destinations are zero-rated under VATA 1994, section 30, and certain cross-border business-to-business services fall outside the scope of UK VAT; however, private investment management, custody, and financial intermediary services performed for the family are generally VAT-exempt rather than zero-rated, which restricts input VAT recovery under the partial exemption rules and preserves the 0% headline effective indirect tax cost.

  • Customs Export Declaration (UK Customs Declaration Service export entry, or Gibraltar customs exit return where goods physically leave via Gibraltar)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export from the relevant territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank confirmation of receipt of export proceeds in foreign currency)
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and underlying export contract substantiating the transaction
  • Transit or supplementary documentary evidence where goods pass through an intermediate jurisdiction before final destination

Gibraltar imposes no electronic invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping mandate for passive investment holding activities. In the United Kingdom, Making Tax Digital applies to VAT-registered businesses and requires digital record-keeping and API-based quarterly submission to HMRC; no government-prescribed e-invoicing format or central clearance platform is required as at 2025. The Bermuda and Gibraltar domiciles similarly impose no certified e-invoicing or fiscal stamping obligation on exempted investment companies.

Export zero-rating claims are subjected to strict documentary scrutiny and retrospective audit review by HMRC in the UK and by the Gibraltar Commissioner of Income Tax where a Gibraltar customs or tax relief is involved. Deliberate inaccuracy or unsupported zero-rating attracts penalties up to 100% of the tax understated plus interest under FA 2007, Schedule 24, with the availability of time-unlimited assessments in cases of fraud or evasion. Fraudulent export claims can result in criminal investigation, unlimited fines, seizure of goods, and clawback of input VAT previously recovered on related costs in both the UK and Gibraltar; the Bermuda exempted status does not shield the underlying beneficial owners from personal liability for fraudulent conduct.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar Pound (GIP) is issued by the Government of Gibraltar at a 1:1 peg to GBP and is freely convertible; the UK meets IMF Article VIII obligations, so both current-account flows (dividends, management fees, investment income) and capital-account flows (capital injections, portfolio investments, intra-group loans) are fully liberalized with no approval requirement. For the Bermuda Exempted Company, non-resident designation removes all BMA restrictions on holding foreign-currency accounts and remitting proceeds offshore. Profit remittance from Gibraltar is unrestricted under the Gibraltar Companies Act 2014 and there is no withholding tax on dividends; the only practical hurdles are bank-level source-of-funds evidence and satisfaction of economic-substance documentation for the SFO.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. The UK Exchange Control Act 1947 was repealed in 1979, and neither the UK nor Gibraltar imposes statutory surrender timelines, mandatory conversion ratios, or retention quotas on export or foreign receipts. A Bermuda Exempted Company designated non-resident is likewise exempted from the surrender and conversion obligations of the Bermuda Exchange Control Act 1972. For this ISIC 6430 single-family office there are no trading/export proceeds; investment realization proceeds may be retained indefinitely in the original currency.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar has no independent central bank; monetary policy and sterling convertibility are set by the Bank of England, while the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the prudential and licensing authority under the Financial Services Act 2019. The Bermuda leg is overseen by the Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA) under the Exchange Control Act 1972, which applies to the Bermuda Exempted Company unless the entity obtains non-resident exchange-control designation.
