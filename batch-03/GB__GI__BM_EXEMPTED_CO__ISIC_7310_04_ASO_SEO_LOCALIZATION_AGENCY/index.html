Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office (GITO) operating through the MyGOV electronic taxpayer portal (https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation); Companies House Gibraltar for overseas-company and branch registration under the Companies Act 2014; HM Customs Gibraltar (Excise and Customs Division) for customs and excise duties on dutiable physical goods; and, for the originating corporate status, the Bermuda Registrar of Companies (Economic Substance Compliance Division) under the Companies Act 1981 and the Economic Substance (Companies) Act 2018. Corporate online filing is available on the Gibraltar Companies House e-registry; Bermuda corporate filings are made through the Bermuda Registrar's Rosetta portal.

E-government filing portal

Step 1 – Bermuda home leg: incorporate the Bermuda Exempted Company under the Companies Act 1981 as an exempted company and obtain the Certificate of Incorporation, the exempted-company status certificate and the Certificate of Good Standing from the Bermuda Registrar; because Bermuda imposes no corporate income tax, no Bermudian TIN or VAT ID is issued or required (a Bermudian Payroll Tax file number would only arise if the entity employed Bermuda-resident staff, which this agency does not). Step 2 – Gibraltar branch/overseas-company registration: if the Exempted Co establishes a place of business, branch or local agency in Gibraltar, file the overseas-company particulars with Companies House Gibraltar under the Companies Act 2014, supplying the Bermuda certificate, constitutional documents, registered-office details and the appointment of a Gibraltar-resident authorised representative. Step 3 – Gibraltar tax registration with GITO: submit the new taxpayer application on MyGOV to obtain a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN), annexing (a) the certified Bermuda incorporation and good-standing certificates, (b) the Gibraltar branch or registered-office certificate, (c) a director-signed statement identifying the place of central management and control (CM&C), and (d) business-account confirmation from the chosen payment rails (Wise B2B or Payoneer) evidencing where fees are received. Step 4 – Residency self-declaration: where CM&C is exercised outside Gibraltar (for example board meetings and strategic decisions in Bermuda or another non-Gibraltar venue), formally notify GITO that the entity claims non-resident status and will file a non-resident company return limited to Gibraltar-source income or attributable Gibraltar permanent-establishment profit; where CM&C is in Gibraltar, apply as a resident company liable at 12.5%. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone – the Bermuda Exempted Company is not subject to Bermuda corporate income tax, capital gains tax or withholding tax on distributions, and can be structured (non-Gibraltar central management and control plus absence of a Gibraltar permanent establishment) to remain outside the Gibraltar 12.5% income-tax net on its global digital-marketing fee income. The classification is territorial/source-based only to the limited extent required by Gibraltar law: income derived from, or attributable to, a Gibraltar permanent establishment would fall within Gibraltar chargeable profits at 12.5%, while the entity retains the legal form of an exempted limited-liability company.

Available election forms

No U.S.-style check-the-box election exists in either Bermuda or Gibraltar; classification follows statute. Available elections and markers: (i) Bermuda Economic Substance (Companies) Act 2018 – election to be treated as a Pure Equity Holding Company (Form PEH) where the Exempted Co holds only equity participations and earns only dividends or capital gains, reducing economic-substance compliance to registered office and statutory records; this election is not available for the active agency income unless the trading activity is ring-fenced in a separate vehicle. (ii) Gibraltar residence election – through a written representation to the Income Tax Office under the Income Tax Act 2010 residence provisions, supported by board minutes evidencing where CM&C sits; a non-resident company may also elect to be assessed on a Gibraltar permanent-establishment basis. (iii) UK VAT voluntary registration and the special digital-services accounting scheme election are available if the entity decides to create UK VAT nexus for B2C supplies to UK consumers (these are HMRC elections, not Gibraltar elections). (iv) Hybrid-mismatch elections are immaterial because the Bermuda nil-tax and Gibraltar 12.5% territorial combination does not produce a deduction-without-inclusion or double-deduction outcome on agency fees; any intercompany recharge for the SEO/localization services must still be arm's length under Gibraltar transfer-pricing and UK CFC/hybrid rules. (v) No tax consolidation or fiscal-pooling election is recognised in Gibraltar for a foreign exempted company.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is legally pegged at par to GBP 1:1 and is freely exchangeable with Bank of England sterling. Both GIP and GBP are legal tender in Gibraltar. The territory has open current and capital account convertibility; no exchange controls restrict inward or outward remittances. A Bermuda Exempted Company, as a non-resident offshore entity with no Gibraltar-source income, is not subject to Gibraltar tax or withholding tax on dividends, interest, royalties, or service fees. Profit remittance to Bermuda or shareholder jurisdictions is unrestricted, subject only to standard bank verification and AML reporting thresholds. No central bank approval or exchange control permit is required for contract payments, intercompany funding, or shareholder distributions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender, forced conversion, or mandatory repatriation timeline applies to export receipts in Gibraltar. Service exports from an ISIC 7310 digital agency are treated as current account earnings accruing to a non-resident exempted company. Receipts denominated in USD, EUR, GBP, or any freely convertible currency may be retained in the company's offshore operational accounts indefinitely. The only compliance condition is accurate recording for the Gibraltar Annual Returns and, if applicable, economic substance reporting; there is no requirement to remit export proceeds into the local banking system.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority for GBP/sterling; issues banknotes if required); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the sole statutory licensing and prudential regulator; HM Government of Gibraltar (Exchange Control policy custodian, though no modern capital control apparatus is active)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Viable for USD/EUR/GBP collections and vendor disbursements only. Wise (Wise B2B) and Payoneer do not hold Gibraltar banking licences; they operate as authorised payment institutions under UK FCA or European EMI passports and serve Gibraltar as a cross-border customer segment. Legal constraints: (i) neither can provide a full suite of commercial lending, escrow, or trade finance; (ii) funds are not covered by the Gibraltar Deposit Guarantee Scheme; (iii) incoming wire routing must remain within their licenced corridors; (iv) high-volume B2B flows are subject to enhanced due diligence because the underlying entity is a Bermuda exempted company operating from Gibraltar. Overall feasibility rated HIGH as a supplementary treasury rail, but MEDIUM as a primary bank of record. For statutory substance and creditor credibility, a licensed Gibraltar bank held account should remain the anchor.
