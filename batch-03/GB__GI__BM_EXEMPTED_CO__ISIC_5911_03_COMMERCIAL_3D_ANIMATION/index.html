Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a Gibraltar private company limited by shares, including the exempted template; the founder typically subscribes one share of GBP 0.01 or USD 1.00. No escrow or traced-funds account is triggered because the commercial CGI/animation activity is non-licensed and does not involve regulated activities, public fundraising, or deposit-taking. If a share premium above par is introduced, the subscribing member must deposit the funds into a Gibraltar-licensed bank before allotment; those funds remain at the company's free disposal without a mandatory escrow period.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety clearance from the Gibraltar Fire and Rescue Service under the Fire Safety Regulations 2021 (BS 9999 basis) for premises occupied by more than 10 persons; structural load assessment by a Gibraltar-registered engineer for server and render-farm floors rated at a minimum of 8 kN/m²; portable appliance testing (PAT) on all animation workstations under the Electricity at Work Regulations; occupational health clearance from the Gibraltar Industrial Safety Department confirming DSE workstation ergonomics, first-aid coverage, and noise levels not exceeding 70 dB(A) in editing suites.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, H.M. Government of Gibraltar - Income Tax Office (ITO), administering the Income Tax Act 2010 and operating the Gibraltar Tax System (GTS) online portal at www.income-tax.gov.gi.

E-government filing portal

The Bermuda Exempted Company (non-Gibraltar resident) carrying on business in Gibraltar must obtain a Foreign Constitutions Certificate under the Companies Act 2014 from the Registrar of Companies and concurrently apply to the ITO for a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN/GRN). Procedure: (i) file certified Bermuda certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles, certificate of good standing and a registered office/agent letter; (ii) submit Form IT-01 'Registration of Non-Resident Entity' with a statement of the Gibraltar representative, settled address, and expected turnover; (iii) obtain the Gibraltar Reference Number (GRN) within 5-10 working days; (iv) if employing local staff, register as an employer for PAYE via Form IT-04; (v) if importing physical media, apply separately for a Customs EORI registration under the Customs Duty Act 2015.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone (Territorial_Scope variant): a foreign company not managed and controlled in Gibraltar is chargeable only on Gibraltar-source income. Commercial 3D animation and CGI/VFX services rendered to non-Gibraltar clients, invoiced abroad, and completed without a Gibraltar permanent establishment fall outside the charge to Gibraltar income tax. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (Territorial_Scope variant): a foreign company not managed and controlled in Gibraltar is chargeable only on Gibraltar-source income. Commercial 3D animation and CGI/VFX services rendered to non-Gibraltar clients, invoiced abroad, and completed without a Gibraltar permanent establishment fall outside the charge to Gibraltar income tax. No capital gains tax, inheritance tax, wealth tax, or stamp duty applies to the offshore branch.

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no check-the-box regime. Available elections under the Income Tax Act 2010: (i) election to standardise transfer pricing and thin-capitalisation documentation under UK-Aligned Pillar One rules; (ii) participation exemption election for disposal gains on non-Gibraltar situs shares; (iii) branch attribution election adopting the authorised OECD PE attribution approach rather than the domestic Gibraltar-source formula; (iv) written election to treat a foreign hybrid entity as transparent for Gibraltar tax where UK CTA 2010 Part 6TA or OECD BEPS Action 2 adjustments are triggered in the parent jurisdiction.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST in Gibraltar; indirect taxation is limited to Customs Duty and Excise Duty under the Customs Duty Act 2015 and the Excise Ordinance - importation of goods and selected domestic excise (alcohol, tobacco, petrol) are dutiable.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - Gibraltar imposes no VAT/GST and thus no turnover threshold exists for indirect tax registration. For customs purposes, every importer (including the BVI/Bermuda exempted branch) must hold a Gibraltar EORI from the first import with no de minimis threshold; customs duty is assessed on the CIF value of the goods imported.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in the VAT sense because Gibraltar has no value added tax. The supply of commercial CGI/3D animation and VFX production services to overseas clients is outside the scope of Gibraltar taxation, provided that (i) central management and control remains in Bermuda; (ii) contracts are negotiated and invoices are rendered by the non-Gibraltar entity; (iii) no fixed place of business or dependent agent in Gibraltar exists; (iv) service recipients are non-Gibraltar residents and the deliverables are not and do not relate to immovable property or goods situated in Gibraltar. Re-exported physical deliverables (storyboards, masters, film media) may qualify for a relief from customs duty under the temporary admission/re-export regime upon submission of a Return on Export (GCA-EXP) to the Customs Department.

  • Customs Export Declaration (GCA-EXP / SAD equivalent) - electronic filing receipt and unique Movement Reference Number (MRN) evidencing physical exit of the production media from Gibraltar
  • Bill of Lading or House Air Waybill - negotiable transport document establishing shipment to the non-Gibraltar client or downstream VFX studio
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List - detailed description of the delivered 3D animation deliverables, contractual value, and incoterms
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) - bank-generated proof from an authorised Gibraltar bank evidencing receipt of export proceeds via SWIFT/Wise B2B rails within the banking-prescribed currency repatriation window
  • Statement of Ownership and Export Control Classification - certification that deliverables are not ITAR/EAR-controlled and are classified ECCN EAR99/unrestricted

No real-time fiscal clearance mandate, no B2B e-invoicing requirement, and no digital stamping in Gibraltar. The ITO accepts conventional digital invoices. Customs declarations are filed electronically through the Gibraltar Customs Automated (GCA) system using CUSCAR/CUSDEC EDI formats interoperable with the UK GVMS and the Common Transit Convention; no continuous transaction-level clearance (CTC) to the tax authority is required and no API-based government clearance is imposed.

False customs declarations attract civil penalties up to the greater of GBP 3,000 or 200% of duty evaded, plus forfeiture of goods under Customs Duty Act 2015 ss.78-82. Incorrect branch returns, undisclosed permanent establishment, or misstatement on Form IT-02 trigger assessments back-dated six years, interest at 8% per annum, and tax-geared penalties of up to 100% of the understated tax where carelessness or deliberate behaviour is proven (Income Tax Act 2010 ss.99-100). Fraudulent misrepresentation of exempt status is prosecutable under the Criminal Finances Act 2017 with unlimited fines and up to seven years imprisonment; disgorgement of the full contract fee is recoverable through the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015, and UK court orders are enforceable in Gibraltar under the reciprocal enforcement arrangements.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar and the United Kingdom operate a fully liberalised, no-exchange-control regime. Both current account and capital account transactions are freely convertible into any currency; foreign-currency accounts, payments, and remittances do not require central bank approval. Profit remittance from a Gibraltar-managed operation to a Bermuda Exempted Company is not subject to Gibraltar withholding tax or dividend restriction, provided the company is not managed and controlled in Gibraltar or the UK and complies with UK transfer-pricing, CFC, and substance rules where applicable. The practical hurdles are documentary rather than regulatory: source of funds, beneficial ownership, and proof of economic substance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory export proceeds surrender or conversion ratio applies in Gibraltar or the UK. Exporters may retain 100% of foreign-currency receipts in bank accounts, convert at market rates, or maintain natural currency offsets. Receipts must nevertheless be reflected in ordinary accounting records and may be subject to Bank of England balance-of-payments reporting thresholds, UK CFC/transfer-pricing scrutiny for related-party export licenses, and sanctions screening on all counterparties.

Central bank supervision: HM Treasury and the Bank of England for sterling monetary policy and sanctions; the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the licensing and conduct authority for banks and e-money institutions in Gibraltar. There is no separate Gibraltar central bank; the Gibraltar pound is issued by the Government of Gibraltar and is pegged at parity with sterling.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

High feasibility as an ancillary payment rail for Wise B2B and other FCA-regulated e-money institutions, but low viability as the primary treasury solution. An EMI is not a bank, cannot accept deposits, does not offer depositor protection under the Gibraltar Deposit Guarantee Scheme, and may apply restrictive acceptable-use policies to Bermuda Exempted Companies. Legal use is therefore limited to low-balance, well-documented vendor payouts and small inbound receivables; all substantial capital, payroll, and trade settlements must be cleared through a licensed bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity injections must be executed exclusively by SWIFT MT103 or domestic better-payment rails and never via cash, crypto, or third-party apps. Use the following compliant protocol: beneficiary is the Bermuda Exempted Company account at Gibraltar International Bank Limited; payment reference should state 'BM_EXEMPTED_CO-SUBSCRIPTION-ALLOTMENT-[DATE]'; purpose code should be UK BOP category F1 or the bank's equivalent for 'direct investment / equity capital contribution'; currency should ideally be GBP or EUR. Verification must include a certified board resolution approving the share allotment, updated register of members, source-of-funds evidence from the remitting account, confirmation that the sending bank has completed sanctions screening, and an AML questionnaire. No exchange-control approval is required. GBP amounts below GBP 25,000 may use Faster Payments/CHAPS; EUR amounts may use SEPA/TARGET2; larger amounts require SWIFT MT103 via a correspondent bank.Commercial trade settlements for 3D animation production are primarily service payments, not goods movements. For vendor payouts below GBP 25,000 use Faster Payments, SEPA Credit Transfer, or Wise B2B where the beneficiary has an EMI-compatible IBAN. For larger amounts use SWIFT MT103 with full beneficiary name, account number, and invoice reference. USD settlements should clear via SWIFT MT103 with a reputable correspondent bank rather than ACH.
