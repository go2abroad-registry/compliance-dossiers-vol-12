Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar: HM Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office (official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation); United Kingdom: HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC), VAT Registration and Customs Declaration Service (official portal: https://www.gov.uk/vat-registration).

E-government filing portal

A Bermuda Exempted Company acting as a non-resident entity without a Gibraltar permanent establishment is not required to obtain a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number. If a Gibraltar branch/place of business is established, first file branch registration with the Gibraltar Companies Registry and obtain a company number, then apply to the Gibraltar Income Tax Office for a Tax Reference Number using the prescribed registration form, submitting certified constitutional documents, registered office/agent evidence, beneficial ownership particulars, and details of local management and control. For UK VAT on automated dropshipping sales into Great Britain, apply online through HMRC Government Gateway (company VAT registration), providing legal entity name, registered address, business activity (ISIC 4791), EORI number, expected UK taxable turnover, and details of online marketplaces/payment rails (Stripe, PayPal). Registration is mandatory when taxable turnover exceeds the UK VAT threshold or when the entity is liable as a supplier of imported consignments not exceeding £135 sold to UK consumers.

Available election forms

No UK/Gibraltar check-the-box election is available. The Bermuda Exempted Company is a corporate entity with separate legal personality that is not Gibraltar-resident absent central management and control in Gibraltar; the entity is treated as a separate taxable person only if a Gibraltar/UK permanent establishment or tax residence is created. VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Gibraltar: no VAT/GST turnover threshold. UK VAT: £90,000 taxable turnover in a rolling 12-month period for UK-established businesses; for overseas sellers dispatching goods into Great Britain, there is no distance-selling threshold for consignments not exceeding £135, and VAT is payable at the point of sale (marketplace or seller liability). Northern Ireland applies EU VAT rules under the Windsor Framework.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: UK VAT zero-rating is available where the supplier is VAT-registered, the goods are exported from Great Britain/Northern Ireland to a destination outside the UK as a direct result of the sale, and the supplier retains valid commercial evidence of export within 3 months of the time of supply (or longer in specified cases). Automated dropshipping platforms should document the onward shipment to the overseas consumer and separate UK domestic deliveries. Services: electronically supplied services (e.g., marketplace/fulfilment/payment processing) are usually outside UK VAT scope when supplied by an overseas business to UK consumers under the place-of-supply rules, unless the reverse-charge or UK establishment rules apply; no Gibraltar VAT zero-rating exists.

Gibraltar: no real-time e-invoicing, clearance, digital stamping, or SAF-T obligation. UK: HMRC Making Tax Digital (MTD) requires VAT-registered businesses to maintain digital records, submit VAT returns via MTD-compatible software through HMRC APIs, and keep prescribed digital links; no PEPPOL/ChorusPro-style government clearance or digital stamping is mandated. Gibraltar authorities may similarly impose fines and penalties for false customs declarations or misdeclaration.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound is fully convertible at a fixed 1:1 peg to GBP. There are no exchange controls or monetary restrictions on either current account payments or capital account transfers. Profit remittance, dividend distribution, and capital repatriation from Gibraltar settlement infrastructure are unrestricted for qualifying non-resident offshore entities. The Bermuda Exempted Company classification does not itself create currency hurdles; however, each clearing bank retains independent compliance discretion requiring demonstrated source of funds, economic purpose, and sanctions screening clearances.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender obligation exists under Gibraltar or United Kingdom law. The Bermuda Monetary Authority likewise does not impose mandatory conversion or surrender requirements on export receipts of an exempted company. Trade receivables may be retained in any convertible currency and applied directly to supplier settlements or treasury hedging operations, subject only to bank-level anti-money laundering verification and the provision of underlying commercial documents.

Central bank supervision: HM Treasury (United Kingdom) and the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC); the Gibraltar pound operates as a currency board anchored at parity to GBP, with the Bank of England serving as the monetary anchor for sterling liquidity.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High
