Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: the Bermuda Exempted Company holds a statutory exemption from Bermuda income/capital taxes (tax exemption certificate). Available election forms

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar (GI): no VAT/GST/sales tax - import duties/excise only on goods; services are outside the scope of any Gibraltar indirect tax. United Kingdom (GB) pillar: UK VAT (VATA 1994) at 20% standard rate applies on taxable B2C digital supplies to UK consumers; B2B services are reverse-charged. Bermuda: no indirect tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Gibraltar (GIP/GBP pegged): nil local threshold - no VAT/GST registration exists. UK pillar: £0 threshold for non-UK established businesses supplying B2C digital services to UK consumers (mandatory registration from first £1 of supplies); £85,000 annual threshold for UK-established businesses; B2B services to UK businesses are outside the supplier's registration obligation (reverse charge).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: 0% VAT zero-rating under VATA 1994 s.30 and SI 1995/2518 reg.129 where goods are exported from the UK pillar to an outside destination within 3 months, with export evidence retained; goods exported from Gibraltar bear no VAT. Services: the market-research SaaS platform supplies are outside the scope of UK VAT (B2B reverse charge; B2C place-of-supply where the customer belongs) and cannot be 'export zero-rated' - claiming 0% B2C digital supplies would be an unlawful tax advantage.

  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill (sea/air export evidence)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank evidence of export proceeds; supports the 180-day repatriation verification)

No real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping in Gibraltar (no VAT). UK pillar: Making Tax Digital (MTD) requires digital records and API-based VAT filing via HMRC-recognised software (VAT Notice 700/63); Non-UK OSS quarterly portal filings; customs export declarations via the Customs Declaration Service (CDS); no pre-clearance or HMRC invoice stamping.

HMRC assessments under VATA 1994 s.73 if export evidence is deficient (4-year standard window; 20 years for deliberate inaccuracy); Sch. 24 Finance Act 2007 penalties up to 30% (careless), 70% (deliberate), 100% (deliberate and concealed) of lost UK VAT; clawback of input VAT with statutory interest; s.72 VATA 1994 criminal evasion - unlimited fine and/or up to 7 years' imprisonment; HMRC Civil Investigation of Fraud (CIF) procedures.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound is pegged at 1:1 to sterling and enjoys full current-account and capital-account convertibility. Gibraltar maintains no exchange-control approvals, no quantitative restrictions, and no profit-remittance licensing. A Bermuda Exempted Company can therefore receive survey-platform revenues and remit dividends, loan repayments, or branch profits through Gibraltar bank accounts in GBP, USD, or EUR, provided source-of-funds and beneficial-ownership documentation, together with any applicable economic-substance filings, are in place. Practical friction arises only where remittances flow to sanctioned, high-risk, or non-cooperative jurisdictions, which triggers enhanced due diligence rather than a currency-conversion permit.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar imposes no statutory exchange-control surrender obligation on service-export proceeds. The market-research platform may retain 100% of foreign-currency earnings in a designated bank account, with no mandatory conversion ratio and no central-bank conversion deadline. The only obligations are transaction-reporting thresholds under the Gibraltar AML/CFT framework, sanctions screening, and adequate tax-substance documentation for the Bermuda Exempted Company if it maintains economic activity in Gibraltar.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority for the sterling zone) and the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the competent banking, e-money, and foreign-exchange regulatory authority for Gibraltar

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: HIGH as a settlement and disbursement rail, but LOW as a primary banking account. Wise Business and PayPal Mass Payout are legally viable for consumer-panel mass payouts after GFSC-compliant onboarding, but neither is a Gibraltar-licensed credit institution and neither can replace the principal operational account. Offshore and UK/EU digital EMIs must pass sanctions screening, cannot offer commercial-loan or FX-hedging infrastructure, and should be used only for micro-payments and batch payouts, with a Gibraltar-licensed bank as the funding and treasury hub.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (Bermuda exempted-company rate); Gibraltar standard rate of 12.5% applies only if the company is managed and controlled from Gibraltar or has a Gibraltar permanent establishment.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% in Bermuda and Gibraltar for dividends paid by the exempted company; no Gibraltar withholding tax on distributions to non-resident shareholders.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GBP 1,000,000 aggregated connected-party transactions in an accounting period for Gibraltar Local File; Master File required for a multinational group with consolidated turnover above GBP 50,000,000. Bermuda ESR transfer pricing directions require contemporaneous documentation for material cross-border related-party transactions above USD 1,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Bermuda and Gibraltar both apply OECD-aligned Economic Substance Regulations. The consumer panel survey platform is not a listed relevant activity unless it earns royalty income from IP, operates as a related-party service centre, headquarters, distribution centre, or financing entity. If any listed activity exists, the entity must demonstrate board meetings, qualified employees, business premises and core income-generating activities in Bermuda, or in Gibraltar if tax-resident there. Annual ESR filings must be consistent with transfer pricing records. For a Bermuda exempted company claiming no Gibraltar tax presence, substance must be real and not satisfied by the use of PayPal Mass Payout or Wise payment rails.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion and fraudulent misdeclaration

Statutory sanction: Additional assessment up to 100% of tax evaded, civil penalties up to GBP 3,000 per return, criminal fines unlimited on indictment, and confiscation orders under Proceeds of Crime legislation.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for unlawful distributions and unpaid tax in civil recovery; courts may impose travel bans, asset freezes, disqualification for up to 15 years, and imprisonment for up to 7 years for fraudulent evasion.
Shell company and sham economic substance

Statutory sanction: Bermuda: BD$10,000 initial penalty plus BD$10,000 per day of continuing default and cancellation of exempted status. Gibraltar: striking-off from the register and de facto permanent establishment assessment including interest and penalties.

Director personal liability: Reckless directors may be personally liable for corporate liabilities under wrongful trading principles; disqualification from managing any Bermuda or Gibraltar company; reciprocal UK enforcement may include travel watchlisting and removal from registered officer positions.
Anti-money laundering and payment-rail abuse

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar AML fines up to GBP 250,000 and serious crime confiscation; Bermuda fines under its anti-money laundering framework; suspension of payment-processor accounts; regulatory disqualification of officers.

Director personal liability: Directors serving as money laundering reporting officers face personal criminal liability, asset restraint, travel bans and imprisonment up to 14 years under Gibraltar's Proceeds of Crime and AML legislation; civil recovery may pierce the corporate veil where the company is found to be a sham.
