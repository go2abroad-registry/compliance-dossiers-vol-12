Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope — Gibraltar taxes only income accruing in or derived from Gibraltar; the Bermuda Exempted Company is Tax_Exempt_Zone in its Bermuda domicile (no Bermuda income tax until 2035/2050), and Entity_Level_Taxation in the UK at the 19% small-profits / 25% main corporation tax rate applies only on UK PE profit attribution. The entity is a C-corporation equivalent (not pass-through) in all three territories.

Available election forms

(a) Gibraltar GIBS election — a company may elect to compute taxable income under the gross income based system, using qualifying gross income with capital allowance deductions instead of deemed-profit margins (Income Tax Act 2010). (b) Branch profit attribution election — choose arm's-length separate-entity accounts versus the statutory formulary attribution method for an overseas company's Gibraltar branch. (c) Tax residence election — a non-resident company managed and controlled in Gibraltar may elect residence, becoming subject to worldwide taxation but obtaining territorial credits. (d) Group relief / loss surrender election for 75%-owned Gibraltar tax groups. (e) Hybrid mismatch rules — no 'check-the-box' regime exists in the UK, Gibraltar or Bermuda; the UK hybrid and other mismatch rules (TIOPA 2010 Part 6A) and Gibraltar's Anti-Hybrid Rules treat the Bermuda entity as a separate C-corporation with no transparency election available.

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

UK: £90,000 annual taxable turnover threshold (effective 1 April 2024) for UK-established businesses, and nil threshold for non-established suppliers — the Bermuda Exempted Company must register for UK VAT immediately upon supplying B2C digital CFO services to UK consumers; pure B2B flows to UK customers are reverse-charged without supplier registration. Export 0% zero-rating conditions

UK export zero-rating under VATA 1994 Sch. 8 Group 12 applies only to goods physically exported from the UK within 3 months of supply with proof of removal to a non-UK destination. Outsourced virtual CFO services (fractional CFO advisory, multicurrency ledger bookkeeping, ISIC 6920 / NAICS 541219) are generally outside the scope of UK VAT under the B2B place-of-supply rules, with the business customer accounting for local reverse charge; such services are zero-rated only where ancillary to exported goods or received by a non-UK customer outside the UK. Where the customer is a UK consumer (B2C), UK VAT applies at 20% regardless of the supplier's location.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility applies without exchange-control licensing on both current and capital account. Gibraltar imposes no prior-approval mechanism, no repatriation restriction, and no FX swap obligation for profit remittances, dividends, interest, management fees, royalties, or liquidation proceeds. A Bermuda exempted company carrying on outsourced virtual CFO and multicurrency ledger bookkeeping from Gibraltar may freely hold, denominate, and remit GBP, EUR, and USD in unrestricted amounts. The only material hurdles are documentary: the onboarding bank and any correspondent must evidence commercial genuineness of each payment flow through engagement letters, invoices, board resolutions, and source-of-funds records under the GFSC AML/CFT Guidance Notes. Repatriation to non-resident shareholders is unimpeded and is not subject to Gibraltar withholding tax. If central management and control is found to be exercised from Gibraltar, the entity should co-ordinate its Bermuda exempted status with Gibraltar corporate taxation (12.5%) before declaring dividends so that no inadvertent tax-residence exposure arises.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable in Gibraltar. The jurisdiction imposes no statutory surrender timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation quota for export receipts, including cross-border service fees from ISIC 6920 activities (fractional CFO advisory, bookkeeping, multicurrency ledger administration). Foreign-currency proceeds may be retained indefinitely without surrender obligations. The only binding discipline is AML/CFT record-keeping: invoices, engagement contracts, settlement receipts, and underlying service evidence must be preserved for five years and made available immediately to the Gibraltar Money Laundering Reporting Officer under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015, as amended.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar maintains no independent central bank. The Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC), established under the Financial Services Commission Act 2007, is the statutory regulator of all banking, payment, electronic money, investment, and fiduciary business. The Government of Gibraltar, through the Currency Committee acting under the Currency Note Issue Ordinance, issues the Gibraltar pound (GIP) under a currency-board discipline with statutory 1:1 parity to pound sterling; sterling also circulates as legal tender. De facto monetary and exchange-rate policy therefore tracks the Bank of England, while the GFSC administers all prudential supervision, AML/CFT enforcement, and foreign-exchange-related compliance testing in the jurisdiction.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

CONDITIONAL-MODERATE. The Bermuda exempted company, being neither UK- nor Gibraltar-incorporated, will generally be declined by mainstream UK-licensed EMIs (Wise, Revolut Business) because those platforms restrict account-holding entities to locally registered companies in supported jurisdictions; Wise does not currently support a Bermuda exempted company as a direct treasury account holder. Stripe operates as a merchant acquirer rather than an EMI and will underwrite payment acceptance only for a UK- or Gibraltar-registered operating company, not for the Bermuda vehicle itself. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder equity injections must be routed as SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's regulated bank. Field 50K must identify the investing shareholder; Field 57A must identify the Gibraltar beneficiary bank; Field 59 must identify the Bermuda exempted company with its Gibraltar account number; Field 70 must state VERBATIM: 'EQUITY SUBSCRIPTION / SHARE CAPITAL PAYMENT — NON-TRADE, NO REPAYMENT OBLIGATION — REF BOARD MINUTES DATED [date]'. Where the remitting bank applies BOP/ISO 20022 classification, the purpose code 'EQUI' (equity investment) or the UK balance-of-payments category 'A.2 Equity Capital' is to be used. For EUR inflows, a SEPA Credit Transfer with the beneficiary's GI-prefixed IBAN is the most efficient rail; for GBP inflows, CHAPS or Faster Payments provides same-day clearing through UK infrastructure; for USD inflows, SWIFT MT103 through the bank's London or New York correspondent is required. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: CONDITIONAL-MODERATE. The Bermuda exempted company, being neither UK- nor Gibraltar-incorporated, will generally be declined by mainstream UK-licensed EMIs (Wise, Revolut Business) because those platforms restrict account-holding entities to locally registered companies in supported jurisdictions; Wise does not currently support a Bermuda exempted company as a direct treasury account holder. Stripe operates as a merchant acquirer rather than an EMI and will underwrite payment acceptance only for a UK- or Gibraltar-registered operating company, not for the Bermuda vehicle itself. A legally permissible alternative architecture exists through the UK-Gibraltar financial-services continuity framework adopted after EU exit, which permits a GFSC-authorised electronic-money institution to passport into the UK; however, no mature Gibraltar-licensed EMI currently offers the full multi-currency corporate treasury suite required. RECOMMENDED TREASURY ARCHITECTURE: (1) maintain primary liquidity and capital escrow at a Tier-1 Gibraltar licensed bank in the name of the Bermuda exempted company; (2) establish a Gibraltar branch registration or a UK-administrative agent entity to onboard Wisely and Stripe for B2B fee collection and merchant acquiring; (3) sweep net settlement balances daily to the primary Gibraltar bank via SWIFT MT101/MT103 to maintain concentration control.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory where annual Gibraltar turnover exceeds GIP 50,000,000 or cross-border related-party transactions exceed GIP 1,000,000 in the period; Master File is mandatory where group consolidated turnover exceeds GIP 500,000,000. Documentation must be prepared in English and submitted to the Income Tax Officer within 30 days of notice.

Economic substance scope: The Income Tax (Economic Substance) (Amendment) Act 2020 imposes an economic substance test on companies conducting geographically mobile activities, including service centre, headquarters and distribution functions. A virtual CFO and multicurrency bookkeeping business falls within the service centre category. The company must perform core income-generating activities in Gibraltar, maintain an adequate physical office and full-time staff proportionate to turnover, ensure board meetings and strategic decisions occur in Gibraltar, and file an annual ESR notification and substance return with the Commissioner within 30 days of the corporate tax filing deadline.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Tax-geared penalty up to 200% of the understated tax; criminal fine up to GIP 100,000; imprisonment up to 7 years under the Criminal Justice Act 2011 and Income Tax Act 2010; confiscation orders for the benefit obtained.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax and penalties, subject to civil forfeiture and asset freezing orders, court-ordered travel bans, director disqualification for up to 15 years, and imprisonment up to 7 years on conviction.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: ESR penalty of GIP 100,000 plus GIP 10,000 per day of continuing default; mandatory strike-off from the Gibraltar Companies Register; denial of tax residence certificates; disqualification from regulated outsourcing contracts.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally assessed for daily default penalties and restoration costs; travel bans may be imposed pending compliance; imprisonment up to 2 years and fines up to GIP 100,000 for failure to comply with an ESR enforcement order.
Anti-Money Laundering (DNFBP) Failure

Statutory sanction: Financial penalty up to GIP 1,000,000 per contravention; revocation of any licence or registration; disgorgement of profits; criminal prosecution for non-reporting of suspicious activity with fines and imprisonment up to 7 years.

Director personal liability: Directors and the appointed Money Laundering Reporting Officer face personal criminal liability, imprisonment up to 7 years, travel bans, disqualification from serving as a director of any regulated entity for up to 10 years, and personal civil restitution orders for funds laundered through the business.
Unlicensed Payment Services / Client Money Handling

Statutory sanction: Unlicensed activity penalties of up to GIP 5,000,000 or twice the economic benefit derived; confiscation orders; imprisonment up to 7 years; any related payment instruction may be voidable and client funds subject to third-party claims.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for client money shortfalls, subject to automatic disqualification, court-issued travel bans, personal confiscation orders, and repayment of all client funds from personal assets where the corporate entity is insolvent or struck off.
