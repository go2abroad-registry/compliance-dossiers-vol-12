Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (administration of income tax and GIBVAT); online filing via the Government of Gibraltar taxation portal (gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation). HMRC (UK) has no income tax or VAT jurisdiction in Gibraltar; however, for customs purposes Gibraltar is part of the UK customs territory under the Taxation (Cross-border Trade) Act 2018 as applied by the Customs (Gibraltar) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019. The Bermuda Registrar of Companies administers no income tax but receives economic substance filings under the Bermuda Economic Substance Amendment Act 2018.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: register the Bermuda Exempted Company as an external company with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under the Companies Act 2014 if it establishes a place of business in Gibraltar; obtain the Gibraltar company registration number. Step 2: apply to the Income Tax Office for the Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) before commencing chargeable activity; a foreign-incorporated company becomes Gibraltar tax-resident if central management and control is exercised in Gibraltar. Step 3: register as an employer for tax-deduction (PAYE-equivalent) withholding and social insurance if staff are engaged in Gibraltar. Step 4: register for GIBVAT only if taxable non-zero-rated turnover exceeds the statutory threshold (see indirect tax nexus). Step 5: file the Bermuda economic substance notification/declaration with the Bermuda Registrar of Companies and, if Gibraltar tax-resident, comply with the Gibraltar Economic Substance (Companies and Limited Partnerships) Act 2020 notification requirements.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: the Bermuda Exempted Company enjoys exemption from Bermuda income, capital gains, dividend and withholding taxes under the Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act 1966 (exemption assurance to 2035), and Bermuda levies no indirect tax. The Tax_Exempt_Zone classification is preserved only while the company is not managed and controlled in Gibraltar; a Gibraltar tax-resident company is subject to entity-level Gibraltar corporation tax at 12.5% on worldwide profits under the Income Tax Act 2010, as amended, with the former 0% qualifying-company regime abolished in 2010.

Available election forms

No US check-the-box election is available because the Bermuda Exempted Company is a per se corporation under US Treas. Reg. § 301.7701-2(b)(8). Gibraltar allows no election to opt out of corporation tax; a foreign-incorporated company may align its residence by ensuring that central management and control is, or is not, exercised in Gibraltar (Income Tax Act 2010). Gibraltar has adopted hybrid mismatch rules aligned with OECD BEPS Action 2 and ATAD, but no check-the-box or hybrid instrument election is available or relevant to this standard ISIC 6201 no-code website-builder structure.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar maintains a hard peg of the Gibraltar pound (GIP) to pound sterling (GBP) at par. There are no exchange controls restricting current or capital account transactions; profit remittances are freely permitted for companies holding a Gibraltar tax-exempt certificate (ETA) provided they satisfy the Economic Substance regulations. For a Bermuda Exempted Company not tax-resident in Gibraltar, distributions to shareholders are generally not subject to Gibraltar withholding tax, but cross-border payments must be supported by substance and beneficial ownership register filings to avoid classification as passive fund movements.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar does not, as a British Overseas Territory, require export proceeds to be surrendered to a central authority. There is no mandatory conversion quota or statutory timeline. Export receipts may be credited to any segregated account in foreign currency and used freely for operational disbursements, subject to AML record-keeping and documentary evidence of the underlying delivery of services.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority for sterling); in Gibraltar, the Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the banking and financial regulator, and the Government of Gibraltar (Ministry of Finance) administers local currency issuance and national exchange-control formalities under the Gibraltar Currency Notes Act 1934 and Exchange Control Act 1972.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

High feasibility through UK-regulated electronic money institutions (EMIs) such as Wise, Revolut Business, and Bunq, which accept Gibraltar-incorporated companies as low-risk clients via remote onboarding; medium feasibility through EEA EMIs due to reduced SEPA passporting protections in Gibraltar. Legal constraints require the EMI to hold adequate e-money licenses, apply FATF-compliant KYC, and in some cases place limits on Bermuda-incorporated shareholders. A Gibraltar-registered branch office or local substance provider materially increases EMI approval rates.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound funding of a Gibraltar treasury requires SWIFT MT103 wire transfers from the parent/Bermuda account, using a GIP/GBP or USD purpose code such as 'Capital / contribution to working capital', 'Intercompany funding', or 'Shareholder loan'. Supporting verification must include (i) an inbound remittance letter from the beneficiary bank, (ii) the underlying board resolution authorizing the capital placement or loan, (iii) the duly executed facility agreement, and (iv) the beneficial ownership register and tax-residence evidence. Funds should land in a designated Capital Escrow Account or segregated operational account to allow clearing and reconciliation.Outbound clearing is executed through SWIFT MT103 for general disbursements, MT202 COV for interbank transfers, and optionally a Commercial Letter of Credit (LC) for contractual services procurement. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

10% flat corporate income tax on chargeable income accruing in or derived from Gibraltar (Income Tax Act 2010, s.8); the Bermuda domicile of the exempted company imposes a 0% corporate income tax rate at the sovereign level.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by Gibraltar-resident companies to any shareholder, including the Bermuda parent and other non-resident recipients; there is no domestic dividend WHT legislation.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Return due within 9 months of the accounting period end (i.e., 30 September for a 31 December year-end) unless the Commissioner's return notice under s.41 Income Tax Act 2010 prescribes a different date; the tax assessed is payable within 30 days of the notice of assessment, with interim payments on account collectable where the prior-year charge exceeds the de minimis.

Late or non-filing triggers an automatic penalty of GBP 300 plus a daily surcharge of up to GBP 60; inaccurate returns attract a penalty of up to 100% of the tax undercharged, reducible for prompted voluntary disclosure; fraudulent returns are prosecuted criminally with an unlimited fine and imprisonment of up to 7 years; late payments bear statutory interest from the due date.
Box 11 — Tax computation Net chargeable income multiplied by the 10% flat rate; deduct interim payments, credits, and unilateral relief; state net tax payable or repayable. Nil charge where no Gibraltar-source income arises; file nonetheless to obtain a nil assessment and preserve the statutory limitation period. Late or non-filing triggers an automatic penalty of GBP 300 plus a daily surcharge of up to GBP 60; inaccurate returns attract a penalty of up to 100% of the tax undercharged, reducible for prompted voluntary disclosure; fraudulent returns are prosecuted criminally with an unlimited fine and imprisonment of up to 7 years; late payments bear statutory interest from the due date.
Box 12 — Declarations and ESR attachment Director declarations, audit opinion summary, ESR relevant-activity notification (Y/N), and beneficial-ownership register reference. A Bermuda exempted company earning IP income must complete the Gibraltar Economic Substance declaration and annex the substance report. Late or non-filing triggers an automatic penalty of GBP 300 plus a daily surcharge of up to GBP 60; inaccurate returns attract a penalty of up to 100% of the tax undercharged, reducible for prompted voluntary disclosure; fraudulent returns are prosecuted criminally with an unlimited fine and imprisonment of up to 7 years; late payments bear statutory interest from the due date.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File: required where aggregate controlled transactions exceed GBP 1,000,000 per annum (GIP pegged 1:1 to sterling); Master File: required where Gibraltar group turnover exceeds GBP 50,000,000; all controlled transactions below the threshold must still be supported by contemporaneous arm's-length evidence on demand.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance (Companies and Partnerships) Act 2019 (ESR) governs. The no-code website-builder business that owns or licenses software IP is an 'Intellectual Property Business' relevant activity, requiring: (i) core income-generating activities performed in Gibraltar (development supervision, licensing, versioning, support); (ii) adequate qualified employees, premises, and expenditure proportionate to the IP; (iii) annual ESR filing alongside the tax return; and (iv) board minutes evidencing management and control in Gibraltar. A Bermuda exempted company that is only paper-registered in Gibraltar, or that manages its IP from the UK, fails the substance test; conversely, an entity wholly managed and controlled in Bermuda with no Gibraltar-source activity has no Gibraltar tax or ESR exposure. The Tax-Exempt-Zone classification does not displace ESR obligations where any Gibraltar-source IP income is earned.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Misstatement

Statutory sanction: Assessment of the full tax evaded plus penalties up to 100% of the undercharge with aggravated rates for concealment; criminal prosecution for fraudulent evasion of income tax with an unlimited fine and imprisonment up to 7 years; asset-freezing orders, income-netting orders, and restitution of evaded sums with statutory interest.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners who knowingly participate in or by neglect permit the offence are personally prosecuted; courts may issue travel bans and passport-surrender orders during confiscation proceedings, impose personal restitution orders, and disqualify directors for up to 15 years under the Gibraltar Companies Act.
