E-government filing portal

A foreign Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) seeking Gibraltar tax nexus must first file an application for registration as a limited partnership under the Limited Partnerships Act and obtain a certificate of registration from the Gibraltar Companies House (Registrar of Companies). Thereafter, the ELP must submit Form P01 (Partnership Registration for Taxation) to the Commissioner of Income Tax and obtain a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN). Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar maintains full current and capital account convertibility. The GIP is freely convertible into GBP at parity and into other major international currencies at floating market rates. Non-resident partners of a Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership may remit profits, returns of capital, and interest without exchange‑control authorisation or withholding tax. The only practical constraints arise from AML/CFT documentary obligations, source‑of‑funds verification, and enhanced due diligence triggered by the offshore pass‑through structure.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no statutorily prescribed export proceeds surrender timelines or mandatory conversion ratios under Gibraltar exchange control legislation. All foreign‑currency export receipts may be retained, freely utilised, or converted through authorised banks. Exporters are required to maintain auditable payment records and comply with GFSC AML reporting thresholds (currently set at EUR 10,000 for entry into the vigilance register).

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) acts as the regulatory authority for banking and financial services; the Bank of England provides the monetary anchor through the statutory fixed parity of the Gibraltar pound (GIP) to the British pound (GBP) at 1:1. No separate central bank with autonomous exchange‑control competence exists.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Medium-High feasibility. UK/FCA-authorised EMIs (e.g., Wise Business, Revolut Business) and EU/EEA EMIs provide fast multi-currency clearing in GBP, EUR, and USD. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on dividends, and distributions made by an Exempted Limited Partnership to non-resident partners are not subject to Gibraltar deduction at source.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 aggregate annual value of controlled transactions with related parties; Country-by-Country Reporting applies where the multinational group's consolidated revenue equals or exceeds EUR 750,000,000. Local File and Master File documentation must be prepared and maintained in line with OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines once the threshold is exceeded.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations 2019 apply. Pure industrial quick-freezing is not automatically a named relevant activity, but a factory that also conducts group procurement, distribution, or service functions is within the 'distribution and service business' category. The Gibraltar ELP must demonstrate locally directed board meetings, qualified technical staff, adequate premises, and operating expenditure proportionate to the quick-freezing and dim-sum factory activities; failing this, the tax exemption may be denied.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Self-Assessment

Statutory sanction: Under the Income Tax Act 2010, fraudulent or negligent under-declaration attracts an additional penalty of up to 100% of tax evaded, compound interest, and criminal fines up to GIP 100,000; upon indictment, imprisonment of up to 7 years may be imposed. The Commissioner may also raise a protective estimated assessment and enforce immediate collection.

Director personal liability: Directors and the general partner who knowingly authorise false returns are personally liable for civil restitution; directors may be disqualified under Gibraltar company law, subjected to travel bans and passport surrender, and sentenced to imprisonment. The corporate veil will be pierced where the ELP is used as a mere facade for tax evasion.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Penalties under the Economic Substance (Gibraltar) Regulations 2019 escalate from GIP 10,000 for the first failure to GIP 40,000 for the second and GIP 80,000 for subsequent failures; the Registrar may strike off the ELP, revoke its tax exemption, and cause all previously exempt profits to be reassessed at 12.5%.

Director personal liability: The directors of a corporate general partner face personal financial penalties, disqualification orders, and civil recovery actions for reckless non-compliance. Strict travel bans and asset freezes may be imposed where the entity is being used as a conduit for untaxed proceeds.
AML, Sanctions, and Suspicious Transaction Reporting

Statutory sanction: The Proceeds of Crime Act and applicable Money Laundering Regulations impose unlimited fines, asset confiscation, and imprisonment for up to 14 years for money laundering offences. The Gibraltar Financial Services Commission may suspend bank facilities and impose supervisory penalties for defective AML controls, even in a 'Low_Risk_Standard' food-processing classification.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners are personally exposed to confiscation orders, civil penalties, travel bans, and imprisonment for deliberate AML non-compliance. Personal liability attaches where the director knew or suspected that the ELP's payment rails were being used to disguise the proceeds of tax evasion.
