Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

For the Gibraltar entity intended as the registered presence of the cross-border courier business, a virtual office is permissible for registered office purposes only if it satisfies the Gibraltar Companies Act 2014 requirement that the registered office be physically in Gibraltar, open to the public, and accessible during normal business hours. A pure virtual office that is merely a mailing address without physical reception, document storage, and accessibility is NOT acceptable. The registered office must be staffed by a licensed Gibraltar corporate services provider or law firm. For the Cayman Exempted Company, a registered office in the Cayman Islands at the address of the registered office provider is mandatory. Virtual office permissibility for operational substance: not permitted for a company claiming tax residence or economic substance in Gibraltar; however, because the composite structure is a Cayman Exempted Company (tax-exempt) operating through a Gibraltar branch or using Gibraltar as the operational hub, the Gibraltar branch must maintain real and substantive economic presence. Given ISIC 5320 cross-border express courier activity, the Gibraltar entity must retain a local branch office with an operations manager, warehousing or customs clearance facilities (licensed by HM Customs of Gibraltar), and at least one employee in Gibraltar. Pure virtual offices create a 50% risk of challenge under Gibraltar's economic substance rules and a 35% risk of UK anti-avoidance challenge under the composite structure.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar Income Tax Office (ITO), HM Government of Gibraltar, with official portal at https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation; indirect tax and customs matters are handled by HM Customs and Excise Department, Gibraltar.

E-government filing portal

A Cayman Exempted Company that carries on a taxable trade in Gibraltar (or has a Gibraltar permanent establishment or locally managed place of business) must register for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) by filing the company tax registration form with the Gibraltar Income Tax Office. The filing must include certified copies of the Cayman Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles of Association, register of directors/members, and evidence of the appointment of a Gibraltar tax residence/contact agent. The Income Tax Office evaluates the extent of Gibraltar-source income and, if satisfied, issues the TRN within 10-15 working days. Where the company has no Gibraltar nexus, no TRN is required. There is no VAT/GST tax identifier in Gibraltar; however, if the company physically imports or exports goods, it must additionally apply to HM Customs and Excise Department for an economic operator customs registration before making declarations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a check-the-box or comparable transparency election. A Cayman Exempted Company may, if it qualifies, apply for status as a Gibraltar Qualifying (Non-Resident) Company, which permits exemption from Gibraltar corporation tax on foreign-source income in exchange for an annual government fee. No hybrid mismatch election is required unless the entity becomes fiscally transparent in another jurisdiction, in which case the UK hybrid mismatch rules (Part 6A TIOPA 2010) may require an election or filing to align tax treatment.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

N/A - Gibraltar imposes no VAT, GST, or sales/use tax; indirect taxation is limited to customs and excise duties on prescribed goods.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable; no VAT/GST registration threshold exists in Gibraltar. Any entity importing or exporting commercial consignments in the cross-border courier business must obtain an economic operator customs registration with HM Customs and Excise irrespective of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar does not operate a VAT/GST system, so the domestic export zero-rating concept does not exist. If the Cayman Exempted Company is separately registered for UK VAT (because it has UK-established fixed establishment or supplies services from the UK), UK VAT zero-rating may apply to exports of goods where the exporter obtains and retains proof of export within the prescribed time limits under VAT Notice 700/6. Qualifying courier services may be zero-rated when the recipient belongs in a third country or the transport relates to exported goods; all other services are outside the scope of UK VAT under place-of-supply rules.

  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT credit advice evidencing overseas payment
  • Signed proof of delivery from destination customs/consignee

Gibraltar has no legal requirement for electronic invoicing, digital stamping, or real-time fiscal clearance. Customs entries must be transmitted electronically through the Gibraltar customs EDI/automated declaration system; no continuous invoice reporting to the tax authority is mandated.

Unsupported or fraudulent export zero-rating claims attract HMRC interest and penalty assessments up to 100% of the VAT evaded for deliberate concealment (30% for careless errors), with a normal assessment window of 4 years extended to 20 years for deliberate inaccuracies. In Gibraltar, false customs or tax declarations constitute fiscal offences punishable by fines of up to three times the duty/tax evaded and imprisonment of up to seven years; retrospective clawback may be enforced within the general statutory assessment periods for negligence or fraud, and refusal of export evidence will automatically invalidate any claimed exemption and require repayment of all avoided tax or duty.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The UK and Gibraltar maintain unrestricted current-account and capital-account convertibility. There are no exchange controls, no central-bank approval for foreign-currency accounts, and no restrictions on the repatriation of dividends, management fees, or intercompany loan repayments. A Cayman Exempted Company may freely remit profits from its Gibraltar/UK bank accounts to shareholders provided each flow is evidenced by a board resolution, transfer-pricing documentation, and an unbroken source-of-funds trail. The only meaningful convertibility hurdle is the enhanced AML/EDD scrutiny triggered by the Cayman tax-exempt classification, which is neutralized by proving genuine economic substance in the GI/GB operating chain and arm's-length conduct of the express-courier trade.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Neither Gibraltar nor the UK imposes any mandatory surrender, conversion ratio, or fixed retention timeline on export proceeds. The cross-border express-courier enterprise may invoice in GBP, EUR, or USD; retain 100% of foreign-currency receipts; and convert at commercial interbank rates at its discretion. Records of export-related credits, including airway bills, customs entries, commercial invoices, and bank statements, must be retained for five years under the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and the UK Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing Regulations 2019, but no reporting to the central bank is required for lawful trade proceeds.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (BoE) is the apex monetary authority for the sterling area to which the Gibraltar pound (GIP) is pegged at 1:1 with full convertibility and mandatory backing. The Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the integrated prudential and conduct regulator for all banks, e-money institutions, and payment service providers licensed in Gibraltar. HM Treasury retains extraterritorial sanctions, anti-money-laundering, and counter-terrorist-financing policy sovereignty over both the UK and Gibraltar, giving the BoE/GFSC combined supervisory architecture.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

High for FCA-authorized EMIs such as Wise Business, Airwallex, Currencycloud, and Revolut Business, and for GFSC-licensed e-money institutions, provided onboarding routes through each EMI's institutional/commercial desk rather than the automated mass-market flow. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder capital is injected from the Cayman treasury or the UK parent via SWIFT MT103 / ISO 20022 pacs.008 credit transfer with purpose code 'EQUI' for equity or 'LEND' for shareholder loans, and the remittance narrative 'share capital contribution per board resolution dated [date].' US ACH Direct customer receipts are pooled in a USD virtual-account suite of the EMI and swept via CHIPS/Fedwire as an MT103/MT202 to the GI bank's USD correspondent, then converted to GBP/GIP per the pre-agreed FX forward. Card-POS receipts are captured by the merchant acquirer, settled through the Visa/Mastercard scheme, and swept daily to the same EMI wallet for consolidation. Any inbound transfer exceeding GBP 25,000 requires the Cayman board resolution, the register of allotments showing fully paid shares, a source-of-funds statement from the remitting bank, and a short-term treasury forecast confirming the influx is not a disguised repayment of connected-party debt.Supplier payments for airline blocked-space cargo agreements, last-mile delivery partners (DHL, UPS, Royal Mail), customs brokers, and IT/telecom platforms execute as follows: SWIFT MT103 for non-UK/non-EEA counterparties; Faster Payments or CHAPS for UK vendors; SEPA SCT/SCT Inst for European partners; and Fedwire or ACH for US suppliers. Every MT103 carries the supplier invoice number, airway-bill reference, and an FX purpose classification such as 'cargo handling' or 'transport brokerage,' and is reconciled monthly against the IATA Cargo Account Settlement System (CASS) statement where interline capacity is procured. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: High for FCA-authorized EMIs such as Wise Business, Airwallex, Currencycloud, and Revolut Business, and for GFSC-licensed e-money institutions, provided onboarding routes through each EMI's institutional/commercial desk rather than the automated mass-market flow. Legal constraints: (i) the EMI applies enhanced due diligence to any Cayman-incorporated tax-exempt entity and may reject the file without a full substance package; (ii) EMIs cannot lawfully extend credit or pay interest, so the GI bank account must remain the final settlement anchor; (iii) under the UK Payment Services Regulations 2017 and the EU-derived e-money framework, directors must exercise genuine control over payment initiation; and (iv) EMIs generally permit a documented courier trade-flow, including US ACH Direct collection and card-POS acquiring settlement, but hard-commit in writing that no sanctioned jurisdictions are served.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File: total Gibraltar annual turnover exceeding GIP 7.5 million, or controlled cross-border related-party transactions exceeding GIP 1 million; Master File: group consolidated revenue exceeding GIP 50 million. All thresholds denominated in Gibraltar Pounds.

Economic substance scope: The Cayman Exempted Company is subject to the Cayman Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) and Gibraltar's economic substance requirements. Because the express courier activity is managed and controlled in Gibraltar, the company must demonstrate real presence, qualified staff, premises, and core income-generating functions; a mere letter-box office will not satisfy either jurisdiction's substance test and will trigger anti-avoidance reclassification.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Understatement

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of 100% of the understated tax, plus statutory interest; criminal conviction for fraudulent evasion of tax may result in imprisonment for up to seven years and unlimited fines.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly authorise false returns are jointly and severally liable for the tax loss; the court may pierce the corporate veil, issue travel bans and confiscation orders, and disqualify directors for up to ten years.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Cayman ESR penalties start at CI$12,000 for the first failure, CI$10,000 per additional month and possible strike-off; Gibraltar can recharacterise the entity as a Gibraltar resident company and reassess at 10%, adding penalties up to 100%.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal enforcement notices, a ban on obtaining working permits or visas, and liability in tort for continuing violations; persistent non-compliance may lead to disqualification and criminal sanctions.
Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Failure

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit and GFSC may impose a fine of up to 10% of annual turnover or GBP 1,000,000 per breach; criminal money-laundering convictions carry up to 14 years' imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Directors are individually criminally liable if they approve deficient AML systems; they may be disqualified, subject to civil restitution, travel bans, and personal confiscation orders.
Transfer Pricing Non-Disclosure and Excessive Intercompany Payments

Statutory sanction: Tax authority may make an arm's-length adjustment, disallow the deduction, add interest and a further penalty of 50-100% of the adjusted tax under the anti-avoidance provisions.

Director personal liability: Where directors approved undocumented or sham charges, personal liability attaches to the additional tax and penalties; the corporate veil will be pierced to the extent of the unjust enrichment.
