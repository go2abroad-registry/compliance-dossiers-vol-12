Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Cayman Islands: Cayman Islands General Registry, Registrar of Companies — ciregistry.gov.ky; Cayman Islands Tax Information Authority (TIA), Ministry of Finance & Economic Development — tia.gov.ky; Customs & Border Control for import/export physical goods. Gibraltar (where economic presence is created): Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar — gibraltar.gov.gi; HM Customs Gibraltar. United Kingdom (where UK-resident owners or a UK parent exist): HM Revenue & Customs — gov.uk/hmrc.

E-government filing portal

For the Cayman Exempted Company: (1) file Memorandum and Articles of Association with the Cayman General Registry through a licensed registered-office provider and obtain a Certificate of Incorporation and Registration Number; (2) pay the annual exempted-company government fee and file the annual return; (3) register with the Cayman Islands Tax Information Authority (TIA) where FATCA/CRS or economic-substance filing applies, obtaining a TIA reference number; (4) apply under the Companies Act for a tax exemption certificate to secure Cayman direct-tax exemption; (5) if central management and control is exercised from Gibraltar, register the company with the Gibraltar Income Tax Office as a taxable company and obtain a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number; (6) if a UK-resident shareholder controls the entity, ensure HMRC Corporation Tax, CFC, and transfer-pricing filings are in place; the Cayman entity itself has no UK registration merely for being tax-exempt offshore.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (Cayman statutory status: no Cayman tax on profits, income, capital gains, estate, inheritance, or VAT; only annual fees and reporting obligations). Caveat: if the entity is managed and controlled from Gibraltar or maintained a permanent establishment there, Gibraltar applies Entity_Level_Taxation at 12.5% to Gibraltar-source and assessable foreign-source income; UK CFC rules may also attribute undistributed offshore profits to any UK-resident shareholder.

Available election forms

Cayman: election to incorporate as an exempted company; application for a tax-exemption undertaking under section 6(1) of the Companies Act guaranteeing no future Cayman direct taxes; no Cayman check-the-box election. Gibraltar: election of accounting period; election to exclude foreign-source income where management, control, and economic activities are outside Gibraltar; participation-exemption election for qualifying dividends; hybrid-mismatch treatment is constrained by OECD BEPS Action 2 and UK/Gibraltar anti-hybrid legislation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST in Cayman or Gibraltar; indirect taxes are limited to Cayman import/customs duty and Gibraltar import/excise duty. UK VAT at 20% may apply separately only where the Cayman entity or a UK group company makes taxable supplies to UK consumers through a UK VAT-registration nexus or an online marketplace/merchant-of-record arrangement.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A in Cayman and Gibraltar because no VAT/GST turnover threshold exists. For UK VAT purposes, a non-UK supplier may need to register if it makes taxable supplies of digitally supplied services to UK consumers above the relevant HMRC threshold or through a payment/marketplace operator; physical imports into Cayman require Customs & Border Control registration or an import permit.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

There is no VAT/GST legislation in Cayman or Gibraltar, so exported digital services attract 0% indirect tax in those territories by default. If the company were to be treated as a Gibraltar taxable company, receipts for services supplied to non-Gibraltar consumers would qualify as foreign-source where the services are carried out, managed, and delivered outside Gibraltar; the customer's non-residency, place of effective use, API access logs, and written contracts are decisive evidence. A UK VAT registration, if any, would require statutory zero-rating evidence for B2B exports to non-UK businesses.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Cayman Customs e-Form or Gibraltar SAD) only where physical media or hardware is exported; not applicable to pure cloud SaaS delivery
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill for any physical consignment; for digital delivery, use API access logs and service-usage records as the delivery proof
  • Inward Remittance Certificate, SWIFT MT103, or Stripe/Paddle settlement report evidencing receipt of export proceeds in foreign currency
  • Signed SaaS subscription agreement, order form, and pricing schedule identifying customer jurisdiction, place of performance, and type of service
  • Cayman Companies Act tax exemption certificate and, if claimed, Gibraltar foreign-source income working papers or economic-substance documentation

None in Cayman or Gibraltar; no real-time fiscal clearance, API e-invoicing, digital stamping, or mandatory B2B e-invoicing regime applies to SaaS/SEO analytics tools. Invoices should comply with Gibraltar record-keeping requirements (six years) and Cayman AML record retention (five years); Stripe/Paddle as merchant of record may generate tax-compliant invoices for UK/EU consumer VAT.

No export zero-rating refund or clawback regime exists in Cayman or Gibraltar because there is no VAT. However, false reliance on the Cayman tax exemption or Gibraltar foreign-source treatment may trigger reassessment and penalties: Gibraltar Income Tax Act penalties up to 100% of the under-assessed tax plus interest for deliberate understatement; Cayman economic-substance false declarations attract CI$20,000 penalties and up to six months imprisonment; TIA CRS/FATCA non-extraction or false filing penalties may reach CI$10,000 per obligation; retrospective assessments generally cover six years, with no limitation period for fraud.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar maintains full current-account and capital-account convertibility. The Gibraltar pound is pegged at parity to GBP and is freely movable. Profit and dividend remittances from the Cayman Exempted Company's Gibraltar treasury account can be executed without prior exchange-control approval, and no statutory withholding is imposed at source. Practical hurdles are AML/KYC-driven, not FX-driven: the bank requires a valid legal existence certificate, UBO declaration and economic substance explanation before capital repatriation; for MarTech/SaaS collections, the merchant acquiring arrangement must be in the entity's name.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender requirement applies in Gibraltar. Export and SEO/SaaS subscription proceeds may be retained in the currency of invoice, partially converted, or fully converted into GIP/GBP/EUR/USD at a tier-1 bank rate. All receipts must be traceable by SWIFT reference or merchant settlement report and matched to a commercial invoice or contract; supporting records must be retained for six years under GFSC AML/CFT requirements.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) acting as the integrated banking, payments and exchange-control supervisor, together with the Government of Gibraltar Currency Office responsible for the Gibraltar pound issue; no separate central bank exists for Gibraltar, and sterling-denominated monetary operations remain anchored to the Bank of England framework.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

High feasibility from a legal perspective, moderate practical capacity. Fully licensed Gibraltar- or UK-authorised electronic-money institutions can onboard the Cayman Exempted Company remotely and provide GBP/EUR IBANs, Stripe/Paddle settlement wrappers and domestic clearing access. However, EMIs cannot extend credit, issue commercial letters of credit, or provide escrow-linked trade financing; transaction velocity limits may restrict monthly treasury flows. A GFSC-licensed EMI is suitable for e-money float and subscription receivables, while a tier-1 bank remains necessary for capital escrow, trade settlement and FX hedging.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File: Gibraltar-resident group with annual turnover exceeding GBP 10,000,000 and related-party cross-border transactions exceeding GBP 1,000,000 per annum. Mandatory Master File: group consolidation turnover exceeding GBP 500,000,000. For lower-turnover structures, GITO can demand transactional documentation under anti-avoidance enquiry powers when profit margin of the Gibraltar entity is substantially below the OECD TNMM benchmark.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar has enacted Economic Substance Regulations consistent with OECD and EU Code of Conduct requirements. A Cayman Exempted Company claiming Gibraltar tax residence must demonstrate substantive activity: board meetings held in Gibraltar, independent directors, physically present employees, marketing and sales management decisions taken in Gibraltar, and purpose-fit office premises. The relevant activity for ISIC 6201 / NAICS 541511 is data processing and analytics services; the pure equity holding exemption does not apply. Failure to meet substance requirements causes disclosure of the relationship under exchange of information to the Cayman Islands authorities and reclassification as a shell company.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Criminal prosecution under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act and Criminal Justice Ordinance; unpaid tax recovery with compound interest; fines of up to GBP 500,000; forfeiture of assets derived from the evasion; compulsory strike-off of the exempted company.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign a false return or cause diversion of profits are jointly and severally liable for the tax plus civil restitution; travel bans can be imposed under Gibraltar proceeds of crime procedures; custodial sentence of up to seven years is applicable.
Shell Company and False Substance Declaration

Statutory sanction: ESR penalty notice of GBP 10,000 for the first year and GBP 100,000 for repeated failure; cancellation of tax residence status; automatic exchange of information to the Cayman Islands and the UK; re-opening of previous assessments with 20% additional charge.

Director personal liability: Each director can face a personal penalty of GBP 50,000 for authorising a false substance statement; disqualification of directors for up to 15 years; loss of limited liability protection for intercompany creditors because the corporate veil is pierced where the structure is a fraudulent façade.
Non-Arm's-Length Transfer Pricing

Statutory sanction: GITO transfer pricing adjustment to an arm's-length result at 12.5%; deficiency interest; accuracy penalty of 50% of the additional tax; criminal surcharge of 100% where the TP file is fabricated after 30 days.

Director personal liability: When an unrealistic TP policy is knowingly documented, directors are jointly liable for any tax deficiency; civil fraud proceedings can be brought by the Crown; where the company becomes insolvent, personal liability reaches the UK super-dominant director under the Insolvency Act ordering assets to be surrendered.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Violation

Statutory sanction: Failure to file beneficial ownership return attracts a GBP 5,000 civil fine and daily default fine of GBP 1,000; money laundering can be prosecuted with unlimited fines, confiscation orders under the Proceeds of Crime Act, and up to 14 years' imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally exposed to investigatory orders including unexplained wealth orders; intentional concealment is an imprisonable offence; travel restrictions are granted against the individual, and a charging order attaches to their personal property including UK real estate.
Failure to File and DTA Misclaim

Statutory sanction: Automatic estimated assessments may be issued and become conclusive if unanswered; late filing penalties build to GBP 60 per day with judicial enforcement; fraudulent treaty relief claim is assessed at double the normal tax with a 100% penalty.

Director personal liability: Directors can be pursued before the Magistrates' Court for criminal non-filing; disqualification orders for repeated default; personal civil liability is triggered by the company's inability to pay when directors diverted the taxeable proceeds or paid dividends without reserving tax.
