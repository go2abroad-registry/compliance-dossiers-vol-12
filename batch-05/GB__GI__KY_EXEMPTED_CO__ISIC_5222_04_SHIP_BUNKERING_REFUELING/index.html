Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar Income Tax Office (ITO), acting through the Commissioner of Income Tax; official portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax-office. For customs, import duty and ship's stores control: HM Customs Gibraltar, portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/customs. Gibraltar is fiscally autonomous from the United Kingdom; HM Revenue & Customs is not the Gibraltar tax authority.

E-government filing portal

A Cayman Exempted Company has no automatic Gibraltar TIN. Registration with the ITO is required only if the company establishes a branch or permanent establishment in Gibraltar, is centrally managed and controlled in Gibraltar, or carries on a taxable trade in Gibraltar through an agency or other taxable presence. Procedure: (i) appoint a Gibraltar-resident tax representative or process agent; (ii) file the company registration notice with the ITO together with certified Cayman Certificate of Incorporation, constitutional documents, register of directors and members, and evidence of the Gibraltar contact address; (iii) obtain the Gibraltar Assessment Number / TIN; (iv) register separately with HM Customs Gibraltar for any bonded marine fuel or ship's stores account and obtain a customs trader identifier; (v) file annual returns even if no tax is payable. A non-resident Cayman company with no Gibraltar-source profits is not required to complete income-tax registration, but a nil or explanatory return is recommended to evidence non-residence.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no check-the-box, transparency election, or hybrid-mismatch entity classification under domestic tax law. The Cayman home-law classification of the entity is Tax_Exempt_Zone, but that status does not confer an exemption from Gibraltar-source income tax. Available elections are limited to evidencing residence or non-residence by central management and control, registering the foreign Cayman company as an overseas company branch under the Companies Act, claiming capital allowances or reliefs in the Gibraltar corporation tax return, and filing transitional OECD GloBE/Pillar Two safe harbour notifications where the group qualifies.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST or general sales-and-use tax in Gibraltar; the relevant indirect-tax regime is Gibraltar customs import duty and excise on specified goods. Marine fuels supplied as ship's stores to vessels on international voyages may be removed duty-suspended from bonded storage and are outside the scope of Gibraltar VAT.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — Gibraltar has no VAT/GST turnover registration threshold. For customs-duty purposes there is no turnover minimum; a duty-suspension and ship's stores account requires registration with HM Customs Gibraltar and, where required, provision of a customs security or bank guarantee.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar does not operate a VAT export-zero-rating system and therefore no 0% VAT refund claim exists. Marine bunker fuel supplied from duty-suspended storage to a qualifying ship engaged in international navigation is treated as ship's stores: the customs duty is suspended or relieved, not zero-rated or repaid. Eligibility requires a qualifying sea-going vessel, physical delivery of the fuel as ship's stores, removal from bonded warehouse under customs control, and submission of a ship's stores declaration before the vessel departs Gibraltar.

  • Customs Export Declaration / Single Administrative Document (SAD) or outward clearance endorsement issued by HM Customs Gibraltar
  • Bill of Lading or equivalent marine delivery order / port log / vessel departure evidence
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank/FX settlement voucher evidencing repatriation of the export proceeds
  • Bunker Delivery Receipt (BDN) and commercial invoice aligned with the bunker supplier licence
  • Ship's Stores Certificate and vessel registration/IMO evidence confirming the international voyage

No real-time fiscal clearance, tax-authority e-invoicing, digital stamping, or mandatory API filing applies in Gibraltar for bunkering activities. HM Customs Gibraltar accepts electronic customs declarations via the Single Administrative Document / integrated customs process, and commercial invoices and Bunker Delivery Receipts may be transmitted electronically, but invoices are not required to be cleared or approved by the tax authority on an invoice-by-invoice basis.

There is no VAT zero-rating refund to claw back; the comparable exposure arises from customs duty suspension. If HM Customs Gibraltar determines that the vessel was not engaged in an international voyage, the fuel was misdeclared, or the ship's stores documentation is false or incomplete, it may retrospectively assess unpaid duty and interest, impose civil penalties up to 100% of the duty or the applicable statutory maximum penalty, forfeit goods and conveyances, and refer fraudulent evasion for criminal prosecution. The ITO and HM Customs may jointly open a six-year audit window for bunker suppliers, and the Cayman exempted status does not shield the Gibraltar branch from these liabilities.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar maintains full current-account and capital-account convertibility with no exchange-control approval required for inward equity injection, outward profit remittance, dividends, or trade-related payments by a non-resident Cayman Exempted Company. GIP/GBP parity removes local translation risk; the entity may freely hold and settle in USD, EUR, or GBP, and no Gibraltar withholding tax applies to outbound distributions to non-residents.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Gibraltar imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio for banked bunkering receivables. Export proceeds from marine fuel sales may be retained in original currency or converted at commercial rates; only GFSC-required AML/CTF large-cash thresholds and routine statistical balance-of-payments reporting to the bank apply.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar has no independent central bank. The Government of Gibraltar (Currency Notes Act 1934) is the sovereign issuer of the Gibraltar pound (GIP); the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the prudential and AML/CTF supervisor of all banks, payment institutions, and trade-finance providers; the Bank of England anchors the value of GIP through the statutory 1:1 peg to GBP.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low. UK- and EU-licensed digital EMIs such as Wise and Revolut Business typically refuse Cayman Exempted Companies due to offshore tax-haven classification and incomplete UBO transparency. GFSC-licensed electronic-money or payment-institution authorisations are scarce and equally conservative for non-resident offshore structures. An EMI solution is viable only as a white-label module operated behind a licensed Gibraltar bank; it is not recommended as the primary operating account for bunkering receivables.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Gibraltar does not impose any withholding tax on dividends paid by Gibraltar-resident companies. A Gibraltar branch of a Cayman Exempted Company may remit after-tax branch profits to the Cayman head office gross, and the Cayman entity may distribute onward to shareholders without Gibraltar WHT.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due by 30 November following the close of the Gibraltar Year of Assessment (1 July to 30 June) in which the company's accounting period ends. Account periods straddling two years of assessment are apportioned on a time or trading basis. Example: an accounting period ending 31 December 2024 is apportioned into YOA 2023/24 and YOA 2024/25; the YOA 2024/25 return and balancing payment are due by 30 November 2025. The Commissioner may require earlier filing on formal notice, particularly for regulated or licensed bunkering operators.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Gibraltar statutory Local File/Master File filing threshold is enacted under ITA 2010; the Commissioner may issue a formal TP information notice requiring contemporaneous documentation where connected-party transactions exceed GBP 1,000,000 in a fiscal period or the Gibraltar net margin falls outside the arm's-length range. Multinational groups with consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more must additionally maintain GloBE/Pillar Two computation records for the Gibraltar constituent entity.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar Economic Substance guidelines, derived from the EU Code of Conduct and ITA 2010 substance provisions, require any entity performing a 'relevant activity' — including shipping and marine bunkering support — to demonstrate adequate substance: a physical office in Gibraltar, resident directors with actual decision-making power, locally-based employees and core income-generating activities performed in Gibraltar. Failure to satisfy the test invalidates the Gibraltar TRC and exposes the entity to anti-avoidance re-assessment. In parallel, the Cayman Exempted Company remains subject to the Cayman Islands Economic Substance Regulations for its Cayman-head-office status and must file the annual ESR return; a shipping-relevant activity carried on without Cayman substance attracts ESR penalties and strike-off.
