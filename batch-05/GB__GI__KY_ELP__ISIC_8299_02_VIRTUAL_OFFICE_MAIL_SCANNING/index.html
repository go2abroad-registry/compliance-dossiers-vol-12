Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar Income Tax Office, Her Majesty's Government of Gibraltar; online filing and registration portal: Income Tax E-Tax / Self-Serve system (https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax).

E-government filing portal

A Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is fiscally transparent; it does not obtain a separate corporate TIN. The ELP's general partner (or designated tax representative) must submit Form TRN-01 (Partnership Tax Reference Number application) to the Income Tax Office, accompanied by the ELP's Certificate of Registration, the Limited Partnership agreement, proof of registered office address in Gibraltar, and detailed identification of each partner. In the absence of a VAT regime, no VAT ID registration is required. Non-resident partners are not required to register unless they derive Gibraltar-source income, in which case a partner-level TIN is issued under the Income Tax Act.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible — the ELP is treated as fiscally transparent; all profits and losses are attributed directly to partners, and no Gibraltar corporation tax is levied at the partnership level. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible — the ELP is treated as fiscally transparent; all profits and losses are attributed directly to partners, and no Gibraltar corporation tax is levied at the partnership level. The liability of limited partners is contractually capped, but tax attribution follows the economic entitlement in the partnership agreement.

Available election forms

Under Gibraltar's Income Tax (Assessment and Collection of Tax) Act, no explicit 'check-the-box' election exists. An ELP may evidence pass-through status by filing a partnership return with allocations substantiating each partner's share; it may also elect under the Limited Partnerships Act to be treated as an 'investment limited partnership' where qualifying income is exempt. Hybrid mismatch elections may be made in accordance with Gibraltar's implementation of the OECD BEPS Action 2 rules, primarily to neutralize an arrangement that is deductible in Gibraltar and non-taxable in the partner's jurisdiction; any such election must be filed with the Commissioner of Income Tax before the statutory filing deadline.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Not_Applicable — Gibraltar operates no VAT, GST, or general sales and use tax; only customs and excise duties are imposed on physical imports into Gibraltar, and certain duties on alcohol, tobacco, and fuel are collected via the Customs Department.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — there is no VAT/GST turnover threshold. Registration with Gibraltar Customs is required solely for importers/exporters of controlled or dutiable goods; such registration is independent of turnover and applies from the first import or export transaction.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — because Gibraltar does not levy VAT or GST, there is no domestic export zero-rating provision to claim. For goods physically exported from Gibraltar, customs duty is not charged unless the goods are subject to excise/export controls. For a virtual office and mail scanning business, any cross-border service invoiced to an overseas recipient is outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect taxation; it is not a 'VAT export' for Gibraltar purposes. If the ELP also incurs UK VAT costs in mainland UK and invoiced services fall under the UK place of supply rules, UK VAT 'zero-rating' may apply only where the recipient is a UK VAT- and HMRC-registered business or the place of supply is outside the UK; such treatment is administered by HMRC, not the Gibraltar tax authorities.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD/Entry Summary Declaration) lodged in the Gibraltar Customs electronic system, where physical goods are exported
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or CMR consignment note evidencing physical movement from Gibraltar to a destination outside Gibraltar
  • Commercial Invoice and underlying service contract describing the exact counterperformance for virtual office/mail scanning services
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank transfer documentation evidencing actual receipt of foreign-currency consideration

No real-time fiscal clearance, B2B/B2C e-invoicing mandate, digital stamping, or API-based clearance regime applies in Gibraltar. Service providers, including virtual office and mail scanning businesses, may issue ordinary commercial invoices. For physical goods flows, customs declarations must be filed through the Gibraltar Customs electronic declarant system, using a customs-compliant SAD/Entry Summary Declaration, and may be subject to random post-clearance audit.

As no VAT zero-rating regime exists in Gibraltar, there is no positive clawback of a tax credit. However, false declarations to the Customs Department or a fraudulent attempt to secure duty-free status attract penalties of up to 100% of the excise or customs duty evaded, plus default interest at 8% per annum. Where the ELP or its partners rely on UK or other VAT zero-rating in a foreign jurisdiction, any misapplication may be subject to the foreign tax authority's clawback, interest, and penalties; in Gibraltar, criminal prosecution for tax fraud may lead to imprisonment of up to seven years and unlimited fines.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 aggregate annual cross-border related-party transactions; a Master File is required where the combined group turnover exceeds GIP 50,000,000, and a Local File is required at GIP 250,000 for intra-group service transactions.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) require any Gibraltar tax-resident entity undertaking 'relevant activities' to demonstrate economic substance. Digital mail scanning and virtual-office CMRA operations are not explicitly enumerated as relevant activities; however, if the ELP acts as a distribution or service centre supporting foreign group companies, it will be subject to ESR scrutiny. A genuine ELP should maintain a physical presence, at least one local director/management person, scanning staff physically present in Gibraltar, and board minutes reflecting strategic control. A pure mailbox operation lacking local management, premises, and commercial substance will be presumed to fail the ESR test, exposing all profits to re-characterisation and penalty.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (Fiscal Fraud)

Statutory sanction: A fine of up to 250% of the tax underdeclared; confiscation of assets equal to the hidden profits; publication of a 'serious default' notice; liability to criminal prosecution under the Income Tax Act 2010 and the Criminal Justice (Money Laundering) Act 2021.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil will be pierced where the ELP is used as a mere sham; directors and general partners are jointly and severally liable for tax not withheld or evaded. Individual culpability carries imprisonment of up to 3 years, a disgorgement order for the full hidden tax plus interest, and a travel ban pending extradition or arrest.
Shell Company / Substance Avoidance

Statutory sanction: Compulsory strike-off under the Companies Act (Gibraltar); a fine of GIP 10,000 for each ESR breach; denial of treaty benefits and revocation of the ELP's exempt status; a prohibition order issued by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission.

Director personal liability: Directors may be subject to a 'wrongful trading' order requiring them to contribute assets of the ELP to satisfy tax liabilities. Persistent substantive non-compliance results in criminal liability with imprisonment up to 14 years, disqualification from serving as a director of any UK or Gibraltar company, and an order to repay all professional fees received.
AML/CFT Breach (Medium-Risk CMRA)

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty up to GIP 500,000 or 10% of attributed turnover; criminal sanction for failing to report a suspicious transaction to the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit; account freezing orders and mandatory revocation of the CMRA licence under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015.

Director personal liability: Every partner, director, and compliance officer of the general partner is criminally liable unless they prove they took all reasonable steps. Penalties include imprisonment of up to 7 years, a financial penalty not exceeding GIP 1 million, and personal confiscation of assets purchased from mail-scanning revenues.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Default

Statutory sanction: An additional assessment of up to 30% of undercharged tax; a fixed penalty of GIP 50,000 for each document not produced, capped at GIP 250,000; a daily default fine of GIP 500 for each day of continued refusal to produce documentation.

Director personal liability: Directors of the general partner may be joined in the assessment where the ELP or the corporate partner is insolvent and cannot pay; the court may issue a personal restitution order for the full underpaid tax, interest, and penalties, and impose a UK-wide travel restriction pending payment.
