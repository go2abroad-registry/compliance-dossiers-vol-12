Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar, 3 Secretary's Lane, Gibraltar; HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC), 100 Parliament Street, London SW1A 2BQ, United Kingdom; and Cayman Islands Tax Information Authority for Cayman TIN issuance. Online portals: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation; https://www.gov.uk/hmrc.

E-government filing portal

A Cayman Exempted Company is not required to register for direct tax in the Cayman Islands merely by reason of incorporation. Obtain a Cayman Islands Tax Reference Number from the Cayman Tax Information Authority only if FATCA/CRS, AML onboarding, or a banking relationship demands it. If the company establishes a permanent establishment or commences a taxable trade in Gibraltar, it must register with the Commissioner of Income Tax within 30 days of commencement and obtain a Gibraltar tax reference number; if it hires employees, it must also register as a Gibraltarian employer. Gibraltar does not levy VAT or GST, so no Gibraltar VAT identification number exists. A UK VAT registration with HMRC is required only if taxable supplies are made from a UK business establishment; it is not required for a purely Gibraltarian export operation.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Cayman election: an Exempted Company may apply to the Governor-in-Cabinet for a Tax Exemption Undertaking under the Cayman Islands Companies Act, evidenced by a certificate, which preserves Cayman tax-exempt status for a stated period, typically 20 years, and prevents future Cayman direct taxation on profits, capital gains, and distributable income. No US-style check-the-box election exists in Gibraltar or the Cayman Islands. In Gibraltar, the entity may be assessed on a territorial-source basis if it is not centrally managed or controlled in Gibraltar; Gibraltar group relief and unilateral double-taxation relief elections are available under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act if the entity becomes Gibraltar tax-resident or joins a Gibraltarian group.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar excise duty and customs duty regime; no VAT or GST

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable: Gibraltar imposes no VAT/GST registration threshold. Importers of scheduled excise goods such as fuel, alcohol, and tobacco must register with the Gibraltar customs authority, and commercial customs declarations are required for all commercial consignments regardless of value. Zinc-alloy scale-model automotive replicas are not excise goods and carry no Gibraltar indirect tax charge.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar has no VAT or GST, so no Gibraltar export zero-rating mechanism exists; goods exported from Gibraltar are outside indirect taxation and may benefit from customs duty suspension or re-export relief if they were imported under customs control. If the Cayman Exempted Company makes supplies exported directly from Great Britain or Northern Ireland, UK VAT zero-rating may apply under section 30 Value Added Tax Act 1994 and Regulation 129 of the VAT Regulations 1995, provided the supplier is accountable for UK VAT, the goods physically leave the UK, the buyer is a non-UK customer, and contractual and transport evidence demonstrates export.

  • Commercial invoice and packing list describing 1:18 scale zinc-alloy die-cast automotive replicas with applicable HS classification
  • Customs export declaration submitted through UK CDS/EIDR for UK exports or through Gibraltar Customs for exports from Gibraltar
  • Bill of Lading, airway bill, or courier receipt evidencing physical departure of the goods from the relevant customs territory
  • Signed delivery confirmation or equivalent proof-of-export record retained for six years
  • Inward Remittance Certificate, SWIFT MT103, or bank statement evidencing receipt of export proceeds from the non-resident buyer

Gibraltar has no real-time fiscal e-invoicing, digital stamping, or continuous transaction control mandate for die-cast collectibles. Commercial invoices must be retained in original form. Customs clearances from Gibraltar are processed electronically through the Gibraltar Customs environment. If the goods are exported directly from Great Britain or Northern Ireland, a UK Customs Declaration Service (CDS) export declaration must be submitted electronically to HMRC; no additional digital stamping is mandated for this product type.

HMRC may deny UK export zero-rating if export proceeds are not received within 180 days or if the documentary evidence is insufficient, resulting in VAT assessment, interest, and penalties under Finance Act 2007 Schedule 24, with deliberate misstatements attracting penalties up to 100%; criminal liability may also arise under section 72 Value Added Tax Act 1994. Under Gibraltar law, customs or excise duty may be recovered with interest, goods may be forfeited, civil penalties up to treble the duty/excise value may be imposed, and fraudulent misdeclaration may trigger criminal prosecution under the Customs Ordinance as well as anti-money-laundering referral.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The GIP is fully convertible at a fixed 1:1 parity to GBP, and there are no exchange-control restrictions on current account payments, capital account movements, or profit remittance. The Cayman Islands dollar is pegged to the USD at 0.8333 CIR/USD. In both jurisdictions, dividend distributions and capital repatriations are freely permitted, but banks require documentary proof of economic substance, source of funds, board approval, and a clear CRS/FATCA classification before processing outbound remittances.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no mandatory export proceeds surrender rules in either Gibraltar or the Cayman Islands. Export receipts may be retained in any currency for an indefinite period; the statutory surrender ratio is 0%. The effective timeline is immediate credit to the exporter's operational account upon receipt through the correspondent clearing network, subject only to standard AML verification and a declared export contract/purpose code.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) for Gibraltar banking and AML/CFT regulation, in coordination with HM Treasury on the Gibraltar pound (GIP) peg to GBP; the Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA) remains the home-state supervisor of the Cayman Exempted Company.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM for UK-regulated electronic money institutions such as Wise, Revolut Business, and Airwallex; LOW for Gibraltar-only or non-EEA EMIs because post-Brexit passporting has lapsed and most offshore Cayman exempted companies lack sufficient local presence. UK EMIs generally accept Cayman entities only when the full UBO chain, a substance memorandum, and a Gibraltar/UK operating address are provided. EMIs cannot take deposits and are not a substitute for a regulated commercial bank for long-term capital escrow or trade finance.
