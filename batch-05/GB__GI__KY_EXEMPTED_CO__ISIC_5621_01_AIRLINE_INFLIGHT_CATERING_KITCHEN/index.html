Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar Income Tax Office (ITO) - portal: https://www.ito.gov.gi; corporate registration: Gibraltar Companies House (https://www.companieshouse.gov.gi); customs and export controls: HM Customs Gibraltar, HM Government of Gibraltar. Gibraltar operates a separate tax and customs system from the United Kingdom and is outside the UK VAT system.

E-government filing portal

The Cayman Exempted Company has no Cayman income tax TIN; its operative fiscal identity for the Gibraltar business is the Gibraltar corporate tax reference. Procedure: (1) file the Cayman certificate of incorporation, articles of association and registered-office certificate with Gibraltar Companies House as an overseas company under the Companies Act 2014; (2) appoint a Gibraltar-resident agent, registered office and company secretary; (3) submit the Income Tax Office corporate application Form 1CR within 30 days of commencing business, disclosing the Cayman ultimate parent, directors and the Gibraltar catering branch; (4) obtain the Gibraltar TIN (tax reference number) and register for payroll and social insurance where kitchen staff are engaged; (5) if territorial exemption is sought, file the Qualifying Company statutory declaration and economic substance return with the ITO before the end of the first year of assessment.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone - the Cayman Exempted Company is outside Cayman income tax (annual government fee only). In Gibraltar, however, central management-and-control exercised at the Gibraltar kitchen converts the classification to Entity_Level_Taxation at 12.5% corporate income tax on worldwide profits unless the Qualifying Company territorial election restricts the charge to Gibraltar-source income only.

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a US-style 'check-the-box' passthrough election. Available statutory elections: (a) Qualifying Company (QC) election under the Qualifying (Companies and Persons) Act 2019 for territorial-source-only taxation; (b) group relief / loss surrender election between 100%-owned Gibraltar subsidiaries; (c) hybrid-mismatch filing under the Income Tax (Hybrid and Other Mismatches) Rules; (d) capital allowance election on bonded kitchen plant and machinery; (e) transfer-pricing advance agreement or bilateral arrangement with HMRC for UK-connected airline counterparties.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No general VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax operates in Gibraltar; imports are subject to selective General Consumption Duty and customs/excise duties under the Gibraltar Customs & Excise Ordinances. Inflight catering supplies delivered to aircraft operating international sectors are outside the scope of any Gibraltar indirect tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - Gibraltar imposes no VAT/GST turnover threshold. Every commercial operator must nevertheless obtain: (a) Income Tax Office registration for the corporate tax reference (no minimum turnover exemption), and (b) HM Customs Gibraltar approval as a bonded aircraft-stores/catering warehouse for duty-suspended exports to international flights.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Because Gibraltar sits outside both the UK and EU VAT territories, inflight meals and stores loaded onto aircraft operating international sectors qualify as customs exports free of Gibraltar consumption duty. Conditions: the kitchen holds a bonded catering license and HM Customs approval; goods are delivered to the ramp under customs supervision for an international route evidenced by IATA flight plan and aircraft stores manifest showing a non-Gibraltar destination; goods remain on board until departure; and export proceeds are received through the onshore banking system within the 180-day repatriation deadline. No service-based offshore zero-rating applies to physical meal production.

  • Gibraltar Single Goods Declaration / customs aircraft-stores list accepted by HM Customs Gibraltar for the flight
  • Certified Aircraft Stores Manifest and Catering Load List signed by the airline station manager
  • Air Waybill, cargo manifest, or IATA Flight Plan proving the next destination is outside Gibraltar
  • Bonded catering warehouse stock-in/stock-out ledger with HACCP production records reconciling meals to the aircraft
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103 confirmation showing receipt of the airline's payment from abroad
  • Airside ramp delivery receipt evidencing physical loading before international departure

None in force - Gibraltar does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, Continuous Transaction Controls (CTC), SAF-T, e-invoicing APIs, or digital stamping for B2B, retail, or export invoicing. Airline customers may contractually require electronic load manifests and IATA Cargo-XML data exchange, but these are commercial rather than statutory fiscal controls.

Claims are audited jointly by HM Customs Gibraltar and the Income Tax Office under the Customs Ordinance (Cap. 104) and the Income Tax Act 2010 general anti-avoidance rules. False or unsupported export declarations trigger: retrospective clawback of all duty relieved; monetary penalty up to three times the value of the goods or GIP 10,000; imprisonment up to seven years for fraudulent evasion; an associated income tax penalty of up to 200% of the tax understated plus statutory interest; revocation of the bonded catering license and airside access; and referral to the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit where the export cycle is used to round-trip airline proceeds.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The GIP is fully convertible to GBP at a fixed 1:1 parity, and there are no exchange control restrictions on current-account or capital-account transactions. A Cayman Exempted Company holding treasury accounts in Gibraltar may convert, retain, or remit USD, EUR, or GBP freely. No prior approval is required for profit remittance or dividend distribution, provided the entity is not tax-resident in Gibraltar (i.e., neither managed nor controlled from Gibraltar), in which case only Gibraltar-sourced profits, if any, are subject to corporate tax at 10%. Banking counterparties will nonetheless conduct enhanced due diligence on flows from Cayman because of its offshore centre classification.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion ratio. Airline catering receivables earned by the Cayman Exempted Company for in-flight meal production and ramp delivery may be settled in USD, EUR, or GBP and retained in foreign-currency denominated accounts or freely converted at the market rate. No obligation exists to remit proceeds through Gibraltar for any mandatory exchange, and no central bank approval is required for holding or disbursing export receipts.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar does not operate an independent central bank. Monetary and financial stability functions are arranged through HM Treasury (United Kingdom) and the Bank of England, with the Gibraltar pound (GIP) issued by the Government of Gibraltar under a currency-board peg (1 GIP = 1 GBP). The Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the statutory licensing and supervisory authority for all banks, EMI activities, and cross-border payment institutions in Gibraltar.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium — feasible only as a secondary clearing layer. Authorised Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) supervised in the UK/EEA can provide virtual IBANs and fast payment processing; however, they are not passportable into Gibraltar unless separately licensed under the Gibraltar Financial Services (Electronic Money) and Payment Services legislation. Primary treasury accounts should remain with a fully licensed Gibraltar bank; EMIs may be used only for low-value ACH-like payments, monthly airline catering supplier disbursements, or expense floats, and must not receive Cayman capital injections.

Capital contributions from the Cayman parent to the Gibraltar treasury account must be executed via SWIFT MT103 with a clear ISO 20022 purpose code indicating equity injection (e.g., 'EQUI' or 'GEQU'). Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 in annual cross-border related-party revenue from purchases, sales, royalties, interest or services. Above this threshold a Gibraltar Local File is mandatory; a Master File is required where the consolidated group turnover exceeds GIP 400,000,000. Even below the threshold, a contemporaneous arm's-length memorandum must be retained.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar ESR applies to tax-resident companies carrying on a relevant activity; the in-flight catering kitchen is a 'service centre' activity where services are provided to or sourced from related parties, requiring a local board, premises, qualified staff, and core income-generating activity in Gibraltar. The Cayman Exempted Company must separately file its Cayman Economic Substance notification and evidence that it is tax resident in Gibraltar, or face Cayman penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Deliberate Understatement

Statutory sanction: Criminal prosecution with an unlimited fine and imprisonment up to 10 years; civil assessment of the true profit, penalty up to 100% of tax evaded, and interest at 4%.

Director personal liability: Director disqualification orders, travel bans, freezing of personal assets, and joint and several personal liability for the assessed tax where the director knowingly signed false returns.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar ESR penalties up to GIP 100,000 for a first offence and deregistration for continued non-compliance; Cayman ESR penalties of up to KYD 1,000,000 for false substance filings.

Director personal liability: Personal fines, disqualification from directorship, adverse immigration records, and travel restrictions pending substance remediation or winding-up.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Arm's-length adjustment to Gibraltar taxable profits; penalty up to 50% for negligent and 100% for deliberate transfer-pricing errors; daily monetary penalties for non-production of the Local File.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or approve false TP documentation become jointly and severally liable for penalty amounts and may be disqualified under the Gibraltar Companies Act.
AML / Money Laundering Abuse

Statutory sanction: Fines up to GIP 1,000,000 and imprisonment up to 14 years per money-laundering count under the Criminal Justice (Money Laundering) Act; seizure of proceeds by the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit.

Director personal liability: Personal criminal exposure for directors and compliance officers, prohibition from holding directorships, travel bans, asset freezing, and mutual legal-assistance extradition risk.
False Declaration / Fraudulent Filing

Statutory sanction: Penalty for perjury and false certification up to GIP 50,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years; automatic denial of reliefs claimed and a penalty of 100% of the evaded tax.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced; each director and the tax preparer become personally liable for the full true tax liability without right of indemnity, and may face section 212(2) disqualification proceedings.
