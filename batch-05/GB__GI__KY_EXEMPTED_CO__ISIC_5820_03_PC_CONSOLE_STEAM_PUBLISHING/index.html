Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar - Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax) for direct, corporate and residence taxes; HM Customs Gibraltar (Customs and Immigration Department) for indirect taxes, customs duties and Purchase Tax. Official portal: https://www.gibraltartax.gov.gi and https://www.customs.gov.gi.

E-government filing portal

A Cayman Exempted Company is not automatically Gibraltar-tax registered. It must apply to the Commissioner of Income Tax using Form 1 (Application for Registration as a Body of Persons/Taxpayer) within 30 days of commencing a taxable activity, submitting: (1) certified Cayman Certificate of Incorporation and Register of Directors/Members, (2) memorandum and articles of association, (3) a Gibraltar registered office address, (4) identity and residence details of beneficial owners/directors for AML/CRS/FATCA compliance, and (5) a Gibraltar tax representative appointment. The Income Tax Office issues a local TIN reference number. Gibraltar is outside the EU VAT area, so no EU VAT registration number is available; for Purchase Tax/Customs purposes, registration with HM Customs is made through the customs registration form (SAD Reg) to obtain a Customs/Trade Identification Number.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a US-style check-the-box or fiscal-transparency election. The Cayman Exempted Company may hold a Cayman Tax Exemption Undertaking (15-20 years) but that has no effect on Gibraltar taxation. Under Gibraltar law, a non-resident company is subject to entity-level territorial taxation at 12.5% on Gibraltar-source income; if central management and control is in Gibraltar, worldwide profits are taxed. A company may apply for a formal residence ruling. Anti-hybrid rules under the Income Tax (International Tax Compliance) Regulations align with OECD/UK standards and do not permit elective disregarded treatment; no elective hybrid mismatch filing is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Sales and Use Tax (Gibraltar Purchase Tax under the Purchase Tax Ordinance; customs/import duties under the Customs Ordinance; Gibraltar does not operate a VAT/GST system)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No economic turnover threshold under Purchase Tax law; registration for Purchase Tax/Customs is obligatory for any business importing or selling taxable goods in Gibraltar, irrespective of turnover (GIP/GBP). Digital-only supplies by a non-resident Cayman company such as Steam publishing are outside the scope of Purchase Tax registration unless physical goods are imported into Gibraltar. For direct tax, any company carrying on a business in Gibraltar must register for Gross Income Tax even with zero turnover; the statutory deadline is 30 days from commencement.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

As Gibraltar does not operate VAT, there is no '0% VAT rate'. Goods physically exported from Gibraltar are outside the scope of Purchase Tax and are entitled to customs duty relief/suspension provided export occurs within 180 days and the exporter holds an approved Customs Export Certificate. Electronically supplied services by the Cayman Exempted Company for PC/console game publishing via Steam (Valve Corporation) and PlayStation Store (Sony Interactive Entertainment) are outside the scope of Gibraltar Purchase Tax because the supplies are made to consumers outside Gibraltar and effectively used outside Gibraltar; no zero-rating certificate or refund mechanism is required for such digital services.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD/C88) for physical goods or platform-service statement for digital exports
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing removal of goods from Gibraltar
  • Commercial invoice from the Gibraltar-registered entity to the foreign buyer/platform
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 evidencing receipt of export proceeds through Steam/Valve payout and PlayStation Store payout rails
  • Licence and contract documentation for Steam Publishing and PlayStation Store digital distribution
  • Certificate of Origin or supporting transfer pricing documentation where related parties are involved

Gibraltar has no real-time e-invoicing clearance, mandatory digital stamping, or SAF-T filing regime. HM Customs operates an electronic Single Administrative Document (SAD) portal for import/export declarations. Electronic invoices are acceptable if they contain supplier name/address, invoice number, description, quantity, rate and tax amount where applicable; records must be retained for at least 6 years for audit purposes.

HM Customs and the Income Tax Office may audit export files, cancel export relief, and clawback uncollected duty/tax with retrospective effect for up to 6 years. False declarations attract penalties of up to 200% of the relieved duty, fines under the Customs Ordinance of up to GBP 50,000, and imprisonment for up to 7 years. Income Tax fraud and evasion provisions permit additional assessments equal to up to double the tax evaded plus daily interest at 8% per annum; the Cayman exempted company's exempt status does not shield a Gibraltar permanent establishment or branch from these penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Pound sterling and Gibraltar pound are fully convertible on both current-account and capital-account transactions. The UK and Gibraltar maintain no exchange controls, no pre-authorisation for profit/dividend remittances, and no restriction on the Cayman exempted company's receipt or retention of foreign currency. Compliance obligations are limited to AML/KYC source-of-funds verification, tax information reporting, and sanctions screening.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export-proceeds surrender rule and no mandatory conversion ratio in the UK or Gibraltar. Digital publishing receipts (Steam/PlayStation royalties) may be retained in any eligible currency indefinitely; the only hard deadlines are record-retention obligations under the Money Laundering Regulations (five years after business relationship ends).

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as banking and e-money supervisor; Bank of England as sovereign sterling monetary authority; HM Treasury as the exchange control authority

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Moderate. A UK/EEA-licensed e-money institution (e.g., Wise for Business, Revolut Business) can be used to receive/route payments if its terms permit Cayman-exempted clients and if the beneficial owners pass KYC; however, any entity holding itself out as providing e-money services in Gibraltar requires a GFSC licence, which precludes unlicensed offshore EMI activity within Gibraltar. Legal constraints include safeguarding of client funds under Gibraltar's E-Money Regulations, post-Brexit passport restrictions, and refusal by many EMIs to onboard pure tax-exempt offshore vehicles without a genuine commercial rationale.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Gibraltar uses the Gibraltar pound (GIP). A Local File is mandatory when controlled transactions with the non-resident group exceed GIP 1,000,000 annually. A Master File is required when the group's global consolidated revenue exceeds GIP 75,000,000 and Gibraltar-related intra-group transactions exceed GIP 5,000,000. Documentation must be contemporaneous and delivered within 30 days of the Income Tax Officer's demand.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations (Income Tax (Substance Requirements) Regulations 2019) apply fully. For Steam/console publishing, the core income generating activities (strategy decisions, distribution contracts with Valve/Sony, IP management) must be performed by paid staff physically in Gibraltar, supported by offices, board meetings, servers, and adequate expenditure. Annual substance filings are integrated into the CT-1 return.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Non-Declaration

Statutory sanction: Tax-geared penalty of up to 100% of the tax evaded, interest at 8% per annum, criminal conviction fined up to GIP 50,000 (or the statutory multiple of unpaid tax), confiscation of proceeds under the Proceeds of Crime Act, and, in aggravated cases, imprisonment for up to 2 years.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced on fraud or sham: directors are jointly and severally liable for the concealed tax and penalty, personal bankruptcy proceedings may be commenced, travel bans and asset freezes are imposable under the Criminal Justice (Confiscation) framework, and custodial sentences up to 2 years (or longer for money-laundering predicate offences) may be ordered.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Fine of GIP 10,000 for substance non-compliance, mandatory strike-off from the Gibraltar registry, denial of treaty residence certificates, disqualification of the company from the publishing trade, and any surviving entity taxed on a deemed basis; the Cayman ESR parallel penalty is US$10,000.

Director personal liability: Regulators hold directors specifically liable for knowingly certifying false substance returns; culpable directors face a civil restitution order up to the value of profits earned outside Gibraltar, a 5-year directorship ban and removal from the Register of Directors, and a custodial sanction for perjury or false statutory declaration.
Transfer Pricing and Manipulation of Arm's-Length Principle

Statutory sanction: Adjustment of the taxable profits upward to the median arm's-length figure, penalty of 50%-100% of the transfer pricing adjustment, late-payment interest from the original due date, and publication of the adjustment in the official gazette for repeated defaults.

Director personal liability: The Commissioner may issue a personal notice of liability to a director who signed a transfer pricing return knowing the price was not arm's-length; the director's personal assets may be attached after unsatisfied corporate demand, and a deliberate overpricing scheme that defrauds revenue is prosecuted as tax fraud with imprisonment up to 2 years.
AML, Proceeds of Crime and Sanctions Violation

Statutory sanction: Regulatory fine up to 5% of annual turnover under the financial services enforcement code, revocation of the EMI/banking licence and blocking of payment rails, criminal forfeiture of the transactions, and monetary penalties under the Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act.

Director personal liability: Duty to report suspicious activity is statutory; omission is an indictable offence. Directors face personal fines, confiscation of personal assets, travel restrictions imposed by the Gibraltar Court pending investigation, and imprisonment up to 14 years for money laundering or sanctions evasion offences.
