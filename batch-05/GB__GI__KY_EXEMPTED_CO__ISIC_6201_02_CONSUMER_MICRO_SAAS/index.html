Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (Commissioner of Income Tax). Official public portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation; e-filing system: TOGA (Tax Office Gibraltar Application) at https://toga.gov.gi.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Determine Gibraltar tax nexus. A Cayman Exempted Company is not automatically registrable solely because it opens a Gibraltar EMI bank account. Registration is required only where (a) the company has a Gibraltar permanent establishment (offices, dependent agents, contract-closing authority), (b) it derives Gibraltar-source income (e.g., from customers located in Gibraltar), or (c) de facto management and control is exercised from Gibraltar, making the company Gibraltar tax resident. Step 2: If any nexus exists, file Form OR.S.1 (Registration of Body Corporate) with the Income Tax Office, together with certified copies of the Cayman Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles of Association, Certificate of Good Standing from the Cayman Registry (CIR), board resolution appointing a Gibraltarian tax representative, and full legal owner/beneficial ownership chart under the Gibraltar Beneficial Ownership (Amendment) Regulations 2022. Step 3: The Income Tax Office issues a Tax Reference Number (TRN). Step 4: File annual company tax return (Form GIB-CT) within 6 months of the accounting period end (calendar-year filers: by 30 April); if no nexus/no liability, lodge a formal 'nil tax liability' position letter supported by a legal opinion to preempt the Commissioner's assertion of residency.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (Cayman exempted vehicle holds a 20-year Tax Exemption Certificate and zero Cayman income tax; in Gibraltar the entity is a non-resident foreign company unless management and control shifts to Gibraltar. Non-resident status confines Gibraltar taxation to Gibraltar-source profits. For the consumer micro-SaaS selling via Merchant-of-Record platforms, revenue is contractually sourced under the platform agreements (UK/IE based platforms) and not from Gibraltar, preserving a nil Gibraltar tax footprint. If the board were to hold all meetings in Gibraltar and operational decisions are made there, entity-level taxation at 12.5% on worldwide profits would follow, overriding the Cayman exemption.)

Available election forms

Gibraltar implements no check-the-box or entity classification election regime; corporate status is determined by statute and OECD/central management and control doctrine. Available elections: (1) Accounting period election – align Gibraltar tax return period with Cayman financial year to simplify filing; (2) Capital allowance pooling election – accelerate tax relief on fixed assets (servers, hardware); (3) Investment company/qualifying asset holding company election under the Income Tax Act, subject to pre-clearance, if the Cayman entity migrates assets to Gibraltar; (4) Hybrid mismatch election/self-certification – the Gibraltar Tax (Hybrid Mismatch) Rules require submission of Form HMRC-1 (Gibraltar equivalent) and election to apply OECD-precedent ordering rules, ensuring no deduction/non-inclusion outcomes on intra-group financing; (5) No US-style 'check-the-box' is available and any attempt to treat the Cayman company as transparent in Gibraltar must be validated via a private tax ruling from the Income Tax Office.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/sales-tax system in Gibraltar. The jurisdiction is expressly outside the EU VAT area and, following Brexit, outside the UK VAT unification. The former Purchase Tax was abolished effective 1 January 2010. No Gibraltar digital services tax, turnover tax, or excise on SaaS exists. Import duties subject to UK-Gibraltar customs union may arise only on physical imports; digital downloads and cloud subscriptions are outside the customs tariff.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable – there is no VAT/GST registration mechanism and no economic turnover threshold. For customs purposes, an EORI (Economic Operator Registration and Identification) number is obtainable at zero threshold from HM Customs Gibraltar for physical-goods traders; a micro-SaaS with no tangible cross-border movement does not require EORI.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable under Gibraltar law – the zero-rating concept is unique to VAT jurisdictions and Gibraltar levies no VAT. Cross-border SaaS exports from Gibraltar attract no output tax. The practical tax-neutrality threshold relates to Moroccan/Cayman substance: to maintain the Cayman 20-year Tax Exemption and avoid Gibraltar 12.5% worldwide taxation, the company must document that the Merchant-of-Record contracts (Paddle Inc., Lemon Squeezy Inc.) were executed outside Gibraltar, that servers/AI processing are hosted in third-party cloud regions (e.g., AWS eu-west-2, but contractually supplied by cloud vendor, not by the Cayman company), and that the PayPal/Stripe payouts settle into the Cayman company's account (initially a Gibraltar EMI, but capable of being characterized as a treasury function only). No Gibraltarian zero-rating certificate or export approval is required.

  • Customs Export Declaration – not required for digital SaaS; only if physical media (dongles/USB) is shipped, a UK-Gibraltar customs export declaration would apply
  • Bill of Lading – not required; substitute: platform settlement statements from Paddle/Lemon Squeezy evidencing transaction dates, fees, net remittances and customer tax/VAT collected
  • Inward Remittance Certificate – obtainable from the Gibraltar EMI/PSP to evidence that the intra-group royalty-free remittances are not Gibraltar-sourced revenue; if a Cayman bank account is used, the certificate is replaced by Cayman bank statements
  • Annual Residency & Economic Substance Certificate (Cayman) – demonstrating board control in the Cayman Islands under the International Tax Co-operation (Economic Substance) Law
  • Tax Residency Certificate from the Cayman General Registry (if available via the competent authority) to rebut any Gibraltar central-management-and-control presumption

Not applicable – Gibraltar imposes no e-invoicing or real-time transactional clearance obligation. There is no fiscal digital stamping, continuous transaction controls (CTC), SAF-T, or B2B e-invoice reporting. Tax invoices are governed by common commercial practice; the company should nevertheless maintain structured records (platform settlement statements, payout confirmations from Paddle/Lemon Squeezy, bank statements) to evidence turnover should the Commissioner challenge residency or source.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar and the Cayman Islands maintain full current-account and capital-account convertibility. The Gibraltar pound is pegged at par to GBP and freely exchangeable; the Cayman Exempted Company faces no Central Bank approval for profit remittance, no dividend-blockage consent, and no restriction on repatriation of SaaS subscription revenues. The only binding constraints are OFAC, UN, UK, EU and Gibraltar financial sanctions screening, FATCA/CRS reporting, and the substance-triggered risk that if management and control of the Cayman entity is exercised in Gibraltar, Gibraltar corporate income tax at the standard 10% rate may apply to worldwide profits, creating a tax-planning rather than exchange-control issue.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Neither the United Kingdom, Gibraltar, nor the Cayman Islands imposes any statutory export-proceeds surrender timeline or mandatory conversion ratio. The Cayman Exempted Company may retain SaaS receipts in USD, GBP and EUR as collected, and may hedge residual currency exposure under a natural-hedge-first treasury policy. The company must nevertheless retain invoicing, merchant-of-record payout reports, MT103 confirmations and board treasury minutes for a minimum of six years for AML, FATCA and tax-audit evidentiary purposes.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC); Bank of England (sterling clearing and note-issue anchor); Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA) as the exempted-company supervisory authority with no exchange-control function.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-Low for direct onboarding with offshore digital EMIs such as Wise Business, Airwallex or Currencycloud because most EMIs will reject a Cayman tax-exempt vehicle and treat CAYMAN as a restricted jurisdiction. Feasibility rating: High-Medium through regulated Merchant-of-Record aggregators, specifically Paddle and Lemon Squeezy, which act as the licensed seller of record, collect cross-border card proceeds, and remit net payouts to the Cayman company commercial account. Legal constraints: any EMI servicing UK or Gibraltar customers must hold a UK FCA EMI authorisation or a Gibraltar Financial Services (Electronic Money) Regulations 2018 authorisation; an unauthorised offshore EMI may only be used as disbursement rail, not as a deposit-taking or treasury-sweep account; FATCA/CRS registration of the Cayman company and UBO screening remain mandatory.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant wire transfer protocol uses SWIFT MT103 from the founders personal or existing company bank into the Cayman Exempted Company capital escrow account at the Gibraltar bank. Remittance field cites ISO purpose code S301 for equity injection and the reference CAYMAN EXEMPTED CO SUB-CAPITAL INCREASE 2025. The bank requires a board resolution, updated register of members, share-premium statement and source-of-funds bank statements for any transfer above GBP/USD/EUR 10,000. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Gibraltar Local File: mandatory where the Gibraltar-resident entity or PE has controlled transactions in aggregate exceeding GBP 1,000,000 (or EUR equivalent) in a fiscal year; Gibraltar Master File: mandatory where the worldwide group consolidated revenue exceeds GBP 50,000,000. Cayman: no statutory transfer pricing documentation threshold, but the annual Economic Substance filing requires disclosure of revenue, expenditure, employees, and premises regardless of size; any related-party pricing adopted must be defensible against a future 12.5% Gibraltar assessment.

Economic substance scope: Cayman ESR applies under the International Tax Co-operation (Economic Substance) Act 2018. A consumer micro-SaaS holding IP and deriving subscription income is an Intellectual Property Business or Headquarters Business; the company must demonstrate CIGA performed in Cayman - local board meetings, full-time employees, physical premises, and operating expenditure proportionate to the income. Gibraltar applies no standalone ESR law but incorporates OECD/ATAD substance concepts to deny treaty and territorial benefits where a Cayman head fails those tests.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Sham Arrangements (GAAR)

Statutory sanction: Recomputation of the full taxable margin at 12.5% Gibraltar corporation tax; penalties up to 100% of the tax under-declared; interest at 3% above base rate; surrender and strike-off powers under the Cayman Companies Act; automatic exchange of information to UK HMRC under the OECD AEOI standard.

Director personal liability: Civil restitution orders, director disqualification for up to 15 years, travel bans, and imprisonment up to 7 years under Gibraltar and UK Proceeds of Crime provisions where the arrangement is prosecuted as fraudulent evasion.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Cayman default penalty of CI$10,000 (approx. US$12,200), escalating by CI$5,000 per month; automatic AEOI exchange with HMRC; downgrade of exempted status, penalty assessments, and strike-off of the company.

Director personal liability: Statutory duty to prevent the contravention; officers risk personal fines up to US$50,000, imprisonment up to 2 years, restriction orders preventing directorship, and HMRC may lift the corporate veil to collect unpaid tax from personally liable directors.
Transfer Pricing Misdeclaration

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar transfer pricing adjustments upheld at 12.5% plus late payment interest; penalties of 35% to 100% of the additional tax; the GTA may reopen closed periods and impose daily default penalties under the CT return regime.

Director personal liability: Joint and several liability for the tax agent of record and directors where the adjustment results from intentional or reckless transfer pricing manipulation; professional indemnity insurance is void where conduct is dishonest.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Compliance

Statutory sanction: Cayman AML fines up to US$500,000 per breach under the Proceeds of Crime Act and AML Regulations; the Registrar may strike off the company and issue confiscation orders over the SaaS revenue.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners face up to 5 years' imprisonment, travel bans, worldwide freezing orders, and personal confiscation of assets derived from the non-compliant arrangement.
