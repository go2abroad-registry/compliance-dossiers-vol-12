Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Government of Gibraltar - Income Tax Office and Customs and Excise Department; e-Services portal: tax.gibraltar.gov.gi and customs.gov.gi (official HM Government of Gibraltar portals).

E-government filing portal

For a Cayman exempted company not managed and controlled in Gibraltar, registration for tax is triggered only where a Gibraltar tax nexus arises (Gibraltar bank account, employees, imports, fixed establishment, or taxable supply). Procedure: (1) file an overseas company registration Form CO7 with the Gibraltar Companies Registry, appoint a Gibraltar-resident agent, and obtain a registered place of business; (2) apply through the Income Tax Office e-Services portal for a Tax Reference Number (TRN), attaching notarised Certificate of Incorporation, Cayman Certificate of Good Standing, Gibraltar agent consent, and board resolution; (3) where importing or re-exporting goods, open a Customs deferment account with HM Customs and obtain approved exporter/importer status; (4) register for GST under the Goods and Services Tax Act 2019 if any taxable supplies are effected from a Gibraltar establishment; (5) the Commissioner issues the TRN and GST registration number, normally within 10 business days.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box election applies under Gibraltar or Cayman law. The entity may hold a Cayman Islands Tax Exemption Certificate under the Companies Act (as amended), confirming exemption from direct taxation in the Cayman Islands for 20 years. In Gibraltar, an overseas company may elect to be treated as a non-resident company where central management and control are not exercised in Gibraltar, and may make hybrid-mismatch elections under Gibraltar's hybrid mismatch legislation to eliminate deduction/non-inclusion mismatches in cross-border green coffee bean trading transactions.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Goods and Services Tax (GST) - Gibraltar zero-rated VAT-equivalent regime

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GIP 0; no economic threshold applies. Any person making taxable supplies in Gibraltar from a fixed establishment or importing goods into Gibraltar must register for GST before the transaction, and importers must register with HM Customs for customs duty deferment regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Supplies of single-origin green Arabica coffee beans to customers outside Gibraltar qualify for zero-rating under s.19 Goods and Services Tax Act 2019 (export of goods) where the goods physically leave Gibraltar within 90 days of supply and the supplier retains full export evidence. Associated services (freight, insurance, handling, L/C confirmation, demurrage) supplied to non-resident customers may also be zero-rated if directly linked to the export or used and enjoyed outside Gibraltar. No GST is chargeable where the recipient is outside Gibraltar and the benefit is received outside Gibraltar.

  • Customs Export Declaration (EXS/SAD or electronic EMS)
  • Bill of Lading / Sea Waybill
  • Certificate of Origin / Phytosanitary Certificate (where required)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or Bank Credit Advice

Gibraltar does not operate a mandatory B2B e-invoicing clearance system, real-time fiscal stamping, or Continuous Transaction Controls (CTC). Invoices must satisfy the GST Regulations content requirements (invoice date, supplier name and TRN, recipient details, GST classification, unit price and consideration). Export shipments are subject to electronic customs declarations (EXS/EMS or SAD) filed through the Gibraltar Customs Integrated Tariff (GCIT) gateway, with real-time risk scoring by HM Customs; no digital stamping is currently required.

HM Customs and the Gibraltar Revenue Department may audit export zero-rating claims for up to six years. Failure to provide the mandatory documentation bundle invalidates the zero-rating claim and triggers disallowance of any input tax deduction or customs duty drawback claimed, together with interest at the statutory rate (currently 6% p.a.) from the date of supply. Civil penalties of up to 100% of the evaded liability, default surcharges of up to 10%, and criminal prosecution for fraud or false accounting (imprisonment up to seven years) apply to fraudulent export zero-rating and forged documents. Retrospective clawback may extend to the full amount of any refund previously granted.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current-account and capital-account convertibility applies. The Exchange Control Act 1947 (UK) was repealed in 1979, and Gibraltar has never reimposed exchange controls. No exchange licence, no prior central-bank approval, and no repatriation restriction applies to capital inflows, dividend distributions, interest, royalties, management fees, or inter-company payments. The Cayman Exempted Company may freely remit profits from its Gibraltar treasury hub to shareholders or creditors. The only practical hurdles are bank-level documentary requirements: (i) a source-of-funds pack for inbound capital, (ii) a trade document match for supplier disbursements, and (iii) confirmation that funds do not touch UK/Gibraltar-sanctioned jurisdictions. The GIP is pegged at par with GBP and is not an independently floating currency; trade FX exposure is therefore effectively GBP/USD and GBP/EUR.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender rules exist in Gibraltar or the United Kingdom. Exporters and trading hubs may retain 100% of proceeds in any freely convertible currency, and there is no mandatory conversion timeline or ratio. For this specialty green coffee mandate, where Gibraltar acts as a procurement and settlement hub, the only documentary expectations are: (i) customs or excise entry declarations for goods transiting Gibraltar; (ii) a bank trade-verification pack (contract, invoice, bill of lading, inspection certificate) for trade-based AML screening; and (iii) proof of title transfer under Incoterms where the Gibraltar entity is the importer of record. Proceeds may be held indefinitely in USD, EUR, or GBP without conversion obligation.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the integrated financial regulator, supplemented by the Government of Gibraltar Currency Department (Currency Notes Act 1934) as issuer of the Gibraltar pound, and the Bank of England as the monetary anchor maintaining GIP/GBP 1:1 parity. Gibraltar has no independent central bank or monetary policy authority; sterling-area clearing and high-value payment infrastructure are accessed through UK correspondent banking relationships.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Rating: MEDIUM. UK FCA-authorised electronic money institutions (Wise Platform, Airwallex, Ebury, Equals) can support ancillary FX conversion, sub-USD-GBP 100k supplier disbursements, and collections. However, post-Brexit these entities no longer enjoy EU passporting rights into Gibraltar, and a UK-authorised EMI seeking to operate in Gibraltar must either (i) hold a GFSC e-money/branch authorisation under Gibraltar's Financial Services legislation or (ii) operate through a Gibraltar-licensed commercial bank as a banking-as-a-service sponsor. The Cayman Exempted Company must not rely on an EMI as primary treasury infrastructure because EMIs cannot issue documentary letters of credit, cannot provide SF? trade finance facilities, and typically cannot access CHIPS/Fedwire USD clearing without a full banking partner. Use EMIs for low-value, high-frequency operational payments where the trade documentation trail is not critical; keep all commodities financing, LC issuance, and concentrated USD clearing within the GFSC-licensed banking tier.
