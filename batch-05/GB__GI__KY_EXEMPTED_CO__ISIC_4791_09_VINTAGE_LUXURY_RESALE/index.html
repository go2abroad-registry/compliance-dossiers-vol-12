Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (official portal: www.gibraltar.gov.gi); customs and excise administration: HM Customs Gibraltar (customs.gov.gi)

E-government filing portal

A non-resident Cayman exempted company trading in or through Gibraltar must appoint a Gibraltar-resident company secretary/tax representative, file a Foreign Company Branch Registration with the Gibraltar Companies Registry, and apply for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) with the Income Tax Office via the official tax portal. If goods move through Gibraltar as an export/trade gateway, the company must also obtain an EORI number and register with HM Customs for electronic import/export declarations. No VAT registration is required because Gibraltar does not levy VAT.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Cayman Exempted Company status may be secured by a Section 6 Tax Concessions Act undertaking guaranteeing no Cayman direct tax for 20 years. Gibraltar does not recognise check-the-box elections; however, a foreign company may file a non-residence declaration so that Gibraltar tax applies only to Gibraltar-source income. Hybrid mismatch elections are not available and anti-hybrid rules may apply under UK/Gibraltar aligned legislation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar Customs and Excise Duties (no VAT or GST; UK VAT may apply to goods entering Great Britain through distance sales)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No Gibraltar VAT threshold applies as Gibraltar imposes no VAT. Customs registration is mandatory for every importer/exporter regardless of turnover. If the Cayman entity makes affected supplies into Great Britain, the UK VAT distance-selling threshold for consignments is effectively nil where marketplaces are used and goods are sold directly to UK consumers.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar has no VAT, so export zero-rating as a Gibraltar tax concept is not applicable. If UK VAT registration is triggered for the Cayman entity, zero-rating may be claimed on goods only where the goods physically leave the UK/EU VAT territory within three months of supply, legal and beneficial title passes to the non-resident purchaser, and complete export evidence is held. Offshore services supplied to a non-resident business may be outside the scope of UK VAT or zero-rated under the Place of Supply rules.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD/CDS electronic export declaration)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank payment evidence
  • VAT proof of export where UK zero-rating is claimed

Gibraltar requires electronic customs declarations through the Gibraltar Customs Declaration System (GCDS) using the Single Administrative Document format; there is no domestic B2B e-invoicing clearance or digital stamping mandate. For goods moving into the UK/EU, invoices and customs entry data must be submitted electronically and retained for at least six years, with EORI and, where applicable, UK VAT registration identifiers included.

HM Customs Gibraltar and HM Revenue & Customs (UK) may reverse fraudulent zero-rating claims, recover 100% of the evaded tax, impose daily penalties, and charge interest. Criminal prosecution for dishonest tax evasion can result in terms of imprisonment up to seven years under Gibraltar criminal legislation. Retrospective clawback can cover up to 20 years in cases of serious fraud, and the responsible Gibraltar tax representative may be made jointly and severally liable.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar Pound is exchangeable on a one-to-one basis with Pound Sterling and there are no exchange controls, capital controls, or surrender requirements in Gibraltar. Both current-account and capital-account transactions may be freely converted across GBP, EUR, and USD in authorised bank accounts. Profit remittances from an Exempted Company holding in Gibraltar are unrestricted, subject to corporate compliance and source-of-funds verification, with zero withholding tax on dividends paid to non-resident shareholders under current single-tier taxation arrangements.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar imposes no statutory timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts. Vintage luxury resale proceeds, whether received in GBP, EUR, or USD, may be retained in full in any authorised Gibraltar bank account or any offshore account. The UK's foreign exchange controls were abolished in 1979, and Gibraltar has not reintroduced any repatriation or surrender obligations.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) acting under the Financial Services Act 2019 in coordination with HM Government of Gibraltar. The Government of Gibraltar, through the Currency Notes Act 1934, issues Gibraltar Pound banknotes and coins at parity with GBP. There is no separate Central Bank of Gibraltar; HM Treasury and the Bank of England remain relevant for macro-prudential and sanctions infrastructure.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Moderate-to-Low as a sole treasury platform. A Cayman Exempted Company engaged in cross-border vintage luxury resale may use GFSC-licensed or UK FCA-registered e-money institutions (EMIs) for collection-only and disbursement-only service rails, especially for card and escrow payments through Stripe and Escrow_Pay. Such EMIs cannot provide the full treasury concentration role because of reduced deposit protection, non-availability of commercial LC/guarantees, and enhanced third-party due diligence friction. Non-EEA offshore EMIs without both GFSC/UK or equivalent licensing should be rejected for settlement custody.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Gibraltar has not enacted a unique statutory threshold for TP documentation; however, by administrative practice, entities with related-party cross-border transactions exceeding GBP 1,000,000 (or EUR 750,000) per annum must maintain a Local File and, where group turnover exceeds EUR 750 million, a Master File in accordance with OECD TP Guidelines.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar's Economic Substance Regulations (as per Income Tax (Economic Substance) Act 2019) apply to 'relevant activities'. The online consignment and secondhand luxury goods retail operation is categorised as a 'distribution and service centre' activity. To satisfy substance, the exempted company must maintain (i) a physical office in Gibraltar, (ii) a minimum of two full-time employees in the jurisdiction, (iii) locally incident decision-making and board meetings, and (iv) an adequate level of annual operating expenditure proportionate to turnover. Failure to meet ESR results in financial penalties and potential exchange of data with EU/UK tax authorities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion & Fraudulent Exemption Claims

Statutory sanction: Back assessment of tax at 12.5% on all undeclared Gibraltar-source income, plus a civil penalty of 100% of the tax unpaid and daily interest of 1.5% per month. Criminal sanctions under the Criminal Justice (Tax Evasion) Act include an unlimited fine and imprisonment for up to 7 years.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for restitution of all unpaid taxes if they knowingly signed false self-assessments. Travel bans under the Court of Appeal's asset-preservation jurisdiction and disqualification as a director for up to 15 years are possible.
Shell Company Abuse & Economic Substance Violation

Statutory sanction: Economic Substance penalties: initial penalty of GBP 1,000 for missing documentation; escalating up to GBP 10,000 per month of continued non-compliance; power of the Commissioner to dissolve the company after 12 months of non-compliance. The entity may lose its exempt status retroactively.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for the annual ESR compliance filings and may be fined up to GBP 5,000 per individual failure. Directors found to have facilitated a sham company may be held personally liable for all unpaid corporate taxes and banned from holding directorships in Gibraltar for 10 years.
Anti-Money Laundering (AML) & High-Risk Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Under the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and the Financial Action Bureau's AML Code, penalties range from a regulatory fine of up to GBP 1 million for failing to maintain adequate AML systems, to criminal liability of up to 14 years' imprisonment for money laundering. Feature-based penalties: each suspicious transaction not reported carries a fine of up to GBP 10,000.

Director personal liability: Directors' personal criminal liability if they knowingly or negligently failed to report suspicious activities. Personal assets may be frozen under a confiscation order; travel restrictions apply pending investigation.
VAT & Cross-Border Reporting Frauds

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar-based penalties under the Income Tax Act do not cover VAT, but cross-border authorities may impose local VAT assessments (e.g., UK VAT evasion penalties up to 100% of evaded tax and imprisonment up to 6 months). Information exchange under the UK-Gibraltar Mutual Assistance arrangement triggers these sanctions regardless of the entity's exempt status.

Director personal liability: Directors of a Gibraltar exempt company can be named in a personal liability notice for unpaid UK VAT under Section 45 of the UK Finance Act 1998 if they engaged in fraud or negligence. Disqualification as a UK director and seizure of personal assets in Gibraltar are enforceable via reciprocal judgment.
