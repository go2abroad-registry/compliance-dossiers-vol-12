E-government filing portal

A non-resident Cayman Exempted Company establishing commercial substance or a permanent establishment in Gibraltar must: (1) register the overseas company branch with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies; (2) file a Notice of Commencement of Business and a tax registration application with the Income Tax Office, thereby obtaining a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN); (3) appoint a Gibraltar-resident general representative or agent where the company is non-resident, such agent being jointly and severally liable for the company's Gibraltar tax; (4) obtain e-filing login credentials and the statutory compliance calendar; (5) file audited accounts, as required, and corporate income tax returns on the territorial/source basis under the Income Tax Act 2010.

No Gibraltar check-the-box or equivalent entity-classification election exists; tax classification is determined by the Income Tax Act 2010 and the Companies Act 2014. The Cayman Exempted Company benefits from a Cayman Tax Exemption Certificate rendering it tax-exempt in its domestic jurisdiction, while any Gibraltar branch or permanent establishment is subject to Gibraltar income tax on Gibraltar-source income only. Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is pegged 1:1 to sterling and fully convertible with GBP at par; no conversion tax, margin control, or surrender requirement exists. Both the capital account (equity injection, shareholder loans, intercompany funding) and the current account (Apple/Google app-store payouts, royalty and licensing disbursements, profit remittances) are fully liberalised under Gibraltar's continued sterling-area legacy and the UK's post-1979 abolition of exchange controls. A Cayman Exempted Company may hold, switch, and repatriate USD, GBP, and EUR without prior Bank of England or GFSC approval. Practical hurdle: profit remittance is frictionless only if the beneficial ownership chain, economic substance documentation (Cayman Economic Substance Returns), and source-of-funds trail are complete; otherwise the paying Gibraltar bank will apply enhanced AML/CFT review and may freeze the distribution pending sanctions and tax compliance screening.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Neither Gibraltar nor the Cayman Islands imposes a statutory surrender of export or app-store proceeds, mandatory conversion timelines, or retention quotas on a Cayman Exempted Company. Voice-streaming receipts from Apple and Google payment aggregators may be settled in USD to a designated clearing account and kept in original currency, converted through the bank's corporate FX facility, or hedged via permissible forward instruments. No GB/GI regulatory filing is required for the receipt of such digital export proceeds.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority for the British Overseas Territory of Gibraltar; sets policy for the Gibraltar pound at par with GBP) acting alongside the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the integrated regulator for banking, e-money, and payments. There is no separate Gibraltar foreign exchange administration; currency issuance is delegated to the Crown Government of Gibraltar via the Currency Commissioner, and the Cayman Exempted Company falls outside the UK regulatory perimeter for banking supervision purposes.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM with material legal constraints. Gibraltar-licensed and UK passported e-money institutions (EMIs) can legally hold customer funds and execute payment transactions under the Financial Services (E-Money) Act 2019 and the UK-Gibraltar financial services treaty. However, for a Cayman Exempted Company, offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Revolut Business, Wise, Currencycloud-type platforms) are classified as non-bank payment accounts, not bank accounts: they cannot provide a protected statutory capital escrow arrangement, do not offer a formal Gibraltar account relationship, and are not an eligible venue for statutory capital injection evidence. Additionally, high-velocity Apple/Google payout streaming through a pure EMI triggers re-categorisation as a money-service business and may require an enhanced GFSC EMIR/MiCA-style assessment. Viable use case: small-value operational FX conversions and contractor payouts in EUR/GBP below GBP 25,000 per transaction, with the primary treasury balance and all intercompany distributions held at a Tier-1 licensed Gibraltar bank.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

Cayman Islands: 0% corporate income tax; Gibraltar territorial corporation tax on locally sourced or effectively managed/controlled profits: 12.5% standard rate.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Gibraltar: 0% withholding tax on dividends; Cayman Islands: 0% withholding tax on dividends.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 750,000 (contemporaneous Local File readiness threshold applied by Gibraltar Tax & Customs for intercompany product, royalty and financing transactions; Master File required for multinational groups with GIP 7,500,000 aggregate turnover).

Economic substance scope: Cayman Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) are mandatory: a voice streaming app holding user contracts, IP, or performing distribution and service centre functions must demonstrate proportionate core income-generating activity staff, premises, governance, expenditure and management presence in Cayman. Gibraltar also applies OECD BEPS-aligned substance requirements; a Cayman exempted company managed from Gibraltar, with no real Cayman presence, is a classic shell-company red flag and will be treated as tax resident in Gibraltar where management and control is located.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of up to 100% of the tax evaded on top of the corrected tax; criminal conviction for fraudulent tax evasion may result in an unlimited fine and imprisonment. In Cayman, the Tax Information Authority Act permits severe fines and information exchange to HMRC.

Director personal liability: Directors and shadow directors are jointly and severally liable for the tax deficiency if they knowingly participated in or concealed evasion; criminal imprisonment up to 7 years, travel bans and freezing of personal assets can be imposed.
Shell Company

Statutory sanction: Under Cayman ESR, failure to file an ESR return attracts an initial penalty of CI$10,000 and daily penalties of CI$1,000 to CI$10,000, with eventual strike-off and dissolution; the Cayman Tax Information Authority may also make an adverse determination report to the UK, triggering a UK enforcement cascade.

Director personal liability: Directors may be disqualified, fined up to CI$30,000 under the Companies Act, and made personally liable for the company's unpaid penalties where they connived in the false declaration; in Gibraltar, non-compliance with substance requirements exposes directors to restriction orders and travel restrictions.
AML and Payment-Channel Abuse

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar criminal fines reach up to 4% of global turnover or GBP 4,500,000 per AML breach; Cayman CIMA AML penalties reach CI$250,000 per instance plus imprisonment up to 5 years; overseas authorities can seize proceeds routed through the Cayman entity.

Director personal liability: Individual directors are criminally liable if they consented to or connived in the whole or part of the AML misconduct; personal travel bans, bank account freezes, asset confiscation orders and extradition to prosecution jurisdictions are all possible.
Transfer Pricing Misstatement

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar Tax & Customs makes an upward transfer pricing adjustment, applies interest at the statutory rate and imposes penalties of up to 50% of the additional tax liability, increased to 100% where transfer pricing documents are absent or materially inaccurate.

Director personal liability: Directors who certified or caused the incorrect tax return are personally subject to civil restitution, a director's penal assessment equal to the unpaid tax and penalties, and in severe deliberate misstatement cases, imprisonment and departure bans.
