Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

The Cayman Exempted Company is not registrable in Gibraltar for income tax unless it is managed and controlled (M&C) in Gibraltar or carries on activity through a Gibraltar permanent establishment (PE). If registration is triggered: (1) file Company Registration form IT-1 (non-Gibraltar company) with the Department of Taxation within 30 days of commencement; (2) attach the Cayman Certificate of Incorporation and Certificate of Good Standing, Memorandum and Articles, register of directors/members, UBO declaration, Gibraltar service address, and board resolutions adopting the tax classification; (3) obtain the Gibraltar TIN (company tax reference) from the Commissioner, which is issued within 5 business days of complete filing and is used for all corporate tax, CRS and FATCA reporting; no minimum capital is required; (4) if a branch is constituted, file form EBR-1 with the Gibraltar Companies Registry and obtain the branch number; (5) file annual returns (Form C) thereafter and update the ITD on PE activities. The Cayman-side tax registration requirement is limited to the 20-year Exempted Company tax-exemption certificate under Cayman law; no Cayman income-tax TIN is issued.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists in Gibraltar. The relevant statutory elections are: (i) Qualifying Company (QC) election under the Qualifying (Companies and Individuals) Rules 2011 - 2% tax rate on qualifying offshore profits, subject to economic substance, 80% non-domestic income cap and a minimum annual tax of £1,000; (ii) branch separate-entity election under the Income Tax Act 2010 s.19(8) for loss-relief and treaty allocation; (iii) group relief election under the Income Tax (Group Relief) Rules for 75%+ groups; (iv) UK anti-hybrid classification statements under TIOPA 2010 Part 6A where inconsistent opacity/transparency between UK and Gibraltar/Cayman treatment exists; and (v) non-resident 'no PE' declaration for the avoidance of doubt. The pre-2011 Gibraltar 'tax-exempt company' route is closed; the Cayman entity's exemption derives solely from its Cayman tax-exempt status and absence of Gibraltar-residing management and control.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full capital and current account convertibility. The UK and Gibraltar have maintained complete freedom of exchange controls since 1979. The Gibraltar pound is convertible at par with sterling; no licensing, exchange approval, or repatriation permit is required for profit remittance, dividend distribution, loan repayments, or capital outflows from either jurisdiction. For the Cayman exempted company, the Cayman Islands impose no exchange controls and no withholding taxes on dividends, interest, or royalty flows. Practical hurdles are limited to documentary compliance (beneficial ownership attestations, board resolutions, tax residency statements, FATCA/CRS registration) and bank KYC rather than statutory approval.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or repatriation requirement exists in the UK, Gibraltar, or the Cayman Islands. Export proceeds from organic matcha powder sales are freely retainable in any convertible currency (USD, EUR, JPY, GBP, or offshore CNH). There is no mandatory conversion ratio, no mandated holding period, and no statutory deadline for converting or remitting export receipts. Treasury policy may nonetheless elect to draw down FX forward lines to hedge JPY/EUR exposures.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority for sterling) jointly with the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) (banking licensing, prudential and AML/CFT supervision of Gibraltar banks); the Government of Gibraltar issues Gibraltar pound (GIP) banknotes pegged at par to GBP. For the Cayman Islands exempted company, the Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA) has regulatory oversight of the corporate services provider and AML regime, but holds no exchange control function.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: HIGH, subject to conditionality. UK-authorized Electronic Money Institutions (e.g., Wise Platform and Revolut Business, regulated by the FCA under the Electronic Money Regulations 2011) will generally onboard a Cayman exempted company engaged in low-risk physical goods trade, provided the Cayman beneficial ownership register is current and UBEO documentation is complete. Legal constraints: EMIs are not deposit-taking banks, offer no FSCS-style deposit protection, and maintain client funds in safeguarded accounts; they cannot issue commercial letters of credit or provide credit/hedging lines. The EMI route is recommended as a tactical multi-currency operating rail (SWIFT TT and GBP Faster Payments) alongside a regulated Gibraltar bank account for settlement finality, escrow capacity, and documentary trade services.
