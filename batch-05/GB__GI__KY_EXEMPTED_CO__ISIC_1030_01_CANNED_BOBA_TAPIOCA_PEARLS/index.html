Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Her Majesty's Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office (ITO) and HM Customs Gibraltar; e-filing portal: https://www.ito.gi and Gibraltar Trade Single Window via HM Customs

E-government filing portal

For the Cayman Exempted Company to obtain a Gibraltar tax identity, it must: (1) determine tax residence based on central management and control exercised in Gibraltar; (2) if Gibraltar residence is intended, register the company or a branch with the Registrar of Companies under the Gibraltar Companies Act; (3) file a completed tax registration application with the Income Tax Office together with certified notarial copies of the Cayman certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, directors' and shareholders' details, and a letter from a Gibraltar-resident tax agent; (4) obtain a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN) and assessment reference; (5) appoint a Gibraltar registered office and, where required, a tax representative; and (6) comply with economic substance notification requirements for any resident company carrying on relevant activities.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (Cayman exempted company non-resident in Gibraltar; Gibraltar-resident status only if central management and control is exercised in Gibraltar)

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not apply the US check-the-box election; the Cayman exempted company may apply for 'Qualifying Company' status under the Income Tax (Qualifying Companies) Regulations to be treated as Gibraltar-resident for double taxation treaty access, provided it meets substance conditions. Gibraltar law requires hybrid mismatch payments to be neutralised under OECD BEPS Action 2 and the Gibraltar Finance Act where mismatches arise between the Cayman exempted company, the Gibraltar permanent establishment, and the UK VAT/corporation tax position.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST applies within Gibraltar; the applicable indirect regime is Gibraltar customs and excise duties. Supplies made in Gibraltar do not attract UK VAT, and Gibraltar is outside the EU/UK VAT area.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - Gibraltar does not impose VAT or GST, and no economic turnover threshold triggers VAT registration. Commercial importers and exporters must obtain a customs registration / excise licence with HM Customs Gibraltar, but no minimum turnover threshold applies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar has no VAT, so a '0% VAT export rate' does not apply to goods exported from Gibraltar. Duty relief and export drawback are available where canned boba tapioca pearls are physically removed from Gibraltar to a destination outside Gibraltar, evidenced by a Gibraltar customs export declaration. If the entity separately holds a UK VAT registration and supplies goods removed from the United Kingdom to a third-country destination, UK zero-rating under Value Added Tax Act 1994 section 30 may apply, subject to possession of valid export evidence, a zero-rated invoice, and removal from the UK within the prescribed time limits.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration / Single Administrative Document (SAD) or IE840 equivalent
  • Commercial invoice and packing list identifying seller, buyer, and product HS classification for tapioca pearls (ISIC 1030 / NAICS 311999)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure and foreign destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank FX receipt proving export proceeds repatriation within 180 days
  • Certificate of Origin where preferential customs treatment is claimed

Gibraltar does not mandate real-time electronic invoicing, fiscal clearance, or digital stamping for VAT purposes. Exporters must transmit export declarations electronically through HM Customs Gibraltar using the Single Administrative Document (SAD) format or an approved portal. Invoices and supporting customs documents must be retained in English for a minimum of six years. No API-based e-invoicing clearance or government pre-authorisation of invoices is currently required.

HM Customs Gibraltar and the Income Tax Office may conduct post-clearance and post-filing audits for up to four years after the relevant declaration. Fraudulent export zero-rating or forged export evidence triggers retrospective clawback of duties and surcharges of up to 100% of the duty evaded, fixed civil penalties of up to £3,000 per declaration, forfeiture of the goods, and criminal prosecution for fraudulent evasion. Where UK VAT is involved, HMRC may issue assessments on a four-year or, in cases of deliberate concealment or internationally mobile arrangements, a twenty-year basis, with penalties of up to 100% under Schedule 24 Finance Act 2007, plus interest.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound is pegged at parity to GBP sterling and fully convertible on both current and capital account. There are no exchange-control approvals, central bank allocation requirements, or restrictions on profit remittance. A Cayman Exempted Company is not a Gibraltar tax resident and may freely remit dividends, management fees, and liquidation proceeds subject only to bank-level source-of-funds verification and, where applicable, Gibraltar VAT on taxable supplies. No withholding tax or capital gains tax applies to non-resident shareholders or treasury transactions by a Cayman exempted vehicle.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar maintains no statutory export-proceeds surrender rules and no mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts. Exporters may retain invoices in GIP, GBP, USD, EUR, or any freely selected currency. The sole compliance obligations are AML record-keeping: retain underlying trade documentation for at least five years and file suspicious activity reports through the GFSC-designated Financial Intelligence Unit. For sterling-denominated clearing, no Bank of England prior authorisation is needed for export receipt retention.

Central bank supervision: Government of Gibraltar (Ministry of Finance) is the issuer of Gibraltar pound banknotes; HM Treasury and the Bank of England anchor sterling monetary policy and financial stability. Foreign-exchange supervision and AML/CFT enforcement are administered by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) under the Financial Services Act 2019. There is no separate Gibraltar central bank with discretionary exchange-control powers.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low-to-Medium. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Revolut Business, Payoneer, MultiPass) are not treated as tier-1 clearing providers for a Cayman Exempted Company because (i) most EMIs decline non-resident Cayman structures absent UK/Gibraltar directors, (ii) they do not support SWIFT MT103/MT700 trade settlements or documentary letters of credit, and (iii) Gibraltar does not passport the EU/UK e-money licence into its domestic settlement rail. Such EMIs may be used only as a secondary disbursement channel for small supplier payments after GFSC-compliant KYC, never as the primary liquidity clearing rail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital from the Cayman parent into the Gibraltar operational account must be executed by SWIFT MT103 (or MT202/COVER for bank-to-bank funding). The beneficiary must quote the statutory purpose code for equity capital injection (Direct Investment code G3000 / A100, or 'EQUITY' under the UK BOP statistical classification). Sender must include the Cayman Company Number, board approval reference, and tax-exemption certificate number. Pre-advice must be given to the relationship manager containing the source-of-funds pack: Cayman bank statement, board resolution, equity subscription agreement, and audited group accounts. The receiving Gibraltar branch will apply enhanced due diligence for first credit and may hold funds pending confirmation of the corporate KYB pack. No GFSC approval is required for the injection itself.Outbound trade payments for canned boba/tapioca pearls should be routed through SWIFT MT103 for open-account supplier payments; SWIFT MT700/MT710 for issued/amended commercial letters of credit; and SWIFT MT400/MT470 for documentary collections. For shipments requiring guarantee of performance, a confirmed irrevocable Letter of Credit issued against the Gibraltar account is the preferred rail. In each case, funds are debited following our standard internal controls: supplier invoice match with purchase order, specified Incoterms 2020, and screening of the beneficiary against OFAC/EU/UN sanctions. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 10,000,000 (aggregate value of cross-border related party transactions; Local File and Master File required beyond this threshold)

Economic substance scope: Applicable under Gibraltar's Economic Substance (Implementation) Regulations 2019, as amended. A Cayman exempted company that is tax resident in Gibraltar or has a PE in Gibraltar and carries on a 'relevant activity' (including distribution and service centres, finance, shipping, headquarters) must maintain adequate physical presence, qualified employees, and core income-generating activities in Gibraltar. Manufacturing of tapioca pearls per se is not a 'relevant activity', but if the entity also conducts IP exploitation or distribution, substance requirements may apply. The company must submit an annual economic substance return.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Deliberate Under-declaration

Statutory sanction: Penalty of up to 100% of the tax underpaid, plus daily penalties up to GIP 300 per day. Criminal conviction leads to fines of up to GIP 20,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly authorise fraudulent returns are jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax. Criminal exposure includes imprisonment up to 5 years and disqualification as a director in Gibraltar for up to 10 years.
Artificial Avoidance of Permanent Establishment Status

Statutory sanction: Retrospective assessment of up to 6 years, plus interest at the official rate and penalties of up to 50% of the tax evaded. Anti-avoidance provisions allow the Commissioner to disregard artificial schemes.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held personally liable for the tax if they were involved in designing or implementing the avoidance scheme, and may face civil penalties up to GIP 50,000 and travel bans pending resolution.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Economic substance fines of up to GIP 100,000 per annum; continued failure leads to strike-off from the Gibraltar Companies Registry and automatic assessment of all profits. Penalties for false filings under the ESR regime can reach GIP 300,000.

Director personal liability: Directors may be disqualified and prohibited from acting as directors in Gibraltar for up to 5 years. Personal financial liability is possible for the costs of the strike-off and liquidation proceedings.
AML/CFT Breaches in Cross-Border Repatriation

Statutory sanction: Financial penalties under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 (Gibraltar) of up to GIP 5 million per breach; freezing orders on bank accounts; potential confiscation of assets derived from the proceeds of crime.

Director personal liability: Directors who direct or knowingly facilitate such transactions can be personally criminally liable with imprisonment up to 14 years. Civil asset recovery actions may be brought against directors personally.
