Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a Cayman Exempted Company (registration capital is typically 50,000 Ordinary Shares of US$1.00 each, unissued; one USD 1.00 share may be issued). Under the Gibraltar Equivalent Protection Test (s.273), the external company must maintain a fully paid-up share capital commensurate with shipyard liabilities. No statutory escrow is imposed at registration; however, the Business Licensing Office may require a performance bond or escrowed installment guarantee (commonly USD 250,000) before issuing the shipyard operating licence.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certificate and annual re-inspection by the Gibraltar Fire and Rescue Service (GFRS) under the Fire Safety Act 2004, covering dry-pipe and water-spray suppression systems on slipways, flammable gas storage separation, welder hot-work permits, and evacuation drills. Structural load certification of slipways, gantry cranes, and floating docks is required from the Principal Building Control Officer, Development and Planning Commission under the Building Act 2013. Occupational clearance is registered with APRAW under the Safety at Work Regulations 2024: medical fitness, confined-space, scaffold, welding, rigging, and crane-operator certifications for all yard personnel. If docking approaches port waters, the Port Marine Safety Code statement must be filed with the Gibraltar Maritime Administration.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a Cayman Exempted Company (registration capital is typically 50,000 Ordinary Shares of US$1.00 each, unissued; one USD 1.00 share may be issued). Under the Gibraltar Equivalent Protection Test (s.273), the external company must maintain a fully paid-up share capital commensurate with shipyard liabilities. No statutory escrow is imposed at registration; however, the Business Licensing Office may require a performance bond or escrowed installment guarantee (commonly USD 250,000) before issuing the shipyard operating licence.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (Ministry of Finance); official information and filing portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation; customs authority: HM Customs Gibraltar, https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/customs

E-government filing portal

A Cayman Exempted Company with no Gibraltar permanent establishment is not required to obtain a Gibraltar tax identification number. Where the shipyard constitutes a permanent establishment (a site, factory, shipyard premises or building yard on the Rock), the company must register as a non-resident corporate taxpayer with the Income Tax Office within 30 days of commencing activity. The registration procedure requires the completion of the Income Tax Office's corporate registration form, accompanied by a certified copy of the Cayman Certificate of Incorporation, the memorandum and articles of association, a board resolution appointing a Gibraltar-resident tax agent or principal, evidence of the Gibraltar place of business, and an estimate of Gibraltar-source profits. The Income Tax Office then issues a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) which is distinct from the Cayman registered company number and from any UK Unique Taxpayer Reference. No VAT identification number can be issued because Gibraltar does not levy VAT or GST.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (territorial scope: the branch profits of the Cayman Exempted Company attributable to the Gibraltar permanent establishment are chargeable to Gibraltar corporation tax at 10% for ISIC 3011 shipbuilding manufacturing, with utility-type licensees at 20%; the Cayman legal entity remains tax-exempt in the Cayman Islands under a Tax Exemption Certificate covering non-Cayman-source income)

Available election forms

No check-the-box or pass-through election exists in Gibraltar. Applicable elections and regimes: (i) Cayman Islands Tax Exemption Certificate election under the Tax Concessions Law (effective for 20 years and renewable) preserving exempt status in Cayman; (ii) Gibraltar participation exemption election for qualifying shareholdings and, where applicable, certain capital gains; (iii) double taxation relief claims under the UK-Gibraltar Double Taxation Arrangement as amended by the 2019 Protocol, which governs attribution of profits to the Gibraltar permanent establishment; and (iv) anti-hybrid mismatch disclaimers and alignment elections under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010 and OECD BEPS Action 2 where the Cayman vehicle is fiscally transparent in a foreign jurisdiction.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None - Gibraltar imposes no VAT, GST, or general sales and use tax; import and excise duties under the Gibraltar Customs Ordinance (modeled on the UK Global Tariff) are the operative indirect taxes

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable for VAT/GST because no such tax exists in Gibraltar. For corporation tax registration of a foreign company's permanent establishment there is no turnover threshold; registration is mandatory from the first commercial shipyard activity. Commercial importers of shipbuilding inputs must register with HM Customs Gibraltar for import duty purposes regardless of consignment volume.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar has no VAT, so a 0% export VAT rating is statutorily inapplicable. The export of a newly constructed ocean container vessel from Gibraltar is outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect taxation where title passes to the foreign buyer and the vessel departs Gibraltar for registration and permanent operation in a foreign jurisdiction. For income tax purposes, the profits from the vessel sale remain within Gibraltar's territorial tax net insofar as negotiation, execution, and payment occur through the Gibraltar permanent establishment. Relief may be available under the UK-Gibraltar treaty profit-attribution rules and, for eligible international shipping income, the Maritime Transport provisions of the Income Tax Act 2010 where the shipyard or its affiliate qualifies as an operator of ships in international traffic; the Cayman exempt status does not eliminate Gibraltar-source shipyard profits.

  • Gibraltar Electronic Customs Export Declaration (SAD Summary or Full Exit Declaration) classifying the vessel under HS heading 8901 (ISIC 3011) and stating the export value
  • Bill of Lading (ocean, clean on board) evidencing delivery FOB, CIF, or other agreed Incoterms to the foreign buyer
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT MT103 evidencing final installment settlement under the Maritime Installment Letter of Credit or Escrow Account release
  • Builders' Certificate confirming new-build container vessel status, tonnage, and purchaser
  • Certificate of Origin evidencing Gibraltar origin of the vessel
  • Input duty suspension/end-use declaration for shipbuilding materials imported duty-free and subsequently incorporated into the exported vessel

No real-time fiscal clearance mandate, national e-invoicing network, digital stamping, or tax-authority online invoicing approval applies in Gibraltar. Customs declarations for imported materials and exported vessels are filed electronically through the Gibraltar electronic customs declaration system using a Single Administrative Document or equivalent export/import declaration. The specified payment rails (Maritime Installment Letter of Credit and Escrow Account) do not attract any stamp, clearance, or digital invoicing duty.

Gibraltar Customs and the Income Tax Office may conduct joint audits of export transactions for up to six years from the due filing date, with no time limit in cases of fraud. False export, zero-rating, or end-use claims attract civil penalties under the Customs Ordinance including forfeiture of goods, a monetary penalty of up to 100% of the duty evaded, or up to 100% of the invoice value for fraudulent declarations. Fraudulent evasion of corporation tax carries a penalty of up to 100% of the tax undercharged plus interest at the prescribed rate and personal liability for responsible officers. A retrospective clawback of duty relief applies where the vessel never leaves Gibraltar waters; reassessment of the full contract consideration as Gibraltar-source profit may occur under section 5 of the Income Tax Act 2010, with criminal prosecution exposure for wilful evasion.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GIP/GBP is fully convertible for both current account and capital account transactions. There are no statutory exchange controls, no prior central bank approval for inward or outward remittances, and no restriction on profit repatriation. The only practical hurdles are GFSC- and bank-level AML/KYC verification, demonstration of the Cayman exempted company's beneficial ownership, economic substance compliance in Gibraltar, and confirmation that neither the entity nor its counterparties are subject to UK, EU, or OFAC sanctions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar imposes no export proceeds surrender requirement and no mandatory conversion ratio. Shipbuilding export receipts may be retained in the invoiced currency or any freely convertible currency. If conversion to GIP/GBP is required, the Gibraltar clearing bank will apply standard market rates and may require the underlying export contract, invoices, bills of lading, and classification certificate (ISIC 3011 / HS 8901) to evidence a genuine maritime trade transaction.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) and HM Government of Gibraltar Financial Services (Currency Notes) Directorate. No separate Gibraltar central bank exists; the Bank of England is not the foreign exchange authority for Gibraltar. The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is issued under the Currency Notes Act 1934 and is pegged at par to GBP.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium-low for a Cayman exempted company engaged in shipyard construction. A GFSC-regulated Gibraltar electronic money institution (EMI) can support operational FX and small supplier disbursements, but cannot hold capital escrow or shipbuilding deposit proceeds as a substitute for a licensed commercial bank. Offshore EMIs without a Gibraltar physical presence are treated as unregulated foreign MFBs, are not permitted to execute Gibraltar-denominated clearing, and are generally refused for structural capital and LC transactions. Legal constraints include the Financial Services Act 2019, the E-Money Regulations 2011, and the loss of EEA passporting after Brexit.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Cayman exempted company with no visible economic substance or operational footprint

Root cause: Cayman exempted company with no visible economic substance or operational footprint

Defense evidence: Gibraltar Economic Substance Act 2020 filing; notarized certificate of incumbency; audited financial statements; management accounts; physical lease or co-working contract in Gibraltar; payroll records; and a detailed shipyard activity plan demonstrating that the Cayman entity is part of the operational value chain.
High-value cross-border shipbuilding deposits from foreign shipowners

Root cause: High-value cross-border shipbuilding deposits from foreign shipowners

Defense evidence: Signed shipbuilding contract; payment schedule; hull identification; export classification certificate (ISIC 3011 / NAICS 336611 / HS 8901); International Chamber of Commerce UCP 600 compliance note; sanctions screening report; and class-society inspection certificates (DNV, ABS, or Lloyd's Register).
Use of corporate or nominee directors/shareholders that obscure ultimate beneficial ownership

Root cause: Use of corporate or nominee directors/shareholders that obscure ultimate beneficial ownership

Defense evidence: Full UBO register from the Cayman registered office; trust deed or nominee declaration; source-of-wealth and source-of-funds documentation; and a clean legal opinion from a licensed Cayman counsel confirming the exempted company's good standing.
Discrepancy between registered office address, mailing address, and actual shipbuilding operations

Root cause: Discrepancy between registered office address, mailing address, and actual shipbuilding operations

Defense evidence: Valid lease or service provider agreement; utility receipts; comprehensive corporate booking evidence; director travel and meeting records; and confirmation of the Gibraltar branch registration if integrated operations reside in Gibraltar.
Large undocumented owner loans or intercompany transfers from the Cayman parent

Root cause: Large undocumented owner loans or intercompany transfers from the Cayman parent

Defense evidence: Notarized loan agreements; board minutes approving each transfer; audited group financial statements; contemporaneous treasury records; transfer pricing analysis; and a tax opinion confirming that the intercompany terms are at arm's length.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For the issuance of share capital or shareholder loans from the Cayman exempted company to the Gibraltar bank account, execute SWIFT MT103 with a compliant purpose code (e.g., CUST/Financial Institutions, ECIC for capital injection, or the bank's specific equity-injection code). Field 70 must reference the board resolution, share issuance date, and capital account ledger. The receiving Gibraltar bank will perform a supplementary UETR (Unique End-to-End Transaction Reference) trace, verify the Cayman UBO against HMT/OFAC lists, and require a certified copy of the board resolution, the register of members, and a certificate of incumbency before crediting the operational account. Shipowner trade prepayments for the vessel must be routed via International Chamber of Commerce UCP 600-compliant MT103 or MT700 (documentary credit), with invoice and construction-stage verification performed by the bank's trade desk.Settlement of maritime equipment, steel procurement, and subcontractor services must use commercial documentary letters of credit (UCP 600) issued by a top-tier international bank with advice from the Gibraltar account bank. For non-LC disbursements, use SWIFT MT103 with unforgeable payment instruction and explicit VAT/reference information; for intra-group funding, use direct participant transfer through the Gibraltar bank's nominee UK CHAPS account. The final clearing rail must contain the beneficiary bank BIC, field 72 with 'BENONLY-PAYMENT', and must not be released without an approved import/export compliance certification and any applicable OFAC/UK Trade Sanctions screening.Outright FX forwards (GIP/GBP, USD, EUR, CNY) with a licensed Gibraltar bank or a UK/EEA counterparty, transacted under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement or local master risk terms.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) only for currencies that are not freely deliverable in the Gibraltar/UK market (e.g., CNY, KRW, INR) and executed through a bank authorized to trade in the underlying restricted currency.Cross-currency interest rate swaps (GBP/USD, EUR/USD) for managing vessel installment payment mismatches, documented under ISDA 2002 with a credit support annex.FX vanilla options (GBP/USD, EUR/USD) to hedge conversion of future shipbuilding installments, purchased on a call/put basis without embedded leverage.Currency swaps backed by executed shipbuilding contracts, strictly limited to commercial hedge exposure and not for speculative trading.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (Cayman Islands); 10% (Gibraltar corporate rate) and 19% (UK main rate) apply only where the exempted company creates a permanent establishment, source income, or is treated as tax resident in those territories.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Cayman: 0% (no dividend withholding tax). Gibraltar: 0% on dividends paid to non-resident shareholders. UK: no dividend withholding tax on distributions by a UK company; but the Cayman entity is not a UK-resident distributing company. Therefore the effective statutory rate on profit repatriation from the Cayman company is 0%.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Annual return must be filed within 12 months after the end of the chargeable accounting period; payment of corporation tax is due 9 months and 1 day after the period end for the corresponding instalment/balancing charge, as directed by the Commissioner of Income Tax.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Filing due on or before 31 January of each calendar year, together with payment of the annual fee; a newly incorporated exempted company must file the return and pay the fee within 90 days after the date of incorporation.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Filing due 12 months after the end of the accounting period; payment of corporation tax due 9 months and 1 day after the period end (or quarterly instalments for large companies).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 250,000 aggregate cross-border controlled transactions per annum; above this threshold the company must prepare a local file and a master file in accordance with OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines, and the Gibraltar tax authority may also apply UK-style Schedule 24 penalties for inaccurate transfer-pricing documentation.

Economic substance scope: The Cayman Economic Substance Regulations apply to 'relevant activities' carried on by a Cayman exempted company. Commercial shipbuilding / shipyard manufacturing is not on the Cayman relevant activity list (banking, financing/leasing, fund management, HQ, holding, insurance, IP, distribution/service centre, and shipping). Since the company performs its active trade through a Gibraltar branch, it must not hold a Cayman 'headquarters' or 'holding company' position unless the relevant activity tests are met. If the company receives any passive dividend/interest income, it must satisfy the pure equity holding company exemption by demonstrating adequate substance in Cayman for holding assets. The Gibraltar branch itself must satisfy the physical presence, employment, and core income-generating activity tests in Gibraltar. Failing this, profits may be re-attributed to Cayman, Gibraltar, or the UK under economic substance, transfer pricing, and CFC rules.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate Income Tax Evasion and False Statements

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar: criminal prosecution for tax fraud; fine up to GBP 10,000 and/or imprisonment up to 7 years, plus unpaid tax, interest and penalties of up to 100%. Under UK criminal law, the company is also exposed to the Corporate Offence of Failure to Prevent the Facilitation of Tax Evasion if senior managers facilitate evasion or non-compliance.

Director personal liability: Civil and criminal liability attaches to every director who knows or reasonably ought to know of the false return. Personal fines, disqualification for up to 10 years, asset freeze orders, travel bans, and in cases of deliberate fraud, extradition and imprisonment. The corporate veil is pierced where the company is a sham or used for evasion, exposing directors to restitution.
