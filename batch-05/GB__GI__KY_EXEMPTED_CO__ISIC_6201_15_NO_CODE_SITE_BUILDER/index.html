Competent corporate registry

Companies House Gibraltar (Office of the Registrar of Companies), 10-12 Harbours Walk, New Harbours, Gibraltar; online e-Registry portal operated under the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission.

Governing statute

Gibraltar Companies Act 2014 (as amended by the Companies (Amendment) Act 2022), Part II ss. 3-21 (registration and incorporation), s. 24 (name and Certificate of Incorporation), s. 26 (registered office), s. 30 (allotment of shares), s. 31 (share capital and subscriber minimum), s. 175 (company secretary); Income Tax Act 2010 (Gibraltar) ss. 4 and 50A (qualifying-company tax-exempt status for foreign-source income).

Formation / constitutional filings

Form CO1 (Application for Registration of a Private Company Limited by Shares) together with notarized Memorandum of Association and Articles of Association.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 14 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up capital required by statute; nominal capital typically adopted as GBP 100 divided into 100 Ordinary Shares of GBP 1.00 each; no escrow account, capital deposit, or banker's certificate is required by the Registrar.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certificate under the Fire Precautions Act 1993 (low-occupancy business premises under 60 persons, means of escape and emergency lighting inspection); Building Control structural load certificate for Class E2 business occupancy; and Occupational Safety and Health clearance under the Health and Safety at Work Act (Cap. 197), confirming first-aid provision, electrical installation safety, and sanitary facilities at a ratio of 1 WC per 20 persons.

Paid-in capital rule: No minimum paid-up capital required by statute; nominal capital typically adopted as GBP 100 divided into 100 Ordinary Shares of GBP 1.00 each; no escrow account, capital deposit, or banker's certificate is required by the Registrar.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar Income Tax Office, Her Majesty’s Government of Gibraltar; official e-Services portal: eservices.gibraltar.gov.gi. Ancillary Cayman-side authority: Department for International Tax Cooperation, Cayman Islands (portal: ditc.ky) for CRS/FATCA and Economic Substance registration.

E-government filing portal

A Cayman exempted company first files an overseas-company registration with the Gibraltar Companies Registry under the Companies Act 2014 (Form OC), then submits Form A1/TFN application to the Gibraltar Income Tax Office within 30 days of acquiring a taxable presence, supported by the Cayman Certificate of Incorporation, exempted-status declaration, charter documents, directors’ resolution, and Gibraltar registered-address certificate. Concurrently, the company files with the Cayman DITC its Economic Substance Notification (assessing whether website-template and low-code development services constitute a relevant activity) and its CRS/FATCA registration to obtain the Cayman TIN. The Gibraltar Tax Reference (GRN) is the operating tax ID for Gibraltar PE return filing; if central management and control is exercised in Gibraltar, the GRN attaches to a fully resident-company tax file.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (Cayman exempted-company status), with a Gibraltar override: upon central management-and-control or a permanent establishment in Gibraltar, the classification becomes Entity_Level_Taxation, taxing a resident company on worldwide assessable profits at 10% corporation tax, or a non-resident company with a PE on territorial profits attributable to the PE.

Available election forms

Neither Gibraltar nor Cayman provides US-style check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections. Gibraltar offers statutory group relief surrender elections among qualifying connected companies, unilateral or treaty double-taxation relief elections, and deductions-reallocation elections under the Income Tax Act 2010; anti-hybrid rules per OECD BEPS Action 2 are mandatory and cannot be elected away. Cayman law provides only the exempted-company undertaking declaration under section 6(1) of the Companies Law, not a flow-through tax election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar levies no VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax; the only domestic indirect impost is Gibraltar Import Duty under the Customs Tariff Ordinance, which applies to physical goods only and is outside scope for digital no-code website-builder services. EU VAT obligations may arise separately when B2C digital services are supplied to EU final consumers.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Gibraltar domestic: not applicable; no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. EU OSS (non-union seller): no de minimis threshold; mandatory registration from the first euro of B2C digital services supplied to EU consumers through the non-union OSS scheme in a chosen EU member state. B2B supplies of web-template/licensing services to EU-established recipients are reverse-charged, requiring no supplier VAT registration. Where Paddle or Stripe acts as merchant of record, the payment-platform entity assumes statutory liability for underlying VAT/sales-tax settlements.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in Gibraltar for digital services: SaaS/web-editor access, template licensing, hosting, and low-code application outputs are outside the scope of Gibraltar import duty and no Gibraltar VAT is chargeable to export. Under EU VAT rules, B2B digital supplies are reverse-charged at the customer’s location and B2C digital e-services are taxed at the customer’s member-state rate, with no 0% export rating. Physical goods may qualify for duty-free export only when leaving Gibraltar customs with evidencing movement documentation.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration (for physical goods movements only)
  • Commercial Invoice and service contract evidencing digital supply (subscription, template license, low-code output)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill (physical shipments only)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or external bank-credit settlement record evidencing FX repatriation of sales proceeds
  • IP/log-usage report substantiating reverse-charge treatment for B2B recipients

None; Gibraltar does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, SAF-T, or API-based invoicing clearance. Standard electronic invoicing (PDF, ZUGFeRD, PEPPOL) is sufficient. EU OSS filings are made quarterly through the selected EU member state’s OSS portal, reflecting aggregate cross-border B2C supplies without per-invoice fiscal authorization. The Cayman Islands impose no e-invoicing clearance regime.

Gibraltar law imposes penalties under the Income Tax Act 2010 for false or misleading records: the lower of 100% of tax evaded or GBP 3,000 per return, rising with repeat defaults and criminal liability for fraudulent evasion. In the EU OSS context, misapplied 0% or incorrect member-state rate mapping is treated as VAT underdeclared, giving rise to reassessment, default interest, penalties up to 100% of the VAT due, and exclusion from the non-union OSS scheme. Misuse of dormant physical export declarations attracts customs forfeiture and civil penalties of up to three times the duty.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar uses the Gibraltar Pound (GIP) at par with GBP, and Sterling (GBP) is also legal tender and freely convertible for current and capital account transactions. The Cayman Islands use the Cayman Islands Dollar (KYD) pegged at 0.80 USD per 1 KYD, with USD accepted as customary and fully convertible. There are no exchange controls in Gibraltar or the Cayman Islands restricting current account transfers, dividend distributions, capital repatriation, or profit remittance. The corporate structure remains tax-exempt in the Cayman Islands, and Gibraltar provides a territorial tax system for non-resident controlled companies; profit remittance is freely permitted, subject to anti-money laundering (AML) source-of-funds verification.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender timeline or mandatory conversion ratio applies in Gibraltar or the Cayman Islands. Export receipts may be retained in foreign currency, converted at commercial rates, or used directly for settlement, subject only to the record-keeping requirements under the Anti-Money Laundering Regulations and Economic Substance (International Tax Compliance) regulations where applicable.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) in coordination with the Gibraltar Monetary Authority functions, operating under the UK Crown Dependencies framework; the Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA) is the relevant authority for the Cayman Exempted Company's domicile.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

High feasibility for Gibraltar-licensed Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) and UK-authorized EMI passported into Gibraltar, but cautious acceptance for Cayman Exempted Companies; Cayman does not issue fintech EMI licenses under a dedicated regime, so digital-only account opening is best achieved through Gibraltar-licensed fintechs (e.g., Globalise, ANNA, or equivalent) or through regulated international EMIs that accept Cayman corporate structures; legal constraint requires clear beneficial-ownership disclosure and a valid substance/economic purpose in the target market for UK or EU payment routing.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections from UK/EU investors should be routed through SWIFT MT103 payments to a Gibraltar operational account, using purpose code 'A.01' (capital contribution) under the UK/OECD payment reporting classification and 'C.70' for software service pre-payments. Each remittance must be accompanied by a board resolution, issued-share certificate, subscription agreement, and an economy-specific source-of-funds declaration. For Cayman-side escrow funding, use SWIFT MT103 with reference 'Cayman Administrative Purpose' and make a subordinated loan or equity contribution into Gibraltar through a UK correspondent account.
