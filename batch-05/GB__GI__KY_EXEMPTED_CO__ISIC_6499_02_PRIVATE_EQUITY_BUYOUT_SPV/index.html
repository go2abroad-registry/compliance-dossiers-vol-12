Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax), HM Government of Gibraltar, Main Street, Gibraltar; digital services portal operated under the Gibraltar Government Taxation Division (www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation). E-government filing portal

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Cayman layer: the Exempted Company is automatically outside Cayman income, corporate, capital gains, and withholding taxation and may hold a Tax Exemption Certificate under the Tax Concessions Act for a fixed concession period; no check-the-box election exists. Gibraltar layer: there is no US-style check-the-box or elective transparent-filing regime; the SPV may elect into the Corporate Tax Act 2019 participation exemption to shelter qualifying dividends and disposal gains from portfolio subsidiaries (typically a minimum 10% shareholding, a 12-month holding period, and substance conditions in Gibraltar). Hybrid instrument and hybrid entity mismatches (OECD BEPS Action 2) are controlled by Gibraltar legislation, and Economic Substance Rules (International Tax Co-operation (Economic Substance) Regulations 2018) apply where financing/headquarters income is booked in Gibraltar; elections in the Cayman/Jersey?/Luxembourg parent chain are irrelevant for Gibraltar filing absent a Gibraltar election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GBP 25,000 annual taxable turnover for suppliers of specified local taxable services (utilities, electricity, gas, water, telecommunications, insurance, gambling); commercial importers of goods must register for Purchase Tax with the Gibraltar Customs Department from the first import, with no de minimis exemption. Pure holding, lending, share-dealing, and syndication financial services of a PE buyout SPV are outside the scope of Purchase Tax and are excluded from the threshold computation.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from Gibraltar to a foreign destination (including the UK, EU member states, and third countries) qualify for 0% Purchase Tax where ownership passes to the customer, the goods are exported in the seller's name, and the goods leave Gibraltar within three months of supply. Services: the SPV's financial holding, lending, and syndication services are outside the scope of Gibraltar Purchase Tax rather than subject to an export zero-rating claim, so no refund election is triggered; any input Purchase Tax on overheads is recoverable only by a registered taxable person making taxable supplies, which a passive PE SPV is not. Distinct exported goods (e.g., movable assets or equipment disposed of by a portfolio company) require full export evidence for the 0% rate to apply.

  • Customs export declaration (Gibraltar Single Administrative Document) endorsed by the Gibraltar Customs Department
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or ferry cargo manifest evidencing physical departure from Gibraltar
  • Original commercial invoice stating the goods and the 0% (or outside-scope) Purchase Tax treatment
  • Inward remittance certificate or SWIFT MT103 evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Transport contract and insurance documents (delivery order, port log, CMR note, or mate's receipt)
  • Where available, the destination country's import customs entry or VAT import declaration

No real-time fiscal clearance, mandated government API, or digital stamping regime exists in Gibraltar. Importers file electronic customs declarations (single administrative document) through Gibraltar Customs, and Purchase Tax is settled at the point of import clearance. Taxable suppliers issue ordinary commercial invoices; there is no mandatory B2B e-invoicing format, no qualifying encryption standard, and no fiscal certification of invoicing software. Commercial invoices, customs declarations, and supporting transport documents must be retained for a minimum of six years for audit.

Gibraltar Customs performs risk-based verification of export claims. Failure to produce the required documentary bundle within the prescribed period triggers reassessment of the supply at 10% Purchase Tax plus statutory interest from the date of supply. Incorrect or fraudulent zero-rating claims incur a tax-geared penalty of up to 100% of the underpaid tax, additional daily penalties for uncorrected deliberate errors, and criminal liability for directors and responsible officers for fraudulent evasion. Refunds are clawed back retrospectively within a six-year limitation period, extended to twenty years in cases of fraud or other dishonest conduct.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar Pound is fully convertible into Sterling and is universally accepted alongside GBP within Gibraltar; there are no exchange-control restrictions on current-account or capital-account transactions. Profit remittances, dividend flows, capital injections and redemption proceeds may be repatriated freely without central bank approval. A Cayman Exempted Company may lawfully maintain multi-currency operating and treasury accounts in GBP, EUR and USD at licensed Gibraltar banks, subject only to standard AML/KYC and CRS/FATCA reporting requirements

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender requirement exists in Gibraltar. Export receipts and all other trade settlement proceeds may be retained in the original currency, freely converted, or transferred without mandatory conversion ratios or time limits. This rule applies equally to a non-resident exempted company holding a Gibraltar bank account for PE acquisition and exit proceeds

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (as the UK monetary and exchange-rate anchor for the sterling zone) in coordination with the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission ('GFSC') as the integrated financial services and banking regulator; the Government of Gibraltar issues the Gibraltar Pound (GIP) under a fixed 1:1 sterling pegged currency issuance arrangement

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium-High viability for pre-banking liquidity movement and low-value GBP/EUR transfers, subject to strict onboarding limitations. A licensed Gibraltar or UK Electronic Money Institution ('EMI') is legally permitted to hold safeguarded client funds and conduct account services for corporate clients, but a Cayman Exempted Company without a proven deal track record will often be classified as a higher-risk customer, triggering enhanced due diligence, geographic shielding of fund flows, and a prohibition on use of the EMI as the sole treasury rail. A fully regulated local bank remains mandatory for the principal acquisition closing account

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Gibraltar does not impose a statutory withholding tax on dividends paid by a Gibraltar-resident company, and the Cayman Islands do not impose any withholding tax on distributions made by a Cayman exempted company. Repatriation of PE profits to a Cayman exempted company is therefore free of Gibraltar withholding.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File mandatory where the Gibraltar SPV has controlled transactions in aggregate exceeding GIP 1,000,000 in the accounting period, or where the PE buyout SPV's related-party revenue/expense is above GIP 300,000 for a non-resident branch. Master File is required for a group with consolidated turnover exceeding GIP 20,000,000; lower-threshold companies must nonetheless prepare and retain contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation on enquiry.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar Economic Substance (Company and Financial Provision) Regulations 2019 require every Gibraltar tax-resident entity and every non-resident Cayman exempted company with a Gibraltar PE to demonstrate adequate economic substance proportionate to its activity. A pure equity-holding SPV must maintain in Gibraltar or the relevant jurisdiction of management and control: a registered office, board minutes, statutory records, and adequate human resources. A leveraged buyout financng SPV engaged in financing or fund-management activities must satisfy the full substance regime, requiring full-time employees, physical office premises, local board meetings, and core income-generating activities performed in Gibraltar; Cayman's parallel ESR framework cannot be used as substitution when management and control is in Gibraltar.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Tax Declaration

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty up to 100% of the tax understated plus interest at 8% per annum; criminal prosecution with fines up to GIP 1,000,000 and imprisonment up to 7 years under the Income Tax Act and Crimes Act 2011.

Director personal liability: Directors, shadow directors, and signatories are jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax and civil restitution; courts may impose asset freezes, travel bans, director disqualification orders, and up to 7 years' imprisonment. The corporate veil is pierced where the Cayman exempted company is found to be a mere façade for the ultimate fund principals.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Under Gibraltar Economic Substance (Company and Financial Provision) Regulations, financial penalties up to GIP 250,000, compulsory strike-off, and disclosure of defaulting entities to the Commissioner. Under Cayman ESR, fines of CI$10,000 plus daily penalties and potential dissolution.

Director personal liability: Directors and corporate officers face personal fines up to GIP 100,000 and disqualification from acting as directors for up to three years; cross-border enforcement may include travel bans and asset restrictions; nominee directors cannot shield themselves behind the exempted-company form.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Adjustment of taxable profits, additional tax assessments, interest at 8% per annum, and surcharges from 25% to 100% of the undercharged tax; failure to provide TP documentation carries separate penalties up to GIP 50,000.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve or sign defective transfer pricing declarations are personally liable for negligence damages and penalties; in cases involving deliberate concealment, criminal tax evasion sanctions and money-laundering investigations may be brought, including imprisonment and travel bans.
AML and Beneficial Ownership Concealment

Statutory sanction: Under Gibraltar's Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and Beneficial Ownership legislation, fines up to GIP 1,000,000 and imprisonment up to 14 years; Cayman AML penalties include monetary penalties and confiscation orders; financial sanctions enforcement and asset freezing orders are routinely applied.

Director personal liability: Directors and Money Laundering Reporting Officers are criminally liable for failure to report suspicious transactions; courts may impose travel bans, civil asset forfeiture, and imprisonment of up to 14 years; the corporate veil is pierced where the exempted company is used as an instrument of financial crime.
Persistent Non-Filing and Administrative Striking-Off

Statutory sanction: Automatic accrual of late filing penalties and daily surcharges, administrative strike-off, loss of legal capacity to transact, freezing of Gibraltar bank accounts, and referral to tax enforcement for collection of penalties and debts.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for penalties and debts incurred after insolvency if they allowed the entity to continue trading while non-compliant; public blacklisting in the Gibraltar corporate registry, travel bans, asset seizure, and disqualification are available enforcement measures.
