Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax), HM Government of Gibraltar, 2nd Floor, Bristol House, 8 Market Street, Gibraltar GX11 1AA; online portal: myTax GI (https://mytax.gi). HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) has no fiscal jurisdiction over Gibraltar, which is a self-governing UK Overseas Territory excluded from the UK VAT area and the UK Corporation Tax system.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-incorporated entity, including a Cayman Exempted Company, becomes registrable in Gibraltar only if it carries on a trade in or from Gibraltar through a permanent establishment (PE) or is managed and controlled from Gibraltar. Step 1: file an application for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) via the myTax GI portal. Step 2: submit the Cayman Certificate of Incorporation, Certificate of Good Standing, register of directors and ultimate beneficial owners (under the Beneficial Ownership (Access) Act 2018) and, where applicable, a certified resolution appointing a Gibraltar-resident agent for service. Step 3: if a chargeable presence exists, file the annual Form 20 Corporate Tax Return and audited accounts. Step 4: where no PE or Gibraltar management-and-control nexus exists, file a protective nil-return or non-residence notification to document the position. In the Cayman Islands, the exempted company files no income tax return and pays no income tax; it must simply maintain its registered office and statutory register under the Companies Act (Cap. 22).

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (Cayman Islands exempted company with no Cayman income, capital gains, or withholding taxes and, upon obtaining a Tax Exemption Undertaking, a 20-year exemption from future Cayman taxes, extendable in ten-year increments up to 30 years) combined with Territorial_Scope in Gibraltar (only Gibraltar-source profits of a permanent establishment or profits arising from Gibraltar management-and-control are taxable; the standard corporate rate is 10%).

Available election forms

(1) Cayman: elective undertaking under Section 6 of the Companies Act (Cap. 22) and the Tax Concessions Law, conferring an exemption from future Cayman tax legislation for up to 30 years; (2) Gibraltar: no check-the-box or pass-through election exists; a foreign company may submit a protective non-residence declaration, and a company undertaking an 'identified activity' under the Economic Substance (Amendment) Act 2020 must notify the Commissioner of Income Tax of its core income-generating activities and satisfy the economic substance test; (3) hybrid mismatch elections are unavailable in Gibraltar because the territory operates a territorial income tax system and is outside the scope of the UK CTA 2010 Part 5 hybrid mismatch rules.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None_No_VAT_GST (Gibraltar is a non-VAT customs territory; indirect taxation is limited to Import Duty under the Customs Ordinance 2010 and excise duties on alcohol, tobacco and fuel).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not_Applicable — Gibraltar imposes no VAT or GST and maintains no general turnover tax registration threshold. Import Duty registration with the Gibraltar Customs House arises only upon physical importation of goods into Gibraltar; pure cross-border vessel brokerage and transit-trade commission income derived from overseas principals is outside the charge.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not_Applicable — Gibraltar levies no VAT, so no 0% export VAT rate, zero-rating claim mechanism, or VAT refund system exists. Supplies of vessel brokerage and agri-commodity transit-trade services rendered from Gibraltar to non-resident counterparties fall entirely outside the indirect tax charge. Where physical goods are moved through Gibraltar under transit or warehousing arrangements, liability to Import Duty is suspended rather than zero-rated, and no refund is necessary if the goods remain in transit.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Entry (Electronic Goods Declaration / Single Administrative Document)
  • Bill of Lading or Non-Negotiable Sea Waybill
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin (where preferential origin is claimed)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT credit confirmation evidencing FX repatriation of export proceeds

Not_Applicable — Gibraltar operates no real-time electronic invoicing, fiscal clearance, or digital stamping regime. For physical imports and exports, customs entries are filed electronically using the Gibraltar Customs electronic declaration system based on the Single Administrative Document (SAD); no fiscal invoice clearance API or digital stamping is mandated for brokerage services.

Fraudulent or reckless customs declarations attract civil penalties up to GBP 10,000 per declaration, forfeiture of goods, and, in aggravated cases, criminal liability on indictment with imprisonment up to 7 years and unlimited fines. For direct tax, deliberate under-declaration of brokerage income allows the Commissioner of Income Tax to issue discovery assessments retrospectively within 6 years (extendable to 12 years for fraud), together with penalties up to 100% of the tax undercharged and late-payment interest. Where an artificial arrangement is used to mask a Gibraltar PE or management-and-control presence, the Commissioner may recharacterise the arrangement, claw back tax at 10% on the full gross brokerage commission, and add penalties without prejudice to economic substance enforcement under the Economic Substance Act 2020.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar is a British Overseas Territory with no exchange controls or restrictions on capital account convertibility. The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is pegged on a one-to-one basis to GBP sterling and is fully convertible for both residents and non-residents. Profit remittances from a Gibraltar Pawn licensed entity or a non-resident Cayman exempted company operating through Gibraltar are freely permitted, subject to GFSC anti-money laundering (AML) screening, tax residence calibrations, and sanctions compliance. No profit remittance surcharge or withholding tax is levied in Gibraltar, but clear beneficial ownership and economic substance documentation must be maintained.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender requirement exists in Gibraltar. All foreign currency export receipts may be retained, maintained offshore, or converted freely. There is no mandatory conversion ratio and no statutory timeline requiring repatriation or conversion into Gibraltar pounds. Businesses may choose to hold USD, EUR, or GBP operating balances to optimize treasury efficiency under their internal liquidity policy.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority) in coordination with His Majesty's Government of Gibraltar (Gibraltar pound issuer) and the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as financial regulatory authority

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility: Medium. Gibraltar licensed e-money institutions and payment institutions operate under the Financial Services (Electronic Money) Act and the FS Act. Offshore EMIs domiciled in the European Economic Area (EEA) no longer enjoy automatic passporting into Gibraltar post-Brexit, and UK EMIs cannot passport into Gibraltar without separate GFSC authorization. Digital EMIs from the UK, Lithuania, or Poland may be viable only if they have an active GFSC temporary or full license, or operate into Gibraltar under a reverse solicitation model accepted by GFSC. For the Cayman exempted company structure, a locally licensed Gibraltar EMI is strongly preferred for settlement speed, while an offshore EMI is legally constrained by sanctions screening, bank sponsor requirements, and inability to offer Gibraltar IBAN-based clearing.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections from the Cayman parent to the Gibraltar operating treasury should be executed via SWIFT MT103 or MT202 COV using the Gibraltar bank's BIC, with the wire tagged as 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION' or 'SHAREHOLDER SUBORDINATED LOAN' under direction of the GFSC. For GBP, use CHAPS coexistence with SWIFT; for EUR, settle through TARGET2 via correspondent banks; for USD, settle via Fedwire or CHIPS. The statutory purpose code for direct investment capital transfer should reference 'FDI' or 'Capital Investment', and the beneficiary account must be a capital escrow account unless a signed subordination agreement permits an operational account. Banks will require the signing of a declaration that the capital does not derive from criminal proceeds, and sanctions filtering is applied at both the originator and beneficiary wheels.Trade settlements for bulk grain and soybean purchases use: (1) Irrevocable, transferable documentary letters of credit (LCs) issued under UCP600, with SWIFT MT700 or MT707 for issuance/amendment and MT798 for transfer; (2) SWIFT MT103 for wire disbursements to suppliers not using the LC structure; (3) CHAPS for urgent GBP payments; (4) Fedwire for USD supplier settlements. Confirmation of LC is recommended through a top-tier European or UK correspondent bank to mitigate unilateral cancellation risk. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

Gibraltar applies a headline corporate income tax rate of 10% on assessable profits of tax-resident companies. A Cayman Exempted Company is generally non-resident for Gibraltar tax purposes unless central management and control is exercised in Gibraltar, in which case the 10% rate applies to Gibraltar-source and certain foreign-source income. For a qualifying offshore-oriented exempted company with no Gibraltar-source trading activity, the effective tax liability is nil.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% under Gibraltar domestic law. No Gibraltar withholding tax is imposed on dividends paid by a Gibraltar-resident company to a non-resident shareholder, and dividends received by a non-resident Cayman exempted company from a Gibraltar subsidiary are not subject to Gibraltar tax.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual return must be filed within 12 months after the end of the accounting period. For a December year-end, the statutory deadline is 31 December of the following year. Payment of assessed tax is due 30 days after notice of assessment; interim payments generally do not apply to non-resident exempted companies with nil taxable profits.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 (approximately GBP 1,000,000) in aggregate related-party transactions in a fiscal year triggers mandatory transfer pricing documentation, including a master file and local file. A Cayman exempted company with only third-party bulk commodity brokerage transactions and no Gibraltar-connected parties will generally fall below the threshold.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar applies economic substance rules aligned with OECD BEPS Action 5, requiring resident companies conducting relevant activities to demonstrate management, employees, premises, and expenditure within Gibraltar. A Cayman Exempted Company that is not Gibraltar-resident is outside the Gibraltar ESR regime; however, the Cayman Islands separately mandates annual Economic Substance Notification and, where relevant activities exist, a Cayman Economic Substance Return. Failure to maintain actual substance in the claimed state of residence may result in a review by the Gibraltar Income Tax Office or the Cayman Tax Information Authority.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Declaration of Non-Residence

Statutory sanction: Additional assessment of 10% tax, tax-geared penalties of up to 100% of the understated tax, discretionary fines up to GIP 50,000, interest at 8% per annum, and criminal prosecution leading to fines up to GIP 100,000 and imprisonment for up to 7 years.

Director personal liability: Directors signing false returns or participating in the sham management structure may be jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax and penalties. Director disqualification orders up to 15 years, travel bans, passport seizure, and imprisonment up to 7 years can be imposed.
Shell Company / Artificial Substance Arrangement

Statutory sanction: In the Cayman Islands, penalties up to CI$20,000 for ESR failure, potential strike-off and dissolution of the company, rendering contracts voidable and bank accounts frozen. In Gibraltar or the UK, the arrangement may be recharacterised, leading to assessment at 10% or diversion of profits tax at 25%.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors and shadow directors may face criminal liability, disqualification, personal civil claims for amounts improperly distributed, and fines up to GIP 100,000 or imprisonment up to 5 years for false record-keeping.
AML / Sanctions and Suspicious Payments

Statutory sanction: Unlimited fines under the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act; confiscation of proceeds; GFSC fines up to GIP 100,000 per breach; OFAC civil penalties up to USD 330,000 per violation and potential complete exclusion from the US banking system.

Director personal liability: Directors may be criminally prosecuted for facilitating sanctions evasion or money laundering, facing imprisonment up to 14 years, unlimited fines, confiscation of personal assets, stop-list travel bans, and international arrest warrants.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: The Gibraltar Commissioner may adjust profits to the arm's length result, assess additional 10% tax, impose transfer pricing documentation penalties up to GIP 50,000, and in cases of deliberate concealment impose penalties up to 200% of the additional tax.

Director personal liability: Directors facilitating dishonest tax evasion arrangements may be liable under the UK Criminal Finances Act 2017, with imprisonment up to 14 years and unlimited fines; joint and several liability for the additional tax may arise for signatories.
Treaty Abuse / Artificial Profit Extraction

Statutory sanction: UK diverted profits tax at 25%; recharacterisation of payments as distributions; GAAR penalties up to 100% of the tax avoided; disallowance of deductions in the paying entity.

Director personal liability: Individuals involved may be criminally liable for cheating the public revenue, with UK imprisonment up to 21 years or Gibraltar imprisonment up to 10 years; civil recovery orders and director disqualification may also apply.
