Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar, 169 John Mackintosh Square, Gibraltar GX11 1AA; e-filing portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation

        
E-government filing portal
The Cayman Exempted Company is not automatically within the Gibraltar tax net. If it establishes a physical place of business or derives Gibraltar-source income, it must first register as an overseas company with the Gibraltar Companies Registry under Part 9 of the Companies Act 2014. Within 30 days of that registration, the entity must apply to the Income Tax Office on the prescribed non-resident company application form, attaching certified copies of the Cayman Certificate of Incorporation and Certificate of Good Standing, the registered office address, local contact details, and a declaration of the nature of Gibraltar activities. A Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) is issued within 15 working days. If the entity merely conducts cross-border online sales through Kickstarter/Stripe/PayPal without a permanent establishment, no TIN is required and no Gibraltar income tax return is due.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Tax_Exempt_Zone

        
Available election forms
Gibraltar does not operate a check-the-box regime. Available statutory elections include: (a) group relief election under the Income Tax Act allowing surrender of trading losses within a 75% group; (b) an application for an advance tax ruling confirming non-resident status and territorial scope; (c) a procedural election under the Income Tax (Hybrid and Other Mismatches) Rules 2019 for alignment of accounting periods; and (d) participation exemption election on qualifying shareholdings. No election is available to treat the Cayman Exempted Company as a pass-through or transparent entity; it remains an entity-level taxable foreign company only to the extent it has Gibraltar-source profits.

      

    

    

      
VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

      

        
Standard VAT / GST rate
Gibraltar has no VAT or GST (outside the EU VAT area); indirect taxation consists of import and excise duties on goods entering Gibraltar, while exports and offshore sales are not subject to any local consumption tax.

        
Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)
GIP 0.00 (no VAT registration threshold because no VAT law exists; any business undertaking import or export transactions must register with HM Customs of Gibraltar regardless of turnover).

      

      
      

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
Exports of goods manufactured in Gibraltar or re-exported from Gibraltar are outside the indirect tax base, creating an effective 0% rate by statutory design. To qualify, the goods must physically leave Gibraltar for a foreign destination, title must pass to the overseas customer, and the exporter must hold evidence of departure. There is no separate VAT refund mechanism because no input VAT exists; the zero-rating is achieved through non-taxability of the export transaction.

No real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping mandate. Invoices must be retained under Gibraltar record-keeping rules. Customs declarations must be filed electronically through the Gibraltar Electronic Customs Declaration System (ECDS) using a Single Administrative Document (SAD); an electronic declaration reference is generated for each shipment. No government pre-approval of invoices is required.

      
Export transactions are scrutinised jointly by HM Customs of Gibraltar and the Income Tax Office. If the export zero-rating is claimed falsely or documentation is found to be fraudulent, the transaction is recharacterised as a local supply. The Commissioner may raise a Gibraltar income tax assessment on the full profit at 12.5% (or the applicable corporate rate), add interest at 8% per annum, and impose penalties up to 150% of the additional tax. Customs offences attract forfeiture of goods, fines up to £10,000, and imprisonment for up to 7 years. Any previously granted exemption or relief is subject to retrospective clawback for a period of up to six years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

      
Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
Capital account and current account convertibility are fully unrestricted in Gibraltar and in the Cayman Islands. The Gibraltar pound is pegged at parity to GBP, and both GIP and GBP are freely convertible within the jurisdiction. For a Cayman Exempted Company operating through Gibraltar, profit remittance is not subject to withholding taxes or exchange-control approval. Dividends, royalties, loan repayments, and liquidation distributions may flow without deduction or prior authorisation. The only practical hurdles are anti-money-laundering documentary requirements, tax-residency determination, and any economic substance reporting obligations in the Cayman Islands.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
No statutory export-proceeds surrender or conversion ratio applies in Gibraltar or the Cayman Islands. A Cayman Exempted Company is free to retain export receipts in any convertible currency, with no mandatory timeline for repatriation and no obligation to convert into GIP, GBP, or KYD. The effective mandatory conversion ratio is 0%; all proceeds hold immediately and indefinitely at the Company's disposition.

      

      
Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC), acting as the integrated banking and financial-services regulator under the Financial Services (Banking) Act; Gibraltar has no separate central bank, with sterling monetary authority residing in the Bank of England and HM Treasury, and note issuance for the Gibraltar pound undertaken by the Government of Gibraltar under statutory fiat.

    

    

      
Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

      

        
        

          

            Stripe
            Fit: High
          

          
Feasibility rating: Low to Medium. Gibraltar- and UK-licensed electronic money institutions (e.g. Wise Business, Revolut Business, or specialised B2B FX platforms) generally decline Cayman Exempted Companies because of the non-resident tax-exempt status, opacity of the corporate register, and perceived Shell-company risk. Legal constraints under Gibraltar's Financial Services Act and UK e-money regulations prevent an unlicensed Cayman entity from holding e-money directly; it must be onboarded as a customer of a licensed EMI. Where an EMI does accept the structure, transaction limits are frequently capped and enhanced KYC is imposed. For Kickstarter/Stripe recurring collections, a dedicated merchant acquiring solution via a licensed PSP is materially more reliable than a general-purpose EMI.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

      
Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

    

    

      
Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)
10% (flat Gibraltar corporate income tax rate on chargeable profits under the Income Tax Act 2010, s.10)

      
Dividend withholding tax (WHT)
0% - Gibraltar does not impose withholding tax on dividends paid by a Gibraltar company or Gibraltar branch to its shareholders, including non-resident shareholders of a Cayman Exempted Company.

    

    

      
Mandatory Return Breakdown

      
      

        
        
Deadline:  · Frequency:

              Late or non-filing: fixed penalty of GIP 300; continuing daily penalty up to GIP 100 per day; the Commissioner may issue an estimated assessment with interest from the due date; fraudulent or negligent returns incur a tax-geared penalty up to 100% of the tax under-assessed, plus summary or indictable criminal fines.

              Compute as Box 4 minus Box 6 and Box 7; non-residents must maintain contemporaneous local accounts reconciling the PE result.
              Late or non-filing: fixed penalty of GIP 300; continuing daily penalty up to GIP 100 per day; the Commissioner may issue an estimated assessment with interest from the due date; fraudulent or negligent returns incur a tax-geared penalty up to 100% of the tax under-assessed, plus summary or indictable criminal fines.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

      
Master / Local File threshold: GIP 500,000 aggregate value of related-party transactions in an accounting period requires a full Gibraltar Local File; a GIP 5,000,000 consolidated group revenue triggers a Master File; below these thresholds the company must still retain a one-page TP policy, functional analysis and benchmarking memorandum based on OECD principles.

      
Economic substance scope: Gibraltar applies the OECD/EU Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) for geographically mobile activities, including distribution and IP exploitation. The Cayman Exempted Company using Gibraltar as a booking or payment gateway must demonstrate: physical office or workshop in Gibraltar; minimum of one director and one employee physically present (where applicable); board meetings in Gibraltar with substantive minutes; core income-generating activities (dice design, mould maintenance, order fulfilment decisions, payment risk management) performed in Gibraltar; and adequate operating expenditure. Non-compliance triggers denial of tax residence certificate, reassessment of profits and administrative penalties, plus possible strike-off.

      

    

    

      
Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

      
      

        Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Tax Return
        
Statutory sanction: On summary conviction: fine up to GIP 10,000 and/or imprisonment up to 12 months; on indictment: unlimited fine and/or imprisonment up to 5 years; the Commissioner may raise escaped assessments for up to 6 prior years with interest and a penalty of 100% of the tax.

        
Director personal liability: Directors who sign or knowingly cause a false return are personally and severally liable for the assessed tax shortfall, face criminal conviction, disqualification from acting as a director for up to 15 years, and the court may impose a departure prohibition order preventing them from leaving Gibraltar until the tax and penalties are settled.

      

      
      

        Shell Company / Sham Economic Substance
        
Statutory sanction: Disregard of the corporate form; reassessment of all Gibraltar-source profits at 10%; ESR administrative fine of GIP 5,000 to GIP 20,000 per failure; cancellation of the Gibraltar tax residence certificate and Companies Registry strike-off; referral to the Criminal Investigation Unit.

        
Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for the reassessed taxes and ESR penalties, face disqualification orders under the Companies Act 2014, seizure of personal assets under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015, and travel bans while the tax debt is under investigation.

      

      
      

        AML/CTF Failure in Payment Rail Operations
        
Statutory sanction: Under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and the Gibraltar AML/CFT Code: unlimited fines on conviction for money laundering (up to 14 years imprisonment); regulatory penalties of up to GIP 5,000,000 or 10% of annual turnover; account freezes and forfeiture orders; mandatory revocation of EMI and payment-processing arrangements.

        
Director personal liability: Directors are personally exposed to confiscation orders over personal wealth derived from the offending, disqualification, imprisonment for up to 14 years for money laundering, and cross-border travel bans pending extradition or mutual legal assistance requests.

      

      
      

        Artificial Transfer Pricing / Base Erosion
        
Statutory sanction: Commissioner's TP adjustment restoring the arm's length profit with interest; disallowance of the royalty/fee deduction; penalty of 100% of the under-declared tax; joint audit by the Gibraltar Commissioner and HMRC under the UK-Gibraltar exchange of information arrangement; publication of the adjustment as a serious-compliance breach.

        
Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly execute artificial TP policies are jointly and severally liable for the adjusted tax and penalties, may be prohibited from holding any Gibraltar corporate appointment, and face criminal prosecution for cheating the public revenue with imprisonment up to 5 years.
