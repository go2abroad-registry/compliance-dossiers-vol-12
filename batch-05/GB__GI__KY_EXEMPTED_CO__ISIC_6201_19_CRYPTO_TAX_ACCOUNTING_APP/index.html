Stage 03
Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

      
Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

    

    

      
Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (responsible for corporate income tax self-assessment and issuance of Tax Reference Numbers); corporate registry filings are made with the Registrar of Companies, Gibraltar. Official portal: www.gibraltar.gov.gi (Government of Gibraltar / Income Tax Office online services).

        
E-government filing portal
A Cayman Exempted Company establishing a branch or place of business in Gibraltar must: (1) register as an Overseas Company with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies within one month of establishing a place of business under the Companies Act 2014, filing certified Cayman incorporation documents, constitutional documents, register of directors/members, and a Gibraltar registered office address; (2) upon Companies House registration, the Registrar automatically notifies the Income Tax Office, which issues a Tax Reference Number (TRN); (3) file a self-assessment tax return each year of assessment (YA runs 1 July to 30 June) based on accounts for the accounting period ending in the preceding calendar year. No minimum capital requirement applies.

      

      
Tax ID type & application path: Territorial_Scope - The Cayman Exempted Company remains wholly exempt from direct taxation in the Cayman Islands under the Companies Act (Cayman), while its Gibraltar branch is chargeable to Gibraltar income tax on a territorial basis only: Gibraltar-source trading profits are taxed at 12.5%, foreign-source income is excluded, and no tax is levied on capital gains, dividends, interest, or royalties where sourced outside Gibraltar. — A Cayman Exempted Company establishing a branch or place of business in Gibraltar must: (1) register as an Overseas Company with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies within one month of establishing a place of business under the Companies Act 2014, filing certified Cayman incorporation documents, constitutional documents, register of directors/members, and a Gibraltar registered office address; (2) upon Companies House registration, the Registrar automatically notifies the Income Tax Office, which issues a Tax Reference Number (TRN); (3) file a self-assessment tax return each year of assessment (YA runs 1 July to 30 June) based on accounts for the accounting period ending in the preceding calendar year. No minimum capital requirement applies.

    

    

      
Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Territorial_Scope - The Cayman Exempted Company remains wholly exempt from direct taxation in the Cayman Islands under the Companies Act (Cayman), while its Gibraltar branch is chargeable to Gibraltar income tax on a territorial basis only: Gibraltar-source trading profits are taxed at 12.5%, foreign-source income is excluded, and no tax is levied on capital gains, dividends, interest, or royalties where sourced outside Gibraltar.

        
Available election forms
Gibraltar has no US-style check-the-box election. Available statutory elections under the Income Tax Act 2010 include: (1) election for Gross Income-Based Assessment (GIBA) applicable to regulated financial services businesses (e.g., insurance, banking, trust companies) at reduced gross-basis rates instead of profit-based assessment; (2) election for the qualifying Intellectual Property (Patent Box) regime for eligible software/crypto-tax-IP royalties, subject to economic substance and nexus requirements; (3) application of hybrid mismatch rules under Gibraltar's Income Tax (Amendment) legislation aligning with OECD/EU ATAD principles, disallowing mismatched deductions otherwise arising from the Cayman/Gibraltar parent-branch structure.

      

    

    

      
VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

      

        
Standard VAT / GST rate
None - Gibraltar levies no VAT, GST, or sales and use tax. Gibraltar is outside the EU VAT area and the UK VAT regime (20%) does not extend to the territory. Limited customs/excise duties apply only to imports of physical goods such as alcohol, tobacco, fuel, and personal effects.

        
Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)
N/A - No turnover-based indirect tax registration threshold exists in Gibraltar because no VAT/GST/sales tax system is in force. A physical import of goods may attract excise/import duty per the Gibraltar Customs tariff schedule, but no registration threshold applies to the delivery of digital SaaS services or cryptocurrency payment rails.

      

      
      

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
Not applicable in the traditional VAT sense, as no VAT is imposed in Gibraltar. The equivalent export-favourable outcome is the territorial sourcing exclusion under the Income Tax Act 2010: revenue earned by the Cayman Exempted Company's Gibraltar branch from the supply of digital on-chain tax calculation and bookkeeping SaaS services to non-resident customers is treated as foreign-source income and excluded from Gibraltar chargeable income, provided the contracts are negotiated and executed outside Gibraltar, the services are performed in part or wholly outside the territory, and the branch maintains demonstrable economic substance in Gibraltar. No formal zero-rating application is required.

No mandatory real-time fiscal clearance, electronic invoicing, API reporting, or digital stamping regime exists in Gibraltar. The Income Tax Office accepts standard electronic invoices and accounting records; taxpayers must retain supporting documentation for at least six years. Commercial e-invoicing and blockchain-based transaction records are permitted.

      
The Commissioner of Income Tax may reopen assessments within 6 years for negligent or incorrect returns and up to 21 years where fraud or wilful default is established. Penalties under the Income Tax Act 2010 include: up to 100% of the tax undercharged for incorrect/negligent returns; up to 200% of the tax undercharged for fraudulent returns; daily default penalties of GIP 10; and interest at 8% per annum on unpaid tax. If the foreign-source income exclusion is claimed but the branch lacks economic substance or artificially routes revenue through the Cayman Exempted Company to avoid Gibraltar tax, the Income Tax Office may reverse the exclusion, re-characterise the full amount as Gibraltar-source income, impose the 12.5% corporation tax, and apply penalty and interest retroactively.

Stage 04
Banking & Treasury Architecture

      
Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
The GIP is pegged at 1:1 to GBP and is freely convertible; sterling is legal tender in Gibraltar. Both current account and capital account transactions are effectively free of exchange-control restrictions following the repeal of UK exchange controls in 1979. A Cayman Exempted Company may therefore invoice, settle, repatriate profits and remit dividends in any convertible currency without obtaining prior central-bank approval. The practical hurdles are not regulatory approvals but rather bank/EMI KYC acceptance, source-of-funds substantiation, and Gibraltar economic-substance obligations if the company is centrally managed and controlled in Gibraltar.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
There are no statutory surrender rules, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no prescribed timeline requiring export receipts to be converted into GIP/GBP. Export/sales proceeds may be retained in the original invoice currency, including USD, EUR, GBP, and USDC where the receiving institution maintains compliant stablecoin or crypto-asset custody/settlement rails. For SaaS/crypto tax-accounting receivables, the company should document underlying licence agreements and invoice references to preserve a clean audit trail.

      

      
Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority for GBP and the pegged Gibraltar pound, GIP) and Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) (banking, payment services and DLT regulatory supervisor; Gibraltar has no separate central bank and no exchange-control authority).

    

    

      
Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

      

        
        

          

            Stripe
            Fit: High
          

          
Medium-to-High for payments-only fintech/EMI services such as Stripe, Wise or UK-licensed EMIs where the Cayman Exempted Company can evidence genuine software/SaaS activity and no client-money custody. However, post-Brexit UK/EEA EMI licences are not automatically passportable into Gibraltar; a GFSC-recognised e-money/payment institution or a Gibraltar DLT-licensed entity is required for regulated stablecoin payment services. The crypto tax-accounting app itself does not require a statutory banking licence, nor does processing only its own SaaS revenue create a payments-firm trigger, but any future custody of customer tokens or client money would broaden the GFSC authorisation perimeter.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

      
      

        Cayman Exempted Company with no audited financial statements, no physical office and no granted economic substance in Gibraltar
        
Root cause: Cayman Exempted Company with no audited financial statements, no physical office and no granted economic substance in Gibraltar

        
Defense evidence: Provide orphan/agent structure chart, Cayman certificate of incorporation, good standing certificate, registered agent incumbency letter, articles of association, share register, beneficial ownership declaration, management/audited accounts, business plan, client contracts, and a written premise that management and substance remain in the Cayman Islands/UK/Gibraltar as applicable.

      

      
      

        Crypto-asset / digital ledger business activity flagged as high AML risk by bank compliance
        
Root cause: Crypto-asset / digital ledger business activity flagged as high AML risk by bank compliance

        
Defense evidence: Submit the GFSC DLT/Travel Rule risk assessment, AML/CTF policies and procedures, token-screening logic on the tax-accounting SaaS platform, evidence that the firm does not custody client assets or execute unlicensed payment processing, proof of voluntary GFSC DLT registration (if any), external audit report on blockchain analytics, and regulated-bank references.

      

      
      

        Ultimate beneficial owner or key principal domiciled in a high-risk or heightened-risk jurisdiction
        
Root cause: Ultimate beneficial owner or key principal domiciled in a high-risk or heightened-risk jurisdiction

        
Defense evidence: Compile source of wealth statement, source of funds trail, certified passport and proof of residential address, tax residence certificate, professional reference from a regulated legal/accounting firm, explanation of corporate commercial purpose, and clearing exchange/wallet forensic report for any crypto-derived beneficial-owner proceeds.

      

      
    

    

      
Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

      

        Inbound equity or shareholder loan injections from the Cayman parent/beneficial owners into the Gibraltar operating account should be routed via SWIFT MT103 with purpose codes denoting equity capital contribution (e.g. P0101 general commercial payment / P1105 intra-group financing) and must be supported by an executed board resolution and share/loan documentation. SaaS subscription receipts may arrive via Stripe, USDC, or direct SWIFT/Ach; USDC collections should be converted through a GFSC/UK FCA-compliant exchange under a two-account structure (hot wallet with withdrawal whitelists and cold treasury storage), then settled in GBP/EUR/USD to the bank account with a matching invoice reference. Every inbound transfer should include the LEI-embedded legal entity name and the specific subscription/customer reference for reconciliation.Supplier settlements for cloud infrastructure, licensing, legal, payroll and software tools should be executed by SWIFT MT103 straight-through processing in the supplier's own currency, or by card and local payment rails (SEPA, Faster Payments/Chaps) where the Gibraltar bank provides coverage. Commercial letters of credit (sight LC or usance LC) are permissible but generally only appropriate for physical-goods transactions; for this software tax-accounting SaaS, seller-hosted recurring payments and smart-contract USDC disbursements are preferred. A controlled zero-balance treasury should be used, with same-day sweeps from operating account to a regulated deposit/MMF account, and all outbound crypto disbursements must pass through a GFSC-compliant travel-rule and sanctions screening engine.Outright FX forward contracts in GBP/USD and GBP/EUR with a GFSC-regulated or UK FCA-authorised bank, limited to underlying contractual fx exposure.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) in GBP/USD where the counterparty bank accepts a GBP reference rate; NDFs directly on GIP are generally unavailable due to the hard parity peg to GBP.Cross-currency swaps between USD and GBP/EUR for balance-sheet hedging of long-term crypto-denominated receivables and debt instruments.Vanilla European-style FX options (call/put) for hedging subscription revenue or procurement costs in USD/EUR/GBP.Zero-cost FX collars and tunelling structures for medium-term treasury risk management when traded with a licensed Gibraltar/UK bank under ISDA documentation.
      

      
E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Medium-to-High for payments-only fintech/EMI services such as Stripe, Wise or UK-licensed EMIs where the Cayman Exempted Company can evidence genuine software/SaaS activity and no client-money custody.
