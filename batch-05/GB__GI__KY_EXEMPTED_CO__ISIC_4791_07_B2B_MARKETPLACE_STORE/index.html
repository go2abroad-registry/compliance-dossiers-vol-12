Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A registered office physically located in Gibraltar is mandatory under s. 43 of the Companies Act 2014; a post-office box or a virtual-only address does not satisfy the statutory requirement. The registered office must be a real physical address within Gibraltar, typically the office of a licensed Corporate Service Provider. A virtual office may be used only for incidental administrative mail handling and cannot replace the registered office. For any entity claiming the Qualifying Company or grandfathered exempt status under the Income Tax Act 2010 (ss. 40A-40C), genuine economic substance is required in Gibraltar, including local premises, employees, and effective management and control; a virtual-only presence would jeopardize the tax status and may trigger regulatory challenge.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current-account and capital-account convertibility is available in the United Kingdom, Gibraltar, and the Cayman Islands. No exchange-control approval, currency licensing, or quantitative restriction applies to B2B marketplace revenues, equity injections, trade payables, or profit distributions. The Cayman Islands dollar is pegged to the US dollar at KYD 1.00 = USD 1.20, but the entity may freely hold and settle in GBP, USD, EUR, and CNH accounts. Profit remittance to shareholders is unblocked at law: no Cayman dividend withholding, no Gibraltar withholding, and no UK withholding arises provided the Cayman Exempted Company is not UK/Gibraltar tax resident, has no UK/Gibraltar permanent establishment, and satisfies Cayman economic substance filing obligations. Residual hurdles are AML, enhanced due diligence, and substance-evidence requirements rather than currency-convertibility restrictions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender regime, mandatory conversion ratio, or repatriation timeline exists in the United Kingdom, Gibraltar, or the Cayman Islands. B2B marketplace export receipts may be retained indefinitely in the invoiced currency in any onshore or offshore operating account. For transactional prudence, the collecting bank will expect underlying sales invoices and marketplace settlement statements when large or recurring credits are received or converted, and the company must retain such records for at least five years to satisfy GFSC AML expectations and Cayman TIA documentation duties.

Central bank supervision: Not applicable as a single authority: the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) supervises Gibraltar-licensed banks and enforces the AML/CFT framework; the Bank of England is the monetary authority for GBP across the United Kingdom and Gibraltar; HM Treasury and the Office of Financial Sanctions Implementation (OFSI) administer sanctions and residual exchange control matters; and the Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA) issues the Cayman Islands dollar but imposes no exchange controls.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium as a settlement-node rail but Low as a primary treasury account for a Cayman Exempted Company. Gibraltar-licensed and UK-FCA-authorised electronic money institutions (EMIs) may legally onboard a Cayman offshore corporate, but enhanced due diligence is triggered, and post-Brexit EEA passporting no longer allows an EU-only EMI to serve Gibraltar without a GFSC or FCA licence. EMIs cannot grant credit or pay interest on e-money float balances, and their safeguarding obligations require client funds to be ring-fenced with eligible credit institutions or central banks, creating additional counterparty and operational friction. The recommended architecture is to confine EMIs to segregated settlement accounts and vendor-payout rails, while holding treasury cash, escrow balances, and all FX hedging instruments at a Tier-1 Gibraltar licensed bank.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Root cause: Cayman Exempted Company status with a tax-exempt profile, non-resident directors, and no visible local economic substance is automatically classified offshore high-risk under GFSC enhanced due diligence.

Defense evidence: Submit the Certificate of Incorporation and Certificate of Good Standing; Memorandum and Articles of Association; Cayman Tax Information Authority tax reference and Economic Substance Notification or Return evidencing pure-equity-holding or out-of-scope status; Register of Directors and Beneficial Ownership Register with nominee declarations; audited financial statements or a notarised opening balance sheet with source-of-funds evidence; and a legal opinion from Gibraltar-licensed counsel confirming no Gibraltar/UK tax residence and no permanent establishment.
B2B marketplace payment flows with multi-jurisdiction counterparties, high-velocity settlement cycles, and aggregated vendor payouts resemble an unlicensed payment-intermediary typology.

Root cause: B2B marketplace payment flows with multi-jurisdiction counterparties, high-velocity settlement cycles, and aggregated vendor payouts resemble an unlicensed payment-intermediary typology.

Defense evidence: Provide a legal classification memo confirming ISIC 4791 / NAICS 425120 electronic wholesale marketplace status and the absence of a statutory banking licence requirement; executed merchant and vendor master agreements; platform terms of service; a payment-flow diagram evidencing full segregation of client funds from treasury funds; the platform's own AML/KYC policy for downstream merchants; the sanctions-screening framework; daily reconciliation and exception reports; and an independent AML systems-and-controls review.

Initial capital injection from UBO or related-party accounts is tainted by intermingled source of wealth involving other businesses, trusts, or digital-asset holdings.

Root cause: Initial capital injection from UBO or related-party accounts is tainted by intermingled source of wealth involving other businesses, trusts, or digital-asset holdings.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Initial equity and shareholder loan injections must be routed as SWIFT MT103 wires from the UBO's or holding company's account to the Cayman company's segregated Gibraltar account, using a clear remittance narrative such as 'Paid-up share capital subscription' or 'Shareholder loan' and the internal client reference; interbank cover should use MT202. GBP credits should clear via CHAPS or Faster Payments; EUR credits via SEPA or TARGET2 using the bank's BIC and the company's IBAN; USD credits via Fedwire or CHIPS through the Gibraltar bank's US correspondent, with OFAC screening applied. The receiving bank requires a pre-notification KYC referral one business day before the first inbound credit, together with the allotment board resolution, issued share certificates, updated register of members, and the complete source-of-funds file. Only remitter names that exactly match the registered beneficial owners are accepted; mismatched third-party remitters trigger automatic holding and further due diligence.Supplier and vendor settlements should clear via SWIFT MT103 with remittance narratives matching the underlying sales invoices and marketplace payout schedules. For confirmed documentary trade, issue irrevocable commercial letters of credit under UCP600 using SWIFT MT700/MT701, with MT707 for amendments and MT202 for reimbursement instructions to the negotiating bank. GBP payouts clear over CHAPS, EUR payouts over TARGET2 or SEPA, and USD payouts over CHIPS or Fedwire with full OFAC screening at the US correspondent. For China-related lanes, Alibaba Pay and Alipay international merchant settlement can serve as the front-end disbursement rail, paired with a CNH-denominated account at a Gibraltar or Hong Kong bank, converting RMB-derived proceeds to CNH and onward to GBP or USD via spot or covered forward.
