Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A pure virtual office is not acceptable for the Cayman Exempted Company itself because Cayman law requires a physical registered office in the Cayman Islands maintained by a licensed corporate services provider. For the Gibraltar external-company registration, the Companies Act requires a registered or principal office address in Gibraltar for service of process; a virtual address may be tolerated for correspondence but will not evidence commercial substance. For the Low_Risk_Standard unregulated project cargo logistics activity, a contracted physical registered office with shared meeting facilities is acceptable and is the prevailing market practice.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Her Majesty's Government of Gibraltar, Income Tax Office, 6-8 Parliament Lane, Gibraltar; official portal https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi; e-filing via https://etax.gibraltar.gov.gi

E-government filing portal

The Cayman Exempted Company is not subject to Cayman Islands income tax, but if it conducts a trade or business from a permanent establishment in Gibraltar, it must register with the Gibraltar Income Tax Office within 30 days of commencing activity. Procedure: file a foreign company branch registration with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies, obtain the Gibraltar company number, submit Form 1 (declaration of commencement of business) and the company's constitutive documents, and apply to the Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar TIN. The TIN is the corporate registration number. Non-resident companies not carrying on a trade in Gibraltar are not required to register.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election regime. A Cayman Exempted Company operating in Gibraltar is taxed at entity level on Gibraltar-sourced territorial income. It may, by formal notice to the Income Tax Office, elect treatment as a non-resident company where central management and control is maintained outside Gibraltar, and may claim group relief, loss relief and capital allowances pooling under the Income Tax Act 2010 where conditions are met.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST – Gibraltar imposes customs and excise duties on imports of controlled goods and does not operate a general indirect consumption tax

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable; no VAT/GST registration threshold exists. Any importer or dealer in excise goods must obtain a Gibraltar customs and excise registration regardless of turnover, together with an Economic Operator Registration and Identification (EORI) number for goods movements related to the United Kingdom customs territory.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar is outside the EU VAT area, so no 0% export VAT applies and there is no VAT refund mechanism to claim. For customs purposes, the export of goods, including out-of-gauge project cargo, may be relieved from Gibraltar customs duties where a valid export declaration is made and the goods are physically dispatched. For income tax purposes, export profits are assessable under Gibraltar's territorial rules; no exceptional zero-rating applies.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic SAD / UK Single Trade Window submission)
  • Bill of Lading (Port of Gibraltar issue in the name of the exporter)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (evidencing receipt of the export sale proceeds)

Gibraltar does not mandate real-time fiscal e-invoicing, API-based clearance or digital stamping. Commercial invoices are legally sufficient for tax and customs purposes. Goods declarations must be submitted electronically through HMG Gibraltar Customs using the Single Administrative Document / UK Single Trade Window interface where applicable; no continuous fiscal clearance controls are imposed.

Fraudulent claims of export relief or false customs declarations on OOG project cargo are criminal offences under Gibraltar customs law, attracting penalties up to ₤20,000, forfeiture of goods, and imprisonment for up to five years. Retrospective clawback of any duty relief granted is required upon discovery of misdeclaration, with additional civil penalty of up to 100% of the evaded duty and disallowance of export income deductions for Gibraltar income tax purposes.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar Pound (GIP) maintains a 1:1 fixed exchange rate with GBP and is fully convertible on both current and capital account. No exchange control permission is required for capital inflows, dividend remittances, management fees, or intercompany settlements originating from a non-resident Cayman Exempted Company. Profit repatriation is unrestricted, subject only to standard AML/KYC source-of-funds due diligence.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Gibraltar imposes no mandatory export-proceeds surrender timeline, conversion quota, or central bank approval for export receipts. Exporters may retain foreign currency balances in licensed Gibraltar banks or convert them freely. This applies equally to project cargo milestones invoiced to non-Gibraltar counterparties.

Central bank supervision: Government of Gibraltar (Financial Services Commission and Currency Commissioner) with monetary policy anchored to the Bank of England for sterling clearance.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-Medium (ancillary use only). Post-Brexit, UK and EEA electronic-money institution licences do not passport into Gibraltar; an EMI must hold a separate GFSC e-money licence. A Cayman Exempted Company without substantive local presence is unlikely to obtain local EMI authorisation. Offshore digital EMIs can be used as a secondary payment rail for petty expenses or FX intermediation, but cannot provide letters of credit, guarantees, or full treasury clearing for project cargo heavy-haul operations. Any transactional use should exclude high-risk counterparty corridors.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Cayman-registered domicile with zero minimum capital and non-resident ownership structure

Root cause: Cayman-registered domicile with zero minimum capital and non-resident ownership structure

Defense evidence: Certified Certificate of Incorporation, Certificate of Good Standing, register of directors and shareholders, updated beneficial ownership statement, Cayman tax-exemption certificate, and a legal opinion confirming no bearer shares and compliance with Cayman Economic Substance Rules.
Cross-border heavy-haul contracts potentially touching sanctioned/high-risk jurisdictions

Root cause: Cross-border heavy-haul contracts potentially touching sanctioned/high-risk jurisdictions

Defense evidence: Full underlying project and charter party contracts, export licence declarations, end-user certificates, bills of lading, vessel registry and voyage routing, counterparty name-screening reports from World-Check/Dow Jones, and a written compliance confirmation that cargo is non-military and free of dual-use restrictions.
Third-party milestone disbursements with no clear linkage to equity injection

Root cause: Third-party milestone disbursements with no clear linkage to equity injection

Defense evidence: Shareholder equity subscription or loan agreement, source-of-wealth statements including bank transaction history and sale-of-assets documents, group organisational chart with invoice-flow explanation, itemised milestone schedule against the project cargo P&L, supplier licence evidence, and custom import/export e-invoices.
High-frequency FX conversions and urgent transfers to overseas shipping counterparties under letters of credit

Root cause: High-frequency FX conversions and urgent transfers to overseas shipping counterparties under letters of credit

Defense evidence: SWIFT MT103 confirmations with unique transaction references, UCP600-compliant letter of credit and conforming documents, board resolution approving FX and hedging policy, evidence that the destination bank is a regulated licensed counterparty, and a consolidated compliance attestation identifying the purpose and economic substance of each transfer.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections from the Cayman parent must be remitted by SWIFT MT103 with explicit purpose code 'P11' (Foreign Direct Investment) or 'C' (Capital Contribution), depending on the receiving bank's reporting taxonomy. The wire should originate from the Cayman entity's corporate bank account, not from a personal account, and must reference the corresponding board resolution and capital account entry. For sterling settlement, use CHAPS or the Gibraltar Faster Payments System via a correspondent clearing bank; for USD/EUR flows, clear through London or Frankfurt correspondents. Any single inbound credit exceeding £10,000 may trigger an automatic source-of-funds request; having a pre-approved capital escrow account mitigates delay.Milestone payments to heavy-haul subcontractors and vessel operators must be transmitted via SWIFT MT103 with a detailed purpose description (e.g., 'CHARTER HIRE MILESTONE NO.4 - OOG PROJECT CARGO'); for capital-intensive equipment purchases, a commercial letter of credit under UCP600 issued by the Gibraltar bank and confirmed by a London prime bank is the preferred clearing rail. A standby letter of credit (SBLC) can secure performance bonds and demurrage obligations. Reimbursements under the LC may use MT400/MT202 messages; all outbound messages must preserve audit trail links to underlying invoices and compliance approvals.FX Forwards (plain vanilla, up to 12 months) for non-speculative hedging of project cargo bids in USD, EUR, and GBP; requires an active ISDA master agreement.Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDFs) on restricted emerging-market currencies, executed through a GFSC-licensed bank or its foreign exchange broker, limited to actual commercial exposure.Cross-currency swaps for long-term asset financing and fixed-price project cargo contracts, subject to board-approved treasury standing orders.European-style call and put currency options with licensed broker-dealers to hedge bid-to-fixed-price conversion risk, with no gearing permitted.
