Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, HM Government of Gibraltar, Income Tax Office (official portal: taxation.gi), jointly with HM Customs Gibraltar for indirect tax registration (customs.gov.gi)

E-government filing portal

A foreign Cayman exempted company not managed or controlled in Gibraltar and without a Gibraltar permanent establishment is not generally required to register for Gibraltar income tax. However, if a Gibraltar tax presence arises, the company must: (i) appoint a Gibraltar-resident tax agent or registered office; (ii) complete the prescribed application forms establishing identity, beneficial ownership, and constitutional documents; (iii) provide the Cayman certificate of incorporation, certificate of good standing, and board resolution authorising registration; and (iv) obtain the Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) from the Commissioner of Income Tax. No VAT registration is possible because Gibraltar does not operate a VAT regime. For customs purposes, the entity must register with HM Customs Gibraltar as an economic operator and obtain a Customs Trader Identifier before importing or exporting goods.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar Pound (GIP) is pegged at 1:1 to GBP and, through the United Kingdom's IMF Article VIII obligations, both GIP and GBP enjoy full current-account and capital-account convertibility without exchange-control restrictions. Profit remittance from Gibraltar to Cayman shareholders is unrestricted and subject to 0% Gibraltar withholding tax on dividends and interest, requiring only standard AML evidence of economically genuine substance and UBO disclosure to dispel shell-company characterisation. The Cayman Exempted Company remains exempt from Gibraltar corporation tax only if it is not managed and controlled in Gibraltar; any such management nexus would require a Stage 6 tax-residence mapping.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory export-proceeds surrender, conversion ratio, or repatriation timeline is imposed by the United Kingdom, Gibraltar, or the Cayman Islands. Export receipts may be retained in USD, EUR, or GBP in any licensed account indefinitely; the only statutory obligations are the retention of supporting commercial records for five years under Gibraltar AML/CFT legislation (Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 as amended).

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (central monetary authority of the sovereign parent United Kingdom); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the licensed banking and financial-market regulator of Gibraltar; Currency Commission of Gibraltar as the GIP note-issuing authority; no dedicated national exchange-control administration exists in Gibraltar.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% standard Gibraltar corporation tax on taxable profits; 6% prescribed rate applies only to utility companies; no separate branch profits tax; no capital gains tax.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Gibraltar domestic law imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by a Gibraltar company or branch to shareholders, whether resident or non-resident. A Gibraltar branch remitting profits to the Cayman Exempted Company head office is similarly subject to 0% branch remittance tax.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due no later than 30 June following the end of the Year of Assessment (e.g., FY ending 31 December 2024 return due 30 June 2025). Non-resident companies with no Gibraltar permanent establishment are not required to file unless they derive Gibraltar-source trading income; a non-resident with a Gibraltar PE files on the same 30 June rule.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Box N1 - Entity Identification Legal name, Gibraltar Company Number (CRN) or foreign registration number, registered office or Gibraltar branch address, and tax reference number. For the Cayman Exempted Company enter the Cayman Islands registration number and the Gibraltar branch address only if a permanent establishment exists; otherwise state 'Non-resident, no Gibraltar establishment'.
