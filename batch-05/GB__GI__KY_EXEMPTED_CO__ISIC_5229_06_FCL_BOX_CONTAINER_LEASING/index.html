Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar: Income Tax Office, H.M. Government of Gibraltar (https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation); Cayman Islands: Tax Information Authority (https://www.tia.gov.ky) and Registrar of Companies.

E-government filing portal

The Cayman Exempted Company is incorporated by the Cayman Islands Registrar of Companies, which issues a company registration number used as the Cayman TIN for CRS purposes (no separate tax registration is required because Cayman levies no income tax). If the company creates a place of business, branch or permanent establishment in Gibraltar, it must file an External Company registration with the Gibraltar Companies House, then apply to the Gibraltar Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar corporate tax reference number. No VAT ID is obtainable because Gibraltar does not operate a VAT system. Any import or export activity will additionally require registration with Gibraltar Customs.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

N/A under Cayman and Gibraltar domestic law. A Cayman exempted company may obtain a 30-year Tax Exemption Certificate under Cayman law. Gibraltar offers no check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election. For United States federal tax purposes, the entity may make a classification election on IRS Form 8832, if applicable and eligible.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None (Gibraltar has no VAT, GST or sales/use tax system; indirect taxation is limited to customs and excise duties on imports and certain goods)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable; no economic turnover threshold exists. Any importer or exporter of physical goods must register with Gibraltar Customs regardless of value or turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable. Gibraltar does not impose VAT, so there is no 0% export VAT rate or export VAT reclaim mechanism. The cross-border leasing of ISO containers is outside any Gibraltar VAT charge by design. For customs purposes, qualifying exports may be relieved from customs duty where an electronic export declaration and proof of physical departure are provided. Cayman levies no export tax or indirect tax on container leasing services.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Gibraltar Customs or UK Customs Declaration Service)
  • Bill of Lading or Sea Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / FX bank payment confirmation
  • Commercial Invoice and ISO Container Lease Agreement
  • Packing List
  • ISO container identification list with BIC/GS1 markings
  • Certificate of Origin (where a customs preference is claimed)

No e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API clearance, or digital stamping mandate exists in Gibraltar or Cayman. Customs export/import declarations are lodged electronically through Gibraltar Customs systems and, where applicable, the UK Customs Declaration Service.

Fraudulent or unsupported export declarations can trigger recovery of unpaid customs duty and penalties of up to three times the duty value in Gibraltar, together with criminal liability for directors in cases of evasion. In Cayman, abuse of exempted company status can lead to revocation of the tax exemption certificate, retrospective withdrawal of exempt status, company strike-off, and director liability. No VAT clawback applies because no VAT is chargeable.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility without exchange controls. Both current account items (container lease rentals, management fees, trade disbursements) and capital account items (equity injections, intercompany loans, dividends, profit remittances) are freely convertible between Gibraltar pounds, GBP, USD, EUR and other major currencies. Profit remittance from Gibraltar to the Cayman parent is permitted without central bank approval or withholding once the paying entity has audited distributable profits. Practical hurdles are limited to bank-level AML source-of-funds review, UBO register clearance and economic substance documentation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Gibraltar does not impose export proceeds surrender rules, statutory conversion timelines or mandatory conversion ratios. Revenues from cross-border container leasing and freight-related services may be retained in foreign currency indefinitely in Gibraltar or external accounts. No surrender declaration is required; banks may, however, request export invoices or EORI/VAT evidence where the trade is routed through UK customs for VAT or trade-reporting purposes.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (ultimate monetary authority for the Gibraltar Pound, which is pegged at par to GBP) and the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the designated prudential and conduct regulator for banks and financial services in Gibraltar. There is no separate Gibraltar central bank; the Government of Gibraltar issues currency notes and coins at par with GBP.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-moderate feasibility. Gibraltar is outside the EEA passporting framework, so UK/EEA e-money institutions and payment institutions cannot passport into Gibraltar without securing a local GFSC licence. A GFSC-licensed EMI can provide virtual account and cross-border payment routing, but settlement through CHAPS/TARGET2 and SWIFT MT103 coverage for high-value container leasing transactions remains operatorially safer through a licensed commercial bank. A Cayman exempted company should use a Tier-1 Gibraltar bank for capex and lease settlement flows, reserving EMIs only for ancillary low-value expense payments where GFSC authorisation is verified.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% (Gibraltar standard corporate income tax rate under the Income Tax Act 2010; banking companies chargeable at 18%; Pillar Two top-up to 15% applies to multinational groups with consolidated revenue exceeding EUR 750 million for accounting periods commencing on or after 1 January 2024)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid to non-resident shareholders, including distributions from a Cayman Exempted Company branch or Gibraltar-resident subsidiary. The removal of the former imputation system in 2010 means dividend distributions are free of Gibraltar corporate withholding obligations and exempt from further Gibraltar taxation in the hands of non-residents.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The completed Form C-IT must be filed within 12 months of the end of the accounting period; for a 31 December year-end the operative filing deadline is 31 December of the following year. Any assessed tax is payable within 30 days of the issue of the Notice of Assessment, and unpaid balances accrue statutory late-payment interest. An extension of up to 60 days may be granted by the Commissioner upon written application before the due date.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 of aggregate annual related-party transactions (including intercompany container lease rentals, financing, and management charges). Master File threshold: group consolidated revenue of EUR 750,000,000, or any multinational group subject to the OECD Pillar Two Global Anti-Base Erosion rules; such groups must also file the Gibraltar Country-by-Country Report gateway declaration. Transactions below these thresholds require a contemporaneous Benchmarking Memorandum on request by the Commissioner.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar Economic Substance Rules (ESR) apply fully to foreign-incorporated exempted companies carrying on 'relevant activities', including the leasing of moveable assets such as intermodal ISO containers, with effect from 1 January 2019. The doctrine requires that in relation to the Gibraltar-sourced container leasing business the company: (i) directs and manages the leasing activity, including board meetings held in Gibraltar at adequate intervals; (ii) employs full-time staff in Gibraltar proportionate to the container fleet size; (iii) incurs annual operating expenditure in Gibraltar consistent with workforce and activity; and (iv) performs core income-generating activity (vessel/container procurement decision-taking, lease negotiation, invoice management, credit control) in Gibraltar. Compliance is certified by filing the ESR Return together with Form C-IT. A Cayman Exempted Company that holds Cayman tax exemption but fails the Gibraltar ESR test is treated as a 'non-resident shell' and is exposed to enforcement action described below.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Deliberate Material Misstatement

Statutory sanction: Under the Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended): civil penalty of up to 200% of the tax evaded; a fine on conviction of up to GIP 100,000; imprisonment up to seven years; compulsory filing of corrective returns for up to six prior years; and publication on the Gibraltar Tax Defaulters' List with revocation of the company's tax registration.

Director personal liability: Directors and shadow directors are jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax where the evasion arises from their direction or neglect, including restitution of evaded tax with interest; criminal conviction triggers automatic disqualification under the Company Directors Disqualification Act (Gibraltar) for up to 15 years; a Magistrate's Court may impose a travel ban prohibiting departure from Gibraltar pending settlement, and assets of the container fleet may be subjected to restraint orders and confiscation under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015.
Shell Company / Deficient Economic Substance (ESR)

Statutory sanction: ESR failure attracts an initial penalty of GIP 10,000, escalating to GIP 100,000 per year of continuing non-compliance; the Commissioner may issue a direction requiring remedial action within 30 days, and failure leads to publication of the company's name as an ESR non-compliant entity, cancellation of tax residence, and strike-off from the Gibraltar Companies Register under the Companies (Economic Substance) Rules 2019; in the Cayman Islands a parallel exempted company assessment can result in automatic loss of the tax exemption certificate.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for ESR penalties where they failed to take reasonable steps, with each director jointly and severally liable for the GIP 100,000 cap; the Financial Services Commission may seek a disqualification order and a freezing injunction over personal assets; severe cases involving misrepresentation to the Companies Registrar expose directors to criminal prosecution for false statements and a travel ban pending the completion of the substance rectification plan.
AML / CTF Breach and Concealed Beneficial Ownership

Statutory sanction: Money laundering on conviction is punishable by a fine at large and/or imprisonment up to 14 years; regulatory penalties for AML failures reach GIP 1,000,000 per breach or 10% of annual turnover; suspicious transaction reports not filed within the prescribed statutory period attract separate fines; the court may issue a confiscation order over the container fleet, lease receivables, and any proceeds of crime traced to Gibraltar or UK banks.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and beneficial owners face personal criminal liability for AML consent failures, including imprisonment up to 14 years and travel bans issued by the Gibraltar courts; civil restitution orders compel personal recovery of laundered lease proceeds plus interest; directors of the Cayman Exempted Company are disqualified from acting in Gibraltar-licensed financial services roles and may be extradited to the United Kingdom under the UK-Gibraltar surrender arrangements where the predicate offence concerns tax evasion on UK-source container leases.
