Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a US-style check-the-box election; the Cayman exempted company remains an entity-level taxpayer. Available elections include: (i) group relief and loss-pooling election under the Income Tax Act; (ii) election to claim capital allowances on pre-trading expenditure; (iii) treaty-based election to invoke the UK-Gibraltar Double Taxation Agreement mutual agreement procedure for PE profit attribution and residence tie-break; (iv) the qualifying company regime is statutorily limited to Gibraltar-incorporated companies and is not available to this Cayman entity. Hybrid mismatch treatment follows Gibraltar anti-hybrid rules without any elective classification mechanism.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST or sales-and-use tax in Gibraltar. Indirect taxation is limited to Import Duty under the Customs and Excise Act and Excise Duty on alcohol, fuel and tobacco; port stevedoring services are outside the scope of every Gibraltar consumption tax.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A - Gibraltar levies no turnover-based indirect tax and therefore has no registration threshold. Import duty attaches per customs entry irrespective of turnover or entity form.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in the VAT sense because Gibraltar has no VAT. Export of goods from Gibraltar is outside the scope of any indirect tax, and import duty relief is available where goods leave Gibraltar under a bonded or transit regime within the statutory time windows. Stevedoring and terminal services rendered at the bulk port to international carriers are not subject to any Gibraltar indirect tax, so no refund mechanism is triggered.

  • Gibraltar Customs export declaration (SAD/XML entry submitted via GCMS)
  • Bill of Lading or Sea Waybill evidencing physical export from Gibraltar
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Original import entry and bonded warehouse release where duty relief on re-export is claimed
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT wire confirmation as evidence of payment

None. Gibraltar does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, SAF-T, digital stamping, or government API e-invoicing. Customs declarations are filed electronically through the Gibraltar customs declaration system (GCMS) and NCTS for transit movements; commercial invoices and port documents may be issued in any format but must be retained for at least six years for audit purposes.

The Commissioner may assess within six years with no time limit in cases of fraud or negligence, revoke export duty relief retrospectively, and impose surcharges and penalties equalling or exceeding the tax undercharged; false customs declarations attract criminal fines, imprisonment, forfeiture of goods and AML reporting obligations under the Customs and Excise Act.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar Pound (GIP) is pegged at a fixed 1:1 parity with GBP sterling, and GBP notes and coinage are additionally legal tender within Gibraltar. There is no Gibraltar exchange control legislation, no requirement for central bank approval on foreign currency conversion, and no restriction on moving funds across the current account or capital account. Stevedoring fees, port tariff income, tug and mooring charges, and ancillary terminal revenues may be invoiced in GBP, EUR or USD and converted freely. Capital account transactions — including equity injections from the Cayman parent, intercompany loans, repayment of shareholder advances, and dividend distributions — are likewise unrestricted. Profit remittance to the Cayman exempted company, UK, EU, or US counterparties faces no withholding tax at the Gibraltar source; the only practical hurdles are CRS/FATCA reporting triggers, economic substance documentation, and normal bank-level source-of-funds verification.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation threshold applies in Gibraltar. Receipts from bulk port stevedoring and related marine services are classified as service exports and may be retained offshore in any freely convertible currency, or converted into GIP/GBP, EUR, or USD as treasury policy dictates. The bank will only require supporting trade documentation — stevedoring service contracts, port authority tariff sheets, bills of lading or cargo manifests, and invoices — to evidence the commercial nature and legitimate purpose of the funds.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority for sterling and for the Gibraltar Pound reserve backing at par); Government of Gibraltar (sole issuer of Gibraltar Pound banknotes and coinage); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) (prudential supervisor for all licensed banks, electronic money institutions, and portfolio services firms operating in Gibraltar).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium feasibility with meaningful constraints. Gibraltar-licensed electronic money institutions, operating under the Financial Services (Electronic Money) Act and GFSC e-money regulations, and select UK FCA-authorised EMIs can support pre-funded disbursements such as crew payroll, port tariff payments, small vessel-agent settlements, and operational expense flows. However, mainstream digital EMIs routinely decline the Cayman Exempted Company structure because of enhanced due diligence thresholds; acceptance is conditional on the entity being registered as a foreign/overseas company in Gibraltar, on production of an active port concession or terminal lease, on submission of audited financial statements, and on clean beneficial ownership disclosure. EMIs are not acceptable counterparties for the Capital Escrow Account, for trade finance instruments (Commercial LC or bank guarantees), or as clearing banks for high-value SWIFT MT103 settlement; those channels must remain with Gibraltar Tier 1 banks.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity is routed by SWIFT MT103 from the Cayman parent bank (or an issuing EU/UK/Asian correspondent) to the Gibraltar Tier 1 receiving bank. Field 70 narrative must state the ISO 20022 purpose category for a capital contribution to an affiliate, supported by a fully descriptive narrative referencing the board resolution number, share subscription date, and a pre-agreed treasury unique identifier. The receiving bank requires pre-advice for any credit exceeding GBP 25,000 equivalent, including source-of-funds evidence, the executed subscription agreement, an updated share register, and the conversion ticket if the funds are exchanged into GBP/EUR. Verification is completed only when the wire reference, invoice/contract reference, and treasury identifier reconcile; the funds are then housed in the Capital Escrow Account pending drawdown under board-approved project milestones.Trade settlements for port equipment, bunkers, vessel agents, and sub-contractors clear via SWIFT MT103 standard payments; documentary collections are handled under URC 522, and confirmed Commercial Letters of Credit are issued via MT700 with reimbursement under MT740/MT750. Domestic GBP clearing uses CHAPS for same-day high-value payments and Faster Payments for low-value port tariffs, utilities, and crew payroll. Port Authority clearing rails are used for harbour dues, berth levies, and stevedoring concession fees through the Gibraltar Port Authority tariff settlement system, with each settlement mirrored in the bank treasury statement. For EUR-corridor flows, SEPA-CT is acceptable below EUR 100,000; above that threshold, SWIFT MT103 with pre-hedged conversion is mandatory to control float and correspondent charges.Outright FX Forwards (GBP/EUR and GBP/USD) up to 12 months tenor with GFSC-licensed or UK counterpartiesNon-Deliverable Forwards (NDFs) for restricted or less-liquid corridor currencies where physical delivery is impracticalCross-Currency Swaps (GBP/EUR) aligned with port concession debt or terminal equipment lease liabilitiesVanilla FX Options (call/put) for hedging quote/tender exposure and tariff-indexed USD/EUR revenue volatility

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

Gibraltar corporate tax: 12.5% on taxable profits attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment; Cayman tax: 0% for the exempted company itself on non-Cayman-source income

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. Gibraltar imposes no statutory withholding tax on outbound dividends; Cayman similarly imposes no dividend withholding tax on distributions by an exempted company.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The return is due on 30 November immediately following the fiscal year-end. For a 31 December 2025 accounting period, the filing deadline is 30 November 2026. Extensions are not discretionary beyond this date unless the Commissioner of Income Tax grants written consent; non-resident entities with a Gibraltar permanent establishment must also file within one month of receiving a notice to file.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: A Local File is mandatory in Gibraltar where taxable turnover attributable to the PE exceeds GBP 10 million in an accounting period or where affected related-party transactions exceed GBP 1 million. A Master File is required for multinational groups with consolidated revenue above GBP 20 million. A Country-by-Country Report is required where group revenue exceeds GBP 100 million and the Gibraltar group member is in the consolidated reporting perimeter.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Income Tax (Economic Substance) Rules require any Gibraltar-resident company or permanent establishment carrying on a 'relevant activity' to demonstrate adequate premises, employees, local board direction, and core income-generating activities in Gibraltar. Bulk port stevedoring is an industrial operational activity and is not itself classified as a geographically mobile relevant activity; nevertheless, if stevedoring contracts, procurement, billing, and risk functions are attributed to the Gibraltar PE, the operation must evidence substance through staff levels, dock leases, terminal equipment, local management attendance, and board minutes; otherwise the Cayman parent may be challenged as a shell and the PE treated as a sham for tax and licensing purposes.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act, the Commissioner may impose civil penalties of up to 300% of the tax evaded, together with compounded interest at 8% per month. On false statements, criminal liability may be pursued under the Criminal Justice Act; conviction carries an unlimited fine and imprisonment up to 10 years.

Director personal liability: A director who approves false branch accounts or transfers pricing arrangements can be assessed jointly and severally for the unpaid tax and penalties after the corporate veil is pierced. Courts may order civil restitution, impose a travel ban, and disqualify the director from holding office for up to 15 years under the Companies Act.
Shell Company / Artificial Substance Arrangement

Statutory sanction: The Income Tax Commissioner may, after an economic substance assessment, issue a financial penalty of GBP 50,000 per period and a remedial direction; continued non-compliance may lead to a further penalty of up to GBP 100,000. The Gibraltar Registrar may strike off the branch, and the Cayman Companies Registry can be notified for sanction against the parent.

Director personal liability: Directors and the Gibraltar resident manager are jointly and severally liable if they knowingly executed the artificial arrangement. Sanctions include a five-year travel ban, disqualification from acting as a director in Gibraltar and Cayman, and personal seizure of dock assets where the structure is used to evade port authority licensing fees or public dues.
AML / Money Laundering in Cargo Settlements

Statutory sanction: The Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act and AML/CFT Regulations empower the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission to impose regulatory penalties up to GBP 1 million per breach, suspend or revoke the terminal operator’s port licence, and refer the matter for criminal prosecution; a willful blindness conviction for facilitating money laundering carries imprisonment up to 14 years.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for failing to appoint a Money Laundering Reporting Officer, omitting regular risk assessments, or consenting to the placement of tainted cargo proceeds. Courts may issue asset freezing orders, travel embargoes, seizure of the shore assets and cargo handling plant, and impose custodial sentences of up to 7 years on directors personally implicated.
