Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar; Commissioner of Income Tax. Her Majesty's Customs Gibraltar for import/export, customs duty and trader registration. Official portal: www.gibraltar.gov.gi (Income Tax and Customs sections); customs declarations are lodged electronically via the Gibraltar Customs Single Window / electronic SAD entry system.

E-government filing portal

A Cayman Exempted Company is a foreign corporation and is not automatically registered in Gibraltar. Step 1: residence screening — central management and control must be exercised outside Gibraltar (board minutes and decision-making outside the territory); Cayman incorporation alone does not confer Gibraltar residence. Step 2: file the non-resident registration application with the Commissioner of Income Tax, attaching the Cayman Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles of Association, registered office certificate, certificate of incumbency, current government annual fee status certificate, and evidence of offshore management and control. Step 3: the Commissioner issues a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN), and on request a Certificate of Non-Residence usable for bank onboarding. Step 4: where the company undertakes any importing or re-exporting activity through Gibraltar, it must additionally obtain a customs trader registration reference with Her Majesty's Customs Gibraltar. Registration is mandatory if a permanent establishment, office, employees or Gibraltar-source trading activity is present; the filing deadline is six months from commencement of chargeable activity.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope — non-resident Cayman Exempted Company chargeable to Gibraltar corporation tax only on profits of a trade carried on in Gibraltar or on income sourced in Gibraltar; offshore wholesale remarketing profits on used excavators sold and delivered to non-Gibraltar buyers are outside the Gibraltar charge. In the Cayman Islands the same entity holds Tax_Exempt_Zone status, being statutorily exempt from all local income and capital taxes.

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no US-style check-the-box entity classification; classification is determined by the residence test established on facts. Available written mechanisms: (i) application for a Certificate of Non-Residence evidencing offshore management and control; (ii) claim on the corporation tax return for exclusion of non-Gibraltar-source profits; (iii) election for group relief and loss surrender between Gibraltar-resident group companies under the Corporation Tax Act 2019; (iv) election for capital allowances on qualifying plant and machinery held by a Gibraltar business; (v) election to claim double taxation relief (foreign tax credit or treaty relief) if any Gibraltar-source profits also suffer foreign tax.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST regime in force; Gibraltar is excluded from the EU VAT area. The operative indirect taxes are Gibraltar import duty, excise duties on fuel, tobacco and alcoholic beverages, and customs transit/re-export formalities under the Customs and Excise Duties Ordinance. Used construction machinery is not dutiable when exported or re-exported, and no output VAT is chargeable on the resale.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — there is no VAT account and therefore no turnover threshold for VAT registration. However, any trader importing or re-exporting used excavators through Gibraltar must obtain a Gibraltar Customs trader registration (import/export licence reference) from Her Majesty's Customs Gibraltar; there is no turnover de minimis exemption for customs registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Formally not applicable because Gibraltar imposes no VAT: the trader neither charges output VAT nor claims input VAT credits, and the sale price is agreed exclusive of any indirect tax. Functionally, the export substantiation regime preserves two benefits: (i) under territorial classification, the profit on used construction excavators resold to non-Gibraltar buyers is excluded from Gibraltar corporation tax where title, possession, invoicing and payment occur outside Gibraltar or the goods are dispatched from Gibraltar to an overseas buyer; and (ii) no import duty is exigible on qualifying transit or re-export. Eligibility requires the machinery to be removed physically from Gibraltar within the prescribed period and the transaction to be documented as a true export.

  • Gibraltar Customs electronic export/re-export declaration (Single Administrative Document or equivalent SAD entry)
  • Signed commercial invoice and packing list identifying the excavator by serial number and value
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical dispatch and exit from Gibraltar
  • Cayman Exempted Company constitutional and incumbency pack (certificate of incorporation, registered office certificate, current status certificate)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate, SWIFT MT103, or bank credit advice evidencing payment receipt from the overseas buyer

None in force. Gibraltar does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, SAF-T data transmission, digital stamping or government invoice pre-approval. Commercial invoices and packing lists must be retained, and customs entries must be submitted electronically via the Gibraltar Customs single administrative document (SAD) entry system; customs and income tax records remain subject to post-clearance and post-assessment audits without fiscal pre-clearance.

Her Majesty's Customs Gibraltar conducts post-clearance audits and may verify the physical exit of the excavators; false or fictitious export declarations expose the trader to seizure and forfeiture of the goods, penalty of up to 300% of the duty value, and revocation of the customs trader licence. For income tax, fabricated export documentation enables the Commissioner to deny the territorial-source exclusion and assess notional Gibraltar-source profits, with discovery assessments permitted for up to six years and penalties of up to 100% of the tax under-assessed, plus criminal prosecution for wilful evasion. Fabricated invoices also constitute a money-laundering predicate under Gibraltar AML law for the reporting bank.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar applies no exchange-control legislation after the 1979 abolition of the UK Exchange Control Act, which extends to Gibraltar under the Sterling Area framework. Both capital and current account items are deemed fully convertible; GIP banknotes are interchangeable with GBP at par, and USD/EUR/CHF receipts may be freely held, sold, or hedged. Profit-remittance hurdles are practical, not statutory: a Gibraltar receiving bank will require the Cayman Exempted Company to evidence economic substance, file a Cayman Economic Substance Notification, and prove UBO source of wealth before permitting recurring dividend, loan-interest, or trade-fee remittances to the Cayman shareholder. No prior Central Bank approval or convertibility cap applies.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender timeline or forced-conversion ratio applies to export proceeds in Gibraltar, the UK, or the Cayman Islands. Export receipts from used excavator sales (HS 8429, invoiced via the Gibraltar trading entity) may be retained in the original currency, converted at market rate, or rolled into GBP deposits. The only obligations are statistical: banks complete Gibraltar balance-of-payments returns, customs declarations accompany each shipment, and the Cayman entity must preserve its Economic Substance Notification and audited records proving genuine trading operations if challenged by either the Cayman Registrar or GFSC.

Central bank supervision: Government of Gibraltar Ministry of Finance, acting jointly with the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC); the Bank of England is the monetary anchor as the Gibraltar Pound (GIP) circulates at a fixed 1:1 parity with GBP.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium feasibility with significant legal constraints. Gibraltar does not permit remote passporting of non-Gibraltar EMIs post-Brexit; a foreign electronic-money firm must hold a Gibraltar licence (or be a Gibraltar branch of a UK-authorised EMI) to service this structure. UK FCA-authorised EMIs (Airwallex, Currencycloud, ANNA Money, ClearBank) generally reject a Cayman Exempted Company unless full substance and UBO verification are provided, because offshore-incorporated commodity traders are a standard de-risking category. Viable route: use a tier-1 Gibraltar bank for receipt of capital and LC flows, and a UK FCA EMI only for small operational EUR/GBP payouts after the bank has performed the KYC onboarding. The 2024 GFSC guidance on remote digital onboarding makes a remote EMI relationship technically possible but the bank-level compliance floor remains the deciding factor.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound equity and shareholder loans are wired by SWIFT MT103 (with MT202 COV when the ultimate correspondent must be advised in USD). The wire must show the Cayman-company name, prepayment purpose 'CAPITAL INJECTION – EQUITY CAPITAL SUBSCRIPTION', and the Gibraltar bank's BIC. The Gibraltar bank codes the credit as direct-investment equity capital for balance-of-payments recording. Pre-delivery the bank requires a board resolution from the Cayman exempted company authorising the capital increase, an updated share register, a current certificate of good standing, and a certified source-of-funds pack (shareholder bank statement and source-of-wealth declaration). If the corporate is tax-exempt, the bank will also ask for evidence that the funds are not proceeds of any prohibited activity and that the Cayman Economic Substance Notification is aligned. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File and Master File mandatory where the multinational group's annual consolidated turnover exceeds GIP 750 million and Gibraltar-resident connected transactions exceed GIP 1 million; simplified documentation required for any related-party cross-border transaction above GIP 100,000.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to entities carrying on 'Distribution and Service Centre' activities, which covers used construction excavator wholesale trading. The Cayman Exempted Company (if tax-resident in Gibraltar) must demonstrate (1) full-time qualified employment in Gibraltar, (2) physical registered office and operational premises, and (3) strategic direction and control in Gibraltar. Annual ESR notifications must declare the relevant activity and quantum of income; failure triggers fines up to GIP 50,000 and potential reassessment of all Gibraltar-source revenue.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty up to 100% of tax evaded; criminal prosecution under Gibraltar's Income Tax Act with fines up to GIP 10,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years; forfeiture of proceeds under the Proceeds of Crime Act.

Director personal liability: Directors and shadow directors who knowingly participate face personal restitution orders, asset seizures, travel bans, and disqualification from board membership for up to 15 years.
Shell Company and Artificial Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: Reallocation of profits to Gibraltar under OECD ALP/transfer pricing adjustments; penalty of 50% of any adjusted net profit difference; additional tax-geared fines and cancellation of any no-tax treatment.

Director personal liability: Directors of the Gibraltar branch or local management company may be jointly and severally liable for the reassessed tax where they knowingly engineered the artificial structure; probation orders and prohibitions against holding public company directorship.
AML/CFT Breach

Statutory sanction: Fines up to GIP 5 million under the Immigration and Asylum Act (AML provisions); suspension or revocation of any requisite financial licenses; criminal imprisonment up to 14 years for money laundering; confiscation orders equivalent to the value of tainted property.

Director personal liability: Compliance officers and directors face civil liability for breaches of AML regulations, including personal fines and travel bans; courts may impose restitution from personal assets where they actively facilitated transactions for high-risk jurisdictions.
