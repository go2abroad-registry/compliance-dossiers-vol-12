A pure virtual office is not permissible in the Cayman Islands for a company engaged in the physical distribution of goods, including alcoholic beverages. The Cayman Islands registered office must be a physical address in the Islands maintained by a licensed corporate services provider, satisfying the Registered Office requirement under section 54 of the Companies Law (2021 Revision). For this ISIC 4632 spirits export enterprise, the applicant should maintain a physical registered office in the Cayman Islands and a substantive commercial hub in Gibraltar with a local principal office, local agent, and adequate staff footprint to evidence core income-generating activities.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar - Income Tax Office (ITO), within the Ministry of Finance; customs and fiscal duties: Gibraltar Customs and Excise Department. Portals: www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation and www.gibraltar.gov.gi/customs-and-border-control

E-government filing portal

For a Cayman Exempted Company: (1) Obtain the Cayman Islands tax status letter (Cayman TIN from the General Registry, plus relevant Tax Exemption Certificate if grandfathered pre-2019). (2) Determine Gibraltar tax residence under the Income Tax Act 2010: a company is resident if incorporated in Gibraltar or if its Central Management and Control (CMC) is exercised in Gibraltar. (3) If CMC is in Gibraltar, file the ITO registration application (Form CIT-1), appoint a Gibraltar resident tax representative, and obtain the Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) within 30 days of commencing activity. (4) If the entity will import, warehouse, or re-export alcohol, register with Gibraltar Customs and Excise as a registered consignee/warehouse keeper and obtain the relevant alcoholic beverages wholesale licence under the Intoxicating Substances and Alcoholic Beverages Act. (5) If CMC remains outside Gibraltar and no permanent establishment / Gibraltar-source income exists, no Gibraltar tax registration is required; maintain economic substance documentation in Cayman to support the exempt status.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (Cayman exempted company: statutorily exempt from all Cayman income, corporate and capital taxes; pre-2019 entities may hold a 20-year Tax Exemption Certificate). In Gibraltar the same entity is classified under Territorial_Scope: non-resident company taxable only on Gibraltar-source income and foreign-source income remitted to Gibraltar, at the standard 12.5% corporation tax rate, unless it becomes Gibraltar-resident through CMC in Gibraltar, which would trigger Entity_Level_Taxation on a territorial basis.

Available election forms

No check-the-box regime exists in Gibraltar (US concept). Available elections and instruments: (1) Cayman Tax Exemption Certificate under the former Tax Exemption Law for grandfathered entities; statutory 0% income tax for all new exempted companies; (2) election to maintain non-resident status in Gibraltar by keeping CMC, strategic decision-making, and board records outside Gibraltar; (3) if Gibraltar-resident, claim foreign tax credit relief under the Income Tax Act for taxes paid in Cayman/destination jurisdictions; (4) hybrid mismatch election/notification where the entity is treated as tax-transparent in another jurisdiction; (5) election under the Import Duties Act 2019 to place goods in an approved bonded/duty-suspension warehouse for subsequent export.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT, GST, or sales and use tax exists in Gibraltar. The only relevant indirect tax for baijiu is Gibraltar Import Duty (Import Duties Act 2019), an excise-type duty charged on alcoholic beverages entering Gibraltar. Baijiu (typically 38-53% ABV) is classified as 'spirits' and charged per litre of pure alcohol at rates set annually in the Import Duties Regulations, broadly aligned with UK spirits duty (approximately GBP 28-32 per litre of pure alcohol).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VAT: not applicable; no VAT registration threshold exists because no VAT regime is in force. Import Duty: no de minimis threshold - duty arises on the first litre of alcohol imported into Gibraltar unless the goods are entered for transit/transshipment or export via a bonded warehouse. Operators must register with Gibraltar Customs and Excise, obtain a liquor wholesale licence (Intoxicating Substances and Alcoholic Beverages Act), and, for duty suspension, be approved as a bonded warehouse keeper with a customs guarantee.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - Gibraltar has no VAT to zero-rate. Under the Import Duties Act 2019, duty is not chargeable on Chinese baijiu that is (a) shipped directly from China to a destination outside Gibraltar without entering Gibraltar, or (b) entered into an approved bonded warehouse and thereafter exported from Gibraltar under customs control. The duty-free claim requires the exporter to be an approved warehouse keeper/registered customs agent, to lodge a prior electronic export declaration via GEDIS, and to prove physical exit of the goods from Gibraltar within the authorised period. Direct-shipment sales never touching Gibraltar attract no Gibraltar import duty and generate no Gibraltar-source profit.

  • Completed Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration (SAD Goods Declaration for Export) submitted via GEDIS
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List clearly describing the product as Chinese Baijiu (sorghum spirit) with declared ABV strength
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing shipment from origin to destination market
  • Bonded Warehouse Removal / Duty Suspension Authorisation (warehouse removal document approved by HM Customs Gibraltar)
  • Certificate of Origin (where preferential origin is claimed) and Export Health Certificate / Certificate of Analysis for human consumption
  • Intoxicating Substances and Alcoholic Beverages Licence endorsement for the exporting entity
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT credit advice evidencing receipt of export proceeds within 180 days

Gibraltar does not impose a B2B e-invoicing mandate, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping for invoices. Customs clearance is electronic: declarations must be submitted through the Gibraltar Electronic Data Interchange System (GEDIS) using the Single Administrative Document (SAD) format. Alcohol movements in duty suspension require an electronic administrative document equivalent to the EU EMCS, and all shipping/export documents must be retained for at least six years.

HM Customs Gibraltar and the Income Tax Office jointly audit duty-free export claims and bonded warehouse movements. Misdeclaration, diversion of goods into local consumption, or failure to substantiate physical export triggers retrospective duty clawback at the full statutory rate plus interest, civil penalties up to 100% of the duty amount, criminal liability under the Import Duties Act 2019 (fines up to GBP 10,000 and/or imprisonment up to 7 years), seizure of goods and conveyances, withdrawal of the warehouse licence and AEO status, and, where false invoices or diverted high-proof baijiu are involved, investigation and confiscation under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current and capital account convertibility. Gibraltar is an IMF Article VIII jurisdiction with no exchange controls; the GIP is pegged at par to GBP and circulates alongside GBP banknotes. Profit remittance and capital repatriation are unrestricted, subject only to standard CRS, FATCA, and anti-money laundering reporting. The Cayman Exempted Co structure triggers no FX approval requirement.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender, conversion, or repatriation timeline applies in Gibraltar, the UK, or the Cayman Islands. Export receipts in USD, EUR, CNH, or other permitted currencies may be held offshore, freely converted, or applied to trade settlements without statutory deadlines.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (BoE) as monetary authority for the British pound sterling (GBP) and the Gibraltar pound (GIP) issued by the Government of Gibraltar; banking and foreign-exchange supervision delegated to the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium feasibility. Gibraltar does not automatically accept all UK-licensed e-money institutions (EMIs) without a separate Gibraltar authorisation or a licensed Gibraltar agent. A Cayman Exempted Co will trigger enhanced due diligence at most digital EMIs, including Airwallex and Currencycloud. Practical viability requires a Gibraltar physical nexus, a regulated corporate services provider attestation, and a documented economic substance file. EMIs are suitable for disbursement sub-accounts only, never as the sole treasury rail.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% (Gibraltar standard corporate income tax rate under the Income Tax Act 2010, applicable to any company that is resident in Gibraltar or that carries on a trade in Gibraltar through a permanent establishment).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid to non-residents; the Cayman Islands likewise impose no dividend withholding tax. Dividends distributed from the Cayman exempted company to its Gibraltar or UK shareholders are paid gross with no source WHT.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory where: (i) aggregate related-party transactions exceed GIP 1,000,000 in the accounting period; or (ii) related-party transactions exceed 10% of gross revenue and gross revenue exceeds GIP 7,500,000. Master File is mandatory where the group consolidated revenue exceeds EUR 750,000,000. Country-by-Country Report is required where the group's consolidated revenue exceeds EUR 750,000,000, filed with the GRA within 12 months of the group year-end, with notification filed on the GRA portal by the reporting deadline.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations apply on two limbs. (1) Gibraltar ESR (2019): a distribution and service centre activity must be directed and managed in Gibraltar, core income-generating activities (CIGA) must be performed in Gibraltar, and the entity must maintain proportionate physical premises, full-time employees, and annual operating expenditure. (2) Cayman Islands Economic Substance Law (2019): as a Cayman Exempted Company, the entity must file an annual economic substance notification and return on the DITC/TARA portal; a distribution and service centre activity is excused only if it is a pure equity holding entity, otherwise CIGA must be performed in the Cayman Islands. Hybrid booking (Cayman legal seat combined with Gibraltar management) without demonstrable substance in either territory is an instant red flag and triggers cross-border information exchange under the UK-Gibraltar arrangement and the Cayman-Gibraltar MoU.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion via Central Management and Control Mismatch

Statutory sanction: Assessment on worldwide income at the 12.5% Gibraltar rate; penalty of up to 200% of the tax evaded; interest at 2% per month; criminal convictions with fines up to GIP 250,000 and imprisonment up to 7 years under the Income Tax Act 2010, s.116; additional excise duty assessments and seizure of the alcoholic stock

Director personal liability: Director's signature on the GIL1 gives rise to personal exposure; the Commissioner may issue director liability notices and collect unpaid tax directly from personal assets where corporate assets were stripped; travel bans and asset freezes under the Tax Collection (Enforcement) Act remain in force until full settlement.
Shell Company / Impaired Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar ESR penalty of up to GBP 100,000 plus GBP 10,000 per month for continued default and potential compulsory strike-off; Cayman Economic Substance Law penalties of CI$10,000 escalating to CI$100,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years for knowingly filing a false substance declaration; the tax authority may reclassify the entity as Gibraltarian tax resident for all open years
