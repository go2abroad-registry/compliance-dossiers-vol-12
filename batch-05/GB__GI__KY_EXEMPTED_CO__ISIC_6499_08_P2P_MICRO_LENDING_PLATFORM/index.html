E-government filing portal

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; Territorial_Scope. The foreign-incorporated Cayman Exempted Company is Gibraltar tax resident only if central management and control is exercised in Gibraltar; resident companies pay corporation tax at 12.5% on profits arising in or derived from Gibraltar. In Cayman the entity retains Tax_Exempt_Zone status with no income, capital gains or withholding taxes and an exemption certificate running to a fixed date. If central management and control is not exercised in Gibraltar, the company is treated as non-resident but is still taxable on Gibraltar-source income attributable to any Gibraltar permanent establishment.

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no check-the-box or US-style entity classification election. Available statutory structuring and elections: (i) residence governance — board meetings and strategic decision-making may be structured to avoid central management and control in Gibraltar, subject to GFSC substance obligations and economic substance rules for financing/relevant activities; (ii) Notional Interest Deduction (NID) — an OECD-aligned allowance on increases in qualifying new equity, reducing the effective cost of capital; (iii) group relief — surrender of trading losses between Gibraltar-resident group companies; (iv) Substantial Shareholdings Exemption (SSE) on disposal of qualifying participating shareholdings; (v) advance agreements and clearance opinions from the Commissioner of Income Tax for transfer pricing and financing structures; (vi) UK-Gibraltar double taxation agreement (signed 2019, in force 2020) residence tie-breaker for dual-resident companies; (vii) no hybrid mismatch election is available — Gibraltar does not apply ATAD anti-hybrid rules, and domestic law treats the Cayman Exempted Company as opaque, aligning with BEPS Action 2.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar has no VAT, no GST and no Sales and Use Tax. The territory is outside the EU VAT area and does not implement the VAT Directive. The only indirect levies are Import Duty and Excise Duty on physical goods at importation under the Customs Ordinance; most industrial goods are duty-free while alcohol, tobacco, fuels and motor vehicles attract specific duties. Lending interest, algorithmic underwriting fees, platform matching fees and collection charges of the P2P micro-lending business are entirely outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect taxation.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — Gibraltar has no VAT/GST system and therefore no statutory economic-turnover threshold triggering indirect-tax registration. Import duty is assessed per shipment at the border; there is no services turnover threshold for lending activities. For context, corporation tax in Gibraltar applies from the first pound of Gibraltar-source profit with no de minimis threshold, and a GFSC licence is required for regulated lending regardless of revenue level.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable — in the absence of a Gibraltar VAT/GST, there is no zero-rating mechanism, no export VAT claim and no input-VAT refund process. Cross-border supplies of financial services (loan origination, algorithmic underwriting, P2P matching, collection servicing) are outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect tax irrespective of borrower residence. Physical goods exported from Gibraltar attract no export duty and require no zero-rating declaration.

Audit and penalty exposure: (i) customs — false declarations or evasion attract forfeiture of goods, monetary penalties up to three times the value of the goods, and imprisonment on conviction; (ii) income tax — the Commissioner of Income Tax may raise assessments for up to six years, with no time limit in cases of fraud or wilful default, plus penalties up to 100% of the tax undercharged and statutory interest; (iii) economic substance — a Gibraltar-resident financing or lending company must demonstrate adequate employees, premises and board activity in Gibraltar under the territorial substance rules; failure triggers civil penalties, corrected filings, and cross-border information exchange with HMRC and Cayman authorities; (iv) residence recharacterisation — if the Revenue establishes that the Cayman Exempted Company is de facto managed and controlled in Gibraltar, back-year assessments on the full Gibraltar-source lending margin, penalties and interest will apply. No VAT-related clawback can arise because Gibraltar has no VAT system.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Fully liberalised capital and current account regime. The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is pegged at 1:1 to the British pound and is freely convertible; the UK Exchange Control Act 1979 has been repealed, leaving no exchange control restrictions on inward or outward remittances, profit repatriation, or dividend streaming. The KYD leg is fixed at 1 KYD = 1.20 USD and does not impede USD funding. The only practical hurdles are documentary: economic substance filing confirmation in Gibraltar, CRS/FATCA reporting, and beneficial-ownership disclosure. No quota, prior-approval, or surrender requirements apply to lending platform flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable to this financial services treasury. Neither the UK nor Gibraltar imposes statutory export proceeds surrender timelines, mandatory conversion ratios, or repatriation deadlines. Loan principal recoveries, interest income, and platform fees are current account receipts under Bank of England statistical reporting and are freely retainable in GIP, GBP, or USD. Treasury conversions are elective at prevailing market rates.

Central bank supervision: HM Treasury and the Bank of England as the ultimate UK monetary authority for Gibraltar, with the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the territorial licensing and AML/CFT supervisor of all Gibraltar-licensed banks and e-money institutions; the Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA) retains registrar and AML/CFT oversight over the KY Exempted Company through its licensed corporate service providers.

EMIs apply monthly omnibus throughput caps, wallet balance ceilings, and mandatory enhanced due diligence windows; most will refuse direct onboarding of the Cayman entity and will require the Gibraltar or UK licensed operating company to be the named account holder.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 (approximately EUR 1,100,000) aggregate annual related-party cross-border transactions triggers mandatory Local File preparation; Master File required where the consolidated group turnover exceeds GIP 50 million; all transfer pricing documentation must be submitted within 30 days of the Tax Office's request

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations 2019 require a Cayman Exempted Company, if tax-resident in Gibraltar, to maintain its core income-generating activities in Gibraltar for the relevant 'relevant activity'. A P2P micro-lending platform is a 'financing and leasing' relevant activity, requiring full-time employees, physical premises, and active decision-making in Gibraltar. The Cayman exemption does not apply to Gibraltar-resident operations. Failure to satisfy economic substance results in a GIP 10,000 fine (increasing on continued default), an annual return notification, and potential information exchange with the Cayman Islands; the tax authority may disallow deductions and re-characterise the business as pure holding, which exposes the group to UK CFC rules if the entity is controlled from the UK.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Incorrect Returns

Statutory sanction: Unlimited fine before the Gibraltar Supreme Court on conviction by information; on summary conviction, a fine not exceeding GIP 10,000; the tax authority may raise best-of-knowledge assessments, impose a penalty of up to 200% of underpaid tax, and charge interest from the date the tax ought to have been paid

Director personal liability: Directors knowingly concerned in tax evasion face personal criminal liability, imprisonment for up to 5 years, unlimited fines, and disqualification as a company director for up to 5 years; the Tax Office may also issue travel bans and seizure orders in respect of personal assets
Economic Substance Failure (Shell Company Abuse)

Statutory sanction: Regulation by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission may impose fines up to GIP 1,000,000 plus restitution orders; operating an unauthorised lending business constitutes a criminal offence

Director personal liability: Individual directors may be subject to disqualification orders of up to 10 years and personal restitution for funds advanced through the platform if the corporate veil is pierced
AML and Counter-Terrorist Financing Violations

Statutory sanction: Fine up to GIP 500,000 per breach under the AML legislation; the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit can impose restrictions on the payment account and freeze the platform's funds

Director personal liability: Money-Laundering Reporting Officers and directors who know or suspect the offence face imprisonment up to 14 years and confiscation orders under the Proceeds of Crime Act; travel bans can be imposed during the confiscation investigation
