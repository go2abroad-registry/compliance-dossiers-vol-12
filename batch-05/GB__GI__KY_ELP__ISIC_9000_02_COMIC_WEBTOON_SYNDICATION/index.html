UBO Ultimate Beneficial Owner Screening Rules Official Registry Only

>=25% ownership / control

Applicable AML statute: Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 (as amended), the Limited Partnerships Act 2016, the Financial Services Act 2019, the Beneficial Ownership (Access to Beneficial Ownership Information) Regulations 2019 (GN 2019/108), the Companies (Beneficial Ownership) Regulations 2015 (analogous standard applied to corporate general partners), the Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering (Overseas Territories) Order 2018 and Gibraltar local sanctions regulations, and the Financial Services Commission (GFSC) AML/CFT Guidance Notes (current consolidated edition). The Registrar of Companies maintains the central beneficial ownership register for limited partnerships; the registered general partner bears the filing obligation and must keep an internal UBO ledger updated within 14 days of any change.
Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A fully virtual (address-only) office is not permissible as the statutory registered office. The Limited Partnerships Act 2016 requires a physical Gibraltar address at which process and official notices may be served and at which statutory records are kept. A licensed Corporate Service Provider (CSP) may supply this registered office and a resident agent, allowing remote commercial operations; however, the entity must maintain physical books and records at the registered office (or at the agent's premises), and a non-resident general partner must appoint an authorized Gibraltar resident agent.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar, acting through the Commissioner of Income Tax; official portal and taxpayer e-services at https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax-office

E-government filing portal

Non-resident applicant procedure: (1) register the Exempted Limited Partnership with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies to obtain the entity registration number; (2) submit the prescribed entity registration application to the Income Tax Office to obtain the Gibraltar Income Tax reference number (TIN); (3) file annual tax returns showing partner profit allocations if the ELP has Gibraltar-source taxable income; (4) non-resident partners are not separately required to file where they have no Gibraltar-source income. No VAT identification number can be issued because Gibraltar does not impose VAT.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible - the ELP is statutorily transparent under the Income Tax Act 2010; partnership profit is allocated to partners in accordance with the partnership agreement and taxed at the partner level (s. 13 Income Tax Act 2010), with the ELP itself outside the corporate income tax net. — Non-resident applicant procedure: (1) register the Exempted Limited Partnership with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies to obtain the entity registration number; (2) submit the prescribed entity registration application to the Income Tax Office to obtain the Gibraltar Income Tax reference number (TIN); (3) file annual tax returns showing partner profit allocations if the ELP has Gibraltar-source taxable income; (4) non-resident partners are not separately required to file where they have no Gibraltar-source income. No VAT identification number can be issued because Gibraltar does not impose VAT.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible - the ELP is statutorily transparent under the Income Tax Act 2010; partnership profit is allocated to partners in accordance with the partnership agreement and taxed at the partner level (s. 13 Income Tax Act 2010), with the ELP itself outside the corporate income tax net.

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a US-style check-the-box election. Tax transparency for ELPs is automatic; a deviation from transparency can be granted only by application to the Commissioner of Income Tax, who may direct separate treatment on the facts. Hybrid mismatch considerations are managed through OECD BEPS Action 2 substantiation and advance tax ruling requests.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No territorial VAT or GST: Gibraltar is outside the EU VAT area and imposes no general sales tax. Indirect levies are limited to customs Import Duty and the Purchase Tax regime (closed-list categories: motor vehicles, fuel, alcohol, tobacco, and selected goods). Revenue from Apple-IAP and Google-Play webtoon syndication is outside the Gibraltar indirect tax net.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT/GST registration threshold exists in Gibraltar; Purchase Tax and Import Duty obligations attach at importation of designated goods rather than by reference to economic turnover. The ELP's digital B2C chapter-unlocking revenue has no territorial indirect-tax registration trigger (local currency: GBP).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - because Gibraltar has no VAT/GST, no 0% export rate can be claimed and no VAT refund route exists for digital webtoon exports or offshore services. Export character still matters for income tax source analysis: profits derived from overseas platform sales will not bear Gibraltar tax where the trade is effectively managed outside Gibraltar and no permanent establishment or source nexus arises in Gibraltar; conversely, management-and-control exercised in Gibraltar may render the transparent profits taxable at partner level at the prevailing rates.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic declaration or approved re-export certificate where merchandise movement is documented)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / electronic transport record evidencing the cross-border route
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank credit advice evidencing foreign-currency receipt of export proceeds

No real-time fiscal clearance, statutory e-invoicing, API-integrity system, or digital stamping mandate is in force in Gibraltar. The Income Tax Act requires the ELP to keep adequate books and records and to issue conventional invoices or credit notes; customs transactions are processed through the electronic single-window for import/export declarations where physical goods are involved.

No VAT clawback can arise in Gibraltar, but misstated export evidence, misapplied partner allocations, or abuse of the transparent structure triggers Income Tax Act 2010 sanctions: tax-geared penalties for careless/deliberate inaccuracy (up to 100% of tax), daily filing penalties, statutory interest, assessment windows extended to 6 years (or 20 years for fraud/negligence), and criminal fines/imprisonment for false declarations. Platform sales remain at the margin of UK nexus risk: if UK-consumer digital supplies are deemed connected with the UK, the Non-UK Union VAT registration route may necessitate HMRC VAT registration and VAT accounting, with penalties and interest for non-compliance.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Fully open capital and current account convertibility. The GIP is pegged at par to GBP and is freely convertible without restriction at any licensed Gibraltar bank; GBP is also legal tender in Gibraltar, eliminating any dual-currency friction. The UK and Gibraltar apply IMF Article VIII obligations and the OECD Code of Liberalisation of Capital Movements: capital contributions, profit remittances, royalty distributions, and loan repayments by a non-resident-owned ELP are unrestricted and require no regulatory approval. Practical hurdles are limited to bank-level source-of-funds due diligence, CRS/FATCA reporting, and platform-level requirements imposed by Apple IAP and Google Play Billing (developer account legal-name matching), not to any exchange-control limitation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Gibraltar imposes no statutory surrender obligation, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation deadline for export proceeds or digital-service receipts. Webtoon subscription, Apple IAP, Google Play Billing, and advertising royalties earned in USD, EUR, or other currencies may be retained indefinitely in multi-currency accounts and converted at the treasury's discretion. The only binding retention framework is GFSC AML/CFT record-keeping (five-year preservation of payment and KYC evidence), the ELP's annual economic-substance declaration, and CRS/FATCA automatic exchange filings; no tax or exchange-control notice is triggered by holding foreign currency balances.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary anchor through the GBP statutory parity regime); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as sole banking and AML/CFT supervisor in Gibraltar; Government of Gibraltar as issuer of Gibraltar Pound (GIP) notes and coin under the Currency Notes Act 1934; HM Treasury (UK) retaining residual exchange-control competence. No exchange-control licence, permit, or no-objection filing is required from any of these authorities for ordinary treasury operations of a Gibraltar Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility: Medium; acceptable strictly as a secondary clearing passenger, never as the sole treasury account. Legal constraints: post-Brexit, the Gibraltar ELP is outside the EEA and outside UK passporting; acceptance by FCA-authorised EMIs (e.g., Wise Business and similarly positioned UK-licensed e-money institutions) is discretionary under the UK Electronic Money Regulations 2011 and the EMI's own onboarding matrix. E-money balances are safeguarded assets, not bank deposits, and carry no FSCS or Gibraltar DSD guarantee; EMIs cannot operate true escrow, custody, or interest-bearing structures. Recommended structure: onboard one FCA-authorised EMI for high-velocity Apple/Google USD/EUR receipts and same-day interbank-style FX conversion, sweep balances above GBP 20,000 daily to the Tier-1 Gibraltar bank, and disclose the EMI as a named collection sub-account in the bank's liquidity and control statement.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% flat corporate income tax on assessable profits under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010, as amended. The Exempted Limited Partnership (ELP) is fiscally transparent; the ELP itself pays no corporate tax, and the corporate general partner and partners are assessable at 12.5% on their respective shares of Gibraltar-source trading profits.
