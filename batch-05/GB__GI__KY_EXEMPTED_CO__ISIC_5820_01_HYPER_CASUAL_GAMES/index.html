Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
HM Government of Gibraltar Income Tax Office — Official Portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation

        
E-government filing portal
A Cayman Exempted Company not incorporated in Gibraltar must register for Gibraltar taxation only if it becomes tax-resident in Gibraltar or establishes a permanent establishment there. To obtain a Gibraltar TIN, the entity submits Form 1G to the Income Tax Office, accompanied by its Cayman certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, register of directors and members, and a certified English translation of any relevant records. If the company is not resident and has no permanent establishment in Gibraltar, no Gibraltar TIN or corporate tax registration is required.

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
Gibraltar has no VAT and therefore no statutory export zero-rating mechanism. For direct tax purposes, a Cayman Exempted Company earning digital gaming revenues solely from overseas users has no Gibraltar source income and no taxable permanent establishment in Gibraltar; such profits are outside the scope of Gibraltar corporate income tax. No Gibraltar tax refund mechanism arises in the absence of a relevant tax charge.

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
Full current-account and capital-account convertibility across the UK, Gibraltar and the Cayman Islands. No exchange-control approvals, central-bank permits, or treasury consents are required for inbound capital injections, trade settlements, dividend distributions, or profit repatriation. The GIP is fully redeemable at 1:1 GBP parity; the Cayman Islands dollar (KYD) is pegged at 0.8333 KYD per USD under the Cayman Islands Currency Law 1974. A Cayman Exempted Company distributing dividends to non-resident members incurs zero withholding tax under Cayman Islands income tax law; Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid to non-residents under the Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended). The only material friction is documentary substantiation of beneficial ownership and source of funds to AML-regulated intermediaries; no regulatory conversion or repatriation bar exists.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
No statutory surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio apply in any of the three relevant territories. The United Kingdom repealed all exchange-control and export-proceeds surrender obligations under the Exchange Control Act 1979; Gibraltar operates no exporter surrender regime and follows the UK monetary framework; the Cayman Islands abolished the Exchange Control Regulations effective 1981. USD-denominated hyper-casual gaming proceeds from AdMob and AppLovin payouts may therefore be retained in USD indefinitely, converted at commercial discretion, or netted against foreign-currency disbursements without central-bank intervention.

      

      
Central bank supervision: Bank of England (BoE) as sovereign monetary authority for the United Kingdom and for the Gibraltar pound (GIP) currency-board peg at 1:1 parity to GBP under Gibraltar's Currency Notes Act 1934; Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as prudential regulator supervising all licensed banks, e-money institutions and payment services providers within Gibraltar; Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA) as the home-jurisdiction regulator for the Cayman Exempted Company; HM Treasury retains final sanctions, counter-terrorist financing and financial crime policymaking authority extending to all three territories.

    

    

      
Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

      

        
        

          

            Wise
            Fit: High
          

          
Feasibility rating: Medium contingent on establishing a Gibraltar-registered branch or operating subsidiary. UK-licensed EMIs including Wise, Revolut Business, ClearBank and Modulr generally exclude Cayman-incorporated direct clients under their jurisdiction matrices. GFSC-licensed EMIs under the Financial Services Act 2019 do not categorically prohibit Cayman clients, but internal underwriting policy applies enhanced due diligence identical to that of tier-1 commercial banks. Legal constraints include: (i) statutory safeguarding of client funds at an authorized credit institution; (ii) EMI transaction monitoring may reclassify high-frequency, low-value ad-network credits as 'unexplained wealth' without platform-level payout attestations; (iii) EMI account structures do not support large-capital escrow or multi-currency concentration. Recommended architecture: retain the tier-1 Cayman/Gibraltar commercial bank as the primary concentration and escrow point, and deploy the EMI solely for payable sub-accounts and automated disbursements to contractors and user-acquisition platforms.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

      
      

        Zero-tax Cayman Exempted Company flagged as an offshore tax-haven legal wrapper
        
Root cause: Zero-tax Cayman Exempted Company flagged as an offshore tax-haven legal wrapper

        
Defense evidence: Cayman Corporate Registry Certificate of Good Standing dated within 90 days; certified Memorandum and Articles of Association; Certificate of Incorporation; Register of Directors; Cayman Tax Information Authority certificate confirming tax-exempt filing status; Gibraltar substance file comprising office lease, local employment contracts and board minutes evidencing economic substance; and a tax structure memorandum demonstrating the commercial rationale for the Gibraltar/Cayman architecture.

      

      
      

        Non-resident beneficial ownership with non-resident directors and remote signing authority
        
Root cause: Non-resident beneficial ownership with non-resident directors and remote signing authority

        
Defense evidence: Extract from the Cayman Beneficial Ownership Register or PSC-equivalent; passport copies with dual-use residential address proof (utility bill and bank statement dated within three months); professional reference letter from a licensed Cayman or Gibraltar corporate services provider; biographical narrative and source-of-wealth file for each ultimate beneficial owner; and a board resolution approving the account and treasury mandate, executed under the company's constitutional signature rules.

      

      
      

        High-volume, low-value advertising payouts from AdMob and AppLovin flagged as commingled or gambling-adjacent credits
        
Root cause: High-volume, low-value advertising payouts from AdMob and AppLovin flagged as commingled or gambling-adjacent credits

        
Defense evidence: Executed Google AdMob and AppLovin platform publisher agreements; payout history statements for the preceding three to six months evidencing platform-to-bank wiring; eCPM and impression-volume reports; IAB UK Transparency & Consent Framework compliance attestation; and a signed monthly treasury reconciliation policy mapping gross ad revenue to net bank credits.

      

      
      

        Hyper-casual gaming content characterized as high-risk digital content under evolving UK consumer-protection and gambling-adjacent screening
        
Root cause: Hyper-casual gaming content characterized as high-risk digital content under evolving UK consumer-protection and gambling-adjacent screening

        Absence of audited financial statements and short operational track record
        
Root cause: Absence of audited financial statements and short operational track record

        
Defense evidence: Board resolution approving account opening and treasury mandate; pro-forma twelve-month cash-flow forecast; executed shareholder loan or subscription agreements; letters of intention from acquisition network partners confirming expected ad-revenue pipeline; and, where applicable, an executed engagement letter for a first-year audit from a recognized Cayman or Gibraltar audit firm.

The UK receiving bank applies Bank of England statistical BOPP classification for inward direct investment in equity capital or inter-company lending; Gibraltar imposes no FX declaration and no exchange-control permit. Verification file required by the receiving bank: board resolution approving the equity injection or loan; executed subscription agreement or inter-company loan agreement with clear interest terms; source-of-funds evidence from the shareholder's bank; and the certificate of inward remittance or transaction advice to be retained for future outbound dividend or loan-repayment calibration.Primary outbound rails: (i) SWIFT MT103 for supplier settlements to game-development studios, cloud-host providers, and external legal and accounting advisers, using ISO 20022 purpose code 'TRAD' or UK BOPP services category for Gibraltar/UK outbound settlements; (ii) Commercial Letter of Credit issued via SWIFT MT700 for procurement above US$100,000, irrevocable, available by deferred payment, with document schedule and charges clearly allocated in field 71D; (iii) SEPA and instant-SEPA rails for EU-based contractors and operational expenses; (iv) automated clearing and commercial card rails for user-acquisition spend on Meta, Google and TikTok; and (v) AdMob/AppLovin contractual netting, where the network deducts its take-rate and remits the net balance by wire on net-30 or net-60 terms.
