Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

    

    

      
Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
HM Government of Gibraltar – Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax) for direct corporate taxation; HM Government of Gibraltar – Customs and Excise Department (VAT Office) for value added tax; Gibraltar Companies House for external-company branch registration; consolidated access via the official HMGoG e-services portal (www.gibraltar.gov.gi).

        
E-government filing portal
Step 1 – External Company Registration: a Cayman Exempted Company establishing a branch or place of business in Gibraltar must register as an 'external company' with the Gibraltar Companies House within one month of commencement (Companies Act 2014), filing a certified copy of the Cayman certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles, register of directors and shareholders, and a Gibraltar registered-office address, obtaining a Gibraltar company registration number. Step 2 – Direct Tax Registration: apply to the Income Tax Office for a Tax Reference Number (TRN) using the foreign-company registration form; submit the Cayman incorporation certificate, certificate of good standing, constitutional documents, particulars of directors, a Gibraltar-licensed tax agent appointment letter, and a declaration of the place of central management and control; the CEC is classified as a non-resident company unless management and control is exercised in Gibraltar. Step 3 – VAT Registration: submit the VAT registration application (overseas-entity form) to the Customs and Excise VAT Office; provide the Cayman incorporation certificate, proof of Gibraltar representative/agent, bank account details, projected turnover from Gibraltar taxable activities, and evidence of intended exports; a GI-prefixed VAT identification number is issued. Step 4 – Employer/Payroll Registration: if hiring Gibraltar staff, register as an employer for Payroll Tax (PAYE-equivalent) within 30 days. No minimum capital requirement applies; processing times are typically 5–10 working days for Companies House and up to 15 working days for VAT registration.

No minimum capital requirement applies; processing times are typically 5–10 working days for Companies House and up to 15 working days for VAT registration.

    

    

      
Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Territorial_Scope: the Cayman Exempted Company is tax-exempt in its home jurisdiction under a Cayman Tax Exemption Certificate (Companies Act, s.6, 20-year undertaking), and in Gibraltar it is a non-resident company charged to income tax only on profits arising in or derived from Gibraltar (Income Tax Act 2010, s.8); foreign-source income from offshore e-commerce sales and contract manufacturing is outside the Gibraltar tax charge; the entity is not eligible for pass-through treatment and does not access Gibraltar's individual gross-income-based assessment.

        
Available election forms
Gibraltar has no US-style check-the-box regime. Relevant statutory mechanisms under the Income Tax Act 2010 include: (i) application/election for deemed-profits assessment (s.20) where accounts-based computation is impractical for a non-resident company; (ii) group-relief election (s.41) permitting surrender of losses among companies within a 75% group; (iii) election to apply the small-company effective-rate bands on assessable profits up to GBP 500,000, subject to the annual Income Tax (Rates and Allowances) Regulations; (iv) capital-allowances pooling elections (s.31); and (v) compulsory, non-elective application of the OECD/ATAD-aligned hybrid mismatch rules (Income Tax (Hybrid Mismatch) Regulations 2020), which neutralise deductions without any opt-out election.

      

    

    

      
VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

      

        
Standard VAT / GST rate
12.5%

        
Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)
GBP 100,000 annual taxable turnover for businesses established in Gibraltar (measured on a rolling 12-month basis); non-established/overseas businesses making taxable supplies in Gibraltar have a zero threshold and must register before the first taxable supply; voluntary registration is permitted for those making only zero-rated or export supplies.

      

      
      

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
Zero-rating applies to the supply of silicone stress-relief fidget toys (ISIC 3240) dispatched or transported by the supplier or on its behalf to a destination outside Gibraltar, including shipments to the EU, UK, and third countries, under the Gibraltar Value Added Tax Act 2010 export provisions. Conditions: (i) goods must leave Gibraltar within 3 months of the time of supply; (ii) the supplier must obtain and retain a validated Customs Export Declaration / AES movement reference number; (iii) the customer must be established outside Gibraltar or take delivery outside Gibraltar; (iv) sale terms should be EXW, FOB, CFR, or CIF from a Gibraltar port or airport; (v) the exporter of record must be the registered person claiming the zero rate. Related offshore services (platform facilitation, marketing, logistics coordination supplied to non-Gibraltar recipients) are outside the scope of Gibraltar VAT under place-of-supply rules. No statutory licence is required for the manufacture or sale of food-grade silicone novelty fidgets; however, EU/UK food-contact silicone compliance (EU 1935/2004, EU 10/2011, UK SI 2018/1137) should be evidenced to sustain customs classification (HS 9503.00 or 3924.90) and zero-rating on import/export.

No real-time clearance, continuous transaction control, API fiscal e-invoicing, or digital stamping mandate in force in Gibraltar. VAT invoices must satisfy the content requirements of the Gibraltar Value Added Tax Regulations (sequential invoice number, supplier name/address/GI VAT number, customer details, description, tax point, rate, VAT amount). Electronic invoices (PDF, EDI, UBL) are valid provided authenticity of origin, integrity of content, and legibility are assured (e.g., via digital signature or controlled exchange). Paper invoices remain lawful. VAT returns are filed quarterly through the HMGoG Customs & Excise VAT e-services portal; no separate fiscal clearance is required for export declarations beyond the movement reference number obtained from the Customs Declaration System.

      
Gibraltar Customs & Excise and the Income Tax Office cross-match AES export declarations, VAT returns, invoice trails, and bank inflows to verify zero-rating claims. Where export evidence is missing or false, the Commissioner may: (i) issue a retrospective VAT assessment at 12.5% for up to 6 years (extended to 20 years in fraud or negligent-conduct cases); (ii) impose a civil penalty of up to 100% of the VAT undercharged under the Value Added Tax Act, plus daily default penalties and interest; (iii) pursue criminal prosecution for fraudulent evasion of VAT, exposing the company and responsible directors to unlimited fines and/or up to 7 years' imprisonment; (iv) claw back input VAT credits and revoke zero-rated status with immediate effect; (v) apply transfer-pricing adjustments if export prices to related offshore distributors are not at arm's length, generating additional corporate tax at 12.5% plus penalties; and (vi) refer suspicious unrepatriated proceeds to the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015, creating parallel AML exposure.

Stage 04
Banking & Treasury Architecture

      
Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
Full convertibility applies on both current and capital accounts. GBP and GIP are freely floating against other currencies, and neither the UK nor Gibraltar maintains exchange-control approval requirements for foreign direct investment, dividend remittances, profit repatriation, or capital liquidation. A Cayman Exempted Company faces zero statutory remittance hurdles in the Gibraltar/UK corridor; the only practical frictions are bank-level AML/CDD checks, including source-of-funds and source-of-wealth documentary evidence. Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on dividends or interest paid by a qualifying company, and no central-bank clearance is required for any currency conversion or cross-border transfer.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
None. The UK and Gibraltar impose no export-proceeds surrender obligation, no mandatory conversion timeline, and no minimum conversion ratio. The Cayman Exempted Company may retain 100% of its export and platform sales proceeds in USD, GBP, EUR, or any freely convertible currency without central-bank approval, earmarking, or repatriation requirement.

      

      
Central bank supervision: Bank of England (the 'BOE'), apex monetary authority for the sterling zone; HM Treasury retains residual exchange-control and financial-sanctions powers; the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (the 'GFSC') licenses and supervises all GIP-licensed deposit-takers; the Government of Gibraltar issues Gibraltar Pound (GIP) notes and coins at a fixed 1:1 parity with GBP pursuant to the Currency Notes Act 1934. No separate Gibraltar central bank exists.

    

    

      
Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

      

        
        

          

            Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails
            Fit: High
          

          
Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-HIGH, restricted-use. A Gibraltar-licensed electronic money institution (licensed under the Financial Services Act 2019) or a UK FCA-registered EMI may onboard the Cayman Exempted Company and provide multi-currency IBANs, virtual accounts, and rapid platform payouts; however, post-Brexit Gibraltar EMIs lack EU passporting rights and UK EMIs cannot passport into Gibraltar without a local branch or partner. Unregulated offshore digital EMIs are not viable as a primary treasury rail because Gibraltar correspondent banks will reject settlement flows from non-equivalent third-country jurisdictions. Recommended architecture: deploy a licensed EMI solely as a high-velocity collection rail for Amazon/Shopify payouts, with automated daily MT103 sweeps into the licensed Gibraltar settlement bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

      

        SWIFT MT103 from the UBO's personal or source-company account to the Cayman Exempted Company's Gibraltar operational account. Wire field 70 (Remittance Information) must state: 'CAPITAL INJECTION / SHARE SUBSCRIPTION / REF [registration no.]'. The receiving bank executes standard AML source-of-funds checks against the capital contribution agreement, three months of source bank statements, and a licensed accountant's net-worth certificate. No Gibraltar FX-control license is required for the inbound remittance; funds may be credited in USD or GBP and recorded to share capital or a capital-reserve ledger. If the remittance exceeds GBP 50,000 and the documentation bundle is incomplete, the bank may apply enhanced monitoring rather than outright rejection.Clearing rails: (i) SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfer from the Gibraltar account to the Asian supplier's USD or CNY account, settled through a New York or London correspondent clearing bank; (ii) irrevocable commercial Letter of Credit (sight or 30-to-120-day usance) issued by the Gibraltar bank through a Chinese advising or confirming bank (e.g., Bank of China) for production batches above GBP 50,000, with documents comprising commercial invoice, full-set clean on-board bill of lading, packing list, quality inspection certificate, and insurance certificate; (iii) platform settlement rails (Amazon and Shopify) automatically sweeping goods-and-services sales proceeds into the Gibraltar operational account; no LC is required for platform payouts. For CNY disbursement legs, the treasury executes an FX forward or non-deliverable forward to fix the four-way FX exposure (GBP account base versus USD gross receipts versus CNY costs).Outright FX Forwards - deliverable GBP/USD, EUR/USD, and GBP/CNY contracts with Gibraltar-licensed banks or UK/EU counterparties, tenor 1-12 months, documented under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement or a short-form FX Master Agreement.Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDFs) - GBP/CNY and USD/CNY NDFs referencing the offshore CNH fixing or CFETS benchmark, executed via a major international bank (e.g., HSBC or Standard Chartered) when onshore deliverable CNY liquidity is unavailable to the Cayman entity.Cross-Currency Swaps - fixed/floating GBP/USD or USD/CNY swaps exchanging principal and interest flows to hedge long-dated trade payables against receivables, executed under a negotiated ISDA 2002 Credit Support Annex.
      

      
E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-HIGH, restricted-use. A Gibraltar-licensed electronic money institution (licensed under the Financial Services Act 2019) or a UK FCA-registered EMI may onboard the Cayman Exempted Company and provide multi-currency IBANs, virtual accounts, and rapid platform payouts; however, post-Brexit Gibraltar EMIs lack EU passporting rights and UK EMIs cannot passport into Gibraltar without a local branch or partner. Unregulated offshore digital EMIs are not viable as a primary treasury rail because Gibraltar correspondent banks will reject settlement flows from non-equivalent third-country jurisdictions. Recommended architecture: deploy a licensed EMI solely as a high-velocity collection rail for Amazon/Shopify payouts, with automated daily MT103 sweeps into the licensed Gibraltar settlement bank.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

      
Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File and Master File apply when the Gibraltar entity or branch engages in controlled transactions aggregating GIP 1,000,000 or more in a chargeable period, or when the worldwide group revenue exceeds GIP 50,000,000; below these thresholds a condensed TP disclosure must nevertheless be filed with the Corporation Tax Return.

      
Economic substance scope: The Cayman exempted company is subject to the Cayman International Tax Co-operation (Economic Substance) Law; distribution/manufacturing and financing are 'relevant activities' requiring core income-generating activities, physical premises, qualified local employees and annual ESR filing in the Cayman Islands. Where the trading activity is managed from Gibraltar, the Gibraltar economic substance rules (mirroring OECD and EU Code of Conduct commitments) require local management and control, board meetings in Gibraltar, and adequate staffing; failure in either jurisdiction voids the tax exemption and reactivates the 12.5% Gibraltar charge.

      

    

    

      
Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

      
      

        Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Tax Avoidance
        
Statutory sanction: Civil tax-geared penalties up to 100% of the tax undercharged, interest at 4% above base rate, and daily default fines; criminal conviction carries fines of up to GIP 100,000 and imprisonment of up to 7 years; UK Criminal Finances Act liability may attach to any EMI or payment platform facilitating the evasion.

        
Director personal liability: Directors and shadow directors face personal civil restitution for the tax loss, worldwide freezing orders, travel bans and disqualification orders; knowing participation in evasion triggers criminal liability of up to 7 years' imprisonment and director disqualification for up to 15 years.

      

      
      

        Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure (ESR)
        
Statutory sanction: Cayman ESR penalties: KYD 10,000 for the first year and KYD 100,000 per year for continued failure, with mandatory strike-off and deregistration; Gibraltar penalties up to GIP 1,000 plus daily default fines, with back-assessment of 12.5% on the full Gibraltar-source profits for the entire non-compliant period.

        
Director personal liability: Directors are jointly liable for ESR penalties, lose limited-liability protection through veil-piercing where the entity is a mere façade, and may be barred from managing any Cayman or Gibraltar company or from holding EMI banking mandates.

      

      
      

        AML-CFT / Beneficial Ownership Disclosure Breach
        
Statutory sanction: Gibraltar Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act fines up to GIP 250,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years; Cayman Beneficial Ownership Law fines up to KYD 500,000 and imprisonment; proceeds-of-crime confiscation and account freezes apply to the full value of undisclosed flows.

        
Director personal liability: Directors, nominated officers and shareholder signatories are personally liable for AML fines and confiscation; travel bans, refusal of Gibraltar police certificates and exclusion from payment-platform mandates are imposed; criminal conviction follows on proof of consent or connivance.

      

      
      

        Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure / Artificial Profit Shifting
        
Statutory sanction: The Commissioner adjusts chargeable profits to the arm's-length standard and imposes a TP adjustment penalty of up to 100% of the additional tax (50% where reasonable care was exercised), with interest from the original filing date; the full local-file master-file sanction regime applies.

        
Director personal liability: Directors signing false TP declarations incur personal fines up to GIP 50,000 and imprisonment up to 2 years; the adjustment reduces distributable reserves, blocks dividend repatriation, and creates shareholder claw-back claims where distributions were made while the Gibraltar PE was insolvent.
