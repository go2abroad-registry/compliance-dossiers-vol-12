Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, HM Government of Gibraltar (Income Tax Office), portal: www.gibraltar.gov.gi. HMRC has no direct jurisdiction in Gibraltar; import and excise duties are administered by the Gibraltar Customs and Excise Department.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (Cayman-side status is Tax_Exempt_Zone but that exemption does not pass to Gibraltar; the exempted company is taxed as a body corporate at the 12.5% Gibraltar rate on profits attributable to a Gibraltar PE or, if managed and controlled in Gibraltar, on worldwide income).

Available election forms

No check-the-box or pass-through election exists. Statutory elections under GIB tax law include: (i) group-relief surrender among Gibraltar-resident 75% group members; (ii) capital-allowance and first-year allowance elections; (iii) a written applied-treatment election with the Commissioner fixing non-resident territorial PE treatment versus resident worldwide treatment for a three-year binding period; (iv) hybrid-mismatch disclosure and neutralisation elections under the ATAD-aligned rules - no election can exempt the Cayman vehicle from Gibraltar tax where real brokerage management is exercised in Gibraltar.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None in force - Gibraltar levies no VAT, GST or sales-and-use tax; the applicable regime is Customs and Excise Duty (alcohol, tobacco, hydrocarbon oil and import duty). UK VAT and EU VAT directives do not extend to Gibraltar.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - there is no VAT/GST registration threshold. For income-tax purposes any Gibraltar permanent establishment or central-management-and-control nexus triggers tax registration irrespective of turnover; customs registration applies only to physical importers of dutiable goods.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - because Gibraltar has no VAT, no 0% export VAT rate can be claimed. Offshore securities brokerage services (ISIC 6611) are outside any Gibraltar indirect tax base. Any physical goods transiting or re-exported from Gibraltar may obtain customs-duty suspension or remission under bond, but no zero-rating certificate is issued.

No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping or e-invoicing API mandate is in force in Gibraltar. The Income Tax Office accepts auditable PDF/paper records; the GFSC-regulated broker must retain electronic trade confirmations and settlement records under the Record-Keeping Regulations. Invoicing to UK/EU B2B customers triggers the standard reverse-charge or outside-scope treatment, with no Gibraltarian fiscal stamp.

The Commissioner may reopen assessments for six years (twelve where fraud or wilful default is shown). Mischaracterising brokerage income as Cayman-exempt offshore income while management and control is actually exercised in Gibraltar is treated as fraudulent evasion and triggers: penalty of up to 200% of the tax lost plus interest, daily default fines, public naming of penalised persons and criminal prosecution under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act. The GFSC may separately suspend or revoke the brokerage licence and impose disciplinary fines for artificial profit attribution to the Cayman exempted company.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound (GIP) and UK pound sterling (GBP) are fully convertible on both current and capital accounts. No exchange-control permissions are required for securities brokerage receipts, client fund movement, interest, or principal repatriation. Dividend and capital distributions from a Gibraltar-licensed brokerage to the Cayman parent are unrestricted and free of Gibraltar withholding tax; the Cayman Exempted Company is not subject to local income tax. Practical remittance friction is limited to standard banking due-diligence on source-of-wealth, sanctions screening, and the presentation of executed trade settlement confirmations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Gibraltar has no statutory export proceeds surrender rules, mandatory conversion timelines, or marketing margins for brokerage service income, client fee receipts, or FX dealing proceeds. Revenues may be retained in any freely chosen currency. The UK Exchange Control Act 1947 was repealed and no residual overseas-territory exchange control restrictions are in force.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) acts as the integrated regulator for banking, AML/CFT, and investment services; monetary issuance is vested in the Government of Gibraltar under the Currency Board arrangement, maintaining a 1:1 parity with the Bank of England pound sterling. The Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA) remains the home-state regulator of the Cayman Exempted Company but has no extraterritorial FX-control authority over Gibraltar banking activity.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility: Medium with strict pre-conditions. UK/EU digital EMIs (e.g., Currenxie, Revolut Business, Airwallex, Wise) can provide EUR/GBP/USD collection and disbursement rails; however, most automatically decline a Cayman-incorporated retail securities brokerage unless it presents a GFSC investment services licence (or FCA-equivalent), a sponsor-bank comfort letter, and a live transaction monitoring system. Legal constraints: EMIs do not hold full banking licences and cannot provide formally designated segregated client money safeguarding under Gibraltar's CASS-equivalent rules. The EMI may serve only as a collection/disbursement channel rather than the principal treasury segregation vehicle, and the core segregated client account must sit with a fully licensed Gibraltar commercial bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity and working-capital flows from the Cayman parent must route via SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 for interbank value) from a Cayman-licensed bank with an established UK/Gibraltar correspondent chain. Field 70 should explicitly state 'TREASURY CAPITAL INJECTION – SECURITIES BROKERAGE' and include a unique transaction reference (UTR) tying to the Board resolution and Cayman source-of-funds file. GBP inbound volumes should settle through CHAPS, EUR through SEPA Instant/TARGET2 with the brokerage's Gibraltar IBAN, and USD through Fedwire via a US correspondent. GFSC-compliant banks will require the source-of-funds narrative to be captured on every transfer above USD 10,000 equivalent, and the beneficiary account reference must match the sanctioned account login to satisfy automated screening.Retail client withdrawals and profit payouts are executed through segregated client wire instructions transmitted as SWIFT MT103 with Settlement Instruction codes (71A OUR via the bank's fee schedule). Counterparty trade settlements with liquidity providers, market makers, and prime brokers utilise commercial Letters of Credit (MT700) or MT202 cover payments for OTC derivative margin calls. EUR disbursements clear via SEPA and TARGET2, GBP via CHAPS, and USD via Fedwire through the bank's correspondent network. FX trades settling through CLS are supported for major currency pairs; non-CLS exotic currencies settle on a T+2 bilateral basis using confirmed MT300 trade confirmations. All outbound instructions must carry the GFSC-mandated purpose reference and be matched against the pre-approved beneficiary list to avoid sanctions or anti-money-laundering referral.Outright FX Forwards up to 12 months tenor, permitted without physical delivery restriction and without mandatory underlying trade-ticket requirement under Gibraltar law.Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDFs) for illiquid currencies referencing EUR, GBP, or USD as the settlement currency, available through licensed counterparty banks.Cross-Currency Interest Rate Swaps (CCIRS) for long-term mismatches between funding liabilities and brokerage fee receivables.European-style FX Options purchased or written with licensed credit institutions solely for treasury hedging purposes, subject to board-approved risk limits.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% flat corporate income tax rate under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended), effective from tax year 2019/20 onwards.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% (nil) — Gibraltar does not impose a withholding tax on dividend distributions. Dividends paid by the Gibraltar-resident Cayman exempted company to any non-resident or resident shareholder are paid gross, with no statutory Gibraltar tax deducted at source.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The Form GS1 return and the balancing tax payment must be delivered within 12 months after the end of the company's accounting period (chargeable period). For a 31 December 2024 year-end, the statutory filing and payment deadline is 31 December 2025. The chargeable period is assessed within the Gibraltar Year of Assessment (YoA) running 1 July to 30 June.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 (approximately EUR 1,100,000) aggregate controlled transactions per accounting period triggers mandatory Local File preparation under the Income Tax (Transfer Pricing) (Documentation) Regulations 2019. A Master File is mandatory where the multinational group's consolidated turnover equals or exceeds GIP 50,000,000 (approximately EUR 50,000,000). Micro and small enterprises (fewer than 10 employees and turnover or balance-sheet total below GIP 2,000,000) are exempt from the documentation obligation but remain subject to arm's-length pricing rules.

Economic substance scope: Applicable — the entity is subject to the OECD BEPS Action 5 substance minimum standard. Under Cayman law, the International Tax Co-operation (Economic Substance) Act requires the Cayman exempted company to demonstrate economic substance in Cayman unless it proves tax residence in another jurisdiction (Gibraltar via management-and-control). In Gibraltar, the entity must perform its core income-generating activities (CIGAs) locally: strategic brokerage decisions, client-facing dealing desks, qualified compliance and risk staff, physical premises, and annual board meetings. Failure to maintain real presence in Gibraltar undermines the tax-residence exclusion in Cayman and exposes the company to dual-substance challenges, GFSC licence revocation, and retroactive tax assessments.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Unauthorised Brokerage / Regulated Financial Activity

Statutory sanction: On conviction: a fine not exceeding GIP 2,000,000 (approximately EUR 2,300,000) and/or imprisonment not exceeding 7 years. The Supreme Court may also order winding-up of the unauthorised entity, restitution to clients, and confiscation of the gross commissions derived from the unauthorised activity.

Director personal liability: Every director and senior officer who consented to or connived in the unauthorised dealing is personally liable to the same fine and imprisonment. The GFSC may issue prohibition orders banning individuals from managing a financial services firm for up to 10 years, and the Supreme Court may impose travel bans and asset-freezing orders to secure enforcement.
Tax Evasion / Deliberate Under-Declaration

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty up to 100% of the tax evaded in addition to the underpaid tax and daily late-payment interest at 8% per annum. On indictment, a fine not exceeding GIP 1,000,000 and/or imprisonment up to 7 years; carried-forward losses are quashed and the taxpayer is subjected to extended audit powers.

Director personal liability: Where evasion is attributable to a director's deliberate act, the court may pierce the corporate veil: the director is jointly and severally liable for the tax arrears and penalties, is disqualified from acting as a director, is disqualified from GFSC licensing, and may be sentenced to imprisonment by the Supreme Court. Departure (travel ban) orders may be issued pending payment or trial.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation / Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: Penalty up to 100% of the additional tax assessed after transfer pricing adjustment; denial of the deduction for non-compliant intercompany amounts; fixed penalties of up to GIP 50,000 for missing documentation; and accrual of statutory interest on all underpayments.

Director personal liability: Directors certifying false transfer pricing returns face personal examination by the Commissioner and may be subjected to prosecution for false statements. Where the corporate veil is abused to fabricate intercompany substance, the Supreme Court may order directors to personally contribute to the tax shortfall and to the costs of the investigation, with associated travel bans and disqualification orders.
AML/CFT Failure and Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: GFSC regulatory financial penalty up to 5% of annual turnover or EUR 5,000,000 per contravention; criminal money-laundering conviction attracts an unlimited fine, forfeiture of the laundered instrumentalities, and/or imprisonment up to 14 years under the Criminal Justice (Money Laundering) Act 2018; the brokerage licence may be suspended or revoked.

Director personal liability: Confiscation and benefit-recovery orders are enforced personally against directors and senior managers. Directors found unfit are removed by GFSC, are subject to 10-year prohibition orders, and face arrest and travel bans where proceeds are transferred abroad. A director who causes the company to process laundered proceeds may be prosecuted as a principal offender.
Shell Company / Abusive Economic Substance Exploitation

Statutory sanction: Cayman-side penalties under the International Tax Co-operation (Economic Substance) Act: fixed penalties starting at KYD 3,000 and escalating to KYD 60,000–100,000 per year of persistent failure, with possible strike-off of the exempted company. Gibraltar-side: the tax return is invalidated, the GFSC licence is revoked, and profits may be re-attributed to the place of effective management for reassessment, including under the anti-abuse provisions of the UK-Gibraltar DTA.

Director personal liability: Directors attesting false substance declarations are liable for false-statement offences in Gibraltar, are subject to travel bans and asset preservation orders, and may be personally assessed for the Cayman substance penalties. Repeated abuse supports personal disqualification by GFSC and personal restitution of dividends or distributions made while the company was insolvent, pursuant to the Gibraltar Companies Act provisions on fraudulent trading.
