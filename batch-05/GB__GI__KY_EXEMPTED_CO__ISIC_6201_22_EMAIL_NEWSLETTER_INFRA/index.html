Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

For a Cayman Exempted Company, no Cayman income tax TIN is required. If the company creates a Gibraltar tax nexus (branch, office, or central management and control exercised in Gibraltar), it must file the Gibraltar Income Tax Act registration application with the Income Tax Office, supply the certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, Gibraltar registered or business address, director and beneficial-owner information, and intended profit model; the ITO will issue a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number. There is no Gibraltar VAT registration because Gibraltar does not operate VAT. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope / Tax_Exempt_Zone. The Cayman Exempted Company is a non-pass-through corporation with limited liability. In the Cayman Islands it is exempt from income, corporation, capital gains, and withholding taxes, subject to an annual government fee and the Cayman economic substance regime. Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election is available in the United Kingdom or Gibraltar. Gibraltar applies automatic residence rules based on incorporation or central management and control; UK applies general entity taxation rules and does not permit a foreign company to be treated as tax transparent. The Cayman Exempted Company may apply under the Cayman Islands Tax Concessions Act for a government undertaking confirming exemption from any future Cayman taxation on profits, income, gains, or assets for up to 20 years, extendable to 30 years in approved circumstances. No hybrid mismatch election is available; UK anti-hybrid rules under TIOPA 2010 Part 6A and Gibraltar substance rules operate automatically, and elective disclaimer is not permitted.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

None in Gibraltar (no VAT, GST, or sales/use tax). United Kingdom VAT is the only relevant indirect tax and applies only if the Cayman/Gibraltar structure has a UK taxable establishment or makes taxable supplies of electronically supplied services in the UK.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Gibraltar: not applicable because no VAT/GST exists. United Kingdom: GBP 90,000 annual taxable turnover registration threshold for UK-established businesses; for non-established suppliers of UK B2C digital/electronic services, the registration threshold is nil, and UK VAT registration is required from the first supply unless the Non-Union OSS scheme is used.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar has no VAT and therefore no export zero-rating or VAT refund mechanism. For UK VAT purposes, exports of physical goods are zero-rated only where statutory export evidence is retained. Digital email/API/newsletter services are not 'zero-rated' in the traditional sense; they are outside the scope of UK VAT when supplied under UK Place of Supply rules to a non-UK customer, provided the supplier is not UK-established and the customer location is substantiated. For B2B supplies, the customer VAT registration number or a valid reverse-charge statement is required; for B2C supplies, two non-conflicting proofs of customer location are required. UK VAT refunds for non-established taxable persons may be claimed under the UK VAT refund scheme for Third Country businesses, subject to evidence and reciprocity conditions.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current-account and capital-account convertibility applies in Gibraltar; GIP is freely interchangeable with GBP and no exchange-control approvals are required. In the Cayman Islands, KYD is pegged at 0.8333 per USD; the exempted company must obtain and continuously maintain Exchange Control Non-Resident designation so that share subscriptions, dividends, interest, and liquidation proceeds are freely remittable without CIMA approval. Profit remittance from the SaaS platform is unencumbered because Cayman imposes no income or withholding tax and Gibraltar levies no withholding tax on non-resident payees. The controlling risk is tax residence: if the Cayman entity is managed and controlled from the UK or Gibraltar, it may become UK-resident or exposed to Gibraltar corporation tax at 12.5% through a permanent establishment; board meetings, central management, and control must therefore remain in Cayman or a neutral jurisdiction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Cayman Exchange Control Regulations impose a repatriation duty on resident exporters requiring export proceeds to be paid into a Cayman account with an authorised dealer and offered for sale in KYD within the statutory window, with a 90-day default applied by authorised dealers when the underlying contract is silent. The Cayman Exempted Company is fully exempt from this obligation while its Non-Resident designation remains valid. Gibraltar imposes no export-proceeds surrender or mandatory-conversion rules of any kind. As the activity is intangible email-infrastructure SaaS with no physical export of goods, the only compliance touchpoints are documented intercompany transfers and absence of an unintended permanent establishment.

Central bank supervision: No indigenous central bank exists in Gibraltar; currency issuance rests with H.M. Government of Gibraltar for the Gibraltar Pound (GIP), pegged at par to Sterling, while banking conduct is regulated by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) under the Financial Services Act 2019. For the Cayman Exempted Company, the Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA) acts as the Exchange Control Authority under the Exchange Control Regulations (2004 Revision). The parent-sovereign anchor is the Bank of England for monetary policy and the UK HM Treasury sanctions regime.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Subscriptions flow by SWIFT MT103 from the UBO's bank to the Gibraltar International Bank capital-escrow account in the Cayman entity's name, with Remittance Information field 70 coded as 'SHARE CAPITAL SUBSCRIPTION - BOARD RESOLUTION [DATE]'. The beneficiary IBAN must use the Gibraltar 'GI' country code and the beneficiary legal name must exactly match the Cayman exempted company's registered name. The remitting bank must include UBO and source-of-funds attestation; the receiving bank applies enhanced screening against UK/HMT, EU, and UN sanctions lists. No Gibraltar exchange-control approval is required; on the Cayman side, a Form of Allotment, updated Register of Members, and board minutes must be filed with the Cayman registered agent, while the Non-Resident Exchange Control designation is maintained.Operational disbursements for cloud, transactional email API, and newsletter infrastructure settle by SWIFT MT103 in USD/EUR or SEPA SCT in EUR from the Barclays Gibraltar operational account. Sterling disbursements clear via CHAPS through London clearing; USD payables clear through the UK clearer's USD correspondent account. Commercial letters of credit (MT700/MT707) are used only for physical hardware or data-centre capex and are not applicable to digital services. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low-to-Medium. Mainstream EMIs (Wise Business, Revolut Business, Tide, Qonto) do not onboard Cayman-incorporated exempted companies and do not issue Gibraltar or UK IBANs to offshore vehicles unless a locally registered subsidiary exists. Specialist EMIs (Currenxie, Airwallex, Dukascopy) may accept the Cayman entity on a case-by-case basis subject to enhanced due diligence, audited financials, and a UK or EU operating subsidiary; they remain unsuitable as the primary treasury rail because Stripe cannot settle merchant-of-record accounts in either GIP or KYD. Recommended architecture: a UK Ltd acts as merchant-of-record on Stripe while the Cayman entity owns the IP and API platform; intercompany AP/AR migrates to a traditional Gibraltar bank.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% standard Gibraltar corporate income tax rate under the Income Tax Act 2010, applying to resident companies; 0% in the Cayman Islands for a properly maintained Cayman Exempted Company under the Tax Concessions Law if the entity avoids Gibraltar central management and control.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% in Gibraltar and 0% in the Cayman Islands. Distributions declared by the Cayman Exempted Company to its beneficial owners are paid gross with no statutory Gibraltar or Cayman withholding obligation.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Gibraltar Transfer Pricing Regulations 2015 impose mandatory Local File and Master File documentation for large enterprises, defined as an associated-enterprise group with Gibraltar turnover exceeding GBP 50 million or a balance-sheet total above GBP 43 million. Below the threshold, the taxpayer must still apply the arm's length principle in the self-assessment and maintain contemporaneous evidence; the Cayman Economic Substance Return obligation applies regardless of turnover.

Economic substance scope: The Cayman Exempted Company is subject to the Cayman Economic Substance Law 2018: the email-infrastructure SaaS activity is classified as a relevant activity (distribution and service centre, headquarters or IP business), requiring Cayman-Islands direction and management, adequate employees, premises and expenditure, and an annual CIMA Economic Substance Return. In Gibraltar, the central-management-and-control test determines residence, while the Qualifying Company regime and the Gibraltar beneficial-ownership framework require demonstrated physical presence and real decision-making. The 2024 UK-EU Treaty on Gibraltar will progressively align Gibraltar with EU VAT and substance standards from 2025-2026, increasing scrutiny of letterbox arrangements.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Deliberate Under-Declaration

Statutory sanction: Assessment of the underpaid Gibraltar 12.5% corporate tax (or 25% where a UK permanent establishment is found), interest at 8% per annum, a civil penalty of up to 100% of the tax understated, confiscation orders, and criminal fines imposed by the Gibraltar courts.

Director personal liability: Directors and shadow directors face personal fines up to GBP 25,000, imprisonment up to 5 years, travel bans, and disqualification orders; the corporate veil is pierced on fraud or sham grounds, exposing shareholders and directors to civil restitution.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Cayman Economic Substance Law penalties: initial fine up to KYD 20,000 (approx. USD 24,000) plus KYD 2,000 per day of continuing default; Gibraltar re-characterisation as resident on central-management-and-control grounds triggers full 12.5% assessment; the company may be struck off; UK CFC rules under Part 9A TIOPA 2010 and the UK Diverted Profits Tax at 25% can apply to the misattributed profits.

Director personal liability: Cayman directors signing false Economic Substance Declarations commit an offence punishable by imprisonment up to 2 years and a fine of KYD 10,000; Gibraltar directors may be jointly and severally assessed, subjected to interim asset-freezing orders, and disqualified from acting as directors for up to 15 years.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: Arm's length adjustment increasing assessable Gibraltar profits; disallowance of the excessive recharges; penalties up to 100% of the additional tax; interest at 8% per annum; and application of the UK Diverted Profits Tax at 25% on the booking structure if the UK market-facing activity is artificially separated.

Director personal liability: Directors who certified false transfer pricing returns become personally liable for aggravated penalties of up to double the under-declared tax and may be subject to travel-restriction orders issued in Gibraltar tax enforcement proceedings.
Anti-Money Laundering and Payment Railing Breach

Statutory sanction: AML fines up to GBP 500,000 per contravention, daily default penalties, suspension of the Stripe merchant account, freezing of settlement funds by the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit, and parallel Cayman AML Act penalties against the Cayman-incorporated entity.

Director personal liability: Directors, money-laundering reporting officers and compliance officers face personal fines, imprisonment up to 7 years for money laundering, confiscation orders, director disqualification, and seizure of travel documents on a global arrest warrant.
Unauthorised Payment Services and E-Money Activities

Statutory sanction: Criminal offence under the Gibraltar Financial Services Act 2019 and Electronic Money Act: an unlimited fine and/or imprisonment up to 7 years; the underlying arrangements are void and unenforceable, so Stripe settlement proceeds cannot be lawfully retained and restitution is ordered to subscribers.

Director personal liability: Officers who knowingly operate the unlicensed e-money service are personally criminally liable, jointly and severally liable for subscriber restitution, subject to travel bans, and permanently barred from holding financial-services directorships in Gibraltar or the Crown Dependencies.
