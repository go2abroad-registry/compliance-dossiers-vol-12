Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar Tax & Customs Authority (GTCA), HM Government of Gibraltar; official portal: https://www.taxation.gi/

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity (e.g., a Cayman Exempted Company) must register with the GTCA within 30 days of establishing a taxable presence or permanent establishment in Gibraltar. File the Overseas Company Tax Registration Form (GTR-OV) accompanied by certified copies of the certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, board resolution appointing a Gibraltar-resident tax representative, and evidence of local activity or place of business. The GTCA will issue a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (GTRN). No VAT ID is issued because Gibraltar does not operate a general VAT/GST registration system for services.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign entity (e.g., a Cayman Exempted Company) must register with the GTCA within 30 days of establishing a taxable presence or permanent establishment in Gibraltar. File the Overseas Company Tax Registration Form (GTR-OV) accompanied by certified copies of the certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, board resolution appointing a Gibraltar-resident tax representative, and evidence of local activity or place of business. The GTCA will issue a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (GTRN). No VAT ID is issued because Gibraltar does not operate a general VAT/GST registration system for services.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a 'check-the-box' election. Hybrid mismatch rules are implemented via the Income Tax (Hybrid and other Mismatch) Act 2019, as amended, with an elective 'same country' exemption. A qualifying foreign entity may elect for treatment as a Gibraltar Qualifying Company under the Income Tax (Qualifying Companies) Regulations 2010, which permits a 0% effective tax rate on qualifying income upon payment of an annual exempt duty and meeting substance and reporting conditions.

Standard VAT / GST rate

GST

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in the conventional sense: Gibraltar does not levy a positive-rate GST/VAT on professional services. Marine insurance survey services supplied to non-resident underwriters, shipowners, or cargo interests are deemed outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect taxation; therefore no zero-rating claim, refund procedure, or fiscal credit recovery arises.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Gibraltar SAD)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT confirmation evidencing foreign currency payment

No real-time fiscal clearance, API concession, or digital stamping mandate exists in Gibraltar. Commercial invoices must satisfy the Invoice Content Requirements under the Customs and Consumer Protection Regulations and be retained for at least 6 years. E-invoicing is permitted where contractually agreed, with no prior fiscal authorization.

Although no export zero-rating refund is available, misdeclaration under the Customs Ordinance 1968 attracts a monetary penalty up to £10,000 per transaction, plus 200% of any excise duty evaded. Retrospective clawback of benefits previously claimed under the Qualifying Company or Exempt Company regimes (including 0% tax treatment) may be imposed for false declarations, late filing, or non-repatriation of receipts within the 180-day statutory deadline, with interest accruing at 1% per month on any amounts recoverable.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility on both current and capital accounts in Gibraltar, the United Kingdom, and the Cayman Islands; no exchange controls, no prior approval thresholds, and no surrender requirements. GIP is freely convertible 1:1 into GBP; USD and EUR accounts are freely permitted. A Cayman Exempted Company may remit dividends, interest, fees, and liquidation proceeds without withholding or conversion hurdles in Gibraltar. The marine insurance surveying fee stream is a current-account service export, fully convertible and freely repatriable. Practical friction is limited to documentary AML/EDD screening, not currency regulation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Gibraltar, the UK, and the Cayman Islands impose no statutory surrender ratio, no mandatory conversion timeline, and no forced repatriation window for export service proceeds, including marine survey fees and third-party claims disbursements. All proceeds may be retained in original currency, hedged freely, or repatriated without deadline, declaration, or penalty.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as banking supervisor; Government of Gibraltar Commissioner of Currency as sole issuer of the Gibraltar Pound (GIP) under a currency-board arrangement with 100% sterling backing; Bank of England as the de facto monetary anchor (GIP pegged at par to GBP).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium-High for Wise Business (the designated payment rail) and Global Reach (a GFSC-licensed Electronic Money Institution based in Gibraltar); Low for Revolut Business and Airwallex, which generally decline Cayman-domiciled exempted companies lacking local substance. Legal constraints: a Cayman Exempted Company is treated as an offshore legal arrangement by most EMI payment service rules, requiring CRS/FATCA registrations (Cayman DITC and IRS GIIN), a current Certificate of Good Standing, a Beneficial Ownership Register extract under the Cayman Beneficial Ownership Transparency Act 2023, and a Gibraltar-registered agent sponsor. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

No exchange-control approval is required in Gibraltar, the UK, or the Cayman Islands. Protocol: SWIFT MT103 or MT202 from the shareholder's corporate account to the receiving Gibraltar bank with remittance information clearly stating 'equity capital contribution / subscription for ordinary shares; no commercial goods or services'. USD-cleared injections route via the bank's New York correspondent (CHASUS33 or CITIUS33) with ABA routing and the same equity-contribution narrative; EUR injections may use TARGET2 or SEPA. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is required where aggregate cross-border controlled transactions exceed GIP 1,000,000 in the accounting period (indicative administrative threshold); Master File is required for multinational groups with consolidated annual revenue exceeding EUR 750,000,000; the Commissioner may demand documentation for any material transaction not at arm's length.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations 2019 apply to a marine insurance surveying business as an insurance-ancillary service centre and/or distribution and service centre. The Cayman tax-exempt status confers no relief. The company must prove: (i) boards of directors meeting and making strategic decisions in Gibraltar; (ii) qualified employees physically present in Gibraltar proportionate to the surveying activity; (iii) physical premises and adequate operating expenditure; and (iv) core income-generating activities (vessel and cargo inspection, claims assessment, report issuance) performed in Gibraltar. An annual ESR notification must be filed with the Gibraltar Companies Registry.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Filing

Statutory sanction: Penalty up to 200% of the understated tax; interest at 10% per annum; criminal fine of up to GIP 100,000 and imprisonment of up to seven years under the Income Tax Act 2010.

Director personal liability: Directors who consent, connive or act negligently are jointly and severally liable for penalties; they may be disqualified for up to 15 years, subjected to Supreme Court travel bans and asset freezes, and imprisoned upon conviction.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: First-year ESR penalty of GIP 10,000 and GIP 40,000 for each subsequent year; strike-off from the Gibraltar Companies Registry; exchange of information with the Cayman Islands and HMRC under the TIEA network; revocation of the Gibraltar Tax Residence Certificate.

Director personal liability: Directors and the Gibraltar resident agent face personal fines, disqualification, reporting to the Financial Intelligence Unit, and travel bans pending investigation; civil restitution may be ordered against directors for regulatory costs.
Transfer Pricing and Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: Arm's length re-assessment with an adjustment to taxable profits; penalty of up to 100% of the additional tax; interest at 10% per annum; the adjustment may be cross-reported to HMRC and Cayman authorities under exchange of information arrangements.

Director personal liability: Directors who approved non-arm's length pricing without adequate documentation are jointly liable for the surcharge; repeated breaches lead to disqualification and restriction from acting as directors of Gibraltar companies.
AML/CFT Breaches in Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Unlimited financial penalty and imprisonment of up to 14 years under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 (Gibraltar); regulatory fines imposed by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission; confiscation of funds under the Criminally Acquired Property Law; the company may be wound up.

Director personal liability: Individual officers are criminally liable as principal offenders or accessories; the Supreme Court can impose travel bans, passport surrender and asset freezes; civil asset recovery proceedings may attach personal assets.
Unauthorized Regulated Activity

Statutory sanction: Fine of up to GIP 500,000 and imprisonment of up to five years; confiscation of unlawfully derived premiums; the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission may petition for a winding-up of the company.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for restitution to policyholders and principals, are subject to disqualification, and may face travel restrictions pending the resolution of enforcement proceedings.
