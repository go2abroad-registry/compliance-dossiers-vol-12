Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — the Cayman Exempted Company holds a renewable Cayman Tax Exemption Certificate guaranteeing 0% Cayman income tax, capital gains tax, and withholding for renewable 20-year periods. In Gibraltar the company is prima facie non-resident and outside Gibraltar corporation tax; however, if it is managed and controlled from Gibraltar it becomes Gibraltar-resident and subject to 10% corporation tax on worldwide profits, mitigated only by the statutory Gross Income-Based cap.

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no US-style check-the-box regime. Available statutory elections and mechanisms: (1) the Gross Income-Based (GIB) cap election under the Income Tax Act 2010, under which tax is the lower of 10% of profits or the GIB cap (maximum 18% of gross assessable income); (2) election for non-resident treatment of a foreign-incorporated company that is not managed and controlled in Gibraltar and earns no Gibraltar-source income, preserving the Cayman zero-tax position; (3) grandfathered transitional protections for legacy Gibraltar exempt companies, where applicable; (4) election to align the Gibraltar accounting period with the ultimate parent for administrative consistency; (5) reliefs available under the UK-Gibraltar Double Taxation Agreement (2019) for permanent establishments, royalties, and other cross-border items; (6) no VAT-related election is possible because Gibraltar is outside the VAT system.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Import Duty (single-stage customs duty under the Customs and Excise Duties Ordinance; Gibraltar does not levy VAT, GST, or any general sales and use tax)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GIP 0 — no turnover threshold applies because Gibraltar imposes no VAT/GST; nevertheless, every economic operator importing or exporting goods must obtain a Gibraltar EORI number and register with H.M. Customs before the first transaction, and pharmaceutical API wholesalers must additionally hold a valid GDP/wholesale distribution licence from the Gibraltar Medicines Licensing Authority.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

VAT zero-rating is not applicable in Gibraltar (no VAT regime). The functional equivalent is full relief from Gibraltar import duty on exports: goods, including API consignments, exported from Gibraltar under customs control are outside the scope of duty, yielding an effective 0% export rate. For supplies into the UK/EU, VAT is triggered at destination under import VAT or reverse-charge rules. Offshore trading services supplied by the non-resident Cayman Exempted Company are sourced according to place of contract, risk, and performance; if the substance and delivery occur outside Gibraltar, the income is non-Gibraltar-source and taxed at 0% in both Gibraltar and Cayman. Any Gibraltar permanent establishment performing the wholesale activity would enter the 10% corporation tax net.

  • Gibraltar customs export declaration (SAD) lodged with H.M. Customs Gibraltar
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill / CMR evidence of physical export from Gibraltar
  • Commercial invoice with Incoterms 2020 and HS classification (Chapter 29, API codes 2933-2942)
  • Certificate of Pharmaceutical Product (CPP) issued by the Gibraltar Medicines Licensing Authority
  • Good Distribution Practice (GDP) licence reference of the Gibraltar wholesale entity
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 bank credit advice evidencing FX repatriation
  • Purchase order, packing list, insurance certificate, and proof of delivery at destination

Gibraltar imposes no mandatory e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping because there is no VAT. Customs clearance is electronic: import/export declarations must be lodged as Single Administrative Document (SAD) records with H.M. Customs Gibraltar via EDI/API or the authorised online customs portal; structured commercial invoices, packing lists, batch certificates, and transport documents must be digitally retained for audit.

H.M. Customs Gibraltar and the Income Tax Office audit exporter substance, HS-code accuracy, and physical export evidence. Fraudulent export or duty-relief arrangements — including misdescribed API classifications, fictitious exports, and false duty-drawback claims — trigger forfeiture of goods, customs penalties up to three times the duty evaded, criminal prosecution for fraudulent evasion (unlimited fine and imprisonment), retrospective clawback of any duty relief plus interest, revocation of the GDP licence and EORI number, extended audit records of 6-10 years, and automatic exchange of information with HMRC under the UK-Gibraltar Tax Co-operation Agreement and the OECD Multilateral Convention. For income tax, recharacterisation of profits as Gibraltar-source attracts 10% corporation tax plus penalties of up to 200% of the tax and default interest.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GBP is fully convertible under IMF Article VIII; neither the United Kingdom nor Gibraltar maintains exchange controls on current or capital account transactions. Profit remittances, dividend distributions, intercompany loans, and capital disposals may be executed without central bank approval. The sole operational hurdles arise from payment channel screening obligations under the Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018 (UK) and the Gibraltar Sanctions Act 2020, which may block or delay transfers involving designated persons, sanctioned jurisdictions, or unexplained wealth triggers.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory requirement exists in the UK or Gibraltar for exporters to surrender export proceeds to a central authority, convert receivables into GBP within a fixed timeline, or maintain mandatory FX balances. API wholesale receipts may be retained in USD, EUR, or any freely usable currency. Firms should nevertheless file accurate transaction records under UK international trade statistics (CDS) and comply with HMRC anti-avoidance transfer-pricing documentation where proceeds are concentrated in low-tax jurisdictions.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority for GBP and ultimate oversight of the UK banking system); HM Treasury (financial sanctions policy and licensing); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the integrated financial regulator supervising Gibraltar-licensed banks and electronic money institutions; the UK Prudential Regulation Authority (PRA) and Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) regulate the clearing banks engaged for sterling payment infrastructure.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: shareholder and intercompany capital injections from the Cayman parent or a UK ultimate parent are routed via SWIFT MT103 to the designated Gibraltar clearing bank account using ISO 20022 purpose code A0100 (capital injection) or 'INV' (direct investment), with the field-70 remittance line citing the board resolution and the Cayman registered capital entry. GBP injections from UK resident shareholders may clear via Faster Payments or CHAPS on the Bank of England infrastructure without FX conversion. EUR injections from EU corporate parents use SEPA Instant or SEPA Credit Transfer; for amounts exceeding EUR 10,000, originator and beneficiary identification must accompany the transfer pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2023/1113 (travel rule) and the Gibraltar AML/CFT framework. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: MEDIUM for Gibraltar-licensed electronic money institutions (supervised by the GFSC under the Financial Services Act 2019) which are acclimatised to offshore corporate vehicles; LOW for UK-based EMIs (Revolut Business, Wise Business, Airwallex, Payoneer, Currencycloud) which generally decline Cayman Exempted Companies engaged in high-risk pharmaceutical API wholesale without a material UK physical and economic nexus. Legal constraints: UK EMIs under the Electronic Money Regulations 2011 must apply equivalent customer due diligence to banks, and their risk policies classify (a) Cayman-domiciled non-resident entities and (b) pharma API wholesale as dual red flags; most prohibit the business. A hybrid structure of a full-service Gibraltar commercial bank plus a Gibraltar EMI ancillary account provides the only viable digital-banking overlay.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% flat rate of corporate income tax (Gibraltar Income Tax Act 2010, as amended), applicable to resident companies and to non-resident companies operating through a Gibraltar permanent establishment.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%; Gibraltar levies no withholding tax on dividends paid by a Gibraltar-resident or Gibraltar-branch company to any shareholder, and the Cayman exempted company is not subject to any Cayman dividend withholding tax.

The return for a chargeable period (the calendar tax year) must be filed within 12 months after the end of that period; a company with a 31 December year-end must file by 31 December of the following year. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File mandatory where annual controlled transactions of the Gibraltar enterprise exceed GIP 1,000,000; Master File where the multinational group's consolidated turnover exceeds GIP 60,000,000; Country-by-Country Report where consolidated group revenue equals or exceeds EUR 750,000,000 (approximately GIP 650,000,000). Documentation must be contemporaneous and available to the Income Tax Office within 6 months of the year-end.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations 2019 fully apply: pharmaceutical API wholesale distribution is a 'distribution services' relevant activity, and the Cayman exempted company is also caught by the Cayman Islands Economic Substance Regime 2018. The entity must be managed and directed from Gibraltar, engage adequate full-time staff, maintain physical premises, and incur minimum operating expenditure proportionate to the distribution activity; an annual economic substance return must be filed in Gibraltar (and the parallel Cayman annual ESR filing made unless the entity declares Gibraltar tax residence). Demonstrating mere Cayman incorporation relief is insufficient to escape either regime.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Misstatement

Statutory sanction: Civil: tax-geared penalty of up to 100% of the tax understated (200% if deliberate), plus interest and daily continued penalties. Criminal: on summary conviction, a fine of up to GIP 5,000 and/or imprisonment for up to 2 years; on indictment, an unlimited fine and imprisonment for up to 5 years.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or cause the false return are jointly and severally liable for the penalties; courts may order restitution of the evaded tax, freeze personal and corporate assets, impose travel bans pending recovery, and disqualify directors from managing Gibraltar companies; personal imprisonment attaches to the individual, not the corporate shell.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar ESR: initial penalty of GIP 10,000 and GIP 500 per day of continuing failure; on second consecutive failure, penalties up to GIP 150,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years; Cayman ESR provides parallel penalties of KYD 12,800 per year and imprisonment of up to 2 years.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners are personally liable for ESR penalties and costs; the Commissioner may strike off the company, revoke its Gibraltar tax registration, disclose the beneficial owners, and seek travel bans and asset freezes as debt enforcement measures.
AML / Money Laundering Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and AML/CFT framework: on conviction for money laundering, an unlimited fine and/or imprisonment up to 14 years; regulatory sanctions from the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission may reach GIP 10,000,000; parallel Cayman AML enforcement (CIMA) applies to the exempted company.

Director personal liability: Directors, the appointed Gibraltar MLRO and the Cayman officers face individual criminal exposure, disqualification, asset freezing orders and travel bans; the corporate veil is pierced where the shell was used to move illicit proceeds, allowing recovery from personal estates.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Income Tax Office adjustment of the taxable profits on an arm's-length basis, interest on the underpayment, and a civil penalty of up to 100% of the additional tax; deliberate or fraudulent arrangements are punishable by criminal fines and imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Directors approving false intercompany pricing and TP documentation are jointly liable with the company; secondary adjustments may be imputed to the Cayman parent; disqualification orders and personal recovery orders follow in the case of culpable conduct.
