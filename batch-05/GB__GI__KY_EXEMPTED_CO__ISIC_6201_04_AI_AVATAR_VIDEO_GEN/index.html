Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar — Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax); official e-services and guidance portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation.

E-government filing portal

A Cayman Exempted Company is treated as an overseas company for Gibraltar registration purposes. The foreign entity must first register with the Registrar of Companies under the Gibraltar Companies Act 2014 as an overseas company (appointing a Gibraltar registered office and a local authorised agent), then file the Income Tax application for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN) with the Income Tax Office. The file must include the Cayman Certificate of Incorporation, the Certificate of Good Standing issued by the Cayman Registrar of Companies, constitutional documents, director/shareholder register, declaration of central management and control, and AML/KYC identification of beneficial owners; the TRN is issued upon a satisfactory substance and residence assessment.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: as a Cayman Exempted Company the entity holds Cayman exempt status free of income tax; in Gibraltar classification is separate and residence-based. If the board of directors passes resolutions and exercises senior management control in Gibraltar, the company will be classified Entity_Level_Taxation — resident and taxable at the 12.5% Gibraltar corporate rate on trading profits with worldwide territorial scope consistent with OECD practice; otherwise, if control remains in Cayman and only a sales presence exists in Gibraltar, the company is non-resident but remains exposed to Gibraltar corporation tax on Gibraltar-source income attributable to a permanent establishment.

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not offer a check-the-box pass-through regime. Available statutory elections: (1) Qualifying Company (QC) election — available to a non-resident company with exclusively foreign-source income, introducing a deemed gross-margin charge rather than an ordinary accounting profit basis, subject to OECD Substance Requirement compliance; (2) group relief election to surrender losses within a Gibraltar tax-consortium group; (3) administrative advance ruling pathway whereby the Commissioner may certify non-residence based on board-meeting minutes, staff locations, and closed management records held outside Gibraltar.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

VAT/GST is not levied in Gibraltar. The applicable indirect tax architecture is Import Duty and Excise Duty under the Customs and Excise Duties Act, Business Property Duty (on occupational premises), and Insurance Premium Duty. Cross-border digital services (AI video-generation SaaS performed abroad) fall outside all Gibraltar indirect tax heads of charge.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No Gibraltar VAT registration is possible because no VAT regime exists; no local economic-turnover threshold is therefore applicable. Import Duty applies to physical goods entering Gibraltar irrespective of monetary threshold. The Cayman entity's digital SaaS export has zero indirect tax liability in Gibraltar. Separate UK VAT registration may be triggered where UK B2C digital supplies exceed the GBP 85,000 threshold (HMRC Making Tax Digital e-return obligations then apply).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating per se is not available as Gibraltar has no VAT. Instead, the AI avatar video-generation SaaS qualifies for a complete statutory exclusion from Gibraltar Import Duty as an offshore services supply. The claim requires demonstrable export substance: contracts concluded outside Gibraltar or via a non-Gibraltar payment processor, software hosted on non-Gibraltar cloud infrastructure, and no handover or performance occurring in Gibraltar customs territory.

  • Gibraltar Customs Export Declaration for movement of any physical servers/hardware plus a service-export statement for digital SaaS deliverables
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill for physical exports, or cloud hosting / CDN access logs, signed AI-SaaS subscription agreements and Stripe/Paddle payout records for digital supplies
  • Inward Remittance Certificate from a Gibraltar-licensed bank identifying the counterparty, invoice reference, and currency settlement
  • Intercompany services agreement and master file evidencing OECD-aligned transfer pricing between the Cayman entity and any UK/EU affiliates

Gibraltar imposes no real-time fiscal clearance, API clearance, digital stamping, or B2B/B2C e-invoicing network mandate. Issuance of commercial invoices in electronic form is unrestricted; invoices must satisfy the Income Tax Act content requirements (date, unique number, supplier/recipient identification, TRN, currency). Retention in an accessible e-archive for six years is required, with periodic Income Tax risk-based assessment audits able to require invoice-level data extracts via Gibraltar Regulatory Authority data-sharing powers.

Export-relief claims and offshore routing are reviewed by the Income Tax Office under the UK-Gibraltar Exchange of Information Agreements. A failed substantiation results in an upward assessment of the full AI SaaS turnover at 12.5% Gibraltar tax plus (i) penalty for careless inaccuracy of up to 30% of underpaid tax, (ii) fraud penalty of up to 100% of underpaid tax, (iii) statutory late-payment interest from the original due date, and (iv) unfettered six-year reopening of assessments, extended indefinitely if fraud is proven. Directors risk disqualification orders under the Companies Act 2014 for false documentary certification.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Cayman Islands maintains fully unrestricted current-account and capital-account convertibility. The KYD is pegged to the United States dollar at the statutory rate of 1.00 KYD = 1.20 USD (equivalently, USD 1.00 = KYD 0.8333); US dollars circulate freely and are accepted in commerce. There are no restrictions on dividend distributions, profit remittances, royalty payments, intercompany loans, or liquidation proceeds payable to the Gibraltar parent or any foreign shareholder, and no CIMA permission or exchange-control consent is required. The Gibraltar leg is equally liberal: the Gibraltar pound (GIP) is pegged at par to sterling and capital moves freely between Gibraltar, the United Kingdom, and the rest of the world. For the United Kingdom sovereign overlay, the Exchange Control Act 1947 has been effectively repealed since 1979, so no Bank of England, FCA, or HMRC approval is required for UK investors funding the structure. The operative constraints are bank-level rather than regulatory: source-of-funds verification, Cayman economic-substance evidence under the International Tax Co-operation (Economic Substance) Act, and OFAC, EU, and UN sanctions screening at correspondent banks in New York or London.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Neither the Cayman Islands nor Gibraltar imposes any statutory timeline for repatriating export proceeds, any mandatory conversion ratio, or any surrender obligation in respect of foreign-currency receipts. A Cayman exempted company carrying on AI-avatar-video SaaS business may retain 100% of its Stripe and Paddle settlement proceeds, subscription revenue, and other export receipts in USD, EUR, GBP, or any freely convertible currency, and may hold such proceeds offshore indefinitely without CIMA notification. Correspondent banks in the United States and United Kingdom will, however, apply OFAC and financial-sanctions screening, and wire transfers must carry complete originator and beneficiary information in accordance with FATF Recommendation 16.

Central bank supervision: Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA) — the monetary authority, currency board, and financial services regulator of the Cayman Islands, responsible for issuing and backing the Cayman Islands dollar (KYD); for the Gibraltar treasury leg, the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) under the Financial Services (Banking) Act, together with Her Majesty's Government of Gibraltar Treasury. Neither territory operates a conventional central bank with exchange-control powers; CIMA administers monetary stability but does not impose foreign-exchange controls.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Gibraltar Corporation Tax Fraud

Statutory sanction: Under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act and criminal law, an unlimited fine on indictment and imprisonment of up to seven years; civil penalties up to 200% of the underpaid tax, plus statutory interest and freezing of payment processor accounts.

Director personal liability: Directors and the ultimate beneficial owner can be made jointly and severally liable for the evaded tax; the court may pierce the Cayman corporate veil, issue travel bans, disqualify directors, and impose personal confiscation orders.
Economic Substance Violation / Shell Company Abuse

Statutory sanction: Cayman Economic Substance Law penalties: US$10,000 for an initial ESR violation, US$10,000 for each subsequent six-month period of default, with a maximum penalty of US$500,000; Gibraltar ESR Act penalties include substantial fines, default daily penalties, and cancellation of the Gibraltar permanent establishment registration.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for ESR filings and can be disqualified for up to ten years in Gibraltar and five years in Cayman; personal asset restraint orders and director travel bans are available where the company is a pure shell used to shelter SaaS revenue.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Filing Failure

Statutory sanction: Under the Cayman Proceeds of Crime Act and AML Regulations, penalties include fines, imprisonment of up to two years on summary conviction and five years on indictment; Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act sanctions include up to seven years' imprisonment and unrestricted fines for laundering arrangements or false beneficial ownership declarations.

Director personal liability: The nominated officer and each director are personally liable for a false beneficial ownership register and for failure to file suspicious activity reports; travel bans, extradition to the United Kingdom or United States, and personal confiscation orders are available.
Transfer Pricing / Profit Shifting Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar tax authorities may adjust chargeable profits under OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines and impose penalties ranging from 10% of the adjustment to 100% of the understated tax in serious non-disclosure cases, plus daily default fines; Cayman ESR penalties up to US$500,000 also apply for an uncorrected false return.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign the Gibraltar return without contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation are personally liable for negligent certification; false transfer pricing declarations trigger criminal fraud liability, director disqualification, and if the Cayman company is treated as a sham, joint and several liability to third-party creditors.
