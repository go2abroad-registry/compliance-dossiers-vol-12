E-government filing portal

A Cayman Exempted Company acting as a captive reinsurance SPV that establishes a Gibraltar presence must file a company registration application with the Income Tax Office prior to commencing taxable activity, submitting certified constitutional documents, certificate of incorporation, Gibraltar registered office details, director and member information, and a completed company registration form. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not maintain a US-style check-the-box or pass-through election. Classification follows the Income Tax Act by operation of law. A foreign company may elect to become resident in Gibraltar by placing central management and control in Gibraltar; otherwise it remains non-resident and is taxed only on Gibraltar-source income. Hybrid mismatch rules (ATAD-aligned) apply mandatorily; the former Gibraltar Exempt/Qualifying Company election is closed to new entrants and grandfathering has expired.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT or GST (Gibraltar has no general consumption tax; the indirect tax framework consists of customs duties, excise duties, stamp duties, and the Financial Services Levy applicable to licensed insurers)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST registration threshold exists in Gibraltar. For import/export purposes, any person shipping goods must obtain a Gibraltar/UK EORI number from HM Customs, irrespective of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable as a VAT export zero-rating claim because Gibraltar does not levy VAT. Exports of goods fall outside the scope of any Gibraltar consumption tax; in the customs duty context, export relief is available on evidence that goods have physically left Gibraltar under the Customs and Excise Act.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic SAD/EXS filed through the Gibraltar Customs system)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank SWIFT MT103 evidencing receipt of foreign premium and FX repatriation
  • For captive reinsurance services: executed reinsurance agreement, underwriting file, risk premium ledger, and GFSC annual return

Gibraltar does not impose real-time fiscal e-invoicing, digital stamping, or mandatory clearance-API requirements. Customs declarations are submitted electronically through the Gibraltar customs electronic system under the UK-Gibraltar customs arrangements; e-invoicing is permitted but not mandated.

False declarations made to obtain customs duty export relief are subject to penalties up to treble the duty evaded, forfeiture of goods and conveyances, and criminal prosecution for fraudulent evasion. Because Gibraltar has no VAT, there is no VAT zero-rating clawback mechanism; however, the Income Tax Office may issue discovery assessments without time limit in cases of fraud or wilful default and seek retrospective adjustment where export or premium evidence is falsified.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound is freely convertible at a fixed 1:1 parity with British sterling; there are no exchange controls, no foreign-exchange licences, and no restrictions on current-account or capital-account movements. Captive reinsurers may receive premiums, dividends, and shareholder loans in any freely convertible currency, hold reserve assets offshore, and remit profits, dividends, and capital to non-resident parents without prior official approval. The only material constraints are the enhanced AML/CTF verification expectations of the receiving correspondent banks and the FSC's economic-substance/monitoring regime applicable to insurance undertakings.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender rule, mandatory conversion ratio, or disposal timeline applies in Gibraltar or in the United Kingdom for captive reinsurance income. Inward reinsurance premiums are treated as current-account receipts and may be retained in the original contractual currency. However, receipts exceeding the designated reporting threshold must be captured under the FSC/RGPF AML reporting framework and reflected in the captive's annual statutory insurance return.

Central bank supervision: Financial Services Commission (FSC) of Gibraltar, acting with the Currency Notes Authority of HM Government of Gibraltar for currency issue; Bank of England and HM Treasury (United Kingdom) exercise monetary and sterling convertibility functions for the Gibraltar pound (GI£), which has no independent central bank.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-Medium as a primary banking relationship; acceptable only as a supplemental treasury corridor. Regulated digital EMI institutions licensed in the UK or Gibraltar (cases such as clearing products offered under the UK Electronic Money Regulations 2011 or the Gibraltar Financial Services (E-Money) regime) can provide fast B2B payment initiation and low-value settlement. However, a captive reinsurer writing third-party risk is expected to maintain arm's-length insurance reserves in an FSC-regulated bank, not in an unbanked or offshore EMI; most EMIs have strict transaction caps and are not prudentially suitable for reinsurance premium receivables or loss-claims disbursement. Use EMIs only for payment acceleration, not as a repository of underwriting reserves.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound capital and reinsurance-premium injections are routed by SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 COV at a correspondent-bank level) from the Cayman Exempted Company's own regulated bank to the Gibraltar captive's settlement account. The remittance instruction must satisfy SWIFT screening by citing the captive's FSC reference, the insurance policy number, the period of risk, and a purpose code that distinguishes financial-capital movement from goods/services payment. Approved purpose labels are: 'FI-INS' for reinsurance premiums and claims bordereau; 'CF-CAP' for paid-up share capital and additional paid-in capital; and 'LD-RES' for shareholder loans/loss-reserve funding. The receiving bank must obtain the signed reinsurance slip, premium invoice/debit note, and FSC annual premium-recognition statement to release funds; no exchange-control approval is required, and funds may be held in any permitted currency.Outbound disbursements for accumulated claims, commissions, ceding exchanges, and settlement of reinsurance recoveries are cleared via SWIFT MT103 bilateral credit transfers when counterparties are not within the same clearing system; for exceptionally high-value treaty settlements, or where a counterparty's finance office requires documentary confirmation, a commercial letter of credit (SWIFT MT700, governed by UCP600) may be placed on a standby basis. Local sterling and Gibraltar-pound settlement is executed over CHAPS same-day gross settlement or the UK Faster Payment Service; euro-denominated disbursements can be cleared using TARGET2 or cross-currency payments via the bank's nostro network.
