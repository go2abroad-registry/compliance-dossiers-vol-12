Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

A Cayman Exempted Company entering Gibraltar to act as fiduciary administrator must first appoint a Gibraltar-resident company secretary/agent and maintain a registered office address. The agent files Form 'Overseas Company Registration' with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies, then submits an application for tax registration (Form IT-1) to the Gibraltar Income Tax Office. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope – Non-resident entity subject to Gibraltar tax only on Gibraltar-source income; central management and control in the Cayman Islands is respected if board meetings, fiduciary decisions, and trust administration occur outside Gibraltar. A Cayman Exempted Company itself enjoys tax-exempt status in the Cayman Islands, but as a Gibraltar-registered overseas company it remains assessable in Gibraltar on certain local-source profits at 12.5%.

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate check-the-box or common-law hybrid entity elections. Available statutory mechanics include group relief elections for resident-group losses, capital allowances elections on fixed assets, double taxation relief treaty elections, and the 'portfolio income exclusion' under the Income Tax Act for qualifying offshore fund and trust activities. Hybrid mismatch adjustments under OECD BEPS are not available as elective regimes; Gibraltar applies specific anti-hybrid rules in conformity with UK-aligned standards.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST; Gibraltar operates import and consumption duties under the Customs Duties Act and a limited Purchase Tax on specified goods

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT registration threshold exists because Gibraltar is outside the European Union VAT area. No general indirect tax registration is required for trust administration services. Businesses importing goods into Gibraltar are registered as 'dealers' with HM Customs and are liable to import duty on a transaction-specific basis.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable to Gibraltar domestic law: there is no VAT/goods and services tax, and accordingly no 0% export VAT zero-rating regime. For direct tax purposes, fiduciary administration and associated services provided to overseas trusts are classified as offshore income and are generally excluded from Gibraltar assessment, provided the services are performed wholly outside Gibraltar and all settlement funds originate and remain outside Gibraltar.

  • Not applicable for pure fiduciary services; for any goods consigned through Gibraltar: Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document), Bill of Lading or Air Waybill, commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, and Inward Remittance Certificate proving overseas settlement
  • For trust services: executed trust deed, client due diligence file, fee invoice and overseas SWIFT wire confirmation showing repatriation of trustee fees to a Gibraltar or Cayman bank account

Gibraltar imposes no real-time fiscal clearance, no government e-invoicing API, and no digital stamping. Taxpayers and regulated trustees must retain invoices under the Companies Act and AML/CFT legislation for minimum record-keeping periods; electronic invoicing is permissible but voluntary.

Misclassification of export or offshore trust income to exploit Gibraltar territoriality or to claim unwarranted relief is treated as fraudulent tax evasion. Penalties under the Income Tax Act and Customs Duties Act include additional assessments with interest, civil penalties up to 150% of the tax avoided, public disgorgement orders, and potential criminal liability for responsible directors and trust officers (fines and imprisonment). Where governmental grants or exemptions have been obtained by false statements, the authorities may claw back all benefits plus statutory interest without limitation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound is pegged at 1:1 to British pound sterling and is fully convertible with zero friction. No exchange control legislation applies in either Gibraltar or the Cayman Islands for current or capital account transactions. The Cayman Exempted Company, being tax-exempt in its home jurisdiction, faces no profit remittance tax, dividend withholding, or interest repatriation restriction. Gibraltar applies a territorial basis of taxation, but provided the trust administration activities are properly licensed and substantiated in Gibraltar under the Financial Services Act 2019, no remittance hurdle arises for discretionary distributions to beneficiaries. The sole material constraint is the statutory licensing perimeter: fiduciary administration of an irrevocable offshore spendthrift trust under ISIC 6430 is a regulated activity requiring GFSC authorisation, full AML/CFT policies, and robust economic substance in Gibraltar.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Zero statutory surrender or conversion obligation. Neither Gibraltar nor the Cayman Islands imposes any mandatory timeline, percentage, or designated-account requirement for the repatriation or conversion of export or trade receipts. The trustee may hold settlement funds, trade proceeds, and trust distributions in GBP, EUR, USD, or any freely convertible currency without central bank approval. Best practice, however, requires all external receipts to be routed through the segregated Fiduciary Trust Account at a GFSC-regulated bank before any onward disbursement, so that source-of-funds verification is demonstrable to the controlling AML/CFT supervisor.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as principal financial regulator; Bank of England as the monetary authority providing sterling backing for the Gibraltar pound (GIP) at absolute parity; HM Government of Gibraltar as issuer of GIP banknotes and coinage.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Medium-to-Low direct viability; High auxiliary viability if layered behind a primary licensed bank. UK FCA-authorised e-money institutions (Revolut Business, Wise, Currencycloud) will generally decline or place extraordinarily high EDD thresholds on a High_Risk_Special_License entity engaged in irrevocable spendthrift trust administration because the product is a regulated fiduciary service, not a pure e-money flow. A GFSC-licensed PSP or EMI operating under the Financial Services Act 2019 is viable for subordinated collection and disbursement rails, but it cannot substitute for the primary transaction account at a bank with correspondent clearing access. Fully digital offshore EMIs without a physical regulated nexus in Gibraltar or the UK are not viable for this structure.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

First settlement contributions from the settlor must be remitted exclusively by SWIFT MT103 from the settlor's own bank account, with the reason for payment coded as a trust capital injection under the UK/Gibraltar BoP classification for private transfers or an ISO 20022 purpose code 'TRUST'; no cash, cryptocurrency, bearer instrument, or third-party funding is acceptable. For GBP sterling injections within the UK clearing system, CHAPS is the compliant rail; EUR injections move via TARGET2; USD injections use the SWIFT USD correspondent network. The receiving Gibraltar bank will hold funds in a segregated Fiduciary Trust Account and requires the trustee to submit the trust deed extract, UBO register, and an EDD confirmation before crediting. All inbound credits must be reconciled to the settlement schedule of the trust deed and recorded in the trust ledger with full audit integrity.Outbound disbursements are executed via SWIFT MT103 for beneficiary wires and MT202/MT205 for bank-to-bank cover where the trust maintains correspondent relationships. Commercial Letters of Credit are issued and confirmed under UCP 600 rules through Jyske Bank (Gibraltar) Ltd or Gibraltar International Bank PLC for trade-finance-facing distribution flows. GBP time-critical payments use CHAPS; EUR disbursements to EEA beneficiaries use SEPA or TARGET2; USD payments route via Fedwire through the interbank correspondent. Every outbound leg must be matched to either a trust deed distribution schedule, an invoice, or a beneficiaries' entitlement computation, and must pass OFSI/UN sanctions screening, FX conversion margin checks, and the GFSC-mandated transaction monitoring threshold before release.Outright foreign exchange forward contracts (tenor up to 24 months) with GFSC-regulated banks or prime counterparties, used for locking conversion rates on known contribution and distribution flows.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for emerging-market or restricted-convertibility currency exposures forming a documented part of the trust corpus or beneficiary liabilities.Cross-currency swaps (XCCY) executed under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement for structural rebalancing of GBP, EUR, and USD trust liabilities over multi-year distribution periods.European-style vanilla FX call and put options restricted exclusively to hedging known disbursement obligations, prohibited for speculative trading under the trustee's fiduciary duty.FX swap transactions for temporary liquidity rebalancing between Fiduciary Trust Account currencies, each leg subject to MT103/MT202 messaging and full collateral documentation.Cash-settled forwards on gold or index baskets if permitted by the trust deed as a hedge against inflation affecting the spendthrift protection corpus.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

A company tax return must be filed within nine months of the accounting period end. For a standard 31 December year-end, the statutory filing date is 30 September of the following calendar year. Provisional tax is payable by estimated instalments as directed in the annual Assessment Notice.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 10,000,000

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar's Economic Substance (Requirements for Entities) Regulations 2019 require a tax-resident entity carrying on a relevant activity, including fiduciary administration and investment fund or asset management, to demonstrate core income generating activities in Gibraltar. For a discretionary asset protection trust administered by a foreign exempted company resident in Gibraltar, the entity must maintain a physical office, have qualified employees proportionate to the trust portfolio, hold board meetings in Gibraltar at adequate frequency, keep all accounting records in Gibraltar, and ensure all strategic investment and distribution decisions are made by Gibraltar-resident directors or senior managers. A pure equity holding entity is subject to reduced substance but must still comply with filing and record-keeping duties. Failure to meet the economic substance test results in a financial penalty, the exchange of information with foreign tax authorities, and possible strike-off of the company.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion via False Non-Residence Declaration

Statutory sanction: Under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act, the penalty may reach 200% of the tax undercharged, plus interest at the monthly rate of 1.5% and an additional fine up to GIP 100,000. Criminal conviction for tax evasion can result in imprisonment of up to seven years and confiscation of the proceeds of the trust under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign false residence declarations or permit the company to be managed from Gibraltar while filing as non-resident face personal liability for the unpaid corporate tax, civil restitution to the trust beneficiaries, a travel ban, and disqualification under the Companies Act. In aggravated cases, directors may be prosecuted personally and sentenced to imprisonment.
Shell Company / Insufficient Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The Economic Substance Regulations impose escalating financial penalties from GIP 10,000 to GIP 500,000 for repeated non-compliance. The Gibraltar Commissioner may strike the company off the register, notify the beneficial owners' states through the exchange of information mechanism, and require the surrender of the trust administration licence held by the corporate trustee.

Director personal liability: Directors of a corporate trustee that holds itself out as an asset protection administrator without real substance are jointly and severally liable for penalties; they can be barred from serving as directors or licensed trust service providers for up to ten years and may be subject to an asset freezing order pending investigation.
AML / CFT Breach by Fiduciary

Statutory sanction: Under the Gibraltar Financial Services (Trust and Company Service Providers) Act and the AML/CFT framework, the Financial Intelligence Unit can impose fines of up to GIP 500,000, revoke the fiduciary licensing, and restrain all trust assets. Criminal money laundering carries a maximum term of 14 years' imprisonment and an unlimited fine.

Director personal liability: Directors and the nominated MLRO face personal criminal liability for each suspicious transaction not disclosed. They are also liable in civil proceedings for the full amount distributed to beneficiaries in breach of the trust deed and for any loss suffered by creditors, with travel bans and passport confiscation during investigation.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner may adjust assessable income upward to the arm's length amount, levy a penalty of 100% of the additional tax, and impose a separate documentation penalty of up to GIP 50,000. Continuous non-compliance is treated as an aggravating factor in the economic substance review.

Director personal liability: Directors who cause the company to omit transfer pricing documents or who knowingly approve a non-arm's length fee arrangement are jointly liable for the resulting tax shortfall and may be disqualified from holding any fiduciary position. If the mispricing is used to conceal trust distributions, directors may also be prosecuted for fraud.
