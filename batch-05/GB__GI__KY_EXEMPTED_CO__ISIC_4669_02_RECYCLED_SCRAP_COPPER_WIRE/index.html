Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (Commissioner of Income Tax) for direct taxes; HM Customs of Gibraltar for indirect/customs taxes. Filing portals: Gibraltar Online Services System (GOSS) and Customs Declaration Service (CDS).

E-government filing portal

A Cayman Exempted Company is a foreign legal person; it is not automatically registered in Gibraltar. If it acquires Gibraltar tax nexus (central management and control, permanent establishment, or Gibraltar-source income), it must file Form TN-1 with the Commissioner of Income Tax within 21 days to obtain a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN), and must appoint a fiscal agent resident in Gibraltar if it cannot be represented. For cross-border goods movements, it must apply separately to HM Customs for a Gibraltar EORI number (GI prefix) through CDS before filing any export/import declaration. Because Gibraltar is outside the EU VAT area, no VAT registration ID is issued.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A Cayman Exempted Company is a foreign legal person; it is not automatically registered in Gibraltar. If it acquires Gibraltar tax nexus (central management and control, permanent establishment, or Gibraltar-source income), it must file Form TN-1 with the Commissioner of Income Tax within 21 days to obtain a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN), and must appoint a fiscal agent resident in Gibraltar if it cannot be represented. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

The company may elect under section 50 of the Cayman Companies Act for a 20-year Tax Exemption Certificate, preserving its tax-exempt-zone status. For Gibraltar direct tax, the company may elect to be assessed as a non-resident company under section 6 of the Income Tax Act if central management and control is outside Gibraltar, filing Form 5 with proof of place of management. Check-the-box elections are unavailable under US Treasury Regulation § 301.7701-2(b)(8)(i), as a Cayman Exempted Company is a per se corporation; hybrid mismatch elections are not separately granted by Gibraltar law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GIP 0 — no annual turnover threshold; any person importing goods into, or supplying chargeable goods/services within, Gibraltar must register for Consumer Tax with HM Customs. Purely export transactions are outside the charge and do not trigger registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The 0% rate applies where physically exported goods (including shredded recycled copper wire) are removed from Gibraltar to a destination outside the EU/Gibraltar customs union area within 3 months of despatch; the exporter must hold proof of export and the goods must not have been released for free circulation in Gibraltar. Offshore services supplied to a non-Gibraltar recipient whose direct benefit is enjoyed abroad are also zero-rated. All zero-rating claims require timely filing of a Consumer Tax return using export data from CDS.

  • Customs Export Declaration (C88/SAD)
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Weight/Scale Certificate
  • Cayman Exempted Company Tax Exemption Certificate (if challenged)

No mandatory e-invoicing, API fiscal clearance, digital stamping or transaction-control regime is enacted. Customs and tax declarations are lodged electronically via CDS and GOSS; no real-time government pre-approval of invoices is required.

HM Customs may audit zero-rating claims up to 6 years after export. If the required export/proof-of-payment documentation is not produced on first audit demand or before repatriation of sale proceeds, the supply is reclassified as taxable in Gibraltar; Consumer Tax becomes payable at 10%, plus default interest of 1% per month and a penalty of up to 100% of the tax evaded. Fraudulent zero-rating (e.g. forged bills of lading or customs declarations) is a criminal offence under the Customs Duties Act, carrying fines of up to GIP 50,000 per invoice, seizure of goods, and revocation of the exporter's customs/consumer tax registration.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Both current-account and capital-account transactions are fully convertible into Sterling, US Dollar, Euro and other major currencies. No statutory FX licence is required for a Cayman Exempted Company operating from Gibraltar. Profit remittance to the Cayman parent is permitted without conversion hurdle, but the corporate bank will require an evidentiary source-of-funds chain and proof of beneficial ownership to satisfy Gibraltar's anti-money-laundering (AML) legislation; there is no statutory limitation on remittance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Gibraltar has not imposed exchange-control or export-proceeds surrender requirements since the 1979 abolition, and no mandatory conversion ratio applies. Export receipts may be retained in the currency of collection.

Central bank supervision: Her Majesty's Treasury (HM Treasury) and the Bank of England for Sterling; the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) for regulated banking and foreign-exchange intermediation. Gibraltar has no separate central bank, and the Government of Gibraltar issues its own currency notes under the Currency Note Act, with residual exchange-control delegation from HM Treasury.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Medium. Gibraltar's Fintech Policy incorporates EU PSD2 and enables GFSC-licensed Electronic Money Institutions to open accounts for Cayman-incorporated clients. However, a non-EU/offshore digital EMI without a Gibraltar licence is prohibited from rendering payment or e-money services to Gibraltar-based entities; and for a physically-goods scrap-copper trader, most virtual banks will decline onboarding absent a consolidated physical-banking relationship.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections from the Cayman parent must be settled via SWIFT MT103 or MT202 COV with an appropriate BOPS/ISO 20022 purpose code (e.g., Share Capital Contribution or Shareholder Loan). GBP funds clear same-day through CHAPS, EUR through TARGET2 via SEPA, and USD through Fedwire/CHIPS.
