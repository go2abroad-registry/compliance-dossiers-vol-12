Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed for the exempted company. The company must issue at least one share with a par value, and the government capital duty is nil where the authorised share capital does not exceed USD 50,000. Subscribed funds need not be escrowed under statute, but the subscription proceeds must be credited to the corporate account or the registered agent's client account immediately following the board resolution.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar Income Tax Office, Ministry for Finance, Government of Gibraltar. Tax registrations, taxpayer reference numbers, returns, and related correspondence are administered through the Gibraltar Tax Centre and applicable official tax-service channels.

E-government filing portal

A Cayman exempted company must determine whether it is carrying on a trade, business, or other taxable activity in Gibraltar, has a permanent establishment, employs personnel, owns or leases business premises, or is otherwise within Gibraltar tax filing requirements. The company should first register any Gibraltar branch or foreign company presence with the Gibraltar Companies Registry where required under the Companies Act. It should then submit the prescribed tax registration application to the Gibraltar Income Tax Office, together with the certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, certificate of good standing or equivalent, registered-office and principal-business details, beneficial ownership information, director and authorised-agent details, business activity description, accounting period, expected Gibraltar-source income, and evidence of Gibraltar operations. The Income Tax Office issues a Gibraltar taxpayer reference or tax account number following acceptance. A separate VAT ID is not available because Gibraltar does not operate a general VAT or GST system. Customs registrations, including an Economic Operator Registration and Identification number where applicable for customs activity, are obtained separately through Gibraltar Customs. Registration should be completed before commencing taxable Gibraltar operations or the relevant filing deadline, and changes in legal ownership, management, address, activities, or tax status must be notified promptly.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not generally provide a United States-style check-the-box election for entity classification. A Cayman exempted company is ordinarily treated according to its legal form and the factual basis of its Gibraltar tax residence, Gibraltar permanent establishment, and Gibraltar-source income. Tax residence is determined principally by management and control and applicable statutory rules; incorporation in the Cayman Islands does not itself create a Gibraltar tax exemption. No general hybrid-mismatch election converts the company into a transparent or pass-through entity. Any treaty, exemption, group relief, or special-regime treatment must be established under the applicable Gibraltar legislation and factual conditions.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No general VAT or GST system. Gibraltar applies customs duties, import controls, and other sector-specific charges; Gibraltar is outside the United Kingdom VAT regime and the European Union VAT territory.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable. Gibraltar has no general VAT or GST registration threshold. Customs registration and import documentation may nevertheless be required for importing titanium, machinery, medical-device components, or finished orthopedic implants. Product-specific import duties, customs treatment, and licensing requirements must be determined by tariff classification and origin.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A Gibraltar export does not qualify for a Gibraltar 0% VAT rate because Gibraltar has no general VAT system. Exportation may remove goods from Gibraltar customs territory and may prevent Gibraltar import or domestic consumption charges from arising, subject to the applicable customs procedure. For titanium orthopedic implants, the exporter should establish that the goods physically leave Gibraltar, are correctly classified, satisfy any medical-device, customs, origin, and export-control requirements, and are supported by contemporaneous commercial and transport records. Offshore services are not subject to Gibraltar VAT zero-rating; their Gibraltar direct-tax treatment depends on source, residence, permanent-establishment, and transaction facts. Any foreign-country VAT or GST on the destination side is governed by the destination country's rules.

  • Commercial invoice identifying the exporter, consignee, transaction value, currency, product description, quantity, tariff classification, and Incoterms
  • Packing list and detailed product specifications

There is no general Gibraltar real-time VAT invoice-clearance mandate, mandatory VAT e-invoice format, or digital VAT stamping system. Businesses must retain commercially and fiscally reliable invoices, accounting records, import records, export evidence, and supporting contracts. Electronic records may be used where they remain accessible, complete, authentic, and capable of audit. Customs declarations may be submitted through applicable Gibraltar Customs electronic systems or authorised customs intermediaries, subject to the customs procedure and commodity classification.

There is no general Gibraltar statutory foreign-exchange repatriation deadline and no Gibraltar VAT export-refund clawback because Gibraltar has no general VAT system. Customs authorities may reclassify goods, reject an export procedure, demand unpaid customs duties or other charges, impose statutory interest, seize or detain goods, and apply civil or criminal penalties for false declarations, undervaluation, prohibited exports, or documentary fraud. The Income Tax Office may make or amend assessments, disallow unsupported deductions or claimed exemptions, charge interest and statutory penalties for late or inaccurate filings, and pursue recovery against the company and responsible persons where permitted by law. Deliberate evasion, fraudulent records, or false customs declarations may also create criminal exposure. Any foreign VAT or GST refund or zero-rating claim must be substantiated under the law of the relevant foreign jurisdiction; that jurisdiction may retrospectively deny the claim, recover the tax, charge interest, and impose penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility on both current and capital account under IMF Article VIII obligations extended to UK territories; no foreign-exchange controls, no advance approval regime, and no restriction on USD/EUR account maintenance. The Gibraltar Pound is exchangeable at par with GBP. Profit remittance and capital repatriation from Gibraltar to the Cayman entity incur zero withholding tax and no regulatory obstruction, subject only to standard AML source-of-funds verification for transactions above EUR 50,000.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No export proceeds surrender rule applies in Gibraltar, the UK, or the Cayman Islands; the exporter may retain 100% of USD export receipts in any approved bank without statutory conversion. Mandatory conversion ratio: 0%; statutory repatriation timeline: not applicable. Documentary records of export proceeds must be retained for a minimum of 5 years under the GFSC AML/CFT Code and the UK Criminal Finances Act 2017 framework.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) is the sole prudential licensing and AML/CFT supervisory authority; the Bank of England anchors sterling monetary stability and liquidity support; HM Government of Gibraltar issues Gibraltar Pound banknotes at a fixed 1:1 parity with GBP; Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA) retains regulatory oversight of the Cayman Exempted Company's domicile and its licensed corporate services providers.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: CONDITIONAL MEDIUM. UK FCA-regulated EMIs (Wise, Revolut Business) accept Cayman-incorporated clients acting through a Gibraltar registered agent and provide GBP/EUR virtual IBANs for low-value payables automation. However, EMIs cannot support MT700 letters of credit or large USD correspondent settlements, and they impose enhanced due diligence on offshore tax-exempt entities. GFSC-licensed EMIs are sparse post-Brexit, with no EEA passporting access. EMI rails may serve only as secondary disbursement channels, never as the primary treasury nexus.
