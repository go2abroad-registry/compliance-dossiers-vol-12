Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar — official tax registration portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation

E-government filing portal

A Cayman Exempted Company doing business in or through Gibraltar must apply to the Commissioner of Income Tax for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (GibTIN/TRN). Procedure: (1) File an Application for Tax Registration with certified copies of the Cayman Certificate of Incorporation, Certificate of Tax Exemption (section 6 of the Cayman Companies Act), Memorandum and Articles of Association, and a UBO declaration; (2) Provide particulars of directors, shareholders, and any Gibraltar registered office, branch, or agency; (3) Confirm whether the company is managed and controlled in Gibraltar or has a permanent establishment there; (4) Receive a GibTIN/TRN and thereafter file annual tax returns (or nil returns if exempt) and payroll returns for any Gibraltar employees. There is no separate VAT ID because Gibraltar has no VAT/GST regime.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no check-the-box election. A foreign Cayman Exempted Company is presumed a non-resident entity and thus outside Gibraltar corporate tax unless its central management and control (CMC) is exercised in Gibraltar; in that case it may elect to become Gibraltar tax resident and be taxed at the entity level under the Income Tax Act 2019 (subject to a £100,000 nil-rate band and 12.5% standard rate). A US federal check-the-box election (Form 8832) is not recognised for Gibraltar purposes. No standalone hybrid mismatch election is available under Gibraltar domestic law, though OECD BEPS Action 2 rules may apply through treaty or qualifying arrangements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST — Gibraltar Import Duty and Customs Regime

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable — no VAT/GST registration threshold exists in Gibraltar. Importers and exporters of tangible goods must obtain a customs registration/an EORI-type identifier with HM Customs Gibraltar; there is no turnover threshold for that registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar does not levy VAT, so there is no statutory 0% export VAT rate and no related refund scheme. Exports of dietary supplements from Gibraltar to non-Gibraltar destinations are outside the scope of Gibraltar import duty provided the goods are genuinely exported and documented. If the Cayman Exempted Company elects to register for UK VAT (e.g., for distance sales into Great Britain), exports of goods to non-UK/non-EU destinations may be zero-rated under the UK VAT Act 1994 subject to HMRC proof-of-export requirements.

  • Gibraltar Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document / ASYCUDA output)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing export from Gibraltar
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice showing receipt of export proceeds
  • Certificate of Origin (where preferential tariff treatment is claimed)

Gibraltar imposes no real-time fiscal clearance, API-based e-invoicing, or digital stamping for nutraceutical e-commerce. Customs declarations are lodged electronically through the Gibraltar National Node and processed via ASYCUDA World. Commercial invoices are required for customs/export substantiation, but no state-mandated B2B e-invoicing protocol (e.g., PEPPOL or XML) is in force.

Fraudulent export zero-rating or false export declarations are subject to Gibraltar customs penalties (civil penalties up to 100% of evaded duty and criminal sanctions including imprisonment). For the UK VAT context, HMRC may impose penalties up to 100% of the VAT undercharged, issue clawback assessment, and retrospectively disallow export zero-rating where evidence of physical export is missing. Gibraltar authorities may also reassess income and duty where export proceeds are not repatriated within the procedural 180-day benchmark, with surcharges for unremitted foreign earnings.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GBP is fully convertible on both current and capital accounts; there are no exchange-control restrictions in the United Kingdom, Gibraltar, or the Cayman Islands, and no capital or current-account licensing regime applies to the Cayman Exempted Company. The Cayman Islands dollar (KYD) is pegged to the US dollar at the fixed rate CI$1.00 = US$1.20. Dividend and profit remittances from the exempted company are free of withholding tax and may be repatriated without central-bank approval; practical friction arises only at the bank level from AML/CFT due diligence and correspondent-bank risk appetite toward the high-risk nutraceuticals e-commerce sector.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender, repatriation timeline, or mandatory conversion ratio applies in the United Kingdom, Gibraltar, or the Cayman Islands. Export and online-platform sales receipts may be retained in GBP, USD, EUR, or any freely convertible currency. For GFSC-regulated bank relationships and periodic tax-compliance reviews, the company must retain commercial invoices, customs entry records, and 3PL shipping manifests to evidence the genuineness of cross-border trade flows.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority and issuer of GBP, the legal tender of Gibraltar); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the local banking and foreign-exchange conduct regulator; Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA) as the home-jurisdiction supervisor of the Cayman Exempted Company.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Viability rating: LOW-MEDIUM. UK FCA-authorized EMIs (for example Revolut, Wise, and Modulr) and most EEA EMD2-licensed EMIs (Lithuania, Latvia, and Malta passports) generally decline Cayman-incorporated tax-exempted companies and high-risk dietary-supplement merchants because of offshore-jurisdiction tagging, chargeback volatility, and card-not-present fraud benchmarks. Gibraltar currently lacks a liquid domestic EMI onboarding pipeline for high-risk e-commerce; any acceptable EMI must be either a fully licensed bank or an e-money institution under GFSC supervision. If EMI onboarding is achieved, expect monthly volume caps, a 7-10% rolling reserve, mandatory 3-D Secure / SCA authentication, and quarterly portfolio reviews with the right to terminate upon breach of chargeback thresholds.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% (Gibraltar standard corporate rate; marginal rate rises to 27.5% on taxable profits exceeding GIP 2,000,000 through the additional top-up charge)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by a Gibraltar-resident company or PE to any shareholder, whether resident or non-resident. The Cayman Exempted Co is likewise subject to zero Cayman income tax and no Cayman withholding tax on dividends.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Return and final payment due on or before 30 November following the end of the Year of Assessment (i.e., 11 months after the 31 December assessment date). Example: YA 2025 return and tax payment due by 30 November 2026. Companies with tax liability above GIP 1,000 must make two equal payments on account on 30 January and 30 June within the year, with the balance settled on 30 November.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Gibraltar applies OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines via the Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended) with no blanket statutory de-minimis. Local File is mandatory where aggregate controlled (related-party) transactions exceed GIP 2,000,000 per annum; Master File is required for MNE groups with consolidated turnover above EUR 750,000,000 (Pillar Two alignment). A contemporaneous benchmarking file (CUP or TNMM) is required for the nutraceuticals e-commerce operating company and for management-fee recharges from the Cayman head office.

Economic substance scope: Dual Economic Substance overlay: (i) Gibraltar Income Tax (Economic Substance) Rules 2019 — a tax-resident company performing a relevant activity (including 'distribution and service centre') must demonstrate premises, proportionate full-time employees, and core income-generating activities (CIGA) exercised and directed in Gibraltar, with CIGA direction meetings held at the registered office; (ii) Cayman Economic Substance Law (ESR) 2018 — the Cayman Exempted Company must satisfy the CIGA/OER tests in Cayman unless it proves foreign tax residence in another jurisdiction (Gibraltar) with adequate substance. The company should therefore obtain a Gibraltar TRC and maintain board minutes, payroll, warehouse/fulfilment leases, and e-commerce platform agreements in Gibraltar to prevent Cayman ESR penalties and re-characterization as a shell.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate tax evasion / fraudulent return

Statutory sanction: Financial penalty up to 200% of the tax understated, fixed fines up to GIP 100,000, interest at 8% p.a., and criminal conviction for fraudulent tax evasion carrying a fine and imprisonment of up to 7 years; confiscation orders under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign the false return or direct the diversion are personally joint and severally liable for the unpaid tax and penalties; courts will pierce the corporate veil; travel bans and asset freezing orders may be imposed; imprisonment (up to 7 years) and disqualification as a director for up to 15 years.
Shell company / absence of economic substance (ESR)

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar ESR: penalty up to GIP 100,000, daily default penalties, and compulsory strike-off; Cayman ESR: penalty up to KYD 12,000 plus KYD 12,000 per month of continued default and up to 5 years' imprisonment for officers; automatic exchange of information will trigger enforcement by both authorities.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal fines, KYD/GIP monetary penalties, disqualification orders, and criminal liability for ESR declarations; the Gibraltar trade license for the high-risk merchant activity will be revoked, exposing directors to personal civil suits from acquirers and customers.
AML / high-risk merchant account abuse (money laundering)

Statutory sanction: Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 (Gibraltar): money-laundering offence carries up to 14 years' imprisonment and unlimited fines; regulatory penalty by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission up to GIP 2,000,000 or 10% of annual turnover; immediate freezing of funds and revocation of the merchant license; chargeback liability and Visa/Mastercard fines for the acquiring bank, recoverable from the company and its principals.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally exposed to criminal prosecution for money laundering and for consenting to AML-framework breaches; personal wealth can be confiscated; travel bans, passport surrender, daily-reporting bail conditions and custodial sentences apply; the corporate veil is pierced for fraud, leaving directors personally liable for restitution to defrauded consumers and acquiring banks.
