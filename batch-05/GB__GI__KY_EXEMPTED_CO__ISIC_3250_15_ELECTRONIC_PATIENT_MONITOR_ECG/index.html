Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax) – HMG Gibraltar Taxation Directorate, portal https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation; and HM Customs Gibraltar for indirect/customs taxes, https://www.customs.gov.gi. The Cayman Exempted Company is also registered at the Gibraltar Companies Registry as an overseas company. UK HMRC is NOT the competent authority for Gibraltar taxation; Gibraltar is outside the UK VAT area.

E-government filing portal

A Cayman Exempted Company ('KY_EXEMPTED_CO') establishing a branch or permanent establishment in Gibraltar must: (1) register as an overseas company with the Gibraltar Companies Registry within 30 days of opening a place of business (file certified Cayman Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum & Articles, register of directors, Gibraltar registered-office address); (2) obtain a Gibraltar TIN from the Income Tax Office by filing the company registration form within 14 days of commencing trade, together with the branch accounts forecast, director identity documents, and Cayman Certificate of Good Standing; (3) register with HM Customs Gibraltar for an EORI/importer reference number for the import of ECG component parts; (4) obtain the statutory medical-device establishment licence (Gibraltar Medical Devices Register, UKCA/MDR 2017/745 compliance) before first placing monitors on the market; (5) file annual Form CT-1 corporation tax returns and, if the branch generates taxable profits, pay Gibraltar corporation tax at 10%. There is no minimum capital requirement and 100% non-resident ownership is permitted.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope: a non-resident Cayman Exempted Company is chargeable to Gibraltar corporation tax at 10% only on profits attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment (assembly/calibration site); foreign-source and non-attributable profits remain exempt. The entity's home-jurisdiction status is Tax_Exempt_Zone (Cayman Exempted Co holds a tax-exemption certificate under s.6 Companies Act); however, if the company were to have no PE and no management-and-control in Gibraltar, it could apply for 'Qualifying Company' status under s.45 Income Tax Act 2010 and be treated as wholly exempt in Gibraltar.

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a check-the-box partnership/flow-through election; the Cayman Exempted Company is mandatorily a body corporate (entity-level taxpayer). Statutory elections available: (a) Qualifying Company election under s.45 Income Tax Act 2010 where no PE/no Gibraltar residence exists; (b) group relief elections between Gibraltar-resident group companies; (c) hybrid-mismatch notifications and elections under the Income Tax (Hybrid and Other Mismatch) Regulations 2017 (implementing OECD BEPS Action 2/ATAD), disclosing the Cayman tax-exempt status and any deductible-payment timing differences; (d) no VAT grouping election is possible because Gibraltar does not apply UK VAT (VATA 1994 does not extend to Gibraltar).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Purchase Tax (single-stage consumption tax on imported goods under the Purchase Tax Ordinance 1961 and the Customs Tariff Ordinance; Gibraltar levies no VAT and no GST; domestic assembly and calibration services are outside the scope of indirect tax; the supply of ECG patient monitors is taxed only on importation where a positive rate applies, and HS 9018.19 electro-diagnostic monitors attract the 0% headline rate).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No de minimis turnover threshold. Any entity importing goods into Gibraltar, irrespective of value or frequency, must register with HM Customs Gibraltar (EORI/importer registration) and lodge an electronic import declaration for each consignment. For comparative framing, the UK's GBP 90,000 VAT threshold is inapplicable; the trigger in Gibraltar is the first import (GIP 0.00).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export relief/drawback: Purchase Tax is chargeable only at importation, so exported ECG monitors never suffer Gibraltar indirect tax. Exporters may claim relief (refund or conditional exemption) on imported components incorporated into finished multi-parameter bedside telemetry monitors where the finished goods are exported from Gibraltar within 6 months of import. Eligibility requires: (1) traceability from imported component lots (bills of materials) to exported finished serial numbers; (2) physical exit from Gibraltar Customs territory to a third-country, the EU, or the UK; (3) a valid Gibraltar EORI and absence of customs debt; (4) the statutory medical-device licence (UKCA/MDR) remaining valid, because regulatory non-compliance voids the relief claim.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic SAD / exit declaration lodged with HM Customs Gibraltar)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure of the ECG monitor consignments
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank/FX confirmation of export sale proceeds received within 180 days) together with the export commercial invoice

Gibraltar mandates electronic customs clearance for all import/export declarations via HM Customs Gibraltar's digital declaration system (no paper SAD). There is no real-time fiscal clearance, no mandatory B2B e-invoicing, no continuous transaction controls, and no SAF-T regime. Commercial invoices supporting clearance must show the importer/exporter EORI, consignee details, Incoterms, HS code (9018.11/9018.19), and unit prices; no digital stamp is applied to medical devices. UK MTD/e-invoicing obligations do not extend to Gibraltar operations.

HM Customs Gibraltar and the Income Tax Office may jointly audit export-relief claims for up to 3 years after export. Failure to produce the customs export declaration, transport documents, or the Inward Remittance Certificate within the 180-day repatriation/evidencing window triggers retrospective assessment of Purchase Tax at the rate applicable to the imported components, interest from the date of import, and clawback of any refund paid. Fraudulent or reckless zero-rating/export claims attract civil penalties of up to 200% of the evaded duty, forfeiture of goods, suspension or revocation of the exporter's EORI/customs authorisation, and criminal prosecution of directors under the Customs Ordinance. Separately, an invalid UKCA/MDR conformity declaration independently triggers product-recall costs and market-surveillance penalties under the Gibraltar Medical Devices legislation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current and capital account convertibility under Article VIII of the IMF Articles of Agreement; zero exchange-control restrictions on dividends, loan repayments, royalties, trade settlements, or intra-group treasury movements. GIP is pegged at par to GBP, eliminating bilateral FX convertibility risk within the jurisdiction. Profit remittance from Gibraltar operations to the Cayman-exempted parent is unrestricted, subject only to routine AML/KYC verification, CRS self-certification, and economic substance documentation; no withholding taxes apply on dividends or interest because Gibraltar levies no such taxes and the Cayman entity is tax-exempt.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Neither the United Kingdom nor Gibraltar imposes any mandatory conversion ratio, surrender timeline, or repatriation deadline for export receipts. Foreign-currency proceeds may be retained indefinitely in any denomination, offset against imports via intercompany netting, or hedged forward without regulatory approval. Reporting obligations are limited to statistical returns by the resident bank under the Bank of England's International Transaction Reporting System and GFSC regulatory returns; no corporate-level surrender filing exists.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (sovereign central bank for GBP monetary policy and Prudential Regulation Authority banking supervision); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the licensing and prudential authority for all Gibraltar-incorporated banks and financial services firms; Government of Gibraltar Currency Note Issuance Office for Gibraltar Pound (GIP) issuance under the Currency Notes Act 1934 at a fixed 1:1 GBP parity.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility: LOW as the primary treasury rail; MEDIUM only as an ancillary expense rail. UK-regulated EMIs (Wise Business, Revolut Business, Airwallex) cannot passport into Gibraltar post-Brexit and, under internal counterparty policy, routinely decline Cayman offshore vehicles during KYB screening. Gibraltar-licensed EMIs under the Financial Services Act 2019 may onboard a Cayman exempted company but are restricted to e-money safeguarding: no commercial LC issuance, no committed borrowing or treasury lines, and mandatory segregation of client funds with a Tier 1 credit institution. Such safeguarded balances are not covered by the Gibraltar Deposit Guarantee Scheme, and the absence of correspondent-bank relationships makes Swift_LC and high-value TT_Wire settlement impossible. EMI rails should be limited to sub-USD 10,000 operational expenses.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Use a single SWIFT MT103 wire from the ultimate parent or Cayman holding account to the Gibraltar operational account. Populate field 50K with the ordering customer's legal name, field 59 with the Gibraltar beneficiary, and an ISO 20022/PM purpose code of 'CAPT' (capital transfer) or 'EQUI' (equity injection); for shareholder loans use 'LOAN'. Pre-notify the receiving bank with the board resolution authorizing the injection, an updated shareholder ledger, and a source-of-funds questionnaire. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility: LOW as the primary treasury rail; MEDIUM only as an ancillary expense rail. UK-regulated EMIs (Wise Business, Revolut Business, Airwallex) cannot passport into Gibraltar post-Brexit and, under internal counterparty policy, routinely decline Cayman offshore vehicles during KYB screening. Gibraltar-licensed EMIs under the Financial Services Act 2019 may onboard a Cayman exempted company but are restricted to e-money safeguarding: no commercial LC issuance, no committed borrowing or treasury lines, and mandatory segregation of client funds with a Tier 1 credit institution. Such safeguarded balances are not covered by the Gibraltar Deposit Guarantee Scheme, and the absence of correspondent-bank relationships makes Swift_LC and high-value TT_Wire settlement impossible. EMI rails should be limited to sub-USD 10,000 operational expenses.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (Cayman Islands imposes no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or withholding tax; the exempted company holds a tax exemption undertaking under section 6 of the Income Tax Ordinance, Cap. 159)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — the Cayman Islands imposes no withholding tax on dividends or distributions; Gibraltar does not impose withholding tax on distributions to non-residents; UK-source dividends are generally not subject to UK withholding tax for non-residents, but anti-hybrid and dividend-stripping rules apply.
