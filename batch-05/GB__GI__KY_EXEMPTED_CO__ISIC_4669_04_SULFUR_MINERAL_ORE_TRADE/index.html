Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Her Majesty's Government of Gibraltar - Income Tax Office and HM Customs Gibraltar; official portal https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation

E-government filing portal

A Cayman Exempted Company establishing a branch or place of management in Gibraltar must file Form FB-1 with the Gibraltar Companies Registry and a Corporate Return with the Income Tax Office to obtain a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (GITIN). The application must be supported by the Certificate of Incorporation, Articles of Association, list of directors, notarized passport copies, and evidence of a Gibraltar registered office. Non-resident companies without a permanent establishment or source of income in Gibraltar are not mandated to file tax returns, but a voluntary tax registration is recommended for opening a Gibraltar EMI bank account, securing LC facilities, and shielding the parent exempted company from adverse 'place of management' attribution.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A Cayman Exempted Company establishing a branch or place of management in Gibraltar must file Form FB-1 with the Gibraltar Companies Registry and a Corporate Return with the Income Tax Office to obtain a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (GITIN). The application must be supported by the Certificate of Incorporation, Articles of Association, list of directors, notarized passport copies, and evidence of a Gibraltar registered office. Non-resident companies without a permanent establishment or source of income in Gibraltar are not mandated to file tax returns, but a voluntary tax registration is recommended for opening a Gibraltar EMI bank account, securing LC facilities, and shielding the parent exempted company from adverse 'place of management' attribution.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not operate a US-style check-the-box election. A foreign company may elect under the Gibraltar Income Tax Act to be treated as resident if its central management and control is in Gibraltar, thereby accessing the domestic branch exemption, the 'category 2' participation exemption, and the unilateral credit pool. A Cayman Exempted Company preserving its exempted status must not execute such election; it should instead rely on the non-residence quietus and the absence of a permanent Gibraltar establishment, while ensuring substance in Cayman remains demonstrable.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar has no VAT or GST. Indirect taxation is limited to Customs Duties, Environmental Duties, and the 'General Services Tax' applicable to a narrow class of locally consumed services. For the cross-border supply of sulfur mineral ore, no Gibraltar consumption tax applies.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general turnover threshold is applicable because no VAT/GST is chargeable; however, a customs registration and Economic Operator Registration Identification (EORI-GI) is required for any import of goods into Gibraltar irrespective of value, producing a de facto threshold of GBP 0. Supplies of intangible trading services by a non-resident merchant fall outside the scope and do not trigger registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is not applicable because Gibraltar does not operate a VAT system. Instead, goods exported outside Gibraltar, including elemental sulfur and granulated sulfur destined for non-Gibraltar buyers, are outside the territorial scope of Gibraltar indirect taxation, thereby achieving economically the same result as a 0% rate. The exporter must satisfy HM Customs Gibraltar that the goods left the jurisdiction within 30 days of loading at a Gibraltar wharf or transshipment zone.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

No real-time fiscal clearance API or digital stamping regime exists in Gibraltar. B2B paper or PDF invoices are legally valid, but must carry the GITIN, transaction date, description, and tonnage of sulfur ore. For customs entry, the UK Common Transit Convention and Gibraltar's own 'Green Line' protocol require an electronic Export Declaration (coded through the UK Customs Declaration Service or the Spanish/Gibraltar ADEP) for any movement into or out of the territory; the declaration must include the standard HS 2503 identifier for sulfur, the vessel call sign, and the outwards arrival/departure record.

Any claim for an exemption from Gibraltar customs duty, or the economic zero-rating of a transshipped mineral cargo, is subject to retrospective audit for up to seven years. A false declaration, forged Bill of Lading, or fictitious inward remittance triggers a penalty of the greater of GBP 100,000 or 200% of the duty avoided, together with confiscation of the goods, retrospective forfeiture of any duty relief, and publication of the corporate taxpayer's name on the Gibraltar Habitual Defaulters publicity list. Criminal prosecution for cheating the public revenue carries a maximum ten-year custodial sentence.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar has broad current-account and capital-account convertibility, with no general exchange controls, mandatory foreign-exchange licensing, or routine approval requirement for conversion of sterling into other currencies. Sterling is the principal settlement currency, although commercial banks may offer USD, EUR, CHF and other currency accounts subject to risk appetite. Dividends, interest, service fees, loan repayments and capital redemptions may generally be remitted cross-border if supported by corporate approvals, contractual evidence, tax records, beneficial-owner information, sanctions screening and source-of-funds evidence. A Cayman exempted company is not automatically entitled to a Gibraltar bank account; banks will assess place of effective management, operating substance, transaction rationale, counterparties, commodity origin, vessel exposure, tax residence, and whether the account creates an unlicensed payment or deposit-taking activity. Gibraltar, UK, Cayman Islands and other applicable tax-residence, economic-substance, transfer-pricing, withholding-tax and beneficial-ownership rules remain relevant notwithstanding the absence of exchange controls.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No general Gibraltar rule requires exporters to surrender export proceeds to a central bank or convert them within a prescribed period. No general mandatory conversion ratio applies. Export receipts may normally remain in foreign-currency accounts or be converted at the account holder's discretion, subject to bank documentation, sanctions controls, customs records, anti-money-laundering requirements, tax reporting and contractual obligations. For sulfur and mineral-ore transactions, retain contracts, invoices, bills of lading, certificates of origin, assay certificates, customs declarations, vessel and voyage information, insurance documents, sanctions-screening results and proof of delivery. Any separate rule imposed by the export country, import country, payment bank, correspondent bank or trade-finance provider must be assessed independently.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar has no separate central bank or general foreign-exchange administration. Relevant authorities are HM Government of Gibraltar, the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission, the Gibraltar Customs Department, and the United Kingdom sanctions authorities where UK sanctions apply. Gibraltar uses sterling under its monetary arrangement with the United Kingdom; the Bank of England is not Gibraltar's banking supervisor or general foreign-exchange regulator.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium for ancillary payment collection and low-to-medium for primary treasury banking. A Gibraltar or UK-authorised electronic-money institution may provide payment accounts, safeguarding, foreign-exchange conversion and international transfers within its permission scope, but an EMI account is not necessarily a bank deposit account and may not support commercial letters of credit, documentary collections, borrowing, cash pooling or unrestricted commodity-trade flows. An offshore digital EMI cannot lawfully provide regulated payment or e-money services into Gibraltar merely because its platform is accessible online; it must rely on an applicable authorisation, passporting or local-permission basis and satisfy Gibraltar and UK financial-crime requirements. The bank or EMI may reject a Cayman exempted company with no Gibraltar substance, a high-risk commodity corridor, opaque beneficial ownership, nested payment activity, third-party receipts, sanctioned-country exposure or unexplained use of personal accounts. Use a regulated bank as the primary settlement account and an EMI only as a documented secondary rail after confirming safeguarding, client-money segregation, jurisdictional permissions, account-name matching, transaction limits, chargeback rules and termination rights.
