Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Her Majesty's Commissioner of Income Tax, operating through the Income Tax Office, Gibraltar; official online filing and payment are administered through the Gibraltar Government eTax portal.

E-government filing portal

A Cayman Exempted Company must first register as an overseas/foreign company with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under the Companies Act 2014, appointing a Gibraltar-registered office and a local process agent. After Companies House registration, the entity files an application with HM Commissioner of Income Tax for a Gibraltar tax reference number, supplying the Cayman certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, directors' particulars, registered office details, and a statement of intended activities. A Gibraltar Tax Identification Number is issued. No VAT ID is required because Gibraltar does not levy VAT or GST. For Cayman tax purposes the entity remains a Tax-Exempt Zone vehicle; for Gibraltar tax purposes it is treated as a territorial-basis, entity-level taxpayer.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists under Gibraltar law. A foreign entity may apply for Qualifying Company (Category 2) status, subject to prior approval and economic substance conditions, which applies a reduced rate to qualifying trading profits. The Cayman tax-exempt status is statutory and is not a Gibraltar election, and it does not exempt Gibraltar-source income.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Sales and Use Tax

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable; no economic-turnover threshold exists for indirect tax registration because Gibraltar imposes no VAT/GST. The entity must nonetheless register with HM Customs for import/export activity and obtain the relevant excise or trade licence for controlled goods.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable for VAT zero-rating because Gibraltar does not impose VAT/GST on goods or supplies. Goods manufactured, stored, or supplied from Gibraltar and exported to a third country are outside the scope of Gibraltar consumption duties, and no input VAT refund arises. If the coronary drug-eluting stents are exported from the United Kingdom or another VAT jurisdiction, zero-rating would require proof of removal from that VAT territory and compliance with the applicable export evidence rules.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

No real-time fiscal e-invoicing, API clearance, or digital stamping obligation applies in Gibraltar. Customs declarations are filed electronically through the Gibraltar Customs electronic single-window platform, and no fiscal stamping is required for VAT purposes.

There is no VAT zero-rating regime in Gibraltar to abuse. False customs declarations, undervaluation, misuse of export or duty-suspension procedures, and fraudulent retention of export-related reliefs may result in reassessment, seizure and forfeiture of goods, monetary penalties of up to three times the duty or value under Gibraltar customs legislation, imprisonment of responsible officers, and retrospective clawback into connected Gibraltar income-tax assessments where the export structure is mischaracterised.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is pegged at 1:1 to GBP and is freely convertible; the UK, Gibraltar and the Cayman Islands maintain no general foreign-exchange controls. Current-account payments, trade settlements, dividends and profit distributions may be remitted freely through licensed banks, subject to standard AML/KYC verification, supporting commercial documentation, beneficial-ownership filings and sanctions screening. Capital-account inflows such as equity injections require only board/company approvals, bank purpose codes and source-of-funds documentation; no central-bank pre-authorisation is required. Profit repatriation from the Cayman Exempted Company must be supported by economic substance, arm's length transfer pricing and a clear corporate governance paper trail to avoid UK/Gibraltar anti-abuse, CFC or attribution challenges.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion ratio exists under UK, Gibraltar or Cayman law. Export receipts may be retained in USD, EUR, GBP or GIP and applied directly to operating expenditure, LC settlement and supplier payments. Banking policy may require that proceeds be routed through the Gibraltar account bank and be matched to export invoices and customs records. The Cayman Exempted Company must record all receipts in its statutory books and preserve underlying documents for CIMA, GFSC and HMRC information-exchange purposes.

Central bank supervision: Government of Gibraltar (GIP currency issuer); Bank of England (UK monetary anchor and banking oversight); HM Treasury (sanctions and financial restrictions); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) (banking and AML/CFT supervision); Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA) (home regulator of the Cayman Exempted Company).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-Medium. A UK- or Gibraltar-licensed e-money institution (EMI) may support low-value contingency payments, FX conversion and onward vendor payments, but it is not a compliant substitute for a full-service bank because EMIs generally cannot issue documentary Letters of Credit, provide trade-finance limits, or handle high-risk Class III medical-device settlement flows without a sponsoring bank. Offshore digital EMIs not licensed or passported into Gibraltar/UK are not viable for the principal clearing infrastructure; any incidental EMI use must be pre-approved by compliance and limited to a de-minimis monthly flow.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Contemporaneous Local File and Master File required where annual cross-border related-party turnover exceeds GIP 5,000,000; Country-by-Country notification applies to groups with consolidated revenue equal to or above EUR 750,000,000

Economic substance scope: Both jurisdictions apply an economic substance doctrine. Gibraltar ESR requires the PE to maintain physical premises, a qualified workforce (laser engineers, QA and regulatory staff), local board oversight and core income-generating activities. Cayman ESR forces the Exempted Company either to satisfy the full substance test in Cayman or to demonstrate that the relevant activity occurs in Gibraltar as a foreign PE. A pure shell platform without substance is fatal: the Cayman entity will be recharacterised, information exchanged automatically, and the Gibraltar-source profit confirmed fully taxable

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Returns

Statutory sanction: Penalties up to 100% of the understated tax; interest at 8% per annum; criminal fines imposed by the Gibraltar Magistrates' Court; confiscation orders under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015; imprisonment up to 5 years

Director personal liability: Directors personally jointly and severally liable for restitution; seizure of personal assets; travel bans pending full settlement; director disqualification for up to 15 years; custodial sentences up to 5 years
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Cayman: KYD 10,000 to KYD 20,000 recurring penalties; imprisonment up to 2 years; strike-off and dissolution; Gibraltar ESR: GIP 10,000 per offence; profit remains fully taxable in Gibraltar as domestically sourced

Director personal liability: Directors face personal fines, information orders from the Cayman Tax Information Authority, cancellation of corporate registration, travel restrictions and liability to repay unlawful distributions
AML and Proceeds of Crime Breach

Statutory sanction: Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 (Gibraltar): unlimited confiscation orders; money laundering imprisonment up to 14 years; Gibraltar Financial Services Commission AML fines up to 10% of group turnover; asset freezing orders

Director personal liability: Directors who knew or were reckless face criminal liability, personal confiscation of assets, travel bans and court-imposed reporting conditions; imprisonment up to 14 years for the core money laundering offence
Transfer Pricing Abuse

Statutory sanction: Arm's length adjustment with compensatory interest; penalty up to 50% of the additional tax; automatic exchange of information triggers secondary assessments in the UK and EU member states

Director personal liability: Directors signing false TP declarations are personally liable for adjustment penalties; travel bans affecting overseas manufacturing site visits; the Cayman entity may be reclassified as a Gibraltar-resident company under managed entity rules
Unlicensed Medical Device Manufacturing

Statutory sanction: Regulatory recall and prohibition orders; administrative fines; criminal prosecution under the Medical Devices Regulations; exclusion from EU MDR and UK MDR registries; seizure of non-compliant inventory

Director personal liability: Directors criminally liable for product safety failures and negligent homicide in the event of patient death; personal disqualification; unlimited fines; extradition risk under cross-border medical device regulation
