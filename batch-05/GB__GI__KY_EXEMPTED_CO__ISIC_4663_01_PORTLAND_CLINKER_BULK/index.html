Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

For the Gibraltar branch of a Cayman Exempted Company, a physical registered office is mandatory; a virtual office providing only a mail-forwarding address will not be accepted. A licensed corporate service provider's physical premises may be used as the registered office. For the Cayman parent, a registered office in the Cayman Islands is mandatory under the Companies Act, and virtual offices are not allowed. In the construction-materials wholesale sector, a substantive physical presence in Gibraltar is not automatically required if central management and control are exercised abroad, but should the Gibraltar branch become the place of effective management, a physical office and local staffing will be obligatory for corporate substance compliance.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar: Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (28 Irish Town, Gibraltar; official e-services portal: www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation). Corporate registration: Registrar of Companies, Gibraltar Companies House. Indirect tax and customs administration: HM Customs Gibraltar (Gibraltar Customs & Excise). UK sovereign reference: HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC), 100 Parliament Street, London; HMRC jurisdiction extends to the UK mainland only and does not apply to Gibraltar.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Register the Cayman Exempted Company as an overseas/foreign company with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies under the Companies Act 2014, filing a certified Certificate of Incorporation, Charter and Memorandum & Articles of Association, register of directors, and particulars of a Gibraltar registered office. Step 2: Appoint a Gibraltar-resident tax representative or agent. Step 3: Submit the Company Registration for Tax Purposes application to the Income Tax Office, attaching apostilled and notarised Cayman constitutional documents, beneficial ownership schedule, and proof of registered office, to obtain a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN/TIN). Step 4: File the annual Gibraltar economic substance declaration if the company carries on a relevant activity (distribution and service centre, shipping, or headquarter activity). Step 5: Register with HM Customs Gibraltar for an importer/exporter trader reference and an electronic export-declaration account (CDS-compatible). No VAT registration or VAT ID is obtainable in Gibraltar because Gibraltar is outside the UK and EU VAT territories. Step 6: If UK mainland taxable supplies are contemplated, register with HMRC as a non-established taxable person on Form VAT1.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Gibraltar law does not provide a check-the-box entity classification election. For US federal tax purposes, the Cayman Exempted Company may file Form 8832 to elect disregarded-entity status (single member), partnership status (multiple members), or association-taxable status; absent an election, a single-member eligible entity is disregarded and a multi-member entity is a partnership. Under Gibraltar tax law, the company is a separate juridical person taxed on a territorial basis; it may elect the Gibraltar participation exemption for qualifying dividends and capital gains and is chargeable at the standard corporation tax rate of 12.5% once Gibraltar-source income arises. OECD/G20 hybrid-mismatch countermeasures, reflected in UK TIOPA 2010 Part 6A, may disallow deductions arising from hybrid financial instruments or hybrid payer arrangements exploiting the Cayman-exempt versus Gibraltar-territorial classification mismatch.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar Import Duty and Excise regime; no general VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax applies within Gibraltar, and the UK VAT Act 1994 is not extended to Gibraltar. UK VAT applies only to supplies made or deemed to be made in Great Britain or Northern Ireland.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Gibraltar: no VAT or GST threshold exists; every commercial import or export of bulk clinker is registrable with HM Customs from the first transaction (no de minimis threshold in GIP). UK VAT registration thresholds: GBP 85,000 (before 1 April 2024) and GBP 90,000 (from 1 April 2024) of UK taxable turnover; a non-established trader must register if any UK taxable supply is made. No GIP-denominated threshold is prescribed for export zero-rating in Gibraltar.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar: zero-rating is automatic by operation of law for exported goods, including bulk Portland cement clinker (HS 2523.10.00), which leave the Gibraltar customs territory free of consumption or import duty at an effective 0% output rate; there is no Gibraltar VAT to reclaim. UK context: direct export zero-rating under section 30(6) VATA 1994 and Schedule 8 Item 1 applies to goods exported from the UK where the supplier obtains proof of physical removal within 3 months, retains the CDS/C88 export declaration, and the sale is made on FOB or equivalent Incoterms transferring risk to the non-UK buyer; offshore services supplied to overseas recipients are outside the scope of UK VAT under the place-of-supply rules.

  • Ocean Bill of Lading (negotiable port-to-port document evidencing export from the loading port)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank or foreign-exchange confirmation of receipt of export proceeds)
  • Commercial Invoice (HS 2523.10.00, Incoterms, vessel name, quantities in metric tonnes)

None. Gibraltar and the UK do not mandate real-time fiscal e-invoicing, digital stamping, continuous transaction controls, or API clearance for B2B invoices. Export declarations for ocean bulk cargo (Portland cement clinker, HS 2523.10.00) are submitted electronically through the Customs Declaration Service (CDS) or the Gibraltar customs SAD system, supported by bill-of-lading and vessel-manifest matching. Commercial invoices and all export substantiation documents must be retained for at least 6 years.

Customs and VAT audits test the genuineness of the export movement, the vessel manifest trail, and the matching of export proceeds. Fraudulent export zero-rating or false customs declarations attract: (a) in Gibraltar, seizure and forfeiture of the goods and vessel, a monetary penalty of up to three times the duty evaded under the Customs Ordinance, and criminal prosecution; (b) in the UK, penalties under VATA 1994 sections 65 and 67 of 15% to 100% of the evaded VAT, civil accuracy penalties up to 100% under FA 2007 Schedule 24, retrospective assessments with default interest, and criminal liability under VATA 1994 section 72 (imprisonment up to 7 years) or CEMA 1979 section 167; (c) retrospective clawback of all claimed export reliefs, input VAT denials, and re-characterisation of zero-rated sales as taxable for up to 4 years (20 years in fraud cases), together with public naming by HMRC; and (d) in Gibraltar, failure to satisfy the economic substance test for distribution or shipping activities may trigger fines, director disqualification, and strike-off of the exempted company.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar maintains full current and capital account convertibility with no exchange controls. Profit and capital remittances are unrestricted, subject solely to standard company law distribution tests and AML/EDD verification. The Cayman Exempted Company, being non-resident in Gibraltar, is not subject to Gibraltar withholding tax on dividends, interest, or royalties, and no exchange control approval is required for cross-border transfers.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender requirement exists in Gibraltar. Export receipts may be retained, held, used, or converted freely in any currency without mandatory conversion timelines or ratios. Banks apply customer due diligence and transaction monitoring but do not enforce any forced sale of export earnings.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority for Gibraltar through the Gibraltar pound, which is fixed at parity to GBP) and the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) as the competent foreign exchange and banking regulator.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium viability. Gibraltar-licensed e-money institutions may provide online cross-border accounts, SWIFT/TT rails, and multi-currency operations for the Cayman entity. However, licensed EMIs are prohibited from taking deposits or providing trade credit, and many are conservative regarding offshore corporate structures with nominee shareholders. Full passported EMI access from the UK/EU was curtailed post-Brexit, so a Gibraltar-regulated EMI or a UK EMI with a Gibraltar-branch presence is necessary.
