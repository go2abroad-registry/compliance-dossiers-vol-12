E-government filing portal

A Cayman Exempted Company is not automatically tax-exempt in Gibraltar. If it carries on business in Gibraltar, has Gibraltar-source profits, employs personnel, owns or leases business premises, or otherwise creates a Gibraltar taxable presence, it should contact the Gibraltar Income Tax Office and obtain a Gibraltar tax reference or registration number through the prescribed corporate tax registration process. The application generally requires the certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, registered office and principal business details, directors and beneficial ownership information, business activity description, accounting period, Gibraltar permanent establishment analysis, and evidence of the authority of the applicant. A foreign company may also need registration or filing as an overseas company under Gibraltar company law if it establishes a place of business or otherwise falls within the applicable foreign-company registration rules. Customs registration, import or export identifiers, and importer or exporter authorisations are separate from income-tax registration. A Gibraltar tax number is not a VAT number because Gibraltar does not operate a general VAT system.

Available election forms

Gibraltar does not provide a United States-style check-the-box election for entity classification. A Cayman Exempted Company generally remains a separate corporate taxpayer for Gibraltar purposes where Gibraltar taxable presence or Gibraltar-source income exists. Cayman exempted-company status does not create a Gibraltar exemption. No general hybrid-mismatch election converts the company into a pass-through entity. VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No general VAT, GST, or sales and use tax system in Gibraltar

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable. Gibraltar has no general VAT registration threshold and no general VAT registration obligation. Import duties, customs charges, excise duties, fees, and other border measures may nevertheless apply depending on the goods, origin, customs procedure, and destination.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

No Gibraltar VAT zero-rating claim exists because Gibraltar has no general VAT system. Consequently, there is no Gibraltar output VAT to charge at 0%, no export-VAT exemption certificate, and no general export VAT refund. A shipment of wooden mechanical puzzles or related components exported from Gibraltar must instead comply with applicable customs, origin, product-safety, import-control, and destination-country indirect-tax rules. Offshore services supplied by a Gibraltar business may be outside the scope of any Gibraltar VAT analysis, but destination-country VAT or GST obligations, including non-resident digital-commerce rules, must be assessed separately.

  • Export customs declaration or applicable electronic customs submission
  • Transport document, such as bill of lading, air waybill, or courier waybill
  • Evidence of export or exit confirmation where required by the customs procedure

There is no Gibraltar VAT real-time invoice-clearance, VAT digital-stamping, or mandatory VAT e-invoicing regime. Businesses should issue commercially sufficient invoices and retain accounting records supporting supplies, pricing, customer identity, payment, and customs status. Customs declarations and related electronic submissions may be required through the applicable Gibraltar or United Kingdom customs systems, depending on the movement route and customs procedure. Amazon Pay and Stripe onboarding may separately require corporate, beneficial ownership, tax-residency, economic-activity, and anti-money-laundering information.

Because Gibraltar has no general VAT export-zero-rating regime, there is no Gibraltar export-VAT zero-rating clawback. False customs declarations, undervaluation, misclassification, prohibited exports, origin fraud, or inaccurate commercial documentation may result in customs reassessment, duty and tax recovery, interest, seizure or forfeiture of goods, civil penalties, suspension of customs authorisations, and criminal prosecution under applicable Gibraltar customs legislation. Income-tax penalties may apply separately for failure to register, inaccurate returns, late filing, failure to maintain records, or deliberate tax evasion. Any destination-country VAT or GST refund or zero-rating claim is governed by that destination jurisdiction and may be retrospectively denied with tax, interest, penalties, and repayment obligations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar and the Cayman Islands both maintain fully open capital and current accounts. GIP is freely convertible at par to GBP, with unrestricted access to EUR and USD clearing; the Cayman Islands dollar is pegged at 1 KYD = 1.20 USD. There are no statutory capital controls, prior-authorisation regimes, or exchange-ticket requirements. Profit remittance via dividends, management charges, or intercompany settlement is unrestricted; Gibraltar levies no withholding taxes on dividends, interest, royalties, or branch remittances to non-resident shareholders, and the Cayman Exempted Company is tax-exempt on Cayman-source and foreign income. Practical hurdles are limited to bank-level AML verification of source of funds, the need to demonstrate beneficial-ownership transparency, and the risk that central management and control exercised from Gibraltar would create Gibraltar tax residence, which must be addressed through a documented substance policy.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender obligation exists in either jurisdiction. HM Government of Gibraltar and CIMA impose no compulsory conversion ratios, no holding-period requirements, and no deadline for repatriation of export proceeds derived from ISIC 3240 wooden puzzle manufacturing. 100% of USD, EUR, and other export receipts may be held in designated foreign-currency accounts. Nevertheless, the authorised bank will require each Amazon Pay and Stripe marketplace settlement to be supported by platform payout statements, settlement reports, and chargeback records to satisfy the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 (Gibraltar) and CIMA AML Rules.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) acting as the integrated prudential, conduct, and AML/CFT supervisor for banking; the Government of Gibraltar, through its Financial Services Unit, is the statutory issuer of the Gibraltar pound (GIP) at a fixed 1:1 parity with GBP and operates convertibility via Bank of England arrangements. For the Cayman-registered entity, the Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA) is the corresponding regulatory body for licensing, currency affairs, and AML oversight, but it does not exercise exchange-control functions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Initial equity subscriptions must be settled exclusively by bank wire via SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's own account in their jurisdiction of residence; cash, bearer instruments, and third-party funded payments are prohibited. Remittance information fields must include the purpose code for direct investment-equity capital (ECB/ONS classification DE1 or 'share capital subscription') and the corporate group reference. The bank will require the allotment resolution, updated share register, certificate of good standing, and a capital structure note before crediting the account. USD inflows clear through New York CHIPS/Fedwire correspondents into the bank's USD nostro; EUR inflows are received via TARGET2 using SWIFT MT103 or SEPA Credit Transfer (IBAN to IBAN); GIP/GBP inflows are settled through UK Faster Payments or CHAPS via Bank of England accounts. The transaction is recorded as inward foreign direct investment when the remitter is a non-resident shareholder, and the entire KYC source-of-funds file, group structure chart, and board minutes approving the capital call must be uploaded to the compliance platform before value is given. No Gibraltar FX licence or regulatory pre-approval is required.Operating disbursements for raw materials (MDF board, laser optics, packaging, bearings) and logistics are structured through four clearing rails: (i) SEPA Credit Transfers for EUR supplier invoices, executed on D+1 from the segregated EUR account; (ii) SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers for USD, GBP, and CNY suppliers outside SEPA, routed via the bank's Hong Kong or Singapore correspondents for Chinese vendors; (iii) UK Faster Payments for Gibraltar-resident services and expenses; and (iv) Commercial Letters of Credit (SWIFT MT700/MT707) for high-value capital equipment such as CO2 laser cutters, incorporating Incoterms 2020 and compliant document packs (bill of lading, packing list, commercial invoice, inspection certificate). E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Cross-border fintech EMI viability is rated MEDIUM-HIGH for specialised offshore-orientated UK FCA-authorised EMIs and LOW for mainstream retail EMIs. Wise, Revolut Business, and Stripe Treasury do not onboard Cayman Islands companies because Cayman is outside their supported-jurisdiction matrices; Amazon Pay settles only to bank accounts in supported markets. Viable acceptance corridors are UK-authorised EMIs and payment institutions that accept offshore structures via a regulated introducer, notably Currenxie, Airwallex, Ebury, and Payoneer, all operating under the Electronic Money Regulations 2011 (UK) with client funds safeguarded in tier-1 UK bank accounts. No Gibraltar-domiciled EMI currently offers the required multi-currency merchant stack, and an EMI account cannot support overdrafts, commercial letters of credit, or segregated FX forward limits beyond the EMI's own risk appetite. The legal constraint is that an e-money account is not a deposit-taking banking relationship and does not attract Financial Services Compensation Scheme deposit protection.
