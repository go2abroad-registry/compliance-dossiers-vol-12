Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Cayman Islands Registrar of Companies for incorporation; Gibraltar Companies House and the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission for any Gibraltar registration, regulated activity, or local corporate compliance requirement.

Governing statute

The specified Cayman Exempted Company cannot be incorporated under Gibraltar law. It must be incorporated under the Cayman Islands Companies Act (As Revised), including the provisions governing exempted companies, incorporation, registered offices, directors, members, and annual returns. Gibraltar operations are separately subject to the Gibraltar Companies Act 2014, the Companies (Accounts) Act, and applicable licensing legislation.

Formation / constitutional filings

Cayman Islands Application for Registration of an Exempted Company, filed through a licensed Cayman corporate services provider, together with the proposed memorandum and articles of association. A Gibraltar business registration or branch filing is a separate instrument and does not create the Cayman company.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Shares may generally be issued for consideration permitted by Cayman law, subject to the memorandum, articles, subscription terms, and director approval. A statutory escrow account is not generally required, but banking providers may require capital funding, source-of-funds evidence, and a local operating balance.

Funding & unlock path: Obtain planning and building-control approval, fire-safety review and completion certification from the Gibraltar Fire and Rescue Service, structural and floor-load certification for racking and refrigeration equipment, electrical and refrigeration commissioning certificates, emergency and evacuation plans, and workplace risk assessments under applicable Gibraltar occupational health and safety requirements. Pharmaceutical cold storage additionally requires validated temperature monitoring, calibration, deviation management, backup power or contingency arrangements, security controls, and documented GDP procedures.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Gibraltar Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax) within HM Government of Gibraltar; online filing and TIN services via the Directorate of Tax Administration e-portal (https://taxation.gi). Corporate registration counterpart: Gibraltar Companies House.

E-government filing portal

A Cayman Exempted Company intending to operate in Gibraltar must first be licensed as a registered foreign company under the Companies (Registration of Foreign Companies) Act by filing a certified copy of the Certificate of Incorporation, Certificate of Good Standing, memorandum, and articles, plus a Gibraltar registered-office address and a Gibraltar-resident authorised representative. Simultaneously, the company must apply to the Commissioner of Income Tax for a Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN); supporting evidence includes the foreign-company registration certificate, beneficial-ownership data, and expected profit-source description. Issuance is typically completed within 5–10 business days. If central management and control is exercised in Gibraltar, the company becomes Gibraltar-resident and subject to corporate income tax at 12.5%; otherwise, only profits attributable to a Gibraltar permanent establishment are taxed.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (territorial and source-based: a non-resident Cayman Exempted Company is taxed solely on profits arising from or attributable to Gibraltar operations; management-and-control presence triggers full Gibraltar residence and worldwide-profits taxation subject to double-tax relief)

Available election forms

No check-the-box or entity-classification election analogous to US subchapter K exists. Available statutory elections under Gibraltar law include: (i) election for group relief within a 75% group to surrender tax losses and gains; (ii) election to apply the Patent Box / qualifying IP asset regime at an effective 6% rate; (iii) election for capital allowances pooling and industrial-buildings allowances on qualifying cold-storage construction; and (iv) hybrid-mismatch restructuring filings pursuant to Gibraltar's OECD hybrid-rules provisions, including mandatory notification to characterise the Cayman vehicle as tax-transparent for Gibraltar alignment purposes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

No VAT/GST/Sales Tax applies (Gibraltar is excluded from the UK VAT territory and the EU VAT area; indirect taxation is limited to import/export duties, excise taxes on specified goods, and minor transfer taxes)

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A — no VAT/GST registration threshold exists because Gibraltar levies no general consumption tax. For cold-storage operators, an Authorised Warehouse / Temporary Storage (AW/TS) customs licence is required to store imported dutiable goods duty-suspended, with no turnover-based registration obligation.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable in the strict VAT sense as Gibraltar has no VAT/GST; therefore there is no 0% export VAT rate to claim. For customs purposes, outbound shipments of pharmaceutical goods (including temperature-controlled biologics) from Gibraltar require an export declaration for customs control; where goods were previously imported under duty-suspension (bonded cold store), a duty drawback or temporary-admission repayment claim may be made upon proof of physical export outside Gibraltar/Gibraltar customs territory.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document - SAD/C88 duly filed via GAM)
  • Commercial invoice and temperature-monitored packing list evidencing cold-chain continuity (2–8°C or defined regulated range)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill issued by a licensed carrier evidencing departure from Gibraltar port/airport
  • Certificate of Origin (where reduced duty or preferential treatment under the Gibraltar-EU trade arrangement is claimed)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (documented bank receipt showing export proceeds credited from the foreign buyer)

Gibraltar operates no real-time fiscal clearance, mandatory B2B e-invoicing, continuous transaction controls, or digital stamping. Invoicing is governed by the Commercial Law and accepted in standard electronic formats (UBL/PEPPOL is voluntary). Customs clearances must be submitted via the Gibraltar Automated Movement (GAM) electronic export module, transmitting the Single Administrative Document (SAD/C88) and Export Control List to UK HMRC for Union/common-transit movement, without any tax-authority digital signing requirement.

Gibraltar Customs and the Income Tax Office jointly conduct retrospective audits for export and drawback declarations for up to six years. A false or fraudulent export declaration triggers, under the Customs Ordinance, a fine of up to £10,000 and/or imprisonment up to two years, plus recovery of the duty originally relieved plus an additional penalty equal to 100% of the duty avoided. Deliberate misdeclaration of non-resident status for corporate tax gives rise under the Income Tax Act to civil penalties of up to 200% of tax undercharged, with interest accruing from the original due date; factsheet-driven clawback of any duty-free relievance and tax loss are enforced by distraint against local assets.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar operates a full-convertibility currency-board-equivalent regime with no residual exchange controls on current or capital account transactions. GIP and GBP circulate interchangeably at par; EUR, USD and other currencies may be held freely in non-resident accounts. Profit remittance is unrestricted: the Cayman exempted company may distribute dividends, pay intercompany service fees, and repatriate liquidation proceeds without prior governmental approval. Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on dividends, interest or royalties paid to non-residents, and the Cayman Islands impose no income tax on the exempted company; the only taxation variable is the residence jurisdiction of the ultimate shareholders. Practical frictions are limited to commercial dealing spreads of 0.00 to 0.25 percent on GIP/GBP conversion and normal EUR/USD correspondent clearing costs.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Gibraltar maintains no statutory surrender, repatriation, or mandatory conversion rules for export receipts. A refrigerated-logistics operator may retain 100 percent of export proceeds in any freely convertible currency in any jurisdiction for an indefinite period. The only binding obligations are commercial and reputational: GFSC record-keeping and sanctions-screening duties under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015, and extraterritorial tax reporting imposed on the shareholders' residence state (for example, UK CFC rules where a UK-resident individual or company holds a controlling interest).

Central bank supervision: HM Government of Gibraltar (Ministry of Finance) acts as the currency and note-issuing authority under the Currency Notes Act 1934; the Gibraltar pound (GIP) is issued at a fixed 1:1 peg to GBP and is 100% backed by Bank of England notes held in the Currency Note Redemption Reserve. Banking and payment-institution supervision is exercised exclusively by the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) under the Financial Services Act 2019, the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015, and the GFSC Banking Rules. The Bank of England sets the monetary policy anchor for GBP but exercises no direct supervisory duty over Gibraltar-licensed banks.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM. UK FCA-authorised EMIs (Airwallex, Wise Business, Ebury) deliver superior multi-currency clearing and lower FX spreads but cannot substitute for a GFSC-supervised bank where statutory escrow, trade finance, or documentary collections are required. Post-Brexit, no EEA EMI passport extends automatically into Gibraltar; the EMI must hold a UK FCA licence and service the Cayman structure through a Gibraltar-licensed payment agent or the firm's own Gibraltar-serviced relationship account. Airwallex and Ebury are the most viable for the Cayman exempted company provided it evidences a genuine Gibraltar nexus: a licensed fiduciary registered office, a Gibraltar management agreement, and an enforceable cold-storage operating contract. Wise Business is acceptable only for low-value operating disbursements. These EMIs are not permitted to hold the capital escrow for the initial injection.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound share-capital injections settle via SWIFT MT103 or ISO 20022 pacs.008 using ISO purpose code TIER (transfer of equity capital) and, in the UK remitting leg, the Bank of England purpose category C (capital transaction). The remitting financial institution must include the Cayman company's Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) and the share-allotment reference. The receiving Gibraltar bank requires: the Cayman board resolution approving the allotment, the updated Register of Members, a source-of-funds declaration, and confirmation that the GIP/GBP enquiry account receives the proceeds at par. GBP-denominated injections may be routed through Barclays' UK presence via CHAPS and re-booked to the Gibraltar Branch at par; USD and EUR injections use their respective correspondent networks with the message text stating 'CROSS-BORDER CAPITAL INJECTION - NO EXCHANGE-CONTROL DECLARATION REQUIRED UNDER GIBRALTAR LAW'.Operational payments for freight, warehousing, and pharmaceutical procurement clear through SWIFT MT103 wire transfers referencing the vendor invoice and the applicable Incoterms 2020 rule. Where suppliers of temperature-controlled equipment or pharmaceutical products require trade-finance cover, commercial Letters of Credit (SWIFT MT700) and documentary collections (SWIFT MT400/MT410) are available through the Barclays Gibraltar trade desk. Domestic sterling and GIP payments clear via Pay.UK CHAPS (high value) and Faster Payments (low value); euro payments settle through TARGET2 via EU correspondents; US-dollar payments settle via CHIPS and Fedwire using Barclays Bank PLC New York as correspondent.
