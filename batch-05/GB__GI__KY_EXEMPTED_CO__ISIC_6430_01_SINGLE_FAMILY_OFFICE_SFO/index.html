Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Cayman Islands General Registry — Companies Registration Office, Government Administration Building, 133 Elgin Avenue, George Town, Grand Cayman KY1-9000.

Governing statute

Companies Act (2023 Revision) of the Cayman Islands, sections 4, 5, 7, 9, 10, 11, 13, 27, 30, 31 and 32; Exempted Company provisions under Part VI. Incidental regulation by the Companies Management Act (2023 Revision) and the Beneficial Ownership (Companies) Regulations.

Formation / constitutional filings

Memorandum and Articles of Association together with Form 1A (Application for Registration of an Exempted Company), registered office consent, and consents of first directors and secretary.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 25 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital. The Memorandum must state an authorized share capital, typically US$50,000, to keep the government filing fee at US$850. No escrow account is required for a passive single family office; banking counterparties may internally require an initial allocation of working capital before account opening.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety clearance from the Gibraltar Fire and Rescue Service or the Cayman Islands Fire Service, as applicable, including a fire certificate and emergency evacuation plan. A structural load inspection certificate from an approved structural engineer is required if any fit-out or partition works are undertaken. Workplace clearance must conform to the Gibraltar Health & Safety at Work Act and the Cayman Islands Labour Code, including employer's liability insurance, first-aid provision, and an electrical installation certificate issued by a certified electrician.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital. The Memorandum must state an authorized share capital, typically US$50,000, to keep the government filing fee at US$850. No escrow account is required for a passive single family office; banking counterparties may internally require an initial allocation of working capital before account opening.

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Not applicable — the Single Family Office is not an industrial undertaking and requires no manufacturing or industrial license. If a physical office is established in Gibraltar, the relevant authority is the Development and Planning Commission for planning consent and the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit for AML registration matters. In the Cayman Islands, the Department of Planning is consulted only for fit-out approvals.

Category: No operating or manufacturing permit is required for a private, non-regulated family office. If a branch is registered in Gibraltar, the operative instrument is the Overseas Company Registration Certificate under the Gibraltar Companies Act 2014; no Financial Services Act 2019 authorization is required unless the office provides investment services to third parties or custodial activities to unrelated persons.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, Government of Gibraltar; primary electronic portal: iTax (e-filing of tax returns, TIN applications, and company registration updates). Gibraltar is a British Overseas Territory with an autonomous tax administration fully distinct from HMRC; the UK-Gibraltar Double Taxation Arrangement 2019 governs residence tie-breakers, exchange of information, and mutual assistance; customs matters are administered separately by the Gibraltar Customs Division.

E-government filing portal

Overseas-incorporated entity procedure for the Cayman Exempted Company (SFO): (1) register via the iTax portal or by written application to the Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TIN), submitting certified Cayman incorporation documents (Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles of Association), register of directors and members, and a Central Management and Control (CM&C) statement; (2) if CM&C or the place of effective management is located in Gibraltar, the company is statutorily deemed Gibraltar-resident and must file annual corporation tax returns with an elected accounting period (12.5% standard corporate rate; 10% applicable to regulated financial-services entities); (3) if not resident and carrying on no trade through a Gibraltar permanent establishment (PE), no Gibraltar income-tax registration is mandatory, though voluntary registration is available for a binding residence determination; (4) if a Gibraltar PE exists, registration is limited to PE-attributable territorial profits and a resident tax agent must be appointed; (5) no licence, minimum capital, or regulatory authorisation is required for ISIC 6430/NAICS 525920 private family investment holding; TIN issuance is typically completed within 3-5 business days; no Gibraltar stamp duty arises on initial registration.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone (Cayman domestic status: the Exempted Company is exempt from Cayman Islands income tax on non-Cayman-source income and is conclusively non-transparent). Gibraltar qualification: the SFO is not ordinarily resident unless managed-and-controlled in Gibraltar; profits falling within Gibraltar's territorial source rules, or attributable to a Gibraltar PE, would be charged at entity level at 12.5%; pure non-resident passive investment, custody, and family-office administration with no Gibraltar-source profit attracts no Gibraltar tax exposure. — Overseas-incorporated entity procedure for the Cayman Exempted Company (SFO): (1) register via the iTax portal or by written application to the Income Tax Office for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TIN), submitting certified Cayman incorporation documents (Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles of Association), register of directors and members, and a Central Management and Control (CM&C) statement; (2) if CM&C or the place of effective management is located in Gibraltar, the company is statutorily deemed Gibraltar-resident and must file annual corporation tax returns with an elected accounting period (12.5% standard corporate rate; 10% applicable to regulated financial-services entities); (3) if not resident and carrying on no trade through a Gibraltar permanent establishment (PE), no Gibraltar income-tax registration is mandatory, though voluntary registration is available for a binding residence determination; (4) if a Gibraltar PE exists, registration is limited to PE-attributable territorial profits and a resident tax agent must be appointed; (5) no licence, minimum capital, or regulatory authorisation is required for ISIC 6430/NAICS 525920 private family investment holding; TIN issuance is typically completed within 3-5 business days; no Gibraltar stamp duty arises on initial registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (Cayman domestic status: the Exempted Company is exempt from Cayman Islands income tax on non-Cayman-source income and is conclusively non-transparent). Gibraltar qualification: the SFO is not ordinarily resident unless managed-and-controlled in Gibraltar; profits falling within Gibraltar's territorial source rules, or attributable to a Gibraltar PE, would be charged at entity level at 12.5%; pure non-resident passive investment, custody, and family-office administration with no Gibraltar-source profit attracts no Gibraltar tax exposure.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box regime exists; classification follows conclusive corporate legal form. Available statutory elections and rulings: (a) application or binding ruling to the Commissioner of Income Tax confirming non-residence, residence, or PE-attribution status for UK-Gibraltar DTA purposes; (b) participation-exemption election for qualifying dividends and disposal gains (subject to the subject-to-tax limbs, a zero-tax Cayman subsidiary will ordinarily fail the qualifying-holding conditions); (c) Gibraltar group-relief election between 75%+ Gibraltar-resident group members; (d) accounting-period, capital-allowance, loss-relief, and branch-attribution elections under the Income Tax Act 2010 (as amended); (e) hybrid-mismatch documentation election evidencing that the Cayman vehicle is non-transparent, preventing deduction-without-inclusion outcomes in Gibraltar/UK-linked structures; the pre-2011 exempt-company regime is repealed and cannot be elected.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar Customs Import Duty only (no VAT, GST, or sales-and-use tax in force; Gibraltar is outside the EU and UK VAT regimes).

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Not applicable - no VAT/GST registration threshold exists because no VAT/GST is levied; the local-currency threshold is nil. Any entity importing or exporting physical goods must register with the Gibraltar Customs Division for a Customs Registration Number (CRN) from the first consignment, with no de minimis exemption; the SFO, engaged solely in services, has no goods-trade registration trigger.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Not applicable - Gibraltar levies no VAT, so no 0% export-VAT zero-rating claim or input-VAT refund mechanism exists. Goods exported from Gibraltar are outside the scope of Gibraltar import duty, with duty-drawback and transit procedures available under the Customs Duties Ordinance for re-exported goods; cross-border services supplied by the SFO (family-office management, investment advisory, custody oversight) to non-resident family trusts and structures are not subject to any Gibraltar indirect tax and require no zero-rating election.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic ASYCUDA World entry)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure from Gibraltar
  • Certificate of Origin (where preferential duty or trade-arrangement treatment is invoked)
  • Transit or Re-export Evidence (customs-warehouse removal or in-transit movement document)
  • Bank transfer or SWIFT payment confirmation (Gibraltar imposes no inward-remittance certificate requirement)

No statutory real-time e-invoicing, fiscal-clearance, digital-stamping, or continuous-transaction-control mandate in Gibraltar. B2B/B2C invoices require only standard statutory particulars (supplier and recipient identity, date, description, consideration, and any applicable duty notation). Customs declarations are cleared electronically via the ASYCUDA World customs-management system; income tax filings are made through the iTax portal; the UK Making Tax Digital requirements do not extend to Gibraltar.

No export-VAT zero-rating fraud or clawback regime exists. Sanctions relevant to the SFO: (i) false export declarations or unlawful duty-exemption/drawback claims attract Customs Duties Ordinance penalties of up to GBP 10,000 and/or three times the value of the goods, plus forfeiture of the goods; (ii) fraudulent or negligent income-tax filings, including false assertions of non-residence or absence of a PE, expose the entity to retrospective assessments beyond the ordinary six-year limitation period, penalties up to 100% of the tax undercharged, and interest; (iii) misuse of UK-Gibraltar DTA benefits triggers treaty-abuse clawback and reporting under Gibraltar's mandatory-disclosure (DAC-6 equivalent) rules; (iv) any tax misstatement is referable by the Income Tax Office to the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission as a compliance-integrity matter and may prejudice the banking, custody, and AML standing of the family-office structure.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Gibraltar operates a fully liberalised capital and current account regime modelled on the United Kingdom. The GIP is freely convertible at par into GBP and both currencies circulate interchangeably. There are no exchange controls, no differentiated capital-versus-current-account treatment, and no restrictions on cross-border dividend, interest, fee or capital-account remittance to non-resident shareholders of the Cayman Exempted Company. The Cayman Islands side equally imposes no withholding, exchange-control or convertibility obstacle for profits or investment principal remitted onward to Gibraltar or to any parent family entity. The only practical hurdle is demonstrating substance and compliance with the OECD/CbCR and Economic Substance requirements, which must be evidenced through board minutes, managed offices, local governance and accounting records.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Gibraltar imposes no statutory timeline, no surrender obligation and no mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds or any other foreign currency receipts. For a Single Family Office classified under ISIC 6430, no trade-export proceeds arise; USD, EUR, GBP and other holdings may be kept freely, converted at market rates or deployed offshore without central bank approval. Mandatory conversion ratio: 0%.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) acting as the statutory financial services regulator and de facto foreign exchange administration; the Government of Gibraltar Ministry of Finance exercises the note-issuing power for the Gibraltar pound (GIP); monetary policy anchor and lender-of-last-resort functions rest with the Bank of England because the GIP is pegged at par to GBP.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low-to-Medium viability. Gibraltar is outside the EU/EEA, and UK FCA-authorised or EU-authorised electronic money institutions do not benefit from passporting into Gibraltar. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise, Revolut or Currencycloud may only attempt unregulated cross-border onboarding, and most automatically decline a Cayman Exempted Company in the Medium_Risk_Enhanced tier unless the entity holds a Gibraltar Economic Substance Certificate, audited financials and a complete UBO file. GFSC-licensed e-money institutions exist but are primarily retail-focus-ed and do not provide securities custody clearing. Feasibility rating: Low for a pure Cayman shelf without substance; Medium for a fully substance-compliant SFO with accounts, local director and professional advisors; recommended route remains tier-1 private banks.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Defense evidence: Substance file containing the Cayman Economic Substance Notification and Economic Substance Return, Gibraltar board minutes evidencing management and control, office lease, employment or service agreements with a named investment manager, and OECD CRS/CbCR declarations where applicable.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant route: family founder or settlor bank, acting via a UK, EU, USD or Cayman settlement bank, transmits SWIFT MT103 or MT202 COV to the GFSC-licensed Gibraltar receiving bank (Jyske, Turicum or Safra). Protocol: the remitting bank must state the exact legal name of the Cayman Exempted Company in the beneficiary line; the remittance narrative should read as capital injection for a family office investment vehicle under ISIC 6430; where ISO 20022 purpose codes are required, use ExternalPurposeCode INVE for investment or capital contribution. No Gibraltar exchange-control permit is required. The receiving bank must complete a funds-arrival AML review for 24 to 48 hours before crediting, and documentary verification must include the certificate of good standing, board resolution authorising the capital increase, passport copies of the ultimate beneficial owner, and the source-of-funds evidence filed in the Single Customer View record.Rails: SWIFT MT103 for non-trade operating disbursements to custodians, investment managers and professional service providers; MT202 or MT202 COV for interbank cover and liquidity repositioning; CHAPS for same-day GBP settlement with UK private banks; SEPA Instant and EUR wire for EU subscriptions and custody flows; Fedwire or CHIPS for USD trades with US prime brokers and securities custodians. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)
