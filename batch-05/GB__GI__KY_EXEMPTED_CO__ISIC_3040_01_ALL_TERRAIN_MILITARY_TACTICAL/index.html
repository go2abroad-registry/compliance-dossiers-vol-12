Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar – Customs & Tax Authority (CTA); online portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation

E-government filing portal

A Cayman Exempted Company, being a non-Gibraltarian legal entity, must first file under the Gibraltar Companies Registration procedure with the Registrar of Companies obtaining a Gibraltar company number. It thereafter applies to the Commissioner of Income Tax for a Gibraltar Tax Reference Number using Form GI-105. If the entity undertakes import/export activity, a parallel registration with the Gibraltar Customs & Tax Authority for an Importer/Exporter Code is required. No automatic Gibraltar income tax registration is triggered solely by incorporation elsewhere; a non-resident entity is registrable only if it acquires Gibraltar tax residence or has a taxable presence (fixed place of business/permanent establishment). A Gibraltar-resident tax representative is recommended and may be statutorily required for compliance and service of process.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Cayman Exempted Companies may elect to be treated as non-resident for Gibraltar income tax purposes if management and control are exercised outside Gibraltar. Under hybrid mismatch rules enacted through the Gibraltar Income Tax (Amendment) Regulations 2019, an entity may be required to file notifications for hybrid mismatch arrangements even where no check-the-box election exists in either Cayman or Gibraltar. A Gibraltar qualifying company election (when available) may be made to obtain income tax exemption; for this offshore entity, no check-the-box election is required.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

VAT

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general VAT registration threshold applies because Gibraltar operates a territorial indirect tax system with a nil VAT headline rate. Customs and excise registrations are mandatory for any importer or exporter of controlled goods, including military tactical hardware, irrespective of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available for (i) physical export of goods from Gibraltar to non-Gibraltarian destinations, (ii) supplies of services directly related to such goods, and (iii) defence procurement goods supplied to foreign governments through diplomatic channels. Although the headline VAT rate is nil, the practical benefit is exemption from import/export levies and customs duty relief on qualifying re-exports. The exporter must hold and present evidence that the goods left Gibraltar and that title and control passed to the non-resident buyer (e.g., foreign government procurement contract or diplomatic vehicle).

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

Gibraltar does not currently mandate real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping for VAT. However, all export consignments must be cleared electronically via the Gibraltar Customs single administrative system and the UK/Gibraltar customs declaration processing platform (Customs Declaration Service). B2B export invoices must comply with the Gibraltar VAT (if any) and UK HM Revenue & Customs cross-border record-keeping requirements, including digital transmission of customs data through the approved API gateway.

Gibraltar civil penalties for abusive export zero-rating and duty relief claims range from 100% to 200% of the avoided duty, plus interest and late-payment surcharge; criminal prosecution may result in imprisonment up to seven years. A retrospective clawback of every zero-rated benefit is imposed where export documentation is falsified, goods are returned without notification, or the inward remittance is not received within the 180-day window; reinstated deemed VAT duty becomes immediately enforceable.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is freely convertible into GBP at a fixed 1:1 peg; EUR, USD and GBP are widely accepted in settlement systems. Neither Gibraltar nor the Cayman Islands maintains capital account or current account restrictions on non-resident companies. Profit and dividend remittances from Gibraltar to a Cayman parent are fully unrestricted; there are no surrender, conversion, or approval hurdles for current-account transactions. Sanctions screening obligations under UK/Gibraltar regulations may, however, prevent transactions to restricted counterparties.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender timeline or mandatory conversion ratios apply to export receipts in Gibraltar or the Cayman Islands. A Cayman Exempted Company may retain export proceeds in any freely convertible currency in its Gibraltar bank accounts. Reporting lies only under AML/CFT thresholds and sanctions compliance; there are no central-bank conversion quotas.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (sovereign central monetary authority for GBP payment systems); Government of Gibraltar (issuer of Gibraltar pound, pegged 1:1 to GBP); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) (banking and payments licensor); Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA) (home financial regulator of the Cayman entity)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Offshore digital EMIs and neobanks will generally decline a manufacturing company in the armored vehicle sector under their acceptable-use policies. Gibraltar-licensed EMIs are technically permitted under the Financial Services Act 2019 (regulatory category 'Electronic Money Institution'), but they are categorically not viable for defense-related LCs and high-value wires; UK-EMIs no longer have passporting rights into Gibraltar post-Brexit and typically disallow 'military/defense' business codes.

Initial penalty of £10,000; further daily penalty of £500 per day after the initial notice period, capped at a total of £100,000; additional discretion for the Commissioner to strike off the company or issue personal liability orders against directors.
ESR-3 – Substance Indicators Employees, physical premises, premises costs, operating expenditure, and records retained in Gibraltar. Provide exact headcount in Gibraltar, square footage, lease agreement, payroll, and bank statements proving real commercial substance. Initial penalty of £10,000; further daily penalty of £500 per day after the initial notice period, capped at a total of £100,000; additional discretion for the Commissioner to strike off the company or issue personal liability orders against directors.
ESR-4 – Tax Residence Declaration Declaration of jurisdiction of tax residence and basis of residence (incorporation versus management and control). Non-resident Cayman companies must attach a Cayman Tax Information Authority certificate confirming residence status and explain how the central management and control is exercised outside Gibraltar. Initial penalty of £10,000; further daily penalty of £500 per day after the initial notice period, capped at a total of £100,000; additional discretion for the Commissioner to strike off the company or issue personal liability orders against directors.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GBP 1,000,000 aggregate annual controlled/related-party transactions; a Gibraltar Local File is mandatory above this threshold. Group Master File may be required where the consolidated group revenue equals or exceeds EUR 750 million.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar applies the Income Tax (Economic Substance) Regulations 2019. A Cayman Exempted Company has economic substance exposure if it is incorporated or registered in Gibraltar, managed and controlled from Gibraltar, or carries on a relevant activity through a Gibraltar branch. For the manufacture and sale of all-terrain military tactical vehicles, the relevant activity is commonly categorized as a distribution and service centre CIGA. The company must demonstrate full-time employees, premises, and operating expenditure in Gibraltar commensurate with the volume of activity and must maintain records confirming board meetings, strategic decision-making, and actual performance of core income-generating activities in Gibraltar.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion by False Return

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty up to 100% of understated tax plus interest; criminal conviction on summary liability to a fine up to £500,000 and/or imprisonment up to 7 years; on indictment an unlimited fine and imprisonment up to 7 years.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign or cause a false return may have the corporate veil pierced; they are jointly and severally liable for unpaid taxes, interest and penalties. Courts can impose travel bans, asset freezing orders, director disqualification, and custodial sentences.
Sham Company and Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: ESR penalty of £10,000, daily £500 penalty up to £100,000, recharacterization of tax residence, 12.5% assessment on all Gibraltar-allocable profits, exchange-of-information findings, and potential strike-off by the Gibraltar Companies Registry.

Director personal liability: Individual directors can be made respondents to substance penalty proceedings, ordered to pay daily default penalties personally, disqualified for up to 15 years, and their personal assets subject to freezing orders if the company is found to be a façade.
Unlicensed Defense Trade and Export Control Violation

Statutory sanction: Unlimited fine and/or imprisonment up to 10 years, forfeiture of the vehicles and related hardware, revocation of export licenses, and blocking of government wire transfers or diplomatic letters of credit under applicable UN/EU/UK sanctions.

Director personal liability: Directors may be prosecuted for consent, connivance or neglect, with personal criminal liability, confiscation orders, travel bans, extradition, and director disqualification for up to 15 years.
Transfer Pricing Misstatement

Statutory sanction: Tax adjustment to chargeable profits, 12.5% additional tax, late payment interest, careless penalty up to 50% of understated tax, deliberate penalty up to 100%; failure to produce a Local File attracts fixed penalties and daily surcharges.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign intercompany invoices or TP documentation without commercial basis may be personally assessed following veil-piercing, with negligent misstatement penalties, disqualification, and liability for any resulting unpaid tax.
Sanctions Evasion and Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Offences under the Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and Sanctions Regulations carry imprisonment up to 14 years, unlimited fines, asset forfeiture, inclusion in consolidated sanctions enforcement lists, and revocation of banking privileges.

Director personal liability: Directors with actual or constructive knowledge of tainted funds face personal criminal exposure, international travel bans, arrest warrants, proceeds-of-crime confiscation orders, and personal restitution orders for the sums laundered through the company.
