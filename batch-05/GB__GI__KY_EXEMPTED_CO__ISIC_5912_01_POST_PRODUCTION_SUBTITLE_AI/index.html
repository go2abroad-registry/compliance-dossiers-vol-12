Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Commissioner of Income Tax, Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (portal: https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation); HM Customs Gibraltar for customs and excise; and, for UK VAT and customs, HM Revenue & Customs (portal: https://www.gov.uk/hmrc).

E-government filing portal

A Cayman Exempted Company is not issued a Gibraltar TIN merely by reason of Cayman incorporation. A Gibraltar tax reference number is required only after the entity creates a permanent establishment, hires a Gibraltar-based employee, or derives Gibraltar-source income. The foreign entity must also file an overseas company registration at Gibraltar Companies House and submit certified constitutional documents, certificate of incorporation, beneficial ownership statement, director identification, Gibraltar registered office or branch address, and an expected-income declaration to the Income Tax Office. Gibraltar does not issue a VAT ID because it has no VAT regime. If the company supplies digital services to UK consumers from outside the UK, a separate UK VAT registration with HMRC is required from the first taxable supply.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Gibraltar has no check-the-box election. Entity classification follows the Companies Act and tax-residence case law: a foreign company is non-resident if central management and control is outside Gibraltar and is taxed only on Gibraltar-source income. No hybrid mismatch election is available; OECD and UK hybrid mismatch rules apply to deductible payments and disregarded entities. A Cayman Exempted Company retains its Cayman exempted status and may obtain a Cayman tax-residency certificate solely for CRS/FATCA evidentiary purposes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar: no VAT or general sales tax; customs duty and excise duty apply to selected imports. UK: VAT applies to supplies made in the UK or supplied to UK consumers.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Gibraltar: no VAT/GST registration threshold applies to any turnover. UK: a supplier established in the UK must register for VAT when taxable turnover exceeds GBP 90,000 (2024-25); a non-established supplier of digital services to UK consumers must register from the first sale, with no threshold, using HMRC's Non-Established Taxable Person scheme.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Gibraltar does not operate VAT, so exports from Gibraltar are outside the scope of Gibraltar indirect tax rather than zero-rated. For UK VAT purposes, zero-rating for goods exports requires evidence that the goods physically left the UK; cross-border digital services supplied to an overseas business may be outside the UK VAT scope where the place of supply is abroad. Automated subtitle and AI voice-cloning services exported from Gibraltar to non-UK customers are generally outside UK VAT, while B2C digital services supplied to UK consumers are subject to UK VAT at the standard rate unless an exemption applies.

  • Gibraltar/UK Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World or CDS entry), Bill of Lading or air waybill, and commercial invoice.
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit confirmation in the exporter's foreign-currency account.
  • Executed service contract, completion certificate, place-of-supply evidence, and IP-address/billing-address evidence where a UK consumer is involved.

Gibraltar does not impose real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, or digital stamping. UK has no continuous transaction control mandate for B2B digital services; electronic invoices are accepted where they satisfy VAT invoice validity requirements, and Making Tax Digital requires digital record-keeping and HMRC API-based VAT return filing. Gibraltar customs declarations are lodged electronically through ASYCUDA World.

Unsupported or fraudulent export zero-rating claims are subject to retrospective VAT assessment, disgorgement of input VAT, interest, and penalties up to 100% of the VAT evaded; dishonest evasion may be prosecuted under the Customs and Excise Management Act 1979 and Value Added Tax Act 1994, with unlimited fines and imprisonment. Gibraltar customs irregularities trigger duty clawback, monetary penalties under Gibraltar customs enforcement legislation, and criminal liability in serious cases. A Cayman Exempted Company engaged in such conduct may also face adverse substance and reputation findings under applicable economic substance and anti-abuse rules.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is at statutory parity with, and fully convertible into, pound sterling (GBP), although it is legal tender only in Gibraltar. Neither Gibraltar nor the Cayman Islands maintains exchange-control restrictions on capital or current-account transactions. The Cayman Exempted Company may remit profits freely: Cayman imposes no withholding tax on dividends, interest, royalties, or technical fees paid to non-residents, and Gibraltar levies no withholding taxes on distributions or cross-border payments to non-residents. Practical hurdles are limited to (i) demonstrating adequate economic substance either in the Cayman Islands (Economic Substance Act 2018, as revised, and DITC notification obligations) or in Gibraltar (Income Tax Act 2010 substance rules applicable to a foreign-company branch), (ii) filing CRS/FATCA self-certifications and beneficial-ownership registers, and (iii) satisfying bank-level enhanced due diligence arising from the zero-tax jurisdiction and the non-resident-director profile.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Neither Gibraltar nor the Cayman Islands imposes any statutory surrender, repatriation, or mandatory conversion requirement on export proceeds or any current-account receipts, including subtitle-transcription, AI voice-cloning, and platform-distribution revenues. Such receipts may be retained indefinitely in the invoicing currency, and any conversion into GIP, GBP, EUR, or USD is at the sole commercial discretion of the treasury function.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary anchor; Gibraltar pound issued at par and fully backed by sterling); Government of Gibraltar, acting through the Commissioner of Currency (currency issuance function); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) (prudential and AML/CFT supervision of banks, EMIs, and payment service licensees).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Medium-High viability with structural caveats. Wise, a UK FCA-authorised Electronic Money Institution, represents the most practical cross-border clearing rail, but only if the Cayman Exempted Company is first registered in Gibraltar as an external company under the Companies Act 2014, giving it a Gibraltar regulatory identity, a local registered address, and a UK/Gibraltar settlement profile acceptable for Wise Business onboarding. Stripe viability is Medium only through an intermediate UK-incorporated operating company or a UK branch of the Cayman entity onboarded via Stripe UK Ltd, because Stripe does not list Gibraltar or the Cayman Islands as supported primary countries. EMIs domiciled solely in offshore centres and lacking an EEA/UK passport or a Gibraltar payments licence are not viable for GFSC-supervised banking corridors and should be excluded from the clearing stack.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Share-capital injections follow the SWIFT MT103 protocol from the Cayman shareholder's bank to the Gibraltar settlement account, with the payment-purpose field populated using ISO 20022 code 'EQUI' (equity transaction) or the equivalent bank-specified rationale of 'Capital injection / paid-up share capital'. For same-day GBP liquidity, the receiving Gibraltar bank settles through the Bank of England RTGS/CHAPS corridor using its UK clearing membership; USD inbound legs clear over Fedwire or CHIPS through the bank's NOSTRO network. The compliance bundle must include the board capitalisation resolution, an up-to-date Cayman register-of-members extract, CRS/FATCA self-certifications, and source-of-funds confirmation, but no central-bank permit or exchange-control approval is required in either Gibraltar or the Cayman Islands.Outbound settlement uses a multi-rail structure: (i) SWIFT MT103/MT202 for USD and EUR vendor payments from Gibraltar NOSTRO accounts; (ii) UK CHAPS or Faster Payments for same-day GBP denominated payments to subtitle contractors and platform suppliers; (iii) SEPA credit transfers through an EU correspondent bank for Euro beneficiaries; (iv) commercial letters of credit, governed by UCP 600 and Incoterms 2020, advised through GFSC-regulated banks for high-value hardware, GPU server, and licensing acquisitions; and (v) Wise Business batch API for recurring low-value contractor payouts. Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The return for an accounting period must be filed within 12 months of the period end; in practice the deadline is aligned to the 30 June year of assessment, requiring filing no later than 30 June of the following calendar year.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: A Gibraltar Local File is required when the value of controlled transactions exceeds GIP 1,000,000 in a fiscal period; a Master File is required when the Gibraltar entity belongs to an MNE group with consolidated revenue at or above GIP 606,000,000 (equivalent to €750,000,000).

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar ESR 2019 applies to every tax-resident company, including a Cayman Exempted Company registered and managed from Gibraltar; likewise, the Cayman Islands Economic Substance Law applies to the Cayman-registered entity, requiring core income-generating activities to be performed, directed, and managed in the relevant jurisdiction with appropriate physical presence, qualified employees, and local expenditure.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Return Filing

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of up to 200% of the additional tax due, disgorgement of concealed profits, interest at the prevailing penalty rate, criminal fine not exceeding GIP 5,000,000, and imprisonment of responsible officers for up to 7 years.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced where the Cayman Exempted Company is a mere façade; directors and shadow directors become jointly and severally liable for evaded tax, are subject to asset freezes and travel bans, and may be declared personally bankrupt or disqualified from acting as a director.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Gibraltar ESR assessment and penalty up to GIP 100,000 per relevant activity, mandatory filing of contemporaneous substance documentation, strike-off of the foreign entity from the Gibraltar register, and referral to the Tax Information Authority for exchange with Cayman authorities.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly permit the sham are personally liable for penalties and enforcement costs; the Gibraltar High Court may issue a disqualification order prohibiting management of any Gibraltar company for up to 15 years and impose an imprisonment term of up to 2 years for contempt.
AML / Money Laundering through Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Fine up to GIP 2,000,000, confiscation of funds under the Proceeds of Crime Act, imposition of anti-money laundering remedial measures, and potential criminal imprisonment of the money laundering reporting officer for up to 14 years.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally accountable for every suspicious transaction routed through the Gibraltar bank accounts; civil restitution to victims is enforced, travel bans are automatically imposed pending investigation, and culpable directors may be sentenced to imprisonment of up to 14 years with personal liability for the full laundered amount.
