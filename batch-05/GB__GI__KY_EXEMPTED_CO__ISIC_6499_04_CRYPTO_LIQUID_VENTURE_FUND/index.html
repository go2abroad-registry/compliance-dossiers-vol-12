Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope — entity-level tax at the Gibraltar corporate headline rate of 10% on (i) income accruing in or derived from Gibraltar and (ii) profits of a trade exercised in Gibraltar. The CEC is an opaque, separate taxable person; there is no pass-through treatment. Foreign-source trading, yield, and custody income arising through non-Gibraltar exchanges and custodians falls outside the Gibraltar charge if the trade is not managed and controlled from Gibraltar. The entity's home status in the Cayman Islands remains Tax_Exempt_Zone (20-year tax exemption undertaking filed under the Cayman Companies Act ss.344-345), requiring annual renewal and reconciliation with Gibraltar economic substance filings.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists in Gibraltar; classification follows statute and place of management. Available mechanisms: (1) Qualifying Company election under the Income Tax (Qualifying Companies) Regulations 2011, as amended 2018: the Gibraltar branch may elect gross-based taxation computed by reference to payroll and occupied premises costs, producing an effective reduction of up to 45% of the 10% headline rate, subject to Commissioner approval and annual substance conditions; (2) Hybrid and reverse-hybrid mismatch notification under Income Tax Act 2010, s.68A (ATAD-aligned): any hybrid mismatch arrangement producing a deduction/no-inclusion outcome must be disclosed to the ITO; no opt-out is available and a denial of benefit plus penalty applies; (3) Treaty residence tie-breaker under the UK-Gibraltar DTA 2019 (in force 2020): if the entity is dual-resident in the UK and Gibraltar, residence is settled by mutual agreement procedure applying place of effective management; (4) The CEC must maintain its Cayman tax-exemption undertaking election, which is the foundational instrument upholding the Tax_Exempt_Zone characterization of the Cayman domicile.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GBP and Gibraltar Pound (GIP) are freely convertible on both current and capital accounts; Gibraltar operates no exchange controls and the GIP is pegged at par to GBP with unrestricted exchangeability. The Cayman Islands maintain a fixed KYD/USD peg (1 KYD = 1.20 USD) with no exchange-control restrictions on inward or outward transfers. Profit remittance and dividend repatriation are unrestricted for a Cayman Exempted Company subject to standard AML/KYC verification of beneficial ownership and tax-residency certification. No withholding taxes apply to outbound dividends or interest under the Cayman or Gibraltar tax regimes.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export-proceeds surrender obligation or mandatory conversion ratio in the United Kingdom, Gibraltar, or the Cayman Islands. Exporters and investment funds may retain foreign-currency receipts offshore without forced conversion into local currency. Fund liquidation proceeds received by a GFSC-supervised entity must be credited to a licensed Gibraltar bank account and are subject to FATF-aligned record-keeping, travel-rule reporting for virtual-asset transfers, and a five-year document retention requirement.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority for GBP); Her Majesty's Treasury (exchange control, sanctions and counter-terrorist financing administration); Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC) (licensing and AML/CFT supervision in Gibraltar); Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA) (regulatory authority for Cayman-domiciled fund banking and custody).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-to-Low. Gibraltar-licensed EMI institutions under the Financial Services Act may provide GBP-denominated payment accounts for the fund, but most cross-border digital EMIs authorised by the FCA or under EU PSD2 apply blanket product bans to crypto hedge funds. EMIs that accept cryptocurrency counterparties will require a GFSC- or FCA-regulated digital-asset custodian, a certified chain-analysis report, audited proof of crypto reserves, and evidence of financial-promotions approval. USD and EUR rail capacity remains constrained by correspondent-bank de-risking policies.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% flat rate on Gibraltar-source profits. A Cayman Exempted Company managed and controlled from Gibraltar is taxable as a Gibraltar-resident company; if no place of management and control exists in Gibraltar, only Gibraltar-source income is assessable.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. Gibraltar does not impose withholding tax on dividends, profit distributions or branch remittances; the Gibraltar operation may remit profits to the Cayman Exempted Company free of tax at source.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 aggregate value of cross-border related-party transactions in a chargeable period triggers the mandatory Local File; the Master File is required where the global group's consolidated turnover exceeds GIP 50,000,000. A simplified contemporaneous transfer pricing summary (OECD-aligned) is recommended for controlled transactions above GIP 100,000 regardless of filing thresholds.

Economic substance scope: Gibraltar's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) 2019 capture relevant activities including fund management, financing and DLT/VCASP activities. The crypto liquid venture fund's core income-generating activities (trade initiation, execution, yield optimisation and risk management) must be directed and managed in Gibraltar. The company must (a) maintain a registered office and physical premises; (b) convene at least two board meetings per annum in Gibraltar with a quorum of locally resident directors; (c) employ adequate full-time staff in Gibraltar proportionate to the activity; and (d) file an annual ESR return (Form ESR-1) within the prescribed period. Pure equity-holding status is unavailable because the fund undertakes active trading. Substance must be independently evidenced by board minutes, employment records and Fireblocks/API trade logs.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Understatement of Crypto Gains

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of up to 100% of the tax evaded plus interest at 8% per annum; criminal prosecution on indictment with an unlimited fine; forfeiture of underlying crypto and fiat assets under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015; mandatory disclosure to the Gibraltar Financial Intelligence Unit and automatic exchange of information with the UK.

Director personal liability: Where fraud or sham is established, the corporate veil is pierced: directors and beneficial owners are jointly and severally liable for civil restitution; on conviction, imprisonment for up to 7 years, travel bans and international asset-freezing orders.
Economic Substance / Shell Company Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Initial financial penalty of GIP 10,000 rising to GIP 100,000 for continuing breach; daily default penalties of GIP 1,000 for late ESR filings; automatic exchange of information with EU tax authorities; compulsory strike-off and liquidation in repeated default cases.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors attesting false substance statements face disqualification for up to 15 years, personal fines up to GIP 120,000 and imprisonment up to 2 years; the Supreme Court may issue travel bans to secure attendance at enforcement proceedings.
Unlicensed Crypto-Asset Activity / AML and Sanctions Breach

Statutory sanction: Financial penalties of up to GIP 100,000 per violation plus daily penalties up to GIP 10,000; suspension or revocation of the DLT licence; GFSC-imposed restitution orders; confiscation of exchange accounts; referral to the Gibraltar police for money-laundering investigation.

Director personal liability: Directors are criminally liable for unlicensed activity: imprisonment up to 7 years, disqualification from holding financial-services directorships, personal liability for restitution, asset restraint orders over corporate and personal crypto wallets, and travel bans pending trial or extradition.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure / Intragroup Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: Tax-geared penalty of up to 100% of the additional tax assessed following an ability-to-pay adjustment, with no statutory cap; daily document-production penalties of GIP 500 per day; potential jeopardy assessment issued by the Commissioner to quarantine assets.

Director personal liability: Knowing participation in the documentation failure triggers joint and several personal liability where the entity defaults and is struck off; officers face additional penalties of up to GIP 10,000, disqualification, and immigration or travel restrictions in cross-border enforcement.
