Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Government of Gibraltar Income Tax Office (Commissioner of Income Tax) — portal: www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation; Registrar of Companies Gibraltar (www.companieshouse.gi) for ELP registration; HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) — Government Gateway via www.gov.uk/vat-registration for UK VAT obligations of non-established suppliers.

E-government filing portal

1) Form the Exempted Limited Partnership with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies. 2) File the ELP exemption application with the Commissioner of Income Tax under the Income Tax Act 2010 (s.44 and Part IX) demonstrating 100% non-resident partners and no Gibraltar-source trading; obtain the Gibraltar Tax Identification Number (TIN / Income Tax reference). 3) Produce the exemption certificate and file annual tax returns confirming continuation. 4) If selling digital subscriptions to UK consumers outside the App Store deemed-supplier rules, register for UK VAT with HMRC as a non-established taxable person using Form VAT1 (no UK establishment required), obtaining a GB VAT number. 5) Maintain evidence of non-resident status and economic substance filings (Taxes (Economic Substance) Regulations 2021) to preserve the exemption.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible — the Gibraltar ELP is fiscally transparent for income tax purposes; profits are allocated to partners and no Gibraltar tax is levied on foreign-source income where a valid exemption applies. Non-resident partners are outside the scope of Gibraltar income tax; an exempt ELP must not carry on trade or business in Gibraltar and must not derive income from Gibraltar sources.

Available election forms

Statutory exemption election under the Income Tax Act 2010 (irrevocable during the exemption period, subject to annual renewal); the ELP may not elect corporate taxation at entity level in Gibraltar. For foreign tax purposes, each partner may file US Form 8832 (check-the-box) to classify the ELP as partnership or association; the UK treats the ELP as transparent by default, with UK CTA 2010 Part 6A anti-hybrid rules acting automatically (no elective hybrid-mismatch election) to deny mismatched deductions where an opaque counterparty exists.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

Gibraltar has no VAT, GST, or sales and use tax (zero local indirect taxation). UK VAT at 20% applies only to B2C digital subscription supplies to UK consumers, collected either directly by the ELP (non-established UK taxable person) or indirectly through Apple IAP / Google Play Billing under the deemed-supplier provisions of VATA 1994.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Gibraltar: none — no local indirect tax or registration threshold in GIP. UK VAT: no threshold for non-established taxable persons supplying digital services; mandatory registration with HMRC from the first pound of UK B2C turnover. In practice, Apple and Google act as deemed suppliers and remit UK VAT on app subscriptions, removing the direct registration obligation of the underlying content provider.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Supplies of digital serialized web fiction subscriptions to non-UK customers are outside the scope of UK VAT (effectively zero-rated) where the ELP is UK VAT registered; to qualify, the ELP must hold evidence of the customer's place of residence or business location (billing address, IP address, bank country, or app store geo data). For Gibraltar purposes, foreign-source digital turnover is exempt from Gibraltar tax, not subject to any refund mechanism, and no Gibraltar zero-rating claim arises.

  • Digital export sales ledger with contract and subscription data
  • Customer location evidence (billing address, IP geolocation, card country for B2C; customer VAT number plus declaration for B2B)
  • Apple IAP / Google Play Billing settlement reports evidencing deemed-supplier VAT remittance
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or payment gateway FX credit statements proving export receipts within the prescribed deadline

Gibraltar: no e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, API stamping, or digital invoice mandate. UK VAT (if registered): Making Tax Digital (MTD) requires XML/JSON API submission of returns through MTD-compatible software; no HMRC fiscal stamping of invoices. Apple IAP and Google Play Billing issue platform settlement invoices and maintain revenue records; the ELP must retain these as primary transaction evidence.

Misdeclaration of export zero-rating or fabricated subscription exports attracts HMRC penalties up to 100% of the understated VAT plus default interest and retrospective clawback assessments for up to 4 years (20 years for deliberate concealment). In Gibraltar, false exemption declarations trigger revocation of ELP exempt status, recovery of tax for up to 6 years, penalties under the Income Tax Act 2010 (up to 100% of tax), and potential partner disqualification under the Limited Partnerships Act.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GIP enjoys full current-account and capital-account convertibility within the Sterling zone; Gibraltar maintains no exchange controls, no capital-transfer authorization, and no approval requirement for profit remittance, partnership distributions, or repatriation of partner capital. WebNovel subscription revenue received in USD, EUR, or GBP may be held in any currency and converted through GFSC-licensed banks without restriction. The only practical frictions are correspondent-bank de-risking diligence and the limited depth of the domestic interbank market for USD/EUR; multi-currency accounts at tier-1 Gibraltar banks and UK/international correspondent relationships provide the effective conversion and clearing path.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Gibraltar imposes no export-proceeds surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, no statutory holding period, and no central counterparty involvement for trade receipts. Apple IAP and Google Play settlement proceeds may be retained indefinitely in the original currency. For fiscal transparency, the ELP should retain merchant settlement statements, app-store payout reports, and ledger entries in the form required by the Gibraltar Income Tax Office and its external auditor.

Central bank supervision: Gibraltar does not maintain an independent central bank or foreign exchange administration. The Gibraltar pound (GIP) is issued by the Government of Gibraltar under the Currency Notes Act and is pegged at 1:1 with the Bank of England pound sterling (GBP), which is also legal tender in Gibraltar; monetary policy is therefore set de facto by the Bank of England. The Gibraltar Financial Services Commission (GFSC), established under the Financial Services Commission Act 2001, is the integrated financial regulator that licenses and supervises all banks under the Banking Act 2008 and administers Gibraltar's AML/CTF framework.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% flat rate on chargeable income (Income Tax Act 2010, as amended); no local corporate surtax, no branch remittance tax.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Gibraltar imposes no withholding tax on dividends or on partnership profit distributions; an ELP distribution to a non-resident partner is paid gross with no Gibraltar statutory deduction or reporting obligation.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Gibraltar does not publish a separate statutory monetary threshold for mandatory Local File or Master File; however, the Commissioner follows OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines Chapter V, and contemporaneous documentation is required for any controlled cross-border transaction where the aggregate annual value exceeds GIP 100,000 (approximately GBP 100,000). The ELP's app-store commission payments to Apple/Google, intercompany royalty payments to the content-IP holding affiliate, and any management-fee payments to the corporate GP or its group are all subject to the arm's-length standard, with a recommended functional and risk analysis and transfer pricing report retained for at least six years.

Economic substance scope: The Gibraltar Income Tax (Economic Substance) Regulations 2019 apply in full to ELPs carrying on a relevant activity. Because the target platform operates as a digital subscription and IP business, the relevant activity is Intellectual Property Business, alternatively Distribution and Service Centre or Headquarters activity if the ELP is restructured accordingly. The ELP must meet the 'adequate substance' test: (a) board and management meetings held in Gibraltar with a quorum physically present; (b) full-time employees proportionate to the CIGA in Gibraltar; (c) adequate premises and equipment; (d) operational spend in Gibraltar; and (e) filing of the ESR-GI return. A purely passive ELP holding worldwide platform income without any Gibraltar CIGA is automatically presumed not to meet the substance test, triggering the penalty staircase and automatic EOI disclosure to the UK, EU member states and Crown Dependencies.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Deliberate Non-Declaration

Statutory sanction: Criminal prosecution under the Income Tax Act 2010 with a fine up to £50,000, imprisonment up to five years on indictment, and a separate civil penalty of up to 150% of the under-declared tax plus interest at the statutory rate; the estimated assessment shall be raised with no deduction for carried-forward losses.

Director personal liability: The general partner and its directors may be jointly and severally liable for the ELP's tax liabilities where they knowingly caused the false return; the Gibraltar court can make a restitution and disqualification order prohibiting directorship for up to 15 years; an arrest warrant and international travel ban can be issued for absconding directors.
Shell Company / Sham Substance Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Disregard of the ELP for tax purposes, re-attribution of income to the beneficial owners, mandatory deregistration of the exempt status under the Limited Partnerships Act, a penalty of up to £250,000 under the Economic Substance regime, and automatic inclusion in HMRC's high-risk arrangements database with UK counteraction under GAAR.

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil of the corporate general partner: directors face personal liability for the entire under-paid tax, plus interest and penalties; civil recovery action may freeze personal assets; criminal liability arises under the Companies Act 2014 for fraudulent trading and offences of delivering misleading statements to the Registrar.
AML and Economic Sanctions Breach

Statutory sanction: Fines under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2015 and Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2019 of up to £1,000,000 per breach; criminal confiscation proceedings against the ELP's assets; freezing orders and suspension of the EMI banking and card-acquiring relationships; referral to the Gibraltar Financial Services Commission with public censure.

Director personal liability: Directors and the general partner commit an offence of failing to make a required disclosure; personal fines of up to £500,000 and imprisonment for up to seven years; directors who knowingly facilitate sanctioned traffic are subject to individual asset freezes, travel bans and international arrest under Interpol channels.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Artificial IP Migration

Statutory sanction: Primary TP adjustment to the Gibraltar chargeable income, secondary wraparound adjustments, penalties of up to 100% of the additional tax, and crossover referral to the UK HMRC Transfer Pricing Panel; the arrangement may be recharacterised as a UK permanent establishment of the non-resident partner subject to UK corporation tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve the artificial pricing are jointly liable for the adjusted tax under Gibraltar's corporate veil-piercing provisions; in severe cases the court can issue a director liability order including compensation orders, disqualification for up to 10 years and custodial sentences for fraudulent trading.
Non-Filing and Chronic Late Payment

Statutory sanction: Escalating fixed and daily penalties reaching £5,000 plus £60 per day; loss of the ELP's exempt status and removal from the Gibraltar Registry; the Commissioner may raise a determined assessment of the platform's insourced revenue and institute winding-up proceedings against the general partner for collection.

Director personal liability: As the ELP has no general partner with limited liability for public tax debts, the general partner's directors face civil liability for unpaid taxes after a determination notice; the Gibraltar court may issue a personal bankruptcy or sequestration order and a restriction to leave Gibraltar pending settlement.
