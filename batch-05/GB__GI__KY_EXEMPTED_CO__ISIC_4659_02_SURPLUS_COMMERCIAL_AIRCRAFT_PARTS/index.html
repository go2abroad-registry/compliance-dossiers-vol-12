Stage 03
Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

      
Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

    

    

      
Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
Direct taxes: Income Tax Office, HM Government of Gibraltar (Commissioner of Income Tax), official portal https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/taxation. Indirect/customs: HM Customs Gibraltar (Customs Division) operating the UK Customs Declaration Service (CDS).

        
E-government filing portal
Step 1: Register the Cayman Exempted Company as an overseas company with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies (Companies Act 2014, Part 22) within one month of establishing a place of business. Step 2: File the tax registration statement with the Income Tax Office to obtain the statutory Gibraltar Tax Reference Number (TRN), attaching Cayman certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles, certificate of good standing, Gibraltar registered office address, and resident agent/general manager details. Step 3: Obtain a UK/Gibraltar EORI number and register as importer/exporter with HM Customs Gibraltar before lodging any customs declarations. No minimum capital is required.

      

      
Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone (Cayman: no income tax, no capital gains tax, no withholding tax). In Gibraltar: the foreign exempted company is a non-resident unless central management and control is exercised in Gibraltar; if resident, it is subject to Entity_Level_Taxation on a Territorial_Scope basis only (12.5% CIT on Gibraltar-source income and income from Gibraltar activities). A Gibraltar branch/permanent establishment is taxed on attributable trading income. — Step 1: Register the Cayman Exempted Company as an overseas company with the Gibraltar Registrar of Companies (Companies Act 2014, Part 22) within one month of establishing a place of business. Step 3: Obtain a UK/Gibraltar EORI number and register as importer/exporter with HM Customs Gibraltar before lodging any customs declarations. No minimum capital is required.

    

    

      
Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Tax_Exempt_Zone (Cayman: no income tax, no capital gains tax, no withholding tax). In Gibraltar: the foreign exempted company is a non-resident unless central management and control is exercised in Gibraltar; if resident, it is subject to Entity_Level_Taxation on a Territorial_Scope basis only (12.5% CIT on Gibraltar-source income and income from Gibraltar activities). A Gibraltar branch/permanent establishment is taxed on attributable trading income.

        
Available election forms
No US check-the-box election. Available elections under the Income Tax Act 2010: (i) participation exemption election exempting qualifying dividends and capital gains on substantial shareholdings; (ii) group relief election for intra-group loss surrender; (iii) formal notification/enrollment for Gibraltar tax residence where central management and control is in Gibraltar; (iv) joint and several liability election for consortium arrangements. Hybrid mismatch rules follow OECD/ATAD; no elective hybrid alignment mechanism is available.

      

    

    

      
VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

      

        
Standard VAT / GST rate
No VAT/GST/sales tax. Gibraltar levies Import Duty on goods entering the UK customs territory under the UK Global Tariff, plus select excise duties; exported goods are outside the scope of duty.

        
Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)
No de minimis threshold: any commercial import/export requires EORI registration and customs declarations regardless of turnover. No VAT registration threshold exists because no VAT is charged. For direct tax purposes, the threshold for TRN registration is the mere existence of Gibraltar-source income or a taxable establishment; no GIP turnover threshold applies.

      

      
      

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
Gibraltar has no VAT, so there is no VAT zero-rating or input VAT refund mechanism; exports are simply outside the scope of import duty. Qualifying civil aircraft parts (HS 8801-8807) are entitled to a 0% applied rate under the UK Global Tariff / WTO Agreement on Trade in Civil Aircraft, provided the goods are airworthy certified civil aircraft components and the export movement is evidenced.

No real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, or digital stamping mandate. Customs clearance is fully electronic and mandatory through CDS using SAD/C88 declarations, with Safety & Security (ENS/EXS) filings; no Continuous Transaction Controls (CTC) are imposed.

      
HM Customs Gibraltar conducts post-clearance audits up to 6 years; failure to substantiate physical export triggers duty clawback with interest, forfeiture of goods, civil penalties and, for fraudulent evasion, criminal liability including unlimited fines and imprisonment. The Income Tax Office may reassess the entity where export profits were omitted or shifted to the tax-exempt Cayman entity, with penalties up to 100% of the tax and extended limitation periods in cases of deliberate misrepresentation or fraud.

Stage 04
Banking & Treasury Architecture

      
Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
Gibraltar operates under a floating but de jure fixed (1:1) currency board regime with the UK. There are no exchange controls; current-account convertibility (import/export settlement, dividends, intercompany fees) and capital-account convertibility (equity injection, escrow top-ups, loan repayments) are unrestricted for non-resident offshore companies, including Cayman exempted entities. Profit remittance faces no Gibraltar withholding tax or FX approval; however, a bank-level AML/KYC review is always conducted and documentary evidence of economic purpose, underlying sale/purchase and beneficial ownership is required for each major remittance.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
Gibraltar imposes no statutory surrender, mandatory conversion quota, or repatriation deadline on export receipts. All export proceeds may remain in foreign currency accounts. In practice, for an ISIC 4659 aviation spares distributor, the receiving Gibraltar bank will require a trade dossier (selling invoice, airway bill, EASA/FAA certified parts certificate and UBO confirmation) to release funds as working capital, and if a GBP/EUR/USD settlement is requested, conversion is done at the bank's commercial rate.

      

      
Central bank supervision: Government of Gibraltar – Financial Services Commission (GFSC), acting with the Bank of England as the ultimate monetary authority; the Gibraltar pound (GIP) is legally pegged to sterling at 1:1.

    

    

      
Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

      

        
        

          

            Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails
            Fit: High
          

          
Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-HIGH using a Gibraltar-licensed EMI under the Electronic Money and Payment Services Act; legal constraints prohibit the use of offshore (unlicensed) digital EMIs for any substantive Gibraltar treasury function because they would constitute unlawful deposit taking in the territory. A licensed Gibraltar EMI, or an FCA-authorised UK EMI acting under a Gibraltar passport, may support API-based virtual IBANs; nevertheless, for aircraft-parts trade the lead treasury account must remain a regulated commercial bank, not an EMI, due to transaction size and documentary letter-of-credit requirements.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

      
      

        Cayman Exempted Company with limited substance and no tax status in Gibraltar.
        
Root cause: Cayman Exempted Company with limited substance and no tax status in Gibraltar.

        
Defense evidence: Provide (i) Cayman certificate of incorporation and certificate of good standing; (ii) certificate of tax exemption (Tax Exempted Company Certificate); (iii) Cayman registered agent letter and registry licences; (iv) declaration of directors/beneficial owners with notarised passports; (v) permitted substance documentation under the Cayman Economic Substance Decree if applicable; (vi) business plan and projected profit margins; (vii) copies of leases and invoice for any Gibraltar substance; (viii) source of funds confirmed by 6-month bank statements.

      

      
      

        Trade in surplus aircraft rotables and avionics raising sanctions and dual-use concerns.
        
Root cause: Trade in surplus aircraft rotables and avionics raising sanctions and dual-use concerns.

        
Defense evidence: Provide a compliance pack consisting of EASA/ICAO/FAA airworthiness certification for each batch, the original contract with the provider of surplus OEM parts, export/import declarations under UK Export Control Order 2008 and Gibraltar law, confirmed End-User Certificates where any item has civil/military interchangeability, a customer due-diligence file on each buyer, and a statement that no item is on the UK Military List or Dual-Use Annex without a licence.

      

      
      

        High-frequency cross-border payments to multiple jurisdictions with rotating beneficiary innovation.
        
Root cause: High-frequency cross-border payments to multiple jurisdictions with rotating beneficiary innovation.

        
Defense evidence: Maintain a full treasury narrative: approved supplier list, expected annual purchase orders, settlement matrix (invoices vs. parts receipt), minutes of the directors' meeting resolving payment schedule, sample of historical SWIFT amends/narratives, bank account confirmations, and a standing auditor's letter confirming that accounts receivable/payable reconciliation is performed monthly. Any first-order payment to a new beneficiary should be supported by a 470-page KYC data-room and a call memo approved by the bank's Money Laundering Reporting Officer.

      

      
    

    

      
Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

      

        Shareholder or group treasury initiates a SWIFT MT103 from the Cayman account to the Gibraltar bank, crediting the dedicated capital escrow account (IBAN). The payment narrative must state a BPM6/balance-of-payments code for direct investment equity capital and the reference 'GI_CAP_549300KY_EX' to avoid misclassification as export revenue. Pre-funding KYC requires a certified board resolution of the Cayman Exempted Company authorising the contribution, a notarised current list of shareholders/subscriber shares, and a source-of-wealth declaration from the contributor. No Gibraltar exchange control consent is required; however, automated sanctions screening is applied to all intermediary and beneficiary banks. Funds are held in the escrow ledger, and upon presentation of the capital-entry certificate they are available for operating use.Core rail is SWIFT MT103 for standard settlement to OEMs, distributors and MROs. For high-value or low-trust first purchases, the bank will establish a commercial letter of credit via SWIFT MT700, with documents being the seller's commercial invoice, original EASA/FAA 8130-3 certificate, airway bill and insurance certificate, and a copy of the buyer-side purchase order. EUR-denominated payments may use SEPA (SCT) for sub-50k items, but USD-denominated rotables settlement uses CHIPS/Fedwire via the Gibraltar bank's New York correspondent. The escrow bank must approve each disbursement with a two-person rule when the instruction exceeds a defined threshold and when the beneficiary country is in a sanctions-adjacent zone. All outbound wires must be prefixed with a clear purpose narrative 'Wholesale distribution of certified surplus aircraft parts (ISIC 4659)' to avoid misinterpretation as personal or financial services.Foreign exchange forwards on USD/EUR/GBP with maximum tenor of 12 months under an ISDA 2002 English law master agreement.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) referencing GIP/USD settled in GBP, used when physical GIP/USD delivery is not available.Cross-currency swaps (USD/GBP or USD/EUR) to hedge long-term trade receivables against funding liabilities.European-style plain-vanilla FX options (call/put) on firm purchase orders, with premium in GIP.Rolling FX spot contracts with same-day value for temporary conversion needs.
      

      
E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-HIGH using a Gibraltar-licensed EMI under the Electronic Money and Payment Services Act; legal constraints prohibit the use of offshore (unlicensed) digital EMIs for any substantive Gibraltar treasury function because they would constitute unlawful deposit taking in the territory. A licensed Gibraltar EMI, or an FCA-authorised UK EMI acting under a Gibraltar passport, may support API-based virtual IBANs; nevertheless, for aircraft-parts trade the lead treasury account must remain a regulated commercial bank, not an EMI, due to transaction size and documentary letter-of-credit requirements.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

      

        

          

            BoxField
            Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
          

          
            
            

              Box 1: Entity Identification
              Insert the full legal name, Gibraltar foreign company registration number (if registered under the Companies Act), and the Cayman Islands exempted company registration number.
              A Cayman exempted company that is not tax resident in Gibraltar must declare its jurisdiction of tax residence as Cayman Islands and provide its Hong Kong EMF or Cayman tax identification number if available.
              Late filing of Form CT-1 attracts an initial penalty of GIP 250, followed by a daily penalty of GIP 50 per day, up to a maximum of GIP 1,000, plus interest at 4% per annum on unpaid tax and a 10% surcharge for tax settled more than 30 days after the due date.
            

            
            

              Box 2: Accounting Period
              Enter the accounting period start and end dates. The period cannot exceed 12 months.
              For a Gibraltar PE or branch, report the accounting period of the Cayman exempted company's global financial statements but limit the return to the activity of the Gibraltar branch.
              Late filing of Form CT-1 attracts an initial penalty of GIP 250, followed by a daily penalty of GIP 50 per day, up to a maximum of GIP 1,000, plus interest at 4% per annum on unpaid tax and a 10% surcharge for tax settled more than 30 days after the due date.
            

            
            

              Box 3: Gibraltar-Source Gross Receipts
              Disclose all gross revenue from the distribution of certified civil aircraft rotables and avionics surplus sourced in Gibraltar or connected to the PE.
              Non-resident trader: include only revenue collected through the Gibraltar PE or economically attributable to sales negotiated within Gibraltar. Do not include sales consummated outside Gibraltar.
              Late filing of Form CT-1 attracts an initial penalty of GIP 250, followed by a daily penalty of GIP 50 per day, up to a maximum of GIP 1,000, plus interest at 4% per annum on unpaid tax and a 10% surcharge for tax settled more than 30 days after the due date.
            

            
            

              Box 4: Deductible Expenses
              Enter all deductible expenses directly incurred for the acquisition, warehousing, inspection, certification, and resale of surplus aircraft parts.
              A non-resident PE may deduct arm's-length costs incurred by the head office; no deduction is allowed for notional management fees or payments remitted to the Cayman treasury.
              Late filing of Form CT-1 attracts an initial penalty of GIP 250, followed by a daily penalty of GIP 50 per day, up to a maximum of GIP 1,000, plus interest at 4% per annum on unpaid tax and a 10% surcharge for tax settled more than 30 days after the due date.
            

            
            

              Box 5: Adjusted Taxable Profit
              Calculate the taxable profit after adding disallowable expenditure and subtracting capital allowances and losses brought forward.
              Where the PE has no taxable profit, file a nil-declaration and attach an explanatory statement confirming the absence of Gibraltar-source income.
              Late filing of Form CT-1 attracts an initial penalty of GIP 250, followed by a daily penalty of GIP 50 per day, up to a maximum of GIP 1,000, plus interest at 4% per annum on unpaid tax and a 10% surcharge for tax settled more than 30 days after the due date.
            

            
            

              Box 6: Reliefs and Incentives
              Claim any double taxation relief, patent box relief, or investment allowance.
              A Cayman exempted company cannot claim treaty relief because no UK-Cayman or Gibraltar-Cayman comprehensive DTA exists; claim only unilateral credit relief if foreign tax has been paid on PE profits.
              Late filing of Form CT-1 attracts an initial penalty of GIP 250, followed by a daily penalty of GIP 50 per day, up to a maximum of GIP 1,000, plus interest at 4% per annum on unpaid tax and a 10% surcharge for tax settled more than 30 days after the due date.
            

            
            

              Box 7: Tax Liability
              Multiply adjusted taxable profit by the 10% rate and subtract any Gibraltar payments on account or tax credits.
              A non-resident company with no Gibraltar PE submits a nil return, certifies true compliance, and must maintain sufficient evidence of extraterritorial management and control.
              Late filing of Form CT-1 attracts an initial penalty of GIP 250, followed by a daily penalty of GIP 50 per day, up to a maximum of GIP 1,000, plus interest at 4% per annum on unpaid tax and a 10% surcharge for tax settled more than 30 days after the due date.
            

            
          
        

      

    

    

      
Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

      
Master / Local File threshold: GIP 1,000,000 aggregate controlled transactions in a fiscal year triggers the mandatory preparation of a Local File; the Master File requirement applies where the ultimate parent group has consolidated turnover exceeding GIP 50,000,000.

      
Economic substance scope: The entity is within the scope of the Gibraltar Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) if it is resident for tax purposes in Gibraltar or is a non-resident carrying on a relevant activity. 'Distribution and service centres' is a specified relevant activity. The Cayman exempted company must demonstrate that within Gibraltar it maintains equal or higher substance through physical offices, full-time staff with suitability, and actual management direction, or it will be classified as a non-compliant shell company.

      

    

    

      
Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

      
      

        Tax Evasion
        
Statutory sanction: Criminal conviction under Gibraltar Income Tax Act with an unlimited monetary fine or imprisonment of up to 3 years for tax evasion; additionally a 100% penalty on evaded tax, interest at 4% per annum, and mandatory filing of corrected returns within 30 days.

        
Director personal liability: The court may order civil restitution, disqualification as a director for up to 10 years, a travel ban pending full payment, and personal liability for the company's unpaid tax and penalties if the director was knowingly concerned in the unlawful evasion.

      

      
      

        Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure
        
Statutory sanction: Penalty up to GIP 100,000 for each relevant activity; automatic strike-off from the Register of Overseas Companies for continued non-compliance; referral to the Registrar for criminal enforcement and compulsory winding up.

        
Director personal liability: Directors face joint and several civil liability for the penalty if the failure is attributable to them; can be prohibited from holding any Gibraltar directorship for up to 5 years, and a travel ban may be imposed under the Proceeds of Crime Act until the penalty is settled.

      

      
      

        AML / Trade-Based Money Laundering
        
Statutory sanction: Unlimited confiscation order, imprisonment up to 7 years, forfeiture of the aircraft parts and escrow assets; imposition of a civil penalty equal to three times the laundered sum under Gibraltar Proceeds of Crime Act 2015.

        
Director personal liability: Directors, MLROs and beneficial owners may be made personally subject to confiscation orders for the value of transferred proceeds; face extradition, a prohibition order against acting as a director or trust manager, and a publicly documented travel restriction.

      

      
      

        Transfer Pricing Documentation Red Flags
        
Statutory sanction: Automatic documentation penalty of GIP 10,000; re-adjustment of the PE profit at tip-to-tail arm's length; 50% accuracy-related penalty on the under-declared tax plus interest running from the due date of the original return.

        
Director personal liability: A director who controls or signs the tax return can be held personally liable for the documentation penalty where the omission is deliberate or without reasonable excuse; severe cases attract civil fraud proceedings, a personal travel ban, and disqualification from managing any company in Gibraltar for up to 7 years.
