Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Cayman Islands General Registry, Registrar of Companies (primary formation authority); for UK branch registration, Companies House, Crown Way, Cardiff.

Governing statute

Cayman Islands Companies Act (2023 Revision), sections 4 (incorporation), 9 (registered office), 13-14 (memorandum and articles), 15 (exempted company), 55 (register of directors), and 59 (annual return); secondary UK registration, where a physical UK establishment is opened, under Companies Act 2006, Part 34, sections 1046-1055 and the Overseas Companies Regulations 2009.

Formation / constitutional filings

Memorandum and Articles of Association filed through the Cayman e-Registry, together with the Registrar's prescribed incorporation application and registered office consent letter; if a UK establishment is opened, Form OSCH01 under the Overseas Companies Regulations 2009.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 32 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a Cayman exempted company; the subscriber may issue one or more ordinary shares for nominal consideration, commonly USD 1. No escrow account or capital deposit is required by Cayman law.

Funding & unlock path: Not required for the Cayman exempted company's registered office because it has no employees or physical industrial premises in Cayman. If a UK customer-support office is opened, the landlord must supply a fire risk assessment under the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005, the employer must comply with the Health and Safety at Work Act 1974, and any server or storage area must meet UK Building Regulations Part B and Part F.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a Cayman exempted company; the subscriber may issue one or more ordinary shares for nominal consideration, commonly USD 1. No escrow account or capital deposit is required by Cayman law.

Not applicable; no industrial licensing board or general manufacturing permit applies to a cloud helpdesk and SaaS support operation. Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Revenue and Customs (HMRC); portal: www.gov.uk (Business Tax Account, Corporation Tax online, VAT online services); Companies House for registration of an overseas company UK establishment.

E-government filing portal

A non-resident Cayman exempted company must register for UK Corporation Tax with HMRC using form CT41G within three months of beginning trade through a UK permanent establishment; HMRC issues a 10-digit Unique Taxpayer Reference. For VAT, a non-established taxable person has no £90,000 threshold and must register before making its first taxable supply in the UK; application is made online via HMRC's Register for VAT service, resulting in a 9-digit VAT registration number with GB (or XI for Northern Ireland) prefix. If the company maintains a branch or physical place of business in the UK, it must also file form OSBR01 at Companies House within one month. For goods exports, a 12-digit UK EORI number must be obtained from HMRC before making export declarations through the Customs Declaration Service.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation under UK law for a non-UK resident company; Tax_Exempt_Zone under Cayman home law; UK territorial scope applies, charging corporation tax only on profits attributable to a UK permanent establishment. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation under UK law for a non-UK resident company; Tax_Exempt_Zone under Cayman home law; UK territorial scope applies, charging corporation tax only on profits attributable to a UK permanent establishment. The Cayman Exempted Company is a separate legal person, not a pass-through entity, in both jurisdictions.

Available election forms

UK law has no check-the-box election; entity classification is statutory. A Cayman exempted company may elect under Cayman law for a 20-year tax exemption certificate, but that does not affect UK classification. Relevant UK elections and administrative mechanisms include: VAT group registration under VATA 1994 s.43 where a UK establishment exists; annual accounting and cash accounting elections for qualifying VAT turnover; and hybrid mismatch counteraction under TIOPA 2010 Part 6A, under which no elective avoidance mechanism exists but dual-inclusion income elections may prevent double-counting in prescribed cross-border cases.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

£90,000 VAT-exclusive taxable turnover in a rolling 12-month period for UK-established businesses; non-established taxable persons such as a Cayman exempted company have no registration threshold and must register before making any taxable supply in the UK. For imported low-value goods consignments not exceeding £135, online marketplace sellers are subject to VAT at point of sale with effectively no distance-selling exemption.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods are zero-rated when they are physically exported from the UK by the supplier or the overseas customer, provided the supplier holds prescribed export evidence within three months of dispatch. Services, including multi-channel customer support and helpdesk SaaS, are zero-rated or outside the UK scope when the place of supply is outside the UK: for B2B supplies, the customer is a taxable person belonging in another country; for B2C remotely supplied digital helpdesk and software-support services, the customer belongs outside the UK and two non-conflicting pieces of evidence of customer location are retained. Services connected with UK land or delivered to UK-established customers do not qualify for zero-rating.

  • Customs Export Declaration and Movement Reference Number (CDS), or C88/SAD paper export declaration where applicable
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export movement
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Contract, purchase order, or service-level agreement for the underlying transaction
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank SWIFT / foreign exchange confirmation showing foreign currency consideration received
  • Evidence of customer's non-UK status, such as customer VAT registration number, business registration certificate, or country-of-establishment proof
  • Proof of export such as port receipts, customs stamps, courier status history, and container gate-in/out records

No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or government invoice pre-authorisation in the UK. VAT-registered businesses must file returns under Making Tax Digital using HMRC-recognised software that interfaces with HMRC APIs, and must keep digital records. Electronic invoices, including PEPPOL/SI-UBL, are accepted and must be preserved in a legible, authentic format. Exports are cleared through the Customs Declaration Service, which issues a Movement Reference Number; no additional stamp is applied to invoices.

HMRC may issue an assessment under VATA 1994 s.73 for any VAT not accounted for, with a standard 4-year assessment window extended to 20 years for fraud or evasion. Wrongly zero-rated export supplies are retrospectively clawed back, together with statutory interest and penalties under Finance Act 2007 Schedule 24, ranging up to 100% of the potential lost VAT for deliberate and concealed conduct. Fraudulent export arrangements, including missing-trader intra-community fraud or sham exports, can lead to denial of input tax recovery, joint and several liability notices to directors, civil penalties, and criminal prosecution under VATA 1994 s.72. HMRC will scrutinise actual physical movement of goods, genuineness of offshore services, customer jurisdiction evidence, and timing of foreign exchange repatriation; absent or post-dated documentation will invalidate the zero-rating.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance
