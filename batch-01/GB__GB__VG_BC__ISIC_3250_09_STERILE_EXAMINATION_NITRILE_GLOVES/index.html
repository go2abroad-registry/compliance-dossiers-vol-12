Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

Step 2 - If a UK PE or UK trade exists, register for Corporation Tax within 3 months of commencement (form CT41G or CT registration); HMRC will issue a 10-digit Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - The BVI BC is treated as a foreign corporation, not eligible for pass-through. Non-UK resident company subject to entity-level UK corporation tax (main rate 25%; small profits rate 19% for profits up to GBP 50,000, with marginal relief) only on profits attributable to a UK permanent establishment and on UK property income. The BVI's domestic tax-exempt status does not exempt UK profits; foreign branches, foreign dividends and capital gains of the BC remain outside the UK corporation tax net unless effectively connected with the UK PE.

Available election forms

The UK has no check-the-box entity classification election; a BVI BC is taxed per se as a company. Available elections include: (a) hybrid mismatch taxpayer choices under TIOPA 2010 Part 6A (including s.259CB(7) and s.259CE(7) elections to increase taxable income or relieve counteraction); (b) elections to be charged to corporation tax on UK property income on a net basis (statutory regime since 6 April 2020); (c) capital allowances pooling and basis-period elections for PE assets; (d) group relief and joint elections if the UK activity is transferred to a UK subsidiary; and (e) Directed/HMRC reconciliation procedures for CDS custom-duty valuation elections. No double taxation treaty exists between the UK and BVI (only a Tax Information Exchange Agreement, 2009), so UK domestic law and unilateral foreign tax credit rules apply, with no creditable BVI tax.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GBP 90,000 taxable turnover in any rolling 12-month period (threshold effective 1 April 2024, previously GBP 85,000). Compulsory registration is required by the end of the month following the month in which the threshold is exceeded; deregistration threshold is GBP 88,000; voluntary registration is permitted for foreign entities to recover input VAT.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating of exported goods is available under VATA 1994 s.30(6) and VAT Regulations 1995 (SI 1995/2518), regs.129-134 where: (1) the BVI BC exports the sterile nitrile gloves to a destination outside the UK; (2) the goods are physically removed from the UK within 3 months of the time of supply (or an HMRC-approved longer period); (3) the supplier holds valid proof of export; and (4) the customer is outside the UK, with the buyer's EORI or foreign VAT registration numbers recorded. UK-consumed sales are standard-rated at 20%, except supplies to handicapped persons or charities under Group 16 of Schedule 8 VATA 1994 if the statutory conditions are met. Directly related B2B services (freight, inspection, logistics) supplied to a recipient outside the UK are outside the scope/zero-rated under the place-of-supply rules (VATA 1994 s.7A; SI 1992/3121).

  • Bill of Lading (sea) / Air Waybill (air) / CMR consignment note (road) evidencing that the goods left the UK
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (UK bank equivalent: foreign-currency credit advice or SWIFT credit confirmation) evidencing receipt of payment from the overseas buyer

No real-time fiscal clearance, no government pre-authorisation of invoices and no digital stamping exist in the UK; B2B invoices are self-issued and valid without HMRC approval. Mandatory digital reporting applies under Making Tax Digital (MTD): every VAT-registered business must keep digital records and file VAT returns through the HMRC VAT MTD API (JSON, OAuth 2.0) using HMRC-recognised 'functional compatible software'. E-invoicing: no B2B mandate; PEPPOL BIS 3.0 (UBL/CII) electronic invoicing is supported and is required for certain public-sector procurement, but HMRC does not operate a continuous transaction controls database. Export declarations must be lodged electronically via the Customs Declaration Service (CDS).

HMRC may deny zero-rating and issue a VAT clawback assessment with statutory interest where the exporter fails to produce prescribed export evidence within 30 days of an HMRC notice (regs.129-130, SI 1995/2518); the burden of proof is on the supplier. Penalties for inaccuracies are assessed under FA 2007 Sch.24 (careless 30%, deliberate 70%, deliberate-and-concealed up to 100% of potential lost revenue, with disclosure reductions). Fraudulent evasion is a criminal offence under VATA 1994 s.72 (unlimited fine and/or up to 7 years imprisonment). HMRC assessment windows: 4 years for ordinary errors and 20 years for fraud/dishonesty (VATA 1994 s.77). Late-payment interest accrues at the Bank of England base rate plus 2.5 percentage points (FA 2009 s.101). Export records and evidence must be retained for 6 years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The United Kingdom operates a fully liberalised exchange control regime for both current and capital account transactions. There are no restrictions on the convertibility of Sterling into foreign currency, nor on the remittance of profits, dividends, or capital proceeds by non-resident corporate entities. The BVI Business Company, as a tax-exempt non-resident enterprise, is free to hold, convert, and remit funds in any major currency, subject only to standard counter-terrorism financing, anti-money laundering, and sanctions screening obligations imposed on UK financial institutions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no statutory export proceeds surrender requirements in the United Kingdom. Exporters are not obliged to convert export receipts into Sterling, and there is no mandatory timeline for repatriation or conversion. However, UK resident companies and UK branches of foreign entities must retain transaction records for HMRC statistical reporting (Intrastat for EU trade) and must comply with all applicable export licensing and medical device regulatory documentation requirements.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary and macro-prudential authority) and HM Treasury (foreign exchange and sanctions policy)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

The viability of using UK-licensed Fintech Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) is moderately high for the present structure. FCA-authorised EMIs such as Wise, Revolut Business, and Airwallex commonly onboard BVI companies on a remote basis if the ultimate beneficial ownership structure is straightforward and the underlying trade documentation is transparent. Legal constraints include the need for a UK registered office or local agent for certain banking functions, FCA e-money safeguarding requirements, and the absolute prohibition on handling proceeds from sanctioned jurisdictions. EMIs remain a secondary rail to Tier-1 banks due to transaction size caps and limited lending or trade finance capabilities.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Only UK-incorporated or UK-resident/PE companies of groups exceeding GBP 20,000,000 turnover or GBP 4,000,000 assets are required to maintain full transfer pricing documentation (Local File); SMEs: exemption for companies / PEs with fewer than 250 employees and either turnover <= GBP 50,000,000 or balance sheet total <= GBP 43,000,000, unless a transaction is non-arm's length in tax avoidance circumstances; UK has not adopted EU-style Local File preparation thresholds but requires a Master File equivalent Schedule 34 FA 2009 for large multinationals; for a BVI BC owning an operating UK subsidiary, the UK entity's filing triggers documentation at the Group level where worldwide group thresholds exceed EUR 750m for country-by-country reporting.

Economic substance scope: United Kingdom applies OECD arm's length principle under TIOPA 2010, Part 4; with respect to a BVI BC, Economic Substance (Companies and Limited Partnerships) Act 2018 in the BVI: relevant activity includes 'headquarters', 'distribution and service centre' and the upstream glove manufacturing business derived intellectual property is relevant; a pure equity holding entity shall file an Annual Economic Substance Return in BVI but is not required to maintain a UK substance; UK, CFC and Mandatory Disclosure Rules require a UK subsidiary to document transactions with the BVI BC to show economic substance at BVI operational level; when the UK subsidiary has a risk-sharing arrangement, distribution or financing from BVI, UK transfer pricing and thin capitalisation rules will adjust the profits where profits are diverted through the tax-exempt BVI business company.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion - Whistleblowing / PSHC

Statutory sanction: Civil penalties of up to 100% of the tax (in addition to interest) under FA 2007 Sch 24; criminal penalties for cheating the public revenue: imprisonment up to 7 years plus unlimited fine on indictment under FA 2012 and TA 2016; HMRC name & shame under PSHC where tax non-compliance exceeds GBP 25,000.

Director personal liability: Directors can be personally liable for deliberate failure to prevent facilitation of tax evasion under the Criminal Finances Act 2017; disqualified as UK directors under CDDA 1986; seizure of assets, travel bans, and imprisonment up to 7 years for fraudulent evasion of income tax and corporation tax; civil restitution orders under HMRC Fraud Investigation process.
Shell Company - Economic Substance Violation

Statutory sanction: BVI International Tax Authority levies USD 5,000 to USD 20,000 penalties for non-compliance with Economic Substance requirements under BVI ESA; possible dissolution of BVI company under BVI BCA; UK CFC rules and GAAR (FA 2013 Part 5) apply counteraction of GBP 50,000 and other compliance which can be as high as the tax at stake plus interest; HMRC can apply property-based penalties of up to GBP 2,000 per day through Schedule 36 notice failure.

Director personal liability: BVI directors/registrants may be liable for penalties and potential striking-off; UK parent directors involved in the orchestration of shell arrangements can be personally liable for penalties and disqualified by Companies Court; travel bans if tax debt is over GBP 10,000 and departure is deemed to damage HMRC collection; imprisonment up to 7 years for deliberate fraud.
AML / Sanctions - Trading Without UK Licence

Statutory sanction: Medical Devices Regulations 2002 (SI 2002/618) criminal sanctions: up to 6 months imprisonment / level 5 fine on summary conviction; on indictment up to 2 years and unlimited fine; HMRC civil evasion penalties up to 100% of duty and VAT; Forfeiture of goods under CEMA 1979; OFAC/OFSI and export control fines where relevant if traded with sanctioned jurisdictions, e.g., Gibraltar? Careful: no.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally criminally liable as 'officers' where consent/neglect is proved under the Medical Devices Regulations, and personal liability under Money Laundering Regulations 2017 in the UK for failures in the AML/CTF framework; can be subject to senior management assessments, travel bans, civil recovery orders, and imprisonment up to 2 years for money laundering.
