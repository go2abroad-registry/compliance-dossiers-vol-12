Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exported from the UK to a destination outside the UK may be zero-rated provided they are exported within statutory time limits (generally three months) and best evidence of export is retained. Services supplied to a customer outside the UK, including export/import brokerage services directly related to goods transport or handling, may be zero-rated under place-of-supply rules where the recipient is in a foreign jurisdiction and the service falls within statutory exceptions.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GBP enjoys unrestricted convertibility across both current-account items (brokerage fees, freight, duties, dividends, interest, royalties) and capital-account items (equity injections, intercompany loans, debt repayments, treasury sweeps); no exchange-control licence, prior approval, or conversion quota applies to non-resident investors or offshore corporate shareholders. A Cayman Exempted Company holding a UK operational account may freely remit profits, service consideration, and capital returns to its shareholders or to any jurisdiction, subject solely to UK AML/KYC verification, FATCA/CRS reporting, and ITRS statistical filing for transactions at or above EUR 100,000.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No export-proceeds surrender requirement exists in the United Kingdom: the statutory mandatory conversion ratio is 0%, the prescribed retention timeline is unlimited, and all export or customs-brokerage service receipts may be held indefinitely in any currency, in any jurisdiction, without any obligation to sell to the central bank or to repatriate funds within a fixed window.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (BoE), co-administering foreign-exchange statistical controls through the UK International Transactions Reporting System (ITRS) jointly with the Office for National Statistics; HM Treasury retains ultimate monetary-policy and exchange-rate oversight; customs licensing and duty enforcement sit with HMRC, not the Bank.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium, subject to enhanced due diligence. FCA-authorised UK Electronic Money Institutions (e.g., Wise Payments Ltd and Revolut Ltd) will accept a Cayman Exempted Company only where the customer presents a signed UK customs-brokerage contract, visible operational substance, and auditable fund flows. Key legal constraints: (i) safeguarding obligations under the Electronic Money Regulations 2011 restrict the EMI's use of segregated client funds; (ii) EMIs cannot issue regulated credit, trade finance bonds, or letters of credit; (iii) post-Brexit EEA passporting is lost, forcing EU-based EMIs into a UK branch or extra-territorial permission; (iv) policy-level rejection of tax-exempt Caribbean vehicles remains common unless UBO transparency and commercial purpose are proven; EMIs should be used only as a secondary corridor for small-value disbursements, never as the main treasury rail.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

UK main rate 25% (from 1 April 2023) for taxable profits above £250,000; 19% small profits rate for profits up to £50,000; marginal relief between £50,000 and £250,000. A non-UK resident Cayman company is taxed only on profits attributable to a UK permanent establishment.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% on dividends paid by a UK resident company or by a UK branch; dividends paid by a Cayman Exempted Company in respect of its global earnings are not subject to UK withholding tax.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory monetary threshold in UK law; all cross-border controlled transactions must be priced at arm's length under TIOPA 2010 Part 4. HMRC expects contemporaneous transfer-pricing documentation (Local File / Master File / CbCR) for material transactions; large businesses with turnover above £200 million or assets above £2 billion should follow OECD TP guidelines and UK Diverted Profits Tax oversight. Penalties apply for inadequate documentation.

Economic substance scope: Cayman Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply only to entities carrying on prescribed relevant activities; customs brokerage is not a listed relevant activity, so no Cayman substance test is triggered for this trade. For UK tax purposes, HMRC will apply the lodgment, permanent establishment and CFC rules; a Cayman entity whose central management and control is in the UK becomes UK tax resident, and profits can be assessed under CTA 2010 Part 9A (CFC) or FA 2015 Part 3 (Diverted Profits Tax, at 25%) where UK economic functions are artificially diverted without substance.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Failure to Notify

Statutory sanction: HMRC may issue discovery assessments, charge interest, and impose penalties of up to 200% of the tax for a deliberate, concealed error. Criminal conviction under TMA 1970 s.106 can lead to unlimited fines and imprisonment up to 7 years.

Director personal liability: Directors and board members can be made personally liable for company penalties where they knowingly equate to fraudulent avoidance; travel bans, asset freezing orders and civil restitution orders may be imposed. Directors of large companies have a Senior Accounting Officer (SAO) personal certification duty.
Shell Company / Artificial Profit Arrangement

Statutory sanction: UK Diverted Profits Tax at 25% on diverted profits (FA 2015 s.77); CFC charge under CTA 2010 Part 9A at UK corporation tax rates; penalties and interest on underlying evasion can be applied. HMRC can also re-characterise the arrangement as a sham.

Director personal liability: Directors who facilitate avoidance under FA 2017 (Promoters of Tax Avoidance Schemes) may be pursued for up to 100% of the avoided tax; disqualification as director for up to 15 years under the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: HMRC adjusts taxable profits to the arm's length amount; additional corporation tax plus interest. Penalties for careless or deliberate TP errors up to 100% of the extra tax; Diverted Profits Tax may add a further 25% punitive charge.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign an inaccurate CT600 can be personally fined up to £3,000 per inaccurate return; in DPT cases, unremitted tax can be recovered from any participant in the avoidance arrangement. High-profile public personality liability may lead to debarment from acting as director.
AML / Financial Crime Compliance

Statutory sanction: FCA enforcement: unlimited fines, suspension or revocation of the customs brokerage authorisation, and prohibition orders. Criminal money laundering offences under Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (ss.330, 331) carry up to 14 years' imprisonment, unlimited fines and confiscation.

Director personal liability: Directors can be personally prosecuted for failing to report suspicious activity; Unexplained Wealth Orders may freeze personal assets; travel bans and restrictions on overseas movement apply where there are reasonable grounds for corruption or tax evasion.
