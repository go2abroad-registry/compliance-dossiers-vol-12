Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Companies Registry, Cayman Islands General Registry, Grand Cayman, Cayman Islands

Governing statute

Cayman Islands Companies Act (2023 Revision), sections 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 13, 14, 19, 22, 37 and Schedule 1; the UK Companies Act 2006 is not the incorporating statute for a Cayman Islands exempted company.

Formation / constitutional filings

Memorandum and Articles of Association of a Cayman Islands Exempted Company, filed together with the Notice of Registered Office and Notice of Directors prescribed by the Registrar of Companies

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 20 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital and no escrow or deposit requirement. The Memorandum must state the maximum authorised share capital (commonly USD 50,000); shares may be issued as nil-paid, partly paid or fully paid in cash or property.

Funding & unlock path: Not required for a virtual remote operation. If a Cayman serviced office or co-working space is used, the landlord must provide fire safety certification from the Cayman Islands Fire Service and a building completion or structural load certificate from the Building Control Board, and the company must maintain employers' liability insurance and an occupational health and safety file for any Cayman employees under the Labour Act.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital and no escrow or deposit requirement. The Memorandum must state the maximum authorised share capital (commonly USD 50,000); shares may be issued as nil-paid, partly paid or fully paid in cash or property.

Pro-Forma First Board Resolution Draft
RESOLVED, as a duly convened first board meeting: 1. That the shares allotted to the subscribers as set out in the Register of Members are approved, and share certificates are issued accordingly. 2. That the registered office first stated in the Notice of Registered Office filed with the Cayman Islands General Registry is adopted as the statutory registered office. 3. That the individuals named in the Notice of Directors are appointed as the first directors, and the registered-office provider is appointed corporate secretary. 4. That the directors are authorised to open bank and EMI accounts, to engage legal, tax and accounting advisers, and to execute all agreements for the SEO content analytics platform, including Stripe/Paddle. 5. That these minutes are adopted and placed in the corporate records.
Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Not mandatory for a pure digital SEO and data-analytics SaaS operation. If physical operations are later established, the Department of Commerce and Investment (DCI) issues Trade and Business Licences under the Trade and Business Licensing Act.

Category: No statutory operational or manufacturing permit applies to ISIC 6201 computer programming activity for a non-resident Cayman Exempted Company; a Trade and Business Licence may become applicable only upon establishing a physical place of business in the Islands.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Revenue and Customs (HMRC), with official online portal at www.gov.uk/taxation-businesses; digital services registration via the Government Gateway and the UK One Stop Shop (OSS) portal.

E-government filing portal

A foreign (Cayman) exempted company not tax resident in the UK must obtain a UK Value Added Tax (VAT) registration number by submitting HMRC form VAT1 (or registering online through the HMRC digital portal) if it makes or intends to make taxable supplies of digital services to UK consumers and is not eligible for any threshold relief; there is no general requirement to obtain a UK Corporation Tax UTR unless the entity has a UK permanent establishment or receives UK-source income, in which case it must register for Corporation Tax by submitting form CT41G within three months of commencing business in the UK. The entity should appoint a UK tax representative or use HMRC's OSS non-UK scheme for digital services. A Unique Tax Reference (UTR) is issued for corporate income tax purposes only upon registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

UK law does not provide a check-the-box election; the Cayman exempted company is treated under UK law as a non-resident company limited by shares, separate from its shareholders, and is not eligible for tax consolidation. Statutory elections available include a claim for UK VAT grouping under VATA 1994 section 43, an option to tax for property under Schedule 10 VATA 1994, and the election to apply the foreign permanent establishment exemption under CTA 2010 Section 18A. Hybrid mismatch rules under CTA 2017 Part 15 may apply to re-characterise or deny deductions in respect of arrangements involving exempted entities.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

£90,000 annual UK taxable turnover for general taxable supplies (effective 1 April 2024); for non-established overseas suppliers of digital services to UK consumers, there is no registration de minimis threshold, and registration is mandatory from the first sale unless supplied through HMRC's non-UK OSS scheme.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of tangible goods from the UK to non-UK destinations are zero-rated for VAT under VATA 1994 section 30 (and SI 1995/2518) if the goods are removed from the UK and evidence of export is retained. For digital content/analytics services, the place of supply for B2B supplies is where the recipient belongs (reversing charge if the recipient is not established in the UK), and B2C supplies to recipients outside the UK are treated as outside the scope of UK VAT; zero-rating applies to physical media or qualifying ancillary goods exported. To claim 0%, the supplier must maintain effective proof of export, contractual terms, payment details, IP address, customer location evidence, and be able to justify the place of supply.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD / Entry in Export System via CHIEF/CDS)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill OR other transport or shipping documentation
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / Bank Transfer or payment evidence
  • Commercial contract including Incoterms and destination of supply
  • Customer VAT registration number (for B2B supplies) or proof of non-business consumer location (for B2C supplies)

No mandatory real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing exchange, or digital stamping is in force for ordinary UK VAT invoices. However, all VAT-registered businesses must comply with Making Tax Digital for VAT (MTD) by maintaining digital VAT records and submitting VAT returns and updates via functional compatible software using HMRC's REST API, MTD Signature and Corporation Tax / VAT endpoints. Invoices may be issued in electronic format subject to authenticity, integrity, and legibility requirements, but no central fiscal clearance is required.

HMRC may retrospectively disallow zero-rating, issue VAT assessments and interest for up to 4 years (or 20 years for deliberate concealment), and impose statutory penalties under Finance Act 2007 Schedule 24 up to 100% of the evaded VAT for dishonesty or lack of reasonable care. Penalties for evading VAT may also arise under VATA 1994 Sections 60-70, with default surcharges; criminal liability attaches in serious fraud cases. HMRC requires full substantiation within 90 days of a specific request; failure to supply evidence will result in clawback, and false statements on VAT returns constitute serious misdeclaration leading to penalties of at least 15% for careless errors and up to 45% for deliberate errors.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The United Kingdom operates a floating exchange rate regime with partial capital account convertibility. Current account transactions, including profit remittances from trading activities, are freely convertible without prior approval. Capital account transactions, such as equity injections or profit distributions, require exchange control registration with the Bank of England. Profit remittance hurdles include mandatory compliance with HMRC economic substance tests, AML/KYC verification, and potential delays for transactions exceeding £10,000 where beneficial ownership disclosure is mandated.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory mandatory surrender of export proceeds applies under UK law. Export receipts may be retained offshore or repatriated freely under current account rules. Entities must maintain full documentation for HMRC reporting of foreign income and ensure all transactions comply with anti-money laundering regulations and economic substance requirements for tax residency.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Offshore EMI licensing for Cayman Exempted Companies is severely constrained under UK FCA regulations. While Stripe and Paddle are approved payment rails for MarTech SaaS operations, full cross-border EMI authorisation is not feasible without local regulatory approval. Legal constraints include PSD2 compliance requirements, strict beneficial ownership verification, and prohibition on operating as a UK-established EMI from a non-UK jurisdiction.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Master File: UK group turnover > £200 million, assets > £20 million, or > 500 employees. Local File: UK entity turnover > £1 million and cross-border transactions with related parties > £200,000.

Economic substance scope: UK does not have a specific Economic Substance Regulation (ESR) but applies general anti-avoidance rules (GAAR) and requires economic substance for tax residence determination. A Cayman Exempted Company with UK management and control may become UK tax resident, triggering full UK taxation.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Penalty up to 100% of tax understated (reduced for disclosure), criminal prosecution with unlimited fines and imprisonment up to 7 years

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for penalties if they knowingly participate; travel bans and asset freezes possible under Proceeds of Crime Act 2002
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Reclassification of profits to UK, additional tax, interest, and penalties up to 100% of tax due; potential strike-off of UK registration

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally assessed for unpaid tax if they knowingly allow the company to operate without substance, and may face disqualification as directors
AML / Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Civil penalties up to £5,000 per breach, criminal fines and imprisonment up to 14 years under Money Laundering Regulations 2017

Director personal liability: Directors can be personally fined and imprisoned if they consent or connive in AML breaches; confiscation orders under Proceeds of Crime Act
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Adjustment of profits, interest on underpaid tax, penalties up to 100% of additional tax, and potential double taxation

Director personal liability: Directors may be liable for penalties if they fail to ensure adequate TP documentation, and may face personal assessments for negligence
