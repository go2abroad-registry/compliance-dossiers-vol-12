Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

KY_EXEMPTED_CO (Cayman Exempted Company) UK entry sequence: (1) Register the UK establishment with Companies House on Form OS IN01 within 1 month of opening a UK place of business; a UK Establishment Number is issued. (2) Register for Corporation Tax on Form CT41G within 3 months of starting UK activities if a UK permanent establishment arises or if central management and control is exercised in the UK; HMRC issues a 10-digit Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR). (3) For VAT, as a non-established taxable person (NETP), submit Form VAT1A electronically via the HMRC Government Gateway; HMRC issues a VAT number in the format GB 999 9999 99 and may require fiscal security (VATA 1994 s.83) and a UK tax representative. (4) If UK employees are engaged, register for PAYE before the first payday. Documents required: certified copy of the Cayman Certificate of Incorporation, memorandum and articles of association (English translation), and proof of appointment of a UK agent or establishment.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (Cayman-resident status). The entity is exempt from Cayman Islands income, corporate and capital taxes pursuant to its tax undertaking certificate under the Companies Act (2023 Revision) s.6. For UK tax purposes it is a non-UK resident company unless central management and control is in the UK; UK Corporation Tax then applies only to profits attributable to a UK permanent establishment (CTA 2009 ss.5, 19) at 19% small-profits rate to 25% main rate (marginal relief between £50,000 and £250,000). The entity is not pass-through; no UK tax transparency election is available.

Available election forms

The UK has no check-the-box regime. Available elections: (1) hybrid and other mismatch relevant choice (TIOPA 2010 Part 6A, s.259CB) to re-characterise transactions involving the Cayman entity and UK counterparties; (2) VAT option to tax (waiver of exemption) on UK land under VATA 1994 Sch.10; (3) VAT group registration election (VATA 1994 s.43) where the company has a UK establishment; (4) Corporation Tax group relief and degrouping elections (CTA 2010 Part 5; TCGA 1992 s.171A) subject to 75% and UK-residence conditions; (5) Advance Pricing Agreement with HMRC to fix transfer-pricing terms for translation and localization service fees; (6) application of the offshore receipts rule election where relevant (FA 2019 s.3).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

£90,000 in taxable turnover in a rolling 12-month period (1 April 2024; deregistration threshold £88,000) for UK-established businesses. For KY_EXEMPTED_CO, a non-established taxable person (NETP), the threshold is £0: registration is compulsory before the first taxable supply is made in the UK, and is also available voluntarily. HMRC may require security from NETPs.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exports: zero-rated under VATA 1994 Sch.8 Group 7 where goods are exported from the UK within 3 months of the time of supply (or an agreed later date), delivered to an address outside the UK, and evidenced in accordance with HMRC Notice 725. Services (multilingual translation, dubbing, media localization - ISIC 7490): B2B services supplied to a business established outside the UK are outside the scope of UK VAT under the general place-of-supply rule (VATA 1994 s.7A; SI 1992/3121) and are recorded as outside scope or zero-rated, subject to holding two valid proof items of the recipient's business status and non-UK establishment. B2C services are outside scope where the place of supply is outside the UK. The UK imposes no statutory FX repatriation deadline; the 180-day benchmark is an internal compliance standard aligned with HMRC's export-documentation expectations.

  • Customs Export Declaration - electronic SAD dataset transmitted via the Customs Declaration Service (CDS) for physical exports
  • Bill of Lading (ocean) or Air Waybill (air) evidencing removal of goods from the UK
  • Inward Remittance Certificate - bank SWIFT MT103 credit confirmation of foreign-currency receipt (not a UK statutory requirement, but HMRC may require it as consideration evidence during audit)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Carrier collection receipt or CMR consignment note demonstrating physical exit from the UK

No real-time fiscal clearance, API clearance, or digital stamping mandate applies in the UK. The mandatory regime is Making Tax Digital (MTD) for VAT: all VAT-registered businesses must keep digital records and transmit VAT returns using MTD-compatible software through HMRC API Services; paper filing is no longer permitted. Electronic invoicing is voluntary, with Peppol BIS 3.0 as the recognised interoperability standard. HMRC performs algorithm-based risk scoring, issue profiling, and information notices; MTD for Corporation Tax is scheduled from April 2026.

HMRC may deny zero-rating retroactively where export evidence is incomplete, assessing 20% output tax plus statutory interest on the full invoice value; assessment periods are 4 years (VATA 1994 s.73) and 20 years for deliberate or concealed conduct. Civil penalties under Sch.24 VATA 1994 reach 30% for careless inaccuracies, 70% for deliberate, and 100% for deliberate and concealed; failure-to-notify penalties under Sch.41 reach 100% of the VAT evaded; default surcharge adds up to 15%. Criminal prosecution under VATA 1994 s.72 for fraudulent evasion carries an unlimited fine and imprisonment of up to 7 years on indictment, with confiscation orders under the Proceeds of Crime Act 2002. For cross-border services, HMRC may recharacterise the supply as made at the supplier's UK fixed establishment (Article 53, EU Reg. 282/2011 as retained via EUWA 2019), thereby clawing back UK VAT on the entire localization contract value plus penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility under both current and capital account per OECD liberalisation commitments and post-Brexit UK legislation (e.g., Exchange Control Act 1979 largely repealed). No restrictions on profit remittance, dividends, or loan repayments. No prior authorisation for foreign currency accounts or outward transfers; only sanctions screening and anti-money laundering (AML) disclosure obligations apply.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory requirement to surrender or convert export proceeds into GBP. UK entities and foreign-incorporated companies operating in the UK may retain receipts in any permitted currency, subject to standard tax reporting and transfer-pricing documentation.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (BoE) as central bank; Prudential Regulation Authority (PRA) for micro-prudential regulation; Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) for conduct and AML supervision; HM Treasury responsible for strategic financial policy and sanctions implementation.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

High feasibility for Wise B2B and Payoneer as FCA-authorised Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs). Wise holds an FCA e-money licence and offers multi-currency accounts, batch payments, and FX at mid-market rates; Payoneer operates through its UK EMI (Payoneer (UK) Limited) with similar capabilities. Legal constraints: EMIs may not issue credit or create money; funds must be safeguarded in segregated accounts; account closure risk due to adverse press or mismatched UBO documentation, requiring robust EDD. No bank status, thus no Financial Services Compensation Scheme coverage for safeguarding deposits.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% main Corporation Tax rate for profits above £250,000 from 1 April 2024; 19% small profits rate for profits below £50,000; marginal relief between £50,000 and £250,000. Related companies must share the lower and upper limits.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — the United Kingdom imposes no statutory dividend withholding tax on distributions made by a UK-resident company; dividends paid by a Cayman exempted company are likewise outside UK withholding tax. UK tax may arise on the shareholder under income tax or capital gains rules only where the shareholder is UK-resident and the distribution is received from an offshore company.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The CT600 return must be delivered to HMRC within 12 months after the end of the accounting period. The Corporation Tax liability is generally due and payable 9 months and 1 day after the end of the accounting period; large and very large companies must pay in quarterly instalments.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory monetary threshold obliges every UK taxpayer to prepare a Local or Master File. Large multinational groups with consolidated revenue of at least €750 million (approximately £644 million) must file a Country-by-Country Report (CbCR) and, on HMRC request, supply Master File and Local File within 30 days. HMRC expects contemporaneous, robust transfer pricing documentation whenever aggregate cross-border controlled transactions exceed £1,000,000 in an accounting period; below that threshold, the company must still retain adequate records to support arm’s length pricing if HMRC opens an enquiry.

Economic substance scope: The United Kingdom applies a de facto economic substance test through the common law doctrine of central management and control: a Cayman exempted company that is managed and controlled from the UK is treated as UK tax resident, regardless of its offshore registration. Separately, the Cayman Islands Economic Substance Regulations require the exempted entity to file an annual economic substance notification and, where it is engaged in a relevant activity, to demonstrate adequate purpose and presence within the Cayman Islands. A multilingual localization agency undertaking active trading will generally be outside the Cayman ESR regime if it neither holds UK qualifying assets nor performs geographically mobile services from Cayman; however, the UK deemed-residence risk is the primary substantive compliance exposure and supersedes Cayman tax exemption.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

UK Tax Evasion / Non-Filing of CT600 with Concealed UK Permanent Establishment

Statutory sanction: HMRC may assess unpaid tax up to 100% in penalties, plus interest and late payment surcharges; criminal liability for cheating the public revenue or fraudulent evasion of tax attracts an unlimited fine and up to 7 years’ imprisonment. Finance Act 2020 additionally creates a corporate offence of failure to prevent facilitation of tax evasion.

Director personal liability: A director who knowingly participates in concealing the UK PE may be personally liable for civil penalties of up to £5,000 per accounting period under FA 2008 Senior Accounting Officer obligations; on liquidated insolvency, HMRC may pursue a contribution order; travel bans and sequestration of assets may accompany criminal conviction.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Abusive Arrangement

Statutory sanction: HMRC will disregard the offshore structure under the Ramsay and GAAR anti-abuse principles, assess UK Corporation Tax on the worldwide trade profits, apply penalties of up to 100% of the tax understated, and impose restrictions under the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986. The Cayman Registrar may also strike off the exempted company for non-compliance with the Economic Substance Regulations.

Director personal liability: Directors or shadow directors may be disqualified for up to 15 years, barred from managing a UK company, and held personally responsible in restitution proceedings for diverted tax. Civil courts may impose travel restrictions and asset freezes; custodial sentences are possible where false documents have been submitted to HMRC.
Transfer Pricing / Profit Shifting Evasion

Statutory sanction: HMRC will adjust pricing under Part 4 TIOPA 2010 to the arm’s length result; penalties for deliberate or careless inaccuracies on CT600 can reach 100% of the tax suppressed, plus interest and tax-geared penalties. Anti-abuse rules may also recharacterise the arrangement as being a UK permanent establishment of the Cayman entity.

Director personal liability: A director who is knowingly aware of artificial transfer pricing and returns no UK tax may be personally pursued for civil penalties and, under Finance Act 2020, prosecuted for failure to prevent the facilitation of tax evasion, carrying imprisonment of up to 5 years and an unlimited fine.
Withholding Tax Non-Compliance on Royalties and Interest

Statutory sanction: The payer remains liable to account for the 20% withholding tax to HMRC; additional penalties of up to 100% of the amount and daily penalties for information failures may be imposed. Interest under IR (Late Payment Interest) 2015 accrues from the date the tax became payable to the date of actual payment.

Director personal liability: Where a director purposely arranges payments without operating PAYE-like UK withholding or obtains forged TRCs, they face criminal prosecution for fraudulent tax avoidance, civil liability for the outstanding withholding, travel restrictions, and disqualification as a director. The statutory personal liability can extend under the Senior Accounting Officer regime to a £5,000 periodic penalty per failure.
