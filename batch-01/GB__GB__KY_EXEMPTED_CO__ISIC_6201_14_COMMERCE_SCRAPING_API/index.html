Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Not permissible as the statutory registered address. The Companies Act (2024 Revision) requires the company to maintain a registered office in the Cayman Islands at a physical location operated by a licensed corporate services provider under the Companies Management Law (2017 Revision). A 'virtual office' consisting solely of mail-forwarding or a non-supervised shell address does not satisfy the statutory requirement and would expose the company, its directors, and its registered agent to regulatory sanction. For the ISIC 6201 data/API gateway business, remote and off-site operations are commercially acceptable, but the registered office must remain a physical, staffed location; the Economic Substance Law (2019 Revision) may additionally require genuine management and employment substance within the Cayman Islands if the activity constitutes a 'relevant activity' such as distribution and service centre business or intellectual-property business.

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registrar of Companies, General Registry of the Cayman Islands (electronic filing via the CIRIS portal, www.registry.gov.ky); UK overlays: Companies House special register of overseas entities and HM Revenue and Customs (HMRC).

Governing statute

Cayman Islands Companies Act (2023 Revision): ss. 4 (mode of incorporation), 26-29 (exempted companies), 32 (registered office), 36 (share capital), 52-53 (register of directors and officers). UK overlay: Economic Crime (Transparency and Enforcement) Act 2022, Part 1 (overseas entity registration); HMRC Corporation Tax registration for UK tax-resident operations.

Formation / constitutional filings

Application for Registration of an Exempted Company (Form 1-EX) with attached Memorandum and Articles of Association, Notice of Registered Office, and Statutory Declaration of Compliance.

Incorporation processing time

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety compliance under the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 for any UK office; Cayman Fire and Rescue inspection only if physical premises are occupied; display-screen equipment and workstation self-assessments under Schedule 6, Health and Safety (Display Screen Equipment) Regulations 1992; structural-load inspection not applicable to virtual or co-working offices.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC); portal: GOV.UK (https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/hm-revenue-customs) and HMRC Developer Hub (https://api.service.hmrc.gov.uk) for compliance APIs.

E-government filing portal

A Cayman Exempted Company is not automatically registered in the UK. It must assess UK nexus and then: (1) register for UK Corporation Tax via HMRC online service using form CT41G if it has a UK permanent establishment, carries on a UK trade, or makes a chargeable gain; HMRC issues a 10-digit Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR). (2) Register for VAT under VATA 1994 if UK taxable turnover exceeds the threshold or, if the company is non-established, from the first UK taxable supply; the application is made online through HMRC's VAT registration service (VAT1) and produces a GB VAT number. (3) Where physical goods are imported/exported, obtain a UK EORI number through HMRC. (4) Appoint an HMRC-authorised agent via form 64-8 for remote compliance. (5) Maintain digital records under Making Tax Digital for all VAT returns.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (Cayman) plus Territorial_Scope (UK). The company is an opaque non-resident corporation; it is not pass-through eligible. UK corporation tax applies only to profits attributable to a UK permanent establishment and to certain UK-source income (e.g. UK property), not to worldwide income if central management and control remains in the Cayman Islands.

Available election forms

The United Kingdom has no check-the-box election. Entity tax classification is non-elective under the Corporation Tax Act 2009/2010 and case law. Available statutory elections include: (i) VAT group registration (VATA 1994 s.43); (ii) option to tax land and buildings; (iii) corporation tax group relief elections; (iv) adjustments/elections within the hybrid and other mismatch rules (TIOPA 2010 Part 6); and (v) election for certain intangible fixed assets/capital allowances. UK residence itself cannot be elected; it follows central management and control.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

£90,000 taxable turnover in a rolling 12-month period (registration threshold). Non-established businesses have no threshold and must register from the first UK taxable supply; digital/e-service suppliers to UK consumers may be deemed taxable even without UK establishment.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For goods: zero-rating applies when goods are exported from the UK to a destination outside the UK and removed from the UK within 3 months of the time of supply; the supplier must hold export evidence (customs declaration and transport documents) and retain it in original form. For API/scraping services supplying customers outside the UK, the correct VAT treatment is generally outside the scope of UK VAT under place-of-supply rules rather than zero-rating: B2B services are treated as supplied where the customer belongs (outside UK), and B2C digital/e-services supplied to non-UK consumers are likewise outside UK VAT; ancillary services to exported goods (e.g. freight relating to export) can be zero-rated under VATA 1994 s.30(3).

  • Customs Export Declaration submitted via HMRC CDS with item-level commodity code, exporter reference, net mass, destination country and export licence exemption.
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, CMR, or other transport evidence proving the goods left the UK and arrived in the foreign destination.
  • Commercial Invoice and/or supply contract dated at time of supply.
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or equivalent bank/Stripe/cryptocurrency settlement record evidencing receipt of export sale proceeds from the overseas customer.
  • Packing list and weight/volume documentation supporting the export consignment.

The UK does not impose real-time invoice clearance or digital stamping. VAT-registered businesses must issue VAT invoices with all statutory content within 30 days and may use compatible electronic invoicing. Making Tax Digital (MTD) for VAT is mandatory: VAT records must be kept digitally and returns submitted via HMRC-recognised APIs/functional compatible software. Customs and export declarations must be submitted electronically through the Customs Declaration Service (CDS); businesses moving goods require an EORI number.

HMRC can require evidence for claimed zero-rating and may issue assessments up to 6 years (20 years in deliberate/fraud cases). Where evidence is missing or false, zero-rating is denied and the supplier must account for 20% UK VAT plus interest. Penalties under FA 2007 Sch 24 can reach up to 100% of the VAT avoided for careless/deliberate inaccuracies; fraudulent evasion of VAT is criminally punishable under VATA 1994 s.72 (unlimited fine and/or imprisonment up to 7 years). HMRC may also impose security requirements, suspend EORI/VAT registrations, and clawback refunds year by year.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The United Kingdom has maintained full current and capital account convertibility since the abolition of exchange controls in 1979, consistent with Article VIII of the IMF Articles of Agreement. No prior central bank approval is required for equity injections, intra-group loans, dividend distributions, or profit repatriation executed through UK bank accounts. The Cayman Islands likewise impose no exchange controls, permitting unrestricted movement of GBP, USD, EUR, and USDC. Practical constraints are limited to (i) OFSI financial sanctions screening, (ii) UK Money Laundering Regulations 2017 enhanced due diligence for offshore entities, and (iii) Cayman beneficial ownership and economic substance filings. Profit remittance is tax-efficient because a Cayman exempted company pays no Cayman income tax and UK withholding taxes do not apply to distributions made by a non-UK-resident company; however, UK banks may apply a source-of-funds review where a distribution occurs within six months of a capital injection.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion regime applies in the United Kingdom or the Cayman Islands. Receipts from API subscription, proxy gateway, and data-commerce sales may be retained in any freely convertible currency for an indefinite period. The treasury blueprint recommends holding a USD concentration account for Stripe and USDC settlements, a GBP disbursement account for UK operating costs, and a EUR float account for EU supplier payments. USDC inflows should be converted to fiat within one business day via an FCA-registered cryptoasset venue to eliminate unhedged stablecoin balance sheet volatility; no conversion ratio or surrender timeline is mandated.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (Prudential Regulation Authority) and HM Treasury (Exchange Control and Financial Sanctions Policy); home-state regulator for the Cayman entity is the Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA), with the Cayman General Registry administering the Companies Act and the Economic Substance regime.

Cayman Annual Return and Annual Fee (Companies Act, as revised, s. 200) - exempted company annual confirmation of particulars On or before 31 January each year for the preceding calendar year; newly incorporated companies pay a pro-rated first-year fee at the next 31 January $610 USD Late penalty of CI$500 (US$610) after 31 January; two consecutive years of non-payment triggers administrative strike-off; struck-off company loses transactional capacity and restoration requires Court application (costs typically US$2,500+)
Economic Substance Notification (ESN) under the International Tax Co-operation (Economic Substance) Law Annually within 12 months after the financial year end (e.g., by 31 December 2025 for a 31 December 2024 year end) $610 USD Failure to file attracts a penalty of up to US$10,000; continuing non-compliance accrues penalties of up to US$10,000 per day and may lead to administrative enforcement including strike-off
Full Economic Substance Return (ESR) - mandatory only if the exempted company is tax resident in the Cayman Islands and carries on a relevant activity (e.g., intellectual property business re API/software licensing); otherwise notification-only status applies Within 12 months after the financial year end on an annual recurring cycle $3659 USD Initial penalty of US$10,000 for failure to satisfy the economic substance test; up to US$10,000 per day for continued default; enhanced cumulative penalties up to US$100,000 and referral for strike-off in persistent cases
Beneficial Ownership Register annual confirmation (Beneficial Ownership Transparency Act, as revised) - attestation of current UBO data following any share transfers Annually aligned with the Annual Return calendar (on or before 31 January reflecting share transfer events during the preceding year) $0 USD Criminal offence for the company and each defaulting officer; fine up to CI$25,000 (US$30,490) and/or imprisonment up to 2 years; daily default fines may apply pending rectification
UK Confirmation Statement (CS01) under the Overseas Companies Regulations 2009 - annual statement applicable only if a UK establishment or branch is registered Within 28 days after the end of each 12-month review period measured from the date of UK establishment registration $43 USD Late filing is a criminal offence; fixed fine up to £5,000 (US$6,350) plus possible daily default fines; persistent default may lead to strike-off of the UK establishment registration
UK Corporation Tax Return (CT600) with computations - applicable only if the exempted company is centrally managed and controlled in the UK (UK tax residence) or derives UK-source taxable income No later than 12 months after the end of the accounting period (or 3 months after HMRC issues a notice to file, if later); corporation tax payment due 9 months and 1 day after period end $0 USD Late-filing penalties from £100 to £1,500 per return plus daily penalties up to £50/day for delays beyond 6 months; up to 100% of tax liability for deliberate error; statutory interest on late-paid tax
