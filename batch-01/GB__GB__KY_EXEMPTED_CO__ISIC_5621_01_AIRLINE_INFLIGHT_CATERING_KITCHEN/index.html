E-government filing portal

20%

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exported from the UK to a destination outside the UK may be zero-rated where the supplier retains full evidence of removal within the statutory time limits; supplies of inflight catering food and drink to airlines for international routes qualify under Schedule 8 VATA 1994 and VAT Notice 48, provided the aircraft departs on an international flight and the goods are delivered directly to the aircraft.

  • Customs Export Declaration (C21 via Customs Declaration Service)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FX repatriation evidence)

Making Tax Digital (MTD) requires VAT-registered businesses to keep digital VAT records and file returns via HMRC-compatible software; there is no government e-invoicing pre-clearance or fiscal stamping regime, but all export declarations must be submitted electronically through the Customs Declaration Service (CDS).

HMRC can claw back zero-rated VAT where export/repatriation evidence is not held or is false, assessing penalties of up to 100% under FA 2007 Schedule 24 and interest from the time of supply; fraudulent zero-rating claims may also lead to criminal prosecution under VATA 1994 and cancellation of VAT registration with retrospective effect; strict compliance with VAT Notice 48 is required for inflight catering supplies.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The UK operates a fully liberalised capital and current account. Sterling is freely convertible into any currency for trade, investment, profit remittance, dividends, branch remittances, loan repayments and capital movements. There are no exchange-control approvals, no limits on foreign-currency accounts within UK banks, and no withholding or clearance obstacle on remitting profits to a Cayman exempted company. The only frictions arise from bank AML/KYC diligence, HM Treasury financial sanctions screening, and corporation tax filing obligations if the Cayman company has a UK permanent establishment.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. The UK imposes no statutory timeline for repatriation, no mandatory surrender ratio, and no conversion obligation for export proceeds or inflight catering contract receipts. Receipts may be retained in GBP, USD, EUR or any freely deliverable currency with an authorised UK bank, subject only to normal money-laundering record-keeping and tax documentation requirements.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (Prudential Regulation Authority) and HM Treasury. There is no separate 'Foreign Exchange Administration' because the United Kingdom abolished exchange controls under the Exchange Control Act 1979; sanctions and AML policy sit with HM Treasury, while banking supervision is exercised by the Bank of England through the Prudential Regulation Authority.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility: Medium-High for initial treasury operations. FCA-authorised electronic money institutions and payment institutions, such as Airwallex, Currencycloud, Revolut Business and Wise, permit fully remote onboarding of Cayman exempted companies and provide multi-currency IBANs for GBP, EUR and USD, plus SWIFT MT103 and local clearing capabilities. Legal constraints: an EMI is not a bank; it does not hold a UK banking licence, cannot provide overdrafts or deposit-taking services, and customer funds are safeguarded under the Electronic Money Regulations 2011 rather than protected by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: For UK tax purposes, transfer pricing documentation (Local File) is mandatory for transactions with connected parties exceeding £1 million in aggregate for goods and services, and £100,000 for intangibles; SMEs (turnover ≤ £25.8m, balance sheet ≤ £12.9m, employees ≤ 250) are exempt unless part of a large group. Master File and Country-by-Country Report required for groups with consolidated revenue ≥ €750 million.

Economic substance scope: As a Cayman Exempted Company, the entity is subject to Cayman Islands Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) if it is tax resident in the Cayman Islands. However, if the entity is UK tax resident (central management and control in the UK), it falls outside Cayman ESR but must satisfy UK substance requirements for tax residency and transfer pricing compliance.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Penalties up to 100% of tax understated; criminal prosecution leading to imprisonment up to 7 years; unlimited fines

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for penalties if they knowingly participate; potential disqualification as director for up to 15 years; travel bans and asset freezes may be imposed
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: HMRC may recharacterize transactions, deny treaty benefits, and impose penalties up to 100% of tax due; potential criminal charges for fraudulent incorporation

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for unpaid tax and penalties; courts may pierce the corporate veil, leading to personal asset seizure and imprisonment for fraud
Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Fines up to £500,000 or 10% of turnover; criminal prosecution for money laundering; suspension or revocation of operating licenses

Director personal liability: Directors can be personally fined and imprisoned up to 14 years for money laundering offences; regulatory prohibition from holding directorship
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Adjustment of profits by HMRC; penalties up to 100% of additional tax due; interest on underpaid tax; potential criminal penalties for deliberate non-compliance

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for penalties if they knowingly approved non-compliant pricing; disqualification and personal financial liability for tax shortfall
