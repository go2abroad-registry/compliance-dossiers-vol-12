E-government filing portal

Step 1 – Nexus determination: A Cayman Exempted Company (KY_EXEMPTED_CO) with no UK registered office and no UK central management and control is not UK tax resident (UK Corporation Tax residency follows the central management and control test per Wood v Holden [2006] EWCA Civ 26). Corporation Tax registration is required only if a UK permanent establishment (fixed place of business, construction presence exceeding 12 months, or dependent agent) arises under CTA 2009 s.19 and OECD Article 5 principles; passive cloud-based sales of AI subtitle and voice-cloning services through Stripe and Wise do not create a UK PE. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — home jurisdiction treatment: the Cayman Exempted Company holds a statutory 20-year Tax Exemption Undertaking under the Cayman Tax Concessions Act s.6, guaranteeing absence of Cayman income, corporate, capital, and withholding taxes. UK identity: the entity is a non-UK resident opaque corporation outside the charge to UK Corporation Tax absent a UK permanent establishment (CTA 2009 s.5(3)); it is within the UK VAT net only for (a) B2C digital services supplied to UK private consumers (place of supply determined by recipient residence under VATA 1994 Sch. 4A para. 14) and (b) any other UK-supplied standard-rated services; UK-source withholding tax at 20% may apply to UK-source copyright or patent royalties paid by UK payers to the Cayman entity under ITA 2007 ss.905-909 and CTA 2010 Part 21, with no comprehensive UK-Cayman double tax treaty available to reduce such withholding, although limited relief may exist under the UK-Cayman Tax Information Exchange Agreement and OECD BEPS participation.

Available election forms

The United Kingdom does not operate a US-style check-the-box election. Available statutory elections relevant to the Cayman entity: (1) Voluntary VAT registration under VATA 1994 Sch. 1 para. 9 even below the GBP 9,000 digital-services threshold, enabling recovery of UK input VAT on UK costs (cloud hosting, AI training datasets, studio software); (2) Election to report B2C digital services through the non-UK OSS scheme for a calendar year, irrevocable within that year; (3) Partial exemption / de minimis election: the entity may elect to apply the standard partial exemption method or the minimal-use simplification to attribute input VAT to foreign (outside-the-scope) supplies; (4) Hybrid mismatch: The Hybrid and Other Mismatches Regulations (SI 2019/1235) operate automatically — a Cayman exempted company is an opaque entity, so no UK-Cayman hybrid mismatch deduction denial arises, but UK counterparties bear disclosure duties; (5) Corporation Tax: no election exists to be taxed as a UK resident; UK CFC rules under TIOPA 2010 Part 9A apply automatically, including the 12-month exempt period and the notional UK tax carve-out; (6) Flat Rate and Annual Accounting schemes are generally unavailable to non-established persons, and the UK cash accounting scheme is unavailable where standard-rated taxable turnover exceeds GBP 1.35 million.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GBP 90,000 (general UK-established business threshold, 12-month rolling taxable turnover, effective 1 April 2024, increased from GBP 85,000); GBP 8,818 (pre-1 April 2024) / GBP 9,000 (from 1 April 2024) for non-UK businesses supplying digital services to UK private consumers under VATA 1994 Sch. 3A; zero threshold for non-established persons making any other taxable supply in the UK; deregistration threshold: GBP 88,000.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Physical exports of goods are zero-rated under VATA 1994 s.30(6) and Sch. 8 Group 14 where the goods are exported to a non-UK destination within 3 months of supply (6 months for approved export removal procedures) and the supplier retains specified export evidence. For pure digital services (automated subtitle transcription, AI voice cloning, dubbing delivered electronically), no physical export exists: B2B supplies are outside the scope of UK VAT where the place of supply is the customer's business establishment (VATA 1994 Sch. 4A para. 16), substantiated by a valid customer VAT registration number and, for EU customers, VIES validation; B2C e-services are outside the scope where the customer is resident outside the UK (Sch. 4A para. 14), requiring two non-conflicting pieces of evidence (billing address, IP address, telephone country code, bank/payment-card country, SIM country code). Supplies of digital services to UK consumers are standard-rated at 20% and cannot be zero-rated. UK input VAT incurred on costs attributable to foreign (outside-the-scope) supplies is recoverable by the registered supplier through the partial exemption and minimal-use attribution rules; a non-established person not registered in the UK may claim a refund of UK VAT under the 13th Directive mechanism (VATA 1994 s.39), subject to HMRC discretion, reciprocity assessment, and the requirement that the VAT was incurred on goods or services used for business purposes.

The UK statute books impose no separate FX repatriation deadline (the Exchange Control Act 1947 was repealed in 1979); the 180-day convention is applied as the international documentary benchmark for export-proceeds realisation in trade finance and transfer-pricing documentation, and the Cayman entity's treasury policy should ensure remittance of net proceeds to its Cayman bank account within 180 days of each invoice to preserve a clean audit trail. Sanctions for incorrect zero-rating or outside-the-scope treatment: (1) HMRC assesses output VAT at 20% on the full consideration with default interest under FA 2009 s.101 running from the original due date; (2) civil penalties under Schedule 24 FA 2007 (inaccuracies) up to 30% for careless errors, 70% for deliberate errors, and 100% for deliberate and concealed errors, mitigated only by qualifying disclosure; Schedule 25 FA 2007 imposes analogous penalties for underassessments; (3) retrospective clawback windows: HMRC may reopen VAT returns for up to 4 years (non-careless), 6 years (careless), or 20 years (deliberate/concealed) under FA 2008 Sch. 39, and zero-rating is disallowed where the required export evidence was not held at the time of supply and within the statutory export window; (4) criminal liability: fraudulent evasion of VAT under VATA 1994 s.72 carries up to 7 years imprisonment and/or an unlimited fine; the Criminal Finances Act 2017 creates a strict-liability corporate offence of failing to prevent the UK facilitation of tax evasion by a UK associated person, punishable by unlimited fines; (5) for digital supplies, where the supplier fails to hold reliable place-of-supply evidence (e.g., a UK IP address conflicting with a foreign billing address), HMRC applies the default UK-taxable rule, backdates the assessment, and adds penalties of up to 100%; (6) transfer-pricing and CFC recharacterisation under TIOPA 2010 Part 9A may reattribute diverted profits to UK-connected entities, with interest and penalties up to 100% of tax avoided.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The United Kingdom has full current and capital account convertibility; all exchange controls were abolished in October 1979. The Cayman Exempted Company may freely receive, convert, and remit GBP, USD, and EUR without Bank of England consent, currency rationing, or profit-repatriation licence. Profit remittance hurdles are therefore not statutory but institutional: UK banks and EMIs apply enhanced due diligence to offshore-incorporated entities, and the company must avoid creating a UK permanent establishment in order to keep service revenue out of the UK corporation tax net. The only material withholding exposure is the 20% UK royalty withholding tax if the AI voice-cloning licence is structured as a royalty rather than as a services contract; treasury documentation must characterise fees as 'post-production services' to preserve treaty-free cash flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

NONE APPLICABLE. The United Kingdom imposes no statutory surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation timeline for export receipts of a non-resident company. The Cayman Exempted Company's invoices for subtitle transcription, AI voice cloning, and dubbing can be settled in any freely convertible currency and the proceeds may remain in a Wise multi-currency wallet, a UK GBP account, or a Cayman treasury account at the company's discretion. The residual compliance duty is documentary: if the company registers for UK VAT or opens a UK branch, HMRC requires transfer-pricing documentation and VAT invoicing continuity, but no FX declaration is filed.

Central bank supervision: The Bank of England (Prudential Regulation Authority) is the central bank and supervisory authority for clearing, RTGS (CHAPS), and liquidity. There is no dedicated Foreign Exchange Administration in the United Kingdom; HM Treasury is the sovereign authority for exchange-control-derived powers chiefly exercised through sanctions and the Office of Financial Sanctions Implementation (OFSI), while the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) supervises payment institutions, electronic money institutions, and FX dealing counterparties.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

HIGH OPERATIONAL VIABILITY but with material legal constraints. Wise Business formally accepts Cayman Exempted Companies and supplies UK sort codes, EUR IBANs, and multi-currency local-rail settlement, making it the natural primary treasury rail. Stripe does not reliably onboard a Cayman legal entity as merchant of record for this AI-studio profile; the correct configuration is a UK-domiciled operating branch or a UK subsidiary holding the Stripe merchant account, with the Cayman entity acting as treasury principal under an intercompany service agreement. Key constraints: (i) EMI balances are safeguarded funds, not bank deposits, so the Financial Services Compensation Scheme does not apply; (ii) EMIs screen every payment and can freeze flows pending source-of-funds evidence; (iii) EMI contracts exclude discretionary FX advice and do not provide committed credit lines; (iv) all UBO and sanctions screening is identical to a Tier-1 bank. Treasury policy must therefore pair an EMI (Wise for disbursements, Stripe for merchant capture) with a HSBC or Standard Chartered relationship account for the Cayman treasury sweep and any future hedging.

Wise Fit: High

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Master File and Local File expected where the group has annual turnover exceeding £200,000,000 or the entity has more than 250 employees and either annual turnover exceeding €50,000,000 or a balance sheet exceeding €43,000,000. Contemporaneous Local File documentation is also recommended for controlled cross-border transactions exceeding £1,000,000.

Economic substance scope: The United Kingdom applies anti-avoidance substance rules through the Diverted Profits Tax (25% tax on diverted profits). The Cayman Islands Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) do not apply if the company is UK tax resident, but if the entity is a non-UK-resident Cayman Exempted Company carrying on a relevant activity (including distribution and service centre or intellectual property holding), it must satisfy Cayman economic substance tests annually and file an ESR return; failure can lead to KYD penalties and strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False and Deliberate Misdeclaration

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty up to 200% of the tax lost plus interest. Criminal prosecution for cheating the public revenue and fraudulent evasion of income tax: imprisonment up to 7 years and unlimited fine.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly participate can be personally assessed for civil penalties, disqualified from acting as directors for up to 15 years, subjected to travel bans, asset freezes, and confiscation orders as the corporate veil is pierced for fraudulent trading.
Failure to File / Persistent Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Automatic £100 to £200 late filing penalties; additional penalty of 10% of tax unpaid; daily interest at Bank of England base rate plus 2%. Continued non-compliance may result in HMRC estimates, asset seizures, and a liability of up to £3,000 per year for failure to provide information.

Director personal liability: Directors can be made personally liable for tax debts if the company has been dissolved to avoid payment; court may issue director disqualification, travel restriction orders, and in egregious cases imprisonment for willful non-payment of tax.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Shell Company Abuse

Statutory sanction: HMRC may adjust profits to arm's length and charge tax up to 25% on diverted profits; penalty up to 200% of the tax understated for deliberate and concealed arrangements. Interest accrues from the due date.

Director personal liability: Directors involved in designing or implementing artificial TP schemes can be personally liable for civil restitution under fraudulent trading provisions, suffer worldwide asset tracing, be prohibited from leaving the UK, and face imprisonment for up to 10 years if fraudulent evasion is proven.
AML / Money Laundering / Sanctions Violation

Statutory sanction: Unlimited fines under the Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds (Information on the Payer) Regulations 2017; forfeiture of assets; imprisonment up to 14 years for money laundering offences.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for failure to report suspicious activity; courts may issue travel bans, worldwide freezing orders, director disqualification, and personal confiscation orders; criminal prosecution can lead to imprisonment and the corporate veil is pierced where the company is a façade for criminal conduct.
