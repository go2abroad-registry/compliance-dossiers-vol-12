>=25% ownership / control

Applicable AML statute: Companies Act (2023 Revision) of the Cayman Islands, including Part II exempted company provisions and register of beneficial owners (ROBO) provisions introduced by the Companies (Amendment) Act 2020 together with the Companies (Amendment) (No. 2) Law 2017; Proceeds of Crime Act (2020 Revision); Anti-Money Laundering Regulations (2023 Revision); Anti-Money Laundering Guidance Notes issued by the Cayman Islands Financial Reporting Authority (2022 Edition); International Tax Co-operation (Economic Substance) Law (2023 Revision, as amended) for relevant activity characterisation of ISIC 4791 online retail as a potential 'distribution and service centre' activity; Beneficial Ownership Transparency Regulations and Orders 2017 (UK-Cayman exchange arrangement)
Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A purely virtual (non-physical) registered office is NOT permissible. The Companies Act (2023 Revision) requires every exempted company to maintain a registered office in the Cayman Islands at a physical address to which legal process may be served. In practice, the registered office is provided by a licensed corporate services provider or registered agent (law firm, trust company, or fiduciary), and the company itself need not lease operational premises in the Cayman Islands. For ISIC 4791 online retail conducted remotely from the United Kingdom through Stripe and PayPal rails, a virtual operational model is fully acceptable, provided the registered agent maintains a physical Cayman address, statutory registered office records, and the ROBO files. The company may not, however, use the Cayman registered agent's address as its own consumer-facing trading address or returns address for UK/EU customers; separate UK/EU fulfilment and correspondence arrangements must be established under the UK Consumer Contracts Regulation framework.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up share capital for a Cayman Islands exempted company. However, if a UK establishment is registered, the overseas company must conform to the UK's capital maintenance and disclosure requirements. No escrow account rule is applicable; the sole subscriber may allot shares at nil paid or at par as determined by board resolution and the registered office provider's policy. A registered office in the Cayman Islands is mandatory and is provided by the licensed corporate services provider.

Funding & unlock path: For an online retail company without premises, fire safety and structural inspections are not mandatory; upon opening a physical storage or returns depot, a Fire Risk Assessment under the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005, building regulation approval, electrical installation certification, and HSE registration must be obtained. If the company elects to occupy only the Cayman registered office, no industrial fire clearance is required beyond the registered office provider's own compliance.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) – GOV.UK HMRC portal (https://www.gov.uk/topic/dealing-with-hmrc)

E-government filing portal

A foreign legal entity (e.g. Cayman exempted company) with no UK establishment obtains a UK VAT number by completing HMRC's online VAT registration (Form VAT1) via the Government Gateway, supplying the certificate of incorporation, registered office address, foreign tax reference, anticipated UK taxable turnover, and details of supplies. HMRC normally issues a VAT registration certificate within 10–15 working days. For UK Corporation Tax, a non-UK resident company that carries on a trade through a UK permanent establishment or derives certain UK-source income must register online (CT form via HMRC) and HMRC issues a Corporate Tax Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR); otherwise no UK tax registration is required beyond VAT where supplies trigger registration.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign legal entity (e.g. Cayman exempted company) with no UK establishment obtains a UK VAT number by completing HMRC's online VAT registration (Form VAT1) via the Government Gateway, supplying the certificate of incorporation, registered office address, foreign tax reference, anticipated UK taxable turnover, and details of supplies. HMRC normally issues a VAT registration certificate within 10–15 working days. For UK Corporation Tax, a non-UK resident company that carries on a trade through a UK permanent establishment or derives certain UK-source income must register online (CT form via HMRC) and HMRC issues a Corporate Tax Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR); otherwise no UK tax registration is required beyond VAT where supplies trigger registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or comparable entity-status election exists in UK tax law. A Cayman exempted company is treated as non-UK tax resident and is generally outside UK Corporation Tax unless it establishes a UK PE or holds UK land/property. It may voluntarily register for VAT (a transactional registration, not an entity election) and, if jointly controlling a UK VAT group, may elect to join a VAT group under VATA 1994 s.43A. For UK property income, a non-resident company may make the non-resident landlord election to be taxed on net rental income. Hybrid mismatch rules apply automatically under TIOPA 2010 Part 6A; no election neutralises or activates them.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

UK-established taxable person: £90,000 taxable turnover in any rolling 12-month period (unless intending to exonerate from registration); non-UK-established (foreign) taxable person: first taxable supply of goods or services in the UK, with no monetary threshold applying.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from the UK to a destination outside the UK within 3 months of the time of supply, with the supplier retaining the prescribed commercial evidence (export declaration, shipping document, commercial invoice, and payment/proof of removal), qualify for VAT zero-rating. Certain offshore services, including digital and intangible services supplied B2B to a recipient outside the UK, are treated as outside the scope of UK VAT or may be zero-rated where the place of supply rules are met. For goods sold by online marketplaces and exported through fulfilment networks the same evidence-based rules apply.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD/C88 via CDS, containing the MRN)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank/SWIFT payment evidence
  • Commercial Invoice (stating UK VAT 0%, Incoterms, and exporter ID)
  • Packing List and goods description supporting the export

No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or mandatory continuous transaction controls. VAT-registered businesses must keep digital VAT records and file VAT returns via Making Tax Digital (MTD) using HMRC-approved API-based software; electronic invoicing is permitted, and HMRC requires a functional commercial invoice for exports, but there is no statutory e-invoicing clearance regime.

HMRC can review exports retrospectively (normally 4 years; up to 20 years for deliberate or concealed behaviour) and claw back incorrectly zero-rated VAT, adding statutory interest and penalties of up to 100% of the tax. Where export evidence is absent, falsified, or goods do not leave the UK within the statutory period, the supply is re-taxed at 20% UK VAT. Criminal prosecution may follow under VATA 1994 s.72 and CEMA 1979 for fraudulent evasion, with severe monetary penalties and personal liability for officers knowingly concerned.
