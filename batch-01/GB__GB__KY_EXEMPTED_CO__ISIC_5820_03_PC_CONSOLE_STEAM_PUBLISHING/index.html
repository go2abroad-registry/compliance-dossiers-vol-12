Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) — GOV.UK tax registration portal (https://www.gov.uk/topic/business-tax)

E-government filing portal

A non-UK established Cayman exempted company making taxable supplies of digital services in the UK must register for VAT directly with HMRC under the Non-Established Taxable Person (NETP) procedure using the online VAT registration service. The company must submit its Cayman certificate of incorporation, registered office/agent details, proof of economic activity, and bank account details. For UK corporation tax, a non-resident company must register with HMRC within three months of commencing a UK permanent establishment and file company tax return CT600; if no UK PE exists, no UK corporation tax registration is mandatory, but UK-source income may still trigger registration obligations. HMRC uses the 10-digit Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR) for corporation tax and a 9-digit VAT registration number (VRN) for VAT; no separate entity classification ID is issued.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

The UK does not operate a US-style check-the-box election; entity classification follows the local legal form. A non-resident Cayman exempted company is treated as an entity-level taxpayer only where it carries on a trade through a UK permanent establishment. Available statutory elections include (i) VAT grouping election under VATA 1994 s.43A, (ii) option to tax land and buildings under VATA 1994 Sch.10, and (iii) election for non-resident companies to self-assess UK corporation tax on UK-source income. Hybrid mismatch elections under TIOPA 2010 Part 6DA are required to neutralise any hybrid mismatch outcomes, but no elective entity-classification election exists in the UK.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Overseas-established suppliers: no registration threshold — VAT registration is compulsory from the first taxable supply in the UK, including electronically supplied digital games to UK consumers. UK-established suppliers: £90,000 (current UK VAT registration threshold, in force from 1 April 2024).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For physical exports of goods, zero-rating applies under VATA 1994 s.30 and SI 1995/2518 reg.129 where the supplier holds valid evidence of export and the goods physically leave the UK within statutory time limits. For digital publishing services (PC/console games sold via Steam or PlayStation Store), zero-rating as export is not applicable; instead, place-of-supply rules determine UK VAT treatment: B2B supplies to non-UK business customers are outside the scope of UK VAT (reverse charge), and B2C supplies to non-UK consumers are outside the scope if the consumer resides abroad. To substantiate the non-UK status of a recipient, two pieces of non-conflicting evidence of the customer's country of residence/business establishment are required (e.g., IP address verification, billing address, bank details, customer self-certification).

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD C88 / UK CDS)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

The UK has no mandatory real-time fiscal clearance e-invoicing system. HMRC's Making Tax Digital (MTD) for VAT requires VAT-registered businesses to maintain digital records and submit VAT returns via MTD-compatible software using HMRC's API. Invoices must comply with VATA 1994 s.16 (content of VAT invoices), but no government digital stamping or pre-clearance validation of individual invoices is required.

Fraudulent or unsupported export zero-rating claims trigger HMRC assessments, interest under VATA 1994 s.74, and penalties under Finance Act 2007 Sch.24 (up to 30% of potential lost revenue for uncareful inaccuracies; up to 70% for deliberate errors; up to 100% for deliberate and concealed errors). Criminal prosecution is available under VATA 1994 s.72 and CEMA 1979 ss.167-170, with penalties of up to 7 years' imprisonment and unlimited fines. Where export evidence fails, output tax is clawed back retrospectively from the accounting period of supply, and the entity may lose concessional nil-POAL treatment and Customs Comprehensive Guarantee eligibility.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full unrestricted convertibility on both capital and current accounts in the UK and Cayman Islands. The UK repealed the Exchange Control Act 1947 in 1979, eliminating all FX licensing and profit-remittance authorisation. The Cayman Entities Law and the Cayman Islands Monetary Authority Law impose no exchange controls. Consequently, Valve/Steam and Sony PlayStation Store royalties received in USD, EUR or GBP may be held and converted freely; no Board of Trade approval, no prior-declaration regime, and no withholding on dividend or royalty flows at the UK corporate banking layer.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Neither the UK nor the Cayman Islands imposes export-proceeds surrender rules, mandatory conversion ratios, or statutory repatriation timelines. USD and EUR gaming-platform remittances may remain in original currency indefinitely. Residual reporting is limited to standard SWIFT/OFSI sanctions screening and Bank of England BOP statistical sampling; no Form A/B/C exchange-control filings exist post-1979.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (UK monetary authority) acting under residual exchange-control competence delegated by HM Treasury; Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA) administers the Cayman Islands Dollar Currency Board (CI$1 pegged at US$1.20).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

High feasibility for parallel payment routing, Medium as a sole treasury anchor.
