Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC); official portal: www.gov.uk/topic/business-tax

E-government filing portal

A non-resident BVI Business Company has no automatic UK tax registration obligation unless it acquires a UK permanent establishment, derives UK property income, employs UK staff, or makes taxable supplies in the UK. To obtain a statutory TIN/VAT ID: (1) apply to HMRC using form VAT1A (Registration of a non-established taxable person) to obtain a GB VAT registration number; (2) register for Corporation Tax through HMRC's 'Register a new company for Corporation Tax' online service to obtain a Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR) if a UK PE or trading presence arises; (3) obtain an EORI number (GB + 12 digits) via HMRC's customs portal before undertaking any import/export declarations; (4) register for PAYE if UK employees are engaged. The BVI BC's constitutional documents, registered office certificate, and proof of economic substance in BVI must accompany any registration application.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

The BVI BC is tax-exempt under BVI domestic law (no income tax, corporation tax, capital gains tax, or VAT in the BVI). For UK tax purposes no 'check-the-box' election exists; statutory classification follows UK domestic rules: a foreign corporation is treated as opaque for Corporation Tax unless an anti-hybrid election applies under TIOPA 2010 Part 6A (e.g., election to treat a foreign transparent entity as opaque or apply switch-over rule) to counteract deduction/non-inclusion mismatches. Other available elections include: VAT margin scheme election for qualifying second-hand goods; option to tax (election for VAT on property, not applicable to goods); and election under CTA 2009 s.18A for loan relationships and derivative contracts. Where a UK-BVI double tax treaty is absent (no treaty in force), residence is tested solely by domestic law: central management and control (UK) determines UK corporate residence regardless of BVI incorporation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

The UK statutory threshold is £90,000 taxable turnover in any 12-month rolling period for UK-established businesses; non-established overseas businesses (including BVI BCs) must register for UK VAT from their first taxable supply in the UK, unless the supply is zero-rated, exempt, or subject to a reverse charge. The distance-selling threshold of £8,818 applies only to consignment sales of goods located in the EU/Northern Ireland at the time of sale.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export of intraocular acrylic lenses to non-UK destinations is zero-rated under VATA 1994, Sch. 8, Group 7 (Exports) provided the goods are physically exported from the UK within three months of the supply, or within six months where removed under customs/duty suspension arrangements, and the supplier receives and preserves valid evidence of export, including a Customs Export Declaration, transport documents (Bill of Lading/Airway Bill), and commercial documentation showing the foreign destination and customer. Where the goods are sold to a UK VAT-registered customer for subsequent export, the supply may be zero-rated if both parties hold the prescribed evidence and conditions in SI 1995/2518 are met. Related services directly linked to the exported lenses (e.g., final assembly, quality testing) are zero-rated when the recipient belongs outside the UK.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic SAD via UK Customs Declaration Service, CDS)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill (or CMR / rail consignment note for non-sea/air exports)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank SWIFT credit confirmation evidencing receipt of foreign currency
  • Certificate of Origin (issued by chambers of commerce, where the destination customs regime requires proof of UK/EU preference)
  • Medical Device Technical File / UKCA-CE marking evidence, if requested by customs authorities for intraocular lens imports
  • Export Licence (dual-use or healthcare-relevant restrictions only where applicable)

No statutory mandate for real-time clearance, digital stamping, or fiscal pre-authorisation of invoices in the UK. E-invoicing is permitted and used voluntarily, but HMRC does not operate a continuous transaction control (CTC) system for VAT. Since April 2022, all VAT-registered businesses must keep digital records and submit VAT returns through Making Tax Digital (MTD) using HMRC-recognised software via the MTD Application Programming Interface (API). Customs declarations must be submitted electronically through the Customs Declaration Service (CDS); export declarations can be lodged via API-enabled systems or the UK 'Exports' digital service. Intrastat (EC Sales/Arrivals) returns for goods statistics require electronic filing where movement thresholds are exceeded.

HMRC operates targeted assurance reviews of zero-rated exporters, particularly medical-device traders using intermediary corporate structures. If the export evidence is missing, incomplete, or false, the supply is recharacterised as standard-rated; VAT at 20% and statutory interest are assessed and clawed back retrospectively. Civil penalties arise under Sch. 24 FA 2007: careless inaccuracy 0-30%; deliberate inaccuracy 20-70%; deliberate and concealed inaccuracy 30-100% of the VAT lost. Criminal prosecution for dishonest evasion of VAT under VATA 1994 s.72 and FA 2000 s.144 carries imprisonment of up to 7 years and/or unlimited fines; the court may also impose director disqualification and confiscation orders under POCA 2002. Retrospective clawback periods: HMRC standard assessment window is 4 years; the window extends to 6 years in the case of careless conduct and 20 years for deliberate non-disclosure/offshore arrangements. Concurrent customs penalties under the Taxation (Cross-border Trade) Act 2018 may reach 100% of the value of the goods plus forfeiture of the consignment; a fraudulent exporter also loses eligibility for the Authorised Economic Operator (AEO) status, triggering 100% physical examination rates.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility for current and capital account transactions. No exchange control restrictions on profit remittance, dividends, or capital repatriation. UK applies no blocking codes under the Foreign Exchange Act; post-Brexit, real-time gross settlement in GBP via CHAPS.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory requirement to surrender export proceeds or convert foreign currency to GBP. Exporters may retain balances in any currency; reporting only for trade statistics via Intrastat/EC Sales List (EU exit adjustments).

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (monetary authority); HM Treasury oversees exchange control policy; no separate foreign exchange administration exists.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Medium feasibility. Legal basis under UK Electronic Money Regulations 2011 (EMR). Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Revolut Business, Wise Business, MultiPass) may offer GBP/IBAN accounts but often refuse high-risk medical manufacturing. EDD enhanced scrutiny; requires UK registered address and economically linked activity to avoid de-risking.
