Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Not permitted for the statutory registered office. Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC), UK Government; online portal at www.gov.uk/government/organisations/hm-revenue-customs. For Cayman Islands tax information exchange matters, the Cayman Islands Tax Information Authority (TIA) under the Department for International Tax Cooperation (DITC) is the competent authority.

E-government filing portal

For a foreign entity with UK tax nexus (UK permanent establishment, UK-source income, or VAT-taxable supplies), registration is made online via HMRC's 'Register for taxes' service. A Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR) and, where applicable, a VAT registration number are issued. A Cayman Exempted Company not carrying on business or making taxable supplies in the UK is not required or eligible to obtain a UK TIN; for identifying the entity in Cayman, the Cayman Islands registered company number (e.g. Exempted Company registration number) serves as the tax reference in FATCA/CRS filings.

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

UK does not operate a 'check-the-box' system; classification follows corporation tax and residence rules. For a Cayman Exempted Company, no UK tax election is available absent UK residence/permanent establishment. Under Cayman Islands Economic Substance (ES) Regulations, the entity may be required to file a return and either demonstrate economic substance or make an 'undertaking' of being tax resident in another jurisdiction; for U.S. federal tax purposes, a Cayman entity may elect entity classification on IRS Form 8832. VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GBP 85,000 taxable turnover in a rolling 12-month period (subject to HMRC threshold changes). Non-established foreign suppliers making taxable supplies in the UK have no registration threshold and must register before supplying.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

UK VAT zero-rating applies to goods where the seller retains evidence that the goods were physically exported outside the UK to a non-UK destination within statutory time limits. Services, including fiduciary/trust administration, are zero-rated when supplied to a customer who belongs outside the UK and the supply falls within a 'goods or services supplied to a person belonging in a place outside the UK' category under the Place of Supply Rules; B2B supplies to non-UK businesses generally fall outside UK VAT, and B2C digital services may be subject to UK VAT if consumer is in the UK. An exempted offshore trust structure must maintain documentary proof of the customer's non-UK status.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Electronic through HMRC CDS/CHIEF)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Commercial Invoice / Trust Administration Fee Invoice
  • Proof of inward remittance / Foreign Exchange repatriation certificate
  • Evidence of export/export freight instructions and insurance documents

HMRC does not operate real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping. VAT-registered businesses must keep digital records and submit VAT returns via Making Tax Digital (MTD) software through HMRC's Application Programming Interfaces (APIs). Electronic invoicing is permitted but not mandatory; where used, it must support MTD-compliant digital record-keeping. Cayman Islands does not impose VAT.

HMRC may raise protective assessments, deny input tax recovery, and claw back any zero-rated or VAT refund claimed without proper evidence. Penalties of up to 100% of the understated VAT may be imposed under Schedule 24 Finance Act 2007 for careless/deliberate inaccuracies in VAT returns, plus interest and potential personal liability where the trust or entity is found to have been used for VAT fraud. Criminal prosecution for fraudulent evasion under the Value Added Tax Act 1994 s.72(1) can result in fines and imprisonment up to 7 years. Retrospective clawback may also be applied to earlier periods up to 4 years (increased to up to 20 years for deliberate default).

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Cayman Islands maintains a fully liberalized foreign exchange regime with unrestricted convertibility for both capital and current accounts. Exempted companies face no approval requirements or delays for profit remittances, dividend distributions, interest payments, or capital repatriation. This structure eliminates typical FX hurdles for fiduciary trust administration entities, enabling seamless global treasury operations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No export proceeds exist for this fiduciary trust administration activity. Exempted companies may freely maintain and operate foreign currency accounts without mandatory surrender or conversion. Remittances of income or capital occur immediately upon receipt with no statutory timelines or prescribed conversion ratios imposed by CIMA.

Central bank supervision: Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Digital EMI services are not viable for this high-risk fiduciary trust entity. Strict AML/CFT licensing under Cayman law prohibits unlicensed offshore digital payment or lending activities; any cross-border EMI would require full statutory licensing and enhanced due diligence.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% standard Cayman Islands corporate income tax rate for a non-resident exempted company outside the scope of the Cayman Islands Corporate Income Tax Act, 2025; 15% applies from fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2026 only to multinational enterprise groups with consolidated annual group revenue of at least EUR 750 million, subject to Pillar Two GloBE rules and the transitional substance-based income exclusion. UK corporation tax at 25% (main rate; 19% small profits rate) would apply only if the company becomes UK tax resident by central management and control or carries on a UK trade through a permanent establishment in the United Kingdom.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - the Cayman Islands imposes no statutory withholding tax on dividends paid by an exempted company to its shareholders, whether UK resident or non-resident. A UK corporate shareholder receives dividends under the participation exemption in CTA 2009 s.931A (tax-exempt subject to anti-abuse conditions); a UK individual shareholder is subject to dividend income tax at 8.75% basic rate, 33.75% higher rate, or 39.35% additional rate above the annual dividend allowance.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Cayman Islands: no statutory transfer-pricing documentation threshold and no Local File or Master File requirement for standard exempted companies. United Kingdom (GBP-denominated thresholds): mandatory Local File and Master File for accounting periods beginning on or after 1 April 2022 where the multinational group has consolidated annual group revenue of at least GBP 200 million and the UK entity has cross-border controlled transactions with connected parties; Country-by-Country Report notification threshold is group revenue of at least EUR 750 million, with a UK domestic threshold of GBP 200 million. Failure to maintain UK documentation may result in daily penalties up to GBP 3,000 and adverse adjustments under Part 4 TIOPA 2010.

Economic substance scope: The Cayman Islands Economic Substance regime applies under the International Tax Co-operation (Economic Substance) Act, 2018 (as amended) to relevant entities carrying on relevant activities, namely banking, insurance, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, shipping, pure holding, intellectual property holding, distribution and service centre business. Fiduciary administration of a discretionary asset protection trust is not expressly listed as a relevant activity; however, the Tax Information Authority may recharacterize the function as a service centre activity where the exempted company renders administrative services to connected parties, or as fund management where the trust operates an investment fund. The substance test requires the entity to be directed and managed in the Cayman Islands, to maintain adequate employees, premises, and operating expenditure, and to perform its core income-generating activities substantially in the Cayman Islands. An exempted company administered entirely from London without local employees or decision-making faces ESR failure, penalties, and UK CFC attribution.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

UK Tax Evasion and Controlled Foreign Company Charge

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty up to 200% of the unpaid tax under FA 2007 Sch 24 (inaccurate returns and deliberate concealment); CFC charge at 25% on apportioned passive profits plus late payment interest; criminal conviction for cheating the public revenue carries an unlimited fine and imprisonment up to 7 years (TMA 1970 s.106; FA 2000).

Director personal liability: Directors knowingly concerned in the evasion may be joined to HMRC liabilities under wilful default rules, disqualified under the Company Directors Disqualification Act 1986 for up to 15 years, subjected to travel bans and passport surrender under the Criminal Finances Act 2017, and criminally prosecuted; extradition from the Cayman Islands to the United Kingdom is available.
