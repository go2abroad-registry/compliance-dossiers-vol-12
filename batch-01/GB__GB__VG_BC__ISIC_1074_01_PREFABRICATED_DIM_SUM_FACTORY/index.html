Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC), operating through the Government Gateway portal and the Customs Declaration Service; official website: www.gov.uk/hmrc

E-government filing portal

For a non-UK (BVI) company carrying on a trade through a UK permanent establishment: (1) register for Corporation Tax within 3 months of commencing UK trading activity using HMRC's 'Register a new company for Corporation Tax' service, obtaining a 10-digit Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR); (2) register for VAT using the online registration service or form VAT1, obtaining a 9-digit VAT Registration Number (VRN) — mandatory for non-established taxable persons making taxable supplies in the UK regardless of turnover; (3) register as an employer under PAYE if UK staff are employed, obtaining an employer PAYE reference; (4) submit information via HMRC online services using a Government Gateway ID; (5) appoint a UK tax representative or fiscal agent where HMRC requires security or a UK address for a non-UK established entity; (6) retain evidence of UK trade address, UK bank account, contractual arrangements, and premises to support registration status.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

The UK has no check-the-box regime; hybrid mismatch rules under Corporation Tax Act 2010 Part 6A may apply to BVI/UK structures. Available statutory elections include: VAT group registration (separate UK entities treated as a single taxable person), option to tax land and buildings (Schedule 10 VATA), and the election to apply the special capital allowances rules for non-resident companies with UK property businesses. No territorial classification election exists; double tax relief is governed by domestic law as the UK and BVI have no double taxation treaty.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

£90,000 annual taxable turnover for UK-established businesses; effectively nil (mandatory registration irrespective of turnover) for non-established taxable persons making taxable supplies in the UK. Import VAT is chargeable on goods entering the UK from non-UK jurisdictions, including from the BVI.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to VAT-registered businesses that export tangible goods from Great Britain or Northern Ireland to destinations outside the UK (e.g., the BVI) where: the goods physically leave the UK within a reasonable time (generally within 3 months of supply); the exporter retains valid supplies of export evidence; the export declaration is made; and payment is received by the UK exporter. Services provided to BVI/non-UK businesses may be outside the scope of UK VAT under the place-of-supply rules; frozen food export requires those goods to be zero-rated only where customs and transport proof is conclusively documented.

  • Customs Export Declaration (CDS entry showing Movement Reference Number MRN)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Bill of Lading / CMR / Air Waybill or other transport evidence
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank payment confirmation showing funds credited to a UK bank account
  • Evidence of export departure (electronic customs exit confirmation, port/airport stamps, or shipping provider report), plus any applicable export health certificates for frozen food products

No real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping is required for domestic B2B e-invoicing. HMRC's Making Tax Digital (MTD) for VAT mandates digital record-keeping and VAT return submission via MTD-compatible software using HMRC APIs. Export declarations must be processed through HMRC's Customs Declaration Service (CDS) with an electronic Movement Reference Number (MRN).

HMRC will deny zero-rating where export evidence is absent, insufficient, or inconsistent; the VAT becomes payable at 20% with statutory interest. Frail or deliberate claims trigger penalties under Schedule 24 Finance Act 2007 (up to 100% of the understated VAT), Schedule 55 Finance Act 2009, and Schedule 41 FA 2008. Criminal prosecution may arise under the Fraud Act 2006 and VATA 1994, with imprisonment and unlimited fines. HMRC may issue assessments retroactively for up to 4 years in normal cases, extended to up to 20 years where loss has been brought about deliberately or dishonestly, and may claw back associated input VAT credits.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GBP is fully convertible on both current and capital account, with unrestricted profit remittance, dividend distribution, and capital repatriation for the BVI parent. There are no exchange licences, repatriation quotas, or withholding-type clearance certificates. Compliance obligations are limited to CRS/FATCA disclosures, UK permanent-establishment tax filings, transfer-pricing documentation, and OFSI financial sanctions screening; none of these constitute currency convertibility hurdles.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. The UK maintains no statutory surrender timeline, mandatory conversion ratio, or foreign-currency holding restriction for export receipts, consistent with IMF Article VIII obligations. Frozen-food export proceeds may be retained in GBP, USD, CNY/CNH, or any freely convertible currency without Bank of England approval, though HMRC transfer-pricing and taxable-income attribution rules continue to apply to the UK permanent establishment.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (Prudential Regulation Authority) for the authorisation and supervision of deposit-taking banks, with HM Treasury and the Office of Financial Sanctions Implementation (OFSI) jointly administering exchange-control-adjacent compliance. The United Kingdom abolished its separate foreign exchange administration under the Exchange Control Act 1947 repeals; no standalone FX licensing or approval authority exists.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: High as a supplementary payment-execution and FX-conversion rail; Low as a replacement for full treasury banking. Legal constraints: (1) UK e-money institutions under the Electronic Money Regulations 2011 cannot take deposits, offer lending, issue documentary letters of credit, or join CHAPS directly; (2) customer funds are safeguarded rather than FSCS-insured; (3) onboarding of a BVI-incorporated company is accepted only under enhanced due diligence; (4) SWIFT MT103 execution is dependent on an upstream sponsor-bank relationship; (5) EMIs are appropriate for supplier payouts and payroll, not for capital-escrow or high-value trade settlements.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% United Kingdom Corporation Tax main rate for profits above £250,000; 19% small profits rate for profits up to £50,000; marginal relief applies between £50,000 and £250,000. The BVI Business Company itself is tax-exempt in the BVI, but UK Corporation Tax applies on profits attributable to a UK permanent establishment or where central management and control is exercised in the UK.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. United Kingdom domestic law does not impose withholding tax on dividend distributions; the BVI Business Companies Act 2004 also imposes no tax on dividends. No UK dividend withholding tax is therefore chargeable on outbound distributions.

CT600 and supporting computations must be filed no later than 12 months after the end of the accounting period. Payment of Corporation Tax is due 9 months and 1 day after the end of the accounting period for non-large companies; large and very large companies pay by quarterly instalments.
