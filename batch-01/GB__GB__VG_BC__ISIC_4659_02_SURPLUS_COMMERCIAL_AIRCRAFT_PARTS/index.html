Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

BVI law imposes no minimum paid-up capital and no escrow or deposit accounting requirement for formation. The Company may issue shares of no par value; shares may be fully or partly paid for such consideration as the board determines. Banking AML/CTF onboarding may require a funded working capital balance, but this is a commercial condition, not a statutory formation requirement.

Funding & unlock path: Fire risk assessment and fire safety certification under the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005; Building Regulations 2010 (England/Wales) Part B compliance and structural inspection of racking and mezzanine floors; workplace clearance under the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 covering means of escape, emergency lighting, fire extinguishers, manual handling and hazardous substance storage; additional security clearance if the warehouse is located airside at a regulated aerodrome.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC), United Kingdom; official portal: GOV.UK (gov.uk) and HMRC Online Services / Government Gateway.

E-government filing portal

The BVI Business Company is a non-UK incorporated entity. For UK tax registration, first determine UK nexus. If the entity makes taxable supplies of aircraft parts in the UK, holds stock in the UK, or has a UK permanent establishment, it must: (1) obtain a Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR) using HMRC's Corporation Tax registration for non-resident companies (via HM Revenue & Customs forms CT41G / 'Register a company' process); (2) register for UK VAT under form VAT1, or VAT1A where the entity is not established in the UK, providing the BVI certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, proof of business address, director details and anticipated VAT effective date; (3) apply for a UK Economic Operator Registration and Identification (EORI) number for customs declarations if exporting or importing; (4) register for PAYE if any UK employees are engaged; (5) if HMRC so requires, appoint a UK tax representative located in the UK. All registrations are completed through the Government Gateway and HMRC's online systems; the VAT number is usually issued within 20 working days. Where the entity is already registered or only makes zero-rated exports, voluntary VAT registration is permitted to claim input VAT refunds.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

The BVI Business Company is tax-exempt in its home jurisdiction, but the UK does not provide a general 'check-the-box' election. For UK tax, the entity is treated as an opaque corporate body unless UK central management and control is established, in which case it is UK tax resident. Where the entity is non-resident, the UK territorial scope limits income tax and corporation tax to UK-source income and profits attributable to a UK permanent establishment. Available statutory elections include: an election under CTA 2009 s.18(3) for non-resident companies to be assessed on UK-source profits without a permanent establishment; UK hybrid mismatch rules may require notification or elections where the BVI entity is fiscally transparent in another jurisdiction; and certain CFC exemption elections may apply if the entity is controlled and undertaking qualifying commercial activities. There is no BVI check-the-box election relevant to UK filings.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GBP 90,000 (rolling 12-month UK taxable turnover; taxable supplies below this threshold may be registered voluntarily; distance sales into the UK exceed the threshold; non-established businesses can be required to register from the first taxable supply depending on place of supply rules)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available where the BVI Business Company exports civil aircraft surplus rotables and avionics as movable goods from the UK to a destination outside the UK, or supplies goods directly for export to an overseas customer, provided the goods are removed from the UK within 3 months of the time of supply. The supplier must hold valid shipping and customs evidence that the goods have left the UK, including where the customer is an overseas airline, MRO or trader. Direct export supplies are zero-rated only if the goods are exported in the course of the supply and the supplier obtains and preserves proof of export within the statutory timeframe. Services connected with export goods may also be zero-rated when the place of supply is outside the UK or the services are ancillary to an export supply, subject to HMRC Notice 703 conditions.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic through the Customs Declaration Service)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT payment confirmation of foreign currency receipt

The UK does not impose a mandatory real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping regime. VAT invoices may be issued electronically subject to authenticity and integrity controls. Making Tax Digital (MTD) requires all VAT-registered businesses, including non-established entities with a UK VAT registration, to maintain digital VAT records and submit VAT returns through HMRC-recognised software using API-connected services. There is no pre-clearance or HMRC digital stamp on invoices; invoices must satisfy the content requirements set out in HMRC VAT Guide (VAT Notice 700/701).

HMRC will rigorously inspect zero-rating evidence for high-value aircraft parts. Failure to retain sufficient export documentation results in VAT being assessed at the 20% standard rate, plus statutory interest. Penalties range from 30% to 100% of the VAT liability for careless or deliberate inaccuracies under Finance Act 2007 Schedule 24. Fraudulent export zero-rating claims may lead to criminal prosecution under VATA 1994 s.72, disqualification of directors, and retrospective clawback of any input VAT recovered. If foreign sales proceeds are not repatriated to a UK/EEA bank account within the 180-day deadline without a valid commercial justification, HMRC may treat the transaction as a domestic supply and recover the VAT with interest and penalties, and may also apply transfer pricing or controlled foreign company measures.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility under IMF Article VIII. No distinction between capital and current account for non-sanctioned jurisdictions; profit remittance and dividend repatriation are unrestricted for BVI intermediate companies, subject to UK anti-abuse provisions (CFC, GAAR) and BVI economic substance compliance. Sterling and major foreign currencies are freely transferable without central bank approval.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or mandatory conversion requirements exist under UK law. Exporters may freely retain receipts in foreign currency accounts and utilize them directly for overseas trade disbursements. Mandatory conversion ratio: 0%.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (Prudential Regulation Authority), HM Treasury (macro-financial oversight and residual exchange control authority), and the Financial Conduct Authority (conduct supervision). No separate foreign exchange administration exists; controls are eliminated except for sanctioned jurisdictions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low-to-Medium.
