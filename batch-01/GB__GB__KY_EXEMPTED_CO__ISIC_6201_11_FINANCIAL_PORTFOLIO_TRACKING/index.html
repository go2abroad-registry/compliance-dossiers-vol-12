Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) — United Kingdom national tax authority; principal digital portals: www.gov.uk/hmrc, Government Gateway, HMRC Online Services, and the Making Tax Digital (MTD) Application Programming Interface (API). Companies House (www.gov.uk/companies-house) administers corporate registration records.

E-government filing portal

Cayman Exempted Company (Cayman Islands-incorporated, not UK tax resident, no UK permanent establishment): no UK Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR) is required absent a UK filing obligation. If a UK corporation tax liability arises (UK-source income or trade through a UK PE), register for Corporation Tax through HMRC's registration service to obtain a 10-digit UTR. For UK VAT on B2C digital supplies to UK consumers: mandatory registration from the first supply — submit form VAT1A to HMRC as a non-UK established business; a GB-prefixed VAT registration number is typically issued within 3–4 weeks; HMRC may require security or the appointment of a UK tax representative. For EU B2C digital supplies, register under the Non-Union OSS scheme in an EU member state, not with HMRC.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (Cayman exempted company; no Cayman Islands income, capital, or indirect taxes). For UK purposes: classified as a non-UK resident foreign company on territorial scope unless central management and control is exercised in the UK (then entity-level UK corporation tax) or the company carries on a trade through a UK permanent establishment (PE profits assessable). No UK pass-through election applies.

Available election forms

No UK check-the-box election exists for foreign companies. Available mechanisms: hybrid mismatch elections under CTA 2010 Part 6A to neutralise deduction/non-inclusion and double-deduction mismatches; DTT tie-breaker residency claims (none available with Cayman — no UK–Cayman comprehensive DTT); Non-Union OSS registration election for EU B2C digital services; VAT group registration election under VATA 1994 s.43 where UK establishments exist; Patent Box election for qualifying software IP at a 10% effective rate; capital allowances elections for qualifying assets; Cayman-side exemption certificate election providing a 20-year tax exemption undertaking under the Cayman Companies Act.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

£90,000 annual taxable turnover threshold for UK-established suppliers (effective 1 April 2024); nil threshold for non-UK established businesses supplying digital services to UK consumers (mandatory registration from the first sale); the distance-selling threshold was likewise abolished for imports of goods by overseas sellers.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Electronically supplied services (SaaS financial-portfolio-tracking platform, app subscriptions via Stripe and Apple IAP) supplied to persons belonging outside the UK are outside the scope of UK VAT (place-of-supply rules: B2C at customer's usual country of residence; B2B at recipient's established location with reverse charge/self-assessment) — an effective 0% UK VAT rate with corresponding input VAT recovery if the vendor makes such outside-scope supplies and incurs UK input tax. Physical export of software on tangible media (e.g., USB drives) may be zero-rated under VATA 1994 Sch 8 Item 1/1A provided export occurs within 3 months and commercial export evidence is retained. Zero-rating does not apply to domestic UK digital supplies.

  • Customs Export Declaration (CDS/CHIEF movement reference and MRN)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / courier proof of export
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FX repatriation/bank payment evidence)
  • Binding contracts and service agreements substantiating the digital service nature
  • Evidence of customer location (IP address logs, billing address, bank country) for place-of-supply determination

No real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control, digital stamping, or invoice pre-authorisation mandate exists in the UK. Making Tax Digital (MTD) for VAT is mandatory: HMRC requires digital record-keeping and API-based quarterly VAT return submission via MTD-compatible software. E-invoicing is legal and voluntary (Peppol standard adopted for public-sector procurement); HMRC may request transaction-level data at audit, but no third-party clearance is required.

Misdeclared zero-rating or outside-scope claims expose HMRC assessments for up to 4 years (6 years for careless failure, up to 20 years for deliberate concealment), retrospective clawback of output VAT, disallowance of input VAT recovery, interest, and penalties of up to 100% of the VAT lost (FA 2007 Sch 24). Fraudulent export claims attract criminal prosecution under VATA 1994 s.72 and the Fraud Act 2006; export-control breaches in a post-Brexit context are penalised under TA 2020 Sch 22; and corporate criminal liability for facilitation of tax evasion may arise under the Criminal Finances Act 2017. HMRC additionally monitors OSS registrations and imposes registration penalties for non-compliant digital-service suppliers.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The United Kingdom maintains a fully liberalised currency and capital account. There is no exchange control or convertibility restriction on GBP for current or capital transactions, and no prior approval is required for cross-border fund movements by a Cayman Exempted Company. The Cayman Islands also has no exchange control legislation. Profit remittance of dividends, interest, royalties, or liquidation proceeds is unrestricted by the Bank of England; the only practical hurdles are UK and EU financial sanctions screening, anti-money-laundering source-of-funds verification, CRS/FATCA reporting, and the Cayman Economic Substance requirements.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender requirement exists in the United Kingdom. HM Treasury does not mandate any timeline for converting export receipts into GBP, and there is no mandatory conversion ratio. The Cayman Islands imposes no foreign-exchange control regime on a Cayman Exempted Company. Applicable timeline: N/A; mandatory conversion ratio: 0%; export proceeds may be retained in any freely convertible currency, subject to sanctions screening and existing banking covenants.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (UK monetary and prudential authority), Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA) for Cayman Exempted Company licensing and economic substance, and HM Treasury for financial sanctions administration

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium for ancillary operating liquidity and merchant settlement; Low for primary treasury clearing, escrow, or high-value cross-border settlements. Legal constraints: an offshore digital EMI that is not FCA-authorised cannot offer UK sort-code accounts or access the UK clearing system after Brexit unless covered by the FCA Temporary Permissions Regime. FCA-authorised EMIs under the Electronic Money Regulations 2011 may safeguard funds but do not provide licensed bank deposits or capital-escrow protection. For a Cayman Exempted Company, such EMIs are acceptable for smaller expense payments, Stripe/Apple IAP payout conversion, and SaaS vendor disbursements, but must not be treated as a replacement for Tier-1 bank treasury rails.
