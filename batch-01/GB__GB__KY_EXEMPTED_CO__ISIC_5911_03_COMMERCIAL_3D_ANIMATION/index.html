Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Companies House, an executive agency of the Department for Business and Trade, for a United Kingdom company; Cayman Islands General Registry for a Cayman Exempted Company.

Governing statute

Companies Act 2006, principally sections 7, 9, 13, 14, 16, 21, 31, 51 and 53. A Cayman Exempted Company cannot be incorporated under United Kingdom law; it must instead be formed under the Cayman Islands Companies Act (As Revised).

Formation / constitutional filings

Form IN01 (Application to register a company) with constitutional documents, including the proposed articles of association. A Cayman Exempted Company requires Cayman incorporation filings and does not use Form IN01.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 30 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

A United Kingdom private company limited by shares has no statutory minimum paid-up capital; one ordinary share with a nominal value of GBP 0.01 is commonly issued. No statutory escrow account is required, although subscription funds should be remitted to the company's bank account and recorded in the register of members. Cayman exempted companies generally have no minimum capital requirement unless specified in their articles or incorporation terms.

Funding & unlock path: No general pre-opening civil-defence licence applies. The operator must complete a suitable and sufficient fire-risk assessment under the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 in England and Wales, or the corresponding Scottish or Northern Irish regime, maintain emergency routes and alarms, and obtain local fire-authority input where required. Building-control approval may be required for structural alterations, server rooms, electrical installations, or increased floor loading. Workplace duties arise under the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 and related regulations, including risk assessments, display-screen, electrical, manual-handling, and emergency controls.

Paid-in capital rule: A United Kingdom private company limited by shares has no statutory minimum paid-up capital; one ordinary share with a nominal value of GBP 0.01 is commonly issued. No statutory escrow account is required, although subscription funds should be remitted to the company's bank account and recorded in the register of members. Cayman exempted companies generally have no minimum capital requirement unless specified in their articles or incorporation terms.

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

No sector-specific industrial licensing board generally applies to commercial CGI, visual-effects, or 3D-animation production. The relevant authorities are the local planning authority, local authority environmental health department, Health and Safety Executive, and, where applicable, the Environment Agency, Scottish Environment Protection Agency, Natural Resources Wales, or Northern Ireland Environment Agency.

Category: No general manufacturing or operational licence is required for ordinary office-based commercial 3D animation. Separate approvals may be required for planning use, building control, waste operations, combustion plant, electrical generation, or regulated premises activities.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) — UK tax administration via the GOV.UK Government Gateway portal; Companies House for registration of an overseas company's UK establishment; HMRC Customs Declaration Service (CDS) for export controls.

E-government filing portal

As a Cayman Exempted Company (non-UK incorporated), the standard UK registration sequence is: (1) Register any UK establishment at Companies House on Form OS01 within one month of opening; (2) Register for UK Corporation Tax on Form CT41G within three months of commencing a trade through a UK permanent establishment, obtaining a Corporation Tax UTR; (3) Register for UK VAT online via Form VAT1/Government Gateway (Form VAT4 where no UK establishment exists), submitting the Cayman Islands Certificate of Incorporation, the ELF code 549300KY_EX, and appointing a UK tax representative under VATA 1994 s.48 if there is no UK establishment; (4) Obtain a GB EORI number for export declarations; (5) Register for PAYE if UK employees are engaged. Non-resident status applies unless central management and control is exercised in the UK.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box regime exists in the UK. A Cayman Exempted Company's UK position is governed by the statutory company-residence tests (CTA 2009 ss.14-31) and, for hybrid mismatches, by TIOPA 2010 Part 6A, which imposes mandatory counteractions rather than elective check-the-box treatment. Available elections include: (i) VAT group election under VATA 1994 s.43 (allowing a non-UK-established subsidiary to be jointly registered); (ii) appointment of a UK tax representative under VATA 1994 s.48 where no UK establishment exists; (iii) the option for a non-resident company to charge UK tax on non-UK trade income through a UK PE under CTA 2010 s.18A / CTA 2009 s.19; (iv) the Non-Resident Landlord (Schedule A) deduction scheme election under ITA 2007 s.971 for UK rental income; and (v) a claim for the UK Audio-Visual Expenditure Credit (AVEC) for a qualifying British-certified 3D animation production.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

£90,000 (standard VAT registration threshold for UK-established businesses effective 1 April 2024, measured by rolling 12-month taxable turnover; the de-registration threshold is £88,000). Non-UK-established businesses (such as a Cayman Exempted Company) have no turnover threshold and must register for UK VAT at £0 if they make any taxable supplies in the UK, or if they supply digital/electronic services (e.g., delivered 3D animation files) to UK consumers under the UK One-Stop Shop rules.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: 0% VAT applies under VATA 1994 s.30(6) where the supplier retains evidence that the goods were exported from the UK to an address outside the UK within three months of sale (VAT Notice 703); direct exports to a foreign customer and indirect exports through an intermediary are both eligible if the statutory evidence bundle is retained within the prescribed time. Services: supplies of commercial 3D animation/VFX production services are outside the scope of UK VAT (treated at 0%) when supplied (i) B2B to a customer belonging outside the UK (place of supply at the customer's business establishment per SI 1992/3121), or (ii) B2C to consumers whose usual country of residence is outside the UK for electronically supplied services (the place of supply is the recipient's residence). The supplier must hold two non-conflicting items of customer-location evidence for B2C digital services and the customer's VAT registration number for B2B services; all export records must be retained for six years.

  • Customs export declaration transmitted through HMRC CDS (SAD C88/electronic declaration) evidencing the Export Movement Reference Number (MRN)
  • Bill of Lading (or Air Waybill / CMR haulage receipt) proving physical export of the goods from the UK to the foreign destination
  • Commercial invoice, contract and the overseas customer's VAT registration number (where applicable), showing the service destination and customer business status
  • Bank payment confirmation (SWIFT MT103 / Inward Remittance Certificate) evidencing receipt of export proceeds

The UK does not operate a government e-invoice clearance, digital stamping, or continuous transaction control regime. Mandatory digital reporting applies through Making Tax Digital (MTD): every VAT-registered business must keep digital records and file VAT returns using HMRC-recognised API-enabled software (mandatory for all VAT-registered businesses, irrespective of turnover, since 1 April 2022). Export declarations must be transmitted electronically via the HMRC Customs Declaration Service (CDS), which generates the Movement Reference Number (MRN) required for zero-rating evidence. Voluntary PEPPOL BIS Billing 3.0 e-invoicing applies to UK central-government procurement; HMRC has announced a roadmap towards mandatory B2B e-invoicing by approximately 2030.

HMRC subjects export zero-rating and refund claims to data-led risk profiling and pre-payment verification. If the statutory evidence bundle is not held, HMRC may assess and claw back the unsubstantiated VAT (VATA 1994 s.73), and input tax credits are recoverable only with valid documentation. Inaccuracy penalties under FA 2007 Sch.24 range up to 30% (careless), 70% (deliberate) and 100% (deliberate and concealed), reducible for prompted or unprompted disclosure; fraudulent evasion of VAT under VATA 1994 s.72 and perjury provisions may lead to criminal prosecution, imprisonment up to seven years and unlimited fines; false customs declarations attract penalties under CEMA 1979 s.167 and s.168. Assessment time limits are two years (standard), four years (careless) and twenty years (deliberate non-disclosure). Although the UK imposes no statutory FX-repatriation deadline, the configured 180-day parameter operates as the contractual banking benchmark for export revenue collection.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The United Kingdom has broad current-account and capital-account convertibility. Sterling and major foreign currencies may generally be received, held, converted, and remitted without a general exchange-control licence, mandatory central-bank approval, or statutory profit-repatriation ceiling. Restrictions arise principally from UK sanctions, anti-money-laundering and counter-terrorist-financing rules, bank risk controls, tax obligations, insolvency restrictions, contractual covenants, and information-reporting requirements. A Cayman exempted company is not automatically UK tax resident merely because it maintains a UK bank account; however, UK corporation-tax, permanent-establishment, withholding-tax, transfer-pricing, economic-substance, and management-and-control analysis must be performed according to the actual activities and governance. Distributions, shareholder-loan repayments, and service-fee remittances should be supported by corporate approvals, accounting records, arm's-length documentation where applicable, and evidence of tax compliance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The United Kingdom has no general statutory export-proceeds surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no general deadline requiring export receipts to be converted into sterling. Export proceeds may ordinarily remain in the invoiced currency or be converted at the company's discretion. Banks may request invoices, contracts, shipping or delivery evidence, customer information, and payment rationale under AML, sanctions, fraud-prevention, and correspondent-banking controls. UK customs, VAT, corporation-tax, accounting, and statistical reporting obligations remain separate from foreign-exchange convertibility.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England. Foreign-exchange conduct, payment-services, and regulated-firm supervision may also involve the Financial Conduct Authority, while HM Revenue & Customs administers relevant tax, customs, transfer-pricing, and international reporting obligations.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Medium-Low. UK electronic-money institutions and payment institutions can provide safeguarded multi-currency wallets, local collection details, card or payment services, and international transfers, but they are not banks and safeguarded funds are not equivalent to deposits covered by the Financial Services Compensation Scheme. Availability for a Cayman exempted company depends on the provider's supported-country policy, beneficial-owner residence, business activity, expected transaction profile, source of funds, sanctions screening, and correspondent-rail access. Wise Business and comparable providers may accept eligible overseas companies only where their current onboarding rules permit the entity, owners, directors, and operating jurisdictions. They may restrict cash deposits, high-risk corridors, regulated activities, third-party payments, unusual pass-through flows, and transactions inconsistent with the stated animation and VFX business. Maintain a UK bank account and a documented treasury backup rather than relying exclusively on an EMI.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Route equity subscriptions and shareholder loans from an account held in the investor's or lender's legal name directly to the Cayman company's verified corporate account through SWIFT or an approved bank payment rail. The United Kingdom generally has no universal FX purpose-code regime for ordinary corporate receipts; the remitting and receiving banks may nevertheless require a payment purpose, invoice or agreement, UBO information, source-of-funds evidence, and sanctions screening. For equity, retain board and shareholder approvals, subscription documentation, issued-share records, and accounting entries. For debt, retain the executed loan agreement, maturity, interest, repayment terms, transfer-pricing support, and withholding-tax analysis.
