Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A Cayman exempted company must maintain a physical registered office within the Cayman Islands, and the registered office holder must be a licensed corporate services provider. A pure virtual or digital-only office with no physical Cayman presence does not satisfy the statutory mandate and cannot serve as the registered office. A virtual office may only operate as an ancillary correspondence point for the company's overseas DevOps and cloud infrastructure operations, and it may never replace the statutory physical registered office.

Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registrar of Companies, Cayman Islands General Registry (Government Administration Building, 133 Elgin Avenue, Grand Cayman KY1-1000)

Governing statute

Cayman Islands Companies Act (As Revised), Part XIII (Exempted Companies) and sections 9–11 (Constitution and Memorandum/Articles); the Cayman Islands is a British Overseas Territory under UK sovereignty, so formation is registered domestically in Cayman rather than under the UK Companies Act 2006.

Formation / constitutional filings

Memorandum and Articles of Association executed by the subscriber, filed with a Notice of Registration and the registered office provider's consent to act, then sealed by the Registrar.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 14 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital and no escrow requirement. Standard structure: authorized capital of US$100 represented by 1,000 ordinary shares at US$0.10 par value; the initial paid-up allotment is 100 shares (US$10) issued at par.

Funding & unlock path: Not applicable — the Company holds no physical premises. If a limited administrative office is later opened, the landlord must evidence fire safety certification under the Fire & Rescue Services Law, structural load clearance, and an occupancy permit before staff entry.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital and no escrow requirement. Standard structure: authorized capital of US$100 represented by 1,000 ordinary shares at US$0.10 par value; the initial paid-up allotment is 100 shares (US$10) issued at par.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC), the United Kingdom tax administration; primary portal: HMRC services via GOV.UK Government Gateway, supplemented by the HMRC Developer Hub for MTD (Making Tax Digital) API filings.

E-government filing portal

For the Cayman Exempted Company (non-resident, foreign-incorporated): (1) Nexus assessment—establish whether central management and control is in the UK (potential UK tax residence) and whether a UK permanent establishment exists under CTA 2010 s.19; if neither applies, no UK Corporation Tax registration is required. (2) Obtain a GB EORI number via HMRC if importing/exporting goods through the UK. (3) Register for UK VAT as a Non-Established Taxable Person (NETP) using HMRC form VAT1/NETP online service; no threshold applies to NETPs supplying UK B2C digital services, and HMRC issues a 9-digit GB VAT identification number. (4) If a UK PE or UK tax residence exists, register for Corporation Tax via form CT41G (UK resident) or CT47 (non-resident company) to obtain a Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR). (5) Register for PAYE via Government Gateway if UK employees are engaged. Identity verification requires passport, Cayman incorporation certificate, memorandum and articles of association, and a power of attorney if an agent acts.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No UK check-the-box regime; classification follows foreign legal personality, so the Cayman Exempted Company is an opaque body corporate. UK hybrid mismatch rules (TIOPA 2010 Part 6A), the Corporate Interest Restriction, and CFC rules apply mandatorily for UK-connected persons and offer no elective mitigation. VAT facilitation elections: registration under the Non-Union One-Stop-Shop (OSS) for EU-distance B2C digital services and optional MTD VAT reporting via HMRC-approved software.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

£90,000 taxable turnover in a rolling 12-month period (or £90,000 projected within the next 30 days) for UK-established businesses. A Non-Established Taxable Person (NETP) has no threshold and must register from the first taxable supply in the UK; non-established suppliers of B2C electronically supplied services to UK consumers must register from the first supply (VATA 1994 Sch. 3A). Voluntary registration is available; imports of goods trigger UK import VAT from the first import.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: zero-rated under VATA 1994 s.13/Sch. 8 where the exporter proves removal from the UK to a destination outside the UK within 3 months of the supply (or 12 months for specified goods), holds valid transactional and transport evidence under VAT Notice 703, and retains proof of freight and delivery terms. Services: a UK supply of services is outside the scope/zero-rated where the place of supply is outside the UK—B2B services to a business customer belonging outside the UK, and B2C digital services supplied to a customer whose usual residence is outside the UK; the supplier must hold corroborating evidence of the customer's non-UK business or residential status. Recoverable input VAT may be reclaimed through the VAT return (UK-established) or the 13th Directive refund mechanism (HMRC form VAT65) for non-UK established businesses.

  • Electronic Export Declaration lodged via HMRC Customs Declaration Service (CDS)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export from the UK
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank/FX settlement records evidencing payment from the overseas buyer
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Evidence of exporter status (GB EORI number registration)
  • Proof of removal within statutory time limits (3 or 12 months from supply)

No mandatory real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, or digital stamping regime. Making Tax Digital (MTD) mandates digital record-keeping for VAT and quarterly VAT return filings via HMRC-approved software through HMRC's MTD application programming interface (API). Customs declarations for exported goods must be lodged electronically through the HMRC Customs Declaration Service (CDS).

HMRC may conduct enhanced verification of export zero-rating claims, demand digital records, and issue retrospective assessments. Civil penalties: up to 70% of the VAT for deliberate and concealed inaccuracies under FA 2007 Sch. 24; a separate dishonest-evasion penalty up to 100% of VAT under FA 2007 s.63. Criminal prosecution under VATA 1994 s.72 can result in unlimited fines and imprisonment of up to 7 years. Assessment time limits extend to 20 years in fraud cases (VATA 1994 s.77). Rejected claims trigger re-levied VAT at 20%, input tax clawback, statutory interest on unpaid VAT, and possible joint-and-several liability notices against directors or connected parties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Both the UK and Cayman Islands operate fully liberalised capital and current accounts. The UK has no exchange control regime since the repeal of the Exchange Control Act 1947; GBP, USD and EUR balances may be held and repatriated freely. The Cayman Islands Dollar (KYD) is pegged to the US Dollar at 0.80 KYD per 1 USD buffered by statutory issuances, but there is no restriction on conversion, profit remittance, dividend distribution, or loan servicing by an exempted company. A Cayman entity must merely remain in good standing with CIMA, satisfy any Economic Substance (International Tax Co-operation) reporting obligations, and pass the Cayman Companies Act solvency test before distributions are declared.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export-proceeds surrender requirement or mandatory conversion ratio in either the UK or Cayman Islands. Exporters and service providers in both jurisdictions may retain, invoice, and disburse receipts in any currency without forced repatriation or conversion. The only binding constraints are UK/EU sanctions (OFSI/OFCAC screening), FATCA/CRS reporting, and the bank's own AML/EDD risk appetite.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (Prudential Regulation Authority) and HM Treasury for the United Kingdom; Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA) for the Cayman exempted company. CIMA administers the Cayman Islands Currency Law (KYD), while the Bank of England operates the UK RTGS/CHAPS payment infrastructure and monetary policy framework.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

High for UK FCA-authorised Electronic Money Institutions (E-Money Regulations 2011) and banking-as-a-service platforms sponsored by UK partner banks (e.g., ClearBank, Modulr), but with critical legal constraints. Mainstream EMIs such as Revolut Business, Wise, Tide and Airwallex UK generally exclude Cayman Islands-incorporated entities from onboarding or treat them as high-risk, often requiring a UK subsidiary or UK-tax-resident branch as the account holder. Route inbound capital from the Cayman exempted company or its parent to the UK account via SWIFT MT103 using a clear 'Capital Contribution / Share Capital Injection' narrative and the receiving bank's direct-investment reason code (typically code '700' or the Bank of England/ONS balance-of-payments equivalent). Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Cayman Islands: no statutory transfer pricing documentation threshold. United Kingdom: mandatory Master File and Local File preparation and Country-by-Country Reporting apply to MNE groups with consolidated group revenue of GBP 750,000,000 or more where a UK entity or UK ultimate parent is present. A Cayman entity transacting with a UK connected party should be included in the UK group's TP documentation.

Economic substance scope: The Cayman Islands International Tax Co-operation (Economic Substance) Act requires every tax-resident entity carrying on a 'relevant activity' to maintain substantive economic presence in the Cayman Islands proportionate to its core income-generating activities. Relevant activities include IP business, distribution and service centre business, headquarters business, financing and leasing, and pure equity holding. A developer infrastructure company holding software IP or charging related-party fees for marketing, distribution or API services must show real local staff, premises, expenditure, and board management in Cayman or declare nil/non-applicable status where no relevant activity exists. UK CFC rules and the UK 'diverted profits tax' may independently recharacterise profit shifted into a low-tax Cayman entity lacking economic substance.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / CFC Avoidance / Sham Arrangement

Statutory sanction: HMRC may raise discovery assessments for up to 20 years, impose penalties up to 100% of the tax for deliberate and concealed behaviour under FA 2007 Schedule 24, add interest, and apply the CFC charge under CTA 2010 Part 9A. Diverted profits tax of 25% may also apply to arrangements designed to route UK-derived profits to the Cayman entity.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners can be made personally liable through director disqualification under CDDA 1986, travel bans, and confiscation orders. Fraudulent evasion of UK corporation tax is indictable as Cheating the Public Revenue, carrying an unlimited fine and up to 14 years' imprisonment.
Cayman Economic Substance / Shell Company Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: The Tax Information Authority may impose automatic penalties of CI$10,000 plus CI$1,000 per day after non-compliance notice; upon indictment the penalty can rise to CI$100,000; non-provision of information carries a default penalty of up to CI$500,000. The Registrar may also strike the company off the register.

Director personal liability: Directors, shadow directors and controlling officers risk personal penalties, disqualification from acting as directors in the Cayman Islands, civil restitution, and criminal imprisonment for knowingly making false statements in the Economic Substance Return.
AML / Beneficial Ownership / Corporate Transparency

Statutory sanction: Under the Cayman Proceeds of Crime Act and the Beneficial Ownership Transparency Act, fines can reach CI$500,000 and imprisonment up to 15 years for money laundering and disclosure failures. UK anti-money laundering and corporate transparency regimes may additionally impose civil fines and confiscation orders where UK persons are shadow directors or beneficial owners.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced: directors and controlling shareholders can be held personally liable for civil restitution of dissipated assets, subjected to travel bans, asset freezes, disqualification orders, and imprisonment for AML offences.
