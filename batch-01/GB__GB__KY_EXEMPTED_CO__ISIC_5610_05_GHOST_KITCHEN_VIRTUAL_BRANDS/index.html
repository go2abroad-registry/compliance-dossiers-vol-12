Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC), 100 Parliament Street, London SW1A 2BQ; official digital portal: Gov.UK (https://www.gov.uk), with Corporation Tax, VAT, PAYE, and Making Tax Digital services accessible through the HMRC Government Gateway and Application Programming Interfaces (APIs).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Register the Cayman Exempted Company with Companies House as an overseas company (Form OS IN01) within one month of opening a UK establishment. Step 2: Notify HMRC for Corporation Tax within 3 months of commencing UK trade using the Corporation Tax new tracker (Form CT41G); HMRC issues a 10-digit Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR), which is the statutory tax identification number. Step 3: Register for UK VAT via Gov.UK (Form VAT1 for a UK-established business, or VAT1A for a non-established taxable person) to obtain a GB VAT registration number; for non-established taxable persons there is no turnover threshold and registration is mandatory from the first taxable supply. Step 4: Register for PAYE (employer reference) if UK employees are engaged. Step 5: Maintain the Cayman exempted company's annual Economic Substance declaration and registered office filing, and disclose the UK PE in Cayman regulatory filings.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (UK Corporation Tax is chargeable on profits attributable to the UK permanent establishment at 19% small profits rate, 25% main rate, or marginal relief between £50,000 and £250,000). The Cayman Exempted Company itself is a Tax_Exempt_Zone entity for Cayman income tax purposes, with no UK tax on non-UK attributable profits; the UK does not recognize check-the-box fiscal transparency for a corporate entity without a formal corporate conversion. — Step 1: Register the Cayman Exempted Company with Companies House as an overseas company (Form OS IN01) within one month of opening a UK establishment. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (UK Corporation Tax is chargeable on profits attributable to the UK permanent establishment at 19% small profits rate, 25% main rate, or marginal relief between £50,000 and £250,000). The Cayman Exempted Company itself is a Tax_Exempt_Zone entity for Cayman income tax purposes, with no UK tax on non-UK attributable profits; the UK does not recognize check-the-box fiscal transparency for a corporate entity without a formal corporate conversion.

Available election forms

The United Kingdom has no check-the-box election; entity classification follows legal corporate form unless a transparent vehicle (e.g., partnership or LLP) is used, subject to OECD hybrid mismatch rules under TIOPA 2010 Part 6A. Available statutory elections relevant to a ghost-kitchen operator: (i) VAT group registration election under VATA 1994 s.43; (ii) option to tax (election to waive exemption) on UK land under VATA 1994 Sch 10; (iii) partial exemption de minimis election; (iv) annual accounting scheme election for VAT; (v) unilateral credit relief election under TIOPA 2010 s.18 for foreign taxes. No UK-Cayman double taxation agreement exists, so no treaty election is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

20%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

£90,000 taxable turnover in a 12-month rolling period (effective 1 April 2024, frozen until 1 April 2026); deregistration threshold £88,000; for non-established taxable persons the threshold is £0 (mandatory registration from the first taxable supply).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under VATA 1994 s.30 and the VAT Export and Removal of Goods Regulations 2014 (SI 2014/106), goods physically exported from the UK to a destination outside the UK are zero-rated where the goods are exported within 3 months of the time of supply and the supplier holds prescribed official evidence of export. Services, including commercially valuable B2B delivery/logistics services to non-UK customers, are outside the scope of UK VAT under place-of-supply rules, entitling the supplier to recover input VAT. For a ghost-kitchen operation, export transactions will be limited or atypical; where hot food or branded meal kits are dispatched cross-border, the trader must show actual removal from the UK and preserve customer non-residency evidence.

  • Customs Export Declaration (CDS/CHIEF) bearing the Movement Reference Number (MRN)
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or CMR consignment note evidencing physical export from Great Britain or Northern Ireland
  • Commercial invoice stating export terms, Incoterms, and overseas destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or Swift MT103 / bank credit remittance evidence showing receipt of payment in foreign currency
  • Evidence of the non-UK customer's identity and residence (e.g., overseas VAT registration number or registered address for B2B; delivery address and payment card records for B2C)

No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or mandatory tax-authority API clearance is in force. However, Making Tax Digital (MTD) is mandatory for all VAT-registered businesses, requiring digital record-keeping and MTD-compatible software with digital links for VAT return submission. The UK has adopted the Peppol BIS 3.0 e-invoicing interoperability standard, with progressive mandates for central-government procurement (e.g., NHS pilot) and a wider statutory e-invoicing mandate anticipated by 2030; no live private-sector clearance regime currently exists.

HMRC conducts post-clearance audits and may withdraw zero-rating where export evidence is missing, insufficient, or false, issuing C18 assessments for under-declared VAT with default interest. Assessment windows are 4 years for careless error and 20 years for deliberate behavior; civil penalties under FA 2007 Sch 24 reach up to 100% of the VAT lost (30% careless, 70% deliberate, 100% deliberate and concealed, mitigated by up to 50% for unprompted disclosure). Fraudulent evasion of VAT is criminal under VATA 1994 s.72, punishable by unlimited fines and up to 7 years' imprisonment, with 'naming and shaming' publicity and joint-and-several liability notices available against directors and controlling officers. Export records must be retained for at least 6 years.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

GBP is fully convertible on both current and capital accounts under the Bank of England Act 1998. No licensing, advance approval, or repatriation restrictions apply to the Cayman Exempted Company's profit remittances, dividends, or capital liquidation proceeds to non-resident shareholders. UK withholding is not triggered on non-UK-source income; the only mandatory gate is source-of-funds documentation under the Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds (Information on the Payer) Regulations 2017.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. The UK imposes no statutory surrender timeline, mandatory conversion ratio, or repatriation requirement for export/operating receipts; proceeds may be held in GBP, USD, or EUR clearing accounts, subject to the receiving bank's transaction-documentation requirements for all inbound international transfers.

Central bank supervision: Bank of England (central bank and Prudential Regulation Authority) with HM Treasury as the sovereign monetary and foreign-exchange policy authority; there is no dedicated UK foreign-exchange control administration.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% (main rate for Financial Year 2025/26; 19% small profits rate applies to taxable profits not exceeding GBP 50,000, with marginal relief between GBP 50,000 and GBP 250,000)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% (UK domestic law imposes no withholding tax on distributions paid by a UK resident company; similarly, a UK permanent establishment's remittance of after-tax branch profits to its foreign head office is not subject to any UK dividend withholding tax or branch profits remittance tax). Any distribution made by a Cayman Exempted Company is also free of Cayman Islands withholding tax.

Box 1 / Entity identity Names, registered office, unique taxpayer reference (UTR), and tax district reference. For a Cayman Exempted Company, the registered office in Grand Cayman and the UK PE address must both be provided; if UK-resident due to central management and control, an ordinary UK company UTR will be issued by HMRC. If the company is not UK-resident but carries on a trade through a UK PE, it registers for Corporation Tax as a non-resident company and enters the UK PE address as its UK establishment. Where the Cayman head office prepares financial statements to 31 December, but the UK trade operates to 31 March, the UK accounting period follows the period for which accounts are drawn up for UK tax purposes. For a non-resident with a UK PE, the accounting period mirrors the 12-month period of the UK PE; any change in activity creating a PE may trigger an earlier commencement date. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory CbCR filing applies to multinational groups with consolidated group turnover of at least GBP 750 million. UK Local File and Master File documentation is expected for groups/UK PEs where UK revenue exceeds GBP 200 million or cross-border related-party transactions exceed GBP 5 million. Small and medium-sized enterprises (fewer than 250 employees and either turnover not above EUR 50 million or balance sheet total not above EUR 43 million) benefit from reduced OECD transfer-pricing documentation requirements.
